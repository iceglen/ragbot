<p>The <b>District 9 male</b> was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> who participated in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. He was also a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of an unspecified prior Hunger Games, somewhere between the early-to-mid 40s.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was presumably <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for and won an unspecified edition of the Hunger Games.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He was reaped alongside a fellow <a href="/wiki/District_9_female_(75th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 9 female (75th HG)">female victor</a> for the 75th Hunger Games.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Chariot_rides">Chariot rides</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, an unknown district's tributes were <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">costumed</a> as loaves of <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> this was presumably District 9 or <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>'s tributes, since both had food-producing industries. In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, he and his district partner were dressed in golden outfits possibly designed to look like wheat or bread instead.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>It is unknown if he showed up for training or not, as he was never mentioned in the novel. However, in the film, he could be seen waiting his turn for his private session in front of the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interview">Interview</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, he wore a sleeveless, long vest with a leaf and plant motif.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After all the tributes were interviewed, they all joined hands in a display of <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a> against <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The District 9 male was killed during the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>,  the District 9 male engaged with <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a> and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a>, ending in him falling into the water and dying shortly after.
</p>
