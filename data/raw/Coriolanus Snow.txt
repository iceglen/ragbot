<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Snow lands on top.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—A phrase used by the Snow family, signaling Snow's eventual rise to power<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Coriolanus Snow</b> was an autocrat who ruled as the tyrannical <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> for more than two decades. In his youth, he was known by the nickname <b>Coryo</b>, originally given to him by his cousin, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>.  
</p><p>Born into the wealthy Snow family, by the time of his upbringing the Snow name had begun to fade into insignificance. At a young age, he would lose both of his parents during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. He later attended the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, where he <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentored</a> the winning <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>. He later moved on to advanced military studies at the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a> and served a brief stint in the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>, demonstrating such aptitude that he tested successfully for an elite program to become an officer with the Peacekeepers, though ultimately, he did not pursue this path. While attending the University and studying advanced military strategy, he also served as an apprentice <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>, quickly instituting several new reforms to the Hunger Games, including the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a>.  
</p><p>Snow would gradually eliminate his enemies one by one, until he finally ascended to the Presidency, the highest position offered in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> and an office he presided, unchallenged, for over 25 years.  During this time, Panem became more authoritarian, with opponents of the regime regularly executed, and the Districts kept in line through the use of propaganda, intimidation, and brutality. Major uprisings did not occur as a result, with many people being too afraid of the consequences they could face if they ever tried to rebel against the Capitol. However, following the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the system that had kept the country in a state of fear and repression for decades finally began to come apart. Events that took place in the arena gave life to a new and determined movement to end the regime. As the Capitol began to crumble around him, Snow desperately tried to cling onto power, but in the end, he was unable to defeat the rebels. Following the defeat of the Capitol, Snow would face the wrath of the same people he had terrorized for so long, and Panem would at last become free.
</p><p>He is the main antagonist of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a> and <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i> and is the main protagonist of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span>"<i>I wouldn’t say he’s a hero, nor would I say he’s a villain; it’s more complex than that.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Audiobook narrator <a href="/wiki/Santino_Fontana" title="Santino Fontana">Santino Fontana</a> regarding the young Coriolanus Snow</dd></dl></div>
<p>He was born in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Snow_family" title="Capitol families">Snow family</a>, a prominent and once rich family. His parents and grandparents were well-known, but his family had fallen on hard times and his only real currency in his youth was charm.<sup id="cite_ref-Entertainment_Weekly_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Entertainment_Weekly-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 200px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a3/Young_Coriolanus_and_Tigris.png/revision/latest?cb=20230924064415" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A young Coriolanus and Tigris during the war" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="168" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Young Coriolanus and Tigris.png" data-image-key="Young_Coriolanus_and_Tigris.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a3/Young_Coriolanus_and_Tigris.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20230924064415"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a3/Young_Coriolanus_and_Tigris.png/revision/latest?cb=20230924064415" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A young Coriolanus and Tigris during the war" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a3/Young_Coriolanus_and_Tigris.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20230924064415" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="168" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Young Coriolanus and Tigris.png" data-image-key="Young_Coriolanus_and_Tigris.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a3/Young_Coriolanus_and_Tigris.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20230924064415"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Young_Coriolanus_and_Tigris.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A young Coriolanus and Tigris during the war</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>By the time Snow was 18, his family status had fallen into decline. Despite living in one of the wealthiest penthouses in the Capitol, he and his family struggled to keep food on the table and often bought most of their clothes from the black market due to lack of money. The family's troubles began when Snow and Tigris were still very young, as <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow's mother">his mother</a> died in childbirth when he was five during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, resulting in the loss of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_sister" title="Coriolanus Snow's sister">Snow's little sister</a> that she was carrying as well.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> All he kept from her was a <a href="/wiki/Silver_compact" title="Silver compact">silver compact</a>. His father, a military general named <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a>, died not long after, being killed by fire from a rebel sniper.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Despite this, he maintained an outward facade that he and his family were still doing well, though those who knew what to look for could see through it.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He had a bad habit of fixating on one thing, thinking that if he could just solve one problem, it would solve his others. This "tendency towards obsession" was hardwired in his brain, an issue that he felt could be his downfall if he couldn't learn to control it. Many rooms of the home he lived in were entirely empty and closed off due to his family selling off their possessions. Others still had extensive damage from the Dark Days which they could not afford to repair. Furthermore, he was forced to watch in sadness as many of the picture books he once enjoyed reading with his mother were sacrificed to flames to keep the family warm during the winter.<sup id="cite_ref-Audiobook_Excerpt_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Audiobook_Excerpt-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mentor">Mentor</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 235px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Snow_Gives_Lucy_Gray_Rose.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240621114227" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Coriolanus meets Lucy Gray at the train station" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="235" height="157" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Snow Gives Lucy Gray Rose.jpg" data-image-key="Snow_Gives_Lucy_Gray_Rose.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Snow_Gives_Lucy_Gray_Rose.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/235?cb=20240621114227"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Snow_Gives_Lucy_Gray_Rose.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240621114227" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Coriolanus meets Lucy Gray at the train station" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Snow_Gives_Lucy_Gray_Rose.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/235?cb=20240621114227" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="235" height="157" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Snow Gives Lucy Gray Rose.jpg" data-image-key="Snow_Gives_Lucy_Gray_Rose.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Snow_Gives_Lucy_Gray_Rose.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/235?cb=20240621114227"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Snow_Gives_Lucy_Gray_Rose.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Coriolanus meets Lucy Gray at the train station</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Snow attended the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> and was one of its top-performing students. As such, in his senior year, he was tapped as a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th</a> annual <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. He realized that something was amiss when his achievement as a top-performing student went unrecognized and he was assigned mentorship of the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> female tribute, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, who was from one of the worst-performing districts.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite his initial shock and irritation at being assigned what seemed like a throwaway tribute, Snow soon discovered that he had been given an unexpected gift. At her <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaping</a>, Lucy Gray enchanted the audience with a musical performance that made her and Snow the envy of all of the other mentors. He then showed an initiative lacking in the other mentors by choosing to meet Lucy Gray upon her arrival in the Capitol. The risky move saw him end up with her briefly in a cage in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> but also brought him more audience attention.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the days leading up the Hunger Games, Snow drew the attention of the Head <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>. Gaul was the Head Gamemaker of the 10th Hunger Games and personally made regular visits to the Academy to oversee the newly created mentor program. She was impressed by Snow's actions in meeting his tribute, even though they drew a demerit from <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, the sour and <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a>-addicted dean of the Academy.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Gaul asked the students of the Academy both to get to know their tributes through <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> and to brainstorm ways to get people more engaged in the Hunger Games, as they had proved unpopular with audiences so far. While worrying about his own tribute, Snow also became embroiled in the personal life of his classmate, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, who was born in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> and resented participating in the Hunger Games, particularly with having to mentor a former classmate.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Snow ended up getting a far more personal taste of the danger of the Hunger Games than he expected when the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> was bombed during a "tour" featuring the mentors and tributes. He was saved by his tribute, Lucy Gray Baird, further cementing the bond between them, while some of the tributes and mentors were injured or killed in the bombings.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Sejanus's tribute, Marcus, however, escaped and was strung up in the arena upon the start of the Hunger Games. Sejanus snuck into the arena to try to help him, and Snow was forced by Dr. Gaul to slip into the arena to rescue him. In doing so, he ended up beating and killing one of the tributes, <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a>, in the process, when the tributes came after them.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Later, Dr. Gaul revealed to Snow that another of the mentors, <a href="/wiki/Gaius_Breen" title="Gaius Breen">Gaius Breen</a>, had died from his injuries and indicated that she had a special punishment in store for the tributes. Snow realized that she planned to unleash her <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> upon the tributes. He then secretly dropped a <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow%27s_handkerchief" title="Crassus Snow's handkerchief">handkerchief</a> with Lucy Gray's scent into the snake tank to prevent them from attacking her.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Snow's machinations to protect his tribute and endear her to the Capitol audience were successful, but they came at a cost. Lucy Gray was not only not bitten by the snakes, but also charmed them with a song, once again making her the darling of those watching. She later used one of the snakes as a hidden weapon to kill another tribute, before going on to win the Hunger Games. Snow was ecstatic, as her victory seemingly also brought him the <a href="/wiki/Plinth_Prize" title="Plinth Prize">Plinth Prize</a>, a newly created award which would pay for all of his tuition to the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a>. However, he was summoned into the office of Dean Highbottom, who confronted him with the evidence of his cheating. Highbottom then offered him a choice: either enroll for a twenty-year term in the Peacekeepers or have his actions revealed publicly.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>With no other choice, Snow enrolled in the Peacekeepers. In the hopes of seeing Lucy Gray Baird again, he asked to be assigned to District 12. His choice was not questioned, as few newly enrolled Peacekeepers willingly chose to go to District 12, so there was no reason to reject the request of someone who was seemingly so willing. However, upon arrival, Snow began to despair. The food was better and more plentiful than he'd usually had for a long time at the Capitol, but the work was tedious and dull beyond belief, and the environmental conditions were terrible. Furthermore, he had no immediate opportunity to see Lucy Gray and no idea when he might. After receiving a discouraging letter from Tigris, he was at the point of contemplating suicide when to his surprise, Sejanus Plinth arrived in his bunk.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Plinth explained that he, like Snow, was in disgrace due to his actions during the 10th Hunger Games. Volumnia Gaul considered him a traitor for his actions in trying to rescue Marcus. On the verge of expulsion, his father <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a> asked that he be allowed to graduate and sign up for the Peacekeepers, and in return, he would gift a new gym to <a href="/wiki/Agrippina_Sickle" title="Agrippina Sickle">Agrippina Sickle</a>. Plinth agreed on the condition that Snow be allowed to graduate as well. He presented Snow with his diploma, which even said that he had graduated with High Honors. With this, Snow even had the opportunity to take the Peacekeeper officer's test if he wanted to, as it was a requirement to have graduated in order to take it. Furthermore, Plinth's arrival meant copious gifts from his <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Ma Plinth</a>, which meant that Snow had the opportunity to see Lucy Gray performing at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Romance_and_rebels">Romance and rebels</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Snow and Plinth both settled into training exercises with the Peacekeepers and were soon recognized for their abilities. Their Academy training gave them a leg-up and despite his best efforts, Plinth could not hide the fact that he was an expert marksman, having personally trained with his father. The two were ordered to attend the hanging of <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a>, a rebel sympathizer who had killed three at the mines. It was here that Snow first heard the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>, taking up the tune after the <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> repeated Chance's last words, "<i>Run, <a href="/wiki/Lil" title="Lil">Lil</a>, Run!</i>"<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He took an instant dislike to the birds, an unnatural muttation of the Capitol-bred jabberjays.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Snow was reunited with Lucy Gray Baird, and she seemed to think that the stars had brought them together, them having saved each other's lives. Though there was clearly a strong bond between them, Snow worried over <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe" class="mw-redirect" title="Billy Taupe">Billy Taupe</a>, Lucy Gray's spurned lover whom she sung about during her interview for the Hunger Games. Taupe kept showing up, trying to get Lucy Gray to take him back, and worse, he seemed to be trying to put together some sort of rebel plot with Plinth.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Still disturbed by the mockingjays, Snow suggested a plan to the Peacekeeper commander, <a href="/wiki/Commander_Hoff" title="Commander Hoff">Commander Hoff</a>, to shoot down the birds from the trees. Before this happened, however, Dr. Kay from the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a> in the Capitol wanted the Peacekeepers to round up fifty jabberjays and fifty mockingjays from the forest in order to be taken back to the Capitol for study. During this time, Plinth revealed to Snow that he had been plotting with the local rebels to assist them in a plan to flee up north, where it was believed that there were still people living free of the Capitol's influence. As he did so, Snow secretly used one of the jabberjays to record what he was saying, then sent it back to the Capitol for his words to be heard by Dr. Gaul. He believed that, if Sejanus was found guilty of treachery, his father Strabo Plinth would buy his son out of the situation like he always had, and he would remain in the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Things came to a head when, during a performance at the Hob, Snow encountered Plinth together with Billy Taupe and another rebel named <a href="/wiki/Spruce" title="Spruce">Spruce</a> in an old shed with a sack of several weapons. They were finalizing their plans to run away when the mayor's daughter, <a href="/wiki/Mayfair_Lipp" title="Mayfair Lipp">Mayfair Lipp</a>, stumbled upon the scene. She made it clear that she intended to spill the plot to <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Lipp" title="Mayor Lipp">her father</a> and Snow shot and killed her. He then shouted for Lucy Gray Baird, who had also stumbled upon the scene, to get back onstage. Billy Taupe, however, declared that he would kill Lucy Gray, so Spruce shot and killed him. Spruce later took his and Snow's firearms to hide it.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Afterwards, Snow worried that someone would find the rifle with his DNA on it, and he would hang just like Arlo Chance had. However, it was instead Plinth who hung as a traitor, Dr. Gaul having received the jabberjay back at the Capitol and played its recording. Snow was stunned by the loss of his friend, having realized too late the serious consequences of what he had done. Though he believed Strabo Plinth would help his son, he had not reckoned on the fact that Sejanus Plinth was an adult now and Strabo did not even learn of his situation until it was too late. Meanwhile, he was convinced that he could be executed at any moment and hatched a new plan to flee north, just himself and Lucy Gray Baird. Before they did so, however, he received the news that he had passed his officer's test and was to be transported to District 2 for an elite training program, having scored exceptionally high.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

While they were traveling, Lucy Gray mentioned that she didn't want to kill any more people, to which Snow replied that, in his case, three was enough. As Lucy Gray knew about Bobbin and Mayfair, she asked who the third person was that he killed. Not wanting to tell her he was the one responsible for Sejanus Plinth's execution, Snow lies that he killed his old self to flee with her. Then, when they stopped to rest at a shack at the woods, Snow was dumbstruck to discover the rifle he had used to kill Mayfair Lipp sitting there. As Lucy Gray Baird stepped out, saying that she was going to gather some <a href="/wiki/Katniss_(plant)" title="Katniss (plant)">katniss</a>, Snow decided then and there that living in the wild was not for him. He would get rid of evidence, then return to the village where he would travel to District 2 to begin his new life as a Peacekeeper officer.

He began shouting for her and searching, claiming that they could work things out. He ended up falling for a trap she set for him and bitten by a snake. She then taunted him with the song that she had created, "<a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">The Hanging Tree</a>," which was about Arlo Chance, but also could reference him, as he had killed three as well. He fired randomly into the woods but could not be sure if he actually struck her. He then fled through the lake, submerging the incriminating rifle on the way, and worried that he was going to die from the snakebite. Upon his return, however, the doctor accepted his story that the snake had simply sprung up out of nowhere and told him that it wasn't even venomous. He was going to be just fine. The next morning, he boarded the <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> for District 2, and found that everything had improved immediately, from the plush seating to the beverage selection.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Snow_lands_on_top">Snow lands on top</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Snow woke up after a pleasant rest on the hovercraft, the melody of the song "The Hanging Tree" humming in his brain. To his surprise, he was not in District 2, but rather in the Capitol. An attendant informed him that they had orders to drop him off there. He was loaded into a Peacekeeper truck, which took him to the Citadel. There, he met Dr. Gaul, who told him she had received his letter and jabberjay and that she was pleased to see him "continuing his studies" in District 12. She asked him about his opinion on the Hunger Games based on his time there, and he told her that they were part of the eternal war, each its own battle. She informed him that she had erased the recordings of the 10th Hunger Games, except for a private copy kept for her own amusement, feeling that they painted the Capitol as far too vulnerable. She then described Snow's time in the Peacekeepers as a "summer vacation," telling him that she had gotten him an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/honorable_discharge" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:honorable discharge">honorable discharge</a> and he was to start classes with her at the University on Thursday.
</p><p>Snow studied advanced military strategy under Dr. Gaul and was also accepted as an apprentice Gamemaker for the Hunger Games. He immediately began introducing new innovations, such as the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Villages</a> for each <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of the Hunger Games to live in. Unaware of Snow's betrayal of his son, Strabo Plinth made him his heir, financing his and his family's whole life: their taxes, the renovations of their apartment, Snow's tuition, their cook and a generous allowance. Snow paid a visit to the Academy, where he met with Dean Highbottom. Snow returned Sejanus Plinth's diploma, claiming charitable motives, that Highbottom would not want it out there, given how he had ended up.
</p>

<p>At the meeting, Highbottom revealed the sordid truth of the Hunger Games. It was true that the Hunger Games were his creation, but only thanks to Dr. Gaul and Snow's father, Crassus Snow. The two attended Dr Gaul's class at the University, and the final project was to create a punishment for one's enemies so extreme that they would never be allowed to forget how they had wronged you. Highbottom was drunk at the time and had always been good at puzzles. Crassus plied him with more alcohol, drawing out his evilest impulse, the Hunger Games, and fleshing out the project, all the while promising him that it was only a private joke between him. In the morning, Highbottom awoke, horrified that Crassus had turned in the project, wanting a good grade. Then, after the war was over, Dr. Gaul dusted it off and presented him to the Capitol as the architect of the Hunger Games.
</p><p>Before leaving, Snow told Highbottom that the Games supported Gaul's view of humanity: that, though people credited children with innocence, even the most innocent could be turned into killers. On the way back home, Snow reflected on a letter he had received in which it was revealed that nobody quite knew what had happened to Lucy Gray Baird, but it was believed by some that she had been murdered by Mayor Lipp in retaliation for Mayfair Lipp's murder. In any case, Lucy Gray Baird could never trouble him again. Nor could Dean Highbottom, as Snow secretly <a href="/wiki/Poison" title="Poison">poisoned</a> his morphling supply during the meeting. He hoped that with his dying breath, Highbottom would realize something, something that one day all of Panem would come to know: "Snow lands on top."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>And now, on to our good President Coriolanus Snow. Such a young man when he rose to power. Such a clever one to keep it. How, you must ask yourself, did he do it? One word. That's all you really need to know. </i>Poison<i>.</i>"</span></dd>

<p>Eventually, Snow achieved his dream as the president of Panem. As the number one person in the country, Snow possesses total power in Panem's government and has proven to be a cruel and manipulative dictator, ruling over the Capitol and its contained <a href="/wiki/Panem#Districts" title="Panem">districts</a>. He also works on the annual Hunger Games and heads the military responsible for oppressing the districts. He has no qualms about using intimidation in pursuit of his agenda, such as when he threatened to kill Katniss Everdeen, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, and their families.
</p><p>Snow maintained control by <a href="/wiki/Poison" title="Poison">poisoning</a> allies and enemies alike who posed a threat to him. Snow often drank from the same poisoned glass to deflect suspicion. Although <a href="/wiki/Medical_treatments#Poison_antidote" title="Medical treatments">antidotes</a> saved his life, he was left with permanent mouth sores that never healed. As a result, he always wore a genetically engineered rose, which covered the scent of blood stained on his breath.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a> for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a> was killed in an accident.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Fellow <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tribute <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> took control of the <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> chariot and carried Louella's body to the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President's mansion">President's mansion</a> where Snow was on the balcony above him in person, standing calm and erect, surveying the calamity of the opening ceremony. Snow smiled at Haymitch, unimpressed with his performance. Haymitch laid Louella's body down, took a step back so that Snow couldn't pretend not to see and gestured to him before clapping, giving credit where credit was due. Snow's expression suddenly changed, and his fingers moved to his signature white rose as Snow took an interest in Haymitch's <a href="/wiki/Flint_striker" title="Flint striker">flint striker</a> token which had been created by <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>. As Haymitch was dragged away by Peacekeepers, the crowd took in an interest in him for <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorship</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The next day, Snow had Haymitch brought before him at the Heavensbee home after he finished with training. When Snow joined them, he was visibly ill and for once, despite his cosmetic treatments, looked his real age. Blood trickled from his mouth and he vomited up a foul mess into a fountain. Haymitch and Plutarch tended to the stricken president, but Haymitch deliberately drank the jug of milk rather than giving it Snow as requested, claiming that it was empty. Despite realizing what Haymitch had done, Plutarch covered for him and went to reprimand his servants. His condition improving, Snow commented that sometimes the cure was worse than the disease, namely incompetence. Plutarch returned with another jug of milk which Snow chugged and demanded more and some bread. Snow claimed that his illness was food poisoning from a bad batch of oysters, but he fared better than <a href="/wiki/Incitatus_Loomy" title="Incitatus Loomy">Incitatus Loomy</a>, the parade master.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR9_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR9-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Taking an antidote that he carried in his pocket, Snow commented on the Heavensbee family, his old classmate <a href="/wiki/Hilarius_Heavensbee" title="Hilarius Heavensbee">Hilarius Heavensbee</a>, and how useful Plutarch was. Snow surprised and disturbed Haymitch was his familiarity with District 12 and examined Haymitch's necklace finding an inscription that Haymitch had missed. Haymitch told Snow that it was from Lenore, but Snow's knowledge of the Covey's naming practices allowed him to tell that there was more to it than that, although he wasn't able to guess her full name. Wondering how Snow knew, Haymitch told him that it was <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove</a>. Hinting at his past with <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, Snow guessed that Lenore Dove was delightful to look at, swished around in bright colors, sang like a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">mockingjay</a> and Haymitch loved her, and she seemed to love him. However, Haymitch wondered sometimes because her plans didn't include him at all. While not exactly correct, Snow got it too close to the truth and he told Haymitch that romantically speaking, he had dodged a bullet with these games. Snow made it clear that Haymitch's behavior had guaranteed his death, but it would allow Lenore Dove and Haymitch's family to live long lives. Snow intended to orchestrate the manner of Haymitch's death based on his behavior going forward: he could die clean and fair, or they could open the Games with the slowest most agonizing death ever to befall a tribute. Haymitch agreed and Snow told Haymitch his favorite saying "Snow lands on top," instructing the boy not to take any more unauthorized chariot rides or to mock the president on or off camera.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR9_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR9-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Snow brought in <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">a body double</a> of Louella controlled by a pump in her chest and an earpiece that sent her commands from the Gamemakers. Snow explained that no one outside of the drunken audience at the parade even witnessed the accident, no one outside of Louella's family and a few friends would notice the difference. People would believe that she was Louella, especially with Haymitch there to coach her, a perfect pair for the perfect Quater Quell that Snow intended to have. Haymitch understood that the people who saw the crash in person would be told that Louella recovered while Snow had poisoned the parade master for his incompetence, the real source of Snow's illness. Snow dismissed Haymitch and the body double as Plutarch rejoined them.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Haymitch suspected that Snow was behind his getting a score of one in training.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On Day 7 of the Games, Haymitch received a <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gift</a> of a jug of milk identical to the jug at Plutarch's home. The gift was marked as being from the Capitol, but Haymitch concluded that it was from President Snow himself and that the milk was full of <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Poison" title="Weapons">poison</a>. By sending it to Haymitch, Snow hoped to force him to either kill <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> or use it to commit suicide in an attempt to save the little girl. Before Haymitch could drink it, <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> found and killed Wellie. Haymitch smashed the pitcher and killed Silka, winning the Games, but he also bombed the arena's generator before the Gamemakers could stop him<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and his tactic of using the generator's <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> against Silka made the Gamemakers look bad.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-30"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite this, Snow had Haymitch saved to be the Quarter Quell's <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> rather than killing him for his actions as Haymitch had expected and he also had <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> tortured for information.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, Snow spared <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>'s life as Beetee was too valuable to kill.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR27_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR27-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During Haymitch's imprisonment, Snow had him fed clips from old Hunger Games, curated especially for him. Before the Victor's Ceremony, Snow arranged for Haymitch to see Lucy Gray's performance of "<a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Lucy_Gray_Baird" class="mw-redirect" title="The Ballad of Lucy Gray Baird">The Ballad of Lucy Gray Baird</a>" during the interviews before the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. Haymitch finally understood some of the mystery surrounding her and realized that the man that Lucy Gray was singing to was Snow and that Lucy Gray was how Snow knew so much about the Covey. The thought of what Snow might be doing to Lenore Dove caused Haymitch to lose it before the Peacekeepers restrained him.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_31-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the ceremony, the Capitol audience was shown a heavily edited version of the 50th Hunger Games while Snow ordered Haymitch through <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> to keep his flint striker out where everyone could see it. Afterwards, Snow descended on a crystal platform wearing a bloodred rose to give Haymitch his crown. Rather than having Haymitch executed as he expected, Snow merely smiled and told Haymitch to enjoy his homecoming.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_31-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the afterparty in Snow's ballroom, he had Haymitch treated like a glorified pet, although Haymitch didn't see Snow himself again all night. This treatment continued for the next ten days before Haymitch was finally sent home.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR26_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR26-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Upon returning home, Haymitch discovered that Snow had his home set on fire, killing his <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">mother</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">younger brother</a>. Shortly thereafter, Lenore Dove died after eating a poisoned sweet that Snow had sent to her.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR26_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR26-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Haymitch realized that it was a message from Snow: he would never be allowed to love anyone ever again or they would end up dying. This caused Haymitch to push away his friends and descend into decades of alcoholism.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR27_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR27-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Capture_and_interrogation">Capture and interrogation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>President Snow holds Peeta, <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a> and <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> captive and tortures them for information about the rebellion. Snow lets Enobaria off easy since she is from the most loyal district and he fully trusted her. During the interrogation, he had two <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avoxes</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a> and <a href="/wiki/Darius" title="Darius">Darius</a> electrocuted to death and executes <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a> (<a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta's</a> stylist) and the other stylists and prep teams. He did this not for answers but for Peeta to see. He also at some point ordered Johanna's head shorn. President Snow keeps Peeta healthy so he can use him to break Katniss. President Snow at some point went to District 12 and left a white rose in Katniss' room to give her a message only she would know: "I can find you. I can reach you. Perhaps I am watching you now."
</p><p>He makes Peeta do an interview with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> so he can manipulate and break Katniss. President Snow appears on screen during Peeta's third interview and introduces him and Caesar. After Peeta implies there will be an attack from the Capitol on District 13, President Snow orders the interview stopped and for him to be taken away. After Peeta is taken away, President Snow orders for him to be <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacked</a> in order to use him against Katniss. During an interview Katniss and Finnick shot to distract the Capitol from the rescue team, Finnick reveals President Snow kills his enemies and allies who can become threats to him. He also reveals that Snow forced Finnick, along with other 'desirable looking' victors into <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/sex_trafficking" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:sex trafficking">sex trafficking</a>, selling their bodies to wealthy Capitol citizens for high prices. President Snow is enraged by this and becomes angry at them but plans to kill Katniss by using a hijacked and brainwashed Peeta. <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a> reveals that retrieving Peeta was far too easy and suspects that President Snow let them take Peeta so he could kill Katniss.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As a youth, Snow had been raised on the belief that his family name meant prestige and power. His experience, however, was tempered by the fact that he was an orphan and that the loss his family's fortune due being invested in District 13 munitions meant that he had spent much of his early life in poverty, though he concealed this from his classmates. He a had a strong bond with his cousin Tigris and was a studious student, one of 24 top-performing students at the Academy. He had a tendency to fixate on a single problem, convinced that if he could just fix it, then he could fix everything about his life.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-40"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He was one of the few classmates of Sejanus Plinth who did not try to make Sejanus Plinth's life a living hell when he arrived in the Capitol. Some took this to mean that such behavior was beneath him while Sejanus took it as decency, though the truth was more complicated than either.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_40-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-40"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Nevertheless, Snow was capable of showing empathy. He was appalled when Clemensia Dovecote was bitten by Dr. Gaul's snake muttations and Gaul showed no concern whatsoever. Relating the incident to Tigris, he described it as "awful" and he was clearly shaken by the turn of events.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_41-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-41"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>His initial interactions with Lucy Gray Baird were based entirely on the idea that he needed to make her feel welcome, as if she were a guest.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_42-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-42"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Over time, however, their interactions blossomed into genuine affection.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_43-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-43"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>43<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Snow came to accept that she had fallen in love with him, though he was not yet sure if he felt the same way at first.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_44-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-44"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He eventually, however, did come to love her,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but in the end chose to eschew love in exchange for power and ambition. Filled with ideas of control and power from Dr. Volumnia Gaul, he made it his ambition to rule over all others, even if people called him an iron-fisted, cruel tyrant.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>By the time he was president, any of the few positive qualities he had from his youth were largely unrecognizable. He had become exactly what he had set out to achieve. He was a ruthlessly cruel, sadistic, wrathful, violent, greedy, arrogant, power-hungry, wicked and purely and utterly evil man, one obsessed with keeping the perfection of the established order and maintaining absolutely no respect or care for human life. He was an extremely tyrannical, megalomaniacal, and egomaniacal ruler who ruled over Panem with an iron fist. He constantly threatened and killed people who stood in his way to holding absolute power over Panem. He was not hesitant to use people for his personal advantage. For example, he sold Finnick and other unknown victors into sex trafficking to affluent Capitol citizens.
</p><p>His obsession with perfection arises out of his paranoia due to his fear of losing power. As a result, he murdered allies and enemies alike (usually by poisoning them), and in his effort to throw off suspicion he drunk his own murderous poison from the same cup and was left with a mouthful of bloody sores (because the antidotes didn't always work) which are the only outward sign of his insanity. He uses the thick perfume of the genetically enhanced roses in his lapel to cover the scent of his bloody mouth. He fails, however, as Katniss describes his aroma as that of faux roses and blood.
</p><p>As a student, he considered group projects a waste of time, feeling them to be only an opportunity to have his own ideas diluted in the name of collaboration, cut entirely, or watered down until they had lost their bite.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Noticeably, Snow was an extremely calm and reserved man, never visibly losing his temper at any point (at least, not in public, as this would risk damaging his public image). He remained completely in control, both figuratively and literally, throughout a conversation and maintained his serenity even if his life were in danger or if he were facing a known enemy. He had absolutely no lapses when it came to discussing the execution or omission of other people, as evidenced by his conversations with Seneca Crane and the almost cynical way in which Snow referenced Crane's execution. When he encountered Katniss after being overthrown, he calmly revealed to her what had really happened in the final battle, in the knowledge that she was confronting him with the belief that he had killed her sister and even showed sympathy (real or otherwise) for Prim's demise. He also suggested that Coin was more dangerous than he was, and even chastised Katniss for breaking their agreement never to lie to one another.
</p><p>His name, Snow, and his white roses symbolize his mental need to appear flawless, despite his obvious flaws. Despite all this, he claims that he is not wasteful. Snow has a very clear understanding of the districts' emotions towards the Capitol, and how fear doesn't make them back down if they have hope- which he allows, in a <i>very</i> limited form, as a motivator. He'll only kill people for well-defined reasons. President Snow is also willing to take the life of children for his own safety as when he was going to use them as human shields during the rebellion in <i>Mockingjay</i>. He was obviously highly intelligent and cunning as evidenced by his manipulation of Katniss during <i>Catching Fire</i>, and his ability to maintain control of Panem for at least 25 years, probably longer. He was also able to almost effortlessly judge whether or not Katniss was still alive after the showdown in District 2, knowing that she would have been martyred if she were truly dead. Despite his intelligence, however, he was fooled and outwitted by <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> and Alma Coin. At the very least, he was quite capable of admitting that he had been overwhelmed, outwitted and defeated because of some of his own mistakes and shortcomings and that he had horribly underestimated the ruthlessness of President Coin.
</p><p>To the very end, Snow showed no remorse for his tyranny and murderous desire for power, often acting self-righteous and blaming Katniss for disrupting the status quo, reasoning that for all his authoritarian propaganda Snow believed the games insured order and control.
</p>

<p>The President was a small, thin man, with paper-white hair and thick lips that seem pulled across his face. His lips were thought to be changed, to make them look fuller and more appealing. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> once stated that his lips smelled of blood and roses, which was later revealed to be from his poisoning people and getting sores despite drinking the antidote. He also smelled strongly of genetically enhanced roses. He always wore a white rose in his lapel to cover the scent of blood. The strong smell invariably made Katniss gag. His eyes were similar to a snake's. Overall, his appearance was quite snake-like. His trademark sign was a single white rose, which he always wore.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 210px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e7/Snow_Portrait.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130308181008" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Snow's Capitol portrait" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="210" height="311" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Snow Portrait.jpg" data-image-key="Snow_Portrait.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e7/Snow_Portrait.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/210?cb=20130308181008"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e7/Snow_Portrait.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130308181008" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Snow's Capitol portrait" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e7/Snow_Portrait.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/210?cb=20130308181008" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="210" height="311" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Snow Portrait.jpg" data-image-key="Snow_Portrait.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e7/Snow_Portrait.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/210?cb=20130308181008"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Snow_Portrait.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Snow's Capitol portrait</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Contrary to the book description, President Snow is played in the films by <a href="/wiki/Donald_Sutherland" title="Donald Sutherland">Donald Sutherland</a>, who was 6'4" (192 cm) tall at his peak and still remained a very tall man. The film version of Snow wears a beard and has no visible signs of cosmetic surgery or body alterations.
</p><p>In the film, he wore a dark suit over a white shirt with a gray vest over it, a tie, matching pants and black shoes. In <i>Catching Fire</i>, he wore his suit closed, a dark fur stole over it and black leather gloves.
</p><p>As an 18-year-old, Coriolanus Snow was very good-looking. He was called "gorgeous" by both Lucy Gray and Sejanus, the District 11 tribute <a href="/wiki/Reaper" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaper">Reaper</a> called him "pretty boy", and many people noted that he looked like his handsome father. He had pale blue eyes, blond curls (except during his stint as a Peacekeeper, when his hair was cut very short), trim and athletic physique, and excellent posture. Lucy Gray thought he had a pleasant smell similar to roses. He was not as tall as he could have been since the malnourishment during the war stinted his growth, as with many of his generation. However, there are no indications of his exact height or comparisons to the other characters in the novel in that respect. In the film, he is played by 6' (183 cm) tall <a href="/wiki/Tom_Blyth" title="Tom Blyth">Tom Blyth</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Residence">Residence</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2><p>
Snow resided in a huge <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President's mansion">mansion</a> in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a> where he sometimes hosted parties. It is described as being large enough "to get lost in." The mansion contains many rooms such as bedrooms, offices, ballrooms, bathrooms, a closet with furs, a library and a room with a long-forgotten bathtub and discarded furniture. Katniss and her mother stayed in one of the rooms while they were in the Capitol. </p><figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/PresidentMansion.png/revision/latest?cb=20130415225216" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The President's mansion during one of his balls." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="82" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="PresidentMansion.png" data-image-key="PresidentMansion.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/PresidentMansion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130415225216"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/PresidentMansion.png/revision/latest?cb=20130415225216" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The President's mansion during one of his balls." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/PresidentMansion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130415225216" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="82" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="PresidentMansion.png" data-image-key="PresidentMansion.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/PresidentMansion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130415225216"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:PresidentMansion.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The President's mansion during one of his balls.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>There is also an internal greenhouse for Snow's roses in a section of the mansion which was kept unoccupied by the rebels after it was captured. Snow was imprisoned in the greenhouse and revealed to Katniss that the rebels had bombed the children in front of the mansion when she found him there. Following their Victory Tour, victors Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark attended one of his ballroom celebrations, where Katniss ended up meeting and eventually dancing with rebel leader Plutarch Heavensbee for the first time. 
</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His parents, Crassus Snow and an unnamed mother, died during the Dark Days. Mrs. Snow died in childbirth, and his younger sister was also lost in the labor.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Coriolanus and his cousin Tigris, also orphaned, were raised by their grandmother, known as the <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma'am">Grandma'am</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_40-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-40"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>President Snow is known to have a <a href="/wiki/President_Snow%27s_granddaughter" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow's granddaughter">granddaughter</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_38-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This also means he must have had at least one child, most likely with <a href="/wiki/Livia_Cardew" title="Livia Cardew">Livia Cardew</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games_trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Katniss_Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>Oh, my dear Ms. Everdeen, I thought we had agreed not to lie to each other...</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Snow to Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 226px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120418193200" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Snow crowning Katniss as Hunger Games victor" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="226" height="172" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss snow crown.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_snow_crown.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/226?cb=20120418193200"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120418193200" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Snow crowning Katniss as Hunger Games victor" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/226?cb=20120418193200" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="226" height="172" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss snow crown.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_snow_crown.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/226?cb=20120418193200"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_snow_crown.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Snow crowning Katniss as Hunger Games victor</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After the 74th Hunger Games, President Snow was angry that she and Peeta Mellark managed to get Seneca Crane to allow them both to be crowned victors but acted calm while visiting her in District 12. He pushed her enough to agree not to lie to each other. While Katniss and the other tributes were in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>, she managed to destroy it, further angering him, and he subsequently ordered District 12 to be firebombed, destroying the district and wiping out the majority of the population. In <i>Mockingjay</i>, he continued to engage Katniss in a twisted game of psychological manipulation, though methods such as leaving roses in the District 12 Victors' Village, torturing Peeta and forcing him to make various interviews, and <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a> Peeta to turn his love for Katniss into a weapon for the purpose of killing Katniss, and subsequently allowing Peeta to be rescued with the other victors. 
</p><p>After his trial, he reunited with Katniss. He was the only one to offer condolences to her for Prim's death and revealed Coin's role in the bombing of the Capitol children. However, this was most likely out of a desire to get revenge against Coin, rather than being rooted in remorse and a desire to atone for his wrongdoing.<sup id="cite_ref-45" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-45"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss subsequently realized that Snow was right after digging further into what he had told her and what Katniss herself already knew.
</p><p>Another likely factor in Snow's antagonistic relationship with Katniss was his past relationship with the first District 12 victor, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>. His time with Lucy left him with a deep bitterness for both her and District 12 that led him to later describe Lucy as "a ghost girl that could fly around District 12 as much as she wanted, but she and her mockingjays would never harm him again," a statement that would become bitterly ironic. Snow likely began associating Katniss with Lucy when he saw Katniss' reaping, where Katniss also made a big first impression similarly to Lucy. The parallels with Lucy continued, such as when Katniss sang Deep in the Meadow to a dying Rue, which a young Snow had heard Lucy singing to Maude Ivory. 
</p><p>This hatred would only have increased when Katniss was dubbed "The Mockingjay," the bird species which Snow had developed a hatred for during his time as a Peacekeeper in District 12. Furthermore, a recording of Katniss singing the song "<a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">The Hanging Tree</a>," which had been written by Lucy Gray, would be used as a propaganda weapon against the Capitol. This in particular would likely have infuriated Snow, as the song was a reminder of his final encounter with Lucy, and the song was the final thing Lucy ever said to him.
</p><p>Despite his hatred for Katniss, Snow always showed her the respect of keeping their deal and being totally honest with her, something that Katniss recognized to the point that she ultimately believed him over Coin. In the end, Katniss realized that despite her and Snow's mutual hatred for each other, Coin was worse, and Katniss chose to kill Coin instead when given the chance to finally kill Snow. Snow laughed at Katniss' actions, his final act before his death.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Seneca_Crane">Seneca Crane</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>President Snow had no known relationship with the Head Gamemaker in the book. But in the film, there were scenes with Snow and Seneca in Snow's rose garden in his mansion, where they met to plan strategies, tactics, and what was going to happen next in the Games. Snow mentions that he likes Crane, perhaps seeing a bit of his young self in him and was probably hoping to take him on as a protégé. Upon Crane's failure, Snow orders his death, either by execution or allowing him to commit suicide, showing that ultimately Snow only cared for Crane as a way to secure his legacy.<sup id="cite_ref-46" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-46"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>46<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Plutarch_Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<dd>—Coriolanus to Haymitch<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>

It can be inferred that Plutarch remained on Snow's good side long enough to become Head <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> for the 75th Hunger Games and that Snow had no idea Plutarch was an underground rebel.
</p>

<p>In his youth, Coriolanus Snow and Tigris lived together, cared for by Snow's grandmother, the Grandma'am. Although the two were cousins, their relationship was really more like that of brother and sister. Snow relied on Tigris and despaired at the idea that people might take advantage of her sweetness and vulnerability.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_40-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-40"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The two survived the bombings of the Capitol together during the Dark Days, something which they referred to as "bomb time." When Snow was injured in the bombing of the Capitol Arena, Tigris described it as being worse than being there, knowing that he was going through it all alone.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Once, when he was still very young, he collapsed in the street from swan flu. Nobody at all stopped to help, but Tigris, despite being ill herself, found him and got him home.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Tigris disliked the Hunger Games and the pressure that they put on Snow. She was also shocked by the behavior of Dr. Gaul in setting <a href="/wiki/Muttation#Snake_mutts" title="Muttation">snake muttations</a> upon Snow's classmate, <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_41-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-41"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later in life, Snow seemed to have drifted apart from Tigris due to a combination of her extreme body modification and political pragmatism. However, the fact that Tigris was still alive despite her dissent suggests that he still cared enough to spare her. Tigris appeared to have been disgusted by what Snow eventually became, as she expresses happiness at hearing Katniss's intention to kill Snow.<sup id="cite_ref-49" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-49"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>49<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Sejanus_Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span>"<i>You know, Sejanus, I'm your friend. More than a friend. You're the closest thing I'll ever have to a brother. And there are special rules for family.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Coriolanus Snow, trying to coax information from Sejanus about the rebel plot</dd></dl></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 227px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3c/Coryo_and_Sejanus.webp/revision/latest?cb=20231229071529" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Coryo and Sejanus mentoring the Hunger Games" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="227" height="152" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Coryo and Sejanus.webp" data-image-key="Coryo_and_Sejanus.webp" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3c/Coryo_and_Sejanus.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/227?cb=20231229071529"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3c/Coryo_and_Sejanus.webp/revision/latest?cb=20231229071529" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Coryo and Sejanus mentoring the Hunger Games" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3c/Coryo_and_Sejanus.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/227?cb=20231229071529" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="227" height="152" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Coryo and Sejanus.webp" data-image-key="Coryo_and_Sejanus.webp" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3c/Coryo_and_Sejanus.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/227?cb=20231229071529"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Coryo_and_Sejanus.webp" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Coryo and Sejanus mentoring the Hunger Games</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Snow first met Sejanus Plinth at the playground when they were both around eight years old. As Plinth and his family were "new money," having originally come from District 2, they represented a threat to everything that the Snows held dear. Despite this, while most of the children took to baiting and belittling him, Snow simply ignored him. Most people took this as meaning that belittling Plinth was beneath him, though Plinth took it as decency. Neither was entirely true.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_40-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-40"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In either, the fact that Snow hadn't belittled him meant that by the time they were both 18, Plinth was at least on speaking terms with him. The two both ended up as mentors in the 10th Hunger Games, and for some reason, Snow found that he kept covering for Plinth when he made disparaging remarks about the Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_50-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-50"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>To Snow's chagrin, their continued contact began to give the impression that they were close friends and he was forced to enter the arena to rescue him when he attempted to bring Marcus's corpse out of the arena, who had been strung up for an escape attempt.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Ultimately, Plinth came to think of Snow as a brother and when Snow was forced to go the Peacekeepers, saw to it that he got his diploma for graduating the Academy.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Snow, however, could not countenance Plinth's rebel activities and thus betrayed him to the Capitol, though he did not realize until it was too late just how much trouble he had landed Plinth in.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Afterwards, he played on Plinth's parents beliefs that the two of them were great friends by allowing them to accept him as their heir and finance his entire lifestyle.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Strabo_Plinth">Strabo Plinth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>My wife needs something to live for. So do I, for that matter. You've lost your parents. We've lost our son. I was thinking perhaps we could work something out.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Strabo Plinth, explaining to Snow why he wanted to make him his heir<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>After the death of his son, Strabo Plinth took on Coriolanus Snow as his heir. He could not legally adopt Snow, as Snow would have been unwilling to give up his name in any case. Despite this, Snow treated Strabo somewhat like a father, referring to him as "sir." Strabo paid to fund his lifestyle and enrollment at the University. The two were very compatible and Strabo never questioned any of Snow's expenditures, nor additions to his wardrobe. He seemed pleased at those times when Snow asked him for advice.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_22-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Volumnia_Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>You don't think I've invested all this time in you to hand you off to those imbeciles in the districts, do you?</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Dr. Gaul considered Coriolanus Snow her protege<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Snow first encountered Dr. Gaul during a class field trip when he was nine years old, and he watched in horror as she melted the flesh off a lab rat with a laser beam. He first met her in person shortly before the 10th Hunger Games after being retrieved from the Capitol Zoo where he had met with his tribute, Lucy Gray Baird. Dr. Gaul commented that he had fallen in a cage, and he decided to play it cool, saying that he landed onstage. She seemed impressed by his nerve, saying that he could one day be a Gamemaker. She said that he understood the importance of keeping the Games alive and that she had a good feeling about him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Afterwards, <a href="/wiki/Satyria_Click" title="Satyria Click">Satyria Click</a>, whom he served as a student aide, said that he had pleased Dr. Gaul, which was good, as she would give a good report to <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Snow was initially pleased at his good fortune at finding an apparent ally in Dr. Gaul. However, things took a turn after she set a class project to put together a formal proposal regarding the idea of a <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorship</a> program for the Hunger Games. Snow was paired up with <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a>, but he ended up doing almost all of the work amidst the incident with <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>. Gaul correctly sussed out that Dovecote had not touched the proposal, setting snake muttations upon her and deeply unsettling Snow.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_51-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-51"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Afterwards, Snow expressed his horror of what had happened to Tigris, who urged him to avoid Gaul, but doing so was easier said than done.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_41-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-41"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Sejanus Plinth sneaked into the arena to rescue his tribute, Dr. Gaul made Snow go in to rescue him. She was unapologetic about his experience in going to the arena and killing Bobbin, and set him a paper, telling him to reflect on the themes of chaos, control and the social contract required for survival.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the Hunger Games, when Snow enrolled in the Peacekeepers, Plinth revealed that Gaul had spoken in Snow's favor, stating that Snow's actions in cheating for Lucy Gray Baird had only been a piece of faulty strategy. This led Snow to realize that he might still have an ally in her, and so he wrote her a letter, telling her that District 12 was an excellent stage to watch the battle between chaos and control play out and that it took "very little to bring the beast [in people] to the surface, at least under the cover of darkness."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_52-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-52"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He later sent Gaul a jabberjay with a recording of Sejanus Plinth describing more planned treasonous activity, which resulted in Plinth's execution.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Ultimately, Snow returned to the Capitol and began attending University thanks to the machinations of Dr. Gaul. Still impressed by his potential, she saw to it that he received an honorable discharge from the Peacekeepers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was impressed by what he had learned in the districts and had him study directly under her at the University, additionally granting him a position as an apprentice Gamemaker.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_22-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Coriolanus's namesake is <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shakespeare" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:William Shakespeare">William Shakespeare</a>'s tragedy <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriolanus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Coriolanus">Coriolanus</a></i>, based on the famed Roman general <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnaeus_Marcius_Coriolanus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Gnaeus Marcius Coriolanus">Gaius Marcius Coriolanus</a>. His surname, Snow, calls to mind the color white, which he is frequently associated with throughout the trilogy. White typically symbolizes innocence and purity, but it's also commonly associated with death.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Snow was given the nickname "Gent" by his fellow Peacekeepers in District 12 for his table manners.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>At 18 years old, he was the youngest person to ever pass the Peacekeeper officer candidate test.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A solar power plant in District 5 is named after him.<sup id="cite_ref-53" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-53"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>If his younger sister had survived, Snow would be the eldest of two siblings, with his sister being about five years his junior.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This would have given him something in common with Katniss, who was also the eldest of two, and was four years older than her sister Prim.<sup id="cite_ref-54" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-54"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Snow's Peacekeeper dog tags list his ID number as 5263099.<sup id="cite_ref-55" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-55"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>55<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>It's commonly theorized that after <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> won the <a href="/wiki/71st_Hunger_Games" title="71st Hunger Games">71st Hunger Games</a>, he had her family killed because she refused to be forced into sex trafficking. <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> is also thought to be a victim of this trade; Haymitch mentioned both of these women's names to Katniss in a conversation on the subject.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Like most of his Peacekeeper squad, Snow was terrible at shooting.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In promotional images for the <i>Mockingjay</i> films and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, Snow was seated on a white throne.</li>
<li>Snow is the only character to appear in every book and film so far.</li>
<li>Snow's actor, Donald Sutherland, died at the age of 88 on June 20, 2024.</li></ul>
