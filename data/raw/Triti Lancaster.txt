<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTriti_Lancaster%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Triti was selected to be a District Hero due to her generosity and kindness to other citizens of District 9. Triti's family quickly agreed to the remuneration they would receive for Triti's service.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTriti_Lancaster%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Triti is portrayed as a dark-skinned woman of average build, described by <a href="/wiki/Egeria" title="Egeria">Egeria</a> as charming and beautiful. In her District Hero portrait, her hair is tied back, she wears a white t-shirt under a yellow apron or overalls, and she carries a sheaf of wheat behind her.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTriti_Lancaster%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In Egeria's letter, she states that although Triti doesn't evoke the same "emotional connection" as her fellow District Heroes, she believes her "gentle, industrious personality [should] excel in illustrating the variety of the districts."
</p>

<ul><li>On June 19th, 2014, <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a> released seven portraits of District Heroes as advertisements for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>. These were original characters, independent from the books or films, serving as pro-<a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propaganda</a> for each of their respective districts.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>According to the District Heroes banners, Triti is 17, however, in Egeria's letters, she is said to be 19. This inconsistency also occurs with <a href="/wiki/Felix_Stam" title="Felix Stam">Felix Stam</a>.</li>
<li>Triti was the first named character from District 9.
<ul><li>She remained the only named character from her district until the release of <a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes"><i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i></a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
