<p>There are only a few known <b>elections</b> in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s history, given that its government was primarily a totalitarian dictatorship and a police state. During the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">the rebels</a> fought to establish Panem as a republic with a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/representative_democracy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:representative democracy">representative democracy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElections%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Political_elections">Political elections</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElections%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Few elections are mentioned throughout the trilogy. Due to widespread inequality and systematic oppression, the districts were largely unrepresented in national politics. Each district had a <a href="/wiki/Mayor" title="Mayor">mayor</a> who oversaw local matters,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but Panem as a whole was overseen by Capitol citizens. The <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">president</a> possessed nearly all governing power, and the position was presumably a lifetime appointment. It is unknown how any officials— presidents, cabinet ministers, secretaries, or mayors— attained power, although most of them were likely appointed. 
</p><p>Following its <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/secession" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:secession">secession</a> from Panem during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> operated as a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/stratocracy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:stratocracy">stratocracy</a>, or government headed by military chiefs, and all of its citizens were drafted into military service at the age of fourteen. Its head of state retained the title of president,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but it is unknown whether one attained this position through election, appointment, or merit. 
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-Second_Rebellion">Post-Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElections%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After the Second Rebellion ended in a decisive rebel victory, President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> was deposed, taken into custody, and <a href="/wiki/Capital_punishment" title="Capital punishment">sentenced to death</a>; in his place, District 13's President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> took charge.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, she declared herself the interim president of Panem and claimed an election would be held after an indefinite period of time, "when the time [was] right".<sup id="cite_ref-:0_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coin was assassinated by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> soon after,<sup id="cite_ref-:3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so an emergency presidential election was organized, and <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Commander Paylor</a> was elected into office as the new head of state. This was Panem's first known democratic election for the purposes of political representation. No other presidential candidates were mentioned,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but in Plutarch's letter to Katniss in <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i>, he said he expected Paylor, as "the voice of reason", to win by a wide margin.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It can be assumed that other elections soon followed after Paylor took office, and a representative democracy was officially instated.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Non-political_elections">Non-political elections</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElections%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a>, each district was forced to vote on the male and female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> they would send to fight in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> for the first <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>. This was intended as a reminder to the rebels that they started the war and sent their young to die.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Another apolitical election was held prior to the reading of the Quell card for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> designed two dozen different wedding gowns for Katniss Everdeen, and Capitol citizens were able to vote for their favorite dress at each stage, narrowing it down to six finalists before one last vote.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss was eventually forced to wear the winning gown during her pre-Games <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interview</a> with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the day of Snow's scheduled <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a>, Coin had the seven surviving <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> vote on a proposal for a <a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">76th Hunger Games</a> featuring the children of Capitol officials. This proposal was approved by a narrow 4-3 majority, but it was never carried out because Katniss assassinated Coin later that day.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElections%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Plutarch's statement that democracy appeared in history books and worked for their ancestors<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> seems to suggest that there are history books in the Capitol that still teach about the political system of the United States. However, considering the Capitol's classical influences, it is also possible that he was referring to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Republic" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Roman Republic">Roman Republic</a>.</li></ul>
