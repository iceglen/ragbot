</p><p>The <b>Battle of District 3</b> is an unofficial term referring to the fight to seize control of <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in and around the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Pre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest">Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Pre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest" title="Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest">Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest</a></i></center></div><p>When <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> visited the district on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, she mentioned that the people there, as well as in <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">Districts 4</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, expressed "genuine elation... at the sight of [them], and under the elation, fury." She also characterized their cheers as cries for vengeance.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Months later, on the day before the reading of the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> card, there were shortages of electric gadgets from District 3 that implied rebel action.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></i></center></div>
<p>Several weeks after the end of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the airing of <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a> about the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_8_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">hospital bombing</a> in District 8 boosted morale in the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>. This allowed rebels to take Districts 3 and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a>, as well as "[make] inroads in several other districts as well".<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notable_persons_involved">Notable persons involved</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Beetee_mockingjay.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140515231714" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Beetee Latier" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Beetee_mockingjay.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140515231714" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="108" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Beetee mockingjay.jpg" data-image-key="Beetee_mockingjay.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Beetee_mockingjay.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Beetee Latier in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>While he was never directly linked to this battle, District 3 <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> made a number of notable contributions to the rebel cause. According to <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, Beetee "essentially redesigned the underground network that transmits all the programming",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so he played a major role in getting propos to air across the districts<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and later inside the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> were also responsible for the strategies behind the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_the_Nut" title="Bombings">bombing of the Nut</a><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="City Circle bombing">City Circle bombing</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
