</p><p><b>Livia Cardew</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She was assigned the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>, <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">Facet</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLivia_Cardew%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her mother ran the largest bank in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>She was excited upon being assigned the male tribute from District 1, grinning, punching the air and shouting "Yes!"<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was the only one of the mentors who did not congratulate <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> for his idea to visit his tribute upon arrival, instead saying that she thought he had cheated and should be dismissed as a mentor immediately.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Snow was later amused to see footage of her flailing through a crowd in the wake of the gunfire from <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>'s tribute, <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a>, being shot dead at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>. He thought it ironic for her to behave this way, given that she had once called him "self-absorbed" for not being able to decipher the deeper meaning of a poem in rhetoric class.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Her tribute was one of several killed in the bombing of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. Following this, she was very vocal, demanding that new tributes be brought in from the districts to replace those who had been killed. Barring this, she wanted to be given <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a>, as his mentor, <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a>, was in the hospital. However, this never came to fruition, so Livia's role in the 10th Hunger Games ended before the competition itself began.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After his ill-fated relationship with <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, Snow mused that he might one day marry someone that he disliked who wouldn't be able to sway his heart and manipulate him. He specifically mentioned Livia Cardew, saying she would be "perfect". He imagined her being his first lady someday once he became <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">president</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLivia_Cardew%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was said to have a "pointed little face".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film she has long blonde hair and blue eyes.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLivia_Cardew%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She had a shrill voice and was known to be prone to gloating.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Livia constantly rivalled Coriolanus as a child, and was known to be rude and cruel toward him. She used to taunt Coriolanus for his parentless state when he was younger. Livia was also a bad sport, as she made it clear that she thought Coriolanus should be dismissed as a mentor following his entry in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> was outraged at her <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>'s death because it meant she would no longer have a chance of a prize.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She also sneered at Lucy Gray, thinking that she didn't have a chance in the Games, and she insulted those who supported the tribute.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>  
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLivia_Cardew%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Livia shares her given name with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livia_Drusilla" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Livia Drusilla">Livia Drusilla</a>, the wife of Rome's founder and first emperor, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Augustus">Augustus</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLivia_Cardew%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Livia likely was a relative or ancestor of <a href="/wiki/Fulvia_Cardew" title="Fulvia Cardew">Fulvia Cardew</a>, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>'s assistant in <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> also draws her name from the same person as Livia--Livia Drusilla.</li>
<li>It is heavily implied that she married Coriolanus Snow later down the line.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This would likely make Coriolanus Snow's descendants also descendants of the Cardews.</li></ul>
