						In the Hunger Games, Castor and Pollux are closely related, sharing a strong physical resemblance and often mistaken for twins. Their names originate from Greek mythology. Pollux, enslaved and turned into an Avox, was rescued by Castor after five years. They then worked as cameramen. Castor, being mortal, dies, while Pollux, likened to the immortal twin in mythology, survives.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCastor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Castor's brother <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> was made into an <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avox</a>, enslaved, and forced to work in the Capitol's underground utility corridors and <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>. After five years, Castor was able to buy Pollux's way aboveground,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and the two worked alongside one another as cameramen.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/30/Mockingjay-castor.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150110115215" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt='Castor filming Katniss when she sang "The Hanging Tree"' src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/30/Mockingjay-castor.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150110115215" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="95" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mockingjay-castor.jpg" data-image-key="Mockingjay-castor.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mockingjay-castor.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Castor filming Katniss when she sang "The Hanging Tree"</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>When Katniss sang "<a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">The Hanging Tree</a>" to Pollux at the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a>, Castor covertly recorded it. In the <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> novel, this footage was never aired, and later it was used to help deprogram <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a>.
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, it was used in a propo and spread across the districts, where it was then used as a protest song.<sup id="cite_ref-MJP1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Katniss's sharpshooter squad, <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>, was accompanied by the film crew throughout their time in the Capitol. When they all headed underground to avoid being spotted alive,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a> killed many of their number, including Castor.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCastor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Castor was described as a burly man<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> with sandy hair, a red beard, blue eyes, and close-bitten nails. He resembled his brother, Pollux.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCastor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Castor and Pollux owe their names to twin brothers in Greek and Roman mythology. The myth states that they had the same mother, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leda_(mythology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Leda (mythology)">Leda</a>, but different fathers (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyndareus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tyndareus">Tyndareus</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Zeus">Zeus</a>, respectively). This made Castor mortal and Pollux immortal, which is fitting because <i>Mockingjay</i>'s Pollux lives and Castor dies.
</p>
