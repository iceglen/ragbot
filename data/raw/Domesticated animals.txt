<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13"><a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDomesticated_animals%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Cut off from the rest of the districts, District 13 had to become self-sufficient, building its own underground farms to sustain its population. In addition to growing crops, they raised chickens.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They also had milk;<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> since they lacked the space for cows, sheep, or horses to graze, and since <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> did not comment on the taste being any different than she was used to, it was likely goat's milk.
</p>
