</p><p><b>Kalia Prescott</b> is the first of the stunt performers specifically cast to portray the training and fight scenes of a tribute that is mentioned only once, that she fell during the initial <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. She portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_3_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 3 female (74th HG)">District 3 female</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>&#160;in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKalia_Prescott%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Like most of the tribute actors, she is also a stunt actor, having done various stunts for films such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_2" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Spider-Man 2">Spider-Man 2</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Dawn_(2012_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Red Dawn (2012 film)">Red Dawn</a></i>. She is the only stunt actor and one of the two tributes which fell during the bloodbath (the other is the <a href="/wiki/District_4_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 male (74th HG)">District 4 male</a> by <a href="/wiki/Ethan_Jamieson" title="Ethan Jamieson">Ethan Jamieson</a>) to be officially credited under the actors section.
</p><p>She is also a Taekwondo black belt and a model and is most famous for her contract with Gucci, which ended in 2011. Since then, she has been modelling for various other businesses as an independent model. In 2013, she participated in the individual movie <i>Sugar</i>, playing the major role of the movie (Sugar, a 16-year-old girl).
</p>
