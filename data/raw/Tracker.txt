<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>A <b>tracker</b> is a small piece of <a href="/wiki/Technology" title="Technology">technology</a> used by the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> to locate each <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> inside the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Shortly before the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol Arena bombing">Capitol Arena bombing</a> opened up a number of passages beyond the cameras' view. Since trackers were not yet in use, this made it difficult to locate tributes and keep track of who was still alive.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Trackers were eventually introduced in later iterations of the Games, likely to circumvent this exact issue.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On the morning of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, a woman in a white coat carried a set of syringes. She inserted trackers into the forearms of <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>. Wyatt asked what happens if they win, if the tracker is taken out. The woman replied that they collect all the trackers from the tributes, dead or alive, as they are reusable. The woman also pointed out that of course for this year, with twice as many tributes, they needed 24 extra. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 256px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Tracker.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20140609045912" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A tracker being inserted into a tribute&#39;s arm." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Tracker.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/256?cb=20140609045912" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="256" height="107" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Tracker.jpeg" data-image-key="Tracker.jpeg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Tracker.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A tracker being inserted into a tribute's arm.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>On the morning of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> grabbed onto a ladder, and she was held in place by an immobilizing current as she was lifted into the <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a>. This current kept her still as a woman in a white coat with a syringe inserted her tracker. Once injected, the current released her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This was presumably the same procedure used for each tribute on their own individual transports.
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, tributes were all transported together in the same hovercraft, and there was no immobilizing current. As a result, a female <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> attendant came around with the syringe to inject trackers only once the tributes were seated.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On the morning of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss was once again injected with a tracker by a doctor before being transported to the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On the third and final day of the Games, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> hit Katniss over the head with a heavy coil of wire, then pinned her down and cut out the girl's tracker. At the time, Katniss thought Johanna was trying to kill her, and when she came upon <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> with a similar gash below his elbow, she assumed someone had attacked him too.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She later found out that Johanna had removed her tracker so she couldn't be traced by the Capitol back to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Considering this alongside Beetee's knowledge of the rebel plan, it's likely that he cut out his own tracker to secure his own rescue.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Trackers were inserted into the inner arm of all tributes during their transport from the Capitol to the arena. A syringe gun was injected into the arm, and when activated, the small metal tracker was placed deep under the skin. Katniss described the injection itself as painful, and she stated that in her anxiety, she pressed on her tracker even though it hurt.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, there is no indication that the tracker itself caused tributes any pain or discomfort once the pain of the initial injection faded.
</p><p>Since Katniss had to be injected with a new tracker just a year after she and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> won,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a>' trackers were presumably removed after their extraction from the arena.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

This scar was the subject of debate among <a href="/wiki/Fulvia_Cardew" title="Fulvia Cardew">Fulvia Cardew</a>, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, and Katniss's <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a>. They discussed covering it up with an armband while she was on camera.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
