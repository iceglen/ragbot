<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayfair_Lipp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Before the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 10th annual Hunger Games, Billy Taupe Clade made a bet that he could have both <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> and Mayfair Lipp. However, the girls found this out. Lucy Gray broke up with Billy Taupe, while Mayfair stayed with him but was furious that he had been dating Lucy Gray behind her back. She then convinced her father to reap Lucy Gray.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When her name was announced, the camera zoomed in on Lucy Gray. She began to navigate through the girls, and when she neared Mayfair, Lucy Gray dropped a snake down her collar. Mayfair fell to the ground, horrified and shrieking, while others whispered to each other. Mayor Lipp flew down the steps to his daughter, and Mayfair tried to smarten herself up, but it was clear that she had wet herself. Mayfair was then led away by a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a>, and her father retaliated against Lucy Gray by slapping her across on the face.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Mayfair was at the <a href="/wiki/Hanging_tree" title="Hanging tree">hanging tree</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a> of <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a>, standing alongside her father and <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Lipp" title="Mrs. Lipp">mother</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the lights went out following one of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>'s performances in the <a href="/wiki/Hob" class="mw-redirect" title="Hob">Hob</a>, Mayfair attempted to leave but was flanked by Peacekeepers. She was frustrated, but allowed herself to be escorted home by them, as otherwise her father would have to be called. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, students of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, found themselves recruited for the job along with a few others, and as they neared her house, Mayfair slipped past them and entered herself.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Mayfair later stumbled upon a secretive rebel meeting where Billy Taupe, Sejanus, and <a href="/wiki/Spruce" title="Spruce">Spruce</a> were plotting to travel up north to escape <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s influence, which Snow has also stumbled upon. Billy Taupe ordered her to go home, but Spruce tried to get her to come with them, although she made it clear that she was going to leave and reveal what was happening to her father. At this, Spruce lifted his shotgun to shoot her, but Billy Taupe blocked him. Snow then shot and killed Mayfair instead, as he would also suffer the consequences if she had reported what she'd heard.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayfair_Lipp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mayfair Lipp had red hair and often, a self-righteous smirk. Her garments were filthy with coal dust, which coated everything in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While in the shed, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> recognized Mayfair's voice from its nastiness and mocking tone.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Billy_Taupe">Billy Taupe</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayfair_Lipp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>According to Lucy Gray, Mayfair did not have any siblings or friends, and the <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miners</a> hated her family for their money, power, and privilege (and likely Mayfair's own attitude did not help dissuade their hostility). She believed that this made Mayfair feel lonely, which allowed Billy Taupe to charm her easily.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayfair_Lipp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>... since he’d spoiled Mayfair rotten and let her run loose like a wildcat, the feeling was he had no one to blame but himself if she had been keeping time with a rebel.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—The Peacekeeper response to Mayfair's death<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Mayor Lipp had a permissive attitude towards Mayfair. Yet, he was willing to do anything to give Mayfair what she wanted and make her happy, as shown when he reaped her rival, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> so she have Billy Taupe all to herself. After her murder, the opinion among Peacekeepers seemed to be that he had spoiled his daughter, so it was his fault as a parent that she had gotten in with a dangerous crowd.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Nothing is known of her relationship with her mother, save that she had one.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
