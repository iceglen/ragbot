<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2a/BoSaSHyAndSol.png/revision/latest?cb=20250309101330" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Hy and Sol chasing Lucy Gray and Jessup Diggs." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2a/BoSaSHyAndSol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250309101330" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="157" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="BoSaSHyAndSol.png" data-image-key="BoSaSHyAndSol.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:BoSaSHyAndSol.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Hy and Sol chasing Lucy Gray and Jessup Diggs.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Sol grew up in District 5 and became her district's female tribute in <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with the other tributes, Sol was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On the afternoon of the second day, Sol began to climb the bleachers at the furthest end of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. As her mentor, <a href="/wiki/Iphigenia_Moss" title="Iphigenia Moss">Iphigenia Moss</a>, sent her food, an alliance comprised of <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>, the two tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a>, the male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>, chased her down. After a brief chase, they caught up with her, and Coral speared Sol through the throat with a trident.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/25/BoSaSSol.png/revision/latest?cb=20250309101509" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Sol yelling at Lucy Gray and Jessup Diggs." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="179" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="BoSaSSol.png" data-image-key="BoSaSSol.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/25/BoSaSSol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250309101509"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/25/BoSaSSol.png/revision/latest?cb=20250309101509" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Sol yelling at Lucy Gray and Jessup Diggs." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/25/BoSaSSol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250309101509" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="179" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="BoSaSSol.png" data-image-key="BoSaSSol.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/25/BoSaSSol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250309101509"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:BoSaSSol.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Sol yelling at Lucy Gray and Jessup Diggs.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Sol was killed in the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a> by <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>. When the gong rang, she ran to the pile of weapons and grabbed a knife. Sol was trying to avoid the other tributes' weapons when she saw <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> trying to kill <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>. She threw a knife that wounded Treech in the arm and another at Lucy Gray, who dodged it. Sol then attempted to leave, but saw <a href="/wiki/Hy" title="Hy">Hy</a> being chased by <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a>, and they escaped to the tunnels. When they were further inside, they saw Lucy Gray and <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a>, but the tributes managed to get behind a door before Sol and Hy could kill them. <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>'s alliance then appeared. <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> killed Hy. Sol, who was shouting at Lucy Gray, was impaled by Coral's trident and dragged back through the door. She placed 14th.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> described Sol as a rickety little thing. Sol also had what her mentor thought was a "funny accent", making her speech harder to comprehend.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><i>“Sol”</i> translates to the word <i>sun</i> in Latin. As such, her name may come from the term <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/solar_power" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:solar power">solar power</a>, referring to her district's industry.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Sol is the only tribute who survived to see the Games who did not receive a <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gift</a> besides <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>, who died thirty minutes within the Games start.</li>
<li>Sol was described by her mentor as having a "funny accent" and that her name was either "Sol" or "Sal."</li>
<li>Sol is the third female tribute from District 5 in the series to outlive her <a href="/wiki/Hy" title="Hy">male counterpart</a>, similar to <a href="/wiki/%22Foxface%22" title='"Foxface"'>Foxface</a> and the <a href="/wiki/District_5_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 female (75th HG)">District 5 female</a> from the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.</li></ul>
