<p><b>Interviews</b> with each of the <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> were hosted by <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> the night before each <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> began. The tributes dressed up so as to create an impression on <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. Caesar asked them about their life back home, their time in the Capitol so far, their <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>. The interviews occurred in order of districts, with the female preceding the male tribute. Each tribute was allotted three minutes for their interview. The main purpose of the interviews was to show the rest of the Capitol a little about each tribute to help them choose their favorite. After the Games ended, another interview would be conducted with the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> that reviewed their time in the arena.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterviews%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterviews%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The interviews were introduced in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> as <i>The Hunger Games: A Night of Interviews</i>, hosted by <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a>, a colorful weatherman from <a href="/wiki/Capitol_TV" title="Capitol TV">Capitol TV</a>. It was held before a live audience at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> and broadcast throughout <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. At the time of these Games, the Games were not generally popular with citizens of the Capitol and these interviews were an attempt to change that, along with the introduction of the <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentorship</a> program, as well as <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorships</a> and betting on the tributes. It was hoped that by getting to know the tributes better, the audience would become more interested in their fates. The mentors also appeared alongside their tributes for these interviews, due to the newness of the mentorship program, which for these games featured top-performing students from the Academy.
</p><p>Before the interviews were even held, a number of tributes had already died, many of them in an explosion during a tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. Thus, there were only a limited number of interviews and each tribute and mentor pair were given a maximum of ten minutes. <a href="/wiki/Lysistrata_Vickers" title="Lysistrata Vickers">Lysistrata Vickers</a> had her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a>, lift her over his head to demonstrate his strength. <a href="/wiki/Io_Jasper" title="Io Jasper">Io Jasper</a>'s tribute, <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a>, revealed that he could start a fire with his glasses and Io suggested angles and times of day that would be conducive to this. <a href="/wiki/Juno_Phipps" title="Juno Phipps">Juno Phipps</a> admitted that she was at first disappointed to get the male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a>, until he told her that he knew five different ways to kill someone with a sewing needle. The girl from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>, put on a show with a broomstick to demonstrate how she might kill using a trident. <a href="/wiki/Domitia_Whimsiwick" title="Domitia Whimsiwick">Domitia Whimsiwick</a> engaged the interest of the crowd by having her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a>, talk bout his experience in slaughterhouse techniques.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Lucy_Gray_interview_stage_still_11.3.png/revision/latest?cb=20231103154537" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Lucy Gray singing onstage for her interview." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Lucy Gray interview stage still 11.3.png" data-image-key="Lucy_Gray_interview_stage_still_11.3.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Lucy_Gray_interview_stage_still_11.3.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231103154537" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Lucy_Gray_interview_stage_still_11.3.png/revision/latest?cb=20231103154537" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Lucy Gray singing onstage for her interview." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Lucy_Gray_interview_stage_still_11.3.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231103154537" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Lucy Gray interview stage still 11.3.png" data-image-key="Lucy_Gray_interview_stage_still_11.3.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Lucy_Gray_interview_stage_still_11.3.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231103154537" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Lucy_Gray_interview_stage_still_11.3.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Lucy Gray singing onstage for her interview.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The highlight of the evening, however, was <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> with <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, who went last. The two appeared together, Coriolanus in a neatly pressed uniform and Lucy Gray Baird in her rainbow dress which had been expertly repaired and spiffed up by Coriolanus's cousin, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>. Lucy Gray Baird than sang a <a href="/wiki/Songs" title="Songs">song</a> she had composed herself, words set to an old tune, called "The Ballad of Lucy Gray Baird." By the time she was down, one could hear a pin drop in the auditorium. Coriolanus knew that the song would make sponsor gifts pour in for her, but the song also made him deeply jealous, due to its reference to a <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe" class="mw-redirect" title="Billy Taupe">lost lover</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the Games, Lucy Gray Baird was apparently shipped directly back to District 12 and not given another interview<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Even had such a thing been considered, it is likely that Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> would not have allowed, due to her decision to erase the 10th Hunger Games entirely, save a master recording for her own personal amusement.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterviews%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During these interviews, as Katniss and Peeta watched them on the train, Katniss noted it as being overwhelming due to the high number of tributes.
</p><p>Caesar Flickerman opened the interviews with a brief retrospective of the previous Games, describing the 10th Hunger Games as having been a turning point, but not providing details regarding the victor of those Games. He detailed the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a>, still hosted at the time by Lucretius "Lucky" Flickerman, accompanied by Volumnia Gaul, who at the time coined the phrase "May the odds be EVER in your favor!" Following this recap, the interviews went in order as usual by district. <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> bragged about her size, strength and <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> among other things. <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, also of one, flexed his muscles for the audience and bragged about being the biggest and the best, stating that he was all meat. Caesar asked if this meant his brain too. Following this, the remaining members of District 1 and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a> attempted to play up their prowess with weapons and benefits of being Career Tributes to avoid being classified as brainless beasts.
</p><p>The arrival of the District 3 tributes revealed the new alliance of <a href="/wiki/Newcomers" class="mw-redirect" title="Newcomers">Newcomers</a> that had been made against the Careers, but District 4 attempted to highlight their skills with tridents and thus were ill-prepared for Caesar's questioning regarding the Newcomers. The Careers of District 5 attempted to play up their own strengths in order to counter the narrative of the Newcomers, realizing that this would be the last look the audience would have at the Careers. Following this, Districts 6-11 continued to build up the narrative of the Newcomers until finally the time came for the interviews with District 12. Worn down by all of the talk of the Newcomers, Caesar was ready for some "sauce" and egged on <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, getting laughs from the audience when she machine-gunned the midsection of the front row of the audience for their poor taste in clothing, mocking a man wearing a suit of hundred-dollar bills and a woman with surgically implanted cat ears. <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> impressed him by spouting off complicated betting odds and the amount of sponsor dollars it would take to send a tribute a particular gift. <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>, a doppelganger of the deceased <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, proved a sensation by wielding a snake and baring her teeth at the audience. When, however, she was questioned directly by Caesar, she simply hissed in response and then amused the audience by crouching down and holding out the snake. When, however, Caesar asked her what the Newcomers would do if they killed off the Careers, she replied "You'll murder us. You'll murder us," resulting in gasps of disapproval from the audience, which caused her to only shout the statement repeatedly. Caesar called for help, only for Lou Lou to suddenly fall quiet and collapse.
</p><p>When Haymitch was interviewed, he decided to adopt the persona of the "rascal," playing up the fact that he had received a <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> of 1. Caesar asked Haymitch, "So, Haymitch, what do you think about the Hunger Games having 100% more competitors than usual?" Haymitch responded, "Well, they'll still be 100% stupid as usual, so I figured that my odds will be roughly the same," making the audience laugh. He spoke of how he had already been attacked by one of the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">career tributes</a>, <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>. He described himself as being proud of his training score, saying that he had guts as he wasn't afraid to piss off the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>, and that anyone could get a ten but that you had to be a special kind of trouble to get a one. He wrapped the interview by alluding to brooding illegal alcohol and Caesar declared him to be a real rascal.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterviews%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Each of the 24 contestants in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> were given an interview. Some of the noted ones were:
</p>
<ul><li>The girl from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>, <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>, was described as very beautiful and sexy. <a href="/wiki/Katniss" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Katniss">Katniss</a> also notes that her mentor probably didn't have much trouble thinking of an angle of approach to make herself seem a good bet. She wore a translucent gold silk dress in the book, though in the film she wore a short pink dress. In the movie, when asked if she was prepared, she responded, "Yes, Caesar, I am <i>very</i> prepared."</li>
<li>The boy from District 1, <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> showed confidence and made jokes during his interview. He was said to not be the sharpest tool in the shed in the <i><a href="/wiki/Tribute_Guide" class="mw-redirect" title="Tribute Guide">Tribute Guide</a></i>. He wore a light blue suit &amp; tie.</li>
<li>The girl from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, was arrogant and ready for cold-blooded killing. She confirmed to have hery high skills with knives. She wore a pretty orange dress.</li>
<li>The boy from District 2, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, was noted as a monstrous, ruthless killing-machine. He said he was determined to win for his district. He wore a black suit.</li>
<li>The <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (74th HG)">male's interview</a> was said to be nothing special. In the film, you can see him waiting in line wearing a yellow suit.</li>
<li>The girl from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, nicknamed <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a>, was sly and elusive. She spoke very little but when she did Katniss noted that she was quiet. In the film, it was shown that she talked fast and used many intelligent words, such as "evaluate" and "conclude". Her outfit was a strapless turquoise dress. She said; "I find that if I can apply myself to the situation present, I will be able to figure it out." Fans speculate that if you listen carefully at the end of her interview you can hear Caesar say Finch, which could possibly be her name. It is also possible because on the map, the area where 5 is located is full of finches.</li>
<li>The <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">crippled male</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> was very quiet and shy.</li>
<li>The girl from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, was not very shy, saying her best strategy is her speed, saying "If they can't catch me, they can't kill me. So don't count me out." She was dressed in a gossamer gown complete with wings making the audience in awe of her (in the book). In the movie she wore a short blue dress.</li>
<li>The boy from District 11, <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>, was very large, and attempted to answer the questions with single yes or no answers. Katniss thinks that if she had any money, she would bet on him.</li></ul>

<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, the female tribute from District 12, was very nervous and couldn't think straight during her interview. Her <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a>, told her to be honest and pretend like the interview questions are coming from him to calm her down and throughout the interview, Katniss reminded herself to be honest. She won the Capitol over by showing off her outfit and twirling around, making her look beautiful. She said that her favorite thing about the Capitol was the lamb stew and that she would be trying to win the Hunger Games for her little sister, <a href="/wiki/Prim" class="mw-redirect" title="Prim">Prim</a>. She had a dress in which the bottom was fabricated and made her look like she was on fire when she twirled. When Cinna asked her about her unusually high training score, an 11, she was vague on the details, saying only that what she did was a first and, addressing the Gamemakers, that she wasn't supposed to talk about it.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, the male tribute from District 12, won the Capitol over with his looks and sense of humor. He joked and asked Caesar Flickerman if he smelled like roses. He makes a joke about the showers in the capitol, and Caesar states that Peeta is "charming and has a wonderful sense of humor." He also revealed that he has had a crush on Katniss, his female counterpart, for a long time, which started the star crossed lovers from district twelve story.</li></ul>
<p>Only Glimmer, Marvel, Clove, Cato, "Foxface", Rue, and the protagonists have interview scenes in the film. But some of the interviews were cut from the film. The crippled boy and Thresh did not have interview scenes, but both were shown in the queue scene.

<p>At the close of the interviews, Peeta and Katniss led the victors in a spontaneous show of unity by holding hands. Some, mostly the career victors, are reluctant at first, but eventually all the tributes joined in to form an unbroken line, sending the crowd into a chaotic uproar. The chain of hands, though seen to some as a symbol of the victors' friendship and the first public show of unity among the districts since the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, was meant as a symbol of rebellion, saying that even the dreadfulness of the games couldn't sever the bond the victors had formed nor destroy the united strength they held. Nonetheless, this did not stop the tributes from battling to the death once the Games began the following day.
</p>
