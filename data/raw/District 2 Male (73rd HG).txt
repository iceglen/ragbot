<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="73rd_Hunger_Games">73rd Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>This tribute either <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> or was <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for the 73rd Hunger Games. Given his <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>'s reputation for producing <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>, he was likely one of those who trained and eventually volunteered to participate.
</p><p>During the Games, the District 2 male made it to the final two and used his strength to overpower the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(73rd_HG)" title="District 10 male (73rd HG)">male tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>. He then beat his opponent to death with a brick from a pile of rubble, becoming that year's victor.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He was later spared from having to compete for a second time in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. It is unknown whose name was chosen at the reaping, but <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> volunteered to compete.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He was most likely killed at some point during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, either by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> or <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> forces during the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors' Purge">Victors' Purge</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_script">Early script</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In an early version of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> script, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> told <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> he thought the victor would be the boy from 2. Later on, during <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> mentioned to <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> that the 72nd and 73rd Hunger Games were both won by District 2 tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the film, he was portrayed by an African-American actor with short hair. In voiceover, Caesar Flickerman commented that the final fight was between two 16-year-olds.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>His <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> included a red jacket, which matched the film's color coding for tributes from District 2.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>He was the last Career to become a victor, as the 74th Hunger Games were won by <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, and the 75th had no victor.</li>
<li>He was also the final victor to be crowned under normal circumstances, since the 74th Hunger Games had an unprecedented double victory.</li></ul>
