<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgeria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Egeria had been the Director of Labor Operations in District 2 before becoming the Capitol Minister of Affairs. She led the Capitol Ministry of Information, a branch that extended throughout Panem to improve the nation's functionality through data collection and analysis. Egeria's role was to ensure the divisions worked together efficiently.<sup id="cite_ref-cc_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cc-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion"><a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgeria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, Egeria wrote a speech for Snow referring to <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> citizens as <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a>. However, Snow disapproved of her terminology because he didn't want to legitimize the movement, so Egeria put forth "radicals" or "criminals" as alternatives. She later tried to dissuade Snow from launching an assault on the <a href="/wiki/District_8_hospital" title="District 8 hospital">field hospital</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, but he justified the punishment as being derived from the language of her speech. She was also with President Snow in his office when the power cut out after rebels destroyed a dam in <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-p1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, Egeria was present at a dinner with other high-ranking Capitol officials. She watched and did not intervene as Snow poisoned <a href="/wiki/Antonius" title="Antonius">Antonius</a> for placing a high number of <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> into <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, which had fallen into occupation by the rebels. She later woke Snow to inform him that <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was still alive and showed him footage of <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> in the city's sewers and <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>. When she saw blood on Snow's tissue, she asked if she should call for a doctor, and Snow declined.<sup id="cite_ref-p2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Reconstruction">Reconstruction</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgeria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the war, Snow was tried and sentenced to death,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and a number of his collaborators were still awaiting trial at the time of his <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Egeria likely would have been among those arrested.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgeria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Egeria had strong management skills.<sup id="cite_ref-cc_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cc-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Although Egeria was a high-ranking Capitol official, she showed hesitance and did not appear to entirely agree with the Capitol's harsh regime.<sup id="cite_ref-p1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It's likely that she tread carefully to save herself from being poisoned like some of Snow's other associates.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgeria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

Her hair was elegantly twisted, and she had gold eyeliner and jewel earrings.<sup id="cite_ref-cc_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cc-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEgeria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In Roman mythology, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egeria_(mythology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Egeria (mythology)">Egeria</a> was a nymph that served as a counselor of the second king of Rome. She was a skilled speaker and lawmaker. The name Egeria is used to refer to a female advisor.
</p>
