</p><p><b>Ralph Fiennes</b> is an English actor of film, television and theater who is set to play the role of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>. He succeeds <a href="/wiki/Tom_Blyth" title="Tom Blyth">Tom Blyth</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes"><i>The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i></a> and the late <a href="/wiki/Donald_Sutherland" title="Donald Sutherland">Donald Sutherland</a> in the original films.<sup id="cite_ref-Ralph_Finnes_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ralph_Finnes-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRalph_Fiennes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Fiennes is perhaps most known for also having played the main villain of the <i>Harry Potter</i> film franchise, the dark wizard Lord Voldemort. His other major projects have included the Holocaust epic <i>Schindler's List</i>, the comic film <i>The Grand Budapest Hotel</i>, and the political thriller <i>Conclave.</i><sup id="cite_ref-EW_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EW-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRalph_Fiennes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Ralph Fiennes shared the screen with several prominent actors from <i>The Hunger Games</i> franchise:
<ul><li>He co-starred in <i>Land of the Blind</i> (2006) with <a href="/wiki/Donald_Sutherland" title="Donald Sutherland">Donald Sutherland</a>, his late predecessor for the role of Snow.</li>
<li>He starred with <a href="/wiki/Philip_Seymour_Hoffman" title="Philip Seymour Hoffman">Philip Seymour Hoffman</a> (<a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>) in <i>Red Dragon</i> (2002).</li>
<li>He also starred in <i>Conclave</i> (2024) with <a href="/wiki/Stanley_Tucci" title="Stanley Tucci">Stanley Tucci</a> (<a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>).</li></ul></li></ul>
