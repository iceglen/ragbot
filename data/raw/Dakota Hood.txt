</p><p><b>Dakota Hood</b> is an American actress and singer who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_10_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 10 female (74th HG)">District 10 female</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>. She is one of the last actresses to be cast for a tribute that is mentioned only once and fell during the initial bloodbath, and is not involved in the film's bloodbath scenes.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDakota_Hood%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Apart from <i>The Hunger Games</i>, Hood has also been in an episode of <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redrum" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Redrum">Redrum</a></i> as Stacy and was in the film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changeover" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Changeover">Changeover</a></i>. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In 2014, she collaborated on the series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantine_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Constantine (TV series)">Constantine</a></i>, which was released on NBC. Hood has also released her debut single called "Front Page News" in 2018.
</p>
