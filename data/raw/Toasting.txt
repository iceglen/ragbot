<p>The <b>toasting</b> is a marriage ritual in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FToasting%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Traditionally, the bride would rent a wedding dress that had been worn hundreds of times, and the groom wore something clean that wasn't <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">mining</a> clothes. They would go to the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a> to fill out forms and be assigned a house. Friends and family gathered to share a meal, or if they could afford it, some cake. Then the newlywed couple crossed the threshold of their new home as their guests sang a traditional wedding song. Finally, the couple made their first <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a> together and toasted a piece of <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> to share between the two of them. This constituted the wedding ceremony.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FToasting%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>At the time of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, people would sometimes pool their money and have two or three couples get married on the same day. That way, they could afford to hire the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> to provide live music and entertainment.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>During <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> mentioned that <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> would never get to have a wedding on account of the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>. Peeta claimed that although he and Katniss weren't married legally, they already considered themselves so because they had their toasting in total secrecy before the Quell was announced. However, this was a lie meant to sway the <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> audience, so it had not actually taken place.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
