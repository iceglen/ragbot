	<div class="pi-data-value pi-font"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i></div>

</p><p>The <b>Citadel</b> was a military institution located within <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. It housed the Capitol's War Department, guarded at all times by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> as a high-security zone. Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> and her <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> subordinates were based at the Citadel, where they created <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> in underground laboratories.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Other known employees included <a href="/wiki/Dr._Kay" title="Dr. Kay">Dr. Kay</a><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and one or both parents of <a href="/wiki/Gaius_Breen" title="Gaius Breen">Gaius Breen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCitadel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Entrance">Entrance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCitadel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Citadel was gated, and visitors had to stop at the Peacekeeper station for a retinal scan, which was checked against the Capitol's files. Any bags they carried could be left at the station until they returned<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or searched and kept on their person.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With this done, visitors would go down a long, gray corridor and onto an elevator, either escorted by a Peacekeeper<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or unaccompanied, in the case of a trusted repeat visitor.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lab">Lab</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCitadel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The lab was located at least twenty-five floors belowground. Dr. Gaul<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Dr. Kay both worked there, and the mutts they created were stored there as well.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCitadel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4e/Snake_mutts_in_tank.png/revision/latest?cb=20230427235538" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Snake mutts in their tank in the Citadel lab" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4e/Snake_mutts_in_tank.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230427235538" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="221" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Snake mutts in tank.png" data-image-key="Snake_mutts_in_tank.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Snake_mutts_in_tank.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Snake mutts in their tank in the Citadel lab</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>On Friday, July 8th, <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a> and <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> went to the Citadel to hand in a school assignment to Dr. Gaul. However, Coriolanus had been the only one to work on it. Gaul, suspecting this, had one of her assistants line the venomous snakes' case with it and insisted that they reach in to retrieve it. Since Coriolanus's scent was on it, he was able to pass the test unharmed, but Clemensia was bitten instantly by half a dozen of them.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She was rushed away for medical attention, and Coriolanus was left to find his own way out of the facility. He stumbled upon <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avoxes</a>, who had animal parts grafted onto their bodies in some grotesque experiment, being tortured with <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> that echoed their screams. He was then admonished by Dr. Kay for upsetting her birds and escorted to the elevator. When he exited, Peacekeepers returned his and Clemensia's book bags.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coriolanus reported to the Citadel regularly once he secured a Gamemaker internship that fall.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<ul><li>The exterior of the Department of War was filmed at the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Simon_Gallery" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:James Simon Gallery">James Simon Gallery</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_audiocom_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_audiocom-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<li>The place scared Clemensia Dovecote as a child, and Coriolanus Snow admitted that it still scared him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Gaius Breen was considered a "Citadel brat," with one or both of his parents working there.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
