<p><b>Coriolanus Snow's mother</b> was the wife of <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a> and the mother of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>. She lived in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and married into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Snow_family" title="Capitol families">Snow family</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoriolanus_Snow%2527s_mother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mrs. Snow fell in love with Crassus Snow, having convinced herself that he would give her freedom and love.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She gave birth to her son, Coriolanus, and often sang him loving songs at bedtime. About five years later, shortly before the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, Mrs. Snow became pregnant again, this time with a <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_sister" title="Coriolanus Snow's sister">baby girl</a> who later died.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoriolanus_Snow%2527s_mother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the first major air raid a few months into the war, she went into labor and was unable to get to a hospital. Mrs. Snow suffered what was likely a hemorrhage, and while <a href="/wiki/The_Snows%27_cook" title="The Snows' cook">the Snows' cook</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma'am">Grandma'am</a> attempted to stop the flow of blood that soaked her sheets, they were unable to save her. The baby was lost as well.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Compact">Compact</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoriolanus_Snow%2527s_mother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After her death, Coriolanus kept his mother's <a href="/wiki/Silver_compact" title="Silver compact">silver compact</a> in a drawer in his nightstand, and he breathed in the rose scent of her powder to calm himself during difficult times.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This compact was later borrowed by <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> to assist her in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, providing a container in which she could store poison for other <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It was eventually returned to Coriolanus.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoriolanus_Snow%2527s_mother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Snow_family_stained_glass_set_piece.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231117194245" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Stained glass set piece of Mrs" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Snow_family_stained_glass_set_piece.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231117194245" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="81" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Snow family stained glass set piece.jpg" data-image-key="Snow_family_stained_glass_set_piece.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Snow_family_stained_glass_set_piece.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Stained glass set piece of Mrs. Snow with her infant son and husband.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>She had a pretty, youthful appearance, which she often checked in her silver compact. Mrs. Snow also carried a handbag,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and because of the scented powder in her compact, she always smelled like roses. The Grandma'am said Coriolanus had his father's pale blue eyes, but Coriolanus secretly wished he had his mother's eyes instead,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so Mrs. Snow presumably did not have blue eyes.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoriolanus_Snow%2527s_mother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Mrs. Snow was briefly seen in a photograph on her son's desk in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>. She had long brown hair and an orange scarf draped over her shoulders.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoriolanus_Snow%2527s_mother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mrs. Snow was considered to be "the very definition of a lady" by <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a>, as she was always gracious to him despite his background in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was pretty, but could be vapid. According to <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, she had convinced herself that Crassus Snow would give her freedom and love, and seemed fated to be a child forever due to her youth.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mrs. Snow was also described to have "not really been tough enough for this world",<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and had a fondness for music.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
