<p>The District 9 female was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>  Her pedestal was the farthest to the left, to the left of Thresh.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_9_female_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b6/D9girlID.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140428161541" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The District 9 girl's ID card" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b6/D9girlID.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140428161541" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="108" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="D9girlID.jpg" data-image-key="D9girlID.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:D9girlID.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The District 9 girl's ID card</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<ul><li>In the film, she was 14 years old, and she weighed 88 pounds.</li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, she wore a bottle green jacket. This jacket was never actually produced, so it is possible that her portrait is digitally edited and colorized.</li>
<li>She is seen in the sky when the fallen images are shown. Her face was also the last one seen on day one as a production error.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Training_Days" title="The Hunger Games: Training Days">The Hunger Games: Training Days</a></i> board game, she and her district partner, along with District 10 and <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, were not given cards or any recognition.</li>
<li>Since her actress is strictly an extra, the Tribute Parade is her only live-action appearance in the film. This is shared with her district partner and the <a href="/wiki/District_10_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 10 female (74th HG)">District 10 female</a>.
<ul><li>Even though she was never seen outside of the parade, boots for her and the District 10 female's Arena Costumes were still produced.</li></ul></li>
<li>On the scoreboard behind <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius</a>, it shows that she survived for 1 minute and 37 seconds into the bloodbath, placing 18th.</li>
<li>She was the 7th dead, but according to the scoreboard shown when the District 8 female was killed, she was the 3rd to die.</li></ul>
