<p><b>Victors</b> are <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> who have won previous <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. Because of this, they receive income from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> for life, are given a special status in their <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>, and are allowed to live in the wealthiest part of their district called the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a> with their families. Despite the privileges they enjoy, their involvement in the Hunger Games lasts for a lifetime, and they are placed under strict surveillance to ensure that they do not encourage rebellion against the Capitol. Their primary duty as victors is that they must <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> future tributes from their district through the Hunger Games. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life_as_a_victor">Life as a victor</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="1st_to_10th_Hunger_Games">1st to 10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the 1st to <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, victors were not given any specific importance or care by the Capitol. After winning and surviving their individual Games, they were generally sent back to their home districts and left to resume their old lives. It was only during and after the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a> that victors were put into the Capitol's spotlight, forcing them to take a bigger role in future Games. These new changes also gave victors compensation and rewards for their victories, in an attempt to entice a better crop of tributes to <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a> for future Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Victors are often forced to put on false personas for the Capitol audience, such as <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>'s flirtatiousness, or <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s inseparable love. They also adopt hobbies or talents they can be interviewed about by the Capitol media. Peeta's talent was painting, but Katniss's skill at hunting was illegal and she was unwilling to sing, so she tried several alternatives from a list provided by <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>: cooking, flower arranging, and playing the flute. Katniss eventually settled on faking a passion for fashion design. Her <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a>, made things himself for her to pass off as her work.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour">Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a></i></center></div>
<p>The Victory Tour, first implemented for the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Mags_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mags-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> occurs almost midway between the annual Hunger Games, and is held to keep the memory of the year's Games fresh in everyone's mind.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They start in the highest numbered district and count down, skipping the victor's district and saving it for last. In each district, the victor gives a speech commemorating said district's fallen tributes, followed by a dinner with the district's leaders. Prior to returning to their resident district, the victor stops in the Capitol for an interview following up on their life after the Games, with the night concluding with a lavish party at the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">president's mansion</a>. The Victory Tour concludes with the Capitol throwing a huge feast for the victor's district where all the residents attend.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Trauma">Trauma</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>And the more I come to know these people, the worse it is. Because, on the whole, I don't hate them. And some I like. And a lot of them are so damaged that my natural instinct would be to protect them.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> describing the victors reaped for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Most, if not all, of the victors suffer from some form of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-traumatic_stress_disorder" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Post-traumatic stress disorder">post-traumatic stress</a> as a result of their experiences in the Games. For example, both Katniss and Peeta endure nightmares,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> is notably mentally unstable even for a victor.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Substance_abuse">Substance abuse</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Many victors turn to alcohol or drugs after they have won their Games as a means of coping. Some examples can be seen with <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> or <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a> and their heavy drinking, and the <a href="/wiki/Morphlings" title="Morphlings">pair</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> with their addiction to <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a>. These addictions stem from the traumatic experiences and memories of the Games, the demands that the Capitol continues to impose on them, the death penalty on them or their families, and, for those who become mentors, having to train new tributes every year which more likely will end up dying in their Games.
</p>

</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The families of victors can be used as leverage to force them to bend to the Capitol's will.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss also mentioned that the children of victors are reaped to participate in the Hunger Games suspiciously often.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>'s son <a href="/wiki/Ampert_Latier" title="Ampert Latier">Ampert</a> suffered this fate in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mentorship">Mentorship</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">Mentor</a></i></center></div>
<p>While a victor is not obligated to work as part of their district's industry, one of their primary duties is to serve as a mentor for future tributes. In this role, the mentor is to provide tactical advice on how to survive the Games, assess their pre-existing skills and talents and how best to display them during training and their interview, and serve as a lifeline for their tributes once the Games have started by attracting <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a> to finance sponsor gifts in the arena, such as food, medicine, supplies, and weapons. This last duty is one of the most crucial to their mentorship duties, as their relative success (or failure) at attracting sponsors is dependent on the impression their tributes make during training and interviews and their performance in the Games themselves, which can have an enormous impact on whether their tribute dies or survives long enough to be crowned victor themselves.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Third_Quarter_Quell">Third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>While victors are exempted from further eligibility in the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>, this changed for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. The special rule for the third Quarter Quell stipulated that, as a reminder to the rebels that even the strongest among them could not overcome the power of the Capitol, the tributes would be selected from the existing pool of victors. Thus, twenty-four victors across twelve districts were forced to participate in the Hunger Games for a second time.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
