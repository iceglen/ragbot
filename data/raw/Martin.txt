<p><b>Martin</b> was one of the harvesters in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>. He worked alongside <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> in the orchards, and was said to act like a three-year-old despite being older.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMartin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Martin was raised in District 11 and helped with the harvest, sometimes working through the night. He once tried to keep his night vision glasses by hiding them in his pants. As a result, he was <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executed</a> right on the spot by the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>. According to Rue, he probably just wanted to keep the glasses to play with, as he wasn't considered a dangerous person.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Rue and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> were talking about their <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a> and the night vision glasses Katniss got from her <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpack</a>. Rue told her that they were the same glasses they sometimes used in District 11, and she explained Martin's fate. This reminded Katniss of <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae%27s_granddaughter" title="Greasy Sae&#39;s granddaughter">Greasy Sae's granddaughter</a>, who was treated much better by comparison, and she realized that <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> was sort of a "safe haven" compared to the harsh policing in Rue's district.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
