<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGwendoline_Christie%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Gwendoline Christie is most famously known for her&#160;role on the HBO TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Game of Thrones">Game of Thrones</a></i>, in which she portrays <a href="https://gameofthrones.fandom.com/wiki/Brienne_of_Tarth" class="extiw" title="w:c:gameofthrones:Brienne of Tarth">Brienne of Tarth</a>. She also plays the part of <a href="https://wizardsvsaliens.fandom.com/wiki/Lexi" class="extiw" title="w:c:wizardsvsaliens:Lexi">Lexi</a> in the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wizards_vs_Aliens" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Wizards vs Aliens">Wizards vs Aliens</a></i>. She was casted to play as Captain <a href="https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phasma" class="extiw" title="w:c:starwars:Phasma">Phasma</a> in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars_Episode_VII" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Star Wars Episode VII">Star Wars Episode VII</a></i>, which was released in December 2015.
</p>
