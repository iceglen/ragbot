She graduated from the Juilliard School in 1993 and has won many awards for her acting, including an Oscar, a Primetime Emmy Award, two Tony Awards, and a Grammy Award for narrating her memoir.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
