</p><p><b>Philip Seymour Hoffman</b> (July 23, 1967 – February 2, 2014) was an American actor and director. He portrayed <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>. He was in many films, including <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Master_(2012_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Master (2012 film)">The Master</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ides_of_March_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Ides of March (film)">The Ides of March</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moneyball_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Moneyball (film)">Moneyball</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubt_(2008_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Doubt (2008 film)">Doubt</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capote_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Capote (film)">Capote</a></i>, where he played the title role and won the Academy Award for Best Actor. He was found dead in his Greenwich Village apartment, on February 2, 2014, due to a heroin overdose. He was also in and out of rehab before his death after a long period of sobriety.
</p><p>His death did not affect the final film, as he only had "one major, emotional" scene left to film. It was announced that they would not bring him back digitally.
</p>
