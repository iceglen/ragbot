</p><p><b>Jack Quaid</b> is an American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, his feature film debut.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He is the son of actors <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Quaid" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dennis Quaid">Dennis Quaid</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Ryan" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Meg Ryan">Meg Ryan</a> and the nephew of actor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_Quaid" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Randy Quaid">Randy Quaid</a>.
</p><p>Quaid attends <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:New York University">New York University</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tisch_School_of_the_Arts" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tisch School of the Arts">Tisch School of the Arts</a>, Experimental Theatre Wing, and is a member of the sketch comedy group Hammerkatz.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In past years, he has also starred in many comedy videos that he and his peers from college have posted on YouTube.
</p><p>Outside of <i>The Hunger Games</i>, he is most well known for his role as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hughie_Campbell" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hughie Campbell">Hughie Campbell</a> in Amazon Prime's TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Boys_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Boys (TV series)">The Boys</a></i>.
</p><p>In 2020, he took on the voice role of Brad Boimler in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek:_Lower_Decks" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Star Trek: Lower Decks">Star Trek: Lower Decks</a></i>.
</p>
