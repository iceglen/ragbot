</p><p><b>E. Roger Mitchell</b> is an American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. Mitchell has appeared in films such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle:_Los_Angeles" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Battle: Los Angeles">Battle: Los Angeles</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crazies_(2010_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Crazies (2010 film)">The Crazies</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_(2012_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Flight (2012 film)">Flight</a></i>. He has also appeared on the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walking_Dead_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Walking Dead (TV series)">The Walking Dead</a></i>.
</p>
