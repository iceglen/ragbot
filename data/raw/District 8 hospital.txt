<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8_hospital%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Following the bombing, hundreds of people carted the wounded to the warehouse. Although districts ordinarily left people to die at home, the bombing had destroyed many housing units, leaving families with no other choice than to take patients to the makeshift hospital. A mass grave had been started a few blocks west.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8f/Katniss23.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150219055946" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Patients giving Katniss the three-finger salute" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8f/Katniss23.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150219055946" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="108" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss23.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss23.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss23.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Patients giving Katniss the three-finger salute</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The afternoon of the bombing, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was sent from <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> via <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> along with <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>, two other soldiers, and a TV crew. Another hovercraft carrying medical supplies and medics followed. <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Commander Paylor</a> greeted the team and gestured them inside. Katniss was initially repulsed by the conditions of the hospital, but gained confidence after being greeted by <a href="/wiki/Eddy" title="Eddy">Eddy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Eddy%27s_sister" title="Eddy&#39;s sister">his sister</a>. She interacted with the wounded and allowed them to touch her. Many reassured her about <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, and people were devastated upon hearing that Katniss had "lost the baby" she had supposedly been carrying.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">film</a>, patients gave her the <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a> after she told them she would fight.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>However, once Katniss and the crew exited, <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a> received news of incoming bombers targeting the hospital. Although Katniss, Gale, and other soldiers attempted to fend them off, the new wave of bombs collapsed the hospital roof and set the building on fire. A group of rescuers tried to clear a path, but the patients were trapped and killed by falling debris, flames, and smoke asphyxiation. According to Gale, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> had found the patients "expendable" and wanted to scare others off from seeking help. He aired the bombing live as a way of sending a message. In response, Katniss recorded a <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> to warn Snow and encourage the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> to fight back.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, <a href="/wiki/Antonius" title="Antonius">Antonius</a> showed Snow camera footage of Katniss at the hospital and suggested sending hovercraft to target her. Snow ordered him to kill the wounded despite <a href="/wiki/Egeria" title="Egeria">Egeria</a>'s attempts to protest. He argued that everyone in the hospital had committed treason by associating with Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8_hospital%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>The drone of black flies, the moaning of people in pain, and the sobs of their attending loved ones have combined into a wrenching chorus.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;The atmosphere of the hospital<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/20/D8_hospital_interior.png/revision/latest?cb=20250812174626" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The interior of the hospital" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/20/D8_hospital_interior.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250812174626" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="D8 hospital interior.png" data-image-key="D8_hospital_interior.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:D8_hospital_interior.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The interior of the hospital</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The warehouse had the letter H sloppily painted above the entrance. An industrial curtain hung the length of the interior, forming a corridor where corpses were placed with cloths covering their faces. Rows of wounded lay behind the curtain on cots, pallets, and the floor. Black flies were prevalent, and the hospital smelled of soiled linen, rotting flesh, and vomit. Open skylights did nothing to ease the smell, nor did the heat.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8_hospital%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

The scenes outside the hospital were filmed at the Rüdersdorf chemical plant in Germany.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
