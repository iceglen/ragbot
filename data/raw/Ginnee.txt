<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> describing how little <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> and Ginnee's deaths meant to the Capitol.</dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Ginnee</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. Her <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana Ring</a>. She died before the Games even started. During the Ring twins' funeral, the bodies of her and her district partner, <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> (or what remained of them), were draped on horses and paraded down Scholars Road.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 176px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5e/GinneeCapitolZoo.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316063332" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Ginnee in the Capitol Zoo." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5e/GinneeCapitolZoo.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316063332" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="176" height="206" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="GinneeCapitolZoo.png" data-image-key="GinneeCapitolZoo.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:GinneeCapitolZoo.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Ginnee in the Capitol Zoo.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Ginnee grew up in District 6 and became her district's female tribute in <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with the other tributes, Ginnee was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While in the zoo, Ginnee and <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> began a funny dance, and their mentors, <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana</a> and <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo Ring</a>, got some of the audience to clap along. This led to the environment becoming more festive and social.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the start of the Games, the tributes and their mentors were taken on a tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, sending shrapnel that killed Ginnee, as well as her district partner and their mentors.
</p><p>Her body was later draped on the back of a horse and paraded down <a href="/wiki/Scholars_Road" title="Scholars Road">Scholars Road</a> during the Ring twins' funeral.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ginnee's name is an anagram of the word "engine", as in a car engine.
</p>
