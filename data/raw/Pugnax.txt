<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPugnax%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Pugnax was among the many workers and miners who survived the Nut's bombing and fled to the city center, where the <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">rebels</a> (who had launched a major offensive and captured the Nut) were waiting for them. He had a ragged hole in his cheek, likely having been injured by falling stone, scorch marks down the back of his shirt, and a gun. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> saw Pugnax as just another victim from a mine accident and ran to him after ordering the rebels to hold their fire. In response, Pugnax held his gun to her head, asking why he shouldn't kill her. Katniss made an impromptu speech to the Nut personnel, which ended when a man shot her from inside the crowd.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She survived, but she lost her spleen and her ribs were badly bruised, leaving her out of action for a month.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, Pugnax shot Katniss in the stomach after she finished her speech, and the rebels proceeded to riddle him with bullets and kill him.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPugnax%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When he fled from the Nut, Pugnax held a bloody cloth to his cheek, which had been punctured, and had scorch marks down the back of his shirt. He held a gun and had eyes crazed with pain and fear. Pugnax's speech was garbled, and according to Katniss, he smelled of burning things.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
