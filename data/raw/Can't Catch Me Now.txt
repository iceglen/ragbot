</p><p><b>"Can't Catch Me Now"</b> is the second single from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes_(Music_From_%26_Inspired_By)" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)</a></i>. The song was released worldwide on November 3rd, 2023, written and performed by singer-songwriter <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Olivia Rodrigo">Olivia Rodrigo</a>, and co-written by producer <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Nigro" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dan Nigro">Dan Nigro</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Media">Media</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCan%2527t_Catch_Me_Now%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The song's release was first revealed at a promotional event for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> on November 1st, 2023. That evening, a choir sang "<a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">The Hanging Tree</a>" live in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_Square" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Times Square">Times Square</a>, and actors <a href="/wiki/Rachel_Zegler" title="Rachel Zegler">Rachel Zegler</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tom_Blyth" title="Tom Blyth">Tom Blyth</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Josh_Andr%C3%A9s_Rivera" title="Josh Andrés Rivera">Josh Andrés Rivera</a> came out to speak before a trailer played with the first excerpt of the song.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
