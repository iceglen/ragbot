<p>A <b>Quarter Quell</b> was a special edition of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> that was held every twenty-five years. They marked every quarter-century anniversary of the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>' defeat by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Concept">Concept</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FQuarter_Quell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>All known Quarter Quells were characterized by a twist in the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> process or pool, altering the selection of <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> in some way. The <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th</a> and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> also had twists to do with their respective <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arenas</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FQuarter_Quell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Creation">Creation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FQuarter_Quell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Capitol claimed that when they created the laws for the Games at the end of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, they dictated that a Quarter Quell would be held every twenty-five years. It was made to seem that the twists for these Games were written out at that time, with plans for "centuries of Hunger Games".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, the political convenience of the twist for the third Quarter Quell made <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> doubt that it had truly been prepared so far in advance.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> There was also no mention of Quarter Quells in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, which took place ten years after those laws were written. This casts further doubt on the Quarter Quells being intended from the inception of the Games themselves and were likely added to the concept of Games, along with a number additions and modification made Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> and <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, during the Games' early years and evolution into their modern form.
</p>

<p>In a deleted scene from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a> <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> entered an oval-shaped room with two <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>, opened a locker marked with the Roman numeral "LXXV" (75), and burned the envelope containing the original twist for the 75th Hunger Games. The number of lockers implied that, at least in the films, the Capitol had truly prepared around 80 Quarter Quells in advance.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_delscene_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_delscene-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="25th_Hunger_Games">25th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FQuarter_Quell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div><div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>On the twenty-fifth anniversary, as a reminder to the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> that their children were dying because of their choice to initiate violence, every district was made to hold an <a href="/wiki/Elections" title="Elections">election</a> and vote on the tributes that who would represent it.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a>, the citizens of the districts had to vote on who would compete that year.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> is unknown and was presumed deceased by the time of the 75th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Cornucopia is also first introduced.
</p><p>Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> did commentary for the Games with <a href="/wiki/Lucky_Flickerman" class="mw-redirect" title="Lucky Flickerman">Lucky Flickerman</a> and was credited with coining the phrase "may the odds be EVER in your favor" which would later become a way of saying good luck.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FQuarter_Quell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div><div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Sunrise on the Reaping">"<i>On the fiftieth anniversary, as a reminder that two rebels died for each Capitol citizen, every district was required to send twice as many tributes. Two female and two male. In this doubling of reparations, we remember that true strength lies not in numbers, but in righteousness.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;President Snow<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>For the 50th Hunger Games, instead of the usual two tributes, four children were reaped from each district, two male and two female. This meant that a total of 48 tributes would be sent into the arena, and 47 would die.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Two tributes in fact actually died prior to the games themselves: <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> and <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>. Woodbine chose to attempt escape immediately after being reaped and was killed by Peacekeepers and replaced by Haymitch Abernathy.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Louella McCoy was killed in a <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot ride</a> accident and replaced by a doppelganger, <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_10_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_10-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Everything in the arena was poisonous save for rainwater and <a href="/wiki/Food_and_drink" title="Food and drink">provisions</a> from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. The victor was <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, who tricked his last opponent, a <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">District 1 female</a>, into throwing her <a href="/wiki/Axe" class="mw-redirect" title="Axe">axe</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>. Her weapon rebounded and flew right back at her, killing her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The 50th Hunger Games was also notable for every tribute being a part of an alliance, the Careers or <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a>, the latter of which was made up of every non-Career district except for <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="50th Hunger Games arena">50th Hunger Games arena</a> was the target of a rebel plot to disrupt the games. Haymitch and <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> bombed the arena's water tank in Sub-A, causing a number of malfunctions.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After winning, Haymitch attempted to bomb the arena's generator which he was implied to be successful at.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The footage of the 50th Hunger Games was later heavily edited before being displayed to the Capitol audience.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FQuarter_Quell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div><div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>On the seventy-fifth anniversary, as a reminder to the rebels that even the strongest among them cannot overcome the power of the Capitol, the male and female tributes will be reaped from their existing pool of victors.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;President Snow<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>For the 75th Hunger Games, tributes were to be reaped from the pool of living victors of past Hunger Games, forcing them to compete for a second time.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As a result, the usual 12-18 age range was disregarded, marking the first time that adults would compete in the Games. Katniss felt that this twist was too convenient to be a coincidence and she believed it had been selected specifically to kill her in the arena in the hopes of stopping the uprisings she had inspired.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b2/Catching-Fire-Arena2.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120330194527" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The plan of the 75th Hunger Games arena." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b2/Catching-Fire-Arena2.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120330194527" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Catching-Fire-Arena2.jpg" data-image-key="Catching-Fire-Arena2.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Catching-Fire-Arena2.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The plan of the 75th Hunger Games arena.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>During the third Quarter Quell, the only supplies at the Cornucopia were <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">arena</a> was divided into twelve sections like a clock, with a new horror being inflicted upon the tributes who were in a specific section every hour.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Six tributes were alive when the Games were interrupted by the rebels' plan to extract Katniss from the arena, making it the first and only Hunger Games to have no victor.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FQuarter_Quell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Based on the deleted scene from the <i>Catching Fire</i> film, the Capitol had anticipated around 80 Quarter Quells or 2,000 years of Hunger Games.</li>
<li>Both the Second and Third Quarter Quells had a rebel plot surround them. In addition, some of the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> involved in both were <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>.</li></ul>
