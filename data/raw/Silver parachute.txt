<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilver_parachute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Prior_modes_of_delivery">Prior modes of delivery</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilver_parachute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Sponsor gifts were delivered for the first time in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> carried out via <a href="/wiki/Drone" title="Drone">drone</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is unknown whether <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> immediately discontinued the use of drones after that year or if this was a more gradual change. It is also not known whether they tried other modes of delivery before switching over to parachutes.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Hunger_Games">Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilver_parachute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Silver parachutes were in use by the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a> at the latest, when a parachute gave <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> the <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Trident" title="Weapons">trident</a> that won him his victory.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Mockingjay_parachutes.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231028232945" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Parachutes coming down over the City Circle in Mockingjay - Part 2." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Mockingjay_parachutes.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231028232945" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="73" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mockingjay parachutes.jpg" data-image-key="Mockingjay_parachutes.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mockingjay_parachutes.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Parachutes coming down over the City Circle in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="City_Circle_bombing"><a href="/wiki/City_Circle_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="City Circle bombing">City Circle bombing</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilver_parachute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the last days of the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, refugees sought asylum in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a>, and a group of children was positioned outside the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">President's mansion</a> to deter a <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebel</a> assault. On the final day of the conflict, a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> with the <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> insignia dropped silver parachutes into the crowd. By this time, silver parachutes had become synonymous with aid, so children rushed to grab what they believed would be gifts from the Capitol. This proved fatal when the bombs attached to these parachutes detonated. Many children were injured or outright killed, and a second explosion killed the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> and medics that came to their aid.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> later revealed to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> that this bombing was carried out by the rebels, under orders from <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>'s President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilver_parachute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The parachutes were made of a silver fabric<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> that Katniss once described as silk.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the films, the parachutes also let out beeping noises to help tributes locate them.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
