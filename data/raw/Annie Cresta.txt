<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Annie was born in District 4 in 52 ADD. Living in a Career district<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, Annie presumably enrolled in the district's Hunger Games training program at a young age. Throughout her late-childhood and teenage years, she was prepared for the rigors of the Arena by being trained in weapons skills, survival, and combat under the supervision of instructors and past District 4 victors such as <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>. At the age of 18, Annie, as typical for Careers, likely volunteered during the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/70th_Hunger_Games" title="70th Hunger Games">70th Hunger Games</a>. During her training in the Capitol in the lead up to the Games, Annie probably <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">allied</a> alongside her <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a> with the tributes from Districts <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a> as was common for Careers. 
</p><p>During the 70th Hunger Games, Annie witnessed her district partner, the <a href="/wiki/District_4_male_(70th_HG)" title="District 4 male (70th HG)">District 4 male</a>, be beheaded. The event severely traumatized her, resulting in her isolating herself from her allies and other tributes for most of the Games. This presumably worked for her up until the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> triggered an earthquake that broke a dam, flooding the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. Having grown up in District 4, Annie survived by being the best swimmer and was eventually declared victor after outlasting her competition.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Following her victory, Annie suffered from mental instability and PTSD from the trauma she experienced in her Games. She often closed her eyes and covered her ears, laughed at unusual times, and fell into a daze.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Despite this, she received help from Finnick Odair, a fellow District 4 victor of the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a>. Being the only person who could calm her, the two grew close, and eventually became secret lovers.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> As was the duty of victors following their Games, Annie participated in the 70th <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> alongside her mentors six months after her victory.
</p><p>Since <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> could not recall who Annie was though she won the Games only a few years prior to her own victory, it's possible that Annie was not celebrated as much as other Career victors such as <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> or <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. Her mental state after her victory is also indicative she was probably a recluse, as Katniss did not remember Annie being present among other victors in previous Games and only remembered her as the "one who went a little [crazy]". It can thus be inferred that Annie was neither an active participant in her district's Career program nor a mentor for District 4's tributes between the <a href="/wiki/71st_Hunger_Games" title="71st Hunger Games">71st</a> and <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.
</p><p>Due to her mental instability, Annie was not made privy to the conspiracy involving prominent Capitol officials, <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, and several Hunger Games victors, including Mags and Finnick. This was likely done due to her perceived unreliability and by Finnick, who neither wanted her involved and in order to protect her.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i>Catching Fire</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>With the announcement of the rule change for the Third Quarter Quell, Annie was once again eligible to be reaped, only 5 years after her victory. She was present alongside District 4's other victors during the reaping for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, where she was selected as 4's female tribute. Annie's selection sent her into hysterics onstage, leading Mags, an older victor, to volunteer to enter the Games in her place. Afterwards, Annie stayed in 4, likely at Finnick's urging. During the Games, her voice and screams were mimicked by <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> to torture Finnick. During the attack, Finnick stated to Katniss his belief the Capitol tortured Annie to allow the jabberjays to mimic her voice. Following the attack, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> told Katniss about Annie and her relationship to Finnick.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i>Mockingjay</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Following Katniss' destruction of the arena and the outbreak of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, Annie was arrested by the Capitol and tortured, alongside Enobaria, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a>, and Peeta. Finnick explained to Katniss that he believed they didn't take her as a hostage for information about the rebels. Instead, the Capitol perceived that Finnick would never endanger Annie by telling her anything, and took her simply as a weapon to use against him, just as they were using Peeta against Katniss. After Finnick suffered a mental breakdown over Annie's predicament,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>, the president of District 13, launched a clandestine mission to extract the imprisoned victors from the Capitol. The mission was a success, and Annie, alongside Peeta and Johanna, were safely returned to District 13. Upon her arrival, Annie was reunited with Finnick and the two embraced.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 286px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2d/Finnick_and_Annie_kissing.png/revision/latest?cb=20150609215155" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Finnick and Annie getting married in " src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2d/Finnick_and_Annie_kissing.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/286?cb=20150609215155" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="286" height="117" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Finnick and Annie kissing.png" data-image-key="Finnick_and_Annie_kissing.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Finnick_and_Annie_kissing.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Finnick and Annie getting married in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 258px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Annie_Cresta_on_her_wedding_day.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20180207131416" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Annie on her wedding day" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Annie_Cresta_on_her_wedding_day.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/258?cb=20180207131416" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="258" height="261" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Annie Cresta on her wedding day.jpg" data-image-key="Annie_Cresta_on_her_wedding_day.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Annie_Cresta_on_her_wedding_day.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Annie on her wedding day</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Sometime later, in District 13, she and Finnick were married in the traditional District 4 style, their wedding cake being decorated with waves and dolphins by Peeta. Annie wore the dress Katniss had worn in <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> on her <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>. Katniss noticed that, since their <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie's wedding">marriage</a>, Finnick did not let go of Annie's hand and that Annie seemed "lost in some daze of happiness."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Later on, Annie and Finnick met Katniss, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale</a>, Johanna, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee</a>, <a href="/wiki/Delly_Cartwright" title="Delly Cartwright">Delly</a>, and later a rehabilitating Peeta at lunch. When Johanna told Peeta that they were familiar with each other's screams, Annie covered her ears. Through their lunch conversation, Katniss figured that Annie was not all that bad, though a little mentally unstable. Peeta jokingly told Finnick to take care of Annie, or he would take her away from him.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later on, Finnick suggested that Peeta ask about what was real or not real, stating that Annie did the same thing when she tried to remain sane.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Sometime prior to the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, Finnick and Annie conceived a child and she became pregnant.
</p><p>During the Battle of the Capitol, whilst on a mission to infiltrate <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>'s <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President's mansion">mansion</a>, Finnick was decapitated by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a>. Katniss described how she watched his life flash by as if she were Finnick, which included seeing Annie in her wedding dress.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before President Snow's planned execution, President Coin gathered the seven known surviving victors. She asked if they would like one <a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">final Hunger Games</a> using children of the most powerful people in the Capitol in retribution for oppressing the districts and the Games themselves. Annie, along with Peeta and Beetee, voted no, with Annie commenting that Finnick would have voted alongside her. Enobaria, Johanna, Katniss, and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch">Haymitch</a>, however, voted yes. At President Snow's execution, Annie was present alongside the other victors and watched Katniss kill President Coin when she was supposed to kill President Snow.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the war and <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s transition to democracy, Annie returned to her home in District 4, where she gave birth to Finnick's <a href="/wiki/Annie_and_Finnick_Odair%27s_son" title="Annie and Finnick Odair's son">son</a>. She sent Katniss a photo of him to be put into her <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">memory book</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Being a Career, Annie was likely raised glorifying the Games and presumably, through her Career training, became ruthless in her preparations for the Games. Nevertheless, the trauma she experienced in her Games, left her mentally unstable and withdrawn. While Katniss described her as "strange," she felt that Annie was not "mad" as others believed, but simply "unstable".<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Annie would occasionally withdraw into herself, staring off into space with a blank smile on her face until she was brought back to reality. She sometimes laughed at odd points in conversation or dropped out entirely, and when she was triggered, she was known to close her eyes and cover her ears with her hands.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When she was reaped for the 75th Hunger Games, Annie was described as being "hysterical" before Mags volunteered in her place.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite this, post-Games Annie was a very kind-hearted and forgiving young woman, capturing the heart of fellow victor <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and later voting against <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">President Coin</a>'s proposed 76th Hunger Games using Capitol children.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Annie is a young adult woman with flowing brown hair<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and sea-green eyes. After being imprisoned in the Capitol, her hair appeared dark and tangled, "lovely if somewhat bedraggled".<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the movies, Annie has red hair and blue eyes.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Annie was married to<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and widowed by<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, the victor of the 65th Hunger Games. They had <a href="/wiki/Annie_and_Finnick_Odair%27s_son" title="Annie and Finnick Odair's son">a son</a> together following the end of the Second Rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Finnick_Odair"><a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File"><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/06/FinnickAnnieD13.png/revision/latest?cb=20161214164905" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="FinnickAnnieD13" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="196" class="mw-file-element lazyload" data-image-name="FinnickAnnieD13.png" data-image-key="FinnickAnnieD13.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/06/FinnickAnnieD13.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20161214164905"></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p>The two had a deep love for each other where Finnick mentored Annie, and most likely did everything to keep her alive. During the interview with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, Finnick recited a poem for Annie ("his one true love" as stated), though many female admirers in the Capitol thought the poem was aimed towards them.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the third Quarter Quell, Finnick heard Annie's screams during the jabberjay attack because she was who he cared about above all others. He was worried that the Capitol had hurt Annie.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>, Finnick constantly worried about Annie and could not concentrate on anything else but her. He was out of his mind because Annie had been captured by the Capitol so they could torture her for information about the rebels. To set his mind straight, he tied different knots with a foot of rope and brought it everywhere with him, hoping to regain sanity and distract himself. He broke down when he couldn't handle it anymore, and soon after his breakdown, the rebels rescued those captured by the Capitol, which included Annie.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Finnick and Annie were finally able to reunite in District 13.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The two would later marry and conceive a son.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Finnick was killed before Annie gave birth to their son.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Due to their strong relationship, it can be assumed that Annie greatly mourned his death.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Katniss_Everdeen"><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Although Katniss regarded her as mentally unstable, she recognized that Annie was not truly "mad" and treated her with kindness. When Annie got married to Finnick, Katniss went with her to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> and lent her one of her Victory Tour dresses to wear. They seemed to be on good terms, since they kept in touch after the war was over.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Annie is a diminutive version of a number of different names. Her surname, Cresta, seems to be derived from the word "crest", as in the crest of a wave.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Annie and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> were the only known victors still alive after <i>Mockingjay</i> who did not participate in the 75th Hunger Games. However, they were both reaped and volunteered for.</li>
<li>This makes Annie the only surviving victor to have never participated in a Quarter Quell, despite being reaped for the 75th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>Annie's symptoms resemble those of someone suffering from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/complex_post-traumatic_stress_disorder" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:complex post-traumatic stress disorder">complex post-traumatic stress disorder</a> (CPTSD).</li>
<li>Annie supposedly went mad after seeing the male tribute from her district get decapitated. When her husband, Finnick, dies, he is also decapitated.</li></ul>
