</p><p><b>Billy Porter</b> is the American actor and singer set to play <a href="/wiki/Magno_Stift" title="Magno Stift">Magno Stift</a> in the upcoming film <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>. Porter is a three-time Tony winner<sup id="cite_ref-cast_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cast-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and is known for using flamboyant fashion in a political way, such as to question people's perceptions of masculinity.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Having previously worked with him on the show <i>Pose</i>, <a href="/wiki/Nina_Jacobson" title="Nina Jacobson">Nina Jacobson</a> stated that Porter "is one of those rare performers who can dazzle and devastate in equal measure on stage and screen."<sup id="cite_ref-cast_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cast-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
