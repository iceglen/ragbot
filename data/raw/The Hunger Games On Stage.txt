<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Production">Production</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games_On_Stage%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	 <a href="https://thehungergames.fandom.com/wiki/File:The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage_-_London_2024" class="video video-thumbnail xsmall "> 	<img src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" alt="The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage_-_London_2024" height="135" width="180" data-video-key="The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage_-_London_2024" data-video-name="The Hunger Games On Stage - London 2024" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e6/The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage_-_London_2024/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231108005945" class="lazyload" /> 	<span class="thumbnail-play-icon-container"> 		<svg class="thumbnail-play-icon wds-icon"><use xlink:href="#wds-player-icon-play"></use></svg>	</span> </a>  	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage_-_London_2024" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 			<p class="title">The Hunger Games On Stage - London 2024</p> 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Hunger Games On Stage - London 2024 (Official Trailer)</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The stage production has been adapted by playwright Conor McPherson, with collaboration from author Suzanne Collins, and it is directed by Matthew Dunster.<sup id="cite_ref-website_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-website-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Other members of the creative team include Miriam Buether (set designer), Moi Tran (costume designer), Charlotte Broom (choreographer), Lucy Carter (lighting designer), Ian Dickinson (sound designer), Tal Rosner (video designer), Chris Fisher (illusions), Kev McCurdy (fight director), John Maddox (performer flying), Amy Ball (casting director), and Lloyd Thomas (production manager).<sup id="cite_ref-website_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-website-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Including theater construction expenses, the adaptation cost 26 million pounds to produce.<sup id="cite_ref-charlotte_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-charlotte-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Production_history">Production history</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games_On_Stage%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The production was originally set to debut on <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_theatre" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:West End theatre">West End</a> in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:London">London, England</a>, in autumn of 2024.<sup id="cite_ref-website_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-website-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was later delayed to January 2025,<sup id="cite_ref-delay_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-delay-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> then again to autumn 2025.<sup id="cite_ref-delay2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-delay2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On February 27, they announced that the show would open October 20 at the Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre.<sup id="cite_ref-venue_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-venue-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Mia_Carragher" title="Mia Carragher">Mia Carragher</a>, daughter of former football player Jamie Carragher, was announced to play Katniss Everdeen on July 29, 2025.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On July 31, it was revealed that <a href="/wiki/Euan_Garrett" title="Euan Garrett">Euan Garrett</a> and <a href="/wiki/Joshua_Lacey" title="Joshua Lacey">Joshua Lacey</a> were cast as <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, respectively. On August 5, Tristan Waterson was cast as <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, Ruth Everett was cast as <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a>, and Sophia Ally as <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>. On August 6, Tamsin Carroll was announced as <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>, Stavros Demetraki as <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, and Nathan Ives-Moiba as <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The full cast was revealed on August 8.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The show was cut from 3 hours to 2 hours and 15 minutes. Preview performances began on October 20, 2025, with the first preview unexpectedly delayed due to a cast injury. It also took place amidst unfinished construction, provoking complaints about accessibility. The official opening night was scheduled for November 12, and the production has been extended until October 18, 2026 due to demand.<sup id="cite_ref-variety_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-variety-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Venue">Venue</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games_On_Stage%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f5/Troubadour_Canary_Wharf_Theatre.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20251109091854" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="180" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre.jpg" data-image-key="Troubadour_Canary_Wharf_Theatre.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f5/Troubadour_Canary_Wharf_Theatre.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251109091854" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f5/Troubadour_Canary_Wharf_Theatre.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20251109091854" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f5/Troubadour_Canary_Wharf_Theatre.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251109091854" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre.jpg" data-image-key="Troubadour_Canary_Wharf_Theatre.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f5/Troubadour_Canary_Wharf_Theatre.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251109091854" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Troubadour_Canary_Wharf_Theatre.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre, located in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canary_Wharf" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Canary Wharf">Canary Wharf</a>, London, was built specifically for <i>The Hunger Games</i>. It contains a total of 1200 seats on all sides of a round stage, with sections named after the twelve districts.<sup id="cite_ref-venue_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-venue-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District 12 serves as the stage. Upon a tribute's death, their corresponding section of the audience flashes red. The venue also encourages audience participation by displaying expected reactions on screens during scenes with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-variety_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-variety-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The seats in District 1 and District 2 move throughout the performance as part of the immersive experience.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Actors are seen fighting on the walls and in 90-degree angles in front of the upper balcony.<sup id="cite_ref-charlotte_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-charlotte-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The theatre has three floors, including an outside terrace<sup id="cite_ref-venue_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-venue-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and a restaurant named Songbird.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
