<p>A <b>stylist</b> was a citizen of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> who was responsible for designing the clothing for one of two <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from their chosen or assigned <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> leading up to the annual <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Purpose">Purpose</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/Alojamiento_Distrito_12.png/revision/latest?cb=20140303063128" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Stylists Cinna and Portia with the District 12 team." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/Alojamiento_Distrito_12.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140303063128" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="97" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Alojamiento Distrito 12.png" data-image-key="Alojamiento_Distrito_12.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Alojamiento_Distrito_12.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Stylists Cinna and Portia with the District 12 team.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Stylists were responsible for selecting their tribute's wardrobe, styling them for both private and public appearances. These clothing choices played a critical role in conveying the tribute's public persona to the audience, and this could either attract or drive away <a href="/wiki/Sponsor" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor">sponsors</a> before the actual commencement of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Most stylists stayed in the profession for a long time, "constants in the ever-changing pool of tributes." Due to their many appearances over the years, they were familiar to the audience. Newer stylists were typically given the least desirable districts, such as <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>; however, even newcomers to the role could request a certain district.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tasks">Tasks</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7e/Portia.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120426074110" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Portia, the other District 12 stylist." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7e/Portia.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120426074110" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="187" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Portia.jpg" data-image-key="Portia.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Portia.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Portia, the other District 12 stylist.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Stylists created every item of clothing their tribute wore for the duration of their stay in the Capitol. Additionally, each stylist delegated tasks to their own three-person <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> of beauticians.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade">Tribute Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a></i></center></div>
<p>At the <a href="/wiki/Remake_Center" title="Remake Center">Remake Center</a>, a tribute would be prepared by their prep team before being seen by their stylist. Said stylist would introduce themselves and give their tribute the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">costume</a> they would wear while riding in their chariot. These costumes traditionally represented some aspect of their district's industry.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Stylists individually picked out their tributes' <a href="/wiki/Training_outfits" title="Training outfits">training outfits</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the films, the tributes were given uniforms to wear instead.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The stylists also designed <a href="/wiki/Interview_outfits" title="Interview outfits">outfits</a> for the tributes' night of <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, which took place on the evening before the Games. This was the last chance for the tribute and stylist to make an impression on the audience before the Games themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Arena">Arena</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Stylists were the only members of the tribute's team to accompany them right up to the start of the Games. They rode with them to the site of the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> in a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a>, traveled with them through the <a href="/wiki/Catacombs" title="Catacombs">catacombs</a>, and kept them company in their <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">Launch Room</a>. There, they put the finishing touches on their tribute's appearance. Stylists had no say in the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a>, as the uniform was the same for everyone, but they could style the tribute's hair and give them their <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a>. They could only speculate what the arena might be like based on the uniform's materials and the design choices made.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victors">Victors</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>If a stylist's tribute emerged from the Games as a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>, they were responsible for styling the new victor for closing ceremonies.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When the victor's support team rose up from beneath the stage for the occasion, the stylist was the third person introduced, after the prep team and <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a> but before the <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour">Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a></i></center></div>
<p>The stylist of a winning tribute also designed all of the victor's outfits for their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>. Stylists and the rest of the support team personally accompanied the new victor throughout these public appearances.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the position of a stylist had not yet been created. Tributes were simply thrown into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> in whatever they had worn to the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>, which had likely become rather dirty and soiled. The exception was <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, whose colorful dress had been repaired and cleaned up by <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> before her interview.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div>
<p><a href="/wiki/Magno_Stift" title="Magno Stift">Magno Stift</a> was the District 12 stylist for many years, gaining a reputation for frequent negligence, intoxication, and reckless handling of reptiles. Officially, he styled all four District 12 tributes during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>; however, his unreliable behavior forced the prep team— <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a> students <a href="/wiki/Proserpina_Trinket" title="Proserpina Trinket">Proserpina Trinket</a> and <a href="/wiki/Vitus" title="Vitus">Vitus</a>— to take on more responsibility. Proserpina eventually had to bring in her older sister, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>, to help style them.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_12_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_12-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> undertook his first year as a stylist alongside his partner <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a> during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. He specifically requested District 12, and he became the stylist to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, while Portia styled <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>. The two stylists devised a harmless type of flame to use on the District 12 tributes' black costumes, which made Katniss and Peeta stand out, instantly endearing them to the Capitol audience.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Both Cinna and Portia continued to clothe Katniss and Peeta through the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. Cinna made a rebellious statement by modifying Katniss's wedding dress, turning her into a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, Portia and her prep team were <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executed</a> on live television after Peeta was <a href="/wiki/Rescue_mission" class="mw-redirect" title="Rescue mission">rescued</a> from the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> All stylists from the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> died during the war, but it is unclear which side was responsible or through what means this was carried out.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Tigris unofficially styled her cousin <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>'s tribute, Lucy Gray Baird, during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. Due to their personal relationship and Snow's considerable influence on the future of the Games, it is possible that Tigris inspired him to create the stylist position.</li>
<li>Like Tigris, Proserpina Trinket and her older sister Effie unofficially styled District 12 tributes.</li></ul>
