<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tasks">Tasks</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Prep teams were responsible for beautifying <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for public appearances according to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s standards.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade">Tribute Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a></i></center></div>
<p>A prep team would meet their tribute for the first time at the <a href="/wiki/Remake_Center" title="Remake Center">Remake Center</a>. They would then start in on the tasks they needed to complete before the stylist could be brought in. This could take several hours in some cases, and tributes were fully nude throughout this first part of the process. The prep team's duties included, but were not limited to:
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/ca/Hungergames_00996.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130706162407" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Close-up of Katniss while the prep team works on her." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/ca/Hungergames_00996.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130706162407" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Hungergames 00996.jpg" data-image-key="Hungergames_00996.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Hungergames_00996.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Close-up of Katniss while the prep team works on her.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<ul><li>Bathing and exfoliating the tribute</li>
<li>Trimming or filing their nails</li>
<li>Waxing body hair (including eyebrows) and plucking stray hairs</li>
<li>Applying moisturizing or skin-soothing lotion</li></ul>
<p>At this point, the stylist would be brought in to meet the tribute, who would then be allowed to put on a robe. After a lunch break, the stylist would direct the prep team on what to do next. This usually entailed styling the tribute's hair and applying cosmetics, such as makeup, nail art, body paint, glitter, etc. With this done, the tribute would be dressed in their <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">costume</a> and sent out in a chariot for the Tribute Parade.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">Interviews</a> took place only five days after the parade, so the preliminary tasks did not typically need to be repeated. Prep teams would usually focus on the more typical beauticians' tasks of hair and makeup. The <a href="/wiki/Interview_outfits" title="Interview outfits">outfit</a> and finishing touches were personally taken care of by the stylist.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victors">Victors</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>If a prep team's tribute emerged from the Games as a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>, they were responsible for beautifying the new victor for the closing ceremonies. Depending on the duration of the Games and the toll it took on the winning tribute, this process could be quite extensive, or it could be limited only to hair and makeup.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When the victor's support team rose up from beneath the stage for the occasion, the prep team was the first group of people introduced.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour">Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a></i></center></div>
<p>Prep teams also beautified victors for their Victory Tours, accompanying them throughout these appearances alongside their <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, there were no prep teams or stylists, as few people in either the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> or the Capitol cared to watch the Hunger Games. Tributes were simply thrown into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> in the same state and clothing they had been in when they were <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a>. It is possible that things changed at some point not long after these Games, as the 10th Hunger Games introduced a number of changes that were carried over into future installments.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a> students were assigned to serve as the tributes' prep teams for a grade. <a href="/wiki/Proserpina_Trinket" title="Proserpina Trinket">Proserpina Trinket</a> and <a href="/wiki/Vitus" title="Vitus">Vitus</a> served as the prep team for <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. His <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partners</a> each had their own individual team as well.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_5_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_5-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Officially, they were meant to work under stylist <a href="/wiki/Magno_Stift" title="Magno Stift">Magno Stift</a>, but his unreliable behavior forced Proserpina and Vitus to take on more responsibility. Proserpina eventually had to bring in her older sister, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>, to help style them.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_12_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_12-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th-75th_Hunger_Games">74th-75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Flavius" title="Flavius">Flavius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Venia" title="Venia">Venia</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Octavia" title="Octavia">Octavia</a>, who worked under <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They prepared <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> for many of her public appearances, so both she and they had grown to care for one another by the time they had to prepare her for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. They broke down in tears many times throughout the experience, but after Katniss complained to Cinna about it,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> they followed his orders to excuse themselves from the room if they couldn't keep it together.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, Katniss's prep team was evacuated to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> so they could beautify Katniss for her appearances in <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> propaganda. The reasoning was that having familiar faces would comfort Katniss. However, after a starving Octavia took a slice of bread, the three of them were confined in a room and shackled to the wall only half-clothed, with nowhere to relieve themselves or wash themselves. Upon discovering this, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> and Katniss had them immediately freed and taken to <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> for medical treatment.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Flavius, Venia, and Octavia returned to their duties as Katniss's prep team soon after,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and they survived the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, but they were they only prep team to do so. It is not known which side was responsible for killing most of them,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s stylist <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a> and the prep team that worked under her were <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executed</a> on live TV after his <a href="/wiki/Rescue_mission" class="mw-redirect" title="Rescue mission">rescue</a> from the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPrep_team%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the tributes wore hospital gowns and lay on hard metal tables during prep, similar to operating tables. They were also separated from the other tributes by curtains like in an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/intensive_care_unit" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:intensive care unit">intensive care unit</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>During the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> was the first to unofficially work on the appearance of a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>, having repaired <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>'s rainbow dress and cleaned up her face for her <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>. This dress greatly helped her make a better impression on the Capitol citizens and gave her better <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorship</a>, showcasing the effectiveness of preparing the tributes beforehand to make the Games more exciting.
<ul><li>Given how the 10th Hunger Games was the stage to test new ideas to improve the Games, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>'s ensuing involvement as a Gamemaker, Tigris's relation to him, and how her actions in the 10th Games likely inspired the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a> position, it's likely that her actions also inspired the creation of prep teams.</li></ul></li></ul>
