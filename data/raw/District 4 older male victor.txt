<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_4_older_male_victor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Years after his initial victory, <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> announced that <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> would be <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> from the existing pool of victors, forcing them to compete in the Games regardless of age. As a result, this victor was present at <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>'s reaping for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, though he was not chosen to compete and did not <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the end of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, he was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so he presumably died in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors' Purge">Victors' Purge</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJP2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<ul><li>He only appeared briefly in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, but could be seen more clearly in a behind the scenes featurette for the film.<sup id="cite_ref-BTS_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BTS-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>He is the oldest known male victor from District 4.</li>
<li>Although uncredited, he was portrayed by <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm3107570/">Ron Stafford</a>. Ron previously appeared as a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.</li></ul>
