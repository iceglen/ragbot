<p>A <b>feast</b> was a special event during the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, typically held when food was scarce in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> and things were running slow. Through announcer <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>&#160;invited the remaining <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> to a feast at a well-known place, such as the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, to induce another <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFeast%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>At feasts, there would sometimes be a big table of food, while other times, the Gamemakers provided nothing but a stale loaf of <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> for tributes to fight over.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Feasts always ended in fatalities.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFeast%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>That year, a very special feast was held; instead of food, the feast offered the tributes something they each needed desperately. <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> needed <a href="/wiki/Medicine" class="mw-redirect" title="Medicine">medicine</a> to reverse his <a href="/wiki/Health_conditions#Blood_poisoning" title="Health conditions">blood poisoning</a>, so his <a href="/wiki/Ally" class="mw-redirect" title="Ally">ally</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> drugged him with <a href="/wiki/Sleep_syrup" title="Sleep syrup">sleep syrup</a> to ensure he wouldn't follow,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and she went to collect it alone.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>
At dawn, the ground opened up in front of the Cornucopia's mouth, and a round table rose up with four <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpacks</a> sitting on it. Each one was marked with the number of the remaining tributes' <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>. <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">Districts</a> <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a>'s packs were large and black; <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>'s pack was medium-sized and green, and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s pack was small and orange.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, all the packs were the same size and were color-coded by district, corresponding with the tributes' <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear#The_Hunger_Games_film" title="Arena wear">arena uniforms</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 316px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3e/Katniss_feast.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419083149" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss runs to the District 12 pack, containing Peeta&#39;s medicine." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3e/Katniss_feast.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/316?cb=20120419083149" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="316" height="209" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss feast.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_feast.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_feast.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss runs to the District 12 pack, containing Peeta's medicine.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p><a href="/wiki/%22Foxface%22" title="&quot;Foxface&quot;">Foxface</a> was the first to make an appearance at the feast. She'd hidden inside the Cornucopia, so once the table was in place, she jumped out, grabbed her District 5 pack, and ran away, ignoring the others. Katniss sprinted for her backpack next, but she had to fight <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> on her way to get it. Although Katniss was able to grab the small orange bag, Clove pinned Katniss to the ground and taunted her about <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>'s death, preparing to mutilate her with a <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">knife</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>However, the comment about Rue got the attention of <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>, who was her district partner. He pulled Clove off of Katniss and briefly interrogated her before bashing her head in with a rock. He then questioned Katniss about her alliance with Rue. After hearing that she sung for the young girl on her deathbed, he decided to let her go so they would be even.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> came looking for Clove, Katniss fled, and Thresh took District 2's backpack as well as his own when he left. Cato stayed by Clove's side as she died of her injuries.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Feast_aftermath">Feast aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFeast%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Several days after the feast, Cato set out to fight Thresh, killing him and presumably reclaiming his backpack.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Katniss and Peeta faced Cato in the final three, he was wearing nude-colored body armor that protected him against Katniss's <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">arrows</a>, which Katniss thought must have come from his backpack. However, it also protected his vital organs from the <a href="/wiki/Wolf_mutt" title="Wolf mutt">wolf mutts</a>, keeping him alive for hours as they attacked him until morning. Once the sun rose, Katniss was able to get a clear shot and end his suffering.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFeast%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/School" title="School">School</a> in the districts would often be canceled for major events in the Games, such as feasts.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The feast in the 74th Hunger Games is the only one to take place during the series.
<ul><li>Clove is the only known tribute who was killed at a feast.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>The contents of Thresh and Foxface's backpacks are a common source of fan speculation.
<ul><li>Some theorize that Thresh's pack held a <a href="/wiki/Weapon" class="mw-redirect" title="Weapon">weapon</a>, since he used a rock as an improvised weapon at the feast.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is also possible that Thresh's pack had body armor like District 2's, either to protect against Katniss's arrows or prolong a fight between him and Cato.</li>
<li>Foxface was seen earlier in the novel stealing food from the <a href="/wiki/Careers" class="mw-redirect" title="Careers">Careers</a>' supplies,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and she later stole <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries that Peeta was collecting,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so it's likely that once Katniss destroyed the stockpile, Foxface began to starve. In that case, her pack may have contained food.</li></ul></li></ul>
