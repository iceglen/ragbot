<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> said that she and the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 male (75th HG)">District 6 male</a> both won their respective Games with the use of <a href="/wiki/Camouflage" title="Camouflage">camouflage</a>, hiding until the other tributes were dead.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> They were both heavily addicted to <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a>, which they used to cope with pain and trauma.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Unknown_Hunger_Games">Unknown Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>She won an unspecified Hunger Games. She did not wield any weapon<sup id="cite_ref-Next_Projection_Interview_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Next_Projection_Interview-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and won by hiding, camouflage<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and outsmarting her opponents.<sup id="cite_ref-Next_Projection_Interview_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Next_Projection_Interview-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Post_Games">Post Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>She had developed Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) after winning the Hunger Games, and was prescribed morphling to help her cope with it, which she ended up addicted to.<sup id="cite_ref-Next_Projection_Interview_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Next_Projection_Interview-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While it is unspecified whether she mentored any tributes or not, it is highly likely that she mentored the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 male (75th HG)">Male Morphling</a>, as he won in a very similar fashion to her, and ended up being addicted to morphling just like her.
</p>

</p>

<p>In the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>, the District 6 tributes were often seen painting at the camouflage station. In addition to painting each other, they also painted Katniss into a field of yellow flowers,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and the female morphling painted Peeta's arm.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> They made a mess of the station all throughout training, and probably did so in their private sessions as well.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, she wore a black leather camo-print jacket and matching pants.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She smiled when Katniss revealed the Mockingjay dress, and then later joined hands with her fellow victors in a display of <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a> against <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Bloodbath">Bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The District 6 female survived the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>. At the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, it is most likely that she did not participate and just ran away from the terror. In the film, while Katniss ran on one of the spokes, she looked to her left and saw the morphling running into the jungle along with the <a href="/wiki/District_5_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 female (75th HG)">District 5 female</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She managed to survive whilst her <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 male (75th HG)">district counterpart</a> did not.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Although not a lot of information is known about what happened to her before her death, in the film, she did not have a lot of supplies or weapons due to her running away from the Cornucopia.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, because she was camouflaged behind Peeta when he, Katniss, and Finnick were fighting off the <a href="/wiki/Monkey_mutt" title="Monkey mutt">monkey mutts</a>, she presumably remained camouflaged throughout the first night.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the mutts' attack, Peeta was distracted by Katniss, and a monkey was about to kill him when the morphling jumped in the way to protect Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After Peeta had fought off the monkey, he took her body to the beach and held her. The morphling took some of her blood and drew a flower on Peeta's cheek, which he commented on, saying that it was lovely. The morphling smiled and soon after, bled to death. Peeta took her corpse to the water and let it drift off until a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> came to take it away.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, she died a similar way, but Peeta told her to focus on the colors in the sky, giving her a sense of peace as she bled to death in his arms.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She placed 14th out of 24th.
</p><p>Later, when Katniss was on the <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> after being broken out of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>, Haymitch revealed that District 6's tributes, among others, had known of the rebellion and were a part of the <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> to save Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Due to her morphling addiction, she was bone-thin, with sagging yellowish skin and overlarge eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She also had prominent ribs.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>She was the last District 6 tribute to die in the Hunger Games.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Year">Victory Year</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_female_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As we know she is in her 30s during the time of the 75th Hunger Games, her age is in the range 30-39
</p>
<ul><li>The first Hunger Games she could have won was if she was 39 years old at the time of the 75th, and 15 at the time of victory. 75-39+15=51; thus the first Hunger Games she could have won is the 51st Hunger Games</li>
<li>The latest Hunger Games she could have won was if she was 30 years old at the time of the 75th, and 18 at the time of victory. 75-30+18=63; thus the latest Hunger Games she could have won is the 63rd Hunger Games.
<ul><li>However we know that the 63rd Hunger Games was won by Gloss, and the 62nd Hunger Games were won by Enobaria. That leaves the 61st Hunger Games as the latest.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Thus, she won one of the Hunger Games between the 51st and the 61st.
</p>
