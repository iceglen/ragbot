She was given to Prim for her tenth birthday by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, Prim's sister, and provided milk and cheese for the family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLady%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8f/THG_IE_flashback.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20250116032945" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss and Gale presenting Prim with Lady in The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8f/THG_IE_flashback.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250116032945" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="224" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="THG IE flashback.jpg" data-image-key="THG_IE_flashback.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:THG_IE_flashback.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss and Gale presenting Prim with Lady in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Illustrated_Edition" title="The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition">The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition</a></i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>When Katniss first saw Lady, she belonged to the <a href="/wiki/Goat_Man" title="Goat Man">Goat Man</a>. Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale</a> had shot a deer earlier that day and gotten a decent price for it. Katniss wanted to buy Prim something for her birthday, and was heading to the market on the square to buy dress fabrics when the Goat Man and his herd of goats caught her attention. Lady was lying down in a cart, as her shoulder had been bitten and infected, likely by a dog. Katniss wanted to buy the goat for Prim, realizing it would make her even happier when she could also take care of the goat's wound. The goat was originally going to be bought by <a href="/wiki/Rooba" title="Rooba">Rooba</a>, a butcher from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, for slaughtering, but Rooba recognized Katniss trying to buy the goat and let her have Lady with a wink to Katniss. Katniss bartered with the Goat Man for half an hour before settling on a price. Immediately after purchasing Lady, Katniss bought a pink ribbon and tied it around the goat's neck. Prim was so excited when she got the goat that she cried and laughed all at once. Even though <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> was worried about the goat's odds of survival, she and Prim managed to restore Lady to full health.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Right after the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss told Prim that she could make cheese from Lady's milk to sell after remembering the failed attempt to teach her sister to hunt in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> became badly injured in the 74th Hunger Games, Katniss told him about how she had acquired Lady, altering details about how she had gotten the money as the entire population of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> was listening.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>To cover for Katniss being out in the woods, Katniss and Prim lied about having discussed meeting the Goat Man about getting Lady pregnant. In their story, Katniss had misremembered Prim's directions and gone to the wrong place, explaining her lengthy absence.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_11-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss went back to her home in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> after it was <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombed</a> by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, but could only find Prim's cat, <a href="/wiki/Buttercup" title="Buttercup">Buttercup</a>, so she assumed that Lady had died.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLady%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lady was a white goat with black patches, and when Katniss first brought her home for Prim, Lady wore a pink ribbon around her neck.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
