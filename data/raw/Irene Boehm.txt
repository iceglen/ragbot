<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FIrene_Boehm%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Boehm was born in Berlin, Germany. She is best known for playing <i><a target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" class="text" href="https://babylon-berlin.fandom.com/de/wiki/Toni_Ritter">Toni Ritter</a></i> in the television show <i>Babylon Berlin</i>, which was directed by Tom Tykwer, Achim von Borries and Hendrik Handloegten. She is represented by Rietz Casting &amp; Agentur.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
