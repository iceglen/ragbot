</p><p><b>Honor Gillies</b> is a British actress of Iranian/Scottish descent. She portrayed <a href="/wiki/Barb_Azure_Baird" title="Barb Azure Baird">Barb Azure Baird</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHonor_Gillies%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>After being cast as Barb Azure, Honor Gillies posted a video of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicki_Minaj" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nicki Minaj">Nicki Minaj</a> saying "Calling all Barbs" (the name of Minaj's fanbase). <a href="/wiki/Rachel_Zegler" title="Rachel Zegler">Rachel Zegler</a>, who plays <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, commented "slapping that super bass"— a play on the song "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bass" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Super Bass">Super Bass</a>" and Barb Azure's instrument, the stand-up bass. This started a rumor that Nicki Minaj would be on the film's <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes_(Music_From_%26_Inspired_By)" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)">soundtrack</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
