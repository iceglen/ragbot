<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeacekeepers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Peacekeepers have been known to have been around at least since the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> erupted, and likely existed beforehand. They were the principal tool of control and repression of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, and were responsible for maintaining law and order in the districts. They also serve as a ground army and air force, and are responsible for military combat in the event of war, and successfully defeated Districts 1-12 in the First Rebellion while simultaneously destroying surface-level infrastructure of District 13. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i>The Hunger Games</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeacekeepers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/Peacekeepers-armored.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131128071946" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peacekeepers" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/Peacekeepers-armored.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131128071946" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Peacekeepers-armored.jpg" data-image-key="Peacekeepers-armored.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Peacekeepers-armored.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peacekeepers</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>Most of the story takes place in either District 12 or the arena. While hunting was technically illegal, the Peacekeepers of District 12 were more than willing to let it slide. They were among the customers who regularly bought the poached meat at the black market known as <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>.
</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i>Catching Fire</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeacekeepers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e5/2peacekeepersCF.png/revision/latest?cb=20130415223924" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Two Peacekeepers restrain a  citizen during the ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e5/2peacekeepersCF.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130415223924" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="85" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="2peacekeepersCF.png" data-image-key="2peacekeepersCF.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:2peacekeepersCF.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Two Peacekeepers restrain a <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> citizen during the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The Peacekeepers violently suppress uprisings and unrest that is occurring throughout the various districts. When an elderly man salutes Katniss and Peeta during their Victory Tour in District 11, he is instantly apprehended and shot dead in front of the crowd.  
</p><p>Back home in District 12, the local Peacekeepers were long noted for being relatively lenient compared to those in other districts. The Head Peacekeeper, <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a>, was particularly known for his laxness with the law. However, this all changed with the mysterious disappearance of Cray and his replacement by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>, who imposes a brutal, harsh regime similar to that of District 11. He begins carrying out public executions and whippings on a regular basis, and begins enforcing laws that were long ignored, including the strict prohibition of hunting and black market trade.  
</p><p>The night that Peeta Mellark proposed to Katniss Everdeen, the citizens of District 8 decide to start an uprising, and were able to temporarily take control of their own district, before the Capitol sent in reinforcements and quelled the rebellion. Following this, <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a> disguise themselves as Peacekeepers, dressing in uniforms they had stolen from the <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> factories in an attempt to flee to District 13. Along the way, they encounter Katniss Everdeen, and inform her about the events that had taken place in their district.   
</p><p>Just before Katniss gets sent into the arena for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">Third Quarter Quell</a>, a group of Peacekeepers enter the Launch Room and attack <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a>, beating him unconscious before a terrified Katniss. President Snow had planned this just to throw her off balance before the Games. Following the destruction of the arena and the rescue of various tributes by District 13, the Peacekeepers, under Snow's orders, destroy District 12 with a firebombing raid, killing nearly all of its residents.   
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i>Mockingjay</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeacekeepers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Peacekeepers are the primary foes that the rebels encounter in battle, throughout the various districts and the Capitol itself. During the war, the Capitol deploys the full might of its military force, including its use of bombs and air support for its foot soldiers.
</p><p>It is revealed that most of the Peacekeepers are originally from District 2 where they are trained for such a career path. Up until this moment, it was believed that the Peacekeepers came from the Capitol, and some of them did indeed, with Coriolanus Snow being a key example. However, it was a difficult transition for those used to such a lavish lifestyle to live in the impoverished districts. 
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f2/CapitolBattle.png/revision/latest?cb=20220628023237" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peacekeepers in a firefight with rebel forces in the Capitol during the final moments of the Second Rebellion" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="CapitolBattle.png" data-image-key="CapitolBattle.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f2/CapitolBattle.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220628023237" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f2/CapitolBattle.png/revision/latest?cb=20220628023237" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peacekeepers in a firefight with rebel forces in the Capitol during the final moments of the Second Rebellion" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f2/CapitolBattle.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220628023237" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="CapitolBattle.png" data-image-key="CapitolBattle.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f2/CapitolBattle.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220628023237" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:CapitolBattle.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peacekeepers in a firefight with rebel forces in the Capitol during the final moments of the Second Rebellion. </p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure> 
<p>After the Battle of the Capitol and its takeover by the rebels, the Capitol's Armed Forces are disbanded, with large numbers of Peacekeepers being captured, tried, and executed by the new government. The military and law enforcement structure of the Constitutional Republic are unknown but it is presumed that a new police force is possibly created, separate from the military to prevent abuse of power. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeacekeepers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 123px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Peacekeepers.png/revision/latest?cb=20130425193633" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peacekeepers as shown in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="123" height="278" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Peacekeepers.png" data-image-key="Peacekeepers.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Peacekeepers.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/123?cb=20130425193633" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Peacekeepers.png/revision/latest?cb=20130425193633" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peacekeepers as shown in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Peacekeepers.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/123?cb=20130425193633" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="123" height="278" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Peacekeepers.png" data-image-key="Peacekeepers.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Peacekeepers.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/123?cb=20130425193633" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Peacekeepers.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peacekeepers as shown in <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the uniforms are white with black stripes at the sleeves and collar. They include white helmets, similar to a motorcycle helmet, with a black stripe running down the center with the Capitol Seal and a transparent visor, black leather boots and gloves, with a <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Baton" title="Weapons">baton</a> on their belt. Some of the peacekeepers in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> and most places in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> wear a more ceremonial uniform with a beret instead of a helmet and a sash.
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> film, their appearance is different from the first movie and their uniform is slightly more grey with a full helmet, except for head peacekeepers such as <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a> and a few other peacekeepers around <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>: They have darker visors which hides their face, and heavier looking armor with white boots, knee pads on the pants and grey gloves. The chest plate also seems to be ribbed and covers a wider area. These changes might have just been a tactical uniform for heavier combat, since Peacekeepers were much more aggressive in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> and they were attempting to be more intimidating in the wake of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. It is unknown why Peacekeeper garb is white, but it can be deduced that it reflects upon President Snow's appearance and perfectionist idealism it stems from. They seem to never wear camouflage, suggesting that they’re meant to be a highly visible reminder of the power of the Capitol.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Recruitment_and_training">Recruitment and training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeacekeepers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Peacekeepers are usually recruited from both the Capitol itself and from the loyal <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, though people from other districts can serve as Peacekeepers as well. Those from the districts join out of desire to serve or for the promise of food and compensation.
</p><p>It is more difficult to recruit Peacekeepers from the Capitol, as Capitol citizens are used to a life of privilege and often reluctant to submit to a term of rigorous military service. Some join out of desire for honor or glory. Others join for other incentives, such as having their debts absolved in exchange for their service. They are trained mainly in District 2 before being deployed. Each Peacekeeper is obliged to serve a term of 20 years, and during their term of service, they are forbidden to marry or have children. Furthermore, no Peacekeeper recruited from one of the districts could be assigned to the district from which they had come.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Officers">Officers</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeacekeepers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Officers were a special class of Peacekeepers who commanded other Peacekeepers. Groups of Peacekeepers each had a presiding officer who was responsible for their conduct. Known as a Head Peacekeeper, they were responsible for the Peacekeeper garrison in they district they operated in.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They received greater privileges and had a chance to distinguish themselves in a way that ordinary Peacekeepers did not. 
</p><p>Any Peacekeeper wishing to become an officer had to take the officer candidate test, which was considered quite challenging. One was required to have graduated from a secondary school, such as the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, in order to even be allowed to take the test. The test was mainly designed to measure scholastic aptitude and consisted primarily of verbal, math and spatial problems, though it also contained a military section involving basic rules and regulations. Most Peacekeepers were barely literate and stood no chance of passing it. Those who did pass it were not guaranteed to become officers, but merely allowed to begin training. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> was one such individual who not only took the test, but passed with flying colors, becoming the youngest person ever to pass it. 
</p><p>While normally a Peacekeeper would begin training immediately in the district that they were assigned to, Snow's scores were so high that he was recommended to an elite program in District 2.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, before he even got there, he was diverted back to the Capitol, where he was enrolled in <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a> and as an apprentice <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> under Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Vehicles">Vehicles</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeacekeepers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Peacekeepers utilize both <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> and <a href="/wiki/SJ-7_Peacekeeper_Assault_Transport" title="SJ-7 Peacekeeper Assault Transport">ground vehicles</a>, like <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">trains</a>, to allow quick transport within and between districts. 
</p><p>Peacekeepers also pilot the Capitol's vast fleet of Hovercraft which are used for both transportation and combat. They are capable of carrying large payloads of bombs which can be deployed to devastating effect against rebellious districts, often targeting residential areas, hospitals and factories to demoralize the civilian populations. Hovercrafts are also utilized by Peacekeepers in surveillance roles and to track down and apprehend fugitives attempting to escape Capitol jurisdiction.
</p>

<p>Peacekeepers are tasked with maintaining law and order by arresting and punishing political dissidents, rebels, and other lawbreakers. Punishments are often severe, even for offenses as minor as poaching, and range from public whippings to <a href="/wiki/Capital_punishment" title="Capital punishment">executions</a>.  
The Peacekeepers are shown to have varying levels of strictness throughout the districts. In large districts such as District 11, they are ruthless and brutal, but in District 12, they are much more lenient to satisfy their own stomachs.  
</p><p>While all of the Peacekeepers' duties are very similar, in some districts the punishments differ. In District 11, the Peacekeepers are known to carry out on-the-spot executions for minor crimes, and public whippings and executions are regularly held. By contrast, in District 12, such punishments were rare, at least during the leadership of Cray as Head Peacekeeper. However, the temperament and behavior of the Peacekeepers in District 12 also seemed to change over time. Haymitch Abernathy mentions that prior to the arrival of Cray as Head Peacekeeper of District 12, public whippings were frequently held. In addition, around the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the Peacekeepers were known to have carried out at least two public executions in a relatively short timespan. 
</p><p>In <i>Catching Fire,</i> Cray mysteriously disappears and is replaced by the much more brutal Romulus Thread. In the film, he is shown to have been arrested upon Thread's arrival, suggesting that he was possibly executed by the Capitol for his laxness. Thread proceeds to put an end to the traditional leniency of District's 12 Peacekeepers and imposes a harsh, brutal regime upon the citizens of District 12, carrying out public executions and whippings on a regular basis, and cracking down on hunting and black market trade, activities that were once tolerated by the comparatively lenient Peacekeepers in the district.  
</p><p>During the first two decades after the unsuccessful First Rebellion, it can be assumed that public executions, whippings, and other harsh punishments were regularly held in all of the districts. This was due to the fact that large numbers of former rebel soldiers were still living amongst the populace, and maintained negative attitudes towards the Capitol. Many of them had likely hoped to reignite a rebellion, with <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a> being a primary example. He attempted to sabotage coal production in the district's mines, but was unsuccessful and was caught and publicly hanged for his crime.  
</p><p>Over time, as the Capitol reestablished social control, and as former rebel soldiers aged and became disillusioned, wealthier districts such as District 1 and District 2 needed fewer harsh punishments, as did backwater districts such as District 12. However, large districts such as District 11 continued to face brutal oppression, with public executions and whippings continuing to be the norm. In addition, all forms of political dissent, defiance, and rebellion against the Capitol remained strictly prohibited throughout Panem, whether it be in the Capitol or the districts. This is evidenced by the fact that Katniss Everdeen keeps her hunting bow hidden in the woods, rather than in her home, as well as the fact that children were taught from an early age to not speak their mind on political matters. 
</p>

<ul><li>Peacekeepers must serve the state for 20 years without marriage or children, thus preventing them from having loyalties to anything other than Panem and the Capitol. This is another comparison to the Roman Empire and its military structure, of which Panem is a post-apocalyptic emulation.</li>
<li>According to a report on Peacekeepers on Capitol TV, 87% of Peacekeepers are Male while only 13% are Female. This ratio is reflected in the fact that there are only three known female Peacekeepers, <a href="/wiki/Purnia" title="Purnia">Purnia</a>, another District 12 Peacekeeper and a District 11 Peacekeeper.</li>
<li>Though there are exceptions, such as <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a>, most peacekeepers are very loyal to President Snow and to the Capitol.</li>
<li>The fact that most Peacekeepers are from District 2 and not the Capitol is similar to the popular misconception that the late Roman army consisted mainly of Germanic "barbarians" and few non-barbarians. Recent scholarship from noted historians like Hugh Elton, Hugo Jones, Ross Cowan and many others have debunked this myth, proving that only about 22% of late Roman troops were "barbarians", and even those "barbarians" were actually highly romanized and held to the same standards as legionaries from other areas. However, it is true that few Roman soldiers were actually born in Italy.</li>
<li>Early into <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>'s propaganda film, three men in white <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/hazmat_suits" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:hazmat suits">hazmat suits</a> are shown. They may be Peacekeepers, or predecessors of Peacekeepers as Panem's early military force.</li>
<li>It is possible that the government of Panem improved Peacekeepers after the 74th Hunger Games, explaining their change in appearance, but it is more likely that their change in appearance is just because of the director switch between the first movie and the other three. Alternatively, the government simply supplied existing combat gear to contain the increasingly volatile situation.</li>
<li>The Peacekeepers apparently have a navy, as mentioned in The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes. During that time, Pliny Harrington was a commander.</li>
<li>Although Panem is situated in the former Canada and the United States, the Peacekeepers are never seen wielding American weapons such as the M-16 rifle series; instead they use European and Israeli firearms such as the FN F2000 and the IMI Tavor. Neither model is particularly common in Canada or the US, in civilian or government hands. The filmmakers presumably chose such models for their futuristic looks.
<ul><li>In the case of the G3 rifles seen in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, the rifles may have been chosen due to having an older, antiquated appearance matching the Panem seen in the movie.</li></ul></li>
<li>Peacekeepers were allowed to put in a phone call home twice yearly if they so desired, but only after first having served at least six months. Otherwise all other contact was required to be by mail. There did not seem to be any specific restrictions on this method of communication, other than the speed of the post.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
