<p><b>Festus Creed</b> was a male resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. He was assigned the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFestus_Creed%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Festus was a part of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>'s inner circle since birth. His family came from old <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> money, largely from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> timber. It took a hit during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, but rebounded nicely in the reconstruction effort.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During a session with Professor <a href="/wiki/Crispus_Demigloss" title="Crispus Demigloss">Crispus Demigloss</a>, Demigloss asked the mentors to brainstorm ways to encourage people to watch the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, which were considered grim viewing at the time. Festus's suggestion was to execute anyone in the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> who did not watch and to banish anyone in the Capitol that did not watch to the districts, executing them the next year if they messed up again. The class then began debating the idea, considering it unenforceable because you couldn't send <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> to everyone's door. They also discussed Coriolanus's idea of having the Capitol citizens bet on the tributes, which Festus believed would liven things up.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Festus was present at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>, presumably to meet with his tribute, Coral. However, as <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne</a>'s throat was slit by her <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">own tribute</a>, Festus stood next to her and Coriolanus and they exchanged helpless, worried looks. After Arachne died and was transported away by medics, <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a> managed to join the pair. As the three of them walked home, Festus proposed that they come back to his apartment. However, as they reached his apartment complex he burst into tears, leaving Snow and Clemensia to bid him goodnight.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Festus only received minor injuries from the arena <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombing</a>, sporting a sling on his arm and stitches on his cheek. He visited Snow when he was in the hospital, telling him that the Academy had canceled classes, and choked up upon explaining the deaths of <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana</a> and <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo Ring</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Festus and <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a> were one of the few mentor-tribute pairs who had something to participate with during the interviews.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the class with <a href="/wiki/Dr._Gaul" class="mw-redirect" title="Dr. Gaul">Dr. Gaul</a>, Festus and <a href="/wiki/Hilarius_Heavensbee" title="Hilarius Heavensbee">Hilarius Heavensbee</a> discussed strategies to use for female tributes. Festus rebuked Hilarius' claim that girls didn't fight the same way as boys by arguing that Coral could compete well against the male tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFestus_Creed%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Festus arrived at <a href="/wiki/Heavensbee_Hall" title="Heavensbee Hall">Heavensbee Hall</a> just as Clemensia argued with Lysistrata and Coriolanus. As Festus rebutted Clemensia's anger, she told him to shut up. After she left, Festus took a seat beside Snow and Lysistrata while questioning her hostility toward Coriolanus for her sickness, believing it odd.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the morning of the second day of the Games, Festus showed discontent at not being fed. However, Coriolanus gifted him a bag of bread rolls, proud that he gave something for a change, as he owed the Creeds a lot in return. Later that day, Festus worked with <a href="/wiki/Persephone_Price" title="Persephone Price">Persephone Price</a> to coordinate strategies, and Coriolanus could tell Festus was falling for her.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Coriolanus also gave Festus lima beans further into the Games for breakfast, as he had lost his appetite. He swapped Festus' empty bowl with his own to avoid reprimands.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Clemensia refused to feed her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Reaper" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaper">Reaper</a>, Festus told her to give him food and called her heartless when she responded, asking him to mind his own business.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Later, he apologized when he saw Reaper taking <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a>'s dead body to his makeshift morgue.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Festus's tribute, Coral, was killed by <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> released into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> by Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>. As he was dismissed from the proceedings, Snow gave him a bear hug, emotional at his reliable friend being out of the running.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> considered Creed, along with <a href="/wiki/Lysistrata_Vickers" title="Lysistrata Vickers">Lysistrata Vickers</a>, to be someone who liked <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> better most of his other classmates at the Academy and someone who could be counted on to say nice things about him. He thus invited him, along with Vickers, to a party to celebrate what would have been Plinth's 19th birthday.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFestus_Creed%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He had once dared Snow to have a one night stand with a girl in an alleyway.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He was also entertained by dogfights.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He could be sympathetic to a degree, as he demanded for Clemensia to feed her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Reaper" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaper">Reaper</a>, during the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Festus also had a big appetite, as he quickly ate Snow's bread rolls and lima beans.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFestus_Creed%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Festus was bigger and burlier than most boys his age.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFestus_Creed%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/festus">Festus</a> is a Latin word for "festive" or "joyful". It was also the surname of several notable Roman historical figures, such as Judean governor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porcius_Festus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Porcius Festus">Porcius Festus</a>, grammarian <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sextus_Pompeius_Festus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sextus Pompeius Festus">Sextus Pompeius Festus</a>, and a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festus_(historian)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Festus (historian)">historian</a>. His surname, <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/creed">Creed</a>, is an English word referring to a doctrine or set of beliefs.
</p>
