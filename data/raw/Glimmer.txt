<p><b>Glimmer</b> was the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. Along with her district partner, <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>, she was a member of the "<a href="/wiki/Career" class="mw-redirect" title="Career">Career</a> pack" that banded together to hunt down the weaker competitors<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She was indirectly killed by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, who dropped a nest of <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jackers" class="mw-redirect" title="Tracker jackers">tracker jackers</a> on the Career pack as they slept<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Glimmer was born in <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>, one of the wealthiest districts, and one that is known for producing Career tributes. She presumably spent most of her life training for the games and due to this, she most likely <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. It is known that her father made flowers (namely tulips) out of finely-wrought crystal. This may explain why she is called Glimmer.
</p>

<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, Glimmer, along with her district partner <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>, were spray painted silver and wore bejeweled tunics<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. However, in the film, they were dressed in fuchsia outfits covered in shiny sequins and feathers, representing their district's main export, luxury goods<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Glimmer-training-center1.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20121229150738" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Glimmer at the Training Center." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="214" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Glimmer-training-center1.jpg" data-image-key="Glimmer-training-center1.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Glimmer-training-center1.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20121229150738"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Glimmer-training-center1.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20121229150738" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Glimmer at the Training Center." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Glimmer-training-center1.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20121229150738" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="214" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Glimmer-training-center1.jpg" data-image-key="Glimmer-training-center1.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Glimmer-training-center1.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20121229150738"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Glimmer-training-center1.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Glimmer at the Training Center.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Glimmer's <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a> was the only one known in the history of the Hunger Games to be confiscated. The gemstone of the ring she brought, when twisted, revealed a poisoned spike. She didn't get arrested because nobody could prove she had knowledge of the ring being a <a href="/wiki/Weapon" class="mw-redirect" title="Weapon">weapon</a>, but her token was confiscated nonetheless.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Glimmer was not to be underestimated. Being one of the Career Tributes, she had a good chance of survival and was ready to fight. During her private session with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a>, Glimmer most likely showed the Gamemakers her archery skills<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> (in the book, however, Katniss said that "it is immediately evident that she is incompetent with a bow"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>). However, this is only based on Katniss' perspective of one of her shots. She may have been fine with a bow, or even good.
</p>

<p>The Careers discovered <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> and allowed him in the pack so they could get to Katniss. During the first night, the Careers stumbled upon the <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> female who made a <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a> during the middle of the night<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Glimmer killed her, assisted by Peeta and Cato. After the fire havoc, Glimmer and the other Career members were not as fast as before.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Later, Glimmer along with the Career pack found Katniss and chased her up a tree. When <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> decided to go up the tree Katniss was trapped in, Glimmer offered him her <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow and arrows</a>, but he refused as he could do better with his sword. When Cato fell out of the tree due to his weight, Glimmer decided to try, but she quickly realized that the branches wouldn't support her either. It was obvious that she wasn't familiar with the bow and arrows, as she didn't hold the arrows straight and had a hard time nocking the arrow to the bowstring.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, in the film, she seems to have slight experience with a bow and arrow<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. When she fires at Katniss and misses, Katniss teases her by grabbing the arrow and swinging it around to embarrass her for her lack of knowledge<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> (in the movie, Glimmer and Cato both shoot arrows at Katniss and miss, prompting Katniss to sneer, "Why don't you try throwing the <i>sword</i>?"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>). Furious, Glimmer agrees with Peeta that the pack should wait for Katniss to come down on her own, as she won't be able to get away without them knowing, so they sleep under the tree<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, a decision which would ultimately lead to her death<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<p>Glimmer was killed by tracker jackers that Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, who was originally informed of the nest by Rue<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, sent down on the Career Tributes by cutting off the branch that held it. Glimmer, <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, Marvel, Cato, the District 4 girl (in the book), and Peeta were camping at the bottom of the tree where Katniss was hiding. Shocked at the sudden attack, everyone but Glimmer and the girl from District 4 escaped to the lake. Glimmer screamed hysterically for help, but no one came back for her. Because of her many stings, Glimmer soon collapsed and began twitching and moaning from the tracker jacker hallucinations, and the District 4 girl died later on. Unfortunately for Glimmer, her death was not quick, and for several minutes after collapsing, her heart kept beating and she fell into a coma.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>When Katniss returns for the bow and arrows, she notes that she could not recognize Glimmer anymore, as the venom had bloated her body grotesquely, and her plum-sized stings were oozing green pus and were bursting. When she touched one of the pimple-like wounds on Glimmer's shoulder, it disintegrated in her hand. However, there is a chance that this did not happen as Katniss was experiencing hallucinations herself. Katniss shielded Glimmer's body from being taken away by the hovercraft and desperately tried to take the bow and arrows from her, breaking many of her mutated, petrified fingers with a stone, finally getting her hand on Glimmer's shoulder blade, she pulls Glimmer onto her side to retrieve the quiver of arrows.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, there was no green pus<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, so this may mean that what Katniss saw in the book was a hallucination<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Glimmer was placed 12th out of the 24 tributes, making it halfway.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Muttation">Muttation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p><a href="/wiki/Wolf_mutt" title="Wolf mutt">Wolf mutts</a> made to resemble the fallen tributes were unleashed on the seventeenth day of the Games. Glimmer's was the first one Katniss recognized, based on its silky blond fur, unsettlingly human green eyes, and a jeweled collar with the number "1". Katniss killed it with an arrow through the throat when it tried to climb the Cornucopia.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="After_the_Games">After the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, Katniss and Peeta go to <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and see Glimmer's family<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Peeta takes up painting as a hobby, and one of the paintings is of Glimmer's mutt attacking<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Throughout the story, Katniss has nightmares<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, sometimes involving Glimmer's bloated body after the tracker jacker attack<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and her <a href="/wiki/Mutt" class="mw-redirect" title="Mutt">mutt</a> form<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Katniss described Glimmer as being tall and sexy with emerald green eyes and flowing blond hair.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, Glimmer wore her hair in fishtail braids for most of the time.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Weapons">Weapons</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>Glimmer showed little consideration in choosing her weapon at the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. However, it is not known if the bow would have been her first choice as a weapon, or if she had taken any other weapon from the Cornucopia. She also had a more feminine version of <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>'s sword, which she had strapped to the side of her pants. During the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a> in the film, Glimmer used a knife to stab the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">District 6 female</a> to death<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. For the rest of the games up until her death, she utilized a bow and arrow<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_8-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> which Katniss would later take from her corpse<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and use for the remainder of the games up until her victory<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_24_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_24-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Katniss described Glimmer as "incompetent" when using the bow<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_8-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>It is unknown exactly how many people Glimmer killed. In the novel, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> never saw her kill anyone. In the film, she killed the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">District 6 female</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a> by repeatedly stabbing them to death, and presumably killed the <a href="/wiki/District_8_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 female (74th HG)">District 8 female</a> with <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>'s sword<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Her name refers to light illumination, as <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>'s industry is luxury goods.</li></ul>

<ul><li>In the novel, Glimmer and her district partner <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> are described as being spray-painted silver for the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but in the film, they are wearing fuchsia feathery costumes<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li>

However, the relationship is largely one-sided, and Cato leaves her to die to the <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jackers" class="mw-redirect" title="Tracker jackers">tracker jackers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>

<li>In the Tribute Guide, Glimmer is the only Career tribute to not have a favored weapon, though she carries a bow and arrows in the films.</li>
<li>In the film, Glimmer weighs 130 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>

<ul><li>In a behind the scenes featurette titled <i>The Hunger Games: Capitol Couture - Styles of Panem</i>, makeup designer <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ve_Neill" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Ve Neill">Ve Neill</a> said she suggested to <a href="/wiki/Gary_Ross" title="Gary Ross">Gary Ross</a> that they make a prosthetic body for Glimmer's death scene, in order to spare actress <a href="/wiki/Leven_Rambin" title="Leven Rambin">Leven Rambin</a> from wearing "huge, elaborate makeup". However, Ross liked the look of the makeup better, so the dummy wasn't used in the final film.
<ul><li>According to Neill, <a href="/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson" title="Josh Hutcherson">Josh Hutcherson</a> pranked <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence" title="Jennifer Lawrence">Jennifer Lawrence</a> by putting the Glimmer dummy in her bathroom: "The dummy was sitting on her toilet. It was quite disturbing for her. She— I heard her scream all the way, in the makeup room, with my door closed."</li></ul></li>
<li>On May 16th, 2024, Leven Rambin published a memoir about her acting career titled <i>Glimmer's Game.</i> This included, but was not exclusively about, what it was like to play Glimmer.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a>, the player interacts with Glimmer while in District 1, and she explains that her father is a craftsman who makes flowers (namely tulips) out of finely-wrought crystal.</li></ul>
