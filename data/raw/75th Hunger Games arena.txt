<p>The <b>75th Hunger Games arena</b> is where the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> or third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> took place. This small <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> was divided into twelve sections, with a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> trap in each wedge being activated twice a day at a particular hour.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Following the abolition of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, this and all other arenas were destroyed and replaced with memorials.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Terrain">Terrain</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F75th_Hunger_Games_arena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/CF_cornucopia_concept_art.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240216202544" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Concept art of the Cornucopia for The Hunger Games: Catching Fire." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="102" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="CF cornucopia concept art.jpg" data-image-key="CF_cornucopia_concept_art.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/CF_cornucopia_concept_art.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240216202544" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/CF_cornucopia_concept_art.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240216202544" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Concept art of the Cornucopia for The Hunger Games: Catching Fire." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/CF_cornucopia_concept_art.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240216202544" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="102" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="CF cornucopia concept art.jpg" data-image-key="CF_cornucopia_concept_art.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/CF_cornucopia_concept_art.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240216202544" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:CF_cornucopia_concept_art.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Concept art of the Cornucopia for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">launched</a> on their pedestals in a ring around the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, which sat on an island in the center of a round salt lake. The sky above them was pink. Rather than offering food and other supplies like usual, the Cornucopia only held <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> this year.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Its tail originally pointed to the twelve o'clock sector, but the island could be spun around to disorient tributes, as it was on the second day.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Like a wheel, spokes divided the salt lake into twelve sections, with two tributes in each. These spokes ran from the Cornucopia to the beach. Beyond the beach was a dense jungle, invisibly divided into twelve sections as well. A high-voltage <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> enclosed the entire arena in a dome.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mechanics">Mechanics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F75th_Hunger_Games_arena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The arena was set up like a clock, with each wedge holding a different threat activated by the hour. The cycle began at midnight on the first night when a bell tolled twelve times, marking the start of the second day.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Of the arena's twelve sections, only six are known concretely, with tributes speculating on two others.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Zones">The Zones</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F75th_Hunger_Games_arena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Hour 12-1</b>: Lightning bolts struck a specific tree,<sup id="cite_ref-:5_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> though there were identical trees in each sector to confuse the tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Those in the <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> wrapped a coil of wire around this tree and, securing the wire to one of her arrows, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> shot at the force field and blew it out.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This allowed rebels to extract her, <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> from the arena to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><b>Hour 1-2</b>: Seeing last hour's storm, dehydrated tributes eagerly awaited regular rainfall, only to be soaked and blinded with blood rain.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Casualties: <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a>; blinded by blood, he stumbled into the force field, which stopped his heart.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Hour 2-3</b>: Poisonous fog caused painful blistering, paralysis, and death.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Casualties: <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a></li></ul></li>
<li><b>Hour 3-4</b>: Vicious <a href="/wiki/Monkey_mutt" title="Monkey mutt">monkey mutts</a> with orange fur, fangs, and claws that came out like switchblades.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Casualties: <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 female (75th HG)">District 6 female</a><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li><b>Hour 4-5</b>: <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">Jabberjays</a> tortured tributes with the screams of their loved ones.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Hour 5-6: <i>Unknown</i></li>
<li>Hour 6-7: An unknown threat, presumed to be a giant <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a> "beast", tore a tribute into pieces.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Casualties: <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 10 male (75th HG)">District 10 male</a><sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Hours 7-8, 8-9, and 9-10: <i>Unknown</i></li>
<li><b>Hour 10-11</b>: A giant wave swept across the entire sector.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, the tidal wave seemed to come from behind some mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Casualties: <a href="/wiki/District_5_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 female (75th HG)">District 5 female</a><sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Hour 11-12: Tributes could hear noisy insects coming from this sector, "something with pincers", which they thought could strip them to the bone in seconds.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F75th_Hunger_Games_arena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>For <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, the Cornucopia scenes were filmed at The Beach at <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clayton_County_International_Park" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Clayton County International Park">Clayton County International Park</a> in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonesboro,_Georgia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jonesboro, Georgia">Jonesboro, Georgia</a>. Beach scenes were filmed at Kawela Beach in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawela_Bay,_Hawaii" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Kawela Bay, Hawaii">Kawela Bay</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oahu" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Oahu">Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi</a>.</li>
<li>Jungle scenes were filmed in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waimea_Valley" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Waimea Valley">Waimea Valley</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manoa" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Manoa">Manoa</a>, also both in Oʻahu.</li>
<li>Only five tributes were killed by the arena's timed traps.</li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> or second Quarter Quell, the only sources of safe food and water in the arena were the Cornucopia and rainwater. In the third Quarter Quell, this was reversed: the Cornucopia only provided weapons, so food and water had to be acquired from the arena itself or through sponsors.</li></ul>
