<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Medications">Medications</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Burn_ointment">Burn ointment</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> took a first aid kit off of <a href="/wiki/Loupe" title="Loupe">Loupe</a> after killing him which contained burn ointment. Maysilee told <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> that the burn ointment had kept her from going crazy after the volcano eruption.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/Sponsor_gift_burn_ointment.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231221172858" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A jar of burn ointment." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/Sponsor_gift_burn_ointment.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231221172858" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="102" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Sponsor gift burn ointment.jpg" data-image-key="Sponsor_gift_burn_ointment.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Sponsor_gift_burn_ointment.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A jar of burn ointment.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>On the fourth day of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> received her first <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gift</a>, which contained burn ointment. She used it to treat her burnt hands and calf.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She also used the ointment on her <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> stings to little effect, and later, she used it on <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and on <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s infected leg wound.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Charcoal_tablets">Charcoal tablets</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s grandmother used charcoal tablets to treat her indigestion when she overate.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR15_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She once told Haymitch that the tablets were good for whatever ailed a person's belly, "fire, wind or poison."<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR16_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Tablets of charcoal could be used to treat poison. During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, Haymitch's backpack contained the tablets as a hint to the arena's poisonous nature. After ingesting poisonous water, Haymitch used a large number of the tablets to counteract it. Although Haymitch was sick through the night, he survived.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR15_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR16_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After realizing that <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> had eaten a poisoned sweet, Haymitch frantically asked if she had any charcoal tablets at home, but she didn't think so.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13_injections">District 13 injections</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, <a href="/wiki/York" title="York">York</a> wrote a note for Katniss to the hospital, either requesting or recommending treatment to speed up the healing of her bruised ribs. Katniss received a series of 24 injections around her ribcage, and she endured a terrible, burning pain for days afterwards. She couldn't alleviate her suffering with a painkiller because mixing this medication with morphling had dangerous side effects.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Morphling">Morphling</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">Morphling (drug)</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e7/Morphling1.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140212022316" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Filling a syringe with morphling." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e7/Morphling1.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140212022316" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="88" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Morphling1.jpg" data-image-key="Morphling1.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Morphling1.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Filling a syringe with morphling.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Morphling was a powerful and highly addictive drug that could be taken orally or intravenously. It was administered as a painkiller,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> sedative, or mood stabilizer, and people such as <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> abused it in order to cope with severe trauma.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Poison_antidote">Poison antidote</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>A poison antidote was an antidote used to treat <a href="/wiki/Poison" title="Poison">poison</a>. One known kind of antidote took on the form of a syrupy substance.
</p><p>President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Snow</a> poisoned allies that he felt threatened by, but he drank the poison as well to avoid attracting suspicion. Even though he took antidotes, the poison caused permanent sores inside his mouth that never healed. As a result, he always wore a genetically engineered rose, which covers the scent of blood stained on his breath.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After poisoning <a href="/wiki/Incitatus_Loomy" title="Incitatus Loomy">Incitatus Loomy</a> for his incompetence in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a> before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, Snow took an antidote that he carried in his pocket.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>One of the items included in some of the tributes' <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpacks</a> for the 50th Hunger Games were bottles of antidote for the arena's poisons. <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a> got one<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the Careers got at least one as well which ended up in the supplies of either <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Barba" title="Barba">Barba</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Angler" title="Angler">Angler</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Although some of <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a> ingested the arena's poison, <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> quickly recognized what it was, and they survived thanks to the antidote in Hull's backpack.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> found the Careers' bottle of the antidote while going through their supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the attack by the porcupine mutt, the two used it to treat their own injuries and in a vain attempt to save Hull's life.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Skin_ointment">Skin ointment</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After surviving the poisonous <a href="/wiki/Fog" title="Fog">fog</a> in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss, Peeta, and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> were able to leach out the poison by submerging themselves in salt water.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, they treated their scabbing skin with a tube of "thick, dark ointment" they received from a sponsor. It relieved their itching<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and healed their skin beneath the scabs,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but it had a strong, unpleasant scent of tar and pine needles, and it stained the affected skin gray-green.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, they received no ointment; instead, it was sufficient for them just to leach out the poison.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sleep_syrup">Sleep syrup</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Sleep_syrup" title="Sleep syrup">Sleep syrup</a></i></center></div>
<p>Sleep syrup was a common medication in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> that Katniss had seen <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">her mother</a> use many times. Katniss drugged Peeta using a vial she got from a sponsor, masking its distinctive sweet taste with berries. She sedated him so he wouldn't be able to stop her from going to the <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">feast</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Snow_coat">Snow coat</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Snow_coat" title="Snow coat">Snow coat</a></i></center></div>
<p>A coating of snow and herbs was used to treat <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s lacerated back after he whipped by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>. The mixture was meant to numb the area and relieve pain. Katniss also received a snow coat for the mark on her face.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tracker_jacker_leaves">Tracker jacker leaves</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3d/Tracker_jacker_leaves.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231221201157" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss, having been treated with tracker jacker leaves by Rue." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3d/Tracker_jacker_leaves.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231221201157" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="102" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Tracker jacker leaves.jpg" data-image-key="Tracker_jacker_leaves.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Tracker_jacker_leaves.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss, having been treated with tracker jacker leaves by her <a href="/wiki/Ally" class="mw-redirect" title="Ally">ally</a> Rue.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The people who worked in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>'s orchards carried around a certain type of leaf that could be used to treat tracker jacker stings. Rue used them to treat Katniss's stings in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, and Katniss recognized the leaves as something her mother used.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss later used them to draw out the infection in Peeta's leg.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Unknown_antibiotic">Unknown antibiotic</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Katniss fought at the feast in order to claim a small orange backpack, which contained a box with a hypodermic needle. She injected the unnamed medicine into Peeta's arm; presumably, it was some sort of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/antibiotic" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:antibiotic">antibiotic</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This proved effective, bringing down nearly all the swelling in his leg.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the medication was a topical ointment that Katniss applied to Peeta's leg. Peeta then insisted on using some on a cut on her head. Both of their injuries healed overnight.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_medications">Other medications</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMedical_treatments%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>After being bitten by <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a>, <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a> immediately received an injection of black fluid from a hypodermic needle.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> was prescribed sleeping pills and morphling by a <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> doctor after his breakdown.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> was given a metal can of powder for his heat rash, and he was advised to keep it dry.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>When Katniss spoke of Mrs. Everdeen's period of debilitating depression following the death of <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">her husband</a>, Mrs. Everdeen replied that she could have treated herself if she'd had the medicine that she now had.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, it is unknown what kind of medicine this was, if it was something she took regularly, or if it was something she only took as needed.</li>
<li>Katniss once mentioned that her mother used homemade wine as cough medicine.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>On top of all the other remedies, Katniss tried to combat Peeta's infection with fever pills from a first-aid kit she <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_tactics#Salvaging" title="Hunger Games tactics">salvaged</a> from <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>'s possessions. Mrs. Everdeen occasionally broke down and bought these when home remedies failed.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>The first-aid kit also contained bandages and nausea medication.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>In an early version of the script for the first film, Katniss gave <a href="/wiki/Tygart_Blanken" title="Tygart Blanken">Tygart Blanken</a> two purple berries as a sleep aid for his wife, <a href="/wiki/Corrina_Blanken" title="Corrina Blanken">Corrina Blanken</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-script_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-script-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The District 12 apothecary sold rubbing alcohol.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<ul><li>The <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> received inadequate medical care. After the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol Arena bombing">Capitol Arena bombing</a>, they were only treated by a veterinarian, and her requests to have <a href="/wiki/Panlo" title="Panlo">Panlo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sheaf" title="Sheaf">Sheaf</a> admitted to a hospital were denied, resulting in their deaths.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_39-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Katniss's parents met because Mrs. Everdeen worked at her parents' apothecary shop, and Mr. Everdeen sold her medicinal herbs he'd collected in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>After the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, District 12 closed its <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal mines</a> and planned to build a factory to make medicines.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_41-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-41"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
