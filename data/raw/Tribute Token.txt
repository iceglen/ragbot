<p>A <b>tribute token</b>, also known as a <b>district token</b>, was an item each <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> was permitted to bring with them into the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, which could represent their <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> and remind them of home.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, tokens had to be approved by a review board prior to the competition. Items would be confiscated if they broke the rules of the Games, as would anything that could give the tribute an advantage. A token was usually something that could be worn so the tributes could keep it with them easily.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_tokens">Known tokens</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTribute_token%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTribute_token%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District tokens were not formalized at this time, so there was no review process yet. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> gave <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> a <a href="/wiki/Silver_compact" title="Silver compact">silver compact</a> that once belonged to <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow&#39;s mother">his mother</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and she used it to smuggle rat poison from the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">zoo</a> into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, poisoning her competition.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The compact was later one of three pieces of evidence used against Coriolanus Snow by Dean <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> to show his treachery.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, Snow returned to see Highbottom and reclaimed the compact.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTribute_token%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/90/SotRSunflowerToken.png/revision/latest?cb=20250816024351" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="One of the sunflower tokens." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/90/SotRSunflowerToken.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250816024351" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="165" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="SotRSunflowerToken.png" data-image-key="SotRSunflowerToken.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:SotRSunflowerToken.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">One of the sunflower tokens.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s token was the <a href="/wiki/Flint_striker" title="Flint striker">flint striker</a> given to him by <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>. It was accepted because he pretended it was just a necklace.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_5_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_5-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>'s token was a necklace with a <a href="/wiki/Scrip" title="Scrip">scrip</a> coin attached to it.<sup id="cite_ref-SR17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR17-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> had a collection of necklaces as her token, the gamemakers let all of her necklaces count as a single token.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>'s token was a purple and yellow flower bead necklace, that belonged to Maysilee. Originally, Maysilee planned to give it to <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, so she would have something from home, but never had the chance. After Louella died in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>, Maysilee gave the necklace to Haymitch to put on Louella, as she liked flowers. After Haymitch ran with Louella, and held her up to <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>, medics took her away. Louella's necklace was later put on Lou Lou, to convince everyone that she was the real Louella, with the same token she had on when Haymitch ran with her. After all of the tokens cleared, Maysilee hooked the necklace back on Lou Lou, to wear into the arena.</li>
<li>The <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> tributes originally had sunflower tokens which were broken by <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a> and glued back together by <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They were secretly replaced with <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">sunflower bombs</a> by <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> and rebel sympathizers working with him. The replicas were so exact that Haymitch worried that <a href="/wiki/Kerna" title="Kerna">Kerna</a>'s hadn't actually been replaced.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a>'s was actually the cleverly disguised fuse and blasting cap for the bombs. The black cord on the lariat <a href="/wiki/Buck" title="Buck">Buck</a> made him concealed the fuse.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>One of the other tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> brought a doorknob as a token.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a>'s token was an old bicycle bell.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Miles" title="Miles">Miles</a>' token was a tin train whistle.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>'s tokens were wooden trinkets.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_11-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Notion" title="Notion">Notion</a> and <a href="/wiki/Alawna" title="Alawna">Alawna</a> had dolls in beautifully-sewn outfits as tokens.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_11-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>'s tokens were horseshoes.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_11-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Chicory" title="Chicory">Chicory</a>'s token was a "flower woven of grass."<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTribute_token%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>: Glimmer attempted to wear a ring, but it was taken away from her before the Games began, since the review board realized that twisting the gemstone caused a poisoned spike to pop out. Glimmer claimed to have no knowledge of this, but it was still taken away. It was the only known confiscated token.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>: Rue wore a wooden charm shaped like a star or flower on a length of woven grass. She said that it was a good luck charm.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>: Katniss wore a golden <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> that once belonged to <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> tribute <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> given to her by Maysilee's niece <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was almost confiscated since the sharp part of the pin could be used as a <a href="/wiki/Weapon" class="mw-redirect" title="Weapon">weapon</a>, but it managed to clear the review board.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<ul><li>Lucy Gray Baird wore a dress that had belonged to <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird%27s_mother" title="Lucy Gray Baird&#39;s mother">her mother</a><sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> all the way from <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the reaping</a> to the arena. It provided her with comfort throughout her ordeal; Lucy Gray said it was like "[her mother was] wrapping her arms around [her]."<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>While in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, Katniss gave <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> a bundle of pine needles wrapped in a bandage to remind her of her home in <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>During the 75th Hunger Games, Peeta gave Katniss a <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark%27s_pearl" title="Peeta Mellark&#39;s pearl">pearl</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She later put this into the pocket of her military uniform when she shipped out for the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, calling it "a token of the boy with the bread."<sup id="cite_ref-:1_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTribute_token%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The concept of a tribute token was seemingly eliminated from <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, as <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> appeared to sneak in the mockingjay pin, pinning it to the inside of Katniss's jacket to hide it and holding a finger to his lips.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Tokens were apparently reintroduced in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a>.</i> Similar to the book, Effie obtained matching gold tokens for Haymitch and Peeta to go along with Katniss's mockingjay pin and her own gold wig, to show that they were a team.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>In the first film, Katniss bought the pin from <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> for her sister Prim, reassuring her that it would keep her safe. Later that day, Prim gave it back to Katniss to protect her during the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Both the <a href="/wiki/Flint_striker" title="Flint striker">flint striker</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">Mockingjay pin</a> were created by <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>.</li></ul>
