<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Louella and her family lived three doors down from the Abernathys. She had half a dozen brothers and sisters<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, including <a href="/wiki/Ima_McCoy" title="Ima McCoy">Ima</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cayson_McCoy" title="Cayson McCoy">Cayson</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When she was born, her father took her into town and proudly held her up in the window of a school to show her off to everyone.<sup id="cite_ref-SR6_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When she was five, she had a crush on Haymitch Abernathy for a week and told everyone he was her "sweetheart". She transferred her affections to <a href="/wiki/Buster" title="Buster">Buster</a> after he gave her a bullfrog.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> demanded that twice the number of tributes be <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for the event. They chose this to remind the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> that for every Capitol citizen that was killed during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, two <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> were also killed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> As a result, Louella was reaped alongside <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> (after <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> ran away). Of the four tributes from District 12 that year, hers was the first name to be called.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the reaping, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> asked for her and Haymitch to stay behind so he could rerecord their families' reactions to their reapings. The McCoys refused to give Plutarch what he wanted, so he sent Louella to get on the train.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the way to the Capitol, Louella told Haymitch it wasn't fair how he was handpicked by <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a>. He immediately asked her to be his ally in the Games. After being offered food by an attendant, she refused to eat and when asked why, she was embarrassed to share her family's poor wealth with the attendant. Before she could answer, Maysilee demanded that they bring cutlery and plates. She then advised Louella not to let them treat her like an animal.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the tribute parade, the horses carrying the District 12 chariot were startled. They bolted and caused the chariot to start buckling. Haymitch and Louella tried to hold on as the chariot hit others and crashed, but they were thrown backwards in the air. Louella hit the ground with force, cracked her head open and was killed upon impact. In his grief, Haymitch tried to prevent the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> from taking her body and ran with her to President Snow's mansion, where he lay her broken body in front of it for him to look at and sarcastically applauded.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the Hunger Games ended with Haymitch as victor, Louella's body was returned to District 12 where she was buried.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Replacement">Replacement</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Her death left District 12 with only three tributes, so the Capitol decided to cover up her death by producing a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/body_double" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:body double">body double</a> in the form of a tortured <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> girl. The other District 12 tributes dubbed her replacement "<a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-SR10_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR10-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Although the other districts' tributes questioned her sudden reappearance and "brain damage"<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, only District 11 and 12's tributes ever learned that she wasn't the real Louella.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When Haymitch first saw <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> as a little girl, he noted how she looked like Louella due to her braided hair and spunky attitude. After Katniss volunteered for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Haymitch's nickname for Louella "sweetheart" slipped out towards Katniss due to her reminding him of his friend and fellow tribute. After the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>'s victory, Haymitch revealed his friendship with Louella in order to contribute to Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>'s memorial book.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTRE_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTRE-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Louella had a heart-shaped face, big gray eyes, and long, dark pigtail braids. Her fingernails were bitten down, and she also had a distinctive scar on her forehead from falling off the Abernathys' cistern.<sup id="cite_ref-SR10_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR10-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After her capitol make-over, her eyebrows were more dramatic and penciled-in.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SR6_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Her reaping outfit was a gingham dress that was made by her mother.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Coming from the McCoy family, she was a spunky young girl. She would put on a mask of bravery when put in front of the cameras, but relied on Haymitch to comfort her fears.<sup id="cite_ref-SR6_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her surname McCoy means "son of Aodh," which is an Irish word for "fire." This ties in well with the eventual girl on fire reminding Haymitch of Louella. It may also be a reference to the expression "the real McCoy," alluding to her being the real version of herself as opposed to <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLouella_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Louella was the second tribute to die before the Games in <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>, which was during the Tribute Parade. The first was <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a>.</li>
<li>Louella is also the first known tribute to be replaced by a <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">body double</a>.</li>
<li>A young <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> reminded <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> of Louella.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_Epilogue_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_Epilogue-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Both Louella and her actress <a href="/wiki/Molly_McCann" title="Molly McCann">Molly McCann</a>'s last names begin with "McC."</li></ul>
