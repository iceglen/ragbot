<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Madge was born and raised in District 12, where she lived with her father and <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Undersee" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Undersee">mother</a>. Due to her father's role as the <a href="/wiki/Mayor" title="Mayor">mayor</a>, she grew up relatively wealthy, so she did not have to sign up for <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a> in order to survive as poorer families did. Madge and Katniss were in the same year at school, and as relative loners, they sat together at lunch and partnered up for activities. Despite this, Katniss did not consider them to be close friends.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On the day of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss and her friend <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> came to the back door of <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Undersee%27s_house" title="Mayor Undersee's house">Mayor Undersee's house</a> to sell strawberries. Madge answered the door, already dressed in nice clothes for the reaping. Gale commented on her dress, though Katniss was unsure whether it was a genuine compliment or sarcasm. Madge said that she wanted to look nice if she was picked to go to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. This frustrated Gale since Madge's wealth meant she had probably never taken out tesserae, so her name would not be entered into the reaping extra times. Katniss argued that this was not Madge's fault, and Gale replied that it was no one's fault, and that was just the way it was. After this exchange, Madge's face became closed off, and she paid them for the berries, wishing Katniss good luck.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2f/THG_illustrated_edition_mockingjay_pin.webp/revision/latest?cb=20240118201243" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss receiving the mockingjay pin in The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2f/THG_illustrated_edition_mockingjay_pin.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240118201243" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="225" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="THG illustrated edition mockingjay pin.webp" data-image-key="THG_illustrated_edition_mockingjay_pin.webp" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:THG_illustrated_edition_mockingjay_pin.webp" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss receiving the mockingjay pin in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Illustrated_Edition" title="The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition">The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition</a></i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>When Katniss was taken into a room within the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>, Madge was the second person to come visit her. She explained that <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were allowed to wear a <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">token</a> from their district into the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, and she asked Katniss if she would wear the gold <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> from Madge's own dress. She pinned it onto Katniss's dress for her, then made her promise she would wear it into the arena. Katniss agreed. Madge then kissed her on the cheek before she left. This made Katniss realize that Madge had truly been her friend all along.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-74th_Hunger_Games">Post-74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Once Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> returned home as <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a>, she and Madge spent more time together. At first they were both unsure what to do or say, since they didn't want to talk about their classmates or clothes, as the other girls at school did; however, "after a few false starts", Katniss realized that Madge was "dying" to go into the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a>, and she taught Madge to shoot a <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow and arrow</a>. Madge, a great pianist, tried to teach Katniss to play piano in return, but Katniss preferred to hear Madge play. They also ate meals at each other's houses.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss and Peeta's last stop on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a> was their home district. After arriving at the <a href="/wiki/Train_station" title="Train station">train station</a>, they spent that night at the mayor's house for a dinner with their families. Katniss briefly hugged Madge before her <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> took her to the third floor to get ready, and once she was done, Katniss went down to the second floor to see her friend. After stopping in at the mayor's office,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss went into Madge's room. Madge complimented Katniss's dress, saying she looked like she "came right off the streets of the Capitol." Katniss replied that her pin had started a trend in the Capitol, then asked Madge if she was sure she didn't want it back. Madge told her not to be silly, and that it was a gift. When Katniss asked where the pin had come from, Madge explained it was her aunt's, though it had been in the family for much longer.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss later learned Madge's aunt was <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, a District 12 tribute who died in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Gale was whipped by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a> for poaching, Madge walked over to Katniss's house in a snowstorm to offer up six vials of <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a>, prescribed to her mother for her headaches. Madge claimed her mother said she could take them, and she urged Katniss to use them "for [Katniss's] friend". Then, before anyone could stop her, Madge went back out into the storm. Peeta was surprised, saying that he didn't know Madge knew Gale. Katniss explained defensively that they used to sell her strawberries, and Haymitch commented that Madge "must have quite a taste for them." Katniss was irritated at the implication that something was going on between her friends.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> was announced,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Madge gave Katniss her father's newspapers from the Capitol. These included predictions about who was most likely to be the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Mayor Undersee's house was destroyed in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>. Katniss initially speculated that they could have been evacuated to the Capitol, but she was still overcome with grief at the thought that she could be breathing in Madge's ashes.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She later asked <a href="/wiki/Thom" title="Thom">Thom</a>, who had been recovering human remains from the wreckage, and he told her that the whole family had died as well as the two people who worked for them.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Madge was quiet and solitary in school like Katniss, which contributed to their friendship. She was also very cautious, if not slightly suspicious or wary of people she didn't know, becoming distant and closed off when offended.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, she could be urgent, strong, and steadfast in her way of speech when the occasion called for it.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After Madge's death, Katniss described her as quiet, kind, and brave.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Madge had wavy blonde hair, and she normally dressed in a "drab school outfit"; however, on the day of the reaping she wore an expensive white dress and a small, circular gold pin with a mockingjay on it. Her hair was "done up" with a pink ribbon. Her blonde hair was typical for <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchants</a>, who were District 12's wealthier citizens.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Madge was an only child, living with her mother and father in District 12. Katniss observed that, although Madge's parents seemed nice, the girl did not see much of them. Her father was busy running the district as its mayor, and her mother was often laid up in bed for days with "ferocious headaches".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, it was revealed that Mrs. Undersee's sister, Maysilee Donner, was a tribute in the second Quarter Quell. As a result, Maysilee died years before Madge was born, but there was a strong familial resemblance.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Katniss_Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Cookies. A pin. I am getting all kinds of gifts today. Madge gives me one more. A kiss on the cheek. Then she's gone and I am left thinking that maybe Madge really has been my friend all along.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss after parting with Madge<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Madge and Katniss spent time together at school, though they did not speak much.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss did not consider them friends until Madge came to say goodbye and gave her the mockingjay pin. Madge seemed very fond of Katniss, giving her a kiss on the cheek before she left.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the Games, the two girls became closer, spending time together outside of school, both at each other's houses and in the woods.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss was briefly jealous over the implication the there was something going on between Madge and Gale,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but it did not interfere with the girls' friendship,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and Katniss grieved deeply for Madge after her death.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Gale_Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Katniss and Gale sold Madge and her father strawberries,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but Peeta was not aware that Madge knew Gale at all.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When Madge answered the door on the day of the reaping, Gale openly resented her wealth. However, he had ranted to Katniss about how the tesserae system was meant to divide the district's poor from its well-to-do, so Katniss felt that he knew his anger towards Madge was "misdirected."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Despite this negative interaction, Madge went to Katniss's house in poor weather to offer up painkillers for Gale when he was whipped. Haymitch implied Madge must have done it because she had feelings for Gale, which bothered Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
