<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSylva_Mayleaf%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sylva participated in and won an unknown edition of the Hunger Games before being <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> for the 75th Hunger Games.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSylva_Mayleaf%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Chariot_rides">Chariot rides</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSylva_Mayleaf%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, an unknown district's tributes were <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">costumed</a> as loaves of <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> this was presumably District 9 or <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>'s tributes, since both had food-producing industries. In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, she and her district partner were dressed in golden outfits possibly designed to look like wheat or bread instead.
</p>

<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, she wore a sleeveless top and a white necklace and a black skirt over a pair of slacks matching her shirt color.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the tributes were interviewed, she, along with the other tributes, joined hands as a sign of <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a> against <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSylva_Mayleaf%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Sylva was killed during the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>. She was launched into the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> on the pedestal situated between <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a>'s and <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>'s<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and was killed towards the end of the bloodbath by Brutus.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSylva_Mayleaf%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Along with the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 male (75th HG)">District 5 male</a> she was among the most seen unnamed tributes in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a>.</i></li>
<li>Marian Green confirmed several times on her Twitter that her character was killed by <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a>, but this scene was deleted. <sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A contest was held by Marian Green for fans to name her character. The winning name was <b>Sylva Mayleaf</b> and her preferred weapon was a pitchfork.<sup id="cite_ref-AMA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMA-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>When asked by a fan, she confirmed her weapon was a staff via Instagram.<sup><i>[citation needed]</i></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
