<p><b>Maude Ivory Baird</b> was a member of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> and the younger cousin of <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> and <a href="/wiki/Barb_Azure_Baird" title="Barb Azure Baird">Barb Azure Baird</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She performed in <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> with the rest of the Covey and was said to have an amazing singing voice. After her performance, she would step off the stage to accept spare money and other donations from the audience. Maude Ivory made her appearance in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> at no more than eight or nine years old.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaude_Ivory_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b7/Maude_Ivory_Hob.png/revision/latest?cb=20230610161627" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Maude Ivory performing at the Hob" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b7/Maude_Ivory_Hob.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230610161627" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="152" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Maude Ivory Hob.png" data-image-key="Maude_Ivory_Hob.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Maude_Ivory_Hob.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Maude Ivory performing at the Hob</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Maude Ivory grew up in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> with the Covey, performing around the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> to make a profit and stay alive. She and the rest of the Covey were so distraught about Lucy Gray being chosen at the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> that they didn't perform for the entire time she was in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Maude Ivory was especially affected; she was fine during the day, but ever since Lucy Gray was taken away, Maude Ivory woke up screaming from nightmares. This continued even after her cousin's return.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the Covey's first performance after Lucy Gray returned, Maude Ivory wore a pink dress and curtsied before introducing her fellow Covey members behind the microphone. Later, she sang "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oh_My_Darling,_Clementine" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Oh My Darling, Clementine">Oh My Darling, Clementine</a>", inviting audience members to join in on the chorus. When the show ended, Maude Ivory passed around a basket for the audience to make donations, and was delighted when <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> gave her some of <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Mrs. Plinth</a>'s homemade popcorn balls. She pointed Coriolanus out to Lucy Gray, who was joyful that he had arrived.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a> showed up after the performance, Maude Ivory defended Lucy Gray and ordered him to leave. Later, when Coriolanus Snow and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> visited the Covey at their home, they heard Maude Ivory singing while cracking nuts outdoors in an old dress. She fetched Barb Azure and led the boys to <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a> to meet Lucy Gray, then headed back to the house with Sejanus and the <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> he handed her. However, they encountered Billy Taupe again, and Maude Ivory was irritated that Sejanus hadn't helped her shell nuts.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After Billy Taupe left, she took Sejanus out to milk the Covey's goat <a href="/wiki/Shamus" title="Shamus">Shamus</a>, then went inside again to eat once they were done.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The next time the Covey performed in the Hob, Maude Ivory was again gifted popcorn balls by Coriolanus, and she sang him Lucy Gray's ballad because of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Not long later, Coriolanus and Sejanus went to visit the Covey again, this time accompanying them to the lake. During the walk, Maude Ivory complained of pain caused by the shoes she was wearing, and was carried by the others while she sang and theorized about Lucy Gray's fate in the ballad. When they arrived at the lake, Maude Ivory took Sejanus to pick berries and explained the Covey's unique names to him and Coriolanus. While she napped, <a href="/wiki/Clerk_Carmine_Clade" title="Clerk Carmine Clade">Clerk Carmine Clade</a> gifted her a blue feather to put in her hair. She and <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> later went to the Hob to trade berries for new shoes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During a performance, Maude Ivory had the entire audience caught up in a sing-along when Mayfair and Billy Taupe's murders took place in the shed. She was the one to find the bodies, screaming hysterically and shaken up by the discovery.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The next weekend, at <a href="/wiki/Commander_Hoff" title="Commander Hoff">Commander Hoff</a>'s birthday party in the mess hall at the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers' base">Peacekeepers' base</a>, Maude Ivory wore a buttercup yellow dress and led the audience in singing a birthday song for the commander.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Maude Ivory eventually died and was buried in the Covey's secret graveyard. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> came across Maude Ivory's grave while looking for that of <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a>. Like all of the Covey members, Maude Ivory's grave was marked with a snippet of her name poem.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaude_Ivory_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Maude Ivory was confident, as she was the one who introduced the rest of the Covey at the start of their performances. She was also quite sweet, making her a crowd favorite at the Hob, where she played the drums and sang.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was said to be able to remember anything with a tune after only hearing it once.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, it is evident that she is around 12 years old and not a few years younger.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaude_Ivory_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 163px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7f/Maude_Ivory.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231215145142" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Maude Ivory performing" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7f/Maude_Ivory.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/163?cb=20231215145142" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="163" height="280" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Maude Ivory.jpg" data-image-key="Maude_Ivory.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Maude_Ivory.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Maude Ivory performing</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Maude Ivory was a short, little girl with a wispy frame. During performances, she was seen wearing fancy, colorful dresses and she often curtsied or pirouetted to show thanks. Maude Ivory also had a sweet, squeaky voice.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaude_Ivory_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maud_(given_name)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Maud (given name)">Maude</a> is an Old German name meaning "powerful battler", taken from the poem "Maude Clare" by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Rossetti" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Christina Rossetti">Christina Rossetti</a>. The latter part, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Ivory">Ivory</a>, is the material used for piano keys.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Her surname, Baird, is derived from the word "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bard">bard</a>", meaning "poet" or a professional singer who tells stories through song. It is also only one letter off from "bird", which the Covey are often compared to in the novel.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaude_Ivory_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Since some of the Covey's songs survived into the trilogy through <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, who learned them from <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">her father</a>, it's been theorized that the Everdeens are descendants of the Covey— probably through Maude Ivory, who shared Katniss's ability to memorize music after a listen or two.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a>, the director of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes"><i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i></a> film, supports this theory himself.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i> confirms that the Everdeens are distantly related to the Covey.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Maude Ivory was "crazy for butter", so much so that Tam Amber fashioned her a butter churn for her birthday.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, her date of birth is never stated.</li>
<li>She was afraid of snakes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the film, her singing voice was provided by <a href="/wiki/Josie_Hope_Hall" title="Josie Hope Hall">Josie Hope Hall</a>.</li>
<li>She may be <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a>'s mother as they were both related to <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, Lenore Dove's mother was stated to have died in childbirth and Maude Ivory was dead by the time of <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i> with Lenore Dove being buried near her.</li></ul>
