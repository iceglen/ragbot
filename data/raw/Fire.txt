<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol Arena bombing">Capitol Arena bombing</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> was trapped underneath a burning beam, but <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> was able to free him. His <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma&#39;am">grandmother</a> said afterwards that the <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombing</a> was "just the kind of story that catches fire."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, during <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a> said that he could start a fire with his glasses.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> started a fire by the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a> to roast fish they'd caught.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Lucy Gray returned there with Coriolanus, running away from the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>, she set a fire in the hearth of an old concrete house. She brought an old metal can in hopes of transporting live coals from place to place.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In retaliation for <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s actions during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, President Snow had Haymitch's house set on fire, burning Haymitch's <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">mother</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">younger brother</a> alive.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> and <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a> dressed District 12 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> in black jumpsuits, headdresses, and capes of red, orange, and yellow fabric meant to evoke fire. They also used synthetic flame, amazing the crowd and making their tributes instant stars in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. Katniss and Peeta only felt a slight tickling sensation from the false flames, but their capes and headdress were carefully removed afterward, then doused by Portia with "some kind of spray from a canister". Because of this costume, Cinna nicknamed Katniss "the girl who was on fire",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which was eventually shortened to "girl on fire".<sup id="cite_ref-:1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>For her interview, Katniss's <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> painted flames onto her fingernails and toenails, and she wore a bejeweled dress with a flame design. When she twirled, it gave her the appearance of being engulfed in flames.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> popularized the "Girl on Fire" nickname when he used it for her onstage.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss kept her fiery nails when she went to compete in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h5><span id="Tributes.27_fires"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Tributes'_fires">Tributes' fires</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<p>Long before dawn on the second day, Katniss awoke to the <a href="/wiki/District_8_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 female (74th HG)">District 8 female</a> setting a fire to keep warm. This fire drew the deadly <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> of <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> to the location, who attacked and killed the girl.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, Katniss was able to cook meat over the heated coals.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On the fourth day, Katniss was driven towards other tributes by a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>-created wall of fire; her calf was badly burned when they launched a fireball at her, and she received less severe burns on her hands.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Smoke from this fire temporarily subdued <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jackers</a> that had nests in the area.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the eighth day, Katniss's young ally <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> set two signal fires to draw the Careers away from their supplies so Katniss could destroy them.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Rue had also prepared the site of a third fire, but she never set it.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss returned there after Rue's death to cook,<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and two days later, she set another fire to confuse the remaining Careers while she headed out to find Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After <a href="/wiki/%22Foxface%22" title="&quot;Foxface&quot;">Foxface</a> died on Day 16, Katniss urged Peeta to set a fire so they could cook. She reasoned that their last opponent, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, wouldn't follow it to their location while he believed they were in fighting condition, putting him at a disadvantage. According to Katniss, Peeta was "a whiz with fires", and he was able to set one with damp wood.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>Katniss Everdeen, the girl who was on fire, you have provided a spark that, left unattended, may grow to an inferno that destroys <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>On the first day of the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> Coriolanus Snow visited Katniss in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a> in District 12 to issue her a warning: that she had been the inciting spark for rebellious sentiment and uprisings across the country, and that everything would be destroyed if she didn't help contain it.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When she returned to 12 for the last stop on the Victory Tour, she saw classified intelligence about the <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">uprising of District 8</a>; in this footage, she saw buildings burning while banners with her face on them still waved from the rooftops.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the following Sunday, Katniss went to the concrete house by the lake and set a fire in the hearth, where she thawed out as she waited for <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> to arrived. They roasted chestnuts together and discussed running away to escape Snow's wrath, but Katniss let slip about the uprising she'd seen. Gale, invigorated by the idea of people rebelling, argued that he wanted to stay and fight instead for those who couldn't flee.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Once <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a> took over as District 12's Head <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a>, he had <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> burned to the ground.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Katniss returned to the lake for the first time since he began his reign, she came across <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>, who had managed to stoke a "feeble flame" in the hearth. Katniss helped them build it up properly.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> announcement, Katniss went to <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s house and drank his liquor. She said that it felt like fire inside her, and she liked it.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While watching a recap of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, she described the volcanic mountain as "spewing liquid fire".<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2f/Katnisspeetachariot.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720220644" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss and Peeta in their chariot" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2f/Katnisspeetachariot.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130720220644" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katnisspeetachariot.png" data-image-key="Katnisspeetachariot.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katnisspeetachariot.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss and Peeta set aflame in their chariot.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>At the parade, Katniss and Peeta once again wore dark jumpsuits, this time rigged with lights that flickered like fire over burning coals. When Katniss met <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, he referred to her as "girl on fire". Their costumes from the previous year had also inspired copycats in the other districts; for example, <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>'s tributes were dressed like cows with flaming belts.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>This year, when Katniss twirled during her interview, her <a href="/wiki/Wedding_Dress" class="mw-redirect" title="Wedding Dress">wedding dress</a> burned away to reveal another design underneath, transforming her into a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In his interview right after, Peeta claimed that he and Katniss had secretly gotten married in the District 12 tradition: building a fire with your partner and <a href="/wiki/Toasting" title="Toasting">toasting</a> <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>, then sharing it afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When she was launched into the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>, surrounded by water, Katniss's first thought was that "this [was] no place for a girl on fire."<sup id="cite_ref-:3_28-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She contemplated building a fire to cook meat, but this was deemed unnecessary once Peeta figured out that they could cook things by tossing them at the arena's <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, Katniss contrasted the pain caused by the poison <a href="/wiki/Fog" title="Fog">fog</a> with the pain of fire.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Katniss blew out the force field, trees burst into flames, and the Gamemakers evidently set off fireworks.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>The Capitol firebombed District 12 after the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, destroying the district<sup id="cite_ref-:4_31-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and killing 90% of its population.<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They also firebombed <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, including a field hospital Katniss had just visited. She and Gale took down Capitol <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> with explosive arrows (designed by <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a><sup id="cite_ref-:5_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>). She then filmed a stirring <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> in the burning wreckage, declaring that if the districts burned, so would the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The finalized version of the propo incorporated audio of <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a>, the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">Hunger Games</a> announcer, using her nickname as "the girl who was on fire".<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In a propo he was forced to make for the Capitol, Peeta described war damage allegedly caused by <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">the rebels</a>. This included a granary that had collapsed after a fire.<sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_District_2" title="Battle of District 2">Battle of District 2</a>, a train caught on fire.<sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Gale and Beetee designed <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> and war strategies that involved fiery bombs.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_38-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Their twice-exploding bombs were used in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="City Circle bombing">City Circle bombing</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:7_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> killing <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>. Katniss herself was badly burned, requiring skin grafts, but she also endured significant psychological trauma from watching the fire engulf her sister. She was haunted by the sight long afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As Katniss settled into her post-war life in District 12 with Peeta, she reflected on her relationship with Gale. She said that she did not need "Gale's fire, kindled with rage and hatred" because "[she had] plenty of fire [herself]". Instead, she needed the reminder that life went on and things could be good again, which is what Peeta gave her.<sup id="cite_ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
