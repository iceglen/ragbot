<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Lenore Dove was born in December.<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Her mother died in childbirth, so she was raised by her adoptive uncles, <a href="/wiki/Clerk_Carmine_Clade" title="Clerk Carmine Clade">Clerk Carmine Clade</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a>, in <a href="/wiki/The_Covey%27s_house" title="The Covey's house">the Covey's house</a>. She did not know who her father was, but the Chances were particularly fond of her, which suggested they had a connection. Clerk Carmine did not like her hanging around with them due to their reckless nature.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Lenore Dove met <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> in the fall when they were ten years old. It was the first time Haymitch had ever snuck out of the <a href="/wiki/Fence" title="Fence">fence</a> and into the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> with <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a>, who was a distant relative of Lenore Dove. She was reading in an apple tree above them when she complained about Burdock's singing to the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>. Lenore Dove dropped apples down onto the boys, and she and Haymitch discussed the origins of her name. At age twelve, she was arrested twice for rebellious acts.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Since she was a Baird,<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> she likely had knowledge of <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray</a>, but did not want to discuss her whenever Haymitch wondered who the first District 12 <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> was.<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This was possibly because the story was too secret or too painful. Lenore Dove would add scraps of Lucy Gray's dress to her wardrobe as a way to honor her memory.<sup id="cite_ref-SR25_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR25-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Lenore Dove was dating Haymitch by the time of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She taught his younger brother, <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a> about the stars, which she had learned herself from Tam Amber.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Haymitch went to see her shortly before the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> on his birthday.<sup id="cite_ref-sneak_peek_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sneak_peek-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She gifted him a striker with a snake and a long-necked bird merged together with two small rings that she had designed.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the reaping, she and Haymitch were presumed safe after the four tributes were picked. However, when the last tribute, <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> ran away and was killed, a riot broke out. Lenore Dove tried to help his mother prevent <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> from taking his body away. Haymitch tried to protect her and was thus reaped after being chosen by <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> to take Woodbine's place in the Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Drusilla demanded that the area was cleaned up before the reaping returned to broadcasting, and ordered a Peacekeeper to kill Lenore Dove for her resistance. <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> intervened as he wanted to record her tears over Haymitch's reaping as it would put on a good show for the audience. However when they reenacted the reaping, Lenore Dove refused to cry for <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s entertainment. At the conclusion of the reaping, a Peacekeeper was about to put her in handcuffs when her uncles bribed him to let her go. Although she wanted to go over to Haymitch, they led her away down a sideroad.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Shortly before the train left District 12 for The Capitol with Haymitch, she went to the hill nearby holding gumdrops that he had sent his brother Sid to give her. She wailed at the wind and let out her pain as the train left. Haymitch watched out the window and was glad she saved their goodbye for when they weren't being recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training scores</a> were released and Haymitch only received a 1, Lenore Dove performed forbidden songs on the reaping stage, which still stood outside the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>. Her performance drew a crowd of other discontented kids, so she was arrested and held on the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers' base">Peacekeepers' base</a> in District 12. After the tributes' <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, Plutarch arranged a phone call between her and Haymitch. She told him about her arrest and claimed she would be released the next morning;<sup id="cite_ref-SR14_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> however, she was still incarcerated when Haymitch returned home two weeks later. Her uncles arranged for her to be released on house arrest, and she went to graze her geese in the Meadow, where she reunited with Haymitch.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Upon reuniting with Haymitch after his victory, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy%27s_mother" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch Abernathy's mother">his mother</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">younger brother Sid</a>, and Lenore Dove, were killed as punishment for his rebellious actions during the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In Lenore Dove's case, she was given a bag of red gumdrops, shortly after her release, believing it to be a bag he had left for Sid to give her earlier. However, Haymitch realized too late that the new bag had been planted there by Snow, filled with <a href="/wiki/Poison" title="Poison">poison</a>. Despite Haymitch and Lenore Dove's best efforts to stop it and call for help, the poison killed her. With her last words, Lenore Dove urged Haymitch not to let the sun rise on the reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Lenore Dove was buried in the Covey's secret graveyard beside Lucy Gray and <a href="/wiki/Maude_Ivory_Baird" title="Maude Ivory Baird">Maude Ivory Baird</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR27_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR27-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As a result of her death, Haymitch drove away anyone who could've been considered close to him so that they wouldn't suffer the same horrible fate and became an alcoholic. Haymitch would've welcomed his death if it wasn't for his promise to Lenore Dove that he would somehow keep the sun from rising on the reaping. Haymitch spent weeks looking for Lenore Dove's grave, eventually finding it with the help of <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> and spent two days at her graveside. The story that people were told was that she died suddenly of appendicitis.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR27_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR27-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Haymitch told <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> twenty-five years later about his girlfriend and his family being killed.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, Haymitch no longer found himself haunted by Lenore Dove. Instead, he saw her as having grown old beside him and he felt like she forgave him as Haymitch had at last kept his promise, or at least leant a hand. However, Lenore Dove would tell Haymitch that he couldn't join her yet as he still had to look after the tributes he had come to love as his new family. Haymitch later told Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> his story, about everyone he had ever lost, including Lenore Dove. Haymitch was content in knowing that the Capitol could never take Lenore Dove from him again, and they never really did in the first place.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>Lenore Dove was good with animals and raised a gaggle of geese from their hatching. She believed that anything was possible and that you shouldn't necessarily assume that something would continue to happen just because it had in the past. She was quiet in public, and thus surprised Haymitch with how much she could talk in private. She didn't like singing in public. Even though she was patient when explaining things, Haymitch felt that she might be too smart for him.<sup id="cite_ref-sneak_peek_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sneak_peek-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Haymitch described Lenore Dove as having "a dark view on things."<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lenore Dove had dappled green eyes and her hair had a hint of red in the sunlight. She wore faded overalls and shirts that concealed snips of color salvaged from Lucy Gray Baird's dress, and held a blue handkerchief in her pocket and a raspberry ribbon in her cuff. While performing in <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Allister" title="Mayor Allister">Mayor Allister</a>'s house, she wore a faded green dress with an ivory hair ribbon and orange lipstick. Her voice was "as soft and haunting as moonlight."<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Haymitch_Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>I think of Haymitch, unmarried, no family, blotting out the world with drink. He could have had his choice of any woman in the district. And he chose solitude. Not solitude— that sounds too peaceful. More like solitary confinement.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss Everdeen<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Lenore Dove and Haymitch first met each other when they were ten years old and introduced through Lenore Dove's cousin, <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a>. They developed a friendship and fell in love with each other as they grew up together. Haymitch was very protective of Lenore Dove, warning about her safety due to her history of getting into trouble with the local Peacekeepers. When Haymitch was reaped to replace <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> as a tribute, Lenore Dove was devastated and shared an emotional phone call with him on the night before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. When Snow orchestrated Lenore Dove's death, following his win in the Games, Haymitch was profoundly affected by her loss, causing him to relapse into alcoholism to cope with his grief over her death, along with his family's. Though Haymitch was deeply affected by Lenore Dove's death, he committed himself to somehow fulfill her dying wish to somehow end the Hunger Games. He is not known to have ever married, or even been romantic with another woman after her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF4_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF4-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Haydove.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20251120160702" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Gifting Haymitch the ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Haydove.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251120160702" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Haydove.jpeg" data-image-key="Haydove.jpeg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Haydove.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Gifting Haymitch the <a href="/wiki/Flint_striker" title="Flint striker">Flint striker</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Following the victory of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, Haymitch finally moved on from Lenore Dove's death, having fulfilled his promise to help contribute in ending the Hunger Games, believing Lenore Dove was proud of him for his efforts. After recounting how he won his Games and lost he everyone he loved, including Lenore Dove, to Katniss and Peeta, Haymitch found peace and started raising geese in honor of Lenore Dove, believing his girlfriend's spirit had grown old with him and never left his side since her death.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In line with the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> tradition of naming their children after a ballad and a color, Lenore Dove was named after the tormented man's beloved in Edgar Allan Poe's "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Raven" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Raven">The Raven</a>", and the color of a dove, which the District 12 school's dictionary described as "[w]arm gray with a slight purplish or pinkish tint."<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLenore_Dove_Baird%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In an exclusive excerpt from Chapter 1 of <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i> from People Magazine, her name was revealed as Lenore Dove.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>This prompted fan speculation that she is Covey, since her name fits their unique naming convention: a two-part name with the first part being taken from a ballad and the second being a color. This was confirmed upon the title's release.</li></ul></li>
<li>Lenore Dove is a Baird, and while it is unknown who her mother is because she died in childbirth, it could be <a href="/wiki/Maude_Ivory_Baird" title="Maude Ivory Baird">Maude Ivory</a>, whose headstone appears in the Covey's graveyard alongside Lucy Gray and Lenore Dove's.</li>
<li>Her father's identity is a mystery.</li>
<li>She can play the piano and accordion, the latter of which she calls her "tune box".</li></ul>
