<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrajan_Heavensbee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Trajan had a grand library in the <a href="/wiki/Heavensbee_mansion" title="Heavensbee mansion">Heavensbee mansion</a> that was still in use during Plutarch's lifetime. Above the fireplace was a portrait of Trajan with a white beard, smiling while holding an open book. Plutarch considered him the only ancestor "<i>who's been of any use"</i>. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> talked to <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> in the library and when Snow started violently vomiting, Haymitch wondered what Trajan would have thought of that.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrajan_Heavensbee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trajan" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Trajan">Trajan</a> was the name of a Roman emperor who expanded the Roman Empire until it encompassed more territory than it ever had before. "Heavensbee" likely refers to the spiritual domain of <a href="/wiki/Heaven" title="Heaven">Heaven</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrajan_Heavensbee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4f/Trajan_hb.png/revision/latest?cb=20231228100647" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peter Dinklage in front of the plaque." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4f/Trajan_hb.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231228100647" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="245" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Trajan hb.png" data-image-key="Trajan_hb.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Trajan_hb.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption"><a href="/wiki/Peter_Dinklage" title="Peter Dinklage">Peter Dinklage</a> in front of the plaque.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<ul><li>Trajan Heavensbee was first introduced to the franchise on a monument in Heavensbee Hall, shown briefly in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a>.</i> He was not mentioned in the books until <a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping"><i>Sunrise on the Reaping</i></a>, published in 2025.</li>
<li>His monument was modeled after the face of <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a>, who directed the film and three of its four predecessors. Lawrence was embarrassed by how large the monument was, so he tried to block it by putting <a href="/wiki/Peter_Dinklage" title="Peter Dinklage">Peter Dinklage</a> in front of it in the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> scene.<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
