<p><b>Tam Amber</b> was a resident of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> and a member of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>. Tam Amber was a standout player on his mandolin, performing with lightning-fast fingering while keeping his facial features expressionless and distant.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Tam Amber was found in a cardboard box left on the side of the road and taken in by the traveling Covey when he was just a baby. Because of this, they referred to him as a "lost soul".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He learned to play the mandolin and performed along with the rest of the Covey for profit.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Covey_Genocide">Covey Genocide</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>A few years prior to the events of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> rounded up the Covey and killed many of them. The few remaining Covey were trapped in District 12, where they made their living doing musical performances.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Another member of the Covey, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, had her name drawn in the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> While Lucy Gray was gone, the Covey didn't perform at all, and they only saw <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> once during the televised night of <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>; it had been a clear night in District 12, and Tam Amber had used a lot of tin foil to improve the reception.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Based on their rejection of <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a>, they listened to Lucy Gray sing <a href="/wiki/Songs#%22The_Ballad_of_Lucy_Gray_Baird%22" title="Songs">"The Ballad of Lucy Gray Baird"</a> that night. She had written the song to inform them that Billy Taupe played a role in her being reaped.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the Covey's first performance at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> since Lucy Gray returned as a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>, Tam Amber was the first to be introduced by <a href="/wiki/Maude_Ivory_Baird" title="Maude Ivory Baird">Maude Ivory Baird</a>. He walked to her side while playing his mandolin. Later, he proved to be something of a standout on his mandolin during a solo, playing a lightning-fast piece while remaining expressionless.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Tam Amber had fashioned a butter churn for Maude Ivory's birthday, as she was crazy for butter.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The first time the Covey traveled to the lake outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> with Coriolanus Snow and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, Tam Amber led them across <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a>, into the woods and to the lake. After they swam, Tam Amber fashioned his own fishing poles and hooks. He caught, cleaned, gutted and cut up several fish, which were cooked for lunch. As the traveled back home, Tam Amber took a different route where they could pick berries. He then traveled on with Sejanus and Maude Ivory, taking Maude Ivory to the Hob for new shoes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>He believed there were still people living up north from District 12, outside the influence of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, although Lucy Gray wasn't convinced. When she left home for the last time, Tam Amber had been the only person awake. She told him she was going to find out about a goat, as they had been talking about building a herd and selling milk on the side.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>By the time of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, he had an adopted niece, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a>, who he raised with <a href="/wiki/Clerk_Carmine_Clade" title="Clerk Carmine Clade">Clerk Carmine Clade</a> after her mother died giving birth to her. He was also a very skilled and sought after blacksmith, and many in District 12 owned his creations; including <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> who used arrow tips made by him<sup id="cite_ref-SR_1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Lenore Dove traded eggs for metal with Tam Amber to make her boyfriend, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, a present for his birthday which she helped design.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He taught Lenore Dove about the stars.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> One of his creations was the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> that was owned by <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> and later <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_12_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_12-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the reaping, Lenore Dove got into trouble during the disarray after <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> was killed. Upon its conclusion, Lenore Dove was put in handcuffs by a Peacekeeper until Tam Amber and Clerk Carmine bribed him with money to let her go. They led her down a sideroad when she tried to get to Haymitch, who had been reaped in Woodbine's place.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Lenore Dove was arrested again not long later for playing forbidden <a href="/wiki/Songs" title="Songs">songs</a> in front of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>, causing her uncles great stress, as she was being held in the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers' base">Peacekeepers' base</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR14_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR14-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When she was given permission to return home on house arrest, Tam Amber was concerned about her, but allowed her to go to the Meadow. However, Haymitch met Lenore Dove there and unintentionally fed her poisoned gumdrops planted by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>. She died almost immediately after.<sup id="cite_ref-SR26_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR26-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Tam Amber watched as Clerk Carmine tried to restart her heart, mumbling, "not again."<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tam Amber was a tall, rawboned young man. He wore a feathered hat during his performances.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Since Tam Amber was abandoned on the side of the road as a baby, nothing is known of his biological family, and any adoptive family he had are presumed deceased due to the Covey genocide. After that, the six remaining Covey children were taken in by an old man for a fee. The man didn't raise them, but he didn't "much mess with [them]" either. He died of <a href="/wiki/Health_conditions#Black_lung" title="Health conditions">black lung</a> the year prior to Lucy Gray's reaping, but some of the Covey were old enough to manage things by then.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Tam Amber was around 16 at the end of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SR_3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> which would place him in his mid-to-late twenties at this time.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tam_(name)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tam (name)">Tam</a> is a short Scots version of the name <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_(name)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Thomas (name)">Thomas</a>, meaning "twin". Since Covey names are drawn from ballads,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> his is taken from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tam_Lin" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tam Lin">Tam Lin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Interview_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Interview-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>  The second part, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/amber" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:amber">amber</a>, is a valuable gemstone that sometimes occurs in coal seams, and is a golden yellow.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTam_Amber%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The Covey had been talking about building a herd of goats and selling the milk as a second source of income,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so some theorize that the <a href="/wiki/Goat_Man" title="Goat Man">Goat Man</a> from the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a> may have been one of the Covey— most probably Tam Amber.</li>
<li>He is the only member of the Covey whose surname is unknown.</li>
<li>As revealed in <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>, he is the creator of the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> that was a symbol of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_12_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_12-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
