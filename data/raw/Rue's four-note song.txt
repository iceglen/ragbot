<p>This <b>four-note song</b> was used by <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> to tell her fellow harvesters when the working day was over. She would be the first to see the quitting time flag from her perch high in the trees, then when she sang the tune, the surrounding <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a> would pick up the song and sing it back to those on the ground.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRue%2527s_four-note_song%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Rue <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">allied</a> with <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, and when they split up to each execute their part of the plan to destroy the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>' supplies, Rue used her song to signal Katniss that she was safe, but couldn't get back right away.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, Katniss sang the melody back in answer to let Rue know it was safe to come out.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, rather than using the same melody, Rue sang her four notes to Katniss, who then whistled a different four-note melody back.
</p><p>During Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s visit to District 11 on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, an old man whistled Rue's song from the crowd: a prearranged signal for the audience to give <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a>. As Katniss and Peeta left the stage, the man was hauled up the steps of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a> by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> and shot in the back of the head.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Musical_notes">Musical notes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRue%2527s_four-note_song%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/66/Rue%27s_four_note_song.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231122032704" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Rue&#39;s four note song" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/66/Rue%27s_four_note_song.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231122032704" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Rue&#39;s four note song.jpg" data-image-key="Rue%27s_four_note_song.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Rue%27s_four_note_song.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>Rue's four-note melody is quite simple; in concert pitch (C), it is <b>"Db - F - Eb - Gb"</b> (or enharmonically, "C# - E# - D# - F#"). However, the notes more commonly heard and referred to as "Rue's song" are actually Katniss's reply: <b>"G - Bb - A - D"</b>, also in concert pitch (C).
</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRue%2527s_four-note_song%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The four-note song is often used promotionally in association with <i>The Hunger Games</i> film franchise. For example, it can be heard at the end of the trailer for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, and often at the end of official uploads of clips from the films.</li>
<li>During promotion for <i>Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i>, fans who texted the notes to Rue's song (GBAD) to the official Hunger Games Community feed received a riddle.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This riddle, when solved correctly, was suspected to reveal the date and time of the film's second trailer.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, the correct answer was not found.</li></ul>
