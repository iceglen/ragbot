<p>In the books, she wasn't described during the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>. In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, she could be seen wearing a long black dress with large white wires on her shoulders, standing behind the <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> tributes when <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> came to talk to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

Katniss wished she could mention meeting <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>— two <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> from District 8— in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside her home in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, but she couldn't figure out how to bring it up.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCecelia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Cecelia was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>, placing somewhere between 17th and 23rd. In a deleted scene from the movie, Enobaria bit Cecelia in the neck and then stabbed her.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When Katniss learned of Cecelia's death that night, she grieved for her and remembered the sight of Cecelia's children at the reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the end of <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, when Katniss was on the <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> revealed that Cecelia, among others, had known about the rebellion and was a part of the <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> to save Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCecelia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cecelia had three children by the time of the third Quarter Quell.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Their ages, genders, and fathers are unknown, as is Cecelia's relationship status.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCecelia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cecelia was described as being around thirty or so, and she was one of the younger victors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was depicted with brown hair in the film.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCecelia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cecelia's name comes from the Latin "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/caecus">caecus</a>", meaning "blind", and the plebeian family name Caecilia. In Roman mythology, the founder of this family, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caeculus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Caeculus">Caeculus</a>, was born from a spark.
</p><p>Cecelia also could have been named in connection to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Cecilia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Saint Cecilia">Saint Cecilia</a>, who was a Roman martyr sentenced to death for distributing her possessions to the poor. According to legend, attempts to burn her failed, so she was beheaded. After her death, she became the patron saint of musicians,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and she was also associated with songbirds.
</p>
