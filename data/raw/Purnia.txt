<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPurnia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Since most Peacekeepers were drafted by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> it is likely that Purnia was from there originally.
</p><p>Purnia was a regular customer of <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a>'s at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>. When <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> accidentally presented a poached turkey to <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>, intending to sell it to <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a>, he was publicly whipped. One of Purnia's fellow Peacekeepers, <a href="/wiki/Darius" title="Darius">Darius</a>, grabbed Thread's arm in an attempt to stop the whipping after twenty lashes; however, Thread knocked him unconscious with the butt of the weapon and kept going. After <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> stepped in to protect Gale, Purnia came forward too, albeit cautiously. She lied to Thread, claiming this was Gale's first offense, and convinced him to decrease the number of lashes. Once Thread relented and left, she was one of several Peacekeepers who helped carry Darius away, earning Katniss's thanks.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Fate">Fate</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPurnia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Purnia was never seen or mentioned again, so her fate is left unknown. However, in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, Gale recounted that Peacekeepers forced everyone in the town square watching the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> back into their homes. Around 9pm, the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>'s citizens could hear trucks leaving, evacuating Peacekeepers before the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> If this was the case in the novel as well, she may have survived the bombing, but it is unclear what happened to her during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPurnia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Purnia would appear to be a shortened version of the name <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calpurnia_(wife_of_Caesar)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Calpurnia (wife of Caesar)">Calpurnia</a>, belonging to the last wife of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Caesar" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Julius Caesar">Julius Caesar</a>. This wife had allegedly had a premonition of his death and tried to prevent it; Purnia helping to stop Gale's whipping could possibly be viewed as a loose parallel.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPurnia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Purnia, along with one of the Peacekeepers that visited Katniss's house,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and the Peacekeeper from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> who shoved <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> with her gun,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> are the only women ever mentioned among the Peacekeepers' ranks in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>.</li></ul>
