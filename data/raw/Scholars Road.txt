<p><b>Scholars Road</b> was a street in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> where the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> was located.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FScholars_Road%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>'s funeral, the procession went down Scholars Road and easily held the honor guard of the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>. Behind them came a long flatbed truck with a crane, which hoisted a bullet-ridden <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a>—the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> who had killed Arachne. The remaining tributes of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> were shackled to the truck bed. More Peacekeepers followed, along with horses that pulled a wagon with the coffin, and Arachne's <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Crane_family" title="Capitol families">family</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Panlo" title="Panlo">Panlo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sheaf" title="Sheaf">Sheaf</a>, the two <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> tributes who were killed in the bombing of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, were later draped over the backs of horses and paraded down Scholars Road during <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana Ring</a>'s funeral. <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">Facet</a>, <a href="/wiki/Velvereen" title="Velvereen">Velvereen</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Sabyn" title="Sabyn">Sabyn</a>, tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a> who had attempted to escape during the bombing, were unceremoniously dragged behind. A pair of caged trucks followed them bearing the remaining tributes, separated by sex.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> returned to the Capitol after his stint as a Peacekeeper, the truck turned onto Scholars Road and drove past the Academy to the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FScholars_Road%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Scholars Road was a narrower street than the <a href="/wiki/Corso" title="Corso">Corso</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It could be used to get to the Academy<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the Citadel. There was also a park and a bakery along the way.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
