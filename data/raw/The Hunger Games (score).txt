<p><b>"The Hunger Games"</b> is an instrumental piece composed by <a href="/wiki/James_Newton_Howard" title="James Newton Howard">James Newton Howard</a> for the 2012 film <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i>. It is the theme of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, an annual sporting event in which 24 youths fight to the death in an <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. It was released on March 26, 2012, as part of the film's <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Original_Motion_Picture_Score" title="The Hunger Games: Original Motion Picture Score">score</a>.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="In_the_soundtrack">In the soundtrack</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games_%28score%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/James_Newton_Howard" title="James Newton Howard">James Newton Howard</a> composed the score of the 2012 film <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i>. "The Hunger Games" was written to represent the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, an annual fight to the death between 24 youths, which is central to the film's plot. The film's <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Original_Motion_Picture_Score" title="The Hunger Games: Original Motion Picture Score">score</a> was released on March 26, 2012.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="In_the_film">In the film</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games_%28score%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>"The Hunger Games" plays at the onset of the film. It is heard over a wall of text that displays part of the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a>. The score fades out as the scene transitions to an interview conducted by <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, with <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a>, as his guest.
</p>
