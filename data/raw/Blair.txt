<p><b>Blair</b> was a boy from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> and a friend of <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlair%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Blair and Haymitch would play knife games together, such as trying to hit a target on a shed or a tree. Blair excelled at these games.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> where he wore an oversized dress shirt he received from his older brother, Blair talked to his friends, Burdock and Haymitch. Blair gave Haymitch a pack of roasted peanuts for his birthday and remarked that he was an idiot for being born on reaping day.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Blair tried to help when the home of <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">Willamae</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a> was burned down. Later, Blair was one of the people that carried Willamae and Sid's coffin. He and Burdock caught Haymitch after he collapsed in grief, taking him to his new home in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Following <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>'s death and Haymitch's subsequent withdrawal from his friends, Blair tried to hang on to their friendship but eventually acknowledged Haymitch's position, giving him a final hug before leaving.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
