<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKerna%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKerna%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the start of the Games, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> noticed the sunflower necklace tokens that everyone from Kerna's district had. He instructed Haymitch to get them to join the <a href="/wiki/Newcomers" class="mw-redirect" title="Newcomers">Newcomers</a> as they could <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">use these tokens</a> for their plan to sabotage the arena. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> thought their efficiency and capabilities would make them good allies.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>While training in the shelter booth, <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a> went up to one of the boys and offered them a chance to join the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Careers</a> pack but he refused. Panache was upset that they turned him down and retaliated by targeting Kerna. She was focusing on building pine needles and when she did not respond to his taunt, he grabbed her token and broke it. She was devastated and Haymitch thought it meant more to her than just a piece of home. <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> fixed it and Kerna, along with the rest of 9, decided to join the Newcomers alliance.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Even so, they weren't very sociable and almost appeared exclusive.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Kerna was the last person Haymitch saw before he dashed off into the woods to avoid the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. After running for a weapon,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Kerna died in the initial bloodbath along with everyone else in her district.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As Haymitch finished running, he heard a young girl's shriek.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This may have been Kerna's, as he had just seen her, and Kerna was the smallest girl in her district. <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> retrieved two of District 9's tokens.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKerna%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The District 9 tributes' <a href="/wiki/Training_outfits" title="Training outfits">training outfits</a> were buttery yellow, which reminded <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> of baby chicks.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Like the rest of her district, Kerna was still healing from sunburn, and had calloused hands and lean, muscled arms. She was the smallest tribute from her district.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKerna%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Kerna, like the rest of her fellow tributes from District 9, weren't very sociable and liked to keep to themselves. However, Kerna was seemingly the most open of them, crying when her token was broken, as well as speaking with Haymitch and Maysilee.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKerna%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>"Kerna" is a feminisation of kernel, the seed of a cereal plant.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKerna%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Kerna is the first character from District 9 to have multiple lines of dialogue in the series (other than Clayton or Ryan telling Panache Barker "no").</li>
<li>Due to being described as a small girl, Kerna is probably twelve to thirteen years old.</li></ul>
