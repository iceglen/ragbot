<p><b>The Hunger Games: Girl on Fire</b> was a mobile game for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/iOS" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:iOS">iOS</a> inspired by <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>, created as a teaser for the series' first <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">film adaptation</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Its release date was March 22, 2012, the day before the film premiered.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Synopsis">Synopsis</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Girl_On_Fire%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><i>From the creator of the award-winning indie game <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canabalt" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Canabalt">CANABALT</a> and leading independent film studio <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lionsgate">Lionsgate</a> comes an all-new action teaser game for the iPhone, iPod Touch and iPad — THE HUNGER GAMES: GIRL ON FIRE!</i>
</p><p><i>After <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteering</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> in place of her <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">younger sister</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> spends the afternoon in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">forest</a> outside her home... only to get ambushed by a swarm of <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a>-engineered <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jackers</a>!</i>
</p><p><i>Can you help Katniss make her escape?</i>
</p><p><i>Defend Katniss from her attackers with her <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">hunting bow</a>. Leap through treetops with intuitive touch controls. Help Katniss make her way back to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>!</i><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
