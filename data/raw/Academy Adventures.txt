<p><b>Academy Adventures</b> is an officially licensed <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roblox" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Roblox">Roblox</a> game created by Sawhorse Productions in order to promote <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>The game was launched on September 17th, 2023, first sent as a link to subscribers of <i>The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i>' Community feed before being posted across official social media, including the <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://hungergames.movie">official website for the films</a>.
</p><p>Players are able to enter the game and explore a digital recreation of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, in particular <a href="/wiki/Heavensbee_Hall" title="Heavensbee Hall">Heavensbee Hall</a>, as students; however, initially only the building's exterior and the plaza around it were available. There was a several-day countdown to registration on September 20th, coinciding with the release of the film's second trailer. The game then displayed another countdown to the start of classes on October 1st.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Gameplay">Gameplay</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The game's missions are split into quarters like a <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a> year, with players having to complete registration before proceeding to the first quarter and onwards. By completing missions, players can unlock cosmetic items.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Game_mechanics">Game mechanics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The game has two main types of activities: scavenger hunts and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/quick_time_event" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:quick time event">quick time events</a> (QTEs). In a scavenger hunt, the player explores the school and collects items by walking or running through them. In a QTE, the player has to press a button or key at the right time.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Missions">Missions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Registration">Registration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<p>Students register with Dean <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, claiming allegiance to Team Songbird or Team Snake, before being sent on a scavenger hunt around the campus. Each player has to gather 25 of each collectible item: birds, guitars, roses, and snakes.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="First_Quarter">First Quarter</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<ol><li>The player speaks with <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, who needs their help getting his essay onto Dean Highbottom's desk. He tasks them with collecting six keys from around the campus to get into the office.</li>
<li>Upon returning to Coriolanus, he then gives them the essay.</li>
<li>In the classroom, the player opens a series of vault doors, places the paper on the desk, and writes Coriolanus's name on it, completing the first quarter.</li></ol>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Quarter">Second Quarter</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<ol><li>The player speaks with <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, who wants to collect leftover food from the cafeteria before it's disposed of. First, the player collects six banners from around the school.</li>
<li>Upon returning to Sejanus, he sends the player to the cafeteria.</li>
<li>In the cafeteria, the player follows Sejanus around the room from above, unrolling banners to distract people as he collects the food. Sejanus transfers the food to the player on the far end of the room, and they pack it all into a backpack, completing the second quarter.</li></ol>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Third_Quarter">Third Quarter</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<ol><li>The player speaks with <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a>, who needs their help locating books in the library so she can write a paper about <a href="/wiki/Dr._Gaul" class="mw-redirect" title="Dr. Gaul">Dr. Gaul</a> without the woman finding out. First, the player has to collect six books from around the school to clear their library card.</li>
<li>The player returns to Clemensia, who sends them to the library for six more books.</li>
<li>In the library, the player has to collect one book from each cabinet without being seen by the library monitor. Each time the player starts working on a cabinet, there is a time limit for them to grab the book without being seen. This timer resets between each one. Once the player collects all six books, they complete the third quarter.</li></ol>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Fourth_Quarter">Fourth Quarter</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<ol><li>The player speaks with <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>, who has been asked by a student named <a href="/wiki/Alys" title="Alys">Alys</a> to mend her ripped graduation robe. First, the player has to collect six items from Tigris's sewing kit that have gotten scattered around the school.</li>
<li>The player returns to Tigris and clears a path through the crowd inside Heavensbee Hall, guiding Tigris to the graduation stage.</li>
<li>On the stage, the player locates Alys, takes her gown, and helps Tigris sew it.</li>
<li>Lastly, the player heads up to the podium and throws their graduation cap. A short cutscene plays, and the fourth quarter is completed.</li></ol>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cosmetics">Cosmetics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy_Adventures%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Early registration entitled players to earn a limited edition red backpack styled after <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>'s rainbow dress. 15,000 were available to claim.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Each subsequent quarter offers players a choice between two possible rewards upon completion of that section's mission. The fourth quarter graduation caps were also available in a limited quantity: 15,000 songbird caps and 15,000 snake caps.
</p>
