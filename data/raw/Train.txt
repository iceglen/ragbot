<p><b>Trains</b> are the primary method of travel throughout <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. They are mainly reserved for the shipment of one <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>'s goods and materials to another, and for the transportation of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> officials. Most of Panem's citizens are not authorized to travel by train unless they are <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">the Hunger Games</a>, as travel between the districts is forbidden except for officially sanctioned duties. Each district has at least one <a href="/wiki/Train_station" title="Train station">train station</a> where trains stop to load and unload their cargo.
</p><p>Trains are manufactured in <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a>, and hold a position on their seal.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Prior to his death, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma&#39;am">Grandma'am</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> would always await the train carrying <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a> from the Capitol during the first few months of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>A train arrived to the Capitol on the Wednesday following the reaping for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, carrying the tributes in two cargo cars before they were ushered into a truck.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the Games, Coriolanus was sent to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> via train, having to travel more than twenty-four hours before arriving. The train had originally been full of <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> recruits bound for different districts and gradually emptied until Coriolanus was left alone.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, the trains had holding cells on them with barred windows. The furniture was mostly plastic, and tributes had to sleep on bunk beds and share a small bathroom.
</p><p>On <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s return home, they loaded the bodies of his fellow District 12 tributes in a sitting room on the train. The train was fancier and shinier, and it had holding cells and a dining car. 
</p><p>For Haymitch's Victory Tour, the train was fancier, with steel and chrome. It served booze and had dove-colored velvet upholstery.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Train.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131202015557" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Haymitch, Katniss and Peeta at breakfast." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Train.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131202015557" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="89" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Train.jpg" data-image-key="Train.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Train.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Haymitch, Katniss and Peeta at breakfast.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After being reaped for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> were ushered to their district's train station and into a Capitol train, where they were given their own private chambers. The dinner came in multiple courses and was full of rich food, the likes of which neither tribute was used to.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When the train stopped at a platform to refuel, Katniss threw the packet of cookies <a href="/wiki/Mr._Mellark" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Mellark">Mr. Mellark</a> had given her out the window. Upon approaching the Capitol, the train entered a tunnel going through the mountains, the only route of access to the city from the eastern districts.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After winning the Games, Katniss and Peeta took another Capitol train back to District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>A train was also used to transport Katniss and Peeta through the districts on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>. Claiming to be drunk, Haymitch staggered out of the train during a refueling to talk to Katniss away from potential cameras.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, a part on the train malfunctioned and had to be replaced, delaying the schedule for at least an hour. Katniss visited the car full of Peeta's paintings during this time.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Throughout the Victory Tour, Peeta spent most of his nights roaming the train.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a> escaped <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> by boarding a boxcar full of fabric on a train to <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a>, then fleeing at a fuel stop and walking to District 12 on their own.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>A hundred <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> traveled to District 12 via train following the announcement of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">third Quarter Quell</a>. Katniss and Peeta were soon escorted into a train to the Capitol with little fanfare.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, a derailed train spilled toxic waste in one of the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, there was a train system to transport miners from <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a> to the center of the main town.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_14_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_14-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the Nut was bombed, a pair of trains carrying miners entered the train station. One of them was on fire and had smoke billowing against the windows.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> rode to the Capitol in a cargo train, along with large numbers of District 13 soldiers, in preparation for the invasion.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Near the end of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, Katniss Everdeen rides home to District 12 from the Capitol by train, along with Haymitch Abernathy and Peeta Mellark.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A large box of parchment sheets was sent to Katniss on a Capitol train, which she had requested to make a <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">memory book</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Freight_trains">Freight trains</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Trains are the primary mode of transportation of all the districts' goods, most of which gets delivered to the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The cargo trains used to transport tributes to the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> before the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> had heavy metal chains and padlocks securing the livestock cars they were carried in.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the Second Rebellion, cargo trains were used as a mode of transportation for the rebels to deploy troops across Panem and to the Capitol itself.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="High-speed_Capitol_trains">High-speed Capitol trains</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/ca/Trains.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720220142" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss inside the last car of the train on her Victory Tour." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/ca/Trains.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20130720220142" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="104" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Trains.png" data-image-key="Trains.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Trains.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss inside the last car of the train on her Victory Tour.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Capitol trains travel at an average speed of 250 miles per hour and were often used to transport tributes to the Capitol for the Hunger Games in less than a day. Tributes were given separate compartments, each with a bedroom, a bathroom, and a dressing area containing luxury clothes. The interior design is very plush and expensive, featuring a bar car, polished paneled walls, mahogany tables, and a variety of foods and refreshments for the tributes—including coffee, hot chocolate, orange juice, stew, and rolls.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, Katniss believed that every word they spoke on a Capitol train would be recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The last car of the train has chairs and couches, as well as back windows that retracted into the ceiling to reveal an open view of the landscape.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
