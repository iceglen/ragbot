<p><b>Clerk Carmine Clade</b>, sometimes known as <b>CC</b>, was a member of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>, and he played the fiddle in their band. He was the younger brother of <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a>, and later in life, he was one of <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>'s guardians.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClerk_Carmine_Clade%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClerk_Carmine_Clade%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the Covey's first musical performance at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> after the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, Clerk Carmine played his way across the stage after being introduced by <a href="/wiki/Maude_Ivory" class="mw-redirect" title="Maude Ivory">Maude Ivory</a>. During a song primarily sung by <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, Clerk Carmine embellished the melody with some fancy finger work on his fiddle while the others backed him up.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe" class="mw-redirect" title="Billy Taupe">Billy Taupe</a> appeared at the performance of the Covey after being kicked out, trying to convince them to take him back. After the others rejected him, he tried to appeal to Clerk Carmine, asking him if it was how he felt as well, but Carmine stood his ground and did not reply. Later, when Billy Taupe came back to the house they lived in, Clerk Carmine hung his head off the loft and looked unhappy.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Lucy Gray worried about Clerk Carmine, as he still missed his brother.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Barb_Azure_Baird" title="Barb Azure Baird">Barb Azure Baird</a> took a pan of chilled milk out of the fridge, Clerk Carmine retrieved a churn <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> had made, hoping to make cream for butter for <a href="/wiki/Maude_Ivory" class="mw-redirect" title="Maude Ivory">Maude Ivory</a>. Even though it was said to be a fool's errand, he hoped it might work if they gave it another day, as Maude Ivory was crazy for butter.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>During a discussion of the ballad <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> had been named after, he chimed in after Maude Ivory said that the Lucy Gray was different, calling her a "bonehead" and saying that she was a ghost and had fallen down a bridge and died. However, Maude Ivory didn't think this possible, as the snow would look different if she had fallen. She then appealed to Lucy Gray Baird, asking her to explain, but responded that it was a mystery, like her name. At the lake, Clerk Carmine brought Lucy Gray some <a href="/wiki/Katniss_(plant)" title="Katniss (plant)">katniss</a>, but it wasn't yet in season for eating.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Maude Ivory fell asleep, Clerk Carmine brought over a bright blue feather he had found, wanting it to be an anonymous gift for her. When she awoke, she admired the feather and Lucy Gray fixed it in her hair. While heading back to town, Clerk Carmine spotted a tree full of apples and climbed it to throw down fruit to the others.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClerk_Carmine_Clade%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For thirty years, Clerk Carmine was in a relationship with a man who lived in town. They kept their relationship a secret to avoid harassment and discrimination.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>By the time of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, he had an adopted niece, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a>, who he raised with <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> after her mother died giving birth to her. He did not approve of her relationship with <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, which might have been because the Abernathys used to be known as rebels. He also discouraged her connection to the Chances, who were known for their reckless behavior.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> On the day of the reaping, he called for Lenore Dove to get ready. She had been crying in <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a> with Haymitch, and he shot him a disapproving look when she returned.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the reaping, Lenore Dove got into trouble during the disarray after <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> was killed. Upon its conclusion, Lenore Dove was put in handcuffs by a Peacekeeper until Clerk Carmine and Tam Amber bribed him with money to let her go. They led her down a sideroad when she tried to get to Haymitch, who had been reaped in Chance's place.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Lenore Dove was arrested again not long later for playing forbidden <a href="/wiki/Songs" title="Songs">songs</a> in front of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>, causing Clerk Carmine and Tam Amber great stress, as she was being held in the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers' base">Peacekeepers' base</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When she was given permission to return home on house arrest, Clerk Carmine allowed her five minutes of privacy in the Meadow. However, Haymitch met her there and unintentionally fed her poisoned gumdrops planted by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>. Although he shouted for Clerk Carmine, Lenore Dove died almost immediately after.<sup id="cite_ref-SR26_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR26-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Clerk Carmine took her from Haymitch's arms, trying futilely to restart her heart as he called her name. One night, he played Lenore Dove's song on his fiddle. Haymitch overheard and pounded on the door of <a href="/wiki/The_Covey%27s_house" title="The Covey's house">the Covey's house</a> in desperation.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="In_District_13">In District 13</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClerk_Carmine_Clade%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Clerk Carmine escaped the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_12_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">bombing of District 12</a> and traveled to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> with his fiddle. He accompanied a choir of children at the <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie's wedding">wedding</a> of <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Interview_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Interview-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClerk_Carmine_Clade%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As Lenore Dove's guardian, Clerk Carmine was stern and highly protective of her. He disapproved of her relationship with Haymitch.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClerk_Carmine_Clade%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>At twelve years old, Clerk Carmine was skinny and wore a hat adorned with colorful feathers when performing. He had a similar appearance to his brother.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As an adult, he wore patched overalls, protecting his hands by shoving them in the pockets. He had a voice that carried without him needing to raise it.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClerk_Carmine_Clade%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Clerk seems to be an older form of the name Clark, both coming from the Latin "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/clericus">clericus</a>", meaning a member of the clergy or a scholar. Since Covey names are drawn from ballads,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> his is taken from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clerk_Saunders" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Clerk Saunders">Clerk Saunders</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Interview_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Interview-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The second part of his name, Carmine, is a shade of deep red, and Clade might come from the Ancient Greek "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CE%BA%CE%BB%CE%AC%CE%B4%CE%BF%CF%82">kládos</a>", meaning a young slip or shoot of a tree, the kind broken off for grafting.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClerk_Carmine_Clade%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Clerk Carmine is one of the four characters confirmed to be part of the LGBTQ+ community. The others are <a href="/wiki/Pluribus_Bell" title="Pluribus Bell">Pluribus Bell</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cyrus" title="Cyrus">Cyrus</a>, and fellow Covey member <a href="/wiki/Barb_Azure_Baird" title="Barb Azure Baird">Barb Azure Baird</a>.</li></ul>
