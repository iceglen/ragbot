</p><p>The <b>46th Hunger Games</b> took place 4 years prior to <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i> and 28 years before the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> this year was <a href="/wiki/Palladium_Barker" title="Palladium Barker">Palladium Barker</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Reaping">Reaping</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F46th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 46th Hunger Games in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> pulled a kerosene-soaked, burning, smoking <a href="/wiki/Panem_flag" title="Panem flag">flag of Panem</a> from under the temporary stage. <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> was arrested for the crime, but later let go, even though she was secretly guilty.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F46th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Palladium likely formed a typical <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> with other <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>, since they were from one of the Career districts.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F46th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Palladium was related to <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, one of the two male District 1 tributes in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>This was the first year in which <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and Lenore Dove were eligible for the reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Palladium is the second District 1 victor with a known surname, the other being <a href="/wiki/Augustus_Braun" title="Augustus Braun">Augustus Braun</a>.</li>
<li>Palladium won the year after <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>.</li></ul>
