</p><p><b>Mrs. Plinth</b>, also referred to as <b>Ma Plinth</b>, was the mother of <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, one of the <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a> of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She and Sejanus moved to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> after the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, as her husband <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo</a> had made enough money to integrate himself and his family into the Capitol lifestyle.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Ma Plinth was born and raised in District 2 alongside <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth%27s_sister" title="Mrs. Plinth's sister">her sister</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She later married Strabo and had a son with him several years before the Dark Days began. During the war, Ma Plinth always forced her son to drink posca (watery wine with honey and herbs). Sejanus later told <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> of this, and Coriolanus was secretly repulsed that he was about to be overtaken by somebody who referred to their mother as "Ma".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Move_to_the_Capitol">Move to the Capitol</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The first time the Plinths arrived in the Capitol, Ma was anxious and thought it a disaster, but was calmed down by a young Sejanus.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She had only brought a handful of things from District 2, which she later set up in a corner of her new kitchen. After her move, the only person from District 2 who kept in touch was her sister, who sent her a needlepoint of a mountain scene overlaid with the word "home".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before Sejanus headed to the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> where the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were being held, Ma made sandwiches for each of the tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Coriolanus also took one, finding it to be delicious, and Sejanus agreed to tell his mother he thought so.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Coriolanus was injured in the bombing of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, Ma baked a homemade casserole, which she took over to the Snows' apartment.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> for the Games, Sejanus appeared with Ma and introduced her to Coriolanus. He thanked her for the casserole, but Ma thanked him in return for being a friend to her son. Coriolanus then told her he would be at her service, rendering Ma speechless with emotion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The first night of the Games, Ma went to the Snows' penthouse after her son hadn't come home. She was very worried, but upon turning to leave, she saw Sejanus on-screen in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She told the <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma'am">Grandma'am</a> that he was sprinkling bread crumbs on the dead body of <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>, his tribute, to aid him on his journey. After a call from <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, Mrs. Plinth and Coriolanus went to the arena to collect Sejanus. She apologized for her son and refused to let Coriolanus enter the arena himself. However, when he did, Coriolanus told Sejanus that Ma was waiting for him, and he eventually agreed to come. As thanks, Ma gifted him food, part of which Coriolanus later gave to <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>. He ate two of her egg-and-sausage rolls for breakfast himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coriolanus visited the Plinths the third night of the Games, catching Ma in the kitchen. She offered him milk, blackberry pie and ice cream before he headed up to talk to Strabo.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Sejanus was sent to become a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> along with Coriolanus, Ma sent them packages of baked goods. Because of them, the boys and their bunkmates were able to trade for luxuries such as liquor at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>. Ma also sent Coriolanus a separate letter, thanking him for all he had done and requesting that he continue to keep an eye on her son.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Coriolanus returned home from District 12 and Ma learned of Sejanus's hanging, she and her husband moved into the apartment below the Snows'. Ma came by daily to help the Grandma'am, acting as a maid. On the day that would have been Sejanus's nineteenth birthday, Coriolanus returned his box of personal possessions to Ma and Strabo Plinth.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Sejanus appeared, in another brand-new suit, with a rumpled little woman in an expensive flowered dress on his arm. It didn't matter. You could put a turnip in a ball gown and it would still beg to be mashed. Coriolanus had no doubt this could only be Ma.</i>"</span></dd>

<p>After the interviews, Ma was seen in an expensive flowered dress.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Mrs. Plinth was referred to as "frumpier than ever" and "pathetic" by Coriolanus after she arrived at the Snows' place when Sejanus had gone missing. Often, she was seen clutching a handbag, the contents of which are unknown, with the exception of handkerchiefs that she used to wipe her tears upon getting emotional.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ma Plinth was a loving mother towards Sejanus, and she extended her kindness to Coriolanus, her son's only friend. She was also a skilled cook; Coriolanus described her as "something of an artist in the kitchen" during a visit to the Plinth family's apartment.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
