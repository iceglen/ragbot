<p><b>District 2</b> is one of the thirteen <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a> of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. Landlocked and located in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mountains" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rocky Mountains">Rocky Mountains</a>, extremely close to the <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> itself, it is one of the largest and wealthiest districts. Publicly, District 2 is presented solely as the home of the nation's stone quarries and masonry workers, but it is also where a large auxiliary force of <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> are recruited, trained, and where weapons are manufactured for their usage.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After the alleged loss of <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the seat of the Capitol's military power and manufacturing moved to District 2.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peacekeepers were also commonly sourced from District 2 from then on, becoming a secret second industry unknown to the other districts.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationship_with_the_Games">Relationship with the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="1st-10th_Hunger_Games">1st-10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the first ten Hunger Games, District 2 and <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> were noted as being "more Capitol-friendly" and turning out "better-fed" tributes, resulting in more <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a>; this implies that District 2 had already won more than once in the earliest years of the Games. Districts <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> were also "contenders", though, so 1 and 2 had not won them all.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite this, District 2's tributes did not fare well in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. <a href="/wiki/Sabyn" title="Sabyn">Sabyn</a> fell to her death in a failed escape attempt before the Games began,<sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and while <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a> successfully escaped, he was soon recaptured, brutalized, and displayed in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was the first official kill of the Games, mercy-killed by <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_district">Career district</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Eventually, alongside District 1 and District 4, District 2 began to train <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>. Although technically against the rules, their children were trained to prepare them for <a href="/wiki/Combat" class="mw-redirect" title="Combat">combat</a> inside the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Career training made District 2 tributes popular <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">bets</a> among <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a>. Despite their success in the Games and outward loyalty to the Capitol, some in District 2 secretly resented their arrangement and later sided with the rebels.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:5_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion"><a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 2 was the main loyalist stronghold for much of the war. All but one of its victors were killed during the war and the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>. The only survivor was <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Battle_of_District_2">Battle of District 2</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_District_2" title="Battle of District 2">Battle of District 2</a></i></center></div>
<p>Although the district did not rebel as a whole, certain villages either sided with or were captured by the rebels<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> before the conflict ended with one final assault on <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>. Rebels deliberately triggered avalanches to block entrances on the direction of <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At this point, the district surrendered and unified under the rebels' banner.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Reconstruction">Reconstruction</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Directly after the war, Gale Hawthorne was described as "mopping up Peacekeepers in District 2".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Following a brief return to the Capitol for the execution of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>, Gale took "some fancy job" in a more permanent move to District 2.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The close relationship between the Capitol and District 2 is reflected in their naming conventions: both draw names from ancient Rome (e.g. <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> and <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> from 2, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus</a> and <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca</a> from the Capitol).
</p><p>People from District 2 had a funerary tradition of sprinkling bread crumbs over the bodies of the fallen, so they'd have food on their journey "back where they came from".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> said the taste of goat's milk reminded him of his childhood in District 2,<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> implying that it was something they drank often.
</p>

<p>Children from District 2 were raised with a warrior mindset in preparation for becoming Peacekeepers or tributes. Like other Careers from Districts 1 and 4, those from District 2 tended to be strong and well-prepared, but they had an additional level of brutal discipline. They often <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to compete in the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> From the tributes we've seen, on average, both female and male tributes place 6th.
</p>

<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, at the time of <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, District 2 is together with <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> mentioned to be "contenders" for success in the games. Presumably, this means District 2 produced one or more victors during the first decade of the games.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 220px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/14/District2.png/revision/latest?cb=20241217044838" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 2 during the ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/14/District2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20241217044838" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="91" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District2.png" data-image-key="District2.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District2.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">District 2 during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 2 is located directly to the south of the Capitol, primarily centered in the states of Colorado and New Mexico. This supports District 2's reputation for having close ties and strong loyalty to the Capitol and its industry, masonry, as it's situated in the eastern part of the Rocky Mountains.
</p><p>The district consists of one main town and a number of smaller villages spread out across the mountains. Each one was originally built around a stone mine or quarry, but eventually, they were devoted to training and housing Peacekeepers. A train system runs from the Nut into the main town, and like other districts, District 2 has a central square where their <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a> is located.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Nut">The Nut</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">The Nut</a></i></center></div>
<p>The Nut is a virtually impenetrable mountain in the center of District 2, with much of the Capitol's military arsenal housed inside.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>District 2's role as the center of Peacekeeper training is modelled after the <i>auxilia</i> in Ancient Rome. The auxilia outnumbered the legionary during much of Ancient Rome. Additionally, both auxilia and legionaries worked in masonry.</li>
<li>In an early version of the first film script, <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> said the victors of the two previous Games were District 2 tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Districts 1, 2, <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">5</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a> were not involved in the rebel plot during the third Quarter Quell.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>By the time Katniss became openly involved in the Second Rebellion, District 2 was the last district openly allied with the Capitol. Although the district did not rebel as a whole, certain villages either been sided with or were captured by the rebels before the final assault against the Nut.
<ul><li>Ironically, District 2 was also the richest district with a known rebel (Lyme).</li></ul></li>
<li>The function and fortification of the Nut are similar to the Cheyenne Mountain Complex that houses NORAD, and the rebel strategy at the Nut is similar to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/total_war" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:total war">total war</a> strategy employed during <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherman%27s_March_to_the_Sea" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sherman&#39;s March to the Sea">Sherman's March to the Sea</a>.
<ul><li>In addition, District 2's number, along with its role as Panem's military seat, is analogous to how the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Amendment" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Second Amendment">Second Amendment</a> of America's constitution grants citizens the right to bear arms, in particular those that the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> commonly use.</li></ul></li>
<li>In both the 74th and 75th Hunger Games, both District 2 tributes made it to the top 7.
<ul><li>Additionally, all four District 2 tributes volunteered, and male tributes were the last to die in each of their Games, killed by a tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.</li></ul></li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 2 has 7 quarries in operation with a population of 231,354, making it the second largest district, in terms of population.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 2 claims 1,937 in tesserae, making it the second richest district.
<ul><li>If the population is 231,354 and they claim 1,937 tesserae, that would equal to 0.008 tesserae per person, making it the richest District per capita.</li></ul></li>
<li>Prop currency used in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> names District 2's first governor as <a href="/wiki/Vick_Flemming" title="Vick Flemming">Vick Flemming</a>. He appears on the twenty Panar bill.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 73rd and 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be red oxide (#5a2520 – <span style="background-color:#5a2520; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 2's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be tan (#d2b48c - <span style="background-color:#d2b48c; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear for the 50th Hunger Games was purple (#800080 - <span style="background-color:#800080; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
