<p><b>Tygart Blanken</b> was a resident of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> whose son, <a href="/wiki/District_12_male_(73rd_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 12 male (73rd HG)">Sage</a>, participated in the <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">73rd Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-script_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-script-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTygart_Blanken%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tygart was only mentioned in an early version of a script for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>. He and his wife lived in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>, and their son was <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> for the 73rd Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-script_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-script-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(73rd_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (73rd HG)">District 3 male</a> killed Sage with a bar mace on the second day of the Games. Later that same day, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> saw Corrina and Tygart at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>. He was selling chestnuts "in a fog" when she gave him two purple berries she had picked earlier, explaining that they would help Corrina sleep, and that <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">her mother</a> took them.<sup id="cite_ref-script_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-script-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
