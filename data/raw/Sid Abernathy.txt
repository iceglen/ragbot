<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSid_Abernathy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sid lived in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> with his mother and older brother, Haymitch. He was born sometime around the 40th Hunger Games. In his childhood, his father perished in a coal mine fire.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The morning of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 50th Hunger Games, Sid woke up his brother Haymitch to wish him a happy birthday, and so Haymitch could "get to the woods at daylight."<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Since Haymitch was now sixteen years old,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> he was one of four <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> reaped for the 50th Hunger Games or second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>. Haymitch managed to lead his final opponent, a <a href="/wiki/District_1_female_(50th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 1 female (50th HG)">District 1 female</a>, to the edge of the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> and use its <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> to launch her own <a href="/wiki/Axe" class="mw-redirect" title="Axe">axe</a> back at her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was subsequently crowned as District 12's second <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSid_Abernathy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Two weeks after his victory, Sid, his mother, and Haymitch's girlfriend, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a>, were killed as punishment for his game-winning stunt, which had made <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> look foolish. Haymitch would return home to District 12 to find the family home burning, learning from those nearby that Sid and his mother were both inside, being burned alive. He raced for the house, attempting to either save them or die with them, only to be pulled back by <a href="/wiki/Blair" title="Blair">Blair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a>. <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid March</a> then served him <a href="/wiki/Sleep_syrup" title="Sleep syrup">sleep syrup</a> which knocked him out for over a day.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a> ruled their deaths an accident.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch recounted these events to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> twenty-five years later.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSid_Abernathy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sid was an innocent and sweet ten-year old boy who looked up to his older brother, Haymitch. Sid was also said to be energetic and a morning person as he woke his brother up early on his birthday. Sid was loyal to Haymitch, helping him do chores and deliver gifts to his girlfriend, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>.
</p>
