</p><p><b>Justin Yu</b> is a professional stunt coordinator with an extensive background in martial arts, having competed as part of the USA National Wushu Team. He played the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(73rd_HG)" title="District 10 male (73rd HG)">District 10 male</a> in the <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">73rd Hunger Games</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.
