<p><b>Cato</b> is the secondary antagonist of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i>. He was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. He was the leader of the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>' <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>, the strongest tribute in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s primary adversary.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_24_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_24-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>A monstrous boy who lunges forward to volunteer from District 2.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss's first impression of Cato<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Cato was born and brought up in District 2. He wanted to volunteer for the Hunger Games ever since he was a young child, and trained for them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He was helped by Brutus in his training for the 74th Hunger Games, although it is unclear whether in the capacity of a mentor, or prior to the games.<sup id="cite_ref-Bruno_Gunn_Interview_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bruno_Gunn_Interview-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Reapings">Reapings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>He lunged forward to volunteer for the 74th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade">Tribute Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, Cato and his <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a> <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> wear Romanesque armored <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">chariot costumes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the parade, Cato glares at the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> team because they have outshone the other tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training_Center"><a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>I've been preoccupied with watching the boy from District 2 send a spear through a dummy’s heart from fifteen yards.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss's observations in training<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>For three days, Cato and his fellow tributes train and prepare for the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>In the novel, Cato trains at the spear-throwing station and displays his prowess with the weaponry.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He most likely shows off these skills to the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> in his private session, as he receives a high <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> of 10.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, the Careers target <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s weakness and view him as what Katniss describes as a "meal" until Peeta shows his true strength by throwing a 100-pound metal ball into a rack of spears. Later on in the film, Cato is angered when he thinks that the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 6 male (74th HG)">District 6 male</a> took his knife, which was actually stolen by <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>. He provokes a fight with the other boy, threatening to make him his first target, until the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> separate them, as fighting before the Games is not allowed.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<dd>—Katniss's thoughts during the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>In the novel, Katniss describes Cato as playing up the angle of a "ruthless killing machine."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, when interviewed by <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, Cato responds by saying that he is "vicious and ready to go".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>But it's tempting, so tempting, when I see the bounty waiting there before me. And I know that if I don't get it, someone else will. That the Career Tributes who survive the bloodbath will divide up most of these life-sustaining spoils.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss's thoughts before the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>

<p>Though Cato is not mentioned during the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a> in the novel, he is heavily featured in the film. Like most of the other tributes, Cato runs toward the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> and fights for supplies. He first targets the District 6 male as he'd promised to do in the Training Center. The <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a> and the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a> try to attack Cato, but he pushes them to the ground. He later slashes the District 5 male, making him cough up blood onto a crate, and he stabs the District 6 male with a serrated sword.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Alliance">Alliance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>The strong band together to hunt down the weak then, when the tension becomes too great, begin to turn on one another. I don't have to wonder too hard who has made this alliance. It'll be the remaining Career Tributes from Districts 1, 2, and 4. Two boys and three girls. The ones who lunched together.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss's thoughts during the early stages of the Games<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>As is typical in the arena, the 74th Hunger Games Careers form an alliance. This includes <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>, Cato and Clove from District 2, and the <a href="/wiki/District_4_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 female (74th HG)">District 4 female</a>, whose <a href="/wiki/District_4_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 male (74th HG)">district partner</a> dies in the bloodbath. Peeta Mellark joins the Career pack shortly after<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> because the Careers think he will be useful in finding Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="First_night">First night</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Go on, then, Lover Boy. See for yourself.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Cato to Peeta<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>On the first night of the Hunger Games, the Careers manage to track down the <a href="/wiki/District_8_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 female (74th HG)">District 8 female</a> via the <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a> she foolishly starts in the middle of the night. The Careers attempt to kill her, but when the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> withhold cannon fire,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Peeta goes back to "finish the job". In his absence, the Careers argue over whether they should let him live or not, though they ultimately decide to leave him alive, as he is their best chance at finding Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tracker_jackers">Tracker jackers</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games"><b>Katniss</b>: <i>"How's everything with you?"</i><br><b>Cato</b>: <i>"Well enough. Yourself?"</i><br><b>Katniss</b>: <i>"It's been a bit warm for my taste. The air's better up here. Why don't you come on up?"</i><br><b>Cato</b>: <i>"Think I will."</i></span></dd>
<dd>—Cato and Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<p>A few days into the Games, after an enormous fire forces the tributes together, the Careers find Katniss and chase her up a tree. Cato attempts to follow her into the tree, but he falls due to his heavy weight. Glimmer tries next, but meets the same failure. At the urging of Peeta, the Careers decide to make camp below Katniss's tree so she can't escape without them knowing.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>While stuck in the tree, Katniss cuts down a <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nest ( that Rue showed her), dropping it onto the sleeping Careers and chasing them away from her tree. While Glimmer and the District 4 female are lethally stung, Cato and the others escape to a nearby lake, where they fend off the remaining insects. With the Careers distracted, Katniss manages to pry the bow and arrows from Glimmer's corpse, but Peeta returns and urges her to run. As he does so, Cato cuts his way through the brush<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and realizes what Peeta has done. Enraged at Peeta's betrayal, Cato engages the other tribute in a fight that Peeta swiftly loses,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> though he escapes with a severe leg wound, tracker jacker strings, and burns retained from the earlier fire.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Destroyed_supplies">Destroyed supplies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>When we find her, I kill her in my own way, and no one interferes.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Cato on Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Shoot me and he goes down with me.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Cato to Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>On the last day of the Games, <a href="/wiki/Wolf_mutt" title="Wolf mutt">wolf mutts</a> appear, chasing a bloodied and beaten Cato out of the woods. Katniss and Peeta think he is on his way to kill them at first, so Katniss shoots an arrow at his chest, but it bounces off since he is wearing some sort of body armor.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_24_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_24-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss suspects the armor came from District 2's backpack from the feast.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Ignoring Katniss's arrow, Cato runs straight past Katniss and Peeta, headed for the Cornucopia. Upon sighting the mutts, the two race after him.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_24_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_24-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Once on top of the Cornucopia, Cato takes a few moments to catch his breath before he grabs Peeta in a headlock, effectively cutting off his ability to breathe. When Katniss points an arrow at his head, Cato merely laughs and points out that if she shoots him, Peeta will fall to the mutts as well. Katniss figures out that if Peeta dies, Cato will use his body as a shield against her arrows. Almost out of options, Peeta shakily draws an "X" in blood on Cato's hand with his finger. Katniss then shoots her arrow into Cato's hand, forcing him to reflexively release Peeta and fall to the mutts. At first, Cato fights the mutts off, as the sound of his sword against the Cornucopia can be heard for a long time. Eventually, despite his great strength and skill, he is disabled, and the mutts brutally attack him for what seems like hours, as he is too exhausted to defend himself.
</p><p>When dawn comes, Katniss can't take Cato's moans of pain and despair any longer and decides to use her last arrow to end his suffering. With Peeta's help, she leans down into the mouth of the Cornucopia and finds a savagely bloodied Cato lying at the back. He mouths the word "please", and out of pity, not vengeance, she shoots the arrow into his head, killing him instantly.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>In the film, Cato's death is slightly different. Unlike the novel, he is already on top of the Cornucopia - bloodied and beaten - before Katniss and Peeta arrive. He manages to attack both tributes at once in a last desperate attempt to kill them and puts Peeta in a headlock while Katniss is distracted by the mutts. When she turns around, ready to shoot Cato with her bow, he points out to her that if she shoots him, both he and Peeta will fall to the mutts. He then rants about how <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> has made him into a pawn, as all he knows how to do is kill and his only goal in life is to bring pride to his district. When he falls to the mercy of the mutts, he does not suffer for very long as Katniss kills him out of mercy by shooting an arrow into his head almost seconds after his fall.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_appearance">Physical appearance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Brutal, bloody Cato who can snap a neck with a twist of his arm, who had the power to overcome Thresh[.]</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss's thoughts about Cato's physical capabilities<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>In the novel, Cato's physical appearance is not addressed beyond Katniss's observations that he is a "physical wonder", with a robust stature and powerful, visibly well-muscled arms.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He is the second largest tribute, only being slightly smaller than Thresh. He is capable of snapping a neck with his bare hands and demonstrates his raw physical power several times throughout the novel, most notably when he single-handedly fights off a pack of mutts until they eventually overcome him.
</p><p>In the film, he retains his powerful figure, towering over the other tributes at six-foot-two, shorter only than Marvel. He has short-cropped, slightly spiked blond hair and blue eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>I believe Cato could easily lose his judgment in a fit of temper. Not that I can feel superior on that point. I think of the moment I sent the arrow flying into the apple in the pig's mouth when I was so enraged. Maybe I do understand Cato better than I think.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> compares herself to Cato<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/31/Careers_watch_Peeta.png/revision/latest?cb=20200713211641" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Arrogant and condescending, Cato underestimates tributes from lesser districts" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="104" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Careers watch Peeta.png" data-image-key="Careers_watch_Peeta.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/31/Careers_watch_Peeta.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200713211641"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/31/Careers_watch_Peeta.png/revision/latest?cb=20200713211641" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Arrogant and condescending, Cato underestimates tributes from lesser districts" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/31/Careers_watch_Peeta.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200713211641" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="104" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Careers watch Peeta.png" data-image-key="Careers_watch_Peeta.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/31/Careers_watch_Peeta.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200713211641"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Careers_watch_Peeta.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Arrogant and condescending, Cato underestimates tributes from lesser districts</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Having been raised in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, a district with strong ties to the Capitol and general enthusiasm for the Hunger Games, Cato is aggressive, bloodthirsty,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_24_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_24-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and violent. He shows disregard for his fellow tributes' lives and kills without hesitation, even seeing it as a means of achieving glory and honor. This is made all the more effective by his prowess in battle, as Cato is physically large and well-built, can wield spears and swords with proficiency, and displays impressive endurance and vitality.
</p><p>Cato's competitive nature is even more extreme than those of his fellow Careers, as he harbors a deep resentment of Katniss due to her high training score<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and iterates to his allies that he must be the one to kill her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_14-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When faced with a defeat, Cato abandons all rationality, instead losing his temper, throwing a tantrum, and often taking out his rage on those he perceives as responsible for his predicament. His fury is so extreme that Katniss describes it as "comical," though she is frightened by his instability, immense power, and personal vendetta against her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Catotree.png/revision/latest?cb=20140317014040" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cato displays aggression and ferocity when hunting tributes in the arena" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="104" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Catotree.png" data-image-key="Catotree.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Catotree.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140317014040"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Catotree.png/revision/latest?cb=20140317014040" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cato displays aggression and ferocity when hunting tributes in the arena" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Catotree.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140317014040" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="104" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Catotree.png" data-image-key="Catotree.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Catotree.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140317014040"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Catotree.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Cato displays aggression and ferocity when hunting tributes in the arena</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Due to his strength and skills, Cato is arrogant and overestimates his own abilities. This more often than not leads to his failure to follow through on a kill<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_14-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, most notably when he fails to finish off Peeta<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> after the tracker jacker incident.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Ultimately, his arrogance is his downfall, as he is slow to react to Katniss shooting his hand due to believing he has already won the fight.
</p><p>In the film, Cato suffers a mental breakdown in his final confronation with Katniss and Peeta. Though he retains his brutality and fighting instincts, he laments how he has been bred to kill and how the Capitol does not care whether he lives or dies. He shows hesitance and regret when faced with having to kill Peeta, though he ultimately resolves to do so in order to bring pride to his district.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Possessions">Possessions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>He's got some kind of body armor!</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> on Cato<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>

<p>At the onset of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Games</a>, Cato has access to almost every resource, as he and his allies hold control over the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> supplies. Known possessions of the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Careers</a> include food, water, medicine, night vision glasses, and weapons of all sorts. After the supplies are blown up, the Careers are left with little to sustain themselves. Cato appears to still possess a spear and a sword following the explosion, as he uses both weapons after the rest of his supplies are obliterated.
</p><p>Cato is later given high-tech body armor from the Capitol, presumably from the District 2 backpack Thresh took from the feast. This armor was made of a durable material that could withstand Katniss's arrows, and kept Cato alive under attack from the wolf mutts.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Better clear out so they can get the body before it starts stinking.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Cato reportedly on the District 8 female<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tributes">Tributes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the novel, Cato makes two confirmed kills: the District 3 male<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and Thresh.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, he makes four additional kills during the bloodbath: the District 4 male, the District 4 female, the District 5 male, and the District 6 male.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Methods">Methods</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Cato uses brute strength to kill the District 3 male. In the novel, he grabs the boy in a headlock and jerks his head to the side, killing him instantly.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_16-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, Cato uses a variety of weapons to dispose of his competitors. His first target is the District 6 male, who he beats up and later cuts open with a serrated sword. He injures the District 10 male by using a bar mace on the boy's chest.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Cato refers to several members of the Catones family of the Roman Empire.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Cato survived 18 days in the novel and 9 days in the film.</li>
<li>In the film, Cato is 6'2" and weighs 185 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the film, Cato's odds of winning are 3–1, making him the tribute with the highest odds. He shares these odds with the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> states that Cato killed <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>. However, this is not consistent with the film continuity, as Thresh was killed by the <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">mutts</a> in the first film.</li>
<li>In a deleted scene for the film, Cato mentions during his interview that he has wanted to participate in the Hunger Games since he was a child.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>According to an interview with People magazine for the first movie, Alexander Ludwig shared an experience where he was seen as "too good" at hand-to-hand combat. He was working with a stunt man in a fight scene and hit the guy too hard with a bat, to the point where the stunt person began bleeding. Ludwig waited until they called "cut" before running over, apologizing profusely to the stuntman.<sup><i>[citation needed]</i></sup></li>
<li>NECA made a Cato action figure back in 2012.</li></ul>
