<p><b>Crispus Demigloss</b> was a history professor at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. He was an old man, but was also known to be excitable.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrispus_Demigloss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Before the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, Demigloss hosted a brainstorming session with the 24 <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> students to come up with ideas on how to get more people to watch the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. When <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> said that they should ask the question of whether or not watching was even the right thing to do, he urged the class to stay on topic and scanned the room for a more productive answer.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After that, he listened to <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a>'s suggestion of making viewing mandatory, leading the group to brainstorm ideas as to how this could be enforced. Later, when Festus commented that he got more excited over a dogfight, especially if he was betting on it, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> floated the idea that betting odds could be given to the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The day after <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>'s death, the mentors were scheduled to meet with Demigloss again, although nobody felt like attending. He passed out a mentor sheet complete with the names of the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> participating in the Games, telling the mentors to use it to keep track of their progress.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrispus_Demigloss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>"Crispus" is a reference to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crispus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Crispus">Flavius Julius Crispus</a>, the eldest son of Roman emperor Constantine I.
</p><p>Demi refers to the "half", such as demi-god.
</p><p>"Gloss" is a word referring to the shine of a smooth surface.
</p>
