<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDalton%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During his life in District 10 he had worked on one of the beef ranches, maintaining the genetic diversity of the herd with the implantation of long-frozen cow embryos.
</p><p>He suggests to Katniss that District 13 needs all the refugees they can get because, ever since a pox epidemic killed many of their own and left even more infertile, they have needed a new genetic gene pool to draw from in order to have children and make their population more sustainable.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Dalton was part of a small group of District 13 people who were invited to a meeting by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> to brainstorm ideas to help the propos for the <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>. It was apparent Katniss could not act well and the idea was suggested that she go to <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> to visit an area that was bombed and that the footage be used for a promo/message to <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>. At this meeting Dalton suggests that they should "wash her face," and goes on to say, "She's still a girl and you make her look thirty-five. Feels wrong. Like something <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> would do." Katniss later mentions that she is glad she washed her face because people recognize her with her imperfections, not the makeup.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Dalton was a participant in <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>'s and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_Wedding" class="mw-redirect" title="Finnick and Annie's Wedding">wedding</a>. He held the marriage ritual because it was similar to District 10's own wedding ceremonies.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
