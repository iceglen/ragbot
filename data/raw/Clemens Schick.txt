</p><p><b>Clemens Schick</b> is a German actor and model who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Nero_Price" title="Nero Price">Nero Price</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> film.
</p>
