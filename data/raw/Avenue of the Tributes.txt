In the films, the <b>Avenue of the Tributes</b> is a major road in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> sits at one end, while a large governmental building sits at the other.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvenue_of_the_Tributes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On the second day leading up to the annual <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> rode chariots down the Avenue in specially-made <a href="/wiki/Chariot_Costumes" class="mw-redirect" title="Chariot Costumes">costumes</a> inspired by their <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>' industries. The procession ended when the chariots pulled up in front of a large government building, where <a href="/wiki/President_Coriolanus_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Coriolanus Snow">President Coriolanus Snow</a> gave a welcoming speech.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, the <a href="/wiki/Execution_of_President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="Execution of President Snow">execution of President Snow</a> was held on the Avenue of the Tributes. This also made it the site of President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>'s assassination by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvenue_of_the_Tributes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the novels, the Training Center and the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">president's mansion</a> were both located in the City Circle.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, in the films, the Avenue and City Circle appear to be two different locations, placing the Training Center and the president's mansion in different parts of the city.</li></ul>
