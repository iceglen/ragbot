</p><p><b>Justin Hix</b> is an American actor who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 male (75th HG)">male morphling</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, his major motion picture debut. He has also been featured in AMC's <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walking_Dead_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Walking Dead (TV series)">The Walking Dead</a></i> as a recurring zombie.
