<p><b>Eddy's sister</b> and her younger brother, <a href="/wiki/Eddy" title="Eddy">Eddy</a>, were <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> that were injured in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_8" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 8">bombing of District 8</a>. It is unknown if Eddy and his sister had any other living family.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEddy%2527s_sister%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Eddy and his sister were both injured when <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> bombed their district. She ended up in the <a href="/wiki/District_8_hospital" title="District 8 hospital">hospital</a> with an injured leg, and her brother was located a few beds down from her. When <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> came to District 8 to filming <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a>, Eddy's sister was the first person to recognize her in the hospital. She was amazed to see Katniss and excitedly yelled to her brother that Katniss was there.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEddy%2527s_sister%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Right after Katniss left the hospital, the Capitol initiated a second air strike on District 8, this time targeting the hospital. Eddy and his sister were among the deceased, since there were no survivors.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
