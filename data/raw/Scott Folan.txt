</p><p><b>Scott Folan</b> is an actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Junius" title="Junius">Junius</a>, better known as Beanpole, in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Folan's credits include the Comedy Central sitcom <i>Brotherhood</i> and the feature film <i>Nativity 2</i>. He is also a singer-songwriter whose music is available to stream on all major music platforms. He is represented by Denton Brierley and Unbreakable Entertainment.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
