<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRemake_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Remake Center was at least two stories tall, with the upper floor(s) being dedicated to prep rooms and sitting rooms for the tributes. Each tribute was sat down on a table in a private room with cold white walls and floors, where they would then sit through several hours of prep.
</p><p>Just off of the room where the tribute was prepped, there was a sitting room with three blank walls and a window taking up the entire fourth wall, which provided a view of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> outside. Two red couches faced each other with a coffee table in between. The press of a button on this table would split the tabletop, through which a second table would rise with a spread of food for lunch.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The building's ground floor was "essentially a gigantic stable". There, each tribute boarded one of twelve horse-drawn chariots alongside their <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRemake_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the tributes wore hospital gowns and lay on hard metal tables similar to operating tables. Rather than each getting their own private room for the first and most invasive part of prep, they were only separated from the other tributes by curtains, like in an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/intensive_care_unit" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:intensive care unit">intensive care unit</a>. They would be taken to a private room afterwards to await their stylist, but without any of the luxuries described in the book.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRemake_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The day after the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> met her prep team and her stylist <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> for the first time in the Remake Center.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Once all the tributes were dressed in their <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">chariot costumes</a> and taken to the ground floor, each district pair kept to themselves by their chariots.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
