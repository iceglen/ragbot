<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHolo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2><p>
The holograph installed in the Holos was obtained by a rebel and handed over to <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> when they left the Capitol. Each commander in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> was issued a Holo to aid in their infiltration of the Capitol, including <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>, who led <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, after Boggs was mortally wounded by a <a This bypassed the squad's second-in-command, <a href="/wiki/Jackson" title="Jackson">Jackson</a>, and effectively made Katniss the new squad leader. Afterward, Jackson helped Katniss operate the Holo<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and taught her the basic commands needed to operate it.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While keeping watch in the <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>, Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> used the Holo to investigate nearby pods.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Despite attempting to find a different route, they were soon pursued by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a>, and Katniss detonated the Holo into the remaining mutts after <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHolo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A Holo is used to produce a holographic image of the Capitol, with pods represented by flashing lights<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> both above and belowground. This does not show pods the rebels were unaware of. The most basic commands consist of entering the coordinates of the nearest map grid intersections.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It cannot send nor receive signals.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A Holo also has fingerprint recognition and a password for additional security. When Boggs transferred the Holo's prime security clearance to Katniss, a green shaft of light burst from the device and flickered over Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>A Holo can only be activated by a specific commander's voice. Once on, it responds to the other voices from the squadron so someone else can take over if the commander is killed. If anyone in the squadron flicks the switch and says "<a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a>" three times in a row, the Holo explodes everything in a five-yard radius, which serves the purpose of killing them if they get caught and preventing the Holo from falling into enemy hands.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
