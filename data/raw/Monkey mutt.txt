<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMonkey_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0f/FinnickandKatnissArenaCF.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131116173710" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss and Finnick fighting the mutts." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0f/FinnickandKatnissArenaCF.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131116173710" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="136" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="FinnickandKatnissArenaCF.jpg" data-image-key="FinnickandKatnissArenaCF.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:FinnickandKatnissArenaCF.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss and Finnick during the monkey fight.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Toxic <a href="/wiki/Fog" title="Fog">fog</a> dissipated around three o'clock in the morning on the second day of the Games. Soon after, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> spotted two monkeys and pointed them out to his <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">allies</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>. Unlike most mutts, the monkeys did not immediately attack them, and the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> watched the monkeys for a while before retreating to the beach, where they purged the poison by soaking in salt water. When they returned to the jungle to tap a tree for water, Katniss realized that the monkeys had assembled in greater numbers. She tried not to alert Peeta of their presence because she knew eye contact was a sign of aggression, but he realized they were there and looked up.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0c/DF63.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140125161317" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The female morphling sacrificing herself for Peeta." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0c/DF63.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140125161317" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="DF63.jpg" data-image-key="DF63.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:DF63.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The female morphling sacrificing herself for Peeta.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>At this point, it became clear that the monkeys were mutts due to their peculiar adaptations. The tributes held them off, but since there were so many, Katniss ran out of <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">arrows</a>. She called to Peeta, who had another sheath for her, but one mutt took the opportunity to lunge at his chest. Katniss threw a <a href="/wiki/Knife" class="mw-redirect" title="Knife">knife</a> and missed, so she ran towards him to intervene, but the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 female (75th HG)">District 6 female</a> got there first, and the monkey sank its fangs into her chest instead.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/MonkeyMutt.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20251119134157" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="MonkeyMutt" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="191" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="MonkeyMutt.jpeg" data-image-key="MonkeyMutt.jpeg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/MonkeyMutt.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251119134157" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/MonkeyMutt.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20251119134157" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="MonkeyMutt" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/MonkeyMutt.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251119134157" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="191" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="MonkeyMutt.jpeg" data-image-key="MonkeyMutt.jpeg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/MonkeyMutt.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251119134157" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:MonkeyMutt.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>Peeta killed the monkey that had attacked the female <a href="/wiki/Morphlings" title="Morphlings">morphling</a> and readied himself to fight off more, but the mutts withdrew into the jungle; Katniss figured that the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> had called them off. Peeta then carried the District 6 female onto the beach, laid her down on the sand, and talked to her about painting— something she had shown interest in during <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">training</a>— until she died.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Katniss realized that the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a> was a clock, with a new threat in a particular section every hour. The monkeys were triggered at three o'clock<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and left at four.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, the female morphling was bitten in the neck instead of her chest. Rather than staying on the sand of the beach, Peeta took her out into the water and told her to focus on the colors of the sky, giving her a sense of peace as she bled to death in his arms.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><br />

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMonkey_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The mutts were tree-climbing monkeys with orange fur, about half the size of an adult human. They had sharp fangs, as well as claws that came out like switchblades. They were also incredibly fast, and they could jump unnaturally far. Katniss described fighting "scores" of them, indicating that the mutts were in a large troop.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>In the film, the mutts had the body of a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drill_(animal)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Drill (animal)">drill</a> and the coloration of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/mandrill" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:mandrill">mandrills</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMonkey_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
