</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the world was devastated by natural disasters and war, the nation of Panem was established in North America. It consisted of thirteen districts, with its capital and seat of power, the Capitol,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> situated in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mountains" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rocky Mountains">Rocky Mountains</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the Capitol's version of events, they brought "peace and prosperity" to the districts,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so the true circumstances that led to the rebellion are not told.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="War">War</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Students at the Capitol's <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> were taught that District 13 had instigated the conflict, disseminating arms and communications to rebels throughout the other districts.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Capitol_air_raids">Capitol air raids</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>A few months into the war, the first major rebel air raid on the Capitol immobilized the city for several days.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Every citizen was assigned a bomb shelter near their residence. However, the Capitol's surveillance system relied on electricity, and rebel interference in <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> caused frequent blackouts. Under these circumstances, air raid sirens were unreliable, so citizens were often caught off guard and forced to shelter in place.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Retaliation_by_the_Capitol">Retaliation by the Capitol</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The Capitol staged high-profile <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executions</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, making it a prime target for rebel bombers.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Muttations">Muttations</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">Muttation</a></i></center></div>
<p>Alongside regular military units, the Capitol used genetically-modified muttations against the rebels. <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">Tracker jacker</a> nests were strategically placed around the districts like land mines, but could be sedated by smoke.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In Districts <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> were used to spy on rebels, but they were countered by simply feeding them false information.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Rebel_siege">Rebel siege</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Rebels were able to sustain the fight on their end with support from District 13's military arsenal. For the last two years of the conflict, the Capitol was under siege, entirely cut off from outside supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As a result of this, food supplies ran low, forcing desperate families to sell their goods and furniture for rations, or else face starvation. In extreme cases, some Capitol citizens resorted to cannibalism.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Failed_rebel_invasion">Failed rebel invasion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<p>Eventually, the rebels attempted to break the siege by scaling the Rockies and invading the Capitol itself. The terrain left them exposed to Capitol aircraft, likely resulting in heavy rebel casualties. This turned the tide of the war in favor of the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Ceasefire_deal">Ceasefire deal</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">"<i>After all, it was 13 that started the rebellion that led to the Dark Days, and then abandoned the rest of the districts when the tide turned against it.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Realizing that the rebels had lost their advantage, District 13 abandoned the other twelve districts in order to secure its own independence.  13's forces wrested control of the Capitol's <a href="/wiki/Nuclear_weaponry" title="Nuclear weaponry">nuclear missiles</a> and trained them on the city, threatening <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/mutually_assured_destruction" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:mutually assured destruction">mutually assured destruction</a>.
</p><p>Unbeknownst to most of Panem's residents, District 13 set up a secret ceasefire deal with the Capitol in which they would withdraw their forces from the war and present the illusion that the Capitol had destroyed District 13. The Capitol reluctantly agreed to the terms. They allowed District 13 to withdraw from the war and retreat to their existing underground facilities, before demolishing the infrastructure above through repeated carpet bombing.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Capitol then used the ruins in propaganda as a warning or threat to the other districts. Without the support of District 13's military, rebel forces in the other districts were overrun and reunified under the firm grip of the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Treaty_of_Treason">Treaty of Treason</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a></i></center></div>

<p>Following the collapse of the rebellion, the Treaty of Treason introduced new laws to subjugate the remaining districts. It instituted the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> as an annual reminder of the failed rebellion, keeping the districts divided and suppressed so they couldn't rise up against the Capitol again.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13">District 13</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Cut off from the other districts, District 13 struggled to survive and nearly collapsed several times. Despite this, it fought to become self-sufficient and rebuilt its military forces and arsenal over the next seventy-five years— all while remaining underground.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This eventually allowed them to stage the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDark_Days%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The Dark Days lasted for 3 years, as Snow was 5 years of age when the first year the war broke out, and was 8 years old when the Hunger Games officially began.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>When the jabberjay project failed, the Capitol abandoned the birds, releasing them into the wild and inadvertently creating <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>, which ironically became the symbol of the Second Rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The outcomes of both rebellions were decided through air raids conducted in the Rocky Mountains.
<ul><li>In the Dark Days, the Capitol conducted a massive air raid on rebel forces scaling the mountains, thwarting an invasion and winning them the war.</li>
<li>During the Second Rebellion, the rebels conducted an air raid on <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>. This disabled the Capitol's air force, leading to a rebel victory.</li></ul></li></ul>
