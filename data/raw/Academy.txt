<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Administration">Administration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Dean <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> oversaw the school. The Academy provided students with free meals and uniforms for daily use, though they were required to finance their own clothing for special events.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Uniform">Uniform</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Academy-issued uniform was not described in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> novel, but it was noted as including underwear and socks.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">film adaptation</a>, uniforms consisted of a light blue dress shirt with a mandarin collar and covered placket, a red lapelless blazer in a shade that <a href="/wiki/Lucky_Flickerman" class="mw-redirect" title="Lucky Flickerman">Lucky Flickerman</a> identified as "Academy rouge", and a golden pin of the Academy's logo. Regardless of gender, all students wore red skirts over their matching red trousers with a slit on each side, presumably for ease of pocket access.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Academic_standing">Academic standing</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At graduation in midsummer, the Academy presented monetary prizes to high-performing students. Those students who performed well at the Academy often went on to attend higher education at the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Mentor_program">Mentor program</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 225px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/10th_Hunger_Games_Mentors.png/revision/latest?cb=20231004222640" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The 10th Hunger Games mentors." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/10th_Hunger_Games_Mentors.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/225?cb=20231004222640" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="225" height="83" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="10th Hunger Games Mentors.png" data-image-key="10th_Hunger_Games_Mentors.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:10th_Hunger_Games_Mentors.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The 10th Hunger Games mentors.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the Academy's top 24 students were selected to <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from the twelve <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>. This was used as a means to make citizens of the Capitol more interested in the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, the program resulted in the deaths of four students, so it was discontinued once the Games concluded that year. The <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a>, Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, considered it a catastrophic and embarrassing failure.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Disciplinary_action">Disciplinary action</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAcademy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Students at the Academy who committed serious infractions might receive a demerit. Three such demerits would result in a student's expulsion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Academy had a tradition of offering expelled students a lifeline. For <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, this came in the form of an offer to join the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> for a twenty-year-term, as an alternative to public disgrace.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, after being expelled, Snow received a diploma indicating he had graduated with high honors, which <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> made a condition of accepting his own offer to join the Peacekeepers after he was expelled as well. Having an Academy diploma allowed Snow to take the Peacekeeper officer test<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and later to enroll at the University after Dr. Gaul arranged for his honorable discharge from the Peacekeepers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>The Academy had a gathering area called <a href="/wiki/Heavensbee_Hall" title="Heavensbee Hall">Heavensbee Hall</a>, which hosted events such as viewing the districts' <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reapings</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The high biology lab hosted the mentors' meetings with Dr. Gaul,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and there were at least fourteen other classrooms, since they were able to accommodate 14 different mentor-tribute pairs as they prepared for their <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
