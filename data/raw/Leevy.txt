<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeevy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A year before <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i> took place, <a href="/wiki/Leevy%27s_brother" title="Leevy's brother">Leevy's brother</a> caught the measles, and <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> kept him alive.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> was whipped by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>, Leevy took Katniss's arm and asked her if she needed help. Katniss asked her to fetch Gale's mother, <a href="/wiki/Hazelle_Hawthorne" title="Hazelle Hawthorne">Hazelle Hawthorne</a>, and not to let Hazelle bring Gale's siblings. Leevy replied that she would watch the children herself.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Leevy survived the bombing of District 12 and was evacuated to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, where she was given automatic citizenship alongside every other refugee.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is unknown whether or not she had any family who survived the bombing, since her little brother was not mentioned in <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>.
</p><p>Leevy cautiously greeted Katniss's <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> when they joined a group from the Seam for lunch in the dining hall.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was also present for a meeting in Command, where <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> asked a group of people to tell him times when Katniss had moved them emotionally. Leevy mentioned how Katniss had <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to take <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a>'s place at the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, despite facing near certain death.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
