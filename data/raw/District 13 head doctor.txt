<p>The <b>District 13 head doctor</b> is a doctor who specialized in mental health care in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_13_head_doctor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>This doctor spent his whole life in District 13.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Following the <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">rebels</a>' extraction of captured <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, he took on <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> as a client while she was recovering from her torture. He came by for daily sessions to help her recover, but in Johanna's estimation, he was a "complete idiot" who reminded her "at least twenty times a session" that she was "totally safe."<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Because of his <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> was only treated by District 13 natives,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so this doctor may have been a part of his recovery team as well.
</p><p>When Johanna sought discharge from the hospital, she was denied the opportunity to live alone even if she kept up daily sessions with her head doctor. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> attributed this to the possibility that they may have caught onto Johanna's <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> addiction and deemed her "unstable". With convincing from Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, Johanna was approved to live in a compartment with Katniss soon after.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>According to Johanna, her head doctor was right about one thing: "There's no going back" to the people they were before the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. She also quoted him as warning her against censoring her thoughts, as a part of her therapy.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
