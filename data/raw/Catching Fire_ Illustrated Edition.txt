</p><p><i><b>Catching Fire: Illustrated Edition</b></i> is an edition of <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i> illustrated by artist <a href="/wiki/Nico_Delort" title="Nico Delort">Nico Delort</a>. It released on October 7, 2025.
</p><p>Like <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Illustrated_Edition" title="The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition">The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition</a></i>, it contains over 30 black-and-white scratchboard drawings, with each part opener integrating a different plant.
</p>
