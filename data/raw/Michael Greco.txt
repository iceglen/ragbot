</p><p><b>Michael Greco</b> is an English actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMichael_Greco%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 2023, Greco portrayed Pothinus in <i>African Queens</i>, directed by Jada Pinkett-Smith. He also played Bill Staton in the Emmy and Golden Globe-winning <i>Hatfields &amp; McCoys</i>. Other feature film credits include <i>Misfire</i>, <i>Revenge</i>, and <i>Gone</i>. Greco's most notable British TV credits include <i>Eastenders</i>, <i>Doctors</i>, and <i>Murder in Mind</i>, all for BBC, as well as ITV's <i>Chasing Shadows</i>.
</p><p>Greco is represented by Mark Jermin Management.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
