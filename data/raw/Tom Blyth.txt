</p><p><b>Tom Blyth</b> is an English actor and producer who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTom_Blyth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Blyth's most notable roles include the films <i>Robin Hood</i> (2010), <i>Benediction</i> (2021), <i>The Gilded Age</i> (2022), and <i>Billy the Kid</i> (2022).<sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On May 16, 2022, <i>People</i> reported that Blyth had been cast in the role of Coriolanus Snow. Regarding the casting, producer Nina Jacobson stated "Tom's performance will both fulfill and disrupt everything you think you know about Coriolanus Snow. He's a tremendous talent who will show why Snow always lands on top."<sup id="cite_ref-Tom_Blyth_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tom_Blyth-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTom_Blyth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Before <a href="/wiki/Rachel_Zegler" title="Rachel Zegler">Rachel Zegler</a> was officially cast as <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, director <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a> had her do a chemistry read with Blyth over Zoom, and Lawrence asked her to sing a folk song called "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildwood_Flower" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Wildwood Flower">Wildwood Flower</a>". Blyth was moved to tears by the performance.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Tom Blyth attended Juilliard with <a href="/wiki/Maya_Hawke" title="Maya Hawke">Maya Hawke</a> for a little bit and first met her at auditions for Juilliard. Hawke would later end up being cast as young <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
