<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWhitney_Peak%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Whitney  Peak was born the youngest of four siblings in Kampala, Uganda, to a Ugandan mother, a hairdresser, and a Canadian father, a helicopter pilot and engineer. She went to boarding school, and swam competitively. Her family spent time between Canada and Dubai, where her father worked, and where she was coached by a Sudanese former Olympian. After moving to Vancouver, British Columbia, at the age of nine, Peak got into dance, starting with acrobatics, and wanted to be on television. She also transitioned to a public school. She was in love with fashion and thought she would become a doctor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWhitney_Peak%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_Beginnings">Career Beginnings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWhitney_Peak%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Upon hearing a commercial on the radio for a <i>Disney Channel</i> casting call, she was able to get into acting. Her first acting part was in Aaron Sorkin's <i>Molly's Game</i>. She later became a series regular on the <i>Netflix</i> original series, <i>The Chilling Adventures of Sabrina</i>.
</p><p>In 2021 Whitney booked the lead role of Zoya Lott on the HBO Max revival of <i>Gossip Girl</i>, which required her to fly from Canada to the United States. She lived alone at the age of eighteen in the height of the COVID-19 pandemic, where she began filming the series with her thirteen co-stars in a secluded area of New York. She was still in high school at the time of filming, so production assigned her a teacher that would help her graduate. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Upcoming_Projects">Upcoming Projects</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWhitney_Peak%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Whitney will next appear in the crime drama Trap House, releasing on November 14, 2025, followed by the heist comedy 4 Kids and a Bank on April 17, 2026. She will then star in the survival thriller Shiver, set for release on July 3, 2026, before taking on the role of Lenore Dove Baird in <i>The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</i>, the sixth installment of the franchise, arriving in theaters on November 20, 2026.
</p>
