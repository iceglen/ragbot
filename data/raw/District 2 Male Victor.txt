<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_victor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Years after his initial victory, <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> announced that <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> would be <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> from the existing pool of victors, forcing them to compete in the Games regardless of age. As a result, this victor was present at <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>'s reaping for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is unknown whether or not this man was reaped, but <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> ultimately <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to participate.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_2_male_victor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the end of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, he was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so he presumably died in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors' Purge">Victors' Purge</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
