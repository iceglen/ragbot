<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_11%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_11%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Even by the standards of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s other outer <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>, District 11 was heavily impoverished, policed, and repressed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Despite its industry being agriculture,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> nearly all of their produce was shipped off to serve <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s overwhelming greed. The local government was very strict, and its <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> were known to carry out public whippings<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executions</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Pre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest"><a href="/wiki/Pre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest" title="Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest">Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_11%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Uprising_of_District_11">Uprising of District 11</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_11%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_11" title="Uprising of District 11">Uprising of District 11</a></i></center></div>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, District 11 citizens staged an uprising after the death of <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, having been inspired by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s compassion towards their young <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the book, there was no overt rebel action taken, but they sent a <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gift</a> of <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> to Katniss as a symbol of gratitude.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-74th_Hunger_Games">Post-74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_11%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>When Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> visited the district on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, an <a href="/wiki/Unidentified_District_11_citizen" title="Unidentified District 11 citizen">elderly man</a> whistled <a href="/wiki/Rue%27s_four-note_song" title="Rue&#39;s four-note song">Rue's four-note song</a>, signaling the rest of the crowd to perform <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a>. As Katniss and Peeta left the stage, this man was shot dead,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> with several more gunshots following after once the two <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> were inside the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the day before the reading of the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> card, there were rumors of uprisings in <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">Districts 7</a> and 11.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_11%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></i></center></div>
<p>Shortly after the end of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, District 11 had seized control of transportation.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Several weeks later, the airing of <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a> about the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_8_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">hospital bombing</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> boosted morale in the districts, allowing rebels to take <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">Districts 3</a> and 11 and "[make] inroads in several other districts as well".<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
