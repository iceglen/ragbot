</p><p>The <b>District 12 music teacher</b> was a woman who taught music at the <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_12_music_teacher%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s first day of school at age five, their music teacher asked if anyone knew <a href="/wiki/The_Valley_Song" class="mw-redirect" title="The Valley Song">the valley song</a>. Katniss's hand shot up, so the teacher stood her up on a stool and had her sing it for the class. When Katniss finished the song, Peeta realized he had a crush on her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point in the next eleven years, the teacher told her students about <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_(music)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Air (music)">mountain airs</a>. As Katniss thought of a song to sing to <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> as she lay dying in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, she thought that her teacher would use that term to describe "<a href="/wiki/Deep_in_the_Meadow" class="mw-redirect" title="Deep in the Meadow">Deep in the Meadow</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The teacher was briefly mentioned again when Peeta recounted the music assembly story to Katniss during the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
