</p><p><b>Mayor Allister</b> was the <a href="/wiki/Mayor" title="Mayor">mayor</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Allister%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Clerk_Carmine_Clade" title="Clerk Carmine Clade">Clerk Carmine Clade</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> worked out a deal with Mayor Allister that <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a> would be able to practice music on the piano in Mayor Allister's house, provided that she occasionally performed at dinners and gatherings.
</p><p>At the start of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 50th Hunger Games, Mayor Allister tried to neutrally read the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a>, but a hint of disapproval was evident. As such, she was likely to be replaced soon after. She was considered a decent mayor by the people of District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
