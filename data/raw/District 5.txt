<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Dark_Days"><a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Rebel interference in District 5 caused frequent blackouts in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. The city's surveillance system ran on electricity, so these measures rendered air raid sirens unreliable, catching citizens off guard and forcing them to shelter in place.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 5 was the only non-<a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> district that chose not to partner with the newly formed alliance known as <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a> during the Second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, instead forming an alliance with the Careers.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Two of the tributes died in the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the other two died in the volcanic eruption on the fourth day.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion"><a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 5 rebels bombed a hydroelectric dam, causing a massive power outage in the Capitol. This weakened their ability to broadcast <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propaganda</a> and allowed rebel forces to infiltrate the city and rescue three captive <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 5 is located directly southwest of the Capitol, primarily centered in the state of Arizona and southern Utah. This geographical location makes sense, as it would have to be located in close proximity to meet the power needs of the city. Being located around the Colorado River, which in the modern day is dammed by a number of hydroelectric dams, this directly ties in to its industry of power generation (such as the dam destroyed during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>) and further supports its location. This also explains how District 12, which lies in the Appalachian Mountains in eastern Panem, does not have enough electricity to continually light their homes or power the electric fence which surrounds the district. District 5 borders <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> (to the northeast), <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> (to the north), <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a> (to the northwest), <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> (to the east), and <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> (to the southeast).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The district's industry, along with <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a>, are the last ones to be revealed. Nowhere in the original trilogy books suggest that District 5 provides power and utilities to the nation, unlike <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> being the grain district. They are only revealed at the time the film adaptation of <i>The Hunger Games</i> has been announced. The hydroelectric dam attack in District 5 is a new addition to the Second Rebellion.</li>
<li>Districts <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, 5, <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, and 10 were not involved in the rebel plot during the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>, District 5, and <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> are the only districts without a known rebel.</li></ul></li>
<li>District 5 is the only non-Career district who allied with the Careers rather than <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a> during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. These four tributes fell victim to the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a> and a volcano eruption.
<ul><li>This makes District 5 the only non-Career district to have never been involved in an in-game rebel plot, whether directly or indirectly, within the entire continuity.</li></ul></li>
<li>In the last two Hunger Games, both males were killed in the bloodbath<sup id="cite_ref-:1_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but both females survived, only to die later to one of the arena's dangers— the opposite of District 10.</li>
<li>Sol from the 10th Hunger Games<sup id="cite_ref-:0_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the male from the 75th<sup id="cite_ref-:2_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> were both killed by a District 4 tribute with a trident.</li>
<li>A solar power plant, Coriolanus 9, is located in the heart of District 5, named after <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>District 5 is possibly one of the wealthier districts, such as District 1 or <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> due to the low amount of residents who take <a href="/wiki/Tesserae" class="mw-redirect" title="Tesserae">tesserae</a>. This may also attribute to the theory of District 5 not being as impoverished as outlying districts (<a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>).</li>
<li>District 5 was the 5th - 10th district to pry from Capitol rule.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 5 has 15 power plants in operation with a population of 134,345, making it the fifth largest district, in terms of population.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 5 claims 2,881 in tesserae, making it the third richest district. It is, in fact, richer than <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, making it the only non-Career district to be wealthier than a Career district and the wealthiest non-Career district.
<ul><li>If the population is 134,345 and they claim 2,881 tesserae, it would equal to 0.021 tesserae per person, making it the 2nd richest district.</li>
<li>This would also make District 5 wealthier than District 1, another Career district, in terms of tesserae per capita.</li></ul></li>
<li>It is considered a wealthy district.</li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the district's color seems to be ripe eggplant brown (#492d35 – <span style="background-color:#492d35; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 5's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be slate purple (#6A5ACD- <span style="background-color:#6A5ACD; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear for the 50th Hunger Games was orange (FF6700 - <span style="background-color:#FF6700; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
