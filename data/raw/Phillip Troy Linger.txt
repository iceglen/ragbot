</p><p><b>Phillip Troy Linger</b> is an American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Mr. Everdeen</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.
</p><p>His part in the movie was limited, as he only appeared in a flashback that occurred when <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> was hallucinating under the effects of <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> stings, after dropping a nest of the <a href="/wiki/Muttations" class="mw-redirect" title="Muttations">muttations</a> on top of the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a>' camp. However, his picture also appears in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">Part 2</a></i> after Katniss retrieves it from her home in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.
</p>
