<p>This type of <b>fog</b> was a poisonous, corrosive vapor released by the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a> in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a> as one of the hourly horrors. It came between two and three and spread throughout the jungle. It resembled an "after-rain fog", had a sickeningly sweet odor and advanced in a straight line,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> growing and becoming thick and acrid. It killed muscles, made its victims twitch, and could lead to death. The fog was made by the Gamemakers of the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFog%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 258px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/38/Katniss_looking_at_fog.png/revision/latest?cb=20170522014520" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss and the fog" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/38/Katniss_looking_at_fog.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/258?cb=20170522014520" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="258" height="124" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss looking at fog.png" data-image-key="Katniss_looking_at_fog.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_looking_at_fog.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss and the fog</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The effects of the fog included itchy scabs, convulsions, painful pustules, and rapidly atrophying muscular tissue and sometimes death. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> recounted that when she was helping <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> escape, the fog had reached one side of his face, causing the muscles on that side to collapse, shudder, and die.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Treatment">Treatment</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFog%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> gave Katniss a thick, tar-like ointment to treat her scabs and scars, which she found very soothing although it turned her skin a sickly green color while it healed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> gave Peeta quite a scare while their skin was green. Soaking in salt water also relieved the effects of the fog, though this was a very painful process. Using this method required that the affected enter the water bit by bit, or else they risked intense agony.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFog%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 264px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Fleeing_from_fog.png/revision/latest?cb=20240620070945" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss, Finnick, Mags, and Peeta fleeing from the fog." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Fleeing_from_fog.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/264?cb=20240620070945" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="264" height="110" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Fleeing from fog.png" data-image-key="Fleeing_from_fog.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Fleeing_from_fog.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss, Finnick, Mags, and Peeta fleeing from the fog.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>, the oldest <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in the 75th Hunger Games, was the only known tribute to die from the fog. As the fog approached, she realized that <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> would die unless <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a> carried him. Since Finnick was unable to carry both Peeta and Mags, she sacrificed herself. Up until that moment, Finnick had carried her everywhere, but she realized that Peeta would die if they continued with this. She got off Finnick's back, kissed him good-bye, and walked into the fog. Her death was described by Katniss as being like a dance, which demonstrated the fog's power of muscle manipulation. Mags was noted and respected by Katniss Everdeen for her actions. Peeta, Finnick, and Katniss later suffered severe muscle damage by stray droplets of the fog.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
