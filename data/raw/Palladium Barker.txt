</p><p><b>Palladium Barker</b> was the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of the <a href="/wiki/46th_Hunger_Games" title="46th Hunger Games">46th Hunger Games</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>. They were related to <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, one of the two male District 1 tributes in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Presumably, Palladium is either Panache's cousin or sibling, due to the close proximity in age between them and Panache.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPalladium_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="46th_Hunger_Games">46th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPalladium_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Palladium was presumably a <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a> in the 46th Hunger Games and likely  <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to participate.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Post_Games">Post Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPalladium_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>It is likely that Palladium mentored their relative Panache in the 50th Hunger Games.
</p><p>As District 1 victors are known to continue training even after they win<sup id="cite_ref-Cashmere_CC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cashmere_CC-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, it is likely that Palladium continued their training.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPalladium_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Although the cause and time of death are unknown, Palladium was dead by the end of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, as they were absent for the vote on a final <a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">76th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It's possible they were a victim of the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPalladium_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palladium" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Palladium">Palladium</a> is a silvery-white metal and chemical element with the symbol Pd and atomic number 46. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barker_(surname)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Barker (surname)">Barker</a> is an English surname meaning "a tanner of leather".
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Gender">Gender</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPalladium_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
