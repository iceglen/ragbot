<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaximinius_Ravinstill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Presidency">Presidency</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaximinius_Ravinstill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Ravinstill has no known predecessors, and it is unknown how he came into office because no political <a href="/wiki/Elections" title="Elections">elections</a> are ever mentioned. The duration of his time in office is also unknown. However, he had been president "long before" the war.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Dark_Days">Dark Days</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaximinius_Ravinstill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>President Ravinstill ruled over Panem for the duration of the war. After three years of fighting,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> threatened <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> with mutually assured destruction using their nuclear arsenal. They demanded that they be allowed to secede in exchange for pulling out of the war. Ravinstill presumably negotiated the secret ceasefire deal: District 13 citizens retreated into their existing facilities underground, and the Capitol bombed the surface to make the other <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> believe 13 had been destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Once <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> subdued the remaining rebel forces, President Ravinstill oversaw the implementation of the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a>. This document imposed many restrictions on the defeated districts, including the creation of the annual Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaximinius_Ravinstill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Ravinstill's great-nephew, <a href="/wiki/Felix_Ravinstill" title="Felix Ravinstill">Felix Ravinstill</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> was one of 24 <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> students chosen to be <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a> in the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> On the day of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>, the president appeared on television and recited a brief passage from the Treaty of Treason.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Days later, Ravinstill attended the funeral of <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>, where he was seated in the front row. After <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> sang the <a href="/wiki/Gem_of_Panem" title="Gem of Panem">national anthem</a>, Ravinstill gave him an approving nod. Ravinstill delivered a eulogy soon after, claiming that Arachne had died valiantly and they would honor her sacrifice. This eulogy marked the beginning of a grisly funeral procession.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death_and_successors">Death and successors</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaximinius_Ravinstill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Due to his age, Ravinstill presumably died long before the time of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>, but the circumstances of his death are unknown. The presidential line of succession is unclear, so his immediate successor is also unknown. However, he was eventually succeeded by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaximinius_Ravinstill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>By the time of the 10th Hunger Games, his hair was streaked with silver. He wore his prewar military uniform as a reminder that he had been in control of Panem long before the rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
