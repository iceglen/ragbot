<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade">Tribute Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJason%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, he was dressed in a flowing costume that resembled a moon. Also, a crescent moon-shaped head piece that curved around his face was worn by both him and his <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">district partner</a>. It may have been based on space shuttles and space travel, because of the moon theme.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJason%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the film, he was first seen practicing hand-to-hand combat with the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a>. Later, he got into a fight with <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, who thought he took his knife, and Cato threatened to target him in the arena. However, it was actually <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> who stole the knife and was hiding in the net on the ceiling, much to <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a>' amusement.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Jason was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He stood on the pedestal next to the <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">District 7 female</a>. When the gong rang, he ran straight towards the left as <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> stabbed the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a>. After arriving at the Cornucopia, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> targeted him, beat him up and left him to die, but he was able to get up. He then got into a physical altercation with <a href="/wiki/Stele" title="Stele">Stele</a>. Afterwards, he fought the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a> over a backpack, but was elbowed to the ground. While Glimmer killed his district partner near a crate, he looked for supplies. He then attacked the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a>, pinned him onto a crate and strangled him. The District 5 male gained an advantage and hurled his head into a crate. He grabbed a sword and attempted to kill <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>, but Cato tripped him, grabbed a serrated sword and slashed his stomach twice, then finished him off with a stab.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJason%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>His name is unspecified in the books, and is revealed in the movies during the training scene.</li>
<li>Jason is the name of a Greek hero and leader of the Argonauts, who sought the Golden Fleece. This might relate to he and his district partner's costumes.</li>
<li>In the film, his odds of winning were 24–1, he was 16 years old, and he weighed 150 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, he wears a tomato red jacket.</li>
<li>In the film, he was the 10th dead, but when the <a href="/wiki/District_8_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 female (74th HG)">District 8 female</a> was killed, the scoreboard behind <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar</a> states that he was the 4th dead.</li>
<li>In the film, his portrait, as well as his district partner, was not explicitly shown during the fallen.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Training_Days" title="The Hunger Games: Training Days">The Hunger Games: Training Days</a></i> board game, he and his district partner, along with <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a>, were not given cards or any recognition.</li></ul>
