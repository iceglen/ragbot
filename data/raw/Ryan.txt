<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRyan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRyan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the start of the Games, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> noticed the sunflower necklace tokens that everyone from Ryan's district had. He instructed Haymitch to get them to join the <a href="/wiki/Newcomers" class="mw-redirect" title="Newcomers">Newcomers</a> as they could <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">use these tokens</a> for their plan to sabotage the arena. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> thought their efficiency and capabilities would make them good allies.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>While training in the shelter booth, <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a> went up to either Ryan or <a href="/wiki/Clayton" title="Clayton">Clayton</a> and offered them a chance to join the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Careers</a> pack but he refused. Panache grabbed one of their tokens and broke it in retaliation. <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> fixed it and Ryan, along with the rest of 9, decided to join the Newcomers alliance.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They weren't very sociable and almost appeared exclusive.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ryan died in the initial bloodbath along with everyone else in his district.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> retrieved two of their tokens.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRyan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The District 9 tributes' <a href="/wiki/Training_outfits" title="Training outfits">training outfits</a> were buttery yellow, which reminded <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> of baby chicks.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Like the rest of his district, Ryan was still healing from sunburn, had calloused hands and lean, muscled arms.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRyan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ryan wasn't very sociable and liked to keep to himself.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRyan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ryan is an Anglicization of the Old Irish name 'Rían'. The exact meaning of this name is uncertain, but it is often explained as meaning 'little king'. However, in this case Ryan was chosen as it sounds similar to rye, a plant grown for its grain.
</p>
