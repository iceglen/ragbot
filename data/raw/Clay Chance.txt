<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClay_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Clay was a member of the Chance family, who were known for their reckless and sometimes rebellious behavior.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Execution">Execution</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClay_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The winter after the <a href="/wiki/46th_Hunger_Games" title="46th Hunger Games">46th Hunger Games</a>, Clay Chance was sentenced to be hanged in the gallows for an unknown crime. The night before his execution, someone filed halfway through the rope, so when they tried to hang him in front of a crowd the next day, the rope snapped. Clay fell to the ground, where he was shot dead by "a dozen <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> bullets".<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClay_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>A witness in town reported that they saw twelve-year-old <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> leaving the square, so she was taken to the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers&#39; base">Peacekeepers' base</a> for questioning. However, Clay's sister <a href="/wiki/Binnie_Chance" title="Binnie Chance">Binnie Chance</a> claimed responsibility. She had been living "on borrowed time for a year" due to a heart condition, so three days after being taken into custody, Binnie died in her cell and Lenore Dove was released.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<li>It is also unknown what the familial relation is between Clay, his sister and <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a>.</li></ul>
