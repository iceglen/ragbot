<p><b>Camouflage</b>&#160;is the practice of concealing oneself from view. <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">Tributes</a> used camouflage during the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> to avoid being seen. The <a href="/wiki/Morphlings" title="Morphlings">morphlings</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> were rather fond of this <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">station</a> in their training sessions, as they spent the whole time there before the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> also used this skill, which he learned by applying skills from frosting cakes at his family bakery.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Advantages_and_disadvantages">Advantages and disadvantages</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCamouflage%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 164px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/91/Perets_como.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120529155457" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta demonstrating his camouflage skills." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/91/Perets_como.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/164?cb=20120529155457" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="164" height="210" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Perets como.jpg" data-image-key="Perets_como.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Perets_como.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peeta demonstrating his camouflage skills.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>This tactic was usually employed by tributes who preferred relying on survival skills over confronting their rivals directly in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. By blending in convincingly with their surroundings, tributes could hide in plain sight. This gave them the privilege of being able to ambush their targets without any warning; it was also ideal for <a href="/wiki/Concealment" class="mw-redirect" title="Concealment">concealing</a> themselves whenever they sustained injuries.
</p><p>The one flaw in this tactical approach lies in the fact that camouflage permits limited to no mobility. A camouflaged tribute who moved could run the risk of shedding their camouflage on accident, especially if it didn't adhere to the tribute's body. They could also find themselves under attack if another tribute observed the moving piece of scenery and chose to investigate.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCamouflage%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Peeta used mud to camouflage himself into the river bank when he was injured, as <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> stabbed his thigh to the bone and Peeta had no way of healing himself.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> also used mud to camouflage her bright orange <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpack</a> with her surroundings.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Careers</a> did not pay much attention to this tactic during training (or the Games), due to their fighting style being limited to direct conflict as opposed to <a href="/wiki/Stealth" class="mw-redirect" title="Stealth">stealth</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCamouflage%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the District 6 tributes spent most of their time at the camouflage station. To Peeta's annoyance, they left a big mess and not a lot of paint behind by the time he had his personal session with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the Games, Katniss mentioned that the skin cream her alliance received would be good camouflage in the jungle.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 female (75th HG)">female morphling</a> suddenly emerged from where she had camouflaged herself into the beach, throwing up her arms in sacrifice to save Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
