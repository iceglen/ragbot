<p><b>Horace</b> was a ten-year-old boy who gave <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> a potato while she was in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHorace%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>At some point, Horace heard of the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> and realized that he wanted to help feed them. He saved a boiled potato from dinner, then went to the zoo with his father, hovering in the crowd until <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> approached him. When Lucy Gray arrived, Horace held the potato out to her and introduced himself. Lucy Gray complimented it and thanked him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
