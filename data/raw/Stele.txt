<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStele%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Stele was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He was stationed on the right of <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a> and on the left of <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> on the pedestals. When the gong rang, he ran towards the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. He first got into a physical altercation with <a href="/wiki/Jason" title="Jason">Jason</a>. Afterwards, he tried to attack the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a> but ended up getting shoved to the ground. Then he saw the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a> attack his <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">district partner</a>, so he grabbed a spear and knocked down the District 5 male. He was about to kill him, but <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> got in the way. He tried to stab Thresh, but Thresh kicked the spear out of his hands and slashed part of his face off, killing him.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStele%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the film, his odds of winning were 5–1, he was 17 years old, and he weighed 110 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, he wears a russet brown jacket.</li>
<li>In the bloodbath, he replaces the <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">District 9 male</a>, making him appear to have two deaths.</li>
<li>According to the death toll behind <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius</a>, it is seen that he died 37 seconds after the Games started, so he placed 19th.</li>
<li>He was the 6th death, but the scoreboard behind Caesar states that he was the 7th death.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage" title="The Hunger Games On Stage">The Hunger Games On Stage</a></i>, his name is <b>Stele</b>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stele_(biology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Stele (biology)">stele</a> is the central core of a plant's root and stem system.</li></ul>
