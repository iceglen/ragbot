<p>The <b>District 10 male</b> was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> who participated in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. He was also the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of an unspecified <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. He appears to have been quite young, so it can be presumed he won somewhere in the 60s.
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_10_male_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 239px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20190106074346" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The District 10 male fighting with Peeta in the water." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="134" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg" data-image-key="PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20190106074346"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20190106074346" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The District 10 male fighting with Peeta in the water." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20190106074346" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="134" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg" data-image-key="PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20190106074346"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:PeetaFightDistrict9Male.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The District 10 male fighting with Peeta in the water.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i> novel, he survived the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but in the film, the District 10 male fought <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> underwater and drowned.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the novel, he was among the dead tributes shown in the sky during the <a href="/wiki/Horn_of_Plenty" title="Horn of Plenty">anthem</a> on the second evening of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He either died to the tidal wave at ten o'clock that morning,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> or a large, unidentified <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a> termed "the beast" that tore its victim apart around six o'clock that evening.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_10_male_%2875th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>He and the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a> from the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> are similar, as they both outlived their female counterpart and were killed by an unknown source. They both also died after a District 1 female tribute was killed.</li>
<li>His portrait in the sky is not seen on any day during the film.</li>
<li>In the film, the <a href="/wiki/District_5_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 female (75th HG)">District 5 female</a> could be heard screaming as she was killed by the arena's tidal wave sector.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She and the District 10 male were the only tributes whose cause of death was unaccounted for that day in the novel, so fans commonly presume that she died to the wave in the novel as well. This would make the District 10 male the victim of the beast.</li></ul>
