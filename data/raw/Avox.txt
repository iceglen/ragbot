They spent their lives as the lowest class in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, permanently serving <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> as slaves.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Work">Work</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvox%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Most Avoxes would have been hunted down and caught by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>. They were then tasked to occupations seen as undesirable to the Capitol citizens.
</p><p>There were Avoxes enslaved as domestic servants by the government of Panem,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> as well as by private Capitol citizens.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Most Avoxes in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a> were seen waiting on <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>.
</p><p>Other Avoxes were enslaved as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_service" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Civil service">civil servants</a>, charged with the day-to-day maintenance of the Capitol. They worked shifts in the sewer network and the <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a> under the city.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> They also operated in other lower level positions within waste management, such as driving <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/garbage_truck" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:garbage truck">garbage trucks</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Abuses">Abuses</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvox%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As an enslaved class of people stripped of all their human rights, Avoxes were subjected to many abuses.
</p><p>They were subject to later being killed in any manner for any reason and at any time, as being made an Avox is tantamount to <a href="/wiki/Capital_punishment" title="Capital punishment">capital punishment</a>.
</p>
<ul><li>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> witnessed Avoxes in the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a> having had <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">animal parts</a> grafted onto their bodies in some grotesque experiment, as well as being tortured with <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> that echoed their screams.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Avoxes are unable to speak, due to their tongues being cut out. Citizens are not permitted to talk to them unless giving them an order.</li>
<li>According to <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, the two Avoxes who served <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s tributes during the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> were imprisoned with him in the Capitol; <a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a> was killed by an electric shock, while <a href="/wiki/Darius" title="Darius">Darius</a> was badly beaten, tortured and mutilated for days until he succumbed to blood-loss and shock from his wounds.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The Avoxes that worked underground never saw sunlight, and had to navigate dangerous <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pods</a> everyday just to do their jobs.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a> were unleashed in the tunnels during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> could hear the guttural screams of the Avoxes as they were being killed.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvox%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Avoxes constitute the lowest social class of all of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. They serve a punishment tantamount to execution, and are a stark reminder that even Capitol citizens can lose all social standing for committing any offense. They are the ultimate victims of the Capitol's cruelty, subject to a life of silence, servitude, and dehumanization. Unlike the impoverished citizens of the districts, Avoxes are treated as less than human—erased from society and forever punished. Their suffering represents the most extreme form of oppression in Panem, making them the true bottom rung of the social ladder.
</p><p>Citizens were not permitted to speak to Avoxes unless giving them an order.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The term <i>Avox</i> was also nearly synonymous with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/muteness" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:muteness">muteness</a> or mutism.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Despite this, Avoxes were able to communicate through gestures such as nodding,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> pointing,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> or writing.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the films, the Avox <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> and his brother <a href="/wiki/Castor" title="Castor">Castor</a> could occasionally be seen conversing in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Sign_Language" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:American Sign Language">American Sign Language</a> (ASL).<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The fate of the Avoxes is grave, as they may be subject to death via any means, such as what happened to both Lavinia and Darius. They were also among the first targets during the Second Rebellion. Only one known Avox escaped from Capitol rule and regained social standing.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvox%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Avoxes' physical appearances were not markedly different from those of their lives as free Capitol citizen, with the exception of their tongues, which were removed so they could no longer speak.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, they could be recognized by their distinctive way of swallowing,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and those enslaved at the Training Center wore white uniform tunics.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the first film, the Avoxes serving the tributes wore crimson robes, gold flats, and had their hair tightly pulled back.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the second film, these Avoxes wore sleeveless white tunics, haunting dark eye makeup, and cages around their faces.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the last film, Avoxes standing in the background in the President's mansion wore a red uniform, along with a mask covering their mouths.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_Avoxes">Known Avoxes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvox%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a> - the redheaded girl whose capture <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> witnessed in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. She served District 12's tributes during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th</a> and 75th Hunger Games, during which time Katniss considered her sort of a friend.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Darius" title="Darius">Darius</a> - a former District 12 Peacekeeper, known to be flirty, boyish, and fun. He and Katniss were friends in 12 before his enslavement.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> - a rebel cameraman with <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. He worked for five years in the Capitol's underground tunnels, making him a tactical asset to Squad 451.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvox%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The term "Avox" is derived from the Ancient Greek prefix <i>ἀ-</i> (<i>a-</i>, “not, without” - e.g. "<i>a</i>moral") and the Latin word <i>vox</i> ("voice"). In other words, "avox" means "voiceless."
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAvox%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Pollux is the only known Avox that survived the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a> and regained social standing.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A brief written piece for <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a> misidentified the <a href="/wiki/District_7_stylist" title="District 7 stylist">District 7 stylist</a> as a random Avox servant, romanticizing their state of forced servility.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>According to <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Mrs. Plinth</a>, the Avoxes enslaved in her household liked foods that were easy to swallow, such as custards.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
