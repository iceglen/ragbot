</p><p><b>Amandla Stenberg</b> is an American actress of mixed African-American and Danish descent who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> "Amandla" means "power" in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zulu_language" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Zulu language">Zulu</a>, and "Stenberg" is Danish for "stone mountain." 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAmandla_Stenberg%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She made her feature film debut in 2011 in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombiana" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Colombiana">Colombiana</a></i> as young Cataleya. Stenberg has been featured in dozens of commercials since the age of 4, modeling for Disney. She also appeared in the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleepy_Hollow_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sleepy Hollow (TV series)">Sleepy Hollow</a></i>.
</p><p>She began playing the violin in 2013 and singing at Los Angeles venues along with singer/songwriter Zander Hawley. The duo released their first EP in August 2015.
</p><p>In 2018, Stenberg auditioned for the role of Shuri in the Marvel film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Panther_(film" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Black Panther (film">Black Panther</a></i>. However, she turned down the role because she believed the character should be played by a darker-skinned actress.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In 2017, she starred in the romantic drama <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everything,_Everything_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Everything, Everything (film)">Everything, Everything</a></i>, and in 2018, she played the lead role in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hate_U_Give_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Hate U Give (film)">The Hate U Give</a></i>,
