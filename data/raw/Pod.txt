<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPod%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The creation of pods dates back to the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, though it's unknown whether or not any were actually weaponized during that war. More pods were added over the years. As a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> designed a number of them himself.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Battle_of_the_Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPod%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>With the use of a <a href="/wiki/Holo" title="Holo">Holo</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">rebel</a> units during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a> could access a digital map marked with pods. Using this information, rebels could attempt to navigate around the marked pods or purposefully trigger them at a safe distance, like defusing a bomb.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Plutarch's map had been secured by a rebel insider before they fled the Capitol with him; however, the intel was several months old by the time it was put into use, so some of the data points were outdated. For example, on <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>'s fourth morning in the Capitol, <a href="/wiki/Leeg_2" title="Leeg 2">Leeg 2</a> hit a mislabeled pod that was supposed to release <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a> gnats, but she was shot in the brain by a "sunburst of metal darts" instead.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Several days later, the squad was set to film a <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> on a block with several active pods: one set off a "spray of gunfire", while the other was meant to net an unfortunate rebel for "interrogation or execution". The gunfire pod was triggered without causing any injuries, but their commander, <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>, stepped on an unlabeled pod that blew off his legs.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He would die soon after from blood loss.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p><a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> and <a href="/wiki/Leeg_1" title="Leeg 1">Leeg 1</a> shot at other pods in the area in an attempt at minesweeping, triggering explosions and a wave of a black, tar-like substance. In the chaos, the <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacked</a> <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> found himself triggered and tried to attack <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>. Mitchell intervened, but Peeta threw Mitchell into the other marked pod; it turned out that the net was made of barbed wire, piercing him instantly and hoisting him overhead. The rest of the squad got away, and the black wave cleared the way for them by setting off several other pods, including a swarm of <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jackers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
Later that night, Squad 451 decided to travel through the sewers because there were less pods than aboveground, and <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> had insider knowledge, having formerly worked there. Katniss mentioned being guided away from pods that would trigger gas, live wires, and rats the size of beavers.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After a rest, they were chased by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a> into the <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>, where Katniss quickly detonated a pod that held flesh-eating rats. She planned on leading the others to avoid a pod that would destroy the ground underneath their feet and feed them into "something called a Meat Grinder".<sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>However, <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> stopped Katniss from running headlong into an unlabeled pod: a "wide shaft of golden light" that melted <a href="/wiki/Messalla" title="Messalla">Messalla</a>'s flesh off his body. As the rest of the group pushed onwards, <a href="/wiki/Jackson" title="Jackson">Jackson</a> and <a href="/wiki/Leeg_1" title="Leeg 1">Leeg 1</a> stayed behind to hold off the mutts at the Meat Grinder, where they were then killed.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is unknown whether the pod or the mutts ended their lives.
</p><p>While sheltering at <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>'s shop, the remainder of the squad checked a paper map and determined they could walk to the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">President's mansion</a> through a zone where pods were deactivated for the safety of citizens who lived there.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They also learned from television reports that rebels had begun letting unmanned cars drift down the street to trigger pods. However, the Capitol quickly disabled some and reenabled them when rebel forces followed after the vehicles; Katniss mentioned watching a group of rebels get "blown to bits by a row of potted rosebushes in front of a flower shop".<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Squad 451 split up and progressed towards the mansion, Katniss and Gale witnessed a pod ahead of them release a "gush of steam" that boiled its victims to death. Another pod, spanning an entire block, gave off a purple beam of light that caused all the people within it to bleed from every orifice; it only turned off once everyone was dead. At the last pod they encountered, the two sides of the street folded down like flaps into an underground space below, where mutts crawled in the shadows to kill anyone who survived the fall. Katniss narrowly managed to hang onto the edge, inch over to the corner, and swing one leg up to pull herself out.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
