</p><p><b>Kjell Brutscheidt</b> is a German actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKjell_Brutscheidt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Brutscheidt is a German actor who studied acting at the August Everding Theater Academy in Munich. He appeared in many stage productions in Munich and Berlin, as well as at the Opernhaus Zürich and at the Schauspielhaus Düsseldorf. In the 2019/20 season, Brutscheidt was part of the first interdisciplinary studio at Theater Bielefeld. In 2020, he was in front of the camera in Spreewaldkrimi – Tödliche Fastnacht and for the feature film Der junge Häuptling Winnetou, as well as in the Instagram series <i>I am Sophie Scholl</i> (2021) and the made for television-movies <i>Polizeiruf 110</i> and <i>Staatsfeinde</i> (2022). He played a secondary role in the Disney+ series <i>Pauline</i> (2024), and in the upcoming ARD series <i>Schwarzes Gold</i> (2025), as well as a feature in the fifth season of <i>Babylon Berlin</i> (2017). He played in the stage adaptation of <i>Früchte des Zorns</i> (<i>The Grapes of Wrath</i>) at the München Volkstheater in the 2024-2025 season, and returned to Theater Bielefeld for the end of 2025 until 2026.  
</p><p>Brutscheidt is represented by Lucky Punch Management.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
