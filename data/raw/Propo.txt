<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Development">Development</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)#Rebellion" title="Mockingjay (bird)">Mockingjay</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was the star of the rebels' propaganda initiative. An entire team was dedicated to making her over, writing her speeches, and orchestrating her appearances. <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> was a key member of that team, likely drawing on his experience as a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> to market the revolution through her. The rebels also intended to use <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> in propos, though they couldn't initially due to his condition after the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/MJP1_Katniss_and_Effie_propo_behind_the_scenes.JPG/revision/latest?cb=20231227193936" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss behind the scenes of this first failed propo with Effie Trinket in Mockingjay - Part 1" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/MJP1_Katniss_and_Effie_propo_behind_the_scenes.JPG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231227193936" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="119" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="MJP1 Katniss and Effie propo behind the scenes.JPG" data-image-key="MJP1_Katniss_and_Effie_propo_behind_the_scenes.JPG" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:MJP1_Katniss_and_Effie_propo_behind_the_scenes.JPG" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss behind the scenes of this first failed propo with <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> in <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Katniss's first scripted test propo was regarded as a failure and never went past filming.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During a brainstorming session held by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>'s President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> agreed to send Katniss out into the field with a camera crew to draw a more authentic and spontaneous performance out of her.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The results from this were so promising that immediate plans were made to produce more,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and propos were created all the way through to the end of the war.
</p><p>According to Plutarch, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> "essentially redesigned the underground network that transmits all the programming",<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so he was in charge of getting the propos to air across the districts,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and later, in the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Rebel_propos">Rebel propos</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="First_failed_propo">First failed propo</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>People of Panem, we fight, we dare, we end our hunger for justice!</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss Everdeen<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The first propo that Katniss attempted to film was just a single slogan, which was meant to be edited into a short test propo to show President Coin. However, Katniss thought the line was ridiculous so she was unable to deliver it convincingly, and nobody liked the way it turned out.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5f/Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-6098.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200919042202" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss in District 8 in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5f/Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-6098.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200919042202" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-6098.jpg" data-image-key="Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-6098.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-6098.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss in District 8 in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h4><span id=".22If_we_burn.2C_you_burn_with_us.22"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="&quot;If_we_burn,_you_burn_with_us&quot;">"If we burn, you burn with us"</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>I want to tell the rebels that I am alive. That I'm right here in District Eight, where the Capitol has just bombed a hospital full of unarmed men, women, and children. There will be no survivors. I want to tell people that if you think for second that the Capitol will treat us fairly if there's a cease-fire, you're deluding yourself. Because you know who they are and what they do. This is what they do! And we must fight back! President Snow says he's sending us a message? Well, I have one for him. You can torture us and bomb us and burn our districts to the ground, but do you see that? Fire is catching! And if we burn, you burn with us!</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss Everdeen<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>About a month into the war, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> was heavily <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_8_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">bombed</a> one morning, and Katniss, Gale, <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cressida" title="Cressida">Cressida</a>, and other District 13 citizens and camera crews were sent to film a propo. Katniss met injured rebel soldiers and civilians in the field hospital, many of whom were excited to see her. However, after exiting, a second wave of Capitol bombers came in to kill the patients and medics inside. Katniss and Gale took down the <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> with explosive arrows, utilizing the same technique they used to hunt geese. Then Katniss filmed a stirring propo in front of the burning wreckage.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>This was the first spot to be broadcast, and it was rerun at least seventeen times. It played in every district, although Beetee wasn't able to get through to the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Subsequent_propos">Subsequent propos</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<p>The team behind the Airtime Assault planned to make two more propos using footage from the day of the hospital bombing. The first was under the theme "Because you know who they are and what they do". This one, put together by Cressida's assistant <a href="/wiki/Messalla" title="Messalla">Messalla</a>, was shown later that evening on the television. It focused on Katniss interacting with the patients— especially children— and then the bombing and its aftermath. Katniss found it difficult to watch, burying her face into her pillow when the bombs came down. She only looked back up for a brief clip of herself at the end, once everyone was dead.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The second spot was themed under "Fire is catching". It was to be a highlight reel of "Katniss's best moments intercut with scenes of rebel uprisings and war footage".<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<hr />
<h4><span id=".22We_Remember.22"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="&quot;We_Remember&quot;">"We Remember"</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p><a href="/wiki/Fulvia_Cardew" title="Fulvia Cardew">Fulvia Cardew</a> had come up with the idea to run a series of propos narrated by Finnick that paid tribute to the memory of past <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> from each of the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>, the theme being "We Remember." It was an effort to reach out to any people in the districts who were still unwilling to join the rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> According to Plutarch, Finnick did very well with these, and Cressida said they were "painful to watch" because Finnick had "[known] so many of them personally".<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Beetee managed to break through the Capitol's feed in the middle of a Capitol propo with <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>. He was able to play five- to ten-second clips from the "We Remember" spots while Capitol technicians attempted to regain control over the broadcast.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Wreckage_of_District_12">Wreckage of District 12</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Katniss and Gale went with a film crew to document the ruins of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. Katniss, overcome with emotion, was unable to perform, so Gale recounted the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_12_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">bombing</a> and the mistreatment they'd faced from <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> in the months leading up to it. People in the districts then picked it up as a protest song.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p>After the bombing of <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>, Haymitch was going to feed Katniss a speech line by line that would be televised in order to get <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> to surrender to the rebels. However, she jumped in to save the life of a victim of the bombing, and she ended up being held at gunpoint herself. She gave a spontaneous speech, albeit with some prompting from Haymitch, but then she was shot<sup id="cite_ref-:2_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> by someone farther back in the crowd.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Squad_451">Squad 451</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a></i></center></div>
<p>Squad 451 was an elite unit of sharpshooters formed to film propos during the Battle of the Capitol. They were also known as the Star Squad.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On their fourth morning in the Capitol, <a href="/wiki/Leeg_2" title="Leeg 2">Leeg 2</a> was killed when she hit a mislabeled <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pod</a>, which shot metal darts into her brain.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Their most notable propo was filmed on their seventh day in the Capitol. That afternoon, the whole squad was needed to stage a more complicated propo that included a special block set aside for filming that had a few active pods. However, the squad wasn't able to take the acting seriously, and after admonishing them, their commander <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a> mistakenly stepped on a pod, blowing off his legs.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This set off a chain of events that, over the next few days, would end with a total of nine deaths: seven soldiers (including Leeg 2) and two members of the film crew.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>At one point, the squad was all prematurely declared dead by the Capitol. This resulted in an address from President Snow, but rebels broke in with their own message. President Coin addressed the nation at large for the first time as the head of the rebellion, eulogized Katniss, and encouraged the rebels to keep fighting in her name. This was followed by a "heavily doctored photo" of Katniss "looking beautiful and fierce with a bunch of flames flickering behind [her]", unaccompanied by any words or slogans. The image of her face was all they needed by then.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p>While he was imprisoned in the Capitol, Peeta was forced to film three separate interviews in the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">President's mansion</a>, which documented his declining physical and mental condition. His first propo was a sit-down interview with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>— presumably filmed soon after the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>— who asked Peeta about the chaotic events that unfolded at the end of the Quell. Peeta then called for a ceasefire. This propo wouldn't be aired for several weeks.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Peeta's second interview with Caesar was aired four or five days after the first, just after the rebels' "if we burn, you burn with us" spot. Caesar asked Peeta about rumors that Katniss was filming propos for the districts, and Peeta aggressively responded that they were using her. When asked if he had anything to tell Katniss, he told her to think for herself, evaluate whether she trusted the people she was working with, and if she didn't know what was going on, to find out.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_5-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Unlike the previous two, Peeta's third propo was aired live. He was made to recount war damage allegedly perpetrated by rebels: a broken dam in <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>, a derailed <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> leaking toxic waste, a granary collapsed after a <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a>, and the bombing of a water purification plant. District 13 was able to break into the broadcast with their own "We Remember" propos.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After wresting control back from the rebels, President Snow asked Peeta if he had any "parting thoughts" for Katniss, and Peeta revealed that the Capitol was planning to bomb District 13. After that, he was knocked to the floor and beaten, and the footage cut out.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span id="President_Snow.27s_speeches"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="President_Snow's_speeches">President Snow's speeches</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D18&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i>, <a href="/wiki/Egeria" title="Egeria">Egeria</a> wrote a speech that was later given by President Snow on television. He declared that any association with the mockingjay symbol would be punishable by death; to prove it, Peacekeepers staged live <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executions</a> before crowds in District 8 and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> at the same instant.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the members of Squad 451 were all mistakenly declared dead, President Snow appeared on television from his desk, commending Peacekeepers for ridding the nation of the Mockingjay. He derided Katniss as a "poor, unstable girl with a small talent with a <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow and arrow</a>". Then, through his doubt in her, he expressed that the rebels had "no real leader among them". This was interrupted by a rebel broadcast from 13, after which Snow declared that when they recovered Katniss's body, the rebels would see her for what she truly was: "A dead girl who could save no one, not even herself".<sup id="cite_ref-:4_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPropo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D25&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Beetee is the only named member of the crew behind the Airtime Assault who was not from the Capitol. He came from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>.</li>
<li>During Haymitch's brainstorming session after the failed test propo, he brought <a href="/wiki/Dalton" title="Dalton">Dalton</a>, <a href="/wiki/Leevy" title="Leevy">Leevy</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a> in to consult, though they were otherwise not involved in the Airtime Assault.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Dalton made the last comment at that meeting, saying to wash Katniss's face of makeup: "She's still a girl and you made her look thirty-five. Feels wrong. Like something the Capitol would do."<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A variation of this line was given to Boggs in the film adaptation.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>In the <i>Mockingjay</i> films, the character of Fulvia was replaced with Effie.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_9-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>On this matter, director <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a> said, "How can anybody replace <a href="/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks" title="Elizabeth Banks">Elizabeth Banks</a> as Effie Trinket in these movies? When <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> saw <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, she called and one of the first things she said was, ‘There’s no way Effie Trinket cannot be in the <i>Mockingjay</i> films.’ Effie brings such warmth and fun and levity to these dark stories. She’s the fish out of water in this one and fans will love how she has adapted to the world of District 13."<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
