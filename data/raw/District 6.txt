<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 6 tributes <a href="/wiki/Miles" title="Miles">Miles</a>, <a href="/wiki/Atread" title="Atread">Atread</a>, <a href="/wiki/Velo" title="Velo">Velo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> joined the <a href="/wiki/Newcomers" class="mw-redirect" title="Newcomers">Newcomers</a> along with Districts 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the time of the third Quarter Quell, the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from Districts <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, 6, <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">7</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> were all involved in a rebel plan to protect Katniss and break out of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The two District 6 <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> for these Games were heavily addicted to <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which could potentially imply the district at large has a drug problem.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion"><a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>One evening, the Capitol aired a live <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> of <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, who was made to recount war damage allegedly perpetrated by rebels. This included a derailed train leaking toxic waste,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which could have been linked to District 6 on account of their industry.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 6 is located in the midwest area of North America, primarily centered in the states of Michigan and Wisconsin and the southern part of the Canadian province of Ontario. This ties into its district industry as the Midwest, particularly Michigan, was renowned for its automotive industry during the 20th and 21st century. District 6 borders <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> (to the south and east), <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> (to the northwest), and <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> (to the east).
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, District 6 is mentioned as being the third district from the east, after Districts 12 and 8 and before District 11.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 258px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/86/District6CatchingFireFilmStill.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20250326032307" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 6 as seen in the Catching Fire film adaptation." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/86/District6CatchingFireFilmStill.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/258?cb=20250326032307" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="258" height="107" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District6CatchingFireFilmStill.jpeg" data-image-key="District6CatchingFireFilmStill.jpeg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District6CatchingFireFilmStill.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">District 6 as seen in the <i>Catching Fire</i> film adaptation.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>The district's industry, along with <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, are the last ones to be revealed, only at the time the film adaptation of <i>The Hunger Games</i> has been announced. Nowhere in the original trilogy books suggest that District 6 provides transportation, unlike the case of <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> being hinted from the then-unidentified wheat and bread district.
<ul><li>In fact, Katniss stated in <i>Catching Fire</i> that <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> manufactures automobiles.</li></ul></li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Tribute_Guide" title="The Hunger Games: Tribute Guide">The Hunger Games: Tribute Guide</a></i>, it says that the people of District 6 dislike traveling, even though their industry is transportation.</li>
<li>District 6 was the 5th - 10th district to pry from Capitol rule.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 6 has 52 active hubs with a population of 784,453, making it the largest district in Panem, in terms of population, with almost four times as many citizens as District 2, the second-place holder. If this is true, due to being the largest district in terms of population, it is also extremely unstable, making Morphling a district-wide problem.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 6 claims 68,366 in tesserae, making it the sixth most impoverished district.&#160;
<ul><li>If the population is 784,453 and they claim 68,366 tesserae, that would equal to 0.087 tesserae per person, ironically making it the 4th richest District.</li>
<li>This would make District 6 the only other non-Career district (aside from District 5) richer than a Career district (District 4) and the second wealthiest non-Career district.</li></ul></li>
<li>It is considered a lower middle-class district.</li>
<li>According to <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a>, District 6 would be situated directly north of District 3, which fits more appropriately with what was seen in <i>Catching Fire</i>.</li>
<li>In the film, District 6 was the first District to lose both of its tributes in the 74th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>The people of District 6 seem to wear darker clothes as shown in Catching Fire during the victory tour. Their choice of clothes also seems to be more form-fitting to the body (People with skin-tight pants and shirts can be seen along with snug jackets).</li>
<li>Despite District 6 being the district responsible for transportation, Katniss mentions that District 3 manufactures automobiles.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>This is known before District 6's district has been revealed.</li></ul></li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be hello fall orange (#a15431 – <span style="background-color:#a15431; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 6's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be slate grey (#708090 - <span style="background-color:#708090; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear for the 50th Hunger Games was dove (#B3ADA7 - <span style="background-color:#B3ADA7; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
