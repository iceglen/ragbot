<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>We're a team, aren't we? And I'm so proud of my victors. You both deserved so much better, I'm truly sorry...</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Effie Trinket to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Effie Trinket</b> is the <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a> for the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>, acting as an advisor to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, while she accompanies them to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and various other activities. She also acted as a <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a> and escort for <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> for his Games. After the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, she is the only escort from the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th</a> and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> alive.
</p><p>She initially appears to be very posh, unaware, and entitled like many other <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> residents, but she had more than average empathy for the districts because she too had suffered by her ancestors' choices. She later becomes openly aware of a lifetime of false propaganda used by the Capitol against the districts and starts seeing residents as humans who deserve more than basic decency and rebels. She is <a href="/wiki/Proserpina_Trinket" title="Proserpina Trinket">Proserpina Trinket</a>'s older sister.
</p>

				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">How does Effie Trinket's character evolve throughout the Hunger Games series?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						Effie Trinket, initially a superficial character focused on manners and appearances, evolves throughout the Hunger Games series. She starts as Katniss's escort, irritating her with her obsession with decorum. However, Effie shows a softer side, expressing joy when Katniss survives the first Games. Known for her colorful outfits and wigs, Effie maintains her individuality even in District 13 by creatively adapting her uniform. Her relationship with Katniss deepens, and she becomes a supportive figure, assisting Katniss in preparation for interviews and expressing genuine concern for her wellbeing.

She advises tributes, including Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark, and assists in arranging sponsor gifts for their survival. She expresses pride in her tributes and shows regret for their challenging situations.

<p>Effie had a younger sister, <a href="/wiki/Proserpina_Trinket" title="Proserpina Trinket">Proserpina Trinket</a>. During her schooling years, Effie was president of the Capitol Cohorts Chapters and created the Spring Saturnalia in her freshman year, considered the best party of the year.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Effie implies that her life, especially in the earlier years, was somewhat difficult in the Capitol.  The citizens had a low opinion of her family due to her <a href="/wiki/Messalina_Trinket" title="Messalina Trinket">Great-Aunt Messalina</a> bringing shame and "disgrace to the name of Trinket" that "We'll be recovering from that for years."<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She is so affected by this reputation, that as a young professional she tries to downplay or hide certain successes that may cause public conflict because "It's hard enough being a Trinket."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i>Sunrise on the Reaping</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Effie's sister, Proserpina, was tasked by her school with being part of the prep team for the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> competing in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. Prosperina phoned Effie when the stylist, <a href="/wiki/Magno_Stift" title="Magno Stift">Magno Stift</a>, did not show up on interview night as she was worried it would ruin her grade. Effie told her that a positive attitude was ninety-seven per cent of the battle<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and then showed up at the apartment to style the tributes herself.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Effie later stood by Haymitch when it came time for him to be presented as the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of the Games, despite everything he had done to defy the Capitol. She also accompanied him as his escort on his <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> after <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> breaks her hip. Haymitch was touched at first, but soured when she stated that "But they really are for a greater good. The Hunger Games."<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i>The Hunger Games</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>Effie was now working as an escort for the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">Hunger Games tributes</a> of District 12 prior to its destruction. She was most notable for escorting and advising <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th</a> and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. Her responsibilities included drawing the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> tributes' names every year at <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reapings</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and subsequently escorting them to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> via a <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was very well known for her Capitol accent, upbeat attitude for the Games, and the Capitol phrase, "May the odds be <i>ever</i> in your favor!"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Effie lived in the Capitol and remained there for most of the year. The only reason she travelled to District 12 or any of the other districts was because of the Games or the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>.
</p><p>Effie reaps <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> and Peeta Mellark's names from the District 12 <a href="/wiki/Reaping_balls" title="Reaping balls">reaping balls</a>. When Katniss <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteers</a> in place of her sister, Effie gushes about her spirit and attempts to lighten the atmosphere. At one point in the reaping, a drunk <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> stumbles onstage and hugs Effie without her consent, causing her wig to become crooked, then promptly falls face-first off the stage and lies on the floor, unconscious. Effie attempts to shake this off, although her wig remains askew, and continues the reaping ceremony.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Effie takes the tributes on the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> to transport them to the Capitol. She tells them about the pre-Games routine on the train and her plans. Effie eats dinner with Katniss and Peeta and is relieved when she sees that Katniss and Peeta has manners unlike the last pair of tributes, saying, "At least you two have decent manners. The pair last year ate everything with their hands like a pair of savages. It completely upset my digestion." 
</p><p>To toy with Effie, Katniss begins to eat like an animal by wiping her fingers with the tablecloth, successfully upsetting Effie. Afterward, she sits with Katniss and Peeta and watches the recap of the reapings in the other districts. Katniss and Peeta joke about Haymitch's behavior during the reaping, which annoys Effie. Immediately afterward, Haymitch enters the room and vomits all over the floor. Effie is disgusted and leaves the room.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Effie eats breakfast with them and retires to her room to make arrangements for their arrival at the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After Katniss and Peeta's amazing performance in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a> through the Capitol, Effie talks them up in an attempt to win <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a> for her tributes. She falsely states that coals turn to pearls when enough pressure is applied, referencing Peeta and Katniss being from District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Effie and Haymitch also drill them for details about their sessions in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>, determined to whip them into shape.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Effie is horrified when Katniss tells them about her seemingly disastrous private session, where she shot an arrow at the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>' table. However, she then states that it serves them right, as the Gamemakers should not ignore Katniss regardless of her being from District 12. Effie is ecstatic when Katniss and Peeta both manage a high <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

She attempts to teach her how to sit, smile, and talk like a lady, but only becomes frustrated.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the interview and Peeta's astonishing declaration of love, Katniss shoves Peeta into an urn. Effie helps break up the fight while Haymitch tells her about the logistics of his and Peeta's plan.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Heartbroken because she has grown to like them, Effie leaves the room before she can become more upset.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She assists Haymitch in lining up sponsors for Katniss and Peeta and helps them throughout the Games. After Katniss and Peeta's interview with Caesar, she accompanies them back to District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Effie sent <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Katniss's mother</a> a list of potential talents for her daughter, as every <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> was meant to have one.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>She later returns to District 12 for Katniss and Peeta's <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and tells them they have to start off with <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>. On the way there, the train makes an unplanned stop that affects Effie's schedule, angering her. This also irritates Katniss and causes her to yell at Effie, hurting her feelings. When Katniss comes back, she apologizes to Effie, who graciously accepts and forgives her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Effie ensures that everything is prepared for the victory speech Katniss and Peeta must give to <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>'s family. When they arrive in District 11, Effie is displeased with the way they are being treated.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the elderly man is killed for whistling Rue's tune, which signaled the end of a working day, the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> force them all to leave quickly, which annoys Effie due to their lack of manners. She noted that the Peacekeepers were very strict when she was looking around District 11's <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>. She said they ordered her to get back to her quarters and one even poked her with her gun.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the Victory Tour is complete, Effie hustles Katniss and Peeta off to District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Effie's role is expanded from the books, as she essentially takes on the role of <a href="/wiki/Fulvia_Cardew" title="Fulvia Cardew">Fulvia Cardew</a>. In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Mockingjay Part 1</a></i>, she has come to District 13 alongside Haymitch and Plutarch out of sympathy for Katniss. She is initially unhappy about being in 13, despite her loyalties now being with the rebels, mostly because the strict rules in 13 make it impossible for her to dress fashionably, wear makeup, or put on wigs. She assists Plutarch and Haymitch in coming up with ideas for propos and gives Katniss the designs for the Mockingjay suit that Cinna drew. She provides comic relief in the movie, as her exuberant personality clashes with the sober style of District 13. She doesn't seem to be treated with distrust and hostility, as President Coin personally welcomes her.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay_Part_2"><i>Mockingjay Part 2</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The character of Effie is reduced to somewhat of a background character in the film. She is seen at Finnick's and Annie's wedding wearing the dress she was last wearing when forcibly taken as a refugee at the end of <i>Catching Fire</i>. Being distinctive in appearance, she joyfully takes part in the festivities.
</p><p>Effie is once again seen near the end of the film escorting Katniss from the hospital to Snow's mansion, recalling the first time she had brought Katniss there. Despite having a team of stylists prepping Katniss for Snow's execution in the novel, Effie is the sole stylist who is shown to be doing some last minute tidying up before letting Katniss go on to attend the execution. At the execution, Effie takes the stage behind President Alma Coin, the newly self-appointed and alleged temporary President of Panem alongside Coin's high-ranking officials. Effie stands nervously in the background; whether this was the result of having to watch Snow be executed or because of a personal distrust of President Coin is unknown. Immediately after Coin was shot and killed by Katniss, those who were standing nearby behind Coin rushed to her aid; whether Effie was among those trying to help is unknown as well. She witnessed Katniss being taken into custody for murder.
</p><p>After Katniss is released from custody and shortly before she and Haymitch return to District 12, Effie makes her farewell with the two a very emotional one, telling Katniss to promise her (Effie) that she would find "the life of a victor", which possibly implied that she wanted Katniss to go on in life with the strength that helped her win the Games twice. Haymitch tells Effie not "to be a stranger" and gives her a kiss on the lips. As Katniss and Haymitch walked toward the gates of the mansion, Effie began to cry and quickly composed herself for aesthetic reasons.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-30"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

</p><p>In the second film in promotional posters, and while watching the interviews backstage, she wears a ruffled red dress with matching feathered shoes. She also wears a pink wig, magenta sheer gloves with Elizabethan ruffs, a silver belt, and matching silver jewelry.

She also wears a blue bun-styled wig, and lilac sheer gloves with Elizabethan ruffs.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Effie can be described as oblivious to the sorrows and misfortunes that fall upon District 12. She is very strict about manners and being "proper", as that is the Capitol's way. She is not on very good terms with Haymitch at times because he is always drunk and a complete mess, and due to their conflicting personalities. She always tells Katniss and Peeta "You have no manners!" This angers Katniss, due to the fact that she comes from a poorer district, where most people face starvation. Effie is a classic Capitol resident with impeccable manners, although she later rebels against the Capitol.
</p>

<p>Effie is a perfectionist and extremely punctual, complaining about how a five-minute train delay would hugely affect her tightly packed schedule. However, she is known for being bright and bubbly at times when she is not being publicly humiliated or in the presence of undesirable manners.
</p><p>Effie, like most Capitol citizens, sees the Hunger Games as just fun entertainment as shown by her extreme happiness at the reaping regarding tributes being chosen. 
</p><p>However, Effie is also described as having a "keen instinct" by Katniss, and seems to be at least partially aware of the political uproar caused by Katniss and Peeta's attempted suicide. In the Reaping for the 75th Hunger Games, she is noticeably less excitable than previous years, presumably having grown fond of the two younger Victors. She appears to lose some of her shallow nature as the series progresses, eventually defecting from the Capitol during the Rebellion.
</p>

<p>Peeta seemed to get along well with Effie, never giving her much trouble. Peeta did anger her when he and Katniss joked around about Haymitch being drunk during the reaping and making a fool of himself on national television. Effie yelled at them, saying that he was their only source of real help when it came to surviving. However, Peeta was kind to her and always followed her plans. <dd>—Haymitch, to Effie<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>, Effie first meets <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> when she agrees to help her sister <a href="/wiki/Proserpina_Trinket" title="Proserpina Trinket">Proserpina Trinket</a> on her school assignment being part of Haymitch's prep team for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. Effie annoys <a href="/wiki/Haymitch" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch">Haymitch</a> greatly with her prim Capitol ways. Since Haymitch isn't familiar with Effie's behavior, manners, deadlines, or appearance, he becomes frustrated when she nags him about this. In the end, though, Haymitch becomes grateful for Effie for her stubbornness and dedication to her job, though she is still misguided by the Capitol's propaganda<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Haymitch is technically Effie's first tribute and victor of any District (though it is heavily implied that District 12 was her only assignment throughout the entirety of the series) as an escort after she takes over the job for his victory tour after <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> is injured and Plutarch Heavensbee's recommendation.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the original series, they first encounter each other when Haymitch shows up drunk and in poor condition at the reaping for the 74th Hunger Games. He hugs her which messes up her pink wig. He reappears again when he throws up on the train, which only makes Effie more enraged. Haymitch and Effie work together to help Katniss and Peeta, and she helps him line up sponsors. Haymitch and Effie are shown to be the same during the Victory Tour as he shows poor manners and Effie annoys him with her nagging about his overall demeanor. The two seem to let Katniss and Peeta make their own decisions during preparations for the third Quarter Quell.
</p><p>At the end of <i>Mockingjay</i>, it is hinted that the two do in fact share a friendship, as it is revealed by Katniss' prep team that Haymitch and Plutarch Heavensbee had to "fight to keep her alive" during the rebellion.
</p><p>At the end of <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i>, the two shared a kiss.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Cecelia">Cecelia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Cecelia" title="Cecelia">Cecelia</a> was a victor from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>.
When she was <a href="/wiki/Reaped" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaped">reaped</a> as the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> for District 8 in the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, her three children clung to her, refusing to let her go.
Effie expressed dismay and commented "Oh no, not Cecelia" when she learned Cecelia was chosen, possibly indicating that Effie feels sympathy towards Cecelia.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The name "Effie" is a shorter form of the Greek name Euphemia, which means "well-spoken". This may be a reference to Effie's career in elocution and public speaking in the districts.
</p><p>The name "Trinket" refers to a small ornament or item of jewelry that is of little value. This is very fitting for Effie, and the rest of the Capitol citizens, as they prize meaningless possessions that though may have cost a lot, have no real purpose and are just for show. This helps to emphasize the poverty and starvation in the Districts, where people can not even afford to eat, let alone waste money on flashy objects and clothing.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, a website made to promote <i>The Hunger Games</i> film, wrote an article on Effie about her eccentric clothes, shoes, and wigs. Effie was also a model for Capitol Couture.</li>
<li>The name Effie is an Anglicization of the Greek name <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphemia_(given_name)">Euphemia</a> which means "All-Praised". This ties into the many Greco-Roman references and allusions of the trilogy.</li>
<li>Aside from all the unnamed tributes, Effie was the only other character whose name was not said in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_film" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games film">The Hunger Games film</a>, even though she is a constant through the movie and her name is mentioned multiple times in the book.</li>
<li>Elizabeth Banks, who portrays Effie in the film, has stated that her performance was inspired by actress, <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Russell">Rosalind Russell</a> for her role in the 1958 classical film, <i>Auntie Mame</i>. She also mentioned how Effie's outfits were extremely impractical to wear, as she needed help to do practically anything.<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Effie is the only known named escort in the original series.</li>
<li>The evolution of Effie's look reflects her character development. For example, in <i>Catching Fire</i>, she was dressed in poorly balanced shoes and her waist was cinched in too tight to show how she was always on edge and walking a fine line with her changing feelings towards the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_doc_35-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_doc-35"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i>, Effie tried to maintain her individuality in District 13 through her clothes. At first, she wore things that she had been wearing when she was taken to District 13, but later experimented with the uniform she was issued. For example, she buttoned two shirts together to make a dress with four sleeves, she wore a sweater as a cape, and she used a scarf to cover her hair in place of her usual wigs.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ1_doc_36-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ1_doc-36"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
