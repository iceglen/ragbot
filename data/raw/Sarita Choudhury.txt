</p><p><b>Sarita Choudhury</b> is an English-Indian actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Egeria" title="Egeria">Egeria</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">the Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSarita_Choudhury%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After her debut film <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0102456?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Mississippi Masala</a> (1991) became an art house hit, Sarita Choudhury was determined not to "go Hollywood," focusing her acting energies on independent film instead.
</p><p>She studied economics at Queens University in Ontario before switching to acting. She casually auditioned for Mississippi Masala (1991) and wound up cast as the lead opposite <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000243?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Denzel Washington</a> in the singular interracial romance between a Southern African American man and a transplanted Indian woman. Despite the film's surprise success, Choudhury stuck to her non-Hollywood roots, putting her exotic looks and talent to versatile use as a Pakistani country-western singer in <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0105820?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Wild West</a> (1992), a Chilean maid who is raped in <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000806?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Bille August</a>'s adaptation of <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0107151?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Åndenes hus</a> (1993), and a lesbian mother in <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0109843?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Fresh Kill</a> (1994).
</p><p>Choudhury worked with Mississippi Masala (1991) director <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0619762?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Mira Nair</a> again in <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0114113?ref_=nmbio_mbio">The Perez Family</a> (1995) and played the cuckolded queen Tara in Nair's frankly-sensual feminist parable <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0116743?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Kama Sutra: A Tale of Love</a> (1996).
</p><p>By the late 1990s, Choudhury added a touch of Hollywood to her repertoire with supporting roles in the glossy <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000033?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Alfred Hitchcock</a> remake, <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120787?ref_=nmbio_mbio">A Perfect Murder</a> (1998) and the <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001023?ref_=nmbio_mbio">John Cassavetes</a> retread <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120683?ref_=nmbio_mbio">Gloria</a> (1999).
</p>
