</p><p><b>Vipsania Sickle</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. She was assigned the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>, <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVipsania_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, wanting a guitar for his tribute, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, to use for a performance at her <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interview</a>, asked his classmates if they could lend one. The best result he got was a "maybe" from Vipsania. Coriolanus feared a trap, as Vipsania had inherited her aunt <a href="/wiki/Agrippina_Sickle" title="Agrippina Sickle">Agrippina</a>'s love of competition. He told her that she was a lifesaver, but secretly continued to search, finally securing a guitar from his neighbor <a href="/wiki/Pluribus_Bell" title="Pluribus Bell">Pluribus Bell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the games, her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a>, snatched two water bottles that were sent as <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsor</a> gifts for another tribute, <a href="/wiki/Dill" title="Dill">Dill</a>, who had failed to claim them. Vipsania commented that this was good thinking, as it saved her money, and she didn't have much to work with.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was the only mentor who thought to bring bedding to sleep overnight in <a href="/wiki/Heavensbee_Hall" title="Heavensbee Hall">Heavensbee Hall</a> at the Academy and thus slept comfortably in it while her fellow mentors sat sleeping in padded chairs. After <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> killed Treech with a <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutt</a> she'd hidden in her skirt, Vipsania was escorted away by <a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a> of <a href="/wiki/Capitol_News" title="Capitol News">Capitol News</a>. She thanked the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> through gritted teeth.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVipsania_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Vipsania is the feminine form of the name <i>Vipsanius</i>, which is the Roman clan of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Vipsanius_Agrippa" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa">Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa</a>. Its most prominent female members were his descendants, including the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vipsania_Agrippina" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Vipsania Agrippina">first wife</a> of the emperor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiberius" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tiberius">Tiberius</a>; she was exiled with her son and died of starvation. Additionally, her surname, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sickle" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sickle">Sickle</a>, refers to a tool used to harvest crops.
</p>
