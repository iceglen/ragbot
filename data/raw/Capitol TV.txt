<p><b>Capitol TV</b> is the main television network within <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. It was used for promotional purposes in regards to the film <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, where <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> addressed the nation of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. The TV network was also known for broadcasting the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.
</p><p>As it was created by Lionsgate, Capitol TV was not mentioned in the original <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">The Hunger Games trilogy</a> as an official network. However, it did appear in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, alongside another organization called <a href="/wiki/Capitol_News" title="Capitol News">Capitol News</a>.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius "Lucky" Flickerman</a> worked as a weather reporter for Capitol TV and was later tapped to host the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Capitol TV also covered the funeral of <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> as well as the <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombing</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Together_As_One">Together As One</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_TV%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<dl><dd><span>"<i>Panem Today, Panem Tomorrow, Panem Forever.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;President Snow, addressing the nation of Panem.</dd></dl></div>
<p>"President Snow's Panem Address: Together As One" was the first address from President Snow. The address was made in efforts to preserve a peaceful and prosperous Panem with <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> standing by President Snow.
</p><p>It was released to the public on June 25, 2014 as the first teaser trailer for <i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</i>.

<p>"President Snow Presents: Unity" is the second Panem address by President Snow. In this address to the nation, <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> Peeta Mellark and <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> stand by President Snow as he commends citizens for their cooperation and unwavering dedication to a unified Panem.
</p><p>Halfway through the video, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> appears on the screen and announces that "The Mockingjay Lives" before his transmission is cut short.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_TV%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Despite being created by a Capitol-run network, every District Voices video contains hidden anti-Capitol messages or includes within them information which can be used against the Capitol.
<ul><li>District 2's video includes a list of techniques Peacekeepers use in close-combat, ostensibly to allow rebels in the Districts without weapons to counter their moves and take them down in physical combat (which would likely have assisted the rebels in the dam bombing shown in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>)</li>
<li>District 5's video highlights a major power producing location for the rebels to take down.</li>
<li>District 6's video lists all the statistics of the <a href="/wiki/SJ-7_Peacekeeper_Assault_Transport" title="SJ-7 Peacekeeper Assault Transport">SJ-7 Peacekeeper Assault Vehicle</a>, including the strength of explosives required to destroy them as well as inform rebels of a method by which not to attack the vehicles by (which would later be put into effect as shown in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>)</li>
<li>The scissors gesture at the end of the District 8 video, <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://youtu.be/EQ2NkOjfi34?list=PLdtylR-rJhlGzQx7x7AUU3dZa1CZCr5S7&amp;t=117">when paused</a> at the right time, appears as a hidden <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a>.</li>
<li>The flour on the baking sheet in the District 9 video <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://youtu.be/19WhhFJOLyY?list=PLdtylR-rJhlGzQx7x7AUU3dZa1CZCr5S7&amp;t=83">is spread</a> in the shape of a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a> pin before the preparation of the tarts.</li></ul></li></ul>
