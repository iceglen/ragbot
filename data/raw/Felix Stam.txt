<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFelix_Stam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Felix was selected to be a District Hero due to his quiet understanding of the ways of animals and the circle of life that unites us all.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He initially rejected the "standard contingency fee", but "quickly reconsidered" after he was "presented the alternative of reduced <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a>."
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFelix_Stam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Felix has fair hair, blue eyes and a beard. In his District Hero portrait, he has a septum noise piercing, smokes a pipe, and wears shaggy furs.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFelix_Stam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Felix is a male given name that stems from Latin (fēlix, felicis) and means "happy" or "lucky".
</p>

<ul><li>On June 19th, 2014, <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a> released seven portraits of District Heroes as advertisements for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>. These were original characters, independent from the books or films, serving as pro-<a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propaganda</a> for each of their respective <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Felix is the second named character from District 10, after <a href="/wiki/Dalton" title="Dalton">Dalton</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/de/Egeria_Letter-Felix.png/revision/latest?cb=20200819191546" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="To: The Office of President Snow From: Egeria Subj: Re: District Hero Candidate President Snow, For the representative of DIstrict 10, we believe Felix Stam may be best suited given his outstanding aesthetic match. He initially rejected the standard contingency fee, but quickly reconsidered when presented the alternative of reduced tesserae. We are certain he will come across as convincing to audiences. Attached you will find a photograph of him. If you approve of this selection we will move forward with a background check" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/de/Egeria_Letter-Felix.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200819191546" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="104" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Egeria Letter-Felix.png" data-image-key="Egeria_Letter-Felix.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Egeria_Letter-Felix.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Egeria's letter to <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> about Felix</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure></li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/Egeria" title="Egeria">Egeria</a>'s letters, it says that Felix is 36, but according to the District Heroes page, he is 35. Either way, he is the oldest known District Hero.
<ul><li>According to the letters, Felix was threatened with reduced rations to make him agree to be District Hero. Contrary to Capitol propaganda, he was selected by powerful figures in the Capitol rather than his family and friends.</li></ul></li></ul>
