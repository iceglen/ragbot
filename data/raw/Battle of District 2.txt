</p><p>The<b> Battle of District 2</b> is an unofficial term referring to a pivotal <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a> battle. In this conflict, district <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">rebels</a>, supported by military forces from <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, clashed with <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> loyalists for control of <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>. 
</p><p>This was the final district to fall under rebel control and a most crucial conflict, as this battle all but decimated the Capitol's military, leaving them with little capability to resist the subsequent invasion. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the end of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the rebel war effort collapsed following the alleged loss of District 13,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the seat of the Capitol's military power moved to District 2.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Directly after the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the Capitol established a military compound inside a mountain, later nicknamed <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a> during the Second Rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District 2's citizens were generally better-fed than in other districts,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but their children were still <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> to participate in the annual <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> with no apparent preferential treatment.
</p><p>However, over time, District 2 curried enough favor and privilege to be able to train <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> for the event without consequences,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the district became the Capitol's main source of <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District 2's close association with the Capitol offered citizens education and employment opportunities, and Capitol propaganda nurtured an image of the Hunger Games as a unique opportunity to earn untold wealth and glory.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As a result of these factors, District 2 had a strong loyalist contingent.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></i></center></div>
<p>When war broke out in the other districts, there was mixed support for the uprising in District 2. Nevertheless, the rebels, led in part by former <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> <a href="/wiki/Lyme" title="Lyme">Lyme</a>, eventually managed to capture the outer villages. However, the town center was divided, and the Nut remained under loyalist control.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Bombing_of_the_Nut">Bombing of the Nut</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Two weeks after the arrival of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and District 13's soldiers, a meeting was held to tackle the issue of the Nut. Lyme guided those assembled through a virtual tour of its interior and fortifications, and walked them through previous failed attempts to claim it. At the end of Lyme's presentation, strategists debated for hours; <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> suggested that he might be able to override computer systems, and others contemplated using several spies they had on the inside. Throughout the afternoon, people kept coming back to the failed strategy of storming the entrances, frustrating Lyme.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Eventually, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> put the idea forth that rather than taking the Nut, they ought to disable it. He and Beetee pointed out avalanche paths that they could strategically bomb, burying the entrances and all the people inside.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was ultimately decided that they would leave a <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> tunnel into the square open to give evacuees a chance at surrender. The plan proceeded, and the Nut's survivors fled to the city center, where the rebels were waiting for them.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>With prompting from <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> over an earpiece, Katniss was poised to give a speech to the Nut personnel when she suddenly rushed to the aid of a wounded man, shouting for rebels to hold their fire. He pulled a gun on her and demanded that she give him a reason not to shoot her. She replied that she couldn't, confusing him enough to make him pause. Despairing over all the pain the rebels and Capitol had caused each other, she lowered and even dropped her <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow</a> as she told him to shoot her. Continued prompting from Haymitch allowed her to turn her statement back around into a compelling speech about district unity against the Capitol. However, this ended when an unknown man in the crowd shot her.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath_of_the_bombing">Aftermath of the bombing</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBattle_of_District_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Katniss survived, but she suffered a ruptured spleen and her ribs were badly bruised. She was transported back to District 13 to recover. Meanwhile, District 2 became the last district to ally with the rebels.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The destruction of the Nut knocked out much of the Capitol's air force and logistical capabilities, dealing a crushing blow to its war effort.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This was the point where the war turned in favor of the rebels and set the stage for the rebels' <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">final assault</a> on the Capitol itself.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
