<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>On the twenty-fifth anniversary, as a reminder to the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> that their children were dying because of their choice to initiate violence, every <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> was made to hold an <a href="/wiki/Elections" title="Elections">election</a> and vote on the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> that who would represent it.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> relaying the details of the 25th Hunger Games<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The <b>25th Hunger Games</b> was the first <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> in the history of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, held 49 years prior to the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. The twist was that the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> had to vote in their <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> instead of <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaping</a> them by a lottery.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Summary">Summary</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F25th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Innovations">Innovations</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F25th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Dr. Gaul</a> served as a commentator alongside host <a href="/wiki/Lucky_Flickerman" class="mw-redirect" title="Lucky Flickerman">Lucky Flickerman</a> where she first coined the phrase, "<i>may the odds be EVER in your favor</i>", a saying which caught on and turned into a popular way to wish someone good luck during the Games. For this anniversary, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">gamemakers</a> also implemented the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot ride</a> through the streets in district-flavored costumes. Rather than scouting nature, a unique <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> was built for a single use for the first time, and the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> made its first appearance.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F25th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>After hearing about the twist, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> felt that this must have been worse than being chosen in the reaping, because it would mean being betrayed by your own neighbors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a> claimed that when they created the laws for the Games, they dictated that a Quarter Quell would be held every twenty-five years. It was made to seem that the twists for these special editions of the Games were written out at that time, with plans for "centuries of Hunger Games".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, the political convenience of the twist for the third Quarter Quell made Katniss doubt that it had truly been prepared so far in advance.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
