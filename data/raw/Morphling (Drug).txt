<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphling_%28drug%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Morphling is similar to the real-life <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/opiate" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:opiate">opiate</a> <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/analgesic" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:analgesic">analgesic</a> <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/morphine" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:morphine">morphine</a>, and "morphling" is likely a corruption of the drug's original name. It can be taken through intravenous injection<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and orally in the form of tablets<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or liquid.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Morphling is quite expensive, available only to those with connections to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. As a depressant, it dampens all sensation, including severe pain and regular emotion.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Use_in_treatment">Use in treatment</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphling_%28drug%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Morphling is most commonly used to treat severe pain, but since it also depresses emotion, it is sometimes prescribed as a mood stabilizer.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This is not the case for real-life morphine, as the risk of addiction is too severe.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Addiction">Addiction</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphling_%28drug%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Morphlings_reaping.png/revision/latest?cb=20140323224641" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The morphlings from District 6 at the reaping" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Morphlings_reaping.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140323224641" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="108" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Morphlings reaping.png" data-image-key="Morphlings_reaping.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Morphlings_reaping.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The morphlings from District 6 at <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the reaping</a></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Morphling is also a highly addictive substance. Addicts are known as "<a href="/wiki/Morphlings" title="Morphlings">morphlings</a>". Long-term abuse of the drug can result in intense weight loss and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaundice" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jaundice">yellowing of the skin</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which is indicative of liver damage. Withdrawal symptoms include tremors and vomiting.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphling_%28drug%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphling_%28drug%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Casca Highbottom, the dean at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, was often seen "doped up" on morphling, which he took orally as a liquid. His long-term abuse led to a shrunken physique and sagging skin.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He first tried it after <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> introduced him as the architect of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. Coriolanus later used Highbottom's morphling addiction to end his life; he scooped up rat <a href="/wiki/Poison" title="Poison">poison</a> from a back alley and squeezed it into a morphling bottle, which the dean would later pull from the trash and consume.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> was given morphling at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Hospital" title="Capitol Hospital">Capitol Hospital</a> after receiving a concussion in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_the_Capitol_Arena" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of the Capitol Arena">bombing of the Capitol Arena</a>. The drug muted any pain or joy he would have otherwise experienced.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> was later prescribed morphling, taken orally as Highbottom had, to stabilize his emotional outbursts.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphling_%28drug%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> mentions that the original recipe for nepenthe contained morphling, but that its addictive tendency got it banned. By the time of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, the drink was just "[g]rain alcohol covered with berries."<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphling_%28drug%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Mrs._Undersee" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Undersee">Mrs. Undersee</a>, the wife of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Undersee" title="Mayor Undersee">mayor</a>, received vials of morphling from the Capitol to treat her debilitating headaches.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She likely also used it to cope with the loss of her sister, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a> brought her mother's morphling to <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> to help treat <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> after he was viciously whipped by the new Head Peacekeeper, <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Both <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> were heavily addicted to morphling.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphling_%28drug%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> developed an addiction to morphling while hospitalized in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> for the torture she endured in the Capitol. When she was weaned off, she instead dipped into Katniss's supply. Katniss had been prescribed the drug for bruised ribs and a ruptured spleen she suffered in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_District_2" title="Battle of District 2">Battle of District 2</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Johanna later endured withdrawals so she could train alongside Katniss for the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> came up with the idea of using morphling to help <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> recover from his <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a>. He would receive a dose of the drug to calm him, then would have a memory brought up in order to retrain his fearful, agitated responses into more positive ones.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_14_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_14-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss was given another round of morphling after being burned in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="City Circle bombing">City Circle bombing</a>, and she developed an addiction.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She stayed on morphling tablets throughout her imprisonment for the assassination of <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>. She contemplated committing suicide by overdosing, but ultimately decided against it and overcame her addiction.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
