</p><p><b>Julianne Moore</b> is an American actress who portrayed President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">2</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJulianne_Moore%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Julianne began her career with a series of television roles. From 1985 to 1988, she was a regular in the soap opera <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_the_World_Turns" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:As the World Turns">As the World Turns</a></i>, earning a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytime_Emmy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Daytime Emmy">Daytime Emmy</a> for her performance. She made her film debut in 1990, and continued to play supporting roles throughout the early 1990s. Moore made her breakthrough with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Altman" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Robert Altman">Robert Altman</a> <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_Cuts" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Short Cuts">Short Cuts</a></i> (1993), followed by critically acclaimed performances in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanya_on_42nd_Street" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Vanya on 42nd Street">Vanya on 42nd Street</a></i> (1994) and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safe_(1995_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Safe (1995 film)">Safe</a></i> (1995). Starring roles in the blockbusters <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Months" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nine Months">Nine Months</a></i> (1995) and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_World:_Jurassic_Park" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Lost World: Jurassic Park">The Lost World: Jurassic Park</a></i> (1997) subsequently established her as a leading actress in Hollywood. She won an Oscar for her performance in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Still_Alice" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Still Alice">Still Alice</a></i> in 2015.
</p>
