<p>In the schoolwide assembly after the death of <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a> at the hands of her <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">tribute</a>, Hippocrata invited anyone with grief issues to visit her office, especially if they were having violent impulses towards themselves or others.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHippocrata_Lunt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her first name may be a play on <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hippocrates" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hippocrates">Hippocrates</a>, a Ancient Greek physician sometimes called the father of medicine. Literally translated, Hippocrates means "horsepower" (<i>híppos</i> meaning “horse” and <i>krátos</i> meaning “power”). 
</p><p>Her surname, <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/lunt">Lunt</a>, is a word for a slow-burning match or smoke from a tobacco pipe. 
</p>
