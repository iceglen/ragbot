<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Dark_Days"><a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Little is known of the district's role in the Dark Days, but when District 11 rebelled against <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, the loss of its crops would have contributed to starvation conditions in the city while it was under siege.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationship_with_the_Games">Relationship with the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the first ten years of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, District 11 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were considered "contenders" in the competition,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> presumably due to strength built up by hard physical labor. Sometime before the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a> killed a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a> in District 11, though he was never caught.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In addition to building strength, involvement in the district's industry also gave tributes knowledge of edible and medicinal plants.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

After Rue's death, Katniss wreathed her body in flowers. <p>At the time of the third Quarter Quell, the tributes from Districts <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, 7, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, and 11 were all involved in a rebel plan to protect Katniss and break out of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>. Soon after the plan was enacted, District 11 had seized control of transportation.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion"><a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Once Katniss's <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> after the hospital <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_8" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 8">bombing of District 8</a> aired across Panem, reinvigorated rebels were able to take control of Districts 3 and 11, with "inroads in several other districts as well".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The signature <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> from this district is a dark, crescent-shaped roll dotted with seeds.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Music is important to the people of District 11; they sing at home and at work. While in the treetops of the orchard, Rue sang a four-note tune to the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>, who would then copy it, in order to signal to the other workers below when it was quitting time.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Demographics">Demographics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Most characters from this district have been described with dark brown skin and eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> has explicitly confirmed that Rue and Thresh are African-American,<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so other characters described the same way almost certainly are as well. There are two known exceptions, one being <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a>, who has the same olive skin and straight black hair as people from <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> in District 12. The one feature that sets her apart is her golden-brown eyes, rather than the typical Seam gray.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The other exception is <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>, who looks identical to <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a> with her dark pigtail braids, gray eyes and presumably, olive skin. During the riot scene in the first film, there appears to be more racial diversity among the citizens of District 11.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Industry">Industry</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3><p>
District 11 specializes in agriculture. There are many <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nests in the district, causing workers to keep medical leaves on hand. In the orchards, small children are sent to high branches to pick the highest fruits. They work long hours from sunrise to sunset. During the harvest they often work until well after dark, using torchlight and night vision glasses. Crops are sent to the Capitol, and people in the district are not allowed to eat them.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In addition to tending the fields and orchards, District 11 is responsible for herds of dairy cattle.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 200px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/dd/Untitled3.png/revision/latest?cb=20140608143210" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The fence surrounding District 11." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/dd/Untitled3.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20140608143210" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="83" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Untitled3.png" data-image-key="Untitled3.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Untitled3.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The fence surrounding District 11.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Government">Government</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 11 is heavily policed. The fence surrounding the district is at least 35 feet high, topped with coils of barbed wire, with "enormous" metal plates lining to base of it to prevent anyone digging underneath. It is also guarded by evenly-spaced watchtowers manned by armed Peacekeepers.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The punitive response to even minor offenses is extreme. According to Rue, the mayor was so strict that anyone caught eating the crops would be publicly whipped. She also said that Peacekeepers once killed a mentally disabled boy named <a href="/wiki/Martin" title="Martin">Martin</a> for trying to keep a pair of night vision glasses to play with.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_Geography">Location and Geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 11 is located in the far southeast of North America, along the Gulf Coast. From west to east, its territory corresponds primarily to the U.S. states of Oklahoma, Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, and Georgia. District 11 borders four districts: District 12 (to the northeast), <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> (to the north), <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> (to the southwest), and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> (to the northwest).
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, District 11 is described as the fourth district from the east, after Districts 12, 8, and 6.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss noted it was comfortably warm in District 11 when the Victory Tour took place, whereas it was still snowing in District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This warm climate would be ideal for a lengthy growing season and provide an ideal climate for a large variety of crops, which ties well to the district's industry of agriculture.
</p><p>District 11 is very large. When Katniss and Peeta arrived for their Victory Tour, Katniss described the place as vast and endless, with things being spread out across great distances. On the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a>, they passed small communities of shacks; Katniss commented that "by comparison the houses in the Seam"— the poorest part of District 12— "are upscale". The dwellings were deserted at the time of their arrival, as everyone was out working on the harvest.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Town_square">Town square</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The train had to travel far to get from the fence to the main square, which was "ringed with run-down storefronts, most of which [were] abandoned". The square also housed the "huge marble structure" of the Justice Building, which had degraded over time.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>District 11's placement as among the poorest districts and the second highest numbering suggest that agriculture is the most dominant occupation in pre-industrial societies, and is heavily associated with slavery and low social class.</li>
<li>The racial demographics, overpolicing, and economic exploitation in District 11 are extremely reminiscent of both historical American slavery and institutionalized racism in the present day. This has been a frequent topic of discussion in academic literature around the trilogy and the films.
<ul><li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1"><i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</i></a><i>, </i>District 11's citizens are dressed in attire resembling that of the Antebellum South, further evoking the time of slavery.</li></ul></li>
<li>In the 74th Hunger Games, District 11 had both the tallest tribute (Thresh), and the smallest and youngest tribute (Rue).</li>
<li>All known tributes from 11 have names related to plants and agriculture.
<ul><li>Rue is the name of an herb with a small yellow flower, and Thresh is a reference to "threshing", part of processing harvested grain.</li>
<li>Seeder could be a reference to "seeding" or planting a field, and "chaff" is the outer hull of grain that is often removed during the refining process.</li>
<li>Dill is the name of a herb, and Reaper refers to "reaping", cutting grain for harvest.</li></ul></li>
<li>After being the 2nd - 4th districts (eventually the 2nd) to pry from the Capitol's rule, communications went out in Districts 1, 3, 4, and 7. Afterwards, District 11 sends food to rebelling districts in hopes of winning the war.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 11 has a cultivated acreage of 12 with a population of 138,546, making it the fourth largest district, in terms of population.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 11 claims 822,568 in tesserae, making it the second most impoverished district.
<ul><li>If the population is 138,546 and they claim 822,568 tesserae, it would equal to 5.937 tesserae per person, making it the 10th richest district.</li></ul></li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be cocoa brown (#36291f – <span style="background-color:#36291f; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 11's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be royal blue (#4169E1 - <span style="background-color:#4169E1; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear  for the 50th Hunger Games was dark green (#023020 - <span style="background-color:#023020; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
