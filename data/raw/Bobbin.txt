<p><b>Bobbin</b> was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> <a href="/wiki/Reaped" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaped">reaped</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Juno_Phipps" title="Juno Phipps">Juno Phipps</a>. During his <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interview</a> with Phipps, he impressed the public by explaining five different ways to kill someone with a sewing needle. He died during the night of Day 1 and finished in 13th place of the 24 tributes. He was preceded in death by <a href="/wiki/Dill" title="Dill">Dill</a>, the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, and followed in death by <a href="/wiki/Sol" title="Sol">Sol</a>, the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>. Bobbin was the first person <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> killed directly.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 132px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/af/Bobbin_reaping.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240215033025" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Bobbin at the reaping." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/af/Bobbin_reaping.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/132?cb=20240215033025" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="132" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Bobbin reaping.jpg" data-image-key="Bobbin_reaping.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Bobbin_reaping.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Bobbin at the reaping.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Bobbin grew up in District 8 and became his district's male tribute in the reaping for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He and the other tributes were taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> before the Games began, his mentor, <a href="/wiki/Juno_Phipps" title="Juno Phipps">Juno Phipps</a>, admitted that she'd been disappointed to get a District 8 tribute. However, Bobbin impressed her by telling her multiple ways he could kill somebody just by using a sewing needle.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the Games, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> snuck into the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> to perform a traditional <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> death ceremony on his assigned male tribute <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>'s dead body. Coriolanus Snow was sent into the arena to coax Sejanus out, but, as they exited, Bobbin leaped out from the shadows in which he was hiding and attacked them with a <a href="/wiki/Knife" class="mw-redirect" title="Knife">knife</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In defense, Coriolanus seized a board and beat Bobbin so violently that the surrounding area was covered in blood, dislodged one of the boy's eyes, and rendered his face so swollen and bruised it was unrecognizable, ending Bobbin's life.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Snow only found out about Bobbin's death from Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>. Although guilt was Snow's immediate feeling after learning that he had killed Bobbin, it changed after <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> reacted with horror at the thought of him being in the arena and commented "Poor <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray</a>." For the first time, he felt glad in the realization he killed Bobbin and the effect it would have on his tribute's chances. From Bobbin's death, Snow started to fully accept and understand the lessons being taught by Dr. Gaul involving the perceived reasoning, reality, and need for the Hunger Games along with the conclusion that humanity is defined by who has, how to keep, garner support, the enforcement, and survival of control. Through Bobbin's attack on Plinth and Snow—even after recognizing the two as the only mentors to publicly show empathy to the well-being of the tributes, along with how there was nothing to gain by their deaths since they were not in direct competition with them—did Snow truly grasp the depth of the districts' hate of him and all the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He also discovered, through his own reaction to Bobbin's attack, what Dr. Gaul meant by her opinion on human's inherent animalistic nature: that no matter who you are, where you live, or how much money you have, one's response to real and perceived threats will always be violence, not self-sacrifice. Bobbin's death furthered Snow's realization that to protect his and the Capitol's earned entitlement, power, and control, as well as to save the districts from themselves, the war would never be over. But by limiting hope and creating realistic expectations, it could be contained indefinitely.
</p><p>Since the events leading up to and following Bobbin's demise all occurred after all the mentors and attendants had left for the night and <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a>'s play-by-play recessed, the view inside the arena was limited and any movement hard to decipher without explanation. The arena itself contained little to no light other than the natural moonlight and the Capitol's view was only a single wide angle lens. Darkening an already dimmed stream could be explained away by clouds, so this eased the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>'s job of damage control and Bobbin's death was easily covered up.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Luckily, Lucky and the mentors were initially more confused on how Marcus' body was moved and less on Bobbin's grotesque body and death, other than his mentor Phipps. No explanation was given and his memoriam was quickly glossed over to instead celebrate the Games were officially half-way to crowning their winner, ignoring Phipps's objections and demands for answers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Bobbin's body was eventually moved by <a href="/wiki/Reaper" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaper">Reaper</a>, along with the other dead tributes and covered, in part, by a <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> flag<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> until the Games had its <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">Victor</a> and was then removed for the arena's cleaning and repair with the other 13 dead tributes who had died in the Games.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>When the gong rang, he ran towards the pile of weapons, but hid when he saw the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. At night, Bobbin saw <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> in the arena, then chased them to one of the exits. Seeing that Bobbin was holding a knife, Coriolanus grabbed a block of concrete and hit him until Bobbin had died. Bobbin placed 11th.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Coriolanus describes Bobbin as a boy "who looked about ten years old but had to be at least twelve."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film adaptation, Bobbin has short blond hair. His right arm ends in a stump. He is wearing a green shirt, blue trousers with patches and a brown wool vest decorated with metal buttons.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A bobbin is a type of spool used to hold thread or yarn.
</p>
