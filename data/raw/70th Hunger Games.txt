</p><p>The <b>70th Hunger Games</b>&#160;was an edition of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> that took place four years before the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_tributes">Known tributes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F70th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F70th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At some point during the Games, Annie saw the boy from her district get beheaded. Traumatized, she ran off by herself and hid from her competitors. An earthquake broke a dam, flooding most of the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>; however, Annie came from the fishing district, so she outlasted the other tributes because she was the best swimmer.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F70th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> couldn't recall ever seeing Annie at the Games again,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and when she was <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> five years later, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> described her as being "hysterical" before <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to take her place.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Katniss later personally met her, she noticed that Annie "[laughed] at odds points in the conversation or [dropped] out of it entirely".<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Annie also seemed to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissociation_(psychology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dissociation (psychology)">dissociate</a><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and/or hallucinate; <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> said she asked him when she was uncertain if something was real.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F70th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Katniss did not remember the 70th Hunger Games particularly well because it happened the summer after <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">her father</a> died, when she was just beginning to run the household.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>This also marked the first year that Katniss was eligible to have her name entered into the reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>The District 4 male was the second tribute known to be beheaded in the Games, with  <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> in the 50th Hunger Games in <a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a> being the other.
<ul><li>However, many characters were decapitated by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, including <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> and various members of <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>. Annie's husband, Finnick, was one of the mutts' victims.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
