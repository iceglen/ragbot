<p><b>Pedestals</b> were fire-resistant metal plates that <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> stood on during the countdown before a <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. All 24 pedestals were placed at equal distances from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. Tributes had to wait 60 seconds before the Games began, otherwise a mine would explode. These mines automatically disconnected after the gong sounded.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPedestal%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPedestal%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3a/Capitol_Arena_interior.png/revision/latest?cb=20230427235227" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tributes leaving their &quot;pedestals&quot; during the 10th Hunger Games" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3a/Capitol_Arena_interior.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230427235227" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="119" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol Arena interior.png" data-image-key="Capitol_Arena_interior.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_Arena_interior.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Tributes leaving their "pedestals" during the 10th Hunger Games</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the podiums were red circles with black numbers on them and were used from the first Games until the 10th Hunger Games. They surrounded the center of the amphitheater where the Capitol seal was located, but after the bombing of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, the center was covered by a mountain of concrete rubble and beams that fell from the ceiling when it collapsed. In the film, the tributes were led to the red circles with their respective numbers, followed by armed <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> ready to shoot the tributes if they moved before the gong sounded. From where <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> stood, she could see <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a> crying in fear to her right and <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a> looking at her with hatred. To her left, she saw <a href="/wiki/Dill" title="Dill">Dill</a> coughing from her illness, <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a> looking at the weapons, <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a> crying inconsolably, and <a href="/wiki/Teslee" title="Teslee">Teslee</a> being dragged to her place. She also saw <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a> on the other side of the rubble, hanging from concrete beams.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Tribute positions in the film, starting from Lucy Gray's left side: Lucy Gray (12), Dill (11), Tanner (10), Lamina (7), Teslee (3), empty podium at <a href="/wiki/Sabyn" title="Sabyn">Sabyn</a> (2), <a href="/wiki/Hy" title="Hy">Hy</a> (5), <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> (4), <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a> (8), empty podium at <a href="/wiki/Velvereen" title="Velvereen">Velvereen</a> (1), empty podium at <a href="/wiki/Ginnee" title="Ginnee">Ginnee</a> (6), <a href="/wiki/Reaper" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaper">Reaper</a> (11), Marcus (2) placed on the beams above his podium, <a href="/wiki/Jessup" class="mw-redirect" title="Jessup">Jessup</a> (12), <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> (6), <a href="/wiki/Sheaf" title="Sheaf">Sheaf</a> (9), <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> (7), <a href="/wiki/Panlo" title="Panlo">Panlo</a> (9), <a href="/wiki/Sol" title="Sol">Sol</a> (5), empty podium at <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">Facet</a> (1), Coral (4), <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a> (3), empty podium at <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a> (10), Wovey (8), and again Lucy Gray.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPedestal%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, which had twice as many tributes, the number of pedestals doubled to 48. These pedestals surrounded the Cornucopia, which was filled with weapons and supplies that could help the tributes during the Games.
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was being taken to the <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">Launch Room</a>, he saw the order of these rooms on his left. Four rooms with a 6, then four more rooms with a 7, then four with an 8... and so on until he reached the four rooms with an 11 and finally the four rooms with a 12. Once in the arena, Haymitch says that next to him (on the right) are the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>. <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> is on his right, then <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, then the other girl from 1, <a href="/wiki/Carat" title="Carat">Carat</a>, and then the other boy from 1, <a href="/wiki/Loupe" title="Loupe">Loupe</a>. After them are the four tributes from District 2, the four tributes from District 3, and the four tributes from District 4. If we follow the order of the list Haymitch saw during the <a href="/wiki/Tribute_parade" class="mw-redirect" title="Tribute parade">tribute parade</a>, we can conclude that this order was used to place them. First, a girl is placed, then a boy, then another girl, and finally another boy. Therefore, the position of the tributes is easy to figure out.

</p><p>In the film, as this was the second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, they had quite striking colors, such as the red platform and the golden metal circle. These, in turn, had flowers of different colors in front of them, forming arrows that pointed to the Cornucopia, which was shaped like a golden, silver, and purple crown. From the new trailer, we know that the position of the pedestals is different from that in the book, as all the tributes are mixed together. We find Haymitch between Silka (to his right, followed by Clayton) and Panache (to his left), who throws his cape on the ground, followed by a girl from District 7 (Autumn). In another shot, we see Lou Lou and, to her left, a male tribute from District 11 (Hull or Tile).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p>In the book, Katniss mentions that tributes surround the golden Cornucopia and that Peeta is five podiums to her right.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film version, the pedestals do not form a circle, like in other iterations of the Games, but a semicircle. When Katniss emerges from her launch tube, she looks to her left and sees <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Tippet" title="Tippet">female tribute from District 4</a>, the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">male tribute from District 8</a>, the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">female tribute from District 6</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>. To her right, she sees <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Stele" title="Stele">male tribute from District 7</a> on their pedestals. Four tribute actors didn't participate in the initial bloodbath recording: the girl from 8, both from 9, and the girl from 10. Because of this, only 20 pedestals were added instead of 24. It can be assumed that of those four tributes, two tributes were standing on each end of the semicircle. <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/Brutus_and_district_5.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140207232540" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Brutus and the District 5 male" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Brutus and district 5.jpg" data-image-key="Brutus_and_district_5.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/Brutus_and_district_5.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140207232540" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/Brutus_and_district_5.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140207232540" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Brutus and the District 5 male" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/Brutus_and_district_5.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140207232540" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Brutus and district 5.jpg" data-image-key="Brutus_and_district_5.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/Brutus_and_district_5.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140207232540" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Brutus_and_district_5.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Brutus and the District 5 male</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The pedestals are on the water surrounding the Cornucopia. Between each pair of pedestals is a path of rocks separating the sectors of the arena. In the books, <a href="/wiki/Woof" title="Woof">Woof</a> stands on Katniss's right side.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Katniss climbs out of her tube, she frantically scans the arena and sees <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a>, <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cecelia" title="Cecelia">Cecelia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Finnick" class="mw-redirect" title="Finnick">Finnick</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>, the <a href="/wiki/District_9_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 female (74th HG)">District 9 female</a>, and the male tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 male (75th HG)">Districts 5</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 10 male (75th HG)">10</a> on their pedestals.
</p><p>Tribute positions in the film, starting from Katniss's left side: Brutus (2), District 5 male, Woof (8), <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a> (7), <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 female (75th HG)">District 6 female</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a> (3), <a href="/wiki/Johanna" class="mw-redirect" title="Johanna">Johanna</a> (7), <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> (3), <a href="/wiki/Cecelia" title="Cecelia">Cecelia</a> (8), <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">District 9 male</a>, District 10 male, Peeta (12), <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a> (4), <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 male (75th HG)">District 6 male</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_10_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 10 female (75th HG)">District 10 female</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sylva_Mayleaf" title="Sylva Mayleaf">District 9 female</a>, Finnick (4), Chaff (11), District 5 female tribute, <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a> (11), <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> (2), <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> (1), Cashmere (1) and again Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>These positions make sense, since putting all the rebel tributes together would more easily allowed them to join forces and then flee. On the other hand, putting all the Careers together close to Katniss may be an indication of Snow's intention to kill her. They also coincide with the positions found throughout the bloodbath, for example, the District 10 male tribute near Peeta and Mags watching their fight. It would not have made sense for that tribute to run to the other side to attack Peeta or Seeder and Woof, who are the first to fall after the District 5 male tribute because their podiums are closer to the Careers. And for Beetee, Wiress, and Blight's pedestals to be near Johanna's pedestal, as she mentions that Beetee was wounded in the fight and that they all fled together.
</p>
