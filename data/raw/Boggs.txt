<p><b>Boggs</b> was <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">President Coin</a>'s second-in-command. Boggs' rank was colonel and he was Head of Security of District 13. When <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> assumed the role of the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_Deal" title="Mockingjay Deal">Mockingjay</a>, he became her protector.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBoggs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Working_with_the_Mockingjay">Working with the Mockingjay</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBoggs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the beginning of Second Rebellion, Boggs served as President Coin's second-in-command. When Katniss fled the room after seeing <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s interview in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, Boggs tried to stop her. However, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> got in his way and was caught in the nose by Boggs' elbow.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During a meeting <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> had organized, Boggs was one of the first to speak up about a moment when Katniss had moved him.
</p><p>Boggs was assigned to guard Katniss while she filmed <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a>, and they planned to go to <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> for their first shoot. After their run-in with <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> in his underwear shortly before leaving, Katniss decided that she liked Boggs.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When they arrived at District 8, they went to one of the hospitals and decided to comfort some of the patients. Boggs kept a close eye on Katniss and made sure nothing happened to her. When the Capitol attacked the hospital, Boggs tried to get Katniss to safety, but she and Gale climbed up to fight, and Gale broke Boggs' nose with his boot.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the attack was over and Katniss made a brief speech, Boggs carried Katniss back to the <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a>, and they returned to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> to receive medical treatment. The group met in the command room and discussed the complete propo. After they finished, they decided to allow Katniss go back in combat and shoot more propos when she had fully recovered. Boggs spoke with the head of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">rebellion</a> to discuss more topics and objectives they had to deal with.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Boggs assisted the head of the rebellion in evacuating the entire district to safe bunkers after Peeta warned them of the bombing on District 13 by the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_10_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_10-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Boggs, Katniss, Gale and the others resurfaced to the heavily damaged remains to shoot more propagandas, but Katniss and Finnick broke down after being unable to take it. Boggs and President Coin formed a rescue team that he would lead to save Peeta, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>, <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> from the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Later, Boggs knocked Peeta out after he tried to strangle Katniss upon his return.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He went with Katniss and Gale to <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, where they all met <a href="/wiki/Lyme" title="Lyme">Lyme</a>. The group argued over the idea of bombing <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a> while it was filled with people, and Boggs gave them the choice of blowing them up or giving them a chance of escape. He also updated them of Peeta's condition after they retreated to the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Boggs was the leader of <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>, the sharpshooter's squad, and went with them to the Capitol. The group trained and filmed for the propaganda. When <a href="/wiki/Leeg_2" title="Leeg 2">Leeg 2</a> was killed by a <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pod</a>, Peeta was sent as a replacement.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When Katniss Everdeen expressed her concerns, Boggs informed her that President Coin had always seen her as a thorn in her side and a threat to her power and agenda, although she was willing to tolerate it as she needed Katniss to rally the districts to rebel against the Capitol. With victory being all but assured for the rebels, Coin now saw Katniss as a threat, as she would hold considerable influence in choosing the new leader of Panem after the war. <sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBoggs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>Don't trust them. Don't go back. Kill Peeta.  Do what you came to do.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Boggs' last words<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 320px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f0/Boggsdead.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140713201536" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Boggs' corpse in a street" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f0/Boggsdead.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/320?cb=20140713201536" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="194" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Boggsdead.jpg" data-image-key="Boggsdead.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Boggsdead.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Boggs' corpse in a street</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>As the <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Star Squad</a> cleared a street of <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pods</a>, Boggs accidentally stepped back onto a tile and had his legs blown off by a mine pod.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Within a building, as they sheltered from the effects of yet another pod, Boggs transferred the <a href="/wiki/Holo" title="Holo">Holo</a> over to Katniss' control. He also warned her not to trust them, and encouraged her to continue on her mission to assassinate <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>. Although Katniss never found out who he was referring to in regards to not trusting "them", it was most likely President Coin and her supporters. Boggs died from blood loss, and his corpse was left behind when the Star Squad moves on. The <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> found his body, and before realizing what had really happened, they presumed that the others were dead as well.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBoggs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Despite his status as a high-ranking member of the government of District 13 and being a close associate of President Alma Coin, Boggs was a respectable individual who genuinely believed in the cause of freedom and disapproved of Coin's power-hungry ambitions. He had a huge respect for Katniss Everdeen, shown in his willingness to lay down his life to protect her. He also warned her about the fact that Coin had never liked her and viewed her as a thorn in her side, and by the end of the war, she would be seen as a threat to Coin's lust for power. Just before succumbing to his injuries, Boggs warned Katniss not to trust "them", most likely referring to President Coin and her allies. He allowed Katniss to take control of the Holo moments before his death, showing his absolute trust in her.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBoggs%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the novel, he was in his mid-forties, with close-cropped gray hair and blue eyes. He was also noted as having incredible posture.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>However, in the film, he was bald with brown eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
