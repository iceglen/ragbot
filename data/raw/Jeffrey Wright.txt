</p><p><b>Jeffrey Wright</b> is an American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">2</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJeffrey_Wright%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Before Wright started his career in the film business, he was seen in the theaters. In 1990, he got his first film role in the movie <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presumed_Innocent_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Presumed Innocent (film)">Presumed Innocent</a></i>. Later, he appeared in movies such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremely_Loud_%26_Incredibly_Close_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Extremely Loud &amp; Incredibly Close (film)">Extremely Loud &amp; Incredibly Close</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ides_of_March_(2011_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Ides of March (2011 film)">The Ides of March</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_Code" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Source Code">Source Code</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:W. (film)">W.</a></i>, but is best known for playing CIA agent <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Leiter" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Felix Leiter">Felix Leiter</a> in the James Bond films <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casino_Royale_(2006_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Casino Royale (2006 film)">Casino Royale</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_of_Solace" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Quantum of Solace">Quantum of Solace</a></i>, and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Time_to_Die" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No Time to Die"><i>No Time to Die</i></a>. Wright also appeared in the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:House (TV series)">House</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boardwalk_Empire" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Boardwalk Empire">Boardwalk Empire</a></i>.
</p>
