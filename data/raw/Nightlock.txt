<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Not these, Katniss. Never these. They're nightlock. You'll be dead before they reach your stomach.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212; <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">Mr. Everdeen</a> to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a>, on the dangers of consuming nightlock<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Nightlock </b>is a wild plant with extremely poisonous berries. Katniss first heard about this plant from her father, who cautioned her that anyone who ate nightlock would be dead before the berries even reached their stomach. Although this phrasing may be an exaggeration, the plant's effects are indisputable: <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a> indeed died very quickly after consuming nightlock. The only known sources for these deadly berries are the land around Districts <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">13</a>, though they were also featured in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNightlock%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNightlock%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 320px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/Foxfacedead.png/revision/latest?cb=20240223071644" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta and Katniss find Foxface&#39;s body after she ate the berries." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/Foxfacedead.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/320?cb=20240223071644" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="212" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Foxfacedead.png" data-image-key="Foxfacedead.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Foxfacedead.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peeta and Katniss find Foxface's body after she ate the berries.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Foxface, the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, stole nightlock berries that Peeta had gathered, and died after consuming them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peeta himself had mistaken them for edible berries<sup id="cite_ref-THG_24_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_24-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> (possibly the blueberry-like berries that <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> had shown Katniss when they had formed an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss stopped him from eating any, remembering her father's warning, though she and Peeta kept some in a bag in case they could trick <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> into eating them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_24_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_24-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>On the last morning of the Games, after Cato's death, the previous rule change allowing two <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> was revoked. Instead of battling against Peeta, however, Katniss pulled out the nightlock berries, which she and Peeta threatened to consume and leave the games with no victor at all. Before they could swallow them, <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> took back the change, and both were announced as victors of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Later_uses">Later uses</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNightlock%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Head <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a> later lost his life for allowing both Katniss and Peeta to emerge as victors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> His specific manner of death is never explained in the novels, though near the end of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i> film, he was locked in a room containing only a bowl of nightlock berries. The shot ended before anything else happened, though the implications are clear. This cruel twist was likely ordered by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, soldiers from District 13, including Katniss herself, were given specifically formulated poison capsules to swallow in the event they were captured. Dubbed "nightlock", they were known to have the same effect as the berries themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Nightlock was also the keyword for the <a href="/wiki/Holo" title="Holo">Holo</a> used by <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> during their infiltration of the Capitol. When said three times in a row, the word caused the Holo to self-destruct, blowing up everything in a five-yard radius.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Real_plants_similar_to_nightlock">Real plants similar to nightlock</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNightlock%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 220px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/77/Belladonna.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200926160504" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Deadly nightshade (atropa belladonna)" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/77/Belladonna.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20200926160504" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="165" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Belladonna.jpg" data-image-key="Belladonna.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Belladonna.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Deadly nightshade (<i>atropa belladonna</i>)</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The name "nightlock" is derived from two real-life plants: <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atropa_belladonna" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Atropa belladonna">Atropa belladonna</a></i>, also known as deadly nightshade, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conium_maculatum" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Conium maculatum">Conium maculatum</a></i>, also known as poison hemlock, both of which are extremely poisonous. Deadly nightshade berries are similar in appearance to nightlock, and both nightlock and real-life hemlock are commonly mistaken for edible plants—nightlock for Rue's berries and hemlock for wild carrots. Seneca Crane's execution by nightlock berries in the film is reminiscent of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socrates" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Socrates">Socrates</a>' execution by hemlock, after he was tried for "corrupting the youth" of Athens, as well as "questioning the gods".
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNightlock%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Nightlock is said to look like an edible type of berry Rue had found in the arena, which may be blueberries, since they fit the same description.</li>
<li>In the making of the first movie, the stylists were said to be annoyed with the berries, as they stained the actors' clothing.</li></ul>
