</p><p><b>Bruno Gunn </b> is an Italian-American actor who played <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. He is best known for his popular roles in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Office_(U.S._TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Office (U.S. TV series)">The Office</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Teacher" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Bad Teacher">Bad Teacher</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbie:_Fully_Loaded" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Herbie: Fully Loaded">Herbie: Fully Loaded</a></i>.
</p>
