</p><p><b>"Safe&#160;&amp; Sound"</b> is the first single released from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Songs_from_District_12_and_Beyond" title="The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond">The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond</a></i>. The song is performed and written by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Taylor Swift">Taylor Swift</a> and features <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Civil_Wars" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Civil Wars">The Civil Wars</a>. This is one of two songs by Taylor Swift that were written for the film; the other song being "<i><a href="/wiki/Eyes_Open" title="Eyes Open">Eyes Open</a></i>."&#160;Although the song was written for the film, it isn't featured in the film, but in the credits.
</p><p>Taylor has since re-recorded many of her songs that were originally written and published prior to 2018, "Safe &amp; Sound" and "Eyes Open" included. Both songs, now bearing the title of "(Taylor's Version)," were released at midnight on March 17th 2023 after being teased by Swift the previous day.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Music_video">Music video</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSafe_%2526_Sound%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The music video for <i>Safe &amp; Sound</i>, which features the Civil Wars, premiered on Monday, February 13, 2012, during "MTV First:Taylor Swift". The video features Swift walking barefoot through the woods, wearing a long white gown. In between scenes The Civil Wars are singing in front of a fireplace, as they accompany Swift. At one point in the video, Swift is shown holding a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a>.
</p>
