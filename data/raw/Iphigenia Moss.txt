</p><p><b>Iphigenia Moss</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She was assigned the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sol" title="Sol">Sol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FIphigenia_Moss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her father oversaw the Agriculture Department, which controlled the flow of food around <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. She was considered "forgettable," and despite her father's job, always seemed to be on the verge of malnutrition. She gave her school lunches to classmates and even blacked out on occasion. <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a> told <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> that this was Iphigenia's revenge on her father, but did not provide any further details.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Sol" title="Sol">Sol</a> emerged in the arena, Iphigenia began to send every bit of food she could, but her tribute was killed by a <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">trident</a> to the throat from <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>, the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>. Afterwards, she spoke briefly with <a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a> of <a href="/wiki/Capitol_News" title="Capitol News">Capitol News</a>, referring to her tribute as being named either "Sol, or maybe Sal" and saying that she had a funny accent. In response, Lepidus congratulated her on getting her tribute to the second half and mistakenly addressed her as "Albina."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FIphigenia_Moss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iphigenia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Iphigenia">Iphigenia</a> was the eldest daughter of King <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agamemnon" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Agamemnon">Agamemnon</a>, who led the campaign of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_War" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Trojan War">Trojan War</a>. After he hunted one of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemis" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Artemis">Artemis</a>'s sacred stags, the goddess prevented the troops from reaching Troy unless King Agamemnon killed Iphigenia as a human sacrifice to the gods.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FIphigenia_Moss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Iphigenia has an unspecified <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/eating_disorder" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:eating disorder">eating disorder</a> that most closely resembles <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/anorexia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:anorexia">anorexia</a>.
<ul><li>Given that Clemensia characterizes Iphigenia's condition as "revenge" on her father, it's possible that he was abusing her.</li></ul></li></ul>
