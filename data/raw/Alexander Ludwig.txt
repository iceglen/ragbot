</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlexander_Ludwig%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He is best known for his lead role in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_to_Witch_Mountain" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Race to Witch Mountain">Race to Witch Mountain</a></i>. Ludwig also composed a single entitled "Liv It Up (Teenage Wasteland)." The song is about his euro trip with his high school friends. He also starred in the film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grown_Ups_2" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Grown Ups 2">Grown Ups 2</a></i>, portraying Braden.
</p><p>Ludwig has also worked as a model for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abercrombie" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Abercrombie">Abercrombie</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollister_Co." class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hollister Co.">Hollister</a>.
</p><p>Ludwig graduated from the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:University of Southern California">University of Southern California</a>, where he studied theatre.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> His mother, Sharlene, is a former actress and his father, Harald, is a businessman and a former co-chairman of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lions_Gate_Entertainment" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lions Gate Entertainment">Lions Gate Entertainment</a>, the distributor and one of the studios behind <i>The Hunger Games</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He is best known for his acting in the History-Series <a href="https://vikings.fandom.com/wiki/" class="extiw" title="w:c:vikings:">Vikings</a>. Since season 2 he represented Bjorn Ironside, son of Ragnar and Lagertha. He is still appearing in season 3, 4, and 5.
</p><p>He has also starred in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/When_the_Game_Stands_Tall" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:When the Game Stands Tall">When the Game Stands Tall</a>, and was in the 2013 film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lone_Survivor_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lone Survivor (film)">Lone Survivor</a></i>.
</p>
