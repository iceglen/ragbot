<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMackenzie_Lintz%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mackenzie comes from a family of actors, including her mother, Kelly Lintz, and her three younger siblings, Madison, Matt, and Macsen Lintz.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  She attended Covenant Christian Academy in Cumming, Georgia. Mackenzie, a lifelong Auburn fan, enrolled in 2014 as a freshman at Auburn University in Auburn, Alabama.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMackenzie_Lintz%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lintz first auditioned for the role of Mattie Ross in the remake of <i>True Grit</i> by the Coen brothers. She was cast in an episode of Lifetime's <i>Drop Dead Diva</i> as well. 
</p><p>She is the second last actor to be cast for the role of a tribute who served a nontrivial role in the books, and is excluded from the bloodbath scenes despite surviving the bloodbath. <a href="/wiki/Imanol_Yepez-Frias" title="Imanol Yepez-Frias">Imanol Yepez-Frias</a>, who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">District 9 male</a>, is doubled by <a href="/wiki/Sam_Ly" title="Sam Ly">Sam Ly</a>, who also portrayed a <a href="/wiki/District_7_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 7 male (74th HG)">tribute of trivial role</a>.
</p><p>In May 2013, she auditioned for the role of Norrie Calvert-Hill in another book-to feature remake, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Dome_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Under the Dome (TV series)">Under the Dome</a></i>, and was subsequently cast, portraying the role through all three seasons of the series.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In February 2014, she was nominated for the Best Performance by a Younger Actor in a Television Series award at the 40th Saturn Awards.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After filming Under The Dome, Mackenzie went on to be in two more films (<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love,_Simon">Love, Simon</a> and Flying Cars) before retiring from acting. She is now pursuing a career in law.
</p>
