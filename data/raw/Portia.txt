<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Portia was a native of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, and the stylist for <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>, Peeta. This was her partner <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a>'s first year as a stylist. She may have also been new to her profession because, according to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, newcomers often get assigned to 12 because it's "the least desirable <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>". However, Cinna specifically asked to style District 12;<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> it is unclear whether or not this was the case for Portia as well. 
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Chariot_rides"><a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/THGChariot1.png/revision/latest?cb=20240728075953" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Portia and Cinna at chariot parade" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/THGChariot1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240728075953" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="104" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="THGChariot1.png" data-image-key="THGChariot1.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:THGChariot1.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption"> Portia and Cinna at the chariot parade</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>According to Cinna, he and Portia decided to dress the District 12 tributes in complementary <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">chariot costumes</a>. They felt that the district's usual <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> jumpsuits were overdone, and they both saw it as their job to make Katniss and Peeta "unforgettable".<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Once Peeta's <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> finished up, Portia got him into his outfit, identical to the one Katniss wore: a black unitard covering him from ankle to neck, knee-high leather boots, and a fluttering cape of red, orange, and yellow with a matching headpiece.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Katniss described Portia and the prep team as "absolutely giddy with excitement". When the tributes stepped onto their chariot, Portia and Cinna adjusted their positions and capes to make them absolutely perfect. Cinna then lit the capes and headdresses with a torch of synthetic flame he and Portia had devised together. Once the chariots pulled into the Training Center, Portia and Cinna both helped the tributes down and removed their capes and headdresses. Portia then put the fire out with "some kind of spray from a canister".<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Afterwards, Portia stood with Cinna and Peeta on a balcony overlooking the city, and at dinner, Katniss reflected that the stylists seemed to have a "civilizing effect" on <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a> <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>. Portia and Cinna stayed after the meal to rewatch the opening ceremonies, and when Haymitch asked whose idea it was for Katniss and Peeta to hold hands in their chariot, Portia replied that it was Cinna's.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

</p><h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training"><a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">Training</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Portia and Cinna dressed Katniss and Peeta in identical clothes; on the first day, they had both tributes wear black pants, leather shoes, and long-sleeved burgundy tunics. They weren't present at dinner for the first two days of training,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but they dined with the District 12 team on the third day. Like everyone else, Portia stopped eating when Katniss confessed to shooting an arrow at the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> during her private session. She eventually reassured Katniss that even if they gave her a low <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a>, "no one pays much attention to the bad or mediocre ones," and that some might even assume she'd strategically scored poorly on purpose. Once Katniss's score was announced as an 11, Portia congratulated her along with the rest.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews"><a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">Interviews</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>For Peeta's interview with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, Portia designed a black suit with flame accents.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Afterwards, Portia reassured Katniss that her reaction to Peeta's love confession had been perfect. She suggested that if Katniss had known, "it wouldn't have read as real", and the way Katniss blushed made her think Katniss could love him, too. The team sat down to eat dinner then, but Portia had to lead Peeta off to get medical treatment for his cut-up hands.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The next morning, although not shown, Portia would have ridden with Peeta in a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> to the site of the Games. She then equipped him with his <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena uniform</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">Launch Room</a> before he was lifted into the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="74th Hunger Games arena">arena</a> above.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After Katniss and Peeta were declared the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a>, given medical attention, and cleared to leave the hospital, Katniss met with the District 12 team; she noted that Effie, Haymitch, and Cinna were all present to greet her, with the exception of Portia. This gave Katniss a bad feeling. Worried that Peeta could be dead, she blurted out her concern; however, Haymitch immediately confirmed that Peeta was fine and that Portia was with him.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>For the official recap, Portia dressed Peeta in black pants and sturdy black boots; he also had a slim metal cane to help him keep his balance on his new prosthetic leg,<sup id="cite_ref-:2_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but as medical equipment, it's unclear whether Portia had a hand in its design. After the prep teams and Effie were introduced to the audience, Portia and Cinna were brought out on stage to "huge cheers". The next morning, Portia pulled Peeta away to do a fitting for the new victors' interview. She put him in a red and white outfit. There wasn't much time afterwards for Katniss and Peeta to say goodbye to their stylists, though Katniss mentioned that they would see them again in "a few months" for the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour">Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Although not shown, Portia prepared Peeta for his first televised appearance of the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a> in District 12. She then accompanied him, Katniss, and the rest of the District 12 team on their cross-country train journey. They all ate dinner together that evening,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and the next morning, she and Peeta's prep team were assumed to be sleeping in because he didn't need to be prepped for their appearance in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> the same way Katniss did. Portia was awake and eating lunch with Peeta, Effie, Haymitch, and Cinna by the time Katniss joined them.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Portia and the rest of the District 12 team waited inside District 11's Justice Building, watching on a wall-mounted screen as Katniss and Peeta gave their speeches outside. According to Effie, the screen had gone to static just after Katniss's speech, and then they'd heard what Haymitch had identified as gunfire.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Unbeknownst to them, an <a href="/wiki/Unidentified_District_11_citizen" title="Unidentified District 11 citizen">elderly man</a> from the crowd had just been shot in the head.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss believed Effie was being kept out of the loop, but she said she wouldn't be surprised if Portia and Cinna knew what had occurred outside. Before dinner that evening, Effie proclaimed herself "something of an expert in architectural design", and Portia said she'd heard that, just to fill in the gap in conversation.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the party at the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President's mansion">President's mansion</a> in the Capitol, Portia introduced Katniss and Peeta to <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, the new Head Gamemaker. At nearly midnight, Effie went around the party to collect Katniss, Peeta, Haymitch, Cinna, and Portia, in order to make it to their train punctually by one.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Parade">Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>This year, Portia once again dressed Peeta in an outfit identical to Katniss's: a fitted black jumpsuit covering him from the neck down, and a metallic half-crown, like a heavy, black version of the gold one he'd been given at his and Katniss's <a href="/wiki/Victor_crowning" class="mw-redirect" title="Victor crowning">victor crowning</a>. Upon pressing a button on the inside of the sleeve at the wrist, the outfit would glow with "a soft golden light... gradually transforming to the orange-red of burning coal". According to Cinna, he and Portia spent "a lot of hours" watching fires in order to get the desired effect.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Peeta told Katniss that Portia said the two of them were "to be very above it all. No waving or anything." Once the chariots pulled into the Training Center, Katniss seemed relieved to see Cinna and Portia waiting for them there, "pleased with [Katniss and Peeta's] performance".<sup id="cite_ref-:5_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The stylists were both present at dinner that evening, and they watched the opening ceremonies on television with the rest of the team, same as the year previous. When the program was done, Katniss stood up and thanked Cinna and Portia for their "amazing work".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training_2">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>On the third and final day of training, Portia and Cinna once again joined the team for dinner. When Peeta said he'd used the dyes from the <a href="/wiki/Camouflage" title="Camouflage">camouflage</a> station in his private session, but "not exactly camouflage", Portia asked him what he'd used them to do. Peeta admitted to painting a portrait of <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, Katniss's twelve-year-old <a href="/wiki/Ally" class="mw-redirect" title="Ally">ally</a> who had died the previous year, to hold the Gamemakers accountable for killing her. As Haymitch and Effie expressed fear and concern for him, Katniss noticed that Portia and Cinna were silent, but that they had very serious expressions on their faces.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss spoke up and announced that she'd hung a dummy and painted <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a>'s name on it. After some discussion, Peeta remarked that "You'd have thought we'd planned it," to which Portia replied, "Didn't you?" As she did so, Katniss described her as "[pressing] her eyelids closed as if she's warding off a very bright light." Portia was also present for the announcement of training scores afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews_2">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Once Katniss was dressed for her interview, she and Cinna met Effie, Haymitch, Peeta, and Portia at the elevator. Portia had dressed Peeta in an "elegant" tuxedo with white gloves, in order to go with Katniss's wedding gown.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games_2">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The next morning, as Katniss boarded her hovercraft to the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>, she realized that she hadn't said goodbye to Portia and told Cinna so. He replied that he would tell his partner; however, he would never get the chance, as he would be beaten and dragged away by Peacekeepers in Katniss's Launch Room.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Although not seen, Portia presumably would have collected Peeta at dawn and rode with him to his own Launch Room, then given him his uniform like normal. It's unknown whether she was apprehended or harmed at this time the same way Cinna was.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>About five or six weeks after the end of the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Peeta and other captured victors were <a href="/wiki/Infiltration_of_the_Capitol" title="Infiltration of the Capitol">rescued from imprisonment in the Capitol</a>. According to Plutarch Heavensbee, Portia and Peeta's prep team were <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executed</a> by <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> on live television that night. Hearing this, Katniss was so overwhelmed with guilt and grief that she had to be sedated.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Portia, much like Cinna, was very level-headed, encouraging, and comforting. For example, she reassured Katniss about her training score and affirmed that Katniss's reaction to Peeta's love confession had been convincing. Based on her demeanor, she didn't seem to be as out of touch as many other Capitol citizens, which may be why she and Cinna appeared to work so closely and so well together.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPortia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The name <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Portia">Portia</a> comes from the Latin name Porcia— the feminine form of the family name <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Porcius#Latin">Porcius</a>— likely derived from "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/porcus#Latin">porcus</a>", meaning pig.
</p><p>She was likely named after <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porcia_(wife_of_Brutus)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Porcia (wife of Brutus)">Porcia</a>, who was the second wife of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Junius_Brutus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Marcus Junius Brutus">Brutus</a>, one of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Caesar" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Julius Caesar">Julius Caesar</a>'s most notable assassins. In <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_Lives" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Parallel Lives">Parallel Lives</a></i>, the historian <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plutarch" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Plutarch">Plutarch</a> said that Brutus refused to tell his wife about the murder plot for fear that she would give something away under torture. In order to prove herself, Porcia endured an untreated, self-inflicted wound on her thigh; when she overcame the pain, she returned to her husband and bade him to tell her the truth. However, they were interrupted before Brutus could, and the plot was carried out without her having been informed.
</p><p>The narrative of Porcia and Brutus could be tied back to Portia and her partner Cinna, since he had ties to the rebellion, while the extent of Portia's own involvement is unknown.
</p>
