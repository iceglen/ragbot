<p>In <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, an <b>execution</b> was the state-sanctioned killing of citizens by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>, typically by hanging or by firearm, as retribution for a perceived crime. This practice is also known as <a href="/wiki/Capital_punishment" title="Capital punishment">capital punishment</a>. Following the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, the power to carry out executions was transferred over to the rebels.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FExecution%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/9c/Capitol_Arena_exterior.png/revision/latest?cb=20230427235043" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Exterior of the Capitol Arena" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/9c/Capitol_Arena_exterior.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230427235043" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="122" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol Arena exterior.png" data-image-key="Capitol_Arena_exterior.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_Arena_exterior.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Exterior of the Capitol Arena</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> staged high-profile executions of rebels in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, which would later be appropriated for the first ten annual <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> There were also many brutal executions after the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a> was signed.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FExecution%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the lead-up to the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>, <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a>, was shot by Peacekeepers after killing her <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">Facet</a> and <a href="/wiki/Velvereen" title="Velvereen">Velvereen</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> were shot dead while trying to escape the Capitol Arena.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Arlo_Chance.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231222170315" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Arlo Chance at his execution" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Arlo_Chance.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231222170315" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="135" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Arlo Chance.jpg" data-image-key="Arlo_Chance.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Arlo_Chance.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Arlo Chance at his execution.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Three formal executions took place in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, the first being the execution of ex-soldier <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a>. After killing three people in the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_the_District_12_mines" title="Bombings">bombing of District 12 coal mines</a>, he was sentenced to death by hanging. At the <a href="/wiki/Hanging_tree" title="Hanging tree">hanging tree</a>, he called for his lover, <a href="/wiki/Lil" title="Lil">Lil</a>, to run.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> His execution inspired <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> to write <a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">"The Hanging Tree" song</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Lil_execution.webp/revision/latest?cb=20231222165351" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Lil at her execution" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="135" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Lil execution.webp" data-image-key="Lil_execution.webp" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Lil_execution.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231222165351" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Lil_execution.webp/revision/latest?cb=20231222165351" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Lil at her execution" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Lil_execution.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231222165351" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="135" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Lil execution.webp" data-image-key="Lil_execution.webp" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/48/Lil_execution.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231222165351" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Lil_execution.webp" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Lil's execution</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Lil was later executed for treason as well, alongside Peacekeeper <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, who had been planning to run away from the districts. The two were described as "weak with terror"; Lil's legs were shaking so badly that she had to be hauled up by Peacekeepers. Sejanus first called out to his friend <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, who had betrayed him to this fate, and his last act was to cry out for <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">his mother</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Spruce" title="Spruce">Spruce</a> was hanged alongside his sister and Sejanus.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FExecution%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The winter after the <a href="/wiki/46th_Hunger_Games" title="46th Hunger Games">46th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Clay_Chance" title="Clay Chance">Clay Chance</a> was sentenced to be hanged in the gallows for an unknown crime. The night before his execution, someone filed halfway through the rope, so when they tried to hang him in front of a crowd the next day, the rope snapped. Clay fell to the ground, where he was shot dead by "a dozen Peacekeeper bullets".<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>A witness in town reported that they saw twelve-year-old <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> leaving the square, so she was taken to the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers&#39; base">Peacekeepers' base</a> for questioning. However, Clay's sister <a href="/wiki/Binnie_Chance" title="Binnie Chance">Binnie Chance</a> claimed responsibility. She had been living "on borrowed time for a year" due to a heart condition, so three days after being taken into custody, Binnie died in her cell and Lenore Dove was released.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FExecution%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>When <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> was <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, he attempted to run into an alleyway. Before he could get there, he was shot dead with a single bullet through the back of his head<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> by a young Peacekeeper, stationed as a sniper on top of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>. When Woodbine's family tried to claim his body, Lenore Dove aided them, and her boyfriend <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> stepped in when a Peacekeeper tried to hit her. As a result of the chaos, the reaping of another boy had to be staged to patch into the broadcast. For his role in the struggle, <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> singled out Haymitch for punishment, replacing Woodbine in the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FExecution%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> explained that Peacekeepers in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> once executed <a href="/wiki/Martin" title="Martin">Martin</a>, a developmentally disabled boy, on the spot for stealing. He'd tried to take a pair of night vision glasses from the orchard to play with.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FExecution%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a>, was executed for allowing <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> to both win the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> His manner of death is unknown, but during her private session in front of the Gamemakers for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss hanged him in effigy using a training dummy.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Oldmanabouttobeshot.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720234741" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The old man about to be shot" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="130" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Oldmanabouttobeshot.png" data-image-key="Oldmanabouttobeshot.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Oldmanabouttobeshot.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130720234741" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Oldmanabouttobeshot.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720234741" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The old man about to be shot" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Oldmanabouttobeshot.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130720234741" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="130" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Oldmanabouttobeshot.png" data-image-key="Oldmanabouttobeshot.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Oldmanabouttobeshot.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130720234741" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Oldmanabouttobeshot.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The old man about to be shot by Peacekeepers.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>During Katniss and Peeta's stop in District 11 on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, an <a href="/wiki/Unidentified_District_11_citizen" title="Unidentified District 11 citizen">old man</a> whistled <a href="/wiki/Rue%27s_four-note_song" title="Rue&#39;s four-note song">Rue's four-note song</a>, and in a show of rebellion, the people of the district performed District 12's traditional <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a>. As <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">co-victors</a> Katniss and Peeta left the stage, the old man was dragged up the steps to be immediately killed with a single shot to the back of the head,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and at least two more gunshots followed, quite probably signaling more deaths.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">uprising of District 8</a>, citizens were forbidden from leaving their homes, and the only time the television didn't display static was when they aired the hangings of suspected instigators in the town square.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>District 12's new Head Peacekeeper, <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>, had a new whipping post, several stockades, and a gallows built in the center of their square. According to Katniss, these new installations were in frequent use, but it's unknown whether anyone was executed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FExecution%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Peeta was rescued from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, President Snow televised the execution of Peeta's <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a>, and his <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> By the end of the Second Rebellion, all stylists from the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> were dead, and all but one prep team had been killed. In that same time frame, all but seven Hunger Games victors were killed in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>. 18 died in the 75th Hunger Games, and for 32 of the 34 subsequent deaths, it's unknown how many of them were executions, civilian deaths, or casualties of combat.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 200px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/db/District8VictorsPurge.png/revision/latest?cb=20200720002750" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="One of the district executions in Mockingjay - Part 1" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="83" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="District8VictorsPurge.png" data-image-key="District8VictorsPurge.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/db/District8VictorsPurge.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20200720002750" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/db/District8VictorsPurge.png/revision/latest?cb=20200720002750" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="One of the district executions in Mockingjay - Part 1" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/db/District8VictorsPurge.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20200720002750" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="83" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District8VictorsPurge.png" data-image-key="District8VictorsPurge.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/db/District8VictorsPurge.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20200720002750" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District8VictorsPurge.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">One of the district executions in <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, Snow gave a televised speech, announcing that possessing images of the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a> would be considered treason, punishable by death. While this happened, two people from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> and three from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> were hauled out in front of their respective <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Buildings</a> and a crowd of their peers. With sacks over their faces, each victim was shot once in the back of the head.<sup id="cite_ref-MJP1_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP1-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They are often presumed to be victors due to the purge.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Execution_of_President_Snow">Execution of President Snow</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FExecution%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Execution_of_President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="Execution of President Snow">Execution of President Snow</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f8/Katniss_aims_at_Snow.png/revision/latest?cb=20230705210621" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss aims to shoot Snow." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss aims at Snow.png" data-image-key="Katniss_aims_at_Snow.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f8/Katniss_aims_at_Snow.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230705210621" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f8/Katniss_aims_at_Snow.png/revision/latest?cb=20230705210621" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss aims to shoot Snow." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f8/Katniss_aims_at_Snow.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230705210621" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss aims at Snow.png" data-image-key="Katniss_aims_at_Snow.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f8/Katniss_aims_at_Snow.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230705210621" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_aims_at_Snow.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss aiming to shoot Snow.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Following the end of the war, Snow was tried and sentenced to death.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The plan was for him to be shot in the heart with a single <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">arrow</a> by Katniss Everdeen, symbolically firing the last shot of the war. However, she changed her target at the last second and assassinated Interim President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> instead.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the ensuing chaos, Snow was either trampled to death by the crowd or choked on his own blood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>According to Coin, "hundreds of [Snow's] accomplices" were also awaiting death sentences at the time of his execution,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_21-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but it is unknown how many of these were actually carried out, if any, under her successor President <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Paylor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is also unknown whether capital punishment was retained or repealed afterwards.
</p>
