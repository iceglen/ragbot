</p><p><b>Stef Dawson</b> is an Australian actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>. She also reprised her role in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStef_Dawson%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Dawson made her first short film role as an extra in <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1946172/">Chocolates</a></i>. Later, Dawson landed a role in <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0163924/?ref_=nm_flmg_act_22">All Saints</a></i> as Tully McIntyre; where she made her television debut in the episode, "<i>The Blink of an Eye</i>". Dawson landed the lead role as the title character in the short film <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1679259/?ref_=nm_flmg_act_23">Ophelia</a></i> and also, received a role in <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2041498/?ref_=nm_flmg_act_21">The Consulate</a></i> as Sarah Rael. Dawson made another television appearance in <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1179658/?ref_=nm_flmg_act_20">Out of Blue</a></i> as an extra, and in <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1316564/?ref_=nm_flmg_act_19">Swift and Shift Couriers</a></i> as Kylie Brown in the episode "<i>The Polar Bear Suit</i>". Dawson co-starred as one of the lead roles, Leah Thompson, in <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1754912/?ref_=nm_flmg_act_16">Wrath</a></i>. Dawson made a film appearance in <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2527660/?ref_=nm_flmg_act_14">Foxfur</a></i> as Pearlwing. Later, she co-starred as Meghan in <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2005282/?ref_=nm_flmg_act_13">Manhaters!</a></i>.
</p>
