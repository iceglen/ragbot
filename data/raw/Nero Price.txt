<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNero_Price%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Price was a titan in the railroad industry who lived with his daughter in a town house on the <a href="/wiki/Corso" title="Corso">Corso</a>. Like many Capitol citizens, they fell upon hard times during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>; at one point, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> witnessed him carving off the leg of a young maid from the Crane family for food. Although Coriolanus never spoke of it, the memory was forever burned into his mind, and the act horrified him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNero_Price%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was named after the Roman emperor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nero" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nero">Nero</a>, who had a reputation for cruelty and debauchery that has been questioned in contemporary historical circles.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNero_Price%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Nero is one of two confirmed cannibals in the series, the other being <a href="/wiki/Titus" title="Titus">Titus</a>. Both are featured in scenes that took place before the book they were mentioned in.
<ul><li>Furthermore, both are connected to railroads. Nero is a titan in the railroad industry, and Titus hails from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a>, which is the railroad district.</li></ul></li>
<li>In the film, Nero was only listed in the credits as "Respectable Man".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The actor originally chosen for the role of Nero had gotten a "monk haircut" for a different role and no longer looked the part, so it was recast.<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
