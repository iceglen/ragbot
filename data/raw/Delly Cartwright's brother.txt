</p><p><b>Delly Cartwright's brother</b> is the younger brother of <a href="/wiki/Delly_Cartwright" title="Delly Cartwright">Delly Cartwright</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDelly_Cartwright%2527s_brother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was born in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> and raised alongside his older sister, Delly, in the <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a> sector of town. Their parents ran the shoe shop,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  and it was typical for merchants to live above their stores,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so the Cartwrights likely lived right above theirs.
</p><p>He and Delly both survived the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, though their parents did not. The siblings were evacuated to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> with the other refugees.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They accounted for two of "fewer than a dozen" total merchant survivors.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDelly_Cartwright%2527s_brother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His surname, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartwright_(surname)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cartwright (surname)">Cartwright</a>, is an English occupational surname for a maker of carts.
