<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage">Usage</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTribute_tube%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/Catacombs" title="Catacombs">catacombs</a> beneath the arena, each tribute had an underground <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">Launch Room</a> where they were dressed in the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena uniform</a> and readied by their <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>. After an uncertain amount of time, a pleasant female voice announced that it was time to prepare to launch. The tribute would then go stand on a metal plate, and the glass cylinder of the tribute tube lowered to keep them in place just before the platform lifted them into the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the films, the tube was nearly soundproof; it muffled sound from the Launch Room to the tube, and vice versa.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This was something <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> only speculated on in the <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i> novel. Also, despite their glassy appearance, the tubes appeared to be very durable.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTribute_tube%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>Katniss was first lifted through a tribute tube at the start of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> For the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> the following year, the rising of her platform was slightly delayed so she could be forced to watch as <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> viciously beat <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> and dragged him away.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTribute_tube%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a> had other surefire ways of keeping tributes in place, like the immobilizing current used to keep them still as they were lifted into <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> and injected with their <a href="/wiki/Tracker" title="Tracker">trackers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As a result, it's possible that the tribute tube only exists for dramatic effect.</li></ul>
