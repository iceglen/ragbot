<p>After the death of <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> tried to console his grieving mother. She tried to convince the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> to let her see her son's body for a little while longer, but they wouldn't budge, and she kept insisting. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> came over to try and help the situation, so <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> chooses him to replace Woodbine. Plutarch told Cassia to record Lenore Dove's weeping reaction.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
