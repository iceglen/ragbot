<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 128px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/38/Dill_reaping.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240215034221" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Dill coughing at the reaping." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="128" height="180" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Dill reaping.jpg" data-image-key="Dill_reaping.jpg" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/38/Dill_reaping.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/128?cb=20240215034221"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/38/Dill_reaping.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240215034221" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Dill coughing at the reaping." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/38/Dill_reaping.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/128?cb=20240215034221" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="128" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Dill reaping.jpg" data-image-key="Dill_reaping.jpg" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/38/Dill_reaping.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/128?cb=20240215034221"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Dill_reaping.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Dill coughing at the reaping.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Dill grew up in District 11 and became her district's female tribute in <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Along with the other tributes, Dill was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car, then transferred into a truck.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>There, she told <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, the mentor of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tribute <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, that Reaper, her district partner, had once killed a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a>. In response, Reaper told her to shut it, but she asked who cared now. When Lucy Gray stopped the other tributes from attacking Snow, saying that he was her mentor, Dill asked "<i>How come you get a mender?</i>" When Snow said that they each got one, Dill asked where they were and why they hadn't come. Lucy Gray replied that they just weren't as inspired. The tributes were then dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>That evening, Snow discovered fellow mentor <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> at the zoo, apparently trying to coax Dill to take a sandwich. He wondered why Plinth would do this when she wasn't even his tribute.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> for the Games, <a href="/wiki/Felix_Ravinstill" title="Felix Ravinstill">Felix Ravinstill</a>, her assigned mentor, tried to make an impression with her, but she was so sickly that her coughs were barely audible.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On the first day of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, Reaper could seen be carrying Dill, who was on the verge of death, out of a tunnel and onto a sunny spot of a piece of charred wood. She was also shivering despite the heat. Her mentor was surprised she even made it that long, as she was likely suffering from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/tuberculosis" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:tuberculosis">tuberculosis</a>. She received two bottles of water by <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a>, but did not even acknowledge them. <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> came later and quickly stole them. As the day ended, Dill's body convulsed and violently coughed up blood. Blood soaked the filthy dress that she wore.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>When the gong rang, she began to move around until Reaper appeared and protected her from an attack by Circ. Until the center of the arena was vacated, she and Reaper remained there, watching everything unfold. Then they decided to hide in the tunnels until the next day, when Dill emerged and unknowingly drank from a bottle of water that Lucy Gray had poisoned. Lucy Gray watched from behind a vent as Dill collapsed, horrified because her intended target had been <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>. Dill placed 7th overall.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Dill's fate replaced that of <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a>'s in the book.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> first saw Dill on the train platform, he described <a href="/wiki/Reaper" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaper">Reaper</a>, her district partner, first, to be "a tall boy with dark brown skin". He then described Dill as "a girl with similar coloring but a skeletal frame and a hacking cough."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the arena, Dill seemed to have shrunk due to illness. Her body was curled in the fetal position and she wore a filthy dress that became soaked with blood upon her death.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Dill may be named after the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/dill" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:dill">dill</a> herb.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Dill had some sort of mutual bond with her fellow District 11 tribute, <a href="/wiki/Reaper" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaper">Reaper</a>. On the first day of the Games, before Dill died, Reaper carried Dill out of the tunnel and made a lackluster attempt to comfort her. This is a vaguely similar relationship as that of <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> and <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> from the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.</li></ul>
