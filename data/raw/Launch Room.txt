<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Purpose">Purpose</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games">The Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Each tribute and their stylist took a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> to the location of the arena, then climbed down a ladder, through a tube, into the <a href="/wiki/Catacombs" title="Catacombs">catacombs</a> below. After that, they followed instructions to locate the correct Launch Room. There, the tribute would be able to shower and brush their teeth before being seen to by their stylist for any finishing cosmetic touches. The stylist received a package containing the tribute's <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena uniform</a> on-site and helped them dress. Food and water were available to the tribute if they had the appetite.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 203px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/The-Hunger-Games-57.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120310213754" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss in her tribute tube." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c4/The-Hunger-Games-57.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/203?cb=20120310213754" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="203" height="276" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="The-Hunger-Games-57.jpg" data-image-key="The-Hunger-Games-57.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:The-Hunger-Games-57.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss in her <a href="/wiki/Tribute_tube" title="Tribute tube">tribute tube</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After an uncertain amount of time, a pleasant female voice announced that it was time to prepare to launch. The tribute would then go stand on a metal plate, and the glass cylinder of a <a href="/wiki/Tribute_tube" title="Tribute tube">tribute tube</a> lowered to keep them in place just before the platform lifted them into the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>All tributes were launched at the same time, arranged in a circle around the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. After the launch, each tribute had to remain on their launch plate for 60 seconds; a wrong step would trigger land mines and kill them instantly. At the end of this period, the land mines were deactivated, and the Games began.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Like the arena, Launch Rooms and tribute tubes both appeared to contain hidden cameras; the first footage of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> arena was shown from the perspective of one of the tributes as she launched.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Since each arena was only used once, each Launch Room was only ever used by one tribute.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tourism">Tourism</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Prior to the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, arenas and their catacombs became vacation spots for <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> citizens to tour after the Games concluded.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Any known Launch Rooms were not described in great detail. Each one was built brand-new for that year's Games, and both of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s rooms contained a shower, a couch, and the metal plate and tribute tube.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Haymitch Abernathy's launch room was circular. It contained the glass plate and tribute tube, as well as a chair with his arena wear.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In his launch room, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> dressed in his <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> and waited to be launched. Before this could happen, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> came in to do final prep, adjusting his <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a> and telling him to "keep a positive attitude." An announcement interrupted, saying that if tributes weren't in their tribute tube in 30 seconds, they would be disciplined. Tributes were then launched into the arena, greeted by an intercom.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In her Launch Room, Katniss Everdeen showered and cleaned her teeth before her stylist <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> braided her hair and helped her get dressed. The two speculated about what the arena might be based on the uniform, and Cinna gave Katniss her <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> before they sat down on the couch together. Katniss's nerves prevented her from eating or making conversation, but when she reached out her hand to Cinna, he held it between both of his until she was called to stand on her platform and be launched.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In her Launch Room, Katniss took a shower, then Cinna braided her hair down her back and helped her dress in simple undergarments. As they had the previous year, they speculated about the arena based on the uniform, Cinna gave her the mockingjay pin, and they sat down together, holding hands. When it was time for Katniss to stand on her plate, the launch was delayed, so she was forced to watch from inside the tribute tube as <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> beat Cinna and dragged him away. Only then was she launched into the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span id="Second_Rebellion.2FAftermath"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion/Aftermath">Second Rebellion/Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLaunch_Room%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the Second Rebellion, the Hunger Games were abolished and all past arenas were "completely destroyed".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This presumably included the catacombs and Launch Rooms underneath.
</p>
