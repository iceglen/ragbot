</p><p>The <b>Capitol Zoo</b> is located in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. Before the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the Capitol Zoo was full of many types of animals from "the Capitol's genetic ark."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, many of the enclosures were left empty, ravaged by the war. Few animals remained, such as a lone tortoise and a bedraggled toucan.
</p><p>Following the Dark Days, the zoo was infested by rats. In order to try to control the problem, the Peacekeepers guarding the zoo were supplied with rat poison. The rats, however, were smart and wouldn't go anywhere near the stuff.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> later gave <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> <a href="/wiki/Silver_compact" title="Silver compact">his mother's compact</a>, which she used to smuggle the rat poison into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Zoo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Zoo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> were brought to the Capitol Zoo upon arrival to the Capitol. They were dropped through a chute into the monkey enclosure and held there until the Games began.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Capitol citizens were encouraged to come see the tributes as if they were zoo animals. After <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> made a scene by riding with the tributes to the zoo and being dropped into the cage with them, he encouraged citizens to come visit the tribute he was mentoring, Lucy Gray. <a href="/wiki/Pontius" title="Pontius">Pontius</a> and <a href="/wiki/Venus" title="Venus">Venus</a> were among the children that she talked to, and <a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a> from <a href="/wiki/Capitol_News" title="Capitol News">Capitol News</a> interviewed her.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/57/Sejanus_at_the_Zoo.png/revision/latest?cb=20231229072752" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt=" offering food through the bars" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/57/Sejanus_at_the_Zoo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231229072752" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="122" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Sejanus at the Zoo.png" data-image-key="Sejanus_at_the_Zoo.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Sejanus_at_the_Zoo.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption"><a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> offering food through the bars</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After this, Capitol citizens frequented the zoo, bringing food and water for the tributes, feeding them like animals through the enclosure bars. Initially, many of the tributes were hesitant to accept food. However, after the urging of Lucy Gray and Coriolanus, many of them grew comfortable enough to.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Many citizens visited the zoo, and several tributes would perform for them, like <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ginnee" title="Ginnee">Ginnee</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Sheaf" title="Sheaf">Sheaf</a>, but the tributes remained underfed and mistreated. However, after Coriolanus brought up the issue to <a href="/wiki/Satyria_Click" title="Satyria Click">Satyria Click</a>, the tributes were fed bread and cheese.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>One day, <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a> taunted her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a>, with a sandwich. This angered Brandy, and she killed Arachne with Arachne's own knife.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This incident caused mass panic, and <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> were to guard the zoo from then on. Coriolanus had to be given permission from Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Gaul</a> just to see his tribute, Lucy Gray Baird.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Zoo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>One of the parties that <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was forced to attend after winning the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> was an all-night party at the Capitol Zoo. Afterwards, he was finally sent home to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Zoo%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The monkey house had a stretch of sand, tall artificial rock formations, and rows of metal bars engraved to resemble vines.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A shallow moat separated the rocky structures and the bars, but was bone-dry by the time of the 10th Hunger Games. The bars were spaced about four inches apart.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
