					<b class="trfc161__question">Why was Pollux made an Avox in the Hunger Games series?</b>

				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						In the Hunger Games series, Pollux was transformed into an Avox by the Capitol as punishment. He was enslaved and compelled to labor in the city's subterranean utility corridors and the Transfer. An Avox is an individual whose tongue has been removed, making them mute, as a penalty for rebellion, treason, desertion, or criminal behavior. Typically, Peacekeepers would apprehend such individuals, who would then live as Panem's lowest class, serving as slaves to the Capitol.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPollux%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Prior to his appearance in <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>, Pollux was made an Avox by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. He was forced to work in the city's underground utility corridors and the <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>. His brother, <a href="/wiki/Castor" title="Castor">Castor</a>, eventually bought Pollux's way aboveground after five long years.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point after his liberation, Pollux began to work as a cameraman. Katniss described him and his brother as looking like insects because of the heavy mobile cameras that usually encased their bodies.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Katniss and her filming group were at the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, Katniss sat next to Pollux during a break. They started whistling tunes and birdcalls to <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a> and wait for their return whistles. Pollux then wrote the word "<i>SING?</i>" in the dirt, asking Katniss to sing. She sang "<a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">The Hanging Tree</a>" for him, which made him very emotional.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, he instead tapped his lips and pointed to Katniss to convey the same idea.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b1/Pollux.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20140514032508" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Pollux" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b1/Pollux.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140514032508" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="171" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Pollux.PNG" data-image-key="Pollux.PNG" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Pollux.PNG" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>His experience working underground made him very knowledgeable about the Capitol's underground, so he was able to guide <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> through the passages towards <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>'s <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President's mansion">mansion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After his brother and much of the squad were killed by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Pollux hid out with the other survivors at <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>'s shop and grieved.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_23_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_23-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He and <a href="/wiki/Cressida" title="Cressida">Cressida</a> eventually set out on the mission with the others, but Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> lost sight of them in the crowd of refugees.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_24_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_24-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He survived the war, and as the only surviving member of Cressida's team, Pollux joined her again in the postwar period, filming war damage in the other districts.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Pollux is described as a burly man<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> with sandy hair, a red beard, and deep blue eyes. He resembles his brother, Castor. He has no tongue, as it was cut off when he became an Avox.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPollux%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Castor and Pollux owe their names to twin brothers in Greek and Roman mythology. The myth states they had the same mother, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leda_(mythology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Leda (mythology)">Leda</a>, but different fathers (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyndareus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tyndareus">Tyndareus</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Zeus">Zeus</a>, respectively). This made Castor mortal and Pollux immortal, which is fitting because <i>Mockingjay</i>'s Pollux lives and Castor dies.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPollux%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Pollux is the only known Avox to survive the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He is also the only known Avox to regain social standing.</li>
<li>In <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i>, when Katniss first met Pollux and the rest of the crew, he could be seen in the background using <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Sign_Language" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:American Sign Language">American Sign Language</a> with his brother. Pollux asked if he thought Katniss was beautiful, and Castor responded with a simple "yes".<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, while they were in the sewers, Pollux signed to his brother "Home sweet home."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
