<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Coriolanus found himself paired with Bug, who turned out to be a first-class climber, having been raised in District 11, where the children helped to tend the orchards. <dd>&#8212;Bug's talent at climbing<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Bug</b> was a diminutive male individual who enlisted as a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. He was roommates with <a href="/wiki/Smiley" title="Smiley">Smiley</a>, <a href="/wiki/Junius" title="Junius">Junius</a> (Beanpole), <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> (Gent), and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> (Bull's Eye).<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Having been raised in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, Bug was a talented climber and was easily able to catch <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">mockingjays</a> in the woods.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBug%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bug grew up in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, where he tended the orchards along with the rest of the children and learned to quickly climb trees during harvest.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At some point, he was sent to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> to become a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> and became friends with his bunkmate, <a href="/wiki/Smiley" title="Smiley">Smiley</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Junius" title="Junius">Junius</a> arrived at the Peacekeeper base and cleaned up, Smiley and Bug collected them and led them to the mess hall, where they ate and conversed.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> arrived, Smiley and Bug greeted him warmly, having been cleaned out at the poker table earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Bug went to the <a href="/wiki/Hob" class="mw-redirect" title="Hob">Hob</a> along with his bunkmates to watch the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> perform. The day after that, they were hungover and bruised from the night before, and were disappointed to learn that they weren't to leave the base alone.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the Peacekeepers were told to trap the forest to collect <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">mockingjays</a>, Bug was paired up with Coriolanus and hauled traps into the branches with ease.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He began to work with <a href="/wiki/Dr._Kay" title="Dr. Kay">Dr. Kay</a> after impressing her with his agility, and later, she requested him to help again. Bug collected the trapped jabberjays, replacing their cages with newly baited ones, and stated that they should be left in the traps. As he continued his work, Dr. Kay watched him approvingly. Back at the base, he and Coriolanus unloaded the cages and helped the scientists bring them to the birds' temporary home in an old hangar, agreeing to care for the birds before they were taken to the <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a>. However, Bug believed that they would be happier if just left in the woods.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Bug and Coriolanus helped set up mist nets to capture the mockingjays, which had evaded the other traps. Bug was a natural at it and gentle with the birds, contrary to Coriolanus. They attended to the birds and took them to the lab table to be numbered and tagged, returning after dinner to fasten cloth covers on the cages and carry them to the hovercraft. He was disappointed about the birds leaving, but cheered up when Sejanus arrived with news that <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Mrs. Plinth</a> had sent another delivery of baked goods.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
