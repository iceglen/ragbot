</p><p><b>Coriolanus 9</b> is a solar thermal power plant located in the heart of <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>. It generates over 7% of the Capitol's energy needs by itself, using falling water to produce 20,000 volts of electricity at a time.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<ul><li>The power plant was featured in a series by <a href="/wiki/Capitol_TV" title="Capitol TV">Capitol TV</a> to promote the release of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> in 2014.
