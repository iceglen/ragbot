<p><b>Merrilee Undersee</b> (née <b>Donner</b>) was the daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Donner. She was <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>'s twin sister who was one of the female tributes for <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. She was <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Undersee" title="Mayor Undersee">Mayor Undersee</a>'s wife and <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a>'s mother. She lived in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> with her family.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerrilee_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When Merrilee was younger, she lived with her parents and twin sister Maysilee above a sweet shop on the <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a> side of District 12. She was close with her sister's best friend, <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Merrilee always went along with what her sister did and often dressed in the same clothing as her sister by their mother.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point, Merrilee and her sister were gifted bird pins made by <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a>. Merrilee was given a hummingbird pin while Maysilee was given a mockingjay pin which she greatly disliked, much to the disdain of Tam Amber's adopted niece <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Merrilee "carelessly" lost her hummingbird pin by dropping it into a well shortly after receiving it.   
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Losing_her_twin">Losing her twin</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerrilee_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the reaping of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, Merrilee and Asterid were devastated when Maysilee was selected as a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> for the Second Quarter Quell. After Maysilee's death during the Games, Merrilee and her parents attended her funeral, along with burials of <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Jethro_Callow" title="Jethro Callow">Jethro Callow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">Willamae Abernathy</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a>. During the memorial service, Merrilee was mistaken as Maysilee by the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of the 50th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, causing her and her father to break down in tears.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Adulthood">Adulthood</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerrilee_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Years after her sister's death, Merrilee was never the same afterwards, suffering from massive headaches in her adulthood that caused her to lie in bed all day.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Her daughter, Madge, passed on this information to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, and when Katniss asked why they didn't get medicines from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, Madge responded that even the mayor had his restrictions.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> was whipped, Madge asked her mother if she could bring some of her medicine to him and took it to the Everdeens' home, where Gale lay recovering.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Merrilee, her husband, and <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge</a>, as well as the two people who worked for them, did not survive the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerrilee_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerrilee_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Merrilee's husband was the mayor of District 12. She had one daughter, Madge. She also had a twin sister named <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee</a>, who died in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> after being attacked by a large number of bright, <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">candy pink birds</a>. Merrilee attended her twin's funeral alongside their mother and father.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerrilee_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Merrilee and Maysilee Donner are the only confirmed pair of twins in the trilogy.
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Leeg_1" title="Leeg 1">Leeg 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/Leeg_2" title="Leeg 2">Leeg 2</a>, despite being played by identical twin actresses in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2"><i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i></a>, are not explicitly referred to as twins in the book, though it is mentioned they look so similar in uniform that others can't tell them apart.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> and <a href="/wiki/Castor" title="Castor">Castor</a> are also described as having a strong physical resemblance to one another, and their names refer to a pair of mythological Greek twins, but they are not explicitly confirmed to be twins in the text.</li></ul></li>
<li>On her and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee</a>'s 13th birthday, their father gave them pure gold pins, previously owned by his mother and fashioned by <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a>. Merrilee received a gold hummingbird pin.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Her twin sister <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee</a> would often match outfits with her, including during the reaping of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>She was confirmed by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Ray_(screenwriter)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Billy Ray (screenwriter)">Billy Ray</a> to be included in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">movie</a>. Fans held questions about whether or not she would be included due to her daughter, <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a>, not being adapted for the original Hunger Games trilogy films. <sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
