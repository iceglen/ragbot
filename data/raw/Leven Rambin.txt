</p><p><b>Leven Rambin</b> is an American actress. She portrayed <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeven_Rambin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rambin was born in Houston, Texas. Her father, Joseph Howard Rambin III, is co-founder of Moody-Rambin, the largest locally owned commercial real estate firm in Houston. Her mother is the former Karen Stacy Guthrie. Her older brother, Joseph Rambin, is currently enrolled at The University of Alabama. Her older half-sister is Mary Rambin, blogger of "More Than Mary" and designer of the Moe handbag line, currently residing in Houston, Texas. She is the granddaughter of J. Howard Rambin, Jr, former CEO and president of Texaco oil company.
</p><p>She began performing in school plays. She studied at the Houston School of Film and Theatre and at St. Francis Episcopal Day School where she starred in her first school play before relocating to Connecticut and later New York when she was cast on <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_My_Children" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:All My Children">All My Children</a></i> (her first professional acting role). While acting, she also pursued her high school diploma through the Texas Tech University Independent School District.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeven_Rambin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rambin joined the cast of ABC's <i>All My Children</i> in February 2004, portraying sisters Lily Montgomery and Ava Benton. In 2007, she was nominated for a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/34th_Annual_Daytime_Emmy_Awards#Outstanding_Younger_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:34th Annual Daytime Emmy Awards">Daytime Emmy Award for Outstanding Younger Actress in a Drama Series</a>. She would later reprise her role as Lily for the show's 40th anniversary in 2010.
</p><p>She also made appearances on <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey%27s_Anatomy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Grey&#39;s Anatomy">Grey's Anatomy</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminator:_The_Sarah_Connor_Chronicles" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Terminator: The Sarah Connor Chronicles">Terminator: The Sarah Connor Chronicles</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scoundrels_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Scoundrels (TV series)">Scoundrels</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wizards_of_Waverly_Place" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Wizards of Waverly Place">Wizards of Waverly Place</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Miami" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:CSI: Miami">CSI: Miami</a></i>. In addition to various recurring and series regular roles on television, she made her film debut in Killer Film's independent picture, <i>Gigantic</i>, starring and produced by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Dano" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Paul Dano">Paul Dano</a>.
</p><p>In April 2011, it was announced that Lionsgate and director <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Ross" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Gary Ross">Gary Ross</a> cast Rambin in her first major studio film, in the role of Glimmer in the film adaptation of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i> novel. She was supposed to return to <i>All My Children</i> in August 2011 before the show's ABC network end in September, but her appearance had to be canceled due to a time conflict with her <i>Hunger Games</i> production schedule.
</p><p>In late April 2012, Rambin was cast in a lead role in <a href="https://riordan.fandom.com/wiki/Percy_Jackson:_Sea_of_Monsters" class="extiw" title="w:c:riordan:Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters"><i>Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters</i></a>. She portrayed <a href="https://riordan.fandom.com/wiki/Clarisse_La_Rue" class="extiw" title="w:c:riordan:Clarisse La Rue">Clarisse La Rue</a>.
</p>

<h3><span id="Glimmer.27s_Game"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Glimmer's_Game"><i>Glimmer's Game</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeven_Rambin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On May 16th, 2024, Rambin published a memoir about her acting career titled <i>Glimmer's Game</i>. This included, but was not exclusively about, what it was like to play Glimmer.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personal_life">Personal life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeven_Rambin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rambin got engaged to producer Geoffrey James Clark in 2009 in the Bahamas. They broke off their engagement in November 2011. She married actor in Jim Parrack in 2015.
</p>
