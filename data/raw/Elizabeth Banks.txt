</p><p><b>Elizabeth Banks</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1"><i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay</i> - Part 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2"><i> The Hunger Games: Mockingjay</i> - Part 2</a>. She stated that the worst part of playing the part of Effie was the long fingernails as she could not do anything, even go to the bathroom on her own while wearing them.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElizabeth_Banks%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Banks has been in films such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_on_a_Ledge" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Man on a Ledge">Man On A Ledge</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_To_Expect_When_You%27re_Expecting_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:What To Expect When You&#39;re Expecting (film)">What to Expect When You're Expecting</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_Perfect" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pitch Perfect">Pitch Perfect</a></i>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lego_Movie" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Lego Movie">The Lego Movie</a></i>. She is also known for her appearances in TV shows such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrubs_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Scrubs (TV series)">Scrubs</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Family" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Modern Family">Modern Family</a></i>.  Since 2019, she has been the host of ABC's revival of the 1980s game show <i>Press Your Luck</i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personal_life">Personal life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElizabeth_Banks%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Banks met her husband, sportswriter and producer <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Handelman" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Max Handelman">Max Handelman</a>, on her first day at college on September 6, 1992. They were married in 2003. She and her husband had their first child, Felix, who was born through a surrogate in March 2011. In November 2012, Banks announced the birth of another son, Magnus Mitchell, also by surrogate.
</p><p>Banks went through parts of the process of conversion to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Judaism">Judaism</a>, her husband's faith, and studied with rabbis. In 2013, speaking of her religion, she stated that she practices Judaism, though "I did not have my <i>mikveh</i>, so technically I'm not converted", but that she has "been essentially a Jew for like 15 years", adding "Frankly, because I'm already doing everything [practicing religious rituals], I feel like I'm as Jewish as I'm ever going to be".
</p>
