<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FReal_or_Not_Real%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Peeta Mellark was captured by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> forces in the immediate aftermath of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While imprisoned, he was subjected to a highly secretive form of torture known as <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a>, which used <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> venom to modify his memories of Katniss. These changes were designed to make her appear more threatening, prompting him to attack her on sight.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peeta made some progress in his recovery, but despite his continued instability, he was sent to join Squad 451 during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Creation">Creation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FReal_or_Not_Real%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The morning after Peeta's arrival, <a href="/wiki/Jackson" title="Jackson">Jackson</a> devised the game "Real or Not Real" to help him sort out his real memories from the false ones. Peeta would mention something he thought had happened, and his fellow soldiers would tell him if it was real or not real, with an explanation as necessary. The game was also based off the word of <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>; he was the first to suggest that Peeta ask when he was uncertain of reality, since that was what <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> did.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
