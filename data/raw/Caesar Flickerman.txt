<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_career">Early career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>According to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, he was the host of the Hunger Games for more than forty years.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The earliest known edition he hosted was the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, 24 years prior to the start of the trilogy. That year, he had to interview twice the regular number of tributes in honor of the second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is suggested that he had hosted many games before that as Haymitch indicates he changes his suit every year, meaning this is not his first.
</p>

Rue says that she is fast and if they can't catch her they can't kill her and to not count her out, to which Caesar replies "<i>I wouldn't in a million years</i>."

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">"<i>We have never seen anything like it before... And we will never see anything like it again.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Caesar Flickerman in <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>As he did with the previous Games, Caesar helped the tributes in <a href="/wiki/The_75th_Hunger_Games" class="mw-redirect" title="The 75th Hunger Games">the 75th Hunger Games</a> with their interviews. This year, his hair, lips and eyelids were dyed lavender. While interviewing the tributes, his questions were met with snide remarks from tributes. Most of them were bad mouthing the Capitol as a sign of rebellion before they went into the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> for a second time. 
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 224px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a8/Katniss_caesar_quarter_quell_interview.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20250328053134" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss and Caesar" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="224" height="149" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss caesar quarter quell interview.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_caesar_quarter_quell_interview.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a8/Katniss_caesar_quarter_quell_interview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/224?cb=20250328053134"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a8/Katniss_caesar_quarter_quell_interview.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20250328053134" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss and Caesar" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a8/Katniss_caesar_quarter_quell_interview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/224?cb=20250328053134" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="224" height="149" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss caesar quarter quell interview.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_caesar_quarter_quell_interview.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a8/Katniss_caesar_quarter_quell_interview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/224?cb=20250328053134"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_caesar_quarter_quell_interview.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss and Caesar</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>When he interviewed Katniss, he mentions her upcoming nuptials to Peeta. As per <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">Snow</a>'s request, Katniss was wearing her wedding dress. Katniss was unaware that <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> had made a few adjustments so that as she twirled, the dress would morph into a black mockingjay outfit.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Caesar then interviews Peeta, who claims that he had been unofficially married to Katniss for months and that she is pregnant. While both claims are false, they explain Katniss' sudden "breakdown" and obtain sympathy. This revelation caused the audience to go wild, upstaging all of the other tributes' interviews (as Peeta also did the year before<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>). This also upset the Capitol audience enormously because they had grown to love Katniss and Peeta, and the thought that they would not both survive after being so close to each other, and that an unborn baby would probably be killed in the arena, overwhelmed them.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This resulted in some of them supporting the rebellion, not the Games.
</p><p>At the end of Caesar's interviews, all of the <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> stood and held hands as a show of unity; this influenced the Capitol audience further still<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Rebellion">Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 350px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5f/MfilmPeetainterview.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140915160047" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Caesar interviews Peeta" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="350" height="143" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="MfilmPeetainterview.jpg" data-image-key="MfilmPeetainterview.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5f/MfilmPeetainterview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/350?cb=20140915160047"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5f/MfilmPeetainterview.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140915160047" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Caesar interviews Peeta" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5f/MfilmPeetainterview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/350?cb=20140915160047" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="350" height="143" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="MfilmPeetainterview.jpg" data-image-key="MfilmPeetainterview.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5f/MfilmPeetainterview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/350?cb=20140915160047"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:MfilmPeetainterview.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Caesar interviews Peeta</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>, Caesar interviews a tortured Peeta. Caesar asks him questions like how he feels about the rebels and Katniss. While Peeta is explaining, he tells him and the audience how it feels to be in the Hunger Games, which fascinates them because no victor has ever spoken of this. Caesar tries to make Peeta as comfortable as possible during the interview. It is cut short when Peeta asks to go back to his cell, due to Caesar's comments on Katniss and her place in the rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Caesar interviews Peeta for the second time. This interview is short, during this interview the two exchange empty comments to each other. Caesar asks Peeta about the rumors of Katniss shooting a <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a>. Peeta tells him that she probably doesn't know what they are doing to her and to not trust them. The last thing Peeta says before the interview ends is for Katniss to find out what they are doing.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, Caesar interviews Peeta a third time in place of President Snow. During the interview, Peeta states he thinks they should end the war with a ceasefire because if they have a war it could mean the end of all living things. Caesar questions Peeta's statement and asks him more on his thoughts of the rebellion. Peeta warns the rebels of the impending bomb attack from the Capitol on <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. The interview is cut short when the Peacekeepers go on stage and beat Peeta and take him away to receive a punishment.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ-1_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ-1-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, Caesar is featured in a Capitol announcement about the alleged death of Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> in a Peacekeeper attack, when actually only <a href="/wiki/Leeg_1" title="Leeg 1">Leeg 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/Leeg_2" title="Leeg 2">Leeg 2</a> were killed<sup id="cite_ref-MJ-2_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ-2-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Caesar's ultimate fate is not revealed in the books or films, leaving it up for speculation whether he died or survived the war. Despite often-repeated claims that Suzanne Collins confirmed in a 2011 interview that he was spared from execution, no such statement can be located.
</p>

<p>He appeared virtually the same over the course of his career. Every year, he wore the same pure white makeup, the same hairstyle, and the same "ceremonial" midnight blue suit, "dotted with a thousand tiny electric bulbs that twinkle[d] like stars." The only thing that changed annually was the color of his hair, and his eyelids and lips were done up to match.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Caesar is known for being helpful and brings out the best in the tributes. Katniss also notes that she thinks Caesar acts like this so that the tributes don't get worried or nervous. He is also known to like the Capitol's lamb stew. He thinks of himself as a little on the "tubby" side and loves Katniss' interview dress in the first book and asks her to twirl. It says in the book that Caesar can make a weak response into a memorable one. Like most Capitol citizens and TV personalities, Flickerman has had noticeable surgery done on his face as noted by Katniss, and is rather interested in the arts of fashion as displayed with his vibrant and colorful costumes and suits. He is well liked by the Capitol citizens audiences, yet seems to have some sympathy for the tributes of the Games and honestly tries to bring the best out of them and increase their chances of survival.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>  
</p><p>Stanley Tucci remarked that he thought Caesar to be "creepy, with the sort of false generosity and duplicity." Others view Flickerman's deameanor more as that of an amiable talk show host looking to help the tributes through the interviews.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the novel, <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> and Caesar have no known relationship. Yet, in the film adaptation, Caesar is placed as a co-host to Claudius during the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a> and actual Hunger Games<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>It is unknown whether any particular real-world talk show hosts were inspirational to the character of Caesar Flickerman, though Stanley Tucci did suggest that some existed in a special feature on the Blu-Ray of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. It appears that Caesar is a sort of conglomerate of them in general.</li>
<li><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> features a character named <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius "Lucky" Flickerman</a>, a popular weatherman for <a href="/wiki/Capitol_TV" title="Capitol TV">Capitol TV</a> who was tapped to be the first ever host for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. Their shared last name suggests a familial relation, and a legacy of involvement in both television and the Hunger Games.
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, Lucky could briefly be heard making a restaurant reservation for a "party of two and a high chair", hinting at having a child. Fans have speculated that he was referring to Caesar. This would also make Caesar 65-66 by the time of Mockingjay. <sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li>If Caesar's comments during his interview with <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a> are to be taken seriously, then Caesar failed biology.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Caesar Flickerman is not allowed to bet on tributes contractually. <sup id="cite_ref-Interview_Deleted_Scene_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview_Deleted_Scene-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
