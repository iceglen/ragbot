</p><p><i><b>Predator or Prey: Making The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</b></i> is a feature-length documentary film about the making of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>. It is available exclusively on 4K Ultra HD and Blu-ray discs, and is not included in the digital copy.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
