</p><p><b>Chris Mark</b> is a Canadian actor, martial artist, stunt performer, and fight choreographer who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChris_Mark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mark made an appearance on the popular TV show "The Boys" as a character named Blindspot and reunited with <a href="/wiki/Jack_Quaid" title="Jack Quaid">Jack Quaid</a>, the actor that played <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>. Like most tribute actors, he is also a stunt performer. He was the lead in <i>Kill Order</i> and <i>Enhanced</i>, both directed by his brother, James Mark. Mark has hopes of becoming the next big martial arts actor. He lives in Toronto, Ontario.
</p><p>He is the first male actor cast specifically to portray the fight scenes of a Tribute that fell during the initial bloodbath.
</p>
