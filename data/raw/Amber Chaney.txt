</p><p><b>Amber Chaney</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  and also appeared in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. She has also appeared in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walking_Dead_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Walking Dead (TV series)">The Walking Dead</a></i> and <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2402571/">Cupid's Requiem</a></i>.
</p>
