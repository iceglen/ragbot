</p><p><b>Mamaw</b> was <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s grandmother.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMamaw%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mamaw was the mother of <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy%27s_father" title="Haymitch Abernathy&#39;s father">Haymitch's father</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid</a> was born, she said that he was born looking on the sunny side. She told Haymitch that smells stick in your memory the strongest, more than sounds or sights.<sup id="cite_ref-SR16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She also said that "you never really knew who'd swim in a flood."<sup id="cite_ref-SR20_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR20-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Her favorite candy was <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>'s hot pepper cherry taffy.<sup id="cite_ref-SR20_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR20-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At an unknown time, she passed away and her family made bean and ham hock soup, which is the traditional meal eaten after a loved one passes away in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
