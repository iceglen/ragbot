</p><p><b>Stephanie Leigh Schlund</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStephanie_Leigh_Schlund%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Schlund is best known for her role in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Song_(2010_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Last Song (2010 film)">The Last Song</a></i>, alongside Miley Cyrus and fellow cast member <a href="/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth" title="Liam Hemsworth">Liam Hemsworth</a>, and her roles in the television shows <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drop_Dead_Diva" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Drop Dead Diva">Drop Dead Diva</a>, </i>and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Following" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Following">The Following</a>.</i> She has also appeared in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stomp_The_Yard" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Stomp The Yard">Stomp The Yard</a></i> and was a model on <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Price_is_Right" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Price is Right">The Price is Right</a></i> before becoming an actress.
</p><p>In a behind the scenes featurette, <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a>'s actor <a href="/wiki/Alan_Ritchson" title="Alan Ritchson">Alan Ritchson</a> mentioned that he and Schlund had quickly developed a "brotherly-sisterly kind of love" while filming. They messed around with each other on set, which created the sense of a real sibling relationship.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
