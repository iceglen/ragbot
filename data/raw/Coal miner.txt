<dd>&#8212;Description of coal miners in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Coal mining</b> is the main occupation available to the residents of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. The job requires its workers to dig for coal in District 12's underground mines. The mines are open to either gender,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the only real qualification required is that employees have to be over the age of 18.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coal mining is an extremely dangerous profession, as accidents such as cave explosions are frequent. Some coal miners caught in these accidents are mutilated and often lose their limbs, like <a href="/wiki/Ripper" title="Ripper">Ripper</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while many others die in these mining accidents, as did both <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s fathers, <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">Mr. Everdeen</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Mr._Hawthorne" title="Mr. Hawthorne">Mr. Hawthorne</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Some coal miners can also experience trouble breathing at an older age due to working in the coal mines.
</p><p>Towards the beginning of the uprising in <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, District 12's Head <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a>, is replaced by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In response to the rebellion, <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> is burned down and the mines are closed for two weeks. When they reopen, workers' wages are cut, their hours extended and the work sites become more and more dangerous. This comes a few months after Gale starts work in the mines along with <a href="/wiki/Bristel" title="Bristel">Bristel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thom" title="Thom">Thom</a>, affecting the lives of themselves and their families.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effect_on_the_Games">Effect on the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCoal_miner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Coal mining is one of the few occupations in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> that only opens to residents once their eligibility for the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">the Hunger Games</a> is over, resulting in severely undertrained <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>. While the children of some <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>, even <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> (whose children are included in the harvests from an early age), have the chance to build their strength and skills in their respective industries for years, the children of District 12 do not have the opportunity to develop special skills or strength that they would gain working in the mines.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This helps to explain why District 12 only had four <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> over the course of 74 years.
</p><p>However, students in District 12 learn the basics of explosives in preparation for their work in the mines.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As a result, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was able to use the <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">sunflower bombs</a> during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.
</p>
