<p><b>Cinna</b> was <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a> during her campaigns in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th</a> and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, who was later revealed to be a supporter of the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. Cinna played a major role in Katniss' rise to prominence in the rebel movement and with her political persona as the Mockingjay.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Little known about Cinna's background - although he made his name in the Capitol as a stylist, his life prior to the 74th Hunger Games is unknown. As a newcomer, Katniss assumed he was assigned the worst district (<a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>) and was told that he actually asked to style District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> As Cinna helped cement her image among the rebels by establishing her as the "girl on fire", designing her Mockingjay armor, and entrusting those designs to <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> and his colleagues, it was likely Cinna was involved in the underground rebel network with District 13 (although the novels give no specific indication).
</p>

<p>The 74th Hunger Games was Cinna's first year as a stylist. Cinna was assigned to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> - contrary to the tradition of assigning the "lesser" districts to new stylists, Cinna specifically asked for District 12 for reasons unknown. After the <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> prepared Katniss for the pre-game ceremonies, Cinna introduces himself to Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a>, the two stylists prepare complementary costumes for the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>, hoping to create an image of unity between Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>. He specifically encourages the couple to hold hands to further strengthen the bond between the two in the public eye.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> His unusual and intricate designs during the Tribute Parade aid with creating "the girl on fire" theme for Katniss, leading to a huge success with the crowd. When she confesses her fear of not impressing anyone during the <a href="/wiki/Interview" class="mw-redirect" title="Interview">interview</a> process, Cinna suggests that she simply be herself and imagine that she is conversing with him during the interview as a way of staying relaxed.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

As Caesar asks her about Katniss' choice to replace <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">her sister</a> as tribute, Cinna notably has a look of sadness on his face because of her sacrifice.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He later celebrates with the District 12 tributes and their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> over the successful interview. Once transported to the arena for the start of the Games, Cinna helps Katniss dress and attempts to comfort her. He tells that, although he isn't allowed to place <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">bets</a> on tributes, if he could, he would bet on her. When the two are separated, Cinna motions for her to hold her head high to project her courage.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Despite Cinna's death, his designs remained crucial to the rebellion. Likely knowing his death was unavoidable with the incoming war, Cinna had already designed her <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Designs">mockingjay uniform</a>, secretly designing it in the few weeks before the start of the 75th Hunger Games and gave it to <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>. The Mockingjay uniform was concealed as Katniss's wedding dress requested by President Snow, which transformed after becoming ignited when Katniss began twirling on stage.
</p><p>His final message to Katniss was written under a sketch of her Mockingjay pin in the sketchbook containing his designs for the Mockingjay uniform, reading: "I'm still betting on you." The realization that Cinna was actually a rebel and that he wanted for her to be the Mockingjay gives Katniss an additional edge to support the rebellion. This knowledge was kept from Katniss at Cinna's request until she agreed to be the Mockingjay, as he wanted her to autonomously make her decision.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Cinna also helped with the efforts to kidnap Katniss' prep team, saving their lives as all the stylists and prep teams were killed, so that they could continue their work with Katniss as she felt comfortable with them.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Cinna had thought of everything when designing her Mockingjay dress, including an easily-accessible hiding place for a suicide pill in case she was captured.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When trying to use this to escape what she believes will be torture and execution after assassinating President Coin, Katniss calls it "Cinna's last gift" and is distressed when Peeta prevents her from using it.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Designs">Designs</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cinna's outfits were mainly focused on Katniss and what suited her best. Based on descriptions in the novels, most of his designs learned towards utilitarianism but had an underlying elegance, and sometimes incorporated technological twists for dramatic effect. Cinna wanted Katniss to look outstanding in all of the outfits, and many of his designs were made to play up her image as "the girl on fire".
</p>

<p>Katniss' parade outfit for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> consisted of a simple black unitard, leather boots that laced up to the knees, and a cape made of red, orange, and yellow streamers. The cape, along with a matching headpiece, was lit with artificial but highly realistic flames that streamed behind Katniss as she rode in the chariot. This outfit first established Katniss' reputation as the "girl on fire".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training_Clothes">Training Clothes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>While not specifically stated to have been designed by Cinna, Katniss and Peeta wore identical outfits in the Training Center (the only tributes to do so); therefore, it's safe to assume that their stylists had some influence. The outfits consisted of long-sleeved burgundy tunics, tight black pants, and leather shoes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interview_Dress">Interview Dress</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 239px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/df/Fire_Dress.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120516015403" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss's dress for her interview with ." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="179" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Fire Dress.jpg" data-image-key="Fire_Dress.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/df/Fire_Dress.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20120516015403"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/df/Fire_Dress.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120516015403" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss's dress for her interview with ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/df/Fire_Dress.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20120516015403" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="179" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Fire Dress.jpg" data-image-key="Fire_Dress.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/df/Fire_Dress.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20120516015403"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Fire_Dress.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss's dress for her interview with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>For Katniss's interview with Caesar Flickerman, Cinna designed a dress completely covered with jewels. The inside of the dress was "silken" (presumably for comfort), and red, yellow, white, and blue gems decorated the outside in a flame design. Katniss estimated that the dress weighed forty pounds, and when she twirled, she looked like she was engulfed in pure flames.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-Games_Dress">Post-Games Dress</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>For her interview after the Games, Katniss wore a soft, yellow dress that fell to her knees and had no sleeves. It was flimsy, gathered at her ribs, and included padding over her breasts to disguise the weight she had lost in the arena. 
Katniss considered this dress to be "a very calculated look", and Cinna's intentions soon became clear: In the eyes of the Capitol and following their own constructed narrative, Katniss and Peeta were so head-over-heels in love with each other that they were willing to sacrifice themselves and be together in death, rather than one of them emerging as victor and being alone. This story had become incredibly popular among the Capitol citizens, and was officially given as the reason why the two tributes acted in such blatant defiance against the government. To reinforce this narrative and to appease potentially angry officials, Cinna tried to make Katniss look as innocent and girlish as possible; however, in keeping with Katniss' image as the "girl on fire", the fabric of the dress had a soft, ripply glow that made it appear as though she were wearing candlelight.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour">Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Cinna remained Katniss' personal stylist for her Victory Tour, which took place about 6 months after the Games. For the tour, he designed a myriad of dresses and other clothing for her to wear as the tour passed through each district. Effie tells Katniss that "Cinna was working on your garment car. He must have over a hundred outfits for you... evening clothes are exquisite." In addition, due to Katniss' inability to find a "talent," Cinna helped her pretend to be interested in fashion, and designed a series of outfits for Katniss to publicly claim were hers. During this time, Katniss came to regard him as a close friend.
</p><p>For the first day of the Victory Tour - and the live broadcast where Katniss shows the clothes that she "designed" - Cinna creates an outfit that Katniss describes as, "Flowing black pants made of thick, warm material. A comfortable white shirt. A sweater woven from green and blue and gray strands of kitten-soft wool. Laced leather boots that don't pinch my toes."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> For the cold, Cinna adds a coat made of white ermine fur, leather gloves, a bright red scarf, and a pair of earmuffs.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In District 11, where the weather is considerably warmer than in 12, Katniss wears "a pretty orange frock patterned with autumn leaves."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> when speaking in front of the Justice Building. Cinna also designed dresses for Katniss to wear to banquets and balls following her public appearances, and it's mentioned that Cinna gives up on Katniss looking girlish and innocent and begins to "take in her (my) clothes around the waist". The dress for District 11 was pale pink, strapless, and paired with a silver shoulder wrap.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> While in <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, she wears a dress made of green silk, which she later gives to <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> to use as her <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie's wedding">wedding</a> dress.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, she wears what <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> calls a "strapless number", made of dark blue velvet and decorated with diamonds.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Throughout the books, Cinna keeps Katniss's hair in its "customary" braid, to keep her recognizable.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Wedding_Dresses">Wedding Dresses</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Katniss accepted Peeta's proposal during the Victory Tour stop in the Capitol, Cinna designed a set of wedding dresses. Initially, two dozen dresses were created, and by the time Katniss' modeling shoot arrived, they had been narrowed down to six. However, most of the dresses are not described in great detail, and some not at all, except through several strings of words:
</p>
<ul><li>"Creamy lace and pink roses and ringlets."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>"Ivory satin and gold tattoos and greenery."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>"A sheath of diamonds and jeweled veil and moonlight."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>"Heavy white silk and sleeves that fall from my wrist to the floor, and pearls."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_20-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<p>If it can be assumed that each of these sentences describes a single gown, then it would seem that only four of the six dresses are given form in the narrative. After the modeling shoot, the Capitol audience voted to select the one that would be used for the wedding; based on later descriptions, the favorite seems to have been the white silk gown with hanging sleeves and pearls.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Parade_Outfit">Parade Outfit</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>This outfit consisted of a fitted black jumpsuit, paired with a crown made of heavy black metal. When a button inside the sleeve was pressed, the suit would light up and give the appearance of being coated in glowing embers. At the same time, the crown would glow red-hot and highlight the wearer's face.
</p>

This symbolized that the rebellion was still alive and confident, but sadly this brave and rebellious act caused Cinna's death.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay_suit">Mockingjay suit</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_suit" title="Mockingjay suit">Mockingjay suit</a></i></center></div>
<p>The final outfit that Cinna designed was Katniss's mockingjay uniform, which she wore throughout the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>. When she officially agreed to help the rebel efforts, Katniss is handed a sketchbook containing Cinna's drawings of the uniform, and she recognizes his style at once: "Only one person could have designed the outfit, at first glance utterly utilitarian, at second a work of art."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Aside from a protective breastplate, the outfit incorporated carefully-tailored layers of body armor, hidden weapons in the boots and belt, and special reinforcements over the heart.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> For extra protection, the uniform included a helmet made of interwoven mesh that could be pulled back like a hood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In keeping with Katniss' role as "the Mockingjay" (a living symbol of defiance against the Capitol), the uniform was black in color, except for white patches on the underside of the sleeves, mimicking the pattern on actual Mockingjay's wings. On the final page of the sketchbook, under a drawing of Katniss' <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a>, Cinna wrote, "I'm still betting on you."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_14-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss also mentions that Cinna thought of everything when he created the uniform, like a pocket by her collarbone to hold a <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> pill that was strategically placed so Katniss could bite the pill-free if she was handcuffed.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Katniss_Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>I had expected someone flamboyant, someone older trying desperately to look young, someone who viewed me as a piece of meat to be prepared for a platter. Cinna has met none of these expectations.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> on Cinna<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Katniss initially expected that Cinna would be shallow and indifferent to her wellbeing based on the Capitol citizens she had already met. However, Cinna completely surprises her with his quiet, yet outgoing, supportive, and encouraging manner. He becomes one of her few allies during the 74th Hunger Games and helps her to make an incredibly spirited and positive impression upon Panem. The effort and work he put into ensuring her success made the two become fast friends. During the pre-launch phase, Cinna gives her hope by telling her that out of all the tributes, he would bet on her if he could. Cinna is one of the few people who understands her defiant spirit and courage, and in turn, he creates the "girl on fire" theme to remind the audience of her personality.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c2/KatnissCinnaTHG.png/revision/latest?cb=20240721122945" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna and Katniss before her interview" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="100" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="KatnissCinnaTHG.png" data-image-key="KatnissCinnaTHG.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c2/KatnissCinnaTHG.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240721122945"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c2/KatnissCinnaTHG.png/revision/latest?cb=20240721122945" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna and Katniss before her interview" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c2/KatnissCinnaTHG.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240721122945" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="100" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="KatnissCinnaTHG.png" data-image-key="KatnissCinnaTHG.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c2/KatnissCinnaTHG.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240721122945"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:KatnissCinnaTHG.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Cinna and Katniss before her interview</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In her victor's house, there is a phone which had no use before she started to chat with Cinna about her "talent." Since the only thing she was good at was hunting and singing (which she'll never do in front of the Capitol), Cinna suggested fashion designing. He took care of everything for her, including creating a book of sketches that she could show to the cameras. Cinna designed many things for her, including her opening ceremony garment for the Hunger Games, which sparked his theme for her major public appearances, "<i>Katniss, the girl who was on fire."</i>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Cinna_%26_Katniss.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120428065109" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna embraces Katniss before she enters the arena." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="160" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Cinna &amp; Katniss.jpg" data-image-key="Cinna_%26_Katniss.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Cinna_%26_Katniss.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120428065109"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Cinna_%26_Katniss.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120428065109" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna embraces Katniss before she enters the arena." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Cinna_%26_Katniss.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120428065109" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="160" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Cinna &amp; Katniss.jpg" data-image-key="Cinna_%26_Katniss.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Cinna_%26_Katniss.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120428065109"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Cinna_%26_Katniss.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Cinna embraces Katniss before she enters the arena.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure> 
<p>Katniss feared for his safety before the Quarter Quell, and indeed, as Katniss is in her glass cylinder, about to launch into the 75th Hunger Games, Peacekeepers enter the launch room and beat Cinna unconscious. This act causes Katniss to unhinge for a point, fretting that they will kill him for his design of her wedding dress during the interview. They drag him away and, according to Plutarch Heavensbee, he is killed during interrogation. Katniss suspects that President Snow planned this to throw her off-guard while in the Hunger Games arena.
</p><p>Despite Cinna's death, his designs live on in the third book, <i>Mockingjay</i>. He had already designed her mockingjay uniform, sketching it out in secret a few weeks before the start of the 75th Hunger Games, knowing there would be a war. His last message to Katniss is written under a sketch of her pin in the sketchbook containing his designs for her Mockingjay uniform. The message reads, "I'm still betting on you." The truth about Cinna being a rebel was kept from Katniss at his own request until her acceptance of being the Mockingjay. Cinna, knowing Katniss would be indecisive, chose not to reveal himself and influence her decision one way or another as everyone else tried. Once Katniss knew her trusted friend was a rebel and continued to support her, she was extremely comforted and more confident in her decision. Cinna's final design gave her everything she needed to be the Mockingjay, both in appearance and during battle. When Katniss tried to commit suicide using a nightlock pill hidden away in a compartment of the uniform Cinna designed in case she got captured, Katniss referred to it as "Cinna's final gift" to her.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Portia">Portia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/THGChariot1.png/revision/latest?cb=20240728075953" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna and Portia after chariot parade." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="104" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="THGChariot1.png" data-image-key="THGChariot1.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/THGChariot1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240728075953"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/THGChariot1.png/revision/latest?cb=20240728075953" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna and Portia after chariot parade." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/THGChariot1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240728075953" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="104" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="THGChariot1.png" data-image-key="THGChariot1.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/THGChariot1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240728075953"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:THGChariot1.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Cinna and Portia after chariot parade.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a> and Cinna are both stylists in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i>. They came up with the amazing idea of the District 12 tributes on <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a>. Cinna and Portia seemed to get along very well. They were good friends, both were young and friendly to their tributes. They were a team and were talking before the Tribute Parade in the 74th Hunger Games. Portia and Cinna had a strong friendship.
</p><p>Cinna and Portia then became the District 12 stylists in the 75th Hunger Games. They came up with the next idea, then when the Games started, Cinna was killed by the Capitol, with Portia later suffering the same fate. Cinna was clearly part of the rebellion; although Portia never said she was, she likely teamed up with Cinna.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>I asked for District 12.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Cinna, to Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/THG04.png/revision/latest?cb=20240803104535" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna keeping Katniss' secret about the mockingjay pin" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="102" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="THG04.png" data-image-key="THG04.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/THG04.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240803104535"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/THG04.png/revision/latest?cb=20240803104535" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna keeping Katniss' secret about the mockingjay pin" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/THG04.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240803104535" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="102" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="THG04.png" data-image-key="THG04.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/THG04.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20240803104535"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:THG04.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Cinna keeping Katniss' secret about the mockingjay pin</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Cinna's designs suggest that he is intelligent and crafty, able to create noteworthy imagery around his tributes so that they will be noticed. He maintains strong control over his emotions so that his subtle acts of defiance towards the Capitol will not be too obvious. He personally told Katniss that he channels his emotions through his work. He does however show care for Katniss and a deep desire to oppose the Capitol through his knowledge of fashion. He is so good at hiding his true allegiance that no one, not even Katniss, knew that Cinna was in fact a rebel and not just subtly rebellious, as revealed in <i>Mockingjay</i>, where his designs were given to Plutarch Heavensbee, someone who was not known to be a rebel either until the 75th Hunger Games. Unlike the other rebels, Cinna's work was more subtle and allowed him to blend in with the Capitol without issue.
</p><p>Cinna is described as being a "brilliant, enigmatic, lovely" person who takes pride in his work.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He is also a very caring person. Despite being Capitol raised, in the movie, he says he is sorry that Katniss became a tribute to her, unlike most, who just congratulate her. He attempts to calm Katniss down right before the 74th Hunger Games by holding her hand and saying if he was allowed to bet, he would bet on her. He seems to be something of a father figure for her, as well as a friend. Katniss originally wonders if his "calm and normal demeanor masks a complete madman."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>I'm taken aback by how normal he looks.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/Cinna.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130306001028" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna's Capitol Portrait" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="267" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Cinna.jpg" data-image-key="Cinna.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/Cinna.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130306001028"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/Cinna.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130306001028" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cinna's Capitol Portrait" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/Cinna.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130306001028" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="267" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Cinna.jpg" data-image-key="Cinna.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/79/Cinna.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130306001028"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Cinna.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Cinna's Capitol Portrait</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Cinna's appearance and comportment were very different from his contemporaries: Most people who live in the Capitol follow highly outlandish fashion trends, wear heavy makeup, and tend to have at least some of their physical features highly altered. By contrast, Cinna dressed in simple clothing and wore very minimal makeup. Also, though he spoke with the distinctive Capitol accent, it was noticeably less pronounced than many other citizens. While purely speculative, all of this suggests that he may not have been born in the Capitol. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Books">Books</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <i>The Hunger Games</i>, Cinna is described as wearing a simple black shirt with matching pants. Wearing gold eyeliner is his unique and bizarre fashion choice, exposing gold flecks in his green eyes, an attractive look for Katniss. Besides that, Cinna looks very normal, with short brown hair.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film">Film</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the film, Cinna has brown eyes, a mustache and beard, short black hair, dark skin, and six gold earrings: four in the left ear and two in the right.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cinna appears to be named after <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnaeus_Cornelius_Cinna_Magnus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Gnaeus Cornelius Cinna Magnus">Gnaeus Cornelius Cinna Magnus</a>, better known simply as Cinna, who was the first person pardoned by the emperor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Augustus">Augustus</a> for his conspiracy against him.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Director Francis Lawrence, who directed both <i>Catching Fire</i> and <i>Mockingjay Part 1</i> and <i>2</i>, stated that Cinna was originally going to appear in <i>Mockingjay Part 1 </i>during the take in which President Snow gives a speech to the districts and orders stricter punishments as well as executions. The concept for the scene was that President Snow's speech would have been delivered during a series of grim executions that were broadcast live, with Cinna's execution being carried out last along with a specific message to Katniss. This idea was later discarded and reworked. Actor and musician Lenny Kravitz told various media outlets during the premiere of <i>Catching Fire</i> that he was hoping to appear in the first adaptation, even if it was just for a few takes. Unbeknownst to him, filming for Mockingjay had already begun.</li></ul>
