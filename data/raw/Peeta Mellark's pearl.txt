<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>Sometimes when I'm alone, I take the pearl from where it lives in my pocket and try to remember the boy with the bread, the strong arms that warded off nightmares on the train, the kisses in the arena.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>This <b>pearl</b> was found by <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> inside an oyster while he was diving for seafood in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>. He gave it to Katniss Everdeen as a gift and promised that he would protect her. Although a small token, the pearl was a clear message of love from Peeta, and Katniss knew it; she kept it close by her and constantly protected it, as Peeta had vowed to protect her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While residing in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> following her rescue from the arena, Katniss kept it in the drawer that held her clothing and few possessions.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeeta_Mellark%2527s_pearl%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeeta_Mellark%2527s_pearl%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/20/Peeta-oyster.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140213183635" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta finding the pearl in an oyster." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/20/Peeta-oyster.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140213183635" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="158" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Peeta-oyster.jpg" data-image-key="Peeta-oyster.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Peeta-oyster.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peeta finding the pearl in an oyster.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a> made a plan to shock the water in the arena of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the tributes decided to make a feast of seafood. This included diving for oysters, and when Peeta pried one open, he found the pearl. He told <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> that when enough pressure was placed on coal, it would turn into pearls.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This was an inside joke between him and Katniss, referring to <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>'s mistaken pronouncement before their last <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">Hunger Games</a>, which confused pearl creation with the creation of <a href="/wiki/Diamonds" title="Diamonds">diamonds</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peeta gave Katniss the pearl, telling her "for you", and Katniss closely guarded his gift, thinking that it was the only gift she could truly accept. She knew that her <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">mother</a> and <a href="/wiki/Prim" class="mw-redirect" title="Prim">Prim</a> would know to return the pearl to Peeta if she died protecting him, as she was positive she would.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeeta_Mellark%2527s_pearl%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>I sit back on my bed cross-legged and find myself rubbing the smooth iridescent surface of the pearl back and forth against my lips. For some reason, it's soothing. A cool kiss from the giver himself.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss being comforted by the pearl<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Katniss later brought the pearl to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, waking up in the middle of the night and looking at it. She kept it in a drawer in her unit inside a <a href="/wiki/Silver_parachute" title="Silver parachute">silver parachute</a> that also held the <a href="/wiki/Tools_and_gear#Spile" title="Tools and gear">spile</a>, which she had received in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Just before the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_13" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 13">bombing of 13</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> went back to Katniss' room and retrieved it, along with her hunting jacket, her mother's wedding photo, the plant book, and the contents of her dresser drawer.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_10_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Katniss also brought the pearl on her mission to assassinate <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> with <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>; she kept it in a pocket in her uniform throughout the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Katniss dropped the keys to unlock Peeta's handcuffs in her pocket, she heard them clinking together.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After that, the pearl was returned to Katniss's house in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>, along with her other belongings.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
