<p><b><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://ok-win.games/">Betting</a></b> was a major part of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, both for people from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and those in the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>. It was introduced in the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> mentioned people from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> betting on the two <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> reaped, and that "odds [were] given on their ages, whether [they were] <a href="/wiki/Seam" class="mw-redirect" title="Seam">Seam</a> or <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a>, and if they [would] break down and weep."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the Capitol, betting took place throughout the Games, starting before <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">training</a> and increasing in intensity until a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> is determined. Various statistics are tracked and reported to help people place their bets, such as each tribute's list of kills.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Some people close to the Games, such as head <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylists</a> and presumably <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a> as well, were not allowed to bet. Cinna&#160;mentioned that he was not allowed to bet, but if he could, he would put all his money on Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Proceeds from betting were funneled toward the cost of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Betting activity was high around the opening of each Games, when the initial casualties occurred.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, betting rates further increased in the Capitol after the final eight, when interviews were conducted with the tributes' families.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBetting%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>Betting was first implemented for the 10th Hunger Games. The idea was introduced by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> after a comment from <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a> while the mentors were in a meeting about how to make the Games gain more viewers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While the Games occurred, crowds of Capitol citizens gathered around the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> as they supported their tribute. On the third day of the Games, a fight between <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a> fans and <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> fans started over who would be the victor, and Coriolanus was happy that fans of his tribute, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, were better mannered.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Betting odds were not created yet. Prior to the start of the Games, Capitol citizens could visit their local post office between eight in the morning to eight at night to place bets.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the Games themselves, a phone number was made available for betting, though those who wished to bet in person at the post office could still do so.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBetting%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Betting played a role in how the tributes conduct themselves. Katniss tried to appear strong in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, because she believed that nobody would bet on "a tribute who keeps sniveling over the deaths of her opponents."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When realizing Katniss intended to go to the <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">feast</a> in spite of her telling him otherwise, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> told her to never bet at cards, as she would "lose [her] last coin."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e0/Statsreference.png/revision/latest?cb=20140317003459" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Statsreference" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e0/Statsreference.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140317003459" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Statsreference.png" data-image-key="Statsreference.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Statsreference.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure>

<p>The odds for the tributes in the last 6 districts would be considered unknown as they are exact or near copies of the odds of the tribute with the same gender but six districts earlier (with the exception of Rue and Thresh).
</p><p>Cato had the highest odds at 3–1. <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> and the <a href="/wiki/District_3_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 3 female (74th HG)">District 3 female</a> ended with the lowest known odds of winning in the film, but it is likely the odds for the tributes in the latter half have lower odds.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBetting%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The betting scene in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> was originally filmed as a scene at a cafe, but during the editing process, director <a href="/wiki/Gary_Ross" title="Gary Ross">Gary Ross</a> felt there wasn't enough to link that scene with the one that came before it: Katniss and Peeta catching Rue watching them during training. He storyboarded what eventually became the CGI-animated sequence of the betting board, which completely transformed the scene.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The odds expression <i>b-a</i> is the expected number of wins, <i>a</i>, after <i>b</i> losses. In other words, it is the chance of winning after <i>a+b</i> games, and the chances is expressed as <i>a/(a+b)</i>.
<ul><li>Cato, with an odds of 3–1, would be killed three out of four games he participated (in case "time-reversals" are allowed). He therefore approximately had a 25% chance of winning the Games. Rue, with an odds of 60–1, will have roughly a 1/61 chance of winning the games.</li>
<li>If every tribute has equal odds of winning the Games, they will all have odds of 23–1. This is held by the District 6 female and possibly the male as well.</li></ul></li>
<li>There are two scenes in the film where the odds tables are shown.
<ul><li>In the first, the camera focuses on the District 11 tributes, showing Thresh's relatively high chance of winning, and Rue's extremely low chance of winning. The District 10 female's odds are also shown, with an odds of 10–1.</li>
<li>In the second, the camera focuses on the entire betting board. Note the odds on the tributes listed on the right side are almost copies of the odds of the tributes listed on the left side. Rue's odds also inexplicably went up to 7-1 due to this phenomenon.</li></ul></li>
<li>The odds tables are extremely inaccurate (possibly even incomplete) and over-stated even for a real-life morning line odds, and they sum up to much greater than unity.
<ul><li>In fact, the odds of the tributes on the right side of the betting board, from District 7 to 12, are mostly copies of the same-gender tribute six districts earlier. This explains why Rue has a betting odds of 7–1, and the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a> is also given odds of 3–1, tying with Cato. Because of this reason, most betting odds of tributes from the later 6 districts have unknown and likely very low odds, as the film staff did not put in any effort in determining the odds of the remaining tributes.
<ul><li>Rue is a notable exception, as she has a 60-1 odds, but on the scene where the listings of all the tributes are posted, her odds are 7–1, the same as Foxface. Thresh is also an exception, as he has 10-1 odds decreasing to 11-1, but on the next view his odds became 8-1, the same as the District 5 male.</li></ul></li>
<li>From the morning line odds board, the chances a tribute from the <i>first</i> 6 districts will win is approximately 126%, which is a high normal for the sum of probabilities in a typical real-life betting board. If the last 6 districts have odds that are exact copies, then the total outcome sums up to an absurd 250%.</li>
<li>Nevertheless, the District 1 and 2 tributes have a 70.83% chance of winning the games, consistent with how the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> were highly favored by the Capitol, but still giving enough chance for all the remaining districts except for possibly District 12 to get at least one male and one female victor in the previous 73 games.
<ul><li>This already pushes the odds of the remaining 20 tributes to only 68-1 if the odds sum up to 100%, and to only 36-1 of the odds sum up to 125%. This is lower than even the District 3 female's odds of winning! (From 29.17%/20 = 1.459% and 54.17%/20 = 2.709% respectively).</li></ul></li></ul></li></ul>
