</p><p><b>Toby Jones</b> is an English film actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i></a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FToby_Jones%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>One of his roles included voicing Dobby in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Chamber_of_Secrets" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets">second</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Deathly_Hallows_-_Part_1" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows - Part 1">penultimate</a> <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Harry Potter">Harry Potter</a></i> films. Another role he portrayed was Dr. Arnim Zola in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_America:_The_First_Avenger" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Captain America: The First Avenger">Captain America: The First Avenger</a></i> alongside <a href="/wiki/Stanley_Tucci" title="Stanley Tucci">Stanley Tucci</a>. He also played the Dream Lord in the 2010 episode of <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Who" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Doctor Who">Doctor Who</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy%27s_Choice_(Doctor_Who)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Amy&#39;s Choice (Doctor Who)">Amy's Choice</a></i>. He has also portrayed <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Hitchcock" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Alfred Hitchcock">Alfred Hitchcock</a> in the 2012 HBO Film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girl_(2012_HBO_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Girl (2012 HBO film)">The Girl</a></i>.
</p>
