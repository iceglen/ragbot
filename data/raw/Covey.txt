<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Dark_Days">Dark Days</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Since they were not tied to a specific location, the Covey remained neutral during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, siding with neither the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> nor <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During their wandering days, they typically stuck to the east of Panem, but also claimed to be familiar with lands to the north, which were beyond the Capitol's control.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Genocide">Genocide</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>"A few years" prior to the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the Covey were rounded up by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>. Those who resisted were killed by being beaten or shot,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the survivors were forced to settle in the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Life_in_District_12">Life in District 12</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 242px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e2/The_Covey.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231229080752" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Covey performs in the Hob" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e2/The_Covey.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/242?cb=20231229080752" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="242" height="190" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="The Covey.jpg" data-image-key="The_Covey.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:The_Covey.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Covey performs in <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>One group of survivors was "obliged to stay" in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When only six Covey children remained there, an old man took them in for a fee. He died the year before the 10th Hunger Games took place, but a few members of the Covey were already old enough to manage things by then.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They made their money singing at special occasions like weddings<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and birthdays,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or putting on regular performances at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>. Evidently, Peacekeepers were some of their best customers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, outside of their work as performers, the Covey were seen as outsiders in District 12, and people were suspicious of them.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>A year later, the Covey lived in a <a href="/wiki/The_Covey%27s_house" title="The Covey&#39;s house">house</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>, near <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> beyond it.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They considered the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a> their "second home".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-prequel">Post-prequel</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the epilogue, a new commander took over the Peacekeepers from <a href="/wiki/Commander_Hoff" title="Commander Hoff">Commander Hoff</a>, and his first act was to outlaw music shows at the Hob.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This removed a major source of the Covey's income, though they may have still been hired to play at events like weddings and birthdays. For reliable wages, they likely had to turn to the <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal mines</a> for work like the rest of the district, or other kinds of work. Tam Amber became a blacksmith.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Clerk_Carmine_Clade" title="Clerk Carmine Clade">Clerk Carmine Clade</a> escaped the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a> with his fiddle and performed at the <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie&#39;s wedding">wedding</a> of <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, although no other Covey members accompanied him.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Interview_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Interview-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>

<p>The Covey held a cultural identity distinct from any particular district. They were musicians by trade, known for their love of travel, music, and bright colors, which featured prominently on their clothes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The six Covey alive at the time of the 10th Hunger Games "varied in complexion, hair, and features".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Names">Names</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Covey had a very particular naming convention. Their first names had two parts: the first was taken from a traditional folk <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ballad" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:ballad">ballad</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the second was a color. The one exception to this rule was Lucy Gray; the first and second part of her name both came from the same ballad.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Music">Music</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Covey had a wide array of folk songs in their repertoire, some of which were passed down through the generations, while others they wrote themselves. They were known to sing complex harmonies and play a number of instruments, including the guitar, drum, mandolin, fiddle, bass,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_13-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and accordion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Cultural_values">Cultural values</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Can’t travel. Can’t perform without their say-so. Can only sing certain types of songs. Fight getting round up, and you get shot dead like my daddy. Try to keep your family together, and you get your head broken like my mama. What if I think that price is too high to pay? Maybe my freedom’s worth the risk.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Lucy Gray on life under Capitol rule<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The Covey deeply cherished their freedom of movement and freedom of expression: being at liberty to sing, speak their minds, and hold onto their community and cultural identity. Lucy Gray also mentioned that the Covey believed everyone's purpose on earth was to "reduce the misery, not add to it."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Turns_of_phrase">Turns of phrase</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Birds. Always birds with her, when it came to the Covey. Singing, perching, feathers in hats. Pretty birds all.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Coriolanus Snow<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>"<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/covey">Covey</a>" is a noun used to refer to a flock of birds, particularly a <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/brood">brood</a> of young birds. This is fitting because the remaining Covey are all young, they show a particular fondness for mockingjays,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_4-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and their harmonies have been compared to the mockingjays' birdsong.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_13-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCovey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Based on their nomadic lifestyle, colorful clothing, and love of music/performing, the Covey appear to be inspired by the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romani_people" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Romani people">Romani people</a>.
<ul><li>Some have also speculated that, given that District 12 is located in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Appalachia">Appalachia</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and people from the Seam are described as typically having olive skin,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> members of the Covey may be <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melungeon" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Melungeon">Melungeon</a>; however, it should be noted this is a derogatory term with racist and classist connotations.</li></ul></li>
<li>In District 12, the Covey kept a pet goat named <a href="/wiki/Shamus" title="Shamus">Shamus</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>They had also been talking about building a herd of goats and selling the milk as a second source of income,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so some theorize that the <a href="/wiki/Goat_Man" title="Goat Man">Goat Man</a> from the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a><sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> may have been one of the Covey— most probably Tam Amber.</li></ul></li>
<li>Since some of the Covey's songs survived into the trilogy through <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, who learned them from <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">her father</a>, it's been theorized that they are descendants of the Covey— probably through Maude Ivory, who shared Katniss's ability to memorize music after a listen or two.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i> confirms that <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> and by extension Katniss is related to the Covey with <a href="/wiki/Barb_Azure_Baird" title="Barb Azure Baird">Barb Azure Baird</a> most likely being Burdock's mother.</li></ul></li></ul>
