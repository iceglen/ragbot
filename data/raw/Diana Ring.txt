</p><p><b>Diana Ring</b>, also known by the nickname "Didi", was a student at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> and a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> female tribute, <a href="/wiki/Ginnee" title="Ginnee">Ginnee</a>, during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She was killed in a bombing while taking her tribute on a tour of the arena.
</p><p>She was one of the two Ring twins, the other being her brother <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo Ring</a>. She and her brother wholly embraced their status as twins, sporting identical outfits and sunny personalities.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDiana_Ring%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>While in the zoo, Ginnee and her district partner, <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a>, began a funny dance, and the Ring twins got some of the audience to clap along. This led to the environment becoming more festive and social.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the start of the Games, the tributes and their mentors were taken on a tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, sending shrapnel that killed Diana, as well as Apollo and their assigned tributes. A double funeral was held the following day.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDiana_Ring%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diana_(mythology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Diana (mythology)">Diana</a> was the Roman virgin goddess of the hunt, wild animals, fertility, and the moon. She was the twin sister of the god <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Apollo">Apollo</a>.
</p>
