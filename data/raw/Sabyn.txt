<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sabyn grew up in District 2 and became her district's female tribute in <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with the other tributes, Sabyn was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During a tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, several explosions planted by the rebels were detonated. The bombs exploded a large hole near the entrance of the arena, and through the chaos, each tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> made a run for it. Sabyn made it to the river, farther than <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">Facet</a> and <a href="/wiki/Velvereen" title="Velvereen">Velvereen</a> had, but leaped over the wall only to die in the fall. Her body was later tied to a horse and dragged down <a href="/wiki/Scholars_Road" title="Scholars Road">Scholars Road</a> during <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana Ring</a>'s funeral.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, Sabyn isn't seen escaping with <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a> or shot down by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> alongside Facet and Velvereen. Because of this, it can be speculated that she died in the initial explosion.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sabyn's name may be a reference to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabines" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sabines">Sabines</a>, an ancient Italian people situated near Rome, whose society existed during Roman times. Legend has it that the Romans abducted Sabine women to help populate Rome; this is analogous to how Sabyn was reaped by the Capitol to compete in the Games.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Despite being reaped, Sabyn never competed in the 10th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>She was implied to be one of the stronger tributes, as <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> repeatedly mentioned throughout the book that with the deaths of the <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and 2 tributes, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray's</a> odds of winning significantly increase.</li></ul>
