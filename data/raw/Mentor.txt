<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Creation">Creation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>For those of you who don't know, I'm Dr. Gaul, the <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a>, and I will be mentoring your mentorships.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> explaining her position<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The mentor program was established for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. It was said to be the idea of <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, the dean of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, who originally created the Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In truth, Highbottom abhorred the Hunger Games and the project was in fact the brainchild of the <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a>, Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 289px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/10th_Hunger_Games_Mentors.png/revision/latest?cb=20231004222640" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Student mentors for the 10th Hunger Games during the reaping ceremony." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bc/10th_Hunger_Games_Mentors.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/289?cb=20231004222640" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="289" height="106" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="10th Hunger Games Mentors.png" data-image-key="10th_Hunger_Games_Mentors.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:10th_Hunger_Games_Mentors.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Student mentors for the 10th Hunger Games during the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> ceremony.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>24 of the top-performing Academy students were invited to be mentors, each paired with a tribute from one of the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>. It was their job to then groom their tribute for the Games, with the idea being that those who did well would receive a monetary prize. In this early stage, there were few specific rules or guidelines for mentors.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Dr. Gaul regularly visited them, encouraging them to consider the nature of the Hunger Games and, as viewership was low at the time, she asked them to brainstorm ways to get people more engaged.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the Games, mentors wore bright yellow mentor badges on lanyards around their necks. They were also issued <a href="/wiki/Communicuff" title="Communicuff">communicuffs</a>, which they could use to track donations and send <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a> to their tributes in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ultimately, Dr. Gaul considered the 10th Hunger Games a failure and felt that it was a mistake to involve Academy students. Several had been killed over the course of the event, particularly in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol Arena bombing">Capitol Arena bombing</a>. This presented <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> as being far too vulnerable.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Evolution">Evolution</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At an unknown point, the mentorship program was reimplemented, and past <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> of the Hunger Games began to serve as mentors. However, since this was not mentioned during planning for the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> it was presumably not reinstated that year.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Purpose">Purpose</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">"<i>Your role is to turn these children into spectacles, not survivors.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—<a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> on the job of a mentor<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>A mentor's primary job was to convince their respective district's tributes to perform for the Capitol, both leading up to the Games and within the arena itself. Since victors had firsthand knowledge of the Games, they could provide invaluable knowledge to their tributes about how to play the audience and survive. By developing potential strategies based on a tribute's skills (or lack thereof), mentors played an important role in their tributes' potential for survival and victory.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Selection">Selection</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 215px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1d/Ballad_tribute_and_mentor_pairing.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240505181008" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Highbottom's notecards listing tribute and mentor pairings." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1d/Ballad_tribute_and_mentor_pairing.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/215?cb=20240505181008" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="215" height="90" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Ballad tribute and mentor pairing.jpg" data-image-key="Ballad_tribute_and_mentor_pairing.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Ballad_tribute_and_mentor_pairing.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Highbottom's notecards listing tribute and mentor pairings.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>For the 10th Hunger Games, the top 24 students at the Academy were selected to serve as mentors, and each was assigned a single tribute before watching <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> ceremonies from all the districts. These assignments were not random, and the wealth and influence of a student's family could afford them a more favorable placement.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>For example, when <a href="/wiki/Livia_Cardew" title="Livia Cardew">Livia Cardew</a> was assigned to mentor the <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">District 1 male</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> believed this was because her mother ran the largest bank in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> also suspected that his own father— war profiteer <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a>— had arranged for him to get the <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">District 2 male</a>, because Sejanus and his family had emigrated from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> after the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Later_years">Later years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/Haymitch_train.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419081422" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Haymitch Abernathy advising Peeta Mellark and Katniss Everdeen." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="89" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Haymitch train.jpg" data-image-key="Haymitch_train.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/Haymitch_train.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120419081422"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/Haymitch_train.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419081422" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Haymitch Abernathy advising Peeta Mellark and Katniss Everdeen." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/Haymitch_train.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120419081422" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="89" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Haymitch train.jpg" data-image-key="Haymitch_train.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/19/Haymitch_train.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120419081422"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Haymitch_train.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Haymitch advising <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Little is known of how mentors were chosen in subsequent years, but when a district only had a single living victor, it was a compulsory position. For example, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was forced to serve as <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s only mentor<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> since his victory in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Prior to Haymitch's victory, District 12 mentors were selected from a pool of victors from other districts not tapped to oversee their own districts' tributes. Ostensibly, these were very talented candidates, though, in truth, these were outcasts who would be pariahs if they led District 12 tributes to victory while their own districts' tributes died and most years, those of District 12 did not even hear about who were selected as mentors.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_3_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite the compulsory nature of mentoring, not all victors became mentors, as was the case for <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>. <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> could not recall seeing her in the public eye after her Games,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so it's possible that if a victor was too fragile to be presentable, they could be exempted. Furthermore, in districts with multiple victors, the newest victors were not always drafted for the job; older victors could also continue their work despite younger victors being eligible.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>There were no gender restraints, so a male victor could mentor a female tribute (such as Coriolanus Snow and <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>),<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and a female victor could mentor a male tribute (like <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tasks">Tasks</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Strategy">Strategy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In general, mentors were responsible for advising their tributes throughout the process of going into the Games. This included helping them devise and implement strategies to use during <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>, <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">training</a>, televised <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, and the actual Games themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sponsors">Sponsors</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">Sponsorship</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Sponsors.png/revision/latest?cb=20130703232218" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Haymitch talking to sponsors." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Sponsors.png" data-image-key="Sponsors.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Sponsors.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130703232218"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Sponsors.png/revision/latest?cb=20130703232218" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Haymitch talking to sponsors." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Sponsors.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130703232218" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Sponsors.png" data-image-key="Sponsors.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Sponsors.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130703232218"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Sponsors.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Haymitch talking to sponsors.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Mentors particularly focused on helping their tributes appeal to prospective sponsors. Mentors were the only ones who could seal sponsor deals for their tributes,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and with that money, they could choose what items to send to their tribute in the arena and when.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victors">Victors</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>If a mentor's tribute emerged from the Games as a victor, they were obliged to make an appearance at closing ceremonies. When the victor's support team rose up from beneath the stage for the occasion, the mentor was the last person introduced before the new victor.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour">Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The mentor of a winning tribute also accompanied them throughout their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> across <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMentor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The 10th Hunger Games are the only Hunger Games in the series where full names are given for every mentor.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> are the only 2 victors not to be mentors as they were sent back in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Games</a> the year after they won.</li></ul>
