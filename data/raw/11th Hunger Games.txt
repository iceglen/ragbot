</p><p>The <b>11th Hunger Games</b> were held one year after the events of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and sixty-three years prior to the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> was sixteen-year-old <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>, the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Pre-Games">Pre-Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F11th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">Betting</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorship</a>, sponsor gifts, and <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>— all introduced during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>— were carried forward into the 11th.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> New developments this year aimed to increase engagement in both <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>, and to encourage stronger tributes to <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a>. These additions included a monetary prize for the victor, food parcels for their entire <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>, and the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The tributes had minimal training.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> For the first time, the Games took place in the wild, as opposed to the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F11th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Mags valued having <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">allies</a>, and they took turns keeping watch at night. Her main desire was to protect her <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F11th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Mags would have been the first new victor to move into a Victors' Village and the first to officially receive compensation for winning the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. District 4 would have been the first to receive the promised food parcels as well.
