</p><p><b>Felix Ravinstill</b> was a great-nephew of <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a> and a student at <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s prestigious <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. He was one of the school's 24 top-performing seniors selected to be <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. He was assigned the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, <a href="/wiki/Dill" title="Dill">Dill</a>.
</p>

<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Felix_at_arena.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240803155000" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Felix visiting the arena." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Felix_at_arena.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240803155000" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="142" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Felix at arena.jpg" data-image-key="Felix_at_arena.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Felix_at_arena.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Felix visiting the arena.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Felix visited the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> with the other mentors and their tributes.
</p><p>Felix tried to promote his tribute during the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, but his tribute's sickly disposition gave him little opportunity to make an impact.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He seemed to know <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, the dean of the Academy, well, and caught up on gossip together after the pre-show.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Dill placed fourteenth, passing away from complications of <a href="/wiki/Health_conditions#Tuberculosis" title="Health conditions">tuberculosis</a> shortly after entering the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. Felix commented that he was surprised that she had lasted the day when he saw her emerge, sending her two bottles of water, although they were stolen by <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a>, the male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>. Felix was graceful in defeat and was complimented excessively by <a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a>, though that may have been due to Felix being the president's grandnephew.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/28/Felix_Ravinstill_announcement.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240215030003" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The television announcement about Felix." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/28/Felix_Ravinstill_announcement.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240215030003" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="136" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Felix Ravinstill announcement.jpg" data-image-key="Felix_Ravinstill_announcement.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Felix_Ravinstill_announcement.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The television announcement about Felix.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, Felix was the son of the president. He was hospitalized and in critical condition after the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_the_Capitol_Arena" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of the Capitol Arena">bombing of the Capitol Arena</a>, succumbing to his injuries during the Games only a few days later. An image of his body was televised during the broadcast, and his death was the justification for the Head <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>, Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, to send <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> into the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFelix_Ravinstill%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Felix was the one of the four sons of the Roman god <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_(mythology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Saturn (mythology)">Saturn</a> and a mortal woman, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entoria" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Entoria">Entoria</a>. In Latin, the word <i>felix</i> means "lucky" and "blessed". It was adopted as a cognomen with that meaning by the Roman dictator <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulla" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sulla">Lucius Cornelius Sulla Felix</a>.
</p>
