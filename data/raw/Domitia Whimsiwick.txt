<p><b>Domitia Whimsiwick</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She was assigned the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDomitia_Whimsiwick%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Domitia was the 'dairy heiress' and thus had extensive knowledge on livestock. This turned out to be a useful asset as during the interviews, she got Tanner to talk about different ways of slaughtering cows, leading to the biggest applause from the crowd, with the exception of <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>'s interview.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> started reading aloud the essays that the mentors wrote on what they most enjoyed of war, Domitia commented that she felt as if she was a part of something bigger, having to make sacrifices to save the country of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a>, was killed by his own allies, <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>. Afterwards, Domitia was gracious in defeat, saying that her tribute was a very good-natured person and that District 4 took advantage of it. She said it was one of the many things she learned from being a mentor, believing that she would always cherish her experience. After Domitia wished the remaining mentors the best of luck, <a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a> stated that she had just shown how to be a good loser.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDomitia_Whimsiwick%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She had a bubbly personality and was good at talking with people, which she showed with her tribute during the interviews.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Domitia was also rather sensitive and kind, as she told <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> that his story of the turkey was touching, unlike what many of the other mentors said,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> showing her ability to have empathy as well. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDomitia_Whimsiwick%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She had a ponytail on the top of her head.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDomitia_Whimsiwick%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Domitia is the feminine form of the Roman clan <i>Domitius</i>, which produced the father of the Roman emperor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nero" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nero">Nero</a>. The most notable woman by this name was Nero's <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domitia_(aunt_of_Messalina)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Domitia (aunt of Messalina)">paternal aunt</a>, but it was also shared by the wife of emperor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domitian" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Domitian">Domitian</a> and the mothers of emperors <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrian" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hadrian">Hadrian</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Aurelius" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Marcus Aurelius">Marcus Aurelius</a>.
</p>
