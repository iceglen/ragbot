</p><p><b>Recelio Goldsmith</b> was a citizen of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a clerk for the Capitol Residential Real Estate Company. His name appeared on an eviction notice in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, Goldsmith was the clerk who sent an eviction notice to the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Snow_family" title="Capitol families">Snow family</a>, stating that they had to move out of their <a href="/wiki/The_Snows%27_apartment_building" title="The Snows&#39; apartment building">apartment</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Corso" title="Corso">Corso</a> within 15 days.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRecelio_Goldsmith%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Recelio is a name of unknown origin, but it may be used here as a pun on reselling, since the real estate company meant to evict the Snows and resell their apartment to new buyers. Goldsmith is an occupational surname for one who made or sold gold, but eventually became associated with banking.
</p>
