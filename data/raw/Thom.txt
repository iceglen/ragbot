</p><p><b>Thom</b> is <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s old mining friend, having become a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> around the same time as Gale did. Thom was native to <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Thom is Gale's friend and crewmate, and has often been seen with <a href="/wiki/Bristel" title="Bristel">Bristel</a>, a fellow miner. He and Gale shared similar interests; both came from <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> and went into the occupation of coal mining together during <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s time in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. When she came back, Gale's time with Thom was minimized to just working in the mines. 
</p><p>Thom's loyalty to Gale was shown after his friend was whipped for breaking the law, as he helped Katniss carry Gale to <a href="/wiki/Mrs_Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs Everdeen">Mrs Everdeen</a> while piecing together what had happened. For his actions, he was given money by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> before going home.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> burned down District 12, Thom was one of just under 800 survivors that managed to escape the district safely. They were brought from the bordering forests to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> a few days after the disaster, and Thom, like Gale, became a soldier in the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">rebellion</a> against the Capitol.
</p><p>Thom made his way through the war and was seen again by Katniss when she was confined to District 12 after assassinating <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">President Coin</a>. He was sifting through the dirt for remains in front of where the <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Undersee" title="Mayor Undersee">mayor</a>'s family had lived, and talked to Katniss, whom he was on good terms with. It was Thom who informed Katniss that <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a> and her family, along with two of the family's workers, did not survive the destruction of District 12. It is presumed that Thom now lives a normal life in the rebuilt District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
