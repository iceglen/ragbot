</p><p><b>Athena Strates</b> is an actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Persephone_Price" title="Persephone Price">Persephone Price</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAthena_Strates%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Strates is a South African born actress best known internationally for <i>Genius: Picasso</i>, in which she appears opposite Antonio Banderas as Maya Picasso; <i>The Good Liar</i>, starring Helen Mirren and Ian McKellen; and in 2020, her lead role as Texel Textor in Kike Maíllo's psychological thriller <i>A Perfect Enemy</i> opposite Tomasz Kot.
</p><p>In addition to her international work, Strates also has several German television credits to her name, including <i>Aufbruch ins Ungewisse</i>, a German television series produced by CBS Studios and directed by Kai Wessel and Ze Networkby Christian Alvart. Strates is represented by Baumbauer Actors and Independent Talent Agency.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
