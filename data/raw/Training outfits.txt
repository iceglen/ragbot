<p><b>Training outfits</b> were clothes worn by <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> while they <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">trained</a> to compete in the annual <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="1st-10th_Hunger_Games">1st-10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the first ten years of the Games, there was no training period.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In any case, no changes of clothing were provided to tributes; they kept on whatever they had worn to their <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> right into the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="49th_Hunger_Games">49th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Magno_Stift" title="Magno Stift">Magno Stift</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a> for <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, sent the District 12 tributes to the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> in their training outfits and was placed on probation because of it.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Capitol provided the outfits for training and the arena itself, and thus everyone was dressed the same.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_7_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> By the time of the interviews, the District 12 tributes had been wearing their training outfits for three days straight with them becoming worn and dirty, and worried that Magno would pull the same stunt as the previous year.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They were saved by <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>, who arrived with clothing from the wardrobes of her Great-Aunt <a href="/wiki/Messalina_Trinket" title="Messalina Trinket">Messalina</a> and Great-Uncle <a href="/wiki/Silius_Trinket" title="Silius Trinket">Silius</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>It makes me irritated that Peeta is wearing exactly the same outfit I am. I need to say something to Cinna. This twins act is going to blow up in out faces once the Games begin.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> on her and Peeta's training outfits<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Ahead of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, tributes' clothes were individually chosen by their <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylists</a>. The <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> stylists, <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> and <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a>, dressed their respective tributes exactly alike during the three-day training period. On the first day, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> wore tight black pants, long-sleeved burgundy tunics, and leather shoes. Every tribute had a cloth square with their district number pinned to their back, identifying them to the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games_film"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the tributes all wore identical uniforms. They had black short-sleeved shirts with red and light gray stripes on the sleeves and underarms; black sweatpants with similar stripes down the sides; and black leather shoes. The only difference in uniform was their district numbers, which were printed on each of their sleeves and their backs.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During preparation for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the tributes still wore outfits chosen by their stylists, assuming they bothered to show up for training at all. None of their outfits were described; however, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> took hers off to oil up at the wrestling station.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire"><i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, the tributes wore different uniforms to the previous year's. They had on black tank tops with light gray stripes along the straps; tight black athletic shorts with a similar gray stripe down the front, gathered just below the knee; and black track jackets with stripes and patches of light gray and gold. The tank tops' gray stripes were slightly different between the male and female tributes, with the females' stripes taking up less of the straps and coming down farther on the front.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
