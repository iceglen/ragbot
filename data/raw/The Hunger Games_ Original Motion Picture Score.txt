</p><p><i><b>The Hunger Games: Original Motion Picture Score</b></i> is the official soundtrack for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>. It was produced by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-Bone_Burnett" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:T-Bone Burnett">T-Bone Burnett</a>, who was also acting as the film's executive music producer. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcade_Fire" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Arcade Fire">Arcade Fire</a> wrote the <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> national <a href="/wiki/Anthem" class="mw-redirect" title="Anthem">anthem</a> for the score called "<a href="/wiki/Horn_of_Plenty" title="Horn of Plenty">Horn of Plenty</a>".
</p>
