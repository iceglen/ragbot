</p><p><b>Lynn Cohen</b> was an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>, an eighty-year-old woman in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> who <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> for <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>'s female tribute/victor, <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLynn_Cohen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cohen started her acting career with the TV series <i>Texas</i> in 1982. She was best known for portraying Magda in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_and_the_City" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sex and the City">Sex and the City</a></i>&#160;and Gola Meir in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Speilberg" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Steven Speilberg">Steven Speilberg</a>'s <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_(film" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Munich (film">Munich</a></i>. She has portrayed Judge Elizabeth Mizener several times on <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Law &amp; Order">Law and Order</a></i>. She has also made appearances in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagle_Eye" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Eagle Eye">Eagle Eye</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invincible_(2006_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Invincible (2006 film)">Invincible</a></i>. Cohen was also active in theater. She made her debut on Broadway in 1989 in the play <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orpheus_Descending" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Orpheus Descending">Orpheus Descending</a></i>. In 1997 she also appeared in a play called <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivanov_(play)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Ivanov (play)">Ivanov</a>. She died on February 14th, 2020, at age 86.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Since her death, she has posthumously been credited for several short films.
</p>
