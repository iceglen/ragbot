</p><p><b>The Meadow&#160;</b>is a scruffy field on the outskirts of&#160;<a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, near <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>. It lies just inside the <a href="/wiki/Fence" title="Fence">fence</a>&#160;and before the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> where&#160;<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>&#160;hunts.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Meadow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Meadow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> was known to work on her music in the Meadow early in the morning so as to avoid disturbing her neighbors. The <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>'s goat, <a href="/wiki/Shamus" title="Shamus">Shamus</a>, grazed there,<sup id="cite_ref-:2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the Covey entered the woods from there as well,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> though they didn't have to contend with the fence because it hadn't been built yet.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Meadow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a> called the Meadow "the friend of the condemned" because it was a safe refuge from <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games_trilogy"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Meadow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Katniss recounted that the last time she'd sung "<a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">The Hanging Tree</a>" when she was seven, <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">her mother</a> had gotten very angry at <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">her father</a>. Frightened because Mrs. Everdeen never usually yelled, Katniss ran off to her only hiding spot: underneath a <a href="/wiki/Flowers#Honeysuckle" title="Flowers">honeysuckle</a> bush in the Meadow.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the depths of Mrs. Everdeen's depression, Katniss saw a dandelion in the schoolyard, and she was inspired to harvest dandelions with her sister <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a> in the Meadow. They collected dandelion greens, stems, and flowers and used them to make salad.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Katniss later ventured into the woods, the Meadow became her usual route because it was near <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen%27s_house" title="Katniss Everdeen&#39;s house">her house</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Reconstruction_of_District_12">Reconstruction of District 12</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Meadow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, the Meadow was dug up, and it became a mass grave for the people who died in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Eventually the Meadow regrew, and twenty years after the war, Katniss&#160;watched her and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_son" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark&#39;s son">son</a> and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_daughter" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark&#39;s daughter">daughter</a> play there together, unaware of what it used to be.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Resources">Resources</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Meadow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Dandelions,<sup id="cite_ref-:3_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Flowers#Rue" title="Flowers">rue</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:4_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and honeysuckle<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> were all edible plants that grew in the Meadow. It was also a decent place for animals to graze, as Katniss said of Prim's goat <a href="/wiki/Lady" title="Lady">Lady</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Meadow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>It's possible that the District 12 Meadow features in the song <a href="/wiki/Deep_in_the_Meadow_(Lullaby)" title="Deep in the Meadow (Lullaby)">"Deep in the Meadow"</a><i>.</i><sup id="cite_ref-:1_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Katniss said that the yellow flowers in the Meadow made her think of her fallen ally, <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, who had been named after a yellow flower herself.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss also sang "Deep in the Meadow" to her as she died.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the Covey<sup id="cite_ref-:2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> both lived in the same part of the Seam near the Meadow, and they both let their goats graze there.</li></ul>
