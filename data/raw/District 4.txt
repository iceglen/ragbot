<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationship_with_the_Games">Relationship with the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the first ten years of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, District 4 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were considered "contenders" in the competition,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> presumably due to skills they picked up in the fishing industry. They may have also had better, more stable diets than other districts because they procured their own food.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_district">Career district</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Eventually, alongside <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, District 4 began to train <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>. Although technically against the rules, their children were trained to prepare them for <a href="/wiki/Combat" class="mw-redirect" title="Combat">combat</a> inside the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They were strong competitors, but they could be overconfident or even arrogant, ending in their defeat. However, in the films, only 1 and 2 produced Careers.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Unlike most games, the tributes did not do as well as previous games. The Male was killed in the bloodbath (explicitly by Cato in the films). The female was killed by <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">Tracker jacker</a> stings alongside <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> (the bloodbath in the films).
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath_of_the_74th_Hunger_Games">Aftermath of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Despite District 4's relative wealth and success, it had a strong <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> underground. During the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour (74th HG)</a>, the people of Districts <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>, 4, and <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a> expressed "genuine elation" at the sight of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, "and under the elation, fury," crying out for vengeance.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Also, on the day before the reading of the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quell</a> card, the district had been in revolt for several weeks, resulting in seafood shortages that were explained away as a consequence of bad weather.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>At the time of the third Quarter Quell, the tributes from Districts <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>, 4, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">7</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> were all involved in a rebel plan to protect Katniss and break out of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion"><a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>All but one of its victors were killed during the war and the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>. The only survivor was <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Reconstruction">Reconstruction</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the war,  <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Asterid Everdeen</a> went to District 4 to help in setting up a hospital there.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Everyday_life">Everyday life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 220px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a4/District4444.png/revision/latest?cb=20140121192057" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 4 as seen in " src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a4/District4444.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20140121192057" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="89" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District4444.png" data-image-key="District4444.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District4444.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">District 4 as seen in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Not much is known about District 4's way of living aside from their specialization in fishing. This makes them adept at tying knots, weaving, and wielding <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> like spears and tridents. They can also make and use nets, improvise fish hooks, and identify edible marine life.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Presumably, they also practice aquaculture. They also have a district accent,<sup id="cite_ref-:5_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> though the way it sounds is never described.
</p><p>The traditional <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> from this district is a salty, fish-shaped loaf, tinted green by seaweed.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Special_occasions">Special occasions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Weddings">Weddings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The ceremony used in District 4 is similar to the one used in <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>, but it has its own unique touches. During their vows, the couple is covered with a net woven from long grass. Then they touch each other's lips with salt water, and children sing an ancient wedding song likening marriage to a sea voyage. Finally, the union is sealed with a kiss.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 4 is located on the western coast of the continent, roughly corresponding to California and Oregon, with notable territories in Nevada and southern Washington. This ties in with the district's industry of fishing, and the fact that tributes from District 4 tend to be very good swimmers. The district borders <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> (to the north), <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> (to the east), and <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> (to the southeast). During their Victory Tour, Katniss and Peeta notice the ocean when visiting one of the districts, strongly implied to be District 4. It is the richest district not to share a border with the <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Finnick Odair was the youngest known victor, winning his Games when he was just 14 years old.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Finnick was a mentor for the 74th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>After winning the 11th Hunger Games, Mags was the first victor to embark on a <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>She was also the oldest tribute in the third Quarter Quell at 80 years old. She <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> in place of Annie Cresta.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>In the novels, the three known deceased female tributes from District 4 died similarly, each poisoned by a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> creation: Coral by snake mutts, the unnamed girl in the 74th Hunger Games by tracker jackers, and Mags by the fog.</li>
<li>District 4 citizens are known to smell horrible; mainly of fish. They are also nose blind to the fact they repulse others around them.</li>
<li>According to <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a>, District 4 is located on the west coast of Panem, just to the southeast of <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>.
<ul><li>In one or two maps seen in the movies District 4 is also located on the west coast of Panem.</li></ul></li>
<li>District 4 is the only Career District to assist in the rebels' plan to protect Katniss and break out of the arena in the 75th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 4 has 12 fisheries in operation with a population of 111,453, making it the seventh largest district, in terms of population.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 4 claims 26,357 in tesserae, making it the fourth richest district.
<ul><li>If the population is 111,453 and they claim 26,357 tesserae, it would equal to 0.236 tesserae per person, making it the 5th richest District.</li>
<li>This makes District 4 the only Career District poorer than a non-Career District.</li></ul></li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be plantation teal (#415149 – <span style="background-color:#415149; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 4's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be cadet blue (#5F9EA0- <span style="background-color:#5F9EA0; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear for the 50th Hunger Games was deep sea blue (#00008F - <span style="background-color:#00008F; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
