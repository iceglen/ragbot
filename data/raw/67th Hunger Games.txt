</p><p>The <b>67th Hunger Games</b> took place seven years before the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. They were won by <a href="/wiki/Augustus_Braun" title="Augustus Braun">Augustus Braun</a>, the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>. After his victory, he was hailed as <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s "Favorite Son" and was known as the "Cavalier Career".<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F67th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>According to <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, Augustus cultivated an unkempt persona in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F67th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Evidently, he was widely popular in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>; they reportedly sang his praises and labelled him as a figure of reconciliation of the people due to his patriotic manner. On his <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(67th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="Victory Tour (67th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, Capitol Couture describes him as having a bright and optimistic ensemble, in contrast to how he appeared in his Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F67th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Augustus and his Games were not mentioned in the original trilogy, but revealed by Capitol Couture, a Hunger Games website promoting the movies.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The German translation of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i> says that <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> won seven years prior to the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, so at least in that version of the text, this would make her the victor of the 67th.</li></ul>
