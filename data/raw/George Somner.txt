</p><p><b>George Somner</b> is an English actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Spruce" title="Spruce">Spruce</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGeorge_Somner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Somner starred in the feature film <i>Kindling</i> and was seen in a guest role in BBC One's adaptation of Adam Kay's <i>This is Going to Hurt</i>. He also reprised his series regular role in Netflix's hit <i>Sex Education</i>. Somner was a leading regular in the Channel 4 comedy <i>Bad Move</i> and was featured in Alexandre Aja's feature film <i>Crawl</i>, starring Kaya Scodelario. He was also part of the UK Tour of the award-winning <i>People, Places, &amp; Things</i>. Further screen credits include a guest role in <i>Grantchester</i> and the short film <i>Eden</i>. 
</p><p>Somner is represented by A&amp;J Artists and The Artists Partnership in the UK and Luber Roklin Entertainment in the US.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
