</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEvan_Ross%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ross was born Evan Olav Næss on August 26, 1988, in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenwich,_Connecticut" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Greenwich, Connecticut">Greenwich, Connecticut</a>, to entertainer Diana Earle Ross and Norwegian businessman and mountaineer, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arne_N%C3%A6ss_Jr." class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Arne Næss Jr.">Arne Næss Jr.</a> He has three maternal half-sisters (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracee_Ellis_Ross" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tracee Ellis Ross">Tracee Ellis Ross</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhonda_Ross_Kendrick" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rhonda Ross Kendrick">Rhonda Ross Kendrick</a> and Chudney Ross), three paternal half-brothers and two paternal half-sisters (Christoffer, Nicklas, Louis, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leona_N%C3%A6ss" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Leona Næss">Leona Næss</a> and Katinka) and only one brother from both (Ross Næss). In 2004, Ross's father was killed in a climbing accident while in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:South Africa">South Africa</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Acting_career">Acting career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEvan_Ross%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Ross began his career as an actor while a student at <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenwich_High_School" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Greenwich High School">Greenwich High School</a>. His first major role was in the film, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATL" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:ATL">ATL</a>, released in March 2006, in which he co-starred with rappers <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T.I." class="extiw" title="wikipedia:T.I.">T.I.</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Boi" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Big Boi">Big Boi</a>. Ross received rave reviews for his role as a troubled teenager in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_Support" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Life Support">Life Support</a>, along with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Latifah" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Queen Latifah">Queen Latifah</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracee_Ellis_Ross" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tracee Ellis Ross">Tracee Ellis Ross</a>, who portrayed Evan's elder sister in the movie and is his real life sister. Ross later co-starred in the biography, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pride" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pride">Pride</a>, in which he portrayed a good natured teen with a speech impediment. He also appeared (uncredited on-screen) in episode 171 of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girlfriends" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Girlfriends">Girlfriends</a>. The episode, titled "What's Black-A-Lackin'?", also had <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrisette_Michele" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Chrisette Michele">Chrisette Michele</a> as a guest star, and was directed by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracee_Ellis_Ross" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tracee Ellis Ross">Tracee Ellis Ross</a>. Ross is involved in several other movies, including Linewatch with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba_Gooding,_Jr." class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cuba Gooding, Jr.">Cuba Gooding, Jr.</a> in 2008, and he finished a role in the indie film <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_Is_Hot_in_Cracktown" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Life Is Hot in Cracktown">Life Is Hot in Cracktown</a>. He starred as the love interest of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilary_Duff" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hilary Duff">Hilary Duff</a>'s character in the 2009 comedy drama <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/According_to_Greta" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:According to Greta">According to Greta</a>. He also appeared in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notorious_B.I.G." class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Notorious B.I.G.">Notorious B.I.G.</a> 2005 video <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasty_Girl" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nasty Girl">Nasty Girl</a> and the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Richie" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lionel Richie">Lionel Richie</a> video Just Go.
</p><p>In 2010, Ross joined the cast of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CW" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:CW">CW</a> series <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/90210" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:90210">90210</a> in its third season, where he plays Liam Court's (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Lanter" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Matt Lanter">Matt Lanter</a>) half brother and love interest to Annie Wilson played by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenae_Grimes" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Shenae Grimes">Shenae Grimes</a>. His next high-profile 2011 film is the critically acclaimed film festival winner, "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mooz-lum" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Mooz-lum">Mooz-lum</a>" that also stars <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Glover" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Danny Glover">Danny Glover</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nia_Long" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nia Long">Nia Long</a>. According to IMDB, he has 21 film projects in various stages of production. He will be starring alongside <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brittany_Snow" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Brittany Snow">Brittany Snow</a> in the thriller <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/96_Minutes" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:96 Minutes">96 Minutes</a>. Which was released April 28, 2012 in select cities. Evan won the "Breakout Acting" award at SXSW.
</p><p>Ross has been cast to portray music producer <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Austin" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dallas Austin">Dallas Austin</a> in the upcoming VH1 biopic, "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TLC" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:TLC">TLC</a>", about the 1990s girl group.
In the upcoming The Hunger Games: Mockingjay (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1"> Part 1</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2"> Part 2</a>), scheduled for release in 2014 and 2015, respectively, Ross was cast as Messalla.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Music_career">Music career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEvan_Ross%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In 2007, Ross began recording his debut album, which will encompass R&amp;B and pop. After four years in the studio, Evan released his single "Yes Me" on February 25, 2011, which was produced by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_DeNiro" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tony DeNiro">Tony DeNiro</a> &amp; written by them together.
</p>
