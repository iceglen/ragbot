</p><p><b>Patrick St. Esprit</b> portrayed <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. Esprit has appeared in films such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_8_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Super 8 (film)">Super 8</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Zone" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Green Zone">Green Zone</a></i>. Esprit currently portrays LAPD Commander Robert Hicks in CBS action series <i>S.W.A.T.</i>
