</p><p><b>Luna Steeples</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Dill" title="Dill">Dill</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p>Steeples was born in downtown Los Angeles to parents who are also actors and filmmakers. She fell in love with the film industry when she was four years old.<sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

She is represented by Jackson Entertainment Management (JEM) and Skrzyniarz &amp; Mallean.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
