<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Plinth%2527s_sister%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>This woman was born and raised in District 2 alongside her sister, who later married <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a> and had a son, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus</a>. Strabo was able to profit off the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> and ingratiate himself and his family with <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, eventually moving there at the end of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>She was the only person from District 2 to keep in touch with Mrs. Plinth after the move; she even sent her a needlepoint of a mountain scene overlaid with the word "home".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
