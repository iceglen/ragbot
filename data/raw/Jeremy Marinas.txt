<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJeremy_Marinas%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Marinas is primarily a stunt actor and performer. Along with fellow tribute actor <a href="/wiki/Tara_Macken" title="Tara Macken">Tara Macken</a>, he was on the web series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Legion_of_Extraordinary_Dancers" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Legion of Extraordinary Dancers">The Legion of Extraordinary Dancers</a></i>. Marinas is a very successful stunt double, having appeared in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_America:_Civil_War" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Captain America: Civil War">Captain America: Civil War</a>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Green_Hornet" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Green Hornet">The Green Hornet</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/After_Earth" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:After Earth">After Earth</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tomb" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Tomb">The Tomb</a></i>, and several other films. He is of Filipino descent and lives in California.
</p>
