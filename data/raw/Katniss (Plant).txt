<p><b>Katniss</b> is an aquatic plant that grows around <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, particularly in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> and by the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a>. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was named after it.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKatniss_%28plant%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Some people call them swamp potatoes, but I like katniss better. Has a nice ring to it.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> to <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The plant grows tall, with green leaves shaped like <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">arrowheads</a> and flowers with three white petals. It also has small, edible tubers.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKatniss_%28plant%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/Katniss_plant_ballad.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240403225134" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Clerk Carmine bringing katniss to Lucy Gray." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/Katniss_plant_ballad.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240403225134" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss plant ballad.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_plant_ballad.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_plant_ballad.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Clerk Carmine bringing katniss to Lucy Gray.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/Clerk_Carmine" class="mw-redirect" title="Clerk Carmine">Clerk Carmine</a> brought <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> some katniss, but she examined it and deemed it to be too early in the season to eat it. She told <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> that in a few weeks, it would produce decent-sized potatoes, which could be roasted and eaten.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Two weeks later, she slipped away from Snow when the two were supposedly escaping together, claiming that she was going to go dig up some katniss, which would be ready by that time.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the deaths of <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a>, <a href="/wiki/Buck" title="Buck">Buck</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Chicory" title="Chicory">Chicory</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> sat down in a clump of katniss to have a conversation about defying <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss Everdeen was named after this plant by <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">her father</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Katniss first began to hunt and gather in the woods with her father, they would go to the lake to shoot waterfowl and collect katniss roots. Katniss was one of the first foods she found while hunting.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss found this plant again while wading in a stream after her father died, and remembered her father saying to her, "As long as you can find yourself, you'll never starve." She picked this plant and fed her family off of it until she could find something better to sustain them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKatniss_%28plant%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The plant is known by many names, including <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/sagittaria" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:sagittaria">sagittaria</a>, arrowhead, duck potato, swamp potato, swan potato, tule potato, and wapato.
</p>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: https&#58;//www.nytimes.com/2018/10/18/books/suzanne-collins-talks-about-the-hunger-games-the-books-and-the-movies.html">"<i>[Katniss Everdeen's] name came later, while I was researching survival training and specifically edible plants. In one of my books, I found the arrowhead plant, and the more I read about it, the more it seemed to reflect her. Its Latin name has the same roots as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagittarius_(constellation)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sagittarius (constellation)">Sagittarius</a>, the archer. The edible tuber roots she could gather, the arrowhead-shaped leaves were her defense, and the little white blossoms kept it in the tradition of flower names, like <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> and <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose</a>. I looked at the list of alternative names for it. Swamp Potato. Duck Potato. Katniss easily won the day.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> on naming Katniss Everdeen.<sup class="noprint">[<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://www.nytimes.com/2018/10/18/books/suzanne-collins-talks-about-the-hunger-games-the-books-and-the-movies.html%7C">[src</a>]]</sup></dd></dl></div>
