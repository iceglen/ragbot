<dd>&#8212;Description<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Tribute_Guide" title="The Hunger Games: Tribute Guide">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>District 12</b> was the smallest and poorest of the thirteen districts of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. Located in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Appalachia">Appalachia</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> it had a population of around 8,000 people prior to being firebombed by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District 12's main industry is <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal mining</a>, but after the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, their main industry was converted to medicine. District 12 is the home of <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationship_with_the_Hunger_Games">Relationship with the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Since District 12 was the poorest of Panem's districts, its <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were almost guaranteed a quick death. Prior to the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the district had produced only two victors.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  Most of its tributes were malnourished, impoverished, and unaccustomed to the glamour of the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Most District 12 tributes were inexperienced with weapons compared to other districts, holding them at a major disadvantage since their residents were only allowed to become miners at 18.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">Sponsors</a> typically refused to support District 12 tributes given how rarely they had stood out or succeeded in the past.  
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div>
<p>Lucy Gray Baird became the district's first victor.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Around the time of the reaping, <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a> set off <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_the_District_12_mines" title="Bombings">an explosion</a> in the coal mines in an attempt to sabotage production and possibly get the rebellion going again. His attempt was not successful and he ended up killing three men: two District 12 bosses and a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a>. He was executed a month later at the <a href="/wiki/Hanging_tree" title="Hanging tree">hanging tree</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> inspiring Lucy Gray to write <a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">"The Hanging Tree" song</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Haymitch Abernathy became the district's second victor in the second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, which had double the number of tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was the first of 12's victors to move into the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>, and he would be the first and only one to <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> future tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div>
<p>Most notably, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> introduced a rule change that would allow for <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partners</a> to win together if they were the last two people remaining.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, when they attempted to retract the rule at the last second, Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark threatened double suicide with deadly <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries. Faced with the possibility of having no victor at all, the Capitol allowed them both to live. This made Katniss and Peeta the first pair of victors in the history of the Games, and the third and fourth victors from District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the Games, <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a> started up a collection to sponsor Katniss and Peeta, and once they survived into the final eight, reporters came looking for personal stories and friends of theirs. Katniss having a close male friend could have undermined her onscreen romance with Peeta, so "some genius" devised the false story that <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> was her cousin.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The stunt with the berries inflamed rebellious sentiment across the country,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and uprisings broke out in other districts in the wake of Katniss and Peeta's <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Soon after Katniss and Peeta's return to 12, the Peacekeeper presence was increased— most notably with its brutal new Head Peacekeeper, <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>. Within a few days of his arrival, machine gun nests were set up around the square, as well as a whipping post, several stockades, and a gallows. Peacekeepers also burned down the <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">Hob</a>. The mines were closed down for two weeks, and the district experienced food shortages: crucial <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tessera</a> grain deliveries never came, and the monthly food parcels the district was owed for its victory arrived spoiled and "defiled" by rodents. Offenses that would have been ignored before were now punished viciously.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Following the events of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">third Quarter Quell</a>, the Capitol <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_12_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">bombed District 12,</a><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> only sparing the Victors' Village. Led by Gale, escaping citizens were able to tear down the fence between the district and the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> and evacuate about 800 people to the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a>. The survivors were rescued three days later by <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> hovercraft; however, the majority of the district's people were killed in the firebombing.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After its destruction, the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> utilized District 12 as a transportation base to send troops to the Capitol. Katniss and the rest of <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> were taken there before being transported to the Capitol by cargo train.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Reconstruction">Reconstruction</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Panem's second war, Katniss and Haymitch returned to District 12, and Peeta eventually returned as well. A mass grave was dug for victims of the bombing, and a medicine factory was constructed as District 12 began to rebuild.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a>, District 12 is located in the northeast of North America. Its territory corresponds primarily to the U.S. states of Kentucky, Ohio, West Virginia, and Virginia. Historically, this region is known for its strong coal mining industry and its extensive coal beds, particularly in West Virginia and Kentucky, which ties well with the district's industry. However, given District 12's small population and the fact the entirety of its citizenry reside within a single settlement, the district likely remains largely uninhabited, with the probable exception of several coal mines scattered throughout its territory.
</p><p>District 12 bordered four districts: District 13 (to the northeast), <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> (to the south and southwest), <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> (to the south), <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> (to the northwest).
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Areas_and_places">Areas and places</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Seam">The Seam</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>District Twelve. Where you can starve to death in safety.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss Everdeen<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The Seam was the poorest part of District 12 where the <a href="/wiki/Everdeen" title="Everdeen">Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Hawthorne</a> families lived. It was located on the border adjacent to the unmanaged <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woodland</a> around District 12. Food was extremely difficult to acquire and almost all families lived in abject poverty. Many who lived there lost families due to mining accidents or from starvation. Katniss mentioned that they only had electricity a few hours a day, so they mostly used candles for light. As a result, any adult who reached elderhood was venerated. In contrast, those who were well fed and young were scorned. 
</p><p>Early in the mornings, the streets of the Seam were crowded with exhausted coal miners heading to work, who faced the lethal risks of collapsing caverns and the odorless but noxious fumes of carbon monoxide on a daily basis. One safeguard in the mines was a <a href="/wiki/Canary" title="Canary">canary</a> who would sing - when the canary went silent, it indicated the mine air was no longer safe to breathe. With the primary outputs of coal in the region, everything in the Seam seemed to be coated in a layer of coal dust. 
</p><p>Residents of the Seam typically had straight black hair, olive skin, and gray eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span id="Town_square.2Fmerchant_section"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Town_square/merchant_section">Town square/merchant section</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">Merchant</a></i></center></div>
<p>The square was where District 12's merchant class lived, above their places of work. One notable shop was the <a href="/wiki/Bakery" title="Bakery">bakery</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Merchants' appearances distinguished them from residents of the Seam, as they typically had blonde hair and blue eyes. The mayor and his family were seemingly counted among their ranks, and <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Undersee%27s_house" title="Mayor Undersee&#39;s house">their own house</a> was nearby. All the district's citizens would be forced to gather in the square to watch that year's annual <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaping</a>, which took place on the stage out front of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> There was an apothecary and a sweetshop owned by the Donners.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Justice_Building">Justice Building</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<p>The Justice Building was used for formal administrative tasks, including marriage registrations. Tributes had an hour to say goodbye to their loved ones inside,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the eldest children of miners killed in accidents received medals of honor here, including Katniss and Gale.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hob">The Hob</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">The Hob</a></i></center></div>
<p>The Hob was District 12's black market, notably where Katniss and Gale sold or traded their excess game. Many things were available for purchase or trade at the Hob, including <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a>'s soup, goats, or white liquor made by <a href="/wiki/Ripper" title="Ripper">Ripper</a>. It was burned down by Peacekeepers in <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span id="Victors.27_Village"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Victors'_Village">Victors' Village</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a></i></center></div>
<p>The Victors' Village is a small area where the <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> of previous <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> dwell. The houses are grand and luxurious compared to the rest of District 12; however, the village was wholly neglected for years due to a lack of victors in District 12, housing only three victors in the span of 75 Hunger Games: <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and her family, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>. The only other victor from District 12, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, had won <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">her Games</a> and disappeared before the village was constructed.
</p>

<p>The sole settlement of District 12 was surrounded by a twenty-foot high electric fence, though it was rarely on due to power shortages. While officials informed the public that the fence was meant to ward away wild animals, it was more likely to prevent people from escaping out into the wilderness.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  This seems to be supported by the fact that the fence did not exist at the time of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a>.</i> When Romulus Thread became Head Peacekeeper of District 12, the fence's electricity was reactivated.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was later torn down by escaping refugees.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Meadow">Meadow</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">The Meadow</a></i></center></div>
<p>This scruffy field just inside the fence sits near <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen%27s_house" title="Katniss Everdeen&#39;s house">Katniss Everdeen's house</a> in the Seam. A number of plants and flowers grow there, both edible and medicinal. This was Katniss's main entry point into the woods.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the war, it became a mass grave for those who died in the bombing,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but the plants there regrew.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Three-finger_salute">Three-finger salute</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a> is a symbol made with the three middle fingers of your left hand, bringing them to your lips, and then holding them out to another person. In District 12, it's an expression of admiration, gratitude, and a way to say goodbye to someone you love.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The sign is later picked up by rebels in other districts.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Music"><a href="/wiki/Songs" title="Songs">Music</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Due to poverty, most people in Diatrict 12 don't own books, so they pass on their stories through songs. By Katniss' time, songs are largely discouraged.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Special_occasions">Special occasions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Weddings">Weddings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>Maybe it's old-fashioned, but no one really feels married in District 12 until after the <a href="/wiki/Toasting" title="Toasting">toasting</a>.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss Everdeen<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Weddings in District 12 were simple events conducted with humble materials. Traditionally, women would rent a well-worn white dress, while men donned clothing outside of their normal mining clothes. The couple would register as spouses at the Justice Building to be assigned a home. For a ceremony, there would be a small gathering of close friends and family members enjoying a meal and if available, a piece of cake. The reception would then sing a traditional song to welcome the couple into their new home as they crossed the threshold. Afterwards, the newlyweds would make their first fire and do the traditional <a href="/wiki/Toasting" title="Toasting">toasting</a> of bread to share together.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Harvest_Festival">Harvest Festival</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The Harvest Festival is always celebrated on the last day of the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, typically observed with a meal at home or with a few friends if one can afford it.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As decoration, people affix bunches of brightly colored corn to their front doors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> For the conclusion of the tour, Harvest Festival celebrations were a public affair put on by the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span id="New_Year.27s_Day"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="New_Year's_Day">New Year's Day</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>A bell was always rung in the Justice Building on New Year's Day.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was also seen as an occasion special enough to warrant a cake from the bakery, if one could afford it.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Covey">The Covey</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a></i></center></div>
<p>At the time of the prequel, a nomadic minority group known as the Covey had been forced to settle in District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They had a number of their own unique cultural practices, such as their names, colorful clothes, and musical performances. Some of their songs, like <a href="/wiki/Deep_in_the_Meadow_(Lullaby)" title="Deep in the Meadow (Lullaby)">"Deep in the Meadow"</a> and "The Hanging Tree", still survived at the time of the trilogy through Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District 12's first victor, Lucy Gray Baird, was Covey.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Escorts">Escorts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>From the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a> to the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, District 12 had a female Capitol escort named <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a>, a mean-spirited and snobby woman who had no consideration for the lives of people in the districts, always in pursuit of being promoted to a better district.
</p><p>Drusilla was succeeded as escort by <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>, a bubbly, pink-haired, slightly air-headed woman<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> who starts working as District 12's escort during the 51st Hunger Games up to the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. Like Drusilla, Effie is initially reluctant and slightly unwelcoming to the tributes, however, she later grows emotionally attached to the people of the District 12 and the rebellion, fully realizing the mistakes of the Capitol.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="General_trivia">General trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>District 12's industry of mining and the poorest living conditions reflect on how mining is used as a punishment reserved for the lower class in ancient Roman society.
<ul><li>In Ancient Rome, being sent to the mines is often referred to as <i>damnatio ad metallum</i>.</li></ul></li>
<li>With District 5's industry being revealed as electricity (with an emphasis on renewable electricity), it is unlikely that coal is used for electricity in Panem.
<ul><li>Notably, quantities of coal are exported to District 1 to be converted into diamonds. Coal is also used in many chemical and industrial applications such as metallurgy and making plastics.</li></ul></li>
<li>The Harvest Festival bears several similarities to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanksgiving" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Thanksgiving">Thanksgiving</a>, such as a shifting date of celebration, its observation through a family meal, and harvest-themed decorations. As a result, it's possible it may take place in November like American Thanksgiving.</li>
<li><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_River_Mill_Village" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Henry River Mill Village">Henry River Mill Village</a> in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hickory,_North_Carolina" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hickory, North Carolina">Hickory, North Carolina</a> was used as a filming location for a number of District 12 scenes in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">the first film</a>, including the Everdeen house and the Mellarks' bakery.<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The village's onscreen fame later led to its purchase and use in tours, both for fans of the film and those interested in the place's history.</li>

<li>According to Capitol.PN, District 12 had 15 mines in operation with a population of 7,935, making it the smallest of the twelve operational districts in population.</li>

<ul><li>If the population was 7,935 and they claimed 862,568 tesserae, that would equal to 108.704 tesserae per person. The unbalanced ratio suggests that this number represents tesserae claims throughout the 75 years of the games and not an annual number, or that all the children in the district claim them, essentially nullifying the social control aspect and hazard of signing up for them.</li>
<li>The second poorest district in terms of tesserae, District 10, has an average of just over 15 tesserae per person. If this is true, then District 12 is suggested to be far more poor than all the other districts.</li></ul></li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 50th and 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be black (#000000 – <span style="background-color:#000000; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
