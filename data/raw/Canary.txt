Like <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>, they are songbirds.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCanary%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miners</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> brought canaries down into the mines to warn them of bad air. The canary would stop singing, alerting them of danger so they could get out. However, if the air was too bad, the bird would die, and the miners would, too.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>District 12 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> once had a pet canary. After she died in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, her parents gave the songbird to Maysilee's friend, <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is unknown if this pet canary was <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou_(canary)" title="Lou Lou (canary)">Lou Lou</a>, or if Maysilee had multiple canaries.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCanary%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>During the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Beetee compared his <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a> <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> to a canary for her intuition. Soon after, Wiress stopped singing right before <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> attacked the rebel <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>, just like a canary in a mine would.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Canaries were also <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentinel_species" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sentinel species">used to detect bad air in real mines</a>. They were sometimes replaced by other birds like pigeons, sparrows, etc.</li>
<li>On the tribute <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> before the 50th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> insulted <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a>'s outfit by saying she looked "like someone mated a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> and a canary."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
