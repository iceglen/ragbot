He did not live to see the birth of his daughter, <a href="/wiki/Posy_Hawthorne" title="Posy Hawthorne">Posy</a>. His wife gave birth to her just after he died. He worked as a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
