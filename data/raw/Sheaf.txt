<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sheaf grew up in District 9 and became her district's female tribute in <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with the other tributes, Sheaf was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While in the monkey enclosure, it was mentioned that Sheaf was "a limber little girl" and performed a back handspring to receive food from the <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> citizens that were watching.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the start of the Games, the tributes and their mentors were taken on a tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, heavily injuring the District 9 duo and their mentors. Sheaf was denied hospital care, in spite of repeated requests made by the veterinarian. Unlike <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> and <a href="/wiki/Ginnee" title="Ginnee">Ginnee</a>, the two tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a>, Sheaf managed to survive a little longer, but eventually succumbed to her wounds. 
</p><p>Her body was later draped on the back of a horse and paraded down <a href="/wiki/Scholars_Road" title="Scholars Road">Scholars Road</a> during <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana Ring</a>'s funeral.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Sheaf survived the bombing and was instead killed in the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a> by <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a>. When the gong rang, she initially tried to go to the pile of weapons, but decided to flee in another direction, being chased by Otto. She tried to find a way out, but Otto grabbed a crossbow and shot her in the leg, then the back. Sheaf placed 18th.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A sheaf is a bundle of grains tied together after they are harvested.
</p>
