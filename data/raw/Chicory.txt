<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChicory%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChicory%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the start of the games, Chicory joined an alliance between District <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">7</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a> tributes in an attempt to counter the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> pack. Chicory and her district partners were considered "<i>a game changer</i>" by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the way to the arena, the District 11 tributes shared a hovercraft with the tributes of District 12. One of the female District 12 tributes, <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a> fixated on Chicory's token - a flower made of grass - and sung the District 11 harvest song. Chicory was surprised that someone not from her district knew the song and sung a different one to find out if Lou Lou knew that one. When she joined in with Chicory, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> admitted that the original "Lou Lou" (<a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>) was dead and her doppelganger was also from District 11. Chicory did not know who Lou Lou could be, and when she asked, Lou Lou grabbed her ear and started shrieking.<sup id="cite_ref-SR15_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the Games begin, Chicory survived the initial bloodbath and joined the rest of the Newcomers on the mountain. After a volcanic explosion, Chicory, Hull and <a href="/wiki/Buck" title="Buck">Buck</a> escaped together.<sup id="cite_ref-SR21_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChicory%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>On the fourth day, Chicory, Hull and Buck encountered a porcupine <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a>, which attacked them by shooting its long, metallic quills at them. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and Maysilee discovered Chicory and Buck writhing in pain and pawing at the quills embedded in their bodies. Hull was well enough to run away but refused to leave his allies. By the time Haymitch and Maysilee chased the porcupine away, Chicory was dead.<sup id="cite_ref-SR21_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, Haymitch faced Chicory's distraught, grieving family.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChicory%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Chicory was shown to be an intelligent and kind girl, as she instantly recognized that Lou Lou was not from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> when she started singing a District 11 harvest song. Upon learning of Lou Lou's condition, Chicory showed empathy towards the girl and offered to find her family if she won the Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-SR15_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
