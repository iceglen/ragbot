</p><p><b>Lou Lou</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Canary" title="Canary">canary</a> owned by <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> prior to the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. The name of this canary was the inspiration for the nickname of <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>, <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>'s body double.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_10_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Lou Lou may have been given to <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid March</a> after Maysilee's death.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLou_Lou_%28canary%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sometime prior to the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 50th Hunger Games, Maysilee Donner came into possession of Lou Lou. When <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> heard about Maysilee's new pet, she was enraged that Maysilee had encaged a bird, let alone a songbird. Lenore Dove complained about Lou Lou to <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the Second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> brainwashed the daughter of <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> rebels into thinking she was the recently deceased Louella McCoy. The other <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> did not want to refer to this body double as Louella, so they tried to think of a name that was close enough to not confuse her, but also not Louella. Maysilee suggested Lou Lou after her pet canary.  Although Lenore Dove had opposed Maysilee owning Lou Lou, Haymitch did not think this was an ample reason to reject the nickname for "Fake Louella," so the name was adopted.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_10_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLou_Lou_%28canary%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, Asterid mentions that after Maysilee died in the Games, Mr. and Mrs. Donner gave her Maysilee's old pet canary.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is not entirely sure if this canary was Lou Lou. In <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>, Maysilee words it that she "used to have a pet canary by that name" (the name of Lou Lou).<sup id="cite_ref-SR_10_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This could be understood as meaning that either she had Lou Lou before, but that she died sometime prior to the Reaping, or that she had Lou Lou up to the reaping, but no longer considered Lou Lou hers because she would never see her again. The latter is more likely, because neither <i>Sunrise on the Reaping</i> nor <i>Catching Fire</i> ever indicate that Maysilee had more than one canary.</li></ul>
