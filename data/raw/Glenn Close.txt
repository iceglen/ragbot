</p><p><b>Glenn Close</b> is the American actress set to play <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> in the upcoming movie <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>. Close has received eight Oscar nominations and is a three-time winner of the Emmys, Tonys, and Golden Globe Awards.<sup id="cite_ref-cast_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cast-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Producer <a href="/wiki/Nina_Jacobson" title="Nina Jacobson">Nina Jacobson</a> stated that "Glenn Close is a dream Drusilla. She brings so much of her intellect and imagination to each role, creating characters who are unforgettable and iconic."<sup id="cite_ref-cast_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cast-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
