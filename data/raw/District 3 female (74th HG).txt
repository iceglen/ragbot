<p>In the movie, she was first seen in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>, waving to the crowds along with her <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (74th HG)">district partner</a>. They wore silver outfits that represented the intricate inner working of electronic devices, the industry of their <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">film</a>, she was listening to <a href="/wiki/Atala" title="Atala">Atala</a>, who was giving the tributes training advice.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In a deleted scene of the movie, she was running on the <a href="/wiki/Training_stations#The_Gauntlets" title="Training stations">gauntlets</a> during training, and was knocked off. As a result, she split her lip. <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>, <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> laughed at her, and Glimmer elbowed her district partner, who laughed as well.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the film, she was the first tribute omitted from the interview queue. She would have lined up between Cato and her district partner.
</p>

<p>The District 3 female was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was stationed to the right of the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a>. In the film, she ran for supplies at the Cornucopia. She grabs a <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpack</a>, but the <a href="/wiki/District_4_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 female (74th HG)">District 4 female</a> confronts her and amputates her leg with a sword, injuring her. The <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a> spots her on the ground and slashes her throat with a sickle, effectively killing her. Her face was the first seen by <a href="/wiki/Katniss" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Katniss">Katniss</a> on the first night of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the film, her odds of winning were 30–1, and she was the tallest female tribute at 5'9". She has the second lowest known odds of winning in the morning line odds, after <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>. <sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>She was the first tribute to be played by a stunt actor. Stunt actors are usually cast specifically for tributes who fell during the initial bloodbath.</li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, she wore a mustard yellow jacket.</li>
<li>The District 3 female appeared in the special features scenes of the DVD.</li>
<li>She was the 3rd dead, but, when the <a href="/wiki/District_8_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 female (74th HG)">District 8 female</a> was killed, on the scoreboard behind <a href="/wiki/Caesar" class="mw-redirect" title="Caesar">Caesar</a> it can be seen that she was the 11th dead.</li>
<li>Her death is confirmed when it is shown in the special features of the DVD.</li>
<li>She had her own page (112) in <i>The World of the Hunger Games </i>book, in which she was featured in the Training section. </li>
<li>By closely observing the trailer of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> it can be noted that while visiting District 3 on Katniss' and Peeta's <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> that three young men and an older man are standing on the pedestal, preserved for the family members of a death tribute. The man could possibly be her father, meaning the District 3 female had a father, three siblings and no mother.</li>
<li>She was one of the tributes who had both their portrait and corpse shown.</li></ul>
