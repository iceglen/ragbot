</p><p><b>Michael Lesslie</b> is a British writer and producer who wrote the screenplay for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> with <a href="/wiki/Michael_Arndt" title="Michael Arndt">Michael Arndt</a>. He is also known for writing a TV adaptation of <i>The Little Drummer Girl</i>, as well as the 2016 film <i>Assassin's Creed</i>.
</p>
