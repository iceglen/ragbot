</p><p><b>Dakota Shapiro</b> is an Australian actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDakota_Shapiro%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Shapiro was one of the top ten finalists for the 2017 Heath Ledger Scholarship. His list of credits include a series regular on Matthew Carnahan's limited series <i>Valley of the Boom</i> (Nat Geo), where he played real life tech pioneer Stephan Paternot, and a series regular for the pilot for <i>The Lost Boys</i> (The CW), where he played the role of David. Shapiro is represented by Clear Talent Group and Henriksen Talent Management.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
