<p><b>Tessera</b> (plural: <b>tesserae</b>) was a form of voluntary food rationing, offered by the government of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> to people in the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>. If a family was struggling for food, people between the ages of 12 and 18 - those eligible to participate in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">the Hunger Games</a> - could sign up for tesserae. A single tessera was worth one year's supply of grain and oil for a single person, collected on a monthly basis. The person could also claim further tesserae for as many members of their family as needed. Depending on the family and their particular circumstances, tesserae may or may not have been enough to live on, and alternate sources of food - sometimes acquired illegally - may also have needed to be pursued. 
</p><p>While tesserae was vital to many families in the districts, it came at a heavy price: In exchange for this extra food, the person's name would be added to the <a href="/wiki/Reaping_balls" title="Reaping balls">reaping balls</a> an additional time for every tessera claimed that year. These entries were also cumulative, so if tesserae had to be claimed year after year, the chances of being reaped could increase dramatically, depending on the number of people that had to be fed. Tessera grain was apparently unrefined, as it is described as dark and dense, likely prioritizing nutrition over taste.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="In_the_districts">In the districts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTessera%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The practice of exchanging reaping entries for tesserae was mainly claimed in the poorer districts, such as districts <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>, while far less common in richer districts such as districts <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>. The largest gap was between districts 1 and 12, with a ratio of more than 1:500. District 12 was considered the poorest district since it had the most amount of people taking tesserae, while District 1 was considered the richest district since it had the fewest amount of people taking it. 
</p><p>It is a possibility that people in <a href="/wiki/Career" class="mw-redirect" title="Career">Career</a> districts took tesserae in order to get their name added into the reaping ball more times; however, this is unlikely because most <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> from the Career districts were <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteers</a>. If the tesserae taking was not public, then a Career who wished to avoid the arena could secretly take the maximum amount each year, both for extra food and to claim to be unable to volunteer after being reaped through greater chance, ineligible to join the usual glut of career-volunteers.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Characters_taking_tesserae">Characters taking tesserae</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTessera%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bb/Hieelf.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120307064525" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="&#39;s tessera advertisement." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bb/Hieelf.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120307064525" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="250" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Hieelf.jpg" data-image-key="Hieelf.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Hieelf.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption"><a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a>'s tessera advertisement.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> of District 12 took tesserae for each of the 3 members of her family (herself, <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">her mother</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>, her sister) every year since she turned 12. This gave her three extra entries in the reaping per year, with a total of 20 by the time of the reaping for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, at age 16. At the same reaping, Katniss' friend, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, age 18, had a total of 42 tesserae entries: one for each of the 5 members of his family (himself, <a href="/wiki/Rory_Hawthorne" title="Rory Hawthorne">Rory</a>, <a href="/wiki/Posy_Hawthorne" title="Posy Hawthorne">Posy</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Vick_Hawthorne" title="Vick Hawthorne">Vick</a>, his siblings; and <a href="/wiki/Hazelle_Hawthorne" title="Hazelle Hawthorne">Hazelle Hawthorne</a>, his mother).<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, things in District 12 became so dire that Rory was forced to take tesserae, to Hazelle and Gale's discomfort.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Similar to Katniss, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> also took tesserae for three family members and had a total of 20 entries by the time he turned sixteen.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8a/Tesseraead.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130403195115" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Another Capitol advertisement" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="180" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Tesseraead.jpg" data-image-key="Tesseraead.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8a/Tesseraead.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130403195115" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8a/Tesseraead.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130403195115" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Another Capitol advertisement" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8a/Tesseraead.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130403195115" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Tesseraead.jpg" data-image-key="Tesseraead.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8a/Tesseraead.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130403195115" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Tesseraead.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Another Capitol advertisement</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Although they also lived in District 12, <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> never needed to take out any tesserae because Madge was the <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Undersee" title="Mayor Undersee">mayor</a>'s daughter and <a href="/wiki/Mr._Mellark" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Mellark">Peeta's father</a> owned a bakery.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, because of food shortages, people in District 12 who took out tesserae never received the grain they were due, owing to the disruptions of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>By the end of <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> the system was abolished, along with the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, after rebel victory against the Capitol government in the Second Rebellion.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTessera%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Tessera takes its name from a kind of tiny, cube-shaped tile; colored gold and ebony, they were apparently used as decorative elements in the homes of wealthy Capitol families before the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. On <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> day of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, in which he served as a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> wore a shirt whose buttons had been fashioned from recycled tessera tiles.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Considering that tesserae is described to consist of grain and oil, it's likely that most of it was produced with grain from <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a>, animal oils from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>, and plant-based oils from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>.</li></ul>
