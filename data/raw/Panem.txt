<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File"><audio src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5e/Horn_of_Plenty.mp3/revision/latest?cb=20241004082946" width="180" class="mw-file-element" style="max-width: 100%; width: 180px;" controls=""><a href="https://thehungergames.fandom.com/wiki/File:Horn_of_Plenty.mp3">https://thehungergames.fandom.com/wiki/File:Horn_of_Plenty.mp3</a></audio><figcaption><i>Horn of Plenty</i>, the anthem of <b>Panem</b></figcaption></figure>
<p><b>Panem</b> is a sovereign post-nuclear state and current democratic constitutional republic established sometime after a series of ecological disasters and a global conflict brought about the collapse of modern civilization. It is situated in North America<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, consisting of a federal district, <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, and thirteen outlying districts.
</p><p>For years, Panem's central government operated as a totalitarian dictatorship and police state with society, particularly in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, modeled on ancient Rome. The outlying districts were subservient to the Capitol, expected to provide services in exchange for protection given by the Capitol's armies of <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a>, largely to the detriment of the districts' populace. This unequal relationship was brutally enforced for several years, culminating in a civil war known as the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, led by <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, Panem's then-center of its military-industrial complex.
</p><p>After three years of conflict, the rebellion collapsed with the apparent destruction of District 13, bringing an end to the war. However, unbeknownst to the wider populace of Panem, District 13 had survived, having organized a separate peace with the Capitol after securing a significant portion of the Capitol's stockpile of <a href="/wiki/Nuclear_weaponry" title="Nuclear weaponry">nuclear weaponry</a>. In accordance with the ceasefire, District 13 disengaged from the insurrection and appeared destroyed in exchange for its independence and a non-aggression pact. The remaining districts were quickly subdued with the resulting <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a> enforcing a harsh peace on them, as well as instituting the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, an annual event established as a continual reminder of the failed rebellion. Public executions and whippings, restrictions on civil rights, and mass surveillance became widespread throughout Panem in the following years. Travel and communication between districts were banned.
</p><p>For the next seventy-four years, the districts endured oppression at the hands of the Capitol through the Hunger Games. Despite the Games' intended goal of snuffing out future insurrections, they further incited rebellious activity that gradually built up over the decades. By the time of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>'s rule, civil unrest was mounting in a number of districts which, following the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, further escalated despite several brutal crackdowns. This culminated in the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a> following the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, with the districts once again being led by District 13 in a revolution to overthrow Panem's totalitarian government. Within a year, the Snow regime was deposed and Panem was transformed into a democratic constitutional republic, led by Rebellion commander, <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Paylor</a>.
</p><p>The name "Panem" derives from the Latin phrase <i>Panem et circenses</i>, which literally translates into "bread and circuses". The phrase itself is used to describe entertainment used to distract public attention from more important matters. Furthermore, by the government providing ample food, entertainment and protection, the citizens would give up their political rights.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>According to the Capitol, Panem has a population of 4,556,778 people.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 302px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ea/HungerGamesPanem.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20250512073712" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="HungerGamesPanem" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="302" height="225" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="HungerGamesPanem.jpg" data-image-key="HungerGamesPanem.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ea/HungerGamesPanem.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/302?cb=20250512073712" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ea/HungerGamesPanem.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20250512073712" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="HungerGamesPanem" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ea/HungerGamesPanem.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/302?cb=20250512073712" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="302" height="225" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="HungerGamesPanem.jpg" data-image-key="HungerGamesPanem.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ea/HungerGamesPanem.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/302?cb=20250512073712" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:HungerGamesPanem.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Panem is located on the continent of North America, with its territory encompassing areas formerly controlled by the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The administrative center of the country, the Capitol, is located in the Rocky Mountains, roughly corresponding to being within the area of the former U.S. states of Wyoming and Utah.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Each one of its thirteen outlying districts are situated across the continent and given their large size, cover significant portions of former U.S. states, Mexican states and Canadian provinces.
</p><p>The size of the country is known not to be as large as North America today, given that, according to Katniss, large areas of land across the world were flooded by rising sea level (significant portions of the Atlantic, Pacific, and Gulf coastlines of North America are shown to be inundated, including the entirety of Florida). Nevertheless, maps of Panem indicate it is about as large as the former contiguous United States, spanning from the east coast to the west coast of North America and stretching from southern Canada in the north to central Mexico in the south. Given the territory it covers, Panem's geography largely resembles that of the former United States.
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, District 12 is the easternmost district, followed by Districts 8, 6, and 11.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Government">Government</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>For presumably the majority of its history since its founding, Panem was run as a totalitarian dictatorship led by a <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">president</a> who served for life and wielded absolute power over the government and military. Four presidents of Panem are present in the book series: <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">Ravinstill</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, and <a href="/wiki/Paylor" class="mw-redirect" title="Paylor">Paylor</a><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Previous presidents are unknown; Coriolanus Snow was mentioned to have been the president during the 50th Hunger Games, meaning he held power for at least 25 years. Ravinstill had been in power before the Dark Days and up to and including at least the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. The exact details of the national government are unknown, but it is at least known the President possesses a cabinet, with ministers that presumably oversee different areas of the government.
</p><p>The government overall is modeled after that of ancient Rome, reflected in Capitol society by the citizenry taking names of Greco-Roman origin and the architecture of the city. It is unknown if Panem possesses other branches of government such as a judiciary or a legislature, although given the authoritarian nature of the Panemian central government, both either don't exist or are under the complete control of the executive branch headed by the President. Following Panem's transition into a constitutional republic after the Second Rebellion,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> it is implied some form of representative democracy was established through the holding of presidential <a href="/wiki/Elections" title="Elections">elections</a>.
</p><p>Due to its authoritarian government, Panem politically and ideologically resembled historical extremist dictatorship-led nations, with its centrally planned economy and all major industries run by Capitol oligarchs under state oversight. The Capitol set minimum production quotas for each of the Districts, and the Districts are subject to a system of central planning rather than a market economy. Many aspects of society were also quite conservative. For example, by the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, homosexuality was criminalized. Because of its totalitarian dictatorship governing Panem, citizens, particularly in the districts, have little civil liberties and virtually no capacity for self-governance or say in how to be governed. Even in the Capitol, its citizens have surrendered such rights in exchange for "bread and circuses." As such, Capitol citizens partake in lavish and hedonistic lifestyle dominated by mass entertainment, parties, and extravagant fashion that is largely removed from the oppressions and destitution faced by the outlying districts.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Agriculture_Department">Agriculture Department</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the Agriculture Department was headed by the father of <a href="/wiki/Iphigenia_Moss" title="Iphigenia Moss">Iphigenia Moss</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Communications">Communications</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After the Second Rebellion, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> was appointed Secretary of Communications in the new republic.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="War_Department">War Department</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the War Department was housed in the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, it was headed by the <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a>, Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Defense_Department">Defense Department</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Ministry_of_Information">Ministry of Information</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>At the time of the Second Rebellion, the CMI was a new branch dedicated to improving Panem's functionality through information collection and analysis. It extended through the Capitol and its districts, and was headed by <a href="/wiki/Egeria" title="Egeria">Egeria</a>, who was also the Minister of Affairs.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Economy">Economy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Currency">Currency</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i> gave Panem's currency as dollars,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while coins appeared to be the main form of currency in the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <i>Ballad</i> film used coins and paper currency known as Panars, presumably a portmanteau of "Panem dollar".
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Taxation">Taxation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Prior to the 10th Hunger Games, Panem only collected property taxes from the districts. They began to tax Capitol citizens as well in order to fund post-war reconstruction in the city.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Military">Military</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a></i></center></div>
<p>Law enforcement is provided through a military/police force known as the Peacekeepers. Each district possesses a local garrison of Peacekeepers led by a Head Peacekeeper. This individual is responsible for commanding the district's Peacekeeper force and the enforcement of the laws of Panem within their designated district. It is assumed that the President of Panem is commander-in-chief of the Peacekeepers.
</p><p>The Peacekeeper force was originally headquartered in District 13, the then center of Panem's military-industrial complex. Following the Dark Days, the Peacekeepers' headquarters were relocated to District 2. Presumably for the next seventy-five years, the Capitol's command and control center for its military was centered in <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>, a heavily fortified mountain fortress that overlooked District 2. As a result of District 2's position regarding the Capitol military, most soldiers who enlisted in the Peacekeepers came from District 2, although a significant portion originated from the Capitol, with a small minority coming from the other Districts.
</p><p>Peacekeepers were distinguished by their near-all white uniforms. In times of intense civil unrest, such as what occurred in the aftermath of the 74th Hunger Games into the lead up of the Second Rebellion, there is a tactical variant of their uniform that is more heavily armored.
</p><p>The Peacekeepers had a number of mechanized equipment at their disposal including armored ground vehicles as well as the Capitol's vast fleet of <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a>. In addition, the Capitol is known to possess a significant arsenal of <a href="/wiki/Nuclear_weaponry" title="Nuclear weaponry">nuclear weapons</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Much of Panem's history is shrouded in mystery, though known history dates back approximately seventy-five years prior to the beginning of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Trilogy" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games Trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>.
</p>
<div class="tabber wds-tabber"><div class="wds-tabs__wrapper with-bottom-border"><ul class="wds-tabs"><li class="wds-tabs__tab wds-is-current" data-hash="Founding"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">Founding</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="The_Dark_Days"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">The Dark Days</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="The_Hunger_Games"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">The Hunger Games</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="The_Second_Rebellion"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">The Second Rebellion</a></div></li></ul></div><div class="wds-tab__content wds-is-current"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p>An unspecified time before the events of the story, the Earth was devastated by climate change and global nuclear war(s) that led to the collapse of modern civilization. Surviving populations that survived this initial calamity were further decimated by the subsequent nuclear winter and catastrophic ecological events that followed, which Katniss states were droughts, fires, storms, and rising sea levels.
</p><p>Some time after this apocalyptic event, survivors from the collapse of civilization in North America eventually founded a new nation in the region of the Rocky Mountain that would come to be known as Panem. It is unknown precisely when Panem was established and how long it has existed, though it is certain that Panem has been around for more than seventy-five years, and it's entirely possible for it to be at least a century or two old.
</p><p>Panem eventually grew large enough that it was segmented into thirteen separate districts, each responsible for producing goods of a particular industry to serve the growing needs of the nation, and all operating under the auspices of Panem's oppressive Capitol. The exact method of Panem's expansion is unknown; it is possible that some of the separate districts may have even once been smaller, separate survivor nations that were eventually annexed or conquered by Panem.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-none show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 200px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Panem_in_NA.png/revision/latest?cb=20200828064147" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A map of Panem superimposed over a map of present-day North America." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="150" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Panem in NA.png" data-image-key="Panem_in_NA.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Panem_in_NA.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20200828064147" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Panem_in_NA.png/revision/latest?cb=20200828064147" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A map of Panem superimposed over a map of present-day North America." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Panem_in_NA.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20200828064147" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="150" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Panem in NA.png" data-image-key="Panem_in_NA.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e4/Panem_in_NA.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20200828064147" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Panem_in_NA.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A map of Panem superimposed over a map of present-day North America.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a></i></center></div>
<p>Approximately seventy-four years before the events of <i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy, the various districts of Panem, led by <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, rebelled against the Capitol. The primary reasons of the revolution largely lay with the oppressive fashion in which the Capitol government ruled over and exploited the districts. This civil war lasted three years, and although the exact number of casualties on either side is not known (the Capitol claims that for every dead Capitol citizen, two rebels died), both factions sustained heavy losses, while Panem itself was ravaged by the war. 
</p>
Over the course of the conflict, the tide shifted between both sides, but in the war's waning days, it decisively shifted against the rebelling districts. Realizing the rebellion was lost, District 13 sought a separate peace with the Capitol after securing a significant portion of its nuclear arsenal. Unwilling to escalate the insurrection into a nuclear war, the Capitol agreed to 13's demands for independence and a non-aggression pact on the condition it renege its support for the other districts and appear destroyed on the surface, which District 13 accepted. Following the apparent destruction of District 13, the Capitol quickly subdued the remaining districts in the final stage of the conflict known as the Dark Days. With the pacification of the remaining twelve districts the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a> was signed and through it the Capitol enacted sweeping new laws restricting civil liberties and enforcing heavy surveillance on the defeated districts in order to guarantee peace and order. Furthermore, as a cruel reminder to the districts each year so that the Dark Days should never happen again, the Capitol instituted the Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games (event)</a></i></center></div>
<p>In the wake of the rebellion, the Capitol established the Hunger Games, an annual event in which twenty-four children between the ages of twelve and eighteen, one boy and one girl from each district, are chosen from a <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)#Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">lottery</a>. Tributes could also sign up for <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a> in exchange for more slips in the lottery. The selected <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> as they were called were brought to the Capitol, where after a brief period of survival and combat training, were entered into a gladiatorial competition where they must fight each other to the death until only one remains standing. This individual was subsequently crowned a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> and was ensured a life of luxury and extraordinary privilege.
</p><p>The event served three purposes. The first was for the event to serve as a continual reminder to the districts' populace of the cost of insurrection and to never again make an attempt at another rebellion. This was emphasized in the special quarter-century iterations of the Games, the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, whose special rules were tailored toward the actions of the rebels during the Dark Days. The second was to demonstrate the overwhelming power the Capitol had over the districts by taking their children by force and making them slay each other in a no-rules free-for-all competition. It was to serve as proof that the Capitol's control over its people was so inexorable and unstoppable that they could do something so sadistic as to force the children of their conquered foes to slay each other in a battle royale. The third purpose was purely for entertainment, particularly for the citizens of the Capitol. The Hunger Games were a nationally televised event in the same vein as a reality show that all of its citizens were required to watch. The tributes and the inevitable victors are practically considered celebrities and the events of the Games themselves are dramatised and glorified.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Over the next seventy-five years, the districts were continually oppressed and heavily surveilled by the Capitol through its ever present Peacekeeper force. Laws were strictly enforced by the districts' resident Peacekeepers, and public whippings and executions became commonplace. To add further insult to injury, the districts were forced to annually bear witness to the humiliation and horror of the Hunger Games, hoping one of the two tributes reaped return as a victor. The first ten iterations of the Hunger Games were held in the Capitol and were quite primitive and flawed. Following the disastrous <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the modern attributes of the Hunger Games were eventually implemented including that the victor shall live a life of luxury and privilege, that they shall reside in a lavish community in their district known as the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>, and that the Games would be held in specially crafted arenas in presumably isolated regions throughout North America. At some point in the history of the Games, the wealthier districts, particularly 1, 2, and 4, began instituting <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">training programs</a> led by victors to prepare children for the Hunger Games. Despite this being illegal according to the rules of the Hunger Games, the Capitol turned a blind eye to this practice, likely for it adding a further degree of excitement to the Games itself and as a means of further dividing the districts by driving a wedge between the Career and outlier districts.
</p>
Nevertheless, despite the Hunger Games' intended purpose of ensuring no further revolts occurred amongst the districts, the Games ironically acted to only further incite the district population to rebel. Popular resentment subtlety rose amongst the districts over the course of decades of Hunger Games. An unknown amount of time prior to the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, a conspiracy involving several Hunger Games victors, high-ranking members of the Snow regime, and District 13 arose with the intended purpose of ending the Hunger Games and overthrowing the totalitarian government of Panem.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></i></center></div>
<p>The Second Rebellion's origin laid in the aftermath of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, although momentum toward the revolution had been building for decades since the institution of the Games. It was sparked by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s near double-suicide at the end of the Games<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, which was interpreted as an act of rebellion by many residents of the districts. As a result, it caused a dramatic rise in civil unrest in the districts in the months following the 74th Games and into the lead up to the start of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, which was also the Third Quarter Quell. 
</p><p>During the finale of the Third Quarter Quell, whose special rule stipulated that past <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> were to be reaped into the games, Katniss Everdeen shot an arrow attached to a piece of conductive wire into an imperfection in the force field surrounding the arena. The wire, which was connected to an artificial tree, was then struck by lightning, with the energy of the strike passing through the wire into the force field, destroying it in the process. She was subsequently rescued and transported to District 13, which had been rebuilt as an underground city-sized bunker before being bombed during the Dark Days. Other surviving victors such as <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a> were also rescued, while Peeta, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> were left behind and captured by the Capitol. Immediately following the impromptu conclusion of the 75th Games, multiple districts throughout Panem went into full-blown revolt against the Snow regime, marking the start of the Second Rebellion. As an act of retribution for Katniss's actions at the end of the Third Quarter Quell, the Capitol destroyed District 12, echoing the faked destruction of District 13 during the Dark Days<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, although a small number of citizens would escape prior due to the actions of <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> and be taken in by District 13.
</p><p>With the revolution now in full swing, Katniss was recruited to be the 'Mockingjay' at the urging of District 13 President, <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, for the purpose of serving as a symbol of the rebellion and rallying call for the resistance to overthrow the Capitol. Over the course of the following year, Katniss performed multiple <a href="/wiki/Propos" class="mw-redirect" title="Propos">propaganda shoots</a> at several battlefields throughout Panem, inspiring citizens to continue the fight against the Capitol by showcasing the atrocities they inflicted on the districts. In a similar vein, the Capitol utilized Peeta Mellark as their own spokesperson to convince Panemian citizens that the continuation of the war will lead to the country's destruction and should be stopped immediately. Despite this effort, Capitol forces were gradually pushed backed as District 13 military forces, alongside local rebel forces, liberated each one of the outlying districts. Following the destruction of <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">The Nut</a>, the Capitol's command and control center for their military forces, and the fall of District 2, its most loyal district, the city of the Capitol itself was the only territory left under their control.
</p><p>In the final battle of the war, the Capitol itself was placed under siege in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, with rebel forces invading the city. Fierce fighting overtook the city, as rebel forces pushed back Capitol forces block by block, despite the latter's liberal use of sadistic traps and mutts alongside their Peacekeeper forces. At the battle's climax, with rebel forces pushing into the City Center, President Coin ordered a false flag operation by using a Capitol-marked hovercraft to bomb Capitol civilians being used as a human shield in front of the President's Mansion. After a second round of bombings resulted in the deaths of several Capitol children, an impromptu ceasefire was declared as all remaining support collapsed for the Snow regime and President Snow himself was subsequently arrested.
</p>
Following the fall of the Snow regime, President Coin instated herself as interim president and issued a vague promise of holding democratic elections at some point in the future once reconstruction from the revolution was completed. In reality, she intended to consolidate power and even continue the Hunger Games with one final Games involving Capitol children that saw narrow approval amongst the surviving victors. During the public execution of Coriolanus Snow that was to be conducted by the Mockingjay at her own request, Katniss, at the last moment, assassinated President Coin, putting an end to her authoritarian ambitions before they could start, while Snow died from what is rumored to have been from either suffocation or because of choking on his own blood. After Coin's death, <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Commander Paylor</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> was elected President of Panem and the Hunger Games were officially abolished. Soon after, President Paylor oversaw the liberalization and restructuring of Panem into a democratic constitutional republic.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div></div>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Districts">Districts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="tabber wds-tabber"><div class="wds-tabs__wrapper with-bottom-border"><ul class="wds-tabs"><li class="wds-tabs__tab wds-is-current" data-hash="Panem"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">Panem</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="The_Capitol"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">The Capitol</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="1"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">1</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="2"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">2</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="3"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">3</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="4"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">4</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="5"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">5</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="6"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">6</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="7"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">7</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="8"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">8</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="9"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">9</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="10"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">10</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="11"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">11</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="12"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">12</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="13"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">13</a></div></li><li class="wds-tabs__tab" data-hash="Career_Districts"><div class="wds-tabs__tab-label"><a href="#">Career Districts</a></div></li></ul></div><div class="wds-tab__content wds-is-current"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p>The nation of Panem is separated into a total of thirteen self-contained subdivisions known as districts spread across North America. For seventy-five years after the Dark Days, twelve of the districts were recognized as operational by the Capitol, while District 13 was destroyed during the closing days of the war (in reality having secretly seceded as an independent nation-state as part of a separate peace with the Capitol). Following the Second Rebellion, District 13 was reintegrated into Panem's political structure. 
</p><p>Each of the districts were responsible for producing, procuring, or refining goods in a particular industry as dictated by the Capitol, with the vast majority of said goods being shipped to the Capitol to fuel the hedonistic and lavish lifestyles of the city's populace. Each district provides something different to the Capitol; for example, District 12 provided coal, District 11 provided agricultural produce, District 7 provided lumber, and District 4 provided seafood. Prior to the Second Rebellion's close, District citizens had little freedom to choose careers outside of their district's designated industry as they were not allowed to travel outside of their respective district. Free markets outside of the Capitol, such as the Hob in District 12, are illegal, though some grey market exchange is tolerated, and District 12 itself does have a merchant sector that provides goods such as a bakery to provide food to its citizens. With all major industries privately owned by wealthy Capitol families under state oversight, Panem operates as a semi-feudal fascist state. All trade and commerce between the districts is generally forbidden, as most of the districts' produced goods are sent to the Capitol. Welfare levels vary significantly, and are often dependent on the goods it produces; some districts especially those whose industry specializes in food and less appealing goods, such as <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>, are far more impoverished than wealthier districts like <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, though no district is nearly as wealthy as the Capitol itself. Following the restructuring of Panem after the Second Rebellion, it is assumed the districts were allowed to diversify their economies as a rebuilt District 12 opened a medical facility.
</p><p>All of the districts are subject to the unrelenting will of the authoritarian Capitol, and have no known influence concerning the national politics of Panem beyond their own individual territories. As such, the local government of the individual districts are not entirely known. District 12 possessed a mayor<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, with a garrison of Peacekeepers composed of local and Capitol-sourced soldiers overseeing law enforcement under a Head Peacekeeper and it can be assumed the other Districts are governed in a similar way. It is unknown how the mayor of a district is selected, whether by election of the districts citizens or via appointment from the Capitol, although given the authoritarian nature of the Capitol, the latter is more likely.
</p>
While it is unclear as to the level of interactions the districts had prior to the Dark Days, following the implementation of the Treaty of Treason, all inter-district travel and communications was banned as a means of isolating the district from one another. As such, the districts retain very little if any interactions with one another and each one has developed a unique culture unaffected by the other districts. Information on the other districts is similarly heavily restricted amongst the basic citizenry, beyond knowledge of the other districts' industry in relation to the Capitol. During her conversation with <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> about <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> wonders if their conversation is being censored by the Gamemakers in order to prevent the watching districts from learning about another district’s way of life.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a></i></center></div>

<p>The Capitol, as its name suggests, is the seat of government of Panem and has long been considered the center or heart of the nation. For nearly the entire history of Panem, the Capitol has dominated all of the districts, whose food and resources are forcibly taken to propagate the city's economy and the prosperity and lifestyle of its inhabitants.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="People_and_Lifestyle">People and Lifestyle</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The residents of the Capitol are generally the wealthiest and most decadent in of all Panem; living lives of conspicuous consumption, the people are most known for their lavish and "eccentric" sense of fashion, to the extent of radical body modifications such of dying their skin different colors and even having whiskers and talons implanted, a thought that disgusts and is seen as bizarre by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and many of the citizens of the outlying districts. The city's prosperity is fueled by the industries and forced labor of the districts, and Capitol citizens are rarely, if ever, seen engaging in manual labor or any other serious work of their own. Instead, the lives of the Capitol citizens are centered around the latest fashion trends and the vast array of entertainment provided by the city, including the Hunger Games, which is considered the largest and most celebrated form of entertainment for its citizens.
</p>

The Capitol is located in the western part of the country, in an area formerly known as the Rocky Mountains, specifically situated in the southern and northern part of the U.S. states of Wyoming and Utah respectively. The Capitol is surrounded by the thirteen outlying districts and while it is a prominent territory, it is not considered a district in-and-of-itself.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File"><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ea/District_12_Seal.png/revision/latest?cb=20250622180757" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 12 Seal" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="100" height="119" class="mw-file-element lazyload" data-image-name="District 12 Seal.png" data-image-key="District_12_Seal.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ea/District_12_Seal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20250622180757" /></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s industry is coal and is located somewhere near what was the Appalachian mountains. The district has the distinction of being the most deprived region in all of Panem. Before <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> won the 74th Hunger Games, the district had not had a winner of the Hunger Games emerge from the ranks of its residents for twenty-four years since <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, a raging alcoholic and an embarrassment to the district, won the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. Before Haymitch, there was only one other victor (<a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>) from District 12, forty years before Haymitch's victory in the 50th Hunger Games and the last female victor before Katniss's joint victory with Peeta in the 74th Hunger Games, 64 years later.
</p><p>Besides potential tributes coming unprepared for the Games and the extremely dangerous nature of their work during their adult lives, District 12 is considered the laughingstock of Panem and it is considered dishonorable for Capitol citizens to have any direct involvement with this district. During the opening hours of the second rebellion shortly after the abrupt end of the third Quarter Quell, District 12 was destroyed by the Capitol using firebombs. Less than 900 people escaped, mostly thanks to the foresight of <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>. 
</p><p>District 12 was rebuilt at the end of the Second Rebellion; the mines were permanently closed and the district switched industry to medicine production. According to the Mockingjay podiums, District 12 had 3 victors; however in the book it was revealed District 12 had a total of 4. 
</p>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File"><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4a/District_13_Seal.png/revision/latest?cb=20250623000108" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 13 Seal" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="100" height="119" class="mw-file-element lazyload" data-image-name="District 13 Seal.png" data-image-key="District_13_Seal.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4a/District_13_Seal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20250623000108" /></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> was one of the thirteen districts of Panem. Its main industry was nuclear research and technology, including the production of nuclear weapons. In addition it served as the Capitol's original command and control center for its military-industrial complex. District 13 was the instigator of the Dark Days and led the other twelve districts in revolution against the oppression of the Capital. At the climax of the Dark Days, the district was supposedly obliterated as a warning to the other twelve districts of the Capitol's might and that further resistance was futile. In the years following the Dark Days, the Capitol led Panem's population to widely believe that the main industry of District 13 was graphite mining and that the district was now an uninhabitable ruin, supposedly still smoldering from the toxic bombs dropped upon it. 
</p><p>It is revealed in <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> that the district is in fact still operational. During the Dark Days, District 13 secured a significant portion of the Capitol nuclear arsenal, and under threat of escalating the rebellion into a nuclear war, demanded independence and a non-aggression pact. The Capitol acquiesced to these terms on the condition that District 13 abandon the remaining rebelling districts and its surface infrastructure be destroyed. As such, District 13 seceded from the nation of Panem and operated covertly in a vast city-sized underground bunker. Per the terms of the non-aggression pact, the Capitol left District 13 to its own devices. As such, none of its citizens ever participated in the Hunger Games.
</p><p>While the surface of District 13 remained scarred and supposedly uninhabitable, its residents lived deep underground, hidden away from the world and the eyes of the Capitol. Over the subsequent decades, District 13 gradually rebuilt its strength, and in the lead up to the 74th Hunger Games, began subtly supporting a conspiracy alongside Hunger Games victors and high-ranking members of the Snow regime to instigate a second revolution. In the immediate outbreak of the Second Rebellion, District 13 served as the leading force and supplier of military equipment for the rebels in the other districts. Following the successful overthrow of the Snow regime at the conclusion of the Second Rebellion, District 13 was reintegrated into Panem. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Location">Location</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
According to the Panem map shown in <i>The Hunger Games Adventures</i>, District 13 is located north of District 12 in a region encompassing western New York, and northwestern Pennsylvania, as well as the southern part of the Canadian provinces of Ontario and Quebec.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p>
Many of the wealthier districts such as Districts 1, 2, 4 and 5, known as "Career districts," have a more positive orientation toward the Hunger Games. As residents in these districts do not generally face extreme poverty and malnutrition as is experienced in the more impoverished districts, their tributes are generally healthy and strong. In these districts, it is not uncommon for children to volunteer to be tributes, as participating and winning the Hunger Games is held as a great privilege and honor in these districts. Although it is against the rules of the Hunger Games, many children in these districts attend specialized training academies run by past victors, where they are specifically raised to participate in the Hunger Games, and thus are trained in the arts of <a href="/wiki/Combat" class="mw-redirect" title="Combat">combat</a> and survival. They are typically known as Career Tributes, or "Careers" for short and are commonly cited as being regular contenders for being crowned a victor, to the ire of the residents of outlying districts who look down upon them in disgust and disdain.</p></div></div>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Behind_the_scenes">Behind the scenes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Panem and the general setting of <i>The Hunger Games</i> has many references to the Ancient Roman Empire. The Games themselves are reminiscent of the Roman <i>munera</i>, or gladiatorial games. The districts surrounding the Capitol are similar to the Roman provinces that surrounded the city of Rome. Many names, particularly of those from the Capitol are Roman names, such as Cinna, Seneca, Caesar, Coriolanus, Portia etc.
</p><p>The name "Panem" itself comes from the Latin expression <i>panem et circenses</i>, which means "bread and circuses", a political strategy to keep the people satisfied with the current leaders by distracting them with cheap food ("bread") and entertainment ("circuses"), the two basis of Panem's control over the districts and the people within. The <i>Hunger Games</i> version of <i>panem et circenses</i> is far darker and crueler than the Roman version, as food and entertainment are also used as a means of coercion (through threats of being withheld) as well as for distraction.
</p><p>Additionally, Panem may also have a secondary derivation as a corrupted form of "Pan-Am" ("Pan-America"), hinted by the fact that the name "Panem" is pronounced in the movies with both syllables stressed.
</p><p>Panem had 13 districts, possibly an allusion to the original Thirteen American Colonies that rebelled against their British rulers.  
</p><p>You can find a map of all 13 districts of Panem <a href="/wiki/Map:Panem" title="Map:Panem">here</a>.  
</p>
