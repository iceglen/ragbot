<p><b>Persephone Price</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She was assigned the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPersephone_Price%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Persephone lived with her father <a href="/wiki/Nero_Price" title="Nero Price">Nero Price</a> in a town house on the <a href="/wiki/Corso" title="Corso">Corso</a>. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> once witnessed her father cutting off the leg of a maid to cannibalize during a food shortage, Coriolanus tried to avoid her whenever possible; he couldn't help but wonder if she was aware of the ingredients of her wartime stew. At one time she inspired fear in him, though this faded to disgust, despite his knowing that she herself was innocent.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Regarding the affection that people in the Capitol had for Snow's tribute, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, she commented that "People love a good song," referencing the song that Lucy Gray sung at her <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaping</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While the 10th Hunger Games took place, Persephone worked together with <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a>, trying to come up with a joint strategy for their tributes, who both came from District 4. Festus was falling for Persephone and was often seen deep in discussion with her.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Her tribute, Mizzen, placed fifth (fourth in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">film</a>) in the 10th Hunger Games, ensuring her a respectable prize for a strong performance as a mentor. She sent him several drones with food and water from <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a>, but the drones were hacked by <a href="/wiki/Teslee" title="Teslee">Teslee</a>, the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>, the technology district. Mizzen tried to flee from the swarm of redirected drones, but ended up falling and snapping his neck. Afterwards, she spoke with <a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a> of <a href="/wiki/Capitol_News" title="Capitol News">Capitol News</a>, expressing both regrets for not getting further, but also gratitude for the opportunity to be a mentor. He told her that she had made it to the top five, which nobody could take away from her, and, though dubious, she agreed that it was the sort of thing that stuck.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>She also visited Snow's apartment when he was discharged to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> alongside <a href="/wiki/Lysistrata_Vickers" title="Lysistrata Vickers">Lysistrata Vickers</a> and Festus.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPersephone_Price%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She had dimples and hazel green eyes and was generally considered the prettiest girl in Coriolanus Snow's year at the Academy, with the possible exception of <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPersephone_Price%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persephone" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Persephone">Persephone</a> was the Greek goddess of the underworld, springtime, flowers and vegetation. She was abducted by Hades, god of the dead and the underworld, who tricked her into eating pomegranate seeds grown in his realm. Similarly, Persephone Price consumed the maid's leg her father harvested while likely being unaware of its source.
</p>
