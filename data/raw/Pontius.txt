</p><p><b>Pontius</b> was a young boy who talked to <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> when she was in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>. He had a younger sister named <a href="/wiki/Venus" title="Venus">Venus</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPontius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Pontius was among a group of small children clustered near the bars of the monkey house, where the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> were located. When <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> walked over and asked the children if they would like to meet Lucy Gray, Pontius was eager for the opportunity, having seen her on <a href="/wiki/Capitol_TV" title="Capitol TV">Capitol TV</a>. He shook hands with Lucy Gray and told her about his sister.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPontius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontius_Pilate" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pontius Pilate">Pontius Pilate</a> was a Roman governor that served under Emperor Tiberius, known for presiding over Jesus' trial and crucifixion.
