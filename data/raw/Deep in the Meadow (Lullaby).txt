<p><b>"Deep in the Meadow (Lullaby)"</b> is a bonus track from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Songs_from_District_12_and_Beyond" title="The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond">The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond</a></i>, included as a download with purchase of the soundtrack.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The song is performed by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sting_(musician)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sting (musician)">Sting</a> and was released on March 27th, 2012.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Deep in the meadow, under the willow<br />
A bed of grass, a soft green pillow<br />
Lay down your head, and close your sleepy eyes<br />
And when again they open, the sun will rise.<br />
<br />
Here it's safe, here it's warm<br />
Here the daisies guard you from every harm<br />
Here your dreams are sweet, and tomorrow brings them true<br />
Here is the place where I love you.<br />
<br />
Deep in the meadow, hidden far away<br />
A cloak of leaves, a moonbeam ray<br />
Forget your woes, and let your troubles lay<br />
And when again it's morning, they'll wash away.<br />
<br />
Here it's safe, here it's warm<br />
Here the daisies guard you from every harm<br />
Here your dreams are sweet, and tomorrow brings them true<br />
Here is the place where I love you.
