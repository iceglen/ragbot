<p><b>Mrs. Mellark</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Baker" title="Baker">baker</a> who lived in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. She was the mother of three sons, including <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> and <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>. She ran the family bakery with her husband <a href="/wiki/Mr._Mellark" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Mellark">Mr. Mellark</a> and had an unkind spirit.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mrs. Mellark had three sons: the <a href="/wiki/First_Mellark_son" title="First Mellark son">elder</a> <a href="/wiki/Second_Mellark_son" title="Second Mellark son">sons</a> are unnamed, and Peeta Mellark is the youngest. She and her family owned the <a href="/wiki/Bakery" title="Bakery">bakery</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a> section of District 12, and they all worked there.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/27/Mrs_mellark_and_peeta.png2.png/revision/latest?cb=20120425140703" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Mrs. Mellark with Peeta outside the family bakery." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/27/Mrs_mellark_and_peeta.png2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120425140703" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="95" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mrs mellark and peeta.png2.png" data-image-key="Mrs_mellark_and_peeta.png2.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mrs_mellark_and_peeta.png2.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Mrs. Mellark with Peeta outside the family bakery.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>When <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was eleven years old and starving, she passed by the bakery and searched its trash cans, hoping to find food. Mrs. Mellark spotted at her and yelled insults, telling her to move on. However, after Mrs. Mellark reentered the bakery, Katniss witnessed her scolding and beating Peeta after he burnt two loaves of bread and telling him to feed them to the pigs just before disappearing to help a customer.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>She was not supportive of Peeta when his name was drawn to compete in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. When she met with him to say goodbye before he boarded the train to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, she attempted to cheer him up by saying that District 12 might finally have a winner, but was referring to Katniss, the other tribute, rather than her own son.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Along with Mr. Mellark and their two oldest sons, Mrs. Mellark was killed in the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombing</a> of District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mrs. Mellark had quite a temper and often turned to beating her children when they had done something that had made her angry in their younger years. For example, when Peeta was young and burnt some of the bread she was baking, she hit him, saying that no decent person would buy burnt bread. If she had known that he had given it to Katniss, a girl from <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>, it would have ensured a terrible beating.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>She was cold and not very kind. She had very little faith in Peeta's ability to win the Games; later in the book when Peeta was talking to Katniss, he told her that his mother said, "Maybe District 12 will finally have a winner. She's a survivor, that one", insinuating that she didn't think Peeta would come home, but that Katniss had a better shot.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Mrs. Mellark was married to <a href="/wiki/Mr._Mellark" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Mellark">Mr. Mellark</a>, who was a <a href="/wiki/Baker" title="Baker">baker</a>. They had three sons, the <a href="/wiki/First_Mellark_son" title="First Mellark son">first</a> and <a href="/wiki/Second_Mellark_son" title="Second Mellark son">second</a> of which are unnamed and the third of which is Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Her husband mentioned to five-year-old Peeta that he wanted to marry <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Katniss' mother</a>, but she was in love with someone else, <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">Mr. Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Mr. Mellark was said to have a habit of buying Katniss' squirrels, but usually when his wife was not with him. He was much kinder without the influence of his wife.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
