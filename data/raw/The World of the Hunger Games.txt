</p><p><i><b>The World of the Hunger Games</b></i> is the fourth and final of the official tie-in books to <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is the second of the tie-ins by Kate Egan and is hardback, unlike the other tie-in books. <i>The World of the Hunger Games</i> was released on March 23, 2012, the same day as the film. It is primarily a visual chronicle of the film, covering basic information on the <i>Hunger Games</i> universe with quotes by <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> that provide additional insight.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
