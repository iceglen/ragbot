<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 212px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ee/Katniss_Peeta_Seeder.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140702123301" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss, Peeta and Seeder waiting their private sessions" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ee/Katniss_Peeta_Seeder.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/212?cb=20140702123301" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="212" height="154" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss Peeta Seeder.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_Peeta_Seeder.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_Peeta_Seeder.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss, Peeta and Seeder waiting their private sessions</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>
During the time between training and the opening to the Quarter Quell, Katniss decided she wanted Seeder as her ally in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, but overall decided against having allies as she was getting too close to the other competitors, thinking she wouldn't be able to kill them in the end to ensure <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a>'s victory. Katniss originally wanted Seeder as an alliance as well as <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cecelia" title="Cecelia">Cecelia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, however, she was not too sure whether she wanted to be in an alliance with Chaff as well.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Seeder_interview_costume.JPG/revision/latest?cb=20230826002511" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Seeder's interview dress" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Seeder_interview_costume.JPG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230826002511" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="294" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Seeder interview costume.JPG" data-image-key="Seeder_interview_costume.JPG" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Seeder_interview_costume.JPG" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Seeder's interview dress</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSeeder%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During Seeder's <a href="/wiki/Interview" class="mw-redirect" title="Interview">interview</a> with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, she quietly talked about how the people of District 11 all think <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> is all-powerful and asked, "If he is so powerful why couldn't he just change the Quell?"<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Seeder, along with every other victor, held hands after the interviews as a sign of solidarity, that Snow interpreted as a rebellion<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>In the film, she wore a light brown dress that reached her shins and wore small brown boots<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSeeder%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Seeder was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a> on the first day of the Games<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Like many of the other tributes, she ran for supplies. Upon arrival, she was not able to reunite with the Rebels Alliance, and was killed by <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a>. She placed around 17th-23rd.
</p><p>Katniss was dismayed by her death and grieves for her, the male morphling, and <a href="/wiki/Cecelia" title="Cecelia">Cecelia</a> later that night<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSeeder%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Seeder had olive skin, straight black hair streaked with silver, and golden brown eyes. Katniss thought that aside from her eyes, she looked like she came from <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>. She appeared to be about 60 years old in <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>. Katniss took to her not only because she was kind and gentle as a person, but because she ultimately reminded her of Rue.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSeeder%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, Seeder had dark skin like the majority of people from District 11, and she had short, curly black hair<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSeeder%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>They’re safe.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Seeder reassuring Katniss on Thresh and Rue’s family<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Seeder was a very kind, polite, gentle and positive woman. The first thing Seeder did when she saw Katniss was whisper in her ear that Thresh and Rue's families were safe, which gave Katniss relief. Seeder was friendly to Katniss and ate with her during lunch during training. She also seemed to be confident<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>; During her interview, she strongly and quietly stated that the Quarter Quell was wrong and <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> could stop it if he wanted<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Seeder was very motherly as well, all of which prompted Katniss to want her as an ally.
</p>
