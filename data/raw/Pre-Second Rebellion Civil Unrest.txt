<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Uprising_of_District_11">Uprising of District 11</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_11" title="Uprising of District 11">Uprising of District 11</a></i></center></div>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the first uprising since the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> took place in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. A riot broke out in response to the death of <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, their 12-year-old female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>. The conflict lasted until after dark, when <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> reinforcements arrived with batons, shields, and water cannons to quell the uprising. It was presumably shut down swiftly.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Conclusion_of_the_74th_Hunger_Games">Conclusion of the 74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i> novel, open rebellion only began to gain momentum when <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, threatened to commit double suicide by deadly <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries unless they were both allowed to live.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This served as a major turning point for national politics, and their act of defiance unintentionally inspired rebellious sentiment across <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<hr />
<h3><span id="Victory_Tour_.2874th_HG.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)"><a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour (74th HG)</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When Katniss and Peeta visited District 11 on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, an <a href="/wiki/Unidentified_District_11_citizen" title="Unidentified District 11 citizen">elderly man</a> whistled <a href="/wiki/Rue%27s_four-note_song" title="Rue&#39;s four-note song">Rue's four-note song</a>, signaling the rest of the crowd to perform District 12's <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a>. As the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> left the stage, this man was shot dead,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> with several more gunshots following after once the two were inside the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During their visits to other <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>, Katniss mentioned that the crowds in <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">Districts 3</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a> expressed "genuine elation... at the sight of [them], and under the elation, fury." She also characterized their cheers as cries for vengeance.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Uprising_of_District_8">Uprising of District 8</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">Uprising of District 8</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Uprd8.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130723101546" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A silhouetted person raising up the symbol of the mockingjay." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Uprd8.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130723101546" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Uprd8.jpg" data-image-key="Uprd8.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Uprd8.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A silhouetted person raising up the symbol of the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the novels, the uprising of District 8 was the first full-scale rebellion since the Dark Days.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It occurred during Katniss and Peeta's engagement in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> on their Victory Tour. Rebels carried out a coordinated assault on centers of power and resources, temporarily overwhelming Peacekeepers until reinforcements arrived. The increased Peacekeeper response, combined with suppressive <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombings</a>, quelled the uprising within 48 hours. A week-long period of martial law followed, and when it was lifted, citizens returned to work. Many were killed when the Capitol then bombed the factory where the uprising was planned.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<hr />
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_acts_of_resistance">Other acts of resistance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On the day before the reading of the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> card, District 4 had been in revolt for several weeks, resulting in seafood shortages that were explained away as a consequence of bad weather. There were shortages of electric gadgets from District 3 that implied rebel action there as well, alongside rumors of uprisings in <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">Districts 7</a> and 11.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ee/Catchingfire_06714.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140519192528" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The victors holding hands." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/ee/Catchingfire_06714.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140519192528" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Catchingfire 06714.jpg" data-image-key="Catchingfire_06714.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Catchingfire_06714.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The victors holding hands.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>As retaliation for the districts' unrest, <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> announced that for the third Quarter Quell, the tributes would be <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> from "the strongest among them": the existing pool of victors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This rule outraged even residents of the Capitol, which the victors capitalized on during their <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, in an ultimately futile effort to put a stop to the Quell.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At the conclusion of the program, the victors all held hands in a rebellious display of district unity.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Unbeknownst to the Capitol, rebels (including <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>) had begun to devise a plan soon after the reading of the Quell card. Tributes from Districts 3, 4, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, 7, 8, and 11 worked in tandem to protect Katniss Everdeen and break out of the 75th Hunger Games arena. At midnight on the third night of the Games, Katniss fired an electrified arrow into the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> unknowingly allowing for her extraction to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> along with <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Fifteen minutes later, Capitol bombers <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">razed District 12</a>, and the Second Rebellion officially began.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
