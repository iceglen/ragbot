<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the novels, the Cornucopia was described as a giant golden horn shaped like a cone with a curved tail. Its mouth was estimated to be at least twenty feet high,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and it was designed to look like the woven horn filled at the harvest, so its surface had a number of ridges and seams that made it possible to climb.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The films took a more abstract approach to the Cornucopia. In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, it was black instead of gold, and it followed the same general shape as a Cornucopia, but the top was flatter and wider so the actors could fight on top of it. In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, it was silver and far more angular, made of long, wide strips of metal, though the overall structure still suggested the same general shape.
</p>

<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>, the Cornucopia is a symmetrical white and metallic structure made up of six tall, triangular spires. The spires are arranged in three paired sets, each pair contains a taller inner spire and a shorter, wider outer spire; the outer spires partially enclose the inner ones like shells. The exterior surfaces are white while the interior faces are painted deep purple, creating a strong visual contrast. Golden strips run along the geometric edges of both the interior and exterior, and on the inner faces these strips radiate from quarter-circle motifs at the base, producing the effect of sunbeams. Each spire tapers to a sharp point. The Cornucopia rests on a raised platform with vertical faces clad in gold plating, shaped like a three-pointed star, with staircases leading to the Cornucopia at the center of each side.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Purpose">Purpose</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Supplies">Supplies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the beginning of the Games, <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> stood on metal platforms that pushed them upwards into the arena, where they found themselves arranged in a circle with the Cornucopia at its center. Less valuable supplies would be scattered around the Cornucopia, with the most valuable items being at the mouth of the horn. This was meant to entice tributes to fight over the stockpile and give the audience an exciting <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 272px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/Cornucopia_Supplies.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130604124916" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Cornucopia in  before the bloodbath." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="272" height="173" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Cornucopia Supplies.jpg" data-image-key="Cornucopia_Supplies.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/Cornucopia_Supplies.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/272?cb=20130604124916" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/Cornucopia_Supplies.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130604124916" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Cornucopia in  before the bloodbath." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/Cornucopia_Supplies.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/272?cb=20130604124916" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="272" height="173" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Cornucopia Supplies.jpg" data-image-key="Cornucopia_Supplies.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/Cornucopia_Supplies.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/272?cb=20130604124916" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Cornucopia_Supplies.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Cornucopia in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> before the bloodbath.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The Cornucopia always offered an array of <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>. It would sometimes include food, water bottles (empty or filled), medicine, items of clothing like spare socks, and/or <a href="/wiki/Tools_and_gear" title="Tools and gear">other survival supplies</a>. Tributes could also grab <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpacks</a> that already held a selection of some such items so they could make a quick getaway without leaving empty-handed.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the bloodbath, an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> of <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> typically laid claim to the Cornucopia's remaining supplies early on, and they relied heavily on this stockpile in order to feed themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="1st-10th_Hunger_Games">1st-10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the first nine years of the Games, there was no pageantry in the way they were presented, so there was no Cornucopia. The tributes were simply locked inside the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> with no supplies other than a pile of <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="25th_Hunger_Games">25th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Cornucopia was introduced for the first time in a one-time use arena during the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a>, also known as the first <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="49th_Hunger_Games">49th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> recalled that the cornucopia for her Games was silver. The disorienting nature of the arena for the games meant that most of the tributes immediately went nuts and thus were unable to readily claim supplies. Wiress, however, carefully maneuvered away from the Cornucopia, finding packs of supplies where none appeared to be.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Cornucopia was an established convention of the Games by the time of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, also known as the second Quarter Quell. That year, the Cornucopia was in the middle of a green meadow. Everything in the arena was poisonous, so sponsor gifts, rainwater, and the Cornucopia were the only safe sources of food and drink. That year, the Cornucopia was golden and resembled the pupil of an eye in the greater eye-shaped arena.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> immediately targeted a spring-green backpack near the tail of the Cornucopia, claiming both a spear and a knife on his way to it. He claimed the backpack, then immediately made for the woods, avoiding the initial bloodbath. The backpack proved to contain a mesh hammock, a pair of binoculars, two gallon jugs of water, a number food supplies and charcoal tablets Haymitch at first believed to simply be for indigestion and therefore a joke on the part of the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_15_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_15-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> These, however, proved lifesaving when they turned out to be an antidote for poison after Haymitch drank the water from a stream.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_16_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_16-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>A feast was held on Day 13 of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, offering every tribute something they desperately needed<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> inside a backpack with their <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> number on it. <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a> hid inside the horn and sprung out to grab her bag. <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> was the feast's only casualty; in the novel, <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> dented her skull with a rock,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, he bashed her head against the metal Cornucopia.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Cornucopia was also the site of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s final fight with <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>. The three tributes were chased to the Cornucopia by <a href="/wiki/Wolf_mutt" title="Wolf mutt">wolf mutts</a>, and they all climbed the metal to get out of the mutts' reach. After a struggle, Peeta was able to throw Cato off and into the wolf pack below. However, Cato's body armor kept him alive, so he was mauled all night until Katniss could administer him a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/coup_de_gr%C3%A2ce" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:coup de grâce">coup de grâce</a> with her <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow and arrow</a> around dawn.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<ul><li>Once, the Cornucopia's only weapons were spiked <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Mace" title="Weapons">maces</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>One year, a pack of "hideous reptiles" destroyed the Careers' food supply.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Another year, a flood washed away the Careers' supplies found in the cornucopia.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornucopia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> is a Latin loanword that can be literally translated literally as "horn of abundance", but is more commonly known as a horn of plenty.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCornucopia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<ul><li>According to Greek mythology, a cornucopia was created when <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heracles" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Heracles">Heracles</a> wrestled with the river god <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achelous" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Achelous">Achelous</a>, and Heracles tore one of the god's horns off of his head.
<ul><li>Another myth credits an infant <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Zeus">Zeus</a> with creating the cornucopia when he accidentally ripped off the horn of a goat.</li></ul></li>
<li>The Cornucopia appears to have been the inspiration for <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s anthem in the films, "<a href="/wiki/Horn_of_Plenty" title="Horn of Plenty">Horn of Plenty</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
