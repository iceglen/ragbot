<p>There are many different <b>training stations</b> inside the gymnasium of the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> where <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> could learn and practice skills that helped them survive in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. These skills included training with many types of <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>, as well as survival techniques, like setting traps and recognizing unusual sources of food. Each station was staffed by an expert, and assistants were on hand to spar with the tributes if they wished, as they were forbidden to fight with each other before the entering the arena. 
</p><p>Tributes were free to move from station to station according to their own preferences and their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>'s instructions, though some stations - particularly weapons training - were more popular than others.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, <a href="/wiki/Atala" title="Atala">Atala</a>, the head trainer and overseer, strongly advised the tributes not to ignore the survival skills, as disease and exposure could be just as lethal as armed confrontation.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the end of the third day of training, each tribute was given a private session with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a> to show off a particular skill. Afterwards, they were each given a training score on a scale of 1–12 (1 being the worst, 12 being the best), which could help attract potential <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a> during the competition.
</p>

<p>In the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee</a> was given a booth in training, something which normally wasn't given to mentors, but the Gamemakers didn't want him to miss a minute, as his own son, <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a>, was being made to compete in the Games as punishment for his having attempted to sabotage <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s communication systems. Beetee taught the tributes how to turn a potato into a battery, or potentially something else such as a lemon in the absence of potatoes. He also emphasized to Haymitch Abernathy that there would be clues about the nature of the arena in training.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_8_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>The gauntlets are daunting obstacle courses used in the Training Center. They consist of ascending platforms that rise up to a landing. Tributes must jump from platform to platform to finish in the fastest time possible. To make it even more difficult, trainers swing padded clubs at the tributes. This station is very popular and all the <a href="/wiki/Careers" class="mw-redirect" title="Careers">Careers</a> have a go at this first. The station is not very popular to the tributes who are shy, less able, or want to stay inconspicuous. 
</p>
