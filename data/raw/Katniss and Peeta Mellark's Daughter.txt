<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKatniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%2527s_daughter%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Peeta lied about Katniss being pregnant to try to protect her from the Games,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and Katniss later stated that she miscarried due to an electric shock with the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> to avoid further questioning.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, Peeta desperately wanted children, and approximately fifteen years after the end of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, Katniss eventually agreed and she got pregnant legitimately.<sup id="cite_ref-Epilogue_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss described terrible fear at the feeling of her daughter moving inside her during her pregnancy. The only cure for this fear was finally holding her baby in her arms. Katniss truly loved her children, appreciating their ignorance towards the Games. However, she was remorseful, knowing that someday she would need to tell her children about their parents' role in the Games and the rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-Epilogue_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss and Peeta's daughter liked to dance in <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Epilogue_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
