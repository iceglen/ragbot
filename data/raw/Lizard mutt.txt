<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLizard_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On their seventh day in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> went into the city's underground maintenance tunnels to avoid the people and <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pods</a> on the streets above, and they settled in for the night around three in the morning on the eighth day. Shortly before seven o'clock, they began to hear mutts hissing <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s name.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Due to his <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a>, Katniss thought <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> was programmed to respond to the mutts because he whispered Katniss's name in his sleep, but when he woke up, he was still in control of himself.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The squad redistributed their weapons to arm Peeta and the camera crew, then continued on the course Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> had planned for them. When they heard the sounds of <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avox</a> slaves screaming, Katniss argued that she should lead the mutts off while the others go on ahead with the "mission". However, while they argued, the scent of blood and roses grew stronger, and they had a brief gunfight with six <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> before the lizard mutts appeared. The mutts decapitated the Peacekeepers before chasing the squad into the <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the Transfer, <a href="/wiki/Messalla" title="Messalla">Messalla</a> was killed by a pod that melted his skin like candle wax, and <a href="/wiki/Jackson" title="Jackson">Jackson</a> and <a href="/wiki/Leeg_1" title="Leeg 1">Leeg 1</a> died holding off mutts at the "Meat Grinder", a pod with dangerous, spinning blades. As Katniss, Peeta, Pollux, <a href="/wiki/Cressida" title="Cressida">Cressida</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> climbed a ladder out of the Transfer, the mutts decapitated <a href="/wiki/Homes" title="Homes">Homes</a>, <a href="/wiki/Castor" title="Castor">Castor</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>. Finally, Katniss said the keyword "<a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a>" three times into the <a href="/wiki/Holo" title="Holo">Holo</a>, activating its self-destruct, and she dropped it to blow up the mutts below.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLizard_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">

<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/87/MJP2_castor_death.png/revision/latest?cb=20240415034845" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Castor&#39;s death." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/87/MJP2_castor_death.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240415034845" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="117" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="MJP2 castor death.png" data-image-key="MJP2_castor_death.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:MJP2_castor_death.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Castor's death.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Egeria" title="Egeria">Egeria</a> alerted <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> that Squad 451, although previously presumed dead, had been spotted alive on surveillance cameras heading underground. Sometime later, lizard mutts were sent after Katniss and her squad in the sewers. When Jackson turned around to look behind her, the mutts emerged to kill her first. The other members of the squad fled, and while running, Castor was ambushed and killed as well, then Homes.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The remaining soldiers came to a room where they had to fight the mutts head-on before they could escape, and eventually, Finnick fought them by himself so the others could climb the ladder out. However, when he tried to climb after them, he was pulled down and swarmed. He yelled Katniss's name, and she activated the Holo's self-destruct to kill the mutts and end his suffering. Since the mutts had been killed in the sewers, the squad never fought them in the Transfer.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLizard_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Each mutt was the size of a grown human and skittered around on all fours. They were naked with tight, white skin and clawed hands and feet. They also had long reptilian tails, heads that jutted forward, and their faces were a "mess of conflicting features". They gave off a strong scent of roses that was meant to unsettle those familiar with President Snow, and they hissed Katniss's name.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>They came in great numbers and they were very durable, since they could withstand dozens of bullets and still keep coming. They tracked Katniss by her own scent, which drove them into a destructive frenzy, and they killed their victims by biting their necks to decapitate them.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/76/Mockingjay-part-2-tigris-is-that-you-doc-connors-is-that-you-528000.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150726160935" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A lizard mutt attacking Katniss." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/76/Mockingjay-part-2-tigris-is-that-you-doc-connors-is-that-you-528000.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150726160935" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mockingjay-part-2-tigris-is-that-you-doc-connors-is-that-you-528000.jpg" data-image-key="Mockingjay-part-2-tigris-is-that-you-doc-connors-is-that-you-528000.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mockingjay-part-2-tigris-is-that-you-doc-connors-is-that-you-528000.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A lizard mutt attacking Katniss.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i>, the mutts were more humanoid in overall build, and they could move on two legs as well as four. Like some real cave-dwelling creatures, they lacked pigmentation and they had no eyes, so their other senses had strengthened to compensate.<sup id="cite_ref-audiocom_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-audiocom-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They also had reptilian nostrils and ears.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLizard_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Gale Hawthorne was the only person known to have survived a neck wound from the mutts.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Finnick's wife, <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, was traumatized from watching her <a href="/wiki/District_4_male_(70th_HG)" title="District 4 male (70th HG)">district partner</a> get beheaded during the <a href="/wiki/70th_Hunger_Games" title="70th Hunger Games">70th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> only for the same thing to happen to her husband.</li>
<li>In <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i>, the lizard mutts were played by members of the stunt team in motion capture suits so the actors could get more physical with the combat.<sup id="cite_ref-audiocom_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-audiocom-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The lizard and <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> each killed four people in the film franchise.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Due to their humanoid frames in <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i>, some fans have speculated that the lizard mutts were modified humans— possibly made from Avoxes, due to human experimentation Snow witnessed in the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a> as a young man.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Despite Katniss's suspicion, it is unclear whether Peeta had truly been programmed to respond to the lizard mutts. He was known to whisper her name in his sleep during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, long before he was ever hijacked.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>However, in the film, he struggled more with his self-control during their escape, and he even told Katniss to leave him behind because he was a mutt.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
