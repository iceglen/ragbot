<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FProspero%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>This tribute only appears in an early version of a script for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, where he is named Prospero. Sometime during day 2, he was able to kill <a href="/wiki/Sage_Blanken" title="Sage Blanken">Sage Blanken</a>, the District 12 male, with a <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Mace" title="Weapons">mace</a>. <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> stated, "Sage Blanken goes down, knocked out by that Prospero boy from District Three - who certainly is handy with a mace!"<sup id="cite_ref-script_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-script-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FProspero%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>His time and manner of death are unknown, but he did not make it to the final two, as the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> was the <a href="/wiki/District_2_male_(73rd_HG)" title="District 2 male (73rd HG)">District 2 male</a> and second place was the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(73rd_HG)" title="District 10 male (73rd HG)">District 10 male</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
