<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>The <b>Mockingjay suit</b> is an outfit designed by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a> <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a>, based on the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay bird</a>. As the face for the <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> cause, Katniss wore this suit for <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a> and major public appearances during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_suit%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Cinna%27s_sketchbook_mockingjay_part_1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230826235837" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss paging through Cinna&#39;s sketchbook." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="76" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Cinna&#39;s sketchbook mockingjay part 1.png" data-image-key="Cinna%27s_sketchbook_mockingjay_part_1.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Cinna%27s_sketchbook_mockingjay_part_1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230826235837" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Cinna%27s_sketchbook_mockingjay_part_1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230826235837" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss paging through Cinna&#39;s sketchbook." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Cinna%27s_sketchbook_mockingjay_part_1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230826235837" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="76" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Cinna&#39;s sketchbook mockingjay part 1.png" data-image-key="Cinna%27s_sketchbook_mockingjay_part_1.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Cinna%27s_sketchbook_mockingjay_part_1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230826235837" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Cinna%27s_sketchbook_mockingjay_part_1.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss paging through Cinna's sketchbook.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Cinna drew several pages of designs for the suit in his sketchbook sometime between the announcement of the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> and a few weeks prior to the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">Games</a> themselves. At that point, he entrusted this book to <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, making him promise not to show Katniss until she decided to be the Mockingjay on her own.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The actual uniform was presumably created within that timeframe and entrusted to the rebels as well.
</p><p>Katniss first donned the suit for a test propo, filmed as proof of concept for <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>'s <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>. Due to the design of the sleeves, a scar from having her <a href="/wiki/Tracker" title="Tracker">tracker</a> cut out was clearly visible, so they wrapped a fake bloody bandage around her forearm to conceal it.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The next time she wore the suit was to <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, where she visited with wounded citizens in a hospital shortly before watching it get <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_8_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">bombed</a>, killing everyone inside.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss wore the suit again to film propos in the ruins of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and after the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Second_Bombing_of_District_13" title="Bombings">bombing of District 13</a>, her <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> dressed her in it once more to film aboveground.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It's unknown whether she wore her Mockingjay uniform for the propo she filmed to distract <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> from the <a href="/wiki/Rescue_mission" class="mw-redirect" title="Rescue mission">rescue mission</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> though it is likely.
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_District_2" title="Battle of District 2">Battle of District 2</a>, Katniss was suited up in full Mockingjay gear to give a speech after the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_the_Nut" title="Bombings">bombing of the Nut</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The suit's armor stopped a bullet when someone in the crowd shot at her, saving her life, although the impact still bruised her ribs and ruptured her spleen.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Katniss was deployed for the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a> with the rest of <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>, her Mockingjay suit was kept back at the rebel encampment.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She wouldn't wear it again until the <a href="/wiki/Execution_of_President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="Execution of President Snow">execution of President Snow</a>, where she assassinated Coin instead.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Once apprehended, she was held in her old room at the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>. She stripped off the uniform to shower and someone removed it from the room.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After that, the outfit's whereabouts are unknown.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Design">Design</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_suit%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>In the <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> novel, the suit was described as a black uniform that was "at first glance utterly utilitarian, at second a work of art." In particular, Katniss appreciated "the swoop of the helmet, the curve to the breastplate, [and] the slight fullness of the sleeves that [allowed] the white folds under the arms to show." As she flipped through the sketchbook, she noted layers of body armor, hidden <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> in the boots and belt, and special reinforcement over her heart.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss later mentioned that the suit had short sleeves, leaving her forearms bare.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The shoulder of her left sleeve had a small pocket that held an emergency <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/suicide_pill" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:suicide pill">suicide pill</a> that she could bite into in case of her capture.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_suit%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span>"<i>Symbol and soldier was our mantra.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Kurt and Bart on Katniss's Mockingjay outfit</dd></dl></div>
<p>In <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i> and <i>Part 2</i>, the Mockingjay costume was black with very dark blue-black accents for details like the breastplate. Unlike the novel, it had long sleeves rather than short, there was no helmet, and it lacked the white underarm folds that Katniss described. The armor on her back had ridged wing details, and on her right hand, she wore a specialized <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/archery" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:archery">archery</a> glove.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> There was also an arm guard to protect <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence" title="Jennifer Lawrence">Jennifer Lawrence</a> from the bowstring lashing her arm, which she had mentioned was an issue in the first film.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Costume designers Kurt Swanson and Bart Mueller— better known jointly as Kurt and Bart— stated that they took inspiration from the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Museum_of_Art" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Metropolitan Museum of Art">Metropolitan Museum of Art</a>'s armor collection, historical depictions of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_of_Arc" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Joan of Arc">Joan of Arc</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:2_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Japanese archery gear. Since the design in-universe was crafted by Cinna, they mentioned having fun trying to get into his head as a character and approach it the way he would: with a concern for the practical application of the outfit in combat. According to Kurt and Bart, both they and director <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a> were keen that she "not become a superhero or be too flashy."<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was also important for the costume to be modular so that more ceremonial details, like the wings or the breastplate, could be removed in moments of combat for mobility purposes. Another aspect of the outfit's functionality was a channel for the quiver to slot in, so it wouldn't bounce around whenever Katniss went to draw an arrow.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
