<p><b>Posy Hawthorne</b> is the youngest child and the only female child in the Hawthorne family. She grew up in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> and has three older brothers: <a href="/wiki/Vick_Hawthorne" title="Vick Hawthorne">Vick Hawthorne</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rory_Hawthorne" title="Rory Hawthorne">Rory Hawthorne</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>. Posy's mother, <a href="/wiki/Hazelle_Hawthorne" title="Hazelle Hawthorne">Hazelle Hawthorne</a>, took care of her and her brothers. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPosy_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i>Catching Fire</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPosy_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Around the time Gale was whipped for illegal poaching and had to be taken care of by <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a>, Posy became ill with measles and was nursed back to health by her mother. Mrs. Everdeen also sent medicine for Posy to recover.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was able to hear <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> mimicking Posy's cries in one section of the arena. This contributed to her giving up trying to hunt the birds and instead curling up in a ball, trying to block out the noise.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i>Mockingjay</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPosy_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Posy found refuge in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> after the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombing of District 12</a>, along with the rest of her family. At one meal, Posy asked if Octavia, a member of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a>' <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a>, was sick because the pallor of her skin was a light shade of green. At first Octavia seemed saddened about it, but then Posy said Octavia would look pretty no matter what shade of color her skin was. This gave Octavia a fondness for little Posy.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPosy_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Posy lived with her mother, Hazelle Hawthorne, and her three brothers; <a href="/wiki/Rory_Hawthorne" title="Rory Hawthorne">Rory</a>, <a href="/wiki/Vick_Hawthorne" title="Vick Hawthorne">Vick</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale</a>. She never met her <a href="/wiki/Mr._Hawthorne" title="Mr. Hawthorne">father</a>, as he died just before she was born in the same explosion that killed the father of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss Everdeen and her family are very much like family to them, due to her close relationship with Gale. During the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the Hawthornes were publicly referred to as Katniss' cousins, to keep them safe from her and Gale's friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPosy_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/posy">posy</a> is a small bouquet of flowers. Her surname comes from hawthorn bushes or trees, but could also be a reference to author <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathaniel_Hawthorne" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nathaniel Hawthorne">Nathaniel Hawthorne</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPosy_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Katniss heard Posy's voice, along with those of her siblings when the <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> mimicked the voices of loved ones being tortured in the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>. Thus it could be inferred that she was important to Katniss as part of Gale's family and her "cousins", Katniss described her as " Posy, helpless little Posy".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>She is one of the youngest characters mentioned by age in the series.</li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, Katniss Everdeen says to Gale at the beginning of the movie that they couldn't live out in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> because she has <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> and Gale has his brothers to look after, though she does not mention his younger sister, Posy.</li>
<li>Posy's favorite color is said to be bright pink, which was revealed when Gale teased her and told Octavia that Posy would like her even more if she were bright pink.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>All four Hawthorne siblings have four-letter names: Gale, Rory, Vick, and Posy.</li></ul>
