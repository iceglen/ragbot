</p><p><b>Ethan Jamieson</b> portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_4_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 male (74th HG)">District 4 male</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, his major motion picture debut.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Jamieson has also appeared in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Tree_Hill" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:One Tree Hill">One Tree Hill</a></i>. He hasn't been very active of late, but still has his curly hair.
</p><p>He is the only non-stunt actor (and one of the two listed as actors in the credits) to portray a Tribute that fell during the initial bloodbath. He is tied with <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> as the smallest and youngest Tribute. In fight sequences, he is shown hiding under the supply crates in the Cornucopia away from action; this is probably an intentional casting choice. 
</p>
