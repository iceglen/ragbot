</p><p><b>Latarsha Rose</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i></a> film, which was her feature film debut.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Rose was going to quit acting until she was called for an opportunity to audition for the role.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Rose announced via Twitter that she would not return for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, and that audiences would have to wait until <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> to find out what happened to Portia. <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
