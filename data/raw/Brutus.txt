<p><b>Brutus</b> was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of an unknown past <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Games</a>, presumably in the mid to late 40s. He was also a <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a>.
</p>

<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/de/Still02-brutus.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131125195511" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Brutus throwing spears in the Training Center." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/de/Still02-brutus.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131125195511" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Still02-brutus.jpg" data-image-key="Still02-brutus.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Still02-brutus.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Brutus throwing spears in the Training Center.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>During training, Brutus and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> got on each other's good side. Along with <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>, they practiced at the spear-throwing station. When Brutus saw Katniss's skill with a <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow and arrow</a>, Brutus expressed his interest in an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> to Haymitch.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss turned down his offer, and she and Peeta told Haymitch that they should go into the arena without any allies. Brutus received a fairly high score at the end of the training, though the exact number was not specified.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the interviews with each of the tributes, Brutus did not try to express any anger or feelings of betrayal, but he did reluctantly join hands with the other victors as a chain symbolizing their strength. He wore a silver chest plate, a black pair of shorts, and black boots.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="During_the_Games">During the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBrutus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

At the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, his pedestal was between the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 male (75th HG)">District 5 male</a> and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>. When the gong sounded, he jumped to his spoke and swam to the Cornucopia. Inside the arena, Brutus, <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> approached the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, where he showed a unique way of dodging Katniss' arrows with his belt. However, he, along with the other Careers, were driven off by Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, who got there before the Careers. While Katniss and Finnick left with <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a> and Peeta, Brutus and the other Careers stayed and helped massacre the competition.<sup href="/wiki/District_9_female_(75th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 9 female (75th HG)">District 9 female</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Twitter_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Twitter-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He was able to secure a spear during the bloodbath, which he likely used to kill his victims.</p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a Over time, the alliance began to shrink with the deaths of Mags<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, the female morphling<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and Blight, and the remaining members (minus Chaff who was off on his own) traveled back to the Cornucopia to retrieve any <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> they needed that had been left behind. In the film, he was nearly killed by Finnick, but saved after Enobaria threw a knife at Finnick's arm.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Gloss killed Wiress and he and Cashmere died shortly after, forcing Brutus and Enobaria to retreat from the Cornucopia.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Back in the jungle, the alliance soon learned that, with the deaths of the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 10 male (75th HG)">District 10 male</a> and the <a href="/wiki/District_5_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 female (75th HG)">District 5 female</a>, the Games were now down to them, Chaff, Enobaria, and Brutus. Beetee set up a plan to electrocute the water and cut off Brutus and Enobaria's resources.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He sent Katniss and Johanna to run the line across the jungle, pursued by Brutus and Enobaria.

In the chaos that followed, Enobaria stabbed Beetee again but didn't manage to kill him before Finnick chased her away.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Brutus ran into Peeta, who had recently met up with Chaff. Shortly after his death, Katniss destroyed the arena and ended the Games, leaving six surviving tributes<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBrutus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As a victor and Career Tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, he presumably managed to kill many tributes in his original Hunger Games. In the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, he killed the <a href="/wiki/District_9_female_(75th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 9 female (75th HG)">District 9 female</a> in the bloodbath.<sup id="cite_ref-Twitter_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Twitter-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Toward the end of the Games, he killed <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>, which led to his death<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBrutus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Brutus' name is another reference to Ancient Rome as several Romans in history went by this name, most famously Julius Caesar's assassin, Marcus Junius Brutus the Younger, who often went by the name Brutus alone.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBrutus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Brutus was also the last tribute to die in all the Hunger Games, and therefore the last District 2 tribute to die.</li>
<li>Brutus and Enobaria most likely mentored <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, which could explain their similar qualities.
<ul><li>In the book, Katniss talks about the District 2 tributes, saying "Cato. Clove. Brutus. Enobaria. I've seen their eagerness. I've seen their bloodlust, too"<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li></ul></li>
<li>In the 74th and 75th Hunger Games, both District 2 males (Cato and Brutus) were the last to die, and their last kills were the District 11 males.</li>
<li>Both male volunteers from District 2 in the 74th and 75th Hunger Games were killed by volunteers from District 12. Cato was killed by Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and Brutus was killed by Peeta<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li>
<li>The book states that Brutus killed Chaff and caused Peeta to go on a rampage killing Brutus<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_13-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. In the film, it is shown that Chaff was killed, but Brutus' fate is ambiguous. That being said, a cannon was fired prior to the arena destruction, indicating that he was killed, likely by Peeta.</li></ul>
