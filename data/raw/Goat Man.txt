<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGoat_Man%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Goat Man lived on the other end of the Seam from <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen%27s_house" title="Katniss Everdeen's house">Katniss Everdeen's house</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> at the slag heap next to the east entrance to the mines.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He spent years working as a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a>, and he was considered lucky to save up enough money for his goats.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>'s tenth birthday in late May, the Goat Man had been at the market in the town square that afternoon. Something had mauled the shoulder of <a href="/wiki/Lady" title="Lady">one of his goats</a>, and he'd planned to sell her to <a href="/wiki/Rooba" title="Rooba">Rooba</a> the butcher. However, seeing that <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> had taken an interest in the animal, Rooba backed out. Angry, the Goat Man haggled with Katniss for half an hour over the price. This drew "quite a crowd" of people, who each took sides one way or the other on the animal's worth and the value of the deal. Eventually, Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> came away with the goat, and they presented it to Prim with a pink bow around its neck. Prim named the goat Lady, and she and <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">her mother</a> nursed it back to health.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, Katniss came home from the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> one day after being stuck outside the electrified <a href="/wiki/Fence" title="Fence">fence</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> had come to her house in <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a> looking for her. The excuse she gave for her absence was that she had gone to see the Goat Man about getting Lady pregnant, but she had misheard directions from Prim; supposedly, Katniss had gone to the west entrance of the mine, while Prim had said the east entrance.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGoat_Man%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Goat Man wasn't mentioned among the refugees in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, so he presumably died in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a> along with most of the district's population.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGoat_Man%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Katniss described him as a "filthy and impatient" old man. His joints were swollen, "twisted in painful angles", and he had a "hacking cough" from his time in the mines.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
