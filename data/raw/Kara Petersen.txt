</p><p><b>Kara Petersen</b> is an American stunt actress who was specifically cast to portray the training and fight scenes of a tribute that fell during the initial <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a> and is assigned as the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">District 6 female tribute</a>&#160;in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Like most tribute actors who fell during the bloodbath, she is also a stunt actress.  As a stunt actress, she has her own stunt reel. She is also known for stunts in <i>Jurassic World</i> and <i>The Darkest Minds.</i>
</p>
