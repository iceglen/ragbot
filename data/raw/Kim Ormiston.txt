</p><p><b>Kim Ormiston</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Leeg_2" title="Leeg 2">Leeg 2</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKim_Ormiston%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Kim is a model who mainly does shoots along with her twin sister, <a href="/wiki/Misty_Ormiston" title="Misty Ormiston">Misty Ormiston</a>. One of her most famous shoots was in 2003, where they were featured in an issue of <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxim_(magazine)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Maxim (magazine)">Maxim</a></i>.
Kim has played a few small roles on such TV shows as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treme_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Treme (TV series)">Treme</a></i> and <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2192495/">New Orleans Pie</a></i>. She has also appeared in the TV movie, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scream_of_the_Banshee" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Scream of the Banshee">Scream of the Banshee</a></i>, and also the short movie, <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2149848/combined">Theraphobia</a></i>.
</p>
