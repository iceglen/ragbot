<p><b>Wiress</b> of <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> was the victor of the <a href="/wiki/49th_Hunger_Games" title="49th Hunger Games">49th Hunger Games</a>. Known for her odd demeanor and eccentricities, similar in nature to her fellow victor and district colleague <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, she was relatively well known for her nickname "Nuts". A woman of impressive intellect, Wiress mentored many tributes over the years following her victory including the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tributes in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">2nd Quarter Quell</a>. Despite the trauma she had suffered as a result of her games, and the aftermath of her indirect involvement in Beetee's rebel machinations, Wiress continued to serve the rebellion, joining the victor alliance following her reaping into the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Wiress was born approximately 31 to 37 years after the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a> and the institution of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>. Thanks to her district's primary export of technology, Wiress would have been well-versed in the crafting and engineering of cutting edge tech for <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s benefit. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="49th_Hunger_Games">49th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Sometime during her adolescence, Wiress was reaped into the <a href="/wiki/49th_Hunger_Games" title="49th Hunger Games">49th Hunger Games</a>. After being transported to the Capitol, Wiress and her fellow tributes were kept locked up in the barracks. During training, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> laid out an assortment of clues as to the nature of the arena: foil blankets, metal bowls, and a small round mirror at the fire station.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The arena would prove incredibly disorientating to the tributes, who were forced to wear shimmering tunics, with reflective surfaces placed everywhere. While the others were driven insane by the mind-bending environment, Wiress was able to outwit her competition by navigating the light beams projected by the shiny reflective surfaces. She quickly discovered a rock over a lake that was a blindspot, where she was able to hide for a majority of the games. At the final two tributes, Wiress did not have to fight as her final remaining <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(49th_HG)" title="District 6 male (49th HG)">competitor</a> caused his own death by cracking his skull on the surface she was hiding near and drowning in the lake. Wiress was declared the victor, having outsmarted the Gamemakers.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_6_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During these Games, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> was her mentor.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_8_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Quarter_Quell">Second Quarter Quell</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>One year after her victory, Wiress was assigned as a mentor for the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tributes reaped into the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">2nd Quarter Quell</a> along with <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> victor <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>, as there was no living victor available for mentorship. Wiress took her new role seriously, and told the four tributes <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>, and Capitol replicant <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a> (a hijacked young girl brought in to replace deceased tribute <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>) of her experiences during her own games, hoping to aid them in their strategy. <sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_6_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Although Wiress and Mags were not directly involved in Beetee and his son <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a>'s plans to sabotage the arena, they were tortured all the same due to their perceived roles. Following this torture, Wiress suffered a severe mental break that she would never fully recover from.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_25_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_25-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the years following, Wiress and Beetee would grow close. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Third_Quarter_Quell">Third Quarter Quell</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>As one of the few living District 3 victors , Wiress was once again reaped for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">3rd Quarter Quell</a> along with Beetee. During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>, Wiress and Beetee were dressed with studded electrical lights, signifying District 3's export, as was customary.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During this year's training sessions, Wiress and Beetee were approached at the fire-making station by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, who had been prompted by her Haymitch to seek out allies for the games. After Wiress identified a "chink" in the "armor" if the invisible force field surrounding the Gamemaker's balcony, an impressed Katniss expressed her desire to enlist both District 3 victors into her alliance. Although most of the other victors took Wiress and Beetee less than seriously, Haymitch would honor Everdeen's request and begin collaborating with the eccentric pair.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Wiress survived the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>, and she and Beetee managed to escape with the help of <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> and <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a>, where they headed into the one to two o' clock section of the arena. During the first night of the games, Wiress was able to surmise rather quickly the clock-like design of the arena, due to the twelve bell tolls, and attempted to communicate this with her allies. However, Johanna dismissed her theory as her acting crazy. As the disasters began, they were caught in a blood rain torrent, and Blight was killed after he ran headfirst into the force field.
</p><p>They eventually made it back to the beach, and stumbled upon Katniss, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>. Katniss treated and cared for her and Beetee, washing the blood off their worn and damaged bodies. Wiress continued to try to communicate, uttering "Tick, tock" over and over again. Eventually, Katniss was able to figure out her meaning, concluding the arena's design, and causing Wiress relief.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Wiress was described to be shy by Katniss, who mentions that in the training room, she seldom completed her sentences and Beetee had to finish them for her<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. When she, along with Johanna and Beetee, team up with Finnick, Katniss, and Peeta, she could only recite comments and songs about clocks - referring to the clock shaped arena<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Beetee said that Wiress was not only smart, but intuitive<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She was an intellectual genius, inventive, and clever. As shown where for both the <a href="/wiki/49th_Hunger_Games" title="49th Hunger Games">49th Hunger Games</a> and the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, she was the first to successfully figure out the mechanisms and tricks of the <a href="/wiki/Nest_of_Mirrors" title="Nest of Mirrors">two</a> <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">arenas</a> in order to use them to her advantage.
</p>

<p>Beetee and Wiress were shown to be close friends. They were allies in the 75th Hunger Games and both part of the <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>. He was also her mentor during her first Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_8_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Beetee often finished Wiress' sentences, suggesting then he was used to working with her.
</p><p>After Wiress dies, Katniss says that Beetee was with her in his thoughts. Also, in the movie, Beetee seems to be saddened by her death.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Haymitch_Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<blockquote><p><i>First avoid the slaughter,</i>
</p><p><i>Get weapons, look for water.</i>
</p><p><i>Find food and where to sleep,</i>
</p>
<p><i>Fire and friends can keep.</i></p></blockquote>
<p>She was assigned as his mentor for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> next to <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>. Abernathy at first didn’t trust her, as she hadn’t fought for her win in the previous games and hadn’t yet mentored anyone. Eventually, he grew to be fond of her, giving him the idea to break the arena like a machine and giving him a little song that he used to figure out his next steps during the games.
</p><p>Years later, Wiress and Abernathy remained close and were a part of a rebel plot to break <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> out of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. It is unknown of Haymitch reacted to Wiress's death.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Katniss_Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>During training, Katniss, Wiress and Beetee were talking together at the fire-making station where Wiress points out a force field, which helps them later in the Games<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Katniss later realizes that Wiress' "tick tock," actually hints that the arena is a clock with 12 sections that each hold a new disaster that repeats<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She begins to understand Wiress, which no one but Beetee has accomplished. When Wiress' throat is slit open by Gloss, Katniss closes her eyes and says goodbye<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, Wiress, like Beetee, is described as having ashen skin and black hair.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Her name includes the word "wire", which is appropriate as she is from the technology district. The suffix "ess" can represent her being a woman.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWiress%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Wiress was one of a few characters to be parodied in the Sesame Street sketch "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://youtube.com/watch?v=eT7nD02Im5E">the Hungry Games: Catching Fur</a>". In the sketch she is referred to as the "Tick Tock Lady". She, along with her allies Cookieness, Pita, and Finnicky win the Hungry Games.</li>
<li>Johanna calls her and Beetee "Nuts &amp; Volts"<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. </li>
<li>Wiress is the only one applauding Katniss after she shoots arrows during training, while the other tributes were surprised<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. </li>
<li>In the novels, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, Wiress and the <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (74th HG)">District 3 male</a> were all described by Katniss to have ashen skin. This was probably a characteristic of the district's people.</li>
<li><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i> implies that Wiress' state in <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i> is a result of the torture that she suffered after the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.</li>
<li>In the European Portuguese translation of <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, she's nicknamed "Ampere" instead of "Nuts."</li></ul>
