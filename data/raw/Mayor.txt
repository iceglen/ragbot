<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>A <b>mayor</b> was a citizen, presumably appointed by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, who ran their <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>'s local government.&#160;This position afforded them a degree of wealth and privilege unavailable to most district citizens.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tasks">Tasks</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The mayor's role in local government amounted to carrying out the will of the Capitol within their district. They had no apparent hand in policymaking, but they could put pressure on the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> to enforce laws more harshly, as was the case with the mayor of <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>. <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> said he was "very strict" about publicly whipping people who ate the crops they grew for the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The mayor's other known duties were largely ceremonial.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Reapings">Reapings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">Reaping</a></i></center></div>
<p>Each district's mayor was obligated to host the annual <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. First, the mayor gave a scripted speech about the disasters and wars that led to the creation of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, leading into a recollection of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. Then they read a list of the district's past <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> before introducing the <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>, who would conduct the actual reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Once the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were chosen and called onto the stage, the mayor concluded the event by reading aloud the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a> and having the tributes shake hands.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the first ten years of the Games, the escort role did not yet exist, so mayors selected and read out the tributes' names themselves. That made it simple for a mayor to rig the results.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other">Other</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>In <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, the mayor awarded medals to the families of <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miners</a> who were killed in mining accidents.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Mayors greeted new victors who stopped in their district on the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Status">Status</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mayors and their families enjoyed a number of privileges. For example, they had access to classified information not available to the rest of the district population, such as television updates on the <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">uprising of District 8</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Capitol newspapers.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Mayors were also considerably wealthy compared to other district citizens. They could feed their families and still have money to spare, so although their children were not exempt from the reaping, they would never have to sign up for <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a>, so it was less likely that their names would be picked.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Lipp" title="Mayor Lipp">Mayor Lipp</a> was wealthy enough to afford a car,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which was uncommon in District 12;<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> however, even then, he owned an outdated model by Capitol standards.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Undersee" title="Mayor Undersee">Mayor Undersee</a> was also able to afford <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> to treat <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Undersee" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Undersee">his wife</a>'s debilitating headaches.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite their advantages, mayors were subject to the same travel rules as ordinary district citizens. They were not allowed to go to the Capitol unless invited, so Mrs. Undersee could not receive treatment there.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, when the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a> took place, Mayor Undersee, his wife, and daughter <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge</a> were left to die with the rest of District 12 instead of being evacuated to the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

He was able to mobilize their forces to search people's homes for firearms that could be traced back to <a href="/wiki/Mayfair_Lipp" title="Mayfair Lipp">his daughter</a>'s killer.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
