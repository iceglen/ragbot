<p><b>Casca Highbottom</b> was the Dean of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> and the unintentional creator of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. When the creation of the games was officially announced, Highbottom served as the public face of the event, beginning a years-long downward spiral that would end with his assassination by future president <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCasca_Highbottom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="University_days">University days</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCasca_Highbottom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>The evilest impulse, cleverly packaged into a sporting event. An entertainment. I was drunk, and your father got me drunker still, playing on my vanity as I fleshed the thing out, assuring me it was just a private joke.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Casca Highbottom, describing the creation of the Hunger Games to Coriolanus Snow<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>In his youth, Highbottom attended the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a> with <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a>, the father of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>. They were known to be close, and often drunk in <a href="/wiki/Pluribus_Bell" title="Pluribus Bell">Pluribus Bell</a>'s nightclub every night.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> While studying under Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, whom he loathed, Highbottom came close to failing her class. For the class's final project - which saw him paired up with Snow - the students were challenged to create a punishment for one's enemies so extreme that they would never be allowed to forget how they had wronged them. This suited Highbottom, as he had always excelled at puzzles, and in a drunken stupor he conceived what would become the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. In the process, Snow got him even drunker, playing on his vanity as he fleshed out the project, all the while assuring him that it was just a private joke. The next morning, Highbottom awoke, horrified to find that Snow had turned in the project to get a good grade. He never forgave Snow for this betrayal.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-war">Post-war</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCasca_Highbottom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After <a href="/wiki/The_Dark_Days" class="mw-redirect" title="The Dark Days">The Dark Days</a>, Dr. Gaul pulled out Highbottom's Hunger Games idea and publicly credited Highbottom as its creator, introducing him to all of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> as the architect of the event. That night, he took <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> for the first time, which gradually led to heavy dependence on the drug. He thought that the Hunger Games would eventually die out, due to their ridiculous and horrific nature, but Dr. Gaul took the idea and ran with it. Highbottom even developed a grudge against <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> for his father's actions, maintaining a dislike of the boy throughout his years at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCasca_Highbottom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Casca Highbottom personally oversaw the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> program, presenting himself to the students and announcing who had been assigned to which tribute.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> By this point, he was often heavily dosed up on morphling, causing Coriolanus Snow to mock him by creating the nickname "High-as-a-Kite Bottom," which quickly spread throughout the campus of the Academy. Snow initially suspected this nickname to be Highbottom's reason for hating him, supposing the Dean had caught wind of it (this later proved incorrect, however, as the grudge Highbottom held against Snow's father was made clear). At the time, there were also rumors circulating that, due to his declining health and addiction to morphling, the board of the Academy was losing patience with him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the buffet after <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a>, Highbottom handed Coriolanus Snow a large slice of pie and focused on him. He asked what Coriolanus's plans would be after the Games, reminding him of his family's poor financial situation and laughing at the mentor's discomfort.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Later, when <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> asked Coriolanus about his actions in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>, the dean asked him what he thought the purpose of the Hunger Games was and told Dr. Gaul that it had been a failed experiment. Highbottom then gave Coriolanus a demerit for engaging in reckless behavior.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Highbottom was questioned by Dr. Gaul after <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> had spoken out rebelliously in the classroom, he fiddled with a pen and disagreed that their goal should be to make the audience feel passionately about the tributes, saying his idea had all been theoretical.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>'s funeral, Highbottom gave his condolences to Coriolanus and admitted that he found the funeral excessive and in poor taste. Coriolanus blamed his loss of self-control on the dean, wanting him to be locked up or deported to a faraway outpost, although he didn't say it aloud.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Highbottom made television appearances for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, alongside <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a>. He seemed largely bored and disinterested with the proceedings, proving an uncharismatic companion for the weatherman. He was also not amused by Flickerman's magic tricks, or his antics with his pet bird.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> At five o'clock on the first day, Highbottom dismissed the student body and later the mentors, suggesting bringing a toothbrush and a change of clothes in the future.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> entered the arena, Highbottom observed him and, along with Dr. Gaul, sent Coriolanus into the arena to rescue his friend.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The two adults debated audio levels and watched both versions of the feed, the real one and the artificially darkened one that was visible to normal viewers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="After_the_10th_Hunger_Games">After the 10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCasca_Highbottom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Do you hear that, Coriolanus? It's the sound of Snow falling.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Casca Highbottom, taunting Coriolanus Snow upon expelling him from the Academy<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Highbottom was instrumental in blackmailing Snow and forcing him to enlist as a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a>: He presented evidence of Snow illegally assisting <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">arena</a>, cheating to ensure she would be safe from Dr. Gaul's <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> and helping her poison others during the Games. He even appeared to take some level of pleasure in having Snow sent away, continuing to punish the boy for his father's actions.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before Sejanus joined the Peacekeepers himself, he and <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a> convinced the Academy to let both boys graduate. However, Highbottom brought up that Coriolanus had broke a rule in the Games to help Lucy Gray and technically had not graduated secondary school, and proposed he should be denied a diploma, but he was outvoted.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCasca_Highbottom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Snow eventually returned to the Capitol, where Highbottom returned his mother's compact and commented on his conscientiousness after Snow removed Sejanus Plinth's diploma from its frame. During this meeting, he finally divulged his reasons for hating the boy. This, however, would prove to be the his undoing: Thoroughly tired of Highbottom's obstructive actions, as well as the Dean's opposition and hatred of him, Snow slipped some poison into Highbottom's <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a>. The Dean cluelessly took it and died shortly after, becoming the very first victim of the future president's signature murder method.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCasca_Highbottom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Though he once possessed a fine physique, Casca Highbottom later became shrunken and draped with sagging skin. Doped up on <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a>, he had the appearance of a sleepwalker, with dreamy eyes, a close-cropped haircut and a crisp suit. Casca Highbottom also wore glasses.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCasca_Highbottom%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>It wasn't mentioned if Highbottom was the one who created the concept of and scenarios for the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quells</a>. Given his disgust with his own creation, it's possible that these were created years later, perhaps even around the time when the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a> occurred.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, Highbottom sitting down by the mentors at the reaping was adlibbed by <a href="/wiki/Peter_Dinklage" title="Peter Dinklage">Peter Dinklage</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
