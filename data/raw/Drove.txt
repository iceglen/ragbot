The District 10 male's film debut was in the Tribute Parade, dressed in golden cowboy attire alongside his partner, symbolizing District 10's livestock industry.

Later, once the chariots were parked, they could be seen talking to each other behind the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> chariot.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrove%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">training</a> in the film, Drove was shown practicing with a staff against a trainer, dodging and hitting each other's <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> as Katniss watched, impressed. After <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> threw a heavy weight, he and the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a> were in the background looking doubtfully at the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>. He was also briefly seen wrestling with the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 6 male (74th HG)">District 6 male</a> while the <a href="/wiki/District_7_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 7 male (74th HG)">District 7 male</a> threw a spear with the help of a trainer.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a> fell and hurt his leg during training, and was later sent into the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> with a cast on his leg.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This was likely a nod to Drove's disability in the book.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrove%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>He was described as being "very quiet" when he was <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviewed</a> by <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, he wore a gray suit with black pants and gray shoes as he waited in line backstage, looking bored.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrove%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 174px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c5/10mgydb.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140607155822" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Drove getting a duffel bag." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="174" height="200" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="10mgydb.jpg" data-image-key="10mgydb.jpg" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c5/10mgydb.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/174?cb=20140607155822"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c5/10mgydb.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140607155822" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Drove getting a duffel bag." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c5/10mgydb.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/174?cb=20140607155822" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="174" height="200" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="10mgydb.jpg" data-image-key="10mgydb.jpg" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c5/10mgydb.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/174?cb=20140607155822"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:10mgydb.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Drove getting a duffel bag.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Drove survived the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, like majority of the tributes, he ran to the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> for supplies. He grabbed a yellow sleeping bag, but the District 7 male and the District 6 male charged at him in opposite directions. He quickly pushed the District 7 male back and elbowed the District 6 male in the chest. The <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">District 6 female</a> stole the bag from his hands, while <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> came from behind to pull her hair, and shove her away from him. After Glimmer killed the District 6 female, Drove collided with her and took the bag away from her. Glimmer pinned him to a crate to stab him, but he was too quick for her. <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> confronted him and tried to slash him with an axe, but he successfully dodged. At some point in the bloodbath, he and the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a> tried to kill <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> while the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 6 male (74th HG)">District 6 male</a> watched from behind a crate. Katniss later spotted him running in the forest with his bag, and she ran the opposite direction.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrove%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cf/640px-D4_female_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest?cb=20140516182942" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A corpse at the bloodbath played by the same stuntman." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="640px-D4 female killed by Cato.png" data-image-key="640px-D4_female_killed_by_Cato.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cf/640px-D4_female_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140516182942"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cf/640px-D4_female_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest?cb=20140516182942" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A corpse at the bloodbath played by the same stuntman." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cf/640px-D4_female_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140516182942" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="640px-D4 female killed by Cato.png" data-image-key="640px-D4_female_killed_by_Cato.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cf/640px-D4_female_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140516182942"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:640px-D4_female_killed_by_Cato.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A corpse at the bloodbath played by the same stuntman.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The cannon signaling his death, likely at the hands of the Careers, woke Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> in the morning on day eight.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss didn't realize who it had been until she saw his picture during that evening's <a href="/wiki/Horn_of_Plenty" title="Horn of Plenty">anthem</a> recap. He placed 10th out of 24.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Rue later recounted his death as occurring after Glimmer's, at some point in the several days that Katniss slept after being stung by <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jackers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The stuntman who played this character also played an extra death there in the film, presenting a continuity error. He was also the second shown to be injured by another tribute during the initial bloodbath, after the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a>. Based on the arena wear and the angle of the shots, he might have doubled his district partner.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrove%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the film, the tribute boards showed that he was 18, weighing 165 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>He, Cato, and <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> were the oldest and largest tributes in the 74th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>He, <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a>, Rue, and Thresh were the only tributes alive when Katniss dropped the tracker jacker nest who were not known to have gotten stung.</li>
<li>He and the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 10 male (75th HG)">District 10 male</a> from the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> are similar because they both died after a <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">District 1 female</a> tribute was killed. They also both outlived their district partners, then later died to an unknown cause.</li></ul>
