</p><p><b>Samuel Tan</b> is an American stunt actor specifically cast to portray a Tribute that is mentioned only once and fell during the initial bloodbath. He portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He also appeared in the "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocketeer" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rocketeer">Rocketeer</a>" music video by the band <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_East_Movement" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Far East Movement">Far East Movement</a>. Tan is known for his work on <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mano_Po" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Mano Po">Mano Po</a></i> (2002) and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mano_Po_6:_A_Mother%27s_Love" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Mano Po 6: A Mother&#39;s Love">Mano Po 6: A Mother's Love</a></i> (2009). Of late, Sam hasn't been very active, but he is still good friends with the actor who played the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 6 male (74th HG)">District 6 male</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ashton_Moio" title="Ashton Moio">Ashton Moio</a>.
</p>
