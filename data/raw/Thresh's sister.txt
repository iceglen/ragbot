<p><b>Thresh's sister</b> is a citizen of <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>. She was presumably raised by her <a href="/wiki/Thresh%27s_grandmother" title="Thresh&#39;s grandmother">grandmother</a> along with her brother, <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThresh%2527s_sister%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was only seen during the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, standing at a special platform constructed at the bottom of the stage.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was certain that she was shot, after hearing gunshots that she knew killed an <a href="/wiki/Unidentified_District_11_citizen" title="Unidentified District 11 citizen">old man</a>, but was proven wrong by <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a>, as when Katniss asked Seeder before the start of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, she answered that they were alive. Her current fate is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
