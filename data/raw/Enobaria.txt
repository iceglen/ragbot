<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="62nd_Hunger_Games">62nd Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>According to <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, she <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> for, participated in, and became the victor of the 62nd Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During her Games, she became famous for ripping another tribute's throat out with her bare teeth. She later altered her teeth into sharp points, like fangs, and had them inlaid with gold, likely to keep the story of her victory present everywhere she went.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Third_Quarter_Quell">Third Quarter Quell</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After getting selected for the 75th Hunger Games, Enobaria showed extreme enthusiasm and an apparent bloodlust. She bared her fangs and raised her fist in the air. She wore a gold outfit and a matching headband.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bloodbath">Bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Enobaria was located on the pedestal next to <a href="/wiki/Woof" title="Woof">Woof</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> At the start of the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a>, Enobaria made it to the Cornucopia after Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> had their pick in weapons. When Katniss shot an arrow at her, she was able to dodge it by diving back into the water before it could hit her. The <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute#75th_Hunger_Games_Career_Pack" title="Career Tribute">Career alliance</a>, formed by Enobaria, <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a>, stayed at the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the bloodbath she killed <a href="/wiki/Cecelia" title="Cecelia">Cecelia</a> and possibly more.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Throughout the Games, she and the other Careers stalked Katniss, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>, <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a>, <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 female (75th HG)">female morphling</a>, as they regulated through the alliance of the rebellion they all wanted.
</p><p>Katniss' alliance made its way to the Cornucopia to get a figure out the clock mechanism of the arena. The Careers attacked, leading to the deaths of Wiress, Cashmere, and Gloss. Enobaria attempted to stab Peeta with her sword, but Finnick stepped in the way and received the blow to his leg before she and Brutus left,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> then stalked the group again until Beetee set his plan into action. Together, Enobaria and Brutus chased Johanna and Katniss across the island, until Johanna dug out Katniss's <a href="/wiki/Tracker" title="Tracker">tracker</a> and made her appear dead so the two would pass her by.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> At an unknown point in time, they split up. Brutus found his way to Chaff and Peeta and fought them. In the battle, Brutus killed Chaff, but Peeta killed Brutus,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> leaving Enobaria and Finnick as the only remaining Career Tribute.
</p><p>Enobaria fought her way to Beetee and managed to stab him so he could not complete his mission of destroying the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">arena</a>. She was chased off by Finnick while Katniss figured out Beetee's intentions. By the time Enobaria and Finnick had made it back to Katniss and Beetee, Katniss had destroyed the arena and called a stop to the Games, leaving only six tributes alive.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was one of the three tributes picked up by the Capitol <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p><a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> was able to rescue Beetee, Finnick, and Katniss. However, Enobaria, Peeta, and Johanna were apprehended by the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> While Johanna was tortured with electroshock tactics<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and Peeta was being tortured by <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Boggs speculated that the Capitol let Enobaria go home as they were unable to retrieve her in the <a href="/wiki/Rescue_mission" class="mw-redirect" title="Rescue mission">rescue mission</a> that brought the others to District 13.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When District 2 was conquered<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> had shown them that they were needed in the rebellion, Enobaria joined the rebels. Enobaria's life was spared by the rebels because of the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_Deal" title="Mockingjay Deal">Mockingjay Deal</a> that Katniss had <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">President Coin</a> agree to, since Katniss felt that was only fair,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> though she believed they would not torture her as District 2 was still on the Capitol's side at that point.
</p><p>After the war was finished and the execution of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> was all that was left on the agenda, the only remaining victors were called to discuss having a final Hunger Games that only Capitol children would be part of. Enobaria, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a>, Katniss, Peeta, and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch">Haymitch</a> were the only surviving victors.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Only after President Snow was captured did she finally join the rebels. Whether the <a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">76th Hunger Games</a> were to occur would be decided by a vote of the remaining victors. Enobaria, Johanna, Katniss, and Haymitch all said "yes" to another Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is likely Enobaria went back to living her life in District 2 after President Snow's execution.
</p><p>In the film adaptation, Enobaria is first seen when Katniss enters the room where the remaining victors have gathered. President Coin proposes a final Hunger Games involving the children of those from the Capitol, and after Johanna votes in favor of one final Games, so does Enobaria, saying they should have a taste of what it involves.
</p><p>She is seen the next day when the remaining victors gather with President Coin at Snow's execution. She is standing between Beetee and Annie and watches as Katniss approaches Snow<sup id="cite_ref-MJ-2_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ-2-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Enobaria is not seen again, but presumably returns to District 2 to live out the rest of her life.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Like most Career tributes, Enobaria is strong, aggressive, and bloodthirsty (quite literally as she tore open her final opponent's throat with her teeth in her first Games<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>). She is mentioned when Katniss describes the District 2 tributes, saying, "Cato. Clove. Brutus. Enobaria. I've seen their eagerness. I've seen their bloodlust, too."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The people of the Capitol seemed to like her, as she was not tortured like Peeta Mellark and Johanna Mason following the 75th Hunger Games. She was even thought to have been freed as she seemed like the kind of person who would be on the Capitol's side.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, her approval to have a final Hunger Games with Capitol children, to give them "a taste of their own medicine", indicates that her vicious nature may have manifested from hatred of the Capitol and a sense of desired revenge.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Also, despite her viciousness, there is no indication that she considered the Hunger Games to be a competition of honor, unlike many other tributes from Career districts. She showed a soft spot when she joined hands with Katniss and the other tributes before they were forced to compete in the games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As a victor from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, she likely killed a considerable amount of tributes in the 62nd and 75th Hunger Games.
</p>
<ul><li>In the 62nd Hunger Games, she killed a male tribute by ripping out his throat.</li>
<li>In the 75th Hunger Games, she killed Cecelia with a sword in her back.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Enobaria's most distinctive feature is her teeth, which have been cosmetically altered so each tooth ends in a sharp point, and are inlaid with gold. She is physically strong due to the training she received in District 2 and is most likely in her early 30s or late 20s.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the films, she is portrayed with light brown skin and long brown hair, which is usually tied back in a ponytail.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Enobaria is the feminine form of Enobarbus, who was a character in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shakespeare" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:William Shakespeare">William Shakespeare</a>'s play <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_and_Cleopatra" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Antony and Cleopatra">Antony and Cleopatra</a></i>, based on the real Ancient Roman general and politician <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnaeus_Domitius_Ahenobarbus_(consul_32_BC)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Gnaeus Domitius Ahenobarbus (consul 32 BC)">Ahenobarbus</a>. Notably, he was pardoned by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Caesar" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Julius Caesar">Julius Caesar</a>, similar to how Enobaria was presumably freed by the Capitol and then protected under the Mockingjay Deal.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEnobaria%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, Katniss notes that Enobaria had "no shortage of admirers" in the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Due to this statement, some fans theorize that Enobaria was sex trafficked by the Capitol like Finnick, Conversely, some fans speculate that while she had her teeth filed into fangs to highlight her iconic arena kill, she also may have had done so to make herself look more unappealing and therefore avoid being trafficked.</li></ul></li>
<li>Enobaria is the only living <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a> who participated in the 75th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>Enobaria is the only living victor at the end of <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> to have their last name unreleased, as Beetee's last name was revealed during the film version of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.</li>
<li>Unlike in the novel, in the film <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1"><i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i></a>, Enobaria is not among the people for whom Katniss demands a full pardon. She appears in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2"><i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i></a>; however, she is not confirmed to have been a part of the Rebellion and only appears twice in the end.</li>
<li>Enobaria and <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> are the main tribute antagonists of <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>.</li>
<li>She is one of the six tributes that survived the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> and that also survived two Hunger Games along with <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>.
<ul><li>She is also one of the seven victors to be survived the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a> along with <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
