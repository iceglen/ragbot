</p><p>The <b>63rd Hunger Games</b> took place eleven years prior to the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a>, the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>. He and his sister <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> won in consecutive years.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>

<p>He likely formed a typical <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> with other <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>, since he was from one of the Career districts and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> described him as a "classic Career".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F63rd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, Cashmere and Gloss were both adept with <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Throwing_knife" title="Weapons">throwing knives</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so that may have been his weapon of choice during his first Games.</li>
<li>According to <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, Gloss's sister Cashmere won the <a href="/wiki/64th_Hunger_Games" title="64th Hunger Games">64th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Since <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> won the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a> the following year,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Gloss had to have won the 63rd in order for the siblings' wins to be consecutive.</li></ul>
