</p><p><b>Kieran Culkin</b> is an American actor set to play <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKieran_Culkin%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Culkin made his feature film debut alongside his brother Macaulay in <i>Home Alone</i> (1990). His breakthrough role was in <i>Igby Goes Down</i> (2002), for which he received his first Golden Globe nomination.
</p><p>He went on to play Wallace Wells in <i>Scott Pilgrim vs. The World</i> (2010), and Roman Roy in the HBO series <i>Succession</i> (2018-2023). The latter earned him two Critics' Choice Awards, four Screen Actors Guild Awards, one Emmy Award, and one Golden Globe. He received further accolades for the Jesse Eisenberg picture <i>A Real Pain</i> (2024), which included a BAFTA Award, a second Golden Globe, and his first Academy Award.
</p><p>When told that he would make an "amazing" Caesar Flickerman, Culkin responded: "I don't think so, lower those expectations, please. Please do. I have no idea what I'm gonna do with that."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
