<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Purpose">Purpose</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBaker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bakers sold their wares to other people in the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> for regular consumption. However, people in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s poorest areas were often unable to afford to buy baked goods, so they made their own from <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tessera</a> grain.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Specialty items like cakes could be purchased for special occasions, if one could afford them. Bakers were also aware of, and may have also made, different types of bread native to other districts.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite the expense of bakery goods, people could sometimes acquire them if the baker accepted a trade in place of monetary payment, like <a href="/wiki/Mr._Mellark" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Mellark">Mr. Mellark</a> often did with <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
