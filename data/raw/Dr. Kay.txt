<p><b>Dr. Kay</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a scientist assigned to the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a>. <h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDr._Kay%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Dr. Kay was assigned to District 12 during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> and was the head of the original jabberjay project, which failed after the rebels discovered their use. When the birds were freed, she had thought they wouldn't last the winter, as genetically engineered projects often struggled in the wild, but they turned out to be strong.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After watching <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a>, a fellow <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>, get bitten by Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a>, Coriolanus made a wrong turn into the section of the lab where Dr. Kay experimented with genetically altering birds. She intercepted him, scolded him for disturbing the mutts, and sent him back down to the elevator.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Dr. Kay traveled to District 12 again shortly after Coriolanus had been sent to become a Peacekeeper, explaining their mission to the Peacekeepers after being introduced by another scientist. Because she was familiar, Coriolanus slouched down beside one of his bunkmates and attempted to remain unnoticed.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coriolanus wasn't sure if she remembered him until she addressed him directly by name, following after having been impressed by <a href="/wiki/Bug" title="Bug">Bug</a>, Coriolanus's partner during the project. When she asked him what he thought of the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>, he decided to follow a piece of advice given to him by <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> and "own it," saying that he had learned more about Panem in one day as a Peacekeeper than he had in thirteen years of school. She taught Snow how the jabberjays worked and also explained to him about the nature of mockingjays. As he was disturbed by the mockingjays, he told her that he felt that they should kill them all. When he realized that she was, in fact, fond of the birds, he tried to disguise his reaction by saying that he was worried that they might hurt the other species of birds in the forests.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When no mockingjays were caught by the traps, showing that their survival skills had advanced, Dr. Kay seemed pleased by their instincts. She directed the Peacekeepers in setting up mist nets to trap the birds instead, instructing them on making the experience as trauma-free as possible for the birds and removing the first three from the net herself. However, Coriolanus became injured by a mockingjay after handling it roughly, and Dr. Kay cleaned and bandaged his hand herself.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
