</p><p><b>Incitatus Loomy</b> was the parade master for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FIncitatus_Loomy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Incitatus Loomy was responsible for the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a> during the 50th Hunger Games. However, the Parade turned into a disaster as the nervous horses pulling the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> chariot were startled and bolted. They left a trail of wreckage as other chariots were hit by the rogue horses. A tribute, <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a> was killed and the <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> children were injured along with some Capitol spectators. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> turned it into a bigger spectacle when he ran with Louella's dead body to the house of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> in a statement against the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The next day, President Snow met with Incitatus. Later that day, Snow also met up with Haymitch and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> while being violently ill and claimed he had eaten a bad batch of oysters with Incitatus but fared better than him. Plutarch was surprised and when he asked if it was parade master, Snow just responded "<i>was he?</i>" and changed the topic. <sup id="cite_ref-SR9_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR9-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, President Snow had poisoned the oysters to kill Incitatus and also ate it himself to draw him into a false sense of security. Haymitch deduced what had happened from Snow's comments during their conversation.<sup id="cite_ref-SR10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
