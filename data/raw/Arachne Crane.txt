<p><b>Arachne Crane</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She was assigned the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>, <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her family was part of the old guard of the wealthy from the Capitol. They worked in the travel industry, having developed luxury hotels in vacation destinations, though the industry took a hit during and after the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. However, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> thought that it might be picking up based on Arachne's ability to afford fresh grapes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She had an <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane%27s_brother" title="Arachne Crane's brother">older brother</a> who graduated from the Academy the year prior.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the first mentor-tribute meeting, Arachne's tribute, <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a>, told her about how she spent most of her time in <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> butchering hogs. However, Arachne found it repulsive until she realized she could use her knowledge of District 10 to make up something more brilliant than the details of a slaughterhouse.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During <a href="/wiki/Crispus_Demigloss" title="Crispus Demigloss">Crispus Demigloss</a>' class, Arachne, Coriolanus, and <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a> were chosen by their peers to write a proposal about how sponsorship and betting could be implemented in the 10th Hunger Games. She died before being able to contribute to the proposal.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Arachne was killed by her own tribute, knifed in the throat after she taunted Brandy by reaching through her bars in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> to offer a cheese sandwich, only to then withdraw it. Angered, Brandy used the knife that Crane had sliced the cheese for the sandwich with to accomplish the deed.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> As Arachne's face drained of color, she turned towards the audience for help, but only Coriolanus and one of her other classmates, <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a>, went over in an attempt to help her. Arachne was lifted onto a stretcher by medics shortly after she died.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After her death, a schoolwide assembly was held at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, where Professor <a href="/wiki/Satyria_Click" title="Satyria Click">Satyria Click</a> spoke of Arachne in glowing terms and the school counselor <a href="/wiki/Hippocrata_Lunt" title="Hippocrata Lunt">Hippocrata Lunt</a> invited anyone with grief issues to visit her office. For Arachne's funeral, numerous cameras and Capitol TV reporters were positioned. Coriolanus had been asked to sing the anthem at the beginning, and <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a> followed with a speech.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was a boisterous individual and Coriolanus Snow felt that she had always been very loud and was always mocking people. She was cruel and taunted her tribute, which resulted in her death.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Snow found her funeral ridiculous and out of proportion for someone like her, given how many had died heroically in the Dark Days.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Maximinius_Ravinstill"><a href="/wiki/Maximinius_Ravinstill" title="Maximinius Ravinstill">Maximinius Ravinstill</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Two days ago, Arachne Crane's young and precious life was ended, and so we mourn another victim of the criminal rebellion that yet besieges us. Her death was as valiant as any on the battlefield, her loss more profound as we claim to be at peace. But no peace will exist while this disease eats away at all that is good and noble in our country. Today we honor her sacrifice with a reminder that while evil exists, it does not prevail. And once again, we bear witness as our great Capitol brings justice to Panem.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—President Ravinstill's speech at Arachne's funeral.<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Arachne attended the Academy with President Ravinstill's great-nephew, <a href="/wiki/Felix_Ravinstill" title="Felix Ravinstill">Felix</a>. It is unlikely that they knew each other personally, but President Ravinstill spoke about Arachne at her funeral.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Volumnia_Gaul"><a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Arachne Crane, we, your fellow citizens of Panem, vow that your death will not be in vain. When one of ours is hit, we hit back twice as hard. The Hunger Games will go forward, with more energy and commitment than ever before, as we add your name to the long list of the innocent who died defending a righteous and just land. Your friends, family, and fellow citizens salute you and dedicate the Tenth Hunger Games to your memory.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Dr. Gaul's speech at Arachne's funeral.<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Dr. Gaul helped prepare the mentors (including Arachne) for the 10th Hunger Games.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Coriolanus_Snow"><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>She laid down her life taunting her tribute with a sandwich. Maybe her gravestone could read, 'Casualty of cheap laughs.'</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Coriolanus' thoughts during Arachne's funeral.<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Arachne and Coriolanus were classmates and neighbors. It is said that "even from across the Corso, [Coriolanus] could hear [Arachne] bellowing at night."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Arachne was killed, Coriolanus was initially too stunned to help Arachne, but ran over when <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> urged him to. People thought that she and Coriolanus were great friends and, as such, he was tasked with singing the Capitol anthem "<a href="/wiki/Gem_of_Panem" title="Gem of Panem">Gem of Panem</a>" at her funeral.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In fact, the two were not actually very close at all. They had grown up together, attending important events in each other's lives, as was natural for members of two families of money who lived close together, but did not really like each other.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sejanus_Plinth"><a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span>"<i>No one here likes <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">him</a>, but they do love his money. You know what that's like, don't you, Arachne?</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Sejanus in the film.</dd></dl></div>
<p>Arachne and Sejanus were classmates. 
</p><p>In the film, Arachne teases Coriolanus about liking Sejanus. She then asks Sejanus to spill who won the Plinth Prize. Sejanus refuses, and implies that people only like Arachne for her wealth.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Arachne was a mortal maiden and accomplished weaver from Greco-Roman mythology, who in arrogance declared herself more talented than the goddess Athena/Minerva, the patron of said profession. Convinced of her own superiority, she accepted a challenge proposed by the goddess, which she lost, and as punishment was turned into the first spider. In other versions, Arachne originally won, but Athena/Minerva was jealous and killed her, but then, regretting her actions, turned her into the first spider as a gift. The arrogance of Arachne Crane’s namesake is reflected in the character's personality.
</p>
