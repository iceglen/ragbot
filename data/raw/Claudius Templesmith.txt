<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClaudius_Templesmith%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As the announcer of the Hunger Games, Claudius Templesmith's voice was occasionally projected to the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> inside the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. He usually marked the start of the competition,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> issued invitations to <a href="/wiki/Feasts" class="mw-redirect" title="Feasts">feasts</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and generally ended his announcements with the slogan for the Games: "May the odds be ever in your favor."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClaudius_Templesmith%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Ladies and gentlemen, let the Seventy-fourth Hunger Games begin!</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Claudius Templesmith<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div><figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/6f/Claudius_Templesmith_and_Caesar_Flickerman_74th_Hunger_Games_.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20220211160959" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Claudius and Caesar commentate on the tracker jackers." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/6f/Claudius_Templesmith_and_Caesar_Flickerman_74th_Hunger_Games_.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220211160959" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Claudius Templesmith and Caesar Flickerman 74th Hunger Games .jpg" data-image-key="Claudius_Templesmith_and_Caesar_Flickerman_74th_Hunger_Games_.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Claudius_Templesmith_and_Caesar_Flickerman_74th_Hunger_Games_.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Claudius and Caesar commentate on the tracker jackers.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">first film</a>, Claudius served as a Hunger Games commentator alongside <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>. En route to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> watched a broadcast of the pair discussing a <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">previous Games</a>. Later, he and Caesar reacted to the tributes' <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">costumes</a> as they arrived on their <a href="/wiki/Chariots" class="mw-redirect" title="Chariots">chariots</a>, and the two also reacted to <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> pointing out the <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nest.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Announcements">Announcements</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClaudius_Templesmith%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Just before the sixty-second countdown, Claudius announced the beginning of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, this announcement was absent from the film.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the tenth day of the Games, after the nightly playing of the <a href="/wiki/Horn_of_Plenty" title="Horn of Plenty">anthem</a>, Claudius announced a rule change. Under the new rule, two tributes from the same <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> could both be declared the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> if they were the last people standing.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the twelfth day, Claudius invited the six remaining tributes to a <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">feast</a>. Rather than <a href="/wiki/Food_and_drink" title="Food and drink">food</a>, they were promised something they each needed "desperately". These items would be placed into <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpacks</a> marked with their district numbers, and tributes could claim their prizes at the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> at dawn the next day, if they were prepared to fight for it.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the eighteenth day, after <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>'s death, Claudius announced that the earlier revision to the rules had been revoked, so only one winner would be allowed. Rather than turning against each other, Katniss Everdeen and her <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a> <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> threatened suicide by <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries. A second, more frantic announcement from Claudius quickly followed, declaring them both victors.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClaudius_Templesmith%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3><p>
In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, Claudius only made one announcement to mark the start of the 75th Hunger Games, the same way he had the year previous: "Ladies and gentlemen, let the Seventy-fifth Hunger Games begin!"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2c/HG-FRance.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140121195448" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Caesar and Claudius at the ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2c/HG-FRance.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140121195448" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="HG-FRance.jpg" data-image-key="HG-FRance.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:HG-FRance.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Caesar and Claudius at the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">film adaptation</a>, Claudius returned to commentate over the Tribute Parade alongside Caesar. He was once again very surprised by Katniss and Peeta's fiery costumes, chuckling to Caesar and saying jokingly, "Whoa, fire in the house!"<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FClaudius_Templesmith%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>Katniss Everdeen, the girl who was on fire, burns on.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Claudius<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>

After seeing the propo for the first time, Katniss asked if Claudius was on their side, and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> explained that the line was pulled, unedited, directly from Claudius's commentary during the 74th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
