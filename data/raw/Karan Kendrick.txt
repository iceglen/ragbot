</p><p><b>Karan Kendrick</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Atala" title="Atala">Atala</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKaran_Kendrick%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Karan has acted in many movies and television shows. Some of her most famous film work includes <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parental_Guidance_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Parental Guidance (film)">Parental Guidance</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Odd_Life_of_Timothy_Green" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Odd Life of Timothy Green">The Odd Life of Timothy Green</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Red_Wagon" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Little Red Wagon">Little Red Wagon</a></i> (2012).
