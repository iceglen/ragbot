<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommander_Hoff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was an elderly man described as an "old goat." He observed <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> during Peacekeeper drills and, impressed with their performance, ordered them to attend the hanging of <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a> because he wanted more bodies there for the show. During the hanging, he emerged from a car that had a flag of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> on the hood with six other officers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Hoff spoke with Snow following Sejanus Plinth's execution for treason. Snow thought that the commander had discovered his killing of <a href="/wiki/Mayfair_Lipp" title="Mayfair Lipp">Mayfair Lipp</a> and was about to have him executed as well. Hoff, however, knew nothing of this and instead had called him in because Snow had sent a <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjay</a> to <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, containing a recording which revealed Plinth's betrayal. He comforted Snow, saying that it couldn't have been easy to inform on his friend, and briefly discussed whether or not Sejanus would have been involved with the murders. He then informed Snow that it was his birthday and bid him to join the party. 
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/28/Commander_hoff_promo_still_9.28.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20230928190510" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Promotional still of Commander Hoff." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/28/Commander_hoff_promo_still_9.28.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230928190510" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="112" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Commander hoff promo still 9.28.jpg" data-image-key="Commander_hoff_promo_still_9.28.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Commander_hoff_promo_still_9.28.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Promotional still of Commander Hoff.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The celebration was held in the Peacekeeper mess hall, with a generous supply of beer and whiskey, an enormous frosted cake, and a musical performance by the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>. The next day, Snow was again summoned to Hoff's office. He was once again convinced that he was being brought to his execution, but Hoff instead congratulated him, telling him that he had passed the officer candidate test and would soon leave for <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> in a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> to attend an elite training program.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ultimately, Snow was sent back to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> instead of District 2 and began studies at <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a>, having been granted an honorable discharge from the Peacekeepers by the manipulative Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>. At some point not long after Snow returned, he learned that Hoff had been replaced as the commander in District 12 with somebody new whose first move had been to outlaw shows like those performed by the Covey at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>, because music caused trouble.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
