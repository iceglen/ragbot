<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterview_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tributes">Tributes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterview_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Stylists had not yet been brought in for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, but there was talk of giving the tributes "some grooming for the cameras."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, after the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol Arena bombing">Capitol Arena bombing</a> killed or injured many tributes and their <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a>, it was decided that interviews would be done only on a voluntary basis.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Participating tributes were not given any changes of clothes, so they all wore the same dirty items of clothing they'd had on since the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Among the tributes, the only exception was <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>. <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>, the cousin of her mentor <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, offered up an old dress of hers for Lucy Gray to wear while she washed and ironed Lucy Gray's rainbow dress. It was returned in time for the interviews. Tigris had starched its colorful ruffles, and she also sent a pot of blush for Lucy Gray's lips and cheeks. Coriolanus put a hot pink rosebud in Lucy Gray's hair, which was piled on top of her head.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Capitol_citizens">Capitol citizens</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterview_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Coriolanus wore his <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> uniform, and he had a rosebud on his lapel matching the one in Lucy Gray's hair.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius "Lucky" Flickerman</a>, who hosted the interviews, wore "a high-collared blue suit with rhinestone accents", and he'd applied gel and a coppery powder to his hair.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, Lucky wore a dark gray plaid suit with a silver tie during interviews.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p><a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> hosted the Games for more than 40 years. Every year, he wore the same pure white makeup, the same hairstyle, and the same "ceremonial" midnight blue suit, "dotted with a thousand tiny electric bulbs that twinkle[d] like stars." The only thing that changed annually was the color of his hair, and his eyelids and lips were done up to match.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p>In his first year as a stylist, <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> dressed <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> in a dress studded with red, yellow, white, and blue gems in a flame design. These gems made the dress so heavy that Katniss estimated it was about forty pounds. Her <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> stenciled patterns on her arms, painted flames on her nails, and wove red strands into her hair, which was braided over her right shoulder. She was also given dark eye makeup, full red lips, and "eyelashes that [threw] off light" whenever she blinked, then her whole body was covered in glittery golden powder.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a> dressed <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> in a black suit with flame accents in order to complement Katniss. <sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_5-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>The sheer fabric softly glows. […] In this dress, I give the illusion of wearing candlelight. […] The sleeveless dress is gathered at my ribs, not at my waist, largely eliminating any help the padding would have given my figure. The hem falls just to my knees. </i>"</span></dd>

"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The sleeveless dress itself was girlish, with the hem gathered at her ribs to resemble a young girl's. <div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>His shirt is made of the same yellow material as my dress, but Portia’s put him in long black pants. No sandals, either, but a pair of sturdy black boots.</i>"</span></dd>

<p>Peeta wore another outfit matching Katniss's that consisted of a yellow shirt made of the same fabric as Katniss's dress. He wore long black pants with sturdy black boots. <sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the movie, Peeta wore a teal suit with a navy button-up.

<h4><span id="District_12_.2875th_HG.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="District_12_(75th_HG)">District 12 (75th HG)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterview_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>Heavy white silk with a low neckline and tight waist and sleeves that fall from my wrists to the floor. And pearls. Everywhere pearls. Stitched into the dress and in ropes at my throat and forming the crown for the veil.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss describing her wedding dress<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>

<p>Peeta wore an "elegant" tuxedo with white gloves like a Capitol groom, and <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> personally requested that Katniss wear the wedding dress the Capitol audience had voted on. When Cinna helped her into the gown, she immediately noticed that it felt heavier than when she'd tried it on for a photoshoot. He explained this away as having made alterations for the lighting.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>However, when Katniss twirled during her interview, the white silk burned away to reveal an identical dress underneath made of black feathers. When she lifted her sleeves, she realized they had white patches like the wings of a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a>: a symbol of rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInterview_outfits%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>For <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, Katniss's wedding dress was designed by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tex_Saverio" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tex Saverio">Tex Saverio</a>, and Peeta's suit was by designer Juun J.<sup id="cite_ref-" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>In an interview with <i>The Hollywood Reporter</i>, costume designer <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trish_Summerville" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Trish Summerville">Trish Summerville</a> said that she and Saverio designed the dress together over Skype calls, inspired by another dress he'd designed with a metal bodice. The metal pieces rising up from the bodice symbolized flames. The layers of organza and chiffon ruffles facilitated her twirling for Caesar, and laser-cut feathers hinted at the gown's transformation into the mockingjay dress.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>'s interview dress was from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Taminiau" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jan Taminiau">Jan Taminiau</a>'s 2012 collection, "Nature Extends".</li></ul></li></ul>
