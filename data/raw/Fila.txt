<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade">Tribute Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the film, the District 3 tributes wore silver costumes and silver headdresses to represent their <a href="/wiki/Technology" title="Technology">technology</a> industry during the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training </span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 210px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/9f/2.D3%2B9male.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140321074104" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Fila and the District 9 male light a fire." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/9f/2.D3%2B9male.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/210?cb=20140321074104" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="210" height="95" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="2.D3+9male.jpg" data-image-key="2.D3%2B9male.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:2.D3%2B9male.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Fila and the District 9 male light a fire.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Fila was first seen in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> working with the <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">District 9 male</a> to create a <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a>, and quickly backed away when the fire ignited. He also showed concern when his <a href="/wiki/District_3_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 3 female (74th HG)">district partner</a> was injured in training, but laughed reluctantly when <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> elbowed him, probably to make the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> like him.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, Fila wore a yellow suit with a black shirt under it. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> said that there was nothing special about his interview.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Fila survived the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>. He was stationed to the left of <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>. After arriving at the cornucopia, he grapples over <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>'s axe. When he refused to let go, Marvel shoves his heavy backpack into his face, which causes him to fall to the ground. He is then later seen fighting the <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">District 7 female</a> over a backpack, while <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> is running out of the Cornucopia. He somehow managed to survive and escape into the forest.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>As shown in The Hunger Games film, at the time of the wild fire, Fila was outside the northern part of the Cornucopia. By day eight (although it is presumed to be day five in the book, as he joined when the Careers led the <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jackers</a> to the lake), he had joined the Career pack and booby-trapped the area right around the Cornucopia with land mines he had dug up and then re-armed, which Katniss said in the book that he probably knew due to the fact that he was from District 3 which produces technology. Katniss Everdeen discovered the minefield after observing <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a> steal supplies from the Careers, while <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> distracted the other Careers with several fires. In the film, he was left behind to guard. In the novel, the Careers told him to come with them to look for Rue. 
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/08/District_3_Male_Guarding_Supplies.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120914233307" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Fila at the Careers' camp." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/08/District_3_Male_Guarding_Supplies.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120914233307" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="211" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District 3 Male Guarding Supplies.jpg" data-image-key="District_3_Male_Guarding_Supplies.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District_3_Male_Guarding_Supplies.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Fila at the Careers' camp.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>While Marvel, <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> were hunting for Rue, Foxface stole supplies from the Careers, jumping around the mines in what Katniss described as a sort of "dance". As Foxface ran away behind Fila, he looked around to see who was there and followed Foxface into the woods to take her down. Katniss blew up the Careers' supplies by shooting a suspended bag of apples with her arrows when he left, which in turn set off the mines he planted, destroying everything. After the explosion, Fila came back and saw the blown-up supplies.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>When the Careers returned, Cato, in a fit of rage, confronted Fila and stripped him of his spear, the only means of protection he had, and promptly snapped his neck as Katniss watched from behind cover nearby. He placed 9th out of 24.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the novels, he, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> were all described as having ashen skin. This was probably a general characteristic of District 3's people.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tactics">Tactics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Like most tributes, he participated in the Cornucopia bloodbath and was lucky enough to survive the onslaught. He survived alone close to the Cornucopia until some point after the tracker jackers killed Glimmer and Peeta was kicked out of the Career pack. The surviving Careers found him and allowed him into their pack in exchange for him rewiring the mines around their stash of supplies, which he agreed to. As a tribute of District 3, he was skilled in electronics.
</p><p>
He was also armed with a spear while with the Careers. In the film, when Foxface stole supplies, he followed her in an attempt to kill her, possibly to impress his new companions, but inadvertently left the stash unguarded. <h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFila%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the film, he weighs 120 pounds, same as his district partner. He is 14 years old in the film. His odds of winning were initially 20-1 after training,<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and later 22–1. He was the only tribute to have his odds decrease instead of improving.</li>
<li>When all the tributes are standing on their platforms, he is seen standing next to his district partner.</li>
<li>He was the highest placing unnamed tribute, not including Foxface, as she had a nickname.</li>
<li>In the <i><a href="/wiki/Tribute_Guide" class="mw-redirect" title="Tribute Guide">Tribute Guide</a></i>, his height is said to be 4'8, but when he is seen fighting with the District 7 female, he is taller; his actual height is closer to 5'6", given alongside his betting odds in the film.</li>
<li>In the trailer of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, it can be noted that when Katniss and Peeta visited District 3 on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, only a woman was standing on the pedestal preserved for the family members of a deceased tribute. The woman could possibly have been his mother, and no father or siblings were present.</li>
<li>He survived for 8 days in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">book</a> and for 7 days in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">film</a>. Despite the film does not show how long he lasted in the games, if we count the time that elapses from when Caesar and Claudius appear on the scoreboard explaining the mines surrounding the supply tower until their death, it tells us that it lasted exactly 6 days, 9 hours, 14 minutes, 49 seconds, and 60 milliseconds. Note that the first day is counted as day 0 on the scoreboards.</li>
<li>He had the lowest odds of all the Cornucopia survivors excluding Katniss Everdeen and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage" title="The Hunger Games On Stage">The Hunger Games On Stage</a></i>, his name is <b>Fila</b>, which could derive from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/electrical_filament" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:electrical filament">electrical filaments</a>.</li></ul>
