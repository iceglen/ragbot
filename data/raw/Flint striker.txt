<p><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s <b>flint striker</b> was a birthday gift given to him by his girlfriend, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>, on the morning of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. When struck against any type of striking rock, like flint or quartz, it would start a fire.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was his <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a> for the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlint_striker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The flint striker was forged by <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a>. Lenore Dove traded eggs to get the metal to make it. When Lenore Dove first gave it to Haymitch, before the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">Reaping</a>, he wasn't sure what it was at first. Once he realized what it was, he told her he loved it. It replaced his old striker, which was worn down and passed through <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">his mother</a>'s family. Although Haymitch's mother wasn't a big fan of how much Lenore Dove distracted Haymitch, she was glad to see the new striker and thread a leather bootlace through the rings to be worn on the neck. Before leaving for the Reaping, Haymitch offered to show his brother <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid</a> how to use it that evening.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>While in bed during the first night on the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a>, Haymitch grasped the flint striker and tried to mentally reach out to Lenore Dove.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> were stripping down the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>, a Peacekeeper asked Haymitch if the striker was his tribute token. Haymitch responded affirmatively, saying it was just a necklace, worrying that they might view it as an unfair advantage if they knew its real purpose.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the chariot ride, Haymitch's striker, alongside the newly deceased <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella's</a> token, caught attention. When Haymitch faced up against <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>, he wondered if the president was staring at his flint striker.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Snow recognized the craftsmanship as that of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> due to his <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">previous experience</a> with them and found an inscription on it that Haymitch had missed.<sup id="cite_ref-SR9_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While discussing the plan to bomb the arena, Haymitch showed <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> the flint striker as a means of lighting the fuse. Beetee was impressed, calling it "very clever." When Haymitch turned the token in for appraisal, he was worried they would confiscate it, but hoped they would just see a pretty necklace. He remarked that he felt naked without it.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, it was eventually cleared with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>, alongside the other District 12 tributes' tokens.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>With the help of <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>, Haymitch found a quartz rock to use with the striker.<sup id="cite_ref-SR16_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR16-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He later used it to light the <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">sunflower bomb</a> that he put on the arena's water tank.<sup id="cite_ref-SR18_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR18-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Maysilee and Haymitch found a blowtorch in the supplies of the dead Careers, Haymitch was disappointed that Lenore Dove's gift seemed to become obsolete, at least until the blowtorch ran out of fuel.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While Haymitch's intestines were falling out, as he lit the bomb, he kissed Lenore Dove's flint striker before slipping it under his collar, preparing for death.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_24_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_24-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When he woke up, he was wearing the striker. Before the <a href="/wiki/Victor_crowning" class="mw-redirect" title="Victor crowning">victor crowning</a>, when <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> tried arranging it over his shirt, he tried to resist, but she told him that Snow told her to make sure the striker was out where it could be seen.
</p><p>After Lenore Dove's death, Haymitch buried the flint striker in front of her grave.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_27_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_27-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlint_striker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/49/SOTR_Trailer_18.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20251120170149" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Haymitch using the flint striker" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/49/SOTR_Trailer_18.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20251120170149" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="130" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="SOTR Trailer 18.jpeg" data-image-key="SOTR_Trailer_18.jpeg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:SOTR_Trailer_18.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Haymitch using the flint striker</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Sunrise on the Reaping">"<i>Well, you know I like my pretty with a purpose.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The flint striker was a small C-shaped piece of metal. On one edge, a serpent's head hissed at the beak of a mockingjay, on the other edge. The snake's scales and the bird's feathers gradually blended as they reached the middle, where they became indistinguishable. Two metal rings were welded behind the animals' heads, so if a thread went through the rings, it could be worn around the neck. The "smooth steel edge" was what would be struck up against a rock to spark.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On the back was the inscription "From L.D."<sup id="cite_ref-SR9_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was described as "exquisite."<sup id="cite_ref-SR_6_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
