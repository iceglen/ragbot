<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_4_male_%2870th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He lived in District 4 until he either <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> or was <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> for the 70th Hunger Games. His <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a> was Annie Cresta.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_4_male_%2870th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At some point during the Games, he was beheaded in front of Annie, who then "ran off by herself and hid". She survived to become the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>, though not without significant, long-lasting trauma.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is unclear whether he was decapitated by another tribute, a <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a>, or a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> trap.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_4_male_%2870th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>He is one of two characters known to be beheaded in the Games - the other being <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.
<ul><li>However, many characters were decapitated by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, including <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> and various members of <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>. <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, Annie's husband, was one of the mutts' victims.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
