<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p><a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a> was noted to contain the most powerful and wealthy <b>families</b> in all of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. Commonly, the Capitol citizens had inherited their wealth from the work of generations from their respective ancestors. As a result, old money and nepotism is especially prevalent within Capitol society. These affluent families were the most elite and powerful of all Panem citizens and were known to greatly influence political affairs impacting the rest of Panem. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Lifestyle">Lifestyle</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_families%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The majority of Capitol citizens were noted to be "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/old_money" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:old money">old money</a>", that is, they inherited their wealth rather than earned it. The term described the most elite people's maintenance of wealth and power over multiple generations, reflecting on the lifestyles of Capitol citizens. Capitol families with "old money" used their own assets and money to prevent the family from descending into the lower class, as shown with the Snow family: as their assets were destroyed in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, the family descended into poverty and struggle despite living in the most opulent apartment building in the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Before the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> "new money" was common, but after travel between the Districts was restricted, the Plinths became the last "new money" family.  
</p><p>Although all citizens of the Capitol were of higher class, the most elite of the Capitol families would have <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">avoxes</a> attend to them alongside maids and servants. The Plinth family had personal Avox drivers<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> whilst the Snow family had cooks prior to the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The wealthiest would also have many exclusive privileges such as entering the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">amphitheater</a> in a private entrance away from the crowd.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Many Capitol citizens also lived in luxury penthouse apartments, including the Snows, Cranes, Creeds, and Dolittles. The most powerful and wealthy citizens resided on <a href="/wiki/The_Corso" class="mw-redirect" title="The Corso">the Corso</a>, a palatial and grand residence with a wide avenue made of marble and granite.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This was where the Crane, Dolittle, Snow, and Plinth families resided. Snow mentioned that the Plinths' move to the apartments at the Corso 'solidified their status,' as one of the most elite families of the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Heavensbees were shown to own a large mansion that took up an entire city block. The children of these families would attend the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, an elite secondary school. Those who showed promise at the Academy could continue their education at the University.
</p><p>Families of the elite would also converse with one another exclusively, as the Snow and Crane families were included in a group containing the "old guard of the elite" from the Capitol. As such, some of the highest-status families would form exclusive groups and raise their children together, as with <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Plinth_family">Plinth family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_families%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D36&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Plinth family were natives of <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, and had gathered their wealth by profiting off of the Dark Days, as Strabo Plinth had worked to manufacture <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> and machinery, concentrating on munitions. His act of siding with <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a> and the Capitol alongside their newfound wealth gave them a life in the Capitol. Ten years after the end of the Dark Days, they moved into the Corso, given to the most elite families, cementing their high status.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the death of Sejanus Plinth, the poverty-stricken Snow family conveniently inherited their wealth instead. They were the last known Capitol family to be "new money."
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Snow_family">Snow family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_families%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D46&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Snow family was exceptionally popular within the Capitol, as it was huge news when Coriolanus Snow was revealed with the <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> in the cages.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_7-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They were a well-known and rich family that owned a complex network of factories and research facilities in District 13. However, after District 13 gained its independence, they lost all their assets and squandered all their savings during the 2-year siege of the Capitol, and the family was thrown into poverty.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Eventually, despite their poverty after the Dark Days, the Snow family effectively inherited the wealth of the Plinths',<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> cementing their status as the most elite Capitol family and giving rise to Snow's pathway to power and presidency. They were also a part of an "old guard of the elite," including the Cranes and Creeds.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<li><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> - <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">Mentor</a> of <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> and <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> of Panem.</li>

<li><a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> - A <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a> for the earlier editions of the Games, later a part of an underground rebel group in the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_23_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_23-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
