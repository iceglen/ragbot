<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Peeta_Mellark">Peeta Mellark</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKatniss_Everdeen%2FRelationships%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>What I need is the dandelion in the spring. The bright yellow that means rebirth instead of destruction. The promise that life can go on, no matter how bad our losses. That it can be good again. And only Peeta can give me that.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss about her feelings for <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>

<p>Katniss mentions she can never stop owing things to <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. When she was starving after her father's death, he burnt some bread intentionally, and endured his mother's wrath so he could give some to her. This moment of him showing compassion gave her hope to not give up in this dark time, and to carry on living. Though she wanted to thank him, she never did. However, their eyes met in school the next day, where Katniss held a dandelion in her hands as she and Peeta looked at one another.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>They both became tributes of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> after Katniss volunteered in place of her sister, Prim.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During their training, Katniss refused to trust Peeta when he did nice things for her, believing it to be part of his strategy<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. It isn't until Peeta reveals in an interview that he is in love with Katniss. At first furious, she apologizes for lashing out at him, feeling guilty when she realizes that Peeta's confession would be of great help to her in securing sponsors.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the arena, he joins forces with the Careers<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and encounters Katniss who is hiding in a tree. Peeta suggests that they wait her out<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, but then helps her escape after she drops a nest of Tracker Jackers onto the Careers<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Peeta risks his life to save her, holding Cato off to secure her escape<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. After discovering two tributes from the same team district can win as a pair<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, Katniss searches for <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a>, and after she finds him on the brink of death, nurses him back to health.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They spend several days in a cave until he's better<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, with Katniss risking her life to get the medicine needed to heal his leg<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. During that time, she kisses him, flirts with him, and even sleeps next to him in his sleeping bag, playing the part of a girl falling in love<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> share a passionate kiss in the cave, which Katniss later admits to being confused about, as she experienced a 'hunger' that she had never known during this kiss, and feels it was the only kiss that they shared that was real<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>After they win the Games, Peeta is heartbroken when he discovers that Katniss was mostly acting. There is a rift in their relationship after this, though Katniss admits to being confused about how she feels for Peeta, and fears letting him go and having to face the future on her own.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 182px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/78/Katniss-Holding-Pearl.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140102044338" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss holding the pearl after  hands it to her." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/78/Katniss-Holding-Pearl.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/182?cb=20140102044338" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="182" height="102" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss-Holding-Pearl.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss-Holding-Pearl.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss-Holding-Pearl.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss holding the pearl after <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> hands it to her.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In Catching Fire, they reconcile and resolve to be friends, spending time getting to know one another<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Peeta comforts Katniss in the night when she has nightmares, sleeping next to her during the Victory Tour<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and when they are reaped for the Quarter Quell<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. When Katniss is injured after falling whilst climbing the electric fence around District 12, Peeta helps nurse her back to health, carrying her around her home and sketching and painting alongside her<sup id="cite_ref-CF_11_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_11-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. When they are reaped, Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> spend several days on the rooftops of the training center, enjoying the little time they have left together in peace and contentment, watching sunrises and having picnics<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>During the 3rd <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, Peeta is electrocuted after colliding with a forcefield, and his heart stops<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Though Finnick is able to restart his heart, Katniss cries and screams over him, relieved that he is alive and terrified and broken when she believed he was dead<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Later, on the beach, Peeta tells Katniss that he wants her to live, as she has a family that she loves and needs, and can have a future with Gale, giving her his locket which contains a picture of Prim, her mother, and Gale. Peeta sadly reflects that no one really needs him, and it is here that Katniss realizes that she needs him. That she would never be able to recover if he were to die, and she at least realizes the depth of his love, his willingness to sacrifice everything for her. It is then on the beach she realizes how deeply she actually cares about him and kisses him naturally and passionately, not for the cameras, but out of her affection for Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Like their kiss in the cave in their first Games<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> she wishes that she could shut out anyone who's watching and for once have a moment when it is only the two of them<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. He later gives her a pearl<sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, jokingly referencing a comment that Effie once made<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> to both of their amusement<sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Both the locket and the pearl become precious items to Katniss which she keeps by her side at all times. Peeta is eventually separated from Katniss and is captured by the Capitol while Katniss is taken by <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">the rebels</a>.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 220px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Katniss_kissing_hijacked_Peeta.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150725045544" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss kisses Peeta." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f1/Katniss_kissing_hijacked_Peeta.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20150725045544" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="92" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss kissing hijacked Peeta.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_kissing_hijacked_Peeta.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_kissing_hijacked_Peeta.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss kisses Peeta.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In Mockingjay, Peeta is gone for a portion of the beginning until about halfway through the book, in which he is only shown being interviewed. Shortly after the last interview, he advises Katniss to not trust the rebels<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Peeta is tortured and beaten by the Capitol as a means of breaking and affecting Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. He returns after Gale rescues him<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, but has had his memories tampered with—or hijacked<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. While he is hijacked, he is driven to kill Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_30-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, but through the help of Delly Cartwright, Haymitch, and the doctors in District 13,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_31-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peeta is able to maintain his sanity<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Later, while in the Star Squad, Katniss plays a game with Peeta called "Real or not real," and Katniss helps Peeta recover and remember his deep love for her. Peeta eventually grew self-aware enough to want to die because of the impulses he had to harm Katniss; on several occasions, he asks her to shoot him, give him nightlock, or just leave him to die. Peeta feared he would eventually overpower Katniss. When Peeta was in danger of losing control, Katniss, despite the danger to her life, kissed Peeta, snapping him back into reality, and begged him to "Stay with me", to which he replied "Always".
</p><p>After Prim died, he went to her house and planted primroses around her house as a memorial to her sister, who died because of the exploding <a href="/wiki/Parachute" class="mw-redirect" title="Parachute">parachutes</a>. Over time, Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> grow back together again, and one night, echoing a "game" that they had played while he was having trouble recovering his memory, Peeta asks Katniss: "You love me. Real or not real?" She answers "Real." Katniss expresses how she feels about both Peeta and Gale—that Gale was full of fire, and altogether, not the relationship she needed. Peeta, to her, was like, "a dandelion in the spring...signaling rebirth," emphasizing that Peeta is able to help Katniss heal and move forward, and that with him there was the promise that life could once again be good. She gives in to the 'hunger' that she feels when she is with him, and after reflects that she believes that "This would have happened anyways" indicating that Katniss finally realized how deep her feelings for Peeta ran.
</p><p>In the epilogue, Katniss and Peeta have children after 15 years of being together, and they find a meadow to settle down in. Katniss and Peeta are still haunted by the memories of the Games but find ways to carry on living and cope with their trauma, living in peace with their children.
</p>

<p>When Katniss was twelve, she met <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> in the woods. Their fathers had been killed in the same accident and they both provided for their families.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Gale nicknamed her Catnip, after mishearing her name.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They quickly became hunting partners, and they taught each other about their expertise<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Gale comes to bid Katniss goodbye before she leaves to compete in the Hunger Games and promises to protect Prim<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_34-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<p>Gale unexpectedly kisses Katniss in the woods, revealing his feelings for her. President Snow threatens Katniss that he will kill Gale and his family.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_35-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss is unable to say "I love you" back, only admitting she knows he loves her. When Gale exclaims, he doesn't want anything made in the Capitol, she believes he's referring to her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_36-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-36"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss prevents Gale from being whipped after found guilty of poaching, but she gets lashed across the face while doing so. Whilst Gale is stabilized, Katniss stays with him and kisses him, bringing him back to consciousness.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_37-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the Quarter Quell is announced, Katniss distances herself from him, however, feeling uncertain about what she truly feels and not wanting to waste time on 'what-ifs'. Before leaving, Katniss admits to herself that she loves Gale on some level, albeit in the limited way she can offer.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_38-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the Quell, he tells her that District 12 is destroyed but her mother and sister are okay.
</p><p>Gale tries to make Katniss happy as they get closer in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, and they fall into the companionable relationship they had in District 12<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_39-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_41-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-41"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_42-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-42"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_28-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_14_43-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_14-43"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>43<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. However, as Katniss fixates on Peeta's captivity and then later torture<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_28-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_44-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-44"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, <a href="/wiki/Gale" class="mw-redirect" title="Gale">Gale</a> comes to realize that he can only ever gain Katniss's attention when he is in pain<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_44-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-44"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. A rift develops between them in District 2, where Katniss is shocked by Gale's methods in securing the District<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_45-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-45"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Later, as they storm the Capitol with the Star Squad, Katniss and Gale fight alongside one another before Gale is captured and Katniss is injured in the firebombing before the Presidential Palace.
</p><p>While Katniss recovers in the hospital, Gale does not visit. Later, Katniss believes the firebomb that killed Prim and the Capitol children was made by <a href="/wiki/Gale" class="mw-redirect" title="Gale">Gale</a>. Though part of her wants to deny it, she feels she will be unable to ever disassociate that from Gale, and thus lets him go without a word. Katniss reflects in her head if she and Gale would have ever been truly happy together, or if "The dark, twisted sadness between them have grown up even without the Capitol's help," thus indicating that she feels something would have driven them apart eventually regardless of what they did.
</p><p>Katniss settles back in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> with Peeta and is informed that Gale had gone to District 2 to take up an important job. Instead of feeling longing or anger, Katniss is only relieved, believing that Gale would move on like she would and forget about her. Katniss reflects that Gale was full of fire, much like herself, which made him unsuitable as a partner, whereas Peeta was like 'a dandelion in the spring' and that he alone could help her heal and move forward despite the sorrows of her life.
</p>

<p>Primrose Everdeen was someone who Katniss loved unconditionally as she was also Katniss's younger sister. When she was younger, Katniss hunted and traded to make life easier for her sister. Katniss got Prim a goat for her birthday. Katniss took out tesserae but refused to let Prim do the same.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Prim was reaped, Katniss volunteered in her place but Prim did not want her to<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Prim came to say goodbye to Katniss and made her promise to win so she could come home<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_34-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.

<p>When Katniss is in the arena, she sees that Prim and Rue are very alike<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_46-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-46"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>46<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. As Rue dies, she makes Katniss promise to win the Hunger Games. Katniss remembers her and Prim's promise and tries to win for both of them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

However, when Prim's cat Buttercup (whom Katniss always disliked) arrived back in the Victors' Village searching for her, Katniss snapped in anger and was able to release her grief over Prim's death, allowing her to heal and move on with her life. Peeta brings Katniss evening primroses and plants them around her house in memory of Prim.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Asterid_Everdeen">Asterid Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKatniss_Everdeen%2FRelationships%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Katniss' relationship with <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">her mother</a> is very complicated. After her husband died in a mining accident, her mother became mentally comatose for months, not even stirring herself to feed or support her daughters. As a result, Katniss essentially took over as head of the family, who would have starved without her hunting and signing up for tesserae rations. While there wasn't outright hatred between them, Katniss never completely forgave her mother for her weakness.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After she volunteered as tribute in Prim's place<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, Katniss harshly reminded Asterid that whatever happened in the arena, she could not check out again and leave Prim on her own<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_34-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Following her victory in the 74th Hunger Games, the relationship between them improved; Katniss began to be less harsh with her mother, as well as see things from her mother's point of view<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_47-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-47"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. After Asterid was able to save Gale following his flogging, Katniss grew to appreciate her mother's healing skills in a new way<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_37-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. When the Third Quarter Quell was announced, and it was obvious that Katniss would be reaped again, Asterid helped her and Peeta prepare for the games, putting them on a special diet to gain strength and weight<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_38-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<p><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> is Katniss' mentor in the 74th and 75th Hunger Games, and the victor of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. The only reason that Haymitch decided to help <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> and Katniss at first was because he finally got a pair of fighters that year, since both assault him when he doesn't help them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Both didn't like each other at first<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, especially during Training when Haymitch says that Katniss has the personality of a dead slug<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, but in the arena, Peeta says that Haymitch and Katniss only hate each other because they're so alike<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. To add, Haymitch decides to help Katniss out over Peeta because of the fact that Peeta was already determined to help Katniss out<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Throughout the 74th and 75th Games, Haymitch sends (or doesn't send) supplies to Katniss to inform her about something happening or send her a message<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_12-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_15-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_50-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-50"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_25-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_26-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>In <i>Catching Fire</i>, Haymitch helps Katniss aid <a href="/wiki/Gale" class="mw-redirect" title="Gale">Gale</a> when he was whipped to her house with her mother and Prim<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_37-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Haymitch and Katniss both get drunk<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_38-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> after the reading of the card, which results in Peeta dumping all the alcohol down the drain. Katniss defends Haymitch, saying that she'll get some back.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_38-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the Training, Haymitch is disgusted with Peeta and Katniss after they directly threaten the Capitol (Katniss hanged Crane and Peeta drew Rue)<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. At the end of the Games, Haymitch explains what he was trying to do in the arena and tells her that Peeta was captured, and Katniss scratches his face with anger.
</p><p>Haymitch continues to mentor Katniss despite the end of the Games<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_41-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-41"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_45-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-45"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. After Peeta is captured, Katniss rejects Haymitch because she blames him for his capture<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_41-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-41"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. When Katniss rips her earpiece out<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_42-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-42"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, Haymitch scolds her and threatens her to have an implant instead, but in a large contrast, Katniss also goes to Haymitch for comfort<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_28-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Katniss could not say that Thirteen was well and her too, so she starts to cry, knowing that she'll be hurting Peeta even more, so she turns to Haymitch because she knows he loves Peeta too. She sobs into his shirt, crying about Peeta and the rebellion while he holds her, saying that he understands just before she is knocked out.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch also tries to protect Katniss from knowing Gale left to rescue Peeta the next day<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_30-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Katniss tries to talk to him about Prim's death too, but accidentally rejects her because of his drunken state.
</p><p>Later, Katniss, Peeta, and Haymitch begin to mend back together and Haymitch contributes the kids he had to mentor for the Games for Katniss and Peeta's <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">memory book</a>.
</p>

Upon first meeting them, Effie annoyed Katniss in the first Games by saying the previous year's tributes were 'savages.' This caused Katniss to purposely use bad manners, such as wiping fingers on the tablecloth, to annoy her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_34-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Effie was all about dresses and looks, but she cried once or twice<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. </p><p>At the reaping of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Effie is seen crying for Katniss and Peeta when they are reaped, showing how much she cares for them<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_38-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Katniss is shocked when Effie arrived before Snow's trial because she had been on the rebellion side. Katniss then realizes she does care for Effie.

<p><a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> was Katniss' stylist for the Games and is said to be the only person she "really trusts". She is shocked by how normal he seemed.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_52-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-52"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Cinna consoles her before she enters the arena and says that he is not supposed to, but if he could, he'd bet on her. Katniss finds confidence in his words and thinks that maybe she should really try to win.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In her victor's house, there is a phone which had no use before she started to chat with <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> about her talent. Since the only thing she was good at was hunting, Cinna suggested fashion designing. He took care of everything for her, including creating a book of sketches that she could show to the cameras.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_47-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-47"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> designed many things for her<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_47-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-47"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, including her opening ceremony garment which sparked his theme<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_53-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-53"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Katniss feared for his safety before the Quarter Quell<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_47-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-47"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and as Katniss is in her glass cylinder, about to launch, the Peacekeepers enter and beat Cinna unconscious. This act causes Katniss to unhinge for a point<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_51-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-51"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, fretting that they will kill him for his design of her wedding dress<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. They drag him away<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_51-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-51"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and he is allegedly killed during interrogation. Katniss suspects this was just to throw her off guard<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_23-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> had already designed her mockingjay uniform, knowing there would be a war. His last message to Katniss is written under a sketch of her pin: "I'm still betting on you."
</p>

<p>Rue trusted Katniss when she saw that Katniss was wearing a mockingjay pin<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. After Katniss left Rue alone to blow up the Careers' supplies<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, Rue fell into Marvel's trap and was stabbed (speared in the movies) before Katniss could save her<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_55-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-55"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>55<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and then Katniss killed Marvel. Katniss then covered Rue's body with flowers and gives the farewell symbol to <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This was believed to be a sign of rebellion<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.

Katniss remembers Rue's death, which gives her motivation to go on<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Katniss continues to think of <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> throughout the series, including her in the memory book she creates with Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_54-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-54"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>In <i>Catching Fire</i>, while in Peeta's private session, Peeta painted a picture of Rue covered in flowers<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_22-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.

<p>Katniss first met <a href="/wiki/Finnick" class="mw-redirect" title="Finnick">Finnick</a> at the opening ceremonies for the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> while waiting for the chariots to leave <a href="/wiki/Finnick" class="mw-redirect" title="Finnick">Finnick</a> approaches Katniss and offers her sugar. Katniss remains calm and composed although she feels a little uncomfortable by how close he gets to her and even more so when <a href="/wiki/Finnick" class="mw-redirect" title="Finnick">Finnick</a> implies people pay him with secrets and asks Katniss if she has any secrets worth his time. However, Katniss doesn't break his gaze and simply says she is an open book.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_53-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-53"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the three days of training, <a href="/wiki/Finnick" class="mw-redirect" title="Finnick">Finnick</a> continues to be friendly to Katniss when talking to her and even offers to give her an hour of trident lessons in exchange for Katniss giving him an hour of archery instruction<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_56-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-56"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>At the beginning of the <a href="/wiki/3rd_Quarter_Quell" class="mw-redirect" title="3rd Quarter Quell">3rd Quarter Quell</a>, <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> startled Katniss with his proposal for an alliance. She was unsure of how to react, but accepted his offer after she noticed his golden bracelet<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_23-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> that she knew came from <a href="/wiki/Haymitch" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch">Haymitch</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_56-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-56"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Still not fully trusting him, Katniss remained extremely wary of his every move<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_23-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. However, he proved his usefulness and reliability by reviving <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_24-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> when he accidentally ran into a force field<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_23-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, and saved <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> in the midst of a fight even though it meant he got wounded himself<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_57-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-57"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>57<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>At the beginning of Mockingjay, Finnick was deeply distressed and unable to focus on anything but saving <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, who had been kidnapped and was being tortured in the Capitol. One of the ways he kept himself sane was by making knots with a rope, over and over again<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_39-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. He later gave that same rope to Katniss to aid her during the bombing of District 13<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_29-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Soon, after talking to Katniss before Boggs came, Katniss mentions the trident Beetee made for him and to put on pants, in wear he jokingly whips his hospital gown off revealing only his underwear and jokes to Katniss if she found it distracting, to which she smiles, hinting that she was glad that Finnick could at least joke around<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_41-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-41"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Katniss starts to realize Finnick's problems through the last few years and how he describes what President Snow did to him<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_29-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_30-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>While on the mission to President Snow's mansion, the group was attacked by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a>, and Finnick was decapitated before Katniss could rescue him. As he died, Katniss witnessed brief moments of his life, including a silver parachute bearing the trident that won him his Games, Mags' laugh, his life on the seas of District 4, and Annie's face.
</p>

<p>When they first met, both <a href="/wiki/Johanna" class="mw-redirect" title="Johanna">Johanna</a> and Katniss had a strong dislike for each other due to Johanna stripping in the elevator in front of her, <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch">Haymitch</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_53-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-53"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. However, once inside the arena the two were thrown together into an alliance set up by Haymitch and the rebels of <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_50-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-50"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Even though the two were now allies, they still didn't trust each other<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_58-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-58"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>58<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_25_26-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_25-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_59-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-59"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>59<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Katniss believes one of them will kill the other.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_58-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-58"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>58<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the escape from the arena at the end of the Games, Johanna attacked Katniss to remove a <a href="/wiki/Tracking" class="mw-redirect" title="Tracking">tracking</a> device from her arm<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_59-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-59"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>59<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Katniss eventually realized what Johanna had done and warmed up to her.
</p><p>After being rescued from the Capitol<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_30-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna" class="mw-redirect" title="Johanna">Johanna</a> and Katniss start to become friends. <a href="/wiki/Johanna" class="mw-redirect" title="Johanna">Johanna</a>, knowing that Katniss is indebted to her, begins to siphon off Katniss's morphling supply<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_32-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. As a result of the time spent together in the hospital, the two grow closer<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and eventually become friends. Once they are released from the hospital, Johanna and Katniss willingly move into a compartment in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> together, proving their friendship<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_33-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. They train together to become part of a squad which would enable them to fight for the rebellion against the Capitol<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_33-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Johanna, however, doesn't make it through the final test and has to stay behind while Katniss leaves for the Capitol. Before Katniss's departure, she visits Johanna in the hospital. There, Johanna makes her swear to kill Snow to make him pay for everything he has done.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_60-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-60"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>60<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

Katniss also thanks <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> for sparing her life.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p>Although <a href="/wiki/Haymitch" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch">Haymitch</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> had expectations for <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> and Katniss to join forces in the Hunger Games, the two did not comply. <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> ended up in a brief alliance with the Careers<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_62-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-62"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>62<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, while Katniss teamed up with <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, the small tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Katniss had not made an attempt to form an alliance with <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> until finding out that if both tributes from the same district are the last two alive, they can both win. After hearing the news, she finally attempts to find and form an alliance with <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a>, even though she knows he is severely injured.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, Katniss tries to pretend to be fully in love with him<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_12-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_13-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_14-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_15-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_24_63-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_24-63"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>63<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. They keep this up until the end of the Games, after Cato has died and they are the only remaining tributes. Claudius Templesmith then again informs the two over a loudspeaker that there may only be one victor for the 74th Hunger Games, leaving the two together in the arena to fight each other to the death. Peeta begs for Katniss to kill him, willing to lay down his life for hers, but Katniss thinks of a way to let them both die together. Collecting nightlock (poisonous berries), she instructs Peeta to eat a handful of berries with her once she reaches the count of three in a double suicide. She does so under the pretense that she wishes the two lovers will never be separated, but by death, when really she suspects that rather than have no victor, the Capitol will allow them both to live. The <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a>, is shocked and desperate at the prospect of losing the remaining tributes, and so it is announced that the Games are concluded and both are victors of the 74th Games.
