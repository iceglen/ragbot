<p><b>Alliances</b> were an important and recurring feature of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, formed when tributes recognized the benefits of cooperation to improve their chances of survival. By pooling resources such as <a href="/wiki/Food_and_drink" title="Food and drink">food, water, supplies</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>, allies could protect one another from immediate threats in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> and increase their collective odds of outlasting other tributes.
</p><p>Despite these advantages, alliances of any form were inherently fragile. With the Hunger Games mandating that only one tribute could become a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>, alliances, in their simplest form, were temporary arrangements of convenience rather than lasting bonds. As the Games advanced, alliances typically broke down - sometimes violently, other times through mutual parting to delay the emotional burden of betrayal. Regardless of form, the prospect of eventual confrontation always hung over allied tributes until the Games' conclusion.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Common_types">Common types</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_Partners">District Partners</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">District partner</a></i></center></div>
<p>Alliances between tributes from the same district were among the most common and natural partnerships in the Hunger Games. Such alliances often formed early, sometimes immediately after the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> or in the lead up to the training period in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District partners frequently shared a pre-existing bond - whether friendship, schooling, or simply the shared hardship of district life - which could foster trust and cooperation. However, this was not always the case; for example, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> only had a superficial familiarity with one another prior to the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, and in many cases, tributes from the same district may have had little prior connection at all.
</p><p>District partner alliances offered several natural advantages over ad hoc arrangements formed in the arena. District partners had more time to coordinate strategies, assess one another's strengths and weaknesses, and benefit jointly from the advice of their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>(s). These advantages gave them a stronger foundation of cooperation once the Games began. Notable examples include Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark during the 74th Hunger Games, and <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> and <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a> in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_Alliances">Career Alliances</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a></i></center></div>
<p>The Career tributes - <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a> tributes from Districts <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a> - were distinguished by their lifelong preparation for the Hunger Games. From a young age, many attended specialized academies in their districts, where they trained in combat, survival, and weapons use. This upbringing gave them a significant advantage and instilled a sense of entitlement and confidence in their prospects of victory.
</p><p>For the Games, Careers typically formed the most dominant and feared alliance, colloquially known as the "Career Pack" or simply "the Pack." True to their name, this alliance employed aggressive, pack-like tactics: monopolizing the Cornucopia, securing resources, and systematically hunting down weaker tributes. The Career alliance usually consisted of six members - two from each Career district - though its size could vary if they invited non-Career tributes or excluded weaker volunteers.
</p><p>The Career alliance's unity and strength made them near unstoppable in the early stages of the Games, and it was common for one of their members to eventually emerge as victor. For other tributes, the Career Pack represented one of the main threats they had to contend with, both strategically and psychologically.
</p><p>In the film adaptations, the Careers were depicted as only originating from Districts 1 and 2, omitting District 4, which was not considered a Career district.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Inter-district_Alliances">Inter-district Alliances</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Inter-district alliances were less common than district partnerships or the Career pack, but occasionally formed when tributes from different districts recognized the strategic value of cooperation. These alliances typically arose out of necessity or convenience, usually when the Games were already underway, allowing tributes from diverse backgrounds to pool skills, resources, and knowledge in order to survive environmental hazards in the arena or resist stronger opponents, particularly the Careers.
</p><p>Unlike district partnerships or the Career pack, inter-district alliances lacked the foundation of shared upbringing or long-term training and often were formed haphazardly. As a result, they were generally more fragile and prone to early collapse, as mutual distrust and the absence of deep personal ties left them vulnerable to betrayal or abandonment.
</p><p>The most notable exception was <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a> alliance formed in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. This unprecedented coalition included thirty-two tributes from all other non-Career districts with the exception of <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, making it the largest alliance in Hunger Games history. Although half of its members died in the Second Quarter Quell's <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">opening bloodbath</a>, the survivors maintained their unity throughout the Games and ultimately secured victory for the alliance when <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was declared the victor. This level of cohesion was unique amongst the non-Career districts. However, no comparable alliances is known to have formed in subsequent Games until the 75th Hunger Games.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Role_in_the_Games">Role in the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Purpose_and_benefits">Purpose and benefits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the brutal and unpredictable environment of the arena, alliances served as a critical survival mechanism. By pooling skills, resources, and knowledge, tributes could compensate for individual weaknesses and improve both their short- and long-term prospects. Weaker tributes gained protection from stronger allies, while combat-trained tributes benefited from tributes with specialized skills such as first aid, scouting, foraging, or tactical planning. Alliances also provided emotional support, helping tributes cope with the isolation and paranoia they faced while being in the unfamiliar arena. Notable examples include Katniss Everdeen and Rue in the 74th Hunger Games, whose complementary skills in hunting, foraging, and herbal medicine allowed the two to resist and even go on the offensive against the Careers. Similarly, in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the larger alliance of Katniss, Peeta, <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> saw the group combine their combat expertise and technical ingenuity to not only dominate the smaller than usual Career alliance but also challenge the arena itself.
</p><p>A second major benefit was the attraction of sponsors. Tributes who demonstrated teamwork, strategy, and survival skills were more compelling to Capitol audiences, making alliances an effective way to draw <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsor</a> interest. Gifts sent to alliances tended to be larger or more valuable, as they could sustain multiple tributes and extend the drama of the Games. For instance, Katniss received a whole loaf of bread from District 11 for her dedication and protection towards Rue up until her death, and later, she and Peeta shared sponsor-provided broth during the 74th Games. Career alliances particularly benefited greatly from this system, as their members' reputation and odds of winning made them reliable investments for sponsors, even individually.
</p><p>Finally, alliances allowed for the strategic distribution of resources. Sponsor gifts and supplies taken from the Cornucopia could be rationed and allocated according to each member's strengths or needs, ensuring a group's overall effectiveness. This cooperation, however temporary, often gave alliances an early and lasting advantage over isolated tributes.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Weaknesses_and_dangers">Weaknesses and dangers</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Despite their advantages, alliances were inherently fragile and temporary. Trust was always precarious, as tributes had to balance cooperation with the knowledge that their allies would eventually become rivals. Betrayal could occur at any moment when the partnership ceased to be beneficial or even become a liability. The 74th Hunger Games and likely many previous editions illustrated this dynamic: after Peeta's betrayal and the deaths of <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">two</a> <a href="/wiki/District_4_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 female (74th HG)">Careers</a>, the remaining Careers recruited <a href="/wiki/Fila" title="Fila">Fila</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> for his technical skills in reactivating the pedestal mines. When Katniss destroyed the Careers' hoarded supplies, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> executed Fila  - partly out of anger and frustration, but also because his usefulness to Careers had ended. Ultimately, the Careers themselves embodied the eventual instability of alliances, as their members were destined to turn on one another once all other tributes had been eliminated.
</p><p>Alliances could also create uneven burdens. Partnerships involving weaker or dependent tributes sometimes endangered the group as a whole. During the 75th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags</a> - one of the oldest tributes in the Quell - required Finnick to carry her around. When the group was forced to flee from a rapidly advancing cloud of poisonous <a href="/wiki/Fog" title="Fog">fog</a>, Mags sacrificed herself to ensure the younger victors could escape, recognizing that her presence was slowing the group down. 
</p><p>Finally, alliances were often more conspicuous targets. Groups of tributes were easier to detect, and rival alliances or solo tributes could exploit their visibility. During the 74th Games, <a href="/wiki/%22Foxface%22" title="&quot;Foxface&quot;">Foxface</a> demonstrated this by repeatedly stealing from the Careers' consolidated cache of Cornucopia supplies unnoticed, unintentionally paving the way for Katniss to subsequently destroy it. In that same vein, shared resources, while a major strength, could also become a liability if stolen, destroyed, or mismanaged, creating tension and undermining group cohesion.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Dissolution">Dissolution</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>No alliance in the Hunger Games could last indefinitely. The principle of the Hunger Games as a battle royale - that only one tribute could survive to become the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> - ensured that even the closest partnerships would eventually collapse. The single exception was the 74th Hunger Games, when a mid-Games rule change briefly allowed for two victors from the same district. This permitted Katniss and Peeta to remain allied until the very end. When the Gamemakers revoked the rule to force a final confrontation, the pair threatened mutual suicide with <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock berries</a>, forcing the Gamemakers to declare them both victors.
</p><p>In most Games, alliances dissolved naturally as the field narrowed. Partnerships ended through betrayal, abandonment, elimination by opponents, arena hazards, or Gamemaker manipulation. The nature of dissolution depended on the bond between allies and the circumstances of the arena. During the 50th Hunger Games, for example, Haymitch and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> maintained a close alliance as part of the Newcomers until the final five, before Maysilee chose to part ways peacefully rather than risk a direct confrontation with her ally. By contrast, Career alliances were particularly prone to violent implosion. Once they had eliminated weaker tributes, the aggressive nature of their training and strategy often turned inward, forcing members to kill one another in brutal, final confrontations. 
</p><p>The physical, emotional, and psychological toll of surviving to the endgame virtually guaranteed that every alliance, regardless of its form, would ultimately dissolve to pave the way for the sole victor.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Significant_alliances">Significant alliances</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_Games">Early Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="District_4_and_10_alliance">District 4 and 10 alliance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>All of the tributes in this alliance displayed a familiarity with weapons from their respective districts' industries. They may have been one of the first, if not the first, example of a multi-district alliance. Their teamwork and coordination may have also been an inspiration for all later alliances formed by Careers in subsequent installments of the Hunger Games.
</p><p>On the first night of the Games, <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> from District 4 joined forces with <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a>, the male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>, in pursuit of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>. This alliance lasted into the second day when they worked together to kill <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>. Once she was dead, the alliance dissolved when Mizzen and Coral ambushed Tanner.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> - District 4 male - Fell to his death trying to escape Teslee's drones, placing 5th.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a> - District 4 female - Killed by snake mutts, placing 6th.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a> - District 10 male - First stabbed by Coral with a <a href="/wiki/Trident" class="mw-redirect" title="Trident">trident</a>, then finished off by Mizzen with a <a href="/wiki/Knife" class="mw-redirect" title="Knife">knife</a>. He placed 9th.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> had twice the regular number of tributes,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> with 48 total tributes rather than the regular 24. The 50th Hunger Games was notable for being the only known Hunger Games where every tribute was a part of two opposing alliances: the Career Pack and <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a>. 
</p>
<h4><span id="Career_Pack_.2850th_HG.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_Pack_(50th_HG)"><b>Career Pack (50th HG)</b></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The special rules of the Second Quarter Quell allowed for a super-sized Career Pack of 12 tributes from Districts 1, 2, and 4. The admission of the four tributes from District 5 brought the alliance to 16 members, making it the largest known Career Pack and second largest known overall in Hunger Games history. At the start of the Games, twelve Careers survived the Cornucopia bloodbath and its initial aftermath, then spent the first three days scouring the mountain area of the arena for their opposition. Despite their qualitative numbers advantage, the Careers sustained a devastating loss when half of the remaining alliance, including the entire District 2 representation, was killed in the volcanic eruption on Day 4. Only two Careers would survive to make it into the final eight, with <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> becoming this year's runner-up when she was killed by her own axe after it rebounded off the arena force field while she fought <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>.

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Newcomers"><b>The Newcomers</b></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Nearly all of the non-Career district tributes were a part of this alliance with several being <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> plotting to disrupt the Games. This was the largest non-Career alliance and largest overall in Hunger Games history with a total of 32 tributes hailing from Districts 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12 under the leadership of <a href="/wiki/Ampert_Latier" title="Ampert Latier">Ampert Latier</a> and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>. The alliance's name of "<a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a>" was derived from the epithet "Neddie Newcomer" used by miners in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. Of the 50th Games' two opposing alliances, the Newcomers were more intelligent and better organized than the Careers. Nearly half of the Newcomers died in the initial bloodbath, with Districts 8 and 9 eliminated outright and half of Districts 6, 7, and 10. Further losses would be sustained from the poisonous nature of the arena and the volcanic eruption on Day 4. Nevertheless, the Newcomers consistently outnumbered the Careers and retained their loyalty to each other until the end rather than falling apart. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s victory made the Newcomers the winning alliance of the Games.

<h4><span id="Career_Pack_.2874th_HG.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_Pack_(74th_HG)">Career Pack (74th HG)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<p>The Careers initially started with the typical six members expected of the Career Pack: <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bravura" title="Bravura">Bravura</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Tippet" title="Tippet">Tippet</a>. During pre-Games training, the Careers invited <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> to join their alliance for his size and strength, but Thresh declined. Later, when the Games were underway, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> and <a href="/wiki/Fila" title="Fila">Fila</a> were allowed to join, bringing the Pack's total overall numbers to 8 members. 
</p><p>At the start of the Games, the Careers took an unexpected loss when Bravura was killed during the Cornucopia bloodbath. The Pack was restored to its standard strength after the bloodbath, when Peeta was allowed to join, ostensibly for his demonstrated strength, but also to help locate his district partner, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>. After cornering Katniss up a tree and making camp at its base, the Careers took a devastating loss when Katniss dropped a <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nest on top of them, killing Glimmer and Tippet. Peeta would also betray and abandon the alliance to assist Katniss in escaping, reducing the Pack to three members.
</p><p>Days later, Fila was allowed to join the alliance for his ability of reactivating the pedestal mines, which the Careers used to form a defensive perimeter around their base and Cornucopia supplies. When Katniss exploited this by mass activating the mines, obliterating the Careers' base and supplies, Fila was executed by Cato in a fit of rage. Marvel would shortly after be felled by Katniss in retaliation for him killing <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, reducing the Careers to just District 2. A few days later, during the feast, Clove would be killed by Thresh as revenge for Rue, leaving Cato as the last Career. Cato would ultimately be defeated by Katniss and Peeta during the finale of the Games, where he was mercy killed by Katniss after being mauled for hours by mutts. Unlike most other years, only three Careers made it into the final eight.

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="District_11">District 11</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p><a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> and <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> were in an informal alliance with each other due to being district partners. Thresh, being one of the oldest tributes at 18, felt obligated to protect Rue, who was the youngest tribute of the 74th Games at 12. The two likely trained together in the lead up to the Games, and Thresh showed amusement at Rue swiping Cato's knife and being hidden in the nets above the Training Center gymnasium. Despite this, the two were separated during the bloodbath at the Cornucopia, and remained so for the duration of the Games, leaving the alliance defunct. Rue ultimately allied with Katniss until her death at the hands of Marvel, whereas Thresh remained in isolation in a field of wheat for nearly the entirety of the Games. During the <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">feast</a>, Katniss's alliance with Rue was what spurred Thresh to kill Clove and spare Katniss's life, demonstrating he cared for Rue and was both saddened and enraged over her death.

<p>Katniss's first ally in the 74th Games was <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, whom she first encountered in the tree tops while attempting to escape the Careers. It was Rue who pointed out the tracker jacker nest that Katniss used to give herself an opening to escape. When Katniss fainted from her tracker jacker stings and remained unconscious for three days, Rue protected Katniss and treated her stings. The two quickly became good friends, largely due to Rue's resemblance to Katniss's younger sister, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose</a>, and her young age. Katniss and Rue worked together to undermine the Careers, setting off diversion fires so Katniss could destroy their supplies. When Rue was fatally wounded by Marvel, Katniss sang Rue a lullaby as she died, and she grieved for her for a long time after.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_24-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>When the rule for dual victors from the same district was announced, Katniss sought out Peeta, eventually reuniting and formally allying with him. During that time, Katniss nursed Peeta back to health and the two resumed their onscreen romance,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which grew into something more authentic over time. Katniss also had an unspoken alliance with Thresh, who saved her from Clove at the <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">feast</a>. Thresh chose to then spare Katniss's because she had been Rue's ally.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_27-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h4><span id="Career_Pack_.2875th_HG.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_Pack_(75th_HG)">Career Pack (75th HG)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D20&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<p>District 4 forwent the usual Career alliance this year, leaving a smaller Career pack of just the four tributes from Districts 1 and 2. During the Cornucopia bloodbath, the Careers were placed on the defensive, as the Rebel alliance managed to hold them off long enough for them to consolidate their members and make their escape to the jungle. Nevertheless, the Careers proceeded to take control of the Cornucopia and eliminate any remaining opposition. Following the Rebel alliance determination of the arena's clock-like nature, the Careers ambushed them at the Cornucopia, with <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> killing <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, only for him to be subsequently killed by Katniss. His sister, <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> would be killed by <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> shortly after, reducing the Careers to just District 2. After a brief melee, the Careers retreated from the Cornucopia right before it began to spin.
</p><p>During the Quell's endgame, the Careers attempted to hunt Katniss and her group in the jungle. Brutus would end up encountering and killing <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>, leading Peeta, who witnessed this, to engage and kill Brutus in single combat. Meanwhile, <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> would ambush and grievously wound <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> before being forced to retreat by <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>. Following the abrupt end of the 75th Hunger Games, Enobaria, being the last Career, was taken into Capitol custody.
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Rebel_alliance">Rebel alliance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlliance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D21&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>This was the second-largest known non-Career alliance and third-largest known overall behind <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a> and the 50th HG Career Pack with at least 12 members. The victor tributes and their mentors from Districts 3, 4, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, 7, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, and 11 conspired prior to the Quell to protect Katniss and Peeta at any cost. Their goal was to break open the force field surrounding the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>, so the pair could be extracted to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> for the rebel cause and impending <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_35-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the start of the Games, the alliance members from District 12 and 4 quickly consolidated at the Cornucopia, retrieving their respective weapons before fleeing into the jungle, whereas the members from District 7 and 3 retreated from a different point. Most of the alliance from the other districts died during the Bloodbath at the hands of the Careers, with the few remaining dispersing into the jungle. When Katniss and her group experienced the poisonous fog hour section, Mags sacrificed herself to permit the others to escape and not slow them down. Meanwhile, Blight accidentally walked into the force field and was killed.
</p><p>When Katniss, Peeta, and Finnick were engaged by monkey mutts, Peeta was saved by the District 6 female, who was fatally wounded in the process. Katniss, Peeta, and Finnick would eventually meet up with Johanna, Beetee, and Wiress after they emerged from the blood rain hour section. After the alliance consolidated, Wiress determined the arena's clock-like nature and that each hourly section hosted dangerous hazards. When the alliance made for the Cornucopia, they were ambushed by the Careers, resulting in Wiress' death at Gloss's hand.
</p>
