<p><b>Sage Blanken</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> who participated in the <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">73rd Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSage_Blanken%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sage was born in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>, as was his <a href="/wiki/District_12_female_(73rd_HG)" title="District 12 female (73rd HG)">district partner</a>, and they were both <a href="/wiki/Reaped" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaped">reaped</a> for the 73rd Hunger Games. He was mentored by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>. <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> mentioned to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> that he and his district partner ate like "savages". This upset Katniss greatly, as they came from the poorest region of District 12, where access to food was limited.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_script">Early script</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSage_Blanken%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>An early version of a script for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> gave his name as Sage Blanken, and his parents, <a href="/wiki/Corrina_Blanken" title="Corrina Blanken">Corrina</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tygart_Blanken" title="Tygart Blanken">Tygart Blanken</a>, were vendors at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>. He was killed with a bar mace on the second day by <a href="/wiki/Prospero" title="Prospero">Prospero</a>, the District 3 male.<sup id="cite_ref-script_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-script-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
