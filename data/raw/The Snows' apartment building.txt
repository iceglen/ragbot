<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Snows%2527_apartment_building%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The apartment building had a glass door from the lobby opening out onto the street. It also had an elevator with mirrored walls; however, said elevator had broken early into the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> and would not be repaired for over ten years.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Penthouse">Penthouse</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Snows%2527_apartment_building%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cd/Snow_family_entertainment_weekly.png/revision/latest?cb=20230928191051" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Grandma&#39;am, Coriolanus, and Tigris in their apartment." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cd/Snow_family_entertainment_weekly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230928191051" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Snow family entertainment weekly.png" data-image-key="Snow_family_entertainment_weekly.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Snow_family_entertainment_weekly.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Grandma'am, Coriolanus, and Tigris in their apartment.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The Snow family lived in the building's penthouse, twelve flights of stairs up. They also had access to the roof, where the <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma&#39;am">Grandma'am</a> tended her rose garden.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The apartment itself had twenty-foot-high walls and arched windows, though the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a>' wartime <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombings</a> had caused considerable damage throughout. Financial hardship forced the Snows to sell or trade many of their possessions, or even burn them to keep warm in the last winter of the war. As a result, some rooms were vacant, sparsely furnished, or closed off altogether.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The penthouse was once occupied by <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow&#39;s mother">his wife</a>, and their son <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, alongside the Grandma'am, <a href="/wiki/The_Snows%27_cook" title="The Snows&#39; cook">the Snows' cook</a>, and a maid. After the war, the Grandma'am raised Coriolanus and his cousin <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> there by herself.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the morning of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, Tigris pried <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/tessera" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:tessera">tesserae</a> from inside a cabinet in the maid's bathroom to use as buttons on a dress shirt for Coriolanus.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sub_penthouse">Sub penthouse</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Snows%2527_apartment_building%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Remus_Dolittle" title="Remus Dolittle">Remus Dolittle</a> and his family lived on the floor directly below the Snows.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, the introduction of property taxes in the Capitol<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> meant that the Dolittles could no longer afford to live there.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a> ended up buying the sub penthouse, and he and <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">his wife</a> had moved in by October. He also bought out the Snows' apartment for them, since they could not afford to keep it on their own. There was talk of putting in a spiral staircase and a private elevator between the two families' floors.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Snows%2527_apartment_building%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>'s apartment faced the Snows'. Coriolanus claimed that even from across the Corso, he could sometimes hear her "bellowing" at night.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Nero_Price" title="Nero Price">Nero Price</a> and his daughter <a href="/wiki/Persephone_Price" title="Persephone Price">Persephone</a> lived in a town house on the Corso. Coriolanus counted them among his neighbors.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The Snows' apartment was the only set built for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>. It was designed to look like a snowflake when viewed from above.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_audiocom_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_audiocom-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>On November 13th, 2023, Instagram posted a tour of the set by actress <a href="/wiki/Hunter_Schafer" title="Hunter Schafer">Hunter Schafer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>In the film, the Snows' address on their eviction notice was Road of Hope 25, Capitol, Panem.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Fans have speculated that this building became the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> due to its location on the Corso, the number of floors, and its rooftop garden.</li></ul>
