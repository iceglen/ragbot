<p><b>Capitol News</b> was an organization which covered the news in and of interest to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, along with <a href="/wiki/Capitol_TV" title="Capitol TV">Capitol TV</a>. Their coverage was said to be omnipresent.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Typically, the news would cover the weather, local news, and small things such as recipes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_News%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Capitol News station was host to the offices of the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. They had a handful of shabby offices and a control room.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Communicuff" title="Communicuff">communicuffs</a> issued to the <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> worked only either at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> or at the Capitol News station.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a> was a reporter assigned to cover the 10th Hunger Games for Capitol News.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Androcles_Anderson" title="Androcles Anderson">Androcles Anderson</a>, one of the mentors that year, aspired to be a Capitol News reporter like his mother.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> ended up in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>'s monkey house alongside <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for the 10th Hunger Games, cameras from the Capitol News were positioned at either end of the zoo's visitors. In response, Coriolanus looked straight at them, and he and <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> answered Lepidus Malmsey's inquiries. Their story was then projected on several huge public screens around the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After bombs unexpectedly detonated in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, the Capitol News claimed that rebels from any district could have set them off, or even people that escaped from <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> or a cell in the Capitol itself. <a href="/wiki/Lysistrata_Vickers" title="Lysistrata Vickers">Lysistrata Vickers</a> made a statement to one of the reporters, saying that her assigned tribute, <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a>, had thrown his body over her to protect her.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Capitol News also covered the manhunt for <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>, the male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> who had escaped during the bombing.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Satyria_Click" title="Satyria Click">Satyria Click</a>, the Academy's communications professor, took the mentors to the Capitol News station so they could become acquainted with the machinery there.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, Coriolanus was interviewed at the news station on the opening morning about the Games, as they wanted to know about his relationship with Lucy Gray.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> illegally entered the arena, a Capitol News monitor was used to display a split screen with the real feed of the arena next to the darkened version that the public was seeing.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following his <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> cut himself off from human contact, living in his house in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a> and playing Capitol News on his television set 24/7.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_27_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_27-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_News%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Coriolanus Snow had a low opinion of Capitol News. He knew that they liked to embarrass people, if they could, and worried about how they would portray his ending up in the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>. He suspected that they might play footage of him ad nauseam, adding music and silly captions such as "<i>Snow's meltdown!</i>" or make it part of the weather report - "<i>Too hot for Snow!</i>"<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
