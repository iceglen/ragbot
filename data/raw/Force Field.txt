<p><b>Force fields</b> are invisible electric fields set up by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> that are used to prevent <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from escaping the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> and any other places so they won't run away from <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">the Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It tosses anything that hits it back to where it was before, so tributes couldn't commit suicide by jumping off the roof of the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>. It was also used to protect <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a> from tribute attacks.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FForce_field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> won the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> by using the forcefield on the arena's generator to defeat <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> by letting her throw her axe into it and watching it come back and lodge itself in her head. He was at his cliff a sharp drop off on a rocky ledge that he discovered shortly before <a href="/wiki/Maysilee" class="mw-redirect" title="Maysilee">Maysilee</a>'s death.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The explosion of Haymitch's <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">sunflower bomb</a> caused an earthquake that rocked the Capitol, both literally and figuratively.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This stunt crowned Haymitch <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>; however,&#160;<a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>&#160;didn't like his stunt, thus he killed Haymitch's family and girlfriend.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FForce_field%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The first time <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> ever comes in contact with a force field is on the Training Center's roof with <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, who shows her how the force field works by holding out his hand and then sharply withdrawing when it zaps him. Earlier, Peeta had asked <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> why nobody in the Capitol was worried that tributes would try to jump off the roof.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

	</figcaption> </figure><p>While Katniss was preparing for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> in the Training Center, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> showed her the weak spot in the force field between the Gamemakers&#160;and the tributes: a small wavering square in the air, revealing the invisible force field. Katniss assumed that the field was placed there because of her private session the year before, during which she shot an arrow in the direction of the Gamemakers.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the day before their <a href="/wiki/Interview" class="mw-redirect" title="Interview">interview</a>, Katniss and Peeta spent their day on the Training Center's roof, playing a game where one of them would throw an apple into the force field and the other would catch it when it came back.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the 75th Hunger Games, Peeta almost died after being shocked by a force field, which was far stronger than the one on the roof of the Training Center, <sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but luckily <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a> revived him with mouth to mouth resuscitation<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a>, the male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> also hit this force field. However, Blight was killed, as nobody around him knew how to revive him.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss threw nuts to keep track of where the force field was, discovering that it had them trapped in a small, symmetrical dome. <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> also ate the roasted nuts, and Peeta figured out how to cook rodent meat on the force field.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
To destroy the forcefield around the arena, Katniss used her <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow</a> to fire an arrow with Beetee's wire attached to it. The arrow went straight into the weak spot just as the Gamemakers' lightning struck the tree below, leading to the force field exploding<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the remaining victors being carried out of the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
