<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="49th_Hunger_Games">49th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_6_male_%2849th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He managed to survive the Games until he and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> were the only tributes remaining. Wiress had spent the whole Games in a blind spot where no one could find her, so when she finally emerged, the District 6 male leaped at her. However, the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> was a disorienting "Nest of Mirrors", so he misjudged the reflection, cracking his head on the surface and drowning in the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-SR6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
