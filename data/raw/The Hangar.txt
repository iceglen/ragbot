</p><p><b>The Hangar</b> is a special defense area in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> that is located deep underground. This is where all their <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercrafts</a> are kept, and it also contains large amounts of ammunition and weaponry.
</p>
