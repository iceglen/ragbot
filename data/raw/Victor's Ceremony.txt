<p>The <b>Victor's Ceremony</b> was an event in which the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of that year's <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> sat on a throne and watched a recap of the Games they just participated in. They were then <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviewed</a> and, soon after, crowned victor.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%2527s_Ceremony%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>From the 1st to the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, victors were not given any special attention by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>; once they were removed from the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, they were simply sent back to their <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%2527s_Ceremony%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Following his victory in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was held in the Capitol for an indeterminate period of days before it was finally decided to present him as the victor. He was presented by <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, who described the Games as having been historic, unparalleled and unforgettable. Haymitch was freed of his shackles and presented to the audience, who gave him a standing ovation filled with mad cheers and hot tears. They called for him to show off the scar he had acquired in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="50th Hunger Games arena">arena</a>, which he did. A heavily-edited recap of the events of the Games was then presented, carefully omitted everything Haymitch and the other tributes had done which would reflect negatively upon the Capitol. Afterwards, the <a href="/wiki/Horn_of_Plenty" title="Horn of Plenty">anthem</a> played as President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> descended from a crystal platform and placed a golden crown upon Haymitch's head. "I guess Snow lands on top," commented Haymitch, to which Snow replied "Enjoy your homecoming."<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_25_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%2527s_Ceremony%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was dressed in a yellow&#160;dress.&#160;<a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> wore a yellow suit, as he needed to match Katniss. Their outfits represented candlelight, sticking to the fire theme, but in a more innocent way, to symbolize that Katniss was a young girl who was madly in love, instead of a rebellious victor like some Districts thought. Instead of sitting in a throne or two thrones, they sat in a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/love_seat" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:love seat">love seat</a>, since this was the first and only year the Games crowned two victors. President Coriolanus Snow gave the crown a twist and it separated into two halves that Katniss and Peeta could both wear. He wordlessly crowned Peeta first, and then Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictor%2527s_Ceremony%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i> film, there were two separate crowns which were gold and based on the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/laurel_wreath" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:laurel wreath">laurel wreath</a>, a symbol of martial victory in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ancient_Rome" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:ancient Rome">ancient Rome</a>. Snow crowned Katniss first, then examined her <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a>, which he did not do in the book. Katniss also saw the "twist of his false and angry smile". Katniss stated that she could smell blood from Snow's mouth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
