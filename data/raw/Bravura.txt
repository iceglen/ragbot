<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBravura%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> was a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> that year,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so he either mentored this boy or his <a href="/wiki/Tippet" title="Tippet">district partner</a>.

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade">Tribute Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBravura%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the District 4 tributes were first seen in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>. They both wore light blue outfits with blue crowns that looked like splashing water, adorned with pearls and bubbles. The shoulders of their outfits were pinned together with starfish.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBravura%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the novel, the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> looked at <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> with contempt during training, and they showed off their skills in order to intimidate other tributes. The Careers all received <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training scores</a> between 8 and 10, and he received a 8.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, he stood between <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> and Katniss as <a href="/wiki/Atala" title="Atala">Atala</a> explained the rules. He also stood behind <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> and looked up at Rue on the rope course. Later, he appeared during a montage as <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> read out training scores, but his score was not stated or shown.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBravura%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the film, Bravura waited in line with the other tributes to go on stage for <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>. He wore a black suit with white shoes and a white tie.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBravura%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e9/District_4_Male_Portrait.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20160313002226" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Bravura's portrait in the night sky." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e9/District_4_Male_Portrait.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160313002226" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="112" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District 4 Male Portrait.jpg" data-image-key="District_4_Male_Portrait.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District_4_Male_Portrait.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Bravura's portrait in the night sky.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Night has just come when I hear the anthem that precedes the death recap. ... The first to appear is the girl from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>. ... Then the boy from 4. I didn’t expect that one, usually all the Careers make it through the first day.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss expressing surprise at his death<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Bravura was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>. It is unknown how he died in the book, but Katniss was surprised to see his face in the sky on the first day because he was a Career, and they typically lasted longer.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/640px-Worst_sleepover_ever.png/revision/latest?cb=20121227234506" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Bravura watching the bloodbath." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c7/640px-Worst_sleepover_ever.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20121227234506" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="100" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="640px-Worst sleepover ever.png" data-image-key="640px-Worst_sleepover_ever.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:640px-Worst_sleepover_ever.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Bravura watching the bloodbath.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the movie, he ran to the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> for supplies when the gong sounded. After a few glimpses of him hiding behind crates, he tried to escape with the supplies he'd grabbed only to meet <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, who attacked and killed him with a <a href="/wiki/Sword" class="mw-redirect" title="Sword">sword</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In an early version of the film script, District 4 was still considered a Career district and the male tribute stepped off of his pedestal too early at the start of the Games. This set off <a href="/wiki/Land_mine" class="mw-redirect" title="Land mine">land mines</a> that killed him instantly.<sup id="cite_ref-script_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-script-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBravura%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the films, he is tied with <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> as the smallest and youngest tribute. He has blue eyes and a blonde afro hairstyle.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBravura%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/D4Boy_DIP.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20121227234845" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tribute Identification Card" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="D4Boy DIP.jpg" data-image-key="D4Boy_DIP.jpg" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/D4Boy_DIP.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20121227234845"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/D4Boy_DIP.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20121227234845" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tribute Identification Card" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/D4Boy_DIP.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20121227234845" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="D4Boy DIP.jpg" data-image-key="D4Boy_DIP.jpg" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/45/D4Boy_DIP.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20121227234845"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:D4Boy_DIP.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Tribute Identification Card</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<ul><li>In the film, his odds of winning were 15–1, he weighed 65 pounds, and was 12 years old.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the film, he was the last tribute to die at the Cornucopia bloodbath. <a href="/wiki/Heddle" title="Heddle">Heddle</a> was the first death since the bloodbath, and when she died, the scoreboard behind Caesar indicated that this boy had placed 12th.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Unlike the rest of the tributes (except for the <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">District 9 male</a>), his casting is probably a deliberate choice to contrast with the other Career tributes.</li>
<li>Bravura was a Career in the book, but not the film.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Name">Name</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBravura%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>His name was not revealed in the books or films, but it appeared in the previous version of the script as Bravura.<sup id="cite_ref-script_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-script-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This name refers to the intensity and strength of both the sea and people.</li></ul>
