<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Process">Process</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Eligibility">Eligibility</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Once a person's name was called in the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>, other people of reaping age (12-18)<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and of the tribute's same sex had the opportunity to volunteer in their place. Males could volunteer for the male tribute, and females could volunteer for the female, but not vice-versa. Once a volunteer came forward, any objections from the reaped person or attempts by them to re-volunteer would be ignored, as the volunteer's word took precedent.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Procedure">Procedure</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Typically, once a person's name was called, they would proceed to the stage and the <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a> would ask for volunteers. Someone could then come forward, verbally declare their intent to volunteer, and take the stage in the reaped person's place. Volunteering was "complicated"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> in the <a href="/wiki/Career" class="mw-redirect" title="Career">Career</a> districts of <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, where future tributes trained to take part in the Games;<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> however, the exact procedure there is unknown.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Attitudes">Attitudes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The act of volunteering was rare in most <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>, largely because most people believed that being reaped was a death sentence. In <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> districts, participation was seen as a great honor, so volunteering was far more commonplace. Reasons to volunteer varied from person to person; in Career districts, the goal was usually glory, but elsewhere, volunteers occasionally came forward to protect the reaped child.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Development">Development</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>While planning the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> came up with the idea to construct a neighborhood of lavish mansions, known as the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a>, in each district in order to "tempt a better class of tributes to possibly volunteer." Other incentives included a monetary prize for the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>, and food parcels for their entire district.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Over time, Districts 1, 2, and 4 formed a culture of training and volunteering for the Games, and their perennial appearance earned them the name "Careers".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="62nd_Hunger_Games">62nd Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>According to <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> from District 2 volunteered for and won the <a href="/wiki/62nd_Hunger_Games" title="62nd Hunger Games">62nd Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CC_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CC-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> volunteered from District 2.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>'s name was pulled from the girls' <a href="/wiki/Reaping_balls" title="Reaping balls">reaping ball</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and after a moment of shock, her older sister <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> jumped in to volunteer— the first tribute from 12 to do so in decades.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/57/Marvel_interview.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120418212130" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Marvel during his interview." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/57/Marvel_interview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120418212130" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Marvel interview.jpg" data-image-key="Marvel_interview.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Marvel_interview.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Marvel during his interview.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, Katniss was her district's first volunteer ever, and District 4 was not a Career district, making it less likely that their tributes volunteered to participate. Additionally, in deleted scenes from the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interview</a> sequence, <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> spoke about having wanted to volunteer for years, and <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> introduced <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> as "another volunteer".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVolunteer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, tributes were reaped from the existing pool of victors,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> most of whom were over the age of 18. As a result, the usual rule about being of reaping age was ignored, but only other victors could volunteer to participate.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Volunteers included Brutus from District 2; <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> from District 4, in place of a "hysterical" <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and District 12's <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> on behalf of <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the reaping recaps, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> commented that "<a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a> never could stay out of a fight", implying that he may have volunteered as <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>'s male tribute.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
