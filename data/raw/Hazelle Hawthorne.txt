<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHazelle_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Hazelle lived and worked in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. Her <a href="/wiki/Mr._Hawthorne" title="Mr. Hawthorne">husband</a> died in the same <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal mining</a> accident that killed <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">father</a> one January,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> when her oldest child, Gale, was fourteen.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Hazelle "worked her fingers to the bone" as a washerwoman. She would collect people's dirty laundry, wash it, and then return it to the owner. In the winter, her hands got so red and cracked that they would bleed without a salve from <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Hazelle's business did rather well until Gale was caught poaching and was brutally whipped.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Hazelle came to the Katniss's house in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a>, where Gale was being treated, and comforted him through his pain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Soon afterward, Posy contracted the measles. Afraid to associate with the family because of the whipping, people stopped using Hazelle's services, and Rory had to sign up for <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a>. However, Katniss soon convinced <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> to take Hazelle on as a housekeeper, earning her extra money and increasing Haymitch's standard of living.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Hazelle and her family were among the few people who survived the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, and they were evacuated to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHazelle_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Hazelle was devoted to her family. Her husband died in a horrible mine accident, the same one that killed Katniss' father, leaving her alone at home with her three children. She was already pregnant with Posy and was only a few days away from giving birth. Gale, the oldest, helped take care of the family, bringing in food and money by hunting with Katniss. Her sons, Gale and Rory, also helped by applying for <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tessera</a> when they turned twelve, although it is mentioned that Hazelle and Gale did not want Rory to take tesserae. Vick and Posy, her youngest children, were her reason for working so hard. When her only daughter Posy fell ill with measles, Hazelle worked extremely hard to nurse her back to health, and thankfully, Posy recovered. When Gale was whipped and taken to the Everdeen house, Hazelle went to stay with him. It is known that Hazelle and the rest of her children escaped the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">District 12 bombing</a>. They are seen in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. In fact, her youngest child, Posy, is the one to comfort <a href="/wiki/Octavia" title="Octavia">Octavia</a> after she is released from <a href="/wiki/President_Coin" class="mw-redirect" title="President Coin">President Coin</a>'s capture with Katniss' other <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylists</a>, saying that she looks very pretty.
</p>
