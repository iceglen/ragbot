<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVitus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Vitus grew up in the Capitol. During the time of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, he was a University student and had two more years until graduation, meaning that he and Proserpina were assigned a less-desired district to work with.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVitus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Vitus and Proserpina entered Haymitch's cubicle to begin grooming him for the Games. Vitus reassured him about <a href="/wiki/Magno_Stift" title="Magno Stift">Magno Stift</a> being the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> stylist. He and Proserpina photographed Haymitch, then shaved his body, trimmed his nails, and rubbed goo into his hair. After taking photos of the result, Vitus went to help his mother decorate for a <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> party.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In preparation for the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, Vitus and Proserpina went to Magno's apartment to see the costumes he had prepared, but were shocked to find him vomiting and delirious. Frantic, they ran to find Haymitch.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Proserpina phoned her sister, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>, who brought clothes.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-Games">Post-Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVitus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the Victor's Ceremony, Vitus and Proserpina fussed over each other's makeup. When Caesar Flickerman introduced the team, they ascended to the stage on a metal plate, clapping for themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-SR25_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR25-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At the after-party, Vitus and Proserpina became tipsy, stopping by to congratulate Haymitch on his victory.<sup id="cite_ref-SR26_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR26-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Effie arrived in District 12 on the morning of Haymitch's <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, she had to send him to the tub to clean him up before Vitus and Proserpina were willing to attend to him.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVitus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Vitus had metal apples studding his cheeks.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He never wore black.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVitus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Vitus's grandfather was a <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebel</a> sympathizer, and Vitus was ashamed of him.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He also had a mother that was enthusiastic about the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
