<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>About ten years before the events of <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> "essentially redesigned the underground network that transmits all the programming" in the nation.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath_of_the_74th_Hunger_Games">Aftermath of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, the people of Districts 3, <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a> expressed "genuine elation" at the sight of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, "and under the elation, fury," crying out for vengeance.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Also, by the day before the reading of the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quell</a> card, the district was in revolt, resulting in a shortage of electronic gadgets for Capitol citizens.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>At the time of the third Quarter Quell, the tributes from Districts 3, 4, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">7</a>, 8, and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> were all involved in a rebel plan to protect Katniss and break out of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>Beetee's contributions were instrumental to the rebels' Airtime Assault: the airing of <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a> in the districts and, later, in the Capitol as well, to inform and energize rebels nationwide.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Once Katniss's propo after the hospital <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_8" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 8">bombing of District 8</a> aired across Panem, reinvigorated rebels were able to take control of Districts 3 and 11, with "inroads in several other districts as well".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Beetee also played an important strategic role in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_District_2" title="Battle of District 2">Battle of District 2</a> alongside <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, devising the plan to bury <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a> with avalanches.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This strategy would lead to a rebel victory in the district, decidedly turning the tide of the war against the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, Beetee and Gale were also the minds that created the strategy later implemented in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="City Circle bombing">City Circle bombing</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>All but one of District 3's victors were killed during the war and the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>. Beetee was the only survivor.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>District 3's traditional <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> comes in the form of bite-sized, square-shaped rolls.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Industry">Industry</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3><p>
Children in District 3 learn audio editing techniques in <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:2_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> appear to have practical skills with wiring and programming various electronic devices.This makes it likely that they, unlike children in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:4_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are expected to work in their district's industry before they turn 18. Additionally, despite District 6 being the district responsible for transportation, Katniss mentions that District 3 manufactures automobiles. Similar to <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, District 3 has many factories making it very polluted.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 157px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/60/District_3_Tributes-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20190531025513" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 3 Tributes, 74th Hunger Games" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/60/District_3_Tributes-0.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/157?cb=20190531025513" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="157" height="173" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District 3 Tributes-0.png" data-image-key="District_3_Tributes-0.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District_3_Tributes-0.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">District 3 Tributes, 74th Hunger Games</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_tributes">Known tributes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Due to District 3's industry, tributes are often clever and skilled with electronics. While this is not always useful in the brutal, mock-natural environments of the Hunger Games <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, it can have advantages. For example, <a href="/wiki/Teslee" title="Teslee">Teslee</a> rewired <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gift</a> delivery <a href="/wiki/Drone" title="Drone">drones</a> to swarm <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>; Beetee rigged an electrical trap with wire<sup id="cite_ref-:4_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> that electrocuted six tributes at once;<sup id="cite_ref-:5_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the male tribute in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> was able to rewire land mines from the tributes' launch platforms to protect the <a href="/wiki/Careers" class="mw-redirect" title="Careers">Careers</a>' supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> From the tributes we've seen, on average, female tributes place 9th and male tributes place 5th.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victors">Victors</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, Beetee and Wiress were shown as being District 3's only living victors at the <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaping</a> for the third Quarter Quell.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> - Victor of the <a href="/wiki/34th_Hunger_Games" title="34th Hunger Games">34th Hunger Games</a>, and tribute and survivor of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. After the war, he was the last surviving victor from District 3.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

Died on the second day when <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> slit her throat.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 3 is located in the eastern part of the continent, centered around the states of Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, and Minnesota. It is bordered by Districts <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a> (to the east), <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a> (to the south), <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a> (to the west), and <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a> (to the north). The district contains many factories for the production of electronic devices.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_3%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Beetee, Wiress and the District 3 male (74th HG) were all described by Katniss as having ashen skin, so this is presumed to be a typical trait of District 3.</li>
<li>It is noted that District 3 has strong expertise in both medicine and engineering.</li>
<li>Although it's still one of the wealthier districts, District 3's living attitude is poor, and is the poorer of the wealthier districts, and typically don't do well in the Games.</li>
<li>According to <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a>, District 3 is located directly to the west of District 12.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.pn, District 3 has twelve factories in operation, and a population of 195,329, making it the third-largest district, in terms of population. District has at least one medicine factory.</li>
<li>It was one of the first districts to rebel during the 2nd rebellion, along with Districts 1, 8, and 11. District 3 was the 2nd - 4th district to pry from Capitol rules and eventually, they became the third district to do so.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 3 claims 62,991 in tesserae, making it the fifth richest district.
<ul><li>If the population is 195,329 and they claim 62,991 tesserae, it would equal to 0.322 tesserae per person, making it the 6th richest district.</li></ul></li>
<li>People from District 3 were referred to as "eggheads" by the citizens of <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be palomino beige (#ba7e48 – <span style="background-color:#ba7e48; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 3's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be olive drab (#6B8E23 - <span style="background-color:#6B8E23; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear for the 50th Hunger Games was electric blue (#7D9FF - <span style="background-color:#7DF9FF; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
