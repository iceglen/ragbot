<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>We seem particularly riveting to the pair from District 6, who are known morphling addicts. Both bone thin, with sagging yellowish skin. They can't tear their overlarge eyes away...</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> on the morphlings.<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The <b>morphlings</b> were two <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a>, one <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 male (75th HG)">male</a> and one <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 female (75th HG)">female</a>. They each won a previous unknown <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> and participated in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. As <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a>, they were likely given the responsibility of <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentoring</a> other tributes from District 6.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphlings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The morphlings won two unknown Hunger Games. These victors had turned to a narcotic pain killer called <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> to cope with the horrors of their previous Games and were thus in very poor physical condition.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The morphling had made their skin sag a bit and turn a vulgar, yellow color<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Third_Quarter_Quell">Third Quarter Quell</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphlings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Morphlings_reaping.png/revision/latest?cb=20140323224641" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The morphlings at the reaping." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Morphlings_reaping.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140323224641" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="108" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Morphlings reaping.png" data-image-key="Morphlings_reaping.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Morphlings_reaping.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The morphlings at the reaping.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The two of them were quite fascinated by painting, as they spent most of their time training in the camouflage station painting each other during the training days. This likely is  what they used to present themselves to the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>. This left <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> with very few supplies left over, and a colossal mess when it was his turn.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Before the Games, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch</a> approached <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>, <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>, <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cecelia" title="Cecelia">Cecelia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Woof" title="Woof">Woof</a>, and the morphlings to make an alliance and keep Katniss and Peeta alive<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as he knew he could trust them. He did not tell either Katniss or Peeta about these previous alliances but sent them hidden messages<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. They later joined hands with the rest of the tributes as a sign of strength and <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Deaths">Deaths</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphlings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the start of the Games, the morphlings reached the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> shortly after Finnick and Katniss (because Mags swam to Katniss while Finnick was retrieving Peeta)<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. During the bloodbath, the male was killed but the female escaped<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<p>Later when Peeta, Katniss and Finnick were attacked by <a href="/wiki/Monkey_mutt" title="Monkey mutt">monkey mutts</a>, Peeta was about to be bitten by one when the female morphling jumped in front of him, and she took a lethal bite to the chest instead.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 239px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/32/6m.png/revision/latest?cb=20140128192916" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Male morphling's death portrait." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="152" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="6m.png" data-image-key="6m.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/32/6m.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20140128192916"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/32/6m.png/revision/latest?cb=20140128192916" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Male morphling's death portrait." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/32/6m.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20140128192916" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="152" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="6m.png" data-image-key="6m.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/32/6m.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20140128192916"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:6m.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Male morphling's death portrait.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After Peeta finished off the monkeys, he took her body to the beach where he comforted her by telling her to look at the colors of the sunrise. Katniss observed that she was shaking and didn't know whether it was the shock from the attack, or withdrawal from her morphling, and she soon died in Peeta's arms. After she bled to death, Peeta took her corpse to the water to let it drift off until a hovercraft came to take it away.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He later questioned why she sacrificed herself for him<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMorphlings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The female morphling's death was similar to <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>'s<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>'s<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as all were comforted. All three received peaceful last moments due to a <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> victor (Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, Haymitch<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and Peeta<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>).</li>
<li>Despite the fact they are extremely disabled from years of morphling addiction, they must have been still capable enough for <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> to trust them with the knowledge of the rebellion<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li></ul>
