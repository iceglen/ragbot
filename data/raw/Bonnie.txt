</p><p><b>Bonnie</b> was a girl from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>. Along with <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>, she ran away from her district after the uprising in search of <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. During their journey, the two of them were found in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> by <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBonnie%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In District 8, Bonnie attended <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a> and was taught by <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>. Right after school, she worked a 4-hour shift on the loading dock at a factory where they made <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> uniforms. She spent months securing two uniforms that were intended for Twill and her husband to escape in when it was time.
</p><p>Once the <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">uprising in District 8</a> began, the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> appeared to be under rebel control, until <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> deployed Peacekeepers "by the thousands" to take back the district. The <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> were beaten down and defeated. As punishment, as well as to regain order in the district, the Capitol placed a full lockdown state on District 8 for approximately one week, with no food or coal. Finally, when they were allowed to go back to work, Bonnie and Twill were late getting from the school to the factory after being unable to cross a bombed street. The factory was bombed before they arrived, killing Twill's husband and Bonnie's entire family, but leaving the two of them alive. 
</p><p>Bonnie and Twill disguised themselves in the Peacekeeper's uniforms, Bonnie taking the one meant for Twill's husband, and they boarded a boxcar headed to <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a>. After fleeing the train, they traveled on foot to the woods surrounding <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. However, Bonnie twisted her ankle there, which made them stop and forced her to use a branch as a crutch.
</p><p>There, they met <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>. The two of them informed Katniss that they intended to go to District 13, because they believed there were others there who supported the <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>. While in the cement shack, Katniss offered Bonnie a cheese bun, and she replied, "Is this all for me?" This reminded Katniss of <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, when they were eating <a href="/wiki/Groosling" title="Groosling">groosling</a> drumsticks. As they made tea, Bonnie and Twill informed Katniss about their story and were taught survival skills.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>, Bonnie and Twill did not make it to District 13, so their fates are unknown, but Katniss assumed that they had died.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_appearance">Physical appearance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBonnie%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bonnie looked about Katniss's age in <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>. She had a strawberry birthmark over one of her chocolate brown eyes and had very pale skin. Bonnie had a slight frame and was seen wearing an ill-fitting Peacekeeper's uniform several sizes too large.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
