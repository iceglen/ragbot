</p><p><i><b>The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</b></i> is a film adapted from the <a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">prequel novel of the same name</a>. It is the sixth installment in the film franchise, set to theatrically release on November 20, 2026.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It will explore the world of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> 24 years prior to events of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i></a>, beginning on the morning of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, also known as the Second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Production">Production</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The book and the movie adaptation for <i>Sunrise</i> were both announced on June 6, 2024.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Nina_Jacobson" title="Nina Jacobson">Nina Jacobson</a> returned as a producer alongside Brad Simpson<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, and <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a> would direct.<sup id="cite_ref-Comic_Book_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Comic_Book-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In response to a fan-posted rumor that the film was slated to begin shooting in January 2025, two months before the book's release, Nina Jacobson stated that the book would be published months before production on the film begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Clarifies_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Clarifies-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In an interview with ComicBook.com, director Francis Lawrence discussed the challenges of casting a young <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> for the film, comparing to the process of ultimately casting <a href="/wiki/Tom_Blyth" title="Tom Blyth">Tom Blyth</a> as a young <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>. He stated that his thinking was "How am I going to cast a young person who is believably going to become <a href="/wiki/Donald_Sutherland" title="Donald Sutherland">Donald Sutherland</a>? Who’s going to have that sort of intelligence, sophistication, a little irreverence, gravitas, all of that. Who is going to embody and be believable in the fact that he’s going to turn into that guy and become the president of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>? And it’s the same thing [with Haymitch]."<sup id="cite_ref-Comic_Book_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Comic_Book-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Filming for the title was originally set to begin on January 31, 2025 in Los Angeles.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The location was moved to Germany and filming was further delayed to July 1, 2025.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On April 1, 2025, it was reported that the film's screenplay would be written by <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Ray_(screenwriter)">Billy Ray</a><sup id="cite_ref-Ray_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ray-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, who did rewrites of the initial script for the original <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>; this script was ultimately replaced by a new one written by Collins and director <a href="/wiki/Gary_Ross" title="Gary Ross">Gary Ross</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ditches_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ditches-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As of early April 2025, casting was still underway for the character of Haymitch Abernathy, with Francis Lawrence reporting that the process had been, to some degree, handcuffed by the book not being released at the time.<sup id="cite_ref-Handcuffed_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Handcuffed-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On April 23, 2025, <a href="/wiki/Joseph_Zada" title="Joseph Zada">Joseph Zada</a> and <a href="/wiki/Whitney_Peak" title="Whitney Peak">Whitney Peak</a> were the first to be cast as <a day, <a href="/wiki/Mckenna_Grace" title="Mckenna Grace">Mckenna Grace</a> was cast as <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Maysilee_Cast_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maysilee_Cast-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Jesse_Plemons" title="Jesse joined the cast as young <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a><sup id="cite_ref-Wiress_Cast_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wiress_Cast-13"><span young <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Louella_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Louella-15"><span <h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>This marks the first time in the franchise that both the book and film adaptation were announced the same date. The <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">first film</a> was not announced until all books in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">original trilogy</a> had been released. Lionsgate was already in talks to produce <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> before the <a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">novel</a> was published, but the film was not officially announced until April 2020, the month before the book's release in May.</li>
<li>Both <a href="/wiki/Jesse_Plemons" title="Jesse Plemons">Jesse Plemons</a> and <a href="/wiki/Kelvin_Harrison_Jr." title="Kelvin Harrison Jr.">Kelvin Harrison Jr.</a> have co-starred with the actor who played the older version of their character in the original film trilogy and played the original actor's son in a movie.
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Jesse_Plemons" title="Jesse Plemons">Jesse Plemons</a>, who plays young <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, co-starred with the late <a href="/wiki/Philip_Seymour_Hoffman" title="Philip Seymour Hoffman">Philip Seymour Hoffman</a> (who played Plutarch in the original film trilogy) in the movie <i>The Master</i> (2012). Jesse played Philip Seymour Hoffman's son in <i>The Master</i>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Kelvin_Harrison_Jr." title="Kelvin Harrison Jr.">Kelvin Harrison Jr.</a>, who plays young <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, co-starred with <a href="/wiki/Jeffrey_Wright" title="Jeffrey Wright">Jeffrey Wright</a> (who played Beetee in the original film trilogy) in the movie <i>Monster</i> (2018). Harrison played Wright's son in <i>Monster</i>.</li></ul></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Merrilee_Undersee" title="Merrilee Undersee">Merrilee Undersee</a> is confirmed by <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Ray_(screenwriter)">Billy Ray</a> to be included in the movie. Fans held questions about whether or not she would be included due to her daughter, <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a>, not being in any of the films.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The filming location for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> arena is at <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somiedo_Natural_Park">Somiedo Natural Park</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
