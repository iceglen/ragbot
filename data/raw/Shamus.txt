<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShamus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> first visited the Covey's home in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, Shamus was in <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a> with <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>. After Coriolanus and Lucy Gray kissed, Shamus tugged on Coriolanus's collar, as she needed milking. Lucy Gray then hitched Shamus to a post on the front post to keep her in place.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, <a href="/wiki/Maude_Ivory" class="mw-redirect" title="Maude Ivory">Maude Ivory</a> took Sejanus out to milk Shamus instead, as she disapproved of Sejanus talking to <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe" class="mw-redirect" title="Billy Taupe">Billy Taupe</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Shamus was said to be able to give a gallon of milk a day at her best, and the members of the Covey were attempting to skim enough cream for butter.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> created a butter churn for Maude Ivory's birthday, but the Covey weren't sure if it would work, since goat's milk didn't separate the same way cow milk did. Sejanus also stated that Shamus's milk reminded him of his childhood in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShamus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Shamus is noted in the book as being a boy's name.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It's an alternate spelling of the name Seamus, which is a variation of James.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FShamus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Shamus is one of two named goats in the books, both female; the other goat is <a href="/wiki/Lady" title="Lady">Lady</a>, belonging to <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>.</li>
<li>Her name has the same root as <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s: Hamish is also a variation of Seamus or James.</li></ul>
