<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerchandise%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The merchandise produced for this film includes:
</p>
<ul><li>Bookmarks</li>
<li>A board game</li>
<li>A new mockingjay pin</li>
<li>Backpacks</li>
<li>Bags</li></ul>
<p>Some websites were created to further more promote the movie in any way they could, such as Capitol.pn, where a user could take a quiz to find out their district and connect with Facebook to learn the latest details on the film. Another site titled <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a> shared details on the latest film.
</p><p>China Glaze nail polish line also created a new brand, "Capitol Colours", where each color represented each district's industry to promote the movie.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Chocolate_merchandise">Chocolate merchandise</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerchandise%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Frame" style="width: 270px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f6/Th-11-.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140911004719" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The President Snow chocolate bar." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f6/Th-11-.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140911004719" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="270" height="300" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Th-11-.jpg" data-image-key="Th-11-.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Th-11-.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The President Snow chocolate bar.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The chocolates are made after a specific character including <a href="/wiki/Katniss" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Katniss">Katniss</a>, <a href="/wiki/Effie" class="mw-redirect" title="Effie">Effie</a>, and <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>. The wrappers have different colors with the name and text in a silver font.
</p><p>They have a variety of characters to choose from, along with a different flavor for each one. The district chocolate is the same, except these being a darker color and the district emblem on the front side of the chocolate. They range from district 6's peanut cluster to district 3's chipotle chocolate and district eight's beef jerky and smoked mesquite bar. The chocolate was made by the Vosges candy company to help promote Catching Fire. 
</p>
