</p><p><b>Leigha Hancock</b> is an American actress and stuntwoman specifically cast to portray the training and fight scenes of a tribute that fell during the initial <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a> serving the role as the <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">female</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in&#160;<a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> her feature film debut. She has appeared on <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Make_It_or_Break_It" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Make It or Break It">Make It or Break It</a></i> as a stunt double.
</p><p>She is a graduate of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:North Carolina State University">North Carolina State University</a>, where she was on the gymnastics team.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
