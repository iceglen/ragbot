<p><b>The Capitol</b> of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> is a technologically advanced metropolis and home to the nation's elites. The Capitol is also the colloquial name for the ruling regime of Panem. As the seat of power in Panem, this city rules the nation's thirteen <a href="/wiki/Panem#Districts" title="Panem">districts</a> (twelve after The <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>). <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">The Hunger Games</a> are held within its city limits (protected by anti-aircraft guns and multiple launch rocket systems, or MLRS's).
</p><p>Prior to the Second Rebellion, the Capitol was a tyrannical dictatorship, led by <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> (before his death) and previously <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a>, and held total political and economic dominance over Panem, enforcing its rule through an army of <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>, capital punishment, propaganda, the fear of nuclear devastation (like District 13), and the Hunger Games. It is also the only major city mentioned in the trilogy. The Capitol is known for the luxurious, decadent lifestyles enjoyed by its citizens. Following the events of <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Paylor" class="mw-redirect" title="Paylor">Paylor</a> becomes the new president, and Panem is transformed into a constitutional republic.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location">Location</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 345px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/52/Capitol.02.png/revision/latest?cb=20240126050245" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Capitol 10 years after the end of the rebellion." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/52/Capitol.02.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/345?cb=20240126050245" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="345" height="145" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol.02.png" data-image-key="Capitol.02.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol.02.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Capitol 10 years after the end of the rebellion.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>It is located in the western, mountainous section of former North America. Proximity to both the mountains to the east and the large body of water to the west suggests it could somewhere be near the location of present-day Salt Lake City, Utah. The Rockies also act as a natural barrier and have served the Capitol well, allowing their air force to bomb the <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">rebels</a> who attempted to invade the city during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History_and_founding">History and founding</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Much is unknown about when the Capitol was first established, however it is noted that <a href="/wiki/Trajan_Heavensbee" title="Trajan Heavensbee">Trajan Heavensbee</a> is renowned as the 'Father of Panem', and as such is presumed to have been highly influential in the establishment of the country and Capitol. Influential families such as the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families" title="Capitol families">Phipps</a> were also noted to be key contributors to the founding of the city. Eventually over time, the most affluent families moved over to the Capitol, as the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Snow_family" title="Capitol families">Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Crane_family" title="Capitol families">Crane family</a> were both noted to be a part of an elite circle of the old guard of the wealthy.  
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 343px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/43/Capitol_.png/revision/latest?cb=20240126050128" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Capitol during the reconstruction period 10 years after the end of the Dark Days." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="343" height="142" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Capitol .png" data-image-key="Capitol_.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/43/Capitol_.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/343?cb=20240126050128" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/43/Capitol_.png/revision/latest?cb=20240126050128" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Capitol during the reconstruction period 10 years after the end of the Dark Days." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/43/Capitol_.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/343?cb=20240126050128" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="343" height="142" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol .png" data-image-key="Capitol_.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/43/Capitol_.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/343?cb=20240126050128" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Capitol during the reconstruction period 10 years after the end of the Dark Days.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>It's presumed that many Capitol residents have made a life in the Capitol due to the immense wealth, influence, and power they acquired from the districts, as many Capitol residents are noted to hold assets within the Districts. For example, the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Moss_family" title="Capitol families">Moss family</a> works in the agriculture department, the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Creed_family" title="Capitol families">Creed family</a> works with timber in <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Crane_family" title="Capitol families">Crane family</a> on transportation and travel, owning assets of hotels within <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> and the Snow family owning laboratories within <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. Citizens from the districts were still eligible for citizenship within the advanced city up to right after the Dark Days, as the Plinth family had earned a life in total wealth and power by deciding to side with the Capitol during the war within <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>. At this early point of the country's history, citizens of the Capitol could freely move throughout the districts, as the tourism industry seemed to have flourished as proven by the Crane's family considerable wealth they earned from their hotels. People such as the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> were also free to travel across district borders.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Dark_Days">Dark Days</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a></i></center></div>
<p>The catalyst for the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> is unknown, but it was a civil war between the districts and the Capitol spanning three years. It ended 74 years before the original trilogy.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 280px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/71/Rebel_attack_on_the_Capitol.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200803150351" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Capitol being attacked by rebel forces." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="280" height="114" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Rebel attack on the Capitol.jpeg" data-image-key="Rebel_attack_on_the_Capitol.jpeg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/71/Rebel_attack_on_the_Capitol.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/280?cb=20200803150351" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/71/Rebel_attack_on_the_Capitol.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200803150351" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Capitol being attacked by rebel forces." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/71/Rebel_attack_on_the_Capitol.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/280?cb=20200803150351" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="280" height="114" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Rebel attack on the Capitol.jpeg" data-image-key="Rebel_attack_on_the_Capitol.jpeg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/71/Rebel_attack_on_the_Capitol.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/280?cb=20200803150351" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Rebel_attack_on_the_Capitol.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Capitol being attacked by rebel forces.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Although much of what transpired is unknown, the Capitol was subjected to numerous air raids, and rebel forces in <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> caused routine blackouts that made sirens unreliable. The Capitol was under siege for the final two years of the war as rebels halted food supplies to the city. This led to mass starvation and widespread poverty, as many families were forced to sell their goods including furniture and clothing to the black market to scrape by. In rare instances, some citizens resorted to cannibalism.
</p><p>At some unknown point in the midst of the rebellion, rabies had managed to spread rapidly into the Capitol. With doctors, facilities and supply lines badly affected by bombing prior and stretched thin due to the ongoing war, citizens were unlikely to be provided with adequate access to healthcare. Snow theorized that the virus strain most likely came from a coyote from the mountains, starved dogs, or bats. Due to the poor level of healthcare, the virus strain developed at an unprecedented speed, killing over a dozen capitol citizens before finally being brought under control by a vaccine.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  
</p><p>Near the end of the rebellion, the Capitol had managed a victory against the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> when they attempted to scale the mountains that serve as natural protection for the city. The Capitol's Air Force was able to kill off a large number of ground troops and destroy large amounts of equipment, giving them the upper hand in the rebellion.  
</p><p>The war became stagnant 3 years into the conflict, as both <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> and the Capitol held their <a href="/wiki/Nuclear_weaponry" title="Nuclear weaponry">nuclear arsenals</a> pointed at one another. However, both parties diplomatically agreed to sign a peace treaty to officially end the conflict. This allowed the Capitol to regain full control of the other twelve districts, and District 13 to secede from Capitol rule, establishing its status as an independent nation. However, this decision was unbeknownst to the rest of nation, as the Capitol publicly announced that District 13 had been annihilated by the Capitol itself, and was left radioactive and uninhabitable. Even 75 years later, the Capitol continued using such propaganda to intimidate the districts and discourage rebellion. 
</p><p>After the rebellion, under <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a>, the Hunger Games was announced by <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, credited to <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, serving as an annual event to remind the Districts of the cost of their rebellion. After the end of the Dark Days, Panem began its reconstruction period to redevelop the country, especially the Capitol. 
</p>

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Gem of Panem, Mighty city, Through the ages you shine anew.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Lyrics from the Capitol anthem, "<a href="/wiki/Gem_of_Panem" title="Gem of Panem">Gem of Panem</a>"<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>As the wealthiest of Panem, Capitol citizens tend to possess lucrative jobs, such as fashion designers, news reporters, bank managers, doctors, scientists, university professors, or working directly for the government. Capitol citizens are noted to possess high-ranking and integral roles and occupations over Panem as a whole. For example, <a href="/wiki/Iphigenia_Moss" title="Iphigenia Moss">Iphigenia Moss</a>'s father worked in the agriculture department and as such, determines and regulates the flow of food within Panem. It's also noted that the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Crane_family" title="Capitol families">Crane family</a> worked in the travel industry, owning luxurious hotels. The <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Creed_family" title="Capitol families">Creed family</a> oversaw the production of timber within <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>. <a href="/wiki/Pliny_Harrington" title="Pliny Harrington">Pliny Harrington</a>’s father was also a naval commander within <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> and former President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>'s family had assets for laboratories within District 13. As many Capitol residents live in considerable luxury due to their assets within the Districts, this deepening inequality and blatant exploitation may have played a part in the rising tension between the Capitol and Districts.
</p><p>Capitol citizens possess a sense of superiority over District citizens, viewing District people as savages more akin to animals, as proven when the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> was dumped into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>. Capitol citizens also see themselves as more civilized and intelligent than their district counterparts and typically lacked empathy toward District citizens. Their disdain toward District citizens can even motivate Capitol civilians to dislike one another, as Coriolanus Snow's close relationship with Sejanus Plinth made other Academy students somewhat indifferent toward him.
</p><p>Nepotism and the obsession with 'old money' families are also prevalent within Capitol society, as new money such as the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Plinth_family" title="Capitol families">Plinths</a> were looked down upon by Capitol civilians. However, their <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> ancestry and their move into the Capitol shortly after the end of the Dark Days may have also played a part in the contempt Capitol civilians held against them. Nevertheless, those from affluent families were given special opportunities. For example, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> was easily accepted into the Academy since she was a part of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Snow_family" title="Capitol families">Snow family</a>, and gamemakers such as <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> and <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a> were also part of affluent families themselves. Regardless of family wealth, nepotism also benefitted less affluent families, as <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a>'s position as the first host of the Hunger Games may have also played a part in <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>'s success as a host for the Hunger Games and a prominent interviewer in the following decades. There is also an <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">academy</a> and <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">university</a> for the brightest and most well-connected students, though even for Capitol citizens tuition was very high for the latter. Additionally, even the Capitol had a black market, which Coriolanus Snow once purchased a pair of dark pants from.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

Many Capitol residents are extremely shallow, always looking for ways to be noticed. An outrageous sense of style and fashion are very important to the citizens of the Capitol. It is common for them to tattoo and dye their bodies in extravagant bright colors, as well as undergo plastic surgery to alter their appearances. Known results of the surgeries are whiskers, dyed skin, talons, decorative patterns cut into their skin, and more outrageous fashions alluded to but not mentioned. These alterations are done so heavily that Katniss describes them as disfiguring, wondering if the people of the Capitol realize how horrifying they look to the rest of Panem. Some people of the Capitol also have gems implanted in their skin. Capitol residents frequently wear wigs in a multitude of colors. In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i>, when Katniss and Peeta are talking on the roof the night before their enter to the arena, Peeta admits that it would be hard to tell if they were wearing any fancy costumes, because you might as well mistake their ordinary clothing for a costume.
</p><p>In order to have a good time at a party and eat as much as they want, Capitol residents drink a liquid (similar to ipecac) that causes them to vomit, thus providing enough room in their stomachs for more food. The residents seem oblivious to the fact that, although they go through lots of food and still have plenty left over, many of the districts' residents are starving. The food Capitol residents eat is extremely rich and appears in exotic and beautiful patterns, such as bread rolls shaped like flowers and oranges served with a sauce. Everything is luxurious and overwhelming for the tributes that arrive from the poor districts (for example, food dispensers and showers with over a hundred buttons).
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 178px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Capitol_crowd.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120303015133" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A Capitol crowd." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="178" height="162" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Capitol crowd.jpg" data-image-key="Capitol_crowd.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Capitol_crowd.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/178?cb=20120303015133" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Capitol_crowd.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120303015133" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A Capitol crowd." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Capitol_crowd.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/178?cb=20120303015133" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="178" height="162" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol crowd.jpg" data-image-key="Capitol_crowd.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Capitol_crowd.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/178?cb=20120303015133" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_crowd.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A Capitol crowd.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The people who live in the Capitol are culturally distinct from the rest of Panem, speaking with strange accents; their voices are high pitched, their jaws barely open when talking, and their tone at the ends of their sentences going up as if they're <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_rising_terminal" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:High rising terminal">asking a question</a> (this particular trait is present in real life <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Californian_English" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Californian English">Californian English</a>). They have odd vowels, clipped words, and tend to hiss the letter "s." The only noted exceptions to this are <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>.
</p><p>People living in the Capitol are very easy to win over because, despite the fact that they enjoy theatric bloodshed, they love sentimentality and melodrama. Both Katniss and other former <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> have exploited this fact to further their own agenda. Haymitch also states that it's possible to gain support in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> simply by gushing.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 168px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/90/Capitol_freak.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120214015250" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A woman from the Capitol." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="168" height="269" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Capitol freak.jpg" data-image-key="Capitol_freak.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/90/Capitol_freak.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/168?cb=20120214015250" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/90/Capitol_freak.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120214015250" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A woman from the Capitol." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/90/Capitol_freak.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/168?cb=20120214015250" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="168" height="269" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol freak.jpg" data-image-key="Capitol_freak.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/90/Capitol_freak.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/168?cb=20120214015250" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_freak.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A woman from the Capitol.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Capitol residents have often been said to treat the Hunger Games like a celebrated sporting event and a highly anticipated source of entertainment and past time. They take pride in <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">betting</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> simply for bragging rights, not understanding or showing any regard for the horror their government is committing by forcing the games upon the districts. Some citizens are <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a> to the tributes, paying money for packages of supplies that are then delivered to them in the arena. Also, the more blood and fighting there is in the arena, the greater the entertainment for the Capitol. Unlike the twelve districts of Panem, the Capitol does not have to offer children for the Hunger Games, although the idea was discussed in <i>Mockingjay</i>. 
</p><p>Despite their extreme wealth, life moves very slowly until the Games, which make them all the more anticipated. Despite being at the top of both the economic and social chain, there is very little in the way of celebration or festivity until the Games and <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>. It is also observed that they are unhappy about the previous victors being thrown into the arena once more for the 3rd <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, because they have grown attached to the victors. Many Capitol citizens wanted to stop that Hunger Games from happening.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Government_and_military">Government and military</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Capitol has no localized city government, as the central government of Panem is housed at the Capitol. Capitol citizens control the government as all members of <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>'s cabinet are Capitol citizens. Prior to the establishment of the Constitutional Republic in the wake of the Second Rebellion, no <a href="/wiki/Elections" title="Elections">elections</a> were held in Panem or in the Capitol, so it is unknown how President Snow came to his position. The Capitol exerts all political and economic control over all of Panem, and Snow's regime is known to be exceptionally harsh in keeping law and order in the districts.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 287px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Capitol_officials_at_dinner.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200725113911" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Panem&#39;s high-ranking officials including President Snow, Egeria and Antonius." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="287" height="112" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Capitol officials at dinner.jpeg" data-image-key="Capitol_officials_at_dinner.jpeg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Capitol_officials_at_dinner.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/287?cb=20200725113911" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Capitol_officials_at_dinner.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20200725113911" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Panem&#39;s high-ranking officials including President Snow, Egeria and Antonius." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Capitol_officials_at_dinner.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/287?cb=20200725113911" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="287" height="112" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol officials at dinner.jpeg" data-image-key="Capitol_officials_at_dinner.jpeg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Capitol_officials_at_dinner.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/287?cb=20200725113911" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_officials_at_dinner.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Panem's high-ranking officials including President Snow, Egeria and Antonius.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Headed by the president who is the ultimate authority in the nation, the Capitol imposes a centrally planned economy on the districts, with all major industries under its control. The economic development of the districts is thus extremely limited, and nearly all citizens live in abject poverty.
</p><p>The President of Panem heads a cabinet whose members are chosen by the president himself. The individual members of the cabinet are charged with various duties and portfolios.
</p><p>After the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, the central government dissolved, and its members were arrested, including President Snow himself.
</p><p>The Capitol employs a powerful military force to enforce its laws and police the districts. The majority of the military's manpower comes from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, as Capitol citizens are not known for physically fighting. With few exceptions, the only members of the military who reside in the Capitol are the military commanders. The <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>, the who form the backbone of the Capitol's Armed Forces, are armed with advanced, cutting-edge equipment and technology, including nuclear arms. Following the overthrow of the Capitol, the armed forces are disbanded as well, with large numbers of Peacekeepers arrested, tried, and executed.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationship_with_the_districts">Relationship with the districts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After the collapse of modern civilization, the thirteen districts were brought together under the Capitol's rule, thus forming the nation of Panem. Originally, there were thirteen districts, until the outbreak of the Dark Days, which was a failed attempt to topple the dictatorship.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, unbeknownst to the other districts, was the Capitol's primary weapons supplier as its industry was in military arms production and nuclear weapons development and research, which had been instrumental to supporting the Dark Days and <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>. As the rebellion wavered, 13 managed to get the Capitol to capitulate to a degree through a threat of mutually assured destruction with their nuclear arsenal. 13 was allowed to secede from Panem and would prepare for another rebellion in the following decades, and the Capitol created fear-mongering propaganda of District 13's "annihilation" in the war, whilst hoping that the rogue district would eventually die out.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 255px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e1/Thecapitolcf.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720233452" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Capitol." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="255" height="130" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Thecapitolcf.png" data-image-key="Thecapitolcf.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e1/Thecapitolcf.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/255?cb=20130720233452" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e1/Thecapitolcf.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720233452" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Capitol." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e1/Thecapitolcf.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/255?cb=20130720233452" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="255" height="130" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Thecapitolcf.png" data-image-key="Thecapitolcf.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e1/Thecapitolcf.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/255?cb=20130720233452" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Thecapitolcf.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Capitol.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Though the districts harvest all of the country's resources, such as fish, coal, lumber, textiles, technology, electricity, transportation, grain, livestock, crops, and gems, they must export almost all of their resources to serve the Capitol's citizens and their overwhelming greed. As a result, many districts do not have enough resources to provide for themselves; <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a> lacks adequate coal, and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> lacks food to feed their citizens. Districts <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a> typically have a better relationship with the Capitol and therefore receive more, have better food, and are graced with various other goods.
</p><p>It is said that the Capitol favors District 2 as a lapdog, although District 2 is still not excused from the Hunger Games. Out of all the districts, District 2 is the most loyal to the Capitol. Following the end of the Dark Days and the destruction (in reality, secession) of District 13, the Capitol looked to District 2 as the new center of its military-industrial complex. The Capitol's military operations were thus based in District 2's mountains.
</p><p>With the establishment of the base in District 2, military careers were offered to District 2 citizens. Because of this, many District 2 citizens began to look favorably upon the Capitol. Nevertheless, citizens in the mountainous regions, who remained loyal to the district trade of masonry, maintained a negative view of the Capitol and rebelled during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 179px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/President-coin-capitol.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231216194522" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Alma Coin, Panem&#39;s interim president after the rebellion, although she was killed by Katniss shortly after." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="179" height="119" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="President-coin-capitol.jpg" data-image-key="President-coin-capitol.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/President-coin-capitol.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/179?cb=20231216194522" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/President-coin-capitol.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231216194522" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Alma Coin, Panem&#39;s interim president after the rebellion, although she was killed by Katniss shortly after." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/President-coin-capitol.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/179?cb=20231216194522" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="179" height="119" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="President-coin-capitol.jpg" data-image-key="President-coin-capitol.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/President-coin-capitol.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/179?cb=20231216194522" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:President-coin-capitol.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Alma Coin, Panem's interim president after the rebellion, although she was killed by Katniss shortly after.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The districts have no political representation since all power is invested in the Capitol’s central government, with a President acting as the supreme leader of the nation. The districts' mayors serve as governors, ensuring the districts fulfill their quotas of production. They serve no electoral representative role as parliamentarians or congressional representatives.
</p><p>As a result, the citizens of the districts generally live in abject poverty and under brutal oppression. The Capitol takes advantage of this suffering, using it as a tool to keep the districts in line and in servitude.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Following the victory of Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark in the 74th Hunger Games, unrest began growing in the districts once more, particularly the outlying and most impoverished districts, such as 11 and 8. After Katniss and Peeta's failure to extinguish the dissent within the districts during their Victory tour, President Snow announced the third quarter quell, in which victors were forced back into the arena as tributes. After the 75th Hunger games ended with Katniss blowing up the arena, many districts turned to full-scale rebellion against the Capitol, with Districts 1, 3, 8 and 11 being the first to separate from Capitol control. The full-scale uprising, which after several months led to the takeover of all 12 districts by rebel forces, were led and supported by District 13 and its military arsenal. 
</p>

<p>Rebel forces then managed to set up military bases around the outer areas of the Capitol and effectively managed to make safe pathways leading further into the Capitol despite the pods activated by <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>. By the end of the rebellion, as the Capitol evacuated its citizens residing in the outer blocks of the city, the rebel forces used the opportunity to further their assault within the Capitol. The invasion of the Capitol was ultimately successful, leading to the overthrow of the Snow regime and the arrests of large numbers of Capitol officials, including the president himself.
</p>

<p>Interim President Alma Coin called for the remaining seven Hunger Games Victors to vote on a 76th Hunger Games, using Capitol children. The victors approved by a narrow 4-3 decision. However, during the execution of the deposed President Snow, Katniss Everdeen made the last-minute decision to assassinate Coin. In the chaos that followed, a free election was held, resulting in the victory of Commander Paylor, a rebel military commander from District 8. The 76th Hunger Games were never held, and the event was entirely abolished afterwards. Under the leadership of the new administration, Panem was transformed into a constitutional republic. Massive economic and political liberalization took place afterwards, and the districts are implied to have prospered.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Due to the Capitol not participating in the Hunger Games, no official <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the Capitol was produced in the first movie. Because of this, it is difficult to determine what the Capitol's color could be. However, considering the Capitol's status as Panem's central seat of government, the Capitol's color seems to be metallic gold (#d4af37 – <span style="background-color:#d4af37; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the book, Katniss mentions that the Capitol's Skyline had colorful skyscrapers. In contrast, the movies show brutalist and neoclassical architecture being the dominant styles.</li>
<li>The dominant fashion trend of the Capitol had rapidly changed over time from inter/post-war styles around the time of the 10th Hunger Games to avant-garde and couture by the 74th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>The Capitol bares many resemblances to Pyongyang, North Korea. Both cities are home to the elite, and it's rare for someone from an outlying region to be invited to move to Pyongyang. Buildings resembling Ryukyong Hotel and Rungrado May Day Stadium are seen in the second film.</li>
<li>The Capitol also bears many resemblances to the British Empire. The Capitol's obsession with old money families such as the Snow, Crane, Phipps and Heavensbee families are similar to that of the imperial aristocracy. Furthermore, the Empire extracted resources and labour from its colonies, enriching the highest levels of society whilst allowing its colonial subjects, and even its own citizens in the mainland, to suffer poverty and hardship. Deep prejudice also existed against colonial subjects; in the Victorian era, for instance, human zoos featuring various indigenous peoples were opened to the public, much like what was done for the tributes of the 10th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>A "capitol", named after the Capitoline Hill in Rome, refers specifically to the legislative building of the government in the capital city.</li></ul>
