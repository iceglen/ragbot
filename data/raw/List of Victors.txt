<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_victors">Known victors</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_victors%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>There have been 75 victors throughout the history of the Hunger Games: one for each year from the 1st through <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">73rd</a>, and then an unprecedented double victory in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> No victor was crowned for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> All victors were between the ages of 14 and 18 at the time of their victory.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 75th Hunger Games, 59 victors were still alive, and <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>'s fate is unknown, so fifteen died prior to the third Quarter Quell. The <a href="/wiki/Career" class="mw-redirect" title="Career">Career</a> districts— <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>— had the most victors, but each district had at least one surviving male and female.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Eighteen more died in the Quell,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and by the end of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, only seven victors were shown alive. This means that another 34 presumably died during the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a> and the rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Only 27 victors were mentioned in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>. Two more (<a href="/wiki/Porter_Millicent_Tripp" title="Porter Millicent Tripp">Porter Millicent Tripp</a> and <a href="/wiki/Augustus_Braun" title="Augustus Braun">Augustus Braun</a>) were revealed by the promotional website <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, and another appeared in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, for a total of 30 known victors. The table below lists the 23 named victors. Italicized details, such as rough estimates for ages or Hunger Games, are speculative.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Unknown_victors">Unknown victors</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_victors%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Since there have been 75 victors in the history of the Hunger Games and 31 are known, there are still 44 unknown victors.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Books">Books</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_victors%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>From the 1st-<a href="/wiki/9th_Hunger_Games" title="9th Hunger Games">9th Hunger Games</a>, Districts 1 and 2 already produced more victors than other districts. 4 and 11 were also considered contenders,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so they may have turned out victors as well, or at least had tributes place highly.</li>
<li>Katniss states that District 12 does the worst in the games, with just two victors. By this, it can be assumed that every district from District 1 to District 11 has at least three victors by the seventy-fourth Hunger Games.</li>
<li>When Haymitch was "little", District 4 had a male victor who was visibly distraught during his <a href="/wiki/Victor%27s_Ceremony" title="Victor&#39;s Ceremony">Victor's Ceremony</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>, Mags, shielded him from the cameras and held him as he cried.<sup id="cite_ref-SR6_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Haymitch says before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, that District 12 is the only district to not have any "living victors". Considering <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> still won the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, we know that every district had won the games at least once during its first 49 years.<sup id="cite_ref-SR6_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Due to District 12 being assigned two mentors, it can be inferred that every other district had at least two victors to mentor during the second Quarter Quell.</li>
<li>Districts 1, 2, and 4 have the largest pool of victors at the reaping for the third Quarter Quell, but every district has at least one living male victor and one living female victor.</li>
<li>Given the circular nature of the Victors' Village, and how unnecessarily difficult it would be to add more houses, it's likely that no district had more than twelve living victors at a time.</li>
<li>Career districts were already winning disproportionately by the fiftieth Hunger Games. It can be inferred that the Career alliance was formed at least a decade prior to the second Quarter Quell to allow three districts to disproportionately win.</li>
<li>Katniss recalled a male victor from "a few years ago" who only received a <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> of three.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>At some point in the history of the Games, District 7 had at least one female victor other than Johanna, as she was specified as being the only "living" female victor from her district.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_4-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Effie Trinket's comments during the reaping recaps for the 75th Hunger Games imply that Cecelia wasn't the only eligible female victor from District 8, and Chaff wasn't the only eligible male victor from District 11.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_4-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Since Effie only sent Katniss and Peeta tapes of victors they might have had to face in the third Quarter Quell, Peeta assumed that the victor of the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a> was deceased by then.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_4-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Films">Films</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_victors%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

In order of appearance, they were shown with a sword, a shield, a harpoon, and no weapon.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_monoliths">Tribute monoliths</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_victors%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>For <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i>, a series of "tribute monoliths" were meant to appear in the <a href="/wiki/Rescue_mission" class="mw-redirect" title="Rescue mission">rescue mission</a> scene at the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>. These monuments listed each district's victors,<sup id="cite_ref-D12_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D12-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> with a total of 75 across all twelve districts. However, they did not make it into the finished film. This, alongside factual and thematic inconsistencies, as well as the use of real actors' names, makes them dubiously canon. Furthermore, many of the names below have been transcribed by fans from low-quality images, so they should be taken with a grain of salt. Known victors are bolded.
</p>

Even before the reveal of Lucy Gray in <i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i>, District 12's monolith was inconsistent with information from the books. Prior to Katniss and Peeta's double victory, Katniss mentioned that District 12 had had "exactly two" victors: Haymitch and one who was no longer alive.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With the addition of Katniss and Peeta, the monolith should have had four names total.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div></div>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_victors%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the novel <i>Catching Fire</i>, Katniss said Finnick was "still one of the youngest victors".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Due to the ambiguity of the wording, she could have meant he was either:
<ul><li>A) one of the youngest people to have won the Games.</li>
<li>B) the youngest victor ever.</li>
<li>C) merely one of the youngest victors competing in the Quarter Quell.</li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">film adaptation</a>, Haymitch mentioned that Finnick "won his Games at fourteen, [the] youngest ever."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Of the thirty-one known victors, there have been 16 female (51.6%) and 15 male victors (48.4%).</li>
<li>District 1 is the only district in the trilogy known to have tributes win for two consecutive years: Gloss and Cashmere. They are also the only known sibling victors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_4-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>However, if we include the early draft of the film script, District 2 had two consecutive victors leading up to the 74th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-early_script_23-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-early_script-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Prior to the 74th Hunger Games, District 12 only had one surviving victor, the least in all of Panem.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> But by the end of the war, they had the most surviving victors at three (Katniss, Peeta, and Haymitch). The other four remaining victors (Enobaria, Beetee, Annie, and Johanna) were left as the only ones still living from each of their districts (2, 3, 4, and 7, respectively). All victors from 1, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, and 11 were wiped out.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Since the victors of the 70th and 71st Hunger Games were both female and considering the early film script to be canon, the recent male victor with the training score of three<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> most likely won the 68th or 69th, due to Katniss stating he won 'a few years ago'.</li></ul>
