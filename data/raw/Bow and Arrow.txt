<p>The <b>bow and arrow</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Projectile" class="mw-redirect" title="Projectile">projectile</a> weapon that predates recorded history and is common to most cultures. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archery" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Archery">Archery</a> is the art, practice, or skill of using the weapon. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> learned her archery skills from her <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">father</a> and used a bow to hunt for food in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. The bow also became her <a href="/wiki/Weapon" class="mw-redirect" title="Weapon">weapon</a> of choice in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>&#160;during the&#160;<a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th</a> and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> is also a skilled archer, who learned his skills from Katniss.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBow_and_arrow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> grabbed a crossbow from the middle of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> and used it to kill <a href="/wiki/Sheaf" title="Sheaf">Sheaf</a>. Later, when <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> entered the arena to retrieve <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, a bow and arrows could be seen lying unused out in the open.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i>The Hunger Games</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBow_and_arrow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Some time after her father's death, Katniss worked up the courage to go under the <a href="/wiki/Fence" title="Fence">fence</a> and retrieve the small bow and arrows her father had made her. Within a few hours, she managed to shoot a rabbit.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss later met Gale in the woods and taught him how to shoot, eventually giving him one of her bows.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She used her bow to supply food for her family over the next four years and was able to shoot each animal directly in the eye.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss also made several attempts to copy her father's bows, but with poor results.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>
Katniss stored the bow that her father made for her in a hollowed-out log just outside the boundary of District 12. Due to the extreme restrictions on owning weapons, it would have been too risky to keep in her house or anywhere else that unsympathetic <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> might have found it.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/6f/Katniss_3.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20111226010859" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss with her bow and arrows in the 74th Hunger Games arena." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/6f/Katniss_3.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20111226010859" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="270" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss 3.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_3.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_3.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss with her bow and arrows in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="74th Hunger Games arena">74th Hunger Games arena</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>During her private training session, Katniss put on an impressive display of her archery skills, but the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a> were too focused on their <a href="/wiki/Food_and_drink" title="Food and drink">food and drink</a> to pay her any attention. Enraged, Katniss used the bow to shoot an apple out of the mouth of a roasted pig on the buffet table, shocking the Gamemakers back to attention<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and earning a <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> of 11 (the highest of any <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> initially claimed the bow and arrow from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> during the opening <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While trapped in a tree by the <a href="/wiki/Career" class="mw-redirect" title="Career">Career</a> pack, Katniss dropped a <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nest on the Careers, including Glimmer, who was stung to death. Katniss was able to recover the bow and arrows despite the hallucinations she was experiencing due to the tracker jacker venom.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss then proceeded to use her new bow as her main weapon, hunting for food,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> destroying the Careers' supplies,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and killing <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i>Catching Fire</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBow_and_arrow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Katniss became a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>, she continued to hunt daily despite her wealth in order to provide for Gale's family, because he had begun to work in the mines and refused to accept any money from her. The only time they were able to hunt together was on Sundays.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 278px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/ba/Katniss_Training_75th.png/revision/latest?cb=20131029043739" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss using a bow in the Training Center." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="278" height="116" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss Training 75th.png" data-image-key="Katniss_Training_75th.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/ba/Katniss_Training_75th.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/278?cb=20131029043739" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/ba/Katniss_Training_75th.png/revision/latest?cb=20131029043739" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss using a bow in the Training Center." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/ba/Katniss_Training_75th.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/278?cb=20131029043739" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="278" height="116" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss Training 75th.png" data-image-key="Katniss_Training_75th.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/ba/Katniss_Training_75th.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/278?cb=20131029043739" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_Training_75th.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss using a bow in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>When Katniss was in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>, she decided to go to the archery range for "some sanity." The trainer <a href="/wiki/Tax" title="Tax">Tax</a> tossed fake birds high into the air for her to hit. When she shot down five in one round, she realized that she had gained the attention of most of the victors, who were regarding her with "everything from envy to hatred to admiration."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, numerous bows and quivers were supplied at the Cornucopia. When Katniss obtained her bow, she briefly faced off against <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, who was poised to attack her with his <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Trident" title="Weapons">trident</a>, before they quickly became allies. Katniss fired and missed at <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>, but hit <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> in the calf. <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> deflected her first arrow with his flotation belt and submerged himself before she could fire a second shot.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss later aimed at Finnick, but realized that he was not her enemy and instead fired an arrow with <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>'s wire wrapped around it into the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>, causing lighting to travel along the wire into the field and destroy it.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i>Mockingjay</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBow_and_arrow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> had an entire wall of archery weapons in their Special Weaponry arsenal. In particular, Katniss took note of a militarized bow that was weighed down with scopes and gadgetry; this was the one Gale picked out for his personal use throughout the novel.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <i>Mockingjay</i> films adapted this as a crossbow.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss's Mockingjay bow was designed by Beetee and programmed to only recognize her voice. If she wanted to use it as an ordinary bow, she could turn off its special features by saying "Good night" until the sound of her voice woke it again. Beetee designed a variety of color-coded arrows to go along with it as well: razor sharp, incendiary, and explosive.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She and Gale used their bows during the <a href="/wiki/District_8_hospital_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 hospital bombing">District 8 hospital bombing</a><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and throughout the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss later used her bow to assassinate President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following her return to District 12, Katniss attempted to hunt after being snapped out of her lethargy by Peeta's eventual return and planting of <a href="/wiki/Flowers#Primrose" title="Flowers">primrose bushes</a> outside her house.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBow_and_arrow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Katniss's bows were handcrafted by her father and carefully wrapped in waterproof covers. She had multiple that she kept hidden in the woods outside District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Her father had originally made her a smaller-sized bow to use when she was younger, but Katniss soon progressed to using the full-size bow.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the films, the homemade bow that Katniss hunts with appears to be a version of a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/flatbow" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:flatbow">flatbow</a>—a bow made from a single piece of wood, but shorter than a longbow and with flattened limbs.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The archery station in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> contains bows of many different materials, including wood, plastic, and metal. The arrows have perfectly-cut feathers that are uniform in shape. However, the bowstring and arrows were far more rigid than Katniss was used to.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="74th Hunger Games arena">74th Hunger Games arena</a>, the single bow in the Cornucopia is silver,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a> had a golden bow that Katniss immediately spotted.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Beetee was tasked with designing Katniss's black Mockingjay bow. Katniss described it as having "exquisite balance" and "elegant design"; to her, the curve of the limbs suggested the wings of a bird in flight. His assignment was to make it purely for aesthetics, but Beetee "left the outside simple, and left the inside to [his] imagination."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Beetee also designed three varieties of arrows: razor-sharp, incendiary and explosive, which were color-coded by their shafts. Armed with this, Katniss could accurately shoot targets over a hundred yards away.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_17-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Katniss tried to teach her sister, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a>, how to shoot, but the results were "disastrous".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She also considered giving <a href="/wiki/Rory_Hawthorne" title="Rory Hawthorne">Rory Hawthorne</a> archery lessons.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Before the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss gave an hour of archery lessons to <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> in exchange for an hour of trident instruction.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBow_and_arrow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In a behind the scenes featurette, property master Trish Gallaher Glenn said Katniss's bows, arrows, and quivers were her favorite props in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Katniss's hunting bow in District 12, made by her father, was "kind of" modeled after a "Native American" bow, and the hand-tied arrows were also "Native American-inspired". However, no one specific tribe or nation is cited as the inspiration.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_28-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The strap on her quiver was made from a belt, in order to suggest it had been made from one of Mr. Everdeen's old belts.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_28-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The Capitol bows were made with aircraft aluminum to make them look like steel, and the accompanying arrows were all made by hand.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_28-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>In the same featurette, <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence" title="Jennifer Lawrence">Jennifer Lawrence</a> said she had a love-hate relationship with archery. She liked it, but a wrong move could result in "getting whipped [in the arm] with a guitar string at like over a hundred miles an hour", with repeated injuries to that same spot.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_28-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li><i>Mockingjay</i> costume designers Kurt Swanson and Bart Mueller took this into account when designing the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_suit" title="Mockingjay suit">Mockingjay suit</a>; they added in an arm guard to protect her from the bowstring.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
