<p>A <b>training score</b>&#160;was a numerical score given to&#160;<a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>&#160;after their&#160;<a href="/wiki/Training_Center#Private_sessions" title="Training Center">private training sessions</a>&#160;in front of the&#160;<a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>. Scores ranged from one to twelve, with one being the worst and twelve being the best. These scores gave <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> citizens&#160;an idea of who stood the best chance during the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, and therefore, who to&#160;<a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">bet on</a>&#160;and&#160;<a href="/wiki/Sponsor" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor">sponsor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Other tributes used scores to determine who would be a threat to them in the&#160;<a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mechanics">Mechanics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_score%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Private_sessions">Private sessions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_score%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Private training sessions were held in the afternoon on the third day of training.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Tributes were called into&#160;the gymnasium one at a time, starting with <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and ending with <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, alternating by sex (boy-girl-boy, etc). During their individual session, a tribute could showcase their skills at any of the stations, while the Gamemakers watched and evaluated their performance. After fifteen minutes, or once the Gamemakers believed they had seen enough, that tribute was dismissed and they called in the next one.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Announcing_scores">Announcing scores</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_score%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After their private sessions, tributes returned to their apartments in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> and awaited their scores, which were announced on television in the evening. Going in the same order the tributes were called into the gym, the program showed a photo of the tribute, and then their score was flashed underneath it.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Strategies">Strategies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_score%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>According to <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a>, bad or mediocre scores were ignored, and most people only paid attention when a tribute scored highly. As a result, tributes tended to aim for high scores in order to appeal to sponsors. <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>&#160;from District 1, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> generally scored between eight and ten. However, some tributes were known to hide their skills and get low scores on purpose.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_score%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Katniss and Peeta were the first and only tributes to receive a 12.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>During the <a href="/wiki/71st_Hunger_Games" title="71st Hunger Games">71st Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> kept up a guise of weakness,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so she presumably got a low score on purpose.</li>
<li>"A few years" before the 74th Hunger Games, an unknown male <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> only received a score of three.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>After witnessing Rue leaping from tree to tree, Katniss thought to herself that if this what she showed the Gamemakers, she should have gotten at least a ten.<sup id="cite_ref-HG_14_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HG_14-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
