Turn me into some kind of monster that I'm not. I keep wishing I could think of a way to...to show the Capitol that they don't own me. That I'm more than just a piece in their Games.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Peeta Mellark to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Peeta Mellark</b>, a baker's son from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, is the deuteragonist of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>. He was reaped alongside <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. He and Katniss play out in the arena in order to garner sponsors and manage to both become <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> by tricking the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a>. Although his feelings are genuine, he is hurt to discover that Katniss' were not entirely. He and Katniss become friends despite the fact that they are forced to use their fake romance to both placate and subvert <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> during their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> and the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. At the end of the 75th Games, Peeta is captured by the Capitol and <a href="/wiki/Hijacked" class="mw-redirect" title="Hijacked">hijacked</a> to believe that Katniss is an evil <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a>. He manages to fall in love with her all over again during the end of the war and the years afterward. Eventually, Katniss realizes that she is also in love with him, and they have two children.
</p>

					<b class="trfc161__question">What is the significance of Peeta Mellark being a baker's son in the Hunger Games?</b>

				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						In the Hunger Games, Peeta Mellark's baker background is crucial. It influences his skills and character, as his family's merchant class status in District 12 is higher than the miners. His baking and cake decorating skills prove strategic in the Games for camouflage. Moreover, his access to bakery food enables him to aid Katniss Everdeen, the female lead, by providing her bread during hardship.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bf/Peeta_the_boy_with_the_bread.JPG/revision/latest?cb=20110807123742" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta outside his father's bakery." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bf/Peeta_the_boy_with_the_bread.JPG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20110807123742" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Peeta the boy with the bread.JPG" data-image-key="Peeta_the_boy_with_the_bread.JPG" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Peeta_the_boy_with_the_bread.JPG" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peeta outside his father's bakery.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Although he grew up in the merchant area of District 12, Peeta's childhood was less than desirable, as his family life was difficult. His father, <a href="/wiki/Otho_Mellark" title="Otho Mellark">Otho</a>, was kind,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but his <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Mellark" title="Mrs. Mellark">mother</a> was known to beat Peeta—and presumably his two brothers—if they made a mistake (for example, she hits Peeta for burning the <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ever since he was five years old, Peeta had a huge crush on Katniss Everdeen. On the first day of <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a>, his father pointed her out, saying that he wanted to marry her mother, <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid</a>, but ultimately didn't as she married <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a>, a coal miner. Peeta was confused as to why someone would marry a coal miner instead of his father, but Otho explained that when Burdock sang, the birds stopped to listen. Later, Katniss sang for their music class, and Peeta realized he was in love with her when he saw that the birds were listening like they did for her father. He said that he had often watched her, but the two never spoke until the reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Peeta is <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> as the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from District 12 in the 74th Hunger Games. Katniss recognizes him instantly, knowing that he is the boy who, five years prior, purposely burned bread and gave it to her to save her family and herself from starvation - an act that gave her the hope and courage to feed her family. For this, his mother gave him a black eye for being so clumsy.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Once he is reaped, he goes to the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a> where his family comes to visit him. His mother tells him that perhaps District 12 will have a victor: Katniss, indicating she has no expectations to ever see her youngest son again.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Peeta and Katniss leave the district on the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> to the Capitol, escorted by well-known fashionista and socialite, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>. On the first night on the train, Peeta is irritated by the District 12 <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, because of his drinking problem and his obvious lack of interest in helping the tributes survive. When Haymitch tells them, "Here's some advice: Stay alive." Peeta breaks his wine glass and receives a punch in the jaw as a result. Katniss then jams a knife in between Haymitch's hand and his wine bottle. Haymitch is impressed by their potential and decides that as long as they don't interfere with his drinking, he will stay sober enough to help them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Peeta arrives in the Capitol, he is fixed up by his <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a>, led by his <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a>. For the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, he and Katniss are dressed in black unitards with synthetic fire to represent burning coal. The Capitol receives them more positively than any of the other district tributes during the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">opening ceremonies</a>, where they are instructed to hold hands.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>During the training process, Haymitch instructs Katniss and Peeta to stay together every step and process of the way, though they protest due to their tendency to argue and anger each other. Peeta excels at keeping up the appearance that they are friends, easing Katniss into conversation, and responding to what she's saying at all the right times.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He receives a <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> of 8 after his private session with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a>, saying that he threw some weights around because the Gamemakers were clearly not interested in him.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite having agreed to train alongside Katniss at the beginning, Peeta decides to prepare for his interview separately.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During his interview with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, he reveals he is in love with Katniss, much to her surprise.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This confession begins the idea that the two are the "star-crossed lovers from District 12," which Haymitch believes will help them get <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>The night before the Games, Peeta and Katniss talk on the rooftop about the horrors that await them in the arena. Peeta admits that he does not believe he has ever been a contender in the games; he merely wants to die as himself and show the Capitol that he is "more than just a piece in their games." Katniss finds herself frustrated by his pessimistic outlook, thinking it foolish of him to have given up already.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the beginning of the Games, it is Peeta who distracts Katniss when she looks ready to run into the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a> in order to grab the silver <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow and arrows</a> she spies on top of a mound of blanket rolls. He unexpectedly teams up with the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Careers</a>, as Katniss finds out on the first night. What she does not know is that he is only with them in order to protect her. When an argument breaks out about the dying tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> and whether or not the pack should go back to kill her, Peeta says they are wasting time and finishes her off himself.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The cannon only fires after Peeta returns from "finishing her off" but it's not explicit whether Peeta actually killed her (though this seems unlikely, given his temperament), if she simply took longer to die than the Careers expected, or the Gamemakers held off on the cannon to try and get Katniss discovered for a more interesting fight.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>After the firestorm, none of the Careers die, but they are injured and much slower than before. They find Katniss and chase her up a tree, with Peeta suggesting that they make camp under it, as none of them are able to climb after her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Peeta stays up the whole night under the tree as part of his devotion to Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the morning, they wake up to a <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> attack after Katniss drops a nest on them. The Careers, minus <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>, and the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, who die from their stings, retreat to the lake. Peeta finds Katniss at the scene of the attack as she is removing the bow and arrows from Glimmer's body, and he urges her to run due to <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>'s impending attempt to kill her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Cato witnesses this exchange and cuts Peeta in his upper thigh for his actions, severely wounding him. It's unclear if Cato deliberately just wounded Peeta, or if he was trying to kill Peeta and Peeta fought back and escaped. Either way, Cato is confident enough in the wound that he and the other Careers don't bother hunting Peeta down.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Peeta uses mud and dirt to camouflage himself by a stream, where Katniss finds him after <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> announces the new rule: If the last two tributes standing are from the same district, they both can win. She nurses him back to health as best she can,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but ultimately he suffers from blood poisoning, which she cannot cure without the right medicine. During this time, Peeta plays up the star-crossed lovers angle and Katniss responds as best as she can, which results in sponsor gifts of food.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Katniss knows she has to go, but Peeta won't let her, saying he doesn't want her to die for him. Haymitch then sends Katniss <a href="/wiki/Sleep_syrup" title="Sleep syrup">sleep syrup</a>, which she slyly gives to Peeta in some berries she found; Peeta is hurt by her trickery before he falls into a deep sleep, giving her time to go to the feast.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She manages to return with the medicine, after nearly dying at the hands of <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>. Katniss just manages to inject the medicine into Peeta before fainting from her own blood loss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Peeta wakes up, recovered from the infected wound, and finds Katniss bleeding next to him. He treats her wound, and she wakes up later. They stay in their cave during a thunderstorm, during which <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> dies,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> presumably at the hands of Cato. Peeta uses the time to further promote their star-crossed lovers act, and the two share a passionate kiss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the storm ends, the pair head out to hunt for food, but Katniss is frustrated by the noise that Peeta, who is not used to hunting, makes. They decide to split up, with Katniss teaching Peeta a two-note whistle to signify to each other that they are safe. When Katniss does not hear Peeta whistle after an extended period of time, she becomes frightened and, upon finding him, chastises him for worrying her. She notices that their collection of food, which contains deadly <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries that Peeta mistakenly picked out, is missing a portion, and is irritated with him for having eaten without her, but he denies doing so. A cannon sounds and they see <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a>'s emaciated body being picked up by a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a>; she stole and ate the deadly berries, which almost instantly killed her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

They climb onto the roof of the Cornucopia for safety, and in the process Peeta's calf is bitten by one of the mutts, opening up a large wound. Katniss has to use her last arrow in a tourniquet to stop Peeta from dying from blood loss. Once recovered from running for so long, Cato catches Peeta in a headlock to cut off his air, possibly hoping to choke him to death and use his body as a weapon against Katniss, ensuring both of their deaths, and Cato's victory.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, after the camera crews finally left District 12, Katniss and Peeta had a falling out due to the ambiguity of her feelings for him. They spent the next six months basically ignoring each other.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-25"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Although still heartbroken from learning that Katniss doesn't reciprocate his feelings, Peeta doesn't abandon their image of star-crossed lovers and still "performs" well in front of the camera, playing his role of a boy lost in love. He and Katniss go on the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, which begins in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>. On the train there, when Katniss loses her temper with Effie, Peeta calms her down and suggests that they make a genuine attempt at being friends despite all that has happened between them.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> At night on the train, Katniss often wakes up screaming from nightmares. To comfort her, Peeta holds her while she sleeps; this becomes a nightly routine.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>When it comes time for the 75th Hunger Games, or the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, Katniss and Peeta think they are going to become mentors for two new tributes. They learn of Haymitch's ingenious strategy in which he used the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> around the arena as a tool during his final battle, a plan that amuses Katniss, who believes that it was almost as bad as her pulling out the berries due to the fact that the Capitol had not intended for anyone to recognize or use the force field.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>The Quell ends up being set in jungle terrain with seawater surrounding the Cornucopia. Each tribute is placed on a flotation device in the water. They walk throughout the day, knowing they must set up camp and find drinkable water. Katniss is still doubtful about trusting Finnick when suddenly, Peeta unknowingly walks into a forcefield that surrounds the arena and his heart had stops.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_37-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-37"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss is in hysterics and believes that Finnick, who pushes her away from Peeta's body, is trying to hurt Peeta, until she realizes that Finnick is performing CPR and manages to revive him.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_38-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

During his captivity, he was tortured, beaten, and hijacked using tracker jacker venom to distort his memories of Katniss until he believed she was a mutt trying to kill him. Peeta first appears in an interview with Caesar Flickerman, where he explains what happened in the Quell arena before the force field blew up and aggressively defends Katniss' innocence. Peeta finishes the interview by stating that a cease-fire is the best option for the rebels, otherwise, human life may cease to exist. Katniss notes he appears healthy.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_44-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-44"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss also decides to be the rebels' Mockingjay, on the condition that once Peeta, Johanna, <a href="/wiki/Annie" class="mw-redirect" title="Annie">Annie</a> and Enobaria are rescued, they must not be held liable for any mistakes they have made.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

When they are underground, Peeta huddles himself together. He is frightened of everything that he knows, thinks, feels, and how he acts. Katniss finally gets him to keep going by kissing him, reminding him of memories of times when he loved her, and he relaxes. They later go undercover into crowds of evacuating people, splitting up. Katniss and Peeta say their goodbyes. He goes in first with the nightlock pill that was given to him by Gale.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_24_57-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_24-57"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>57<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Adult_life">Adult life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the epilogue, Katniss explains that it took fifteen years for her to agree to have children with Peeta, and the epilogue itself takes place "more than twenty years" since the war. In those five or so intervening years, they had a <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_son" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark's son">son</a> with Peeta's blond hair and Katniss' gray eyes, and a <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_daughter" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark's daughter">daughter</a> with Katniss' dark hair and Peeta's blue eyes. Katniss feels hesitant to tell her children about the Games, but Peeta assures her that it will be okay, because they have each other. So, one day, they will tell their children of the Hunger Games, share the memory book Haymitch, Peeta, and Katniss compiled together, explain Katniss' hysterical episodes during the night, and inform them of the graveyard they happily play above every day, now a <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">meadow</a>. The graveyard is the burial site for the deceased residents of District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_Epilogue_60-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_Epilogue-60"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>60<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss and Peeta eventually create a <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">memorial book</a>. While Haymitch wants nothing to do with it at first, he eventually shares with them his own story about all of the people that Haymitch had loved and lost. Afterwards, Katniss brings Haymitch some goose eggs and Peeta builds an incubator for them.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_Epilogue_60-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_Epilogue-60"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>60<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>And you're the one who was known for being nice.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss to Peeta<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Peeta has a very likable personality and is very charismatic. Kind and gentle, he is also clever and skilled at manipulating others with his words. Though not brash or arrogant, he can be quite bold, having announced that he was in love with Katniss to the whole nation and offering Thresh and Rue's families part of his and Katniss' winnings. He is a very talented public speaker, to the point that Coin would have preferred to rescue him over Katniss from the Quell. Similarly, he has a good sense of humor and doesn't take himself too seriously.
</p><p>Unlike many victors, Peeta is reluctant to get into physical fights and very non-violent. Finnick thought that he might be the only victor who won the Games "by chance".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_37-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-37"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, when angered, Peeta has been known to throw objects against walls and shout, so he is not without a temper. He is gentle, but not a pushover; as seen when he breaks Haymitch's wine glass after Haymitch sarcastically tells him to "stay alive" in the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Most notably, Peeta is loving and cares deeply about the people around him. He is described as sweet, steady,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> gentle,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and strong<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> by Katniss several times, and always assumes the best in people, which baffles Katniss who is, by contrast, very skeptical and slow to trust. He is selfless (for his willingness to sacrifice his own life for Katniss's) and always knows exactly what to say to make someone feel better, such as when he was able to put <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> at ease underground.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_56-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-56"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He is able to work with Haymitch on goals and strategy and, unlike Katniss, able to keep secrets and is trusted by Haymitch. It is noted throughout the series his deep understanding of situations such as the Hunger Games where he says, "I don't want to be just a piece in their Games." It took Katniss much longer for her to understand the same concept.
</p><p>His favorite color is sunset orange. Katniss notices that he always double-knots his shoelaces, sleeps with the windows open, and never takes sugar in his tea.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_52-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-52"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When he's hijacked, he becomes out-of-control, homicidal, and suicidal. Eventually, through separating "shiny" hijacked memories and real ones, he recovers, though still has flashbacks and trauma.
</p>

<ul><li><b>Combatant</b>: Though not much of a fighter, Peeta is also very strong, which gives him an advantage during hand-to-hand <a href="/wiki/Combat" class="mw-redirect" title="Combat">combat</a>. In the book, Katniss mentions he came in second place in a District 12 wrestling contest, after only one of his older brothers, and in the film, she tells Haymitch that he can toss hundred-pound bags of flour over his head with ease. The Careers admit that he is "handy with a knife" and indeed a knife is his preferred weapon in the Games, though he doesn't often use one in combat. In <i>The Hunger Games: Tribute Guide</i>, his weapons are shown to be Camouflage, Strength, and Spear, indicating he's quite good with spears too.</li>
<li><b>Gifted Intellect</b>: Peeta's greatest talent could arguably be his way with words. Katniss notes that he always seems to know exactly what to say. He easily builds a rapport with Caesar Flickerman and is very talented at public speaking. When deciding to sacrifice herself for him in the Quell, Katniss justifies her decision by saying that Peeta can "change his pain into words that will move people" to rebellion, and that he could lead it.</li>
<li><b>Expert Deceiver</b>: In a similar vein, Peeta is an excellent liar. Katniss is unsure for much of <i>The Hunger Games</i> whether he's lying about his love for her. He lies in her defense when Katniss tries to explain knowing her <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avox</a> server, <a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a>, by saying she looks like Delly Cartwright. He also lies to Panem about him and Katniss being married and Katniss being pregnant.</li>
<li><b>Expert Artist</b>: Peeta is very artistic, ranging from frosting cakes to camouflage to actual painting. His talents developed from helping out at his parents' bakery by making bread and decorating cakes.</li></ul>

<p>Peeta's family consists of five people. His father, a baker, was far gentler than his wife and told a young Peeta that he wanted to marry Katniss' mother.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> By contrast, Peeta's mother was physically and verbally abusive to Peeta. She gave him a black eye for burning bread and screamed at starving children when they looked through the rubbish for something to eat.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Additionally, Peeta has two unnamed older brothers, one who was too old to take his place in the 74th Hunger Games and the other who could have taken his place but didn't. As Katniss says, "Family devotion only goes so far for most people on reaping day."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Peeta's family was neither close nor very happy. Peeta desperately wanted the children and eventually convinced Katniss, who had initially vowed never to have kids for fear of them being reaped. Their first child was a girl and their second child was a boy.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_Epilogue_60-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_Epilogue-60"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>60<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Gale_Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>I was jealous of him before I even officially met you.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Peeta, on Gale, to Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Peeta admitted to having been jealous of Gale for a long time, having watched him and Katniss from afar for many years and realizing the depth and strength of their relationship.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_26-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

When Gale was whipped, Peeta stepped in on his behalf and afterward helped carry him to Victor's Village.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_66-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-66"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>66<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Later, while training for the Quarter Quell, Gale taught Peeta and Haymitch about snares and trapping. Gale stated that he tried to hate Peeta, but it was difficult because Peeta was too good of a person.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_31-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the arena, Peeta gave Katniss a locket with a picture of Gale inside. He planned to sacrifice himself so that Katniss made it out of the Quell alive and told her that he wanted her to have a life and children with Gale after he was gone.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_42-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-42"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i>Mockingjay</i>, Gale volunteered first to be on the squad to break into the Capitol and retrieve Peeta from prison.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_11_48-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_11-48"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>48<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is unknown whether he did this for Katniss or out of genuine concern for Peeta. Confused and suspicious of Katniss due to his hijacking, Peeta expressed jealousy of Gale.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_53-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-53"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When Peeta joined <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>, Gale immediately offered to kill him for Katniss, but did help him play "Real or Not Real?" later.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_52-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-52"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

They spoke like friends and talked about Katniss. Katniss' internal monologue mentioned that Gale and Peeta "aren't friends" and "never have been. Though they're not exactly enemies." During this conversation, Gale expressed regret for not having volunteered in Peeta's place during the 74th Games, preventing her and Peeta's relationship altogether. Peeta reminded him that Katniss would have resented him for abandoning their families. Peeta also claimed that Katniss was in love with Gale, while Gale denied it and asserted that she loved Peeta. When Peeta wondered how Katniss would choose between them, and Gale answered that she will choose whoever "she thinks she can't survive without."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_23_67-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_23-67"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>67<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

</p><p>After both he and Katniss came back alive, Haymitch did everything he could to prevent them from being harmed by the Capitol. However, Peeta was hurt that Haymitch and Katniss had been keeping information from him.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_27-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When the Quarter Quell twist was announced, Haymitch agreed that he would help Katniss save Peeta and was willing to volunteer in Peeta's place.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_31-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Haymitch regretted that he had failed to rescue Peeta before the Capitol captured him.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_68-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-68"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>68<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He was the main person to defend Peeta and insist that District 13 heed his warning in the interview with Caesar.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_10_69-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_10-69"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>69<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Like Katniss, he was concerned for Peeta and worried about the rescue mission.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_49-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-49"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>49<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After his rescue, Haymitch and Katniss tried their best to help Peeta through the stress and effects of the tracker jacker venom they used to hijack his memories. Haymitch continued to work with him and give Katniss updates while she was in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_50-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-50"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The two reconnected after the war, and Haymitch helped Peeta and Katniss with their <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">memory book</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_59-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-59"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>59<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Peeta's name likely comes from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/pita" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:pita">pita</a>, a type of flatbread, since he is the baker's son. It could also be a variation on the name <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_(given_name)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Peter (given name)">Peter</a>, coming from the Latin "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/petra">petra</a>", meaning rock or stone. This could be fitting because Peeta serves as a steadying influence for Katniss. His surname, Mellark, might be a distortion of <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/meadowlark">meadowlark</a>, which is a type of songbird.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Tribute_Guide" title="The Hunger Games: Tribute Guide">Tribute Guide</a>,</i> Peeta has three 'weapons', the most of all 24 tributes.</li>
<li>In the film, Peeta weighs 145lbs and has 23-1 odds of winning the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Katniss and Peeta are the only victors to ever participate in the Games two years in a row.</li>
<li>Peeta is the only male volunteer from District 12.</li>
<li>When hunting, Peeta is very loud compared to Katniss and Gale, who are seasoned hunters and skilled at moving quietly.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_21-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Peeta is the second known District 12 volunteer, the first being Katniss Everdeen.</li>
<li>In the <i>Hunger Games</i> film, when the training scores are revealed, he and Katniss are the only tributes recognized by their first and last names—the other tributes are only referred to by their first names. This is likely a writing error, however, as theirs are the only last names known to the readers.</li>
<li>Peeta's favorite color is orange, like a sunset.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_26-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the book, Peeta has to have his leg amputated and a prosthetic made after a mutt mauls his calf. In the movie, after Katniss gives him the medicine for his blood poisoning, he doesn't sustain any further injuries to his leg.</li>
<li>As a promotion for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Capitol_TV" title="Capitol TV">Capitol TV</a> released an article that explains that while he was captured by the Capitol, President Snow appointed Peeta as his Special Counsel.</li>
<li>Since the Third Quarter Quell ended after Peeta killed Brutus, and since the Quell was the last ever Hunger Games in Panem, Peeta officially has the last ever kill in the Games.</li></ul>
