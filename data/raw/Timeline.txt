<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Approach_to_timeline">Approach to timeline</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTimeline%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A major issue for dating in <i>The Hunger Games</i> series is how to track its history. No official out-of-universe timeline is used, nor is an in-universe dating system mentioned in the books or movies to track the years. To make up for this lack of clarity, this timeline will use an <b>unofficially recognized, non-canonical calendar system</b> in order to provide a relative reference to events in the series.
</p><p>The designations used to label years will be <b>Before the Dark Days (BDD)</b> and <b>After the Dark Days (ADD)</b>. The origin year is 0 BDD and 0 ADD, during which the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> ended, the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a> was signed, and the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> were instituted. BDD counts down to the origin point, while ADD counts up from it. Also, since Panem uses the Gregorian calendar, one year measured in BDD or ADD will begin in January and end in December.
</p><p>The end of the Dark Days as a means of measuring time has been used in props for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>This timeline is considered incomplete because many past, present and future events are as of yet unknown, and the chronology is very loose. As a result, many events will have approximate dates or will be listed as unknown. The dates for these entries are italicized.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_history">Early history</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTimeline%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><i><b>Unknown, BDD</b></i>
</p><p>At some point in the future, the Earth is ravaged by a series of ecological disasters spurred on by climate change. This includes droughts, storms, fires, and rising sea levels, which all lead to widespread resource shortages. A global war breaks out over the remaining resources, leading to the collapse of world governments,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the human population is largely decimated, nearing extinction.<sup id="cite_ref-:48_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:48-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In an early draft of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> script, the events of the series are said to take place 300 years into the future.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Considering that the film was released in 2012,<sup id="cite_ref-:6_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> this would mean that the trilogy begins around 2312, and 1 ADD would correspond to the year 2238. However, since none of this material was included in the final product, this is only speculatory, and will not be carried throughout the rest of this page.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Founding_of_Panem">Founding of Panem</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTimeline%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>An indeterminate number of years after the fall of civilizations in North America, the nation of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> is founded. Sometime during or after its founding, Panem's land and people are segmented into thirteen outlying <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>, with the seat of its government, <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> situated in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mountains" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rocky Mountains">Rocky Mountains</a>. <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> is established in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Appalachia">Appalachia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Whether from its inception as a nation-state or a gradual transformation, Panem becomes a totalitarian dictatorship and police state. The impoverished district citizenry is exploited for their labor to the sole benefit of the Capitol. Their work and resources are likely key factors behind Panem's extensive technological advancement. Over the years, structural inequality stokes resentment in the districts against the Capitol ruling class.
</p>

<h2><span id="The_Dark_Days_.283_-_0_BDD.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Dark_Days_(3_-_0_BDD)">The Dark Days (3 - 0 BDD)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTimeline%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a></i></center></div>
<p><b>3 BDD</b>
</p>
<ul><li>The thirteen districts of Panem launch a revolution against the Capitol, resulting in a major civil war.
<ul><li>According to the Capitol, the war is instigated by rebels in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. As Panem's previous military headquarters, 13 allegedly disseminates weapons and communications to other rebels throughout the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-:54_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:54-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>The conflict quickly spreads to all corners of Panem as loyalist and rebel soldiers wage war across the districts for control of the country. The Capitol liberally uses genetically engineered weapons, <a href="/wiki/Muttations" class="mw-redirect" title="Muttations">muttations</a>, fleets of hovercraft in bombing campaigns, and other advance technologies to quell the uprising. The rebelling districts, in turn, employ espionage and guerilla tactics alongside District 13 materiel to fight on par with the Capitol. As a result, widespread loss of infrastructural damage is sustained throughout the country.</li>
<li>"A few months into the war":<sup id="cite_ref-:12_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:12-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>The first major rebel air raid on the Capitol immobilizes the city for several days. During this time, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow&#39;s mother">Coriolanus Snow's mother</a> dies giving birth to a <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_sister" title="Coriolanus Snow&#39;s sister">daughter</a>, who perishes along with her.<sup id="cite_ref-:12_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:12-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Soon after the death of his wife, General <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a> is killed by a rebel sniper.<sup id="cite_ref-:55_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:55-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Every Capitol citizen is assigned a bomb shelter near their residence. However, their surveillance system relies on electricity, and rebel interference in <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> causes frequent blackouts, rendering their air raid sirens unreliable. Citizens are often caught off guard and forced to shelter in place.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> is born in District 4. <sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<p><b>2 - 0 BDD</b> 
</p>
<ul><li>The Capitol strategically places <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nests around the districts like land mines,<sup id="cite_ref-:37_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:37-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and in Districts <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a>, and 12,<sup id="cite_ref-:55_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:55-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> are used to spy on rebels.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The rebel districts blockade and lay siege to the Capitol in an attempt to starve out the Capitol and its citizens, resulting in city-wide food shortages, rationing, and isolated incidents of cannibalism.<sup id="cite_ref-:13_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:13-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The Capitol stages high-profile <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executions</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, making it a prime target for rebel bombers.<sup id="cite_ref-:14_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:14-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In an effort to boost morale in the last year of the war, the Capitol declares December 15th National Heroes Day, honoring citizens who lost their lives in service to the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Dark_Days">The Dark Days</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTimeline%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a></i></center></div>
<ul><li>Rebel forces attempt to break the siege by scaling the Rocky Mountains and invading the Capitol itself. The terrain leaves them exposed to Capitol aircraft, likely resulting in heavy casualties. This loss presumably has a devastating impact on the rebel war effort, turning the tide of the war in favor of the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-:10_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:10-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Realizing that the rebels have lost their advantage, District 13 decides to abandon its allies and secure its own independence. They seize a significant portion of the Capitol's <a href="/wiki/Nuclear_weaponry" title="Nuclear weaponry">nuclear arsenal</a> and target the city. Knowing the Capitol still has its own nuclear weapons out west, District 13 threatens <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/mutually_assured_destruction" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:mutually assured destruction">mutually assured destruction</a> unless the Capitol strikes a deal.<sup id="cite_ref-:48_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:48-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>District 13's leaders propose that the Capitol allow them to quietly secede from Panem as an independent state, in exchange for their withdrawal of support from the rebel war effort and presenting the illusion that the Capitol destroyed the district. Left with no other viable alternative and unwilling to escalate the rebellion into a nuclear war, the Capitol reluctantly agrees to the terms of the ceasefire.</li>
<li>District 13 consolidates its people and assets in an underground complex, and the Capitol carpet bombs its surface infrastructure.</li></ul></li>
<li>The Capitol uses the decimated ruins of 13 as <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propaganda</a>, making an example of it as the consequence for rebelling. With the loss of support from District 13, the rebellion collapses and is overrun by the Capitol. The remaining districts are pacified and reunified under the firm grip of the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="0_ADD_-_The_Treaty_of_Treason">0 ADD - The Treaty of Treason</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTimeline%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a></i></center></div>
<p>After the districts' defeat and the end of the Dark Days, the Treaty of Treason is written to introduce new laws, ostensibly to guarantee peace, and to prevent the possibility of another district rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Treaty's most prominent provision is the establishment of the Hunger Games. First conceived of by <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> and brought to fruition by Crassus Snow and Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:11_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:11-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the event marks the anniversary of the districts' defeat with a twisted annual celebration. District children between the ages of 12 and 18 are <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> via lottery to fight to the death in the Hunger Games. Each year there are twenty-four <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>— one male and one female from each district— and they compete until only one remains as the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><b>1 ADD</b>

They get drunk together, and they both agree to do whatever it takes to protect Peeta, should he enter with Katniss into the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:23_85-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:23-85"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>85<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li><i>March 16-April 10</i>, 75 ADD , nine months after the 74th Hunger Games reaping:<sup id="cite_ref-:23_85-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:23-85"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>85<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Katniss wakes up hungover and returns home, where her mother and Prim look after her. Peeta tracks down Ripper and threatens to turn her in to the Peacekeepers if she provides Haymitch and Katniss with any more liquor. He also bribes her.
<ul><li>In the Capitol, voting on Katniss's wedding dress ends at noon.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_34-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the afternoon, Peeta dumps out all of Haymitch's remaining liquor supply, and he confronts them both with a plan to train like Careers.<sup id="cite_ref-:23_85-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:23-85"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>85<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>After a few days, Katniss and Haymitch agree to Peeta's plan.</li></ul></li>
<li>April - July<i>:</i> Haymitch, Katniss, and Peeta train daily like Career tributes to prepare for the Games, exercising in the morning, honing their combat skills in the afternoon, and watching recaps of living victors' Games at night.<sup id="cite_ref-:23_85-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:23-85"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>85<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

While their efforts are futile, they nevertheless gain immense sympathy from the crowd, who are deeply upset at the prospect of losing so many of their favored victors.
<ul><li>During Katniss's interview, her wedding dress— chosen by Capitol citizens, insisted upon for the occasion by Snow, and modified by Cinna— transforms into a dress resembling a mockingjay when she spins and the dress catches on fire, to both the audience's and Caesar's praise.</li>

<li>July 11th: The 75th Hunger Games begin, led by Head Gamemaker Plutarch Heavensbee.

<li>July 12th: The arena's clock mechanism comes into effect at midnight.

<li>2am: Katniss, Peeta, Finnick, and Mags are accosted by a poisonous <a href="/wiki/Fog" title="Fog">fog</a>. Mags sacrifices herself to allow the group to escape, and she dies enveloped by the fog.</li>

<li>July 13th: Beetee proposes a plan to kill off the remaining Careers using his wire and the lightning tree.

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTimeline%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D22&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></i></center></div> 
<ul><li>July 14th: Katniss, Beetee, and Finnick are extracted from the arena by rebels, while Peeta, Johanna, and Enobaria are taken by the Capitol. A majority of the districts begin a revolt against Capitol authorities, marking the start of the Second Rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-:31_80-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:31-80"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>80<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>12:15am: Fifteen minutes after Katniss blew out the force field, Capitol bombers arrive and raze District 12 in a massive <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing raid</a>. Of District 12's original population of approximately 8,000, only about 800 survive, and fewer than a dozen of 12's <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a> class are among them. The survivors, led by Gale Hawthorne, watch from the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-:30_35-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:30-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the opening stages of the civil war, communications go out in Districts 7, 10, and 12. District 11 gains control of transportation, bringing hope of food to the other rebelling districts.<sup id="cite_ref-:31_80-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:31-80"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>80<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>July 16th-17th: After three days in the woods, the District 12 survivors are discovered and taken to District 13.<sup id="cite_ref-:30_35-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:30-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<hr /><p><b>The events of <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> occur.</b>  
</p><ul><li><i>July 20-31, 75 ADD:</i> It is revealed to the wider populace of Panem that District 13&#160;was not obliterated at the end of the Dark Days. Instead, District 13 secured its independence from the Capitol in exchange for not escalating the Dark Days into a nuclear war. It once again becomes the primary military benefactor of the rebel war effort.<sup id="cite_ref-:48_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:48-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<ul><li><i>August 21</i>: Katniss Everdeen returns to the wreckage of District 12,<sup id="cite_ref-:30_35-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:30-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peeta Mellark's first Capitol propaganda interview airs at 18:30 (in District 13's time zone), and Katniss decides to agree to be the Mockingjay.<sup id="cite_ref-:48_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:48-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><i>August 22</i>: Katniss and District 13 leadership, headed by President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>, come to an agreement on the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_Deal" title="Mockingjay Deal">Mockingjay Deal</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-86" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-86"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>86<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Coin announces the terms to the district that evening at 18:00.<sup id="cite_ref-87" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-87"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>87<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><i>August 23</i>: This morning, <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_8_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">District 8 is heavily bombed</a>, and Katniss is sent there to film a propo in the afternoon.<sup id="cite_ref-88" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-88"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>88<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The field hospital she visits is bombed, but she and Gale take out Capitol hovercraft and get footage in the wreckage.<sup id="cite_ref-89" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-89"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>89<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The propo airs across the districts at 20:00, with seventeen reruns going into the following day.<sup id="cite_ref-:51_90-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:51-90"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>90<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<ul><li>Katniss's propo boosts morale among district rebels, allowing them to take Districts 3 and 11.</li>
<li>Katniss, Gale, and a camera crew return to shoot propos in District 12, where Gale recounts the bombing and escape. Katniss sings "The Hanging Tree" for the first time in ten years.</li></ul></li>
<li><i>August 27</i>:
<ul><li>At 18:30, there's a live broadcast of Peeta in his third and final interview from the Capitol. Beetee breaks into the programming with Finnick's "We Remember" propos, and Peeta issues a warning to 13 about an <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Second_Bombing_of_District_13" title="Bombings">imminent air strike</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:50_51-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:50-51"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> President Coin issues an air raid alarm, and District 13's citizens evacuate into a bunker.<sup id="cite_ref-:52_91-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:52-91"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>91<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li><i>August 27-29</i>: Bunker missiles<sup id="cite_ref-:52_91-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:52-91"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>91<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are launched at the district over the course of three days. Anyone on the first ten levels of the complex would have been killed, but due to Peeta's timely warning, no lives are lost.<sup id="cite_ref-:53_92-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:53-92"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>92<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

They are both sedated, and <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a> begins to make arrangements to rescue Peeta, Annie Cresta, and Johanna Mason.<sup id="cite_ref-:53_92-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:53-92"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>92<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<li>15:00-16:00: District 13 commandos successfully carry out the <a href="/wiki/Infiltration_of_the_Capitol" title="Infiltration of the Capitol">infiltration of the Capitol</a> and secure the three captured victors.<sup id="cite_ref-:49_93-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:49-93"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>93<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>

Katniss Everdeen and Gale Hawthorne&#160;join Commanders <a href="/wiki/Lyme" title="Lyme">Lyme</a> and Paylor as part of the operation.<sup id="cite_ref-94" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-94"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>94<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

Katniss Everdeen is shot in an assassination attempt on live television,<sup id="cite_ref-95" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-95"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>95<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but survives.<sup id="cite_ref-:25_96-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:25-96"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>96<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>

<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a></i></center></div>
<ul><li>Late autumn:
<ul><li>The final engagement of the Second Rebellion begins as rebels launch a full-on assault against the city. The destruction of the Nut took out the Capitol's air force, leaving the defense of the city to Peacekeeper infantry. Capitol forces retreat further into the city, but the rebel advance is hindered by strategically placed traps known as <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pods</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:26_98-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:26-98"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>98<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The week before the arrival of propaganda unit <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>, rebels secure a ten-block stretch outside the train station.<sup id="cite_ref-:26_98-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:26-98"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>98<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Hovercrafts transport Squad 451 from District 13 to the bombed-out remains of District 12. After several days of travel on a cargo train and a six-hour hike through a mountain tunnel, they arrive at the rebel encampment.<sup id="cite_ref-:26_98-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:26-98"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>98<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Day 4, relative to Squad 451's arrival: <a href="/wiki/Leeg_2" title="Leeg 2">Leeg 2</a> is killed by a pod.<sup id="cite_ref-:26_98-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:26-98"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>98<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Day 5: Peeta Mellark arrives the following evening to fill Leeg 2's place in the unit.<sup id="cite_ref-:26_98-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:26-98"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>98<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Day 6: Jackson invents the game Real or Not Real.<sup id="cite_ref-:62_99-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:62-99"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>99<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Day 7: <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a> is fatally wounded by a land mine<sup id="cite_ref-:62_99-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:62-99"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>99<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and transfers his <a href="/wiki/Holo" title="Holo">Holo</a> over to Katniss. After more pods are triggered, killing <a href="/wiki/Mitchell" title="Mitchell">Mitchell</a>, the rest of the squad is presumed dead by the nation. Using this to their advantage, Katniss leads them on her own personal, unapproved mission to assassinate President Snow.<sup id="cite_ref-100" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-100"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>100<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Day 8: Squad 451 proceeds into the city's sewers and <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>. All but five members of Squad 451 are killed by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a>, including Finnick Odair.<sup id="cite_ref-101" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-101"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>101<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The survivors shelter at Tigris's shop.<sup id="cite_ref-:61_102-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:61-102"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>102<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Day 9: Squad 451 continues to shelter with Tigris.<sup id="cite_ref-:61_102-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:61-102"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>102<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Day 10: The squad stays with Tigris. They learn from a broadcast about strategies being implemented by rebels, and quickly learn how the Capitol is counteracting them. They also learn that shopkeepers are being asked to host refugees, so they plan to move on towards the president's mansion the next morning.<sup id="cite_ref-:32_103-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:32-103"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>103<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Day 11: Katniss and Gale head to the City Circle, narrowly surviving gunfire and deadly pods on their way. Gale is captured by Peacekeepers, so Katniss pushes on alone. She is caught up in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="City Circle bombing">City Circle bombing</a>, where her sister, Prim, is killed.<sup id="cite_ref-:32_103-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:32-103"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>103<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Following the bombing of Capitol children, Peacekeepers, and rebel medics, all remaining support for President Snow and his regime collapses under the assumption that he's behind the massacre. He is taken into custody, and rebels take the Capitol, bringing the Second Rebellion to a close.<sup id="cite_ref-:28_104-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:28-104"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>104<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This also brings an end to the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>; only seven victors survive.<sup id="cite_ref-:29_105-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:29-105"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>105<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>

<ul><li>Winter:<sup id="cite_ref-:29_105-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:29-105"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>105<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Snow is tried and sentenced to death,<sup id="cite_ref-:28_104-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:28-104"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>104<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and his collaborators await trial.<sup id="cite_ref-:29_105-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:29-105"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>105<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He explains to Katniss Everdeen that the bombing was a false flag operation by President Coin to turn his people against him, and that in actuality, District 13 bombers were responsible.<sup id="cite_ref-:28_104-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:28-104"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>104<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="76_ADD"><b>76 ADD</b></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTimeline%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D26&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li><i>December-February, 76 ADD:</i><sup id="cite_ref-:19_106-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:19-106"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>106<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>An emergency election is held to replace Coin as president, and <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Commander Paylor</a> from District 8 becomes Panem's first democratically elected leader.</li>
<li>Katniss spends several months in solitary confinement at the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> during her televised <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/trial_in_absentia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:trial in absentia">trial in absentia</a>. Plutarch Heavensbee testifies in her defense, and due to testimony from <a href="/wiki/Dr._Aurelius" title="Dr. Aurelius">Dr. Aurelius</a>, she is deemed <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insanity_defense" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Insanity defense">not guilty by reason of insanity</a>.</li>
<li>Katniss is released from solitary confinement, but restricted to District 12 until further notice. However, Mrs. Everdeen moves to District 4 to start a hospital, so Katniss is entrusted to the care of her mentor, Haymitch Abernathy.</li>
<li>Beetee Latier returns to District 3, and Gale Hawthorne takes a job in District 2.</li></ul></li>
<li>Early spring, 76 ADD:<sup id="cite_ref-:19_106-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:19-106"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>106<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Peeta Mellark returns to District 12.</li>
<li>Work crews in District 12 clear rubble and retrieve bodies for burial in a mass grave at <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a>.</li>
<li>Katniss finds her sister's cat, Buttercup, and after an emotional breakdown, she tends to his wounds.</li></ul></li>
<li><i>Unknown:</i><sup id="cite_ref-:19_106-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:19-106"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>106<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Katniss, Peeta, and Haymitch create their <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">memory book</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Annie_and_Finnick_Odair%27s_son" title="Annie and Finnick Odair&#39;s son">Annie and Finnick Odair's&#160;son</a> is born.</li>
<li>Several hundred people return to District 12 and contribute to rebuilding efforts. With the closure of the coal mines, the district's economy is diversified; people plow ashes into the earth and plant food, and Capitol&#160;machines break ground to construct a factory for medicines.</li>
<li>Reunited, Katniss and Peeta both make a slow, gradual recovery from the emotional trauma they have sustained over the last two years, rekindling their love for one another in the process.</li></ul></li></ul>
