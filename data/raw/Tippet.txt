<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTippet%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Having been born and raised in District 4, she trained since childhood and possibly volunteered for the Games. She was portrayed to be one of the older, stronger tributes in 74th Hunger Games.
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, she and her <a href="/wiki/Bravura" title="Bravura">district partner</a> were dressed in an aqua-colored gown with starfish pins, representing their district, fishing. 
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTippet%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, she is first seen when in a circle with the other tributes. She stands to the left of the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">District 6 female</a> and the right of the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a> when <a href="/wiki/Atala" title="Atala">Atala</a> is giving instructions and talking about survival. Later when <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> is using a spear to fight with the trainer, she is seen behind learning how to throw a knife with her trainer. The next day, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> slices a dummy hand off. Slightly behind him, she is there, trying to get in the gauntlets. Behind her is the <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">District 7 female</a>. She was also seen using a bow and arrow behind Peeta, when the Careers teased Peeta.
</p>

<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e0/4femalerun.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20120923204632" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tippet running with a bag." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="100" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="4femalerun.PNG" data-image-key="4femalerun.PNG" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e0/4femalerun.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120923204632"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e0/4femalerun.PNG/revision/latest?cb=20120923204632" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tippet running with a bag." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e0/4femalerun.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120923204632" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="100" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="4femalerun.PNG" data-image-key="4femalerun.PNG" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/e0/4femalerun.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120923204632"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:4femalerun.PNG" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Tippet running with a bag.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the book, being a Career, she presumably killed and/or injured several tributes.
In the film, she wasn't a part of the Careers and was instead killed by them shortly after the bloodbath. She stood on the pedestal next to <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> and the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a>. When the gong rings she runs towards the Cornucopia. The <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">District 6 female</a> tries to attack her with a serrated sword but she brutally beats her, pushes her to the floor, and grabs the sword. She slashes the <a href="/wiki/District_3_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 3 female (74th HG)">District 3 female's</a> leg with the sword. She gets attacked by Cato and thrown over a crate but manages to escape him. She is spotted by <a href="/wiki/Katniss" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Katniss">Katniss</a> as she grabs a backpack and heads for the forest. 
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTippet%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 170px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Cato_slits_random_girl%21.png/revision/latest?cb=20120412004455" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cato pushing Tippet during the 74th Hunger Games bloodbath" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="170" height="184" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Cato slits random girl!.png" data-image-key="Cato_slits_random_girl%21.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Cato_slits_random_girl%21.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/170?cb=20120412004455"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Cato_slits_random_girl%21.png/revision/latest?cb=20120412004455" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cato pushing Tippet during the 74th Hunger Games bloodbath" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Cato_slits_random_girl%21.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/170?cb=20120412004455" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="170" height="184" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Cato slits random girl!.png" data-image-key="Cato_slits_random_girl%21.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Cato_slits_random_girl%21.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/170?cb=20120412004455"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Cato_slits_random_girl!.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Cato pushing Tippet during the 74th Hunger Games bloodbath</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the film, she's hunted down shortly after the bloodbath and is killed by the Careers. She placed 13th out of 24. However in the book, she and the other Careers hunted down and killed the <a href="/wiki/District_8_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 female (74th HG)">District 8 female</a>. After the forest fire the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a> had created, she and the Careers found <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> in a lake, tending to her burns that she sustained. They chased Katniss up a tree, but since Katniss was too high up and none of the Careers were able to reach her, Peeta suggested that they wait until morning for Katniss to come down. They agreed and camped under the tree.
</p><p>However, Katniss sent a <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nest down onto the Careers, and both <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> and Tippet received numerous stings. Unlike Glimmer, who was swatting at the tracker jackers frantically, Tippet dropped her belongings and ran out of sight to try and make it to the lake, leaving Glimmer to be stung to death before she could escape. Several minutes after Glimmer died, Tippet had collapsed from the many stings she had received and died. She was picked up by the hovercraft before Glimmer, due to Katniss' close proximity to Glimmer's corpse while trying to take her bow and arrows. She placed 11th out of 24. Rue mentioned her death to Katniss later on Day 9, while she was recovering from her tracker jacker stings.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTippet%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the film, she weighs 105 pounds and is 16 years old. Her <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">odds</a> were initially 10-1<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and then 9–1.</li>
<li>In the movie, the scoreboard behind Caesar and Claudius, it is seen that she died 17 minutes and 23 seconds into the Games.</li>
<li>In the arena, she wore a seastorm blue jacket and tied her hair in two pigtails.</li>
<li>In one of the trailers, she's seen throwing a knife in the training center on Marvel's right side.</li>
<li>In the film, she dies after the Cornucopia bloodbath and placed 13th, but when <a href="/wiki/Heddle" title="Heddle">Heddle</a> was killed, on the scoreboard behind Caesar it can be seen that she was the 2nd dead.</li>
<li>In the book, she died in the tracker jacker attack along with Glimmer. She placed 11th in the book and 13th in the movie.</li>
<li>She is Katniss’s second ever kill.</li>
<li>Her actress, <a href="/wiki/Tara_Macken" title="Tara Macken">Tara Macken</a>, was a stunt performer in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.</li>
<li>In <i>Catching Fire</i>, during the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, two women and an unknown third person can be seen standing in front of her portrait, which means she had a mother, at least one female relative or sibling and an unknown third sibling. Her district partner's family is cut off, implying he only had one family member.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Name">Name</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTippet%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>When asked on Twitter about her character's name, her actress replied that it was <b>Marina</b>.<sup id="cite_ref-name_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage" title="The Hunger Games On Stage">The Hunger Games On Stage</a></i>, her name is <b>Tippet</b>. In fly fishing, the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fly_fishing_tackle#Tippets" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Fly fishing tackle">tippet</a> is a section that connects the "leader" to the fly.</li></ul>
