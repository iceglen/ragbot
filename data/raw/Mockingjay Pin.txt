<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The pin originally belonged to <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a>'s aunt<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> a <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> who died in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was created by <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR12_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR12-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It had a sister pin in a form of a hummingbird that Tam Amber made for <a href="/wiki/Merrilee_Undersee" title="Merrilee Undersee">Merrilee</a>; however, this pin was lost down a well. Maysilee hated her pin due to the fact that the mockingjay was half mutt, and that her sister was gifted a hummingbird pin instead. She wanted to return it to Tam Amber and have it melted down and remade into something prettier, but he declined. She then kept the pin in her dresser, much to <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>'s disdain.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tributes put their tokens back on before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> allowed Maysilee to examine his <a href="/wiki/Flint_striker" title="Flint striker">flint striker</a>, which had also been created by Tam Amber. Haymitch commented that he'd heard that Maysilee owned one of Tam's pins, but Maysilee revealed that she didn't think much of it. While the pin was well made, Maysilee didn't care much for <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>, finding a bird that was half <a href="/wiki/Mutt" class="mw-redirect" title="Mutt">mutt</a> to be unnatural, which Haymitch had never considered before. Haymitch told Maysilee that some people thought that the way the mockingjays had escaped the Capitol and survived was a victory in itself. Maysilee suggested that if she escaped the Capitol and survived, she'd give the mockingjay pin another chance. Haymitch suggested that if Maysilee didn't, Lenore Dove would be happy to take it off of her hands.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR12_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR12-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>When Katniss first saw Madge wearing the pin, she thought that it could keep a family fed with <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> for months, since it was made of finely crafted gold.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Madge came to bid Katniss farewell, she gave it to her as a symbol of District 12 and made her promise to wear it in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> later affixed it to Katniss' shirt while they were in the <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">Launch Room</a>, as Katniss had completely forgotten about it. He revealed that it had barely passed the review board because the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> thought the point could be used as a <a href="/wiki/Weapon" class="mw-redirect" title="Weapon">weapon</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, who had a special relationship with the mockingjays back in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, decided that she could trust Katniss after seeing her wear the pin. She rejected Katniss' attempt to give it to her, saying she had her own good luck charm.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the end of the Games, Katniss found it in her room in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> just before leaving to return home.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>In the first movie, Katniss got her pin at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> from <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a>, and she gave it to her younger sister <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> as a gift, promising that it would protect her. Prim returned the pin to Katniss after the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> because she felt Katniss needed it more. Additionally, the concept of a tribute token was seemingly eliminated from this film; Cinna appeared to sneak in the mockingjay pin, pinning it to the inside of Katniss's jacket to hide it and holding a finger to his lips. Later, during the <a href="/wiki/Victor_crowning" class="mw-redirect" title="Victor crowning">victor crowning</a>, <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> also took notice of the pin and examined it.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p>The mockingjay featured on the cover of <i>The Hunger Games</i> holds an <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">arrow</a> in its beak, and it is attached to the ring by its wingtips and tail feathers. This was designed by artist <a href="/wiki/Tim_O%27Brien" title="Tim O&#39;Brien">Tim O'Brien</a>. His wife Elizabeth Parisi, Creative Director for Hardcovers at <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholastic_Corporation" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Scholastic Corporation">Scholastic</a>, was responsible for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>'s book covers, and she has characterized the creation of the mockingjay pin as a collaboration. According to O'Brien, "It is not just a picture of a bird. It is something vulnerable. It is a symbol of the main character, Katniss, who is tough and beautiful at the same time."<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Before the final version was adopted, he had submitted a design for an arrow and an alternate version of the logo that had the mockingjay's right wing pierced with a chain.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The pin worn by Katniss in the first film was based on the cover design of the first book. The piece was created by jewelry designer Dana Schneider, who was also responsible for creating Peeta's locket for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Variations of the pin, burning with <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a>, were also used as logos for the film franchise.
<ul><li>The pin evolved throughout the series: The first pin variation was inspired by the cover design of the first book, but for <i>Catching Fire</i>, the arrow was gone, and the mockingjay faced right in an attacking position. In <i>Mockingjay</i> (and the end credits of <i>Catching Fire</i>), the mockingjay was seen spreading its wings, and it was centered in the middle of the circle.</li></ul></li></ul>
