<p>The <b>65th Hunger Games</b> took place nine years prior to the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. They were won by <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, when he was only fourteen years old, making him the youngest <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> in the history of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Summary">Summary</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F65th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_tributes">Known tributes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F65th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> - District 4 (victor)<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> - <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentored</a> by <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>, who was said to be "half his family"<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F65th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Finnick was a <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a>, so he may have been a part of a typical Career <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>. He fought well with the <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Spear" title="Weapons">spears</a> and <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Knife" title="Weapons">knives</a> he found in the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. While other tributes struggled to get even small <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a>, Finnick "never wanted for anything, not food or medicine or weapons"; he drew in many sponsors with his good looks and fighting skills.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>It took "about a week" for the other tributes to realize that Finnick was the one to beat. He received a trident as a sponsor gift, which had been extremely expensive, and he used it as if it was a "natural, deadly extension of his arm." Finnick was able to weave <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Net" title="Weapons">nets</a> out of vines he'd found in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, entangling opponents before spearing them with his new weapon. In a matter of days, he was crowned the victor.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F65th_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After winning, Finnick was highly desired by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> citizens, but they "couldn't really touch him for a year or two". At age sixteen, Finnick began to go through "four or five" Capitol suitors with each of his annual visits for the Games. This gave him a reputation as a playboy as he was showered with gifts and attention.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Finnick later revealed that he had actually been forced into sex trafficking by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
