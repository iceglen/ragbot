<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="63rd_Hunger_Games">63rd Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGloss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Gloss participated in and won the 63rd Hunger Games, the year before his sister Cashmere won the <a href="/wiki/64th_Hunger_Games" title="64th Hunger Games">64th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> as tribute,<sup id="cite_ref-exhibition_gloss_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-exhibition_gloss-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> as was common in District 1. He may have mentored his sister through her Games. Because of his <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a> status and good looks, Gloss probably had a lot of <a href="/wiki/Sponsor" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor">sponsors</a> and killed a considerable amount of tributes.
</p>

<p>Gloss was <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> from the pool of existing victors from District 1 to participate in the 75th Hunger Games alongside his sister, <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and wore a sheer silver tunic at his reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>It is implied that both Gloss and Cashmere were friends with some of the existing victors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Additionally, Gloss and his sister mentored <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> and <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-exhibition_c_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-exhibition_c-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

He stuck with his sister, and also mingled with the other stronger Career tributes and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, he showed exceptional skill with throwing knives.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss said he got a very high <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a>, along with Cashmere.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>He was presumably a good swimmer, since he and <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> were the fourth and fifth people to make it to the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. In the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, Katniss noted that he wasn't particularly swift, as he was shot in the calf when he plunged into the waves trying to escape Katniss' arrows. This attack was not fatal; he managed to ignore the pain, and made it to the Cornucopia with his allies.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, Gloss nearly reached the Cornucopia at the same time as Katniss, but he tripped over a stone; when he reached the island, Katniss shot him in the calf.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Alliance">Alliance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGloss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Gloss was one of the members in the Career pack with Enobaria, <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a>, and his sister, Cashmere.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Soon after <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, Peeta Mellark, <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>, and Katniss made their way out of the Cornucopia, Gloss and the other Careers made their way back there to pick off more tributes. After the bloodbath, the Career pack retreated to the jungle.
</p>

</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGloss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Since Gloss was a Career tribute, he most likely killed many tributes in his original Games and during the third Quarter Quell. On Day 2, he killed <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, which led to his own death.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGloss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>There's the classically beautiful brother and sister from District 1 who were victors two consecutive years when I was little.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss on Gloss and Cashmere.<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Cashmere, his sister, was the only person mentioned in Gloss's family, both sharing a close relationship. Gloss and Cashmere won consecutive Games, and were presumably highly honored by District 1, as Career Districts honor their victors.
</p>

<p>Gloss and his sister, Cashmere, were both described as being classically beautiful.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He had blond hair like his sister. Considering that both were Career tributes, Gloss and Cashmere were physically stronger than most of the other victors, being relatively young and from pampered District 1.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGloss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>"Gloss" is a word referring to the shine of a smooth surface. This reflects <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>'s manufacture of luxury items.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGloss%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> and Gloss are the only known pair of siblings who have both separately won the Hunger Games.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>, Gloss, Cashmere, and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a> consecutively won the <a href="/wiki/62nd_Hunger_Games" title="62nd Hunger Games">62nd</a>, 63rd, 64th, and <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a>.
<ul><li>On <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, it was revealed that Cashmere won the 64th Hunger Games,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and since Finnick won the 65th Hunger Games, Gloss had to have won the 63rd.</li></ul></li>
<li>In the films, Gloss was the tallest tribute with the biggest build, like <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Cashmere and Gloss were interviewed together by <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> in the film,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> while in the book they were interviewed separately.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Gloss and <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> from the 74th Hunger Games are quite similar. They were both shot in the neck by Katniss in the books because they killed Katniss's female ally.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the movies, they are both shot in the chest.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Gloss was the last tribute Katniss killed in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, or in any Hunger Games.</li></ul></li>
<li>In a behind the scenes featurette, Gloss's actor <a href="/wiki/Alan_Ritchson" title="Alan Ritchson">Alan Ritchson</a> mentioned that he and Cashmere's actress, <a href="/wiki/Stephanie_Leigh_Schlund" title="Stephanie Leigh Schlund">Stephanie Leigh Schlund</a>, had quickly developed a "brotherly-sisterly kind of love". They messed around with each other on set, which created the sense of a real sibling relationship.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
