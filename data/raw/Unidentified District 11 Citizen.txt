<p>The unidentified male was present during Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark's Victory Tour. Following Katniss's speech commemorating the district's deceased <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>, he whistled <a href="/wiki/Rue%27s_four-note_song" title="Rue's four-note song">Rue's four-note song</a>. This was a signal for the crowd to give the <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a>, which had been used in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> after Katniss <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. Seeing this as a sign of <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> pulled the man from the crowd, beat him, and shot him in back of the head, much to Katniss's shock and horror.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
