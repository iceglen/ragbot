<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>Everything beautiful brings her to mind. I see her in the yellow flowers that grow in the Meadow by my house. I see her in the mockingjays that sing in the trees.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> on Rue<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Rue</b> was the 12-year-old female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> who was selected to participate in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Rue formed an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> with <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> after warning her about a nest of <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jackers</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Rue decided to trust Katniss partly because of the pin she wore over her heart: the famous <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Katniss avenged Rue when <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> killed her. As a memorial to Rue's death, Katniss covered her in flowers and sang to her until she died, indicating to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> that Rue was not just a piece in their Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

					<b class="trfc161__question">What led Rue to form an alliance with Katniss Everdeen in the Hunger Games?</b>

					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						Rue, a tribute from District 11 in the Hunger Games, allied with Katniss Everdeen due to their mutual will to survive. Trust was established when Rue saw Katniss's mockingjay pin, a symbol that reminded her of home. Their similarities further solidified the alliance. Katniss's care for Rue while she was incapacitated from tracker jacker venom also reinforced their bond. The alliance was a survival strategy common in the Hunger Games.

					<b class="trfc161__question">What is the significance of the mockingjay pin that Katniss wore in the Hunger Games?</b>

				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">How did Katniss Everdeen react to Rue's death in the Hunger Games?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						Rue's death in the Hunger Games had a profound impact on Katniss Everdeen. It served as a driving force for her to continue her fight. She pledged to win for Rue, a promise she held above her commitment to win for Prim. In a rebellious act against the Capitol, Katniss adorned Rue's body with flowers and performed the District 12 farewell gesture. Rue's memory persisted with Katniss, who memorialized her in a book of loved ones lost to the Capitol's tyranny.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>I'm usually up highest, so I'm the first to see the flag that signals quitting time. There's a special little song I do. And the mockingjays spread it around the orchard. That's how everyone knows to knock off.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Rue<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0f/Rue%27s_family.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140223091659" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Rue's family" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0f/Rue%27s_family.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140223091659" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="105" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Rue's family.jpg" data-image-key="Rue%27s_family.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Rue%27s_family.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Rue's family</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Rue lived in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> prior to the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. She was the eldest child in her family<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, with five younger siblings<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She worked from sunrise to sunset in the orchards in District 11, alongside the other citizens of her district<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Despite her size, she was able to jump from tree to tree in the orchards, even on the most slender branches<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She could scale the tallest trees in District 11, allowing her to see the flag that signaled quitting time, after which she would sing her four-note song. The tune would be picked up by the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a> in the area, which would spread the message through the orchards that the working day was over. Rue claimed that these mockingjays were her special friends.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Rue was chosen during the reaping at the age of 12 for the 74th Hunger Games along with Thresh, District 11's male tribute. When Rue was chosen, her district escort asked if anyone would like to <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a> in Rue's place, but the district answered with silence, and the only sound that could be heard was the wind. Katniss was deeply disturbed by this, as she was the same age and size as <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose</a>, her sister.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Rue and her tribute partner dressed up as a farmer to represent their district's industry, agriculture, for the tribute parade. During the training sessions in the film, Rue showed her swift and silent abilities by swiping <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>'s knife while he wasn't looking. This caused him to attack another tribute, much to the amusement of her district partner, Thresh<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.

<p>While all the tributes were training, Rue observed them closely to see their strengths and weaknesses—placing a special focus on Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a>. When Peeta informed Katniss that they had "a shadow", Rue peeked out from behind a corner, and Katniss and Peeta smiled at her in response.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She managed to get a seven out of twelve in her private session with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. It is likely she showed them her skill to move about swiftly and unnoticed<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, although this belief was never confirmed.

Caesar replied by saying he wouldn't count her out in a million years. <h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the arena, Rue managed to stay alive<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> by staying off the ground<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, much like Katniss did<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She used her extensive knowledge of plants<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> to collect food. She also retrieved a <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpack</a> from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> in the beginning of the Games, but it contained little because she "had to get away from the Cornucopia fast" and she likely retrieved it at the edge of the clearing<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She found a sharp shard of rock she used as a knife. She made a slingshot for herself as well. Rue's pack contained a small water skin and an extra pair of socks she used to keep her hands warm during the cold nights. Her following of Katniss in the Training Center continued in the arena. It is implied that she had followed Katniss even before the tracker jacker incident.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>After the forest fire (in which many of the tributes, including Rue and Katniss, suffered burns<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>) Rue climbed a tree to avoid the approaching Career pack. Noticing Katniss nearby, she alerted her to a <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nest on a higher branch.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the morning, Katniss alerted Rue that she was about to drop the nest; Rue nodded and leapt out of range<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<p>After the tracker jacker attack, Rue continued to follow Katniss. While Katniss attempted to make a fire, she heard a branch crack, realizing it to be Rue after seeing her boot sticking out from behind the tree. She tells Rue the Careers weren't the only ones who could make an alliance. Rue emerged and offered to heal Katniss's tracker jacker wounds with leaves. Relieved, Katniss offered Rue treatment for her burns in exchange, as well as the leg of a <a href="/wiki/Groosling" title="Groosling">groosling</a> she had hunted. Katniss and Rue then discussed their life in their district. After the conversation, Rue explained about the Careers' camp and the strategy they had for protecting food and supplies, giving Katniss the idea of destroying their supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Rue said she trusted Katniss when she saw that Katniss was wearing a mockingjay pin. She then proceeded to help Katniss with her plan to rid the Careers of their food: Rue would light three fires to distract the Careers away from the campsite while Katniss destroyed the supplies. Rue managed to light the first fire<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but soon after the second fire was lit, she stumbled into a trap set up by Marvel, who speared her through the stomach just before Katniss reached her<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. In the movie, Katniss was able to cut her free before Marvel attacked<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>After Katniss killed Marvel, Rue's dying wish was for Katniss to sing to her. Katniss honored her wish and sang <a href="/wiki/Songs#.22Rue.27s_Lullaby.22" title="Songs">a lullaby</a> until she died. The mockingjays soon picked up Katniss's song and sang it through the entire arena.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Furious with the Capitol for allowing an innocent child to die, Katniss covered Rue's body with beautiful white flowers. Before she left, she made District 12's <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">gesture of farewell</a> to Rue.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After Rue died in the film, an <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_11" title="Uprising of District 11">uprising</a> began in District 11, where the citizens rebelled to avenge her death<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Rue placed 7th out of the 24 tributes.
</p>

Katniss then thanked District 11 for their tributes, Rue and Thresh, telling Thresh's family how much she admired him.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

When Peeta asked where Rue was taking her, Katniss replied that "they never arrived, but they felt happy".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rue was the eldest of six children<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She had five younger siblings, all living in poverty in District 11<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>She usually gave her food rations to her younger siblings, and foraged in the fields to provide more food for them<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. District 11 has very strict laws<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and Rue could be punished for helping her family in this way. During the Victory Tour, Katniss saw Rue's family, noting that her siblings greatly resembled Rue<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Her parents were also seen weeping. After <a href="/wiki/Unidentified_District_11_citizen" title="Unidentified District 11 citizen">a man</a> was shot in the crowd, Katniss heard two more shots, which could possibly mean someone in the family was killed or wounded<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. However, at the Quarter Quell, <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a> assured Katniss that Rue's family was safe<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<p>Rue reminded Katniss of her sister, Prim<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_11-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Like Prim<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, Rue sometimes stood on her toes with her arms slightly extended<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_11-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Katniss described her as "birdlike"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_11-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and after the Games, she could barely look at Prim without noticing Rue in her and being reminded of her death<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Small, quick, intelligent, and young, the two had many similarities<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, which led Katniss to make Rue her ally<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Soon after Katniss asked Rue for an alliance, Rue agreed<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, saying she had already made the decision to trust Katniss because of her mockingjay pin<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Katniss also realized she could trust Rue because Rue watched over her while she was unconscious from the tracker jacker venom<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Rue and Katniss had a loving relationship. Rue told Katniss everything about her, like how her favorite thing in the world was music. Katniss felt empathy and pity for her,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> especially since she had been forced into the arena at such a young age<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Katniss demonstrated her love for Rue by crying and mourning her death, then covering her body in flowers to show that she was "more than just a piece in their Games"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Rue and her bonded the night Katniss awakened from her tracker jacker injuries. Rue received a groosling leg from Katniss after she refused the first few times.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss and Rue discussed their life and district with each other. Rue told Katniss about the Careers' camp to help with her plan to destroy their supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Being the smaller of the two, Rue was in charge of setting three fires to distract the Careers as Katniss approached their camp. To communicate, Rue would sing her four-note melody to let Katniss know that all was going well. However, soon after the second fire was lit, Rue stumbled into a trap set up by Marvel, who stabbed her through the abdomen with his spear while Katniss ran to save her<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. After dispatching Marvel, Rue begged Katniss to win and to sing to her as she died. As an act of defiance and a way to honor Rue's death, Katniss covered Rue's corpse with brightly colored flowers and gave her District 12's farewell gesture.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Thresh">Thresh</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>For the little girl.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Thresh sparing Katniss, referring to her alliance with Rue<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Thresh liked Rue and took pity on her since she was so young, feeling that she did not deserve to be forced into the Games. Thresh was infuriated and saddened by her death<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. During training in the film, when Rue stole Cato's knife, Thresh looked up and smiled at Rue<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Later, at the feast, Thresh overheard Clove taunting Katniss about Rue and taking credit for her death. Enraged, Thresh pushed Clove off Katniss and held her above the ground by her throat, yelling accusations at her. Terrified, Clove insisted she didn't kill Rue (this is true, as it was Marvel who killed her<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>), but Thresh didn't believe her and crushed her skull with a large rock, avenging Rue's death. Due to Katniss' alliance with Rue, Thresh spared her life, suggesting that he cared for her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rue had a gentle and kind nature. Her greatest joy in life was music, and she loved singing with the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a> at home. The mockingjays liked her and were used to her; a few were even her special friends because she was the one who sang a four-note melody that signaled the end of a work day out in the fields.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>While in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, Rue showed excitement at the prospect of adventure - one character trait that <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> noted differed her from <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Rue was clever and quite observant of her surroundings, noticing every piece of information she saw when spying on the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Careers</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Being the eldest child in her family, Rue was a caretaker and always made sure her younger siblings were tended to<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Similarly, she helped heal Katniss in the arena and willingly shared her supplies and knowledge<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Once allied with Katniss, Rue put a great deal of trust in her, falling asleep quickly that night and agreeing to Katniss's schemes<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills">Skills</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Like me, she's clever with plants, climbs swiftly, and has good aim. She can hit the target every time with a slingshot.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss on Rue's skills in the Training Center<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>

<p>Rue had a talent for singing; her greatest joy was music. During work she would sing to the mockingjays and they would sing back. She was also able to whistle a four note song which had meaning in her district that the day of work was over.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Exceptionally talented at climbing, she could silently jump from one tree to another without being noticed; Katniss compared her skill to that of the birds<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Rue was able to stalk and <a href="/wiki/Tracking" class="mw-redirect" title="Tracking">track</a> Katniss and other tributes during the Games to gain information and no one picked up that they were being followed or watched by her<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Also, she climbed to the top of the rafters during training after stealing a knife from Cato in the film<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Rue was also intelligent. She was able to survive by herself before she formed her alliance with Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She survived mostly on roots and berries that she found and discovered could be eaten. She was expected to have survived by staying off the ground and hiding in trees, giving her a significant advantage as most of the other tributes were unable to climb. Rue was very observant as well, picking up every small and important detail about the Careers' camp and some useful information about other tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Rue was very talented with a <a href="/wiki/Slingshot" class="mw-redirect" title="Slingshot">slingshot</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_11-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Possessions">Possessions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rue's <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a> was a grass necklace with a wooden star, used as a good luck charm in the Games<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She received a water skin and an extra pair of socks from the Cornucopia that she used to keep her hands warm. She also made herself a slingshot, which seemed to be of good quality, and a knife that she made from a sharp shard of rock.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rue is named after a small mountain flower called <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruta_graveolens" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Ruta graveolens">Ruta graveolens</a></i>. Considering the flower is also known as "herb-of-grace", it may explain Rue's delicate touch and gentleness.
</p>

<ul><li>Rue received her name from a small mountain flower (Ruta graveolens), just as Katniss received her name from a waterside plant, and Prim received her name from a bush (Primrose bush). Symbolically, both Ruta graveolens and primrose blossoms close at night.</li>
<li>'Rue' is also a word meaning repentance or regret.</li>
<li>There was some backlash from the public at the casting of Amandla Stenberg, an African American actress, when it was first announced, but Stenberg merely responded that she was glad to be a part of the film.</li>
<li>In the promotional posters, almost every character is looking left, with the exception of Katniss (who is looking right) and Rue (who faces left but is looking down).</li>
<li>Rue foraged for food in the <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">meadows</a> before the Games<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, which is a very serious offense in District 11<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li>
<li>If Katniss had not volunteered for her sister, Prim, there would have been at least two 12-year-old girls participating in the 74th Hunger Games from the poorer districts.</li>
<li>She was the shortest female tribute in her Games.</li>
<li>In the film, she received the lowest number of odds to win the games, 60–1, possibly due to her height.</li>
<li>Her odds of winning are shown to change from 60–1 to 7–1 in the film.</li>
<li>Rue received a training score of 7, which was a very high score for her age and size<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li>
<li>At the start of the Games, Rue runs away from the Cornucopia, as she tells Katniss while looking at Rue's supplies<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li>
<li>Rue was the only 12 year old in the 74th Hunger Games that survived after the bloodbath, despite being the tribute with the lowest odds.</li>
<li>Rue trusted Katniss because of her mockingjay pin<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li>
<li>In the book, Rue is the one to show Katniss the mockingjay signal<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_3-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but in the film, it is Katniss that shows Rue<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li>
<li>Rue reminds Katniss of Prim<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_11-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Both Rue's and Prim's deaths heavily affected Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_7-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_17-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-28"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.</li>
<li>Rue's cannon is never heard in the film.</li>
<li>Rue survived for 9 days in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">book</a> and for 6 days in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">film</a>.</li>
<li>Rue, Thresh and Marvel are the only named deceased tributes from <i>The Hunger Games</i> film to appear in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.</li>
<li>In a deleted scene from the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">film</a>, Rue confesses to Katniss that she has a crush on a boy in her district.</li></ul>
