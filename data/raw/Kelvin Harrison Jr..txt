</p><p><b>Kelvin Harrison Jr.</b> is an American actor set to play young <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-cast_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cast-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He started his acting career with small roles in <i>Ender's Game</i> and <i>12 Years a Slave</i>. Harrison is also well know for acting as Tyler Williams in <i>Waves</i>, Luce Edgar in <i>Luce</i> and Fred Hampton in <i>The Trial of the Chicago 7</i>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKelvin_Harrison_Jr.%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Harrison co-starred with <a href="/wiki/Jeffrey_Wright" title="Jeffrey Wright">Jeffrey Wright</a>, who played <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> in the original film trilogy in the movie <i>Monster</i> (2018). Harrison played Wright's character's son.</li>
<li>Harrison has the same birthday as <a href="/wiki/Woody_Harrelson" title="Woody Harrelson">Woody Harrelson</a>, who played <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> in the film trilogy, and the late <a href="/wiki/Philip_Seymour_Hoffman" title="Philip Seymour Hoffman">Philip Seymour Hoffman</a>, who played <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>. Harrelson, Hoffman, and Harrison were all born on July 23rd but different years.</li></ul>
