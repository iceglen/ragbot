<p>A <b>healer</b> is a person who treats the <a href="/wiki/Health_conditions" title="Health conditions">sick and wounded</a> people of their <a href="/wiki/Panem#Districts" title="Panem">district</a>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Since almost nobody could afford doctors in the districts, the apothecaries were the healers. One notable healer of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> was <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid Everdeen</a>, who healed many of the injured in the district,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> such as <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miners</a> suffering from accidents,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> after he was brutally whipped by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> also took an interest in healing after assisting her mother countless times.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She was young, but she took after her healer mother and naturally had a great ability to heal people. After the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombing of District 12</a>, when Gale collected more than eight hundred people and escaped out the forest, Prim and her mother set up a medical area and healed injured people who survived out of the fire. They had very few things to treat from, but they tried their best by gathering whatever they could from the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>After Gale's whipping, when Asterid and Prim were healing him, Asterid remarked that it was lucky that they had snow for a <a href="/wiki/Snow_coat" title="Snow coat">snow coat</a>. When <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> asked what she did in the summer, Asterid put on a weary face and replied that she "tried to keep the flies away."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>It is imperative that a healer is calm and willing to do whatever it takes to keep a patient alive.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Healers like Asterid use numerous plants and natural substances as medicines while helping a patient. Some of these plants are found in the wild, such as the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> and <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On occasion, a healer will use fever pills<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Sleep_syrup" title="Sleep syrup">sleep syrup</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or even <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Since the districts do not have official hospitals or doctors, a healer is their main source of medical care and is equivalent to a doctor.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> had a hospital in the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but it is unknown whether there were actual doctors or healers there.
</p>
