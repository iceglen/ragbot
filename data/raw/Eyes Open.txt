</p><p><b>"Eyes Open"</b> is the third overall single released from the album, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Songs_from_District_12_and_Beyond" title="The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond">The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond</a></i>&#160;and the first official single (the first two were promotional singles). The song is performed and written by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Taylor Swift">Taylor Swift</a>.            
This is one of two songs that Taylor Swift wrote for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>. "<i><a href="/wiki/Safe_and_Sound" class="mw-redirect" title="Safe and Sound">Safe and Sound</a></i>" was featured at the end of the film during the credits whereas "<i>Eyes Open</i>"&#160;was not.
</p><p>Swift has since re-recorded many of her songs that were originally written and published prior to 2018, "Safe &amp; Sound" and "Eyes Open" included. Both songs, now bearing the title of "(Taylor's Version)," were released at midnight on March 17th 2023 after being teased by Swift the previous day.
</p>
