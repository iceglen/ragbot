<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>The Newcomers</b> were an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> of tributes formed by <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> to build an alliance to rival the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Pack</a> during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. Ampert hoped this would even out the odds and combat the hunting tactics used by Careers. A number of them were also <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> involved in a plot to disrupt the Hunger Games. The Newcomers proved themselves to be more intelligent and better organized than the Careers, which resulted in them weathering the arena's dangers better and ultimately winning the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. However, the Capitol later heavily edited the footage of the Games to make it seem like the Careers were the more organized and intelligent alliance.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Newcomers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Inception">Inception</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Newcomers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Historically, the Career Tributes typically had the upper hand in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> as they trained in specialized combat academies, formed an alliance, controlled the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> and hunted down the other tributes. <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a>, a male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>, tried to change this during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> by proposing a similar alliance to the other non-career districts. He quickly secured Districts <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">7</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a> and approached <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> who agreed to think about it.<sup id="cite_ref-SR6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ampert then asked <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> if <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> was interested in joining. Haymitch was unsure<sup id="cite_ref-SR6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but after thinking about it, decided to introduce his fellow District 12 tributes to Ampert and his alliance at the next training session. By then, Ampert had also persuaded <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> to join them. Now with District 12 also on board, Ambert held a meeting with 3, 7, 8, 10 and 12, where he explained that while the Careers consisted of just three districts, the other tributes were three times their numbers so if they banded together as well, they could hunt the Careers instead and show they weren't falling for their mind games. <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> pointed out they still didn't have all of the districts to sway the odds.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After watching a few Careers bully the <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> tributes, Haymitch and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> showed them kindness and told them the offer of an alliance was still open. They immediately followed Haymitch to the rest of the alliance and officially joined. Needing an official name for the alliance, the group proposed several ideas before settling on the "Newcomers". They completed the rest of the training session together.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> By the next training session, Ampert had finally secured District 11's loyalty, and <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> -- who was essential to Ampert's father <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee's</a> sabotage plan -- quietly joined by sitting with the Newcomers at lunch after Maysilee helped the tributes when they were bullied by <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a> for refusing to join the Careers. However, they failed to get <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, who showed they had joined the Careers. It is likely that Careers asked 5 to join their pack because they had noticed the formation of the Newcomers.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Training_scores">Training scores</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Newcomers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Newcomers, now outnumbering the Careers by two to one, got <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training scores</a> averaging four to seven. The District 11 tributes were the only Newcomers to get scores within the eight to eleven range like the Careers. Haymitch's score brought the Newcomers down after his antics towards the Gamemakers during his private session was rewarded with a score of one.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After this, Haymitch felt like the Gamemakers might have it out for him, which along with his secret plan to help <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> destroy the arena, led to his decision to go it alone in the arena instead of putting the Newcomers at even greater risk.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Newcomers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>News of an alliance to rival the Careers was first revealed in public during tribute interviews with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>. <a href="/wiki/Dio" title="Dio">Dio</a> and Ampert addressed the disproportionate number of Career wins and detailed their alliance with names and skills, impressing Caesar and the audience with their intelligence. The tributes from District 4, a Career district, were instantly asked if they had plans to counter the Newcomers and what they reckoned their chances were. As they fumbled to put together a response, District 5 tried to present their case against the Newcomers but despite their efforts to point out the weaknesses of their rivals, proved the Careers had not discussed a counterattack.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>For the rest of the night, the Newcomers continued to prop up their alliance. They said they were going to share provisions, and while they did not have a leader, credited Ampert with bringing them together. This was shaken when <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a> took the stage and when asked what the Newcomers would do when the Careers are dead, she started calling the audience "murderers". Haymitch fixed this by promising the Newcomers were plenty smart. The interviews were considered a successful promotion of their adversary and boosted their chances of gaining sponsors.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Newcomers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Despite the alliance, sixteen of the dead on the first day were Newcomers compared to four Careers, so their numbers were now nearly even.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They were all killed during the initial bloodbath, six by <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a> wielding a sword, during which the Careers took most of the food. <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> was separated from the rest of the alliance, the Newcomers set up on the mountain, while Haymitch ran to the forest on his own and was soon followed by Lou Lou.<sup id="cite_ref-SR17_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Several of the Newcomers were poisoned by the arena but <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a> had a pack of syrup antidote and managed to save them. On the second day, both alliances lost one more tribute each. Ampert tracked down Haymitch, claiming the Newcomers needed him. They lose no-one on the third day.<sup id="cite_ref-SR17_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Despite achieving Beetee's goal to blow the water tank on the fourth day, Ampert was killed by a <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a> and the arena was not destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>This triggered a volcanic eruption on the mountain the Newcomers were hiding on. While waiting for news on the dead, Haymitch and Maysilee reunited and killed three Careers. When the dead were revealed that night, the Newcomers lost seven and the Careers lost nine. With only two Careers and eight Newcomers, the odds were in favour of a non-career victor.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the end, the two remaining tributes were Haymitch of the Newcomers and <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> of the Careers. In an act of defiance, Haymitch exposed the forcefield surrounding the arena by ducking when Silka threw her axe, which rebounded and lodged itself in her skull. As such, the Newcomers successfully stole victory from the Careers.<sup id="cite_ref-SR24_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR24-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Newcomers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the <a href="/wiki/Victor%27s_Ceremony" title="Victor&#39;s Ceremony">Victor's Ceremony</a>, a heavily edited recap portrayed the Newcomers as less unified than they were and the Careers appeared smarter. Much of the Newcomers' activity was deliberately cut or glossed over.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Unlike the Careers, the alliance did not continue to be formed every year in future Hunger Games, as there is no mention of one in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Newcomers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>At 32 tributes, this was the largest <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> in Hunger Games history.</li>
<li>Alternative suggested names for the Newcomer alliance included Loose Cannons (from District 12), Dark Horses (from 10), and Invaders (from 7). They were eventually named the Newcomers, after the tradition of Neddie Newcomer in the District 12 mines.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
