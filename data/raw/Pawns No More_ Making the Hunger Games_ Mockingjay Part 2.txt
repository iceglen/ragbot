</p><p><i><b>Pawns No More: Making the Hunger Games: Mockingjay Part 2</b></i> is a feature-length documentary film about the making of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>. It was included as a featurette on DVD releases.
</p>

<p>The documentary is organized into eight sections:
</p>
<ol><li>Walking Through Fire: Concluding the Saga</li>
<li>Real or Not Real: Visual Design</li>
<li>High Value Targets: The Acting Ensemble</li>
<li>From Head to Toe: Costume, Makeup &amp; Hair</li>
<li>Navigating the Minefield: Production in Atlanta, Paris &amp; Berlin</li>
<li>Collateral Damage: Stunts, Special Effects &amp; Weapons</li>
<li>Tightening the Noose: The Post-Production Process</li>
<li>A Different World: Reflections</li></ol>
