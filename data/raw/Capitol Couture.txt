<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Behind_the_project">Behind the project</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Couture%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Capitol Couture and TheCapitol.PN, another promotional site, were created by the Watson Design Group for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lionsgate">Lionsgate</a>. Capitol Couture was a five-issue fashion magazine that "[blended] in-world storytelling with real-world fashion icons".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>While the custom Capitol Couture domain is no longer up or active, the blog on <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumblr" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tumblr">Tumblr</a> is still available for fans to browse. The blog's custom layout faces a number of cosmetic issues due to lack of maintenance, but as of February 2025, its approximately 400 posts and basic functions for navigation remain intact.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Capitol Couture posts can be searched, viewed, and shared more readily through an alternate URL,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or Tumblr's built-in blog archive feature.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The archive doesn't truly back up or preserve information from older versions of the blog, though; it is simply an alternate portal to search and view its posts through.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notable_campaigns">Notable campaigns</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Couture%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Photographer and motion picture marketing executive <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Palen" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tim Palen">Tim Palen</a> headed much of the marketing for the films, including Capitol Couture campaigns.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Capitol_Portraits">Capitol Portraits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Couture%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>From March 28th to March 29th, 2013, Capitol Couture posted a series of ten portraits that showcased main characters and supporting cast from <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire"><i>The Hunger Games:</i> <i>Catching Fire</i></a>. An eleventh portrait of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was posted May 30th.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>
Each character was shown sitting in either a wooden chair or an ornate upholstered seat. The five <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> wore the same outfits from their <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, and they, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> each had a single white rose clutched in one hand. <h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Cover_Story:_Game_Faces">Cover Story: Game Faces</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Couture%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In this campaign, Capitol Couture posted eleven portraits of tributes who would compete in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> in <i>Catching Fire</i>. Katniss's portrait was the first one posted on August 13, 2013. Others would be posted sporadically over the next few months. The last portrait, belonging to <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, came on October 8th.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>
Each pictured tribute wore their <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena uniform</a>, and they were rated on a scale of 1 to 10 in four categories: coif, pout, poise, and an overall style score. There was also a short written piece commenting on the tribute's appearance or demeanor. <h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Contributions_to_Canon">Contributions to Canon</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Couture%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Game_Faces">Game Faces</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Couture%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>Mags won her Games "over five decades" before the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> for, and won, the <a href="/wiki/62nd_Hunger_Games" title="62nd Hunger Games">62nd Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> was the victor of the <a href="/wiki/64th_Hunger_Games" title="64th Hunger Games">64th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Since she and her brother won in consecutive years,<sup id="cite_ref-:7_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Finnick Odair won the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> had to have won the <a href="/wiki/63rd_Hunger_Games" title="63rd Hunger Games">63rd Hunger Games</a>.</li>
<li>Johanna Mason won the <a href="/wiki/71st_Hunger_Games" title="71st Hunger Games">71st Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Since she was 21 during the Quell,<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> she would have been seventeen at the time of her first victory.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour_posters_2">Victory Tour posters</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Couture%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>Porter Millicent Tripp, from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, won the <a href="/wiki/38th_Hunger_Games" title="38th Hunger Games">38th Hunger Games</a>. Her neck was broken in the final showdown, and due to her spinal injury, she wore a head brace throughout her tour.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Mags' surname Flanagan was first revealed here, and she won the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Since she was eighty at the time of the Quell,<sup id="cite_ref-:7_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> she would have been sixteen then.</li>
<li>Augustus Braun, from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>, won the <a href="/wiki/67th_Hunger_Games" title="67th Hunger Games">67th Hunger Games</a>. He was also known as "The Cavalier Career" and "Panem's Favorite Son". During his Games, he had an "unkempt persona", and he was very popular in the Capitol, seen as a spreading a message of "reconciliation".<sup id="cite_ref-:6_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
