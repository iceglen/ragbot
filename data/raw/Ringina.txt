<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRingina%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRingina%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the start of the Games, Ringina joined an alliance between District <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">7</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> &amp; <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a> tributes in an attempt to counter the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> pack. Ringina's district was the first to join the <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">Newcomers</a> alliance. Ringina and her district partners were considered "nothing special" by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During training, the District 7 tributes were present at the knife throwing <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">station</a> taught by <a href="/wiki/Hersilia" title="Hersilia">Hersilia</a>. At one point, Ringina hit a bulls-eye, which <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> complimented. Haymitch notes that he knew he wouldn't be able to kill her. Immediately afterward, Haymitch was punched by <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, who was then taken away by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>. In their absence, Ringina and Haymitch both considered attacking the remaining Peacekeepers, but chose not to. Ringina returned her knife and checked to make sure Haymitch's ribs weren't broken.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_7_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While discussing how they nearly attacked the Peacekeepers, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> asked why they didn't. Ringina replied that they have the guns, but Plutarch questions whether it was really about that and brought up that the districts have more numbers than the Capitol. Later on, Haymitch introduced her to <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> after District 12 decided to join the Newcomers alliance. While discussing the plan for the Newcomers to hunt down the Careers first, she asked what they do when they've killed them. <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> told her they will have another meeting. Ringina was the first to suggest that the Newcomers needed a name.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_8_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the cannons sounded for the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>, Haymitch worried that she would be one of the deceased. However, later that night, Haymitch watched the night sky reveal those who died on the first day of the Games and was relieved to see that Ringina survived the bloodbath.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRingina%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Ringina made it into the top ten along with the other girl from her district, <a href="/wiki/Autumn" title="Autumn">Autumn</a>. They were killed by Careers <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> and <a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a> in combat.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRingina%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ringina was a slim girl with glossy, black braids. She wore small carved pin of a tree on her shirt as her <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_7_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The District 7 tributes' <a href="/wiki/Training_outfits" title="Training outfits">training outfits</a> were russet brown.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_8_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRingina%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The District 7 tributes are noted by Haymitch as being "confident, but not full of themselves". Ringina herself was shown to be a good sport and willing to work and practice training with the other tributes who were not from her district. She also showed concerned for Haymitch when he was ambushed and attacked by Panache. In addition, despite being a skilled fighter, Ringina wanted to fight Peacekeepers, but knew she couldn't, believing the Capitol to be stronger than the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_7_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRingina%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ringina is a portmanteau of 'ring', referring to the rings of a tree, and the name 'Regina', meaning 'queen' in Latin.
</p>
