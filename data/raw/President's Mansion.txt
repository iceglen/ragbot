</p><p>The <b>President's mansion</b>, also known as the <b>Presidential Palace</b>, is a location which appears in all three books. Found in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a> of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, it was home to President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, and briefly, President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>. After being elected the new president of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, <a href="/wiki/Paylor" class="mw-redirect" title="Paylor">Paylor</a> presumably resides there as well.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPresident%2527s_mansion%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> rode their chariots in a procession through the Capitol into the City Circle, where they stopped in front of the mansion to listen to a welcoming speech given by Snow.
</p><p>Following the end of their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> and arrival in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> attended a grand party put on in the banquet room. When <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> danced with Katniss, he showed her his watch on the pretext of indicating the lateness of the hour and quickly flashed an image of a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">mockingjay</a> by rubbing the watch face. This was a subtle hint at the nature of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">arena</a>, as well as Plutarch's true allegiance to the <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>. During the 75th Games, the tributes also rode by Snow's mansion.
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>, Katniss told <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> that she was on a mission to infiltrate the mansion to kill Snow on Coin's orders. However, the squad never did infiltrate the mansion, because President Snow surrendered to the rebels after a Capitol <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> (operated by Coin) dropped bombs on the Capitol children and some rebel medics, including <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>. Later, the mansion became the new home of President Coin. Katniss woke up inside the mansion and went to the <a href="/wiki/Rose_garden" title="Rose garden">rose garden</a>, where she met some rebel soldiers guarding it, but was let in with Commander Paylor's permission. Inside, she found Snow, shackled to a chair and awaiting execution. Snow told Katniss that he himself did not order the bombing; rather, Coin did to force the Capitol to surrender. Later, the surviving <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> met and voted to have a Hunger Games including Capitol children. Immediately following this, Katniss was to shoot Snow with an arrow (firing the symbolic "last shot" of the rebellion), but shot and killed Coin instead to avenge Prim's death.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPresident%2527s_mansion%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The President's mansion is very large and Katniss presumes it to be full of <a href="/wiki/Pods" class="mw-redirect" title="Pods">pods</a> to discourage intruders. It contains a garden, where Snow grows his genetically enhanced roses. It also contains a ballroom, and a meeting room. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPresident%2527s_mansion%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> utilizes the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swan_House_(Atlanta)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Swan House (Atlanta)">Swan House</a> (an historic mansion in Atlanta, Georgia) as the real life Presidential Palace. However, in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i> the <i>Château de Voisins</i> (Saint-Hilarion, near Paris) was used for filming the presidential reception rooms and the rear facade of the mansion.</li></ul>
