</p><p><b>Smylie Bradwell</b> (born 9 July 2016) is an English child actor. He is set to play <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.
</p>
