<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p><b>Scrips</b> were a form of coin used in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> store in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. Although they were only officially valid in the Capitol store, they were accepted by many of the town <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchants</a>, as well as <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">Mrs. Abernathy</a> for her laundry business. However, the Donner family would not accept scrips. It was distinct from cash. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FScrip%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> traded his bottle of white liquor for cash with <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid March</a>, he was grateful that she gave him cash and not scrips, because he was going to the Donner store next, and the Donners were one of the merchant families that did not accept scrips.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a> saw <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> fiddling with a scrip coin, she realized that he was one of the Booker Boys. That night, she told Haymitch that she didn't want Wyatt to be one of their allies.<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During <a href="/wiki/Training" title="Training">training</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> wove a zinc scrip coin into Wyatt's token, a cord. She designed it so it would be easy to slip the coin out, since flipping it through his fingers helped Wyatt process things.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch carried Wyatt's scrip coin with him in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> after his death in the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SR23_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR23-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> having received it from <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a>, who recovered the coin after Wyatt's death.<sup id="cite_ref-SR17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR17-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
