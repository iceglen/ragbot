<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAnnie_and_Finnick_Odair%2527s_son%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sometime between Annie's <a href="/wiki/Infiltration_of_the_Capitol" title="Infiltration of the Capitol">rescue</a> from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Finnick's deployment,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the couple conceived a child together in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Finnick died in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but Annie survived and gave birth to their son after the war ended. She sent a photo of him to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, who preserved it in their <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">memory book</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
