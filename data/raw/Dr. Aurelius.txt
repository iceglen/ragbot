<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDr._Aurelius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDr._Aurelius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Dr. Aurelius was a male head doctor (presumably meaning a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/psychologist" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:psychologist">psychologist</a> or <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/psychiatrist" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:psychiatrist">psychiatrist</a>) of unknown origin. Directly after the end of the war, he took on <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> as a client during her stay in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. Other doctors were confused when Katniss didn't talk, and tests were conducted on her vocal cords, showing she should've been able to speak. He came up with the theory that Katniss had become a "mental, rather than physical, <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avox</a>," from the emotional trauma of watching <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">her sister</a> die. He instructed the other doctors to leave her alone. Katniss liked him because he didn't offer empty assurances; he just asked her if she'd like to talk, and when she didn't, he used their sessions as opportunities to take naps.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Katniss assassinated <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">President Coin</a>, Dr. Aurelius was summoned to her trial, where he presented her as a "hopeless, shell-shocked lunatic" to save her from a criminal conviction. In the end, Katniss was confined to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, and she was supposed to keep in contact with Dr. Aurelius to monitor her mental condition, although she never answered the phone.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the meantime, Dr. Aurelius treated <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> in the Capitol and later cleared him to return to District 12. Peeta conveyed the doctor's message to Katniss that he can't pretend to be treating her forever, and she eventually did contact the doctor. Dr. Aurelius' advice was to just go through the motions, and Katniss told him her idea about making a <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">book of memories</a>. Dr. Aurelius agreed and sent a large box of parchment paper on the next Capitol <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> to help her with her project.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDr._Aurelius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Dr. Aurelius made a brief appearance at the beginning of the film as a <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">rebel</a> doctor in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. This iteration of the doctor was a female medical doctor who treated Katniss after she was strangled by a <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacked</a> Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDr._Aurelius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Aurelius is a name derived from the Latin "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/aurum">aurum</a>", meaning gold. It could also be a reference to the Roman emperor and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoicism" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Stoicism">Stoic</a> philosopher <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Aurelius" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Marcus Aurelius">Marcus Aurelius</a>.
</p>
