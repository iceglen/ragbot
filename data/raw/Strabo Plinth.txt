</p><p><b>Strabo Plinth</b> was a wealthy ammunitions tycoon. He was originally from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> and later moved to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> with his wife, <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Mrs. Plinth</a>, and son, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>. A war profiteer, his power and influence gave him opportunities most in the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> could only dream of.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStrabo_Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, he made a fortune off munitions, managing to buy himself and his family a place in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, and chose to side with <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a> against the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>. By doing so, and strategically donating a portion of his wealth, he was able to see to it that his family enjoyed privileges that even the oldest and most powerful families of the Capitol had only earned over generations. One such donation allowed his son, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, a place at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>His son, Sejanus, was a top-performing student at the Academy and thus was selected for the <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> program for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. He personally saw to it that his son was assigned the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>. He was trying to teach his rebellious son a lesson, as Sejanus still sympathized with the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Strabo's move backfired, in that Marcus escaped during a bombing of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. Marcus was captured and strung up inside the arena at the start of the games and Sejanus, in a rash move, snuck inside the arena to try to help him. After Sejanus was rescued by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, Strabo created the <a href="/wiki/Plinth_Prize" title="Plinth Prize">Plinth Prize</a> to try to tide things over and keep his son's actions from being revealed publicly. This was a prize that was supposed to be awarded to the student who <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentored</a> the victor of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, a prize large enough to pay for their <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a> tuition. Strabo denied that it had been his idea, instead giving credit to his son.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the third night of the Games, Coriolanus headed to the Plinth apartment, where he met Strabo Plinth. Strabo offered him whiskey, then when he refused, compared Coriolanus to his parents. After thanking Coriolanus for saving Sejanus in the arena, they wished each other good night and Coriolanus left.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ultimately, <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, head <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>, felt that Sejanus's actions constituted treason. Strabo then bought her off by funding a new gymnasium for <a href="/wiki/Agrippina_Sickle" title="Agrippina Sickle">Agrippina Sickle</a>, as well as a laboratory for Dr. Gaul's <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a> projects.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Because of this, they agreed to have Sejanus enroll in the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> to get him out of the way, as well as letting Coriolanus officially graduate. Sejanus was sent to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> with Coriolanus Snow, who had also been disgraced in the games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> There, Sejanus ultimately got involved in a rebel plot. Coriolanus recorded evidence of Sejanus's treachery via a <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjay</a> and sent it back to the Capitol, where it was heard by Dr. Gaul.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>My wife needs something to live for. So do I, for that matter. You've lost your parents. We've lost our son. I was thinking perhaps we could work something out.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Strabo Plinth, explaining to Snow why he wanted to make him his heir<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>With Sejanus dead, Strabo adopted Coriolanus as his heir, entirely unaware of the boy's betrayal of his son. He funded both his tuition and lifestyle, ending the poverty that had plagued his life since the end of the Dark Days. For his part, Coriolanus got along well with him, finding at times that he could almost forget that he had come from one of the districts. Strabo neither questioned Snow's expenditures nor nitpicked new additions to Snow's wardrobe and seemed pleased when Snow asked him for advice.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStrabo_Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was an extremely wealthy man and seemed convinced that with enough money, he could buy anything. To some degree, his strategy seemed to work. He bought himself and his family a place in the Capitol and a position of power. He also was able to buy his son into the Academy.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>However, his money did not result in his son's loyalty or obedience. It was ultimately also unable to save his son's life when Sejanus was made into a Peacekeeper and engaged in rebellious activity in District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStrabo_Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Before announcing the Plinth Prize, Strabo had a stern expression which, according to Coriolanus Snow, seemed to be "carved from the very granite of his home district".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Coriolanus visited him in his apartment, Strabo had a profoundly sad expression on his face and wore an expensive smoking jacket with a velvet lapel. However, Coriolanus thought it suggested that the Plinths were always trying too hard to be Capitol, and clashed with their district personae rather than disguising them.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStrabo_Plinth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The name Strabo may come from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strabo" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Strabo">Strabo</a>, a Greek geographer, philosopher and historian during the transitional period from the Roman Republic into the Roman Empire. 
</p><p>A plinth is a heavy base that supports a statue, vase, or column, coming from ancient Greco-Roman times. This can be a fitting surname as the Plinths supported the Capitol militarily during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>.
</p>
