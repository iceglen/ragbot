</p><p><b>Hilarius Heavensbee</b> was a male resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. He was assigned the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHilarius_Heavensbee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Mine's a runt. Wovey, she calls herself. Well, I tried to train old Wovey for the interview, but zero personality. No one's backed her, so I can't feed her, even if she can avoid the others.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Hilarius speaking to <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a> about Wovey<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Despite his family's extreme wealth, he always seemed to feel oppressed, bemoaning his assignment to a girl from a poorer district. He believed Wovey had no personality and was concerned about her lack of support in the Capitol. He hoped that she would make it to the final twelve so his parents were not disappointed in him.
</p><p>Before the Games began, Hilarius engaged in discussion with <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a> about female tributes, with Hilarius believing female tributes are weaker than the male tributes, thus have a large disadvantage.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the Games began, Hilarius grew concerned that nobody had seen Wovey for a significant period of time, and that he would be unable to send her food or water as a result. He was the only mentor that seemed to be in the mood to talk about the Games directly, and wouldn't shut up about it.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He eventually spotted her and sent her food and water, speaking lengthily about Wovey's merits to <a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a> and presenting her absence as stealth. However, she collapsed and died shortly after drinking from a bottle of water on the ground, having consumed poison left out by <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>. While Hilarius was disappointed, he acknowledged that things could have gone worse, as his tribute made it to eighth place.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Forty years later, President Snow mentioned Hilarius to <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> while they were talking about the Heavensbees. He called Hilarius a "useless whiner".<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_9_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_9-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHilarius_Heavensbee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilarius_(praefectus_urbi)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hilarius (praefectus urbi)">Hilarius</a> was a Western Roman official who was <i>praefectus urbi</i> (urban prefect) of Rome in AD 408. It is also the name of a number of Christian saints, bishops, and priests – including a pope – who lived during late antiquity. "Heavensbee" likely refers to the spiritual domain of <a href="/wiki/Heaven" title="Heaven">Heaven</a>.
</p>
