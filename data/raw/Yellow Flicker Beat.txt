</p><p><b>Yellow Flicker Beat </b>is the first official single from <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_Soundtrack" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay Soundtrack"><i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</i> soundtrack</a>. The song is performed and written by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorde" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lorde">Lorde</a> and was released on September 29th, 2014. Lorde is the sole curator for the <i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</i> soundtrack.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Making_of">Making of</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FYellow_Flicker_Beat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After reading the books and rewatching the movies, Lorde was sent the end of the film by director <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a>, and she was struck by the scene where a <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacked</a> <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> attacked <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>. She decided to begin the song with "these really creepy hums... that were very kind of flawed and cracked-sounding" in order to capture Katniss's instability, as well as her determination to fight back against <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> for what they had done to her and Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Chart_perfomance">Chart perfomance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FYellow_Flicker_Beat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>"Yellow Flicker Beat" performed moderately in the charts all around the world. In the American territory, "Yellow Flicker Beat" became Lorde's third top 40 hit in the official chart, and the third single from a Hunger Games soundtrack to reach this high, after "<a href="/wiki/Safe_%26_Sound" title="Safe &amp; Sound">Safe &amp; Sound</a>" and "<a href="/wiki/Eyes_Open" title="Eyes Open">Eyes Open</a>". Its highest rank was in Lorde's birth country, New Zealand, where it reached #4 and became the fourth top 5 hit of Lorde.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Music_video">Music video</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FYellow_Flicker_Beat%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The music video for "Yellow Flicker Beat" was directed by Emily Kai Bock, and was released on 7 November 2014 at midnight (NZDT), when Lorde turned eighteen years old. She performed a show in Berlin while filming for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i> took place, so some of the producers came to the concert and brought her to see their work on set. She was struck by the vastness of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Tempelhof_Airport" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Berlin Tempelhof Airport">Berlin Tempelhof Airport</a>. This gave her the idea to film the music video in a "cavernous space": <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Avenue_Armory" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Park Avenue Armory">Park Avenue Armory</a> in New York.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
