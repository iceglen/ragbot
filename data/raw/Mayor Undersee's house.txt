<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Undersee%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The mayor's house was a three-story building<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> near the town square.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They had a telephone somewhere in the house.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> There was a piano on the first floor, and the second floor held several guest rooms, the mayor's study, and Madge's bedroom. The master bedroom was presumably on the third floor, since that was where Mrs. Undersee rested whenever she had one of her severe headaches.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Madge had a dressing table with a mirror in her own bedroom.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Undersee%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Undersee%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>But I like Mayor Undersee's house, especially now that his daughter, Madge, and I are friends.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss about the mayor's house<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> often went to the mayor's back door to sell him wild strawberries, and they did so on the morning of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> won the Games, Katniss grew closer to Madge, and the girls spent time at each other's houses. At the mayor's house, Madge and Katniss sometimes ate together, and Madge tried to teach Katniss to play the piano.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour">Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Undersee%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Katniss and Peeta's last stop on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a> was their home <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>. After arriving at the <a href="/wiki/Train_station" title="Train station">train station</a>, they spent that night at the mayor's house for a dinner with their families. Katniss briefly hugged Madge before her <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> took her to the third floor to get ready, and once she was done, Katniss went down to the second floor to see her friend.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss stuck her head into the mayor's study to say hello along the way only to find it empty. She stood and watched television coverage of the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> for a moment. However, when she turned to leave, a beeping noise directed her attention back to the screen for a confidential news update about the <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">uprising of District 8</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss hurried away, and she had an innocuous exchange with the mayor in the hallway before she went into Madge's room. The girls talked about the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> Madge had given Katniss as her <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In addition to Katniss and Peeta's families, <a href="/wiki/Hazelle_Hawthorne" title="Hazelle Hawthorne">Hazelle Hawthorne</a> and her three youngest children— <a href="/wiki/Rory_Hawthorne" title="Rory Hawthorne">Rory</a>, <a href="/wiki/Vick_Hawthorne" title="Vick Hawthorne">Vick</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Posy_Hawthorne" title="Posy Hawthorne">Posy</a>— attended the Victory Tour dinner. Gale was "conspicuously absent", but when Katniss asked Vick about it, he said Gale had been hunting. Katniss concluded that this was probably true.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bombing_of_District_12">Bombing of District 12</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Undersee%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>I pass the wreckage of the mayor's house, where my friend Madge lived. No word of her or her family. Were they evacuated to the Capitol because of her father's position, or left to the flames?</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss wondering what became of Madge and her family<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The mayor's house was destroyed in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>. Katniss initially speculated that they could have been evacuated to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but when she asked <a href="/wiki/Thom" title="Thom">Thom</a>, who had been recovering human remains from the wreckage, he told her that the whole family had died as well as the two people who worked for them.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
