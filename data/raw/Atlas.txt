</p><p><b>"Atlas"&#160;</b>is the first single from&#160;<i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire_Soundtrack" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire Soundtrack">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire Soundtrack</a>,</i> released on September 6th, 2013 worldwide and on September 8th, 2013 in the United Kingdom. The song is performed by the alternative rock band <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coldplay" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Coldplay">Coldplay</a>.
</p>
