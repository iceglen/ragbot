<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%2527s_brother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He graduated from the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> in the year of the <a href="/wiki/9th_Hunger_Games" title="9th Hunger Games">9th Hunger Games</a>, one year before his sister would have.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The following year, Arachne was chosen to <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but she was killed by her <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Her brother presumably attended the funeral alongside their parents. In the procession, the bereaved family rode in a horse-drawn chariot behind Arachne's white coffin, and <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> noted that they "had the decency to look uncomfortable" about Brandy's body being displayed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArachne_Crane%2527s_brother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>With the death of his sister and having no other siblings mentioned, Arachne's brother is presumably the one who kept the Crane name alive, eventually passing it onto <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a> from <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
