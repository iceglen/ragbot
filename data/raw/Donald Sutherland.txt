</p><p><b>Donald McNichol Sutherland</b> (July 17, 1935 – June 20, 2024) was an award-winning Canadian actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i></a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">Part 2</a></i>. For his role in <i>The Hunger Games</i>, Sutherland wrote a long letter to <a href="/wiki/Gary_Ross" title="Gary Ross">Gary Ross</a>, as he had loved the script and wanted to participate in any way possible. They agreed that he would portray President Snow.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDonald_Sutherland%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sutherland began acting in 1962. He studied at the University of Toronto and the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art. Sutherland was an Officer of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Canada" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Order of Canada">Order of Canada</a>, that country's highest civilian honor for merit, and received two <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Golden Globe Award">Golden Globe Awards</a>, a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Primetime Emmy Award">Primetime Emmy Award</a>, and an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Honorary_Award" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Academy Honorary Award">Academy Honorary Award</a>. Members of Sutherland's family are also actors, including his second wife, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Douglas" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Shirley Douglas">Shirley Douglas</a>, and his third wife, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francine_Racette" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Francine Racette">Francine Racette</a>. His sons <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiefer_Sutherland" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Kiefer Sutherland">Kiefer</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rossif_Sutherland" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rossif Sutherland">Rossif</a>, and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angus_Sutherland_(actor)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Angus Sutherland (actor)">Angus</a>, act as well.
</p><p>The films Sutherland starred in include <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klute" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Klute">Klute</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Lampoon%27s_Animal_House" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:National Lampoon&#39;s Animal House">National Lampoon's Animal House</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JFK_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:JFK (film)">JFK</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_the_Body_Snatchers_(1978_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Invasion of the Body Snatchers (1978 film)">Invasion of the Body Snatchers</a></i>.
</p>
