<p><b>Mia Carragher</b> is an English actress who plays <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage" title="The Hunger Games On Stage">The Hunger Games On Stage</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-variety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-variety-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She is the daughter of former Liverpool player <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Carragher" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jamie Carragher">Jamie Carragher</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMia_Carragher%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Carragher graduated from the Tring Park School for the Performing Arts and pursued acting at the Lee Strasberg Theatre and Film Institute in New York. Her first film was <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Night_in_Istanbul" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:One Night in Istanbul">One Night in Istanbul</a></i> (2014). She also appeared in the Channel 4 series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gathering_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Gathering (TV series)">The Gathering</a></i> (2024) and performed alongside <a href="/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson" title="Josh Hutcherson">Josh Hutcherson</a> in the film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Let%27s_Love_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Let&#39;s Love (film)">Let's Love</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-variety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-variety-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
