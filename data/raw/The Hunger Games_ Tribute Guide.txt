</p><p><i><b>The Hunger Games: Tribute Guide</b></i> is the first of the official tie-in books to <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Written by Emily Seife, it was announced by Scholastic on November 3, 2011, and released on February 7, 2012, six weeks before the film release on March 23, 2012.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Tribute_Guide%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<blockquote><p><i>Here is the ultimate guide to the twenty-four Tributes participating in Panem's 74th annual Hunger Games.</i><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></p></blockquote>
<p>The <i>Tribute Guide</i> mainly covers what <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> undergo before entering the arena, but also shines a clear spotlight on <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>. It features portraits of all the tributes, an overview of the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>, and includes many filming photos that do not appear in the trailers or in other promotions
</p>
