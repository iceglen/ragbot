<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Origins">Origins</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Years before the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>, Buttercup was found by Prim as a kitten. Katniss described him as "scrawny... [his] belly swollen with worms, [and] crawling with fleas." Katniss was averse to adopting him because it was another mouth to feed. She tried to drown Buttercup in a bucket, but Prim "begged so hard, cried even", that Katniss reluctantly allowed him to stay. <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> "got rid of the vermin", nursing him back to health.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He was presumably adopted by the Everdeens sometime after the death of <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">Mr. Everdeen</a>, since Katniss was running the household, but it had to have been sometime after Mrs. Everdeen overcame from her depression, since she was able to heal him. As a result, Buttercup is probably about 3-4 years old at the start of the trilogy.
</p><p>Once healed, Buttercup grew to be a "born mouser", even catching rats occasionally. He used to hiss at Katniss, but she built trust by feeding him entrails, so the hissing stopped.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>On the morning of <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the reaping</a>, Buttercup sat at Prim's knees and guarded her while she slept, as he often did.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3b/Buttercup_CF.png/revision/latest?cb=20140516205113" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Buttercup glaring at Katniss." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3b/Buttercup_CF.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140516205113" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="128" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Buttercup CF.png" data-image-key="Buttercup_CF.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Buttercup_CF.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Buttercup glaring at Katniss.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Buttercup moved to the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a> with his family after Katniss won the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, but he disliked their new house, often returning to their <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen%27s_house" title="Katniss Everdeen's house">old home</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> while Prim was at <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a>. On the morning the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a> began, Katniss found Buttercup wailing at the back door of their old home and let him in. She fed him some beaver fat and rubbed between his ears, then told him he was "hideous". When he nudged her for more petting, she scooped him up into her arms and left, but Buttercup sprang away to disappear under a bush.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, Buttercup sat in Prim's lap as she and her family watched the reading of the card for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">third Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When Katniss returned to District 12 after the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing</a>, she found Buttercup in her house in the Victors' Village. Katniss lamented that she had found him and not <a href="/wiki/Lady" title="Lady">Lady</a>, Prim's goat, "an animal of actual value". Katniss mentioned that the cat looked fat and healthy, supposing that he fed on field mice because she "[refused] to consider the alternative"— hinting he might have been feeding on the corpses of victims from the bombing. Katniss asked Buttercup if he wanted to see Prim, to which he meowed at her; then she stuffed him into her game bag, as she doubted he would be allowed on the <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Buttercup growled at Katniss for the mistreatment, but he was freed in their compartment in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. Prim rocked him in her arms and wept as he purred. Katniss nicknamed their hour of reunification as "1800—Cat Adoration." He occasionally paused to hiss at Katniss, and evidently gave her a "particularly smug look" as Prim tied a blue ribbon around his neck.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Reunited, he went back to guarding Prim as she slept.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Once Katniss agreed to work with the rebels, the first stipulation of the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_Deal" title="Mockingjay Deal">Mockingjay Deal</a> was that her family would be able to keep Buttercup. This started an argument between <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> rebels, who thought this was reasonable, and rebels from 13, who pointed out difficulties this would pose. Eventually the request was accepted, and Katniss and her family moved to a compartment with an "eight-inch window aboveground" that Buttercup could use to come and go, feeding himself. He would be locked out if he missed curfew, and if he posed any security issues, he would be shot.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, the citizens of District 13 evacuated to a subterranean bunker during <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Second_Bombing_of_District_13" title="Bombings">a bombing</a>, and Prim went back to their compartment to retrieve Buttercup. They barely avoided getting locked out. Once inside, Prim placed Buttercup on the lower bunk, nestled under a blanket, and she pet him.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During brief periods of socialization amid the bombing, Buttercup kept the people of District 13 entertained with evening games of Crazy Cat: Katniss wiggled a flashlight around and he would try to catch it. It drove him crazy if she left the light shining in a place where he couldn't reach it, and when the light was turned off, he would be confused and upset for a while, but eventually got over it. On the third night, Katniss compared this to the way the Capitol was using <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> against her.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/24/Katniss_and_Buttercup_THGMJ2.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20230627224128" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss hugging Buttercup to her in Mockingjay - Part 2." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/24/Katniss_and_Buttercup_THGMJ2.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230627224128" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss and Buttercup THGMJ2.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_and_Buttercup_THGMJ2.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_and_Buttercup_THGMJ2.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss hugging Buttercup to her in <i>Mockingjay - Part 2.</i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After Prim's death and Katniss's trial for the assassination of <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>, Katniss returned once more to District 12. Several weeks later, she came home one day only to hear Buttercup hissing at her. When she turned to look at him, she saw that he had been clawed by a wild animal, that he held his back paw above the ground, and that the bones in his face had become "prominent". She concluded that Buttercup had traveled all the way from District 13 on foot, searching for Prim. She threw a pillow at him and screamed that Prim was dead, but quickly broke down in tears. Buttercup wailed with her, staying nearby. When Katniss went to bed that night, she found Buttercup waiting there to guard her. The next morning, Katniss tended to his injuries and they cried together, and when <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a> came to make Katniss breakfast, she fed all her bacon to Buttercup.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to Katniss, Buttercup had a mashed-in nose and half of one ear missing, with eyes the color of rotting squash and a muddy yellow coat. She even described him as the world's ugliest cat,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so some of her description may have been colored by her fraught relationship with him.
</p><p>In <i>The Hunger Games</i> film, Buttercup appeared as a black and white cat with yellow eyes. However, in <i>Catching Fire,</i> he appeared as a ginger cat as described in the trilogy. This remained the case for both <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i> and <i>Part 2</i>.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Primrose_Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Prim was the one who found Buttercup, begged Katniss not to drown him, and later healed him, keeping him as a pet. Buttercup always slept with Prim, watching over her during the night.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <i>Catching Fire</i>, though he did not like the new house in the Victors' Village, he always came back for Prim,<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and in <i>Mockingjay</i>, he even traveled all the way from District 13 to find her.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Prim also went to great lengths for Buttercup; she nearly got locked out of the bunker during the bombing in order to rescue him.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was moved to tears when Katniss brought him to District 13 from 12,<sup id="cite_ref-:5_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and she turned to him for comfort in difficult times.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:7_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Katniss_Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games (film)">"<i>I'm still going to cook you!</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss Everdeen to Buttercup<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>At first, Katniss and Buttercup did not like each other at all. Katniss saw Buttercup as another mouth to feed, so she tried to drown him in a bucket of water before Prim begged for his life. After that, Buttercup tended to hiss at her, though he stopped when she started to give him entrails from the game she hunted. Still, Katniss thought he was the ugliest cat in the world.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In<i> Catching Fire</i>, Katniss and Buttercup both disliked the new house in the Victors Village and often visited the old place in the Seam. Katniss said they had formed a new bond over this.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i>Mockingjay</i>, Katniss brought him with her to District 13 for Prim,<sup id="cite_ref-:4_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:5_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and she included him as the first item of the Mockingjay Deal.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When she became Buttercup's owner after Prim's death, the two bonded over their loss, and Buttercup stayed by her side much like he had Prim.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Prim named him Buttercup because she insisted that his muddy yellow coat matched the bright yellow <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/buttercup" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:buttercup">buttercup</a> flower.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FButtercup%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <i>The Hunger Games</i> film, Buttercup was a black and white cat. However, in <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i>, Buttercup was recast as a large, fluffy ginger tabby, portrayed by a seven-year-old <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_Coon" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Maine Coon">Maine Coon</a> named Orion.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Buttercup was afraid of thunder, and liked to be hidden underneath a blanket during storms. Katniss said it was the one thing that frightened him.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Katniss invented Crazy Cat by accident several years before <i>Mockingjay</i>, during a winter blackout.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Buttercup appeared in all three books in the trilogy, and all four films.</li>
<li>Buttercup is the only known cat in the trilogy. The only other cat, appearing in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, is <a href="/wiki/Boa_Bell" title="Boa Bell">Boa Bell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
