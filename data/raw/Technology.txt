<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>The level of <b>technology</b> in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> varies significantly from area to area. <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a> is a technologically advanced metropolis, utilizing advanced science and engineering to make life easier for its plutocratic citizens. Technology in the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a> is not described in as much detail, but it seems that the poorer districts, at least, have progressed little beyond 20th and 21st century&#160;technology, and in some cases even regressed: For example, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> mentions that <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> is lucky to get more than a few hours of electricity in the evening<sup id="cite_ref-:2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, and that remedies made from crudely processed plants are the only medicines available to most people.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Outside of the Capitol, <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> seems to be the most technologically advanced area of Panem. Due to the non-aggression pact established following the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, District 13 gained its independence from the Capitol, but was cut off from the rest of the country, and thus unable to share its technology with the other districts.
</p><p>Technology development&#160;is the primary industry of <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>; the citizens are highly adept at engineering, and the district contains factories that manufacture televisions, computers, and other electronic devices.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Appliances">Appliances</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s quarters in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> contains a shower that has a panel with a hundred different options regarding water pressure, temperature, soaps, shampoos, scents, oils, and massaging sponges.
<ul><li>A mat lies next to the shower that blows heated air to dry off the person's body.</li>
<li>Also in the bathroom is a box that, when placing a hand on it, sends a current through the scalp that untangles, parts, and dries the users hair almost instantly.</li>
<li>A closet can be programmed to prepare certain outfits for the user.</li>
<li>A gigantic menu can prepare any food for the user when spoken into its mouthpiece.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Communication">Communication</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> and <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> were able to communicate with each other from across the country using a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrofuturism" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Retrofuturism">retrofuturistic</a> <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/videophone" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:videophone">videophone</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Upon arriving in District 13, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> was granted a <a href="/wiki/Communicuff" title="Communicuff">communicuff</a>, a device resembling an oversized watch that received worded messages.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was taken away from him at one point but later returned.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>During the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a> used communicuffs to send <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a> to their <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> inside the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the film adaptation, they instead used <a href="/wiki/Communipad" class="mw-redirect" title="Communipad">communipads</a>, which resembled desktop computers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Entertainment">Entertainment</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>During a training session, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> mentioned to Katniss that he has developed a music chip small enough to be concealed in a flake of glitter, but that can hold hours of songs.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the windows of Katniss' bedroom in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> functioned like programmable screens; in addition to acting as normal windows, they could be fixed with a variety of images. These included a ground-level view of a Capitol street (complete with streaming of people and activity), a stretch of desert landscape, and forest scenery, the last of which Katniss briefly paused on before reverting to the normal window<sup id="cite_ref-:6_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> (perhaps it reminded her of home, which quickly became too difficult to think about).</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Fashion">Fashion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>For the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>' <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> and <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a> created artificial but highly realistic flames that were used as a key element in Katniss’ and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>’s costumes.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>In <i>The Hunger Games</i> film, Katniss' interview dress was triggered to light up with artificial flames as the she twirled and to extinguish when she stopped. This was likely the same (or a similar) technology used for her Tribute Parade outfit, but modified to be activated by specific motion.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> possesses a machine that can turn graphite into <a href="/wiki/Diamonds" title="Diamonds">diamonds</a>. The resulting gems are used in jewelry and as decorations on clothing.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>'s interview suit was decorated with many tiny light bulbs that reminded Katniss of twinkling stars.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Tribute Parade:
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> and Beetee wore outfits studded with electric lights, representing District 3's industry of technology and electronics manufacture.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> were dressed as cows and wearing flaming belts.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The latter may have been the same technology utilized by Cinna and Portia used the previous year.</li>
<li>Katniss and Peeta's outfits were equipped with an illumination system that made the ensemble glow as if coated in live embers. The black metal crowns they wore also glowed brilliant red.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>During a training session, Wiress mentioned to Katniss that she was developing a high-tech stitching device; it automatically senses the density of the fabric and selects the appropriate type of thread, thus eliminating human error.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>After getting her <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a> released from custody in District 13, Katniss sees all of them in the district's drab, standardized clothing, and recalls that <a href="/wiki/Octavia" title="Octavia">Octavia</a> once sported a hairstyle that incorporated blinking lights shaped like mice.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Katniss' mockingjay outfit, designed by Cinna, was equal parts fashion statement and bodily protection: the utilitarian design incorporated a breastplate, multiple layers of body armor, a collapsible mesh helmet, and white patches on the black sleeves that mimicked the patterns on a real <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a>'s wings.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Genetic_engineering">Genetic engineering</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>Advanced technology allowed the Capitol to create entirely new species of animals, such as the <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a>&#160;(<a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jackers</a>, and various other creatures).<sup id="cite_ref-:7_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>&#160;</li>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Medicine">Medicine</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>During the 74th Hunger Games, the burn cream Katniss received in the Arena soothed her injuries instantly and significantly reduced her inflammation in a matter of hours.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>After being stabbed in the leg by Cato, Peeta's wound festered and he developed a dangerous fever and infection.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> If not for the medicine Katniss obtained at the Feast, he would have died.</li>
<li>After Katniss emerged victorious from the 74th Hunger Games, she received a “full body polish” erasing not just the scars she acquired in the arena but the ones she acquired from years of hunting in District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>&#160;</li>
<li>The toxic <a href="/wiki/Fog" title="Fog">fog</a> used in the 75th Hunger Games arena caused severely painful burns and blisters upon contact with the skin, which Katniss and her allies suffered during their first night in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The next day, upon Katniss' irritable outcry to Haymitch, they received medicine from a <a href="/wiki/Sponsor" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor">sponsor</a> that, with regular applications, soothed and eventually healed the scabs. It was described as "a thick, dark ointment with a pungent smell, a combination of tar and pine needles."<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>&#160;</li>
<li>Katniss suffered many burns after being caught on the periphery of <a href="/wiki/President_Coin" class="mw-redirect" title="President Coin">President Coin</a>'s bomb drop. The subsequent medical treatment attempted to repair the burns with laboratory-grown skin cells grafted onto her own tissue.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>&#160;</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Security">Security</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>Districts’ <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a><sup id="cite_ref-:2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and 12<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are surrounded by electrified fences to keep the citizens from escaping. Although not specifically mentioned, other districts presumably employ similar measures.</li>
<li>Before the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">Reaping</a> takes place, all eligible tributes sign in and are sorted by age (which also helps the Capitol keep tabs on each district's population).<sup id="cite_ref-:2_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i>The Hunger Games </i>film, <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> use needle-like electrical devices to take a blood sample from each person, and a scanning device to confirm identity.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The roof of the Training Center and the edge of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arenas</a> were encased by force fields that deflected any object they come in contact with, sometimes with intense strength.</li>
<li>Before each Hunger Games began, the tributes were injected with small tracking devices so the Gamemakers could monitor their position/status in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The armory in District 13 contains multiple security checkpoints, including two where the entrant's DNA must be verified before they can proceed.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Transportation">Transportation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>The Capitol controls a high-speed <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> system that can cover the distance between District 12 and the Capitol itself (roughly from Appalachian Mountains to the Rocky Mountains) in less than 24 hours.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">Hovercrafts</a> are a means of aerial transport. Some are designed for military use, while others are meant to transport passengers or carry cargo.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Delivery">Delivery</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<ul><li>During the 10th Hunger Games, sponsor gifts were delivered to tributes via <a href="/wiki/Drone" title="Drone">drone</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They used facial recognition to lock onto their targets, and delivered only one item at a time.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At some unknown point, these drones were replaced with <a href="/wiki/Silver_parachute" title="Silver parachute">silver parachutes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Weaponry">Weaponry</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTechnology%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>During the 10th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Teslee" title="Teslee">Teslee</a> was able to hack a drone and block other drones' homing signals so they would all swarm <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>. This made him lose his balance and fall to his death.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>In <i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i> film, Coriolanus Snow sent <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> sponsor gifts when she was cornered by another group of tributes, intentionally weaponizing the unpredictable drones against them.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d6/District12-14.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131207070542" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Scene where Katniss shoots the apples and blow up the supplies." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d6/District12-14.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131207070542" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="76" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District12-14.jpg" data-image-key="District12-14.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District12-14.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Scene where Katniss shoots the apples and blow up the supplies.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>At the beginning of each Hunger Games, the tributes emerge into the arena on metal plates wired to landmines. If a tribute attempts to leave their plate before the sixty-second countdown is complete, the mines detonate, killing them on the spot.<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>In the 74th Hunger Games, the <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (74th HG)">male tribute</a> from District 3 safely re-positioned the landmines from under the tribute plates around a pile of supplies and reactivated them, creating a protective obstacle around the pile.<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Before <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a> escaped from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, they were able to acquire a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a>’s gun that converted solar energy into “rays of power” (presumably lasers), giving the weapon potentially unlimited ammunition.<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>&#160;</li>
<li>In his first Hunger Games, Beetee used wire to create an electrical trap that killed several tributes at once, earning him his victory.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire_(film)" class="mw-redirect" title="Catching Fire (film)">Catching Fire</a> </i>film, several <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">stations</a> in the Training Center featured mobile, holographic targets shaped like humans, some wielding virtual weapons. This offered tributes a realistic environment for weapons practice- <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a>, <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a>, and Katniss all visited these stations.<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>District 13's industry is nuclear technology, which is used to make bombs, explosives, and missiles. This arsenal of weaponry proved invaluable&#160;in securing 13's independence from the Capitol.</li>
<li>When Katniss agreed to become the face of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">rebellion</a>, Beetee furnished her with a custom-made, military grade <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrows" class="mw-redirect" title="Bow and arrows">bow and arrowss</a>. The weapon fired razor-sharp, incendiary, and explosive projectiles, and allowed Katniss to accurately hit targets from one hundred yards away. The bow could also be deactivated by Katniss saying "good night", and reactivated by her own voice.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> expresses his woe at not having access to the weapons of past centuries to fight Panem's civil war, suggesting that Panem's current level of technology is inferior to that of past civilizations.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">Pods</a>, which are obstacles made in the Capitol, are used to stop the rebels from advancing into the city.</li></ul>
