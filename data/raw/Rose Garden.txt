<p>A <b>rose garden</b> lies within the confined walls of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a>'s&#160;<a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">mansion</a>. It was where Snow and <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a> consulted with each other to discuss the events that were to take place within each <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, such as new tactics, ideas, and above all, the competition and talent with each year's fresh batch of <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span id="The_Grandma.27am.27s_rose_garden"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Grandma'am's_rose_garden">The Grandma'am's rose garden</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRose_garden%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Snow's grandmother, the <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma&#39;am">Grandma'am</a>, maintained a rose garden on the roof of their home. The roses were kept both outdoors and in a small solar greenhouse, and they were highly prized in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRose_garden%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>President Snow and Seneca Crane discussed the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> in this garden while the president tended to his <a href="/wiki/Flowers#Roses" title="Flowers">roses</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRose_garden%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Whilst Snow was chained to a chair in the center of the garden, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> approached him. Snow discussed the death of her sister, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>, saying that it had been unnecessary, but Katniss left after refusing to believe <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> had played them for fools.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, she took a white rose with her, and it was this same rose that Snow wore over his heart during the execution.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRose_garden%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The rose garden in the president's mansion is located at the end of a short hallway, behind glass doors. It contains many rows of roses in various colors.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Scent">Scent</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRose_garden%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/89/Copy_of_donald-sutherland-wes-bentley-the-hunger-games-image.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120501085833" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="President Snow and Seneca Crane consulting in the rose garden." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/89/Copy_of_donald-sutherland-wes-bentley-the-hunger-games-image.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120501085833" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Copy of donald-sutherland-wes-bentley-the-hunger-games-image.jpg" data-image-key="Copy_of_donald-sutherland-wes-bentley-the-hunger-games-image.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Copy_of_donald-sutherland-wes-bentley-the-hunger-games-image.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">President Snow and Seneca Crane consulting in the rose garden.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>President Snow could be seen often wearing an elegant rose on his suit. The scent was sweet and incredibly overpowering, and had a huge effect on Katniss. She often complained that she continued to smell the lingering scent of the roses that Snow wore, especially where he left a rose on the dresser at Katniss's home in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>. She mentions the scent before the announcement of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">Quarter Quell</a>, when Snow threatened Katniss about being unconvinced that the star-crossed lovers scheme with <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>&#160;was real. Katniss thought she could feel the presence of the president everywhere she went, due to the haunting scent of his roses.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Snow also left a rose from his garden in Katniss' home after the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombing of District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRose_garden%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Many other powerful, ambitious figures in stories have rose gardens, such as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Midas" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:King Midas">King Midas</a>. It could be a symbol of arrogance and avarice.</li></ul>
