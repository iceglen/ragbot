The nickname 'Grandma'am' was bestowed by Tigris during her youth, as she believed her grandmother merited an imperial title. Grandma'am is characterized as an elderly lady with white hair and shaky hands. <h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrandma%2527am%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was born or married into the wealthy <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Snow_family" title="Capitol families">Snow family</a>, where she presumably gained much of their wealth and privilege after the death of her husband. She had at least two children, one of them being <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Xanthos_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="Crassus Xanthos Snow">Crassus Xanthos Snow</a> and her other child being the father or mother of Tigris.
</p><p>In the first year of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, she would play the anthem on national holidays for Coriolanus, five years old at the time, and his cousin <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>, eight years old at the time, in order to build their patriotism. When the Capitol was under siege, she would tell them "Remember, children, we are but besieged. We have not surrendered!" then warble the anthem as bombs rained down around their apartment. She could not cook, but often threatened to learn to do so. She had a saying regarding <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avoxes</a> that "tongueless workers make the best workers." Though Coriolanus loved her, he felt she had lost touch with reality a long time ago, maybe indicating some level of early dementia. Even when the Snow family was deep in poverty, she often began her sentences with the phrase "When Coriolanus is president..."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Coriolanus was still young, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow's mother">his mother</a> went into labor, and the Grandma'am and <a href="/wiki/The_Snows%27_cook" title="The Snows' cook">the family's cook</a> attempted to stop the flow of blood before she died, as something had gone wrong.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>One October afternoon during the war, the Grandma'am and Coriolanus visited <a href="/wiki/Pluribus_Bell" title="Pluribus Bell">Pluribus Bell</a> at a nightclub. The Grandma'am decided to take thirty crates of lima beans home, which they hid.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="During_the_10th_Hunger_Games">During the 10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrandma%2527am%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Grandma'am grew roses in a rooftop garden. She was very possessive of them, but was sometimes willing to part with a rose if it would help her grandson. The day of the reaping of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, where Coriolanus was to be a mentor, she offered him a fresh red rose.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Coriolanus returned home from eating in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> with <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, the tribute he was mentoring, the Grandma'am disapproved, taking the worst view of her as she was from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, she eventually warmed up to Lucy Gray when she saved Coriolanus.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a> had been murdered by her own tribute, <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a>, the Grandma'am aired out her best black dress for Arachne's funeral and made sure Coriolanus was uninjured, later declaring him a hero for being the first to take action in the zoo.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Upon hearing that he was to sing at Arachne's funeral, the Grandma'am had him up at dawn to coach him and sacrificed a rose for his appearance.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Mrs. Plinth</a> arrived at the Snows' penthouse late one night when her son, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, had gone missing. The Grandma'am put on a robe, but treated her impatiently and suggested for her to leave.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, she was aghast upon seeing Sejanus in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="After_the_10th_Hunger_Games">After the 10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrandma%2527am%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As the Snow financial situation continued to worsen, the Grandma'am began to descend into denial and eventually despair. When her grandson was made to enroll in the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> and shipped to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, she asked when he would be back and kissed him goodbye, but did not quite seem to know what was going on and twice asked Tigris when he would be home and if they should wait on dinner.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Fortunately, her condition improved greatly after the Plinths arranged to buy the flat the family was living in and came to an arrangement so that the Snows could continue living there. The Plinths paid for everything, ending the Snow family's financial worries, though the Grandma'am had to resign herself to having a new "maid" in the form of <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Mrs. Plinth</a>, who came by every day to help.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Due to her age, she presumably died long before the time of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>, but the circumstances of her death are unknown.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrandma%2527am%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cd/Snow_family_entertainment_weekly.png/revision/latest?cb=20230928191051" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Grandma'am giving Coriolanus a yellow rose." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/cd/Snow_family_entertainment_weekly.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230928191051" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Snow family entertainment weekly.png" data-image-key="Snow_family_entertainment_weekly.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Snow_family_entertainment_weekly.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Grandma'am giving Coriolanus a yellow rose.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>She wore a long, black, flowing tunic, the kind so popular before the war and so outdated as to be laughable now, and a pair of embroidered slippers with curled toes that had once been part of a costume. Strands of her thin, white hair poked from the bottom of a rusty velvet turban. This was the tail end of a once-lavish wardrobe — her few decent items were saved for company or the rare foray into the city.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Description of the Grandma'am’s clothes<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The Grandma'am often wore a long tunic, slippers and a turban, although she saved her more lavish clothing for times where she would be seen by others. An elderly woman, she also had thin, white hair and tremorous hands.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrandma%2527am%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>Crazy old woman, still clinging to the prewar days. He loved her, but she'd lost touch with reality years ago.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Coriolanus Snow describing the Grandma'am<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The Grandma'am was said to always take the worst view of anything related to the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Despite the Snow's worsening financial situation, she always insisted on certain civilities, such as leaving home with a clean handkerchief.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, she cared greatly for her grandchildren.
</p><p>Especially after Coriolanus was sent to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> to become a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a>, she began to lose her mind and often forgot where he was.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrandma%2527am%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>She outlived all her known children: <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a> and his unnamed sibling, who was Tigris's parent.</li>
<li>When a recording of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> anthem, "<a href="/wiki/Gem_of_Panem" title="Gem of Panem">Gem of Panem</a>" started playing, she began singing along in a tremulous soprano voice. She was badly off-key and slightly behind the song's tempo.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the Spanish translation of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, her nickname is <i>abuelatriz</i>, a mix between <i>abuela</i> (grandmother) and <i>emperatriz</i> (female form of emperor), giving her an even higher position of power.</li>
<li>According to Coriolanus, Grandma'am loves chocolate.</li></ul>
