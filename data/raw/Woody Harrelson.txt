</p><p><b>Woody Harrelson</b> (born July 23, 1961) is an American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, and the final installment, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWoody_Harrelson%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Harrelson is best known for his roles in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo:_A_Star_Wars_Story" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Solo: A Star Wars Story">Solo: A Star Wars Story</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rampart_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rampart (film)">Rampart</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friends_With_Benefits_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Friends With Benefits (film)">Friends With Benefits</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zombieland" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Zombieland">Zombieland</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venom_(2018_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Venom (2018 film)">Venom (2018 film)</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingpin_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Kingpin (film)">Kingpin</a></i>. He is most famous for portraying Woody Boyd in the American TV show <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheers" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cheers">Cheers</a></i>, for which he won an Emmy award and was nominated for an academy award. Harrelson was nominated for the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Academy Award for Best Actor">Academy Award for Best Actor</a> for <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_People_vs._Larry_Flynt" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The People vs. Larry Flynt">The People vs. Larry Flynt</a></i> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor">Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor</a> for <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Messenger_(2009_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Messenger (2009 film)">The Messenger</a></i>. He also appeared as Grace's boyfriend in the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_%26_Grace" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Will &amp; Grace">Will &amp; Grace</a></i>. On May 10, 2011, it was announced that Harrelson would portray Haymitch in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>. He also starred in the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:True Detective (TV series)">True Detective</a></i>.
</p>
