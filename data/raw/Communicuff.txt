<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommunicuff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A communicuff looks like an oversized watch, but instead of showing the time, the face displays a printed message. They are used throughout <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> and were also used in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. In District 13, it was considered a great privilege to be given a communicuff, as they were exclusively given to those who were important to the cause of the <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommunicuff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommunicuff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>That's my tribute, <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a>, from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District Seven</a>. And my communicuff is open for business. Never too late to show your support and send a gift!</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Pliny_Harrington" title="Pliny Harrington">Pliny Harrington</a>, urging sponsors to send gifts via his communicuff<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, all of the members of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> program who still had a tribute remaining when the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Games</a> started were issued communicuffs. This made them the objects of envy, as the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> had resulted in the disappearance of much personal technology, since manufacturing had focused on other priorities. The communicuffs buckled onto mentors' wrists and each featured a small screen showing a tally of <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a> in blinking red. The mentors could scroll down a list of food items, select one, and then double-click it in order to signal a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> to send the gift via <a href="/wiki/Drone" title="Drone">drone</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The communicuffs sounded a ping each time that the mentors received a new sponsor gift. The communicuffs only worked through the transmitters at the Academy or the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_News" title="Capitol News">Capitol News</a> station. This meant that many of the mentors chose to stay at the Academy late or even sleep there as the Games continued, so that they could receive instant notification of sponsor gifts.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Communipad">Communipad</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommunicuff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, communicuffs were instead adapted as <b>communipads</b>, which were electronic devices resembling <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrofuturism" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Retrofuturism">retrofuturistic</a> desktop computers. By default, communipad screens featured a rotating image of a tribute, their <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">odds</a>, and the amount they had received in donations. Mentors could also access a selection screen and scroll through different food items to send into the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13">District 13</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommunicuff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, Gale was given a communicuff due to his bravery and for rescuing over 800 <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> citizens. He later lost that privilege after blocking Commander <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>' way when he tried to stop <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> as she dismissed herself from the room. Boggs' elbow hit Gale's nose during the incident, resulting in it bleeding profusely. It seems that communicuffs were given to leaders and taken away from those who didn't deserve it or broke the rules.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, Gale eventually got his communicuff back as a bribe not to tell <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> about <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>.
</p><p>Katniss found his communicuff repelling in some moments when she was furious with District 13's governing. She found herself questioning Gale, thinking he would just go and report everything to <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> with the communicuff in sight.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
