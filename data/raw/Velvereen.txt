<p><b>Velvereen</b> was the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. Her <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Palmyra_Monty" title="Palmyra Monty">Palmyra Monty</a>. She and her <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a> <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">Facet</a> were shot down by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> for attempting to flee amidst the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol Arena bombing">Capitol Arena bombing</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 132px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Velvereen.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316063017" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Velvereen during the reaping." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="132" height="231" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Velvereen.png" data-image-key="Velvereen.png" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Velvereen.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316063017" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Velvereen.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316063017" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Velvereen during the reaping." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Velvereen.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316063017" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="132" height="231" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Velvereen.png" data-image-key="Velvereen.png" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Velvereen.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316063017" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Velvereen.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Velvereen during the reaping.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Velvereen lived in District 1 after the failed rebellion of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> and possibly before. She was <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> as tribute for the 10th Hunger Games on July 4th that year.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Velvereen and the other tributes were taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> by <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> in dirty cattle cars. From there, they were transferred to a truck, which dumped them all into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>While touring the Capitol Arena with her mentor <a href="/wiki/Palmyra_Monty" title="Palmyra Monty">Palmyra Monty</a> and the other remaining 22 tribute-mentor pairs, several bombs planted by the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> were detonated. These explosions created a new cracked opening by the entrance of the arena. Velvereen, along with her district partner <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">Facet</a> and the tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sabyn" title="Sabyn">Sabyn</a> and <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>, used the resulting chaos as an opportunity for a possible escape. She was shot dead by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> before she could reach the opening. 
</p><p>Her body was later tied to a horse and dragged  down <a href="/wiki/Scholars_Road" title="Scholars Road">Scholars Road</a> during <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana Ring</a>'s funeral.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Velvereen's name may be a reference to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/velveteen" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:velveteen">velveteen</a>, a type of cloth made to imitate velvet.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Despite being reaped, Velvereen never competed in the 10th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>She was implied to be one of the stronger tributes, as <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> mentioned that with the deaths of the District 1 and 2 tributes, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray's</a> odds of winning significantly increased.</li>
<li>Velvereen is the only known female tribute from District 1 to not have blonde hair or light colored eyes.</li></ul>
