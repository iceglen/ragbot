</p><p><b>Amanda Plummer</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAmanda_Plummer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Plummer was born in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:New York">New York</a> to film legend <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Plummer" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Christopher Plummer">Christopher Plummer</a> and actress <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tammy_Grimes" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tammy Grimes">Tammy Grimes</a>, later attending college in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Vermont">Vermont</a>.  She started acting in 1979 and is best known for her roles in films such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulp_Fiction" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pulp Fiction">Pulp Fiction</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/So_I_Married_an_Axe_Murderer" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:So I Married an Axe Murderer">So I Married an Axe Murderer</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fisher_King" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Fisher King">The Fisher King</a></i>. Plummer was nominated for several awards. She has won two Emmys and one Tony.
</p>
