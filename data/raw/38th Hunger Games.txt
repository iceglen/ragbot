</p><p>The <b>38th Hunger Games</b> took place thirty-six years before the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. They were won by <a href="/wiki/Porter_Millicent_Tripp" title="Porter Millicent Tripp">Porter Millicent Tripp</a>, the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
