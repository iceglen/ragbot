<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mechanics">Mechanics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJabberjay%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>While in record mode, they were silent, but in neutral mode, they acted like normal birds.
</p><p>By default, jabberjays were set on "neutral." This meant that if they heard a human voice, they would repeat it, but would quickly move on to something else, such as another voice or a snatch of birdsong. Before the end of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the jabberjays were all released into the wild on neutral. In order for them to work for spying, they had to be placed on "record." In this mode, the bird would fall entirely silent and stay at attention until was placed back on "neutral." Once this was done, another command could be executed to get to recite everything that it had recorded. All three commands were accomplished using tones which were inaudible to human beings, but easily registered by the birds. On a good day, a single bird could record for up to an hour.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJabberjay%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the birds were released around District 9, District 11, District 12, and "wherever [the Capitol] thought they'd be of value", in order to record in places that could not easily be bugged with microphones. They were recalled to the laboratory in the dead of night so no one would notice. However, rebels worked out that the birds were spies and fed the Capitol false information.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Following the failure of this project, Dr. Kay released the birds into the wild on neutral.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Exclusively male, jabberjays were not meant to be able to reproduce, but they mated with female mockingbirds and created the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJabberjay%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and other District 12 <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> were assigned the task of helping scientists catch and care for 50 jabberjays and 50 mockingjays.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The jabberjays were relatively easy to catch, and despite being ten years since they had switched settings by remote control, they still worked. Dr. Kay demonstrated this for Coriolanus by recording their conversation and playing it back.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Coriolanus used a jabberjay to record <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a><sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> admitting his escape plan, and he worried whether Sejanus would notice why the bird was so silent. When the 100 birds were sent to the Capitol, <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Dr. Gaul</a> caught the recording, leading to Sejanus's arrest and execution.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJabberjay%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Following the death of <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, President Snow replaced her with a doppelganger who came to be known as "<a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>." <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and the other <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> of District 12 quickly realized that the Capitol had implanted a device in one of her ears that allowed them to listen in on anything she heard. Haymitch compared her to a jabberjay, realizing that he and the others could potentially use Lou Lou to feed the Capitol false information in the same manner the jabberjays had been used.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_11_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_11-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJabberjay%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0f/JabberjayCFIE.png/revision/latest?cb=20251119132646" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A Jabberjay in ''[[Catching Fire: Illustrated Edition]]''" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0f/JabberjayCFIE.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251119132646" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="162" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="JabberjayCFIE.png" data-image-key="JabberjayCFIE.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:JabberjayCFIE.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>By the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the Capitol had replenished the jabberjay population. In the 4 to 5 o'clock section, the birds mimicked the screams of <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a>' loved ones, luring in <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>. A transparent barrier kept them inside, forcing them to listen to the torturously realistic shrieks of <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Katniss' mother</a>, <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rory_Hawthorne" title="Rory Hawthorne">Rory</a>, <a href="/wiki/Vick_Hawthorne" title="Vick Hawthorne">Vick</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Posy_Hawthorne" title="Posy Hawthorne">Posy Hawthorne</a>. After 5:00 had passed, Katniss worried that the Capitol had tortured their loved ones to produce screams for the jabberjays to memorize, but <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> assured Katniss and Finnick that the Capitol had used digital or mechanical means to warp their voices into screams, their voices being taken from the words said in the final 8 interviews with the remaining tributes' families.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Appearance">Appearance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJabberjay%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Jabberjays were described as small, crested black birds, with nothing that visually betrays them as being mutts.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Their black feathers serve as nighttime camouflage.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJabberjay%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Coriolanus Snow became rather fond of the birds when he looked after them in District 12. He was known to play with the birds' recording capabilities: he made them repeat back "silly phrases and sing bits of the <a href="/wiki/Gem_of_Panem" title="Gem of Panem">anthem</a>", and he tried to see how many he could control at once (which was up to four, when their cages were close together).<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avoxes</a> were tortured with jabberjays that echoed their screams.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
