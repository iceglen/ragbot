<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAugustus_Braun%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="67th_Hunger_Games">67th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAugustus_Braun%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Augustus was a <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a> in the 67th Hunger Games and presumably <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to participate.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/AugustusBraun67th.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20151114095908" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Augustus Braun&#39;s Victory Tour poster" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/AugustusBraun67th.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151114095908" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="264" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="AugustusBraun67th.jpg" data-image-key="AugustusBraun67th.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:AugustusBraun67th.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Augustus Braun's Victory Tour poster</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Tour">Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAugustus_Braun%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On his <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a> describes him as having a bright and optimistic ensemble that contrasted his unkempt persona he cultivated during his Games. Furthermore, he was widely popular in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>; they reportedly sang his praises and labelled him as a figure of reconciliation of the people due to his patriotic manner.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Post_Games">Post Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAugustus_Braun%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>It is unclear if he ever mentored for a Hunger Games, as <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> are known to have mentored for the 74th Hunger Games.
</p><p>As District 1 victors are known to continue training even after they win<sup id="cite_ref-Cashmere_CC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cashmere_CC-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, it is likely that Augustus continued his training.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAugustus_Braun%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Although unknown how he died, Augustus was dead by the end of the&#160;<a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, as he was absent for the vote on a final <a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">76th Hunger Games</a>. He was likely a victim of the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAugustus_Braun%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Augustus">Augustus</a> was the first emperor of the Roman Empire. "Braun" is a German surname that also functions as a homonym for "brawn", meaning physical strength.
</p>
