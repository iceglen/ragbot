<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p><b>Muttations</b>, or simply <b>mutts</b>, are genetically modified animals created by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> for use as <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>, both in conflicts against <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> and within <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arenas</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_mutts">Known mutts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Jabberjays">Jabberjays</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">Jabberjay</a></i></center></div>
<p>The jabberjay is a type of exclusively male bird that was created to eavesdrop on enemies of the Capitol during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. They had the ability to memorize and reproduce whole human conversations and repeat them back to their Capitol handlers. However, once the people in the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a> realized how their private conversations were being transmitted, they used the jabberjays to feed endless lies to the Capitol. When they realized they were being played, the Capitol ceased using the jabberjays and abandoned them to die off in the wild. Eventually they did die off, but not before unexpectedly mating with female mockingbirds to produce a whole new species; the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> That new species became a symbol of the Second Rebellion, maybe because of it being born from the Capitol wanting to eliminate jabberjays and failing to.
</p><p>By the time of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the Capitol had replenished the jabberjay population and used them in one of the sectors of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">clock arena</a>, where <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> were trapped and forced to listen to the birds' mimicry of their loved ones' screams. They were described as black and crested, similar to the mockingjays' appearance.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjays">Mockingjays</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">Mockingjay (bird)</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b4/Screen_Shot_2016-03-22_at_2.15.10_PM.png/revision/latest?cb=20241105094545" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A mockingjay as seen in Mockingjay - Part 1" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b4/Screen_Shot_2016-03-22_at_2.15.10_PM.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20241105094545" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="133" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Screen Shot 2016-03-22 at 2.15.10 PM.png" data-image-key="Screen_Shot_2016-03-22_at_2.15.10_PM.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Screen_Shot_2016-03-22_at_2.15.10_PM.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A mockingjay as seen in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Mockingjays are accidental muttations that were created when female mockingbirds mated with male jabberjays. They are mainly black like their jabberjay fathers, with white patches under their wings like their mockingbird mothers. They have a crest, also inherited from the jabberjays, which may be similar to that of a blue jay. They pick up songs very quickly by imitating the pitch of a human's voice and can relay them back and forth to people or other mockingjays. Since the Capitol did not mean for these birds to exist, they are viewed as a symbol of <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a> within the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, after <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> Katniss Everdeen sported a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, mockingjays went into vogue with the people of the Capitol; their likeness often appeared in clothing, jewelry, tattoos, and other accessories.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tracker_jackers">Tracker jackers</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">Tracker jacker</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/86/Trackerjackers.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120325232728" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tracker jackers on a Hunger Games promo poster." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/86/Trackerjackers.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120325232728" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="217" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Trackerjackers.jpg" data-image-key="Trackerjackers.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Trackerjackers.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Tracker jackers on a Hunger Games promo poster.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Tracker jackers are deadly wasps that were made by the Capitol and placed around the districts during the Dark Days. They are larger than regular wasps and have a gold-colored body. Their stings raise lumps the size of plums on their victims, and their venom (engineered to target fear in a victim's brain and alter their memories) causes hallucinations that can drive people to madness. More than a few stings can kill a person; some people die after just one. These mutts will also hunt down anyone who disturbs their nest and attempt to kill them, hence the name <i>tracker</i> jackers. They live in huge hives high up in trees.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Wolf_mutts">Wolf mutts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Wolf_mutt" title="Wolf mutt">Wolf mutt</a></i></center></div><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 220px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1e/Mutts%2874th_HG%29.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140927165358" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A wolf mutt in " src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1e/Mutts%2874th_HG%29.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20140927165358" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="136" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mutts(74th HG).jpg" data-image-key="Mutts%2874th_HG%29.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mutts(74th_HG).jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A wolf mutt in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> (film)</a></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> released a pack of wolves to drive the final three tributes— Katniss Everdeen, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>— to the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. Each one resembled a tribute who had died in that year's Games, wearing a collar marked with the district number, and they had remarkably human eyes. The resemblance was so strong that Peeta wondered if they had been made using the tributes' real eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>They mortally wounded Cato, but did not kill him because he was shielded by body armor. Katniss and Peeta listened to him suffer through the night, and Katniss was only able to take a mercy shot once the sun rose at dawn. The wolves left the arena through a <a href="/wiki/Catacombs" title="Catacombs">hole that opened up in the ground</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>The <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> arena was a mutt heavy program, and featured a number of dangerous mutts, several of which were programmed to target specific tributes and thus ignored the others.
</p>
<ul><li>Stinging butterflies that caused severe pain.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> They were used by the Gamemakers to force <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> to let them take <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>'s body. The effects of the butterfly stings were similar to the electric shocks of a taser and left behind no visible markings. Haymitch was able to burn most of the flock using a gas plant and chased the rest back to their mutt portal under the guise of retaliation.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Bats with transparent wings tinted every color of the rainbow, a vicious face, and two back feet with four-inch curved claws. A flock attacked Haymitch in Sub-A when he bombed the arena's water tank. They were presumably drowned in the flood's initial wave.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR18_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR18-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Golden, fluffy, carnivorous squirrels who attacked in packs.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> A pack was sent to kill <a href="/wiki/Ampert_Latier" title="Ampert Latier">Ampert Latier</a> after the bombing of the arena's water tank<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR18_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR18-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> while another pack killed <a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a> following her murder of a Gamemaker.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Adorable baby deer with spiked hooves that went crazy when Haymitch caused the arena to malfunction.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR18_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR18-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the volcano eruption, the deer stopped running around, but Haymitch spotted a few dead ones under the ash.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR19_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR19-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A trio of fox mutts with fur as orange as sunset who appeared to have died eating poisonous eggs. <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> speculated that they were meant to steal the tributes' food while Haymitch speculated that they were supposed to eat the tributes like the squirrels, possibly even having been programmed to target Haymitch himself.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Ladybugs that were disguised as the holly berries of the arena's hedge. When Haymitch and Maysilee tried to cut through, the ladybugs revealed themselves, latched on to their flesh, inflated to the size of acorns and began exploding, splattering the tributes' faces with blood.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Fleeing from the hedge, Haymitch and Maysilee began plucking the ladybugs out, but the suckers were dug in deep, akin to those on a really determined tick. Gripping near the head and pulling slowly and firmly caused them to pop out in a spray of blood. After clearing himself of the bugs, Haymitch helped Maysilee.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR21_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR21-24"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When the two tried again, Maysilee used a makeshift torch to burn the ladybugs as Haymitch burned through the hedge with a dead Career's blowtorch.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR22_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR22-25"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The heavily edited footage of the attempts by Haymitch and Maysilee to carve through the hedge were edited into one big sequence involving the ladybugs and the blowtorch.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A massive porcupine that sounded like a baby or a harmless animal at a distance. Haymitch suspected that it was created by combining a bear and a porcupine in a lab given its size, claws, and teeth. It had poisonous gold, silver and bronze quills adorning its back, sides and tail. One killed <a href="/wiki/Buck" title="Buck">Buck</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chicory" title="Chicory">Chicory</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a> despite Maysilee and Haymitch's efforts to save their allies. Haymitch was able to lure it away by throwing olives at the porcupine to lead it away.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR21_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR21-24"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Waterbirds resembling the ones at the lake in District 12 with long, piercing beaks and colored candy pink, the color of the bubblegum sold at the Donner family sweetshop. These mutts were responsible for killing Maysilee;<sup id="cite_ref-:0_18-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> despite Haymitch's attempts to defend her, they were specifically programmed to attack her and ignored him.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR23_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR23-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_muttations">Other muttations</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>Weasel mutts a few years before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> who apparently caused long, horrible deaths.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Snow told <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> that the mutts in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> were "far scarier than the weasels," possibly alluding to the squirrels, butterflies, and waterbirds.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR9_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR9-28"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The <a href="/wiki/49th_Hunger_Games" title="49th Hunger Games">49th Hunger Games</a> arena contained shiny beetles that suffocated the tributes.</li>
<li>In the 75th Hunger Games, one of the arena's sections held an unidentified mutt that ripped apart a tribute so badly they could not be identified. Peeta called it "the Beast".<sup id="cite_ref-:4_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Another section contained a large quantity of insects that made continuous clicking noises. The tributes speculated that the mutts likely had pincers and could probably strip them to the bone in seconds.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>While he was imprisoned in the Capitol, Peeta was <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacked</a> into believing that Katniss was not human, but in fact one of the Capitol's mutts.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Conversely, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> suggested that the Capitol's torture replaced Peeta with an "evil-mutt version" of himself.<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This sentiment was later echoed by Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Muttation gnats<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and flesh-eating rats<sup id="cite_ref-:1_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> were mentioned to be in some of the <a href="/wiki/Pods" class="mw-redirect" title="Pods">pods</a> found during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>. Unknown mutts, described as black forms crawling in the shadows, also appeared in a pit below a pod near Snow's mansion.<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>In the sewers, <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> helped steer Squad 451 clear of rats the size of beavers.<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is unknown if these were muttations or simply large rats, although the first is more likely due to the nature of the area.</li>
<li>In an unspecified Hunger Games Haymitch mentions that there were moles that attacked the tributes.</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The name is a portmanteau of the word "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/mutt">mutt</a>", meaning a mixed breed animal, and "<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/mutation">mutation</a>", which refers to heritable changes to genes.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Mutt sculptures in the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a> lab in <i>The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i> film were created by Twilight Creations, a special effects makeup studio based in Berlin. The team was led by Jörn Seifert,<sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> who had previously worked on <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i>Part 2</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Fans have speculated that the aquatic mutts seen near the end of the <i>Ballad</i> film were an Easter egg for the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a>, won by <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> because her district's main industry was fishing.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> advises <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> to find berms as these are where the muttations enter and leave the arena.</li></ul>
