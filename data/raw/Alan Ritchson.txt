</p><p><b>Alan Ritchson</b> is an American actor and singer who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. He is best known for his modeling career and his roles in television shows such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallville" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Smallville">Smallville</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/90210_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:90210 (TV series)">90210</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Mountain_State" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Blue Mountain State">Blue Mountain State</a></i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personal_life">Personal life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlan_Ritchson%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ritchson was born in Grand Forks, North Dakota, the son of Vickie (born June 21, 1958), a high school teacher, and David Ritchson (born June 5, 1956), a retired U.S. Air Force Chief Master Sergeant. He is the middle of three brothers; Eric, the oldest, and Brian, the youngest. During his childhood, his family moved to Rantoul, Illinois. At age 10, Ritchson's family finally settled in Niceville, Florida. He attended Niceville High School and graduated in 2001. Performer Toby Turner was one of his classmates. Ritchson also tried out for American Idol and was knocked out in Hollywood week.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlan_Ritchson%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Modelling">Modelling</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlan_Ritchson%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He began his modeling career in the Abercrombie and Fitch catalog; he also modeled underwear for the men's online underwear store internationaljock.com. In 2008, he was signed to L.A. agency Vision Model Management and appeared on N2N Bodywear. By early 2009, Ritchson made his final appearance in his modelling career for Abercrombie and Fitch.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Television">Television</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlan_Ritchson%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>His television acting credits include a recurring guest star role on the TV series <i>Smallville </i>as Arthur Curry, a.k.a. Aquaman, a small role as an army officer in the 2006 Hallmark Channel movie<i> <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Though_None_Go_with_Me" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Though None Go with Me">Though None Go with Me</a></i> alongside Cheryl Ladd, and the role of Lucian Manet in the 2009 Lifetime Original Movie, Nora Roberts' <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Bayou" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Midnight Bayou">Midnight Bayou</a></i>. His role in <i>Smallville </i>marked the first time that an actor portrayed <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquaman" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Aquaman">Aquaman</a> in an officially licensed live-action production. His <i>Smallville </i>co-star, Justin Hartley, has since portrayed Aquaman in an unaired pilot. Ritchson reprised his role as Aquaman for a cameo appearance in the animated film: <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_League:_The_New_Frontier" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Justice League: The New Frontier">Justice League: The New Frontier</a></i>, and the final season of <i>Smallville</i>. In 2009, he appeared on a third-season episode of Starz's <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_Case" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Head Case">Head Case</a></i> in which he played a male stripper. Ritchson also made an appearance on <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Miami" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:CSI: Miami">CSI: Miami</a></i>, in which he played a dead victim in episode 19 of Season 8. He also appeared in Season 3 of <i>90210 </i>as a love interest for main character Teddy Montgomery. He played Thad Castle in Spike TV's <i>Blue Mountain State</i> as a senior captain on a college football team, until the show was cancelled in February 2012, after its third season.
</p>
