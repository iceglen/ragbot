<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>Once it's in the soup, I'll call it beef.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Greasy Sae to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> on the trade of a wild dog<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Greasy Sae</b> is a "bony" old woman who sold bowls of hot soup from a large kettle at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. Katniss Everdeen and Gale Hawthorne traded with her often and made a conscious effort to keep on good terms with her, as she could consistently be counted on to buy wild dog. One of Greasy Sae's winter specialties included mice meat, pig entrails, and tree bark.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She also made a wild dog and rhubarb stew.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Greasy Sae has a <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae%27s_granddaughter" title="Greasy Sae's granddaughter">granddaughter</a> who wandered the Hob and was generally treated as a pet by the people there, receiving scraps of food from their stands.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, Greasy Sae cooked for Katniss in her home and helped her recuperate, bringing her granddaughter along.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGreasy_Sae%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The first time <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> killed a deer, they had taken the carcass into the Hob, where people began to bid on parts and cut off pieces without permission. Greasy Sae had to intervene and send them to the butcher.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>About six months before the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>, Greasy Sae made a below-standard soup using stock from the bones of a wild dog Katniss and Gale had shot a week earlier. Greasy Sae and Katniss laughed about <a href="/wiki/Darius" title="Darius">Darius</a>'s ridiculous flirtations.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_14_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i>The Hunger Games</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGreasy_Sae%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On their way home from the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> on the morning of the reaping, Katniss and Gale traded half of their greens with Greasy Sae for some paraffin chunks.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, Katniss bought her <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> from Greasy Sae, then gave it to <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>, who later returned it to Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i>Catching Fire</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGreasy_Sae%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Greasy Sae started up a collection in the Hob to help sponsor Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. Katniss helped repay that kindness by continuing to shop in the Hob and making it a point to visit as many vendors as possible. Greasy Sae also reminded Katniss that during her participation in the 74th Hunger Games, Gale was considered her "cousin."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the Hob was burned down, Katniss wanted to check on Greasy Sae, but Peeta shut down the idea.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Greasy Sae was one of the people Katniss made it a priority to help feed during the food shortages in District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, she was pushed onto the ground by a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a>. Before he could beat her, Gale jumped on the Peacekeeper, saving her, but in retaliation, he was publicly flogged by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_script_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_script-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i>Mockingjay</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGreasy_Sae%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Greasy Sae was among the 800 survivors Gale evacuated after District 12 was bombed by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> following the end of the Quarter Quell. She worked in the kitchen in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, which she liked well enough, but she noted that the cooks were lacking in imagination.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was happy to receive real beef from District 10, noting that it was far better than the wild dog that Gale and Katniss used to provide her with.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When Haymitch called people together to tell stories of what Katniss had done to inspire them, Greasy Sae was among them.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie's wedding">wedding</a>, she got up and started dancing with Gale.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the war, Greasy Sae was one of the few hundred people who returned to District 12. Once Katniss returned to her home in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a>, Greasy Sae came over in the morning and evening to cook and do light housekeeping. She also brought her granddaughter, who appeared to live "in her own world." Katniss was unsure whether this arrangement occurred because Greasy Sae was being neighborly or because she had been paid. Greasy Sae encouraged Katniss to go hunting for the first time in many months and updated her with news about Gale. Later, Peeta showed up to Katniss's door with Greasy Sae.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
