<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTibby%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tibby was working as an attendant on the train transporting the four <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tributes to the Capitol for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. He offered them a tray of sandwiches with three different meats and asked which one they would like. When <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a> would not answer him, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> shouted that they need plates, napkins and cutlery. Tibby told her that they were just sandwiches that could be picked up, but Maysilee wanted napkins. Tibby asked <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> if that was okay. When he returned, he encouraged the tributes to try the beef.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Later on, Tibby brought <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> a birthday cake and led the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> with a song. He was disappointed when Haymitch did not look happy with it, and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> told him to take it away.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Tibby appeared with Plutarch again as the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> started preparation. Plutarch told Tibby to bring Haymitch a sandwich.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
