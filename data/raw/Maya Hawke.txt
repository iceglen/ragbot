<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaya_Hawke%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Hawke's debut role was Jo March in the 2017 BBC adaptation of <i>Little Women</i>. She stars as Robin Buckley in the Netflix TV series, <i>Stranger Things</i> in season three. She is also known for <i>Do Revenge</i>, <i>Inside Out 2</i>, <i>Fear Street: Part One - 1994</i>, and <i>Asteroid City</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
