</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlora_Li_Thiemann%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Flora Li Thiemann played a leading role in the coming-of-age drama <i>Tigermilk</i>, as well as a supporting role in the postwar drama <i>The Aftermath</i>. She next will be seen in Amazon's series <i>The Gryphon</i>.
</p><p>Li Thiemann is represented by Rietz &amp; Casting Agency.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
