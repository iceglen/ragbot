</p><p><b>Lil</b> may have been the lover of <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a>, an ex-soldier and <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> who killed three people in a <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombing</a>. She grew up in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> and was seen calling out to Arlo at his <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was the sister of <a href="/wiki/Spruce" title="Spruce">Spruce</a>, who planned to free her from the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers' base">Peacekeepers' base</a>, but she was executed herself when the plan was discovered.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLil%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lil yelled out to Arlo right before he was about to be hanged, attempting to reach him, but Spruce held her back. Arlo screamed for Lil to run, but his voice was cut off by the rope he was hanging from, and his calls were echoed by the <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> As <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> carried her away, Lil let out a last wail of despair, which was also picked up by the jabberjays.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Arlo's hanging, Lil was taken captive and held in a cell at the Peacekeepers' base. During this time, <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a> asked <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> to communicate with her and check whether she was all right, on behalf of her frantic family.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Spruce, Billy Taupe, and Sejanus later attempted to free Lil and run off north, but after <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> exposed them to <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, Lil and Sejanus were hanged together for treason.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLil%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lil was a young woman with olive skin and long black hair.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When she was taken to be hanged, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> thought she looked far younger than she really was, as Lil was weak with terror.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLil%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lil might be short for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily_(name)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lily (name)">Lily</a>, Lillian, or some other variation of the name, coming from the eponymous <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilium" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lilium">flower</a>. Lilies traditionally symbolize innocence and purity, and they are often used at funerals.
</p>
