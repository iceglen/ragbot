</p><p><b>Sam Ly</b> is a Chinese-American actor specifically cast to portray the training and fight scenes of a tribute that fell during the initial <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. He portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_7_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 7 male (74th HG)">male</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Like most of the tribute actors, he is also a stunt performer. He also doubled the <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">male from District 9</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a> due to the <a href="/wiki/Imanol_Yepez-Frias" title="Imanol Yepez-Frias">original actor</a> not being a stunt actor. Ly's success as a stunt actor began to take off after doubling for Jaden Smith in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/After_Earth" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:After Earth">After Earth</a>.</i> He has since been a stunt double for Adam Goldberg on <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Goldbergs_(2013_TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Goldbergs (2013 TV series)">The Goldbergs</a></i> and did stunts on hits such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_Marvel_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Captain Marvel (film)">Captain Marvel</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulan_(2020_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Mulan (2020 film)">Mulan</a>.</i> He currently lives in California.
</p>
