<dd>&#8212;Description of Rue's sister<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Rue's sister</b> is a citizen of <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> and is one of <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>'s five younger siblings. She appeared at the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a> with the rest of her family. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> thought she looked about nine years old, and she resembled Rue very closely.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRue%2527s_sister%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rue's sister was present during Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> after the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. Despite their allotted time for speaking having run out, her look told Katniss that she needed to say something to the people of District 11 about her sister<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, Rue, who died when <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> threw a spear into her stomach<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. Rue had been of great assistance to Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and a great friend to her<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, as she reminded Katniss of her sister, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. When Rue died, Katniss covered her fatal wound with <a href="/wiki/Flowers" title="Flowers">flowers</a> she picked, and gave the people of District 11 the <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">farewell gesture</a> that they used back in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>Rue's sister's expression made it clear to Katniss that if her sister and her district partner, <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>, weren't thanked, they would not be forgiven. Because of this, Katniss gave a long and heartbreaking speech to the people of District 11, saying that although she didn't know Thresh well, he had saved her from <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> and spared her life. Next, she described how much Rue meant to her, stating that she wouldn't be forgotten. That speech is what inspired District 11 to start an uprising.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a>, a previous <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> from District 11, told Katniss that the families of Rue and Thresh were alive.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, Rue's sister was never mentioned after this and her fate is thus unknown.
</p>
