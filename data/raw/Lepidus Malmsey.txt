<dd>—Lepidus, suggesting the tributes be given a visual cue of who remained in the Hunger Games<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Lepidus Malmsey</b> was a man who worked as a fledgling reporter for <a href="/wiki/Capitol_News" title="Capitol News">Capitol News</a>, the main news organization within <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> at the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. He was the chief reporter in charge of covering these <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">games</a> and was present throughout, providing updates to the Capitol.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLepidus_Malmsey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lepidus Malmsey first appeared at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> after the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for the 10th Hunger Games were placed in a cage there prior to the Games. He interviewed <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, asking her about being the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. She told him that her family wasn't really from District 12, but were actually part of a performing group called the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> and only got trapped in District 12 following the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. He told her that her rainbow dress had been a big hit in the Capitol. He then interviewed Lucy Gray's mentor, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, who had ended up in the zoo cage because of his desire to meet with his tribute. Snow told him that he felt that if his tribute was brave enough to be there, then he should be too. He said that he didn't have a choice after hearing her sing, that he was a fan.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Lepidus was later present when <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> brought sandwiches to feed the tributes. He asked what he was up to, saying that surely the Capitol was feeding them. At this, Lucy Gray told him that she had last eaten the night before <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a>, and slightly taken aback, he told her to enjoy her sandwich before turning to the other tributes. She then shouted at him the suggestion that other visitors to the zoo could bring food and he backed away, worried that her comments could be seen as a condemnation of the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Lepidus was next present during the tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> and covered its <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombing</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Throughout the Hunger Games, he talked with the mentors after their tributes were killed in the games, asking them for last words before they left <a href="/wiki/Heavensbee_Hall" title="Heavensbee Hall">Heavensbee Hall</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, where they had been stationed.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLepidus_Malmsey%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lepidus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lepidus">Lepidus</a> was a Roman general and statesman who formed the Second Triumvirate alongside Octavian and Mark Antony.
</p>
