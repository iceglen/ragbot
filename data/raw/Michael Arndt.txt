</p><p><b>Michael Arndt</b> is an Academy Award-winning American screenwriter. He is best known for writing films such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Miss_Sunshine" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Little Miss Sunshine">Little Miss Sunshine</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanted" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Wanted">Wanted</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy_Story_3" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Toy Story 3">Toy Story 3</a></i>. He wrote the script for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> along with <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0064479/">Simon Beaufoy</a>. He was one of three writers for <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2488496/">Star Wars Episode VII</a></i>, as well as an associate producer.
</p><p>He wrote an initial draft of script for the film adaptation of the prequel novel <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, <sup id="cite_ref-Collider_Film_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Collider_Film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> although later iterations were the work of Michael Lesslie.<sup id="cite_ref-Tributes_and_Mentors_cast_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tributes_and_Mentors_cast-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMichael_Arndt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>He was nominated for Best Adapted screenplay for <i>Toy Story 3</i>. That same year, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter%27s_Bone" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Winter&#39;s Bone">Winter's Bone</a></i> was nominated, which was <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence" title="Jennifer Lawrence">Jennifer Lawrence</a>'s breakout film.</li>
<li>He is the first writer in history to be nominated for Best Original and Adapted Screenplays at the Oscars.</li></ul>
