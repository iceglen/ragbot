<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Hospital%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The hospital often treated victims of lab mishaps. <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a> was taken there after she was bitten by Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a>. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> ran to the hospital afterward, wanting to make sure she was still alive, and was given a fizzy drink and crackers by the nurse at the counter.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> was bombed, Coriolanus was hospitalized along with <a href="/wiki/Androcles_Anderson" title="Androcles Anderson">Androcles Anderson</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gaius_Breen" title="Gaius Breen">Gaius Breen</a>, who were in critical condition. The veterinarian had tried to get <a href="/wiki/Panlo" title="Panlo">Panlo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sheaf" title="Sheaf">Sheaf</a>, the two <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a>, admitted to the hospital, but her requests were refused. They succumbed to their wounds,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> as did Gaius some time later.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coriolanus was put on a table under a bright light while doctors tried to assess his concussion, asking him questions and jabbing him with needles, one of which was a <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> drip. <a href="/wiki/Dr._Wane" title="Dr. Wane">Dr. Wane</a> introduced himself afterward and gave Coriolanus a quick checkup. A few days later, Clemensia visited Coriolanus in his room, showing him what the snake venom had done to her before being carried away by two hospital staff. Coriolanus was released the next day.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point during his time in the hospital, Coriolanus received a tube of ointment.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCapitol_Hospital%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The entrance hall is cool, with automated doors that lead to the emergency room.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It has air-conditioning and gives generous portions of bland food to its patients. The hospital also has a large-screen television.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
