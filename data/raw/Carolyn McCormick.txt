</p><p><b>Carolyn McCormick</b> is an American actress who narrated the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/audiobook" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:audiobook">audiobook</a> versions of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>. Recognized by <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AudioFile" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:AudioFile">AudioFile</a></i> as the 2009 Best Voice in Young Adult for her readings of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the trilogy was her first foray into audiobook narration for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/young-adult_fiction" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:young-adult fiction">young-adult fiction</a>, as she had previously narrated <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/crime_fiction" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:crime fiction">crime fiction</a>.
</p><p>McCormick began acting in 1985 and has worked in TV, movies, theatre and voice acting. She is most known for the role of Dr. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Olivet" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Elizabeth Olivet">Elizabeth Olivet</a> in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order_(franchise)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Law &amp; Order (franchise)"><i>Law &amp; Order</i> franchise</a>. Olivet is one of only five characters who have appeared in all four of the <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Law &amp; Order">Law &amp; Order</a></i> series set in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:New York City">New York City</a>.
</p>
