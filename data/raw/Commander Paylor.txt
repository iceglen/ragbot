<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommander_Paylor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 270px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f3/Commander-paylor-district-8.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150110113111" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Paylor in District 8." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f3/Commander-paylor-district-8.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/270?cb=20150110113111" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="270" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Commander-paylor-district-8.jpg" data-image-key="Commander-paylor-district-8.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Commander-paylor-district-8.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Paylor in District 8.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> first meets Paylor when she goes to the <a href="/wiki/District_8_hospital" title="District 8 hospital">District 8 hospital</a> in order to film a <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> for the Airtime Assault on <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. Paylor takes Katniss along with <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>, and the TV crew into the hospital. Paylor watches Katniss closely, to see what she is made of and if they have been right to count on her.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Katniss leaves the hospital, Capitol <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercrafts</a> fly in and begin to bomb the hospital. In an effort to fight against the bombers, Katniss and Gale climb to the roof of a warehouse that has machine gun nests. This is where they run into Commander Paylor again. Together, they all work to destroy the other waves of hovercrafts that fly overhead.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Commander Paylor later returns in the story when Katniss is living in the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President's mansion">president's mansion</a>. Katniss follows the strong scent of <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>'s roses until she finds a room that is being guarded by two soldiers. At first, they do not permit her entrance to the room because they are following <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>'s orders. However, when Paylor appears, she instructs the soldiers to let Katniss go in and they obey her authority. Katniss enters to find President Snow, awaiting his <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a>, and the two of them have a brief conversation. Snow informs Katniss that he was not responsible the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="City Circle bombing">City Circle bombing</a>, which killed many Capitol children and medics, including <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>; rather, President Coin was behind it.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When Katniss emerges, Paylor asks her, "Did you find what you were looking for?", and Katniss holds up a rose, which Paylor takes as an affirmative response.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Katniss assassinates President Coin, an emergency election was thrown together and Paylor was voted in as president. Under her rule, <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">the Hunger Games</a> were abolished, the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arenas</a> were destroyed, and memorials were built. It can be assumed that Paylor successfully set up the republic that <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> announced that they would form after the Capitol's defeat. She also pardoned Katniss for the assassination of Coin and declared her mentally unstable and let her return to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. Plutarch became her Secretary of Communications.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommander_Paylor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Paylor's role is expanded upon in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>. She is seen in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> alongside Commander <a href="/wiki/Lyme" title="Lyme">Lyme</a> and the other rebels. Interacting with President Coin via a live feed, the three discuss ways in which the rebels can capture the Capitol's arsenal of weaponry held inside the large mountain nicknamed "<a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>". Given the suffering that the people of District 8 have already been through, Paylor refuses to commit her troops to an assault just to acquire weapons, as Coin suggests. Paylor and Lyme eventually persuade Coin to agree to <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s plan: disabling the Nut by bombing avalanche paths, burying many of the people and assets inside. One exit was left open to give the Capitol loyalists a chance to surrender.<sup id="cite_ref-MJP2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>After the success of this mission, Paylor is later seen giving a rousing speech to rebel forces assembled on the outskirts of the Capitol. She explains how they will capture President Snow and take control of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-MJP2_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommander_Paylor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Paylor is in her early thirties, and she was mentioned to look too young for her occupation. She has dark brown eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCommander_Paylor%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Although a strong and respectable leader, Commander Paylor was one of the very few rebels who was not overly concerned with vengeance. Though holding President Snow and the Capitol in contempt for their crimes against the districts, Paylor urged the rebels not to attack Capitol citizens and to only focus on capturing Snow.<sup id="cite_ref-MJP2_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
