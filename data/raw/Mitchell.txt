<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMitchell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mitchell lived in District 13, and he was assigned to Squad 451, along with <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>. He trained alongside his new compatriots for several days before deployment for the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> On their sixth morning in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, the Star Squad filmed reenactments of their reactions to explosions in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, and Mitchell badly projected an idea of desperation by grinding his teeth and flaring his nostrils.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMitchell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Squad 451's commander, <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>, accidentally triggered a pod that blew off his legs. Triggered by the commotion, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> lost control due to his earlier <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a> and reacted violently towards <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>. Mitchell tackled Peeta and attempted to pin him to the ground, but Peeta threw him off into a mislabeled pod, activating a barbed wire net that caught and speared him.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/50/Mitchelldiee.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20251016073658" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Mitchell's death" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/50/Mitchelldiee.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251016073658" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="105" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mitchelldiee.jpg" data-image-key="Mitchelldiee.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mitchelldiee.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Mitchell's death</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> and <a href="/wiki/Leeg_1" title="Leeg 1">Leeg 1</a> unsuccessfully attempted to shoot it down by the cables that held it up. A third pod sent a wave of a tar-like substance to cover everything, including Mitchell's body, forcing the others to flee. On their way out of the area, Katniss discovered his hand and gun hanging out of the net.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMitchell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mitchell and <a href="/wiki/Homes" title="Homes">Homes</a> were both described as "older guys".<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Additionally, since Katniss emphasized how much strength it must have taken Peeta to launch Mitchell away,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> he may have been a larger man.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMitchell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mitchell and Homes both didn't speak often, but they could "shoot the dust off your boots at fifty yards", characterizing them as excellent marksmen.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mitchell also became known as the worst actor in the group.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
