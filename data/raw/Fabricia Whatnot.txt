<p><b>Fabricia Whatnot</b> was a fashion designer in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, as well as <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>'s boss. Tigris was meant to be working under her as an apprentice designer; however, Fabricia used her for slave labor. She would make Tigris give her foot massages and clean her hair from the drains. Despite this, Tigris seemed to adore her and would defend her against criticism from others. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> considered Whatnot to be as ridiculous as her name, though nevertheless possessing a talent for derivative fashion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFabricia_Whatnot%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Fabricia held a party to celebrate <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. Tigris later told Snow that everyone at the party was nuts about his <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, feeling that she had "star quality."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the party, Fabricia told Tigris to throw out the stale <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>, but Tigris secretly saved it and brought it home, where she managed to salvage it and turn it into something tasty.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Fabricia discarded a pot of blush with a little bit left in the bottom, and Tigris used it for Lucy Gray's makeup.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFabricia_Whatnot%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Fabricia had long, magenta hair. She was also involved with all the latest fashion trends, and incorporated them into her outfits.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFabricia_Whatnot%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The name Fabricia is based off <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textiles#Fabric" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Textiles">fabric</a>, a material she likely worked with as a fashion designer. "Whatnot" is a word that refers to a nondescript person or thing.
</p>
