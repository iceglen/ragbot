She works for the <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">rebels</a> alongside him in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, and is always by his side.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFulvia_Cardew%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Fulvia threw out a piece of paper with only a few words on it, which was treated as a crime in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>It was originally her idea to film <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a> on a stage with a script for Katniss to read; however, it was unsuccessful because Katniss' persona didn't translate well unless she was being authentic, not performing for cameras on command.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Fulvia's pride is wounded when <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> comes up with a better plan to put her out where the real action is and film her when she is being herself, under the direction of <a href="/wiki/Cressida" title="Cressida">Cressida</a> and her team.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Fulvia redeems herself by coming up with the idea of doing a montage of propos dedicated to the fallen <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>, such as <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> or <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>, an idea which is then supported by Cressida. They would contain commentary from <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> with the theme being "We Remember," read out by <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a>. This would then appeal to the people of the districts on a closer level since many of them would have had personal feelings towards their own tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Fulvia is one of the few named rebels that survived the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.
</p>

<p>Fulvia has silver flowers tattooed onto her plump cheeks.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFulvia_Cardew%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Fulvia is calculating,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but struggles with her rich Capitol upbringing when she tries to fit in with District 13 citizens.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Despite her good intentions, Fulvia is quite rude, as shown when she constantly insults Katniss.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFulvia_Cardew%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the <i>Mockingjay</i> films, the character of Fulvia was replaced with <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>On this matter, director <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a> said, "How can anybody replace <a href="/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks" title="Elizabeth Banks">Elizabeth Banks</a> as Effie Trinket in these movies? When <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> saw <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, she called and one of the first things she said was, ‘There’s no way Effie Trinket cannot be in the Mockingjay films.’ Effie brings such warmth and fun and levity to these dark stories. She’s the fish out of water in this one and fans will love how she has adapted to the world of District 13."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>There may be a chance that Fulvia was a relative or descendant of <a href="/wiki/Livia_Cardew" title="Livia Cardew">Livia Cardew</a>.
<ul><li>They share some traits in common; their general rudeness being one of them.</li>
<li>Both their names end with "via."</li>
<li>As <a href="/wiki/Livia_Cardew" title="Livia Cardew">Livia Cardew</a> is implied to have married <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, and he has children and grandchildren, Fulvia may be related to <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>'s descendants, which makes her status as a rebel ironic.</li></ul></li></ul>
