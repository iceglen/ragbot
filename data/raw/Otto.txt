<p><b>Otto</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo Ring</a>. He died before the Games started. During the Ring twins' funeral, the bodies of him and his district partner, <a href="/wiki/Ginnee" title="Ginnee">Ginnee</a> (or what remained of them), were draped on horses and paraded down <a href="/wiki/Scholars_Road" title="Scholars Road">Scholars Road</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Otto grew up in District 6 and became his district's male tribute in <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with the other tributes, Otto was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> While in the zoo, Otto and Ginnee began a funny dance, and their mentors, <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana Ring</a>, got some of the audience to clap along. This led to the environment becoming more festive and social.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the start of the Games, the tributes and their mentors were taken on a tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, sending shrapnel that killed Otto, as well as his district partner and their mentors.
</p><p>His body was later draped on the back of a horse and paraded down Scholars Road during the Ring twins' funeral.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<p>Instead of being killed in the bombing, Otto survived to see the arena. When the gong rang, he initially headed toward the pile of weapons but changed his mind and chased after <a href="/wiki/Sheaf" title="Sheaf">Sheaf</a>. He tried for some time to kill her before eventually grabbing a crossbow and shooting an arrow into her leg. Before she could get up, he shot another arrow into her back. He then saw other tributes fighting and tried to hide when he saw <a href="/wiki/Hy" title="Hy">Hy</a>, who he chased; however, upon entering the tunnels, he decided to hide under some rubble. Between <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a>'s deaths, he was killed by unknown means offscreen, placing 12th. His body was seen being dragged out from a pile of rubble by Coral and her alliance, suggesting they were responsible for his death.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Otto's name might come from the German engineer Nicolaus Otto, who successfully developed the first modern internal combustion engine during the Industrial Revolution. It could also be derived from the word "automobile".
</p>
