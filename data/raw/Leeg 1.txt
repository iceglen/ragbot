<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_1%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Leeg 1 lived in District 13 with <a href="/wiki/Leeg_2" title="Leeg 2">her sister</a> and an unnamed father.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The sisters were both assigned to Squad 451, and they trained alongside their new compatriots for several days before deployment for the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>. During this time, they were given the nicknames "Leeg 1" and "Leeg 2" to distinguish them. Once in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, they filmed <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a> for several days. However, Leeg 1's sister hit a mislabeled <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pod</a> on the fourth morning, which shot a "sunburst of metal darts" through her brain, killing her instantly. The following evening, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> was sent to take her place.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> That night, Leeg 1 broke down and sobbed over her sister's death inside her tent in the rebel encampment.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>
The next morning, she participated in the filming of another propo. After it went awry, killing <a href="/wiki/Mitchell" title="Mitchell">Mitchell</a> and fatally wounding their commander <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>, Leeg 1 and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> shot through the front door lock of a building so they could take refuge inside, and they unsuccessfully attempted to shoot Mitchell down from his barbed wire net. After Boggs succumbed to his injuries, she asked about taking his body with them as they left to fulfill <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s falsified assassination mission against <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 300px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4a/Snapshotleeg_1.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20220207200053" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Leeg 1 running past ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4a/Snapshotleeg_1.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/300?cb=20220207200053" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="124" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Snapshotleeg 1.jpg" data-image-key="Snapshotleeg_1.jpg" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Snapshotleeg_1.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Leeg 1 running past <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> launched an assault on the building they'd just been in, and upon seeing herself declared dead on television, Leeg 1 felt badly for her father, who would now be grieving both of his daughters.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_1%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The squad traveled underground through the sewers into the <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>, at which point they were attacked by <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a>, activated pods, and Peacekeepers who were eventually decapitated by the mutts. <a href="/wiki/Jackson" title="Jackson">Jackson</a> and Leeg 1 stayed behind to hold the creatures off at a pod known as the Meat Grinder, attempting to buy time for the rest of the squad to make it to safety. It's assumed that Leeg 1 was either killed by the Meat Grinder or the mutts.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_1%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, Leeg 2 survived to see Peeta join the squad, and she was injured in the incident that killed Mitchell and Boggs. Leeg 1 stayed behind to take care of her, and when the Peacekeeper assault on the building began, they returned fire. However, it's assumed that they were ultimately killed.<sup id="cite_ref-film_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_1%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was a young woman in her twenties with "weird yellow flecks in her eyes". This was the only way Katniss could distinguish her from her sister, since they looked so similar in uniform.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, they were identical twins with blonde hair.<sup id="cite_ref-film_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_1%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The Leeg sisters, despite being played by identical twin actresses in <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i>, are not explicitly referred to as twins in the book.</li>
<li>She and her sister were one of two sets of siblings in their squad. The other set was <a href="/wiki/Castor" title="Castor">Castor</a> and <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> from the camera crew.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
