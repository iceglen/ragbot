</p><p>The <b>Corso</b> is a palatial grand avenue containing multiple apartments, housing the most elite and powerful citizens and families of all of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> located in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCorso%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Dark_Days">Dark Days</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCorso%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Prior to the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the avenue was used to display military parades, as it could fit eight chariots side by side, on the anniversary of the Capitol's founding. Alongside much of the Capitol, the avenue was bombed and thus the parades was discontinued.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At some point, the rebel forces had managed to invade the Corso, as <a href="/wiki/Snow" class="mw-disambig" title="Snow">Snow</a> recalled hearing the footsteps of rebel boots from the war in his youth.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The elevator at <a href="/wiki/The_Snows%27_apartment_building" title="The Snows&#39; apartment building">the Snows' apartment building</a> stopped working early into the war, and was only fixed ten years after it ended. This was presumably to appeal to potential buyers after many of the apartments were placed on the market.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCorso%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>By the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the Capitol's reconstruction effort was still ongoing, as debris was still left on the avenue from the rebellion. Just before the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>, due to the fact that many of the residents were unable to pay their respective expenses, homes on the Corso and other wealthy areas were placed on the market. This was also in part due to taxes, which was going to be compulsory for all citizens of Panem, as prior, taxes was only applicable to District citizens. This decision was made by the government to assist with the reconstruction effort.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCorso%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Corso may have been named after the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Via_del_Corso" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Via del Corso">Via del Corso</a>, a major street in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rome">Rome</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCorso%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Despite being a part of the Plinth family, Sejanus never lived within the Corso, having died prior to his parents' move.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Based on the description of the Corso and <a href="/wiki/The_Snows%27_apartment_building" title="The Snows&#39; apartment building">the Snows' apartment building</a>, some fans theorize that the Corso became the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a> and the apartment building became the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>.</li>
<li>The Corso scenes in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> were filmed on Berlin's <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Marx-Allee" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Karl-Marx-Allee">Karl-Marx-Allee</a>, formerly Stalinallee. It was built during reconstruction in East Germany after WWII and named after the Soviet leader. Like the Corso, the real-world location had housing, shops, and monuments. A gigantic statue of Stalin was replaced with Panem iconography for the film.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>The replacement statue depicted a woman holding crossed swords above her head. Alongside design studies by Leif Heanzo, she was referred to as <a href="/wiki/Panema" class="mw-redirect" title="Panema">Panema</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>In the film, the Corso was given the name Road of Hope on an eviction notice to the Snows.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
