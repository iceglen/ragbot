<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Hy grew up in District 5 and became his district's male tribute in the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with the other tributes, Hy was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Hy unexpectedly died of <a href="/wiki/Health_conditions#Asthma" title="Health conditions">asthma</a> complications the night before the Games. The veterinarian had attempted to save him, but wasn't able to.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, he survived to see the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, but was killed in the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a> by <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>. When the gong rang, he decided to grab a weapon from the center. Hy fought with other tributes and was chased to the pile of weapons by <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a>, who was then killed. Despite Hy trying to hide behind the structure, <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> saw and attacked him. Hy searched for <a href="/wiki/Sol" title="Sol">Sol</a> and the two ran towards the tunnels. They went after <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> and <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a>, but the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tributes squeezed through a door just before <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>'s group appeared. Hy was caught in <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>'s net and killed by Mizzen and <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a>, placing 15th.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His name may refer to hydroelectricity, a form of power.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The only time Hy is mentioned in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> is when he dies and on the list of tributes throughout the book. This is the same for his mentor.</li>
<li>Unlike <a href="/wiki/Dill" title="Dill">Dill</a>, who was also very sick prior to the Games, Hy died before the Games started.</li>
<li>Hy is the only character known to have asthma.
<ul><li>Due to his health condition, it's possible that he died of untreated smoke inhalation from the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol Arena bombing">Capitol Arena bombing</a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
