<p><b>"Horn of Plenty"</b> is the national anthem of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> and is played during the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, in public announcements by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>,&#160;and in other official Capitol events or <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propaganda commercials</a>. During the Games, it is played every night throughout the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> while the faces of dead <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> are displayed in the sky. In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">film adaptation</a> of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i>, the instrumental version of this song plays during the opening ceremony and the display of the dead tributes in the arena.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The lyrics are supposed to represent the unity and prosperity of Panem under the leadership of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. However, this is blatantly false, since Panem is a totalitarian country ruthlessly ruled by its Government,  while prosperity is limited to the Capitol and, in part, its most loyal districts such as <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, while the remaining Districts live in poverty and under harsh living conditions.</li>
<li>An instrumental version of "Horn of Plenty" can be heard playing before the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, meaning the song likely existed before the events of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, but was not adopted as the national anthem until later. "<a href="/wiki/Gem_of_Panem" title="Gem of Panem">Gem of Panem</a>" was the national anthem during and before the 10th Hunger Games. This was also the anthem during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, meaning that Horn of Plenty was not adopted as an anthem until sometime after the 50th Hunger Games.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Behind_the_scenes">Behind the scenes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The lyrics are included in the original score sheets labeled "2m5s v6 Chariot Ceremony". In the Blu-ray and DVD special features, a small feature about the score shows the London Voices choir performing the anthem.</li></ul>
