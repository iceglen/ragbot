<p>The <b>Victory Tour</b> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> celebrated <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">co-victors</a> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>. It began in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, cycled through all of the other <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>, and ended in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, Katniss and Peeta's home district.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Participants">Participants</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The 74th Hunger Games victors Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark were featured on the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">tour</a>. They were accompanied by their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>; their <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>; their <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylists</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> and <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a>, and their <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep teams</a>— Katniss's consisting of <a href="/wiki/Flavius" title="Flavius">Flavius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Octavia" title="Octavia">Octavia</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Venia" title="Venia">Venia</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Tour">The Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_11">District 11</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 11 was the first stop in the nationwide tour. Katniss noted on her way in how high the watchtowers were, how great the security was, how scarce the people were, and how shabby the houses looked (even compared to those in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>).
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/40/District11station.png/revision/latest?cb=20140608143657" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Victory Tour arriving in District 11." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/40/District11station.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140608143657" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District11station.png" data-image-key="District11station.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District11station.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Victory Tour arriving in District 11.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure> 
<p>After leaving the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a>, the team and victors were forced into an armored truck. Once they arrived at the Town Square, they realized that only a very small portion of the town was present; most of the rest were completing the harvest. A small platform was erected at the bottom of the stage for the dead tributes' families. 
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 274px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/44/Victor_Tour.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720221746" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 11" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/44/Victor_Tour.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/274?cb=20130720221746" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="274" height="114" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Victor Tour.png" data-image-key="Victor_Tour.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Victor_Tour.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">District 11</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The mayor gave a speech in Katniss and Peeta's honor, and two girls handed them each a bouquet of flowers. After a scripted reply, Peeta began his personal comments about <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>, then added that he and Katniss would be giving the tributes' families one month of their tribute winnings for the duration of their lives. The mayor presented Katniss and Peeta with a plaque. Shortly before the end of the ceremony, Rue's sister gave Katniss a reproachful look, prompting Katniss to give a sentimental speech. Following this, an <a href="/wiki/Unidentified_District_11_citizen" title="Unidentified District 11 citizen">old man</a> whistled <a href="/wiki/Rue%27s_four-note_song" title="Rue&#39;s four-note song">Rue's four-note song</a> and signaled the crowd to give the <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a>, an old District 12 ritual to show admiration and respect. Katniss was then hurried inside, but returned to the veranda to find two <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> shooting a bullet into the old man's head before the crowd.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Two bullets rang out moments afterward, and Katniss suspected that either one of Rue's sisters or Thresh's grandmother had been killed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span id="District_10.E2.80.934"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="District_10–4">District 10–4</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Districts <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a> through <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a> were part of the same routine Katniss and Peeta did in District 11, with the exception that their speeches were provided by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, and no personal remarks were made. Districts <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, 4 and <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a> cheered Katniss' name in vengeance and fury rather than a celebration.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss wore a green dress in <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, which <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, a District 4 victor, later wore at her <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie&#39;s wedding">wedding</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_3">District 3</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (74th HG)">District 3 male</a>, only one woman—presumably his mother—was present, while four of the <a href="/wiki/District_3_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 3 female (74th HG)">District 3 female</a>'s relatives attended.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District 3 was one of the districts that cheered for Katniss as a sign of <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_2">District 2</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> was hard for Katniss as <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, District 2's tributes for that year, could have made it home together if it wasn't for Katniss and Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The two avoided eye contact with their families. In District 2, Katniss wore a deep blue velvet dress with <a href="/wiki/Diamonds" title="Diamonds">diamonds</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_1">District 1</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> was, once again, "its own kind of awful" as Katniss personally killed <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> and <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>, District 1's tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>The Capitol appearance began with the victor <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>. During this interview, Peeta, to the delight of the people of the Capitol, publicly proposed to Katniss. Katniss had suggested this, but Peeta was quite upset as he wanted it to be real. President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> made a surprise appearance to congratulate Peeta and Katniss on their engagement. During the appearance, Snow hinted to Katniss that she failed to convince him.
</p><p>The Victory Dinner was held in the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">president's mansion</a>. The roof had been transformed to look like the night sky, just like it looked in District 12. Katniss tried to eat some of each of the never ending line of exquisite dishes, but failed after ten tables. Flavius handed Peeta a small bottle of clear liquid, which would make him vomit in order to empty his stomach and eat more food, but this disgusts the two victors and Peeta refuses.
</p><p>Katniss discovered that her <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> had become a fashion accessory in the Capitol, and a variety of items had been crafted into its likeness. Portia, Peeta's stylist, introduced the victors to <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, who then showed Katniss his mockingjay hologram watch and said his <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>'s meeting started at midnight. Although she failed to realize it at the time, this was a hint about the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">arena</a> used in the upcoming Games. Heavensbee later told her that he wanted to gain her trust.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>The celebrations in the winning tribute's district always occurred on the last day of the <a href="/wiki/Harvest_Festival" class="mw-redirect" title="Harvest Festival">Harvest Festival</a>. Since the Capitol threw the event, the entire district had full stomachs that night, which rarely happened. A dinner was held at <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Undersee%27s_house" title="Mayor Undersee&#39;s house">Mayor Undersee's house</a> for the victors and their families. After Katniss got ready for dinner, she stopped by the mayor's office, where she learned of the <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">uprising of District 8</a> from his television.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour_%2874th_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>According to <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, the Victory Tour did not exist up to and including the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so the very first tour was <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>'s, after she won the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This means that Katniss and Peeta's Victory Tour would have been the 64th to occur chronologically.</li>
<li>In <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i>, Katniss learned about the uprising on the train from the Capitol back to District 12 at the end of the tour. She was walking down the hallway late at night when she came to a stop outside a control room door to watch the footage.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
