<p><b>Tracker jackers</b> are genetically engineered wasp <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a>, designed and invented by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. They exist only as a conception of the sadistic tendencies of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s dictators. They are genetically programmed to attack anyone or anything that disturbs their nest. They follow their targets far away from their nests, unlike natural <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/vespid" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:vespid">vespids</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the Capitol used tracker jackers nests like <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">land mines</a>, strategically planting them around the districts of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. After the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the Capitol destroyed all the nests around the city, but they left nests in place around the districts— particularly in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>'s orchards.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This was a reminder of their weakness, and to keep them confined within their <a href="/wiki/Fence" title="Fence">fences</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

Presumably, the wasps had been killed by the fumes.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Appearance">Appearance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tracker jackers were larger than normal wasps, and they had solid gold bodies. Naturally, they had stingers that would transfer venom to their victims; one or two stings could cause powerful hallucinations, but several could prove lethal.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Stings">Stings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Tracker jacker envenomation was considered extremely painful and caused localized swelling that progressed dramatically in minutes following the initial exposure; the stung area's final size typically ranged from that of a plum to that of an orange. Without treatment, intense pain could persist for days. The venom could be lethal in a relatively low dose - multiple stings in a quick succession usually led to swift death. <sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Hallucinations">Hallucinations</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Tracker jacker venom was engineered to target the part of the brain that generates fear, creating terrifying hallucinations that can drive a person to madness. The images are bizarre and often seem false afterwards, though they are vivid at the time; however, it can be difficult to discern fact from fiction even once its effects wear off.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Peeta Mellark, who received countless doses of venom, later was able to identify influenced memories because they were "shiny."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Tracker jacker venom causes visual, auditory, olfactory, and tactile hallucinations. After being stung three times, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> experienced a number of false sensations. She believed a "foul-smelling green liquid" was oozing from her stings, and that the same substance exploded from <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>'s. She also thought Glimmer's flesh was disintegrating under her hands. Soon after, she saw a butterfly the size of a house; humming orange bubbles; trees transforming to blood; and she saw and felt ants crawling from her blisters and boring into her eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Even once she passed out, she had nightmares of her loved ones and herself dying in horrific ways, and she slept for two nights.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, Katniss hallucinated <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> stepping out from behind a tree, telling the audience about the effects of the tracker jacker venom.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Hijacking">Hijacking</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">Hijacking</a></i></center></div>
<p>Tracker jacker venom can also be used to "hijack" memories in a highly secretive form of torture. A memory is called up by some sort of stimulus, and tracker jacker venom is injected in small enough quantities that it causes little physical reaction and minimal hallucination, while still targeting the fear center of the brain. The memory then becomes subconsciously associated with fear and pain, as well as being perceptibly warped. Afterwards, the brain rerecords the memory in the altered form.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Counteraction">Counteraction</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Smoke_sedation">Smoke sedation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Like real vespids, smoke can be used to sedate tracker jackers and minimize the risk of being stung in the first place. This technique was the rebels' one defense against the wasps in the Dark Days.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Treatment">Treatment</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Stings_2">Stings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>A certain type of leaf could draw out the poison. This remedy was known to <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> and workers in the orchards of District 11, who often carried the leaves with them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> These leaves could be applied by chewing and applying them topically, as <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> did for Katniss, though Mrs. Everdeen used another method.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss later applied the leaves to Peeta's sting and, since they drew out infection, the leg wound he'd been given by <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Hijacking_2">Hijacking</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p><a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> suggested that doctors from <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> treat Peeta by hijacking him back: calling up memories under the calming influence of <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> in order to associate them with positive emotions.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTracker_jacker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tracker jackers are named for their ability to track victims far from their nests.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The term is also a play on "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/yellowjacket" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:yellowjacket">yellowjacket</a>", which is a type of wasp.
</p>
