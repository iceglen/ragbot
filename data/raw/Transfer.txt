<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>There is a simple network of wide tunnels that directly corresponds to the main street plan above, underlying the major avenues and cross streets. It's called the Transfer, since small trucks use it to deliver goods around the city.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> on the Transfer<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The <b>Transfer </b> was a special network of tunnels built underneath <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, used by small trucks and cargo lorries to transport goods throughout the city. It ran directly underneath the Capitol's aboveground roads, and it was staffed in shifts by <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avoxes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTransfer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTransfer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the time of the prequel, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> described the Transfer as a "network of tracks and roadways built under the Capitol". When <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a> escaped from the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> before the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, a displaced manhole cover suggested that he might have fled underground.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was later confirmed that he'd been trapped in a remote level of the Transfer, where he was eventually apprehended.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTransfer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As an <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avox</a>, <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> was enslaved and forced to work underground for five years without seeing sunlight. His brother <a href="/wiki/Castor" title="Castor">Castor</a> was eventually able to pay for him to be able to work aboveground. The Transfer was mined with <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pods</a> that were activated at night as a security measure.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>According to the <a href="/wiki/Holo" title="Holo">Holo</a>, the Transfer held less pods than the streets above during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>. This fact, combined with the need not to be seen while they were presumed dead, convinced <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> to travel through the sewers<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> before eventually ascending to the Transfer. There, they were confronted by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> and deadly <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the Transfer, <a href="/wiki/Messalla" title="Messalla">Messalla</a> was killed by a pod that melted the flesh off his body; <a href="/wiki/Jackson" title="Jackson">Jackson</a> and <a href="/wiki/Leeg_1" title="Leeg 1">Leeg 1</a> died holding off mutts at a pod called the Meat Grinder; and Castor, <a href="/wiki/Homes" title="Homes">Homes</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> were all decapitated by the mutts. Only <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cressida" title="Cressida">Cressida</a>, and Pollux survived the ordeal.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_22_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
