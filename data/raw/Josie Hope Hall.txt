<p>Josie Hope is the youngest of three sisters. They are all members of The Bennett Hall Band, which was named after their brother who passed away in 2010.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
