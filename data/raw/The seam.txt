Residents of the Seam lived in "squat gray houses"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> with a number of gates, yards, and alleys between them.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Seam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Hunger_Games">Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Seam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> and <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a> were <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> to participate in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Lucy Gray went on to become District 12's first <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Forty years later, <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> were both reaped from the Seam for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, as was <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, the victor that year.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/District_12_male_(73rd_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 12 male (73rd HG)">male</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12_female_(73rd_HG)" title="District 12 female (73rd HG)">female</a> tribute in the <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">73rd Hunger Games</a> were both from the Seam,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the same was true of <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>, who was reaped for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> the next year.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, her older sister Katniss <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to go in her place.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following Katniss's joint victory with <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, hungry children from the Seam benefited from monthly Parcel Day food deliveries. These were given to the district by the Capitol as part of the victors' winnings.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, after only a few months, Parcel Day deliveries and regular shipments of <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tessera</a> grain and oil started to arrive spoiled and "defiled by rodents".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss and Haymitch were both reaped again for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, but Peeta volunteered to compete in place of Haymitch.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Seam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Like most parts of the district, the Seam was destroyed in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, and many of its people were killed. However, fewer than a dozen <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchants</a> escaped, so the vast majority of survivors were from the Seam. <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> led refugees to the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside District 12, and upon discovery and rescue by <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a>, they were all granted citizenship in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Seam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Ethnicity">Ethnicity</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Seam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>People who lived in the Seam tended to have dark straight hair, olive skin, and gray eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> These traits were distinctive enough to residents that Katniss called it "that Seam look",<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and she could tell them apart from <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchants</a> on sight.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> and Prim looked "out of place [in the Seam]... with their light hair and blue eyes", which Katniss attributed to her mother being born into the merchant class.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In a 2011 interview with <i>Entertainment Weekly</i>, <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> said that Seam residents like Katniss and Gale "were not particularly intended to be biracial", nor did she have a specific ethnicity in mind for those characters while writing. However, she stated that, since <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a> is set hundreds of years into the future, "There’s been a lot of ethnic mixing."<sup id="cite_ref-:0_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This suggests that those in the Seam could have mixed ethnic and/or racial backgrounds, but the details of said backgrounds are undetermined.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lifestyle">Lifestyle</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Seam%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 230px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bd/Miners.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130521084417" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 12 coal miners." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bd/Miners.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/230?cb=20130521084417" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="230" height="94" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Miners.jpg" data-image-key="Miners.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Miners.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">District 12 coal miners.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Most citizens here were <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miners</a>; early in the morning, the streets were crowded with them as they headed to work.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> People generally traveled on foot since few could afford cars, though some occasionally rode in wagons. Merchant and Seam children attended <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a> and learned about their district's trade together, preparing them for employment in the mines<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> after their last reaping at 18 years old.
</p><p>Due to rampant poverty, people often could not afford to eat, and many outright starved. However, families with children who took out tesserae were known to make "flat, dense loaves"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or "ugly drop biscuits"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> from their year-long monthly allotments of grain. Most families in District 12 who relied on tesserae were from the Seam; the trade-off was that this increased their children's chances of being reaped for the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In District 12, looking old was an achievement since so many people died young, and plump people were envied because it was a sign that they weren't scraping by.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Despite being the smallest, poorest district in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, the people of 12 knew how to dance.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
