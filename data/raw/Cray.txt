</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCray%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cray was known to exploit starving young women from <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> by offering them money in exchange for sex. In hard times, the most desperate women gathered at his door, essentially competing with each other for the chance to feed their families. According to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, if she had been older when <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">her father</a> died, she might have been among them.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This suggests that Cray also victimized teenage girls.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i>Catching Fire</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCray%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Katniss saw Cray in <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> after she bought white liquor for <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>. Cray, a heavy drinker, warned her of how strong the alcohol was, saying that it could probably kill her. Only seconds later did Cray buy a bottle of the same alcohol.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He was <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s best customer when it came to poached wild turkeys, but Gale was surprised when he knocked on the door one day to find Cray replaced as Head Peacekeeper. <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>, the new Head Peacekeeper, saw the dead turkey hanging from his belt and dragged Gale to the square, where he was forced to plead guilty to his crime and withstand a public whipping. He was whipped at least forty times, and fell unconscious around thirty before Katniss stepped in to stop it.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, Cray greeted Commander Thread upon arrival. However, a bag was thrown over his head and he was immediately arrested. Cray's fate is unknown,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but following his arrest, he was most likely <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executed</a> on <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a>'s orders for his laxness and leniency.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCray%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cray is described as being an older man with "a few strands" of silvery gray hair combed to one side, and he had an unusually red face.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He was also referred to several times as "old Cray",<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> suggesting he may have been closer to elderly than middle-aged.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCray%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cray was disliked by most citizens in District 12 for being a Peacekeeper, and especially for exploiting the young women of the Seam.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He could often be seen drinking liquor instead of doing his job.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>However, for all of his faults, he was quite lenient with the law, and during his tenure as the Head Peacekeeper, he neglected to enforce many of the strict laws that were in place and harshly enforced in other districts. Many laws, notably the ones against black market trading and against hunting and gathering, were left unenforced. As such, a considerable number of citizens actively engaged in such activities, including Cray himself, who was known to frequent the black markets and purchase liquor. 
</p>
