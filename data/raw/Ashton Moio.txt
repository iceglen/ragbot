</p><p><b>Ashton Moio</b> is an American actor and stuntman who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 6 male (74th HG)">male tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAshton_Moio%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Moio is a stunt actor and has been a stunt double for both TV and film; he is cast specifically to portray the fight scenes of a Tribute that is mentioned only once. Besides <i>The Hunger Games</i>, he has also been a stunt actor in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Riding_Hood_(2011_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Red Riding Hood (2011 film)">Red Riding Hood</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_X_(2012_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Project X (2012 film)">Project X</a></i> and an actor in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Let_Me_In_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Let Me In (film)">Let Me In</a></i>. Moio was featured on ABC Family's TV show <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Twisted (TV series)">Twisted</a></i> as the character Rico, but the show was canned after one season.
</p>
