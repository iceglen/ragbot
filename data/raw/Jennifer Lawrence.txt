</p><p><b>Jennifer Lawrence</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i></a>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a>,</i> <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>. She will reprise her role as Katniss in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJennifer_Lawrence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Jennifer Shrader Lawrence was born in Indian Hills, Kentucky and raised in Louisville, Kentucky, to Karen (née Koch), a children's camp manager, and Gary Lawrence, who owns Lawrence &amp; Associates, a small construction company. She grew up on a horse farm and has two older brothers, Ben and Blaine, and is of English, German, Irish, and Scottish descent. In school, she was prescribed medication for social anxiety and hyperactivity. She acted in local theater and, by the age of 14, had decided to pursue an acting career, persuading her parents to take her to New York City to find a talent agent. Prior to finding success in Hollywood, Lawrence attended Kammerer Middle School in Louisville. She graduated from high school with a 3.9 GPA. While growing up and in between acting, Lawrence served as an assistant nurse at the children's summer day camp, which her mother ran.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJennifer_Lawrence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lawrence's most famous roles to date are in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter%27s_Bone" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Winter&#39;s Bone">Winter's Bone</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Linings_Playbook" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Silver Linings Playbook">Silver Linings Playbook</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Hustle" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:American Hustle">American Hustle</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Look_Up" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Don&#39;t Look Up">Don't Look Up</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passengers_(2016_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Passengers (2016 film)">Passengers</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Hard_Feelings_(2023_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:No Hard Feelings (2023 film)">No Hard Feelings</a></i>. She was nominated 2 times for the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Academy Award for Best Actress">Academy Award for Best Actress</a> for <i>Winter's Bone</i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Joy">Joy</a></i>, and won it for her performance in <i>Silver Linings Playbook</i>. She was nominated for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress">Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress</a> for <i>American Hustle</i>. She also won the Golden Globe for Best Supporting Actress in <i>American Hustle</i> and won the Golden Globe for Best Actress in a Motion Picture - Musical or Comedy twice for <i>Silver Linings Playbook</i> and <i>Joy</i>. She has starred in several other major films such as <i>The House at the End of the Street,<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Burning_Plain" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Burning Plain">The Burning Plain</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Poker_House" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Poker House">The Poker House</a>, </i>and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men:_First_Class" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:X-Men: First Class">X-Men: First Class</a></i>.She also reappears in the sequel, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men:_Days_of_Future_Past" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:X-Men: Days of Future Past">X-Men: Days of Future Past</a></i>.
</p><p>She placed 10<sup>th</sup> in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PopSugar" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:PopSugar">PopSugar</a>'s PopSugar 100 Top 10 Rising Stars released in June 2011.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i>The Hunger Games</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJennifer_Lawrence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7d/Jennifer_Lawrence_on_Hunger_Games_set.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120425185911" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Lawrence on the set of The Hunger Games." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7d/Jennifer_Lawrence_on_Hunger_Games_set.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120425185911" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Jennifer Lawrence on Hunger Games set.jpg" data-image-key="Jennifer_Lawrence_on_Hunger_Games_set.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Jennifer_Lawrence_on_Hunger_Games_set.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Lawrence on the set of <i>The Hunger Games</i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Lawrence's casting as Katniss was approved by author <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> and director <a href="/wiki/Gary_Ross" title="Gary Ross">Gary Ross</a> in defense of some speculation that she might be too old for the role.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> For her audition, she played the scene where she pleaded with her mother to speak after her father's death. On May 18, 2011, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%27%27Entertainment_Weekly%27%27" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:&#39;&#39;Entertainment Weekly&#39;&#39;"><i>Entertainment Weekly</i></a> revealed the very first photo of her as Katniss Everdeen on their magazine cover.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Lawrence almost dropped out of the film due to an injury on set, but handled it and continued to play Katniss. While goofing around on the set with co-star <a href="/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson" title="Josh Hutcherson">Josh Hutcherson</a>, Lawrence accidentally kicked him in the head, giving him a concussion. However, he downplayed its effects to continue filming. It has been confirmed that a file of Lawrence's vocals were used for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Songs_from_District_12_and_Beyond" title="The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond">the soundtrack</a> of <i>The Hunger Games</i> film.
</p><p>She is very close friends with her co-star, <a href="/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson" title="Josh Hutcherson">Josh Hutcherson</a>. Lawrence and Hutcherson were raised in towns close to each other.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personal_life">Personal life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJennifer_Lawrence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>While filming <i>X-Men: First Class</i> in 2010, Lawrence began a romantic relationship with her co-star Nicholas Hoult. The couple broke up around the time they wrapped <i>X-Men: Days of Future Past</i> in 2014.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In September 2016, she began dating director Darren Aronofsky, whom she met during the filming of <i>mother!</i>. The couple split in November 2017. In 2018, she began dating Cooke Maroney, an art gallery director, and they became engaged in February 2019. In October 2019, she married Maroney in Rhode Island. She gave birth to their son in February 2022.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As of May 2019, she resides in Lower Manhattan, New York City and Beverly Hills, California.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJennifer_Lawrence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>So far, she is the only actress from the Hunger Games franchise to chart on the American Top 40 hosted by Ryan Seacrest, albeit as a feature.</li>
<li>For the first two films, Lawrence had to dye her naturally blonde hair brown like Katniss, while her co-star, naturally brown-haired Josh Hutcherson, had to dye his own hair blonde. After filming <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i>, Lawrence cut her hair to restore it to its natural state. She wore a wig for the final two films.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>While filming <i>Catching Fire</i>, Lawrence went temporarily deaf in one ear because of the underwater scenes, just as Katniss did in the book when she destroyed the Careers' camp.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
