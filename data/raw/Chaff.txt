<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Chaff won the <a href="/wiki/45th_Hunger_Games" title="45th Hunger Games">45th Hunger Games</a> between the ages of 15 and 18, but lost his left hand in the process. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> speculated that he may have refused a prosthesis from <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> doctors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Following his victory, he turned to alcohol to cope with his trauma, and became friends and drinking buddies with <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Being a previous victor, Chaff was reaped for the 75th Hunger Games (the 3rd <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>)<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Along with <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a>, <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a>, <a href="/wiki/Woof" title="Woof">Woof</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cecelia" title="Cecelia">Cecelia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Morphlings" title="Morphlings">morphlings</a>, Chaff was part of the <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a> and was instructed by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> to try his best to find <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> and to make sure they both lived after they were reaped during the 75th Hunger Games. He was one of the few tributes who knew about the plan to get Katniss out of the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, Chaff and Seeder rode back to the Training Center when all the other tributes had gotten together. After being introduced by Haymitch, Chaff gave Katniss a kiss on her lips which surprised her, causing her to push him away. Chaff and Haymitch laughed at her reaction.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> They were good friends and seemingly both alcoholics.
</p><p>During training, Chaff and Peeta became close and talked often during training. They both were shown training together with <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> at the spear throwing station, and had lunch together.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interview">Interview</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During Chaff's <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interview</a> with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, he stated that <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> could change the Quell if he wanted to but wouldn't because he thought it doesn't matter to anyone<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. After all the interviews were finished, Chaff joined hands with the other victors as an act of solidarity, which Snow took as an act of outright rebellion. He and Katniss were the first to join hands at the interviews, suggesting he was one of the more rebellious victors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

</p><h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Chaff was not seen much during the Games, most likely seeking Katniss as it was his mission to protect and rescue her so she could help the rebels defeat the Capitol. Unfortunately, he could not locate her or Peeta until near the end of the Games.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bf/ChaffDeathPortrait.png/revision/latest?cb=20221111041611" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Chaff's death portrait." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="117" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="ChaffDeathPortrait.png" data-image-key="ChaffDeathPortrait.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bf/ChaffDeathPortrait.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221111041611"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bf/ChaffDeathPortrait.png/revision/latest?cb=20221111041611" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Chaff's death portrait." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bf/ChaffDeathPortrait.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221111041611" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="117" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="ChaffDeathPortrait.png" data-image-key="ChaffDeathPortrait.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bf/ChaffDeathPortrait.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221111041611"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:ChaffDeathPortrait.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Chaff's death portrait.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Chaff was able to make it to the final 8. Later on in the Games, he finally met up with Peeta, only to be killed by <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> moments later. Shortly after, Peeta avenged Chaff's death by killing Brutus.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Chaff placed 8th out of 24, meaning he was one of the last tributes to die. This was unfortunate because with the right survival instincts, he and the other victors could have lived long enough for Katniss to blow up the force field and live.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>He's dark-skinned, about two meters tall, and one of his arms ends in a stump because he lost his hand in the Games he won thirty years ago.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss about Chaff<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Chaff was at least six feet tall, probably as tall as <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>, with dark skin, and had an arm that ended with a stump because he lost his hand in the 45th Hunger Games. He and Haymitch were friends, as they both turned to drinking after they won their Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Chaff was in his mid to late forties when he died. He was killed at the very end of the 75th Hunger Games by Brutus at the free-for-all just minutes before Katniss Everdeen destroyed the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> surrounding the Arena.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Chaff seemed to be a troublemaker, as Effie implied when she said he "never could stay out of a fight".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Chaff loved to laugh and was one of Haymitch's close friends<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, whom he drank with. Chaff joked by kissing Katniss on her lips and laughing with Haymitch at her reaction<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. However, he did have a soft side, shown when he was friendly and kind to Katniss and Peeta during the training sessions<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, as well as when he backed Seeder up during her interview, and when he joined hands with the other Victors at the end of the interviews without hesitation, unlike <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChaff%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>"Chaff" is a word for the outer hull of grain that is often removed during the refining process. It is a countermeasure used to spoof radar.
</p>
