</p><p><b>Maria Howell</b> is an American actress, singer, and comedian who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaria_Howell%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Howell started her career as a jazz singer. She made her film debut by performing in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Color_Purple_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Color Purple (film)">The Color Purple</a></i>. She has appeared in movies such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blind_Side" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Blind Side">The Blind Side</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_to_Expect_When_You%27re_Expecting_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:What to Expect When You&#39;re Expecting (film)">What to Expect When You're Expecting</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daddy%27s_Little_Girls" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Daddy&#39;s Little Girls">Daddy's Little Girls</a></i>, as well as TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meet_the_Browns_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Meet the Browns (TV series)">Meet the Browns</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Revolution (TV series)">Revolution</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Vampire_Diaries" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Vampire Diaries">The Vampire Diaries</a></i>.
</p><p>In 2012, it was announced that Howell would portray Seeder in <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i>. In the same year, she was also cast for the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Revolution (TV series)">Revolution</a></i>. In 2013, she was given a recurring role in the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devious_Maids" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Devious Maids">Devious Maids</a></i>.
</p>
