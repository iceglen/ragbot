<p><b>Remus Dolittle</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>-in-training and resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. He was a downstairs neighbor of the Snow family, which comprised of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>, and their <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma&#39;am">grandmother</a>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRemus_Dolittle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Shortly after Remus had graduated the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a>, his family pulled in every favor they had to get him the position of Gamemaker-in-Training, hoping that it would provide a stepping stone to the future.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the reaping of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> ran down to the Dolittle's so she could have a proper iron to use with the shirt Coriolanus would wear later that day.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Tigris later stated that she knew the Dolittles would consider selling their home for the right price; they had claimed their apartment was too big for them, although they mainly planned on selling it due to the new taxes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the morning of the 10th Hunger Games, Remus appeared at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> to inform the staff there that the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, <a href="/wiki/Hy" title="Hy">Hy</a>, had passed away due to complications from asthma and couldn't be saved. Because of this, <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> and <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>'s conversation with <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> had to be halted for them to address the new loss.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the taxes were implemented and Coriolanus was sent to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> to become a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a>, the Dolittles helped Tigris and her grandmother put their apartment on the market.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> His family later sold their apartment to <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a>, who began living there with his wife, <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Mrs. Plinth</a>, so they could be closer to the Snows.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRemus_Dolittle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In Roman mythology, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Remus">Remus</a> was the twin brother of Romulus, who went on to found and rule Rome. Remus was killed either by his brother or one of his brother's supporters for criticizing the city. The surname Dolittle is rather literal and befitting of the Capitol's upper class: one who does little, an idler.
</p>
