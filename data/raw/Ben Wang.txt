<dl><dd><span>"<i>What really excited me about that character in the first place [is] he’s not what he seems. I think that’s part of the point of his character. He’s supposed to be cold and calculating, but he’s actually the person who does the most illogical and selfless thing.</i>"</span></dd>

</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBen_Wang%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ben Wang is well known for playing Jin Wang in <i>American Born Chinese</i> and a main role as Li Fong in <i>Karate Kid: Legends</i>, and is a minor character as Bo in <i>Chang Can Dunk</i>.
</p><p>In 2025, Wang starred as Hank Olson in a similar dystopian film, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Long_Walk_(2025_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Long Walk (2025 film)">The Long Walk</a></i>, also directed by <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a>.
</p>
