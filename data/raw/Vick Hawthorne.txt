<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVick_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He lived in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> with his family of five.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> They were among the few people who survived the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, and they were evacuated to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Vick was too young to work or take <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a>, and he was a student at his <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVick_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Vick was the youngest son of <a href="/wiki/Hazelle_Hawthorne" title="Hazelle Hawthorne">Hazelle Hawthorne</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Mr._Hawthorne" title="Mr. Hawthorne">father</a> died in a mine collapse, the same one that killed <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">father</a>, leaving his mother to take care of her four children alone. He was the younger brother of <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale</a> and <a href="/wiki/Rory_Hawthorne" title="Rory Hawthorne">Rory</a>, and the older brother to <a href="/wiki/Posy_Hawthorne" title="Posy Hawthorne">Posy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVick_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Vick presumably has dark hair and olive skin, typical to residents of <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVick_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Vick">Vick</a> might be a diminutive version of the name Victor. This is significant because <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> are the only survivors of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. His surname comes from hawthorn bushes or trees, but could also be a reference to author <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathaniel_Hawthorne" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nathaniel Hawthorne">Nathaniel Hawthorne</a>.
</p>
