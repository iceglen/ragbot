However, the plant was modified to be deadly to anyone who inhaled its scent as happened to <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR16_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR16-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games_2"><i>The Hunger Games</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Peeta gave her the <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> that saved her family from starvation, Katniss saw him at school near a field of dandelions. Katniss and her sister also picked dandelions to serve as food when their <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">father</a> died and they were very hungry (she had seen them when she was in school). She learned from her family's <a href="/wiki/Plant_book" title="Plant book">plant book</a> that dandelions could be used for food. When Katniss threw the cookies <a href="/wiki/Mr._Mellark" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Mellark">Peeta's father</a> gave to her after the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> out the window of the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a>, they fell open next to a bunch of dandelions.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games_3"><i>The Hunger Games</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After she woke up from her <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> venom-induced coma, Katniss had a bad taste in her mouth that water couldn't alleviate. She plucked a flower off a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/honeysuckle" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:honeysuckle">honeysuckle</a> bush and used the drop of nectar from its stamen to refresh her palate. The taste reminded her of home.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i>Mockingjay</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Katniss recounted that the last time she'd sung "<a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">The Hanging Tree</a>" when she was seven, <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">her mother</a> had gotten very angry at her father. Frightened because Mrs. Everdeen never usually yelled, Katniss ran off to her only hiding spot: underneath a honeysuckle bush in the Meadow.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Posy">Posy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s little sister <a href="/wiki/Posy_Hawthorne" title="Posy Hawthorne">Posy</a> is named for a flower. <h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay_2"><i>Mockingjay</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When Peeta Mellark returns to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> after the end of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, one of the first things he does is relocate five bushes of primrose from the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> and begins to plant them outside of Katniss's house in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a> as a memorial to Prim. It was this action that snapped Katniss out of the lethargy she had been in for months and she began living again.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>No one will fully understand - how it's not just a flower, not even just President Snow's flower, but a promise of revenge.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss Everdeen<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Roses are the favorite flower of President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>: His <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma&#39;am">grandmother</a> grew roses on the roof of the apartment building he lived in as a youth,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and his mother perfumed herself with rose-scented powder.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later in life, roses became something of an unofficial emblem for President Snow, as he frequently wore one in his jacket lapel.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_21_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_21-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During her time in The Hunger Games and during the Second Rebellion, the rose became a symbol that Katniss strongly associated with Snow, but specifically white roses filled her with fear.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Snow touched his rose when he noticed <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Flint_striker" title="Flint striker">flint striker</a> which Snow recognized as the craftsmanship of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Victor%27s_Ceremony" title="Victor&#39;s Ceremony">Victor's Ceremony</a>, Snow wore a bloodred rose instead of his usual white rose.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The color of the rose matched the <a href="/wiki/Poison" title="Poison">poisoned</a> sweet that Snow had sent to <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> to kill her as a punishment for Haymitch.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When President Snow met Katniss prior to her <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, he wore a rose in his <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/jacket_lapel" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:jacket lapel">jacket lapel</a>, and the smell was so strong that Katniss thought the scent must've been genetically enhanced.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay_3"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Snow leaves a white rose in Katniss's house, which she finds when she comes back to visit her home after the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombing of District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Red and pink roses (white in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">film</a>) were dropped after the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_13" title="Bombings">bombing of District 13</a>, which Katniss believed to be from President Snow, and like the white rose she found in her house in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>, were there to upset her.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> later reveals that, according to <a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> gossip, Snow uses the scent-enhanced roses to hide the ever-present smell of blood that comes from his mouth sores; during his rise to power - and in an effort to keep it - Snow <a href="/wiki/Poison" title="Poison">poisoned</a> any potential rivals and often deflected suspicion by consuming the poisoned drink himself. While he possessed antidotes to these poisons, they didn't always work, so the mouth sores were a constant problem.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When in the Capitol, <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a> is attacked by <a href="/wiki/Mutts" class="mw-redirect" title="Mutts">mutts</a> that smell like roses, and Katniss describes this type of mutt as one that incorporates a psychological twist to truly frighten the victim. The scent-enhanced roses worn by Snow are grown in a special room in the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">presidential mansion</a>; white roses are the president's favorite, but red, pink, orange, and pale blue ones are also cultivated. Towards the end of <i>Mockingjay</i>, Katniss is in Snow's mansion when she finds herself in his rose room. The putrid stench of roses sickens her to the point of gagging.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The roses reminded her of Peeta because the same type of white roses were in the room during one of Peeta and Katniss's interviews in the Capitol right after winning their first <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Real-life_connection">Real-life connection</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFlowers%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Roses possess powerful symbolic meaning in real life. In the broadest traditional sense, they have embodied positive traits such as love and beauty, but as a writer, <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> reverses this symbolism; within Katniss' experience, something that is normally pretty is turned into something painful and fear-inducing.
</p>

<p>After Rue was killed by <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>, Katniss covered her with white, yellow, and purple wildflowers, both to cover her wound and in an attempt to show the Capitol that they were responsible for her death. She covered Rue's body and wound the flowers in her hair to make her look like she was really sleeping in a meadow.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When they were returning to District 12, Peeta and Katniss walked down the train track, and Peeta collected a bunch of wildflowers to give to Katniss. Katniss did not tell him that these flowers were the tops of onions, which reminded her of Gale.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
