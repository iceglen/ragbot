<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p><b>Diamonds</b>&#160;are a type of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/gemstone" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:gemstone">gemstone</a> and one of the most valuable substances on Earth. In <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, diamonds are associated with <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>, whose primary industry is the manufacture of luxury items (such as jewelry). This makes District 1 very wealthy and creates a favorable relationship between them and <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, whose members are always hungry for the finer things in life. According to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a>, there is a machine in District 1 that can turn graphite into diamonds.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, this machine may be somewhat useless at the time the series takes place; <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> used to mine graphite, but since that district was obliterated in the <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>, graphite may no longer be as common as a raw material.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDiamonds%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a> of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from District 1 (<a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> and <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>), were dressed in "tasteful tunics" decorated with jewels.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Diamonds may have been among those decorations.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>, the <a href="/wiki/District_Twelve" class="mw-redirect" title="District Twelve">District 12</a> <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>, mistakenly says that putting pressure on coal turns it into pearls. Katniss speculates that Effie meant diamonds, but notes that that is untrue as well<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> (putting pressure and heat on coal <i>can</i> result in diamonds, but this process happens only rarely and can take millennia to complete, so it is unlikely that Katniss would know about this rare outcome). During the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Effie's words became something of an inside joke; <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> quoted them to <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> after he found a <a href="/wiki/Peeta%27s_pearl" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta&#39;s pearl">pearl</a> inside an oyster. Finnick didn't understand, but Katniss burst out laughing.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> used diamonds in some of his designs for Katniss: The dark blue velvet dress she wore in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> on her <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a> was studded with diamonds - which <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> described as "gorgeous"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> - and diamonds were used to decorate one of Katniss' potential wedding dresses.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
