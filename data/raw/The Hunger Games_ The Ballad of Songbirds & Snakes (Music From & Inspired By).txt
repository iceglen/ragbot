</p><p><i><b>The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)</b></i> is an official companion album to <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> produced by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Cobb" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dave Cobb">Dave Cobb</a>. Many of its songs feature in the film, while others were written by independent artists to complement them.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Development">Development</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%2526_Snakes_%28Music_From_%2526_Inspired_By%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> collaborated with Dave Cobb on the songs for the film, discussing "the historic, philosophical and poetic inspirations for the lyrics... [and] connecting those works to our current world."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Collins also used to be a country music DJ, which gave her a solid foundation in the genre. She is known to be a fan of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dolly Parton">Dolly Parton</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patsy_Cline" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Patsy Cline">Patsy Cline</a>, who served as inspirations for <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>'s voice.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%2526_Snakes_%28Music_From_%2526_Inspired_By%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Five songs featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> novel do not appear on the album: "<a href="/wiki/Songs#&quot;The_Valley_Song&quot;" title="Songs">The Valley Song</a>", "<a href="/wiki/Songs#&quot;That_Thing_I_Love_With&quot;" title="Songs">That Thing I Love With</a>", "<a href="/wiki/Songs#&quot;I&#39;ll_Sell_You_For_a_Song&quot;" title="Songs">I'll Sell You For a Song</a>", "<a href="/wiki/Songs#&quot;Deep_in_the_Meadow&quot;" title="Songs">Deep in the Meadow</a>", and "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oh_My_Darling_Clementine" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Oh My Darling Clementine">Oh My Darling Clementine</a>".
<ul><li>A sixth song, "<a href="/wiki/Gem_of_Panem" title="Gem of Panem">Gem of Panem</a>", is included in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes_(Original_Motion_Picture_Score)" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Original Motion Picture Score)">film score</a>.</li></ul></li>
<li>In response to popular fan demand, an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/acapella" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:acapella">acapella</a> studio version of "<a href="/wiki/The_Old_Therebefore" title="The Old Therebefore">The Old Therebefore</a>" was released on February 9th, 2024. This single omitted the film score and dialogue present in the album version.</li>
<li>Rachel Zegler, <a href="/wiki/Honor_Gillies" title="Honor Gillies">Honor Gillies</a>, and rapper <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicki_Minaj" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nicki Minaj">Nicki Minaj</a> made jokes on social media that started a rumor that Minaj would be on the soundtrack. In reality, Minaj was teasing the release of her own album, which also fell on November 17th.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
