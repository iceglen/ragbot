<p><b>Blight</b> was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> who participated in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. He was also the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of an unspecified <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, somewhere in the early-to-mid 50s.
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlight%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Blight and his <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>, survived the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a> alongside their <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">allies</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>. Beetee ran in to retrieve his <a href="/wiki/Tools_and_gear#Wire" title="Tools and gear">wire</a> from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, taking a knife to the back, but he managed to keep hold of his bounty and the four of them got away.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlight%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/85/BlightSky.png/revision/latest?cb=20140128192944" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Blight's death portrait." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="93" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="BlightSky.png" data-image-key="BlightSky.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/85/BlightSky.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140128192944"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/85/BlightSky.png/revision/latest?cb=20140128192944" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Blight's death portrait." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/85/BlightSky.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140128192944" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="93" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="BlightSky.png" data-image-key="BlightSky.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/85/BlightSky.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140128192944"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:BlightSky.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Blight's death portrait.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The hot, humid arena made them all dehydrated, so when a lightning storm began at midnight, the four of them believed that the coming storm would let them fill up on rainwater. However, when it rained around 1-1:30am, it turned out to be thick, hot blood, temporarily blinding them. They staggered around trying to escape the downpour, and Blight accidentally ran into the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> shortly before 2am, stopping his heart. He placed 16th out of 24.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlight%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Blight's name comes from a type of sickness that affects plants such as trees. The word "blight" is also used more generally to refer to something that spoils or destroys other things.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBlight%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In a behind the scenes featurette, a cameraman asked <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a>'s actress <a href="/wiki/Stephanie_Leigh_Schlund" title="Stephanie Leigh Schlund">Stephanie Leigh Schlund</a> if she had killed anybody yet in the bloodbath. She replied, "I have. I killed Bobby," referring to Blight's actor <a href="/wiki/Bobby_Jordan" title="Bobby Jordan">Bobby Jordan</a>. However, the footage of their fight was ultimately cut since Blight was meant to be killed by the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-bts_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bts-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Blight is the last District 7 tribute to die in the Hunger Games.</li></ul>
