<p>This <b>silver compact</b> belonged to <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow&#39;s mother">Coriolanus Snow's mother</a> and was kept by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> after her death.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilver_compact%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mrs. Snow was often seen pulling this circular compact from her handbag to check and powder her face.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Because of this, she always smelled of roses.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After her death, her son Coriolanus kept the compact in a drawer in his nightstand and often took it out when he was going through a difficult time.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> One instance of this was when he was returning from the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>, having visited <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, his assigned <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the 10th Hunger Games began, Coriolanus took out his mother's compact and offered it to Lucy Gray, saying that he would only loan it to her and expect that it would be returned. He had taken out the powder earlier and indirectly suggested that Lucy Gray use the compact for storing poison, which she would use to eliminate the other tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the arena, the compact assisted Lucy Gray in poisoning <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a> and <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a> with the rat poison she had smuggled from around the zoo, and both tributes were killed. While the Games went on, Lucy Gray claimed that she had clutched the compact so hard that the rose imprinted on her palm.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>However, after she emerged, the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> wrestled her to the ground to obtain the compact. Both Coriolanus and Lucy Gray claimed that he had given it to her as an affectionate token, and that Lucy Gray had been the one to come up with the idea of using poison. However, Dean <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> didn't believe them, and the compact was one of the pieces of evidence that he used to show that Coriolanus had cheated in the Games and was to become a Peacekeeper as punishment.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On what would have been <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>'s nineteenth birthday, Coriolanus headed to the dean's office and requested that he be returned his mother's compact, pocketing it before leaving.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilver_compact%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The compact was silver, containing a cake of rose-scented powder,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and it was decorated with an engraved rose.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, the compact was octagonal in shape, and it had a swirling pattern engraved onto its lid.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
