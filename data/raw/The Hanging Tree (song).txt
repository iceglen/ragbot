</p><p>"<b>The Hanging Tree</b>" is a song first introduced in <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> that carried thematic significance throughout the novel. The song also featured prominently in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, which provided an origin story for the song's creation. In real life, its lyrics were written by <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a>, produced by <a href="/wiki/James_Newton_Howard" title="James Newton Howard">James Newton Howard</a>, with music by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremiah_Fraites" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jeremiah Fraites">Jeremiah Fraites</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Schultz" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Wesley Schultz">Wesley Schultz</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hanging_Tree_%28song%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i>Mockingjay</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hanging_Tree_%28song%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> learned the song from <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">her father</a> when she was young. <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> heard Katniss singing it one day while she and her sister <a href="/wiki/Prim" class="mw-redirect" title="Prim">Prim</a> made necklaces of rope, like the lyrics mentioned. Although the girls did not understand the true meaning of the song, Mrs. Everdeen became fearful because of its morbid and rebellious bent. She yelled at Mr. Everdeen, which drove Katniss into <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a> to cry under a tree. After finding her, Mr. Everdeen told Katniss to forget the song, but Katniss ended up remembering every word of it. After Mr. Everdeen's death, the song played itself over and over in Katniss's head.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss eventually understood that the person singing the song was a dead man calling for his lover to come join him in death. The thought at first seemed disturbing to her, but when she ended up spontaneously performing the song for <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a>, she remembered how after her rescue from the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>, she was about to kill <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> with a syringe to keep him safe from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>; she decided that a life of torture was a far worse fate than death.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While recovering from his <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a>, Peeta was shown a recording of Katniss singing "The Hanging Tree". The clip had never aired, and had thus not been used by the Capitol in his torture, so the doctors in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> were able to use it as part of his deprogramming. Peeta said that when he was six or seven, he heard Mr. Everdeen singing the same song once when he came to trade at the <a href="/wiki/Bakery" title="Bakery">bakery</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hanging_Tree_%28song%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<p>An official version of the song was recorded for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a>.</i> In this film, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> claimed he changed the words "necklace of rope" to "necklace of hope" in the recording for the Capitol. After airing a <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> in which Katniss sang this edited version of "The Hanging Tree", <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> took it up as a protest song as they marched towards a hydroelectric dam. They then launched a human wave attack against a small group of armed <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a>, and they planted crates of explosives inside, destroying the dam and causing a massive power outage in the Capitol. This weakened their ability to broadcast propaganda, allowing rebel forces to carry out a <a href="/wiki/Rescue_mission" class="mw-redirect" title="Rescue mission">rescue mission</a> freeing the remaining <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> who were held captive.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hanging_Tree_%28song%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 209px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0c/Lucy_Gray_Hanging_Tree.png/revision/latest?cb=20231229081915" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Lucy Gray sing Hanging Tree" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0c/Lucy_Gray_Hanging_Tree.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/209?cb=20231229081915" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="209" height="158" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Lucy Gray Hanging Tree.png" data-image-key="Lucy_Gray_Hanging_Tree.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Lucy_Gray_Hanging_Tree.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Lucy Gray sing Hanging Tree</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>"The Hanging Tree" was one of many songs composed by <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, recounting the <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a> of <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a> at the real <a href="/wiki/Hanging_tree" title="Hanging tree">hanging tree</a>. Arlo was said to have been responsible for an <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_the_District_12_mines" title="Bombings">explosion</a> in the mines that killed three people. When he was taken to the gallows, his lover <a href="/wiki/Lil" title="Lil">Lil</a> came forward to protest, and he yelled at her to run.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After he was hanged, <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a> continued to mimic his exchange with Lil.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> came to see Lucy Gray in the Meadow, she was beginning to compose "The Hanging Tree".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She later sang it in full at <a href="/wiki/Commander_Hoff" title="Commander Hoff">Commander Hoff</a>'s birthday party, as a coded message to Snow about where they should meet the next day to run away from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Commander Hoff asked Lucy Gray not to sing it anymore, as he considered it "too dark". However, Lucy Gray believed the real reason was that it was too rebellious.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Soon after, Hoff was replaced by a new commander, who banned all musical performances at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> on the grounds that music caused trouble.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i>Sunrise on the Reaping</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hanging_Tree_%28song%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> sometimes played this song for <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> on her tune box. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> recalled it when returning home to District 12 after the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, contemplating the significance of the song.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, when Lenore Dove was arrested for playing forbidden songs in front of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>, Haymitch assumed one of them had been "The Hanging Tree."<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Lyrics">Lyrics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hanging_Tree_%28song%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="poem">
<p>Are you, are you<br />
Coming to the tree<br />
Where they strung up a man they say murdered three?<br />
Strange things did happen here<br />
No stranger would it be<br />
If we met up at midnight in the hanging tree.<br />
<br />
Are you, are you<br />
Coming to the tree<br />
Where the dead man called out for his love to flee?<br />
Strange things did happen here<br />
No stranger would it be<br />
If we met up at midnight in the hanging tree.<br />
<br />
Are you, are you<br />
Coming to the tree<br />
Where I told you to run, so we'd both be free?<br />
Strange things did happen here<br />
No stranger would it be<br />
If we met up at midnight in the hanging tree.<br />
<br />
Are you, are you<br />
Coming to the tree<br />
Wear a necklace of rope, side by side with me.<br />
Strange things did happen here<br />
No stranger would it be<br />
If we met up at midnight in the hanging tree.
</p>

<h3><span id="The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_.26_Snakes"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_&amp;_Snakes"><i>The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hanging_Tree_%28song%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On October 20th, 2023, <a href="/wiki/Rachel_Zegler" title="Rachel Zegler">Rachel Zegler</a>'s rendition of "The Hanging Tree" was released as the first official single for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>. It also featured on the album <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes_(Music_From_%26_Inspired_By)" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)</a></i>, released on November 17th, 2023.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hanging_Tree_%28song%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>On the last night of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss and Peeta were split up, and they were supposed to meet up at the tree at midnight,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_26_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_26-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> like the lyrics in this song.</li>
<li>When Peeta and the other victors were rescued from the Capitol, Katniss saw him in person for the first time since the Games at midnight.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She also visited him at midnight on the night of <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie&#39;s wedding">Finnick and Annie's wedding</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_16_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_16-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Although the song was written about Arlo Chance, Coriolanus Snow speculated that it could have been about <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a>, Lucy Gray's ex-lover, because the hanging tree had been their private meet-up spot, and Billy Taupe had intended to leave the district with her before he was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, Snow thought she was singing "The Hanging Tree" at him in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> to taunt him about the three people he killed: <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mayfair_Lipp" title="Mayfair Lipp">Mayfair Lipp</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>"The Hanging Tree" was only performed once before it was banned.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Because of this, the only people who could have passed down knowledge of the song were the Peacekeepers attending the party, and the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>, who performed that night alongside Lucy Gray. As a result, many fans speculate that Katniss and her father could be descended from Lucy Gray or another member of the Covey— most commonly through <a href="/wiki/Maude_Ivory_Baird" title="Maude Ivory Baird">Maude Ivory Baird</a>, Lucy Gray's younger cousin. Maude Ivory shared Katniss's ability to memorize music after a listen or two.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
