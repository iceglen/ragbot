<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStamp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStamp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>, the chariot carrying District 10 narrowly missed colliding with <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Before the start of the games, Stamp joined the <a href="/wiki/Newcomers" class="mw-redirect" title="Newcomers">Newcomers</a>, an alliance between the tributes of Districts <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">7</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a> in an attempt to counter the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> pack.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the games, Stamp died on the fourth day, likely due to the volcanic eruption on the mountain he and the other Newcomers had been staying on.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStamp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

</p>
