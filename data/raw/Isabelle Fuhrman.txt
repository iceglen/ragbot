</p><p><b>Isabelle Fuhrman</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She is the daughter of journalist <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elina_Fuhrman" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Elina Fuhrman">Elina Fuhrman</a> and Nick Fuhrman. Also, she is the sister of Madeline Fuhrman.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FIsabelle_Fuhrman%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Fuhrman had originally auditioned for the role of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, but she was told that she was too young, as she was only 14. She was also told she was too old to portray <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> and got a callback asking if she wanted to audition for Clove instead. Many critics have praised her performance as Clove, identifying her as a breakout star.
</p><p>Fuhrman began acting in 2006 and is best known for her role in the film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orphan_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Orphan (film)">Orphan</a></i>, for which she received critical acclaim. She appears often in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Official_Illustrated_Movie_Companion" title="The Hunger Games: The Official Illustrated Movie Companion">The Hunger Games: The Official Illustrated Movie Companion</a></i>.&#160;
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 220px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bb/Clovetalksred.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20160219201754" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Fuhrman talks about being  in " src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bb/Clovetalksred.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20160219201754" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="165" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Clovetalksred.jpg" data-image-key="Clovetalksred.jpg" data-relevant="0" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Clovetalksred.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Fuhrman talks about being <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Fuhrman co-starred with Will Smith and Jaden Smith in the film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/After_Earth" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:After Earth">After Earth</a></i>. Fuhrman's role, however, didn't make the final cut except for a small snippet of her next to Jaden Smith in the beginning. It was announced on the 15th of May 2012 that she would be starring in a remake of the 1977 horror classic, <i>Suspiria</i>. However, the movie got stuck in legal issues, preventing them from filming and producing the movie. She is in the film adaptation of Stephen King's novel,&#160;<i>Cell</i>, a science fiction/action movie, as Alice Maxwell. Fuhrman co-starred with Jessica Alba and Liana Liberato in the drama film <i>Dear Eleanor</i>, playing a character named Max. &#160;
</p><p>Fuhrman has also had experience on voice acting. She voiced Shoko in the hit Cartoon Network Series, <i>Adventure Time</i>, Shelly in&#160;<i>Sammy's Adventure: The Secret Passage</i>, and the video game, <i>Hitman: Absolution</i> as Victoria.
</p>
