<p><b>Juno Phipps</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She was assigned the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJuno_Phipps%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the interviews before the 10th Hunger Games began, Juno admitted that she'd been disappointed when she was assigned Bobbin, a small District 8 boy. However, she stated that he had won her over after describing multiple ways he could kill somebody with a sewing needle.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Unbeknownst to her, Bobbin was killed by her fellow mentor, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, late in Day 1 of the Games in self-defense after Phipps had left for the night.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Bobbin finished in 13th place of the 24 tributes, which would have been considered successful as a mentor had 9 tributes not died in the days leading up to the start of the actual Games. This meant that Phipps only successfully mentored her tribute past two others, <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">one</a> of which had been tortured and strung up in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> prior.
</p><p>Since Phipps' tribute was not actually seen to be killed, she was shocked the next morning when <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a> announced his death with a closeup of his body. No cause of death was given and his memoriam glossed over in lieu of the celebration of the Games being halfway through. Phipps was visibly angry and suspected foul play, remarking that the scene looked fishy. She then demanded to review the tape, but there was not one to be seen, as the events of his death happened at night with the only lighting being the moon and the cameras had been manipulated even darker to obscure <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>'s rebellious actions in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After being questioned what exactly counted as foul play during the Hunger Games, she was directed back to her seat with a consoling pat on the back.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJuno_Phipps%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Juno Phipps was snooty in nature and expressed disappointment, both in getting a tiny boy as a tribute and in getting someone from District 8. She felt that being from the Phipps family, a founding member of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, she was entitled to a better assignment.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After Bobbin's death, when demanding answers on the circumstances, she acted pompously by repetitively waving a leather binder with a Phipps family seal, thinking she was entitled to special treatment. This shows that she felt superior, even among other students with elite family names. She was labeled a sore loser after her poor behavior in exiting the competition.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJuno_Phipps%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_(mythology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Juno (mythology)">Juno</a> was the Roman goddess of marriage and childbirth, as well as the wife of Jupiter, the chief god of the Roman pantheon. Her surname, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phipps_(surname)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Phipps (surname)">Phipps</a>, comes from the given name <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Philip">Philip</a>, meaning "horse-loving".
</p>
