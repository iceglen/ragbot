</p><p><b>Stanley Tucci</b> is an Italian-American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FStanley_Tucci%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He is best known for his roles in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easy_A" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Easy A">Easy A</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burlesque_(2010_American_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Burlesque (2010 American film)">Burlesque</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lovely_Bones_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Lovely Bones (film)">The Lovely Bones</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Devil_Wears_Prada_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Devil Wears Prada (film)">The Devil Wears Prada</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_and_Julia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Julie and Julia">Julie and Julia</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percy_Jackson:_Sea_of_Monsters" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters">Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters</a></i>.
</p><p>Tucci was nominated for an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_Best_Supporting_Actor" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Academy Award Best Supporting Actor">Academy Award Best Supporting Actor</a> for the movie <i>Lovely Bones</i>. He won an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Award" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Emmy Award">Emmy</a> and a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Miniseries_or_Television_Film" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Golden Globe for Best Actor – Miniseries or Television Film">Golden Globe</a> for his performance in the movie <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winchell_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Winchell (film)">Winchell</a></i>. He was also nominated for a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Grammy Award">Grammy</a> for  Best Spoken Word Album for Children for <i>The One and Only Shrek.</i>
</p><p>He also took part in the 2014 film <i>Your Voice In My Head</i> with Emma Watson.
</p>
