</p><p><b>Tara Macken</b> is a Filipino-Irish actress who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_4_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 female (74th HG)">District 4 female</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> and doubled <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">sequel</a>. <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Unlike in the books, the District 4 female is also shown to fall during the initial bloodbath. She is thus excluded from the 74th Hunger Games Career pack.
</p><p>Macken has a small part in the web series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Legion_of_Extraordinary_Dancers" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Legion of Extraordinary Dancers">The Legion of Extraordinary Dancers</a></i> also known as LXD, along with fellow <i>Hunger Games</i> actor <a href="/wiki/Jeremy_Marinas" title="Jeremy Marinas">Jeremy Marinas</a>. 
</p>
