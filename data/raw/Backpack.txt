<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>A <b>backpack</b>, in its simplest form, is a cloth sack carried on a person's back and secured with two straps that go over the shoulders.
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">the Hunger Games</a>, backpacks were typically found at the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> at the start of the competition. <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">Tributes</a> used them to carry and store provisions, tools, and other items needed to survive in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBackpack%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBackpack%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was not mesmerized by the beauty of the arena and was able to secure <a href="/wiki/Weapon" class="mw-redirect" title="Weapon">weapons</a> and a backpack of "choice supplies" like food and water, along with other survival supplies, before most of the tributes had even stepped off their plates. <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> obtained a backpack containing a <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Blowgun" title="Weapons">blowgun</a> with 24 darts, a bowl, and some dried beef. It is unknown how she obtained this, but it is assumed she got it in the bloodbath.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Also included in Haymitch's backpack was <a href="/wiki/Medical_treatments#Charcoal_tablets" title="Medical treatments">charcoal tablets</a> to treat the arena's poisons<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a>'s backpack<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the backpack of either <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Barba" title="Barba">Barba</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Angler" title="Angler">Angler</a> contained a bottle of an actual <a href="/wiki/Medical_treatments#Poison_antidote" title="Medical treatments">antidote</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After killing <a href="/wiki/Loupe" title="Loupe">Loupe</a>, Maysilee took a first aid kit that she found amongst his supplies, possibly from his backpack.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After killing Panache, Barba and Angler, Haymitch and Maysilee found and took their backpacks.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The two also took the backpacks of <a href="/wiki/Buck" title="Buck">Buck</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chicory" title="Chicory">Chicory</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a> when they were killed by a porcupine <a href="/wiki/Mutt" class="mw-redirect" title="Mutt">mutt</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBackpack%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 252px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/Hunger-games-trailer-backpack-a-600x251.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130604131827" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss retrieving a backpack." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/Hunger-games-trailer-backpack-a-600x251.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/252?cb=20130604131827" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="252" height="105" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Hunger-games-trailer-backpack-a-600x251.jpg" data-image-key="Hunger-games-trailer-backpack-a-600x251.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Hunger-games-trailer-backpack-a-600x251.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss retrieving a backpack.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> obtained a backpack at the Cornucopia during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, which she had to fight the <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">boy from District 9</a> over. However, he was killed during the struggle by <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>. Clove then threw a <a href="/wiki/Knife" class="mw-redirect" title="Knife">knife</a> at Katniss, but she was able to block it with the backpack and flee, gaining a knife as her first weapon since her backpack did not contain one. Nonetheless, it was essential to her survival. It contained one thin black sleeping bag that reflects body heat, a pack of crackers, a pack of dried beef strips, a bottle of iodine, a box of wooden matches, a bit of coiled wire, night vision glasses, and an empty black water bottle. A drawback to this backpack was that it was a bright orange and could be easily seen. Katniss realized this and used mud to camouflage it. Due to the limited amount of provisions in the pack, Katniss had to do some hunting right after the bloodbath.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She also was not aware of the night vision glasses' function, mistaking it for a pair of sunglasses that cannot block out the rays of daytime, until <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> explained to her their purpose.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Rue's backpack at the time of her death contained her <a href="/wiki/Slingshot" class="mw-redirect" title="Slingshot">slingshot</a>, an extra pair of socks, a water skin, iodine, some nuts and roots, a bit of rabbit, and Katniss' sleeping bag which she had given to Rue as a contingency. Katniss took <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>'s backpack after she killed him and found that it contained several knives, two extra spearheads, a flashlight, a small leather pouch, a first aid kit, a bottle of water, and a pack of dried fruit.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/98/Cornucopia1.png/revision/latest?cb=20120424211206" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Cornucopia during the feast: with backpacks displayed in front of it on a table." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/98/Cornucopia1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120424211206" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="130" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Cornucopia1.png" data-image-key="Cornucopia1.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Cornucopia1.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Cornucopia during the feast: with backpacks displayed in front of it on a table.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>At the <a href="/wiki/Feasts" class="mw-redirect" title="Feasts">feast</a>, there were four backpacks laid out on a table near the Cornucopia. Each was marked with the respective <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> number and contained what the remaining tributes needed most. There was one each for Districts <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">5</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>, although there were  six surviving tributes at the time, with two each from Districts 2 and 12. The District 12 backpack was small compared to the others as it only contained the medicine needed to help <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a>'s leg infection. The District 2 backpack is believed to have contained <a href="/wiki/Tools_and_gear#Body_armor" title="Tools and gear">body armor</a> which was later used by <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>. It is unknown what the other backpacks contained, although Katniss assumed that <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a>'s had food. In the film, however, every district's backpack is the same size, and the only difference was the district number. Foxface was the first to obtain her backpack by camping in the Cornucopia then darting out and grabbing it. When Katniss tried to obtain hers next, she was attacked by Clove a second time. After <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> intervened and killed Clove, he took his own backpack and District 2's to draw Cato away from Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Backpacks were not present in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> due to the Cornucopia only containing weapons, making the 74th the final Hunger Games in which they appeared.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

</p>

<li>Weapons</li>
