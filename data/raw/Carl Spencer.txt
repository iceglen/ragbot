</p><p><b>Carl Spencer</b> is an actor known for <i>Rocketman</i> (2019), and <i>Matilda</i> (2022), and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, in which he portrayed <a href="/wiki/Smiley" title="Smiley">Smiley</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCarl_Spencer%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was cast as Richard in <i>Rocketman</i> (2019) and Magnus the Escapologist in <i>Matilda</i> (2022). On September 16, 2022, he was announced to be cast as Smiley in <i>The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i> by a <i>Deadline</i> article and his Instagram account.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He stars in the music video for Shan Ako's <i>God's Best</i> and plays Phoenix in <i>Doctors</i> (2019).<i><sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></i>
</p>
