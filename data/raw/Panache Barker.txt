<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPanache_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPanache_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Prior to the Hunger Games themselves, Panache sucker-punched <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> in the rib cage. Before Haymitch could do anything to retaliate, Panache was tased by a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a> and then three more pulled him away.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR7_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Panache's podium was two podiums to the right of Haymitch's.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Panache was part of the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> pack in these Games. During the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>, Panache killed <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> as he protected <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a>, then killed five other <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">Newcomers</a> using a sword that he had claimed.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was also able to acquire a shield to go with his sword.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR19_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR19-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the fourth day, Panache was caught in the eruption of the arena's artificial volcano, but he survived. Panache was left with burns along his arms and legs, cracked lips, and a mad-dog look in his eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR19_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR19-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPanache_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Panache, <a href="/wiki/Barba" title="Barba">Barba</a> and <a href="/wiki/Angler" title="Angler">Angler</a> attacked Haymitch Abernathy following the volcano eruption. Haymitch quickly dispatched the other two Careers before facing off against Panache, still armed with a sword, who was able to effortlessly disarm Haymitch. Haymitch tried and failed to talk Panache down, but he was saved when <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> shot Panache in the throat with a poison dart, killing him. Haymitch chose not to take Panache's sword for himself as it was stained with <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">Newcomer</a> blood which creeped Haymitch out. Haymitch wasn't Panache's heir, the new leader of the pack, and he refused to present himself as such.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR19_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR19-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPanache_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Panache weighed about 300 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR6_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR6-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was left-handed.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR4_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was the physically largest tribute in the 50th Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the training center, he and his district partners wore "snot-green."<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPanache_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Panache was arrogant and hot-headed. He enjoyed taunting the other tributes and proved to be a brutal fighter. Despite his brutality he constantly displayed himself as a vain idiot with many openly mocking his intelligence. Panache also showed a bloodthirsty side where he refused to listen to reason when attempting to kill Haymitch which ultimately led to his death.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPanache_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panache" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Panache">Panache</a> is a French word that implies flamboyancy and reckless courage, fitting with the District 1 tradition of giving their children luxurious or lofty-sounding names and his vain personality. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barker_(surname)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Barker (surname)">Barker</a> is an English surname meaning "a leather tanner."<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPanache_Barker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> practiced evaluating Panache's odds with <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> According to Wyatt:

<li>He scored high with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>.</li>
<li>He had odds of eleven-to-one.</li>
<li>He had a 31.25% chance of winning the Games.</li></ul></li>
<li>Panache is the only tribute with more than one confirmed bloodbath kill in the books. He killed 6 tributes, including <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>.</li></ul>
