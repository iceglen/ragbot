<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationship_with_the_Hunger_Games">Relationship with the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Due to District 8's urban environment, its people knew very little of the natural world, putting its <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> at a serious disadvantage in outdoor <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arenas</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath_of_the_74th_Hunger_Games">Aftermath of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, the people of Districts <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, and 8 expressed "genuine elation" at the sight of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, "and under the elation, fury," crying out for vengeance.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Uprising_of_District_8">Uprising of District 8</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">Uprising of District 8</a></i></center></div>
<p>District 8 was the first known district to carry out a full-scale uprising against <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> since the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. The plan was enacted the night of Katniss and Peeta's televised proposal in the Capitol, but despite its initial success, the movement was put down within forty-eight hours. After a weeklong lockdown, regular work operations resumed, and the Capitol bombed the factory where plans for the uprising began.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h6><span class="mw-headline" id="Refugees">Refugees</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h6>
<p>A District 8 schoolteacher, <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>, had been planning to escape the district with her husband in Peacekeepers' uniforms she took from the factory where she worked. She and one of her students, <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a>, were late coming to the factory one day after school, and they narrowly avoided being caught in the factory bombing. With their families dead, and being presumed dead themselves, they escaped in the Peacekeepers' uniforms with a stolen rifle. They eventually made it to the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> on their way to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. After Katniss stumbled upon the two of them at the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a>, she shared a meal with them, and they told her what had transpired.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, the tributes from Districts 3, 4, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">7</a>, 8, and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> were all involved in a rebel plan to protect Katniss and break out of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 8's territory corresponds to a long, narrow region in the northern part of southeast North America. Its territory is primarily centered on the U.S. states of Kansas, Missouri, southern Illinois, and Tennessee. District 8 borders five other districts: District 12 (to the east and northeast), District 11 (to the south), <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> (to the west), <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> (to the northwest), and <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> (to the north).
</p><p>
District 8 is described in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> as the second district from the east, between Districts 12 and 6.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> describes District 8 as "an urban place stinking of industrial fumes, the people housed in run-down tenements. Barely a blade of grass in sight."<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/District8-victory-tour.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140124055153" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark in District 8." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="District8-victory-tour.jpg" data-image-key="District8-victory-tour.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/District8-victory-tour.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140124055153" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/District8-victory-tour.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140124055153" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark in District 8." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/District8-victory-tour.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140124055153" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District8-victory-tour.jpg" data-image-key="District8-victory-tour.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1b/District8-victory-tour.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140124055153" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District8-victory-tour.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark in District 8.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_8%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>According to <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, as well as a pre-production sketch from the illustrated movie companion book, District 8 has at least six functional textile factories. Other features include a river traversed by a bridge, and a clock tower extending into the sky.
<ul><li>The citizens are seen to be clothed in a palette of vibrant and brighter colors than other districts, considering that District 8's primary focus is that of textile production. Their physical attire resembles that of an urban American 1960s get-up.</li>
<li>It was also located near a shore in the film.</li>
<li>During Katniss and Peeta's stop in 8 on the Victory Tour, the tributes' jackets on the holograms are maroon red, but in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">previous film</a>, they wore golden jackets.</li></ul></li>
<li>The first official rebel <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> was shot in District 8.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>According to the <i>Mockingjay</i> film, bombers from the north were being deployed from the southern border of District 11, during the time Katniss was visiting District 8 to film the first prop. This suggests that District 8 may actually be located around the deep south, specifically south of present-day Atlanta, Georgia, where District 11 is believed to be situated.
<ul><li>The map from <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a> shows that District 8 is located in the southeast of Panem, between District 12 (to the north) and District 11 (to the south).</li></ul></li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 8 has 43 factories in operation with a population of 122,134,&#160;making it the sixth largest district, in terms of population.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 8 claims 69,892 in tesserae, making it the fifth most impoverished district.&#160;
<ul><li>If the population is 122,134 and they claim 69,892 tesserae, that would equal to 0.572 tesserae per person, making it the 7th richest district.</li></ul></li>
<li>The inspiration for District 8 in the films came from the mill town of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence,_Massachusetts" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lawrence, Massachusetts">Lawrence, Massachusetts</a>, which was well cemented in the textile industry at the turn of the 20th century.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>It is considered a lower-middle class district.</li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be dark buff leather brown (#835c27 – <span style="background-color:#835c27; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 8's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be yellow green (#9ACD32 - <span style="background-color:#9ACD32; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear for the 50th Hunger Games was peach (FFE5B4 - <span style="background-color:#FFE5B4; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
