</p><p><b>Alys</b> was a student at the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> who appeared in the officially licensed <a href="/wiki/Academy_Adventures" title="Academy Adventures">Academy Adventures</a> <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roblox" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Roblox">Roblox</a> game, as part of promotion for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAlys%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Alys ripped her graduation robe and asked <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> to fix it at the last minute. The player's goal in the game's fourth quarter was to gather Tigris's sewing supplies from around the campus, then escort her to Alys and help her mend the gown just before the ceremony.
</p>
