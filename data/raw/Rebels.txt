<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>The term "<b>rebels</b>" refers to individuals who challenge the authority of the Capitol, especially through armed insurrection. This term is used frequently throughout the trilogy by various individuals, including President Snow, Capitol officials, and the rebels themselves. The <b>rebel army</b> was composed of militia fighters from Districts 1-12 as well as the better-trained and equipped Armed Forces of <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/18/Hunger_Games_Rebels.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20230921012321" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A crowd of rebel soldiers assembled outside the Capitol just before the invasion of the city" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/18/Hunger_Games_Rebels.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230921012321" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="77" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Hunger Games Rebels.jpg" data-image-key="Hunger_Games_Rebels.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Hunger_Games_Rebels.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A crowd of rebel soldiers assembled outside the Capitol just before the invasion of the city. </p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>While there are underground rebel networks in Districts 1-12 in addition to the Capitol, the rebel war effort is headquartered in District 13, which served as the launching pad for the failed rebellion of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> and in some ways, the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>. During both wars, District 13 was responsible for supplying weapons, communications, and military support to rebel forces in the other twelve districts and played an instrumental role in both uprisings. Following the victorious Second Rebellion, the rebels eventually take power and form a constitutional republic under the leadership of <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Commander Paylor</a> and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Weapons">Weapons</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRebels%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The rebels are shown to be armed with different types of weapons including explosives and firearms. In the film series, most of their firearms come from the era of the Second World War, with models such as the M1 Carbine and MG 42 in use. The books never describe in detail what exact weapons they use, although they are mentioned to have used machine guns and other weapons available in the current era of the 21st Century. In the absence of advanced weapons (firearms and ammunition), the rebels are shown to be using bricks and other melee weapons when combating Peacekeepers.  
</p><p>In the film, District 13 soldiers are shown to be armed with the Heckler and Koch G36 rifle as their standard issue rifle.  
</p><p>Having secured its own political independence after the Dark Days, District 13 had a military arsenal of its own consisting of firearms, ammunition, hovercrafts, bombs, and nuclear arms. During the 75 years between wars, it continued building upon its armed forces and manufacturing new types of weaponry.  
</p><p>Most notable throughout the film series is the lack of American weaponry in rebel hands. Common American models such as the AR-15/M-16 series are surprisingly absent, even though such firearms are extremely common throughout Canada, the United States, and Mexico in the hands of military, law enforcement, and private citizens.   
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Prelude_to_the_1st_Civil_War">The Prelude to the 1st Civil War</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRebels%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The exact cause of the Dark Days is unknown, although it can be assumed that it was the result of years, if not decades of living under brutal oppression at the hands of the Capitol. For most of Panem's known history, the ruling government was a totalitarian dictatorship, a police state which subjected all thirteen districts to a centrally planned economy and a system which severely curtailed civil liberties. The lack of freedom, coupled with the brutal oppression and abject poverty citizens endured, was a major instigator of the Dark Days. 
</p><p>District 13 is mentioned to have started the rebellion. Being the headquarters of Panem's Military Industrial Complex, and possessing nuclear weapons, this made the district a considerable threat to the Capitol's power, and was presumably the first to have rebelled and wrestled control from the Capitol. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Dark_Days">Dark Days</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRebels%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a></i></center></div>
<p>The Dark Days lasted three years. Most of the details of the war are not available, although it is mentioned that the rebels, with support from District 13 and its military arsenal, were able to fight on par with the Capitol's forces for a period of time and pose a significant threat to the Capitol's power. They were able to overtake the bomb alarm system in District 5, which allowed them to send false alarms to the Capitol, allowing them to deal more damage during air raids. 
</p><p>Much of the war was fought on the ground and in the air, marked by indiscriminate surface-based and aerial artillery bombardments, as well as a confused mixture of asymmetrical and conventional ground warfare that resulted in widespread destruction of infrastructure and loss of life throughout the country. In the final two years of the war, the rebels decidedly had the upper hand, having successfully cut off the Capitol from outside support. This led to famine in the Capitol as well as abject poverty, with citizens lacking access to basic goods such as food, heating, and medicine. 
</p><p>At some point, the rebels attempted to end the siege by invading the Capitol through scaling the Rocky Mountains which surrounded the city, serving as a natural barrier and a source of protection. This made the invading force an easy target for the Capitol; the Capitol's air force was able to spot them and kill off large numbers of them, forcing them to retreat. The massive loss of manpower and equipment presumably had a devastating impact on the rebel war effort and was likely the catalyst for the war turning in favor of the Capitol. 
</p><p>Realizing that a complete rebel victory was no longer possible, District 13 trained its arsenal of nuclear missiles on the Capitol, knowing that the Capitol had another arsenal out west. Threatening a Mutually Assured Destruction, District 13 was able to secure its own political independence, on the condition that they withdraw themselves from the war and move into a large underground shelter. Following this, the Capitol's air force annihilated all of District 13's above-ground infrastructure, presenting to other districts the illusion that the district had been annihilated. 
</p><p>With the loss of District 13's military support, the rebellion quickly collapsed as the remaining rebel forces could not continue resisting against the Capitol. The Capitol was able to quickly overrun any remaining resistance in the other twelve districts and reestablish its iron grip on the nation.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRebels%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Following the defeat of District 1-12, the Capitol put forth the Treaty of Treason, which set forth several new restrictions on the districts, including the following.  
</p>
<ul><li>All travel between districts and to the Capitol was prohibited with narrow exemptions.</li>
<li>Prohibition on the ownership and production of weapons, except for those needed for industrial use such as tridents and axes.</li>
<li>The annual Hunger Games, in which each district must offer up a male and female youth between the ages of 12-18 years to fight to death for the purposes of entertainment and intimidation.</li></ul>
<p>Mass executions of rebel leaders, soldiers, and dissidents presumably took place in all twelve districts. For the next 75 years, Districts 1-12 continued to be subject to the oppressive rule of the Capitol. Most rebel activity was thoroughly rooted out by the Peacekeeping Force in the first two decades that followed the Dark Days.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRebels%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, a group of rebels and rebel sympathizers plotted to destroy the arena's "brain" and disrupt the Games. Key members of the plot were <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, while District 12 tribute <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and Beetee's son <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> were to carry out the plan using <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">sunflower bombs</a> disguised as the <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">Tribute tokens</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> tributes who were not in on the plan. Beetee had help from rebel sympathizers within the Capitol with smuggling the bomb components into the arena, most likely Gamemakers and Peacekeepers. <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> also claimed to also be a part of the rebellion and gave Haymitch valuable advice on what to do in the arena.
</p><p>Haymitch and Ampert succeeded in <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_the_50th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="Bombings">blowing up the arena's water tank</a> and drowning the brain, causing the arena to malfunction and damaging it. However, the plan failed to stop the Games and the Gamemakers had Ampert killed by <a href="/wiki/Mutt" class="mw-redirect" title="Mutt">mutts</a> in retaliation. After winning the Games, Haymitch managed to throw a second bomb at the arena's generator with the reaction from the Gamemakers suggesting that he was successful in destroying it.
</p><p>Afterwards, Snow had Mags and Wiress tortured for information on everyone who was involved in the rebel plot, although he spared Beetee's life as he was too valuable to kill. While Snow chose not to kill Haymitch, he had Haymitch's family killed and poisoned Haymitch's <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">girlfriend</a> as punishment, making it clear that he would never be allowed to love anyone again without them being killed. Undeterred by the failure, Plutarch told Haymitch that he had succeeded in shaking up the Capitol both literally and figuratively and that they needed to find someone like Haymitch but who was luckier or better with timing and preferably had an army at their back. Plutarch discussed finding or building that army, but Haymitch wanted nothing more to do with the rebellion after the losses that he had suffered.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Start_of_the_2nd_Rebellion">Start of the 2nd Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRebels%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The controversial victory of Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark in the 74th Hunger Games led to unrest in the districts once more. During the 74th Games, District 11 <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_11" title="Uprising of District 11">revolted</a> following the death of Rue, and in the months that followed, several other districts started rebelling. The Capitol responded harshly to the uprisings, executing instigators and dissidents. 
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Rebels.png/revision/latest?cb=20230921014639" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Rebels fighting Peacekeepers during the Uprising of District 8" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/0e/Rebels.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230921014639" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Rebels.png" data-image-key="Rebels.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Rebels.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Rebels fighting Peacekeepers during the Uprising of District 8. </p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure> 
<p>In an attempt to quell the uprising, President Coriolanus Snow announced a series of changes for the Third Quarter Quell. The tributes were to be reaped from the existing pool of victors, an unprecedented rule which broke the tradition in which former victors were not allowed to compete in the Games. The 75th Hunger Games ended without a victor; three days into the event, Katniss Everdeen destroyed the arena through shooting an arrow at the forcefield, causing blackouts. Following this, uprisings broke out in numerous districts, before three tributes (Beetee Latier, Finnick Odair, and Katniss Everdeen) were rescued from the arena and taken to District 13 in a hovercraft belonging to the rebels. 
</p><p>This marked the start of the Second Rebellion. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_War">The War</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRebels%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Capitol responded to the 75th Hunger Games through a firebombing raid on District 12, completely destroying the district, leaving only 900 survivors, all of whom fled into the woods before being rescued by District 13. 
</p><p>Tactical, operational, and strategic details of the war from this point until the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a> are mostly unavailable. Fighting over the next several months was largely marked by indiscriminate surface-based and aerial artillery bombardments, as well as a confused mixture of asymmetrical and conventional ground warfare that resulted in widespread destruction of infrastructure and loss of life throughout the country. After months of warfare, the rebels were able to take over all twelve districts and the Capitol itself. 
</p><p>Mass arrests of Capitol officials, Peacekeepers, Gamemakers, and cabinet members followed, with large numbers of them being sentenced to death, including President Coriolanus Snow himself. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath_2">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRebels%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>President Alma Coin of District 13 served as the interim President of Panem in the weeks following the rebel victory. Several weeks passed before President Coriolanus Snow was scheduled to be publicly executed in the Capitol by Katniss Everdeen in front of a large number of citizens. On the day of his execution, President Coin held a private meeting with the seven remaining Hunger Games victors, where she proposed a final <a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">76th Hunger Games</a> with the children of high-ranking Capitol officials. A 4-3 majority voted in favor of the Games. 
</p><p>During what was supposed to be Snow's execution, Katniss Everdeen made the last-minute decision to assassinate President Coin after realizing her role in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a> bombing and Snow pointing out how Coin had just used the rebels to seize power for herself. A riot ensued, in which mass numbers of citizens lynched President Snow. A presidential election was soon called, and Commander Paylor (rebel leader from District 8) became the first democratically elected president of Panem. In the weeks that followed, the rebels set up a constitutional republic consisting of presumably a legislative and judicial branch, alongside the presidency.   
</p>
