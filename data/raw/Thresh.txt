				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">How does Thresh die in the Hunger Games?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						In The Hunger Games, Thresh's demise varies between the book and film. The book implies that Cato, a fellow tribute, is responsible for his death. Conversely, the film portrays Thresh being killed by wolf mutts. Prior to his death, Thresh saves protagonist Katniss Everdeen from another tribute, Clove, and advises her to flee from Cato, an act that likely precipitates his death.
					</div>

				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">Who was Thresh's partner in the 74th Hunger Games?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						In the 74th Hunger Games, Thresh was partnered with Rue, a 12-year-old girl. Known for his strength, Thresh was perceived as a formidable competitor by other tributes. His survival skills were enhanced by his knowledge of plants, a skill likely acquired from his home, District 11. Thresh shared a close bond with Rue, and her demise triggered a strong emotional response in him.

				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">Who killed Thresh in the Hunger Games book?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">

				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						Thresh, a character in the Hunger Games book, met his end at the hands of Cato. This contrasts with the film version where wolf mutts cause his death. His demise is linked to his decision to spare Katniss and warn her about Cato.

						In the 74th Hunger Games, Thresh was responsible for the death of Clove, another tribute. Despite this, he chose not to kill Katniss Everdeen, another competitor in the games.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Thresh lived in District 11, presumably with his <a href="/wiki/Thresh%27s_grandmother" title="Thresh's grandmother">grandmother</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thresh%27s_sister" title="Thresh's sister">sister</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The family likely was not fed well, as Thresh looked healthier and stronger after entering the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> than he did beforehand.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Thresh is reaped into the 74th Hunger Games<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> along with his <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a>, 12-year-old <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Since Thresh is one of the strongest tributes that year, the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Careers</a> attempt to recruit him for their <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>, but he refuses.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In his individual assessment, he receives a <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> of 10.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, in the film, he scores a 9.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<div class="quote">

I think about how Foxface grew alarmed when she heard a sound at the site of the explosion. But she didn't turn to the woods, she turned to whatever lies across from it. To that area of the arena that drops off into I don't know what. I feel almost certain that the person she ran from was Thresh and that is his domain.</i>"</span></dd>

The Careers know where he is, but view the field as a hazard since Thresh would be at a major advantage if they were to attack. Hence, they avoid him.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<div class="quote">

<p>Thresh comes out of hiding during the <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">feast</a> to claim his backpack, which contains what he needs the most. While at the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, he overhears Clove threaten Katniss and taunt her about Rue's death. Furious, Thresh attacks Clove, pulling her away from Katniss and yelling at her, effortlessly holding her a foot above the ground. Due to Clove's words, Thresh accuses her of killing Rue, which Clove truthfully denies. When Clove sees Thresh holding a large rock, she screams for her district partner, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Thresh then asks Katniss what Clove had meant about Rue being Katniss's ally. Katniss tells him that <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> had killed Rue and that she had sang to Rue as she had died. <p>After the feast, a storm forms over the arena, which Katniss surmises is intended for Cato and Thresh<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. <h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>I only ever spoke to Thresh one time. Just long enough for him to spare my life. I didn't know him, but I always respected him. For his power. For his refusal to play the Games on anyone's terms but his own. The Careers wanted him to team up with them from the beginning, but he wouldn't do it. I respected him for that.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss commemorates Thresh<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, Katniss considers writing her own personalized statement to give about Thresh and Rue, but gets too emotional every time she tries.
</p><p>Once Katniss and Peeta arrive in District 11, Katniss sees Thresh's grandmother and sister standing on the platform for the tributes' families. Peeta gives a commemorative speech in which he speaks of how both Thresh and Rue had made it to the final eight and helped keep Katniss alive. He then offers to give a month of their winnings to Thresh and Rue's families every year, which shocks the crowd.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Thresh_family.png/revision/latest?cb=20200705035038" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Thresh's family during the Victory Tour" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="103" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Thresh family.png" data-image-key="Thresh_family.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Thresh_family.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200705035038"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Thresh_family.png/revision/latest?cb=20200705035038" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Thresh's family during the Victory Tour" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Thresh_family.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200705035038" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="103" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Thresh family.png" data-image-key="Thresh_family.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8c/Thresh_family.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200705035038"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Thresh_family.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Thresh's family during the Victory Tour</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Afterward, Katniss commemorates Thresh by recounting the time he had spared her life. She explains that she had respected him for refusing to play the Games on anyone else's terms and for rejecting the Careers' offer to join their pack. This causes Thresh's grandmother to smile.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> begin shooting, Katniss worries that Thresh's family will be injured or killed. Peeta reacts with anger to the situation, worrying that the families will not receive a portion of the winnings and that they will instead be punished.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a> assures Katniss that neither Thresh's nor Rue's families were harmed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tactics">Tactics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the Hunger Games, Thresh's main tactic is to occupy a portion of the arena and seldom leave its boundaries.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, Thresh is seen participating in the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Thresh chooses to enter a field of tall wheat,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_9-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> which Katniss presumes is because he views the area as a source of food, shelter, and safety.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_9-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Peeta reveals that the Careers had not wanted to attack Thresh while he was there since he would be at a major advantage.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_9-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the feast, Thresh kills Clove, the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, by smashing her skull in with a rock. He does this after overhearing Clove taunt Katniss about Rue's death.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, in the film, Thresh kills Clove by slamming her against the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> until her neck breaks.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, it is shown during the Cornucopia bloodbath that Thresh killed the <a href="/wiki/District_7_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 7 male (74th HG)">District 7 male</a> by knocking away his spear and slashing his face with a crescent sword.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_appearance">Physical appearance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>The boy tribute from District 11, Thresh, has the same dark skin as Rue, but the resemblance stops there. He's one of the giants, probably six and a half feet tall and built like an ox.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss's first impression of Thresh<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Thresh is a physical giant, being the largest tribute that year.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He rivals Cato in terms of brute strength, and is regarded by Katniss as a "physical wonder". Like his district partner Rue, Thresh has dark skin<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, though Katniss describes his eyes as being a strange golden-brown.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>It is noted that Thresh looks even bigger and more well-fed during the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Being from District 11, a poor district, it is likely he did not get much to eat. In the film, Thresh is shorter than both Cato and Marvel, and less muscular than the former.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, he still has a stronger build than the other tributes.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>I always respected him. For his power. For his refusal to play the Games on anyone's terms but his own.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss's thoughts on Thresh<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Thresh shows mercy to Katniss when they meet in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, even at a time when he has a clear advantage over her, as he believes that he owes her for being in an alliance with Rue, his young district partner.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Through this action, Thresh is shown to have a certain degree of honor and dignity, as well as a caring and gentle side.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/18/Thresh_smile.png/revision/latest?cb=20200705205609" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Thresh's soft side for his district partner, Rue" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="104" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Thresh smile.png" data-image-key="Thresh_smile.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/18/Thresh_smile.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200705205609"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/18/Thresh_smile.png/revision/latest?cb=20200705205609" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Thresh's soft side for his district partner, Rue" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/18/Thresh_smile.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200705205609" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="104" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Thresh smile.png" data-image-key="Thresh_smile.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/18/Thresh_smile.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20200705205609"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Thresh_smile.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Thresh's soft side for his district partner, Rue</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Despite his kind-hearted nature, Thresh can be aggressive and vengeful, as seen when he brutally murders Clove for taunting Katniss over Rue's death<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Additionally, Thresh prefers solitude and does not make any alliances, demonstrating his preference to work alone. He pointedly refuses to join the Career pack.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Being as strong and huge as he is<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, Thresh is seen as a threat by the other tributes<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_9-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. He commands respect and is feared by virtually everyone in the arena, even the Careers<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_9-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Katniss herself thinks that if she were a sponsor, she would bet on Thresh<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Being from District 11, Thresh's knowledge of plants presumably helps him survive the Games<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Rue">Rue</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games (film)">"<i>Just this time, Twelve. For Rue.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Thresh spares Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Thresh takes a shining to his district partner, <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>. Her death sends him into a fit of anger and grief, and causes him to enact vengeance on Clove<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. In the film, during training, Thresh notices that Rue has stolen Cato's knife; this causes him to grin and shake his head in admiration<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Thresh's relationship with Rue drives him to spare Katniss's life after she tells him that she had sung to Rue as she had died<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Katniss_Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>You better run now, fire girl.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Thresh to Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>During the feast, Thresh saves <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> when she is pinned by Clove, killing the assailant before asking Katniss about Rue. Thresh is touched that Katniss had taken care of the young girl, and he feels that he owes her. As a result, he spares Katniss's life and warns her to run from Cato. Thresh also takes Cato's backpack, which allows Katniss to escape.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_4-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This act likely results in Thresh's death, which both shocks and saddens Katniss, as she had liked him and wanted him to win if she or Peeta could not.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the Victory Tour, Katniss honors Thresh by including him in her speech. She states that she had not known him, but had deeply respected him for his strength and his refusal to play the Games on anyone's grounds but his own. Katniss also thanks Thresh for having spared her life.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Thresh's name comes from threshing, a method of harvesting grain that involves flailing the plant around.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the film, Thresh is 18 years old and weighs 170 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the film, Thresh's odds of winning change from 10–1 to 11–1. Afterward, they increase from 11–1 to 8–1.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, Katniss states that Cato killed Thresh. However, this is not in-line with the film continuity, as Thresh was killed by the wolf mutts in the first film. It is likely that Thresh and Cato fought, and Cato inflicted a mortal wound, leaving Thresh defenseless against the mutts.</li>
<li>Alongside the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a>, he is one of the two non-Career tributes to kill another tribute in the initial bloodbath.</li></ul>
