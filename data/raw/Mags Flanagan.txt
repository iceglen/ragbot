<p><b>Mags Flanagan</b> was the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> during the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, for which she <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> in place of <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was also the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> of the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a><sup id="cite_ref-Capitol_Couture_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Capitol_Couture-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and the mentor of <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a><sup id="cite_ref-SR6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> during the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

Post-victory, Mags mentored numerous District 4 tributes, potentially initiating the district's Career Tributes training program.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="11th_Hunger_Games">11th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>At the age of 16, Mags was reaped for the 11th Hunger Games, which she won.<sup id="cite_ref-Capitol_Couture_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Capitol_Couture-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the Games, Mags' main desire was to protect her district partner.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Her skill with fish hooks and other natural skills she likely acquired from living in District 4 probably played a role in her victory. She was the first victor to embark on a <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Capitol_Couture_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Capitol_Couture-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> as the victors before her were simply transported home after their victories.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was also presumably the first victor to move to the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a> directly after their Games, as well as the first to receive a token monetary prize for winning, as both ideas were first implemented in the 11th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Between_the_Games">Between the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As a victor, Mags went on to serve as a mentor for scores of District 4 tributes in the years that followed her victory. At first, she didn't ask the tributes what their goal was, because it seemed obvious that the goal was to survive. Later on, however, she started asking her tributes about their desires, reflecting on how she was unable to protect her district partner.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>At some point after her Games, she got married as she was seen wearing a wedding ring on her left hand. It's unknown who her spouse is and whether or not she had children.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Quarter_Quell">Second Quarter Quell</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, she was made to mentor the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tributes with <a href="/wiki/49th_Hunger_Games" title="49th Hunger Games">49th Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> (<a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>) since there were no District 12 victors available. She met the tributes following the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a> and immediately hugged <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, knowing he had been deeply affected by <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>'s death.<sup id="cite_ref-SR6_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Once Haymitch began to calm down, she had him take a bath and ordered bean and ham hock soup to comfort him. The next day, Mags asked the tributes what their goal in the Games was, saying that hers had been to protect her district partner. She advised them to look for clues about the arena in the <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">training stations</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a> was brought as a body double to replace Louella, Mags offered her stew. She immediately realized Lou Lou was from <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> based on her reaction to the district <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>, then put her to bed. She encouraged the tributes to continue the <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">Newcomer</a> alliance.<sup id="cite_ref-SR10_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR10-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Mags helped the tributes prepare for their <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>. When <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> agitated Lou Lou, Mags took her into the bedroom and took care of her ear when the implant caused it to bleed. She gave strawberry ice cream to <a href="/wiki/Proserpina_Trinket" title="Proserpina Trinket">Proserpina</a>, <a href="/wiki/Vitus" title="Vitus">Vitus</a>, and the District 12 tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the games, she would support Haymitch and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> by using their <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorships</a> to send them meaningful gifts; a pot of bean and ham hock soup to let them know they're not alone, breakfast spread, and black coffee and ice cream after Maysilee died as a reminder of her sass.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Afterwards, she and Wiress were tortured for their role in the rebellion and information, leaving Mags wheelchair bound.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="65th_Hunger_Games">65th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a>, she served as mentor to <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, who went on to become the youngest victor in the history of the Hunger Games at the age of 14<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. It is possible she also mentored <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> or at the very least played a role in her training/preparation as a possible career tribute for the <a href="/wiki/70th_Hunger_Games" title="70th Hunger Games">70th Hunger Games</a>. Annie, too, would go on to become victor of her respective Games, but at a great psychological cost due to the horrific nature of her district partner's death during the Games.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>Mags, an eighty-year-old woman, volunteered for the 75th Hunger Games in the place of <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, becoming a tribute alongside <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> whom she mentored in the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a> and (according to the book and film) practically raised<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She is reputed to be a lovely woman. She mumbled every time she spoke; <a href="/wiki/Katniss" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Katniss">Katniss</a> believes it is because of a possible stroke she may have had in the past<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. While in training for the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, Katniss watched as Mags displayed her skill of being able to make fishhooks out of nearly anything. This skill, plus the fact that she volunteered to take Annie Cresta's place, made Katniss want Mags to be one of her allies in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a><sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Described to be in her eighties, Mags was the oldest tribute in the 75th Hunger Games, while <a href="/wiki/Woof" title="Woof">Woof</a> was the second oldest.
</p>

<p>While competing in the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, Mags joined an alliance with Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>. She was carried by Finnick most of the time as she wasn't able to walk very fast<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Finnick and Mags are flung back when Peeta walks into the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>, due to the force in which Peeta is thrust back in.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When Katniss throws some nuts at the force field to determine where it is, Mags eats a mouthful of them. Katniss is alarmed as they don't know if the nuts are poisonous, but Finnick allows her to since he believes she knows what she's doing. She and Finnick begin to weave objects with big sharp blades of grass while Katniss hunts for water and food. When she returns, they have made a hut and bowls.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mags is deeply saddened when the first day of the games is over and the faces of tributes who have died are shown at night, suggesting she was close to some of the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a> fatalities. When the <a href="/wiki/Tools_and_gear#Spile" title="Tools and gear">spile</a> is sent from the sponsors, she wonders what its use could be. Katniss asks if she can fish with it, but she could not, and is enlightened when they discover it gets water from trees. She and Peeta are not alarmed by the twelve bells rung during the night as they were asleep when the event occurred.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interview">Interview</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the interviews in the film adaptation, she wore a sea blue dress which touched the floor. The top half of it was a lighter blue and had sparkles on it while the bottom half was a duller and darker blue, much like Finnick's. Mags joined hands with the other tributes after the final interview, displaying unity among the districts<sup id="cite_ref-CF_18_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_18-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Mags died on the second day of the Quarter Quell. When the acid fog was emitted, Finnick carried her while she was sleeping. When the fog damaged Peeta's legs and rendered him unable to walk, Katniss carried Mags as she was unable to help Peeta, but Katniss was also affected by the fog, and Finnick was unable to carry both Peeta and Mags. Realizing that they would be killed by the fog if they could not move faster, Mags kissed Finnick and courageously sacrificed herself by walking straight into the fog so Finnick, Katniss, and Peeta could live. Katniss described Mags as "falling to the ground in a horrible dance" when she was dying and Katniss honored Mags for her bravery. Finnick spent several hours of the night grieving for her. Finnick did not even talk about Mags' death for the whole night.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>When <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> states that Mags was half of Finnick's family because she was his mentor, Katniss feels great remorse<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Later, it is discovered that Mags was part of an even bigger alliance (including at least 12 tributes) that was set on keeping Peeta and Katniss alive<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>In the film, Mags dies in much the same manner, although Finnick at first doesn't realize what she's doing and tries frantically to call her back, staring after her in grief as her cannon sounds<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She placed 15th.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>At eighty years old, Mags used a cane to walk<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and had few teeth. She had a District 4 accent and a garbled speech that Katniss found difficult to understand, which might have been the result of a previous stroke.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mags also had gray hair<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and weighed less than seventy pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mags was noted by Haymitch to be a wonderful lady and showed herself to be kind, friendly, selfless, and very brave, as well as pragmatic. She volunteered to save Annie, clearly knowing she was not going to survive<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and willingly walked to her death to allow Finnick, Katniss, and Peeta to escape the poison fog in the arena without being burdened by her, sacrificing herself without hesitation to protect them so that they would survive (and go on to help the rebellion)<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She was impossible to dislike.
</p><p>She clearly loved Finnick and Annie, as it was noted she basically raised Finnick, having mentored him and likely Annie as well<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. She made easy friends with Katniss, who admired the old woman for her bravery and selflessness<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_13-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills">Skills</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mags had the ability to make fishhooks out of anything<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_13-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and weave baskets so well that they could float in water without any leaking in<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mags is derived from the Greek name "Margaret" which means "pearl". Much like other District 4 characters' namesakes relating to fishing and the sea.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMags_Flanagan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<ul><li>Mags is one of the four characters to appear in the main trilogy who have been alive before the Hunger Games started, being five years old when they began. The other characters are <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Clerk_Carmine_Clade" title="Clerk Carmine Clade">Clerk Carmine Clade</a>.</li>
<li>Mags was so old when she volunteered that she "needed a cane to walk to the stage".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_14-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, in the film, she wasn't seen with any mobility aids.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A young Mags was portrayed by Grace Kull in promotional posters.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Mags is one year younger than <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, the deuteragonist of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>. She won the Hunger Games the year after Lucy Gray, and both were sixteen years old at the time of their respective victories.</li>
<li>Mags is the oldest tribute to compete in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, with her being 80 during the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.</li>
<li>Mags is the last ever District 4 tribute to die in the Hunger Games.</li></ul>
