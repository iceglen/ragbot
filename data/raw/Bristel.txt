</p><p><b>Bristel</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. They were crewmates with <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thom" title="Thom">Thom</a>, and are possibly from <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBristel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bristel appeared when Gale was whipped by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a> for poaching in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. Bristel and Thom pieced together the story of what happened as they carried Gale to <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a>'s home. They had seen the whipping, and Bristel knew that it would have been worse if Gale had been carrying more than just a turkey when he was caught. Before going home, Bristel and Thom were given money by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Their fate is unknown, but it is likely that they were killed in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, as they were not mentioned among the refugees in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.
</p>
