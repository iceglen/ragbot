<p><b>Arlo Chance</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> and a <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> soldier during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. Ten years after the war, he was <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executed</a>, inspiring <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> to write <a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">"The Hanging Tree" song</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArlo_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>They're all malcontents, but this one's the ringleader. Name's Arlo something-or-other.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—A Peacekeeper major regarding Arlo Chance<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>He was an ex-soldier and had also been toughened by many years of hard work in the coal mines of District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Although the rebels lost the war, he was determined to try to keep things going. Ten years later, he tried to sabotage coal production at the mine with a <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombing</a>, but accidentally killed three people in the process. He failed in his goal and was arrested by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> for murder.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArlo_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, Arlo was said to have shot and killed two coal mine managers and a Peacekeeper. According to <a href="/wiki/Commander_Hoff" title="Commander Hoff">Commander Hoff</a>, the murder weapon was later found and swabbed for DNA, which proved Arlo's guilt.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArlo_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He was hung publicly at the <a href="/wiki/Hanging_tree" title="Hanging tree">hanging tree</a>, a makeshift gallows that was used in place of the proper one that <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> kept promising to send. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, both newly recruited <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>, were made to attend the execution, as they had performed well during training drills. During the execution, a woman, <a href="/wiki/Lil" title="Lil">Lil</a>, appeared, shrieking his name. Horrified, he shouted for her to run. The trapdoor was then released and he dropped fifteen feet, killed instantly. In the wake of his hanging, the <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a> in the forest took up his cry,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> which was then transformed into music by the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArlo_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Arlo's execution inspired Lucy Gray to write "The Hanging Tree".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The song survived in the memory of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, though its historical context had been lost by the time she learned it.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, Katniss sang it in a <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propaganda video</a>, and district rebels took it up as a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/protest_song" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:protest song">protest song</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJP1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArlo_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Arlo is heavily implied to be related to <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a>.</li>
<li>Arlo and Lil served as the inspiration for the "<a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">Hanging Tree</a>" song.
<ul><li>It's possible that Arlo and Lil were lovers.</li></ul></li></ul>
