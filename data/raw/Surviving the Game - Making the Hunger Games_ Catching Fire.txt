</p><p><i><b>Surviving the Game - Making the Hunger Games: Catching Fire</b></i> is a feature-length documentary film about the making of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. It was included as a featurette on DVD releases.
</p>

</p>
<ol><li>A New Kind of Hunger: Continuing the Saga</li>
<li>Visual Vocabulary: Building a World</li>
<li>Stirring Things Up: The Cast</li>
<li>Fashion Forward: Costume, Make-up &amp; Hair</li>
<li>Let It Fly: Production in Atlanta</li>
