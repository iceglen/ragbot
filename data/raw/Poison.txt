<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPoison%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the 10th Hunger Games, Coriolanus Snow gave Lucy Gray <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow&#39;s mother">his mother's</a> <a href="/wiki/Silver_compact" title="Silver compact">silver compact</a> to sneak rat poison from the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> into the arena as a weapon against the other tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a> later died after drinking from a water bottle poisoned by Lucy Gray<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Reaper Ash drank from a puddle poisoned by Lucy Gray while he suffered from heat exhaustion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Lucy Gray later admitted to Snow that her intention had been to poison <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>, but Wovey got the water bottle instead.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The discovery of Snow's cheating forced him into a sentence of 20 years of Peacekeeper service.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, the arena's flora, water and many of the <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> were poisonous. <a href="/wiki/Carat" title="Carat">Carat</a> and <a href="/wiki/Urchin" title="Urchin">Urchin</a> died of the arena's poisons on the first day<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while the charcoal tablets in <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s backpack saved him, although it left him sick through the night.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR15_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR15-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR16_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR16-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While several of <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">The Newcomers</a> got sick, <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> quickly figured it out and <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a>'s backpack contained a large bottle of an antidote so none of them died.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Poison barbs from a porcupine mutt later killed <a href="/wiki/Buck" title="Buck">Buck</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chicory" title="Chicory">Chicory</a> and Hull despite Haymitch and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>'s efforts to save Hull using the antidote.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR21_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR21-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the 50th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> used a <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Blowgun" title="Weapons">blowgun</a> loaded with darts dipped in the arena's poisonous substances as a weapon.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With this, she killed <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR19_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR19-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Loupe" title="Loupe">Loupe</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Although Maysilee believed that she hit <a href="/wiki/Camilla" title="Camilla">Camilla</a> as well, the Career tribute survived the poison and died in the later volcanic eruption. Maysilee later showed Haymitch how she made her poison when she collected more: Maysilee mashed up handfuls of poisonous berries into a juicy paste in a small bowl and then drained some of the juice into a heart-shaped glass vial that hung from one of her necklaces. The vial was designed for perfume, so it had a good tight lid to prevent evaporation, but Maysilee wished that it held more.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>One of the traps of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> was a poisonous <a href="/wiki/Fog" title="Fog">fog</a>. <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> sacrificed herself to it in order to ensure <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s survival. The surviving tributes who were affected by it used salt water to leech out the poison and ointment sent by Haymitch as a <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gift</a> to treat the scabs and scars that the fog caused.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Use_of_poison_by_President_Snow">Use of poison by President Snow</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPoison%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> and his time in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, Snow took rat poison from the back alley of <a href="/wiki/Pluribus_Bell" title="Pluribus Bell">Pluribus Bell</a>'s nightclub and slipped it into the <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> that <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> had received for his breakdown. When Snow presented Plinth's personal effects to Dean <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, he deliberately slid the bottles of morphling into the trash, knowing that Highbottom - a morphling addict - would later retrieve them, ingest the rat poison, and die. This was his first murder with what would become his signature weapon.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Over the next 64 years, Snow would rise to power as the president of Panem, and he maintained control by poisoning allies and enemies alike who posed a threat to him. Snow often drank from the same poisoned glass to deflect suspicion. Although <a href="/wiki/Medical_treatments#Poison_antidote" title="Medical treatments">antidotes</a> saved his life, he was left with mouth sores that wouldn't heal. He wore roses to cover up the persistent stench of blood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the disastrous <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a> before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, Snow poisoned <a href="/wiki/Incitatus_Loomy" title="Incitatus Loomy">Incitatus Loomy</a> for his incompetence with a plate of oysters that he also ate from. While meeting with <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> at <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>'s home, Snow was visibly ill and was tended to by Haymitch and Plutarch. Snow took an antidote that he carried in his pocket and Haymitch realized what had happened to Incitatus from Snow's comments.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR9_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR9-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the final morning of the 50th Hunger Games, Haymitch received a sponsor gift of what he believed to be a jug of poisoned milk from Snow who was seeking to force Haymitch to kill either <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> or himself with it. Before Haymitch could drink it, Wellie was found and killed by <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> and Haymitch smashed the jug instead.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR24_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR24-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The jug of poisoned milk was edited out of the final Game footage.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As retaliation for Haymitch's actions during the 50th Hunger Games, Snow sent a bag full of bloodred poisoned candy to <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>. When Lenore Dove ate one, the poison in it killed her despite Haymitch's desperate attempts to save her.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Snow had Head Gamemaker <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a> killed, although the method was never revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i>The Hunger Games</i> film, Crane was locked in a room with <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries, suggesting that Snow was executing him by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/forced_suicide" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:forced suicide">forced suicide</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the film <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire_(film)" class="mw-redirect" title="Catching Fire (film)">Catching Fire</a></i>, Plutarch mentioned to Katniss that Crane decided to "quit breathing... that or it was poison berries" implying that he ate the berries and died as a result.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, as a distraction for the rescue mission for <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> performed a live interview to be broadcast across Panem. In the interview, Finnick publicly exposed Snow's habit of poisoning people which Finnick had learned about from his Capitol "clients," having been paid in secrets by them, some of which were about Snow himself.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>By the time of Snow's execution, his sores had seemingly worsened because he occasionally coughed up blood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Ironically, Snow's habit of poisoning people may have ultimately led to his demise; Snow was believed to have either been trampled by the angry mob, or to have choked to death on his own blood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Antidote">Antidote</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPoison%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A poison antidote was an antidote used to treat poison. One known kind of antidote took on the form of a syrupy substance.
</p><p>President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Snow</a> poisoned allies that he felt threatened by, but he drank the poison as well to avoid attracting suspicion. Even though he took antidotes, the poison caused permanent sores inside his mouth that never healed. As a result, he always wore a genetically engineered rose, which covers the scent of blood stained on his breath.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After poisoning <a href="/wiki/Incitatus_Loomy" title="Incitatus Loomy">Incitatus Loomy</a> for his incompetence in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a> before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, Snow took an antidote that he carried in his pocket.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR9_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR9-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>One of the items included in some of the tributes' <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpacks</a> for the 50th Hunger Games were bottles of antidote for the arena's poisons. <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a> got one<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the Careers got at least one as well which ended up in the supplies of either <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Barba" title="Barba">Barba</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Angler" title="Angler">Angler</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Although some of <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a> ingested the arena's poison, <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> quickly recognized what it was, and they survived thanks to the antidote in Hull's backpack.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> found the Careers' bottle of the antidote while going through their supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_12-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the attack by the porcupine mutt, the two used it to treat their own injuries and in a vain attempt to save Hull's life.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR21_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR21-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
