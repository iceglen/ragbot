<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>It is an old and rarely used gesture of our district, occasionally seen at funerals. It means thanks, it means admiration, it means goodbye to someone you love.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>The <b>three-finger salute</b> is used by <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> residents as a gesture of admiration, gratitude, and saying goodbye to someone they love.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The sign is made by pressing the three middle fingers of your left hand to your lips and then holding them out to whomever you intend to salute. In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">books</a>, the left hand was used,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">first film</a> it was the right hand, and the fingers were simply held up by one's side.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, the left hand was used like in the book.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThree-finger_salute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThree-finger_salute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> citizens used the salute at the funeral of the dead tributes, <a href="/wiki/Jethro_Callow" title="Jethro Callow">Jethro Callow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">Willamae Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a>. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> lifted his hand high because he had many to honor.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR26_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR26-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games_reaping">74th Hunger Games reaping</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThree-finger_salute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> was selected as District 12's female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, her older sister <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to go in her place. Once Katniss had introduced herself onstage, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> asked the rest of District 12 to applaud, but instead, they used the three-finger salute to say goodbye to Katniss and show their respects. According to Katniss, this gesture was old and rarely used by this point,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but its usage here inspired a resurgence of the symbol throughout the trilogy.
</p>
<h3><span id="Rue.27s_death"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Rue's_death">Rue's death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThree-finger_salute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 300px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/00/2011-11-15-10-38-33-5-katniss-delivers-a-farewell-salute-this-gesture-i.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20111123012923" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss giving the salute in the arena." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/00/2011-11-15-10-38-33-5-katniss-delivers-a-farewell-salute-this-gesture-i.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/300?cb=20111123012923" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="187" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="2011-11-15-10-38-33-5-katniss-delivers-a-farewell-salute-this-gesture-i.jpeg" data-image-key="2011-11-15-10-38-33-5-katniss-delivers-a-farewell-salute-this-gesture-i.jpeg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:2011-11-15-10-38-33-5-katniss-delivers-a-farewell-salute-this-gesture-i.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss giving the salute in the arena.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>When <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> speared <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> in the stomach,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, Katniss shot him in the upper chest with one of her <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">arrows</a>. Then, to show <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> that she was "not just a pawn in their games," Katniss decorated Rue's body with <a href="/wiki/Flowers" title="Flowers">flowers</a>, which the cameras would have to show when <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> came to collect the body. Before leaving, Katniss honored Rue with the three-finger salute.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film adaptation, Katniss saluted towards the cameras, and the people of <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> did the same shortly before sparking the <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_11" title="Uprising of District 11">uprising of District 11</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Victory_Tour">The Victory Tour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThree-finger_salute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 304px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/44/Victor_Tour.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720221746" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 11 giving the respect sign." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/44/Victor_Tour.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/304?cb=20130720221746" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="304" height="126" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Victor Tour.png" data-image-key="Victor_Tour.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Victor_Tour.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">District 11 giving the respect sign.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>During their stop in District 11 on the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> gave speeches honoring Rue and <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>. After Katniss finished hers, an <a href="/wiki/Unidentified_District_11_citizen" title="Unidentified District 11 citizen">old man</a> whistled <a href="/wiki/Rue%27s_four-note_song" title="Rue&#39;s four-note song">Rue's four-note song</a>, signaling for the crowd to salute Katniss with the sign from her district. As Katniss and Peeta left the stage, the old man was publicly <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executed</a> on the steps of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Quarter_Quell_reaping">Quarter Quell reaping</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThree-finger_salute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the film adaptation of <i>Catching Fire</i>, residents of District 12 used the sign to pay respect to Katniss and Peeta at the reaping for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThree-finger_salute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scout_sign_and_salute" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Scout sign and salute">three-finger salute</a> has been used by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scouting" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Scouting">Scouting</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_Guides" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Girl Guides">Girl Guides</a> since 1908.</li>
<li>Inspired by its use in <i>The Hunger Games</i>, the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/three-finger_salute_(pro-democracy)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:three-finger salute (pro-democracy)">three-finger salute</a> has been used in protests since 2014, primarily to protest military coups and oppressive governments in Southeast Asian countries.</li></ul>
