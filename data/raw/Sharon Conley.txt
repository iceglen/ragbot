</p><p><b>Sharon Conley</b> is the actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Lucia" title="Lucia">Lucia</a>, one of the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSharon_Conley%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Morris appeared in the movie <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_to_Expect_When_You%27re_Expecting" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:What to Expect When You&#39;re Expecting">What to Expect When You're Expecting</a></i> and the TV series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_Wives" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Army Wives">Army Wives</a></i>. She studied speech communications at the University of Georgia. She worked for Coca-Cola after college and helped run a restaurant with her ex-husband. She also appeared in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blind_Side" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Blind Side">The Blind Side</a></i>.
</p>
