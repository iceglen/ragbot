<p><b>Otho Mellark</b> was a baker who lived in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. One of his sons, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> for District 12 in both the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th</a> and the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOtho_Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Otho Mellark was a member of District 12's <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a> class. He attended the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. After <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> was killed for attempting to run, Mellark was bewildered when the crowd was ordered to fall to the ground. <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> had to punch him in the back of his knee before he obeyed.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>In his youth, he wanted to marry <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid Everdeen</a>, but she "ran off with a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a>," Burdock.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mr. Mellark married another woman instead, who became <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Mellark" title="Mrs. Mellark">Mrs. Mellark</a>. They had three sons, with their youngest, Peeta, being born the same year that Mr. and Mrs. Everdeen had their first child, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a>. He and his family lived above the <a href="/wiki/Bakery" title="Bakery">bakery</a> where they worked.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On Peeta's first day of <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a>, Mr. Mellark pointed Katniss out to Peeta and told him of his past with her mother, particularly how she'd fallen for Mr. Everdeen's singing voice.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> tended to trade with Mr. Mellark when his wife was out, offering up squirrels they'd shot in exchange for bakery <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>  Mr. Mellark often commented on how Katniss's arrows never pierced the squirrel's body, instead shooting them cleanly through the eye.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He also traded generous amounts of bread for <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>'s goat cheeses at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOtho_Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After saying goodbye to Peeta after the reaping, Mr. Mellark visited Katniss, gave her a packet of cookies, and promised to keep an eye on Prim and make sure she was eating.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He presumably made this visit because of his past feelings for Katniss's mother, as well as the business relationship he'd developed with Katniss herself over the years.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, he only briefly appeared in the bakery in Katniss's flashback, while his wife beat Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOtho_Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the events of the 75th Hunger Games, he and the rest of Peeta's family were killed when the <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombed District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mr. Mellark was included in the <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">memory book</a> Katniss, Peeta, and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch</a> made after the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">war</a>, highlighting how he brought Katniss the cookies.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOtho_Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mr. Mellark had a wife, Mrs. Mellark, and the two of them had three children. Their <a href="/wiki/First_Mellark_son" title="First Mellark son">first son</a> is unnamed, and he was over 18 at the time of the 74th Hunger Games reaping. Their <a href="/wiki/Second_Mellark_son" title="Second Mellark son">second son</a>, also unnamed, was 17 or 18 at the time, while Peeta was the youngest at 16.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mr. and Mrs. Mellark and their two older sons died in the bombing of District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Peeta survived the Second Rebellion and went on to marry Katniss Everdeen, eventually having a <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_son" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark's son">son</a> and a <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_daughter" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark's daughter">daughter</a> together.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOtho_Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Katniss described Mr. Mellark as a big, broad-shouldered man with burn scars from years at the oven.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He had meaty hands and blond hair.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FOtho_Mellark%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mr. Mellark was not a talkative man, but he was described as being "much nicer" than his wife, and he was quite generous in his trades with Katniss, Gale, and especially Prim, when Mrs. Mellark was not around.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He didn't seem to harbor any resentment towards Mrs. Everdeen for marrying someone else, and he treated her children kindly and respectfully.
</p>
