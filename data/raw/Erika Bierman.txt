</p><p><b>Erika Marie Bierman</b>, born June 14, 2001, is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_granddaughter" title="Coriolanus Snow&#39;s granddaughter">Coriolanus Snow's granddaughter</a> in the <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> film and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>.
