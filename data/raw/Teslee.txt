<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/BoSaSTeslee2.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316062803" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Teslee talking to her mentor." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="168" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="BoSaSTeslee2.png" data-image-key="BoSaSTeslee2.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/BoSaSTeslee2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250316062803" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/BoSaSTeslee2.png/revision/latest?cb=20250316062803" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Teslee talking to her mentor." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/BoSaSTeslee2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250316062803" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="168" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="BoSaSTeslee2.png" data-image-key="BoSaSTeslee2.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d8/BoSaSTeslee2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250316062803" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:BoSaSTeslee2.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Teslee talking to her mentor.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Teslee was reaped for the 10th Hunger Games along with her district partner, <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a>. Prior to the reaping, Teslee (as the girl tribute from her district) was assigned Capitol-born Academy student <a href="/wiki/Urban_Canville" title="Urban Canville">Urban Canville</a> as her mentor for the games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Like the other tributes, Teslee was immediately caged, shackled, and transported into <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> where she and her fellow competitors were dumped into the monkey house in the Capitol Zoo to be gawked at by Capitol citizens.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Like many of the other tributes, Teslee refused to divulge any personal information about herself to her mentor, remaining distant and hostile. Because of this, she did not appear at the interviews before the Games began.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On the first day of the Games, Teslee and Circ collected <a href="/wiki/Drone" title="Drone">drones</a> that landed near <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>'s dead body. While assessing the damage, Teslee reattached some wires on another drone, leading to the lights blinking. When two more drones came in with gifts from <a href="/wiki/Io_Jasper" title="Io Jasper">Io Jasper</a>, Circ's mentor, they collected them and ran back to the barricade after seeing <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a> appear.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When only seven tributes remained in the arena, Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> released a swarm of venomous <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> into the arena in retaliation for the death of <a href="/wiki/Gaius_Breen" title="Gaius Breen">Gaius Breen</a>. Teslee and Circ, who were both running away initially because of <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a>, stopped to look at the snake tank. Unlike Treech, who had sprinted away immediately after the tank had burst, Circ and Teslee stayed only for a few moments of confusion, which came at a great cost. Teslee managed to narrowly escape the swarm of snakes by climbing up a pole, but her district partner was not so lucky, and quickly met his end thanks to the muttations' deadly venom. 
</p><p>Because of this, Teslee sobbed in terror. <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>, the male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, attempted to leap onto the pole across from Teslee for safety, and the snakes regrouped underneath her. As her grip began to fail, she looked towards Mizzen for help, but he was stunned. However, the snakes withdrew from Teslee's pole upon <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>'s arrival.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Teslee shakily slid to the ground and climbed onto a chain-link fence, later disappearing behind the barricade.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Later that day, Teslee appeared with the drone she had collected earlier, though it was slightly altered. As she flipped a switch, the drone ascended and headed towards Mizzen, revealing a cable that connected it to her wrist. As a different drone gifted Mizzen bread, it swerved and came back at him, and the same thing happened with three other drones. Together, they collided with and trapped him until he lost his balance, falling and snapping his neck on the ground. Satisfied, Teslee reeled in her drone and gave it a hug.
</p><p>Unfortunately, she was promptly ambushed by Treech, who cleaved through her head with his axe, ending her life. This happened moments after Mizzen's death, and the screens were closed onto Teslee, so no one noticed Treech until it was too late. Her body was later added to Reaper's makeshift morgue.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Teslee is described by Snow as a small, scruffy girl with dark, frizzy hair.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Teslee's name comes from Nikola Tesla, a famous Serbian electrical engineer and inventor.
