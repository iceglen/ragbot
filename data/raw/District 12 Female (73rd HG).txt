<p>The <b>District 12 female</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> who participated in the <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">73rd Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_12_female_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The District 12 female was born in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> along with her <a href="/wiki/Sage_Blanken" title="Sage Blanken">district partner</a>, and was <a href="/wiki/Reaped" class="mw-redirect" title="Reaped">reaped</a> for the 73rd Hunger Games. She was mentored by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>. <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> mentioned to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> that she and her district partner ate like "savages". This upset Katniss greatly, as they came from the poorest region of District 12, where access to food was limited. In a show of defiance, Katniss then ate the rest of her meal with her fingers and wiped her hands on the tablecloth.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_12_female_%2873rd_HG%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>It's unknown how she died in the Games, but in an early draft of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_film" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games film"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> script, <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> said that "both tributes of District 12 [were] out" after the boy from her district died on the second day,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so it is likely she died on the first.
</p>
