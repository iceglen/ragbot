<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAshley_Liao%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Liao played the lead role in the ACE Entertainment feature <i>Loveboat Taipei</i>. She played the teenage version of Ali Wong in the Netflix romantic comedy <i>Always Be My Maybe</i>, and starred as Princess Eleanor in the Disney+ original movie <i>Secret Society of Second Born Royals</i>. She also had a leading role in the Debby Ryan-produced film <i>Jessica Darling’s It List</i>. She had a series regular role in the Apple TV+ series <i>Physical</i> opposite Rose Byrne. Liao is best known for her role as Lola in the Netflix series <i>Fuller House</i>, and has also guest starred on many series. She is represented by Innovative Artists, Semler Entertainment, and Skrzyniarz &amp; Mallean.<sup id="cite_ref-Tributes_and_Mentors_cast_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tributes_and_Mentors_cast-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
