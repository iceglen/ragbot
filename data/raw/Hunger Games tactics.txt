<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Pre-Games">Pre-Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Persona">Persona</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the week leading up to the Games themselves, tributes had to curate their public persona, typically in order to appeal to <a href="/wiki/Sponsor" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor">sponsors</a>. The nature of their act would be curated or dictated by their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> and <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, then showcased in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">training sessions</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>. <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">Victors</a> would also be expected to maintain these personas long after their Games, for as long as they remained in the public eye.
</p>

<li>Some tributes, like <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, were known for their intelligence.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Tributes like <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> set out to intimidate.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> were presented to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> as sex symbols.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Training_scores">Training scores</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">Training score</a></i></center></div>
<p>Most tributes aimed to impress the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> and sponsors with high training scores. However, a high score could also make a tribute a target, marking them as the one to beat. In some cases, tributes would intentionally keep their skills hidden and get low scores on purpose.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Arena_strategies">Arena strategies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Alliances">Alliances</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a></i></center></div>
<p>Sometimes, tributes would form temporary truces in order to share resources, fight together, and/or protect one another inside the arena. <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">District partners</a> often banded together in this way, but cross-district alliances were not unheard of.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_alliance">Career alliance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Career_Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Career Tributes">Career Tributes</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b0/Careers_chase_Katniss.png/revision/latest?cb=20200712013631" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Career Tributes in the 74th Hunger Games." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/b0/Careers_chase_Katniss.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200712013631" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Careers chase Katniss.png" data-image-key="Careers_chase_Katniss.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Careers_chase_Katniss.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Career Tributes in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>One of the most common kinds of alliance was the Career alliance, also known as a Career pack. Tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">Districts 1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a> commonly trained to compete in the Games,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and they traditionally joined forces to wipe out the rest of the field before turning on one another. It was also typical for them to claim and divide up the supplies from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Newcomers">Newcomers</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">The Newcomers</a></i></center></div>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, the non-Career districts formed their own alliance known as the Newcomers.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, this alliance appeared to not be continued in later games.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tracking">Tracking</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>It was common for tributes to track one another down, usually with the goal of attacking them, as the Careers so often did.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, tributes would sometimes go looking to form an alliance. For example, <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> followed <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> until Katniss proposed an alliance,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Katniss later tracked <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<hr />
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Avoidance">Avoidance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Many tributes tried to bide their time in the arena by avoiding other tributes altogether. However, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> could counter this by using traps,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">feasts</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> to drive them together and force a fight to occur.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Hiding">Hiding</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Even if a tribute couldn't avoid their competition, they could find places to hide and stay unseen. For example, Rue was able to spy on the Careers' camp from a copse with thick foliage in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Katniss later concealed herself there overnight when she was incapacitated.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, this left tributes vulnerable in the event that they were discovered and cornered, like when <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> hid inside an air vent in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="Camouflage">Camouflage</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Camouflage" title="Camouflage">Camouflage</a></i></center></div>
<p>Tributes like Peeta<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the <a href="/wiki/Female_morphling" class="mw-redirect" title="Female morphling">female morphling</a> used camouflage to hide themselves in plain sight.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_21_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_21-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Tributes also camouflaged brightly-colored items on their person, like Katniss with her orange backpack, to prevent themselves from being spotted.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Stealth">Stealth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Stealthy tributes could move quietly throughout the arena without drawing attention to themselves. For example, Katniss developed a slow hunter's tread when she hunted in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Rue avoided other tributes by staying off the ground, leaping from tree to tree.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, stealth could also be used to launch an offensive, like the Career pack's ambush in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Combat">Combat</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>A tribute's approach to combat depended on the <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> at their disposal, as well as their skills and physical condition.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Hand-to-hand_combat">Hand-to-hand combat</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the early years of the Games, tributes from Districts 1, 2, 4, and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> tended to last longer in the Games because they were better-fed: Districts 1 and 2 due to wealth, and 4 and 11 due to their food-producing industries. This gave them a size and strength advantage that would serve them well in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Children in 4<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and 11 also worked in their districts' industries from young ages,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which taught them how to use nets and tridents<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or pitchforks and sickles. Tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> also shared the advantage of existing weapons skills, having learned to use axes for work in the lumber industry.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Once 1, 2, and 4 started training their tributes and became Career districts, most known Careers excelled in this area of combat. Career Tributes also maintained their size and strength advantage over those from more impoverished districts.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, an unarmed tribute could still gain the upper hand in a close-quarters fight against a Career with the use of an improvised weapon, like when <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> used a rock to crush Clove's skull.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_21_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_21-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Long-range_combat">Long-range combat</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Tributes who were at a disadvantage in terms of size and physical strength could turn to long-range combat, like Katniss with her <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow and arrow</a>, or Clove in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They could also ensnare an opponent from a distance, literally or figuratively, with the use of nets, snares, or clever tricks.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> had a particular knack for inventive long-range strategies. From District 3: Beetee was able to kill his opponents in his first Hunger Games with an electrical trap;<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Teslee" title="Teslee">Teslee</a> killed <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> using a salvaged <a href="/wiki/Drone" title="Drone">drone</a>; and the <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (74th HG)">District 3 male</a> in the 74th Hunger Games reburied and reactivated the Gamemakers' land mines, planting them to protect the Careers' supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As for the District 12 tributes: Lucy Gray smuggled rat poison into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> tricked the <a href="/wiki/District_1_female_(50th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 1 female (50th HG)">District 1 female</a> into throwing her axe at the arena's <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>, where it rebounded and killed her;<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Katniss and Peeta also weaponized poison against the Gamemakers themselves, threatening to commit double suicide by <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries unless they were both allowed to live.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Flight">Flight</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>When faced with a fight they knew they couldn't win, a tribute could choose to run away. For example, Foxface immediately fled the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and later in the Games, Peeta fled after being badly injured in his fight with Cato.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Resource_management">Resource management</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Hunting_and_foraging">Hunting and foraging</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D18&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Tributes with some skill and knowledge of hunting, foraging, trapping, or fishing could feed themselves with the arena's plant and animal life. However, this strategy proved fatal during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> because everything in the arena was poisonous.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Salvaging">Salvaging</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D19&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Tributes sometimes took items that other tributes had discarded, or more frequently, they claimed dead tributes' possessions as spoils of war. In the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, they often took each other's <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a>; however, this left them susceptible to decoy tactics where the items left behind could actually be harmful. This was the case when <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a> drank the last mouthful from a discarded water bottle that Lucy Gray had poisoned.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Tributes could gain access to <a href="/wiki/Food_and_drink" title="Food and drink">food and drink</a>, <a href="/wiki/Medicine" class="mw-redirect" title="Medicine">medicine</a>, and weapons by endearing themselves to sponsors during the Games. These people paid for tributes to receive gifts inside the arena, sent by their respective <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the 74th Hunger Games, Katniss and Peeta drew in sponsors by playing up the star-crossed lovers narrative.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_19_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_19-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Stealing">Stealing</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_tactics%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D21&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Tributes have also been known to steal from each other. Foxface maneuvered around a field of land mines to steal from the Careers, taking very little so the items' absence would not be noticed.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_15-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She later stole and ate poisonous nightlock berries that Peeta had been collecting, inadvertently killing her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
