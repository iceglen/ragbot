</p><p><b>Cookie</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a> assigned to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, where he worked as a cook. He was an old soldier who had lost his left ear in the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. <sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCookie%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>At some point Cookie enlisted as a soldier, and he lost his left ear during the Dark Days. He was later assigned to District 12 to cook for the other Peacekeepers in the base.
</p><p>Shortly after enrolling in the Peacekeepers himself, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> was assigned to kitchen detail under Cookie's guidance, stripping to the waist while he washed dishes for hours on end. He soon grew to crave the salty food Cookie favored.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Cookie gave Coriolanus a plastic bag to store ice to take outside in the heat, having been won over by his diligent dish washing.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> later went to talk Cookie out of another plastic bag before heading out to the lake along with Coriolanus and the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As they left the barracks for <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> one Saturday night, Coriolanus and Sejanus were roped into an extra detail by Cookie and unloaded a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> full of crates. Cookie slipped them a quart bottle of cheap whiskey when they were done, which they found to be a big improvement over the local brew.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During <a href="/wiki/Commander_Hoff" title="Commander Hoff">Commander Hoff</a>'s birthday, Cookie laid out an elegant meal, complete with steak, mashed potatoes and gravy, and fresh peas.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_29_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_29-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCookie%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Given that the Peacekeepers from District 12 used nicknames for each other, "Cookie" is probably a nickname given in reference to his role as their cook.</li></ul>
