<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAyomide_Adegun%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Adegun is a Nigerian-British actor who trained at the Royal Welsh College of Music &amp; Drama. During his final year at drama school, he booked a regular role opposite Mel Gibson in <i>The Continental</i>, produced for Starz by Lionsgate Television, and portrayed the younger version of Lance Reddick's iconic character Charon from the <i>John Wick</i> franchise.
</p><p>Adegun is represented by Hamilton Hodell and Goodman, Genow, Schenkman, Smelkinson &amp; Christopher.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
