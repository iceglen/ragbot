</p><p><i><b>The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond</b></i> is the official companion album for the 2012 film <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i>. "<a href="/wiki/Safe_%26_Sound" title="Safe &amp; Sound">Safe &amp; Sound</a>" by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Taylor Swift">Taylor Swift</a>, featuring <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Civil_Wars" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Civil Wars">The Civil Wars</a>, was released as a promotional single for the soundtrack. 
</p><p>On February 14, 2012, "One Engine" was made available for digital download on <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/iTunes" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:iTunes">iTunes</a>. The soundtrack was released on March 20, 2012, three days before the film's release in the United States.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Critical_reception">Critical reception</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Songs_from_District_12_and_Beyond%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Upon its release, the <i>Hunger Games'</i> official soundtrack received a rating of 3 out of 5 stars by the Rolling Stone magazine's reviewers. They stated how the soundtrack seemed to be totally directed for the fans, and how the producers bet on new acts such as the YouTube-phenomenon <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birdy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Birdy">Birdy</a> and the independent duo <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Civil_Wars" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Civil Wars">The Civil Wars</a>. On the website <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Metacritic">Metacritic</a>, based on reviews by six critics, the album scored 72 out of 100, which means "generally favorable reviews". 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Charts">Charts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Songs_from_District_12_and_Beyond%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><i>The Hunger Games: Songs From District 12 And Beyond</i> started at no.1 in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Billboard 200">Billboard 200</a> Albums Chart with 175,000 sales in its first week. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The soundtrack also hit the number one in Rock Albums, Alternative Albums, Folk Albums and Soundtracks. Around the world, it started at no.13 in New Zealand, no.14 in Australia, no.32 in Norway and no.61 in Austria. It ended up being the best-selling soundtrack of 2012 in the United States.
</p>

<p>"<i>Safe &amp; Sound</i>" by Taylor Swift featuring The Civil Wars, was released on December 23, 2011, as a promotional single. It reached number one on the iTunes overall charts in 12 hours. The music video for <i>Safe &amp; Sound&#160;</i>was released on 13th February 2012. Along with separate songs from Swift and The Civil Wars, the soundtrack also featured songs by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Decemberists" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Decemberists">The Decemberists</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maroon_5" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Maroon 5">Maroon 5</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcade_Fire" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Arcade Fire">Arcade Fire</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Secret_Sisters" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Secret Sisters">The Secret Sisters</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Lambert" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Miranda Lambert">Miranda Lambert</a> featuring <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pistol_Annies" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Pistol Annies">The Pistol Annies</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Case" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Neko Case">Neko Case</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kid_Cudi" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Kid Cudi">Kid Cudi</a>, Academy Award winner <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Hansard" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Glen Hansard">Glen Hansard</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Low_Anthem" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Low Anthem">The Low Anthem</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punch_Brothers" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Punch Brothers">Punch Brothers</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birdy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Birdy">Birdy</a>, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayme_Dee" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jayme Dee">Jayme Dee</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_Chocolate_Drops" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Carolina Chocolate Drops">Carolina Chocolate Drops</a>. The soundtrack was released on March 20, 2012. The soundtrack track list was revealed on iTunes On 13th February 2012. Jennifer Lawrence singing "Rue's Lullaby" will be released as a bonus download on March 27, 2012. On 14th February 2012, "One Engine" was released as the second promotional single by iTunes. Taylor Swift's second song, "<i>Eyes Open</i>", was the first official single to be released following two Digital/Promotional singles. "Eyes Open" was officially released to mainstream radio on March 27, 2012. A lyric video for the song was released on May 12, 2012, on Vevo.
</p>
