<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRory_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He lives in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> with his family of five, and is around the same age as <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s sister <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose</a>. He was 12 years old when Katniss left for her and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Rory was too young to work and had not yet learned the art of hunting. During the food shortage when Gale and <a href="/wiki/Hazelle_Hawthorne" title="Hazelle Hawthorne">Hazelle</a> were unable to work, he took out <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a> to help support the family, much to Gale's displeasure.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss wanted to try teaching Rory how to hunt, as Gale had no time to teach his brother himself.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He and his family were among the few people who survived the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, and they were evacuated to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRory_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rory has the same olive skin tone, grey eyes, and dark hair like many other typical of residents of the Seam.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRory_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rory has three siblings: Vick Hawthorne, Gale Hawthorne, and his only sister, Posy Hawthorne. His <a href="/wiki/Mr._Hawthorne" title="Mr. Hawthorne">father</a> died in a mine explosion along with Katniss Everdeen's father, five years prior to the start of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the Hawthornes are referred to as cousins of the Everdeens in order to invalidate a relationship between Gale Hawthorne and Katniss Everdeen.
</p><p>Rory's mother, Hazelle Hawthorne, raised her children by herself since her husband's death when Rory was six or seven.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRory_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The name <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rory" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rory">Rory</a> comes from Gaelic: "ruadh" meaning red, and "rígh" meaning king. His surname comes from hawthorn bushes or trees, but could also be a reference to author <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathaniel_Hawthorne" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nathaniel Hawthorne">Nathaniel Hawthorne</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRory_Hawthorne%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>All four Hawthorne siblings have four-letter names: Gale, Rory, Vick, and Posy.</li></ul>
