					<b class="trfc161__question">What is the origin of the Mockingjay bird in the Hunger Games?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">

				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						In the Hunger Games, the Mockingjay bird originates from unintended mating between mockingbirds and jabberjays. Jabberjays, male muttations created by the Capitol for espionage during the First Rebellion, could memorize and relay conversations. Once their use was discontinued, they mated with mockingbirds, leading to the birth of Mockingjays. These birds, not intended by the Capitol to exist, became symbols of rebellion in the series.

				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">How were Jabberjays used by the Capitol during the First Rebellion?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">

The rebels, however, began feeding the birds misinformation. This led the Capitol to abandon the Jabberjays, expecting them to perish in the wild. Contrarily, they bred with female mockingbirds, resulting in the birth of a new species, the Mockingjay, which later became a symbol of the Second Rebellion.

					<b class="trfc161__question">Why did the Capitol stop using Jabberjays?</b>

					<b class="trfc161__question">What is the significance of the Mockingjay symbol in the Hunger Games?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						In the Hunger Games, the Mockingjay bird symbolizes rebellion against the Capitol. After the 75th Hunger Games, protagonist Katniss Everdeen becomes 'the Mockingjay', embodying this defiance. The symbol is viewed by the Capitol as a rebellious act. Additionally, it signifies remembrance for characters like Madge and represents District 12 for Katniss, reminding her of her father and Rue. The Mockingjay's symbolism was recognized even before the 74th Hunger Games, underlining its enduring importance.

				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">Are Mockingjays based on real birds?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">

				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						Mockingjays, featured prominently in the Hunger Games, are not real birds. They're a fictional species resulting from the breeding of male jabberjays and female mockingbirds. Mockingbirds, which are real and known for mimicking sounds, contributed to the Mockingjays' ability to mimic songs and whistles. The black and crested Mockingjays, with white patches under their wings, symbolize rebellion in the Hunger Games universe.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Origin">Origin</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The father of the species was the jabberjay, an exclusively male <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttation</a> species that <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> invented to spy on <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">rebels</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. Jabberjays could memorize entire conversations and repeat them back to their Capitol handlers. However, rebels caught on and fed lies to the birds, sending them back loaded with misinformation. After that, the Capitol shut down the operation and jabberjays were released into the wild in the hope that they would die off. They eventually did, but not before mating with female mockingbirds, producing the mockingjay.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This was unforeseen, because no one expected jabberjays to be able to reproduce with other bird species.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It was never discovered by the Capitol exactly how the rebels learned the truth about the birds. <a href="/wiki/Dr._Kay" title="Dr. Kay">Dr. Kay</a> speculated two possibilities. The first was that they didn't cover their tracks well enough in making sure the information they acted on could have had a source other than a recording in the woods. The other was that the rebels had a spy at the base the birds were reporting to.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">"<i>The show’s not over until the mockingjay sings.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Lucy Gray Baird<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>At the <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a> of <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a>, mockingjays picked up a melodic arrangement of the man's dying words. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> suggested using the birds as target practice.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the following weeks, he and other <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> were assigned to help scientists catch and care for 50 jabberjays and 50 mockingjays. While the jabberjays were easy to catch, the mockingjays were more suspicious, making them harder to ensnare.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The captured birds were eventually flown back to the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a> in the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Snow joined the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> on a trip to the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a>, he observed mockingjays in the trees, but no jabberjays, and took it as a sign that the birds were reproducing without them. This disturbed him because it meant the birds had grown beyond the Capitol's control.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> tributes put their tokens back on before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> allowed <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> to examine his <a href="/wiki/Flint_striker" title="Flint striker">flint striker</a> which had been created by <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a>. Haymitch commented that he'd heard that Maysilee owned one of Tam's pins, but Maysilee revealed that she didn't think much of it. While the pin was well made, Maysilee didn't care much for mockingjays, finding a bird that was half <a href="/wiki/Mutt" class="mw-redirect" title="Mutt">mutt</a> to be unnatural which Haymitch had never considered before. Haymitch told Maysilee that some people thought that the way that the mockingjays had escaped the Capitol and survived was a victory in itself. Maysilee suggested that if she escaped the Capitol and survived, she'd give the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> another chance. Haymitch suggested that if Maysilee didn't, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove</a> would be happy to take it off of her hands.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR12_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR12-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Mockingjays were present at the burial of Haymitch's family, <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>, Maysilee, <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Jethro_Callow" title="Jethro Callow">Jethro Callow</a>, but they went silent when <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> began to sing. The mockingjays quickly picked up the melody and made it their own.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">Mr. Everdeen</a> was known to sing to the mockingjays,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and when he did, all the birds fell silent to listen. According to <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> had the same talent.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a> gave Katniss her <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_pin" title="Mockingjay pin">mockingjay pin</a> to wear as a <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, Katniss and her ally <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> sang to mockingjays in order to signal each other that they were alright. Rue had done something similar when she worked in the orchards of <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>. From high up in the trees, she was typically first to see the flag that signaled quitting time for the day. She would then whistle a <a href="/wiki/Rue%27s_four-note_song" title="Rue's four-note song">four-note melody</a> that would be picked up by local mockingjays and spread to all the workers.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Appearance">Appearance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3><p>
Mockingjays are black and glossy crested birds with white patches on the undersides of their wings. Coriolanus Snow described them as being slightly larger than their jabberjay fathers.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Like jabberjays, they are excellent mimics, and they're known for their ability to memorize and repeat both birdsong and human voices. Although mockingjays can't enunciate words, they can echo any vocalization from a child's high-pitched warble to a man's deep tones. They can even whistle the melodies of songs with multiple verses, if one has the patience to sing to them. They pick up tunes quickly and spread them to other nearby mockingjays. Supposedly, they will only repeat songs if they enjoy the singer's voice.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></p><div id="gallery-1" hash="bef266051d9fbfa92718c89ccb893161" data-seq-no="1" class="wikia-gallery wikia-gallery-caption-below wikia-gallery-position-left wikia-gallery-spacing-medium wikia-gallery-border-small wikia-gallery-captions-left wikia-gallery-caption-size-medium"><div class="wikia-gallery-caption"></div><div class="wikia-gallery-item" style="width:187px; <h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Habitat">Habitat</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>According to Dr. Kay, mockingjays could be found "all over Panem, wherever jabberjays and mockingbirds cohabited". In particular, she recounted sending jabberjays to <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> and District 11 as well as 12,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so mockingjays could exist in District 9 too. Katniss described them as being "about as rare as rocks. And about as tough", suggesting that they were very common and able to thrive in almost any environment.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Outside of the districts, the birds were also present in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="74th Hunger Games arena">74th Hunger Games arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Symbolism">Symbolism</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Mockingjay_on_fire.gif/revision/latest?cb=20120317155835" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A flaming mockingjay pin." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="184" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Mockingjay on fire.gif" data-image-key="Mockingjay_on_fire.gif" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Mockingjay_on_fire.gif/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120317155835"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Mockingjay_on_fire.gif/revision/latest?cb=20120317155835" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A flaming mockingjay pin." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Mockingjay_on_fire.gif/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120317155835" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="184" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mockingjay on fire.gif" data-image-key="Mockingjay_on_fire.gif" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/16/Mockingjay_on_fire.gif/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120317155835"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mockingjay_on_fire.gif" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A flaming mockingjay pin.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>Mockingjays have a certain level of symbolism in Panem, though the nature of that symbolism can vary widely. 
</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Rebellion">Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Upon realizing that the bird on her pin is a mockingjay, Katniss refers to the birds as "something of a slap in the face to the Capitol". Interestingly, this musing came before the 74th Hunger Games competition actually began, which suggests that the "rebellious" symbolism of the mockingjay was already established - or at least implied - before Katniss even arrived on the scene.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the 74th Hunger Games, largely because of Katniss and her pin, mockingjays became a widespread symbol of rebellion in the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> While out in the woods, Katniss encountered two women from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>, who were rebels on a journey to District 13. Although 13 was commonly believed to have been destroyed by the Capitol 75 years earlier, the women noticed that the exact same mockingjay appeared in many commercials and newsreels about the district, proving that the footage had been faked. To gain Katniss’ trust, they showed her a flat piece of bread that had been stamped with the image of a mockingjay (a sign that they were on her side and wanted to fight).<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> At the end of <i>Catching Fire</i>, Katniss realizes that, by her own volition or not, she is “the Mockingjay”, a living symbol of defiance against the Capitol and a rallying point for the oppressed people of Panem.
</p><p>After the 75th Hunger Games, wearing a mockingjay symbol in any form is considered a sign of rebellion by the Capitol — whether or not the wearer intended any harm.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Remembrance">Remembrance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For Madge, the bird and pin could symbolize remembrance, a relic from a relative that she was never able to know. Katniss wore the pin as her token in both the 74th<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and 75th Hunger Games<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, to represent District 12 and remind her of home. For Katniss herself, the birds are a reminder of her <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">father</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and later of Rue<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="In_the_Capitol">In the Capitol</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_%28bird%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Mockingjays have several distinctive meanings for Capitol citizens. On the one hand, they are a reminder of past mistakes, of the tricks played by the rebels and the Capitol's failure in killing off their own creation of jabberjays. On the other hand, images of mockingjays similar Katniss’ pin became a fashion trend after her and Peeta's joint victory; it was seen in jewelry, fashion accessories, and even tattoos. Everyone wanted to remember a particularly exciting Hunger Games by wearing the winner's token (Katniss mused that this use and popularity of the symbol must drive President Snow crazy). Plutarch even had a watch whose crystal face bore an image a mockingjay, but the image was only visible when exposed in a certain way, underlying Plutarch's secret connection to rebel forces. He showed this to Katniss not only to hint that he was on her side, but also to hint the nature of the arena in the 75th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
