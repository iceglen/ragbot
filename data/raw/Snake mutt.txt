<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnake_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> first encountered snake mutts in the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a> lab. Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a>, used them to determine whether a paper that Coriolanus had given her was solely his work or a collaborative effort. Because the paper had in fact been a solo work, the mutts did not recognize the scent of Coriolanus's classmate, <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a>, and bit her hand.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She was immediately injected with black fluid from a hypodermic needle, at which point she passed out and was taken to the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Hospital" title="Capitol Hospital">Capitol Hospital</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the evening on the third day of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, Coriolanus went to see Dr. Gaul in her lab for a follow-up appointment about his wounded arm. On his way out, he came upon two lab assistants trying to get a cart with a tank of snake mutts onto the elevator. Coriolanus asked where the snakes were headed to, and the assistants claimed they were being taken to another lab. However, Coriolanus was suspicious that they would be used in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, so he dropped a <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow%27s_handkerchief" title="Crassus Snow&#39;s handkerchief">handkerchief</a> into the tank. He hoped that exposing them to the scent of his <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, would deter them from attacking her.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 150px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/06/Lucy_Gray_snakes_in_arena.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231229042259" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Lucy Gray being swarmed by snakes." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/06/Lucy_Gray_snakes_in_arena.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20231229042259" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="126" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Lucy Gray snakes in arena.jpg" data-image-key="Lucy_Gray_snakes_in_arena.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Lucy_Gray_snakes_in_arena.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Lucy Gray being swarmed by snakes.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>As he predicted, the snakes were set upon the tributes on the fourth day of the Games as retribution for the death of <a href="/wiki/Gaius_Breen" title="Gaius Breen">Gaius Breen</a>. They killed <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a> and <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>, then flocked to Lucy Gray<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> harmlessly as she sang "<a href="/wiki/The_Old_Therebefore" title="The Old Therebefore">The Old Therebefore</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>By the fifth and final day of the Games, the mutts were all seemingly dead, either drowned in an overnight storm or unable to handle the cold, wet night. However, Lucy Gray was able to protect one from the elements by keeping it in her skirt pocket. When <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> attacked her, she fished the snake out and it bit him in the back of the neck, killing him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, while <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was recovering in a Capitol lab from his injuries, a snake mutt ate the <a href="/wiki/Wildlife" title="Wildlife">rabbit</a> that had helped him in the Games in front of Haymitch, presumably one of Snow's efforts to torment Haymitch.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnake_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, Lucy Gray killed Treech with rat poison rather than a snake. The mutts were released shortly afterward on the second day of the Games, which would be the last. This was done to avenge the death of <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a>'s son, <a href="/wiki/Felix_Ravinstill" title="Felix Ravinstill">Felix Ravinstill</a>. The snakes killed <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a>, <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Appearance">Appearance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnake_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The snakes were of various bright colors, including neon pink, yellow, and blue. Each was no longer than a ruler and not much thicker than a pencil. They could not see too well and could hear even less, but were bred to have a more acute sense of smell than normal snakes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Dr. Gaul later explained that the mutts were only snakelets, so they were not yet fully grown. At maturity, she expected that they would be able to "easily outrun a human" and scale difficult or unusual terrain.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Effects">Effects</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnake_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/91/Clemensia_and_snakes.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20230926201932" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Clemensia reaching into the snakes&#39; tank." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/91/Clemensia_and_snakes.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230926201932" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Clemensia and snakes.jpg" data-image-key="Clemensia_and_snakes.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Clemensia_and_snakes.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Clemensia reaching into the snakes' tank.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Their venom was fast-acting and lethal, causing bright pus to seep from the victims' wounds. Despite this, one could recover if the antivenom was administered quickly enough, as was the case for Clemensia;<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> however, even then she would still suffer <a href="/wiki/Health_conditions#Muttation" title="Health conditions">side effects</a> for as long as a year afterward.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> These side effects included <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaundice" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jaundice">yellowing of the eyes</a>, motor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/tic" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:tic">tics</a>, growth of scales, and dry, peeling skin.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnake_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSnake_mutt%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>This was the first ever use of mutts in the Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the film franchise, the snake and <a href="/wiki/Lizard_mutt" title="Lizard mutt">lizard mutts</a> each killed four people.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>By the end of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, Snow had also killed four or five people: <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mayfair_Lipp" title="Mayfair Lipp">Mayfair Lipp</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> (indirectly),<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, and potentially Lucy Gray Baird.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
