<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEddy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the bombing, Eddy and <a href="/wiki/Eddy%27s_sister" title="Eddy's sister">his sister</a> were injured and ended up in the <a href="/wiki/District_8_hospital" title="District 8 hospital">makeshift hospital</a> that Katniss Everdeen visited while filming a <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a>. He was in awe when he got the chance to meet her. He and his sister, who was a few beds down, were some of the first patients to notice Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEddy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Katniss left the hospital, <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> began an attack, which destroyed the hospital and killed the people within it. Eddy and his sister were confirmed deceased, as it was stated that there were no survivors from the bombings of the hospital.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEddy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Eddy had damp, curly brown hair that covered his forehead. Half of his face was obscured by bandages while he was in the hospital.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
