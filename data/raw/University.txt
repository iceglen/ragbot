</p><p>The <b>University</b> was an institution of higher education located in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. Many students from the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> later went on to study at the University. The tuition was high, though there were financial prizes available for those who could not afford it. These prizes were awarded to students who had performed well at the Academy.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>, who served as Head <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, was a biology professor at the University<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and also taught advanced classes in military strategy.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FUniversity%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> and <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a> attended the University together. During an assignment from Dr. Gaul's class where they had to create an extreme punishment for the districts, the two of them paired up. Highbottom created the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, and Snow turned it in without his permission, wanting the grade. After the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, Dr. Gaul took out his proposal and introduced it to <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> graduated from the Academy, she passed up the opportunity to attend the University so she could pursue her dream of becoming a designer.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Remus_Dolittle" title="Remus Dolittle">Remus Dolittle</a> graduated from the University shortly before the reaping of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. He became <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>-in-Training after his family pulled many favors to get him the position.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> had long expected to attend the University, like many of his peers, but his perilous financial situation put his chances in jeopardy. He hoped that a good showing as a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> in the 10th Hunger Games would provide him with a financial prize that would enable him to attend.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Later, when <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> asked Coriolanus about his plans after the Games, he responded that he hoped to go on to University and would pay the tuition if not granted a prize, but Highbottom only laughed at him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> entered and emerged from the arena, he and his father, <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a>, announced that they would be giving a prize for a full ride to the University to the mentor whose tribute won the Hunger Games. This was done in an attempt to bury Sejanus's rebellious behavior, and led to excitement and gratitude from the mentors.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coriolanus's expulsion from the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> and enrollment as a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a> appeared to ruin his chance to attend the University.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Ultimately, however, Dr. Gaul manipulated his return to the Capitol and his enrollment at the University.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> His schooling was paid for by <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a>, in the wake of the loss of his own son, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The winter before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, one of the students of the University dyed every fountain on campus pink while there was a shortage of face cream. It was received positively.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
