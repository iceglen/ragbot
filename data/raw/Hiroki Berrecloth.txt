</p><p><b>Hiroki Berrecloth</b> is a British-Japanese actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHiroki_Berrecloth%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Berrecloth appeared in the BBC's <i>Red Rose</i> and the short films <i>Black Lichen</i>, <i>The Intruder</i>, and <i>Why Do You Look at Me with Those Eyes?</i>. He is represented by Simon &amp; How.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Berrecloth originally auditioned to play the role of <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, whose character he "immediately... connected with," before being called back to audition as Treech. In an interview, he stated that he read the books and watched the films as a kid, and <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> was a "standout character" from the original trilogy. <i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i> was his first feature film.<sup id="cite_ref-interview_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-interview-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
