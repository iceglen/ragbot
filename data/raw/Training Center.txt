</p><p>The <b>Training Center</b>, also known as the <b>Tribute Center</b>, is a building located in the middle of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, just behind the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">president's mansion</a> on the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a>. Upon arriving to the city in preparation for the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, it is where <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> live and train. The center has an open roof, which includes a garden and provides an excellent view of the Capitol. It is also enclosed by a <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> to thwart any attempt at suicide.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Apartments">Apartments</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 239px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/61/Training_Center_exterior.png/revision/latest?cb=20131002212805" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Exterior of the Training Center as portrayed in " src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/61/Training_Center_exterior.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20131002212805" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="186" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Training Center exterior.png" data-image-key="Training_Center_exterior.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Training_Center_exterior.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Exterior of the Training Center as portrayed in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The tributes and their entourages each stay on a floor corresponding with number of their district, which are reached via glass-sided elevators that move at rapid speeds. Training Center apartments feature luxury accommodations, including gourmet meals, high-tech showers, and a huge array of clothing to choose from. The apartments are apparently quite large— <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> described her bedroom in the Training Center as bigger than her family's entire house in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>— and contain enough rooms to house the tributes' <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> and <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>, and possibly their <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylists</a> and <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep teams</a> as well, as the latter are a regular presence in the apartment before the Games commence.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avoxes</a> also live in the apartments, and their sole duty is to serve the inhabitants and tend to every person's needs. An Avox can be a citizen of any district, as well as the Capitol, who has committed an offense and has been transferred into Capitol custody. The punishment for their crime is cutting out their tongue so that they are unable to speak. Inhabitants of the apartment may speak to Avoxes to give them orders, but any other communication is strictly forbidden.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Once training for the Games begins, breakfast and dinner are served in the apartments, while lunch is taken in a room off the gymnasium in the basement.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On the last day of training and following individual sessions with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a>, tributes receive their <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training scores</a> on the televisions in their apartments.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games"><i>The Hunger Games</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>Katniss Everdeen and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> are assigned an apartment for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> according to their district. It is where they eat their meals, discuss strategies for the upcoming Games, and prepare for interviews broadcast on television across the country. During her stay, Katniss recognizes a female Avox as someone she and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> had seen while hunting in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside District 12. From a distance and while hidden, they had witnessed her capture as she attempted to run from the authorities. Katniss felt guilty about not attempting to save her, as she had been seen by the girl during her capture.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peeta later learns that her name is <a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire"><i>Catching Fire</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss and Peeta are given the same apartment as the year before. Their new Avox is <a href="/wiki/Darius" title="Darius">Darius</a>, an ex-<a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> for District 12 who was turned into an Avox for his attempt to interfere with Gale's flogging by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss believes that he had been assigned to them on purpose to unnerve her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, in the film, Darius was not introduced, and Effie Trinket said the Tribute Center was built brand new in honor of the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mockingjay"><i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Katniss assassinates <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">President Coin</a>, she is taken back to her old Training Center apartment, blindfolded and handcuffed. She remains there, addicted to <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> tablets and attempting to kill herself, until her mentor <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> comes to get her and tells her that her trial is over, and she is going home.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Gymnasium">Gymnasium</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1e/Katniss_snares_station.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419072451" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss at the snares station." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1e/Katniss_snares_station.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120419072451" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="225" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss snares station.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_snares_station.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_snares_station.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss at the snares station.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Beneath the apartments lies an underground gymnasium where the tributes prepare and practice for the challenges they will face in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. The space is filled with <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">training stations</a> which teach a variety of skills, including knot tying, identifying edible plants and insects, <a href="/wiki/Camouflage" title="Camouflage">camouflage</a>, and practicing with many types of <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>. Experts are on hand at each station to instruct and serve as training partners to the tributes, as they are forbidden to fight with each other before the Games begin.
</p><p>The clothing each tribute wears is determined by their stylist, although each has a cloth square with their district number pinned to their shirt.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the films of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, however, the tributes wear identical training uniforms marked with their district number.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Routine">Routine</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Line_of_tributes.png/revision/latest?cb=20200723034112" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tributes in line for an obligatory gauntlet." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="147" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Line of tributes.png" data-image-key="Line_of_tributes.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Line_of_tributes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20200723034112" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Line_of_tributes.png/revision/latest?cb=20200723034112" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tributes in line for an obligatory gauntlet." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Line_of_tributes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20200723034112" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="147" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Line of tributes.png" data-image-key="Line_of_tributes.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/39/Line_of_tributes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20200723034112" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Line_of_tributes.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Tributes in line for an obligatory gauntlet.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Tributes attend training three days in a row, beginning at 10 A.M. (though almost all the tributes arrived early during the 74th Hunger Games, and some of the tributes in the 75th Hunger Games didn't even show up to training at all). At 10:00 sharp, <a href="/wiki/Atala" title="Atala">Atala</a>, the head trainer and overseer, explains the rules of the gymnasium and its training stations. In <i>The Hunger Games</i> film, she emphasizes to the tributes not to ignore survival skills because they are just as important as the weapon stations. Katniss likes the unpopular snare section, and learns a snare that can leave a human hanging from one foot. The trainees are reminded that they cannot fight with each other before the Games, but if they want to spar, they can request to practice with a trainer. The <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career tributes</a> typically go directly to the stations with the most deadly weapons when training starts in an attempt to intimidate the other tributes. Afterwards, all the tributes eat lunch together in a dining room attached to the gym. <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliances</a> are often formed during this time after the tributes have seen each other's abilities in training and have a chance to talk.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Private_sessions">Private sessions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Katniss_peeta_private_sessions.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419082251" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta tells Katniss to shoot straight in her private session with the Gamemakers." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="162" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss peeta private sessions.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_peeta_private_sessions.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Katniss_peeta_private_sessions.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20120419082251" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Katniss_peeta_private_sessions.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419082251" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta tells Katniss to shoot straight in her private session with the Gamemakers." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Katniss_peeta_private_sessions.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20120419082251" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="162" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss peeta private sessions.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_peeta_private_sessions.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/59/Katniss_peeta_private_sessions.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20120419082251" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_peeta_private_sessions.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peeta tells Katniss to shoot straight in her private session with the Gamemakers.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After a period of time, the tributes show the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> what they are capable of, choosing a particular skill they excel at, and are scored from 1 to 12, 1 being very low and 12 being almost impossible to achieve.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> both received a score of 12 as a result of their actions in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> and during their private sessions. However, they didn't necessarily earn this 12, as it was deliberately given to them so that the other tributes would target them.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/ab/Seneca_impressed.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419072659" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Seneca Crane after Katniss shoots an arrow through the apple." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="160" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Seneca impressed.jpg" data-image-key="Seneca_impressed.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/ab/Seneca_impressed.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120419072659" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/ab/Seneca_impressed.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419072659" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Seneca Crane after Katniss shoots an arrow through the apple." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/ab/Seneca_impressed.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120419072659" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="160" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Seneca impressed.jpg" data-image-key="Seneca_impressed.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/ab/Seneca_impressed.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20120419072659" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Seneca_impressed.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Seneca Crane after Katniss shoots an arrow through the apple.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Katniss always surprised the Gamemakers in her private sessions. In the 74th Hunger Games, she shot an arrow directly at their table, skewering an apple in the mouth of a roasted pig that had been brought in for them, and then walked out without being dismissed.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> For the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, she created an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/effigy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:effigy">effigy</a> of <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a>, wrote his name on it with berry juice, and hung it by a noose.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peeta painted a picture of how <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a> looked after Katniss covered her in flowers. Their odds of winning are then shown in the morning line odds. Both got perfect scores of 12.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_portrayal">Film portrayal</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the building appeared to not be as tall as described in the book, nowhere near as large, but used for more than housing tributes. Among other aspects, the building included a balcony above the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a> in which <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> made his annual speech standing alongside other government officials.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a>,</i> the building appeared much taller and more accurate to the description in the novel. Effie explained that this building was brand new, accounting for differences from the first film.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, belowground levels of the Tribute Center served as a prison for the captured Hunger Games <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a>. As a result, this is where the <a href="/wiki/Infiltration_of_the_Capitol" title="Infiltration of the Capitol">infiltration of the Capitol</a> took place.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTraining_Center%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The gymnasium set for the <i>Catching Fire</i> film was built in a parking garage in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Atlanta">Atlanta</a>, Georgia, and the lobby and elevator sequences were shot at the Atlanta Marriott Marquis. Originally, the District 12 apartment was going to be shot on a soundstage, but they ultimately built the set on the hotel's vacant tenth floor instead.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
