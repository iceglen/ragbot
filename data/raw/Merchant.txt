<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerchant%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Shortly after the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the bakery was owned by an unknown "beet-faced woman" who had "little interest in providing directions to nonpaying customers".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The butcher's daughter also got married around that time, hiring the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> to perform live music at the wedding. Since the event was held in town,<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> her new spouse was most likely another merchant.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Hunger_Games_period">Hunger Games period</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerchant%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As District 12's wealthiest citizens, merchant children did not typically need to take out <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a>, so their names were not entered into the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> extra times in exchange for grain and oil.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This could partially explain why out of District 12's known <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>, only two were merchants. The first was Maysilee Donner, the daughter of the sweet shop owners, friend of Asterid March,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and twin sister of Merrilee Donner. She was reaped to participate in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, and she formed an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> with <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, who ended up becoming the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In their youth, Mr. Mellark had wanted to marry Mrs. Everdeen, but she fell in love with <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">Mr. Everdeen</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> from the Seam.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mr. Mellark married a woman who would become <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Mellark" title="Mrs. Mellark">Mrs. Mellark</a>, and they had three sons together, the youngest of which was Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Mr. and Mrs. Everdeen married, so she moved to the Seam. The same year that Peeta was born, the Everdeens had <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a>, and their second daughter <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose</a> was born four years later.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After Mr. Everdeen died and Katniss took charge of the household, Mr. Mellark traded generously with the Everdeen daughters.<sup id="cite_ref-:10_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:10-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Peeta Mellark was District 12's second known merchant tribute in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. Like Maysilee, he also had his name called in the reaping rather than <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteering</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and he allied with Seam tribute (and later co-victor) Katniss Everdeen.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The following year, Peeta would volunteer in place of Haymitch Abernathy for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerchant%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Like most parts of the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>, the merchant sector was destroyed in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, and most of its people were killed. Fewer than a dozen merchants escaped, so the vast majority of survivors were from the Seam.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The only known merchants that survived the bombing were Delly Cartwright and her <a href="/wiki/Delly_Cartwright%27s_brother" title="Delly Cartwright's brother">younger brother</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> as well as Peeta, who had not been in the district at the time. <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> led refugees to the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside District 12, and upon discovery and rescue by <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a>, they were all granted citizenship in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerchant%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>People belonging to District 12's merchant class were typically described as having blond hair and blue eyes. Since their families were wealthier than their Seam counterparts, merchant children rarely had to take out tesserae.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, merchants were still poor even compared to the citizens other districts, and especially compared to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. Peeta Mellark's family often had to eat stale bread because they couldn't afford to eat their own expensive bakery goods.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_23_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_23-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Merchant and Seam children attended <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a> and learned about their district's coal trade together, preparing them for the possibility of employment in the mines<sup id="cite_ref-:10_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:10-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> after their last reaping at 18 years old.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> But while Seam children grew up unemployed, merchants worked in their parents' shops well before the age of 18.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Merchant goods were far too expensive for most Seam residents to buy,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so their businesses were probably patronized most often by other merchants and <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">peacekeepers</a>. Victors were also known to use their winnings to buy from merchants,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_11_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_11-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but since District 12 had so few victors, this likely did not make up a substantial portion of their income.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMerchant%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid Everdeen</a> is the only member of the merchant sector known to have moved to the Seam.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> is District 12's only merchant victor.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> mentioned seeing run-down storefronts in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>'s town square, but commented that "Wherever the well-to-do live in District 11 live, it's not [there]."<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This suggests that District 11 may not have a merchant class in the same way District 12 does, or at least that the class dynamics manifested differently.</li>
<li>In the films, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was portrayed with blond hair and blue eyes, which could potentially point to him being a merchant. This would be inconsistent with the books, where Haymitch is repeatedly established as being from the Seam.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Lily_Elsington" title="Lily Elsington">Lily Elsington</a>, a character created as part of the <a href="/wiki/District_Heroes" class="mw-redirect" title="District Heroes">District Heroes</a> ad campaign, was dressed up as a coal miner in a portrait promoting <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, her blonde hair and blue eyes suggest that she may actually be from a merchant family.</li></ul>
