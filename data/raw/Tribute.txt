<p><b>Tributes</b> were residents of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s twelve <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> who were <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> or <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> to participate in the annual <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. Except in special cases such as <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quells</a>, 24 children between the ages of 12 and 18 were chosen at random to participate. Every district had to send one girl and one boy to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, who then fought to the death in an <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> until only one <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> remained.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Choosing_the_tributes">Choosing the tributes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">Reaping</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 200px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/eb/Effie-Reaping-Bowl-The-Hunger-Games.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130420112448" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt=" during the " src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/e/eb/Effie-Reaping-Bowl-The-Hunger-Games.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20130420112448" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="151" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Effie-Reaping-Bowl-The-Hunger-Games.jpg" data-image-key="Effie-Reaping-Bowl-The-Hunger-Games.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Effie-Reaping-Bowl-The-Hunger-Games.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption"><a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Each year, all children aged twelve to eighteen were separated into their respective age groups and designated to different areas. There were two glass <a href="/wiki/Reaping_balls" title="Reaping balls">reaping balls</a>, one for the boys and one for the girls. Each one contained slips of paper with the names of the district's children. The district's <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a> picked one name out of each, and the two children they selected became that year's tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>However, not everyone's chances of being reaped were equal; children who were twelve were entered once, thirteen-year-olds twice, and so on until they were eighteen with at least seven entries. A child's name could also be entered more times if they opted to receive <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a>. For each tessera taken by a child (with a maximum of however many people were in their family), their name was added an extra time in exchange for a year's supply of grain and oil for one person. However, these entries were cumulative, so all entries were rolled over to the next year. For example, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> took tesserae for five family members since he was first entered into the reaping, and so had his name entered forty-two times by the 74th Hunger Games. <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> had her name entered twenty times, while her twelve-year-old sister's name was entered only once.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

By rule, once a person's name had been chosen to become a tribute, another eligible boy or girl could volunteer to take their place. Normally volunteers were asked for after the chosen person is introduced, but Katniss Everdeen volunteered spontaneously before her sister <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> could mount the stage. </p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Quarter_Quells">Quarter Quells</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a></i></center></div>
<p><a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quells</a> were special editions of the Hunger Games that were held every 25 years: a quarter of a century, hence the name "Quarter Quell." Each one had a cruel twist that would remind the districts of their failed <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">rebellion</a>, further punishing them for it and keeping the memory fresh.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribute" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tribute">Tribute</a> is wealth, often in the kind that one party gives to another as a sign of respect or, as was often the case in historical contexts, of submission or allegiance. Failure to pay tribute constituted an act of rebellion. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Ancient Rome">Ancient Rome</a> extracted tribute from its provinces and subject kingdoms, whereas the Capitol demanded tribute from the districts in the form of competitors for the Games.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>At the start of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i>, there had been 1,776 tributes in the history of the event.
<ul><li><i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> revealed that the annual reaping was held on July 4th.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This coincides with the United States' <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_(United_States)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Independence Day (United States)">Independence Day</a>, which commemorates the signing of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Declaration_of_Independence" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:United States Declaration of Independence">Declaration of Independence</a> on July 4th, 1776.</li></ul></li>
<li>Over all 75 years of the Hunger Games, there were 1,800 unique tributes. There were also 75 victors,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but eighteen died in the 75th Hunger Games,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so the annual event ultimately claimed 1,743 lives.
<ul><li>However, only seven victors survived the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a> and <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so 1,793 tributes had died by the end of the war— a 99% mortality rate.</li></ul></li>
<li>The word "tribute" is also the official name for fans of the film franchise. It was primarily chosen after numerous fan sites conducted a survey on which names should be chosen; some of the names included in the survey were <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>, rebels, and several other names, such as <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a>.</li></ul>
