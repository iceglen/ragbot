<p>The <b>chariot rides</b>, also known as the <b>Tribute Parade</b> or <b>opening ceremonies</b>, marked the beginning of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>' annual festivities. <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">Tributes</a> were required to ride chariots in a procession through <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> starting at the <a href="/wiki/Remake_Center" title="Remake Center">Remake Center</a> and ending outside the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">President's mansion</a> in the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a>, before finally pulling into the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>. This was a chance for the tributes to make an impression on <a href="/wiki/Sponsors" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsors">sponsors</a>, which could be crucial to their survival in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. This was also the first time the Capitol audience would see the tributes in person, and it would give them an idea of what to expect from each <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>'s competitors.
</p><p>To show their approval, the audience would cheer for the tributes they liked, sometimes even throwing small objects in admiration (in her first parade, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> caught a rose thrown by an audience member).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> To make a good impression, most tributes would smile and wave and appear friendly, but other tactics could be employed: for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> were instructed to appear aloof and uncompromising, as if the entire event was beneath them; this also made quite an impression on the audience.
</p><p>The chariots were typically decorated to reflect the traits of each district's industry, and it was required that the districts&#160;enter the procession in numerical order, starting with <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and finishing with <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChariot_rides%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="25th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChariot_rides%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the 25th Hunger Games, or the first <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, the chariot rides were introduced.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_13_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_13-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChariot_rides%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Thanks to the errors of their <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a>, District 12 was late in joining the procession. All of the other Districts' chariots were tricked out, some looking menacing and others elegant, with District 12 looking rickety by comparison. The horses driving the chariot were poorly trained and nervous from the go. The tributes were met with jeers from a drunken crowd. When someone in the crowd tossed a firework, it panicked the already unstable horses, resulting in them racing through the crowd and the other chariots until they overturned. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> was badly rattled by the experience, but <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a> was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_5_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_5-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Incitatus_Loomy" title="Incitatus Loomy">Incitatus Loomy</a> was the parade master responsible for the affair. As punishment for the disastrous scene, he was killed by being fed a plate of poisoned oysters.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_9_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_9-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
