<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDimitri_Abold%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Abold graduated from New York University's Tisch School of the Arts. In Germany, he has worked with renowned film directors, including Marcus H. Rosenmüller and Dominik Graf. He is also playing the lead role in the upcoming German Amazon series <i>Love Addicts</i>.
Internationally, Abold is best known for his supporting role in the Netflix series <i>Warrior Nun</i> and will appear in an episode of the Amazon Original series <i>Tom Clancy's Jack Ryan</i>. He is represented by Baumbauer Actors.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
