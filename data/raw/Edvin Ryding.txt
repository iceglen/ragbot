</p><p><b>Edvin Ryding</b> is a Swedish actor set to play <a href="/wiki/Vitus" title="Vitus">Vitus</a> in the upcoming film, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Capitolites_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Capitolites-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FEdvin_Ryding%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ryding became internationally known for his starring role as Prince Wilhelm in the Netflix show <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Royals" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Young Royals">Young Royals</a></i>.  Before his global breakthrough, he had already established a strong career in Sweden, appearing in roughly 20 Swedish television shows and films since the age of five. In 2022, he was recognized on the Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe list. He later made his Hollywood debut as <a target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" class="text" href="https://28dayslater.fandom.com/wiki/Erik_Sundqvist">Erik Sundqvist</a> in <i>28 Years Later,</i> this being his first Hollywood role.
