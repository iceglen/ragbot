</p><p><b>Francis Lawrence</b> is an American/Austrian music video, TV, and film director. He directed the four sequels in <i>The Hunger Games</i> film franchise.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFrancis_Lawrence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was announced as the new director of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> after <a href="/wiki/Gary_Ross" title="Gary Ross">Gary Ross</a>, director of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, bowed out of directing the sequel. Lawrence was first reported to be the <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i> director on April 19, 2012<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and was confirmed on May 3.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Previous feature films he directed include the sci-fi hit<i> <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Am_Legend" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:I Am Legend">I Am Legend</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_for_Elephants_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Water for Elephants (film)">Water for Elephants</a></i>. He also went on to direct <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">Part 2</a></i> in 2014 and 2015, as well as the 2023 prequel film, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-Collider_Film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Collider_Film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and is returning to direct the 2026 <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Comic_Book_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Comic_Book-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFrancis_Lawrence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Although <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence" title="Jennifer Lawrence">Jennifer Lawrence</a> and Francis Lawrence share the same last name, they have no relation.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Rachel_Zegler" title="Rachel Zegler">Rachel Zegler</a> said she was too scared to ask <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lionsgate">Lionsgate</a> to release the studio version of "<a href="/wiki/The_Old_Therebefore" title="The Old Therebefore">The Old Therebefore</a>", so she "made Francis do it" for her.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the prequel film, the monument of <a href="/wiki/Trajan_Heavensbee" title="Trajan Heavensbee">Trajan Heavensbee</a> was modelled after Francis Lawrence's face.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Lawrence also directed a similar dystopian film, titled <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Long_Walk_(2025_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Long Walk (2025 film)">The Long Walk</a></i>.</li></ul>
