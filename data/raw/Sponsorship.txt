<p><b>Sponsorship</b> was the program which allowed a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> to send helpful items to a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. These gifts were sent with donations from sponsors, such as rich citizens from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> or family and friends in the tribute's home <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>. Tributes were sponsored based on their first impression at the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training scores</a>, impression in the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, performance in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, as well as a number of other factors.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mechanics">Mechanics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The sponsorship program was created in the lead-up to the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, as part of a project proposed by <a href="/wiki/Dr._Gaul" class="mw-redirect" title="Dr. Gaul">Dr. Gaul</a>. Along with it came the idea of <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">betting</a> on the tributes. This was done so the Capitol audience would engage more with the tributes and draw more attention to the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Through mentors, sponsors could donate money to support their favorite tributes. Mentors could then choose what to send into the arena and when. Tributes could be sponsored by individuals or groups, as was the case for <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>; for example, <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a> started up a collection at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a> to sponsor them in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and a number of people in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> chipped in to send Katniss a loaf of their district's signature <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The longer the Games go on, the more expensive it was to send anything.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sponsor_gifts">Sponsor gifts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sponsor gifts generally consisted of <a href="/wiki/Food_and_drink" title="Food and drink">food and drink</a>, <a href="/wiki/Medical_treatments" title="Medical treatments">medicine</a>, <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Tools_and_gear" title="Tools and gear">tools</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the 10th Hunger Games, mentors sent sponsor gifts via <a href="/wiki/Communicuff" title="Communicuff">communicuff</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and they were able to do so very often, but the catalog was limited to bottles of water and a small selection of food (apples, bread, and cheese). These were delivered by <a href="/wiki/Drone" title="Drone">drones</a> that found their recipients using facial recognition,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but it was a flawed system. The very first gift of water had to be re-sent,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and sponsor gifts were the tributes' only means of obtaining food and water inside the arena. This incentivized tributes stealing each other's gifts. <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> tributes <a href="/wiki/Teslee" title="Teslee">Teslee</a> and <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a> were even able to manipulate the drones themselves, weaponizing them against other tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="49th_Hunger_Games">49th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Throughout the 49th Hunger games, the Gamemakers repeatedly attempted and failed to sponsor gifts to <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, her having found a blind spot in the Arena. They could not discover her blind spot any more than her fellow tributes and attempted to joke about it, but were visibly embarrassed.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_6_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_6-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Later_years">Later years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In later editions of the Games, items were delivered by <a href="/wiki/Silver_parachute" title="Silver parachute">silver parachutes</a>. However, any gift that was sent cost an exorbitant amount,<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> with the cost increasing the longer the Games went on.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Strategies">Strategies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Mentors could use the timing and selection of their gifts to communicate indirectly with their tributes. For instance, in the 74th Hunger Games, Katniss was able to glean ideas about strategy from <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s choice to send or withhold gifts from her.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">Rebels</a> did something similar with the <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> of rebel <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>; they sent coded messages in the form of <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>, based on the number of rolls sent and the district of origin, in order to signal the time and day of their rescue from the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>On the morning of Day 2, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> received a very generous bundle containing a dozen warm white rolls, a block of orange cheese, and a wine bottle of grape juice with its own glass goblet. Haymitch raised a toast thanking his fellow rascals from the Capitol and guessed that having evaded the bloodbath with supplies and weapons, survived a poisoning, made fire, cooked food, torched some butterfly mutts and found a tree to sleep in, he had proved himself to be fairly resourceful and selfish enough to win.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Haymitch and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>, they received a pot of bean and ham hock soup.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Buck" title="Buck">Buck</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chicory" title="Chicory">Chicory</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a> appeared to have recently received a sponsor gift before their deaths as their <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpacks</a> had crackers, baked beans and the unexpected treat of raisins mixed with nuts and candy.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR21_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR21-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the morning of Day 6, Haymitch and Maysilee received a gift of a breakfast of fresh corn bread, buttermilk and peaches.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR22_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR22-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following Maysilee's death, Haymitch received a gift of ice cream and black coffee to help remind Haymitch of his friend's sass.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR23_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR23-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As Haymitch tried to feed an emaciated <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a>, he received a gift of vanilla pudding and chocolate for the little girl, proving that someone in the Capitol still had a heart. Haymitch shared some of the chocolate with <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR23_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR23-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On Day 7, Haymitch received a jug of milk with the jug being either identical to or the same one that he had previously seen in <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>'s house. The gift was marked as being from the Capitol, but Haymitch concluded that it was from President Snow himself and that the milk was full of <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Poison" title="Weapons">poison</a>. By sending it to Haymitch, Snow hoped to force him to either kill Wellie or use it to commit suicide in an attempt to save the little girl. Before Haymitch could drink it, Silka found and killed Wellie. Haymitch smashed the pitcher<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and it was removed from the heavily edited footage of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Differences_between_the_books_and_films">Differences between the books and films</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSponsorship%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D19&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The gifts did not come in a protective silver container in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">books</a>.</li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_film" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games film"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, Katniss only received the burn medicine and soup.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the movies, the parachutes let out a distinctive chime,<sup id="cite_ref-:1_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but in the books, they were silent.</li>
<li>In the movies, the gifts Katniss received were accompanied with a small note from Haymitch to hint what she needed to do with the gift or how she needed to act to stay alive.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_32-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, in the books, there were no notes; Katniss was left to interpret for herself what each gift meant.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, after the first drone was shattered, there was no indication that the gift was re-sent. When Lucy Gray is surrounded by tributes who want to kill her, Snow sends in numerous drones which shatter around them, creating panic and confusion and buying her time to escape.</li></ul>
