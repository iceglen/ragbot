</p><p><b>Sarshee Whitcomb</b> was a girl from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> who participated in an unspecified <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> around the 40th.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSarshee_Whitcomb%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sarshee was the daughter of <a href="/wiki/Lyle_Whitcomb" title="Lyle Whitcomb">Lyle</a>, the boss of <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy%27s_father" title="Haymitch Abernathy&#39;s father">father</a>. Her father died of <a href="/wiki/Health_conditions#Black_lung" title="Health conditions">black lung</a>, leaving her an orphan. In the same year, she was <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> to participate in the Hunger Games. With no family, Haymitch, his father and his <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">mother</a> pretended to be her kin so that she had someone to say goodbye to. His father told her not to let <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> use her.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Sarshee died in the Games, as <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> did not have any victors between the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th</a> and the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Sarshee's name might be a distortion of the Irish name <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saoirse_(given_name)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Saoirse (given name)">Saoirse</a>, meaning "freedom".
