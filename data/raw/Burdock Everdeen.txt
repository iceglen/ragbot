</p><p><b>Burdock Everdeen</b>, nicknamed "<b>Burdie</b>" by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> was the father of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> and <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose</a> Everdeen, as well as being <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid Everdeen</a>'s husband. He lived and worked inside the gates of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> as a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a>, poacher, and Katniss's teacher when it came to hunting in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a>, singing, and making good quality <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bows</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> His teachings are what allowed her to support her family after he died, and herself while she was in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

					<b class="trfc161__question">How did Mr. Everdeen's death impact Katniss's life?</b>

					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">

This event not only burdened her with responsibilities but also molded her into a resilient survivor.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBurdock_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Burdock Everdeen was a friend of Haymitch Abernathy when they were children. Burdock used to sneak out of District 12 under the fence "all the time". When they were ten, he encouraged Haymitch to go under the fence and into the woods for the first time.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Burdock was said to have had a beautiful voice, just like his daughter, Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and it was claimed that even the birds would stop to listen when he sang<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. On the way, he sang and showed Haymitch how the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">mockingjays</a> would sing back. He then introduced Haymitch to his cousin, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a>, who Haymitch would later fall in love with.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When he was a teenager, he started hunting and trading game. He owned and cherished arrows made by <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a>. He also took up wildcrafting and traded them in the apothecary to <a href="/wiki/Asterid_March" class="mw-redirect" title="Asterid March">Asterid March</a>, a healer from the <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a> area of District 12, while he was from <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He impressed her with his singing voice.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Burdock and Haymitch used to sneak into the empty <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a> and peek into the windows.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> On the day of the reaping for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, he went to the apothecary and gave her a sprig of chamomile for good luck.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> was reaped, he saved <a href="/wiki/Otho_Mellark" title="Otho Mellark">Otho Mellark</a> by punching his knee to get him on the ground and out of the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>' line of fire.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Burdock tried to help when the home of <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">Willamae</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a> was burned down. He stopped Haymitch from running into the fire and held onto him, asking Asterid to help soothe his pain with <a href="/wiki/Sleep_syrup" title="Sleep syrup">sleep syrup</a>. Later, Burdock was one of the people that carried Willamae and Sid's coffin. He and <a href="/wiki/Blair" title="Blair">Blair</a> caught Haymitch after he collapsed in grief, taking him to his new home in the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors' Village">Victors' Village</a> and staying there overnight after bringing hand-me-down clothes and more sleep syrup.<sup id="cite_ref-SR26_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR26-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Following <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>'s death and Haymitch's subsequent withdrawal from his friends, Burdock and Asterid stayed with him longer than anyone else, only stopping when Haymitch threw a rock at Asterid.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Burdock found Haymitch in the woods weeks later, still searching for Lenore Dove. He led him to the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> graveyard before leaving.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Sometime later, Burdock and Asterid married and had two daughters named Katniss and Prim together. Their lives were considerably different, and it was a big change for both of them to be together and to move on to their new life.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He taught his daughters <a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Tree_(song)" title="The Hanging Tree (song)">"The Hanging Tree" song</a>, but Asterid yelled at him when she found the girls singing it and making rope necklaces. After that, he told Katniss they'd "better not sing that song anymore."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He also taught Katniss survival skills in the woods and introduced her to the lake, she definitely looked up to her father.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBurdock_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>Mr. Everdeen died in a mine explosion in January five years before the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. The explosion killed both him and <a href="/wiki/Mr._Hawthorne" title="Mr. Hawthorne">Mr. Hawthorne</a> as well as other workers<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-15"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, who were either vaporized or blown into bits<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>That day, sirens went off and all the kids were herded out of <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a> to the entrance of the mine. The elevator brought up many men, but Katniss' father and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s father never emerged and were instantly presumed dead.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss had many nightmares about that day, which involved her screaming and telling her father to escape the mine before it exploded<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Later, Katniss met Gale for the first time when they were given the Medal of Honor for their fathers' sacrifices.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBurdock_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As an adolescent, his voice was "high and sweet like a grown-up woman's but cleaner, nothing warbly about it."<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_5-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> According to Katniss, Mr. Everdeen liked to sing, swim, and hunt, and he had a very level head. He taught Katniss all of these things, and many songs as well<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-17"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.  He was well-liked all over District 12, his wife even leaving her merchant life for the Seam<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p><p>In addition to being a loving father, Mr. Everdeen also seemed to have a rebellious streak, a trait inherited by his eldest daughter. For example, his work as a hunter and gatherer in the woods surrounding District 12 was technically illegal, although the relatively lenient government officials of District 12 allowed it, as they were among the customers who bought the hunted animals<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBurdock_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Haymitch_Abernathy"><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBurdock_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Sunrise on the Reaping">"<i>He has not forgiven me, will never, but is not beyond pity. Perhaps because he knows what it is to love.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Haymitch Abernathy<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Burdock and Haymitch were good friends as children and young men. He encouraged Haymitch to take risks, such as venturing beyond the <a href="/wiki/Fence" title="Fence">fence</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_5-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and the two sometimes went skinny dipping together.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_5_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_5-18"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Burdock comforted Haymitch following the deaths of his family and girlfriend. After Haymitch injured Asterid in an attempt to get them to stop coming by, Burdock never forgave him, but still showed him the location of Lenore Dove's grave, revealing he still cared about Haymitch despite ending their friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBurdock_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His only known family members are his two children, Katniss and Primrose Everdeen, and his wife, <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid</a>. <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> later became his son-in-law, and he and Katniss bore him two grandchildren, a <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_daughter" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark's daughter">girl</a> and a <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_son" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark's son">boy</a><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_E_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_E-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, but he didn't live to see it. He was a distant cousin to <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>. <sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and said to be related to the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> (although the depth of the relation is not clarified, given that he is not shown to be a part of the Covey culture and lacks the color in his name, unlike Lenore Dove).
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBurdock_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Various facts about <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, the winner of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, prompted fan speculation that she could be a relative of Katniss and her father, either through one of her cousins (most popularly Maude Ivory or Barb Azure) or as a direct ancestor (making her Katniss's paternal grandmother or great-grandmother).
<ul><li>Lucy Gray wrote The Hanging Tree, a song Mr. Everdeen taught to Katniss, and that was only sung once in public, not enough for the average person to learn it, meaning someone from the Covey or a Seam citizen who heard it in the Hob could have taught it to him. Lucy Gray also mentioned that Maude Ivory could memorize a song after just one listen, so she may have been able to repeat it.</li>
<li>Lucy Gray and the Covey lived in the Seam, which is where Katniss' father was from.</li>
<li>Lucy Gray knew of wild herbs such as katniss and stated her preference for that name, maybe becoming an influence for later naming his daughter that way.</li>
<li>Lucy Gray and the rest of the Covey knew of the lake and the cabin outside of District 12.</li>
<li>The Covey collected milk from their pet goat Shamus, similar to Primrose Everdeen with her goat Lady.</li>
<li>Lucy Gray performed <a href="/wiki/The_Valley_Song" class="mw-redirect" title="The Valley Song">The Valley Song</a>, which she may have written, in the Capitol Zoo and at the Hob — in the 74th Games, Peeta mentions that on the first day of school, Katniss was the only one in their class who knew the Valley Song.</li>
<li>Lucy Gray used to sing the <a href="/wiki/Deep_in_the_Meadow_(Lullaby)" title="Deep in the Meadow (Lullaby)">Deep in the Meadow</a> lullaby to Maude Ivory, which Katniss used to sing to her sister Prim and also sang to Rue when she died.</li></ul></li>
<li>He was younger than <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, possibly by a few weeks or months.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_5-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBurdock_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His surname, Everdeen, comes from Bathsheba Everdene, the main character in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_from_the_Madding_Crowd" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Far from the Madding Crowd">Far from the Madding Crowd</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The first name Burdock, comes from the burdock plant of the daisy family (Asteraceae), specifically the genus Arctium.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> "Bur" refers to the plant's burrs (prickly seed vessels), and "dock" refers to the plant's large dock-like leaves.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>  The hardy, deep-rooted burdock symbolizes resilience<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, aligning with Collins’ plant-based naming pattern, as Burdock remains a guiding force in Katniss’ life.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_6-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
