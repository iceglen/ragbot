<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNuclear_weaponry%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Before the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, District 13's main industry was nuclear development and military arms production. However, this was kept secret, and <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> instead spread information about the district residents being primarily graphite miners.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While the district did mine graphite on a small scale, their main industry was nuclear weapons development and the production of military weapons. This allowed the district to become the primary benefactor to the rebel war effort during the First Rebellion, which lasted for three years. While the thirteen districts were initially able to stand strong and cut off the Capitol from all outside support during the final two years of the war, the tide turned in favor of the Capitol during the closing phase, presumably after a failed invasion of the Capitol. 
</p><p>During the Dark Days, rebels from District 13 used the Capitol's nuclear missiles against them. Without adequate weaponry to fight back, the Capitol was forced to allow District 13 to secede.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While District 13 had the option of defeating the Capitol with nuclear weapons, they chose not to, as their  actions could have wiped out nearly all of the population.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, the Capitol chose not to attack District 13 using nuclear missiles. <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> explained that these weapons would release radiation into the atmosphere, affect the environment, and badly damage the military compound.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_10_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_10-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
