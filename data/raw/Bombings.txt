<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a></i></center></div>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Rebel_air_raids">Rebel air raids</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>A few months into the war, the first major rebel air raid on the Capitol immobilized the city for several days.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Capitol's surveillance system relied on electricity, but rebel interference in District 5 caused blackouts that made air raid sirens unreliable, so citizens were often caught off guard and forced to shelter in place.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bombing_of_Rebel_Forces_in_the_Mountains">Bombing of Rebel Forces in the Mountains</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For much of the war, the rebel forces, backed up by District 13 and its military arsenal, decidedly had the upper hand, and were winning. In the final two years of the conflict, they had completely cut off the Capitol from outside support, causing drastic food shortages in the city and a massive decline in living standards. In an attempt to break the siege, the rebels attempted an invasion of the Capitol by scaling the mountains that surrounded the city. However, such a task proved to be extremely difficult and costly, as Capitol aircraft were able to locate them easily and kill off mass numbers of rebel soldiers, forcing them to retreat.
</p><p>The massive losses in both equipment and manpower for the rebels presumably had a devastating effect, and was likely the catalyst for the war turning in favor of the Capitol. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="First_Bombing_of_District_13">First Bombing of District 13</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Near the end of the Dark Days, <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> trained their nuclear weapons on <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and forced a stalemate under the threat of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/mutually_assured_destruction" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:mutually assured destruction">mutually assured destruction</a>. The district negotiated an agreement with the Capitol, promising to withdraw from the war in exchange for political independence. Following this agreement, the entire population of the district moved underground, while the Capitol bombed the surface, creating the impression that the district had been destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District 13 was then used as a cautionary tale to the other districts.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hunger_Games_period">The Hunger Games period</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bombing_of_the_Capitol_Arena">Bombing of the Capitol Arena</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f4/Capitol_Arena_bombing.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20231213025708" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Rubble raining down on Capitol students and tributes" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f4/Capitol_Arena_bombing.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231213025708" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="102" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol Arena bombing.jpeg" data-image-key="Capitol_Arena_bombing.jpeg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_Arena_bombing.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Rubble raining down on Capitol students and tributes</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>On Saturday, July 9th, the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> suffered a major bombing prior to the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. All of the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for the Games were present, as well as their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a>, who were the 24 top-performing students from the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, as well as a number of Academy staff and <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>. The bombing horrified those from the Capitol in particular, as it brought back traumatic memories of the frequent bombings which happened during the Dark Days.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Nobody was sure exactly who was responsible for the bombing or even the exact timeline. Due to its dilapidated condition, the Capitol Arena was not considered a high security area, so anyone could have sneaked in at any time since the 9th Hunger Games and planted the bombs without anyone noticing. The first explosion was near the main gate, but there was later a secondary explosion in the stands. During the bombing, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> was trapped and on fire when <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> saved him. <a href="/wiki/Lysistrata_Vickers" title="Lysistrata Vickers">Lysistrata Vickers</a> was also helped by her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a>, who threw himself on top of her to protect her from the blast.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bombing_of_the_District_12_mines">Bombing of the District 12 mines</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bombing_of_the_50th_Hunger_Games_arena">Bombing of the 50th Hunger Games arena</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, a group of rebels and rebel sympathizers plotted to destroy the arena's "brain" and disrupt the Games. Key members of the plot were <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>, while District 12 tribute <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and Beetee's son <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> were to carry out the plan using <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">sunflower bombs</a> disguised as the <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">Tribute tokens</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> tributes who were not in on the plan. Beetee had help from rebel sympathizers within the Capitol with smuggling the bomb components into the arena, most likely Gamemakers and Peacekeepers.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR11_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR11-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR13_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR13-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> also claimed to also be a part of the rebellion and gave Haymitch valuable advice on what to do in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR14_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR14-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Haymitch and Ampert succeeded in blowing up the arena's water tank and drowning the brain, causing the arena to malfunction and damaging it. However, the plan failed to stop the Games and the Gamemakers had Ampert killed by <a href="/wiki/Mutt" class="mw-redirect" title="Mutt">mutts</a> in retaliation.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR18_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR18-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After winning the Games, Haymitch managed to throw a second bomb at the arena's generator with the reaction from the Gamemakers suggesting that he was successful in destroying it.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR24_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR24-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Afterwards, Snow had Mags and Wiress tortured for information on everyone who was involved in the rebel plot,<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> although he spared Beetee's life as he was too valuable to kill.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR27_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR27-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While Snow chose not to kill Haymitch, he had Haymitch's family killed and poisoned Haymitch's <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">girlfriend</a> as punishment,<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR26_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR26-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> making it clear that he would never be allowed to love anyone again without them being killed. Undeterred by the failure, Plutarch told Haymitch that he had succeeded in shaking up the Capitol both literally and figuratively and that they needed to find someone like Haymitch but who was luckier or better with timing and preferably had an army at their back. Plutarch discussed finding or building that army, but Haymitch wanted nothing more to do with the rebellion after the losses that he had suffered.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR27_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR27-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Uprising_of_District_8"><a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">Uprising of District 8</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, rebels used their stop on the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a> as a rehearsal, and they used <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s televised engagement in the Capitol as an excuse to be on the streets after dark. At eight o'clock, groups of rebels donned masks and targeted centers of power such as the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>, the Peacekeepers' Headquarters, and the Communication Center in the square. Other targets included the railroad, granary, power plant, and armory. Peacekeepers were overwhelmed, their weapons appropriated by rebels, but reinforcements arrived by the thousands. The heightened Peacekeeper response and suppressive bombing brought an end to the uprising within forty-eight hours.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After a week of martial law, the district resumed regular business. However, a Capitol bomb destroyed the primary textile factory where rebels had started planning the uprising. <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>'s families were killed, but the two of them were presumed dead, allowing them to escape the district.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span id="Bombing_of_District_12_.28Second_Rebellion.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Bombing_of_District_12_(Second_Rebellion)">Bombing of District 12 (Second Rebellion)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As a result of Katniss Everdeen's actions at the end of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the Capitol's air force launched a massive retaliatory bombing raid on District 12. The Peacekeeper forces in the district withdrew, and the power to the citizens' homes was cut. The entire district was completely silent for fifteen minutes until the bombers arrived. At this time, the bombers dropped their incendiary payloads indiscriminately, razing most of 12 to the ground. The only area spared from the bombs was the <a href="/wiki/Victor%27s_Village" class="mw-redirect" title="Victor&#39;s Village">Victor's Village</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> herded refugees to <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a>, and they worked together to tear down a portion of the <a href="/wiki/Fence" title="Fence">fence</a> and escape into the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a>. Gale then led the survivors to a <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a> several hours from the district on foot. The bombing was over by dawn. Once they settled at the lake, the refugees tended to their wounded, hunting and fishing to sustain themselves for three days. Then they were discovered and evacuated to District 13. Of District 12's original population of approximately 8,000, only about 800 survived the raid. Fewer than a dozen of the district's <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a> class survived.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the films, the total was 915 survivors out of 10,000.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Bombing_of_District_13">Second Bombing of District 13</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D19&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During a live broadcast from the Capitol, Peeta Mellark issued a warning of an imminent strike on District 13.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Despite dissent from higher-up rebels, <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">President Alma Coin</a> issued an air raid alarm. Katniss Everdeen, as the rebels' propaganda figurehead, was ordered to remain calm to provide a role model for the citizens of District 13. Bunker missiles, designed to penetrate deep into the earth before detonating,<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> were launched at the district over the course of three days. Peeta's warning came ten minutes before District 13's own systems would have detected the oncoming assault, at which point everyone in District 13 was already evacuated into a bunker deep underground. Anyone on the first ten levels of the complex would have been killed, but due to the timely warning, no lives were lost.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, Capitol bombers damaged an aquifer in the northwest quadrant, causing District 13 to lose a transport corridor. A surface-to-air launcher in section 3 took a direct hit because Coin's order to fire had revealed its location. Another twelve bombers approached section 3. In order to secure their remaining assets, Coin ordered that they hold their fire. The next hit to section 3 hit bedrock, sending debris into the ventilation shaft, so Coin ordered the concealment of 13's remaining anti-aircraft guns, and the sealing of all launching pads and ventilation corridors. She also had oxygen reduced to 14% to conserve it for the duration of the assault. Peeta's warning gave them eight extra minutes to evacuate civilians.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bombing_of_the_Nut">Bombing of the Nut</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D20&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Due to the better living conditions experienced under the Capitol's rule, there was mixed support for the uprising in District 2, and for several weeks, the Capitol and the rebels fought, with no victory in sight. The biggest hurdle for the rebels' was <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>, the command center for the Capitol's military. Several attempts to capture the Nut were unsuccessful, leading to only more casualties among the rebels.
</p><p>Gale Hawthorne and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> put forth the idea of bombing seismic weaknesses in the mountains with hovercrafts, triggering avalanches which would bury the military base. This plan proved to be a success, leading to the destruction of the Nut and the surrender of loyalist forces in District 2.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  
</p><p>The bombing of the Nut disabled the Capitol's war effort, decimating their air fleet and killing large numbers of Peacekeepers. This was the turning point where the war turned decisively in favor of the rebellion.    
</p>
<hr />
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="City_Circle_bombing">City Circle bombing</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBombings%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D21&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the final weeks of the invasion of the Capitol, the rebel forces marked four pathways which led to the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a>. As rebel forces advanced, President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> surrounded the presidential mansion with the Capitol's refugee children as a last-ditch defense. A hovercraft with a Capitol insignia dropped <a href="/wiki/Silver_parachute" title="Silver parachute">silver parachutes</a> into the crowd, reminiscent of <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a> delivered to tributes in the Hunger Games. However, rather than aid parcels, these were actually twice-detonating bombs. After the first explosion, rebel medics and Peacekeepers united to assist the wounded, and a second explosion killed the aid workers in a double tap strike.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This strategy was devised by <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  
</p><p>This event turned public opinion against the Capitol, and the country was seized for the rebels, ending the war. It was later discovered that District 13's President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> ordered this bombing to shatter what little remaining loyalty anyone had for President Snow.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
