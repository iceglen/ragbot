<p>Many characters in the series struggled with addiction. <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">Victors</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> often turned to substance abuse to cope with their trauma.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_morphling_addicts">Known morphling addicts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">Morphling</a> is a highly addictive drug similar to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/morphine" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:morphine">morphine</a>, and addicts are commonly referred to as "<a href="/wiki/Morphlings" title="Morphlings">morphlings</a>". Long-term abuse of the drug can result in intense weight loss and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaundice" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jaundice">yellowing of the skin</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which is indicative of liver damage. Withdrawal symptoms include tremors and vomiting.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_17-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>

This mutilation prevents them from speaking,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_6_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_6-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and it poses some difficulty for them when swallowing.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_8_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_8-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Black_lung">Black lung</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_lung" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Black lung">Black lung</a> is a respiratory disease caused by long-term inhalation of coal dust. The lengthened version is Coal Worker's Pneumoconiosis or CWP for short.
</p><p>The old man who took in the remaining <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> died of black lung during the year of the <a href="/wiki/9th_Hunger_Games" title="9th Hunger Games">9th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Goat_Man" title="Goat Man">Goat Man</a> also appeared to suffer from black lung, as he had "a hacking cough that proves he spent years in the mines".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This implies that the condition was relatively common among <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miners</a> who survived to old age. 
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Lyle_Whitcomb" title="Lyle Whitcomb">Lyle Whitcomb</a>, father of District 12 tribute <a href="/wiki/Sarshee_Whitcomb" title="Sarshee Whitcomb">Sarshee Whitcomb</a>, also died of black lung.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Blood_loss">Blood loss</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Burns">Burns</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Burns were common injuries in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> of District 12 because they cooked and heated their homes with coal. <a href="/wiki/Asterid_Everdeen" title="Asterid Everdeen">Asterid Everdeen</a> was known to treat burn victims from mining accidents,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Otho_Mellark" title="Otho Mellark">Otho Mellark</a> was described as having burn scars from years of working at the <a href="/wiki/Bakery" title="Bakery">bakery</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss considered burns the worst kind of pain. In the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, she initially had trouble overcoming her squeamishness about the burn on her calf,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but was eventually able to treat it with a <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gift</a> of burn ointment.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Concussion">Concussion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Coriolanus Snow sustained a mild <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/concussion" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:concussion">concussion</a> in the Capitol Arena bombing.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Johanna Mason gave Katniss a concussion when she struck the girl with a heavy coil of wire at the end of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. The effects of this lingered a month later, making it hard for Katniss to sort out her thoughts.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Deafness">Deafness</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When Katniss set off the land mines around the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a>' supplies in the 74th Hunger Games, the explosions gave her profound <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/hearing_loss" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:hearing loss">hearing loss</a>, as well as impaired balance and bleeding in her left ear. Her right ear had begun to improve by the following morning, though she dealt with a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinnitus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tinnitus">ringing sound</a> on that side for some time after.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She was deaf in her left ear for the rest of the Games, after which point Capitol doctors were able to restore her hearing in full.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Woof" title="Woof">Woof</a> was also said to be hard of hearing,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> presumably the result of age-related hearing loss.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Dehydration">Dehydration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Haymitch advised Katniss to find water right away in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, so she was mindful of the threat of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/dehydration" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:dehydration">dehydration</a> early on, which could kill her within a week.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She'd seen enough tributes die that way on television over the years to know that unless a fight was going on somewhere, her death by dehydration would be featured.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Despite her efforts to locate it, she started to suffer the effects of dehydration on her second day in the arena. She mentioned that "what little urine [she'd] been able to pass" was dark brown. She also had a persistent dry patch on her tongue, a headache, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photophobia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Photophobia">light sensitivity</a>, and eventually, fatigue. On the third day of the Games, she collapsed and couldn't get up. However, she realized she was in a patch of mud, so there had to be water nearby. She mustered the strength to crawl to the pond, and she restrained herself from drinking straight from it; instead, she filled up her flask, decontaminated it with drops of iodine, and waited half an hour before drinking.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_27-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Swan_flu">Swan flu</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Swan flu is an illness that a young Coriolanus and Tigris contracted during the Dark Days. Symptoms included a fever, chills, and a spiking pain in the limbs. Coriolanus was once so ill that he collapsed in the street.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is likely a reference to both <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/avian_flu" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:avian flu">avian flu</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/swine_flu" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:swine flu">swine flu</a>.
</p>

<p><a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae%27s_granddaughter" title="Greasy Sae&#39;s granddaughter">Greasy Sae's granddaughter</a> and <a href="/wiki/Martin" title="Martin">Martin</a> both had some form of mental or developmental disability.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_47-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-47"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Woof also exhibited confusion and a lack of focus due to his old age,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> likely pointing to him having a form of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/dementia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:dementia">dementia</a>.

<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">Hijacking</a></i></center></div>
<p>Peeta suffered from memory issues after his <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a>; he had difficulties with recall and telling his real memories apart from false ones.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mental_illness">Mental illness</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D28&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Many characters displayed symptoms of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/post-traumatic_stress_disorder" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:post-traumatic stress disorder">post-traumatic stress disorder</a> (PTSD), such as nightmares, traumatic flashbacks, hypervigilance, and irritability. <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> in particular was singled out as having been profoundly affected mentally by her experience in the <a href="/wiki/70th_Hunger_Games" title="70th Hunger Games">70th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_48-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-48"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>48<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Like Peeta, she also had trouble distinguishing what was real, but her condition was unrelated to hijacking.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_17-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Depression">Depression</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D29&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Mrs. Everdeen fell into a deep, debilitating <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/depression" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:depression">depression</a> after the death of her husband, but was eventually able to recover.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She later told Katniss that she could have treated herself back then if she'd had the medicine that she now had.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_20-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, it is unknown what kind of medicine this was, if it was something she took regularly, or if it was something she only took as needed.
</p><p>Like her mother, Katniss fell into a severe depression after the death of her sister Prim. She also developed a form of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/mutism" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:mutism">mutism</a> from the psychological trauma of watching her die.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_49-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-49"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>49<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Muttation">Muttation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D30&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">Muttation</a></i></center></div>
<p>While most muttations appeared to be non-human creatures, Coriolanus Snow once saw Avoxes with animal parts grafted onto them in the <a href="/wiki/Citadel" title="Citadel">Citadel</a> lab. Some of their conditions included small feathered ruffs around their necks, talons or tentacles in place of fingers, and gills embedded in their chests.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_50-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-50"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Clemensia Dovecote experienced unusual, snakelike side effects after being bitten by <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a>. Four days after the incident, her skin was peeling, the whites of her eyes were bright yellow, and she exhibited several motor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/tic" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:tic">tics</a>: grimacing, jerking motions of the hands, and sticking out her tongue as a snake would. She also showed Coriolanus that a patch of skin along her collarbone and onto her shoulder had sprouted scales, which she said were spreading.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_22-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ten days after the incident, the yellow in Clemensia's eyes had faded to a paler shade and she wore a high-collared blouse to hide her scales. While the severity of her tics had lessened, her tongue was still darting around inside her mouth, and she picked at dry patches of skin on her face.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Fourteen days after the incident, Clemensia told Coriolanus that the scales on her chest had finally started to fade, and that it could take "as long as a year" for them all to be gone. While the scales weren't painful, they caused discomfort because they pulled on her skin.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Pneumonia">Pneumonia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D31&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Starvation deaths in District 12 were often recorded as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/pneumonia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:pneumonia">pneumonia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_29-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, Mrs. Everdeen once treated a starving child who was actually also suffering from pneumonia.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_26_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_26-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Pox_epidemic">Pox epidemic</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D32&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> suffered an unspecified "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/pox" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:pox">pox</a> epidemic" which killed many citizens and rendered a number of survivors infertile.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As a result, District 13's birth rate was flattened, leaving them desperate for more fertile citizens to help repopulate.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_2_51-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_2-51"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Prim once pointed out to Katniss that people bore pox scars and some of their children were disfigured.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_52-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-52"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, it is unclear whether the children had been infected themselves, or if their mothers were infected while pregnant, leaving them with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/congenital_disorder" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:congenital disorder">congenital disorders</a>.
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, Prim told Katniss that this epidemic had been "a few years ago", and that <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a> had lost <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin%27s_husband" title="Alma Coin&#39;s husband">her husband</a> and <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin%27s_daughter" title="Alma Coin&#39;s daughter">daughter</a> to it.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_53-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-53"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Rabies">Rabies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D33&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the vaccination of pets became a very low priority. As a result, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/rabies" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:rabies">rabies</a> was spread from dogs to people, killing "over a dozen Capitol citizens" before a vaccination program got the disease under control.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_17_54-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_17-54"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ahead of the 10th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a> was bitten in the neck by a rabid raccoon on his first night in captivity at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_50-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-50"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As the infection progressed, he began to exhibit erratic and aggressive behavior. Since one of the symptoms is a fear of water, his mentor <a href="/wiki/Lysistrata_Vickers" title="Lysistrata Vickers">Lysistrata Vickers</a> sent water bottles into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> as sponsor gifts to drive him away from Lucy Gray Baird. He died after falling off a ledge.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_17_54-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_17-54"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>According to Lucy Gray, Reaper Ash also contracted rabies when Jessup spat in his eye at the zoo, possibly explaining his own erratic behavior. She reasoned that poisoning him was a mercy kill.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_55-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-55"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>55<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i>The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i>, a young Coriolanus and Tigris were once confronted by a rabid dog in the streets.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_39-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sores">Sores</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D34&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As president, Coriolanus Snow drank from his assassination targets' poisoned cups to deflect suspicion from himself, which caused him to develop mouth sores that never healed. He wore roses to hide the scent of the blood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_56-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-56"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Starvation">Starvation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D35&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the Dark Days, the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> cut off the Capitol's food supply, leaving many people to starve. In extreme cases, some of those who survived resorted to cannibalism.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_28-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Starvation was very common in District 12, to the point where people often collapsed and died in the streets. However, the official cause of death would always be recorded as the flu, exposure, or pneumonia. Katniss, her sister, and her mother were near starvation in the months after Mr. Everdeen's death. However, Katniss received a burnt loaf of <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a> from Peeta one day, saving their lives.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_29-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tracker_jacker_stings">Tracker jacker stings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHealth_conditions%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D36&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">Tracker jacker</a></i></center></div>
<p>Tracker jacker stings commonly range from the size of a plum to the size of an orange, swelling up within minutes. The stings themselves are extremely painful and remain so for days unless treated. The venom was engineered to target the part of the brain that generates fear, causing strange and terrifying hallucinations that can drive a person to madness. Getting stung many times in a short period can be lethal.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Katniss dropped a tracker jacker nest on the Careers in the 74th Hunger Games, she was stung three times, while <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> and the <a href="/wiki/District_4_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 female (74th HG)">District 4 female</a> were both lethally stung.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_21-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (74th HG)">District 3 male</a>, <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, and Cato escaped with their lives, but they were still recovering for days after.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_57-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-57"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>57<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p><a href="/wiki/Dill" title="Dill">Dill</a> suffered from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/tuberculosis" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:tuberculosis">tuberculosis</a>, and she succumbed to the disease on the first day of the 10th Hunger Games. It was a treatable chronic condition in the Capitol, but a death sentence in the districts.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_58-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-58"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>58<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
