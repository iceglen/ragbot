</p><p><b>Peter Dinklage</b> is an American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.
</p><p>Prior to the film's release, Nathan Kahane, President of Lionsgate's Motion Picture Group, stated that "Peter is not just a fan favorite actor who brings a commanding presence to every part he plays. He is one of the best actors alive. He will bring a formidable, charismatic authority to the important role of the dean of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>." Additionally, <a href="/wiki/Nina_Jacobson" title="Nina Jacobson">Nina Jacobson</a>, the film producer, stated that "Peter's magnetism, intensity and dark humor will infuse him with the intelligence, depth and pathos that the role demands."<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPeter_Dinklage%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Dinklage is best known for starring as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrion_Lannister" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tyrion Lannister">Tyrion Lannister</a> on the television series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Game of Thrones">Game of Thrones</a></i> (2011–2019), for which he won the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series">Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series</a> a record four times.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He also received a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor_%E2%80%93_Series,_Miniseries_or_Television_Film" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Golden Globe Award for Best Supporting Actor – Series, Miniseries or Television Film">Golden Globe Award</a> in 2011 and a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Drama_Series" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Male Actor in a Drama Series">Screen Actors Guild Award</a> in 2020 for the role.
</p><p>Dinklage studied acting at Bennington College, performing in amateur stage productions. He made his film debut in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_in_Oblivion" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Living in Oblivion">Living in Oblivion</a></i> (1995), and had his breakthrough with a starring role in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Station_Agent" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Station Agent">The Station Agent</a></i> (2003). His other films include <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elf_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Elf (film)">Elf</a></i> (2003), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lassie_(2005_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lassie (2005 film)">Lassie</a></i> (2005), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Find_Me_Guilty" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Find Me Guilty">Find Me Guilty</a></i> (2006), <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penelope_(2006_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Penelope (2006 film)"><i>Penelope</i></a> (2006), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_at_a_Funeral_(2007_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Death at a Funeral (2007 film)">Death at a Funeral</a></i> (2007), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia:_Prince_Caspian" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Chronicles of Narnia: Prince Caspian">The Chronicles of Narnia: Prince Caspian</a></i> (2008), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_at_a_Funeral_(2010_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Death at a Funeral (2010 film)">Death at a Funeral</a></i> (2010), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men:_Days_of_Future_Past" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:X-Men: Days of Future Past">X-Men: Days of Future Past</a></i> (2014), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Billboards_Outside_Ebbing,_Missouri" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri">Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri</a></i> (2017), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Infinity_War" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Avengers: Infinity War">Avengers: Infinity War</a></i> (2018) and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Dinner_with_Herv%C3%A9" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:My Dinner with Hervé">My Dinner with Hervé</a></i>. He also provided voice-acting for the video game <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destiny_(video_game)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Destiny (video game)">Destiny</a></i> (2014), and in 2023 voiced a character in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformers:_Rise_of_the_Beasts" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Transformers: Rise of the Beasts">Transformers: Rise of the Beasts</a></i>.
</p><p>Dinklage has also performed in theater, with roles including the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_III" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Richard III">title character</a> in Shakespeare's <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_III_(play)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Richard III (play)">Richard III</a></i> (2003), Rakitin in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Month_in_the_Country_(play)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:A Month in the Country (play)">A Month in the Country</a></i> (2015), and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrano_de_Bergerac" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cyrano de Bergerac">Cyrano de Bergerac</a> in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrano_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cyrano (film)">Cyrano</a></i> (2019).
</p>
