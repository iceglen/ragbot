<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FBascom_Pie%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bascom Pie was a bootlegger in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> who sold rotgut to anyone who wanted it, even if its sale was not conscientious. Among his customers were the reckless <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a> and his family.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After running out of <a href="/wiki/Sleep_syrup" title="Sleep syrup">sleep syrup</a> to help him cope with the death of his <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">mother</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">brother</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">girlfriend</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> began to buy sacks of rotgut from Bascom Pie in desperation.<sup id="cite_ref-SR27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
