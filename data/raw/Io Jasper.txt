<p><b>Io Jasper</b> was a female resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She was assigned the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>, <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FIo_Jasper%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was a natural when it came to biology and partnered with <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> at the high biology class at the Academy. Coriolanus felt that the she must have been born with a microscope attached to her eye. He hated working with a partner, but nevertheless behaved graciously around her, resulting in her adoration.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the interviews before the 10th Hunger Games began, her tribute, <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a>, stated that he could start a fire with his glasses. With her scientific knowledge, Io suggested various angle and times of day that would facilitate the task.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Her parents were scientists of some sort.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a> interviewed Io, she discussed her thoughts on what Circ might do with the <a href="/wiki/Drone" title="Drone">drone</a> he had taken and altered, suggesting that he could use its lights to give him an advantage at night. However, she was interrupted when attention turned back to <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a>, who had emerged from the barricade inside the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Her tribute, Circ, died after being bitten by <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> sent into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> by Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>. Snow regretted the loss of her presence, as she tended to be clinical rather than combative.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FIo_Jasper%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In Greek mythology, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Io_(mythology)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Io (mythology)">Io</a> was one of the many mortal lovers of Zeus, the chief god of the Greek pantheon. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasper" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jasper">Jasper</a> is a kind of quartz often used as a gemstone.
</p>
