<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRipper%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2><p>
Ripper was a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> who lost her arm in a mine accident, but was smart enough to find another way to survive— presumably referring to her making money by selling white liquor at the Hob. Two of her biggest customers were <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s second <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the Head <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> </p><figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/af/Ripper_katniss_hob.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131206173331" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Ripper dealing with Katniss in ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/af/Ripper_katniss_hob.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131206173331" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="111" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Ripper katniss hob.jpg" data-image-key="Ripper_katniss_hob.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Ripper_katniss_hob.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Ripper dealing with Katniss in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a>.</i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>After <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> won the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> and moved to the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>, she bought liquor from Ripper that she could stockpile on Haymitch's behalf, preventing him from going into withdrawals.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ripper lost her place of business when Peacekeepers were ordered by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a> to burn down the Hob.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With the replacement of Cray with Thread, she lost him as a customer, and she later lost Katniss and Haymitch's service as well because <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> forbade her from selling them liquor ahead of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">third Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>She may have been killed in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, since she was not mentioned among the refugees in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRipper%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ripper may have been named after a coal mining term, specifically "men who remove the rock above the coal seam and set rings (arches) to raise the height of the gate or road as the coal face advances".
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRipper%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, Ripper was portrayed with both her arms instead of one.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, she and other Hob regulars were smart enough to stay away when Thread ordered it to be burned, so they weren't caught up in the blaze. This event occurred in the weeks after <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s whipping.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, in the film, Katniss had to rescue Ripper from Peacekeepers and briefly treat her injuries before arriving at the town square, where Gale was being whipped.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
