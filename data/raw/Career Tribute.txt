<p><b>Career Tributes</b>, commonly known as <b>Careers</b>, were <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> who trained from a young age to compete in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. They attended specialized combat academies in their districts, where they were instructed by prior <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> and skilled trainers, until they turned 18 - the age at which Careers usually would <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a> for the Games. Careers typically originated from the wealthier <a href="/wiki/Districts" class="mw-redirect" title="Districts">districts</a>: <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, where being a tribute for the Hunger Games was regarded as a great honor by both the district residents and the tributes themselves. 
</p><p>Both before and during the Games, it was common for the Careers from these districts to form an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>, often referred to as the "Career pack" or "the Pack". While these alliances usually exclusively consisted of the tributes from Districts 1, 2 and 4, it was not uncommon for the Careers to extend invitations to tributes from other districts who demonstrated exceptional physical prowess or useful/valuable skills. In the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the Careers were led by <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>, the male tribute of District 2, while in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the Careers had no clear leader.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Inception_and_early_years">Inception and early years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The exact origin of the Career districts' practice of training tributes for the Hunger Games remains unclear. At the time of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the Games still lacked many of the defining features that would appear in future iterations, and the concept of Career tributes as they were known in subsequent Games had not yet emerged. However a pattern had already begun to form: victory disproportionately favored tributes from the wealthier and Capitol-aligned districts of 1 and 2, with Districts 4 and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> also proving to be strong contenders as well. Although the Capitol recognized this trend, the districts themselves often paid little attention to the Games or its outcome unless their own friends or family were involved. The brutal nature and treatment of tributes during the early Games, coupled with the lack of reward for the initial cohort of victors, further disincentivized a need or desire for pre-Games preparation. As a result, the concept of organized training for the Hunger Games was either nonexistent or in its infancy during this period. 
</p><p>After becoming an apprentice <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> instituted several reforms alongside the changes made by Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> during the 10th Games, also done at his suggestion. Such changes included rewarding victors with a lifetime stipend, rewarding the victor's district with a year's worth of food and supplies, and the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Villages</a>. Snow hoped these changes would encourage a better class of tributes to volunteer.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is likely that Snow's reforms, along with the growing prestige of victors, encouraged the future Career districts to establish training programs for the Games.
</p><p>At some point during the early history of the Hunger Games, as Districts 1, 2, and 4 began producing more victors, informal training programs likely emerged, spearheaded by these early winners. Given the relative affluence of these districts, they had the resources to support such initiatives, which were only further reinforced by their proven success. Over time, as these programs yielded more victors, they became increasingly formalized, evolving into dedicated institutions with structured curricula and professional staff to train larger classes of potential volunteers. In the same vein, the Career districts experienced a cultural shift, coming to accept the Hunger Games and viewing participation in it as a chance for honor and glory.
</p><p>Despite one of the explicit rules of the Hunger Games prohibiting training prior to the Games, the Capitol turned a blind eye to these practices. This leniency can be attributed to the Career districts' loyalty to the Capitol regime and the heightened entertainment value that trained tributes brought to the Games. The Careers' skill and ferocity made the Hunger Games more thrilling for Capitol audiences. This, coupled with the Games transition from a brutal and uneventful televised punishment on the districts to a reality TV spectacle, further solidified the Career districts' commitment to their training programs.
</p><p>Once the training programs in the Career districts became firmly established, the presence of Careers became a perennial and defining feature of the Hunger Games. Over the years, Districts 1, 2, and 4 consistently produced victors, further entrenching their dominance. At some point, the term "Careers" was eventually emerged as a catch-all label for the trained tributes of 1, 2, and 4, particularly amongst the non-Career districts, though its exact origins remains unknown. It's earliest known official use by the Capitol was promotional posters for the 67th Victory Tour celebrating <a href="/wiki/Augustus_Braun" title="Augustus Braun">Augustus Braun</a> of District 1's victory in the <a href="/wiki/67th_Hunger_Games" title="67th Hunger Games">67th Games</a>, where he was promoted as the "Cavalier Career". Despite this, the Careers were referred to as such by non-Career tributes as early as the 50th Games.
</p><p>Several notable Career victors emerged from the earlier Games, including:
</p>
<ul><li><b>District 1</b>: <a href="/wiki/Palladium_Barker" title="Palladium Barker">Palladium Barker</a>, victor of the <a href="/wiki/46th_Hunger_Games" title="46th Hunger Games">46th Games</a>, siblings <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> who won the <a href="/wiki/63rd_Hunger_Games" title="63rd Hunger Games">63rd Games</a> and <a href="/wiki/64th_Hunger_Games" title="64th Hunger Games">64th Games</a> consecutively, and Augustus Braun, victor of the 67th Games.</li>
<li><b>District 2</b>: <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> and <a href="/wiki/Lyme" title="Lyme">Lyme</a>, victors for unspecified Games, and <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>, victor of the <a href="/wiki/62nd_Hunger_Games" title="62nd Hunger Games">62nd Games</a>.</li>
<li><b>District 4</b>: <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>, the youngest victor in Hunger Games history at 14, who won the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Games</a> and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, victor of the <a href="/wiki/70th_Hunger_Games" title="70th Hunger Games">70th Games</a>.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The nature of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> as a <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> and its associated twist of doubling the tributes reaped allowed for a super-sized Career alliance of initially twelve members: <a href="/wiki/Carat" title="Carat">Carat</a>, <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Loupe" title="Loupe">Loupe</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> from 1, <a href="/wiki/Alpheus" title="Alpheus">Alpheus</a>, <a href="/wiki/Camilla" title="Camilla">Camilla</a>, <a href="/wiki/Janus" title="Janus">Janus</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Nona" title="Nona">Nona</a> from 2, and <a href="/wiki/Urchin" title="Urchin">Urchin</a>, <a href="/wiki/Barba" title="Barba">Barba</a>, <a href="/wiki/Angler" title="Angler">Angler</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a> from 4. The tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> - <a href="/wiki/Hychel" title="Hychel">Hychel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Anion" title="Anion">Anion</a>, <a href="/wiki/Fisser" title="Fisser">Fisser</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Potena" title="Potena">Potena</a> - were also invited into the alliance during pre-Games training, bringing its total membership to sixteen tributes. This made the 50th Games Career Pack the largest known Career alliance and second largest overall, behind only <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a>, the other opposing alliance of the 50th Games consisting of all other tributes participating. When the Second Quarter Quell began, Loupe and Camilla split off from the Pack on Day 1. Additionally, one boy and one girl from 5 died in the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">opening Bloodbath</a>, placing between 31st and 48th, while Carat and Urchin were poisoned by the arena later that same day, placing between 29th and 30th, leaving the Pack with ten remaining members.
</p><p>On Day 3 into Day 4, the mountain overlooking the arena was revealed to be a volcano that erupted. The event killed several tributes, including Alpheus, Camilla, Janus, Nona, and one boy and one girl from 5, all placing between 14th and 25th. Later on Day 4, Panache, Barba, and Angler attempted to attack <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>. Haymitch axed Angler in the throat, placing him 13th, and stabbed Barba in the gut, placing her 12th, before being disarmed by Panache. However, Panache was brought down by a poisonous dart shot by <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> from 12, placing him 11th. The three tributes' packs were used by Haymitch and Maysilee later on. Silka and Maritte would later kill Autumn and Ringina from 7 on Day 5.
</p><p>On Day 6, Silka and Maritte attacked Haymitch and Maysilee, but the four of them ran into three <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> who were fixing a problem in the arena. Maysilee and Maritte each killed a Gamemaker before all four of them ran. Maritte was killed by a pack of carnivorous squirrel <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">mutts</a>, placing 5th (though through manipulative editing appeared to place 3rd), who were targeted for her as punishment, similarly to the deaths of Maysilee and Ampert from 3. At night on Day 6, Silka began crying beneath a tree, not knowing that <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a> and Haymitch were perched in it. Haymitch dropped down some chocolate for Silka, and instead of attacking she ate it, causing a temporary truce between the three. 
</p><p>The next day, Silka found Wellie under the same tree. Wellie attempted to shoot Silka with one of Maysilee's poisonous darts, but Silka decapitated her. She then fought Haymitch, and sustained an injury to the eye. She chased him to a cliff outside the hedge border and threw her ax, but he collapsed before it hit him. She then stood idly, staunching the flow of blood from her eye socket and waiting for Haymitch to bleed out. However, her ax had hit the force field around the arena generator and came spinning back, landing in her head and killing her, placing her in 2nd.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Careers_mocking.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419064637" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Careers taunt  as they chase her." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Careers_mocking.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120419064637" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="194" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Careers mocking.jpg" data-image-key="Careers_mocking.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Careers_mocking.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Careers taunt <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> as they chase her.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the 74th Hunger Games, the Career pack consisted of eight members total (six in the films): <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> from 1, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> and <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> from 2, <a href="/wiki/Bravura" title="Bravura">Bravura</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tippet" title="Tippet">Tippet</a> from 4 (book only), <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Fila" title="Fila">Fila</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">3</a>. As typical for Careers, the four tributes from Districts 1 and 2 scored high in their training, with Marvel and Glimmer both scoring a 9 and Cato and Clove scoring a 10. The four were additionally specially trained in specific weapons, which they each successfully retrieved from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. Cato wielded a short broadsword, Marvel used javelins (throwing spears), Glimmer carried a bow and arrows (though she was actually a poor shot), and Clove was armed with a wide variety of throwing knives. Sometime after the bloodbath the group accepted Peeta into the alliance, both due to his demonstrated strength and under the pretense of using him to find Katniss. However, several members of the Pack debated on whether or not they should eliminate him early before targeting Katniss. Despite killing at least 6 of the 11 tributes that died during the bloodbath, the Pack sustained a casualty when Bravura also died in the bloodbath on Day 1, possibly due to his lack of skill. They would achieve a 7th kill, after happening upon <a href="/wiki/Heddle" title="Heddle">Heddle</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> at dawn on Day 2, who was mortally wounded by Glimmer and ultimately mercy killed by Peeta.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-right show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/77/282530576593461477DeGrzZsjc.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130618123249" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Careers with ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/77/282530576593461477DeGrzZsjc.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130618123249" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="194" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="282530576593461477DeGrzZsjc.jpg" data-image-key="282530576593461477DeGrzZsjc.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:282530576593461477DeGrzZsjc.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Careers with <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The Pack's fortune progressively declined from then on. On Day 5, after cornering Katniss up a tree and making camp under her, Glimmer and Tippet were killed by <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> stings, which resulted from a nest of the <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> being dropped on them by Katniss. As Katniss went to retrieve Glimmer's <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow</a>, Cato saw Peeta aid her in her escape. This led Cato to severely wound Peeta's leg with his sword and abandon him, reducing the Pack to three members.
</p><p>The Careers later recruited Fila for his technical skills, particularly because he could reactivate the mines located around the tributes' starting pedestals. The Pack dug up the mines and used them to form a defensive perimeter around their supply stockpile from the Cornucopia to deter other tributes from attempting to steal their supplies. Confident in their defenses, the Careers left their supplies in the open, presumably memorizing the mines' locations to avoid triggering them. However, <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a>, the female tribute from District 5, managed to navigate the minefield undetected, allowing her to steal small amounts of food without setting off the explosives. The Careers' overconfidence proved to be their undoing. The land mines were arranged in such a way that a single explosion could trigger a chain reaction, destroying their entire supply stockpile. This is what exactly happened when Katniss shot a burlap sack of apples, causing them to fall and detonate several mines, obliterating the pyramid of supplies and the nearby Career base on Day 8.
</p>

<p>Shortly after Katniss destroyed the Careers' supplies, Cato killed Fila in a fit of rage. Minutes later, Marvel speared and killed Rue, only to be avenged by Katniss, who shot an arrow into Marvel's neck (chest in the film). This reduced the Pack to only the tributes from 2. At the <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">Feast</a> a few days later on Day 13, the Careers suffered yet another loss when <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> killed Clove and took the pack designated for District 2 along with his own, leaving the Pack defunct with Cato as the last Career. Furious at the lost of his district partner, Cato hunted down Thresh in a vengeful rage. The two battled during a rainstorm, and Cato emerged victorious on Day 15.
</p><p>As one of the final three tributes on Day 17, Cato was forced into a final confrontation with Katniss and Peeta, the two other remaining tributes, when a pack of <a href="/wiki/Wolf_mutt" title="Wolf mutt">wolf mutts</a> was unleashed on him. All three were chased through the forest back to the Cornucopia and forced to take refuge on top of it. Cato immediately attacked, eventually grabbing Peeta in a chokehold while Katniss aimed her bow at him, creating a tense standoff. Just as Cato nearly killed Peeta, Katniss shot his hand, allowing Peeta to break free and knock him off the edge of the Cornucopia. Cato fell into the pack of wolf muttations waiting below, which mauled him throughout the night, despite his futile efforts to fight back. At dawn, Katniss ended his suffering with a mercy kill.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-right show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png/revision/latest?cb=20140123102546" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Career tributes of the 75th Hunger Games: Brutus, Enobaria, Cashmere and Gloss." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="141" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png" data-image-key="Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140123102546" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png/revision/latest?cb=20140123102546" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Career tributes of the 75th Hunger Games: Brutus, Enobaria, Cashmere and Gloss." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140123102546" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="141" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png" data-image-key="Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fe/Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140123102546" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Vlcsnap-2014-01-23-18h23m31s250.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Career tributes of the 75th Hunger Games: Brutus, Enobaria, Cashmere and Gloss.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, or the Third Quarter Quell, the Career pack originated with veteran victors <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> from District 1 and <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> and <a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a> from District 2. The tributes from District 4 were not part of the Career pack, as <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> allied instead with Katniss and Peeta.
</p><p>When the Quarter Quell started, the Career pack made for engaging Katniss and her allies, but were ultimately driven back long enough by Katniss and Finnick to allow them and the other members of their alliance to escape. With their biggest rivals fleeing into the jungle, the Careers regrouped and set about eliminating the remaining victors still within the vicinity of the Cornucopia. Once the Cornucopia was under their control, the Careers took the supplies and weapons they needed and presumably ventured into the surrounding forest themselves to hunt the remaining victors throughout the arena.
</p><p>Upon Katniss' alliance return to the Cornucopia and their determination that it was the centerpiece of a clock, their group was once again attacked by the Careers, with Gloss inflicting the first casualty by killing <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>. Katniss immediately retaliated by shooting Gloss in the head (chest in the film), killing him instantly. Cashmere was the second Career to fall when <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a> killed her with her axe. With their District 1 allies eliminated, Brutus and Enobaria ultimately retreated back into the forest, where they remained hidden until the end.
</p><p>During the endgame of the Quell, the Careers successfully tracked down Katniss' alliance to one of the 12 o'clock trees. When Johanna feigned betraying Katniss by cutting out her tracker and pretended to leave her for dead, she successfully lured Brutus and Enobaria away from her. Sometime later,  Brutus and <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a> fought, with Brutus emerging victorious. Enraged upon witnessing this, Peeta fought Brutus, avenging Chaff's death by killing Brutus in single combat. Enobaria, meanwhile, fought her way to <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee</a>, managing to grievously injure him with a knife before Finnick drove her back into the forest. When Katniss destroyed the arena, Enobaria was captured alongside Peeta and Johanna, and they were all taken back to the Capitol.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Little is known on the status of most Career victors with the onset of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a> and whether they sided with the rebels or remained loyal to the Capitol. Enobaria was taken to the Capitol to be tortured for information, as well as <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, a Career victor from District 4. Annie, alongside Johanna Mason and Peeta Mellark, was ultimately saved by <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>'s forces. Enobaria, however, was released prior to District 13's mission and allowed to return to District 2. The reason for this remains unclear, although it is likely due to Enobaria having no knowledge of the victor conspiracy and being a victor from District 2, a district still loyal to the Capitol at the time. Once District 2 <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_District_2" title="Battle of District 2">fell</a> to rebel forces, Enobaria joined the rebellion and was among the victors that survived to vote on the <a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">76th Hunger Games</a> proposed by <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>.
</p><p>Finnick Odair was among the victors saved from the Quell arena, alongside Katniss and Beetee, where they were brought back to District 13. Finnick played an active role in the rebel war effort by filming <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a>, one being the public broadcast of his <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie&#39;s wedding">marriage</a> to Annie and another revealing the Capitol's sexual exploitation of the victors. During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>, Finnick served as a member of <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>, where he died fighting off lizard mutts to allow the other squad members to escape. He is survived by his wife Annie and his <a href="/wiki/Annie_and_Finnick_Odair%27s_son" title="Annie and Finnick Odair&#39;s son">son</a>.
</p><p>Another District 2 victor, <a href="/wiki/Lyme" title="Lyme">Lyme</a>, actively supported the rebel war effort, specifically in District 2, and rose to the rank of commander. Lyme was among the leaders that spearheaded the rebel offensive that ultimately secured District 2 following the fall of <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>. Lyme, however, was not present at the victors' meeting at the end of the war, presumably having died beforehand. 
</p><p>The status of other Career victors during the war remain unknown. Immediately after the end of the Third Quarter Quell, the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a> began and all victors were targeted by the Capitol for arrest and execution—the Careers from 1, 2, and especially 4 were unlikely to be an exception. It is presumed the vast majority ultimately perished by virtue of the purge and the war itself. During the victors' meeting held by Alma Coin, only Enobaria and Annie were present as former Careers, and are presumed to be the only surviving Careers alive at the end of the Second Rebellion.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Career tributes were almost always the strongest, most agile, skilled, and adaptable participants in the Hunger Games, having been trained since childhood. Throughout their years of preparation, Careers underwent rigorous instruction under the supervision of dedicated staff and prior victors at their specialized academies. This training ensured they were well-fed and developed muscular, athletic physiques, giving them a significant physical advantage over their competition. Additionally, future Careers were extensively trained in a wide range of weaponry expected to be available to them in the arena, making them proficient in tools and weapons that most other tributes had never used. This preparation not only enhanced their combat skills but also allowed them to deliver impressive performances during their private <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a> sessions, securing high training scores and attracting valuable sponsors. Combined with their willingness to kill without hesitation - a mindset cultivated through years of training and district support - Careers entered the Games with a tremendous mental and physical advantage over their fellow tributes.
</p><p>Despite the inherent advantages, Careers often suffered from overconfidence, impulsiveness, and a sense of superiority. In the movies, they were portrayed as overly talkative and dismissive of their competition, with Careers such as <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> notably mocking and taunting other tributes they were going to kill or have killed. In the novels, the Career tributes were depicted more stealthy and menacing, but their arrogance remains a consistent flaw. In both depictions, the Careers’ overconfidence led them to underestimate their opponents, often causing them to act rashly without fully considering the consequences.
</p><p>Their upbringing also presented significant disadvantages. Accustomed to being well-fed throughout their lives, Careers were less resilient to hunger compared to tributes from poorer districts, who were used to scarcity and missing meals. As such, Careers tended to be overly reliant on supplies secured from the Cornucopia and sponsor gifts. This in turn made them vulnerable when these resources are removed or are lacking. Additionally, their heavy builds, a result of their physically rigorous lifestyle, often hindered their ability to climb obstacles or chase lighter, faster tributes. 
</p><p>While Careers excelled in combat, particularly against other tributes, their survival skills were generally lacking. If they failed to eliminate their most dangerous opponents in the initial bloodbath, they gradually lost their strategic advantage, unless they maintained control of the Cornucopia’s supplies and systematically removed their strongest competitors. This combination of overconfidence, reliance on resources, and poor survival skills often proved to be their downfall in the later stages of the Games.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Selection">Selection</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Career tributes were known to always volunteer for the Games, causing much more commotion during <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> in their districts than necessary. In the other districts (especially in poorer ones such as districts <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>), becoming a volunteer tribute was viewed as suicide, and thus rare. However, in Career districts, volunteering was very common, as the district populace eagerly supported the Games and thus saw volunteers' participation in it honorable, even if it meant risking their lives. 
</p><p>Typically, Careers were 18 years old when they volunteered, allowing them to maximize their time in training before their eligibility for the Games elapsed. However, Careers could and have been known to enter the Games at younger ages. Finnick Odair won the 65th Hunger Games at 14, the youngest ever tribute to do so. In the 74th Hunger Games, the Careers varied in age, with Cato being the only Career stated to be 18. Marvel and Glimmer were both 17 (as per the film), while Clove was the youngest of the Careers at 15 (also per the film).
</p><p>Despite the institutionalized practice of training tributes for the Games, it is unknown if Career districts had a process for pre-determining who would volunteer during the reaping. It is likely that each Career district had differing means of selecting their most promising Career trainee to represent them, built up over years of Hunger Games. Reapings could go on for hours in the Career districts, implying situations with multiple volunteers.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Role_in_the_Games">Role in the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-right show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Career-Tributes.png/revision/latest?cb=20140318020704" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Careers in training for the ." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="168" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Career-Tributes.png" data-image-key="Career-Tributes.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Career-Tributes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140318020704" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Career-Tributes.png/revision/latest?cb=20140318020704" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Careers in training for the ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Career-Tributes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140318020704" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="168" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Career-Tributes.png" data-image-key="Career-Tributes.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Career-Tributes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20140318020704" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Career-Tributes.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Careers in training for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Prior to the start of each Hunger Games, tributes from Career districts (1, 2, and 4) typically form an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>, colloquially known as the "Career Pack" or "the Pack". This alliance is established during the first day of training, and its members train together exclusively, using this time to assess each other's skills, evaluate their competition, and formalize their strategy for the Games. The standard Career alliance usually consist of six members - two from each Career district. However, being from a Career district does not guarantee entry into the alliance. During the 74th Hunger Games, the male tribute from District 4 died during the opening bloodbath, an unusual outcome that suggests he was excluded from the alliance. It was likely that he was deemed unfit for the alliance due to not meeting the typical Career standards, and similar exclusions may have occurred in previous Games. Conversely, tributes from non-Career districts who demonstrate exceptional physical prowess or useful skills may be invited to join the alliance. <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, for instance, was included into the alliance for his physical strength (as well as to help locate and neutralize Katniss), while <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> was also invited for both his imposing size and strength.
</p><p>Once the Games were underway, the Career alliance's standard opening strategy was to secure <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> and supplies from the Cornucopia while eliminating as many other tributes as possible, particularly their most dangerous opponents. This strategy gives the Careers a significant advantage during the opening <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. Their large alliance, combined with the protection it provides and their pre-planned tactics, allows Careers to emerge from the single most dangerous moment of the Games largely unscathed. As a result, Careers are often responsible for the majority of initial deaths in a typical Hunger Games and in control of the Cornucopia and its cacophony of weapons and supplies. 
</p><p>With abundant supplies, Careers typically claimed their preferred weapon and began a systematic hunt for the remaining tributes. Their dominance in the Games is implied to be a perennial occurrence and a persistent threat that non-Career tributes must face, alongside the inherent dangers of the arena and the Gamemakers interventions. Nevertheless, the Career alliance is not invincible. Tributes with equal or greater cunning and skill, such as Katniss and other non-Career victors, have proven capable of overcoming them and eliminating individual Careers themselves. For example, in the 74th Hunger Games, Katniss used her archery skills and knowledge of survival tactics to constantly outmaneuver the Careers, Foxface used stealth tactics to discretely steal food from the Careers in broad daylight, while Thresh relied on his physical strength and strategic isolation to avoid confrontation when necessary. These examples highlight the potential of non-Career tributes to exploit the alliance's weaknesses, such as overconfidence or internal discord.
</p><p>Despite their training, Careers are not immune to the stressors and challenges the arena offers. Hunger, dehydration, and fatigue can erode their physical and mental resilience, particularly if supplies are purposely scarce in the arena and/or sponsor gifts are not forthcoming. The psychological toll of killing and the constant threat of betrayal within the alliance can destabilize even the most cohesive groups as the Games progress. The overall stability of the Career alliance hinges on its individual members, their internal dynamics, and their ability to handle threats from other tributes and the arena itself. However, alliances among Careers are inherently fragile. While they may collaborate initially, they are ultimately all competitors who have trained and are vying for the same prize, the victor's crown, which only one can claim. This underlying tension makes Careers ironically one of the greatest threats to each other, particularly if the alliance unravels before all other tributes are eliminated. The longer the Games drag on, the more likely it is that mistrust, ego, rivalry, and self-interest will fracture the group, leaving them vulnerable to external threats and internal conflicts.
</p><p>Once most of the other tributes are eliminated, the surviving members of the alliance will turn on one another to determine the victor of the Games. This final phase of the Hunger Games underscores the inherent tension within the Career alliance: while cooperation offers short-term advantages, the ultimate goal of survival and victory forces tributes to betray their allies. This dynamic has been a recurring theme in the Hunger Games, in both non-Career and presumably Career alliances in previous Games.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-Games">Post-Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the Career districts, <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> are celebrated as heroes and amass significant fame after winning the Games, even in the Capitol. Their victories are often portrayed as triumphs of skill and discipline, reinforcing the Career districts' cultural emphasis on glory and honor in the Hunger Games. Examples include <a href="/wiki/Augustus_Braun" title="Augustus Braun">Augustus Braun</a> of District 1, hailed as Panem's "Favorite Son" and the "Cavalier Career" after his victory in the 67th Games; Enobaria of District 2, infamous for ripping out an opponent's throat with her teeth in the 62nd Games; and Finnick Odair of District 4, who won the 65th Games at just 14 years old, earning him fame for his combat skills, charisma, and striking appearance.
</p><p>Despite their origins in the loyal Career districts, victors from District 1, 2 and 4 were still subject to the same post-Games obligations as other non-Career victors. They were expected to fulfill their obligations in the years that followed for life, such as mentoring new tributes, participating in Capitol events, and adopting an official hobby to maintain their public image. Many Career victors likely returned to their district's Career training institution to serve as staff and/or instructors, passing on their knowledge to the next generation of Career tributes. This role not only reinforced their status as local icons, but also ensured the continued success of their districts in future Hunger Games.
</p><p>However, the privileges of victory came at a cost, even for Careers. Like all victors, Career victors presumably experienced severe psychological trauma, which may have been exacerbated by the intense pressure and expectations of their training, particularly from an early age. The brutal nature of the Games, combined with the moral toll of killing children and constant scrutiny from the Capitol, likely left many with long-term mental health issues. A poignant example providing insight to this comes from Cato (in the film), who in his final standoff with Katniss, gives a desperate rant indicating he realizes he has trained his whole life to be only a pawn in the Capitol's spectacle that was the Games. This moment reveals the psychological devastation wrought by the Hunger Games and underscores the futility and despair that even the most hardened Career tributes faced. To cope with their trauma, Career victors, like other non-Career victors, likely turned to vices such as drug abuse and alcoholism. 
</p><p>The lives of Career victors, while outwardly glamorous, were marked by the same struggles faced by non-Career victors. Particularly attractive Career victors, such as Finnick (and possibly Gloss and Cashmere) were subjected to exploitation, including sex trafficking orchestrated by President Snow as a means of control and punishment.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This exploitation served as a reminder that even the most celebrated victors were ultimately tools of the Capitol's propaganda and depravity. Despite their training and status, Career victors were not immune to the systemic oppressions that defined life under the Capitol's rule. Their experiences underscore the broader themes of the Hunger Games: the illusion of glory, the cost of survival, and the pervasive control exerted by the Capitol over all its citizens, even those who appeared to benefit from its favor.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_Careers">Known Careers</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><b>District 1</b>
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Palladium_Barker" title="Palladium Barker">Palladium Barker</a>: Victor of the <a href="/wiki/46th_Hunger_Games" title="46th Hunger Games">46th Hunger Games</a>. Not much is known about them, not even their gender, other than they were related to Panache Barker, one of the male tributes for the 50th Hunger Games. Their fate is unknown, and is presumed deceased either due to natural causes, during the Victor's Purge, or during the Second Rebellion.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/District_1_female_(50th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 1 female (50th HG)">Silka Sharp</a>: Female volunteer tribute and runner up of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. Killed by her own axe when Haymitch ducked and the axe rebounded off the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> and buried itself in her head. Her (presumed) weapon specialization was a throwing axe.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>: Male volunteer tribute for the 50th Hunger Games. He was related to a Palladium Barker, the victor of the 46th Hunger Games. He was killed by <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> after she shot a poisonous dart at his neck before he could kill Haymitch. His weapon specialization was a sword.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Carat" title="Carat">Carat</a>: Female volunteer tribute for the 50th Hunger Games. Not much is known about her, other than she survived the initial Bloodbath, but died later on on the first day after consuming some of the poisoned food or water in the Arena.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Loupe" title="Loupe">Loupe</a>: Male volunteer tribute for the 50th Hunger Games. Not much is known about him, other than he was killed by Maysilee on the second day by a poisonous dart after breaking away from the pack with Camilla from 2.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a>: Male victor of the <a href="/wiki/63rd_Hunger_Games" title="63rd Hunger Games">63rd Hunger Games</a> and the male tribute for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. He was Cashmere's brother and won the Games preceding her own victory. He was killed by Katniss shooting an arrow into his temple (chest in the movie) after he slit Wiress' throat in the 75th Hunger Games. His weapon specialization was throwing knives.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a>: Female victor of the <a href="/wiki/64th_Hunger_Games" title="64th Hunger Games">64th Hunger Games</a> and the female tribute for the 75th Hunger Games. She was Gloss's sister and won the Games following his own. She was killed by <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>, who threw an axe into her chest. Her weapon specialization was throwing daggers.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Augustus_Braun" title="Augustus Braun">Augustus Braun</a>: Male victor of the <a href="/wiki/67th_Hunger_Games" title="67th Hunger Games">67th Hunger Games</a> whose victory earned him great fame, being hailed as Panem's "Favorite Son" and known as the "Cavalier Career". Fate is unknown, and is presumed deceased, having either died due to natural causes, during the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>, or during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a>: Male volunteer tribute for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. He was killed by Katniss with an arrow after he killed Rue by throwing his spear at her. His weapon specialization was a throwing spear (javelin).</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>: Female volunteer tribute for the 74th Hunger Games. She was killed by tracker jackers, whose nest was dropped on her by Katniss. Her weapon specialization was a bow, but was noted to have poor aim.</li></ul>
<p><b>District 2</b>
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Alpheus" title="Alpheus">Alpheus</a>: Male volunteer for the 50th Hunger Games. Not much is known about him other than he died in the volcano eruption with the rest of his district partners.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Camilla" title="Camilla">Camilla</a>: Female volunteer for the 50th Hunger Games. Not much is known about her other than she narrowly escaped death at the hands of Maysilee, only to later he die in the volcano eruption with the rest of her district partners.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Janus" title="Janus">Janus</a>: Male volunteer for the 50th Hunger Games. Not much is known about him other than he died in the volcano eruption with the rest of his district partners.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Nona" title="Nona">Nona</a>: Female volunteer for the 50th Hunger Games. Not much is known about her other than she died in the volcano eruption with the rest of her district partners.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Brutus" title="Brutus">Brutus</a>: Male victor for an unknown Hunger Games and the male volunteer tribute for the 75th Hunger Games. Killed by Peeta after Brutus killed <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">Chaff</a>. His weapon specialization was a spear.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Lyme" title="Lyme">Lyme</a>: Female victor for an unknown Hunger Games. She was alive at the outbreak of the Second Rebellion and a commander of the District 2 rebels, leading the rebel offensive during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_District_2" title="Battle of District 2">Battle of District 2</a>. Fate is unknown following the battle and is presumed deceased at the end of the Second Rebellion.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>: Female victor for the <a href="/wiki/62nd_Hunger_Games" title="62nd Hunger Games">62nd Hunger Games</a> and the female volunteer tribute for the 75th Hunger Games. She was captured by the Capitol after the 75th Hunger Games and still alive after the Second Rebellion. Her weapon specialization was a sword, but she also used throwing knives. Famed for having her teeth filed into fangs.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>: Male volunteer tribute for the 74th Hunger Games. He was killed by Katniss using her final arrow out of pity after being mauled for hours by wolf muttations. His weapon specialization was a short broadsword.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>: Female volunteer tribute for the 74th Hunger Games. She was killed by <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> with a rock slammed into her skull at the <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">Feast</a>. In the film, Thresh bashed her head against the Cornucopia twice, cracking her skull. Her weapon specialization was throwing knives.</li></ul>
<p><b>District 4</b>
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Urchin" title="Urchin">Urchin</a>: Male volunteer for the 50th Hunger Games. He survived the initial Bloodbath, but died later on on the first day after consuming poisoned food or water in the Arena.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Barba" title="Barba">Barba</a>: Female volunteer for the 50th Hunger Games. She was killed by Haymitch after she, Panache and Angler ambushed him, getting stabbed in the stomach. Her weapon specialization was a trident and knife.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Angler" title="Angler">Angler</a>: Male volunteer for the 50th Hunger Games. He was killed by Haymitch after he, Panache and Barba ambushed him, getting cut on the leg and throat slit by an axe. His weapon specialization was possibly a trident.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a>: Female volunteer for the 50th Hunger Games. She was killed by golden squirrel mutts, likely targeted after killing a Gamemaker who was in the Arena. Her weapon specialization was a trident.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a>: Male victor for the <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a> and the male tribute for the 75th Hunger Games. Youngest victor in Hunger Games history at aged 14. Friend and ally of Katniss and Peeta during the 75th Hunger Games and the Second Rebellion who was killed by <a href="/wiki/Muttations#Lizard_mutts" class="mw-redirect" title="Muttations">lizard mutts</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>: Female victor for the <a href="/wiki/70th_Hunger_Games" title="70th Hunger Games">70th Hunger Games</a> and the initial female tribute for the 75th Hunger Games. She was captured and tortured by the Capitol at the outbreak of the Second Rebellion to hurt Finnick. Alive at the end of the rebellion.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Bravura" title="Bravura">Bravura</a>: Male volunteer tribute for the 74th Hunger Games (books). He was killed on Day 1 during the bloodbath, an unusual occurrence for a Career tribute, implying he was either excluded from the alliance or was an unskilled Career. In the film, he wasn't a Career tribute and was killed by Cato during the bloodbath.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Tippet" title="Tippet">Tippet</a>: Female volunteer tribute for the 74th Hunger Games (books). She was killed by tracker jackers alongside Glimmer on Day 5, whose nest was dropped on the Careers by Katniss. In the film, she wasn't a Career tribute and was killed by Cato in the bloodbath.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCareer_Tribute%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, District 4 was not a Career district, so Careers were only known to come from District 1 and 2.</li>
<li>While no official numbers are given for the total number of victors from Districts 1, 2, and 4, it is statistically likely that together they had the highest share of victors of the twelve districts for the Hunger Games.</li>
<li>The term "Pack" was first used by <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a> during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> to describe <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>'s large alliance, where she successfully recruited <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a>—the strongest tributes, who all had some experience with weapons or killing prior to the Games—to hunt down the other tributes with her during the start of the Games.
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> could also be seen as something as an early iteration of a Career Tribute and the two may have even served as the inspirations for their <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">district</a> to begin producing Career Tributes. As their performance in 10th Hunger Games clearly showcased how their prior training with tridents and other fishing tools allowed them to quickly use them as weapon and greatly increased their chances of surviving and winning.</li>
<li>Coral’s large alliance might have also inspired the Careers' usual strategy of gathering the strongest/well trained tributes to work together in a large group to hunt weaker tributes before turning on each other.</li></ul></li>
<li>Haymitch Abernathy nicknamed the Careers the "Neer Beer Careers," "because they're all foam and no kicker."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
