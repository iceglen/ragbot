<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWillamae_Abernathy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Willamae and her <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy%27s_father" title="Haymitch Abernathy&#39;s father">husband</a> married young and had two sons together. Her eldest, Haymitch, was born on July 4th<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, the day of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 34th Hunger Games. At some point in his childhood, they took Haymitch to say goodbye to the orphaned <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> <a href="/wiki/Sarshee_Whitcomb" title="Sarshee Whitcomb">Sarshee Whitcomb</a> after her reaping by pretending to be her kin. Willamae had her younger son, Sid, sometime around the 40th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She was also pregnant once with twin girls who died after they were born too early.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Willamae was widowed young when her husband died in a coal mine fire some years before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She worked as a laundry worker in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. Every year on Haymitch's birthday, she cooked him something nice for breakfast.<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Haymitch started working for <a href="/wiki/Hattie_Meeney" title="Hattie Meeney">Hattie Meeney</a>, his wages were given directly to Willamae.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On Haymitch's sixteenth birthday, she made him corn bread and asked him to fill up the cistern before the reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch ended up being handpicked to be a tribute for the 50th Hunger Games (the second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>), by <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> after they witnessed the killing of <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a>. Desperate to say a proper goodbye to her son, she agreed to give <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> a cued reaction to Haymitch's reaping for the camera. Although he was pleased with her first reaction, he kept pushing her and Sid to do more and more until Haymitch demanded that it stop. As a reward, they got to say their goodbye. Haymitch encouraged her to be brave, and she reminded him not to let <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> use him.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Haymitch managed to lead his final opponent, <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> to the edge of the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> and use its <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> to launch her own <a href="/wiki/Axe" class="mw-redirect" title="Axe">axe</a> back at her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was subsequently crowned as District 12's second <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWillamae_Abernathy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Two weeks after his victory, Sid and his mother were burned alive in their house by arson as punishment for his rebelliousness. <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a> ruled their deaths an accident.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch recounted the deaths to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> twenty-five years later after the Capitol was overthrown and replaced by a new government.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_E_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR_E-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWillamae_Abernathy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Haymitch described his mother as incredibly resourceful, ensuring that nothing went to waste. She worked hard and expected her children to do the same, often assigning them difficult tasks and refusing to accept excuses. Although she was stern, she tried to instill optimism in her children. In the arena, Haymitch recalled her always saying "Where there's life, there's hope."<sup id="cite_ref-SR17_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR17-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWillamae_Abernathy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Although she was only 35, by the second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, sorrowful experiences had already made her wrinkled. Her muscles were visibly straining as she washed a pair of overalls.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
