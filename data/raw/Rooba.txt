<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRooba%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She once butchered a deer for <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, leaving Katniss with enough money to buy <a href="/wiki/Lady" title="Lady">Lady</a> as a birthday gift for her sister, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a>. Rooba also helped Katniss buy Lady from the <a href="/wiki/Goat_Man" title="Goat Man">Goat Man</a>. The butcher had originally had a deal to buy and butcher the injured goat, but she backed out, citing the damage to the meat, and winked to Katniss as she left.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>She was most likely killed in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, since she wasn't mentioned among the refugees in <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>. As a shop owner, she was likely one of the many victims from the <a href="/wiki/Merchant" title="Merchant">merchant</a> section of town; fewer than a dozen merchants survived the bombing.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRooba%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rooba was said to be a "short, chunky woman" and appeared old, yet tough and strong.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRooba%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rooba would buy rabbits, but not squirrels,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and she didn't barter; she gave a fair price that someone could take or leave.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> From her brief appearance in Katniss's story about the goat, she seemed to be firm and uncompromising, but fundamentally a kind, helpful woman.
