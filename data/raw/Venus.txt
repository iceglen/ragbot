<p><b>Venus</b> was a four-year-old girl who talked to <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> when she was in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>. She had an older brother named <a href="/wiki/Pontius" title="Pontius">Pontius</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVenus%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Venus was among a group of small children clustered near the bars of the monkey house, where the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> were located. Prompted by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> walked over and shook hands with Pontius, then asked him about Venus, who stood next to him. Venus complimented Lucy Gray's <a href="/wiki/Songs" title="Songs">song</a> and nodded when Lucy Gray said she would try to sing another. She also asked Lucy Gray about the snake and told her she had a fish named Bub. After this exchange, Venus buried her face in her mother's skirt, garnering laughter from the crowd.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
