<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>In general, a <b>school</b> is a place where children receive an education. There are schools in both <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. 
</p><p>The main purpose of schools is to train children in preparation to work in their districts' industries. Schools also serve as propaganda outlets to indoctrinate them into submission to the Capitol and to discourage rebellion, similar to schools in most countries run by authoritarian regimes.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="School_in_the_districts">School in the districts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_2"><a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Some of the people of District 2 were educated for military jobs in <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>, while others would become quarry or mine workers, and others still were funneled into training as <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_14_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_14-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Also, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>'s elementary school in District 2 took class photos, and they did not have school uniforms.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_12"><a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Every lesson seemed to come back to coal, except for basic reading, math, and the weekly lecture on the history of Panem. According to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, it was highly <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propagandized</a> "blather" about what the districts owed the Capitol, rather than an actual account of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, she learned enough history to know that rebels' attempt to scale the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mountains" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rocky Mountains">Rocky Mountains</a> led to the failure of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Children also learned precious little about geography; Katniss was taught that the Capitol was built in the Rockies, and that District 12 was in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Appalachia">Appalachia</a>, but did not learn much about other districts beyond their industries.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>School subjects were taught by different instructors, since Katniss mentioned having a <a href="/wiki/District_12_music_teacher" title="District 12 music teacher">music teacher</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and a math teacher.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_19_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_19-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Outside of regular lessons, the school held assemblies, sports activities,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and competitions.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The school also organized annual tours of the mines, and students graduated from school to work at the age of 18.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Additionally, teachers informed students about mandatory television programming.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Prior to the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, students were shown how to mine coal and practiced with inert explosives.<sup id="cite_ref-SR11_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR11-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, this practice was apparently discontinued, since Katniss later said District 12 tributes had no hands-on experience with their trade like children from other districts did.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13"><a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 13's school was called the Education Center. Anyone required to attend would have their schedule printed onto their arm in temporary ink each morning. There was a rarely-used supply closet in the Education Center, as the people of 13 were so frugal that they rarely needed to retrieve anything from it.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In terms of classes, there was one on nuclear history.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Delly_Cartwright" title="Delly Cartwright">Delly Cartwright</a> said that school was "much more interesting" than in District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Apart from the Education Center, people over the age of 14 were given combat training in preparation for the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> District 13 also had an orientation for new citizens, the subject matter of which is largely unknown, but <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> said she learned about bunker missiles there.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_10_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_10-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_districts">Other districts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Other than District 12, it seemed to be common practice for other districts' children to begin working before they left school.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<ul><li>In the films, <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">Districts 1</a> and 2 trained in special academies until they turned 18, at which point they would <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a> to compete in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Children in <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> learned to manipulate audio in school.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and her teacher <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a> worked a four-hour shift at a textile factory for Peacekeeper uniforms after school.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, lessons were not held amid the harvest. Everyone, including young children like <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, worked during this time.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">Victors</a> under the age of 18 were not required to keep attending school. A monetary prize from the Capitol meant they would not have to work to survive, so further education in their district's industry was no longer needed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="School_in_the_Capitol">School in the Capitol</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Elementary_school">Elementary school</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the Dark Days, the government provided a free snack of "scratchy, tasteless" nutritional crackers in order to incentivize children to attend.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Secondary_school">Secondary school</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Academy.png/revision/latest?cb=20230427234000" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Academy" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Academy.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230427234000" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="123" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Academy.png" data-image-key="Academy.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Academy.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Academy</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, there was an elite, four-year secondary school known as the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>, with over four hundred students in each class. Tuition was free— though acceptance was dependent on a family's prestige— and uniforms, supplies, and lunches were provided to students.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Students who committed serious infractions would receive a demerit, and three demerits would result in a student's expulsion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, the Academy had a tradition of offering students a lifeline as an alternative to public disgrace.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Subjects taught at the Academy included communications, physical education,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> history,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and high biology, which was restricted to only senior students "gifted in the science".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_4_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_4-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> High-performing Academy students would be presented with financial prizes upon graduation in midsummer, which could pay the steep tuition fees at the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a>. Like in District 12, students graduated at 18.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_23-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Capitol also had other secondary schools whose commencements fell earlier than the Academy's.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Continuing_education">Continuing education</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The only known subject at the University was a "special honors class in military strategy", which was taught by Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> at the University Science Center.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="School_in_post-war_Panem">School in post-war Panem</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>More than twenty years after the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, the Hunger Games were taught about at school, and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_daughter" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark&#39;s daughter">Katniss and Peeta Mellark's daughter</a>&#160;knew that her parents played a role in them.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_Epilogue_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_Epilogue-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSchool%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> fell in love with Katniss when they were five and he heard her sing <a href="/wiki/Songs#&quot;The_Valley_Song&quot;" title="Songs">the valley song</a> in music assembly.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The year before the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Peeta came second in a school wrestling competition, after one of his brothers.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Katniss became friends with <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a> at school. They ate lunch together, sat beside one another in assemblies, and paired up for sports activities.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> did not attend school in District 12, instead working full-time as a musician.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
