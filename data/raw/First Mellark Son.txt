</p><p>The <b>first Mellark son</b> was the first child of <a href="/wiki/Mr._Mellark" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Mellark">Mr.</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Mellark" title="Mrs. Mellark">Mrs. Mellark</a>. He was also the older brother of an <a href="/wiki/Second_Mellark_son" title="Second Mellark son">unnamed boy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFirst_Mellark_son%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Mellarks' first son was born in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. He was never <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. According to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, he was often at the Mellark bakery and was older than 18 when Peeta was reaped for the 74th Hunger Games; he would have been able to <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a> if he were younger.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When District 12 was <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombed</a> by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, he was killed along with the rest of his family, excluding Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
