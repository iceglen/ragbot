<p><b>Weapons</b> were prominent features of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> event. At the beginning of every Games, weapons were among the principal supplies found at the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, and were initially acquired during the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">Tributes</a> who did not obtain weapons at this stage had to acquire them later, either from the Cornucopia itself or from another tribute. Weapons were also sometimes sent to tributes as <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a> from their <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a>.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/9c/Weapons.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20161205210613" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Clove&#39;s knives and Peeta&#39;s medicine ball." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/9c/Weapons.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20161205210613" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="241" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Weapons.jpg" data-image-key="Weapons.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Weapons.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Clove's knives and Peeta's medicine ball.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Outside of the Hunger Games, the ownership of weapons was strictly prohibited. In order to prevent uprisings and rebellions in the districts, the only individuals permitted to own weapons were the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a>, prompting <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> to hide her <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">bow and arrows</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a>. While her <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">father</a> could have made money from selling bows, he would have been publicly <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">executed</a> for inciting a rebellion if he got caught. Despite this, Katniss continued to hunt, and although this was technically illegal, <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s relatively lenient Peacekeepers overlooked it.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Ownership of utilitarian weapons, such as knives, seemed to be permitted,<sup id="cite_ref-:5_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> since they were used for everyday purposes. The industries of certain districts presumably involved the use of weapons under extremely close observation, including spears, nets, and tridents in <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> (fishing), and axes in <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> (lumber).
</p><p>The main exception to these restrictions was <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, which gained political independence from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> and was able to stockpile a vast arsenal of firearms, missiles, and other military equipment. During the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, spearheaded by District 13, the rebels were able to acquire sufficient weapons and support to wage war against the Capitol. Legal restrictions on weapon ownership following the war are unknown, but it is implied that the district citizens have much more freedom to own and use weaponry.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Weapons_in_the_Hunger_Games">Weapons in the Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Awl">Awl</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>An <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/awl" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:awl">awl</a> can take various forms, but typically consists of a slender spike and some form of handgrip (similar in design to a screwdriver). More of a <a href="/wiki/Tools_and_gear" title="Tools and gear">tool</a> than a weapon, it is traditionally used in handicrafts such as wood and leather working: 
</p>
<ul><li>During the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss obtained an awl from the Cornucopia and gave it to <a href="/wiki/Mags" class="mw-redirect" title="Mags">Mags</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the group realized that the trees in the arena were full of water, the awl was used to bore holes in the trunks in preparation for inserting the spile.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_20_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_20-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Axe">Axe</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 230px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/54/Johanna_axes_Heritage_Auctions.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20231230190130" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Johanna Mason&#39;s axes from Catching Fire" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/54/Johanna_axes_Heritage_Auctions.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/230?cb=20231230190130" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="230" height="139" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Johanna axes Heritage Auctions.jpg" data-image-key="Johanna_axes_Heritage_Auctions.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Johanna_axes_Heritage_Auctions.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Johanna Mason's axes from <i>Catching Fire</i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>An <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/axe" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:axe">axe</a> (ax) is an implement that has been used for millennia to shape, split and cut wood. It has also been widely used as a weapon and as a ceremonial or heraldic symbol. Axes can take many different forms depending on their intended use, but generally, they consist of an axe head with a handle, or helve:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a> and <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> both used axes during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>.</li>
<li>During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> escaped from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a> with only an axe that he carried in his belt.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch doubted that Ampert was actually strong enough to wield it.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR18_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR18-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>During the 50th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> armed herself an axe that Ampert described as being razor sharp. Ampert implied that Silka had used it to kill some of <a href="/wiki/The_Newcomers" title="The Newcomers">the Newcomers</a> during the bloodbath.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Haymitch was on the verge of defeat at the hands of <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a>, who was armed with an axe. When she threw it at him as he got to an edge of a cliff, he was able to dodge it, lulling her into believing that he would die first as he had been <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disembowelment" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Disembowelment">disemboweled</a>. However, the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> surrounding the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>'s generator caused the axe to fly back and kill her, allowing Haymitch to emerge as the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR24_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Following Ampert's death, Haymitch grabbed the boy's axe and used it to chop up the area in rage.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR18_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR18-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Unable to find his spear again after the volcano eruption, Haymitch retraced his steps to Ampert's axe which he had dropped when the earth shook and flung him to the ground and took it as a replacement weapon. It also served as a memento of Ampert. When Haymitch was attacked by <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Barba" title="Barba">Barba</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Angler" title="Angler">Angler</a>, he killed Angler by cleaving open his neck with the axe after hamstringing the boy. However, Panache disarmed Haymitch who was only saved by the intervention of <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR19_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR19-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch used the axe against the porcupine mutt that killed <a href="/wiki/Buck" title="Buck">Buck</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chicory" title="Chicory">Chicory</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hull" title="Hull">Hull</a>, throwing it into the mutt's side, but he failed to do any real damage and the porcupine shook the axe loose.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR21_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR21-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch later used it to duel Silka axe-to-axe, landing a few blows before being disemboweled and disarmed.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR24_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_film" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games film"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">District 9 male</a> briefly used an epsilon axe - named for its similarity to the Greek letter epsilon (ϵ) - during the bloodbath at the Cornucopia. He tried to kill Katniss with it, but <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> killed him with one of her throwing knives before he could complete the deed.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a>,</i> <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> was highly proficient with axes, which she used to kill <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a>. Since Johanna came from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>, where the main industry was lumber, Katniss mused that she must have been handling axes since she was a toddler.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Throwing_axe">Throwing axe</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>While axes are normally used as melee weapons, they can also be thrown, and some are especially designed for that purpose. The latter is never specifically mentioned in the novels, but some characters indeed use axes as distance weapons:
</p>
<ul><li>Before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> practiced throwing axes with <a href="/wiki/Ringina" title="Ringina">Ringina</a> during training. The axe that he took from <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> was on the long side, but not too dissimilar from the throwing axe that he'd practiced with.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR21_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR21-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the 74th Hunger Games, the District 3 male acquired one, but <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> stole it from him.</li>
<li>In the 75th Hunger Games, Johanna acquired a pair of axes from the Cornucopia, and she was able to throw one so hard that it lodged into the solid gold surface.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Crossbow">Crossbow</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/crossbow" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:crossbow">crossbow</a> is a mechanized version of the bow and arrow that originated in China over 2000 years ago. Rather than being held upright, the crossbow is mounted horizontally across the handle, or stock, and equipped with a trigger. Although it takes longer to load and fire than a traditional bow, it can be learned much more easily and the missile, or bolt, can be launched at any time without the physical strain of holding back the string.
</p>
<ul><li>In <i>The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i>, <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> used a crossbow during the <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

</p>
<ul><li>During the 10th Hunger Games, Coriolanus Snow gave Lucy Gray <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow&#39;s mother">his mother's</a> <a href="/wiki/Silver_compact" title="Silver compact">silver compact</a> to sneak rat poison from the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> into the arena as a weapon against the other tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a> later died after drinking from a water bottle poisoned by Lucy Gray<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and Reaper Ash drank from a puddle poisoned by Lucy Gray while he suffered from heat exhaustion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Lucy Gray later admitted to Snow that her intention had been to poison Coral, but Wovey got the water bottle instead.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The discovery of Snow's cheating forced him into the Peacekeeper service for 20 years.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<li>During the 50th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> used a blowgun loaded with darts dipped in the arena's poisonous substances as a weapon.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With this, she killed <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR19_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR19-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Loupe" title="Loupe">Loupe</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Although Maysilee believed that she hit <a href="/wiki/Camilla" title="Camilla">Camilla</a> as well, the Career tribute survived the poison and died in the later volcanic eruption. The vial was designed for perfume, so it had a good tight lid to prevent evaporation, but Maysilee wished that it held more.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Use_of_poison_by_President_Snow">Use of poison by President Snow</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> and his time in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, Snow took rat poison from the back alley of <a href="/wiki/Pluribus_Bell" title="Pluribus Bell">Pluribus Bell</a>'s nightclub and slipped it into the <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> that <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> had received for his breakdown. When Snow presented Plinth's personal effects to Dean <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, he deliberately slid the bottles of morphling into the trash, knowing that Highbottom - a morphling addict - would later retrieve them, ingest the rat poison, and die. This was his first murder with what would become his signature weapon.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_37-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Over the next 64 years, Snow would rise to power as the president of Panem, and he maintained control by poisoning allies and enemies alike who posed a threat to him. Snow often drank from the same poisoned glass to deflect suspicion. Although <a href="/wiki/Medical_treatments#Poison_antidote" title="Medical treatments">antidotes</a> saved his life, he was left with mouth sores that wouldn't heal. He wore roses to cover up the persistent stench of blood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_38-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the disastrous <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a> before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, Snow poisoned <a href="/wiki/Incitatus_Loomy" title="Incitatus Loomy">Incitatus Loomy</a> for his incompetence with a plate of oysters that he also ate from. While meeting with <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> at <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>'s home, Snow was visibly ill and was tended to by Haymitch and Plutarch. Snow took an antidote that he carried in his pocket and Haymitch realized what had happened to Incitatus from Snow's comments.<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the final morning of the 50th Hunger Games, Haymitch received a sponsor gift of what he believed to be a jug of poisoned milk from Snow who was seeking to force Haymitch to kill either <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> or himself with it. Before Haymitch could drink it, Wellie was found and killed by <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> and Haymitch smashed the jug instead.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR24_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The jug of poisoned milk was edited out of the final Game footage.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As retaliation for Haymitch's actions during the 50th Hunger Games, Snow sent a bag full of bloodred poisoned candy to <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>. When Lenore Dove ate one, the poison in it killed her despite Haymitch's desperate attempts to save her.<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Snow had Head Gamemaker <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a> killed, although the method was never revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_42-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-42"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_43-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-43"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>43<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i>The Hunger Games</i> film, Crane was locked in a room with <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries, suggesting that Snow was executing him by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/forced_suicide" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:forced suicide">forced suicide</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the film <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire_(film)" class="mw-redirect" title="Catching Fire (film)">Catching Fire</a></i>, Plutarch mentioned to Katniss that Crane decided to "quit breathing... that or it was poison berries" implying that he ate the berries and died as a result.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_15-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, as a distraction for the rescue mission for <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a>, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> performed a live interview to be broadcast across Panem. In the interview, Finnick publicly exposed Snow's habit of poisoning people which Finnick had learned about from his Capitol "clients," having been paid in secrets by them, some of which were about Snow himself.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_12_38-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_12-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>By the time of Snow's execution, his sores had seemingly worsened because he occasionally coughed up blood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_44-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-44"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_45-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-45"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Ironically, Snow's habit of poisoning people may have ultimately led to his demise; Snow was believed to have either been trampled by the angry mob, or to have choked to death on his own blood.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_46-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-46"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>46<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

Prior to knowing Gale, Katniss had not had previous success with snares, although her father had used them.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_49-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-49"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>49<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunflower_bomb">Sunflower bomb</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Sunflower_bomb" title="Sunflower bomb">Sunflower bomb</a></i></center></div>
<p>The sunflower bomb was an explosive created by <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> for the rebel plot to disrupt the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. Unlike the components used in the <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> mines, Beetee specifically designed the ones used in his bombs to be safe, both chemically and structurally and the sunflower bombs couldn't be set off unintentionally by the user or anything else. They could only be detonated by being fully assembled and the fuse lit with <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR11_51-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR11-51"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Beetee made it so that the <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> tributes' sunflower tokens were composed of explosive, coating them with a shellac that could be dissolved with water and friction, leaving the explosives malleable.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR13_52-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR13-52"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> agreed to join the plan to blow up the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="50th Hunger Games arena">50th Hunger Games arena</a>'s water tank and drown its brain, Beetee told him about the bombs that he was building, although Beetee and the other rebels were still working out the details on how to smuggle the materials past security. Being from a mining district, Haymitch had received basic training in dealing with explosives and his <a href="/wiki/Flint_striker" title="Flint striker">flint striker</a> could be used to light the fuse.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR11_51-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR11-51"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, Beetee filled Haymitch in on the final details which amounted to the black cord in <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">Tribute token</a> being replaced with the fuse while the blasting cap was hidden in the weave and all of the <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> tributes now had their sunflower tokens composed of explosive. The tributes themselves were not in on the plan, so Ampert would retrieve one or more the sunflower bombs from their corpses. Beetee began to tell Haymitch about a backup that they had for if Ampert didn't survive, but they were interrupted by <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR13_52-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR13-52"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Beetee had been trying to tell Haymitch that they had secretly replaced the weave of <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>'s token with a backup fuse and blasting cap.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR24_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Haymitch suspected that Beetee had help from someone on the inside to smuggle it all past security,<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR13_52-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR13-52"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> likely Gamemakers and Peacekeepers who were rebel sympathizers.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_32-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Although all of the District 9 tributes were killed in the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR16_19-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR16-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Ampert managed to escape with two of the sunflower bombs, one of which was stained with blood.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After finding Haymitch, Ampert removed the three tokens under the guise of using his to create a snare and put the bomb together, giving the spare to Haymitch. At around midnight, the two made their way to Sub-A using a mutt portal that Haymitch had earlier found where Haymitch successfully planted the bomb on the water tank. The flood damaged the arena's brain, causing it to malfunction and causing damage, but they failed to stop it completely. In retaliation, the Gamemakers unleashed squirrel mutts to devour Ampert.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR18_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR18-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the final morning of the Games, Haymitch discovered that Maysilee's token had a backup fuse to the sunflower bombs. Haymitch decided to use the bomb on the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> as a symbolic gesture, hoping to stage a showdown with <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> there and kill her in the explosion as well. However, Silka found and killed <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> first, leading to a final confrontation where she was killed by her own axe rebounding off of the arena's <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>. Close to death from his own wounds, Haymitch decided to use the bomb on the generator, tearing the 60 second fuse so that there was only a few inches on it. Despite the attempts by the Gamemakers to stop him, Haymitch threw the bomb at the generator, presumably destroying it.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR24_7-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>A trident is a three-pronged spear that can be used both as a weapon and for <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/spear_fishing" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:spear fishing">spear fishing</a>. It was carried by various religious figures throughout history, such as the Greek Poseidon, the Roman Neptune, and the Indian Shiva. In addition to stabbing, the prongs on a trident can be used to trap other weapons and deflect them away from the wielder:  
</p>
<ul><li>During the 10th Hunger Games, both tributes from District 4, Coral and Mizzen, used tridents as their primary weapon. Working together they successfully managed to kill many tributes with their tridents, including a member of their alliance, Tanner.</li>
<li>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a> before the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Urchin" title="Urchin">Urchin</a> knocked <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> from his chariot using a trident.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR16_19-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR16-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>During the 50th Hunger Games, a trident was the weapon of choice of <a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a>. <a href="/wiki/Ampert" class="mw-redirect" title="Ampert">Ampert</a> described <a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a> as being "wicked" with it.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Maritte later used it to kill a Gamemaker and was forced to abandon her trident in the Gamemaker's body.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR22_54-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR22-54"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>During the 50th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Barba" title="Barba">Barba</a> also used a trident. trying to kill <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> with it.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR19_8-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR19-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After her death, Haymitch refused to claim it as he couldn't use a trident.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR20_12-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>During the 65th Hunger Games, a golden trident was delivered to Finnick Odair. Katniss described it as possibly "the most expensive gift" she had ever seen given to a tribute, and it helped Finnick to emerge as the victor.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>During the 75th Hunger Games, numerous tridents and a net were provided at the Cornucopia, which Finnick also used. In <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i>, the design of Finnick's trident included 3 blades protruding from the side of the shaft, as well as a long point extending from the top. With this design, the weapon could slash like a sword, as well as stab and be thrown like a spear.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_15-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<p>During the 74th Hunger Games, the Gamemakers unleashed a massive wall of <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a> to force the tributes closer together in the arena. Because of the fire's height and uniformity, Katniss could tell that it didn't occur naturally. The Gamemakers also shot fireballs at the tributes, severely injuring Katniss's leg. The Careers apparently got caught in the blaze too, as Katniss noticed their raspy voices due to smoke inhalation.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_53-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-53"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Rue's forearm was seemingly burned during the fire attack as well.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_48-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-48"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>48<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Fog">Fog</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the 75th Hunger Games, one of the twelve hourly obstacles encountered in the arena was a <a href="/wiki/Fog" title="Fog">poisonous fog</a>. Any skin that came in contact with the mist instantly erupted in extremely painful swellings, and too long or too heavy exposure resulted in death (as happened to Mags). Submersion in water helped to dull the pain and draw out the poison, but the skin was left heavily scabbed afterwards.
</p><p>Although the Gamemakers could seemingly control the direction and speed of the fog's movement, like all the hourly obstacles, it remained within its designated sector of the arena.
</p>

According to <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i>, the standard Peacekeeper’s rifle could fire a hundred rounds before reloading.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_59-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-59"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>59<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i>Catching Fire</i>, Katniss mentioned machine guns set up on rooftops,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_60-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-60"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>60<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and she said <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>'s stolen Peacekeeper gun could convert solar energy into "deadly rays of power", eliminating the need for ammunition.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_10_55-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_10-55"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>55<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Water_cannon">Water cannon</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>A <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/water_cannon" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:water cannon">water cannon</a> is a device that shoots a high-pressure stream of water. Typically, a water cannon can deliver a large volume of water, often over hundreds of feet. They are used in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/firefighting" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:firefighting">firefighting</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/riot_control" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:riot control">riot control</a>:
</p>
<ul><li>Water cannons do not appear in the books, but are used in <i>The Hunger Games</i> film by the Peacekeepers to suppress the uprising in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_10-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Whip">Whip</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>A whip is a weapon, mostly used for punishment but also for combat. It is used by "cracking" the whip through the air, making it hit an opponent painfully. Whips also typically feature a hard butt that can be used as a bludgeon.
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a> whipped Gale Hawthorne forty times for poaching. Darius tried to stop the whipping and was knocked unconscious with the butt of the whip.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_62-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-62"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>62<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the film, the whip used by Thread bears a strong resemblance to a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/scourge" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:scourge">scourge</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_15-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Weapons_used_by_District_13">Weapons used by District 13</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>I have to admit the arsenal takes my breath away. Row upon row of firearms, launchers, explosives, armored vehicles.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss's first impression of District 13's arsenal<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13_bombs">District 13 bombs</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>District 13 rebels bombed the mountainside of <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a> in District 2.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_63-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-63"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>63<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="City Circle bombing">City Circle bombing</a>, District 13 aircraft with the Capitol seal dropped <a href="/wiki/Silver_parachute" title="Silver parachute">silver parachutes</a> over a crowd of children, who had been primed by Hunger Games sponsor gifts to recognize these as aid parcels. However, these parachutes were actually bombs, and when medics came in to help the wounded, a second explosion killed them as well.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_24_64-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_24-64"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>64<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This turned people in the Capitol against President Snow and ended the war, since everyone believed he was behind the parachute bombs. However, the bombing had actually been done under the orders of District 13's President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_44-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-44"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>44<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>The strategy behind this bombing was devised by Gale Hawthorne and Beetee Latier.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_13_65-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_13-65"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>65<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13_bow_and_arrow">District 13 bow and arrow</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 13 had an entire wall of archery weapons. In particular, Katniss took note of a militarized bow that was weighed down with scopes and gadgetry; this was the one Gale picked out for his personal use throughout the novel.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_66-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-66"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>66<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <i>Mockingjay</i> films adapted this as a crossbow.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_67-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-67"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>67<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_68-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-68"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>68<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13_guns">District 13 guns</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Having gained political independence from the Capitol, District 13 had its own supply of guns, which they distributed to rebel forces across all districts during the First and Second Rebellion. In the films, the standard-issue infantry rifle is shown to be the Heckler and Koch G36K carbine, although rebel forces in the other districts are shown using all types of firearms, including the M1 Carbine, M14 rifle and MG-42 machine gun. Rebel forces in District 8 also used M2 Browning Machine guns.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_13_land_mine">District 13 land mine</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Simulated Street Combat training involved the possibility of triggering a mine in preparation for the assault on the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_61-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-61"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>61<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Nightlock_pills">Nightlock pills</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, soldiers from District 13 were given <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/suicide_pill" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:suicide pill">suicide pills</a> to swallow in the event that they were captured. Dubbed "nightlock", they had the same effect as poisonous <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_69-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-69"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>69<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Following Katniss's assassination of President Coin,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_45-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-45"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>45<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> she attempted to bite into her nightlock pill, but Peeta stopped her.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_46-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-46"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>46<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Squad_451">Squad 451</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a></i></center></div>
<p>Squad 451 was a sharpshooter unit created to film propaganda for the war effort. As a result, some members used special signature weapons alongside or in place of their standard-issue firearms.
</p>
<ul><li>In District 13, Beetee was tasked with designing Katniss's black Mockingjay bow. Katniss described it as having "exquisite balance" and "elegant design"; to her, the curve of the limbs suggested the wings of a bird in flight. His assignment was to make it purely for aesthetics, but Beetee "left the outside simple, and left the inside to [his] imagination."<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_66-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-66"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>66<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>The bow was programmed to only recognize Katniss's voice. If she wanted to use it as an ordinary bow, she could turn off its special features by saying "Good night" until the sound of her voice woke it again. Beetee designed a variety of color-coded arrows to go along with it as well: razor sharp, incendiary, and explosive.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_66-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-66"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>66<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Beetee also made a trident for Finnick with its own special features, though most were not described. The only one Katniss noted was that if Finnick pressed a button on a metal cuff on his wrist, it would call the weapon back to him after he'd thrown it, like a boomerang.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_61-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-61"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>61<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_weapons">Other weapons</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After being airlifted from the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a> by a District 13 <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a>, Katniss grabbed a syringe, intending to use it to mercy-kill Peeta rather than let the Capitol hurt him. After stumbling onto a meeting between Haymitch, Finnick, and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, she attempted to attack them with it, but Haymitch subdued her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_56-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-56"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
