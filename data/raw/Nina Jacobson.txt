</p><p><b>Nina Jacobson</b> is an American film executive who heads American film studio <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_Force_(film_company)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Color Force (film company)">Color Force</a>, which she founded in 2007. She was one of the main producers of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> and also produced <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.
</p><p>She returned as a producer for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-Collider_Film_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Collider_Film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Jacobson was previously the president of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buena_Vista_Motion_Pictures_Group" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Buena Vista Motion Pictures Group">Buena Vista Motion Pictures Group</a> until July 2006. She has also produced the first three films of the <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diary_of_a_Wimpy_Kid_(series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Diary of a Wimpy Kid (series)">Diary of a Wimpy Kid</a></i> series.
</p>
