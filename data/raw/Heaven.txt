Some people believe, she said, it's a possible destination after death. A good world for good people to go to.</i>"</span></dd>

<p><b>Heaven,</b> also known as the <b>hereafter</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> is a domain of the afterlife believed in by some citizens of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. It was believed to be inhabited by angels and good people.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The plural form, "heavens," could also be used to describe the sky,<sup id="cite_ref-:6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or as an exclamation.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeaven%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes"><i>The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeaven%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>There was a tradition from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, which <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> initiated after the deaths of <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>.  It consisted of sprinkling bread crumbs over the recently deceased while praying.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This tradition was to make sure the dead person had enough food on their journey "[b]ack to wherever [they] came from." The citizens of District 2 performed the ritual whenever somebody died. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> felt second-hand embarrassment for Sejanus' <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Plinth" title="Mrs. Plinth">Ma</a> after she explained the ritual. He considered it a waste of resources and thought it an example of "primitive customs."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  
</p><p>Three <a href="/wiki/Songs" title="Songs">songs</a> sung by <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> mention heaven(s): the Valley Song, "<a href="/wiki/The_Old_Therebefore" title="The Old Therebefore">The Old Therebefore</a>," and "Lucy Gray." The Valley Song, sung in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>, features the lyrics, "<i>Birds in the heavens know I love you.</i>"<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a> refused to feed her <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup</a>, her classmate <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a> told her, "Oh, for heaven's sake, feed him, Clemmie!"<sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	 <a href="https://thehungergames.fandom.com/wiki/File:The_Old_Therebefore_(Acapella)_(from_The_Hunger_Games-_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes)" class="video video-thumbnail xsmall "> 	<img src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/57/The_Old_Therebefore_%28Acapella%29_%28from_The_Hunger_Games-_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes%29/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240209052506" alt="The_Old_Therebefore_(Acapella)_(from_The_Hunger_Games-_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_&amp;_Snakes)" height="135" width="180" data-video-key="The_Old_Therebefore_(Acapella)_(from_The_Hunger_Games-_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_&amp;_Snakes)" data-video-name="The Old Therebefore (Acapella) (from The Hunger Games- The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes)" class="" /> 	<span class="thumbnail-play-icon-container"> 		<svg class="thumbnail-play-icon wds-icon"><use xlink:href="#wds-player-icon-play"></use></svg>	</span> </a>  	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:The_Old_Therebefore_(Acapella)_(from_The_Hunger_Games-_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes)" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 			<p class="title">The Old Therebefore (Acapella) (from The Hunger Games- The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes)</p> 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>"The Old Therebefore" was sung during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, after <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Dr. Gaul</a> released the <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">arena</a>. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> assumed that the song was an old one, due to it mentioning heaven. This implies that heaven was viewed as a thing of the past in the <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> after the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>. Its lyrics about "<i>the sweet old hereafter</i>" reminded Coriolanus of <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>-sprinkling tradition. The speaker in the song muses about being on the threshold between the present and the afterlife, and having "loose ends to tie up" before going to heaven.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  
</p><p>Partway through "Lucy Gray," which is a musical adaptation of Wiliam Wordsworth's <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Gray" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lucy Gray">poem of the same name</a> (which is Lucy Gray Baird's <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> ballad namesake), Lucy's Father and Mother cry when they can't find a trace of Lucy in the snow. They say, "In heaven we all shall meet," and then Lucy's Mother finds her footprints. However, they are unable to find Lucy in the end.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sunrise_on_the_Reaping"><i>Sunrise on the Reaping</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeaven%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Sunrise on the Reaping">"<i>“Prophet!” said I, “thing of evil! — prophet still, if bird or devil!/By that Heaven that bends above us — by that God we both adore —/Tell this soul with sorrow laden if, within the distant Aidenn,/It shall clasp a sainted maiden whom the angels name Lenore —/Clasp a rare and radiant maiden whom the angels name Lenore.”/Quoth the Raven “Nevermore.”</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Songs" title="Songs">The narrator in Poe's "The Raven"</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> hadn't heard of heaven before his girlfriend, <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>, explained it to him. It was mentioned in her Covey name ballad, "<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Raven" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Raven">The Raven</a>" by Edgar Allan Poe. She said it was where angels lived, and that it was a "good world for good people to go to" after death. Haymitch thought the only winged being on his mind was Lenore Dove herself. While reminiscing during the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, he was unsure whether he would be able to be with Lenore Dove again in heaven.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After watching the broadcast of the fallen tributes with <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, he considered whether he had actually died already and what he was then experiencing was "one of Lenore Dove's worlds, where [he] continue[s] to dream of life."<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After Maysilee died, Haymitch remarked that "she’s gone to wherever people go when they die."<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> sang "The Old Therebefore" as a dirge at the funeral of <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">Willamae</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt</a> and <a href="/wiki/Jethro_Callow" title="Jethro Callow">Jethro Callow</a>. Haymitch was consoled by the song's implication that his separation from his family and friends was "only temporary." He thought Lenore Dove would approve, too, if she hadn't been in prison. Mourners followed up the song with the <a href="/wiki/Three-finger_salute" title="Three-finger salute">three-finger salute</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the dissolution of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> and the usurping of <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">Presidents</a> Snow and <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Coin</a>, Haymitch started getting better, and stopped drinking out of compulsion. He believed that the goal Lenore Dove wanted to be achieved in her dying breath had finally come to fruition through Katniss after Haymitch had abandoned it decades ago. He considered Lenore Dove to age alongside him, speaking to him and guiding him. He wasn't sure how long his liver would last, and was unsure how long it would be until he moved on from "the old therebefore." He believed that nobody could ever take the woman he loved away from him.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeaven%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The Heavensbee family (including <a href="/wiki/Trajan_Heavensbee" title="Trajan Heavensbee">Trajan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Hilarius_Heavensbee" title="Hilarius Heavensbee">Hilarius</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch</a>), the namesake of <a href="/wiki/Heavensbee_Hall" title="Heavensbee Hall">Heavensbee Hall</a>, likely derived their family name from the term Heaven. This might imply that some people in the <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> still believe in Heaven and not just in the districts, or at least that their ancestors did.</li>
<li>The real-world folk song <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_in_the_Valley_(folk_song)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Down in the Valley (folk song)">Down in the Valley</a> has numerous variations, and the Valley Song is only one. Some other variations mention God and/or angels: "<i>God and his angels know I love you</i>"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or "<i>Angels in heaven know I love you</i>".<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>"The Old Therebefore" contains a few lyrics that may be Biblical references.
<ul><li><i>"When I've paid all my debts"</i> may be a reference to <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Colossians%202%3A14&amp;version=NIV">Colossians 2:14</a>.</li>
<li><i>"When I've emptied my cup"</i> likely derives from <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2020%3A22-24&amp;version=NIV">Matthew 20:22-24</a>, and <i>"When I'm pure like a dove"</i> is reminiscent of <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2010%3A16&amp;version=NIV">Matthew 10:16</a> - "I am sending you out like sheep among wolves. Therefore be as shrewd as snakes and as innocent as doves."</li>
<li>"<i>When I've learned how to love"</i> bears resemblance to <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%2014%3A23&amp;version=NIV">John 14:23</a> - "Anyone who loves me will obey my teaching. My Father will love them, and we will come to them and make our home with them."</li></ul></li>
<li>Heaven is mentioned in the lyrics of multiple songs in the <a href="/wiki/Category:Soundtracks" title="Category:Soundtracks">film series' soundtracks</a>.
<ul><li>"<a href="/wiki/Atlas" title="Atlas">Atlas</a>" by Coldplay from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack">Catching Fire</a></i> - <i>"Heaven we hope/Is just up the road."</i></li>
<li>"<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://genius.com/The-weeknd-devil-may-cry-lyrics">Devil May Cry</a>" by The Weeknd from <i>Catching Fire</i> - <i>"The light will shine through the rain/And heaven will hear them call your name"</i></li>
<li>"<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://genius.com/Molly-tuttle-bury-me-beneath-the-willow-lyrics">Bury Me Beneath the Willow</a>" by Molly Tuttle from <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes_(Music_From_%26_Inspired_By)" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)"><i>The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i></a> - <i>"When shall I see him? Oh, no, never/'Til we meet in heaven above"</i></li>
<li>"<a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://genius.com/The-civil-wars-kingdom-come-lyrics">Kingdom Come</a>" by the Civil Wars from <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Songs_from_District_12_and_Beyond" title="The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond"><i>The Hunger Games</i></a>. The phrase, "Your kingdom come" derives from the Biblical <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord%27s_Prayer" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lord&#39;s Prayer">Lord's Prayer</a> in the Gospel of Matthew, in which it refers to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Heaven_(Gospel_of_Matthew)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Kingdom of Heaven (Gospel of Matthew)">Kingdom of Heaven</a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
