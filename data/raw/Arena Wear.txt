<p><b>Arena wear</b> was the clothing worn by <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> during their time in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. The clothing was new and identical for each tribute. The <a href="/wiki/Stylists" class="mw-redirect" title="Stylists">stylists</a> had no say in the tributes' uniforms, but were often able to use the clothing's composition to provide clues regarding what might be encountered in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Tributes could also wear or carry a <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">personal token</a> in the arena, as long as it didn't give them an unfair advantage in the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena_wear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Since the Hunger Games were not fleshed out in detail in the beginning, the tributes of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> did not have standardized or specialized clothes for the arena. The tributes wore whatever they had on at the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>, which they also wore through the days leading up to the Games. Because of this, most of the tributes' clothes were worn and improper for the event.

The exception to this is the black color of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, which would not contrast the rest of the post's outline well and was replaced with a dark gray color, though it can be speculated that this may be used to represent <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> in other contexts. Considering that this novel is the only one in the book series in which district colors apart from that of District 12 is mentioned and that the stage adaptation retains more elements of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">source novel</a> than the film adaptation does, it is possible that these colors remain consistent throughout the Hunger Games' history in the book canon.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena_wear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the outfits were tawny trousers, a light green shirt, sturdy brown belt, and a thin-hooded black jacket that fell down to the thighs. The jacket was designed to reflect heat, as the nights would grow cold in the arena. The outfit also included boots, which were worn over skin-tight socks. The boots were made of soft leather and had a narrow, flexible rubber sole with treads, which were good for running.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

All tributes wore olive cargo pants, brown buckle boots, and a hooded nylon jacket with silver stripes that ran down the front, the arms, and back; the jacket also had an inner layer that had an orange reflective tape on the hem. Jackets and shirts (V-necks for the girls, crew necks for the boys) were color-coded according to <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="News_reel">News reel</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena_wear%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h5>
<p>The first film briefly showed news reel footage of two tributes from a previous year (thought to be the <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">73rd Hunger Games</a>, corresponding with details from the original script). Both tributes in this scene wore black shoes, black cargo pants, and black, cropped armored vests underneath their colorful jackets. These appear to correspond with the district color coding: red with black stripes for District 2, and gray-brown with yellow stripes for District 10.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
