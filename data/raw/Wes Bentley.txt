</p><p><b>Wes Bentley</b> is an American actor. He portrayed <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a> <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a> in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Bentley was born in Jonesboro, Arkansas to Cherie Baker and David Bentley. He attended the Julliard School's Drama Division, but quit after his first year. He started acting in 1995.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWes_Bentley%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bentley has starred in several movies, including the Oscar-winning movie <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Beauty_(1999_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:American Beauty (1999 film)">American Beauty</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Four_Feathers_(2002_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Four Feathers (2002 film)">The Four Feathers</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P2_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:P2 (film)">P2</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_Rider_(2007_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Ghost Rider (2007 film)">Ghost Rider</a></i>. He also appeared in the fourth and fifth seasons of <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Horror_Story" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:American Horror Story">American Horror Story</a></i>, playing the role of Edward Mordrake in the two-part Halloween special of <a href="https://americanhorrorstory.fandom.com/wiki/Freak_Show" class="extiw" title="w:c:americanhorrorstory:Freak Show">Freak Show</a> and the lead role of Detective John Lowe in <a href="https://americanhorrorstory.fandom.com/wiki/American_Horror_Story/Hotel" class="extiw" title="w:c:americanhorrorstory:American Horror Story/Hotel">Hotel</a>. Additionally, Bentley starred in the 2014 film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstellar_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Interstellar (film)">Interstellar</a></i>.
</p>
