</p><p><b>LaDell Preston</b> is an American stuntman and actor. He played the <a href="/wiki/District_2_male_(73rd_HG)" title="District 2 male (73rd HG)">District 2 male</a> in the recap that <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> saw of the previous year's <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>.
