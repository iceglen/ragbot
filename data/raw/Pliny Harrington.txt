</p><p><b>Pliny "Pup" Harrington</b> was a male resident of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and a student of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. He was assigned the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPliny_Harrington%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His father was a naval commander who watched the waters off <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>. Though his nickname "Pup" was given to distinguish him from his father, his father had nevertheless given him a crew cut and shined shoes to try to mold him in his own image. Even so, Pup was a natural slob who would do things like picking a piece of ham out of his braces and flicking it to the floor.
</p><p>He did not think much of his tribute, and was unable to get anything out of Lamina during a mentor-tribute session, revealing that she cried for fifteen minutes straight and that he didn't think that District 7 had prepared her for a hangnail, let alone the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He also failed to prepare her for the interviews. 
</p><p>However, Lamina took the first kill of the day, and Pup gave a fist pump before giving her a bottle of water as a <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gift</a>. This was the first attempt to deliver a gift to a tribute in the arena, and the bottle crashed, to be replaced with a new one ten minutes afterward. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> invited Pup to join him and <a href="/wiki/Lepidus_Malmsey" title="Lepidus Malmsey">Lepidus Malmsey</a> in an interview later that day, and Pup rattled off every detail he could remember about Lamina, including some possibly fabricated ones as well. He later sent Lamina a slice of bread after she sent <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a>'s corpse tumbling to the ground.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the Plinth Prize, a full ride to the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a> to the mentor whose tribute won the Games, was announced by <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>, Pup let out a whoop.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The third day of the Games, Lamina was approached by <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a>, the pair from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> and the male tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> respectively. Pup kept shouting out his thoughts and advice, getting on the other mentors' nerves. When Lamina was killed, Pup cried out in shock. He left the mentor section, slammed his chair down and strode out of the hall, attempting not to cry.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPliny_Harrington%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Pup had a crew cut and shined shoes, but was still a natural slob. He also wore braces around the time of the 10th Hunger Games, which often had bits of food stuck in.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPliny_Harrington%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His name is likely a reference to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pliny_the_Elder" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pliny the Elder">Pliny the Elder</a> and, nephew and adoptive son of the former, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pliny_the_Younger" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pliny the Younger">Pliny the Younger</a>. The two men were famous literati in their time; the elder wrote an encyclopaedia called <i>Naturalis Historia</i>. The latter was a Roman governor and is notable for his letters to Trajan and others.
</p>
