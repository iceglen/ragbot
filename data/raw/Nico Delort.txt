</p><p><b>Nico Delort</b> is a Canadian and French illustrator who drew the art for illustrated editions of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Illustrated_Edition" title="The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition">The Hunger Games</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire:_Illustrated_Edition" title="Catching Fire: Illustrated Edition">Catching Fire</a></i>. They work out of Paris, France.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
