<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationship_with_the_Games">Relationship with the Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 7 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> seemed to have decent chances in the arena, as their industry involved the handling of potential weapons like axes.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They had the same <a href="/wiki/District_7_stylist" title="District 7 stylist">stylist</a> for 40 years, who always dressed the tributes as trees during the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the tributes in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> were dressed in elaborate paper costumes resembling origami.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath_of_the_74th_Hunger_Games">Aftermath of the 74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>By the time <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> read out the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> card for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, there were rumors of uprisings in District 7 and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the third Quarter Quell, the tributes from Districts 3, 4, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, 7, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, and 11 were all involved in a <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> plan to protect <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and break out of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a>. Soon after the plan was enacted, communications went down in Districts 7, <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">10</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion">Second Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>One evening, the Capitol aired a live <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a> of <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, who was made to recount war damage allegedly perpetrated by rebels. This included a broken dam in District 7,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which may have inspired the <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> dam scene from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a>.</i><sup id="cite_ref-MJP1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/65/District_7_peacekeepers.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150110112258" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peacekeepers aiming into the trees above them in District 7." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/65/District_7_peacekeepers.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150110112258" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District 7 peacekeepers.jpg" data-image-key="District_7_peacekeepers.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District_7_peacekeepers.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peacekeepers aiming into the trees above them in District 7.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Also in <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i>, a crew of loggers in District 7 was escorted to their shift by a group of armed <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>. Someone hummed <a href="/wiki/Rue%27s_four-note_song" title="Rue&#39;s four-note song">Rue's four-note song</a>, and the loggers ran off and scaled the trees. Though many of their number were shot and killed, a man quoted Katniss's District 8 <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propo</a>— "If we burn, you burn with us!"— and the rebels used a remote control to detonate land mines buried below, killing the Peacekeepers.<sup id="cite_ref-MJP1_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJP1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>All but one of its <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> were killed during the war and the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>. The only survivor was <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Nothing is mentioned about the typical lives of citizens. However, Katniss noted Johanna's skill with axes, and she mused that Johanna had probably been handling them since she was a toddler. If that is the case, it seems that citizens from District 7 began working in their district's lumber industry at an early age.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Both <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> also handled axes during the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> appearing to support Katniss's theory.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Creed_family" title="Capitol families">Creeds</a>, a prominent family in the Capitol, made their fortune in District 7 timber.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The extent of their influence and connections in the district is unknown.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/00/District1HGA.png/revision/latest?cb=20210214191314" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District1HGA" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/00/District1HGA.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210214191314" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="133" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District1HGA.png" data-image-key="District1HGA.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District1HGA.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 7 is located in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Northwest" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pacific Northwest">Pacific Northwest</a>, also known as Cascadia. Its territory primarily covers the Canadian provinces of British Columbia, Alberta, and Saskatchewan, as well as the U.S. states of Montana and Washington. According to this map, it is one of the few districts primarily located outside the contiguous United States, alongside <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a>. It borders <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> (to the southwest), <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> (to the south and southeast), and <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">District 9</a> (to the east).
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f4/District_7_aerial_MJP1.JPG/revision/latest?cb=20241209210609" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Overhead view of District 7 in Mockingjay - Part 1." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="District 7 aerial MJP1.JPG" data-image-key="District_7_aerial_MJP1.JPG" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f4/District_7_aerial_MJP1.JPG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20241209210609" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f4/District_7_aerial_MJP1.JPG/revision/latest?cb=20241209210609" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Overhead view of District 7 in Mockingjay - Part 1." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f4/District_7_aerial_MJP1.JPG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20241209210609" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District 7 aerial MJP1.JPG" data-image-key="District_7_aerial_MJP1.JPG" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f4/District_7_aerial_MJP1.JPG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20241209210609" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District_7_aerial_MJP1.JPG" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Overhead view of District 7 in <i>Mockingjay - Part 1</i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>On her way to the Capitol for the 74th Hunger Games, Katniss looked out the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> window and saw the lights of another district, which she thought might be District 7 or 10. Assuming that Katniss knew roughly where the other districts were located, this would mean that contrary to the map, District 7 was somewhere between <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> in <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Appalachia">Appalachia</a> and the Capitol in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mountains" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Rocky Mountains">Rocky Mountains</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> There was also a dam in the district that was destroyed during the Second Rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_9_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_9-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>District 7 presumably had at least one female victor prior to Johanna, since Katniss specified that Johanna was the district's only "living" female victor when the 75th Hunger Games took place.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 7 has 115 mills in operation with a population of 26,354 people, making it the eighth largest district in terms of population.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 7 claims 63,125 in tesserae, making it the sixth richest district.<sup><i>&#91;citation&#160;needed&#93;</i></sup>
<ul><li>If the population is 26,354 and they claim 63,125 tesserae, it would equal to 2.395 tesserae per person, making it the 8th richest district.</li></ul></li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be ironstone brown (#7c5242 – <span style="background-color:#7c5242; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 7's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be medium purple (#9370DB - <span style="background-color:#9370DB; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear for the 50th Hunger Games was russet brown (#80461B - <span style="background-color:#80461B; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
