<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Covey%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The small wooden house was located on the edge of <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, near <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a>. The front yard was a dirt patch with wildflowers planted in an unorderly fashion, and at the back of the house, there was a "makeshift stoop".<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Inside, the house was one large, open room with a lofted area for sleeping. The coal stove, sink, a shelf of dishware, and an "ancient" refrigerator all sat along the back wall, with a window and the back door. The right wall was lined with a clothes rack, and the Covey's musical instruments were arranged along the left wall. In terms of furniture, they had a table and a few chairs, and their television sat on top of a crate, its antenna rigged with aluminum foil.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Covey%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sometime during the year of the <a href="/wiki/9th_Hunger_Games" title="9th Hunger Games">9th Hunger Games</a>, the old man who was paid to take care of the six Covey children died of <a href="/wiki/Health_conditions#Black_lung" title="Health conditions">black lung</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After that, it is unknown whether the Covey stayed in the same house— making this particular dwelling originally his— or if they'd moved and this was their new home.
</p><p>The next January, the house's pipes froze, so the Covey had to melt down chunks of ice on the stove for water. Once it snowed, they were able to melt down the snow instead with less trouble.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Covey%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the 10th Hunger Games, the Covey caught very little of the coverage on their television. However, the night of <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> was a clear one in District 12, so <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a> was able to get it working. They watched <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> introduce <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and they saw her perform "<a href="/wiki/Songs#&quot;The_Ballad_of_Lucy_Gray_Baird&quot;" title="Songs">The Ballad of Lucy Gray Baird</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She intended the song as a message for them about how the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> was rigged against her, and <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a>'s role in it.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>About two weeks after Lucy Gray was sent home from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, the Covey officially kicked out Billy Taupe. Rather than letting him inside, they tossed his belongings into the backyard for him to retrieve, consisting of "a pile of clothes and a few books". This was the same day that Coriolanus and <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> visited the house for the first time.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Covey%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> lived there with her adoptive uncles, <a href="/wiki/Clerk_Carmine_Clade" title="Clerk Carmine Clade">Clerk Carmine Clade</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tam_Amber" title="Tam Amber">Tam Amber</a>. She had the loft to herself.<sup id="cite_ref-SR26_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR26-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Covey%2527s_house%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Based on its proximity to the Meadow, this house was probably in the same part of the Seam as <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen%27s_house" title="Katniss Everdeen&#39;s house">Katniss Everdeen's house</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a> did not exist yet,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> so despite Lucy Gray's status as a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>, she and the Covey never moved there.</li>
<li>If the house survived the 24 years between <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i> and the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>, it probably would have been destroyed alongside the rest of the district in the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>While arranging to film in rural Poland for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, filmmakers expressed interest in building the Covey's house underneath some trees. However, according to <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a>, the landowner was resistant to the idea because he believed there was Nazi gold buried underneath them, and he became suspicious that they were German spies trying to get the gold. The town's mayor and priest had to get involved. Ultimately, the compromise they reached was for filmmakers to build the set 30 feet away. The scene at the house ended up being cut regardless.<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
