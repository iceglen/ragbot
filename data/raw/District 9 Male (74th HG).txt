<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade">Tribute Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In the film, he is seen on the chariots with his district partner in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a> wearing silver suits and headpieces, possibly representing silos (where they store grain). He and his district partner are hardly seen in the chariot scene; as the camera focuses on the <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> partners, they are both seen waving.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>The District 9 male was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>. He grapples over a <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpack</a> with <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, until <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a> throws a knife into his back. He coughs blood on Katniss's face and then falls to the ground, dead.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, it is also shown that he picked up an axe and tried to kill Katniss. He places 24th out of 24 in the book and 20th out of 24 in the film.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He, along with <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>, are the only two tributes to directly engage with Katniss.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He had ashen hair and hazel eyes. Katniss was able to identify the <a href="/wiki/Wolf_mutt" title="Wolf mutt">wolf mutt</a> that resembled him based on these traits.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the films, he is also shown to be one of the smallest tributes.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the film, he was 14 years old, and he weighed 90 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>It was confirmed the <a href="/wiki/District_8_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 female (74th HG)">District 8 female</a>, the District 9 male, female and the <a href="/wiki/District_10_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 10 female (74th HG)">District 10 female</a>, were considered extra characters and they only shot the parade. He also appeared in the training, but his jacket is unnumbered.
<ul><li>Imanol Yepez-Frias, the District 9 boy's actor, was not a stunt actor. This meant that he was not present for the filming of the bloodbath, and so the <a href="/wiki/District_7_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 7 male (74th HG)">District 7 male</a>'s actor, <a href="/wiki/Sam_Ly" title="Sam Ly">Sam Ly</a>, had to take his part. Consequentially, this means that the District 7 male appears to have two deaths.</li></ul></li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, he wears a bottle green jacket.
<ul><li>Since no official arena wear was made for the District 9 tributes, his portrait is most likely digitally altered and colorized.</li></ul></li>
<li>He is the only tribute that fell during the bloodbath to serve a non-trivial role in the games, and the only one with a known physical description.</li>
<li>The District 9 male was one of the five mutts that Katniss recognized in the book, and one of the two that is not named (the other is <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a>).</li>
<li>In the scoreboard, he lives for 35 seconds, dying before his <a href="/wiki/District_9_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 female (74th HG)">district partner</a>.
<ul><li>In the film he was the 5th dead, but when the District 8 female was killed, on the scoreboard behind <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar</a> it can be seen that he was the 1st dead.</li>
<li>In the book, he was the first tribute shown to be killed. After Katniss sees a knife in his back, she said that "the other tributes had already reached the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>."</li></ul></li>
<li>The District 9 boy's portrait is not shown during 'The Fallen' along with the <a href="/wiki/District_10_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 10 female (74th HG)">District 10 female</a>. This is most likely a production error.</li>
<li>He and the <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 male (75th HG)">District 5 male</a> of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> served the same role, because in the films both tried to kill Katniss in order to get supplies and are killed by a <a href="/wiki/Career" class="mw-redirect" title="Career">Career</a> tribute (Clove and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick</a> respectively in this case)</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Training_Days" title="The Hunger Games: Training Days">The Hunger Games: Training Days</a></i> board game, he and his district partner, along with <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6</a>, were not given cards or any recognition.</li></ul>
