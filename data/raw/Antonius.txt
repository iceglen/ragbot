<p><b>Antonius</b> was <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s Defense Minister and a member of the Presidential Cabinet, working closely alongside <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>. He was an entrusted and high-ranking member within <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s political system.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAntonius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Antonius informed President Snow of <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>'s presence in <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> and advised him that <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> could be deployed from the border of <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>. Snow told Antonius to "kill the wounded", meaning everyone within the <a href="/wiki/District_8_hospital" title="District 8 hospital">hospital</a> Katniss had visited. Later, Antonius stood alongside Snow and <a href="/wiki/Egeria" title="Egeria">Egeria</a> when <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> destroyed the hydroelectric dam in <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, cutting the power in the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Antonius was responsible for ordering numerous attacks on rebels who attempted to conquer <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a>, the Capitol's arsenal within the mountains of <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>. When the rebels launched a more uniquely devised attack, the Nut caved in with many <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> still trapped inside. Most of these Peacekeepers had been sent at Antonius's request, many of whom were, as Snow called them, the "best" of the Capitol. As a result of his arrogance, Antonius was poisoned to death during a dinner with the other Cabinet members. Antonius's stunt had cost the Capitol the victory Snow was hoping for, as Antonius had inadvertently allowed the rebels the upper hand.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_P2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_P2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAntonius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Like Snow, Antonius was ambitious and calculating. However, despite his loyalty to the president, Antonius was also arrogant, stubborn, and power-hungry, which ultimately led to his demise.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAntonius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>"Antonius" is a reference to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Antony" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Mark Antony">Mark Antony</a>, a Roman general under Julius Caesar.
</p>
