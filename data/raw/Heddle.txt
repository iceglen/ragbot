<p>In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">film</a>, Heddle was first seen in the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a> with her <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">tribute partner</a>, wearing pink and blue costumes that symbolize jesters and fabrics with a star pattern and bow hats on them. She was seen in quite a few shots during parade scenes, waving at the audience.
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeddle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Heddle survived the initial <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a>, and managed to escape into the forest. It is unknown if she was able to grab any supplies or weapons before leaving the Cornucopia.

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span>"<i>A stone's throw from the biggest idiot in the Games.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> after the girl lit the fire.</dd></dl></div>
<p>She watched the Fallen images and tried to sleep, but she gave in to the cold and starts a fire near a tree which <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> was resting in, which she thought was a "foolish" decision. Sure enough, the light from her fire attracted the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career tributes</a> to her location during early morning. In the movie, <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> hands <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> his sword and she stabs her to death, and after Katniss heard her scream, the cannon sounded. In the book, she is stabbed. The Careers leave to allow them to take the body, but after hearing no cannon, they become uneasy and begin arguing. Peeta goes back to finish her off. She placed 13th out of 24 in the book and 12th out of 24 in the film.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeddle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the scoreboard, she was 13 years old, and weighed 116 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the tribute parade, the females were standing on the left of the chariot and the males on the right except for District 8 where she stood on the right and the male on the left.</li>
<li>In the film, her death was unintentionally foreshadowed by Haymitch when he was giving advice to Peeta on how to survive the games. Peeta mentions the idea of lighting a fire in a cold environment while Haymitch shoots it down by saying; "Well that's a great way to get killed", foreshadowing Heddle's death as she lit a fire in a dark and cold environment which led to the Career tributes in finding and killing her.</li>
<li>Heddle, the District 9 tributes, and the District 10 female were considered extra characters and they only had shots in the parade scene but she appears during the first night. She is also the only extra character to survive the bloodbath.</li>
<li>She lasted 8 hours, 12 minutes and 8 seconds in the Hunger Games.</li>
<li>She was Peeta's only known direct kill in the 74th Hunger Games.</li>
<li>When she died, on the scoreboard behind Caesar, an incorrect order of deaths can be seen: <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">District 9 male</a>; <a href="/wiki/District_3_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 3 female (74th HG)">District 3 female</a>; <a href="/wiki/District_9_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 female (74th HG)">District 9 female</a>; <a href="/wiki/Jason" title="Jason">Jason</a>; <a href="/wiki/District_5_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 5 male (74th HG)">District 5 male</a>; <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">District 6 female</a>; <a href="/wiki/Stele" title="Stele">Stele</a>; <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">District 7 female</a>; <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a>; <a href="/wiki/District_10_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 10 female (74th HG)">District 10 female</a>; <a href="/wiki/Tippet" title="Tippet">Tippet</a>; <a href="/wiki/Bravura" title="Bravura">Bravura</a>.</li>
<li>She, along with Cato, was one of the tributes in the 74th Hunger Games to be mortally wounded and then mercifully killed. She was mortally wounded by Cato and then mercifully killed by Peeta in the book, while Cato was mauled by mutts, and mercifully killed by Katniss.</li>
<li>The night that Katniss was without a sleeping bag, she began to "have more sympathy" for Heddle.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> she, like her district partner, appears as a holographic portrait in the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>.</li>
<li>In the Victory Tour, there is only one man and one woman standing in her pedestal, so Heddle has no siblings, and in the pedestal of her district partner there is only one man, so the District 8 male from the 74th Hunger Games has no mother or siblings.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_On_Stage" title="The Hunger Games On Stage">The Hunger Games On Stage</a></i>, her name is <b>Heddle</b>. A <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/heddle" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:heddle">heddle</a> is the part of a loom that all warp threads pass through.
<ul><li>In the original West End production, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>'s actress Sophia Ally also plays Heddle.</li></ul></li></ul>
