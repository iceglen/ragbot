</p><p><b>Megan Hayes</b> is an American actress who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 female (75th HG)">female morphling</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.
</p><p>In 2012, she was seen in the film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_To_Expect_When_You%27re_Expecting_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:What To Expect When You&#39;re Expecting (film)">What to Expect When You're Expecting</a></i>. She is also very active with the fandom and all her fans on Instagram and Twitter.
