<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRomulus_Thread%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Thread's early life is unknown, but he had most likely been born and raised in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, like most Peacekeepers.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_6-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Harsh and unforgiving, Thread replaced <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a> and was ready to enforce the law, no matter what. 
</p><p>Upon arrival, Thread brutally beat <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, who had been caught poaching a wild turkey (or for tackling Thread and stopping him from attacking an <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">elderly woman</a> in <a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire_(film)" class="mw-redirect" title="Catching Fire (film)">the film</a>). Although Gale had been selling meat to the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> of District 12 for years, they turned a blind eye to this illegal poaching, since they were his best customers. Gale went to sell the turkey to Cray when he unexpectedly found Thread at Cray's house. Thread saw Gale's poaching as a flogging offense and put him under immediate arrest, bringing him to the square and beginning a public whipping after forcing Gale to plead guilty. <a href="/wiki/Darius" title="Darius">Darius</a> tried to intervene after about twenty lashes by grabbing Thread's arm but was struck in the head, knocking him unconscious. Upon first seeing Gale, Katniss yelled for Thread to stop, but was instead whipped across the cheek. <a href="/wiki/Purnia" title="Purnia">Purnia</a> later intervened in a more official manner, and Thread finally stopped. After that display of cruelty, people fled home before nightfall rather than risk incurring the wrath of the new band of Peacekeepers, including Katniss.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the blizzard, Thread hung a flag of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> off the roof of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a> and lined up Peacekeepers to march around the square and be ready with machine guns on the rooftops. He also set up new devices to punish citizens in the center of the square.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>A few days after the blizzard, Katniss crept out of her house and headed to the woods, where she met <a href="/wiki/Bonnie" title="Bonnie">Bonnie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twill" title="Twill">Twill</a>. Upon her return, she found Peacekeepers sent by Thread to deliver a message that the fences would now be electrified 24/7.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_11-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Thread ushered <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> and Katniss out the back door of the Justice Building without giving them a chance to say goodbye to their loved ones.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_13_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_13-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the Capitol <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">firebombed</a> District 12, he was transported out along with the other Peacekeepers in the district. Thread's current status is unknown, as he is not mentioned afterward. He was most likely either killed during the Second Rebellion, or was captured, tried, and executed by the rebel forces afterwards.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRomulus_Thread%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Thread was a tall, muscular man with sharp creases in his pants.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> His face was hard, with deep lines and a cruel mouth. His gray hair was completely shaved, and his eyes were black. Thread had a long, straight nose and a commanding voice with an accent that Katniss found odd.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRomulus_Thread%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>She interrupted the punishment of a confessed criminal.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Thread after Katniss Everdeen stepped in to save Gale Hawthorne from further whipping<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Romulus Thread was a ruthless, pitiless, violent, unstable, and sadistic peacekeeper commander. He was also cruel and vicious and carried out his orders without mercy and without compassion, making him somewhat of a sociopath. He had no qualms about harming or killing anyone who stood in his way. Thread was an idealistic, angry, and powerful man who seemed to terrify everyone with his mere presence no matter where it takes place. He was also very aggressive and declared that anyone caught breaking the curfew he organized in District 12 "will be shot on sight".
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRomulus_Thread%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In Roman mythology, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romulus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Romulus">Romulus</a> was the founder and first king of Rome. His surname, <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/thread">Thread</a>, could be meant as in a story thread: a continued idea or theme, because he brings the harsh subjugation seen in other districts back to District 12.
</p>
