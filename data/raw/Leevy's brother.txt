</p><p><b>Leevy's brother</b> was a boy from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> who lived a few houses down from <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen%27s_house" title="Katniss Everdeen&#39;s house">Katniss Everdeen's house</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeevy%2527s_brother%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A year before <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i> took place, he caught the measles and <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Everdeen">Mrs. Everdeen</a> kept him alive.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> His older sister <a href="/wiki/Leevy" title="Leevy">Leevy</a> survived the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but it is unknown whether he or any other family members made it out, since they were not mentioned in <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>.
</p>
