<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_Hunger_Games">Known Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="1st-9th_Hunger_Games">1st-9th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/9c/Capitol_Arena_exterior.png/revision/latest?cb=20230427235043" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Exterior of the Capitol Arena" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/9c/Capitol_Arena_exterior.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230427235043" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="122" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol Arena exterior.png" data-image-key="Capitol_Arena_exterior.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_Arena_exterior.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Exterior of the Capitol Arena</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>During the first nine Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were held in the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>' stables.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Soon after, they were locked inside the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> with nothing but <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a> while the audience watched from the comfort of their own homes. At the conclusion of each competition, the bodies would be removed and the doors locked until the next year with no further fanfare.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">Districts 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a> produced more <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> in these years, but <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">Districts 4</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a> were also considered contenders.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Head_Gamemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Head Gamemaker">Head Gamemaker</a> was Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>. There was no history of <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> ever being used in the first nine years of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a> complained that she might receive a "pathetic runt girl from one of the poor districts, like <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a> or <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">12</a>" as her tribute, who would die within a few minutes "like they did last year and the year before."<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> If her comment was specific to those two districts, then the female tributes of Districts 8 and 12 died very early into the 8th and 9th Hunger Games.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="9th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/9th_Hunger_Games" title="9th Hunger Games">9th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>On the first evening of the 10th Hunger Games, Dr. Gaul said that "In terms of fatalities, [they were] running neck and neck with last year".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This implies that during the 9th Hunger Games, around 10 tributes had died by 5pm on the first day.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3a/Capitol_Arena_interior.png/revision/latest?cb=20230427235227" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Interior of the Capitol Arena during the 10th Hunger Games" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3a/Capitol_Arena_interior.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230427235227" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="119" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol Arena interior.png" data-image-key="Capitol_Arena_interior.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_Arena_interior.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Interior of the Capitol Arena during the 10th Hunger Games</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The 10th Hunger Games was the first to introduce <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a>, who were the top twenty-four students from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It also introduced <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">betting</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorship</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Additionally, <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> were released into the arena, making them the first mutts to be used in this way.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Prior to the Games, tributes were held in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and they later competed inside the Capitol Arena, the landscape of which had been altered by a <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_the_Capitol_Arena" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of the Capitol Arena">bombing</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Dr. Gaul once again served as Head Gamemaker.
</p><p>Many tributes and their mentors were killed before the Games officially began, and the victor, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> representing District 12, won by cheating with the help of her mentor, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Written off an embarrassing failure, all but one recording of the 10th Hunger Games were destroyed, though aspects like the interviews, betting, sponsorship, and mentorship would be carried forward in future editions.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This also marked the beginning of Snow's rise to power; he would eventually become the <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="11th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the failure of the previous year, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> brainstormed ways to engage the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> and the Capitol for the 11th Hunger Games. New additions included incentives to send forward stronger tributes, such as food parcels for the winning district, the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a>, and monetary prizes for the victors themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The victor this year was 16-year-old <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> from District 4, who became the first to embark on a <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<hr />
<h3><span id="25th_Hunger_Games_.2F_First_Quarter_Quell"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="25th_Hunger_Games_/_First_Quarter_Quell">25th Hunger Games / First Quarter Quell</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div><p>This was the first <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>. Every twenty-five years, a new twist would be introduced for a special edition of the Hunger Games. The twist this year was that each district had to elect its own tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The victor of these Games died sometime before the third Quarter Quell, which was held fifty years later.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><hr />
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="34th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/34th_Hunger_Games" title="34th Hunger Games">34th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>  won this year by setting up an electrical trap with parts scavenged from his arena.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, Haymitch said Beetee had electrocuted six tributes at once.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<hr />
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="38th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/38th_Hunger_Games" title="38th Hunger Games">38th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>These Games were not mentioned in the original <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>, but revealed by <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, a Hunger Games website promoting the movies. According to Capitol Couture, <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> victor <a href="/wiki/Porter_Millicent_Tripp" title="Porter Millicent Tripp">Porter Millicent Tripp</a> sustained a broken neck in the final showdown.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="46th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/46th_Hunger_Games" title="46th Hunger Games">46th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="49th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/49th_Hunger_Games" title="49th Hunger Games">49th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The arena for the 49th Hunger Games was full of shiny surfaces and lakes. The boulders, caves and cliffs were overlayed with mirrors, and the sky clouds above were reflected and turned to vapor. The Gamemakers introduced shiny silver beetles that swarmed and suffocated the tributes. <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a> was the victor.
</p>

<h3><span id="50th_Hunger_Games_.2F_Second_Quarter_Quell"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games_/_Second_Quarter_Quell">50th Hunger Games / Second Quarter Quell</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div><p>For the second Quarter Quell, two boys and two girls were reaped from each district, bringing the total number of tributes to 48. These Games were won by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> from District 12, who survived an <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> full of poison, deadly mutts, and an active volcano. His last opponent, a <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">girl from District 1</a>, was killed when she unknowingly threw her <a href="/wiki/Axe" class="mw-redirect" title="Axe">axe</a> into a <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>, which redirected the weapon back at her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><hr />
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="62nd_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/62nd_Hunger_Games" title="62nd Hunger Games">62nd Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During her Games, District 2 <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and victor <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> killed one tribute by ripping open his throat with her teeth. This made her very popular, and after her victory, she surgically altered her teeth so each one ended in a point like a fang, inlaid with gold.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="63rd_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/63rd_Hunger_Games" title="63rd Hunger Games">63rd Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> from District 1 was the victor of the 63rd Hunger Games. Being from a <a href="/wiki/Career" class="mw-redirect" title="Career">Career</a> district, it's assumed that Gloss was in an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> with the other Career tributes. His sister won the following year.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="64th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/64th_Hunger_Games" title="64th Hunger Games">64th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The victor this year was <a href="/wiki/Cashmere" title="Cashmere">Cashmere</a> from District 1.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She's presumed to have been in a Career alliance, and her brother, Gloss, won the year prior.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_15-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="65th_Hunger_Games">65th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div><p><a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> from District 4 was the youngest victor in Hunger Games history at the age of 14. As a Career tribute, he was adept with spears and knives, and he held his own in the arena until his many sponsors, drawn to him by his good looks, gifted him a particularly expensive <a href="/wiki/Trident" class="mw-redirect" title="Trident">trident</a>. At this point, Finnick secured his victory by netting other tributes and stabbing them with his trident.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><hr />
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="67th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/67th_Hunger_Games" title="67th Hunger Games">67th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>These Games were not mentioned in the original trilogy, but revealed by Capitol Couture. The 67th Hunger Games were won by <a href="/wiki/Augustus_Braun" title="Augustus Braun">Augustus Braun</a> from District 1, who was known as the "Cavalier Career", and hailed as "Panem's Favorite Son."<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="69th_Hunger_Games">69th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D18&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>While these games were not mentioned in the original trilogy, the arena was a "burning desert".<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> recalled an edition of the Hunger Games where the arena was made up of sand, boulders, and scruffy bushes, and many tributes went insane from thirst.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This was likely the 69th Hunger Games took place five years prior to the start of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">The Hunger Games trilogy</a></i>, as it matches <a href="/wiki/Atala" title="Atala">Atala</a>'s description of a desert arena.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="70th_Hunger_Games">70th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D19&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/70th_Hunger_Games" title="70th Hunger Games">70th Hunger Games</a></i></center></div><p><a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> from District 4 was traumatized after seeing her fellow <a href="/wiki/District_4_male_(70th_HG)" title="District 4 male (70th HG)">District 4 tribute</a> get beheaded. She hid for the remainder of the Games until an earthquake broke a dam, flooding the arena. Since Annie came from the fishing district, she was the best swimmer, so she was able to outlast the remaining tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p><a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> victor <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> pretended to be weak and fragile so others would underestimate her before she revealed her dangerous competence in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="76th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">76th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D25&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The idea of a 76th Hunger Games was put forth by <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> President <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>. She proposed that tributes be reaped from the children of Capitol officials, in order to satisfy calls for bloodshed and even genocide against Capitol citizens. This proposal was voted upon by the seven victors who survived the war, but while it was approved,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> it was never officially held.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_Epilogue_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_Epilogue-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Unknown_Hunger_Games">Unknown Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D26&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victors_of_unknown_Games">Victors of unknown Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D27&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>In <i>The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</i>, Haymitch also said that the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 female (75th HG)">District 6 female</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 6 male (75th HG)">District 6 male</a>, also known as the <a href="/wiki/Morphlings" title="Morphlings">morphlings</a>, won their respective Games with the use of <a href="/wiki/Camouflage" title="Camouflage">camouflage</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In <i>Sunrise on the Reaping</i>, Haymitch mentions a male victor from District 4 that was deeply mentally affected following his games and needed to be comforted by his mentor, Mags. He won about ten years prior to the 2nd Quarter Quell.</li></ul>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_Games">Other Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D29&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li>One year, the tributes had to bludgeon each other to death with spiked maces, the only available weapons.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_27-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>One arena had a landscape of boulders, sand, and scruffy bushes. Many tributes were bitten by venomous snakes or "went insane from thirst".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_27-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Another year, half of the tributes froze to death at night because they had no firewood. The Capitol audience considered this "anticlimactic" because so many tributes huddled into balls and quietly froze to death.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_27-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A few years prior to the 74th Hunger Games, the victor was a boy who received a training score of three.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_34-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Also a few years prior, a boy from <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a> named <a href="/wiki/Titus" title="Titus">Titus</a> went insane and began to eat the tributes that he killed. Eventually, Titus was taken out by an avalanche speculated to be the work of Gamemakers.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_35-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>One year, a female tribute dropped her token— a small wooden ball— before the sixty-second countdown ended, setting off the land mines around her platform. She was blown apart and had to be collected in pieces.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_36-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-36"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A pack of hideous reptiles once destroyed the Career tributes' food supply.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_37-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Katniss also mentioned another year (possibly the 70th Hunger Games) where the food supply was washed away in a flood caused by the Gamemakers.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_37-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Haymitch mentions that, a few years prior to his Games, the arena would go dark without warning and giant weasel mutts would emerge to attack tributes. The girl from District 5 had her face ripped off by one. This may have been Palladium's Games.<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FList_of_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D30&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Only four Hunger Games outright occur during the series: the 10th,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 50th,<sup id="cite_ref-Amazon_39-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amazon-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 74th,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_36-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-36"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and 75th.<sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Other Games are referenced or explained in passing.
<ul><li>The first film briefly depicts the ending of the 73rd Hunger Games on television when Katniss is on the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> to the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_28-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In an early version of the first film script, Caesar mentions to Cato that a District 2 tribute won these Games.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_28-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
