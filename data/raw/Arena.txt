<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>An <b>arena</b> is a large, enclosed, outdoor area where the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> were held each year. Arenas were designed by the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> and a new one was built every year. It could be anything from&#160;dense forest to an open meadow,&#160;or a frozen wasteland to a scorched desert. The Gamemakers planted traps and introduced cunning twists to entertain the people of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>.
</p><p>The arenas were considered important historical landmarks and were preserved after the conclusion of each year's competition. They were also popular tourist destinations for many <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> citizens, where they would be able to rewatch the Games, visit the sites of tributes' deaths, and even take part in reenactments.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the end of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>, all the arenas were destroyed, and memorials were constructed in their place.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Key_features">Key features</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Gamemaker_control">Gamemaker control</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Most arenas were seemingly natural environments that were actually under strict control by the Gamemakers. They could remotely trigger traps to herd tributes towards one another, unleash vicious <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> upon them, and manipulate the weather conditions, such as temperature and precipitation. In the films, they could even artificially simulate different times of day.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Arenas were enclosed by dome-shaped<sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force fields</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:6_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Capitol hovercraft were able to come and go through them to extract tributes' remains, and a cloaked hovercraft carrying a huge screen displayed the nightly tally of the dead during the <a href="/wiki/Horn_of_Plenty" title="Horn of Plenty">anthem</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the films, the dead tributes' images were instead projected against a false sky.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Some arenas had a sun in alignment with the real sun, some had multiple suns, and some had no suns at all.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catacombs">Catacombs</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Underneath each arena was a series of <a href="/wiki/Catacombs" title="Catacombs">catacombs</a>, also called Sub-A, that held the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>' <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">Launch Rooms</a>, known to the districts as the Stockyard.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Muttations also entered and exited the arena via the catacombs as well.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Inside their individual Launch Rooms, tributes changed into their <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> and stepped onto metal platforms that lifted them into the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_10_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_10-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Sub-A was a huge subterranean floor underneath the arenas where manual tasks like releasing <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> and stocking up for a feast were carried out. Its existence was kept secret to keep up the pretence that the games were controlled entirely from afar. The most important task here was managing the onsite computer system which was integral when running the Games, kept in a restricted area with high-tech security. It also created and maintained the tribute ecosystem; providing the electricity, plumbing, heating, cooling and ventilation among other things.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_10_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_10-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Once in the arena, tributes would find themselves arranged in a circle around the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>. This provided tributes with <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>, food, survival gear, and other useful supplies, if they went in and fought for it at the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. However, tributes had to remain in place for a sixty-second countdown before the Games could begin, or else they would be blown up by landmines around their platforms.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Major_arenas">Major arenas</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="1st_through_10th_Hunger_Games">1st through <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a></i></center></div>
<p>Up until at least the 10th Hunger Games, the Hunger Games were hosted in the same location: a dilapidated amphitheater that had been used for sporting, entertainment, and military events prior to the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, but fell into disrepair afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Tributes were simply thrown into the arena and left to fight. Since there was no cover, the Hunger Games tended to proceed quickly. Things changed in the 10th Hunger Games when a <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombing</a> of the arena opened up access to various tunnels,<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> resulting in a number of new strategies. Brightly colored <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> were released into the arena on the fourth day.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span id="50th_Hunger_Games_.2F_Second_Quarter_Quell"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games_/_Second_Quarter_Quell"><a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> / Second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="50th Hunger Games arena">50th Hunger Games arena</a></i></center></div>
<p>The 50th Hunger Games arena was a big, beautiful meadow, with flowers, streams, pools, birds and a picturesque mountain and sharp, jagged rocks. But everything was lethal. The tributes of these Games had to face stinging butterflies; fluffy, golden squirrels which were in fact carnivorous; the picturesque mountain that was actually a volcano; flowers that could poison someone if inhaled too directly; pink birds with skewer-like beaks that killed <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>; and dehydration, due to the fact that the only sources of water that weren't toxic were from the bounty at the Cornucopia or rainfall. <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">Victor</a> <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> weaponized the force field at the arena's edge against the <a href="/wiki/District_1_female_(50th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 1 female (50th HG)">District 1 female</a> in their final confrontation.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="74th Hunger Games arena">74th Hunger Games arena</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 270px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/94/HG13.png/revision/latest?cb=20110902161945" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The 74th Hunger Games arena." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/94/HG13.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/270?cb=20110902161945" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="270" height="115" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="HG13.png" data-image-key="HG13.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:HG13.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The 74th Hunger Games arena.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>
The 74th Hunger Games arena was a large expanse of various terrains. In the center around the Cornucopia where the tributes launched was a plain of hard packed dirt. In one direction, there was a wheat field which was not visible from the Cornucopia, because there was a steep downward slope and this area was a lot lower down than the rest of the arena. In another direction there was a large lake, next to which the Careers set up camp and used as their main source of water. In&#160;the other two directions, there was a forest which made up most of the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the woods, there was a stream that led to the lake, and a rocky area with numerous caves beside it. There were also several ponds, a variety of different trees, and a marshy area.</p><figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 239px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fa/Poolthingthatkatnisswasin.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130902185715" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Poolthingthatkatnisswasin" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/fa/Poolthingthatkatnisswasin.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20130902185715" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="179" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Poolthingthatkatnisswasin.jpg" data-image-key="Poolthingthatkatnisswasin.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Poolthingthatkatnisswasin.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>Inhabiting the arena, there were natural animals such as rabbits, squirrels, deer, <a href="/wiki/Groosling" title="Groosling">groosling</a>, and water birds. There were also several Gamemaker muttations: <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jackers</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Muttation#Wolf_mutts" title="Muttation">wolf mutts</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The forest was rigged with traps such as devices that could launch fireballs.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  The Gamemakers could manipulate the temperature so that it was freezing at night and hot during the day, even drying up the stream later into the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<p>In the movie, the arena was mostly a wooded forest, with mountainous higher areas to the south flat plains to the west. A single large river ran through the east of the arena that fed into the large lake by the Cornucopia.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span id="75th_Hunger_Games_.2F_Third_Quarter_Quell"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games_/_Third_Quarter_Quell"><a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> / Third Quarter Quell</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">75th Hunger Games arena</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d5/Arena_of_75th_Hunger_Games.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131126210721" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cornucopia in the 75th Hunger Games." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="93" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Arena of 75th Hunger Games.jpg" data-image-key="Arena_of_75th_Hunger_Games.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d5/Arena_of_75th_Hunger_Games.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20131126210721" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d5/Arena_of_75th_Hunger_Games.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131126210721" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Cornucopia in the 75th Hunger Games." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d5/Arena_of_75th_Hunger_Games.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20131126210721" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="93" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Arena of 75th Hunger Games.jpg" data-image-key="Arena_of_75th_Hunger_Games.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d5/Arena_of_75th_Hunger_Games.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20131126210721" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Arena_of_75th_Hunger_Games.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Cornucopia in the 75th Hunger Games.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The 75th Hunger Games arena was set up like a clock. The 12 to 1 wedge consisted of the tall tree that was periodically struck by lightning during the 12th hour,<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which proved to be a very important factor later on in <a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">the novel</a>. The other sections of the "clock" each had a tall tree identical to the 12 to 1 tree to throw tributes off, and were made up of different "horrors" that were unleashed by the hour. Known threats included blood rain,<sup id="cite_ref-:9_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> acid fog,<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> vicious <a href="/wiki/Monkey_mutt" title="Monkey mutt">monkey mutts</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:9_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Jabberjay" title="Jabberjay">jabberjays</a>, an unknown beast,<sup id="cite_ref-:10_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:10-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> a tidal wave,<sup id="cite_ref-:9_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and unknown insects.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 239px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c3/Arena_75th.png/revision/latest?cb=20131029043339" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="75th Hunger Games arena." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="101" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Arena 75th.png" data-image-key="Arena_75th.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c3/Arena_75th.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20131029043339" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c3/Arena_75th.png/revision/latest?cb=20131029043339" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="75th Hunger Games arena." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c3/Arena_75th.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20131029043339" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="239" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Arena 75th.png" data-image-key="Arena_75th.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c3/Arena_75th.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/239?cb=20131029043339" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Arena_75th.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">75th Hunger Games arena.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>This arena was very small, and circular in shape, enclosed by a powerful force field. The Cornucopia lay at its center, tail pointing in the direction of the 12th hour, and was surrounded by water and twelve spokes between each set of two tributes as they were first raised into the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The trees were the only source of drinkable water, which had to be extracted by a <a href="/wiki/Tools_and_gear#Spile" title="Tools and gear">spile</a> or by hacking at the bark. In the film, this arena appeared to be made of a series of hexagonal screens mounted to a girder system with a force field on the inside that prevented damage from occurring.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the third night inside the arena, Katniss blew out the force field during the midnight lightning storm,<sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> allowing <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">rebels</a> to extract her by hovercraft alongside fellow victors <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Minor_arenas">Minor arenas</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="49th_Hunger_Games">49th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the 49th Hunger Games the arena was full of shiny surfaces lakes that reflected the sky clouds that turned to vapor and boulders and caves and cliffs overlayed with mirrors. The Gamemakers called it the Nest of Mirrors.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="70th_Hunger_Games"><a href="/wiki/70th_Hunger_Games" title="70th Hunger Games">70th Hunger Games</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3><p>
The 70th Hunger Games arena had a dam that was later broken by an earthquake, flooding the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:10_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:10-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 240px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2d/6.73thHG.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140321072955" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The 73rd Hunger Games arena." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="137" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="6.73thHG.jpg" data-image-key="6.73thHG.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2d/6.73thHG.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20140321072955" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2d/6.73thHG.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140321072955" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The 73rd Hunger Games arena." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2d/6.73thHG.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20140321072955" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="137" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="6.73thHG.jpg" data-image-key="6.73thHG.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2d/6.73thHG.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/240?cb=20140321072955" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:6.73thHG.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The 73rd Hunger Games arena.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Unknown_Games">Unknown Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FArena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Some other arenas are mentioned throughout the series, but it is unknown in which Hunger Games they appeared.
</p>
<ul><li>A few years before the 50th Hunger Games, there was an arena that would go darkish without any warning. Out of the shadows, giant coal black weasels with pointy teeth would appear and attack the tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>A ruined city arena is briefly shown in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">the first film</a>, set during an unknown year.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, this is likely drawn from the original movie script, which began with the <a href="/wiki/73rd_Hunger_Games" title="73rd Hunger Games">73rd Hunger Games</a>. That year, the arena was the "abandoned resort town" of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspen,_Colorado" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Aspen, Colorado">Aspen, Colorado</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>There once was a freezing cold arena with no firewood (likely a frozen tundra). The Capitol audience considered this "anticlimactic" because so many tributes huddled into balls and quietly froze to death.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>One arena had a landscape of boulders, sand, and scruffy bushes. Many tributes were bitten by venomous snakes or "went insane from thirst".<sup id="cite_ref-:0_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>"A few years" prior to the 74th Hunger Games, an avalanche killed <a href="/wiki/Titus" title="Titus">Titus</a>, a tribute who had snapped and cannibalized his victims.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> To facilitate the avalanche, the arena probably had a snowy mountain.</li></ul>
