</p><p><b>Sandra Ellis Lafferty</b> is a Colorado-born actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSandra_Ellis_Lafferty%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sandra Ellis Lafferty was a respected Colorado theater actress prior to beginning her Hollywood career, which has consisted mainly of small but memorable parts in horror movies. She is also a former member of the company of the Denver Center for the Performing Arts and OpenStage Theatre &amp; Company in Fort Collins.
</p><p>Her first performance in a film was when she played a crying woman in the 1991 movie <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dogfight_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dogfight (film)">Dogfight</a></i>.
</p>
