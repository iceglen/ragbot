</p><p><b>Robert Knepper</b> is an American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Antonius" title="Antonius">Antonius</a> in both <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRobert_Knepper%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Knepper never intended to work in film and television projects; however, he began his television and film career in 1986 with <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Paper_Chase" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Paper Chase">The Paper Chase</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/That%27s_Life!" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:That&#39;s Life!">That's Life!</a></i>. He has made appearances on well known television series such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek:_The_Next_Generation" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Star Trek: The Next Generation">Star Trek: The Next Generation</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ER_(TV_Series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:ER (TV Series)">ER</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Law &amp; Order">Law &amp; Order</a></i>. Robert has also starred in the films <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.I.P.D" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:R.I.P.D">R.I.P.D</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percy_Jackson:_Sea_of_Monsters" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters">Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters</a></i>, as well as the well known television series Prison Break, where he portrayed Theodore "T-Bag" Bagswell.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FRobert_Knepper%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Knepper came into the role of Antonius because he was the neighbor of Philip Messina, <i>The Hunger Games</i> franchise's production designer. When Knepper asked Messina if there were any roles for him in the upcoming <i>Mockingjay</i> films, Messina replied that he would ask producer <a href="/wiki/Nina_Jacobson" title="Nina Jacobson">Nina Jacobson</a>. According to Knepper, she said, "Yeah, he looks like he could play a bad guy."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
