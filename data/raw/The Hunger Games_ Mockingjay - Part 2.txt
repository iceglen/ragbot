<p><i><b>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay — Part 2</b></i> is the film adaptation of the second half of <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> by <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a> and the fourth film in <i>The Hunger Games</i> film series. It is the sequel to <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, the film adaptation of the first half of the book. It was theatrically released on November 19, 2015, in the United Kingdom, and on the following day in the United States.
</p><p>All of the actors whose roles continue into the final films are set to reprise their roles in <i>Mockingjay</i>. <a href="/wiki/Francis_Lawrence" title="Francis Lawrence">Francis Lawrence</a> directed both <i>Mockingjay</i> films, following his helming of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.
</p><p><i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</i> has gained mainly positive reviews from critics during their critic screenings, and currently holds an 70% approval rating on the review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes, making it the second lowest in the film series. Similarly, the film currently holds a score of 6.6 out of 10 on IMDb.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Synopsis">Synopsis</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Mockingjay_-_Part_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>With the nation of Panem in a full-scale war, <a href="/wiki/Katniss" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Katniss">Katniss</a> confronts <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> in the final showdown. Teamed with a group of her closest friends – including <a href="/wiki/Gale" class="mw-redirect" title="Gale">Gale</a>, <a href="/wiki/Finnick" class="mw-redirect" title="Finnick">Finnick</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta</a> – Katniss goes off on a mission with the unit from <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a> as they risk their lives to liberate the citizens of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, and stage an assassination attempt on President Snow who has become increasingly obsessed with destroying her. The mortal traps, enemies, and moral choices that await Katniss will challenge her more than any arena she faced in <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)"> the Hunger Games</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Plot">Plot</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Mockingjay_-_Part_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Katniss Everdeen (<a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence" title="Jennifer Lawrence">Jennifer Lawrence</a>) recovers after nearly being murdered by Peeta Mellark (<a href="/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson" title="Josh Hutcherson">Josh Hutcherson</a>), who had been "hijacked" by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. She asks <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">President Alma Coin</a> (<a href="/wiki/Julianne_Moore" title="Julianne Moore">Julianne Moore</a>) to send her to District Two, the last district outside rebel control. While there, she quarrels with her friend Gale Hawthorne (<a href="/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth" title="Liam Hemsworth">Liam Hemsworth</a>) over how much mercy should be shown to Capitol supporters. After rebel forces destroy the Capitol's weapons supply in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, with a plan developed by Gale and Beetee Latier (<a href="/wiki/Jeffrey_Wright" title="Jeffrey Wright">Jeffrey Wright</a>), Katniss shoots a propo as the District 2 refugees are cornered and forced to surrender by the rebels. When she attempts to intervene in the situation and prevent further bloodshed, a fight breaks out, during which Katniss is shot. Meanwhile, <a href="/wiki/President_Coriolanus_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Coriolanus Snow">President Coriolanus Snow</a> (<a href="/wiki/Donald_Sutherland" title="Donald Sutherland">Donald Sutherland</a>) rebukes his minister Antonius for having trapped the Capitol's best troops in District Two, and kills him by poisoning. Snow then orders his ministers to withdraw all <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> into the center of the Capitol and have the Gamemakers set up new defenses, to prepare for a bloody final stand against the rebels.
</p><p>During her recovery, Katniss visits Peeta; he is recovering some of his memories but remains unstable and resentful towards Katniss, which causes her to become depressed. She approaches Coin and demands to kill Snow as her "final mission", but Coin declines in favor of preserving Katniss as the symbol of their revolution. During the wedding of Finnick Odair (<a href="/wiki/Sam_Claflin" title="Sam Claflin">Sam Claflin</a>) and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> (<a href="/wiki/Stef_Dawson" title="Stef Dawson">Stef Dawson</a>), <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> (<a href="/wiki/Jena_Malone" title="Jena Malone">Jena Malone</a>) tells Katniss of an aircraft leaving for the outskirts of the Capitol, where <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Commander Paylor</a> (<a href="/wiki/Patina_Miller" title="Patina Miller">Patina Miller</a>) is rallying troops for an all-out assault on the city. Sneaking aboard, Katniss is welcomed by the rebels, to Coin's irritation. Katniss and her camera crew, along with Finnick and Gale, are assigned to <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>, the "Star Squad", led by <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a> (<a href="/wiki/Mahershala_Ali" title="Mahershala Ali">Mahershala Ali</a>). However, they discover that their mission is to trail behind the invasion and be the onscreen face of the rebellion. They use a holographic map called a Holo to evade "pods" (booby traps) created by <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> which line the streets of the Capitol; Finnick cynically compares the pods to an arena by remarking "Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to the 76th Hunger Games.". Peeta is unexpectedly assigned to the team by Coin despite still being vulnerable to the Capitol's conditioning. Boggs warns Katniss to be careful around him, because Coin sees Katniss as a potential threat to her authority, and may have placed Peeta on the team to indirectly eliminate her. Katniss initially acts hostile towards Peeta, but eventually they begin to open up to one another, as she realizes he has trouble telling real memories from hijacked ones.
</p><p>As the squad and camera crew venture deeper into the Capitol, they are ambushed by an unmarked pod that fatally injures Boggs; he transfers command of the unit to Katniss before dying, telling her to "do what you came here to do". Another pod is triggered, which releases a flood of black ooze. Peeta's hijacking drives him to attack Katniss again, during which he pushes <a href="/wiki/Mitchell" title="Mitchell">Mitchell</a> (Joe Chrest) into the ooze but Finnick restrains him long enough for the squad to evade the ooze and escape into a building. With Boggs dead and command passed down to her, Katniss lies and tells her squad that she is under orders from Coin to kill Snow. They are forced to leave behind the Leeg twins (<a href="/wiki/Misty_Ormiston" title="Misty Ormiston">Misty Ormiston</a> and <a href="/wiki/Kim_Ormiston" title="Kim Ormiston">Kim Ormiston</a>)as one of the twins was injured; as they take shelter in another building, Peacekeepers bombard their former hideout, killing the twins. The Capitol broadcasts a message with Snow and <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> (<a href="/wiki/Stanley_Tucci" title="Stanley Tucci">Stanley Tucci</a>) celebrating the supposed deaths of Squad 451 and the Mockingjay, but Beetee hijacks the signal mid-broadcast, allowing Coin to introduce herself to Panem and declare Katniss a martyr for the rebel cause.
</p><p>As they are still far from Snow's mansion, the team decides to venture into the Capitol's sewers in order to avoid the pods, using <a href="/wiki/Pollux" title="Pollux">Pollux</a> (<a href="/wiki/Elden_Henson" title="Elden Henson">Elden Henson</a>) as a guide, since he used to work in the underground. Peeta, remorseful over Mitchell's death, believes he should die rather than endanger the rest of the squad again, but admits to Katniss that his memories are becoming clearer. Snow, discovering Katniss is still alive, unleashes a pack of monstrous reptilian muttations to hunt her down. In the ensuing battle, <a href="/wiki/Jackson" title="Jackson">Officer Jackson</a> (<a href="/wiki/Michelle_Forbes" title="Michelle Forbes">Michelle Forbes</a>), <a href="/wiki/Castor" title="Castor">Castor</a> (<a href="/wiki/Wes_Chatham" title="Wes Chatham">Wes Chatham</a>), and <a href="/wiki/Homes" title="Homes">Homes</a> (<a href="/wiki/Omid_Abtahi" title="Omid Abtahi">Omid Abtahi</a>) are killed. As the squad attempts to flee the sewers, a mutt grabs Finnick as he covers their retreat, forcing Katniss to use the Holo's self-destruct to end his suffering and destroy the remaining mutts. They are attacked by Peacekeepers after reaching the underground railways, during which another pod kills <a href="/wiki/Messalla" title="Messalla">Messalla</a> (<a href="/wiki/Evan_Ross" title="Evan Ross">Evan Ross</a>). The few survivors eventually reach the surface and <a href="/wiki/Cressida" title="Cressida">Cressida</a> (<a href="/wiki/Natalie_Dormer" title="Natalie Dormer">Natalie Dormer</a>) leads them to a safehouse, where a former Hunger Games <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a> (<a href="/wiki/Eugenie_Bondurant" title="Eugenie Bondurant">Eugenie Bondurant</a>), hides them in her basement. While they all mourn the loss of their squad, Katniss confesses that she lied about her orders to kill Snow, and as a result of her lie, the rest of the squad are dead. The squad reveal that they knew this all along, but played along because they trusted her. Peeta comforts Katniss, saying that if she kills Snow, she will avenge the deaths of everyone who has died because of him. That night, Gale and Peeta discuss their love triangle, with Gale stating he thinks Peeta has won her over, but Peeta thinks that Gale has. Gale eventually remarks that deciding which one she can't live without is Katniss's problem, and not theirs.
</p><p>Snow announces that the rebels have penetrated deep into the Capitol and asks refugees to come to <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">his mansion</a>, offering them with food and shelter. With the pods deactivated to allow passage, Katniss and Gale disguise themselves and mingle with the refugees to gain access to Snow. Their covers are nearly blown when the rebels arrive and attack the Peacekeepers, killing many Capitol civilians. In the ensuing chaos, Gale is captured by the peacekeepers; Katniss marches towards Snow's mansion and finds Peacekeepers taking Capitol children to provide Snow with a human shield. As the rebels near the mansion, a hovercraft with Capitol insignias flies by and drop <a href="/wiki/Silver_parachute" title="Silver parachute">silver parachutes</a> (similar to the ones used to deliver <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a> in the Hunger Games) into the crowd of children surrounding the mansion, which explode. Upon the explosion, a team of rebel medics attempt to help the injured, among whom is Katniss' sister, <a href="/wiki/Prim" class="mw-redirect" title="Prim">Prim</a> (<a href="/wiki/Willow_Shields" title="Willow Shields">Willow Shields</a>). Shocked to see her sister on the battlefield, Katniss heads towards her, but just as Prim sees her a second round of bombs go off, killing Prim and badly injuring Katniss.
</p><p>Upon recovering, Katniss learns from Haymitch that the Capitol has been conquered by the rebels, and that Snow has been captured. Katniss confronts the imprisoned Snow, who claims that Coin masterminded the child-bombings in order to turn his remaining supporters against him; Coin has thus played both Snow and Katniss "for fools" because they were both too focused on destroying each other to see her endgame. When Katniss accuses him of lying, he calmly reminds her of their promise to always be truthful to each other. Suspicious, Katniss realizes that the bombs resembled a trap Gale and Beetee had been working on earlier. When she confronts him, Gale remorsefully confesses that it may have been his and apologizes for failing to protect her family; unable to fully forgive him, Katniss tells him goodbye.
</p><p>Later, Katniss attends a meeting with Coin (who has appointed herself interim President of Panem) and the other surviving Hunger Games victors- Peeta, Haymitch, Enobaria, Annie, Beetee and Johanna. Coin proposes having one final edition of the Hunger Games with the children of the Capitol as retribution for the previous games, as an alternative to exterminating most of the Capitol's population as war criminals. Beetee, Annie and Peeta vote no, while Johanna and Enobaria vote yes. Realizing that Snow was right about Coin's ambitions and ruthlessness, Katniss votes in favor of the motion "for Prim," which gains Coin's trust; Haymitch follows her lead. She is awarded the opportunity to execute Snow.
</p><p>At the execution, which is being held before the whole of Panem, Katniss faces Snow once again. As she readies her bow, the two make eye contact, and Snow gives her one last smile. Katniss silently agrees, and instead shoots the arrow into Coin's heart, killing her. While a laughing Snow is lynched by an angry mob, Katniss attempts to commit suicide by consuming a <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> pill, given to them earlier in case of enemy capture, but Peeta stops her attempt. Although initially imprisoned, Katniss is pardoned for her crime (being deemed mentally unwell) and through a letter delivered by <a href="/wiki/Haymitch" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch">Haymitch</a> (<a href="/wiki/Woody_Harrelson" title="Woody Harrelson">Woody Harrelson</a>), <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> (<a href="/wiki/Philip_Seymour_Hoffman" title="Philip Seymour Hoffman">Philip Seymour Hoffman</a>) advises Katniss to return to District 12 until things settle down.
</p><p>Katniss returns to her home in the ruins of <a href="/wiki/Victor%27s_Village" class="mw-redirect" title="Victor&#39;s Village">Victor's Village</a> to recover from her traumatic ordeal, where she is eventually joined by Peeta, who has fully recovered his memories and his love for Katniss. After an initial period of depression, Katniss is eventually able to release her grief over Prim's death. Commander Paylor is elected the new President of Panem, and Katniss, Peeta, and Haymitch bond over their shared trauma. They learn that Gale has become a military commander in District Two, and Annie has given birth to Finnick's son. Katniss and Peeta slowly grow back together, and Katniss eventually admits her love for him.
</p><p>Years later, Katniss and Peeta are shown to have two children. As Katniss' infant child wakes up from a nightmare she reminisces about her own recurring nightmares she still suffers from and explains she plays "a game" where she lists all the good things she has seen someone do. She notes that while the game has grown tedious over the years, "there are much worse games to play", as she smiles into the camera before the screen cuts to black.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Production">Production</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Mockingjay_-_Part_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The film adaptations of <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i> were announced in 2012. It was confirmed it would be split into two parts, with <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Part 1</a></i> releasing on November 21, 2014, and <i>Part 2</i> releasing on November 20, 2015.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This follows what the actors in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i> have been saying about signing on for four films.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Based upon Lionsgate's renaming of the film for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, it is known that the last films would be called <i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</i> and <i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</i>. While filming in Atlanta, Georgia, it was revealed that both parts would be shot back-to-back.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>On February 6, 2015, Lionsgate confirmed that the film will be released in IMAX 3D and 2D. Francis Lawrence calls the film "The most violent of the four." There was also a rumor that the first teaser trailer of <i>The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</i> will release in front of the screenings of <i>The Divergent Series: Insurgent</i>. 
</p><p>On March 18, 2015, the teaser trailer and poster was released two days before <i>Insurgent</i> came out. It was also teased on Facebook and Twitter. 
</p><p>The first official teaser trailer for <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i> was released on June 9, 2015. A feature-length documentary about the film's production, <i><a href="/wiki/Pawns_No_More:_Making_the_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_Part_2" title="Pawns No More: Making the Hunger Games: Mockingjay Part 2">Pawns No More: Making the Hunger Games: Mockingjay Part 2</a></i>, was included as a featurette with the DVD release.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Box_Office">Box Office</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hunger_Games%3A_Mockingjay_-_Part_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Domestically, <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i> earned $281,723,902 at the box office, whilst it earned $653,428,261 worldwide, making it the ninth highest grossing film of 2015.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i> is currently the lowest grossing film of the franchise. Lionsgate co-chairman Rob Friedman cited the lower box office performance was attributed to the November 2015 Paris Attacks, plus the film competing against <i>Star Wars: The Force Awakens,</i> which released about a month later.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
