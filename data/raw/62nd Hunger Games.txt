</p><p>The <b>62nd Hunger Games</b> took place twelve years prior to the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>, the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_tributes">Known tributes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F62nd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> - <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> (victor)</li>
<li>Unknown male tribute<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F62nd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>According to <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a>, Enobaria <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> for the 62nd Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She likely formed a typical alliance with other Career Tributes, since she was from one of the Career districts and <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> described her as a "classic Career".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_19_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_19-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At some point during the Games, Enobaria was doing hand-to-hand combat with an unknown male tribute and she ripped open his throat with her teeth.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F62nd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Enobaria was so famous for ripping out that tribute's throat that, after her victory, she had her teeth surgically altered so each one ended in a point like a fang, inlaid with gold. According to Katniss, she had "no shortage of admirers" in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
