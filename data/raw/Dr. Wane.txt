</p><p><b>Dr. Wane</b> was a doctor who worked at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Hospital" title="Capitol Hospital">Capitol Hospital</a>. He took care of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDr._Wane%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After <a href="/wiki/Clemensia_Dovecote" title="Clemensia Dovecote">Clemensia Dovecote</a> was attacked by <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a>, Dr. Wane was one of the doctors who treated her and administered the antidote. He reassured <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, who had been there at the time of the attack, and suggested that he return home.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_8-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Dr. Wane later treated Coriolanus himself after the bombing of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, which had occurred during the tour for the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a> of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. He sent <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Coriolanus's cousin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma&#39;am">grandmother</a> home before giving Coriolanus a quick checkup and explaining his injuries. When asked about <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, Dr. Wane responded that he didn't know what had happened to her and told Coriolanus to get rest. 
</p><p>A few days afterward, he heard that Clemensia had visited Coriolanus in the night, so he talked to his patient and said that he could visit her. After lunch, Dr. Wane released Coriolanus.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
