<p><b>Train stations</b> are centers where <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">trains</a> load and unload their cargo or disembark passengers. There is at least one station in each <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>, as trains are the primary method of inter-district travel and the movement of both raw and finished materials in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>. Most of Panem's citizens will not make personal use of the train stations, as the oppressed people of the districts are prohibited from traveling to other districts or <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> via train unless they are <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>, <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a>, or otherwise authorized to do so.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Capitol">The Capitol</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain_station%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bd/Centro_detren.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120321002615" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The train station in the Capitol." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/b/bd/Centro_detren.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120321002615" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="110" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Centro detren.jpg" data-image-key="Centro_detren.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Centro_detren.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The train station in the Capitol.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The station ceiling was made out of glass panes, and the platform was wide.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> once met up with a classmate in the alley behind the train station as a dare from <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a>. He didn't consider it a romance, but the event earned him the reputation of being a player.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, Coriolanus arrived early to the train station to greet his tribute, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>. However, the train took hours to arrive, and the station was uncomfortably hot.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He also traveled to the train station before departing for <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> as a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The station attracted many of the city's residents during the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, as they gathered to catch a glimpse of the tributes brought to the city to compete. Before the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> waved to the citizens gathered in order to make himself memorable to the potential <a href="/wiki/Sponsors" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsors">sponsors</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="District_2">District 2</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTrain_station%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>Every bulb must be burning at full wattage inside the train station as well. Even from my position across the square, I can see clearly through the plate-glass front of the long, narrow building. It would be impossible to miss the arrival of a train, or even a single person.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Train station in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>A train tunnel led from <a href="/wiki/The_Nut" title="The Nut">the Nut</a> to a station in the square. The station is a long, narrow building, with a plate-glass front and bright lights. People used the trains in an attempt to escape from the Nut during its <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_the_Nut_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">bombing</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_15_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_15-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: The Hunger Games">"<i>We just stand there silently, watching our grimy little station rise up around us. Through the window, I can see the platform's thick with cameras. Everyone will be eagerly watching our homecoming.</i>"</span></dd>

<p>There was an efficient system that transported coal directly from the mines to the train station.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It took an hour to walk from the train station to the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers&#39; base">Peacekeepers' base</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_21_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_21-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but the station was only a short car ride from the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Katniss and Peeta were reaped, they were taken to their district's train station, which was swarming with reporters.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> These reporters were also present as the two of them returned from the Capitol as victors.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_27_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_27-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A few questions were allowed after Katniss and Peeta were greeted by their friends and family.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
