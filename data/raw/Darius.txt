<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Little is known of Darius's early life. He was presumably born and raised in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> or <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, as most Peacekeepers were,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> before he eventually enlisted.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Peacekeeping">Peacekeeping</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Darius was stationed in District 12, where he regularly frequented the Hob.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Six months before the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, right after New Year's, Darius teased <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> about trading a rabbit for one of his kisses. He leaned up against the post of <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a>'s stall, tickled Katniss's cheek with the end of her braid, and pointed out other women around who serve as "references", making Katniss and Greasy Sae laugh.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Katniss stopped by the Hob on the morning of the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, Darius bought a bowl of soup from Greasy Sae and joked with Katniss about her appearance, lack of "district pride", and impending departure.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span id="Gale.27s_whipping"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Gale's_whipping">Gale's whipping</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After the Victory Tour, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> was whipped by <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>, and Darius was knocked unconscious for trying to intervene after "about twenty lashings". He'd grabbed Thread's arm, then he was struck in the head with the butt of the whip. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> nearly tripped over Darius on the ground when he arrived on the scene. Once Katniss, Haymitch, and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> got Thread to stop, a small group of Peacekeepers stuck behind and lifted Darius by his arms and legs,<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> presumably to carry him back to the base.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Enslavement">Enslavement</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Katniss was later horrified to discover that the Capitol made Darius into an Avox.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Alongside <a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a>, he was forced to serve the District 12 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> during the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. Katniss believed it was no coincidence that Darius was placed in their apartment, and that <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> had ordered him to serve there to frighten her.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>While imprisoned in the Capitol, Peeta witnessed the electrocution death of Lavinia and the torture of Darius. The guards beat him, cut off body parts, and questioned him, but as an Avox, he was unable to answer; he only made "horrible animal sounds" as they tortured him. Peeta believed that the goal had just been to force him to watch. It took days of beatings and mutilations until Darius died,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> presumably having succumbed to his injuries.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Katniss described Darius as probably being in his twenties, but that he didn't seem much older than she did. His messy red hair and his smile gave him a boyish charm and makes him stand out from most of the people living in District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Upon his enslavement as an Avox, the Capitol mutilated his tongue so he could no longer speak.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>As law enforcers go, he's one of my favorites. Never really throwing his weight around, usually good for a joke.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss Everdeen<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Darius was easygoing, often joking around with Katniss and Greasy Sae at the Hob.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> He was also willing to intervene against a fellow Peacekeeper when he saw Gale getting punished so brutally,<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so he seemed to have a strong moral compass.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDarius%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Darius is derived from the Persian name <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dariush" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dariush">Dariush</a>, meaning "holding firm the good"— where <i>good</i> is understood to mean truth and righteousness.
</p>
