<dd>&#8212;Haymitch Abernathy's father to <a href="/wiki/Sarshee_Whitcomb" title="Sarshee Whitcomb">Sarshee Whitcomb</a><sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p><b>Haymitch Abernathy's father,</b> called "Pa" by Haymitch, was the father of <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHaymitch_Abernathy%2527s_father%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mr. Abernathy was born to a family that used to be rebels.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He worked in the coal mines of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. He married <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">Willamae Abernathy</a> and had two sons. They were also going to have twin girls, but they died when they were born too early. Haymitch heard his father crying one night shortly after their loss.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the death of his boss <a href="/wiki/Lyle_Whitcomb" title="Lyle Whitcomb">Lyle Whitcomb</a>, he looked out for his orphaned daughter <a href="/wiki/Sarshee_Whitcomb" title="Sarshee Whitcomb">Sarshee Whitcomb</a>. When she was <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> into the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, he visited her with his wife and Haymitch to say goodbye and advised her not to let <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> use her.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Haymitch's father died in a coal mine fire, leaving his wife to care for their two young children. He was buried in the miner's graveyard, in the family plot. After he died, rumors spread that he died in a seditious event at sabotaging the mines, or that he and his crew were targeted by the Capitol for being "a pack of troublemakers."<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHaymitch_Abernathy%2527s_father%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to Haymitch, Mr. Abernathy was quite intelligent, but kept to himself and didn't share all of his ideas. He was distrustful of people who shared every idea they had.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was rumored to be quite rebellious,<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and this may be evidenced in his words to Sarshee before she went to the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
