</p><p><b>Jacqueline Emerson</b> is an American actress and singer who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a>, the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>, in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She also had a role in the thriller movie <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Curse_of_Downers_Grove" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Curse of Downers Grove">The Curse of Downers Grove</a></i>, which was released in 2015.
</p><p>Emerson made her singing debut with two songs, "Peter Pan" and "Catch Me if You Can".
</p>

<ul><li>At the Los Angeles premiere of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> on November 13, 2023, Jacqueline was asked to share her opinion on a popular fan theory: that Foxface had recognized the <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries as poisonous and intentionally ate them, dying by suicide. According to Jacqueline, director <a href="/wiki/Gary_Ross" title="Gary Ross">Gary Ross</a> said her character's death was accidental; however, she personally believed it was suicide because the film showed the character quizzing herself on plants.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
