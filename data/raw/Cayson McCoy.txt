</p><p><b>Cayson McCoy</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> and the older brother of <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCayson_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The McCoys lived three houses down from <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>, and his younger sister Louella was one of two female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> made them stay to stage reaction shots after the reaping, they refused to give him what he wanted, staring at him with "stony" expressions.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Cayson wasn't working in the mines, he was known to gamble, but he had a special distaste for the Booker Boys, who took and placed bets on tributes in the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games" class="mw-redirect" title="Hunger Games">Hunger Games</a>. He once told Louella that all the gamblers learned coin tricks so they could covertly signal to others when a game was on. Another time, he spit at the mention of <a href="/wiki/Jethro_Callow" title="Jethro Callow">Jethro Callow</a> and said he "didn’t have no truck with people who made money on dead kids."<sup id="cite_ref-SR4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR4-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coming home late one night, he was the person who spotted the Abernathys' house burning and "shouted to wake the dead", rousing everyone to help. At the funeral of <a href="/wiki/Willamae_Abernathy" title="Willamae Abernathy">Willamae Abernathy</a> and her son <a href="/wiki/Sid_Abernathy" title="Sid Abernathy">Sid Abernathy</a>, he had his hands and face bandaged, and he spit on Jethro Callow's grave. At some point, he mentioned to his mother that a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a> he knew told him that <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a> was set to have a hearing with the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers&#39; base">base</a> commander that day.<sup id="cite_ref-SR26_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR26-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCayson_McCoy%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cayson is one of Louella's "half dozen" brothers and sisters. Aside from Louella, only two were of reaping age.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He also had a sister named <a href="/wiki/Ima_McCoy" title="Ima McCoy">Ima McCoy</a>, who was older than Louella.<sup id="cite_ref-SR26_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR26-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
