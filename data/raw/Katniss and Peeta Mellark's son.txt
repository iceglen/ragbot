<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FKatniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%2527s_son%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Katniss' pregnancy with her son was easier than her pregnancy with her <a href="/wiki/Katniss_and_Peeta_Mellark%27s_daughter" title="Katniss and Peeta Mellark's daughter">daughter</a>. He is a toddler during the epilogue and is at least a few years younger than his sister.<sup id="cite_ref-Epilogue_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, in the film, their birth order is reversed, making him a few years older than his infant sister.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_film_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_film-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Katniss truly loves her children, appreciating their ignorance towards the Games. However, she is remorseful, knowing that someday she will need to tell her children about their parents' role in the Games and the rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-Epilogue_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Epilogue-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
