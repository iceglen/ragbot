<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInfiltration_of_the_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> ended abruptly when <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> fired an electrified <a href="/wiki/Bow_and_arrow" title="Bow and arrow">arrow</a> at the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">arena</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>, blowing it open.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A rebel <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> rescued her along with <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>. Soon after this, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a> were captured from the arena by the Capitol,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> was abducted from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> to be imprisoned alongside them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Captivity_in_the_Capitol">Captivity in the Capitol</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInfiltration_of_the_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the victors' capture, Peeta spent time in his quarters building card houses until he was brought out for an interview with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>. Peeta recounted his version of what had happened in the arena and, presumably having been coached by the Capitol, called for the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a> to come to a ceasefire. This interview would not be aired on television for another several weeks; it reached District 13 at 18:30, after Katniss visited the bombed-out remains of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> for the first time.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
Peeta, Johanna, and possibly Annie were held in adjacent cells. As part of their torture, they were forced to listen to each other's screams.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Johanna was starved, beaten, had her head shaven, and she was soaked with water and tortured with electric shocks. This caused her to develop a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/aquaphobia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:aquaphobia">fear of water</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Darius" title="Darius">Darius</a> and <a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a>, two <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avoxes</a> who had served the District 12 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>, were also tortured alongside the victors; Lavinia's heart stopped instantly when she received a lethal electric shock, and Darius was beaten and dismembered for days before he succumbed to his injuries.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Peeta had not yet begun to undergo <a href="/wiki/Hijacking" title="Hijacking">hijacking</a> at this time,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but he was eventually subjected to injections of <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> venom and exposed to upsetting stimuli in order to brainwash him into fearing Katniss, to the point where he would attempt to kill her.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-7200.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200919042423" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta in his second propo." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/4d/Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-7200.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200919042423" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="101" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-7200.jpg" data-image-key="Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-7200.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Mockingjay1-movie-screencaps.com-7200.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peeta in his second propo in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>By the time of his second interview with Caesar, aired four or five days after the first, Peeta had lost "at least fifteen pounds" and showed signs of pain when he moved. He'd also developed a nervous hand tremor, and he had bags under his eyes. This time, Peeta warned Katniss that the rebels were using her as a weapon, and urged her to find out what was going on.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/6f/Peeta_Capitol_third_propo.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20230709050331" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta during his third propo" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/6f/Peeta_Capitol_third_propo.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230709050331" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="92" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Peeta Capitol third propo.jpg" data-image-key="Peeta_Capitol_third_propo.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Peeta_Capitol_third_propo.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peeta during his third propo.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Three days later, Peeta was made to recount war damage allegedly perpetrated by rebels in his final <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propaganda video</a> for the Capitol, this time aired live. District 13 was able to break into the broadcast with their own propos of Finnick. After battling back and forth for control, <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> asked Peeta if he had any "parting thoughts" for Katniss, and Peeta revealed that the Capitol was planning to bomb District 13.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The warning came ten minutes before District 13's own systems would have detected the oncoming assault, at which point everyone was already evacuated into a bunker deep underground.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Bunker missiles<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> hammered the district for three days, causing significant damage to its infrastructure. Anyone on the first ten levels of the complex would have been killed, but due to the timely warning, no lives were lost.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Raid">The Raid</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInfiltration_of_the_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Mockingjay">"<i>Covers will be blown. People may die. But keep in mind that they're dying every day.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;<a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> to Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>With Peeta and Annie in Capitol custody, Katniss and Finnick's mental and emotional states were greatly compromised. Fearing the loss of their most crucial propaganda assets, District 13's special forces launched a rescue mission into the Capitol— the first known rebel action to take place inside the city itself— aimed at freeing Peeta and Annie, as well as Johanna and Enobaria.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The rescue was carried out in the last week of August, five or six weeks after their initial capture, by a team of seven from District 13. This team was volunteer-only, headed by <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a> and including <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:4_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> though the other five operatives are unknown.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/63/District13SpecialForces.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20220624210506" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="District 13 special forces about to extract Peeta from his cell." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/63/District13SpecialForces.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20220624210506" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="District13SpecialForces.jpg" data-image-key="District13SpecialForces.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:District13SpecialForces.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">District 13 special forces about to extract Peeta from his cell.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>At 15:00, District 13 aired a series of propos in the Capitol as a distraction, known as an airtime assault. The most prominent was a damning exposé by Finnick of sexual exploitation and government corruption. During this time, the team and rebel insiders freed three of the victors from an underground prison. As a diversion, rebels detonated a bomb in a government building several miles away, then cut the power to the prison and distributed knockout gas through the vents. Gale received a wound under his shoulder blade, but three of the four victors were successfully recovered. The fourth, Enobaria, was presumed by District 13 to have been released by the Capitol at some point prior.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>, the victors were rescued from belowground levels of the Training Center, and the rescue took place at night. President Snow outright told Katniss over a video call that he knew about the infiltration, but even once the Capitol's air defense system was back online, the team was able to leave without being shot down.<sup id="cite_ref-:5_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInfiltration_of_the_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>The team and rescued victors arrived in District 13 around midnight. Annie was lucid and apparently unharmed, though bedraggled, naked, and only wrapped in a sheet when she ran to hug Finnick. Johanna was still unconscious from the knockout gas, emaciated and bruised with her head shaven. Peeta was just waking up and having his vitals checked by doctors when he attempted to strangle Katniss to death.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Peeta was knocked out by Boggs, and it was revealed soon after that he had been hijacked for this exact purpose. That was why the Capitol had let him go; they intended to use him as a weapon against Katniss. Additionally, the night after the rescue, President Snow aired the live <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a> of <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a>, who was Peeta's <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, and his <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Recovery">Recovery</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FInfiltration_of_the_Capitol%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Katniss had a brief hospital stay for her bruised larynx, and Gale spent some time recovering from his injury; upon his discharge, he and <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> devised death traps for use in the war, justifying it as using the same rulebook as Snow did hijacking Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Johanna remained in the hospital, speaking with a <a href="/wiki/District_13_head_doctor" title="District 13 head doctor">head doctor</a> she despised and receiving doses of morphling. She quickly developed an addiction to the drug,<sup id="cite_ref-:6_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and she battled withdrawals when she and Katniss trained as soldiers ahead of the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, Johanna failed her last assessment when a simulated flood triggered a traumatic flashback to her torture, so she was unable to join the war effort as a combatant.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Doctors from District 13 treated Peeta, at first allowing him only the most innocuous visitors like his childhood friend <a href="/wiki/Delly_Cartwright" title="Delly Cartwright">Delly Cartwright</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, progress undoing his hijacking only began to be made once <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a> suggested using <a href="/wiki/Morphling_(drug)" title="Morphling (drug)">morphling</a> to calm him and hijack him back, creating positive associations to repair his distorted memories.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Despite this, Peeta was not by any means cured when he was sent as an auxiliary member of <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and after the war, he had to remain in the Capitol for several months receiving treatment from <a href="/wiki/Dr._Aurelius" title="Dr. Aurelius">Dr. Aurelius</a> before he was cleared to return home to District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Little is known of Annie Cresta's captivity or recovery. However, she and Finnick were able to have a <a href="/wiki/Finnick_and_Annie%27s_wedding" title="Finnick and Annie&#39;s wedding">wedding</a> in District 13, and Peeta decorated their cake.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They were happily married<sup id="cite_ref-:2_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> for a brief time before Finnick's deployment to the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At some point between her arrival and his departure, Annie became pregnant with their <a href="/wiki/Annie_and_Finnick_Odair%27s_son" title="Annie and Finnick Odair&#39;s son">son</a>, and she sent Katniss and Peeta a photo of her little boy after the war.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
