</p><p><b>Marian Green</b> is a stuntwoman who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_9_female_(75th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 9 female (75th HG)">District 9 female</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, as well as stunt doubling for <a href="/wiki/Sandra_Ellis_Bufferty" class="mw-redirect" title="Sandra Ellis Bufferty">Sandra Ellis Bufferty</a> as <a href="/wiki/Greasy_Sae" title="Greasy Sae">Greasy Sae</a> and <a href="/wiki/Amanda_Plummer" title="Amanda Plummer">Amanda Plummer</a> as <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>. Marian is an accomplished stuntwoman, stunt coordinator, and actress, appearing in renowned films such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Terminator" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Terminator">The Terminator</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir_Dogs" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Reservoir Dogs">Reservoir Dogs</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_One" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Air Force One">Air Force One</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_Harbor" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pearl Harbor">Pearl Harbor</a></i>, and in numerous television shows, including <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffy_the_Vampire_Slayer" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Buffy the Vampire Slayer">Buffy the Vampire Slayer</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnum,_P.I." class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Magnum, P.I.">Magnum, P.I.</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Rider" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Knight Rider">Knight Rider</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:24">24</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarian_Green%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Green was born in Santa Cruz, California and grew up on a farm in Felton, a small town in northern California<sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, the youngest of four children. She excelled in sports in high school and competed in swimming, cross country, and basketball.<sup id="cite_ref-USA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-USA-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She went on to attend UCLA on a chemistry scholarship, where she was a member of UCLA's first women's soccer team and during which time she learned many of the skills necessary to becoming a successful stuntwoman, including gymnastics, martial arts, and SCUBA.<sup id="cite_ref-USA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-USA-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She attended Kahana's Stunt School<sup id="cite_ref-USA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-USA-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> after deciding she no longer wanted to become a physical therapist.<sup id="cite_ref-CS_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CS-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_career">Film career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarian_Green%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Green was a fan of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burt_Reynolds" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Burt Reynolds">Burt Reynolds</a> film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hooper_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hooper (film)">Hooper</a></i>, which was how she became interested in stunt work. One of her first jobs was as an extra on the film <i>Return of the Rebels</i>, starring <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Swayze" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Patrick Swayze">Patrick Swayze</a>. She was accidentally clipped in the middle of a stunt by a motorcycle and sent flying. When the shot made it into the final cut of the film, it made Green eligible to join the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Screen Actors Guild">Screen Actors Guild</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CS_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CS-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Word of mouth soon spread and Green subsequently worked on some of the biggest films and television shows of the 1980s, including <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Terminator" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Terminator">The Terminator</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Rider_(1982_TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Knight Rider (1982 TV series)">Knight Rider</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cagney_and_Lacey" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cagney and Lacey">Cagney and Lacey</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnum,_P.I." class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Magnum, P.I.">Magnum, P.I.</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pee-wee%27s_Big_Adventure" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pee-wee&#39;s Big Adventure">Pee-wee's Big Adventure</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The 1990s proved just as busy, with Marian appearing in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir_Dogs" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Reservoir Dogs">Reservoir Dogs</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Instinct" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Basic Instinct">Basic Instinct</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Returns" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Batman Returns">Batman Returns</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diagnosis_Murder" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Diagnosis Murder">Diagnosis Murder</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seinfeld" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Seinfeld">Seinfeld</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_%26_Robin_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Batman &amp; Robin (film)">Batman &amp; Robin</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffy_the_Vampire_Slayer" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Buffy the Vampire Slayer">Buffy the Vampire Slayer</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_One" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Air Force One">Air Force One</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-IMDb_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDb-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Green ventured into producing with her husband <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0002927/">Michael Hofstein</a> and helped to found <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://europacificfilms.com/">EuroPacific Films</a> along with <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0345846/?ref_=fn_al_nm_1">Stephane Guenin</a>. The production company films primarily in France and in the United States, and their films include <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2249826/combined">Killing Uncle Roman</a></i>, and <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2740452/combined">The Assignment 1.0</a></i>. <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Since appearing in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, Green's stunt career has become very busy and she has been working on television series and films including <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeland_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Homeland (TV series)">Homeland</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solace_(2014_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Solace (2014 film)">Solace</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchorman_2:_The_Legend_Continues" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Anchorman 2: The Legend Continues">Anchorman 2: The Legend Continues</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_the_Dome_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Under the Dome (TV series)">Under the Dome</a></i>. Green is once again being noticed by Hollywood, and she is receiving the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Gibson" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Helen Gibson">Helen Gibson</a> Award at the 2013 <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://actioniconawards.com/">Action Icon Awards</a>, named for the legendary first American stuntwoman.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
