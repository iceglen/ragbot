</p><p><b>Wilbur Fitzgerald</b> is an American actor who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWilbur_Fitzgerald%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He played the role of Bruce Bennett on the television show <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0455275/">Prison Break</a></i>. Wilbur also played District Attorney Darnelle on <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0094484/">In the Heat of the Night</a></i>. While on the series, he joined fellow cast members in recording songs <i>Jingle Bells</i> and <i>Christmas Time's A Comin'</i> for the 1991 <i>In the Heat of the Night</i> Christmas CD. The CD was produced by co-stars Randall Franks and Alan Autry.
</p>
