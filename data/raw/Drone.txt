<p>A <b>drone</b> is a small flying device used to deliver <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a> to <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. They were first used in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrone%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Creation">Creation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrone%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The idea of drones came into play with the help of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>, who suggested the idea of sponsors being able to buy food that would then be delivered to their tribute of choice in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the <a href="/wiki/Control_Room" title="Control Room">Control Room</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> were later seen testing drone prototypes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrone%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The first drone use in the 10th Hunger Games occurred on the first day, when a bottle of water was sent to <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a>. However, five minutes passed before a drone arrived, only for it to crash into the crossbeam and fall to the ground. A replacement drone was sent ten minutes later.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Afterward, <a href="/wiki/Circ" title="Circ">Circ</a> and <a href="/wiki/Teslee" title="Teslee">Teslee</a>, the two tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_3" title="District 3">District 3</a>, emerged from the tunnels to inspect the broken drones. They reattached the wires and took the drones back to the barricade, also carrying bread and water that had been dropped by two other drones. Shortly following this, <a href="/wiki/Felix_Ravinstill" title="Felix Ravinstill">Felix Ravinstill</a> sent two bottles of water to <a href="/wiki/Dill" title="Dill">Dill</a>, although they were stolen by <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Sol" title="Sol">Sol</a> was sent food as soon as she emerged, but was killed by <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a> before the drones could reach her.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Lysistrata_Vickers" title="Lysistrata Vickers">Lysistrata Vickers</a> and Coriolanus sent countless bottles of water into the arena to trigger <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup</a>'s hydrophobia after he contracted rabies, protecting <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_17-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The next day, <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a> collected provisions from six drones; Mizzen, Coral, and Tanner shared the breakfast their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a> provided them; and Teslee retrieved another fallen drone.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Hilarius_Heavensbee" title="Hilarius Heavensbee">Hilarius Heavensbee</a> later tried to send <a href="/wiki/Wovey" title="Wovey">Wovey</a> food and water, but she died soon after the drones arrived. That same day, an oversized drone flew into the arena carrying a tank full of rainbow <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a>, which it released from a height of about ten yards.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> and <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a> were sent food after the snakes were killed. Once drones began to approach Mizzen with his own food, Teslee showed up with an altered drone and launched it into the air, revealing a cable that connected the drone's base to her wrist. Her hacked drone blocked the other drones' homing direction and caused them to bump into Mizzen, eventually killing him when he lost his balance and fell from the beam. Teslee then reeled in her drone and hugged it. However, she was soon killed by Treech, who collected the gifts <a href="/wiki/Urban_Canville" title="Urban Canville">Urban Canville</a> had meant to send her. The last known supplies sent via drone were from Coriolanus to Lucy Gray, who stowed the food in the pockets of her dress.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrone%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, the first drone was sent to <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a> quickly, but a replacement drone never arrived after it fell. Later, instead of Circ and Teslee using a modified drone as a weapon, Coriolanus requested several bottles of water to be sent via drone to Lucy Gray, taking advantage of their dangerous, unpredictable nature to distract the pack closing in on her and allowing his tribute to escape. Requests for deliveries also went through a computer at each mentor's desk rather than a <a href="/wiki/Communicuff" title="Communicuff">communicuff</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Discontinuation">Discontinuation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrone%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Sometime after the 10th Hunger Games, the use of drones in the Games was apparently discontinued; however, it is unknown whether this was an immediate or gradual change. In later years, sponsor gifts were delivered to tributes by <a href="/wiki/Silver_parachute" title="Silver parachute">silver parachutes</a> instead.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrone%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Drones found their recipients via facial recognition and could only carry one item at a time,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which they held in their claws. To deliver a gift using a drone, mentors could simply double-click on a food item from the list on their <a href="/wiki/Communicuff" title="Communicuff">communicuff</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Although the drones' response time was initially very slow, it improved upon each new request.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_17_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_17-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> considered them to be "rickety."<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
