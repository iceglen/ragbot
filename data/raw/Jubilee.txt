<p><b>Jubilee</b> was a parrot belonging to <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a>, a weatherman and aspiring magician with <a href="/wiki/Capitol_TV" title="Capitol TV">Capitol TV</a>. He brought the parrot to his broadcasts of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, hoping to liven up the proceedings. Jubilee was a depressed bird who was battling mange. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJubilee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the studio of the 10th Hunger Games, Lucretius Flickerman brought Jubilee to the set and tried to get him to say "Hi, Handsome!" to Dean <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>. The bird refused to speak at all at first, leading to Flickerman claiming that he was shy in front of strangers, but he did eventually say "Hi, Handsome!" in a depressed voice.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After that, the bird refused to speak again, so Lucky used a high-pitched voice to pretend Jubilee was speaking instead of himself in a conversation. During an attempted weather report, with added commentary from the bird, Flickerman used to high-pitched voice to say that <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> had "<i>...snow, Lucky. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>!</i>" When the camera cut to Coriolanus for his reaction, he gave them a thumbs-up, but in reality was tired of Flickerman's antics.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When eight <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentors</a> remained in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, Jubilee was brought out again, this time confined in a cage "more suited to a rabbit than a bird". In late afternoon, Lucky resumed his coverage, and Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a> sat across from him, holding Jubilee in his cage. Jubilee rocked back and forth, trying to comfort himself, and was eyed worriedly by Flickerman.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJubilee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/jubilee">Jubilee</a> is a word for a time of celebration, ironically contrasted by the bird's depressed demeanor.
</p>
