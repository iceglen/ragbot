</p><p><b>The Hob</b> was <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>'s black market. It was the only place to buy and sell illegal goods like liquor and poached game. It was often open at times when most legal stores would not be, such as on the day of the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hob%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Origin">Origin</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hob%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Hob was originally a coal warehouse, but a more efficient system of coal transport was discovered, allowing coal to be taken directly from the mines to the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">trains</a>. As a result, the warehouse was abandoned, and the empty space was gradually taken over by the black market.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Performances">Performances</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hob%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/22/TBOSAS_Hob_Entertainment_Weekly.webp/revision/latest?cb=20240228183654" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The stage at the Hob." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/22/TBOSAS_Hob_Entertainment_Weekly.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240228183654" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="TBOSAS Hob Entertainment Weekly.webp" data-image-key="TBOSAS_Hob_Entertainment_Weekly.webp" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:TBOSAS_Hob_Entertainment_Weekly.webp" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The stage at the Hob.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Around the time of <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Covey" title="Covey">Covey</a> put on regular musical performances at the Hob on a stage of wooden pallets. District 12 citizens and <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> were frequent customers.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During one of these weekend shows, a fight broke out during a blackout, so Peacekeepers were no longer permitted to leave the base alone. There was even discussion of making the Hob off-limits, though that never came to pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_24_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_24-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Weeks later, during another performance, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Spruce" title="Spruce">Spruce</a> shot and killed <a href="/wiki/Mayfair_Lipp" title="Mayfair Lipp">Mayfair Lipp</a> and <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a> in the shed behind the Hob. <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> witnessed the murders, and due to her relationship with Snow, she went back on stage to perform music that required all of the Covey to be present, postponing the discovery of the bodies. Eventually, <a href="/wiki/Maude_Ivory_Baird" title="Maude Ivory Baird">Maude Ivory Baird</a> found the bodies in the shed.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After that summer, musical performances at the Hob were outright banned by <a href="/wiki/Commander_Hoff" title="Commander Hoff">Commander Hoff</a>'s successor.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Black_market">Black market</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hob%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>By the time of the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the Hob remained more or less an open secret, still patronized by citizens and Peacekeepers alike. Authorities turned a blind eye to the black market because many of them benefited from the goods available there;<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> for example, the Head Peacekeeper, <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a>, bought white liquor from <a href="/wiki/Ripper" title="Ripper">Ripper</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, once Cray was replaced with <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>, he ordered Peacekeepers to set <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fire</a> to the Hob. The build-up of coal dust inside caused it to burn quickly and violently, and those who did business there had to find another way to sell their wares.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hob%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Hob was located about twenty minutes on foot from the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers&#39; base">Peacekeepers' base</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was also on <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>'s way home to <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Shed">Shed</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Hob%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Around the time of the 10th Hunger Games, there was a shed behind the Hob that the Covey used as a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/green_room" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:green room">green room</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_25_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_25-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The shed was actually an old garage, one that could hold "about eight cars." The large garage door was chained shut, but it had one entrance directly across from the Hob's back door, and another door directly across from that one on the opposite wall, propped open by a cinder block. It also had "a few cracked windows".<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Inside, there were tires and "odd bits of furniture" for the Covey to sit on as they tuned their instruments. They also kept their instrument cases and equipment there.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
