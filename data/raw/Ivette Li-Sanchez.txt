</p><p><b>Ivette Li-Sanchez</b> is a Venezuelan actress who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_5_female_(75th_HG)" title="District 5 female (75th HG)">District 5 female</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, which was her major motion picture debut. She was also in the film <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2621162/"><i>Mouthpiece</i></a>.
