<p>The <b>Hunger Games</b> was an annual event in the nation of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, instituted by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in the aftermath of the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> as part of the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a>. As mandated by the peace settlement, each of the twelve surviving <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> was forced to provide one male and one female "<a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>" between the ages of 12 and 18 to participate in a nationally televised fight to the death in a specially designed <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. Conceived as both punishment for the rebellion and a lasting reminder of the Capitol's power, this brutal competition was intended to deter future uprisings by demonstrating the Capitol's absolute control over life and death in the districts.
</p><p>Held without interruption for seventy-five years, the Hunger Games became the central institution of Panem's political order, simultaneously functioning as propaganda, entertainment, and a tool of oppression. Its continuance, coupled with the districts systemic oppression, became the primary catalyst for the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a> in <a href="/wiki/Timeline" title="Timeline">75 ADD</a>. In the wake of the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a>' victory and the fall of <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>'s regime, the Games and the treaty that created them were abolished as Panem transitioned into a democratic constitutional republic.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Premise">Premise</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Each year on Reaping Day, observed on July 4th,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> one male and female citizen between the ages of twelve and eighteen were chosen by <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">lottery</a> from each of Panem's twelve <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> to serve as <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a>. The twenty-four adolescents were subsequently transported to the Capitol, where there would be a week of public festivities, parades, and training under the supervision of Capitol officials called <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>. At the end of this period, the tributes were transported to a specially constructed <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a> and forced to fight to the death in a battle royale under controlled conditions until a single tribute remained.
</p><p>The Hunger Games were broadcasted as a nationally mandated spectacle, with every citizen of Panem required to watch. The contests varied in length over the course of its seventy-five year implementation, lasting anywhere from a few days to weeks, depending on the arena and circumstances. The final surviving tribute was declared the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> and subsequently awarded fame, wealth, and lifelong privileges<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, including a residence for themselves and their families at the luxurious <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a> present in each district. The victor's district also received extra shipments of food and supplies from the Capitol for the following year as a reward for producing a victor.<sup id="cite_ref-:14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Inception">Inception</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Hunger Games originated as an academic project of <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, though its realization was shaped indirectly by Highbottom's best friend, <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow" title="Crassus Snow">Crassus Snow</a> and his professor, Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>. During Highbottom and Snow's final year at the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a>, Dr. Gaul—then a faculty member of the University—tasked her students with a final project: to devise a punishment so severe that one's enemies would never forget the transgression.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Highbottom, known for his talents with puzzles and strategy, began sketching out what would become the concept of the Hunger Games with Snow, who was his partner for the project. Believing it a private joke between the two, he drafted the outline of the Games while heavily intoxicated— while egged on by Snow, who supplied him with more alcohol and padded his vanity. When Highbottom passed out, Snow submitted the proposal to Gaul without his knowledge. By the time Highbottom awoke, horrified and intent on destroying the draft, he learned that Snow had already had the assignment filed.
</p><p>Gaul would preserve the project, recognizing the Hunger Games as an ideal tool for long-term political control. Following the Capitol's victory in the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, Gaul revived the proposal and publicly introduced Highbottom as the "architect of the Hunger Games."<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> For Gaul, the Games were not just a punishment on the districts, but a demonstration: she believed without the Capitol's authority, Panem would collapse into chaos. By choosing children as combatants, she sought to prove that even the young and innocent could be turned into killers, stripping away any illusions of purity or moral high ground among the districts.
</p><p>Highbottom himself regarded the idea as a ghastly academic misstep, believing it would never endure. Instead, under Gaul's influence, the Hunger Games were institutionalized through the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a> and subsequently became the Capitol's most enduring instrument of control.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="First_10_years">First 10 years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The original Hunger Games, began shortly after the end of the Dark Days, were conceived primarily as a tool of punishment and terror. Far from the spectacle they would become in its later years, the early Games were marked by crude logistics, extreme brutality, and indifference to the fate of the tributes.
</p><p>Tributes were originally transported to the Capitol in cattle cars, often shackled and mistreated along the way. Upon arrival, they were confined in degrading conditions—initially in animal stables used for Peacekeepers' horses and by the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, in cages at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Malnourished and at times deliberately starved, many tributes perished before the Games themselves even began.
</p><p>The competitions of the first decade were staged in the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, an old multi-purpose amphitheater that was hastily converted into a killing ground for the Games. Tributes were provided only with basic weapons and no provisions for food or shelter. Once the Games began, the tributes were left to slaughter each other or die of exposure until a single victor emerged. Compared to later editions, the first nine Hunger Games progressed exceedingly fast, with the 10th Games noted to be lasting far longer than previous ones as it stretched on for a few days. Unlike later victors, the winners of these early Games received no rewards, privileges, or special status beyond noted recognition by the Capitol and their district; they simply were sent back to their home districts to resume ordinary life.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The brutality of these early Games did little to secure the Capitol's intended effect. While the districts endured them as a form of ritualized punishment, Capitol citizens showed decreasing interest in the Games with each passing year. For most, the Games were too ghastly to watch without any personal connection or stakes to one of the tributes. Even in the Capitol, doubts arose as to whether the Games should be continued at all, just as Highbottom had hoped for.
</p><p>To salvage the institution, Dr. Gaul, now serving as Head Gamemaker, began seeking ways to transform the Games alongside her associates into a form of national entertainment to ensure its continuity. By the 10th Hunger Games, innovations such <a href="/wiki/Mentors" class="mw-redirect" title="Mentors">mentors</a>, <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">betting</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorships</a> were introduced to increase public investment. Despite the overall fiasco that was the 10th Games, the gambit succeeded, injecting new life into the idea of the Hunger Games amongst the Capitol's populace. This marked a turning point in the evolution of the Games—developing from a crude and despised punishment to suppress the districts into a televised spectacle designed to both captivate and entertain Capitol audiences.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Intervening_years">Intervening years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Over the next six decades, the institution of the Hunger Games underwent a steady evolution into its modern form, transformed from a instrument of punishment into the Capitol's most elaborate spectacle of propaganda and entertainment. Guided by successive generations of Gamemakers—and later directly influenced by <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>—the Games were steadily refined into the ritualized reality television program it was known for in its final years.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-right show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 142px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/44/Capitol_Couture_Mockingjay_Part_1_poster_01.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20151111235946" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Mags Flanagan of District 4 became the first victor to embark on the Victory Tour." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/4/44/Capitol_Couture_Mockingjay_Part_1_poster_01.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/142?cb=20151111235946" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="142" height="208" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol Couture Mockingjay Part 1 poster 01.jpg" data-image-key="Capitol_Couture_Mockingjay_Part_1_poster_01.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_Couture_Mockingjay_Part_1_poster_01.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Mags Flanagan of District 4 became the first victor to embark on the Victory Tour.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>One of the earliest innovations was the introduction of the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, beginning with the <a href="/wiki/11th_Hunger_Games" title="11th Hunger Games">11th Hunger Games</a> and its victor from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a>. The annual tour, held about halfway between editions of the Games, saw the victor paraded through all twelve districts as a living reminder of the Games and the Capitol's dominance. Over time, victors began to be celebrated by Capitol audiences as cherished celebrities, which was further solidified once mentorship duties and the procurement of sponsors were assigned to victors. This in turn bound victors into the perpetuation of the Games and allowed the Capitol to exploit their experiences as narrative devices for Capitol audiences.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Careers_mocking.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419064637" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The emergence of Careers presented a new threat to tributes beyond the arena and mutts." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/13/Careers_mocking.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120419064637" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="194" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Careers mocking.jpg" data-image-key="Careers_mocking.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Careers_mocking.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The emergence of Careers presented a new threat to tributes beyond the arena and mutts.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Material incentives were also expanded at Snow's suggestion to ensure the Hunger Games were aspirational as well as punitive. At some point, the decision was made to reward the victor's home district with monthly food deliveries for a year. Additionally, the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Village" title="Victors&#39; Village">Victors' Village</a> offered lifetime luxury housing for victors and their families, segregating them from the general district population while also granting the Capitol tighter control over them. Victors would also be accorded a lifetime pension and special status within their district. Snow hoped these innovations would inspire a more refined class of tributes to participate in the Games. Indeed, these changes likely contributed to the emergence of the so-called <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tributes</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>,  <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>—children trained in combat skills with the expectation of volunteering for the Hunger Games. In time, Careers became notorious amongst the non-Career districts for perennially dominating and winning many later editions of the Games. At some point, tributes from all districts began receiving formal training in the Capitol for three days leading up to the Games.
</p><p>Technological advancements likewise reshaped the Hunger Games. With the <a href="/wiki/Bombings" title="Bombings">bombings</a> of the Capitol Arena during the 10th Hunger Games rendering the stadium unsuitable for future editions, Gamemakers shifted to hosting the Hunger Games in outdoor arenas, whether in the wilderness or ancient cities. In time, the Capitol began construction of specially-engineered arenas complete with controlled environments and lethal hazards and surrounded by a powerful <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>, all controlled by the Gamemakers in the <a href="/wiki/Control_Room" title="Control Room">Control Room</a>. The introduction of <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> in the 10th Games by Dr. Gaul would likewise return as another hazard tributes would have to face, enabling Gamemakers to construct elaborate battlegrounds that became as much of the spectacle as the tributes themselves.
</p><p>The Games were also punctuated by <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quells</a>, special editions of the Hunger Games held every twenty-five years. These special events introduced unique twists "written into the Treaty of Treason" that changed the regular formula of the Hunger Games while also serving to remind the districts of their transgression during the Dark Days. The <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a> required the districts to vote on the tributes they would send, while the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> doubled the number of tributes (48 instead of 24), producing the bloodiest contests in the Hunger Games history.
</p><p>Through these measures, the Hunger Games matured into a complex apparatus of oppression for the districts while simultaneously becoming a national pastime adored by Capitol citizens. The combination of ritual, spectacle, and technological display reinforced the image of the Capitol's supremacy. By the time of the 74th Hunger Games, the institution had reached its most polished form—complete with pageantry, televised interviews, advanced arena design, and carefully engineered muttations—but also its most precarious, as victors and districts alike began to chafe under the very system meant to keep them subdued after so many years.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Final_years">Final years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/36/Victory-tour-2.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130306041646" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss and Peeta&#39;s unprecedented dual victory set the stage for the civil unrest that would culminate in the Second Rebellion." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/36/Victory-tour-2.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130306041646" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="267" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Victory-tour-2.jpg" data-image-key="Victory-tour-2.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Victory-tour-2.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss and Peeta's unprecedented dual victory set the stage for the civil unrest that would culminate in the Second Rebellion.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> marked the beginning of the end for the Capitol's long-standing institution. For the first time in history, <i>two</i> tributes—<a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>—were crowned as joint victors after threatening an attempted double suicide with poisonous <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries rather than kill one another.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While outwardly portrayed as an act of love for one another, a notion readily accepted by Capitol audiences, to the districts and Capitol political leaders led by President Snow, it was seen as an act of defiance that undermined the Capitol's authority. The two victors were subsequently transformed into symbols of resistance, fomenting <a href="/wiki/Pre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest" title="Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest">unrest</a> in the districts not seen since the Dark Days.
</p><p>The spectacle of dual victors destabilized the Capitol's narrative. President Snow recognized the threat Katniss posed as a living symbol of defiance and sought to reassert control and subdue unrest by coercing her to affirm the love narrative with Peeta through the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">74th Victory Tour</a>. When this failed, Snow moved to utilize the upcoming <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, designated as the Third Quarter Quell, to eliminate her, and by association the threat posed by victors as a whole. For this Quell, the Capitol decreed that the pool of tributes would be drawn exclusively from existing victors, forcing Katniss and Peeta back into the arena alongside a wide assortment of past champions.
</p><p>Instead of restoring order, however, the Quell accelerated the rebellion. A covert alliance of victors—organized in part by <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a>, the latest Head Gamemaker who was working covertly with the rebels and <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>—coordinated an escape plan to secure Katniss from the arena. When Katniss destroyed the force field enclosing the arena, the Quell collapsed mid-contest without a victor. Several tributes, including Katniss, were extracted by the rebel forces led by District 13, while those left behind fell into Capitol custody.
</p><p>The 75th Hunger Games thus became the first and only Games to conclude without the crowning of a victor. Its abrupt end as the last formal Hunger Games held marked not only the collapse of the Games as an institution but also the beginning of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Abolition">Abolition</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the endgame of the Second Rebellion, the Capitol was <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">invaded</a> by rebel forces and President Coriolanus Snow's regime was subsequently overthrown. During the formation of an interim government under District 13 president <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>, the surviving victors were asked to vote on a proposal for a symbolic and final <a href="/wiki/76th_Hunger_Games" title="76th Hunger Games">76th Hunger Games</a>, in which the children of prominent Capitol officials would serve as tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The measure was narrowly approved by the victors assembled, reflecting both the deep trauma left by the Games and Coin's desire to perpetuate their logic of retribution while solidifying her own power.
</p><p>While approved, the plan was never realized. During Snow's public execution, Katniss Everdeen instead assassinated Coin, with Snow himself dying shortly thereafter. With their deaths, the Hunger Games as an institution definitively collapsed.
</p><p>Following an emergency election, <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Commander Paylor</a> of <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> was chosen as president of the new democratic Panem.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Under her leadership, the Hunger Games were formally abolished, the Treaty of Treason repealed, and the former arenas demolished. In their place, memorials were erected to honor the tributes who had perished across seventy-five years of sadistic bloodshed, marking the end of the Capitol's most infamous instrument of control.<sup id="cite_ref-:11_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:11-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Events">Events</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Opening_Ceremonies">Opening Ceremonies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Opening Ceremonies lasted one week, starting with the reaping on July 4th and ending with a night of interviews in the Capitol on July 10th.
</p>
<h4><span id="Reaping_ceremony_.28July_4th.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Reaping_ceremony_(July_4th)">Reaping ceremony (July 4th)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">The Reaping</a></i></center></div>

class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Effie-Reaping-Bowl-The-Hunger-Games.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p <p>Every year on July 4th, the entire population of each district was required to assemble at their respective <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a> for the reaping. Only those who were deathly ill were excused, and if a citizen was found lying, they would be imprisoned. District children between the age of 12 and 18 were separated into pens according to age, under Peacekeeper supervision, while adults were herded to surrounding areas. Two large <a href="/wiki/Reaping_balls" title="Reaping balls">glass bowls</a> were placed at the front of the assembly gathering—one containing slips with the names of all eligible boys, the other with those of all eligible girls.
</p><p>The number of entries in the reaping bowls was cumulative with age. A twelve-year-old child had a single slip; a thirteen-year-old, two slips, continuing until eighteen, when a child's name appeared seven times.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In addition, children could volunteer for <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">tesserae</a>—a system of ration incentives that allowed them to exchange additional entries for grain and oil sufficient to feed one family member for a year. The tesserae system was cumulative across years and for the number of family members it was taken out for, meaning repeated claims rapidly multiplied a child's risk of being reaped. By the time of the 74th Hunger Games, Katniss Everdeen had twenty slips in the bowl due to tesserae claimed for herself and family at sixteen, while <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> carried forty-two entries at eighteen.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The district's Capitol <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a> presided over the ceremony, drawing one slip from each bowl following the mayor's recitation of the Treaty of Treason. The names called became that year's tributes for the district unless another eligible child <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> in their place. In most districts, volunteering was rare, as participation in the Games was regarded as a near-certain death sentence. Exceptions were found the so-called <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> districts—Districts <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a> (1 and 2 in the films)—where children trained extensively in combat and survival with the expectation of volunteering. In these districts, the Games were framed as a path to glory, and tributes often competed for the honor of taking the stage.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Children became ineligible for the reaping once they passed their eighteenth year. Similarly, victors of previous Games were exempt from all future reapings, even if they remained within the eligible reaping age range, though many later served as mentors to new tributes.
</p>
<h4><span id="Journey_to_the_Capitol_.28July_4th-5th.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Journey_to_the_Capitol_(July_4th-5th)">Journey to the Capitol (July 4th-5th)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>After the reaping, tributes were escorted into the Justice Building for a brief farewell period. Each tribute was allotted one hour to say goodbye to family and friends in private rooms under Peacekeeper guard. At the conclusion of this period, they were escorted to waiting Capitol vehicles and transported directly to the district <a href="/wiki/Train_station" title="Train station">train station</a>, where they boarded a luxury train bound for the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The length of the journey depended on the district's distance from the Capitol. For District 12, considered the most remote and easternmost district, the trip lasted a full day, while tributes from districts closer to the Capitol, such as District 1 and 2, endured much shorter rides.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The journey marked the tributes' first exposure to Capitol excess. Each train was outfitted with extravagant dining halls, private compartments, opulent furniture, and amenities typically unavailable to district citizens. Tributes were served lavish buffets and meals—a pointed contrast to the hunger and scarcity experienced in Panem's poorest districts—and given fully equipped quarters with hot baths and full wardrobes. Capitol staff, including <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avoxes</a>, were present to serve the tributes and attend to their every need or desire, further underscoring the stark divide between Capitol luxury and district deprivation.
</p><p>During the ride, tributes typically met their assigned <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>, a victor from their district who would advise them throughout their journey into the Games. Mentors introduced tributes to the politics of survival in the Capitol and in the arena, including the importance of sponsors. They also brainstormed their tributes' strategy for the Games and how to best market them. It was customary for tributes to watch the reaping recap broadcasts, studying alongside their mentors the strengths and weaknesses of their competition. In <i>The Hunger Games</i>, Katniss recalls these replays as her first glimpse into the personalities.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span id="Tribute_Parade_.28July_5th.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade_(July_5th)">Tribute Parade (July 5th)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 274px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Capitol_Chariot_Ride.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720220502" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Tribute Parade along the Avenue of the Tributes for the 75th Hunger Games" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="274" height="117" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Capitol Chariot Ride.png" data-image-key="Capitol_Chariot_Ride.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Capitol_Chariot_Ride.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/274?cb=20130720220502" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Capitol_Chariot_Ride.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720220502" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Tribute Parade along the Avenue of the Tributes for the 75th Hunger Games" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Capitol_Chariot_Ride.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/274?cb=20130720220502" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="274" height="117" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Capitol Chariot Ride.png" data-image-key="Capitol_Chariot_Ride.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/15/Capitol_Chariot_Ride.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/274?cb=20130720220502" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Capitol_Chariot_Ride.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Tribute Parade along the Avenue of the Tributes for the 75th Hunger Games</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Chariot rides</a></i></center></div>
<p>Upon arrival in the Capitol in the early hours of July 5th, tributes were transported to the <a href="/wiki/Remake_Center" title="Remake Center">Remake Center</a>, where they were introduced to their assigned <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a>. Over the following hours, these teams undertook the initial work of grooming and beautifying their tributes to Capitol standards before introducing them to their <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>, who directed the overall presentation. The stylist, as the head of the prep team, determined the tribute's public image and designed the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">costumes</a> the tribute was to wear during the opening ceremonies. These costumes were typically extravagant and eccentric outfits symbolically linked to the tribute's district industry. To this end, stylists often used this occasion to create memorable and provocative impressions, to the betterment or worsening of a tribute's chances of attracting sponsors - the former being successfully demonstrated when Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark's costumes involved them being engulfed in synthetic fire, leading Katniss to earn one of her famous monikers amongst the Capitol: the "Girl on Fire."<sup id="cite_ref-:5_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the evening, tributes were assembled with their district partner and mounted on horse-drawn chariots for the ceremonial parade. Beginning with District 1, the procession advanced down the <a href="/wiki/Avenue_of_the_Tributes" title="Avenue of the Tributes">Avenue of the Tributes</a> and around the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a>, where over a hundred thousand Capitol citizens gathered to watch. For many in the Capitol, the parade was the first opportunity to see the tributes in person, making it a pivotal moment for tributes in establishing audience interest and attracting sponsors. The parade concluded before the <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">presidential mansion</a>, where the President delivered an opening address directed to the tributes, officially commencing the Games and congratulating the tributes on their "courage." Following this, the chariots proceeded to the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:5_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> where tributes were escorted to their respective district apartments by their mentors and began formal preparations for the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span id="Training_.28July_6th-8th.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Training_(July_6th-8th)">Training (July 6th-8th)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Training_stations" title="Training stations">Training stations</a></i></center></div>

<p>Tributes began formal survival and combat training the day after their arrival in the Capitol, spanning three days from July 6th through July 8th. Training took place in a vast underground gymnasium located beneath the Training Center. The program formally commenced each morning at 10:00 am, beginning with an orientation led by the Head Trainer, who explained the rules of conduct, layout of the facility, and practical advice on what survival stations the tributes should focus on.
</p><p>The gymnasium contained numerous specialized stations, each staffed by Capitol experts, covering both survival skills and combat techniques. Stations included fire building, knot tying, plant identification, camouflage, swordplay, archery, and hand-to-hand combat, among others. Barring four compulsory exercises all tributes had to participate in, tributes were free to circulate among the stations individually at will, spending as much or as little time as they wished at each. Lunch was served in a cafeteria within the gymnasium, whereas dinner was taken after the conclusion of training for the day in the district apartments.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Although tributes could socialize and even train alongside each other during their time in the gymnasium, direct combat practice between tributes was forbidden. Instead, training became an opportunity for tributes to observe their rivals, size up potential threats, and begin forming <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliances</a> with one another. This was particularly common amongst Career tributes, who often trained together and formalized their alliance during this time, although it was not unheard of for non-Career tributes to do the same.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Throughout the sessions, tributes were observed by the Gamemakers from an elevated balcony overlooking the entire gymnasium. On the afternoon of the third day of training following lunch, each tribute was required to participate in a private session, demonstrating their learned or pre-existing abilities one at a time before the Gamemakers. Tributes were called in district order from the cafeteria, alternating by gender. Performances varied from practical demonstrations of survival skills to combat displays, with some tributes using the opportunity to attempt dramatic gestures to capture attention - as Katniss Everdeen did when she shot an arrow through the apple in the Gamemakers' roasted pig during her evaluation.
</p><p>That evening, <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training scores</a> were broadcast by the Games' Master of Ceremonies on Capitol television. Scores could range from 1 to 12, with higher numbers indicating greater skills and/or lethality in the eyes of the Gamemakers. These rating directly influenced public perception on and betting odds for the tributes, as well as helped determine how easily tributes could attract sponsor support. This made the private sessions a pivotal step in the pre-Games process of forging the tributes' Games persona.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span id="Interviews_.28July_9th-10th.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews_(July_9th-10th)">Interviews (July 9th-10th)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">Interviews</a></i></center></div>

<p>The final event before the commencement of the Hunger Games was the tribute interviews, held on the seventh day (July 10th) of the pre-Games schedule. On July 9th, tributes spend the entire day preparing with their mentors and escort. Mentors coach tributes on the best persona or angle to project - whether charm, humor, vulnerability, or bravado - while escorts drilled tributes in Capitol etiquette and public presentation.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On July 10th, tributes continued to prepare with their mentors and escorts before reconvening with their prep team and stylists later in the day. Tributes are provided another full makeover and dressed in an extravagant formal attire. In the evening, the tributes are then brought before a live Capitol audience assembled in the City Circle (indoor auditorium in the film), with the stage set at the base of the Tribute Center. Tributes were presented in district order, alternating male and female, by the Hunger Games' Master of Ceremonies, who hosted the interview. For the final thirty-five years of the Games, the interviews were hosted by <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, renowned for his charisma and ability to put tributes at ease.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Each interview lasted approximately three minutes, during which the Master of Ceremonies asked questions about the tribute's background, family, impressions of the Capitol, training score, or outlook  for the Games, among others. Though brief, the interviews were highly consequential: they offered the tributes a final opportunity to directly appeal to sponsors, whose support could later proved critical to survival in the arena. For Capitol citizens, the event served as an entertainment highlight that allowed them to get to know the tributes on a personal level and choose their favorites among the competition.<sup id="cite_ref-:7_18-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span id="The_Games_.28July_11th.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games_(July_11th)">The Games (July 11th)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Pre-Launch">Pre-Launch</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Following the interviews, tributes returned to their district apartments in the Training Center for the final night before the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the early hours of July 11th, each tribute was escorted by their stylist (mentor in the film) to a waiting Capitol hovercraft. Before departure, Capitol technicians implanted a <a href="/wiki/Tracker" title="Tracker">tracking device</a> into each tribute's forearm. These devices transmitted the tribute's vital signs and real-time location, allowing Gamemakers to monitor the participants throughout the course of the Hunger Games. Once aboard, the hovercraft carried the tributes to the arena. Travel time to the arena varied depending on the arena's location in relation to the Capitol; the flight to the 74th Hunger Games arena lasted approximately 30 minutes.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Upon arrival, tributes were escorted underground into the <a href="/wiki/Catacombs" title="Catacombs">catacombs</a> of the arena complex, where they entered their assigned <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">Launch Rooms</a>. In the Launch Room, tributes underwent final preparations with their stylist, including dressing in the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena uniforms</a> and receiving their permitted <a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">tribute token</a>, a personal keepsake authorized by the Capitol. When final call was announced, each tribute stepped into a <a href="/wiki/Tribute_tube" title="Tribute tube">tribute tube</a>, which sealed them inside and raised them into the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_19-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>The ascent culminated in the final countdown to the start of the Games. Tributes emerged in the arena on pedestals surrounding the Cornucopia, where <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> announced the start of that year's Hunger Games before announcing a 60-second countdown, displayed as a holographic counter above the Cornucopia. Prior to launch, tributes were advised to not step off of their platform until the countdown concluded, as doing so would trigger concealed mines beneath each pedestal and kill the offender instantly. When the countdown completed, the Hunger Games officially commenced.<sup id="cite_ref-:9_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Cornucopia_bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a></i></center></div>

<p>Each Hunger Games begins at 9:00 am on July 11th.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The opening act of any Hunger Games was known colloquially as the Cornucopia bloodbath, a violent melee that typically claimed the lives of a large number of tributes within the first few minutes of the Games. At start, tributes were launched into the arena in a circular or semicircular pattern equidistant from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, a giant horn-shaped structure filled with supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-:9_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Cornucopia and its surrounding area typically contained a wide assortment of equipment, most notably a variety of melee weapons, as well as (but not always) backpacks, food, water, tools, and other survival gear. These resources were deliberately placed out in the open to entice tributes into direct conflict. The setup at Games start created an immediate high risk-high reward dilemma: rushing the Cornucopia offered a chance at powerful advantages but came with the near certainty of confrontation with other tributes, while fleeing into the arena substantially reduced the risk of an early death but often at the cost of leaving the tribute poorly equipped and/or armed.<sup id="cite_ref-:9_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The resulting bloodbath was the single deadliest phase of most Games. In the 74th Hunger Games, 11 of the 24 tributes were killed within minutes of launch - nearly half of the field. This pattern was consistent likely across decades of Games, with the Career tributes typically dominating the opening battle. Trained since childhood and often entering the Games as part of a coordinated alliance, Careers usually seized control of the Cornucopia and its stockpile of resources. This gave them a decisive material advantage over tributes who had fled empty-handed or with only minimal supplies.<sup id="cite_ref-:9_20-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Beyond its tactical role, the Cornucopia served as the Hunger Games' most theatrical spectacle. The orchestrated chaos of children rushing to their deaths at the opening horn provided both shock value and instant trauma, setting the tone for the rest of the Games and ensuring it began with maximum impact for the audience.
</p>

<p>At the end of each day in the arena, the <a href="/wiki/Horn_of_Plenty" title="Horn of Plenty">national anthem</a> was played, followed by a display of all tributes that had died that day. The fallen were shown one by one in district order, accompanied by their official Games portrait and district number. This ritual served as a practical mechanism of informing both the audience and the surviving tributes in the arena of the day's casualties and the number of competitors remaining.<sup id="cite_ref-:9_20-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>For Capitol and district audiences watching the broadcast, the nightly death roll was accompanied by footage of each tribute's death, reinforcing the spectacle and trivialization of the death of children in the Games. Inside the arena, however, tributes only saw the portraits of the deceased. This omission preserved a degree of uncertainty, forcing tributes to piece together circumstances of each death from what they had witnessed themselves or could infer from the environment.<sup id="cite_ref-:9_20-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The method of display differed between media: in the novels, the names and faces of the fallen appeared on massive screens carried by cloaked hovercraft that traversed the skies of the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-:9_20-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the film adaptations, the images were projected onto the sky dome that enclosed the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Feasts">Feasts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D19&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 220px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3b/Cornucopia_feast.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419082829" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Cornucopia feast, with four backpacks in front on a table." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="158" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Cornucopia feast.jpg" data-image-key="Cornucopia_feast.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3b/Cornucopia_feast.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20120419082829" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3b/Cornucopia_feast.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419082829" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The Cornucopia feast, with four backpacks in front on a table." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3b/Cornucopia_feast.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20120419082829" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="158" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Cornucopia feast.jpg" data-image-key="Cornucopia_feast.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/3b/Cornucopia_feast.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20120419082829" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Cornucopia_feast.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The Cornucopia feast, with four backpacks in front on a table.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Feasts" class="mw-redirect" title="Feasts">Feasts</a></i></center></div>
<p>When the Gamemakers judged that the Games were progressing too slowly, or when they wished to increase tension amongst the remaining tributes, they staged an event known as a feast at the Cornucopia. The purpose of a feast was identical to that of the bloodbath: to draw tributes into direct conflict by offering resources they could not ignore.
</p><p>The contents of a feast varied widely. In some instances, the Gamemakers provided trivial or meager supplies, with Katniss noting in the novels that the feast of a previous Hunger Games involved tributes fighting over a single loaf of stale bread. In other cases, Gamemakers provided essential and even life-saving resources or survival gear.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the 74th Hunger Games, for example, each of the surviving tributes at that point of the Games were supplied with marked bags with their district number, each containing an item that the Gamemakers knew the tributes would urgently require or find very useful. For Katniss, this involved medicine needed to save Peeta's life from blood poisoning, forcing her to risk the encounter. For Cato, his bag contained a high-tech mesh armor that was able to withstand several hours of being mauled by mutts before failing.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Participation in the feast was not mandatory, and tributes could choose to avoid the risk altogether. However, the Gamemakers often timed and designed the rewards to be irresistible, ensuring that at least some tributes would attend. In this manner, the feast functioned as a deliberate mechanism to accelerate bloodshed, maintain Capitol audience engagement, and remind the tributes that their survival was subject to the Capitol's control. 
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Closing_Ceremonies">Closing Ceremonies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D20&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Remake">Remake</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D21&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Once a tribute was declared a victor, they were immediately transported back to the Capitol for urgent medical treatment. Critical injuries sustained in the arena were stabilized mid-flight, ensuring that the new victor survived long enough to receive proper medical treatment and be presentable during the Victory Ceremony.
</p><p>Upon arrival at the Training Center, the victor was taken to a specialized medical facility in the sublevels of the building, beneath even the training gymnasium. There, Capitol physicians and medical technicians conducted comprehensive physical restoration. Procedures involved advanced surgeries to rapidly heal wounds, the fitting of prosthetics for limbs lost or medically-necessitated amputations<sup id="cite_ref-:13_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:13-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, and the use of cosmetic treatments such as a "full-body polish" capable of erasing scars sustained even before the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:12_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:12-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>These medical interventions were not always voluntary. Victors could be subjected to cosmetic alterations without their consent, and were typically conducted whilst the victor was heavily sedated during surgery. This reflected the Capitol's insistence on remaking the victor into marketable symbols of survival and propaganda. This process underscored the continuing lack of autonomy even for victors who ostensibly "won" their freedom, as their very bodies became property of the Capitol to be displayed and consumed as spectacle, even in the tribute's new life as a victor.<sup id="cite_ref-:12_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:12-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Victory_Ceremony_and_Crowning">Victory Ceremony and Crowning</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D22&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Victor_crowning" class="mw-redirect" title="Victor crowning">Victor crowning</a></i></center></div>
<p>Once a victor had recovered sufficiently from their injuries, they were reunited with their mentor, escort, and stylist. Following pleasantries, their prep team performed a final grooming, and their stylist outfitted their victor with formal attire for the Victor's first public appearances following their Games.
</p><p>In the evening, the victor and their entourage ascended onto a stage above the Training Center gymnasium in a ceremonial order - prep team first, followed by the stylist, mentor, and finally the new victor.<sup id="cite_ref-:12_26-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:12-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A three-hour recap hosted by the Master of Ceremonies was then broadcasted, focusing exclusively on the victor's journey from the opening ceremonies to their final triumph in the arena. This recap forced victors to relive their fresh and raw trauma for public consumption while providing Capitol audiences with a curative narrative of "skill, courage, and inevitability."<sup id="cite_ref-:13_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:13-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 225px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120418193200" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss is crowned victor of the 74th Hunger Games, shortly followed by Peeta." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="225" height="171" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss snow crown.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_snow_crown.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/225?cb=20120418193200" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120418193200" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss is crowned victor of the 74th Hunger Games, shortly followed by Peeta." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/225?cb=20120418193200" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="225" height="171" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss snow crown.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_snow_crown.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a2/Katniss_snow_crown.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/225?cb=20120418193200" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_snow_crown.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss is crowned victor of the 74th Hunger Games, shortly followed by Peeta.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>At the conclusion of the recap, the President of Panem personally crowned the victor with the official victory crown, a gold-stylized laurel. Immediately afterwards, the victor and their entourage attended the Victory Banquet, a lavish party hosted at the presidential mansion in the victor's honor. There the victor dined and mingled with Capitol elites, talked and posed for photographs with sponsors, and was gaudily showcased as the Capitol's newest celebrity well into the morning hours.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Final_Interview_and_Return_Home">Final Interview and Return Home</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D23&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 225px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1d/Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120408142701" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta and Katniss at their post-Games interview and recap." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="225" height="140" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1d/Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/225?cb=20120408142701" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1d/Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120408142701" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Peeta and Katniss at their post-Games interview and recap." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1d/Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/225?cb=20120408142701" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="225" height="140" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1d/Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/225?cb=20120408142701" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss-peeta-capitol-interview.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Peeta and Katniss at their post-Games interview and recap.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Following the victory ceremony, the victor returned to the Training Center to rest for a few hours before being prepared and dressed by their  stylist and prep team for their final interview, hosted by the Games' Master of Ceremonies in the evening. These interviews were significantly longer than the pre-Games interviews and focused on the victor's actions, decisions, and experiences within the arena. Afterwards, the victor returned briefly to their Training Center apartment to gather whatever possessions they brought with them to the Capitol before departing with their mentor on the train back to their district.
</p><p>Upon arrival in their district, the victor was greeted with celebrations and accolades by the district's populace for the victory in the Games and ability to return home. There, the victors would remain for several months until the commencement of their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-:13_25-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:13-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which occurred "almost midway" between their Games and the next reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-:14_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_aspects">Other aspects</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D24&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Arena"><a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">Arena</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D25&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Arenas were controlled and carefully engineered environments designed by the Capitol to host the annual Hunger Games. Over the seventy-five years of Hunger Games (barring the first ten), arenas were designed to represent a wide variety of environments, from temperate forest and ruined cities to deserts and frozen wastelands. After each Games, arenas were preserved as museums and tourist attractions, where Capitol visitors could rewatch the Games, revisit the sites of tribute deaths, tour the catacombs, and even participate in reenactments.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_19-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Following the abolition of the Hunger Games after the Second Rebellion, all arenas were demolished and replaced with memorials to the fallen tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-:11_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:11-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mentoring"><a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">Mentoring</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D26&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Mentors were tasked with the unique responsibility of guiding the annual slate of tributes through the Hunger Games, offering strategic advice for how to perform in the Games and managing their relationship with potential sponsors. The system began with the 10th Hunger Games, when the role was filled by twenty-four top performing senior students at the Capitol's <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In later years, the position was taken over by previous victors, binding them to the Games as recurring participants and forcing them to prepare each year's tributes for survival.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sponsorship"><a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">Sponsorship</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D27&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1c/Katniss_Opening_A_Sponsors_Gift.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131030214545" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A sponsor gift." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="115" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss Opening A Sponsors Gift.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_Opening_A_Sponsors_Gift.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1c/Katniss_Opening_A_Sponsors_Gift.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131030214545" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1c/Katniss_Opening_A_Sponsors_Gift.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20131030214545" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A sponsor gift." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1c/Katniss_Opening_A_Sponsors_Gift.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131030214545" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="115" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss Opening A Sponsors Gift.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_Opening_A_Sponsors_Gift.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1c/Katniss_Opening_A_Sponsors_Gift.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131030214545" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_Opening_A_Sponsors_Gift.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A sponsor gift.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Sponsorships provided tributes with vital resources during the Hunger Games, including food, water, medicine, or occasionally weapons. Gifts were purchased by sponsors - typically Capitol citizens, though occasionally district supporters - and delivered into the arena via <a href="/wiki/Silver_parachute" title="Silver parachute">silver parachutes</a>. The system was first introduced in the 10th Hunger Games, when mentor-ordered gifts were delivered by crude drones.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Sponsorships were heavily influenced by a tribute's performance in pre-Games events, particularly their training score, interview, and overall public persona in the lead up to the Hunger Games. Because gifts were expensive, particularly during the late phases of the Games, contributions had be pooled from multiple sponsors to afford even a single item. As a result, tributes, mentors, and even their stylists all played crucial roles in cultivating sponsor interest, making sponsorships a key determinant of survival in the arena and even the tribute's potential victory.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Muttations"><a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">Muttations</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D28&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Muttations (commonly called mutts) were genetically engineered creatures created by the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>. Originally designed as biological weapons of war or instruments of control during the Dark Days, muttations were repurposed for use in the Hunger Games beginning with the 10th Games. In subsequent editions, muttations were commonly deployed as an additional hazard tributes had to contend with along with the inherent dangers already in place in the arena. Mutts were also commonly used to manipulate events as well as drive tributes into confrontations with one another.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_tokens"><a href="/wiki/Tribute_token" title="Tribute token">Tribute tokens</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D29&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Each tribute was permitted to carry a token from their district into the Games, serving as a personal reminder of home.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> All tokens required approval from a Capitol review board to ensure they could not be used as weapons or provide an unfair advantage. <sup id="cite_ref-:8_19-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Chariot_costumes"><a href="/wiki/Chariot_costumes" title="Chariot costumes">Chariot costumes</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D30&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>For the tribute parade, each pair of tributes was dressed in elaborate matching outfits designed by their stylist. These outfits typically reflected the industry or identity of the tribute's district - for example, jewels for District 1 or coal-themed attire for District 12. The parade was the first major opportunity for tributes to capture Capitol attention, and striking costumes could help secure sponsor interest.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Arena_wear"><a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">Arena wear</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D31&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Tributes were issued standardized arena clothing each year, designed to match the specific environment and conditions of the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:9_20-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Outfits were functional in nature - such as jackets for cold arenas or jumpsuits for tropical environments - but were also chosen by the Capitol to reinforce spectacle and uniformity. While each district's slate of tributes wore the same uniform, they were distinguished by different colors to represent the 12 districts in each rendition of the Hunger Games.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Quarter_Quell"><a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D32&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Every twenty-five years, the Hunger Games were marked by the Quarter Quell, a special edition of the Games that featured unique rules and conditions. These twists were alleged to have been written into the original Treaty of Treason and sealed in envelopes to be opened by the President of Panem at the appointed time. When announced, the President selected the card, outlining the change in the rules of the reaping for that year's Quell. The Quarter Quell's purpose was to remind the districts of the consequence of their defiance in the Dark Days through each Quell's unique twist and keep fresh the concept of the Hunger Games for each generation.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHunger_Games_%28event%29%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D34&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In an early draft of <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a> script, the events of the series are said to take place 300 years into the future.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Considering that the film was released in 2012,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> this would mean that the trilogy begins around 2312. However, since none of this material was included in the final product, this is only speculatory.</li>
<li>Across the seventy-five years of Hunger Games, a total of <b>1,826</b> tributes were entered into the competition. This figure accounts for the doubled pool of tributes in the 50th Hunger Games (Second Quarter Quell), one confirmed body-double replacement, and one known tribute death immediately following a reaping. In terms of unique participants, however, only <b>1,802</b> individuals ever competed, as the 75th Hunger Games (Third Quarter Quell) was limited to previous victors, effectively realigning the excess created in the Second Quarter Quell. Of these 1,802 tributes: <b>1,727</b> died in the Games they were originally reaped for, with an additional <b>18</b> victors killed when forced back into the arena for the 75th Games. This gives the total deaths directly caused by the Hunger Games at <b>1,745</b>.</li>
<li>On the eve of the 75th Hunger Games, there were <b>59 living victors</b> out of the <b>75</b> <b>in total</b>, the discrepancy resulting from the dual victory in the 74th Games and with <b>16</b> victors beforehand having died of natural causes. By the conclusion of the Second Rebellion, further losses among the 75 victors included the 18 that died during the 75th Hunger Games and a further <b>34</b> deaths during the Capitol's <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a> or in combat during the rebellion itself. This brought the total number of dead tributes to <b>1,795</b> out 1,802, leaving only <b>seven surviving victors</b> as the only long-term survivors of the Hunger Games out of all 1,802 individuals ever reaped.</li>
<li>The Hunger Games were inspired by the myth of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theseus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Theseus">Theseus</a> and the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/sacrificial_victims_of_Minotaur" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:sacrificial victims of Minotaur">sacrificial victims of Minotaur</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> as well as Roman <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gladiator" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Gladiator">gladiatorial combat</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
