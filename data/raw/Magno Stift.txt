<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMagno_Stift%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Magno had been the District 12 stylist for several years by the time of the Second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, at least 10 years, since Magno was 12's stylist for as long as <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> could remember. He promoted live-reptile fashion, and for the <a href="/wiki/49th_Hunger_Games" title="49th Hunger Games">49th Hunger Games</a>, a turtle he was using as a belt buckle fell off and bit <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>, who then bit Magno back.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He sent the tributes to their <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> in their <a href="/wiki/Training_outfits" title="Training outfits">training outfits</a> and was placed on probation because of it.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_7-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMagno_Stift%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Magno arrived late to prepare his <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>. Although he had promised new outfits for the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, he dressed the four District 12 tributes—<a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>—in the same <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miner</a> outfits he had used for years.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Proserpina_Trinket" title="Proserpina Trinket">Proserpina</a> and <a href="/wiki/Vitus" title="Vitus">Vitus</a> arrived at Magno's apartment to plan the makeup and hair for the District 12 tributes' <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, they found him sick and inebriated from licking toads for their hallucinogenic venom.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Before the interviews, Magno arrived late carrying a snake, saying he needed to dress his tributes (who had already been dressed with clothes from <a href="/wiki/Messalina_Trinket" title="Messalina Trinket">Messalina</a> and <a href="/wiki/Silius_Trinket" title="Silius Trinket">Silius Trinket</a>). When <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">Lou Lou</a> saw the snake, she said "mine," and Magno instructed her on how to hold it. She wore the snake during her interview.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before Haymitch entered the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, Effie had to meet him in the <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">launch room</a> because nobody was able to find Magno.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As Haymitch set off on his <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> informed him that Magno had been fired for negligence.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMagno_Stift%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Magno had sun-leathered skin tattooed with a snakeskin pattern. He was often seen wearing live reptiles, including as a turtle belt buckle and garter snake earrings. For the chariot rides before the 50th Hunger Games, he wore a long shirt made of metal diamonds, sandals that laced all the way up his legs, and no pants.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_5_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He was older than 60 years, since he had been licking toads since the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">war</a>, which ended 50 years prior.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMagno_Stift%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Despite being the stylist for the tributes of District 12, Magno does not take his job seriously and puts no effort in helping the tributes gain sponsors through the tribute parade and their television interviews. Like his wife Drusilla, Magno does not treat the tributes like human beings, not caring about their lives and making them more presentable to the Capitol.
</p>
