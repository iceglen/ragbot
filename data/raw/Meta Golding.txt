<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMeta_Golding%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Golding was raised in a few different countries. She lived in France, Italy, Haiti, and India. She speaks English, Italian, and French fluently. Golding was once an Italian national figure skater, but a career ending injury made her resort to acting. She is known for roles in television shows such as <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:Crime_Scene_Investigation" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:CSI:Crime Scene Investigation">CSI:Crime Scene Investigation</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:House (TV series)">House</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_Minds" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Criminal Minds">Criminal Minds</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_Case_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cold Case (TV series)">Cold Case</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_of_Proof" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Body of Proof">Body of Proof</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burn_Notice" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Burn Notice">Burn Notice</a></i>, and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_to_Me" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lie to Me">Lie to Me</a></i>. She also appeared in the film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chicago_8" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Chicago 8">The Chicago 8</a></i>, and is known for her regular role in ABC's <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_Break" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Day Break">Day Break</a></i>.
</p>
