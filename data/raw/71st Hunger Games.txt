</p><p>The <b>71st Hunger Games</b> were held three years prior to the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. They were won by <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>, the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Johanna initially presented herself as a "sniveling, cowardly fool",<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> pretending to be weak so that she would be ignored in favor of more threatening tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, when there were only a handful of tributes remaining, "it turned out that she could kill viciously"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>— most likely with an <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Axe" title="Weapons">axe</a>, as she was adept with them in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F71st_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>They can't hurt me. I'm not like the rest of you. There's no one left I love.</i>"</span></dd>

<p>By the time of the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, Johanna had lost any friends or family that she cared for,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_24_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_24-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> presumably killed by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F71st_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Johanna presumably got a low <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> on purpose to keep up her guise of weakness.</li>
<li>Since <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>'s family was killed after <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">his Games</a>, and he mentioned Johanna's name when he spoke about being held up as an example to younger victors,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> it is commonly theorized that Johanna refused to be sex trafficked as <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> had been, so her loved ones were killed in retaliation.</li>
<li>The German translation of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i> says that Johanna won seven years before the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>. This would mean that at least in the German version of the text, she won the <a href="/wiki/67th_Hunger_Games" title="67th Hunger Games">67th Hunger Games</a> rather than the 71st.
<ul><li>In order for her to still be 21 during <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i> per <a href="/wiki/Suzanne_Collins" title="Suzanne Collins">Suzanne Collins</a>' <i>Time</i> interview,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> she would have had to win the 67th Hunger Games at only 13 years old.
<ul><li>Though, this would not be possible, as it was stated Finnick was the youngest victor, winning at the age of 14.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ul>
