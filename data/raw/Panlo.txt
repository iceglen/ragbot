<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Panlo grew up in District 9 and became his district's male tribute in <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">the Reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with the other tributes, Panlo was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the start of the Games, the tributes and their mentors were taken on a tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, heavily injuring the District 9 duo and their mentors. He was denied hospital care, in spite of advice given by the veterinarian. Unlike <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a> and <a href="/wiki/Ginnee" title="Ginnee">Ginnee</a>, the two tributes from District 6, Panlo managed to survive a&#160;little longer, but eventually succumbed to his wounds.
</p><p>His body was later draped on the back of a horse and paraded down <a href="/wiki/Scholars_Road" title="Scholars Road">Scholars Road</a> during <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana Ring</a>'s funeral.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>, he survived the bombing, but was the first tribute killed in the arena's <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. When the gong rang, Panlo ran towards the pile of weapons, pushing <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> along the way and grabbing a sword, with which he attempted to kill another tribute. After several seconds passed, <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a> fought him, and <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>, who was trying to kill <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, speared Panlo in the chest with her trident. Panlo placed 19th.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Panlo's name comes from "pānis", the Latin word for bread. This is the same word that <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> is derived from.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a>,</i> Panlo's odds of winning were 12–1 and he received 346 donations.</li></ul>
