<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Known_district_partners">Known district partners</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_partner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>District partners were more likely to form <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliances</a> with one another rather than with someone outside their district. However, being from the same district did not necessitate or guarantee such an arrangement. On this page, bolded pairs represent district partner alliances.
</p>

<p>On the tenth day of the 74th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> announced a rule change that would allow a pair of district partners to win together if they were the last two people standing.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At the time of this announcement, the only surviving pairs were from Districts 2 and 12.
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> later tried to retract this new rule at the final two, Katniss and Peeta threatened suicide by deadly <a href="/wiki/Nightlock" title="Nightlock">nightlock</a> berries. Faced with the possibility of having no <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> at all, the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a> allowed them both to live.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
