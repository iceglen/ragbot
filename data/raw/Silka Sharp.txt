She was a part of the Career <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>, and she placed 2nd out of 48 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Silka was part of the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> pack in these Games.
</p><p>During the 50th Hunger Games, the Second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> demanded that twice the number of tributes be <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for the event.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This year, Silka Sharp was one of two girls representing District 1, alongside <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Carat" title="Carat">Carat</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Loupe" title="Loupe">Loupe</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_6_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Chariot_rides">Chariot rides</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Silka had gotten along badly with <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> in the locker room before the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>, leading Maysilee to insult her outfit in front of the other tributes. Silka made to attack her, but was stopped by a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_5_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_5-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a> was killed in the chaos of the chariot parade, Silka dismounted her own chariot. When the horses refused to keep moving despite Panache's insistence, Silka removed one of her shoes and beat the outside horse until she drew blood, then was knocked to the ground by the horse's kick.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_6_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Silka was the first tribute to be interviewed, wearing a long green dress. She bragged to <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> about her size and strength, her ability with an <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Axe" title="Weapons">axe</a>, and her receiving a <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> of ten.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR_13_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR_13-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Arena">Arena</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Silka's podium was directly to the right of Haymitch's podium.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Silka was part of a pack of sixteen Career tributes. This alliance was likely responsible for most of the eighteen <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a> deaths. They claimed supplies from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, including the only safe <a href="/wiki/Food_and_drink" title="Food and drink">food and drink</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, and for the first three days, they scoured the mountain area for potential victims. During this time, a number of tributes died to the arena's many poisonous substances. On the fourth day, a volcanic eruption killed another dozen tributes, including half the Careers. At this point, Silka was one of thirteen total tributes left alive. The lava flow forced them into the forest, possibly separating her from her four allies. She managed to survive despite the <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">mutts</a> and poisons.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the sixth day, three Gamemakers were mopping and taking care of the oozing slime on the forest floor of the arena. When <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, Maysilee Donner, Silka, and <a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a> came across them, the Gamemakers tried to flee to <a href="/wiki/Sub-A" class="mw-redirect" title="Sub-A">Sub-A</a>, but all were killed. One had a <a href="/wiki/Trident" class="mw-redirect" title="Trident">trident</a> thrown at him by Maritte, another was attacked by one of Maysilee's darts, and the third plummeted to her death after she leaped headfirst into Sub-A, cracking her head on the concrete. Silka was shocked when she saw Maysilee and Maritte's actions, and Maysilee criticized her for sucking up to the Capitol in a sad effort to win the Games. She then said she was going to kill Maritte, before a bomb was sent by a <a href="/wiki/Hovercraft" title="Hovercraft">hovercraft</a> and separated the tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While Haymitch was taking care of <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a> in a tree, Silka was sobbing at the foot of the tree. Haymitch threw Silka some chocolate that he had received from sponsors. She scampered away in the night, presumably ashamed that she had shown such emotion. Haymitch supposed District 1 didn't reward its tributes for showing humanity. He left Wellie armed with Maysilee's <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">dart gun</a>, loaded with a single poisoned dart. Haymitch returned after receiving a sponsor gift containing what he anticipated to be poisoned milk from <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">President Snow</a> (which he discarded), and an ornate picnic basket. He was met with the sight of a decapitated Wellie, whose body lay in a heap - her head in Silka's left hand, dripping blood onto the forest floor. Silka had decapitated her with her axe. When confronted by Haymitch, Silka claimed Wellie attacked her, which was when he noticed a poison blow dart hanging harmlessly from Silka's sleeve.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Silka made it to the final two, at which point she fought <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, one of the male tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, in hand-to-hand axe combat. Both sustained nearly fatal wounds in their battle; Haymitch gouged out her eye with his knife, and she disemboweled him with her <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Axe" title="Weapons">axe</a>. He had to hold his intestines in while he fled. She chased him to a cliff at the edge of the arena, cornering him, and threw her axe. But, just then Haymitch collapsed on the ground, narrowly dodging the attack. Due to the severity of his wounds, she thought she could outlast him even without her weapon, but Haymitch knew the axe would be flung back by the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> protecting the arena's generator. It was; the axe lodged itself in her head. She initially refused to die, emitting a "strangled gurgle,"<sup id="cite_ref-:0_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> but ultimately succumbed to her injuries, leaving Haymitch as the <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Silka is described as being "bigger than Haymitch and just as fast".<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She was over 6 feet tall.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Throughout the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot parade</a>, <a href="/wiki/Training" title="Training">training</a>, and the games themselves, she was primarily dressed in "snot-green," to represent her district.
</p><p>In the film she has long blonde hair and blue eyes. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality">Personality</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She is described to be very confident. Despite still having the basic arrogance and lack of remorse of the typical <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a>, she was devastated at the death of her final ally <a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>"Silka" is a feminisation of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silk" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Silk">silk</a>, a natural fiber and textile, and luxury product. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharp_(surname)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sharp (surname)">Sharp</a> is an English surname,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> which could relate to her ferocity in the Hunger Games. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSilka_Sharp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>She was the only known tribute outside of <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> who used an axe in the Games.</li></ul>
