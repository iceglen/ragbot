</p><p><b>"The Old Therebefore"</b> is the third single from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes_(Music_From_%26_Inspired_By)" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)</a></i>. The album version, "The Old Therebefore / Singing at Snakes", included dialogue from the scene in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">the film</a>. In response to popular fan demand, an <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/acapella" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:acapella">acapella</a> studio version of <a href="/wiki/Rachel_Zegler" title="Rachel Zegler">Rachel Zegler</a>'s performance was released as a single on February 9th, 2024.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<div class="poem">
<p>You're headed for heaven,<br />
The sweet old hereafter,<br />
And I've got one foot in the door.<br />
But before I can fly up,<br />
I've loose ends to tie up,<br />
Right here in<br />
The old therebefore.<br />
<br />
I'll be along<br />
When I've finished my song,<br />
When I've shut down the band,<br />
When I've played out my hand,<br />
When I've paid all my debts,<br />
When I have no regrets,<br />
Right here in<br />
The old therebefore,<br />
When nothing<br />
Is left anymore.<br />
<br />
I'll catch you up<br />
When I've emptied my cup,<br />
When I've worn out my friends,<br />
When I've burned out both ends,<br />
When I've cried all my tears,<br />
When I've conquered my fears,<br />
Right here in<br />
The old therebefore,<br />
When nothing<br />
Is left anymore.<br />
<br />
I'll bring the news<br />
When I've danced off my shoes,<br />
When my body's closed down,<br />
When my boat's run aground,<br />
When I've tallied the score,<br />
And I'm flat on the floor,<br />
Right here in<br />
The old therebefore,<br />
When nothing<br />
Is left anymore<br />
<br />
When I'm pure like a dove,<br />
When I've learned how to love,<br />
Right here in<br />
The old therebefore,<br />
When nothing<br />
Is left anymore.
</p>
