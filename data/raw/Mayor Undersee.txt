<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A fairly just mayor, Mayor Undersee attempted to improve the living conditions in District 12, a <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> that had many problems and affairs. He opened each the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> with his usual speech along with the district's <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>, <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> When <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a> in place of her sister, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a>, for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, the mayor told Effie to let her come forward.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>His attitude towards law enforcement was relatively lenient; public executions and whippings rarely occurred under his administration.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_15_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_15-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, it was apparent that the severity of law enforcement was also linked to whoever was in charge of the local <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeping garrison</a>; with the arrival of <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a>, laws were much more strictly enforced.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Undersee regularly bought strawberries from Katniss Everdeen and <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, even though their gathering of the fruit was technically illegal.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> As the mayor, he had access to information unavailable to most of Panem's residents, such as updates on the events in other districts, including the <a href="/wiki/Uprising_of_District_8" title="Uprising of District 8">Uprising of District 8</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss accidentally saw the news while preparing for the dinner hosted at his house, leaving the study shortly before Mayor Undersee appeared.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The mayor and his family died in the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombing of District 12</a> following the unexpected end of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">The Capitol</a> chose not to save Mayor Undersee, considering him of minimal importance.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMayor_Undersee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mayor Undersee lived with his <a href="/wiki/Mrs._Undersee" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs. Undersee">wife</a> and daughter, <a href="/wiki/Madge_Undersee" title="Madge Undersee">Madge Undersee</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> who died in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">Second Quarter Quell</a>, was his wife's twin and his sister-in-law.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
