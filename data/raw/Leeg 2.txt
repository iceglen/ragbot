<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Leeg 2 lived in District 13 with <a href="/wiki/Leeg_1" title="Leeg 1">her sister</a> and an unnamed father.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The sisters were both assigned to Squad 451, and they trained alongside their new compatriots for several days before deployment for the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a>. During this time, they were given the nicknames "Leeg 1" and "Leeg 2" to distinguish them. Once in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, they filmed <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a> for several days.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Leeg 2 hit a mislabeled <a href="/wiki/Pod" title="Pod">pod</a> on the fourth morning, which shot a "sunburst of metal darts" through her brain, killing her instantly. The following evening, <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> was sent to take her place.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2</a></i>, Leeg 2 survived to see Peeta join the squad, and she was injured in the incident that killed <a href="/wiki/Mitchell" title="Mitchell">Mitchell</a> and <a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>. Leeg 1 stayed behind to take care of her, and when the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper</a> assault on the building began, they returned fire. However, it's assumed that they were ultimately killed.<sup id="cite_ref-film_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-film-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was a young woman in her twenties, and she lacked the "weird yellow flecks" seen in her sister's eyes. This was only way <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> could tell the sisters apart, since they looked so similar in uniform.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FLeeg_2%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The Leeg sisters, despite being played by identical twin actresses in <i>Mockingjay - Part 2</i>, are not explicitly referred to as twins in the book.</li>
