<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Tribute_Parade">Tribute Parade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, he and <a href="/wiki/Foxface" class="mw-redirect" title="Foxface">Foxface</a> wore shiny and flashy silver costumes that represent District 5's main export, power and electricity.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>The District 5 male was seen in several scenes in the training. During training after <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a> throws the medicine ball, he and the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a> are seen in the background looking doubtfully at the Career tributes. It is unknown if he made any alliances at this time.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>For the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> with <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a>, he wore a pink suit. When Foxface has her interview with Caesar Flickerman, the District 5 male's face is shown on the screen behind along with Foxface.
</p>

<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5c/D10_male_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest?cb=20140516181804" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The District 5 male's corpse." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="D10 male killed by Cato.png" data-image-key="D10_male_killed_by_Cato.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5c/D10_male_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140516181804"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5c/D10_male_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest?cb=20140516181804" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The District 5 male's corpse." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5c/D10_male_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140516181804" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="75" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="D10 male killed by Cato.png" data-image-key="D10_male_killed_by_Cato.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/5/5c/D10_male_killed_by_Cato.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140516181804"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:D10_male_killed_by_Cato.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The District 5 male's corpse.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>The District 5 male was killed in the initial <a href="/wiki/Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Bloodbath">bloodbath</a>. He stood on the pedestal next to the <a href="/wiki/District_4_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 male (74th HG)">District 4 male</a>. Like the majority of the tributes, he ran for supplies. After arriving at the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, he spots the <a href="/wiki/District_3_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 3 female (74th HG)">District 3 female</a> on the ground with a black <a href="/wiki/Backpack" title="Backpack">backpack</a>. He grabs a sickle, slashes her throat and steals her bag. At some point in the bloodbath, he and the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a> try to kill <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> while the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 6 male (74th HG)">District 6 male</a> watches from behind a crate. He is about to kill the <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">District 7 female</a> but then, the <a href="/wiki/District_7_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 7 male (74th HG)">District 7 male</a> confronts him and knocks him down with a spear, about to kill him. Fortunately <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> passes by; distracting the District 7 male. He crawls away, but is attacked by the <a href="/wiki/District_6_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 6 male (74th HG)">District 6 male</a>, who strangles him onto a crate. He gains advantage and hurls the District 6 male's head into the crate multiple times. He later gets into a fight with <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> and she kills him by stabbing him in the abdomen, causing him to cough blood on a crate and eventually die.
</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>In the film, his odds of winning were 8–1, higher than <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a> and both the <a href="/wiki/District_4_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 male (74th HG)">male</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_4_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 female (74th HG)">female</a> tributes from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a>. The betting odds board also shows he was 15 years old, and he weighed 145 pounds.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, the color jacket he wore was plum red.</li>
<li>His death appears in the special features of the DVD.</li>
<li>In the movies, he was the only bloodbath casualty that killed another tribute. This is a possible back-shadowing to how District 5 was the only non-Career district that was part of the Career pack in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> and the only district within that pack to sustain casualties during that same games' bloodbath.
<ul><li>He was also the only unnamed tribute to kill someone, as well as the only seen unnamed tribute from District 5 that did not become a victor.</li>
<li>On the scoreboard, it states he only survived for only 13 seconds, but it also states his victim, the District 3 female, survived for 29 seconds, implying that the time of death for the unnamed tributes were mostly randomly given.</li></ul></li>
<li>He was the 8th dead, but when the <a href="/wiki/District_8_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 8 female (74th HG)">District 8 female</a> was killed, you can see that he was the 5th dead on the scoreboard behind Caesar.</li>
<li>Alongside <a href="/wiki/Thresh" title="Thresh">Thresh</a>, he is one of the two non-Career tributes to kill another tribute in the initial bloodbath.</li></ul>
