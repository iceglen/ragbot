He was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a> and a member of the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> pack.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Marvel was born in District 1, one of the wealthiest districts of <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, and trained most of his life for the Hunger Games, specializing in the use of spears. During the 74th Hunger Games, he was a part of the Career pack along with his district partner, Glimmer<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. His odds were the same as <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>'s, making both of them the second-strongest in the Career pack surpassed only by <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_Life">Early Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

He would watch the Hunger Games, and would relate to the tributes killing the other tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-interview_deleted_scene_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-interview_deleted_scene-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>He showed great skill at fighting and throwing spears, which made him deadly at a distance. While the <a href="/wiki/District_3_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 3 female (74th HG)">District 3 female</a> was on the Gauntlets, she was tripped and he and the other Careers laughed. During training in the film, he saw <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta</a>'s weakness and viewed him as a "meal" until Peeta's true strength was revealed when he threw a 100-pound metal ball at a rack of spears, leaving him and the other Careers surprised. Marvel received a very high training score of 9. He showed the same arrogance as many of his fellow Careers, but lacked the ruthless discipline of Cato and Clove (probably because they were from District 2, which contributed heavily to the Capitol's military); however, unlike his tribute partner Glimmer, he was very well-trained in his weapon of choice, which was a spear, specifically a javelin.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<p>Marvel rose on the pedestal next to Clove and Katniss. When the gong rang, he ran to the Cornucopia to battle for supplies like most of the other tributes and picked up a kukri. He spotted the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a>, slashed his throat, and stabbed him to death with the kukri. He watched Glimmer kill the <a href="/wiki/District_6_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 6 female (74th HG)">District 6 female</a>, then attacked the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">District 10 male</a>, taking the bag the boy stole from Glimmer. He saw the <a href="/wiki/District_3_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 3 male (74th HG)">District 3 male</a> hiding behind some crates, and he grappled with him over an axe, before Marvel hit the District 3 male in the face with his bag. Afterward, he attempted to swing his axe at the District 10 male, who dodged the attack. Marvel then took a spear and saw Clove ram the <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">District 7 female</a> into the ground, so he marched over and speared her through the heart, killing her. <sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Alliance">Alliance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Marvel made an alliance with Glimmer, Cato, Clove, Peeta, the District 3 male, and the <a href="/wiki/District_4_female_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 4 female (74th HG)">District 4 female</a> (book), to form a typical Career alliance that included two non-Careers (Peeta and the District 3 male)<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>

<p>Marvel helped the Careers chase Katniss up a tree<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. In the film, he noticed her before any of his fellow Careers did, yelling "There she is!"<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Realizing that she would be difficult to attack, he and the other Careers formed a camp below her. However, while they were distracted, Katniss sawed through a branch that held a <a href="/wiki/Tracker_jacker" title="Tracker jacker">tracker jacker</a> nest. The nest hit the ground and burst open, and he, Cato, and Clove were stung many times, forcing the Careers (minus Glimmer and the District 4 female in the book, who succumbed to the stings) to flee to the nearby lake.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Marvel and the other Careers recovered from their tracker jacker stings, they began to hunt for more tributes. Rue lit a fire as a decoy, distracting the Careers, who went to kill the tribute who started it. Marvel and the others set out to find Rue, but after the supplies were destroyed by Katniss setting off the landmines<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, the Careers returned just as Katniss dragged herself into Rue's hiding place. Cato blamed the District 3 boy, and Marvel watched Cato snap the boy's neck. Marvel and Clove calmed Cato down, telling him that the tribute who destroyed their supplies was probably killed as well in the explosion. They waited for night to see the faces of those who were killed, but when they found that the tribute did not die, the Careers then set out to hunt the other tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>Cato sent Marvel to kill Rue. Marvel trapped Rue with a net when she was on her way to light the last fire. As Rue screamed for help, Marvel threw a javelin into her abdomen.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_17_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_17-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the book, Katniss witnessed this and shot him through the neck. Marvel tried to remove the arrow by pulling it out, but doing this resulted in his death<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. In the film, Katniss reached Rue first and cut the net. Marvel threw a spear and Katniss  dodged it, but it hit Rue in her abdomen. Katniss skewered him through the heart with an arrow, killing him.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Rue removed her spear but already lost a significant amount of blood<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Overall, Marvel placed 8th out of 24, which was higher than his female counterpart, Glimmer.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Catching_Fire">Catching Fire</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Marvel_flashback.png/revision/latest?cb=20200712060638" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Marvel's appearance in Catching Fire." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Marvel flashback.png" data-image-key="Marvel_flashback.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Marvel_flashback.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200712060638"></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Marvel_flashback.png/revision/latest?cb=20200712060638" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Marvel's appearance in Catching Fire." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Marvel_flashback.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200712060638" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Marvel flashback.png" data-image-key="Marvel_flashback.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/68/Marvel_flashback.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20200712060638"></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Marvel_flashback.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Marvel's appearance in <i>Catching Fire</i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, Katniss and Peeta went to <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> on their <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>. Katniss felt uncomfortable, as she was responsible for the deaths of both Marvel and <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>, Marvel's district partner. She spotted his family, and she tried to not look them in the eye.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">the film</a>, Katniss also had a brief hallucination to her killing Marvel as she was about to kill a turkey. <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> comforted her afterward, but she kept quiet about what she saw.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the book, his most notable victim was <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. In the film, he kills Rue the same way in the book, but throws his spear into her abdomen after Katniss cuts Rue out of the net. He also seemed to have been aiming the spear at Katniss, until she dodged it and shot him in the heart. In the film, Marvel is shown to kill two tributes in the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_Bloodbath" class="mw-redirect" title="Cornucopia Bloodbath">Cornucopia Bloodbath</a>. After the <a href="/wiki/District_8_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 8 male (74th HG)">District 8 male</a> attempts to stab or punch him, Marvel slashes his throat with his kukri knife, throws him onto the ground, and then uses the knife to stab him until he dies. He kills also the <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">District 7 female</a> by skewering her with his spear while she was on the ground.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Possessions">Possessions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the Games, Marvel had medicine, food, water, and other supplies from the Cornucopia due to him being a Career<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. These supplies were blown up when Katniss destroyed the mines planted by the District 3 boy<sup id="cite_ref-THG_16_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>, and Marvel was left only with his spears and a backpack. The backpack held some knives, two extra spear heads, a flashlight, a small leather pouch, and a first-aid kit. However, it only included meager rations - a bottle of water and a pack of dried fruit - which Katniss saw as a sign of extreme arrogance because the Careers had such a bounty at camp<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Katniss thought Marvel as "arrogant" after finding a pack of dried fruit on him following Rue's death, due to the fact that he had bountiful resources with the Careers' supplies<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_10-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. Nevertheless, during the Victory Tour, she felt guilty for not knowing his name during the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During his interviews, he seemed to be going for a comedic approach, most likely to appeal to more sponsors<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. However, at the start of the games he ruthlessly killed at least two tributes, and later <a href="/wiki/Rue" title="Rue">Rue</a>, a twelve-year-old girl<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_10-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>. 
</p><p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Tribute_Guide" title="The Hunger Games: Tribute Guide">The Hunger Games: Tribute Guide</a></i>, it is mentioned that Marvel is "not the sharpest tool in the shed with his fists or brute force but sharp with spears and was properly the best from long distance".
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Marvel is described rather more skinny and tall than muscular and fit, unlike the other Careers.<sup><i>[citation needed]</i></sup> In the movie, he has short light brown hair and green eyes. According to the movie, he was the second tallest tribute at 6'3, after Thresh at 6'6" (book).<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Marvel's name probably comes from the word "marvel", which means "a wonderful object or thing". This might relate to how District 1 creates "wonderful" items to the Capitol.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Difference_from_the_book">Difference from the book</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMarvel%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Marvel´s name is revealed in the first film but it is only revealed once in the second book when Katniss and Peeta are on their Victory Tour. 
</p>

<li>In the film, Marvel has 5-1 odds, just like <a href="/wiki/Clove" title="Clove">Clove</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-stats_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stats-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
