<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrusilla_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Drusilla Sickle was an escort from <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> and had been going to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> to carry out the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> since the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a>. For the reaping of the <a href="/wiki/49th_Hunger_Games" title="49th Hunger Games">49th Hunger Games</a>, she decorated thumbtacks on her face with tiny buzz saw blades.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She was legally married to the District 12 stylist, <a href="/wiki/Magno_Stift" title="Magno Stift">Magno Stift</a>, and had been for at least thirty years by the time of the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games_reaping">50th Hunger Games reaping</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrusilla_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>As the 50th Hunger Games was a <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, there was a special twist and as such, she had to reap four children instead of two. Drusilla pulled out the names of <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> and <a href="/wiki/Woodbine_Chance" title="Woodbine Chance">Woodbine Chance</a>. However, Woodbine decided to run away and was shot dead by one of the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Drusilla berated the Peacekeeper responsible for creating a public mess instead of waiting until the boy was in the alleyway. She began panicking about the broadcast which was due to resume in five minutes and demanded the area was cleaned up beforehand. After screaming that they needed another boy, and referring to Woodbine as a "body", his mother fell into hysterics and tried to prevent them from taking him away with the help of <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove" class="mw-redirect" title="Lenore Dove">Lenore Dove</a>. After observing <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> throw himself into the struggle to protect Lenore, she decided that he would be new tribute to replace Woodbine.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Drusilla allowed cameraman <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> to take some more footage after the reaping, but was very quick to get out of District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She joined Plutarch and the tributes on the train and was complimented by Plutarch, who assured her that she looked young and beautiful during the broadcast. After seeing the strapping <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> tributes, she remarked that four from District 12 better be able to run. When her tributes were displeased with how Plutarch edited the reaping to remove the death of Woodbine, Drusilla berated them for being ungrateful as he made them look good and therefore, easier for them to get sponsors.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While advising the tributes, Drusilla made a rude comment about Louella's dress. Both she and Maysilee snapped back and insulted the other's outfit. Maysilee also commented on Drusilla's advanced age, causing her to slap the tribute. Maysilee retaliated and Drusilla flew into a rage. She started beating Maysilee brutally until Plutarch made her stop. However, Maysilee continued to disparage Drusilla; in response, Drusilla told the girl that she would die a horrible death and if she did somehow win, she had no idea what was in store for her after.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrusilla_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Before the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, Drusilla tried to help the tributes prepare with the help of <a href="/wiki/Mags_Flanagan" title="Mags Flanagan">Mags Flanagan</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> so as to help them get sponsors in the Games. Drusilla played the part of <a href="/wiki/Caesar_Flickerman" title="Caesar Flickerman">Caesar Flickerman</a> in the preparations, but didn't care about Louella having been replaced by <a href="/wiki/Lou_Lou" title="Lou Lou">a body double</a> or the pain that the girl was in, triggering Lou Lou into yelling at her that the Capitol was murdering them. When it was Maysilee's turn, she and Drusilla stared daggers at each other. Maysilee insulted Drusilla's outfit before going on to insult other Capitol citizens, resulting in Drusilla calling Maysilee a shrew and saying she would not make any friends with that approach.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When it was Haymitch's turn, Drusilla objected to his insulting the Gamemakers and Peacekeepers and bringing up Woodbine's death. Drusilla called the events at the reaping a riot that she'd had to cover up, and told Haymitch that people would respond to a bad boy, not a rebel. Drusilla suggested that he be naughty, not dangerous, and offered him tips. While her tips were mostly useless, Haymitch sensed that Drusilla was genuinely trying to help him in her own way and latched onto her idea of him being a rascal: someone who was mysterious and alluded to radical behavior without being specific, allowing the audience to use their imagination. However, the interview prep was interrupted by the news that Magno was apparently hallucinating from licking toads. A furious Drusilla stormed out to deal with him, yelling about filing a formal complaint with the Gamemakers.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Drusilla returned in time for the interviews and made sure that everyone knew that <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> did District 12's clothes. She also told the tributes that since there was no potential for an uprising in the Capitol audience, they were not to mess up and expect her to cover for them. Drusilla also slipped off to speak to Flickerman so that he could know how to approach their interviews.<sup id="cite_ref-SR13_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR13-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-Games">Post-Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrusilla_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Haymitch won the Games, he wondered who was paying the price for taking part in the rebellion. While he guessed that <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a>, Mags and Wiress were being tortured for information, Haymitch hoped that <a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> and the prep team were being spared as they were completely clueless Capitol pawns. However, Haymitch doubted that anyone suspected Drusilla and Magno of being sympathizers, and he didn't care if they were suspected.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Before the <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>, Drusilla broke her hip falling down an escalator and was replaced by Effie as the escort for District 12. Plutarch commented that Maysilee was right about Drusilla's heels after all.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film">Film</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrusilla_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the teaser, Drusilla could be seen being escorted out to the reaping square in a vertical stretcher. This formal and theatrical entrance ties back to <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s authoritarian society.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrusilla_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Drusilla was an older woman with a plastic face and a dreadful fashion sense. She had thumbtacks encircling her face to pull and hold her skin in place.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film, a prop raven can be seen on her head during the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a>. This is likely a reference to "<a href="/wiki/Songs#The_Raven" title="Songs">The Raven</a>" by Edgar Allan Poe, mentioned several times throughout <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrusilla_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Drusilla was a mean-spirited and snobby woman who had no consideration for the lives of people in the districts. She quickly ordered that Lenore Dove be killed, called the tributes "<i>four violent district piglets</i>" and beat tribute Maysilee Donner during an argument.<sup id="cite_ref-SR3_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, to her credit, Drusilla did try to help in her own way, offering Haymitch tips on how to get the Capitol audience to like him for <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsorship</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>. While most of her suggestions were unhelpful, Haymitch latched onto her idea about making himself appear to be a rascal.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDrusilla_Sickle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her name is likely a reference to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Livia">Livia Drusilla</a>, the mother of the Roman emperor <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiberius" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tiberius">Tiberius</a>. Tiberius's first wife, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vipsania_Agrippina" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Vipsania Agrippina">Vipsania Agrippina</a>, is the likely namesake of both Agripinna and Vipsania Sickle. Livia Drusilla divorced her first husband. The name Drusilla means "strong" or "mighty".
</p><p>Additionally, her surname, Sickle, refers to a tool used to harvest crops.
</p>
