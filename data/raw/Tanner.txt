<p><b>Tanner</b> was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Domitia_Whimsiwick" title="Domitia Whimsiwick">Domitia Whimsiwick</a>. For a short amount of time, he was in an <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a> with the <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> duo, <a href="/wiki/Mizzen" title="Mizzen">Mizzen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Coral" title="Coral">Coral</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTanner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 131px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/Tanner_reaping.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240215035917" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tanner at the reaping." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/62/Tanner_reaping.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240215035917" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="131" height="181" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Tanner reaping.jpg" data-image-key="Tanner_reaping.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Tanner_reaping.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Tanner at the reaping.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Tanner grew up in District 10 and became his district's male tribute in the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Along with the other tributes, Tanner was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> While at the zoo, his <a href="/wiki/District_partner" title="District partner">district partner</a> <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">Brandy</a> killed her mentor, <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>, so she was gunned down by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the interviews before the Games began, Tanner's mentor, <a href="/wiki/Domitia_Whimsiwick" title="Domitia Whimsiwick">Domitia Whimsiwick</a>, used her knowledge of cows and bubbly nature to get Tanner speaking about slaughterhouse techniques, which he had grown familiar with during his time in District 10, the industry of which is livestock. He became so engaged in the conversation that <a href="/wiki/Lucretius_Flickerman" title="Lucretius Flickerman">Lucretius Flickerman</a>, the interviewer, had to cut them off when they ran out of time, and he garnered the most applause of the evening so far.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Because of the interview, many Capitol citizens favored Tanner to win.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="10th_Hunger_Games">10th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTanner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Tanner was one of the few tributes that sprinted for the weapons in the center of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> after the gong sounded to signal the beginning of the Games. However, like the others, he quickly dispersed after selecting his weapon. Later that morning, he was seen climbing up and strolling along the top of the arena, but only sat there for a while before vanishing again.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the Games, <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> went into the arena to protest the death of <a href="/wiki/Marcus" title="Marcus">Marcus</a> and hold vigil over Marcus' body. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> was sent into the arena to coax Sejanus out, but as they left, Tanner pursued them, along with Mizzen, Coral, and <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a>. He managed to cut open the back of Sejanus's calf with his <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Knife" title="Weapons">knife</a>, injuring him before Sejanus was able to escape. After this incident, Tanner, Coral and Mizzen formed an alliance.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the second day of the Games, he, Coral, and Mizzen teamed up once more to kill <a href="/wiki/Sol" title="Sol">Sol</a>, the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a>. The alliance chased her until Coral speared Sol's throat with a trident.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_16_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_16-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>On the third day, the trio prepared to kill <a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a>, the female <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> tribute. At first, Lamina took no notice, but was brought to attention when Tanner banged a <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Sword" title="Weapons">sword</a> against one of the poles, as she had taken shelter above. Tanner positioned himself underneath, holding a pair of <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Trident" title="Weapons">tridents</a> and directing the District 4 tributes while they climbed up, so they could corner Lamina and prevent her from escaping. When they reached the beam at the top, Tanner threw Mizzen's trident up for him to catch, but didn't toss it high enough and it fell back to the ground. Because Mizzen had been occupied with attempting to catch the trident, Lamina took the opportunity to injure him and knock him off balance. Tanner ran down to the other end and instead tossed a trident to Coral, who caught it easily and used it to kill Lamina.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTanner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the alliance killed Lamina, Tanner rejoiced over her death, performing a kind of dance over her body. However, as he turned to congratulate Mizzen with a "victory slap," Coral drove a trident into his back. Tanner fell to the ground after being shoved away by Mizzen, who then finished him off using a knife to his neck.
</p><p>Tanner's body was later collected by <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a>, the <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> male tribute, and taken to his makeshift morgue.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Film_adaptation">Film adaptation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTanner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When the tributes and mentors visited the arena to devise strategies, Tanner was shown to have quickly formed an alliance with Coral and Mizzen, standing with them as they surveyed the arena. He watched as Coral invited <a href="/wiki/Treech" title="Treech">Treech</a> to join their alliance, and he witnessed Treech’s decision to abandon his district partner, Lamina.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the gong rang, Tanner pushed Lamina and headed to the center. He began fighting <a href="/wiki/Panlo" title="Panlo">Panlo</a>, who was soon killed by Coral. Tanner, Coral, Mizzen, and Treech gathered before chasing <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> and <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a> into the tunnels. However, the two tributes narrowly escaped through a door.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>They continued to inspect the tunnels until nightfall, when Tanner grabbed <a href="/wiki/Otto" title="Otto">Otto</a>, who had been killed by one of them, out of the rubble. At night, the four saw Coriolanus Snow and Sejanus Plinth running towards the exit and decided to chase them, not stopping even when <a href="/wiki/Bobbin" title="Bobbin">Bobbin</a> was killed. After Coriolanus and Sejanus escaped, the tributes returned to the center of the arena. The next day, the four had cornered Lucy Gray when Coriolanus sent her several drones with water. The drones crashed into the alliance, and Lucy Gray was able to escape.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After killing Lamina, the group turned around to retrieve the water. They saw Lucy Gray and chased her into a ventilation duct. When Tanner reproached Coral for not being able to kill her, Coral stuck her trident into his chest. Tanner placed 8th.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTanner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tanner was described as muscular.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTanner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tanner's name comes from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanning_(leather)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Tanning (leather)">tanning</a>, the process of creating leather from animal skin.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTanner%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Mizzen and Coral may have started the alliance with Tanner to specifically kill Lamina because it required three people to block all of her exits.</li>
<li>Throughout the book, Tanner is referred to as strong. He was one of the only tributes along with Coral, Reaper, and Jessup to receive weapons from the weapons pile at the start of the Games.</li>
<li>Tanner is the third male tribute from District 10 in the series to outlive his <a href="/wiki/Brandy" title="Brandy">female counterpart</a>, similar to District 10 males from the <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 10 male (74th HG)">74th</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_10_male_(75th_HG)" title="District 10 male (75th HG)">75th</a> Hunger Games.</li></ul>
