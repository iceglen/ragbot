<p>A <b>hovercraft</b> is a type of aircraft used in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>, mainly by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>, but is later also used by members of the <a href="/wiki/Rebellion" title="Rebellion">rebellion</a>. They serve a variety of roles from supporting the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(event)" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> to transportation and combat. They are manufactured in <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">District 6</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHovercraft%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Prior to the beginning of the Hunger Games, a hovercraft is used to transport each individual tribute and their <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a> to their respective <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">Launch Room</a>. Hovercraft windows are closed to prevent tributes from obtaining an aerial view of the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>. Once the Games get underway, a hovercraft carrying a giant screen displays the nightly recaps of who died that day.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As soon as a tribute's death is confirmed, their body is recovered by hovercraft along with any weapons lodged in the body or held by the corpse, permanently removing these from the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They also drop <a href="/wiki/Parachute" class="mw-redirect" title="Parachute">parachutes</a> with <a href="/wiki/Sponsor_gift" class="mw-redirect" title="Sponsor gift">sponsor gifts</a> sent by their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>. Since the gifts can appear very quickly, this suggests that hovercrafts are constantly shadowing the tributes throughout the Games. Once a <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> is declared, they are picked up by a hovercraft and returned to the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a>. This hovercraft is equipped with an operating room and carries a medical team to provide immediate medical care to treat any serious injuries that the victor(s) may have sustained.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 320px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Thehovercraft.png/revision/latest?cb=20130720213951" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="View of the hovercraft flying over the Capitol." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Thehovercraft.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/320?cb=20130720213951" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="165" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Thehovercraft.png" data-image-key="Thehovercraft.png" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Thehovercraft.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">View of the hovercraft flying over the Capitol.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the movie, a single hovercraft transports all of the tributes to the arena at once. Hovercrafts are not seen removing bodies from the arena in <i>The Hunger Games</i>, though they do so in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>.
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, Katniss, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Finnick_Odair" title="Finnick Odair">Finnick Odair</a> were rescued by hovercraft after the force field surrounding the arena was destroyed. They were taken to <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>, which the hovercraft belonged to.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire_(film)" class="mw-redirect" title="Catching Fire (film)">film</a>, Katniss is seen lying on the floor beside Beetee, after just being rescued. She gets up and takes a syringe before going through two doors to hear <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch</a>, Finnick, and <a href="/wiki/Haymitch" class="mw-redirect" title="Haymitch">Haymitch</a> discussing <a href="/wiki/Peeta" class="mw-redirect" title="Peeta">Peeta Mellark</a> and his whereabouts. Katniss then bursts into the room and tries to attack them, but Haymitch quickly restrains her.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHovercraft%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A hovercraft may be equipped with a large claw for lifting or grabbing, harpoons attached to cables, and <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Nets" title="Weapons">nets</a>.
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 270px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7e/Unnamed_%281%29.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140122121820" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The hovercraft used in Catching Fire." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7e/Unnamed_%281%29.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/270?cb=20140122121820" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="270" height="112" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Unnamed (1).jpg" data-image-key="Unnamed_%281%29.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Unnamed_(1).jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The hovercraft used in <i>Catching Fire</i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>They have also been seen catching people by sucking them upwards using some kind of <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>. This same force field is used to hold someone in place when they are about to board the hovercraft via its ladder, which is then retracted. This is also used to keep <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> immobilized when a <a href="/wiki/Tracker" title="Tracker">tracker</a> is injected into their forearms. However, in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the tributes are instead injected with the trackers while they are seated and strapped inside the cargo bay.
</p><p>Inside of the hovercrafts used for transporting tributes, there is food and drink for the tributes and stylists to eat as breakfast before entering the arena, as they did not have time to do so at the training center suites. Before the 75th Hunger Games, Katniss refrained from eating despite Cinna's pleas. She only partially drank a glass of water before arriving. 
</p><p>Hovercrafts are equipped with some form of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloaking_device" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Cloaking device">cloaking</a> ability, since they are often described as "materializing". When <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> visited <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> after its <a href="/wiki/Bombings#District_12" title="Bombings">bombing</a>, she mentions that "at least a dozen invisible hovercraft are circling overhead for my protection."<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They are also fairly quiet, not making a sound, which allows them to appear without warning. However, in the film adaptation, the hovercrafts are much noisier, therefore sighted from far off.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a9/180px-Katniss_walking_towards_hovercraft.png/revision/latest?cb=20140122121911" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss heading towards the hovercraft in The Hunger Games." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="98" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="180px-Katniss walking towards hovercraft.png" data-image-key="180px-Katniss_walking_towards_hovercraft.png" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a9/180px-Katniss_walking_towards_hovercraft.png/revision/latest?cb=20140122121911" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a9/180px-Katniss_walking_towards_hovercraft.png/revision/latest?cb=20140122121911" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss heading towards the hovercraft in The Hunger Games." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a9/180px-Katniss_walking_towards_hovercraft.png/revision/latest?cb=20140122121911" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="98" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="180px-Katniss walking towards hovercraft.png" data-image-key="180px-Katniss_walking_towards_hovercraft.png" data-relevant="0" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/a/a9/180px-Katniss_walking_towards_hovercraft.png/revision/latest?cb=20140122121911" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:180px-Katniss_walking_towards_hovercraft.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss heading towards the hovercraft in <i>The Hunger Games</i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The designs of the hovercraft vary from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i> to <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>, as the one used in <i>Catching Fire</i> has a sleeker and stealthier look than the one used in <i>The Hunger Games</i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Combat">Combat</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHovercraft%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Hovercrafts fulfill many combat functions. They are usually piloted and controlled by the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> and military to keep the <a href="/wiki/Panem#Districts" title="Panem">districts</a> in line or to catch runaways and enemies of the Capitol, an example of this was the capture of <a href="/wiki/Lavinia" title="Lavinia">Lavinia</a>. Her companion, an unnamed boy, was killed. They have also been used as transports and as bombers.
</p><p>During the first rebellion about 75 years before the start of the series, the <a href="/wiki/Rebels" title="Rebels">rebels</a> of District 13 captured some Capitol hovercrafts and used these as the nucleus for their own fleet, constructing new hovercrafts and upgrading the older models. The fleet was stationed in <a href="/wiki/The_Hangar" title="The Hangar">the Hangar</a><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and later deployed during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>. During the uprising in <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">District 8</a> that began on the night that Peeta Mellark proposed to Katniss, hovercrafts were used to suppress the rebels by bombing their strongholds.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Transportation">Transportation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHovercraft%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Despite using <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">trains</a> as their main form of inter-district transport, the Capitol also uses hovercraft to travel long distances, as did the rebels later. They are also used to ferry cargo.
</p><p>In the film, just prior to the Battle of the Capitol, District 13 hovercrafts are seen ferrying cargo and supplies to rebel forces gathered in the outskirts of the Capitol in preparation for the final assault to take control of the city. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHovercraft%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The seats in the hovercraft are NASCAR stock car seats that the drivers would sit in.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>According to Katniss, the entire forest goes silent when a hovercraft is about to come. Right before it appears, a <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjay</a> lets out a certain high pitched tune.</li>
<li>Hovercrafts appear to be the successor of airplanes, helicopters, and other air vehicles from the present day, which do not make an appearance in the books or movies. It is most likely that Panem abandoned modern-day air vehicles when they developed hovercrafts.</li></ul>
