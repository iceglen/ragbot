</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/6c/Sign_fence.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140521191141" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A sign placed on the fence." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/6/6c/Sign_fence.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140521191141" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Sign fence.jpg" data-image-key="Sign_fence.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Sign_fence.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A sign placed on the fence.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>The fence was put in place sometime after the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, as it did not exist when the <a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">prequel</a> took place.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Ostensibly, the fence was supposed to keep the district's streets safe from wild animals. It was supposed to electrified 24 hours a day, but due to frequent power outages, this was rarely the case, so it was a simple matter to cross it.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Occasionally, in the autumn, people were known to walk about 60 feet beyond the fence to pick apples, but they never wandered far. Others like <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> would venture further on a regular basis in pursuit of plants and game to bring back to the district. While trespassing and poaching were technically illegal, District 12's <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeepers</a> never enforced those laws, since they also benefited from the labor of hunters.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/74/CF_concept_art_crossing_the_fence.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240216201450" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Concept art of Gale and Katniss crossing the fence." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/74/CF_concept_art_crossing_the_fence.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240216201450" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="74" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="CF concept art crossing the fence.jpg" data-image-key="CF_concept_art_crossing_the_fence.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:CF_concept_art_crossing_the_fence.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Concept art of Gale and Katniss crossing the fence.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After Katniss and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour_(74th_HG)" title="Victory Tour (74th HG)">Victory Tour</a>, <a href="/wiki/Romulus_Thread" title="Romulus Thread">Romulus Thread</a> became the new Head Peacekeeper. Under his orders, the fence was consistently electrified, cutting the district off from a major source of <a href="/wiki/Food_and_drink" title="Food and drink">food</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Second_Rebellion"><a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Bombing_of_District_12" class="mw-redirect" title="Bombing of District 12">bombing of District 12</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> herded refugees to <a href="/wiki/The_Meadow" title="The Meadow">the Meadow</a>, and they worked together to tear down a portion of the fence and escape into the woods. Gale then led the survivors to a <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a> several hours from the district on foot, where they were later found by <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the rebel victory in the Second Rebellion, the district's fence was propped up by branches to keep wild animals out, but citizens were able to freely enter and exit the woods.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Appearance">Appearance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 300px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/09/THG59.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130604131354" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The fence around District 12." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/09/THG59.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/300?cb=20130604131354" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="124" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="THG59.jpg" data-image-key="THG59.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:THG59.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The fence around District 12.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The fence is described as being a 6 meter high chain link fence topped with barbed wire loops. It is quite old and rusty because it has not been well maintained or used. 
</p><p>Despite its oppressive presence, even the fence could offer the district's citizens some resources. Katniss and her sister <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a> were once able to harvest dandelion flowers, greens, and stems along a mile-long stretch of fence.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_4_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_4-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFence%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>There was also a fence in <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>, which Katniss saw briefly from the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> on her <a href="/wiki/Victory_Tour" title="Victory Tour">Victory Tour</a>. District 11's fence was at least 35 feet high,  topped with coils of barbed wire, with "enormous" metal plates lining to base of it to prevent anyone digging underneath. It was also guarded by evenly-spaced watchtowers manned by armed Peacekeepers.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
