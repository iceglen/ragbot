<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_10%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Before the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_families#Crane_family" title="Capitol families">Crane family</a> developed luxury hotels in vacation spots across Panem, including in District 10.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Little is known of the district's role in the war, but when District 10 rebelled against the Capitol, the loss of its meat would have contributed to starvation conditions in the city while it was under siege.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Livestock_industry">Livestock industry</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_10%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>District 10 operated slaughterhouses, butchering hogs<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and cattle. Unlike District 12,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 10 was one of the districts where adolescents participated in their industry, giving some <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> an advantage in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.
</p><p>The district maintained the genetic diversity of its cattle herds by "implanting long-frozen cow embryos".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Location_and_geography">Location and geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_10%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the map of Panem featured in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_Adventures" title="The Hunger Games Adventures">The Hunger Games Adventures</a></i>, District 10 is located in the southwest of North America. As the southernmost district of Panem, District 10's territory primarily corresponds to the western part of the U.S. state of Texas and the northern and central Mexican states of Aguascalientes, Chihuahua, Coahuila, Durango, Guanajuato, Nuevo León, San Luis Potosí, and Tamaulipas. District 10 is one of the two Panemian districts located mostly outside the contiguous United States, alongside <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>, primarily being located in northern and central Mexico. Historically, the flat topography in most of these regions was ideal for raising cattle and other livestock, which ties well with the district's industry. 
</p><p>District 10 borders three districts: <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a> (to the northeast), <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> (to the north), and <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">District 5</a> (to the northwest).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_10%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Districts <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_5" title="District 5">5</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_9" title="District 9">9</a>, and 10 were not involved in the rebel plot during the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th</a> and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th</a> <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, both female <a href="/wiki/Tributes" class="mw-redirect" title="Tributes">tributes</a> from District 10 died during the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> bloodbath, while the male tributes survived a bit longer, but then later died from an unknown cause. This is the opposite of District 5.</li>
<li>District 10 was the 5th - 10th district to pry from Capitol rule during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.</li>
<li>Dalton is the only named person from District 10 in the original trilogy.
<ul><li>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_and_Snakes" title="The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes">The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>, we learn the names of their tributes: Brandy and Tanner (<a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>); Buck, Lannie, Stamp, and Peeler (<a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>).</li></ul></li>
<li>In the 74th and 75th Hunger Games, District 10 was one of the two districts to have no named tributes. The other was District 9.
<ul><li>In these Games, all District 10 tributes died offscreen.</li></ul></li>
<li>District 10 is located close by or within Mexico, as seen in the map shown in <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 10 has a livestock count of 1 million with a population of 19,234, making it the tenth largest district, in terms of population.</li>
<li>According to the Capitol.PN, District 10 claims 291,963 in <a href="/wiki/Tesserae" class="mw-redirect" title="Tesserae">tesserae</a>, making it the third most impoverished district.
<ul><li>If the population is 19,234 and they claim 291,963 tesserae, that would equal to 15.179 tesserae per person, making it the 11th richest District.</li></ul></li>
<li>Based on the <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a> for the 73rd and 74th Hunger Games, the district's color seems to be donkey brown gray (#7d6f67 – <span style="background-color:#7d6f67; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>Although the final cut of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i> changed all the District banner colors to red, pre-release materials show District 10's color for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a> appears to be orchard (#DA70D6 - <span style="background-color:#DA70D6; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The arena wear for the 50th Hunger Games was crimson (#DC143C - <span style="background-color:#DC143C; color:; border:1px solid darkgray;">&#160;&#160;&#160;</span>).</li></ul></li></ul>
