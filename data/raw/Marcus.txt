<p><b>Marcus</b> was the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a> was <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena_bombing" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol Arena bombing">Capitol Arena bombing</a>, he managed to escape, but he faced major consequences after being caught.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, he was in the same class in <a href="/wiki/School" title="School">school</a> as Sejanus Plinth. One day, when Sejanus smashed his finger in a door, Marcus immediately scooped a cup of snow off the windowsill to help bring down the swelling. They weren't exactly friends nor enemies, but Sejanus's move to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> made Marcus despise him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 132px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Marcus_reaping.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240215031629" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Marcus at the reaping." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/96/Marcus_reaping.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/132?cb=20240215031629" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="132" height="170" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Marcus reaping.jpg" data-image-key="Marcus_reaping.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Marcus_reaping.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Marcus at the reaping.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Marcus was <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaped</a> for the 10th Hunger Games, and Sejanus suspected this had been rigged by his father, <a href="/wiki/Strabo_Plinth" title="Strabo Plinth">Strabo Plinth</a>. Marcus and the other tributes were taken to the Capitol in a dirty cattle car, then transferred into a truck, which dumped them all into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Marcus saw Sejanus at the zoo, he was the only tribute that refused to accept food from Sejanus, remaining stone-faced despite his mentor pleading him to take a sandwich. Instead, he got ready for the night and easily broke apart two other tributes who had been fighting over a bale of hay, claiming it for himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Later, during the first official meeting the mentors had with their tributes, Sejanus put a meal of roast beef sandwiches and cake between them while he talked animatedly, but Marcus remained unresponsive.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During a tour of the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a>, several explosions planted by the rebels were detonated. The bombs exploded a large hole near the entrance of the arena, and through the chaos, each tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> made a run for it. <a href="/wiki/Facet" title="Facet">Facet</a>, <a href="/wiki/Velvereen" title="Velvereen">Velvereen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sabyn" title="Sabyn">Sabyn</a> were killed, but Marcus disappeared completely, presumably having climbed underground to the <a href="/wiki/Transfer" title="Transfer">Transfer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Marcus's fate was unknown until the Games begin to broadcast. He was strung up by steel poles in the air at the main entrance to the Arena and left to hang from his manacles, still alive and badly beaten. This left him prone for any tribute to kill him.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Because of this, Sejanus hurled a chair towards the screen in anger, then left the mentor section.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Lamina" title="Lamina">Lamina</a>, the female tribute from <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a>, climbed up to where Marcus hung and spoke to him. After bracing herself, she mercy killed Marcus by driving an axe to his neck three times. His death took place about thirty minutes after the Games started, and was the first kill of the day.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_14_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_14-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the film, he ranked in 13th.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>That night, Sejanus slipped into the arena and arrived at Marcus's corpse. After straightening his body, he sprinkled bread crumbs over it, as was tradition in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>. <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> soon came to persuade Sejanus to return, and they both dragged Marcus's body toward the barricade.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_15_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_15-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, they left his body after being attacked by other tributes, and it later became part of <a href="/wiki/Reaper_Ash" title="Reaper Ash">Reaper Ash</a>'s makeshift morgue in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>Marcus towered above the other tributes<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and had a lot of physical strength, which was shown when he easily tossed two other tributes several yards in the air. However, when <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> spoke to him, he remained stone-faced and unresponsive.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When he was taken to the arena at the beginning of the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, Marcus hung from the steel poles near the entrance, his wrists chained and his body battered and bloody.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Marcus was the only tribute to successfully escape the arena from the bombing, and the only tribute to be left in the open when the Games began.</li>
<li>He was technically the first death in the Games.</li>
<li>He was similar to <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> in that they were both male tributes from District 2 who were mercy killed by a female tribute. While Cato was the last death in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Marcus was the first to die in the actual period of the 10th Hunger Games. Although in the movie adaption, six tributes were killed in the bloodbath before Marcus was killed.</li></ul>
