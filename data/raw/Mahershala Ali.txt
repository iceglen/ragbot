&#160;
<b>Mahershala Ali</b> is an American actor who portrayed&#160;<a href="/wiki/Boggs" title="Boggs">Boggs</a>&#160;in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2"><i>Part 2</i></a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMahershala_Ali%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His first TV appearance was as Dr. Trey Sanders on the television drama series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossing_Jordan" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Crossing Jordan">Crossing Jordan</a></i>. This was followed by shows such as&#160;<i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NYPD_Blue" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:NYPD Blue">NYPD Blue</a></i>, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Threat_Matrix" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Threat Matrix">Threat Matrix</a></i>,<i> <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Crime_Scene_Investigation" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:CSI: Crime Scene Investigation">CSI: Crime Scene Investigation</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunted" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Haunted">The Haunted</a></i>. Subsequently, Ali landed the role of Richard Tyler, a Korean War pilot on the critically acclaimed drama The 4400 on USA Network for three seasons. Ali has guest starred on a number of shows including <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_To_Me" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lie To Me">Lie To Me</a></i> on FOX and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_and_Order_SVU" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Law and Order SVU">Law and Order SVU</a></i>.
</p><p>Ali won the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor">Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor</a> for his performances as a drug dealer in the drama <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonlight_(2016_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Moonlight (2016 film)">Moonlight</a></i> (2016) and as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Shirley" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Don Shirley">Don Shirley</a> in the comedy-drama film <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Book_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Green Book (film)">Green Book</a></i> (2018). He is the first Muslim actor to win an Oscar,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the first Black actor to win two Academy Awards in the same category, and the second Black actor to win multiple acting Oscars.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Ali won the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Children%27s_Program" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Children&#39;s Program">Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Children's Program</a> for executive producing <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Are_the_Dream:_The_Kids_of_the_Oakland_MLK_Oratorical_Fest" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:We Are the Dream: The Kids of the Oakland MLK Oratorical Fest">We Are the Dream: The Kids of the Oakland MLK Oratorical Fest</a></i> (2020).
</p><p>In 2019, he played a troubled police officer in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective_(season_3)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:True Detective (season 3)">third season</a> of the HBO anthology crime series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:True Detective">True Detective</a></i> and in 2020, he starred in the second season of the Hulu comedy-drama series <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramy_(TV_series)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Ramy (TV series)">Ramy</a></i>. He was nominated for Primetime Emmy Awards for both performances. Ali voiced <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prowler_(Marvel_Comics)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Prowler (Marvel Comics)">Aaron Davis</a> in the animated films <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Into_the_Spider-Verse" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse">Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse</a></i> (2018) and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Across_the_Spider-Verse" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse">Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse</a></i> (2023), the former winning the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Animated_Feature" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Academy Award for Best Animated Feature">Academy Award for Best Animated Feature</a>.
</p>
