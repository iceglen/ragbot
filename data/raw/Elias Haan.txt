<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElias_Haan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Elias was chosen to become a District Hero presumably because he believes in District 7's mission to respect the greater forces of nature. He has kept the <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Axe" title="Weapons">axe</a> handed down to him by his great-grandfather as a reminder of the hardships he and his district have overcome.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElias_Haan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Elias has brown eyes, brown hair and a short beard. He is also very muscular, has multiple tattoos along his arms and chest, and he is an amputee, having had one leg amputated below the knee. In his District Hero portrait, he wears leather boots and jeans, and a red flannel shirt lies on the bench beside him. He also has a carved wooden prosthesis that he has removed for the photo.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElias_Haan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elias" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Elias">Elias</a> is the Greek equivalent of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elijah" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Elijah">Elijah</a>, meaning "My God is <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahweh" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Yahweh">Yahweh</a>". <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haan_(surname)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Haan (surname)">Haan</a> is the Dutch spelling of the German surname <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hahn_(surname)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hahn (surname)">Hahn</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElias_Haan%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>On June 19th, 2014, <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a> released seven portraits of District Heroes as advertisements for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i>. These were original characters, independent from the books or films, serving as pro-<a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">Capitol</a> <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propaganda</a> for each of their respective districts.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>He is the second character from District 7 to have a religious name. The first is <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>.</li></ul>
