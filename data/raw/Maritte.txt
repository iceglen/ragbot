</p><p><b>Maritte</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">District 4</a> who participated in the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. She was a part of the Career <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>, and she placed 5th out of 48 <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaritte%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaritte%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the build-up to the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, Maritte received a score of eleven after her private session with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemakers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She was part of the <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career</a> pack in these Games. She retrieved a trident from the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, something she was often featured with pre-games, and wielded it during the initial bloodbath.<sup id="cite_ref-SR17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR17-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaritte%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Maritte and <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> teamed up during the final stages of the Games. They were chasing <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> when they discovered three Gamemakers repairing a mutt portral inside the arena. Maritte was the first to react and killed a Gamemaker with her trident. She and Maysilee then dispatched of the other two. Silka was horrified that Maritte killed Gamemakers, as she did not believe they'd ever let them win now. Maysilee scoffed at the thought of winning, asking if Silka was really still chasing that dream, and remarked to Maritte that District 4 should be on Newcomers' side. Maritte briefly considered an offer to join Haymitch and Maysilee, but ultimately decided to leave.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In punishment for killing Gamemakers, Maritte was chased down and killed by a pack of golden squirrel <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">muttations</a> similar to the ones that killed <a href="/wiki/Ampert_Latier" title="Ampert Latier">Ampert Latier</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the Games, heavily edited recap footage of the 50th Hunger Games was shown. The killing of the Gamemakers was omitted and her death was edited to show Maritte being killed after Maysilee and when <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a> killed <a href="/wiki/Wellie" title="Wellie">Wellie</a>, even though she was already long dead at that point.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaritte%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Likely a diminution of "maritime," an adjective for something marine. It may also be a variant of Marita, a Scandinavian form of "Margaret."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Margaret means "pearl,"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which connects with District 4's aquatic industry.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaritte%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Maritte, along with the other three tributes from District 4, wore a "shimmering deep sea–blue mermaid suit" during the <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">chariot rides</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR5_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR5-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Their <a href="/wiki/Training_outfits" title="Training outfits">training outfits</a> were deep-sea blue.<sup id="cite_ref-SR8_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR8-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Additionally, Maysilee had commented on Maritte saying she couldn't pull off pin curls, implying that Maritte had pin curls as her hairstyle. Based on her physique, she was considered a contender.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMaritte%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> practiced evaluating Maritte's odds with <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR12_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR12-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> According to Wyatt:
<ul><li>Maritte pulled a <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">score</a> of 11 with the <a href="/wiki/Gamemakers" class="mw-redirect" title="Gamemakers">Gamemakers</a>.</li>
<li>She had odds of six-to-one.</li>
<li>She had a 14.29% chance of winning the Games.</li></ul></li>
<li>Maritte technically finished fifth in the Hunger Games, but as her death was publicly altered to show her dying after Wellie and Maysilee, then it would have looked like she finished third.</li></ul>
