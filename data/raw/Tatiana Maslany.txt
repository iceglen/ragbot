</p><p><b>Tatiana Maslany</b> is a Canadian actress who narrated the 10-year anniversary audiobooks for <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FTatiana_Maslany%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She is known for playing multiple characters in the science-fiction television series <i>Orphan Black</i>. In addition to acting for film, TV, and the stage, she has voice acted in a number of shows since 2013 and appeared on various podcasts since 2015.
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/36/Tatiana_Maslany_with_Special_Edition_book.webp/revision/latest?cb=20231213214712" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Tatiana Maslany with the tenth anniversary edition of The Hunger Games." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/3/36/Tatiana_Maslany_with_Special_Edition_book.webp/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20231213214712" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="120" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Tatiana Maslany with Special Edition book.webp" data-image-key="Tatiana_Maslany_with_Special_Edition_book.webp" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Tatiana_Maslany_with_Special_Edition_book.webp" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Tatiana Maslany with the tenth anniversary edition of <i>The Hunger Games</i>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Special_Edition_audiobook" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hunger Games: Special Edition audiobook"><i>The Hunger Games</i></a> was her first audiobook, released on October 30, 2018 to mark the 10-year anniversary of the first novel's publication.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She also recorded versions of <a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire_audiobook_(2019)" class="mw-redirect" title="Catching Fire audiobook (2019)"><i>Catching Fire</i></a> and <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_audiobook_(2019)" class="mw-redirect" title="Mockingjay audiobook (2019)"><i>Mockingjay</i></a>, which were both released on June 17, 2019.
</p>
