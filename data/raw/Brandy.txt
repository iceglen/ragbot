<p><b>Brandy</b> was the female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_10" title="District 10">District 10</a> who was reaped to participate in the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>. She had spent her life butchering hogs. Her mentor was <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 159px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Brandy_reaping.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20240215033924" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Brandy at the reaping." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Brandy_reaping.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/159?cb=20240215033924" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="159" height="200" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Brandy reaping.jpg" data-image-key="Brandy_reaping.jpg" data-relevant="0"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Brandy_reaping.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Brandy at the reaping.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Brandy grew up in District 10, spending most of her time butchering hogs, as the district's main industry is livestock. She became her district's female tribute in the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the 10th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Along with the other tributes, Brandy was taken to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> During the first mentor-tribute meeting, Brandy told her mentor, <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>, of her life in District 10. Arachne was disgusted at first, though she later realized she could come up with something using her knowledge of the district from previous visits there.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Brandy died two days after the reaping, on July 6th. Arachne brought the starved Brandy a sandwich in the zoo, but instead of handing it over, she lectured her and taunted the tribute for the spectating Capitol citizens. However, Brandy grabbed the handle of Arachne's own knife and leaned forward to slit Arachne's throat.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>After the mentor dropped the sandwich and <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> arrived in a futile attempt to help Arachne, Brandy retrieved the sandwich and took a furious bite before being gunned down by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>. Brandy was the first of the tributes to die, before the Hunger Games had even begun. <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> was seen sprinkling breadcrumbs over her body, as was tradition in <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>, before being pulled away by a Peacekeeper.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During Arachne's funeral, Brandy's bullet-ridden body was hoisted high in the air by a crane affixed to a truck, where the remaining tributes were shackled.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Brandy's name comes from the term <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livestock_branding" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Livestock branding">branding</a>, the method of marking livestock with a seal to identify their ownership.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Brandy is the first tribute to die on-screen, though not due to the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.
<ul><li>Because of this, in the film, her face on the screen is never seen without the death logo over it, even in behind-the-scenes photos, where the faces of other tributes killed before the start of the games can be seen.</li></ul></li>
<li>Brandy could have been one of the “stronger” tributes during the 10th Hunger Games like her district partner, <a href="/wiki/Tanner" title="Tanner">Tanner</a>. Given her background in District 10, Brandy would have also had some knowledge and experience using tools as weapons.</li></ul>
