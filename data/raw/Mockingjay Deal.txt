<p>The <b>Mockingjay Deal</b> was a deal struck between <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>'s <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">President</a> <a href="/wiki/Alma_Coin" title="Alma Coin">Alma Coin</a>, along with the other <a href="/wiki/The_Rebels" class="mw-redirect" title="The Rebels">rebel</a> leaders, during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Terms">Terms</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_Deal%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d6/Plutarch_and_Coin.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20230709170835" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Plutarch Heavensbee and Alma Coin listening to Katniss&#39;s conditions." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/d/d6/Plutarch_and_Coin.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230709170835" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="99" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Plutarch and Coin.jpg" data-image-key="Plutarch_and_Coin.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Plutarch_and_Coin.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption"><a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> and Alma Coin listening to Katniss's conditions in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">Mockingjay - Part 1</a>.</i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p>According to the terms of the deal, Katniss would be the rebels' figurehead, known as the Mockingjay, and go along with their plans for her in exchange for:
</p><ol><li>The right to keep <a href="/wiki/Buttercup" title="Buttercup">Buttercup</a> as a family pet.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The right to hunt with <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> in the woods outside District 13.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The right to have Gale by her side on the battlefield and during the making of <a href="/wiki/Propo" title="Propo">propos</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>An official pardon and criminal immunity for captured <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> being held by <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>— <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>— to be announced in public.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Annie_Cresta" title="Annie Cresta">Annie Cresta</a> was added to the immunity list just before the announcement later that day.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The right to "flip" Coin for the opportunity to kill President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ol>
<p>When Coin made her public announcement, she introduced a sixth condition of her own: if Katniss deviated from the rebels' mission, the deal would be considered broken, and both she and the captured victors would be at the mercy of District 13 law to decide their fates.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Outcome">Outcome</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_Deal%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2><p>
The Mockingjay Deal protected four victors from retaliation by the rebels,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_4_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_4-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which likely contributed to them surviving the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></p><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Katniss_in_her_Mockingjay_Suit.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20151015162957" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss suited up as the Mockingjay at Snow&#39;s execution." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/7/7b/Katniss_in_her_Mockingjay_Suit.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151015162957" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="110" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss in her Mockingjay Suit.jpeg" data-image-key="Katniss_in_her_Mockingjay_Suit.jpeg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_in_her_Mockingjay_Suit.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss suited up as the Mockingjay at Snow's execution in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">Mockingjay - Part 2</a>.</i></p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The Mockingjay Deal was mostly carried out by Katniss and the rebels, with several significant exceptions. First, Katniss defied orders when she was sent to cover the <a href="/wiki/Bombings#Bombing_of_District_8_(Second_Rebellion)" title="Bombings">bombing of District 8</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_7_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_7-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and second, when she was deployed to the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Capitol" title="Battle of the Capitol">Battle of the Capitol</a> with propaganda unit <a href="/wiki/Squad_451" title="Squad 451">Squad 451</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_18_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_18-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Katniss instead led her own mission to assassinate Snow at his <a href="/wiki/President%27s_mansion" title="President&#39;s mansion">mansion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_20_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_20-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This mission was ultimately unsuccessful, resulting in the deaths of eight squad members.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_24_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_24-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Finally, Katniss was appointed to carry out the <a href="/wiki/Execution_of_President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="Execution of President Snow">execution of President Snow</a>, but she assassinated Coin in his place.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following an emergency election, <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">President Paylor</a> was instated, and Katniss was kept in solitary confinement at the <a href="/wiki/Training_Center" title="Training Center">Training Center</a> during her <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/trial_in_absentia" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:trial in absentia">trial in absentia</a>. She was found <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insanity_defense" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Insanity defense">not guilty by reason of insanity</a>, but as a condition of her release, she had to continue treatment with <a href="/wiki/Dr._Aurelius" title="Dr. Aurelius">Dr. Aurelius</a> over the phone, and she was confined to <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> until further notice.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMockingjay_Deal%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The term "Mockingjay Deal" was coined by an unknown rebel.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The Mockingjay Deal was made while Katniss was still suffering residual effects from the concussion <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> gave her at the end of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. She mentioned having a headache, and it took her twenty minutes to write down her simple list of terms before presenting them for negotiation.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_3_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_3-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
