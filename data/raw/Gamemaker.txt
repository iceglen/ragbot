<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Tasks">Tasks</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGamemaker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Training">Training</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGamemaker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Gamemakers observed the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> over three days of training, culminating in <a href="/wiki/Training_Center#Private_sessions" title="Training Center">private sessions</a> after lunch on the third afternoon. Each tribute would get around fifteen minutes to showcase their abilities.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Then, based on their performance, the Gamemakers would assign them a <a href="/wiki/Training_score" title="Training score">training score</a> that would be announced on television that evening. Scores ranged from 1 to 12, with 1 being the lowest and 12 being the highest.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Interviews">Interviews</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGamemaker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the <a href="/wiki/City_Circle" title="City Circle">City Circle</a>, the Gamemakers watched the tributes' <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a> from a large balcony on a building to the right of the stage.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_9_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Arenas">Arenas</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGamemaker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">Arena</a></i></center></div><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 250px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f5/Gamemakersala.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120219172918" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Gamemakers in the ." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f5/Gamemakersala.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20120219172918" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="129" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Gamemakersala.jpg" data-image-key="Gamemakersala.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Gamemakersala.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Gamemakers in the <a href="/wiki/Control_Room" title="Control Room">Control Room</a>.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>From their <a href="/wiki/Control_Room" title="Control Room">Control Room</a>, Gamemakers exerted their influence over the arena each year. They decided on the location, biome, plant life and <a href="/wiki/Wildlife" title="Wildlife">wildlife</a>, <a href="/wiki/Arena_wear" title="Arena wear">arena wear</a>, <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>, and other resources available to tributes<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>— both from the arena itself and the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, drawing competitors in for a <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">bloodbath</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Gamemakers also chose what the audience got to see on television from their many hidden cameras.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_12_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_12-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Additionally, Gamemakers controlled the weather inside the arena, like the temperature<sup id="cite_ref-THG_20_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_20-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or precipitation.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It was also within their power to trigger events such as avalanches,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Fire" title="Fire">fires</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and tidal waves.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_22_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_22-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the films, they could even make it seem like it was a different time of day within the arena than it truly was outside.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> These manipulations were usually intended to drive tributes together to fight for the audience's entertainment, but the Gamemakers occasionally killed a tribute just to remind the others that they could.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_13_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_13-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, three Gamemakers were mopping and taking care of the oozing slime on the forest floor of the arena. When <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, <a href="/wiki/Silka_Sharp" title="Silka Sharp">Silka Sharp</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Maritte" title="Maritte">Maritte</a> came across them, they tried to flee to <a href="/wiki/Sub-A" class="mw-redirect" title="Sub-A">Sub-A</a>, but all were killed. One had a <a href="/wiki/Trident" class="mw-redirect" title="Trident">trident</a> thrown at him by Maritte, another was attacked by one of Maysilee's darts, and the third plummeted to her death after she leaped headfirst into Sub-A, cracking her head on the concrete.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Muttations">Muttations</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGamemaker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Muttation" title="Muttation">Muttation</a></i></center></div>
<p>The Gamemakers created genetically altered muttations to weaponize against tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_14_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_14-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Mutts could be sent out and recalled at will, presumably going to and from the <a href="/wiki/Catacombs" title="Catacombs">catacombs</a> beneath each arena.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Announcements">Announcements</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGamemaker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Whenever a tribute died, the Gamemakers set off the sound of a cannon, marking the death for the remaining tributes. The only exception was the Cornucopia bloodbath, in which case they waited until the fight ended to tally up the deceased. Every evening, they played the <a href="/wiki/Anthem" class="mw-redirect" title="Anthem">anthem</a> and showed images of the fallen in the sky. This was done both as a recap for the audience and to help the remaining tributes strategize.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_11_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_11-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Gamemakers would sometimes project the voice of <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> into the arena so he could announce events such as <a href="/wiki/Feast" title="Feast">feasts</a>, enticing tributes to gather and fight. During the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, they also had him announce rule changes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_18_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_18-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-THG_25_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_25-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games" title="The Hunger Games">The Hunger Games</a></i> novel, there were around twenty Gamemakers, both men and women, dressed in deep purple robes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Head Gamemaker could be identified because their robe had a fur-trimmed collar.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the film adaptations, all but the Head Gamemaker wore simple white uniforms in the Control Room.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_film_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_film-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Head_Gamemakers">Head Gamemakers</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGamemaker%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

If the President was dissatisfied, the Head Gamemaker could face <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
