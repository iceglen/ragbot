</p><p><b>Patina Miller</b> is an American actress and singer who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Commander_Paylor" title="Commander Paylor">Commander Paylor</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">2</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPatina_Miller%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 2005, Miller was one of three finalists for the role of Effie White in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreamgirls" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dreamgirls">Dreamgirls</a></i>, which ultimately won Jennifer Hudson the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress. In 2007, she was cast in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_My_Children" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:All My Children">All My Children</a></i> and appeared in 30 episodes of the daytime soap opera. She performed in a <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Park" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Central Park">Central Park</a></i> production of <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Hair">Hair</a></i> in the summer of 2008 and was featured in the musical Romantic Poetry at the Manhattan Theatre Club that fall. She is known best for her role in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_Act" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sister Act">Sister Act</a></i>. Miller won a Tony Award for her performance as the Leading Player in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pippin_(musical)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pippin (musical)">Pippin</a></i> on Broadway in 2013.
</p>
