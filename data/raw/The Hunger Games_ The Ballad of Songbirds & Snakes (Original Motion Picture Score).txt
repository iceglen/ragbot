</p><p><i><b>The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Original Motion Picture Score)</b></i> is the official film score for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a>.</i> As with all previous installations to the franchise, the score was composed by <a href="/wiki/James_Newton_Howard" title="James Newton Howard">James Newton Howard</a>. It also features piano music by <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuja_Wang" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Yuja Wang">Yuja Wang</a>.
</p><p>Music created for the film that was not part of its score is included on the album <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes_(Music_From_%26_Inspired_By)" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes (Music From &amp; Inspired By)</a></i>.
</p>
