<p>The <b>50th Hunger Games arena</b> is where the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> or second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> took place. Following the abolition of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, this and all other arenas were destroyed and replaced with memorials.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is the main location of <i>Sunrise on the Reaping</i>.
</p><p>The arena was built in the shape of an eye, with the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a> acting as the pupil, the meadow making up the iris, and the mountain and forest terrain narrowing to points on the north and south sides to form the whites of the eye. This reflected how <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> was always watching.<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR25_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR25-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Terrain">Terrain</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F50th_Hunger_Games_arena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> were <a href="/wiki/Launch_Room" title="Launch Room">launched</a> on their pedestals in a ring around the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia" title="Cornucopia">Cornucopia</a>, which sat in the middle of a green meadow dotted with colorful flower patches. The sky was azure blue, and the arena had a pleasant smell. The meadow went on for miles, with woods in one direction and a snowcapped mountain in the other. On the fourth day, the mountain turned out to be a volcano that spewed lava, killing a dozen tributes and forcing the surviving ones to take cover in the woods.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The ash wasn't real or hot, but was made of a gel that inflicted chemical burns.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It dissolved like sugar crystals by a light rainfall which returned to arena to the same pristine condition as it was at the start.<sup id="cite_ref-SR19_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR19-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1a/SOTR_Trailer_22.jpeg/revision/latest?cb=20251120170307" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Haymitch and Maysilee walking through the burned hedge" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/1/1a/SOTR_Trailer_22.jpeg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251120170307" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="95" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="SOTR Trailer 22.jpeg" data-image-key="SOTR_Trailer_22.jpeg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:SOTR_Trailer_22.jpeg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Haymitch and Maysilee walking through the burned hedge</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>To the far north just outside the woods, the arena contained a dense hedge maze shaped into a v, that prevented tributes from traveling too close to the end of the arena. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> were only able to break through using a blowtorch, revealing flat, dry earth on the other side. A cliff was found at the end, beneath which were jagged rocks. The cliff was surrounded by a <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a> that would bounce back anything thrown at it—a characteristic that Haymitch later exploited to secure his victory.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The bushes were full of fruit that contained a lethal poison. The scent produced by the flowers was also deadly when deliberately inhaled.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The berms which led to <a href="/wiki/Sub-A" class="mw-redirect" title="Sub-A">Sub-A</a> featured their own poisonous flower identified by a small brass plaque. Known flowers are buttercups<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, crocus, tiger lily, pansy, bee balm, a gas plant<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, a butterfly bush<sup id="cite_ref-SOTR17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOTR17-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and holly.<sup id="cite_ref-SR19_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR19-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Food_and_water_sources">Food and water sources</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F50th_Hunger_Games_arena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>While there were crystalline streams in the arena, the water they carried was poisonous, with the only drinkable source being the rainwater. Similarly, the food found in the Cornucopia was the only safe source of food.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F50th_Hunger_Games_arena%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The arena being in the shape of an eye may be an allusion to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Orwell" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:George Orwell">George Orwell</a>'s dystopian novel <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineteen_Eighty-Four" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Nineteen Eighty-Four">1984</a>, where the eyes of Big Brother were used in propaganda to represent constant surveillance and control.</li>
<li>The filming location for the arena is held in <a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somiedo_Natural_Park">Somiedo Natural Park</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
