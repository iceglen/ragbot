</p><p><b>Mrs. Lipp</b> was an unnamed woman from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, implied to be the wife of <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Lipp" title="Mayor Lipp">Mayor Lipp</a> and mother of <a href="/wiki/Mayfair_Lipp" title="Mayfair Lipp">Mayfair Lipp</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Lipp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The mayor, this woman, and Mayfair rode in their car to the <a href="/wiki/Hanging_tree" title="Hanging tree">hanging tree</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a> of <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a>. She stepped out of the car after her husband, and the three of them formed a tight knot at the side of the platform.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_22_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_22-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> According to <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, the Lipps were despised by the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Coal_miner" title="Coal miner">coal miners</a> because of their money, power, and privilege. She specifically mentioned the Lipps riding in their "flashy car" to watch the hangings,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_27_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_27-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> suggesting that this may have been a common occurrence.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMrs._Lipp%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
