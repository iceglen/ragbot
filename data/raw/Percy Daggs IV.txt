</p><p><b>Percy Daggs IV</b> is an American actor set to play <a href="/wiki/Ampert_Latier" title="Ampert Latier">Ampert Latier</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping">The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</a></i>. He is the son of Percy Daggs III, who is known for his acting role as Wallace Fennel on  <i>Veronica Mars</i>.
