<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWoodbine_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Woodbine Chance and other boys in his family were considered wild by other residents of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>. <a href="/wiki/Hattie_Meeney" title="Hattie Meeney">Hattie Meeney</a> refused to sell them her white liquor for fear they'd get into trouble with the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>, so they bought it from <a href="/wiki/Bascom_Pie" title="Bascom Pie">Bascom Pie</a> instead.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Woodbine was the fourth child to be reaped for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> that called for four instead of two children to be reaped. He was to be the last tribute after <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>. After hearing his name called, Chance decided to run with the encouragement of his family. He ran towards a nearby alleyway where he was shot in the head from the rooftop of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  His death caused disarray as onlookers either tried to flee or help Woodbine. As the Peacekeepers tried to restore order, Woodbine's mother tried to prevent them from taking his body away. His death also signaled the need for another male tribute to be reaped, and <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a> <a href="/wiki/Drusilla_Sickle" title="Drusilla Sickle">Drusilla Sickle</a> chose <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR2-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWoodbine_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Haymitch described Woodbine as the youngest and the handsomest of the Chance boys, "running like greased lightning."<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FWoodbine_Chance%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>He shared the initials "W.C." with the other male tribute for District 12, <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>.</li>
<li>As suggested by his surname, Woodbine Chance may be related to <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a>, possibly a distant cousin.</li>
<li>Woodbine is the first death to occur in <a href="/wiki/Sunrise_on_the_Reaping" title="Sunrise on the Reaping">Sunrise on the Reaping</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch</a> grabs "one of the Chance girls, no more then eight" when he arrives at his house burning. This confirms that Woodbine had at least two living female relatives, likely around his age or younger.</li></ul>
