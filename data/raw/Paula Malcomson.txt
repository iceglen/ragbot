</p><p><b>Paula Malcomson</b>, also known as Paula Williams, is a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Ireland" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Northern Ireland">Northern Irish</a> actress. She portrayed <a href="/wiki/Mrs_Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mrs Everdeen">Mrs Everdeen</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_2" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2">Part 2</a></i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FPaula_Malcomson%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Malcomson was born in 1970, in Belfast, Northern Ireland. Malcomson began her career in the early 1990s with small parts primarily in film before scoring higher-profile roles in the Oscar-nominated <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Green_Mile_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:The Green Mile (film)">The Green Mile</a></i> (1999) and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.I._Artificial_Intelligence" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:A.I. Artificial Intelligence">A.I. Artificial Intelligence</a></i> (2001). In 2006, she joined the cast of HBO's <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadwood" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Deadwood">Deadwood</a></i> (2004) in the role of Trixie, and would also go on to appear in creator David Milch's next series, <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_from_Cincinnati" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:John from Cincinnati">John from Cincinnati</a></i> (2007). From then on, Malcomson made appearances in a number of high-profile series, including <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ER" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:ER">ER</a></i> (1994), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lost">Lost</a></i> (2004), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Law &amp; Order: Special Victims Unit">Law &amp; Order: Special Victims Unit</a></i> (1999), <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Crime_Scene_Investigation" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:CSI: Crime Scene Investigation">CSI: Crime Scene Investigation</a></i> (2000), and had regular roles on both <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sons_of_Anarchy" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Sons of Anarchy">Sons of Anarchy</a></i> (2008) and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caprica" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Caprica">Caprica</a></i> (2009). As her list of television appearances steadily grew, she also was cast in the role of Katniss' mother in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"> film</a> (2012), <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> (2013), and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Mockingjay_-_Part_1" title="The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1">The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1</a> </i>(2014)<i>.</i> She is now a regular on the Showtime original series,&#160;<i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Donovan" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Ray Donovan">Ray Donovan</a></i> (2013).
</p>
