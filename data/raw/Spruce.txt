<p><b>Spruce</b> was a man from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> who was involved in rebellious activities. He was a generally agreeable individual who wanted to free his sister <a href="/wiki/Lil" title="Lil">Lil</a> and escape the influence of <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSpruce%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Spruce attempted to restrain Lil during <a href="/wiki/Arlo_Chance" title="Arlo Chance">Arlo Chance</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Execution" title="Execution">execution</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and he later planned to free Lil from the <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers%27_base" title="Peacekeepers&#39; base">Peacekeepers' base</a>, knock the guards out, and escape north past the fence of District 12. <a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> intended on going along with them.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> encountered Spruce, Sejanus, and <a href="/wiki/Billy_Taupe_Clade" title="Billy Taupe Clade">Billy Taupe Clade</a> working on their escape plan in a shed behind <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>, Spruce pointed a shotgun at him, but relented when Sejanus said Snow was with him. Despite having gathered a fairly large cache of <a href="/wiki/Weapons" title="Weapons">weapons</a>, he thought that more might be needed. When <a href="/wiki/Mayfair_Lipp" title="Mayfair Lipp">Mayfair Lipp</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Lipp" title="Mayor Lipp">mayor</a>'s daughter, stumbled upon the scene, Spruce told her that they had no quarrel with her and suggested she come with them. However, she balked at the idea, and when it became obvious she planned to rat them out, he raised his gun to shoot her. Billy Taupe knocked the barrel towards the floor. Snow then shot her instead.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Billy Taupe threatened to kill <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> (in the film he instead threatened to kill Coriolanus Snow), so Spruce shot him through the chest without hesitating. He then told Lucy Gray that she had better go before somebody came looking, and stuffed the weapons back into his bag. He suggested that Snow should come with them up north, as otherwise he would likely end up paying for what had happened.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Spruce made his way to the <a href="/wiki/Lake" title="Lake">lake</a>, setting down the bag of weapons in the remaining house there.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_30_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_30-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, he was later captured by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a>; Snow witnessed him badly wounded and entering the clinic from an ambulance, evidently having gotten into a confrontation with them. Sometime not long after, he died of his wounds, and most people believed that he had been involved with the murders.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_28_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_28-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
