</p><p><b>Gary Ross</b> is an American screenwriter, director, and producer. <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)">The Hunger Games</a></i> is the third feature film that he has directed following <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleasantville_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pleasantville (film)">Pleasantville</a></i> and <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seabiscuit_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Seabiscuit (film)">Seabiscuit</a></i>, both of which he also produced.
</p><p>On April 10, 2012, Ross released a joint statement with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lions_Gate_Entertainment" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Lions Gate Entertainment">Lions Gate Entertainment</a> announcing that he would not direct the sequel to <i>The Hunger Games.</i>&#160;He cited the time needed to write and prep the movie given its tight production schedule as the deciding factor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGary_Ross%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><i>The Hunger Games</i> is Ross' first film not to star William H Macy or Tobey Maguire.</li>
<li>He has worked with <a href="/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks" title="Elizabeth Banks">Elizabeth Banks</a> twice.</li>
<li>He dropped out of the eventually canceled Spider-Man 4 to do <i>The Hunger Games</i>.</li>
<li><i>The Hunger Games</i> is Ross' first film not to be scored by Randy Newman, who did the score for <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy_Story_3" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Toy Story 3">Toy Story 3</a></i>, which was written by <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i> screenwriter <a href="/wiki/Michael_Arndt" title="Michael Arndt">Michael Arndt</a>.</li>
<li>He read <i>The Hunger Games</i> in one night.</li>
<li>His daughters introduced him to <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy"><i>The Hunger Games</i> trilogy</a>.</li></ul>
