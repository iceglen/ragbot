</p><p><b>Imanol Yepez-Frias</b> is primarily a theatre actor who portrayed the <a href="/wiki/District_9_male_(74th_HG)" title="District 9 male (74th HG)">District 9 male</a> <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, his major motion picture debut.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Imanol is also an expert pianist and has been playing since he was 6.
</p><p>However, the actual fight scene his character is known for is doubled by <a href="/wiki/Sam_Ly" title="Sam Ly">Sam Ly</a>. The roles of the tributes that fell during the initial bloodbath are mostly filled by stunt actors.
</p>
