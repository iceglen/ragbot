These outfits were themed after their <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>' main industries.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChariot_costumes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> was the second <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>, so as a cruel twist, twice the number of tributes were <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reaped</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>: two boys and two girls from each district, for a total of 48.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the parade, <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, <a href="/wiki/Louella_McCoy" title="Louella McCoy">Louella McCoy</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a> all wore the typical coal miners' outfits.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>In his first year as a stylist, <a href="/wiki/Cinna" title="Cinna">Cinna</a> and his partner <a href="/wiki/Portia" title="Portia">Portia</a> dressed <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a> in matching outfits meant to represent burning coal. The tributes wore black unitards and leather boots, with capes and matching headpieces made of streams of yellow, orange, and red. These capes were lit with synthetic fire using a torch, and later extinguished with a canister. Katniss and Peeta's costumes outshone everybody else's, making them instant standouts.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_5_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_5-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This also earned Katniss her nickname as the "Girl on Fire."<sup id="cite_ref-THG_10_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_10-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChariot_costumes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>Kids in costumes are silly, but aging victors, it turns out, are pitiful.... the majority, who are in the clutches of drink or morphling or illness, look grotesque in their costumes, depicting cows and trees and loaves of bread.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>&#8212;Katniss's observations on the opening ceremonies<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Cinna and Portia's stunt with the fire the year before inspired a number of copycats to illuminate their own tributes' costumes for the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>' Tribute Parade. District 3 <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victors</a> <a href="/wiki/Beetee_Latier" title="Beetee Latier">Beetee Latier</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a> wore outfits studded with electric lights, and District 10's tributes were dressed as cows in flaming belts.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Additionally, an unknown district's tributes were dressed like loaves of <a href="/wiki/Bread" title="Bread">bread</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> this was presumably District 9 or District 11, since both had food-producing industries.
</p>

<h4><span id="District_12_.2875th_HG.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="District_12_(75th_HG)">District 12 (75th HG)</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChariot_costumes%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<p>Katniss and Peeta's faces were covered with dramatic highlights and dark shadows to bring out the edges. They each wore a black jumpsuit from the neck down and half of a heavy crown made out of black medal, similar to the ones they had been given as victors the year before. At the press of a button inside the fabric, the costume would illuminate and perfectly mimic the changing colors of glowing embers. Combined, the elements of the costume made Katniss and Peeta look intimidating, powerful, and deadly.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_15_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_15-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><br />

<ul><li>In <i>The Hunger Games</i> film, Katniss and Peeta's CGI flames were designed to look like wings.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In that same film, the District 7 tributes wore fan-like origami paper headdresses and paper clothes, rather than the usual tree costumes described in <i><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">Catching Fire</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
