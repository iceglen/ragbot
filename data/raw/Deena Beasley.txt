</p><p><b>Deena Beasley</b> is an American actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Thresh%27s_grandmother" title="Thresh&#39;s grandmother">Thresh's grandmother</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>. She is best known for her roles in films such as&#160;<i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0828461/">Trust</a>,</i>&#160;<i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1999929/">Mystic Rising</a></i>, and <i><a target="_blank" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1095217/">The Bad Lieutenant: Port of Call - New Orleans</a></i>.
