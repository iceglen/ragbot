</p><p><b>Zoe Renee</b> is an actress who portrayed <a href="/wiki/Lysistrata_Vickers" title="Lysistrata Vickers">Lysistrata Vickers</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FZoe_Renee%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Renee starred opposite Regina Hall in the Amazon Studios thriller <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_(2022_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Master (2022 film)">Master</a></i>, which premiered at the 2022 Sundance Film Festival. She was also seen in Disney+'s high school basketball story <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang_Can_Dunk" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Chang Can Dunk">Chang Can Dunk</a></i> in 2023. Renee is best known for her breakout role in the critically acclaimed festival feature <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinn_(2018_film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jinn (2018 film)">Jinn</a></i>, where she played Summer. She followed Jinn with the motion picture indie <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gully_(film)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Gully (film)">Gully</a></i> opposite Kelvin Harrison Jr., Amber Heard, and Terrance Howard. 
</p><p>She is represented by PANTHEON, Elevate Entertainment, and Meyer &amp; Downs.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
