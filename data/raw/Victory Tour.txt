<p>A <b>Victory Tour</b> was an annual occasion when <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> required the most recent <a href="/wiki/Victor" title="Victor">victor</a> to travel across <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> with their <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>, <a href="/wiki/Prep_team" title="Prep team">prep team</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Stylist" title="Stylist">stylist</a>. Designed to dishearten the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a> and reinforce the Capitol's power over them, the tour was placed "almost midway" between the annual <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a> in order to keep the horror fresh and immediate.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Before the Victory Tour commenced, the prep team and stylists were to arrive at the victor's residence in the Victors' Village, preparing them for public appearances.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_3-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The first appearance was a brief interview at their home in the Victors' Village, after which they immediately left for the districts.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Districts">Districts</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Generally, the tour began in <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, then went in descending district order to 1, then the Capitol. The victor's own district was skipped in the order and saved for a final celebration. The victor gave a speech in each location, written by their escort and mentor, typically giving thanks to the Capitol for their "generosity" and offered condolences to the fallen tributes' family members. They can also add their own personal comments if they had any allies from a particular district. Victors were also sometimes presented with plaques<sup id="cite_ref-CF_4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_4-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or given tours of the places they visited. The victor would then attend a dinner before boarding the train again and going onto the next district.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_5_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_5-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>The tour concluded in the victor's home district. A large dinner would be held, courtesy of the Capitol, then a victory rally the next day in the town square marked the final day of the tour.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a> found <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, victor of the 50th Hunger Games, on the morning that would begin the Victory Tour, sleeping with a knife in his hand. She apologized to him for his family's "accident" and <a href="/wiki/Lenore_Dove_Baird" title="Lenore Dove Baird">Lenore Dove Baird</a>'s death by appendicitis, having swallowed the story made up by President <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a> to cover his murder of Haymitch's family. She gave Haymitch coffee, cleaned up in a bathtub and then prepped by <a href="/wiki/Proserpina_Trinket" title="Proserpina Trinket">Proserpina Trinket</a> and <a href="/wiki/Vitus" title="Vitus">Vitus</a> before boarding a train to <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">District 11</a>.
</p><p>On the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a>, Haymitch discovered that <a href="/wiki/Plutarch_Heavensbee" title="Plutarch Heavensbee">Plutarch Heavensbee</a> was there to record his Victory Tour. Haymitch drunk his way through the festivities in District 11 and was taken aside to a secret attic area by Plutarch, who informed him of certain bits of secret information and told Haymitch that he and others needed his help to one day overthrow the Capitol. Haymitch was skeptical of the idea and avoided speaking to him for the rest of the Victory Tour. At the end, he told Plutarch he had nothing to live for, stating that Plutarch thought he was a good person, but that he had done nothing but put the Heavensbee spin on Capitol propaganda. To this, Plutarch replied that he was at least still in the game.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_27_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_27-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<p>This was the 64th Victory Tour chronologically, and the first to ever celebrate dual victors, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peeta_Mellark" title="Peeta Mellark">Peeta Mellark</a>. It took place during a period of <a href="/wiki/Pre-Second_Rebellion_civil_unrest" title="Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest">civil unrest</a> inspired by their rebellious actions during the Games, so they were under threat from President Snow to sell their love story not only to the districts, but to him, or he would retaliate against their families.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FVictory_Tour%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Since the reaping occurs on July 4th<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the Victory Tour is held "almost midway" between the Games, it most likely takes place 4-6 months later, sometime between the start of November and the end of January.
<ul><li>Katniss mentioned that District 12 always celebrated their <a href="/wiki/Harvest_Festival" class="mw-redirect" title="Harvest Festival">Harvest Festival</a> on the final day of the tour.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Her description of the holiday resembles American <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanksgiving" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Thanksgiving">Thanksgiving</a>, which is celebrated in mid-November.</li></ul></li></ul>
