			<details class="trfc161__group dark-theme" data-question-id="37292" data-revision-id="2">
				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">What significant event happened to Mrs. Everdeen's best friend during the 50th Hunger Games?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						In the 50th Hunger Games, or the second Quarter Quell, Maysilee Donner, Mrs. Everdeen's best friend, was selected as a female tribute. Unfortunately, she was killed in the arena. Mrs. Everdeen and Mrs. Undersee were televised during the reaping.
					</div>
					<div class="trfc161__answer-source">
						Provided by: Fandom
					</div>
					<div class="quick_answers__report-module-root" data-reportable="1"></div>
				</div>
			</details>
			<details class="trfc161__group dark-theme" data-question-id="37293" data-revision-id="2">
				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">How did Mrs. Everdeen cope with the loss of her best friend, Maysilee Donner?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						Maysilee Donner's parents entrusted her pet canary to Mrs. Everdeen following Maysilee's death. This gesture served as a means for Mrs. Everdeen, also known as Katniss Everdeen's mom, to cope with her best friend's loss. She shared her sorrow with Mrs. Undersee, Maysilee's sister. However, she refrained from discussing Maysilee with her daughters, probably to evade the topic of her death.
					</div>
					<div class="trfc161__answer-source">
						Provided by: Fandom
					</div>
					<div class="quick_answers__report-module-root" data-reportable="1"></div>
				</div>
			</details>
			<details class="trfc161__group dark-theme" data-question-id="37294" data-revision-id="2">
				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">What role does Mrs. Everdeen play in the Hunger Games series?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						In the Hunger Games series, Mrs. Everdeen, Katniss Everdeen's mother, is a healer and former merchant/pharmacist. After her husband's death in a mining accident, she fell into depression, neglecting her children and forcing Katniss to become the family's provider. She lived in several locations, including The Seam, Victors' Village in District 12, District 13, and District 4. Notably, she saved Gale's life.
					</div>
					<div class="trfc161__answer-source">
						Provided by: Fandom
					</div>
					<div class="quick_answers__report-module-root" data-reportable="1"></div>
				</div>
			</details>
			<details class="trfc161__group dark-theme" data-question-id="37295" data-revision-id="2">
				<summary class="trfc161__question-wrapper">
					<b class="trfc161__question">Did Mrs. Everdeen survive the events of the Hunger Games?</b>
						<img class="trfc161__question-chevron" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/36002e46-06fe-4633-9d99-27d96cf5bcdc" alt="toggle section">
				</summary>
				<div class="trfc161__answer">
					<div class="quick-answers__answer-text">
						Mrs. Everdeen, Katniss Everdeen's mother, did survive the Hunger Games. Following Primrose's death, she relocated from District 12 to District 4, and is noted to be alive during Katniss' later years.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="50th_Hunger_Games">50th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On the day of the reaping for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, Asterid was working in the apothecary. <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> visited and traded some game and wildcrafting. He gave her a sprig of chamomile and told her it would bring her good luck. <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a> later visited to trade white liquor and she also gave him a sprig of chamomile, and blushed when he asked if Burdock told her it was good luck.<sup id="cite_ref-SR1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR1-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Her best friend <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a> was chosen as one of two female <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, also known as the second Quarter Quell, along with <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>. Asterid, along with Merrilee, was seen on the television during the reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Maysilee died, leaving her sister, <a href="/wiki/Merrilee_Undersee" title="Merrilee Undersee">Merrilee Donner</a> and Asterid in their grief. Maysilee's parents gave Maysilee's songbird to Asterid after Maysilee's death in the arena.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Haymitch realized <a href="/wiki/President_Snow" class="mw-redirect" title="President Snow">President Snow</a> would not let anybody he cares for stay alive, he started drawing off from all his loved ones. Burdock kept trying to hold on, and he brought Asterid, who took Haymitch sleeping medicine. To get them to leave, Haymitch threw rocks at Asterid and Burdock, hitting Asterid on the forehead, drawing blood. Hurting Asterid felt worse for Haymitch than everything he suffered in the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Asterid was the daughter of an apothecary and grew up in the merchant sector of district twelve. During those years, she learned to care for illnesses and injuries. When she married her husband, she moved into the Seam to live with him. Since the Seam is much poorer than the merchant sector, Katniss took that as a sign that she loved her husband very much.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <i>Catching Fire</i>, she still had a tea service set that she had taken with her.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_2_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_2-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>She has two daughters, Katniss and Primrose. Her husband died in a coal mining accident five years before the events of <i>The Hunger Games</i> book. She also has two grandchildren, an elder girl and younger boy, from Katniss and Peeta.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_E_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_E-28"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Primrose died in <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_24_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_24-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_27-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> leaving Asterid unable to go back to District 12 due to the pain of her death.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Work">Work</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Though never formally trained, Asterid is a healer, with extensive knowledge of natural remedies from having grown up in the apothecary in the merchant's district. Following her recovery from her grief over her husband's death, Asterid started a small apothecary business for people in the Seam.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She has helped many people in the Seam who go to her for various things including both injury and sickness. In <i>Catching Fire</i>, Haymitch tells Katniss that there used to be a lot of whipping before the Head Peacekeeper <a href="/wiki/Cray" title="Cray">Cray</a> took over and that it was Katniss' mother that people would go to for aid.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Head Peacekeeper <a href="/wiki/Thread" class="mw-redirect" title="Thread">Thread</a> whips Gale<sup id="cite_ref-CF_7_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_7-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> (and accidentally Katniss<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>) and Asterid treats Gale and Katniss with the help of her youngest daughter, Prim.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_16-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Relationships">Relationships</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Katniss_Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After being emotionally abandoned by her mother for months after <a href="/wiki/Mr._Everdeen" class="mw-redirect" title="Mr. Everdeen">her father</a>'s death, <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss</a> came to distrust her and never truly forgave her.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss also took over the role of caring for Prim. After volunteering,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-29"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Katniss told Asterid that she had to take care of Prim, and her mother promised to.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_11-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After the <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a> is announced, Asterid screamed.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/Mockingjay" title="Mockingjay">Mockingjay</a></i>, after Prim's death,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_24_26-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_24-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_27-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> she buried her grief in her work.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_27-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> She moved to District 4, abandoning Katniss again. However, they bonded over the phone, grieving Prim's death. Asterid helped Katniss with her <a href="/wiki/Memory_book" title="Memory book">book</a> for remembering the good in the world.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Primrose_Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Prim</a> was very close to her mother and completely forgave her for abandoning her daughters after Burdock's death. Together, they held an apothecary business. After the District 12 bombing,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-19"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> they took care of the wounded until they were rescued by <a href="/wiki/District_13" title="District 13">District 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_1_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_1-20"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> After Prim's death,<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_24_26-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_24-26"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MJ_25_27-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_25-27"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Asterid was devastated and could not go back to District 12.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_27_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_27-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Burdock_Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Asterid loved <a href="/wiki/Burdock_Everdeen" title="Burdock Everdeen">Burdock Everdeen</a> to the point where she left her merchant life for <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a> so she could be with him. After he died, she fell into a deep depression and left her two young daughters to fend for themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Gale_Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D13&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After he is whipped by <a href="/wiki/Thread" class="mw-redirect" title="Thread">Thread</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale</a>'s wounds are treated by Asterid and Prim, though Asterid did not have a close relationship with the boy.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_8_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_8-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CF_9_16-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_9-16"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Otho_Mellark" title="Otho Mellark">Otho Mellark</a> had always loved Asterid, but she "ran off with a coal miner".<sup id="cite_ref-THG_22_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_22-30"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her surname, Everdeen, comes from Bathsheba Everdene, the main character in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_from_the_Madding_Crowd" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Far from the Madding Crowd">Far from the Madding Crowd</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Asterid could come from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/aster_(genus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:aster (genus">aster</a>, a flowering plant symbolizing patience, love, and healing—reflecting her role as a healer and caregiver throughout the series. It also relates to the Scandinavian name Astrid, meaning "beloved by the gods". The surname March references springtime and rebirth and the time when the flowers begin to bloom. The surname also subtly aligns with qualities mirrored in the March family from <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Women" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Little Women">Little Women</a></i>, a family known for healing, and overcoming difficult times.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FAsterid_Everdeen%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Although her name is not revealed in the original books trilogy or films, Paula Malcolmson, who played Mrs. Everdeen, revealed in an interview that she named her character 'Clara'. Malcolmson said the name "just came to her".<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Interestingly, burdock (her future husband) and asters (represented by "Asterid") are both plants that are part of the Asteraceae family, commonly known as the daisy or sunflower family.</li></ul>
