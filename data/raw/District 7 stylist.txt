<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7_stylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Under her, District 7's tributes were all dressed as trees in every <a href="/wiki/Chariot_rides" title="Chariot rides">Tribute Parade</a>, for which <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna Mason</a> angrily described her as being "the biggest idiot in <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In the movie, Johanna even said she'd "love to put [her] <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Axe" title="Weapons">axe</a> in her face."<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Assuming her time as District 7's stylist was all consecutive, her career would have begun around the 35th <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. She would remain with the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> for forty years until the Games were abolished. As a result, she likely styled Johanna in both the <a href="/wiki/71st_Hunger_Games" title="71st Hunger Games">71st</a> and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>. It's also possible that she was a stylist to <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a> in his first Games, along with either the <a href="/wiki/District_7_male_(74th_HG)" class="mw-redirect" title="District 7 male (74th HG)">District 7 male</a> or <a href="/wiki/District_7_female_(74th_HG)" title="District 7 female (74th HG)">District 7 female</a> during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FDistrict_7_stylist%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire" title="The Hunger Games: Catching Fire">The Hunger Games: Catching Fire</a></i>, she briefly appeared backstage during the <a href="/wiki/Interviews" title="Interviews">interviews</a>, kneeling beside Johanna to adjust her dress. The stylist wore a full-body, mini check-patterned jumpsuit, covering her face with a similarly patterned mask that only had holes for her eyes and mouth. The ensemble also had matching boots, as well as a cone-shaped hat, and round flaps on either side of her head to mimic ears.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<ul><li>Costume designer <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trish_Summerville" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Trish Summerville">Trish Summerville</a> identified the outfit as belonging to Johanna's stylist, and she described it as being a tribute to <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Paul_Gaultier" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Jean Paul Gaultier">Jean Paul Gaultier</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Capitol_Couture" title="Capitol Couture">Capitol Couture</a> described this outfit as instead belonging to an <a href="/wiki/Avox" title="Avox">Avox</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, the District 7 tributes were shown at the parade wearing fan-like origami paper headdresses and paper clothes rather than tree costumes.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Considering the length of her time as a stylist, it's possible that she styled tributes from District 7 in two different Quarter Quells: the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> and the 75th Hunger Games.</li></ul>
