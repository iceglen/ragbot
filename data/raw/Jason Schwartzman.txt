<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJason_Schwartzman%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Schwartzman is a Gotham, SAG and WGA Award nominee who was seen as the lead in Wes Anderson's <i>Asteroid City</i>, opposite many well-known actors. In 2023, he performed in a starring role opposite Awkwafina and Sandra Oh in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quiz_Lady" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Quiz Lady">Quiz Lady</a></i> from director Jessica Yu, and also voiced a role in <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_Across_the_Spider-Verse" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse">Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse</a></i>.
</p><p>Schwartzman is represented by UTA, Ocean Avenue Entertainment and Sloane, Offer, Weber &amp; Dern.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FJason_Schwartzman%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>During filming for <i>The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i>, Schwartzman decided that Lucky Flickerman needed a "nemesis", so with permission from <a href="/wiki/Festus_Creed" title="Festus Creed">Festus Creed</a>'s actor <a href="/wiki/Max_Raphael" title="Max Raphael">Max Raphael</a>, he singled out Festus to pick on. According to Raphael, "We've got a whole backstory here. Festus, maybe he wants a career in broadcasting. Maybe Lucky babysat him when he was a kid, and he's just never gotten over how annoying he was. I don't know. That's for the fans to decide.”<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
