<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_Life">Early Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Cashmere was born in District 1, and had an older brother, Gloss. She was trained from birth to compete in the Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-Clichemag_Interview_Article_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Clichemag_Interview_Article-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="64th_Hunger_Games">64th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Cashmere participated in and won the 64th Hunger Games at the age of 15,<sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> the year after her brother <a href="/wiki/Gloss" title="Gloss">Gloss</a> won the 63rd Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is likely that she <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteered</a>. Her brother may have mentored her. Because of her <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career tribute</a> status, good looks, and her brother's fame, Cashmere probably had a lot of <a href="/wiki/Sponsorship" title="Sponsorship">sponsors</a> and killed a considerable amount of tributes.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mentorship">Mentorship</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>It is likely that Cashmere and Gloss mentored District 1 tributes in the 65th-73rd Hunger Games, which includes the victor of the 67th Hunger Games, <a href="/wiki/Augustus_Braun" title="Augustus Braun">Augustus Braun</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="74th_Hunger_Games">74th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Cashmere, along with her brother Gloss, mentored the District 1 tributes, <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-exhibition_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-exhibition-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="75th_Hunger_Games">75th Hunger Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Cashmere was reaped along with her brother, Gloss, for the third Quarter Quell. According to <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, she and her brother were "<a href="/wiki/Capitol" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitol">Capitol</a> favorites" who he expected to have "lots of sponsors" during the Quell.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is unlikely they volunteered,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> as they like many other victors attempted to evoke sympathy within the Capitol to stop the Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>It is implied that both Gloss and Cashmere were friends with some of the existing victors.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_16_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_16-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Additionally, Cashmere and her brother mentored <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a> and <a href="/wiki/Marvel" title="Marvel">Marvel</a> in the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-exhibition_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-exhibition-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The siblings appeared to be secretly against the idea of going back to the <a href="/wiki/Arena" title="Arena">arena</a>, as they both made speeches to try and convince the Capitol to call off the Quell.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Despite this, they still became deadly in the arena and were not a part of the <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">alliance</a>.
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D10&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>

<p>On the second day of the Games, the Career pack managed to sneak up on the rebel alliance (now consisting of Katniss, Peeta, Finnick, <a href="/wiki/Johanna_Mason" title="Johanna Mason">Johanna</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beetee" class="mw-redirect" title="Beetee">Beetee</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wiress" title="Wiress">Wiress</a>), as they formulated a plan to avoid the various disasters around the arena and kill the other remaining victors. While Wiress was separated from the others, Gloss slit her throat, prompting Katniss to shoot him with one of her arrows. Immediately after Gloss's death, Cashmere charged at Katniss with a knife, apparently intent on killing her, but Johanna dug her <a href="/wiki/Weapons#Axe" title="Weapons">axe</a> into Cashmere's chest, killing her. Cashmere's cannon sounded instantly afterwards, and Brutus and Enobaria barely escaped with their lives. Cashmere's body was thrown into the water when the Cornucopia spun.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_23_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_23-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Cashmere placed 10th out of 24 tributes and 9th in the film.
</p>

</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Victims">Victims</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Due to the fact that Cashmere was a Career tribute, it is likely she killed a fair amount of tributes during the 64th and 75th Hunger Games. It was confirmed that she killed <a href="/wiki/Seeder" title="Seeder">Seeder</a>.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Gloss">Gloss</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D14&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="quote">
<dl><dd><span title="Source: Catching Fire">"<i>There's the classically beautiful brother and sister from District 1 who were victors two consecutive years when I was little.</i>"</span></dd>
<dd>—Katniss<sup class="noprint"><a href="/wiki/Catching_Fire" title="Catching Fire">[src]</a></sup></dd></dl></div>
<p>Cashmere's brother Gloss was also a tribute in the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, as well as the victor of the 63rd Hunger Games. They were known to have a strong relationship, although Cashmere seemed to have control over Gloss, and they fought side by side until the very end.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D15&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Cashmere and her brother Gloss were both described as being "classically beautiful", <sup id="cite_ref-CF_14_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_14-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and before the tribute interviews, she was noted to have flowing blond curls.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> This is a trait she had in common with another District 1 female tribute, <a href="/wiki/Glimmer" title="Glimmer">Glimmer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> It is probable that she and Gloss were physically stronger than most of the other victors, being relatively young and from District 1.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D16&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Her name comes from a type of expensive woolen fabric, as <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">District 1</a>'s industry is luxury goods.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCashmere%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Cashmere and Gloss are the only known pair of siblings who have won the Hunger Games.</li>
<li>Enobaria, Gloss, Cashmere, and Finnick consecutively won the <a href="/wiki/62nd_Hunger_Games" title="62nd Hunger Games">62nd</a>, 63rd, 64th, and <a href="/wiki/65th_Hunger_Games" title="65th Hunger Games">65th Hunger Games</a>.</li>
<li>In the film, Cashmere was shown to have a skill in balancing pointed weapons, such as a knife, in her hand.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Cashmere and her brother were interviewed together by Caesar in the film,<sup id="cite_ref-CF_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> while in the book, they were interviewed separately.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_17_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_17-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In a behind the scenes featurette, a cameraman asked Cashmere's actress <a href="/wiki/Stephanie_Leigh_Schlund" title="Stephanie Leigh Schlund">Stephanie Leigh Schlund</a> if she had killed anybody yet in the bloodbath. She replied, "I have. I killed Bobby," referring to <a href="/wiki/Bobby_Jordan" title="Bobby Jordan">Bobby Jordan</a> who played <a href="/wiki/Blight" title="Blight">Blight</a>. However, the footage of their fight was ultimately cut since Blight was meant to be killed by the <a href="/wiki/Force_field" title="Force field">force field</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the same featurette, Gloss's actor <a href="/wiki/Alan_Ritchson" title="Alan Ritchson">Alan Ritchson</a> mentioned that he and Schlund had quickly developed a "brotherly-sisterly kind of love". They messed around with each other on set, which created the sense of a real sibling relationship.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-14"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup></li>
<li>She is the last ever District 1 tribute to die in the Hunger Games.</li></ul>
