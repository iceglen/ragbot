<p>The <b>73rd Hunger Games</b> took place one year prior to the start of the <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_trilogy" title="The Hunger Games trilogy">trilogy</a>. These Hunger Games were won by an <a href="/wiki/District_2_male_(73rd_HG)" title="District 2 male (73rd HG)">unnamed boy</a>, the male <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a>.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Arena_uniform"><a href="/wiki/Arena_wear#News_reel" title="Arena wear">Arena uniform</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F73rd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In the film, both tributes shown wore black shoes, black cargo pants, and black, cropped armored vests underneath their colorful jackets. These appeared to correspond with the district color coding also used for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>: red with black stripes for District 2, and gray-brown with yellow stripes for District 10.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Pre-Games">Pre-Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F73rd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Effie_Trinket" title="Effie Trinket">Effie Trinket</a>, the District 12 <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a>, mentioned that the tributes from 12 had poor table manners that year, complaining that they ate like "a couple of savages". According to <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a>, both had been from <a href="/wiki/The_Seam" title="The Seam">the Seam</a>, so they'd had never had a decent meal in their lives.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_3-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games_(film)" title="The Hunger Games (film)"><i>The Hunger Games</i> film</a>, this was <a href="/wiki/Seneca_Crane" title="Seneca Crane">Seneca Crane</a>'s second year as Head <a href="/wiki/Gamemaker" title="Gamemaker">Gamemaker</a>, as the following year was his third and final.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Games">The Games</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F73rd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 220px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c8/Giphy-0.gif/revision/latest?cb=20180517042047" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The boy from 2 killing the boy from 10, making him the victor." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/c/c8/Giphy-0.gif/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20180517042047" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="124" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Giphy-0.gif" data-image-key="Giphy-0.gif" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Giphy-0.gif" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The boy from 2 killing the boy from 10, making him the victor.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>In the film, Katniss briefly watched news reel footage on the <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">train</a> to <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>. This footage portrayed the ending of the 73rd Hunger Games', commentated on by Caesar Flickerman and <a href="/wiki/Claudius_Templesmith" title="Claudius Templesmith">Claudius Templesmith</a> looking back on the event as "the moment when a tribute becomes a victor." The victor was a <a href="/wiki/Career_Tribute" title="Career Tribute">Career Tribute</a>, an unnamed boy from District 2, who bludgeoned the boy from District 10 to death with a brick.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F73rd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D8&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The victor of this Games would have been one of 32 unknown victors who were killed by either Capitol or <a href="/wiki/Rebel" class="mw-redirect" title="Rebel">rebel</a> forces during the <a href="/wiki/Victors%27_Purge" title="Victors&#39; Purge">Victors' Purge</a>, after the third <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_script">Early script</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F73rd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In an early version of the script, the ruined city was said to be <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspen,_Colorado" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Aspen, Colorado">Aspen, Colorado</a>, 300 years in the future. The boy from 12 was named Sage Blanken, and his parents, <a href="/wiki/Corrina_Blanken" title="Corrina Blanken">Corrina</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tygart_Blanken" title="Tygart Blanken">Tygart Blanken</a>, were vendors at <a href="/wiki/The_Hob" title="The Hob">the Hob</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_script_draft_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_script_draft-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>The District 12 female was killed sometime on the first day. Unlike a typical Games where the <a href="/wiki/Cornucopia_bloodbath" title="Cornucopia bloodbath">Cornucopia bloodbath</a> would occur on the first day, a bloodbath had taken place on the second day, with at least 8 tributes in attendance: Sage, Prospero from District 3, an unknown girl, an semi-impaled tribute, and four more unknown tributes using a knife, spear, whip, and bow. Sage narrowly escaped the tributes pursuing him only to be killed with a mace by Prospero.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_script_draft_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_script_draft-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>While they were hunting in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a> told Katniss who he thought would win, guessing correctly that it would be the boy from 2.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_script_draft_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_script_draft-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In interviews for the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>, Caesar mentioned to <a href="/wiki/Cato" title="Cato">Cato</a> that the 72nd and 73rd Hunger Games were both won by <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">District 2</a> tributes.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_script_draft_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_script_draft-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2F73rd_Hunger_Games%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D11&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>This is the final Hunger Games which ended normally with a single victor. After an attempted double suicide, the 74th Hunger Games had two victors,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a> were interrupted by the destruction of the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games_arena" title="75th Hunger Games arena">arena</a>, so a victor was never crowned.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_27_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_27-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>This was the last victory of a Career Tribute, as the 74th Hunger Games were won by District 12, and the 75th had no victor.
<ul><li>However, another District 2 victor, <a href="/wiki/Enobaria" title="Enobaria">Enobaria</a>, would go on to survive both the 75th Hunger Games and the <a href="/wiki/Second_Rebellion" title="Second Rebellion">Second Rebellion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MJ_26_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MJ_26-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
