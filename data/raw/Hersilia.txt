</p><p><b>Hersilia</b> was a trainer for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a> who taught knife-throwing.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHersilia%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Hersilia taught a lesson in knife throwing to the <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> from <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> (<a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maysilee_Donner" title="Maysilee Donner">Maysilee Donner</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Wyatt_Callow" title="Wyatt Callow">Wyatt Callow</a>) and <a href="/wiki/District_7" title="District 7">District 7</a> (<a href="/wiki/Bircher" title="Bircher">Bircher</a>, <a href="/wiki/Autumn" title="Autumn">Autumn</a>, <a href="/wiki/Heartwood" title="Heartwood">Heartwood</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Ringina" title="Ringina">Ringina</a>). After instructing them, Hersilia chose the order that the tributes threw in. Haymitch and Maysilee proved to be particularly talented at it, with Haymitch sticking the target every time. <a href="/wiki/Ringina" title="Ringina">Ringina</a> also hit a bullseye.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Haymitch was attacked by <a href="/wiki/Panache_Barker" title="Panache Barker">Panache Barker</a>, Hersilia confiscated the tributes' weapons. Ringina hesitated before putting her knife in Hersilia's basket. When Hersilia handed Haymitch a knife back to resume training, he briefly considered plunging it into a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeeper's</a> heart.<sup id="cite_ref-SR7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR7-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
