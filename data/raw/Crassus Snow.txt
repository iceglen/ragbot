<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrassus_Snow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="School_years">School years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrassus_Snow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Crassus attended the <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> and the <a href="/wiki/University" title="University">University</a> with <a href="/wiki/Casca_Highbottom" title="Casca Highbottom">Casca Highbottom</a>, who would later go on to become the Dean of the Academy. The two were best friends and would go to <a href="/wiki/Pluribus_Bell" title="Pluribus Bell">Pluribus Bell</a>'s nightclub every night.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, their friendship only lasted until the end of their time in the University, when they had a major falling-out.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_23_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_23-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Both of them studied under Dr. <a href="/wiki/Volumnia_Gaul" title="Volumnia Gaul">Volumnia Gaul</a>. Highbottom was failing Gaul's class due to his loathing of her, which made it impossible for him to willingly participate in her classes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Gaul assigned her students a final project: to create a punishment for one's enemies so extreme that they would never be allowed to forget how they had wronged them. Crassus paired up with Highbottom for the project, getting him more and more drunk to draw out his darkest impulses - which led to the idea of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>. He assured Highbottom that the whole thing was just a private joke, but then turned in the project to Dr. Gaul while Highbottom was sleeping off the alcohol. Having heard his idea had been submitted by Crassus, Highbottom confronted his best friend in Bell's nightclub. Crassus, instead of admitting his mistake, told Highbottom that he should be grateful because he did them a big favor. Highbottom never forgave him for his betrayal.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Later_life">Later life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrassus_Snow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>At some point, Crassus took a <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_mother" title="Coriolanus Snow's mother">wife</a>, and they had their son Coriolanus five years before the Dark Days. Crassus enlisted as a <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Peacekeeper">Peacekeeper</a>, believing that military service enhanced his legitimacy in the family business. He quickly climbed the ranks and was<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>He and his wife conceived a <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow%27s_sister" title="Coriolanus Snow's sister">second child</a>, a daughter, sometime before the Dark Days began. His wife went into labor in the early months of the war, and she died in childbirth alongside the baby.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrassus_Snow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Not long after the death of his wife, Crassus was killed by a rebel bullet, leaving Coriolanus an orphan.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_5_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_5-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_The_Ballad_of_Songbirds_%26_Snakes" title="The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes">The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</a>,</i> Crassus was killed by rebels in the <a href="/wiki/Woods" title="Woods">woods</a> outside <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrassus_Snow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>General Snow was honored on December 15, National Heroes Day, along with around a dozen other Capitol citizens.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Crassus owned a dress shirt, which he wore along with a suit and various medals he had won. This was later altered by his niece, <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>, so Coriolanus could wear it to the <a href="/wiki/Reaping" title="Reaping">reaping</a> for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the Games, Coriolanus exposed <a href="/wiki/Snake_mutt" title="Snake mutt">snake mutts</a> to the scent of his <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tribute</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a>, by dropping his father's <a href="/wiki/Crassus_Snow%27s_handkerchief" title="Crassus Snow's handkerchief">handkerchief</a> into their tank. He knew they would not attack a familiar scent,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_18_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_18-10"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> so this saved her life when the snakes were released into the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Arena" title="Capitol Arena">Capitol Arena</a> the following day.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_19_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_19-11"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The handkerchief was one of three pieces of evidence used to condemn Coriolanus for cheating to help Lucy Gray win.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_20_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_20-12"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_description">Physical description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrassus_Snow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>The <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma'am">Grandma'am</a> said Coriolanus had his father's pale blue eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the film adaptation, Crassus was depicted in stained glass portraits with short, slicked-back platinum blond or gray hair and blue eyes. A photograph on his son's desk showed him wearing a dark, double-breasted blazer<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> that may have been his military uniform.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personality_and_traits">Personality and traits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrassus_Snow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Crassus Snow was known to have an excellent head for strategy and nerves of steel. He was also remote and strict, which was why Coriolanus never felt great love for him, but he had always felt protected.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_3_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_3-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> However, Pluribus Bell described him and Casca Highbottom to be "a pair of wild things" while at the nightclub in their university years.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_11_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_11-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Highbottom sees Crassus as morally corrupt, as he cared about his grade far more than he cared about the cruelty and carnage the Hunger Games would bring. It is possible that similar to his best friend Highbottom, he was also failing Gaul’s class due to their wild attitude, leading to his ruthless decision in their final project.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_Epilogue_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_Epilogue-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Coriolanus remembered him for his deep voice and uncompromising words against the <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">districts</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_12_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_12-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Tigris said that what she remembered most about him was the hate in his eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_film_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_film-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FCrassus_Snow%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D9&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crassus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Crassus">Crassus</a> is the name of a Roman politician and general who was a member of the “First <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triumvirate" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Triumvirate">Triumvirate</a>”, alongside <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pompey" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Pompey">Pompey</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesar" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Caesar">Caesar</a>. He was also known as the richest man in Rome.
</p><p>His middle name, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xanthus" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Xanthus">Xanthos</a>, means yellow, blond, or fair-haired.
</p>
