<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p><b>"Gem of Panem"</b>&#160;was <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a>'s anthem during the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a> and the early days of the <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGem_of_Panem%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The song was performed by the <a href="/wiki/Grandma%27am" title="Grandma&#39;am">Grandma'am</a>, <a href="/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow" title="Coriolanus Snow">Coriolanus Snow</a>'s grandmother, in a quavery, off-key voice. In the first year of the war, she played the recording on national holidays for young Coriolanus and <a href="/wiki/Tigris" title="Tigris">Tigris</a>. When <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> rebels surrounded the Capitol, the Grandma'am began to warble the anthem through a window daily as an act of defiance while their home was under siege, bombs raining down around her and her family.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_1-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>"Gem of Panem" played at the beginning of the early Hunger Games <a href="/wiki/The_Reaping" class="mw-redirect" title="The Reaping">reapings</a>. Everybody listening was required to rise and sing along.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The song was also performed during important events such as funerals. Due to his knowledge of the song from his grandmother, Coriolanus was able to give a strong performance of it during <a href="/wiki/Arachne_Crane" title="Arachne Crane">Arachne Crane</a>'s funeral, impressing his instructors and <a href="/wiki/President_Ravinstill" class="mw-redirect" title="President Ravinstill">President Ravinstill</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_9_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_9-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A recording of his singing was later replayed for the funeral of <a href="/wiki/Diana_Ring" title="Diana Ring">Diana</a> and <a href="/wiki/Apollo_Ring" title="Apollo Ring">Apollo Ring</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_10_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_10-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Despite Coriolanus' familiarity with the anthem, other <a href="/wiki/Academy" title="Academy">Academy</a> students fumbled for the words.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_13_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_13-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Sejanus_Plinth" title="Sejanus Plinth">Sejanus Plinth</a> cited the lyrics of the song when trying to make an argument against the Hunger Games, saying that <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s government was supposed to protect everyone and give them light, not just citizens of the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_6_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_6-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a>, when <a href="/wiki/Dr._Kay" title="Dr. Kay">Dr. Kay</a> first introduced Coriolanus to the <a href="/wiki/Mockingjay_(bird)" title="Mockingjay (bird)">mockingjays</a>, he sung the anthem for the birds to mimic and harmonize.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_26_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_26-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The song was still in use 40 years later, prior to the reaping for the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>. According to <a href="/wiki/Haymitch_Abernathy" title="Haymitch Abernathy">Haymitch Abernathy</a>, district citizens were supposed to sing along, but instead just mumbled whatever.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_1_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_1-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Lyrics">Lyrics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGem_of_Panem%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="poem">
<p>Gem of Panem<br />
Mighty city<br />
Through the ages you shine anew<br />
<br />
We humbly kneel<br />
To your ideal<br />
And pledge our love to you!<br />
<br />
Gem of Panem<br />
Heart of Justice<br />
Wisdom crowns your marble brow<br />
<br />
You give us light<br />
You reunite<br />
To you we make our vow<br />
<br />
Gem of Panem<br />
Seat of power<br />
Strength in peacetime, shield in strife<br />
<br />
Protect our land<br />
With armored hand<br />
Our Capitol<br />
Our life
</p>
</div>
