<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a title="Sign in to edit" class="mw-editsection-visualeditor" data-tracking-label="log-in-edit-section" data-action="edit-section" href="https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthehungergames.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMax_Raphael%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en" data-testid="log-in-edit-section"><svg class="wds-icon wds-icon-tiny"><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-pencil-tiny"></use></svg></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Raphael is a British writer, filmmaker and actor. He is known for starring in <i>Slaughterhouse Rulez</i>, <i>A Plague Tale: Innocence</i>, and <i>School's Out Forever</i>. His last project, <i>A People's Tapestry</i>, is a collection of monologues about living in lockdown and was made in collaboration with Drink The Ink and Nick Hern Books. 
</p><p>Raphael is represented in the UK by A&amp;J Artists and in the US by A3 Artists.<sup id="cite_ref-Deadline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deadline-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<ul><li>During filming for <i>The Ballad of Songbirds &amp; Snakes</i>, <a href="/wiki/Jason_Schwartzman" title="Jason Schwartzman">Jason Schwartzman</a> decided that <a href="/wiki/Lucky_Flickerman" class="mw-redirect" title="Lucky Flickerman">Lucky Flickerman</a> needed a "nemesis", so with permission from Max Raphael, he singled out Festus to pick on. According to Raphael, "We've got a whole backstory here. Festus, maybe he wants a career in broadcasting. Maybe Lucky babysat him when he was a kid, and he's just never gotten over how annoying he was. I don't know. That's for the fans to decide.”<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
