<p>The <b>reaping</b> was an annual event that took place in every <a href="/wiki/District" class="mw-redirect" title="District">district</a> before each <a href="/wiki/Hunger_Games_(event)" title="Hunger Games (event)">Hunger Games</a>, in which <a href="/wiki/Tribute" title="Tribute">tributes</a> of the upcoming Games were chosen. It serves as the opening event in the week immediately prior to the start of the Hunger Games.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After the districts' defeat in the <a href="/wiki/Dark_Days" title="Dark Days">Dark Days</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Capitol" title="The Capitol">the Capitol</a> signed the <a href="/wiki/Treaty_of_Treason" title="Treaty of Treason">Treaty of Treason</a> into law. Among the treaty's many provisions was the implementation of the Hunger Games. The reaping was developed as a means of selecting the tributes to participate in the Hunger Games through a lottery, although the initial implementation of the event gradually evolved into its modern form over the years.
</p><p>For earlier iterations of Games, the <a href="/wiki/Mayor" title="Mayor">mayor</a> of each district was the one responsible for the selection of the male and the female tribute, instead of an <a href="/wiki/Escort" title="Escort">escort</a> sent from the Capitol. During the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mayor_Lipp" title="Mayor Lipp">Mayor Lipp</a> read out the names of <a href="/wiki/Lucy_Gray_Baird" title="Lucy Gray Baird">Lucy Gray Baird</a> and <a href="/wiki/Jessup_Diggs" title="Jessup Diggs">Jessup Diggs</a> in District 12<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>. It is unknown why or when the Capitol started sending escorts to lead the reaping, but since some reapings were rigged so certain tributes would be picked, it may have been an attempt to make it harder for the mayors to use the reapings to their personal advantage.
</p><p>In District 12, Reaping Day was the second-most likely day for people to get drunk, behind New Year's.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2c/Hunger_Games_Exhibition_Map.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150423151439" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="A map indicating the reaping times in the districts" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/2/2c/Hunger_Games_Exhibition_Map.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150423151439" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Hunger Games Exhibition Map.jpg" data-image-key="Hunger_Games_Exhibition_Map.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Hunger_Games_Exhibition_Map.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">A map indicating the reaping times in the districts</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Process">Process</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Reaping_ball.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419092314" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Effie Trinket, District 12&#39;s escort, standing next to one of the reaping bowls." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/f/f7/Reaping_ball.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120419092314" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="230" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Reaping ball.jpg" data-image-key="Reaping_ball.jpg" data-relevant="1" /></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Reaping_ball.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Effie Trinket, District 12's escort, standing next to one of the reaping bowls.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>The reaping is the opening event for the Hunger Games, held on July 4th during the early afternoon, at 2:00pm in the case of <a href="/wiki/District_12" title="District 12">District 12</a> during the <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th Hunger Games</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, the reaping took place earlier for <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">Second Quarter Quell</a>, as Haymitch assumed the reaping would already be over by 1:00pm.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The date of the reaping was always on July 4th. As <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a> spans the breadth of North America, the reapings are not held at the same time for each district due to differing time zones. As such, the&#160;reaping proceeds from the east coast to the west coast, with the first four districts&#160;to hold reapings being 12, <a href="/wiki/District_8" title="District 8">8</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_6" title="District 6">6,</a> and <a href="/wiki/District_11" title="District 11">11</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Most likely because; by this time in the future; they're the districts that use Eastern Time, and the next districts would use Central Time, and so on.
</p><p>The reaping is a mandatory event for the entire district population, especially so for those still of reaping age. Failure to report to the reaping incurs severe penalties such as imprisonment. The only exemption given for attendance is if an individual is severely ill or close to death. Government officials make rounds through the district to confirm if this is the case, in the evening of the day before the reaping is to be held.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As the time for the reaping approaches, the district population is to assemble in the square outside of the <a href="/wiki/Justice_Building" title="Justice Building">Justice Building</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> (Hall of Justice in the films)<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> as was the case for the District 12 reaping, which was in the center of the town. The square is decorated with banners of Panem, with cameras crews set up around the perimeter of the square to both capture the event as well as the reaction of the crowd and selected tributes, as it is televised live for both the citizens of the Capitol and the nation. This is what is typically believed, however, there is, in fact a five-minute hold placed on the broadcast in the event that something should happen that the Capitol does not wish for viewers to see.<sup id="cite_ref-Sunrise_2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sunrise_2-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 200px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Katniss_reaping_sign.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419092035" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss&#39; finger is pricked for blood in order to identify her." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="126" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Katniss reaping sign.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_reaping_sign.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Katniss_reaping_sign.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20120419092035" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Katniss_reaping_sign.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20120419092035" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss&#39; finger is pricked for blood in order to identify her." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Katniss_reaping_sign.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20120419092035" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="126" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Katniss reaping sign.jpg" data-image-key="Katniss_reaping_sign.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/0/07/Katniss_reaping_sign.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20120419092035" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Katniss_reaping_sign.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss' finger is pricked for blood in order to identify her.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>As district citizens enter the square, they are required to register with government officials. In the film, citizens provide a blood sample to confirm their identity,<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> with Katniss commenting that the reaping allows the Capitol to keep tabs on the district population as an informal census and presumably confirm that all required citizens are in attendance. Each age group of reaping eligible children was contained in roped-off areas in the town square separated on each side by gender, with the oldest eligible children in the front and the youngest in the back. Adults and family members of the potential tributes are placed along the perimeter, while late-arriving citizens are herded to the streets adjacent to the square to watch the reapings live on large screens.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>On the stage in the front of the Justice Building, district government officials such as the mayor, the district escort, and previous Hunger Games <a href="/wiki/Victors" class="mw-redirect" title="Victors">victors</a> assemble. At 2:00pm, the mayor steps forward and recites the history of the founding of Panem, the Dark Days, the implementation and rules for the Hunger Games, and the spoils and riches that come from victory in it. Following this, the mayor reads off a list of the district's previous Hunger Games victors. Once this is done, the mayor hands the proceedings over to the district escort, who officially opens the reaping.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After a brief number of comments, the escort proceeds to select a name at random from the reaping bowls. Many citizens of the Capitol, as well as those from the districts, often <a href="/wiki/Betting" title="Betting">placed bets</a> on who would be chosen. It is not known if the order of selection is a codified rule or left to the discretion of the escort. However, for the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ballad_2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ballad_2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th</a><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, <a href="/wiki/74th_Hunger_Games" title="74th Hunger Games">74th</a><sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, and <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a><sup id="cite_ref-:0_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>, District 12's mayor or escort always called the female tributes first, followed by the males. Once an entry slip is selected, the tribute's name is announced by the escort, and the individual selected is to approach the stage where they are presented to the district. Following selection, another reaping-eligible child of the same sex may choose to <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">volunteer</a> in that tribute's place, and the same process is repeated for the other. Otherwise, the two tributes are formally announced as the district's representation in that year's Hunger Games. Following their selection, the mayor of the district recites the Treaty of Treason.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After this, the tributes are taken into custody, brought into the Justice Building, and each are taken to an extravagant room within, which is guarded by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeepers" title="Peacekeepers">Peacekeepers</a> to prevent the tribute from escaping. There, the tributes are allotted one hour to meet with family, friends, and others to say their goodbyes and make their final arrangements. Each set of visitors gets an unspecified allotment of time with the selected tribute through the hour, before they are escorted out by Peacekeepers. Once the hour is elapsed, the tributes are taken by Peacekeepers to a waiting car outside, which transports them and their district partner to the district train station. There they board a train, alongside their escort and <a href="/wiki/Mentor" title="Mentor">mentor(s)</a>, bound for the Capitol.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the 75th Hunger Games, the tributes were forced to forgo the visitation period, instead being taken directly to the train station.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/10th_Hunger_Games" title="10th Hunger Games">10th Hunger Games</a>, the tributes were taken to the Capitol in dirty <a href="/wiki/Train" title="Train">cattle cars</a> and transported to the <a href="/wiki/Capitol_Zoo" title="Capitol Zoo">Capitol Zoo</a> where they were dumped into the monkey enclosure.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Eligibility_and_Tesserae">Eligibility and Tesserae</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Tessera" title="Tessera">Tessera</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb mw-halign-left show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 276px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/Catchingfire_04384.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140519190629" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The District 12 reaping for the 75th Hunger Games" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="276" height="115" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Catchingfire 04384.jpg" data-image-key="Catchingfire_04384.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/Catchingfire_04384.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/276?cb=20140519190629" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/Catchingfire_04384.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20140519190629" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="The District 12 reaping for the 75th Hunger Games" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/Catchingfire_04384.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/276?cb=20140519190629" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="276" height="115" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Catchingfire 04384.jpg" data-image-key="Catchingfire_04384.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/84/Catchingfire_04384.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/276?cb=20140519190629" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Catchingfire_04384.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">The District 12 reaping for the 75th Hunger Games</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Each district's escort has the duty of selecting the name of one male and one female tribute from two separate <a href="/wiki/Reaping_balls" title="Reaping balls">glass balls</a>, both containing all entries of every eligible male and female potential tribute respectively of the district. Those picked are then the official tributes for the upcoming Games. Only people aged 12-18 are eligible to be reaped. When a person turns 19, they are officially ineligible to participate in the reaping and the Hunger Games. Similarly, past victors of the Hunger Games, even if they are still of reaping age, are exempt from further reapings.
</p><p>Once of age at twelve years old, a potential tribute's name was entered into the reaping one time. The entries were cumulative with each passing year, so when a possible tribute was thirteen, their name was added one more time and again at fourteen and so on. This continued until age eighteen, when a potential tribute's name was entered seven times.
</p><p>For the residents of the various districts, anyone between the ages of 12 through 18 could apply for tesserae. This supplied them with a year's worth of grain and oil. They could choose to do this for each of their family members as well. However, each tesserae they signed up for put another slip with their name in the reaping ball, which was also cumulative for each year they applied for tesserae. As such, sixteen-year-old <a href="/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen" title="Katniss Everdeen">Katniss Everdeen</a> had her name put in the glass ball twenty times, having applied for herself as well as her sister and mother for five years since the age of 12. Similarly, <a href="/wiki/Gale_Hawthorne" title="Gale Hawthorne">Gale Hawthorne</a>, who had five family members including himself, did the same for seven years and thus had his name put in the glass ball forty-two times. The amount of tesserae taken was larger in <a href="/wiki/Panem" title="Panem">Panem</a>'s poorer districts, such as District 12 and 11, but much rarer in the more affluent district of 1, 2, and 4.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_1_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_1-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Volunteering">Volunteering</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Volunteer" title="Volunteer">Volunteer</a></i></center></div><figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 288px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/98/Hunger-games-191.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130604130950" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss volunteering for Prim during the reaping of the 74th Hunger Games." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="288" height="121" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Hunger-games-191.jpg" data-image-key="Hunger-games-191.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/98/Hunger-games-191.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/288?cb=20130604130950" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/98/Hunger-games-191.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20130604130950" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Katniss volunteering for Prim during the reaping of the 74th Hunger Games." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/98/Hunger-games-191.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/288?cb=20130604130950" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="288" height="121" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Hunger-games-191.jpg" data-image-key="Hunger-games-191.jpg" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/9/98/Hunger-games-191.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/288?cb=20130604130950" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Hunger-games-191.jpg" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Katniss volunteering for Prim during the reaping of the 74th Hunger Games.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>If someone chose to, another reaping age person may elect to volunteer to take the place of a reaped tribute. Volunteering was made by vocal acclamation, typically to the effect of "I volunteer as tribute." The act of volunteering was rare in most of the districts, largely due to the prevailing view of being selected to participate in the Hunger Games seen as a death sentence, with Katniss being the first volunteer from District 12 in decades.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_2_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_2-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Reasons to volunteer varied from person to person, such as to protect and/or save the reaped tribute from participation as was the case for Katniss when she volunteered for her sister, <a href="/wiki/Primrose_Everdeen" title="Primrose Everdeen">Primrose Everdeen</a>, after she was reaped for the 74th Hunger Games. 
</p><p>Volunteering, however, was far more common in the <a href="/wiki/Career" class="mw-redirect" title="Career">Career</a> districts of <a href="/wiki/District_1" title="District 1">1</a>, <a href="/wiki/District_2" title="District 2">2</a>, and <a href="/wiki/District_4" title="District 4">4</a>, where participation in the Hunger Games is viewed as a great honor. In these districts, many young people are illegally trained in various forms of combat and survival in preparation for the Games since their childhood.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the movies, Careers receive this training in specialized academies, and at the age of 15-18, they volunteer to participate in the Hunger Games with great enthusiasm.<sup id="cite_ref-THG_film_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THG_film-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The rules regarding volunteering are never fully specified and thus remain unclear and vague. The volunteering process was simple in most districts due to its rarity, especially in the outlier districts. However, in the Career districts, volunteering was a more complicated process, as Katniss remarks the reaping in these districts took hours. How a tribute is ultimately selected if there is more than one volunteer is unclear as is likely the case in the Career districts. It is known that a reaping-aged person may only volunteer for a selected tribute that was the same sex as their own; a male volunteer could not take the place of a female reaped tribute, and vice versa. Similarly, an individual not of reaping age could not volunteer for a selected tribute. Additionally, the tribute who is being volunteered for could not in turn volunteer for the volunteer, as Haymitch remarks on what would happen should he be reaped in the lead up to the reaping for the 75th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Quarter_Quells">Quarter Quells</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="border:4px double #ffe600; border-radius:1em;"><center><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Quarter_Quell" title="Quarter Quell">Quarter Quell</a></i></center></div>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 200px"> 	<a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Brutus_enobaria_reaping.png/revision/latest?cb=20131206185242" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Previous District 2 victors, Brutus and Enobaria are reaped for the 75th Hunger Games." src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="134" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Brutus enobaria reaping.png" data-image-key="Brutus_enobaria_reaping.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Brutus_enobaria_reaping.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20131206185242" /></a> 	<noscript><a href="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Brutus_enobaria_reaping.png/revision/latest?cb=20131206185242" class="mw-file-description image"><img alt="Previous District 2 victors, Brutus and Enobaria are reaped for the 75th Hunger Games." src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Brutus_enobaria_reaping.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20131206185242" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="134" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Brutus enobaria reaping.png" data-image-key="Brutus_enobaria_reaping.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/thehungergames/images/8/8d/Brutus_enobaria_reaping.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20131206185242" /></a></noscript> 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a href="/wiki/File:Brutus_enobaria_reaping.png" class="info-icon"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 			<p class="caption">Previous District 2 victors, Brutus and Enobaria are reaped for the 75th Hunger Games.</p> 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p>Every 25 years, the tributes were chosen in an alternate manner as part of a special, quarter-century iteration of the Hunger Games known as the Quarter Quell. These special Hunger Games are held to keep the memory for holding the Games fresh with the populace, whilst adding a notable twist to the regular proceeding of the Games related to the actions of the rebels during the Dark Days. The instructions for the specific year of the Quell were specified inside a special envelope read by the <a href="/wiki/President" title="President">president</a> ahead of the reaping for these particular Hunger Games. The following were the special rules for the reaping of each held Quarter Quell:
</p>
<ul><li>For the <a href="/wiki/25th_Hunger_Games" title="25th Hunger Games">25th Hunger Games</a>, the First Quarter Quell, the districts voted on the tributes who were to be reaped.</li>
<li>For the <a href="/wiki/50th_Hunger_Games" title="50th Hunger Games">50th Hunger Games</a>, the Second Quarter Quell, twice the amount of tributes, two males and two females, were reaped for the Games.</li>
<li>For the <a href="/wiki/75th_Hunger_Games" title="75th Hunger Games">75th Hunger Games</a>, the Third Quarter Quell, the tributes were reaped from the existing pool of victors. For this Quarter Quell, it is widely believed that the original rules for these Games were changed, as part of an effort to force Katniss Everdeen, District 12's only living female victor, to participate again for her actions at the end of the 74th Hunger Games.<sup id="cite_ref-CF_12_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF_12-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As such, the original rules for these Games, if they exist, remain unknown.</li></ul>
