Jennifer Lawrence is an American actress who portrayed Katniss Everdeen in The Hunger Games, The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1, and The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2. She will reprise her role as Katniss in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Her casting as Katniss was approved. For her audition, she played a specific scene. A photo of her as Katniss Everdeen was revealed. She almost dropped out of the film due to an injury on set but continued. While on the set, an accident occurred with a co-star, who continued filming. A file of her vocals was used for the soundtrack of the film.

She has been nominated for and won many awards for her performance in The Hunger Games franchise.

For the first two films, she had to dye her naturally blonde hair brown. After filming one of the sequels, she cut her hair and wore a wig for the final two films. While filming, she went temporarily deaf in one ear because of underwater scenes.
