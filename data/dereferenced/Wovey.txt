Wovey was the female tribute from District 8 during the 10th Hunger Games. She was described as a small, pasty-faced girl. She wore a striped dress and a red scarf. Her mentor referred to her as a runt and noted she had zero personality, which hindered her ability to secure sponsors.

During the Games, she hid in the tunnels for the majority of the event. She survived for days without food or water. When she finally emerged, she appeared skeletal with limbs like sticks and sunken cheeks. She drank from a bottle of water she found and died shortly after. The water had been poisoned.

In a film adaptation, she hid in the tunnels until the final stages. She approached a dropped tank believing the Games were over and was killed by the snakes released from it.

Her name comes from "wove", the past tense of the verb "to weave."
