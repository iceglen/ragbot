The Second Rebellion was a major civil war which began approximately seventy-five years after the end of the Dark Days. The conflict pitted a totalitarian regime against the disparate rebel forces of the twelve outlying districts, supported and nominally led militarily by the forces of an independent district.

The war resulted in a rebel victory. The existing regime was deposed and a federal republic was established. The foundational treaty was repealed. Heavy infrastructural damage was inflicted upon the capital and the districts. One district was destroyed and another was reintegrated. The Hunger Games were definitively abolished.

The war was marked by indiscriminate surface-based and aerial artillery bombardments, as well as a confused mixture of asymmetrical and conventional ground warfare that resulted in widespread destruction of infrastructure and loss of life throughout the country. Fighting included the liberation of the districts and a final, destructive urban battle in the capital itself. The capital's defense was severely hindered by strategically placed traps. The final battle ended when a hovercraft dropped bombs on a group of children, forcing a temporary ceasefire. Believing the capital had bombed its own children, most resistance collapsed, ending the civil war.

In the aftermath, a new government was formed. Travel opened up between districts and the capital, allowing people to move freely, and districts could establish new industries. The Hunger Games arenas were demolished with memorials being built in their place.
