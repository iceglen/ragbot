Nightlock is a wild plant with extremely poisonous berries. The plant's effects are indisputable: consuming nightlock causes death very quickly. The only known sources for these deadly berries are the land around two districts, though they were also featured in an arena.

During a specific Games, a tribute stole nightlock berries and died after consuming them. Another tribute had mistaken them for edible berries. On the last morning of the Games, two tributes threatened to consume the nightlock berries to leave the games with no victor at all. Before they could swallow them, the rule was changed and both were announced as victors.

Later, a Head Gamemaker was executed using nightlock berries. During a rebellion, soldiers were given specifically formulated poison capsules to swallow in the event they were captured. Dubbed "nightlock", they were known to have the same effect as the berries themselves. Nightlock was also the keyword for a Holo used by a squad during an infiltration. When said three times in a row, the word caused the Holo to self-destruct.

The name "nightlock" is derived from two real-life plants, both of which are extremely poisonous. The berries of one are similar in appearance to nightlock, and both nightlock and a real-life plant are commonly mistaken for edible plants.
