The Snows' apartment building was a residential building located on a major street in the Capitol. The apartment building had a glass door from the lobby opening out onto the street. It also had an elevator with mirrored walls; however, said elevator had broken early into the Dark Days and would not be repaired for over ten years.

The Snow family lived in the building's penthouse, twelve flights of stairs up. They also had access to the roof, where a rose garden was tended. The apartment itself had twenty-foot-high walls and arched windows, though wartime bombings had caused considerable damage throughout. Financial hardship forced the occupants to sell or trade many of their possessions, or even burn them to keep warm. As a result, some rooms were vacant, sparsely furnished, or closed off altogether.

On the floor directly below the penthouse lived another family. However, the introduction of property taxes meant that family could no longer afford to live there. Another family ended up buying that sub penthouse. They also bought out the penthouse apartment for the original occupants, since they could not afford to keep it on their own. There was talk of putting in a spiral staircase and a private elevator between the two families' floors.
