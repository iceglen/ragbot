Corrina Blanken was a resident of District 12 whose son participated in the Hunger Games.

Corrina was only mentioned in an early version of a script for a film. She and her husband lived in the Seam, and their son was reaped for the Hunger Games. The son was killed on the second day as Corrina watched on a big screen in the town square, and she wailed in grief before she was led away.

Later that day, another character came across them at the Hob: the husband was in a fog as he tried to sell his wares, and Corrina was catatonic. The character gave the husband some berries, explaining that they would help Corrina sleep.

After the subsequent Hunger Games, Corrina was mentioned among the citizens celebrating in the town square. She was also at the train station when the victors returned.
