A canary is a small yellow bird in the finch family. They are songbirds.

Coal miners brought canaries down into the mines to warn them of bad air. The canary would stop singing, alerting them of danger so they could get out. However, if the air was too bad, the bird would die.

A tribute once had a pet canary. After she died, her parents gave the songbird to her friend.

During a competition, a participant was compared to a canary for her intuition. Soon after, she stopped singing right before an attack, just like a canary in a mine would.

Canaries were also used to detect bad air in real mines. They were sometimes replaced by other birds.
