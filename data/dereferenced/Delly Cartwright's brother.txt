Delly Cartwright's brother is the younger brother of Delly Cartwright.

He was born in District 12 and raised alongside his older sister in the merchant sector of town. Their parents ran the shoe shop, and it was typical for merchants to live above their stores, so the family likely lived right above theirs.
He and his sister both survived the bombing of District 12, though their parents did not. The siblings were evacuated to District 13 with the other refugees. They accounted for two of a very small number of total merchant survivors.

His surname is an occupational surname for a maker of carts.
