Katniss Everdeen is the main protagonist and narrator of The Hunger Games trilogy. After her younger sister was reaped in the 74th Hunger Games, she volunteered to take her place as the female tribute. This action set in motion the events of the entire series. She is the female victor from her district for the 74th Hunger Games, and the female tribute for the 75th Hunger Games.

She is a skilled hunter, adept with a bow and arrow, knives, and snares. Her primary motivation is to protect her family. She is strong-willed, independent, determined, and compassionate, but can be blunt and struggles with social interactions. She suffers from post-traumatic stress due to her experiences.

Physically, she has olive skin, gray eyes, and dark hair. She is small in stature but strong from her outdoor life. She is known by the symbolic titles "the Girl on Fire" and "the Mockingjay."

During the 74th Hunger Games, she forms alliances and uses her survival skills to win, ultimately threatening a double suicide to force a rule change that allows two victors from the same district. Her act is seen as defiance and sparks rebellion.

She is forced to compete again in a special edition of the Games for past victors. After being rescued from the arena by rebels, she becomes the symbolic leader of the rebellion. She participates in the war against the ruling power, during which her sister is killed.

After the war, she returns home. She eventually agrees to have children and builds a life with a fellow victor. She remains affected by her trauma but finds a measure of peace.
