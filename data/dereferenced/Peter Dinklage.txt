Peter Dinklage is an American actor who portrayed Casca Highbottom in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

He is best known for starring as Tyrion Lannister on the television series Game of Thrones, for which he won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series a record four times. He also received a Golden Globe Award and a Screen Actors Guild Award for the role.

He made his film debut in Living in Oblivion, and had his breakthrough with a starring role in The Station Agent. His other films include Elf, Lassie, Find Me Guilty, Penelope, Death at a Funeral, The Chronicles of Narnia: Prince Caspian, X-Men: Days of Future Past, Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri, Avengers: Infinity War and My Dinner with Hervé. He also provided voice-acting for the video game Destiny, and voiced a character in Transformers: Rise of the Beasts.

He has also performed in theater, with roles including the title character in Shakespeare's Richard III, Rakitin in A Month in the Country, and Cyrano de Bergerac in Cyrano.
