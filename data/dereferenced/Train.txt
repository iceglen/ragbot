Trains are the primary method of travel throughout the nation. They are mainly reserved for the shipment of one district's goods and materials to another, and for the transportation of officials. Most citizens are not authorized to travel by train unless they are reaped for the annual games, as travel between the districts is forbidden except for officially sanctioned duties. Each district has at least one train station where trains stop to load and unload their cargo.

Trains are the primary mode of transportation of all the districts' goods, most of which gets delivered to the capital. The cargo trains used to transport tributes had heavy metal chains and padlocks securing the livestock cars they were carried in. During a rebellion, cargo trains were used as a mode of transportation for deploying troops across the nation and to the capital itself.

Capital trains travel at high speed and were often used to transport tributes to the capital for the games in less than a day. Tributes were given separate compartments, each with a bedroom, a bathroom, and a dressing area containing luxury clothes. The interior design is very plush and expensive, featuring a bar car, polished paneled walls, tables, and a variety of foods and refreshments for the tributes. However, it was believed that every word spoken on a capital train would be recorded. The last car of the train has chairs and couches, as well as back windows that retracted into the ceiling to reveal an open view of the landscape.
