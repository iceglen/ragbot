The SJ-7 Peacekeeper Assault Transport is the only ground armored military vehicle used in service.

The vehicle's windows are equipped with bulletproof glass, and its armor can hold against high caliber small arms fire and explosives. The V-Hull design can protect passengers from detonations even if a detonation occurs beneath the vehicle. The vehicle's liquid cooled engine can generate significant power, and its suspensions allow the vehicle to transport and deploy assets in any kind of environment.
