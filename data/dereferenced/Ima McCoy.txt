Ima McCoy is the older sister of Louella McCoy.

The McCoys lived in the Seam. Her younger sister was one of two female tributes reaped for the 50th Hunger Games. When officials made them stay to stage reaction shots after the reaping, they refused to give them what they wanted, staring with stony expressions.

Ima laundered a suit and polished shoes for a funeral.

Ima is one of several brothers and sisters. She is older than Louella. It is unknown if Ima is the same sister that another character said wore coal dust for powder.
