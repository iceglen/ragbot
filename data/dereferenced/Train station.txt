Train stations are centers where trains load and unload their cargo or disembark passengers. There is at least one station in each district, as trains are the primary method of inter-district travel and the movement of both raw and finished materials. Most citizens will not make personal use of the train stations, as the oppressed people of the districts are prohibited from traveling to other districts or the Capitol via train unless they are tributes, victors, or otherwise authorized to do so.
