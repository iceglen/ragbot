Palladium Barker was the victor of the 46th Hunger Games from District 1. They were related to Panache Barker, one of the two male District 1 tributes in the 50th Hunger Games. Presumably, Palladium is either Panache's cousin or sibling, due to the close proximity in age between them and Panache.

Palladium was presumably a Career Tribute in the 46th Hunger Games and likely volunteered to participate.

It is likely that Palladium mentored their relative Panache in the 50th Hunger Games.
As District 1 victors are known to continue training even after they win, it is likely that Palladium continued their training.

Although the cause and time of death are unknown, Palladium was dead by the end of the Second Rebellion, as they were absent for the vote on a final 76th Hunger Games. It's possible they were a victim of the Victors' Purge.

Palladium's gender is never specified.
