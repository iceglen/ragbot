Pawns No More: Making the Hunger Games: Mockingjay Part 2 is a feature-length documentary film about the making of The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2. It was included as a featurette on DVD releases.

The documentary is organized into eight sections: Walking Through Fire: Concluding the Saga; Real or Not Real: Visual Design; High Value Targets: The Acting Ensemble; From Head to Toe: Costume, Makeup & Hair; Navigating the Minefield: Production; Collateral Damage: Stunts, Special Effects & Weapons; Tightening the Noose: The Post-Production Process; A Different World: Reflections.
