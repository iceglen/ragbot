The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (Music From & Inspired By) is an official companion album to The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. Many of its songs feature in the film, while others were written by independent artists to complement them.

The track listing includes songs such as "Can't Catch Me Now", "The Hanging Tree", "Wool", "Nothing You Can Take From Me", "The Garden", "The Ballad of Lucy Gray Baird", "Bury Me Beneath The Willow", "The Old Therebefore / Singing at Snakes", "Burn Me Once", "District 12 Stomp", "Nothing You Can Take From Me (Boot-Stompin’ Version)", "Cabin Song", "Lucy Gray (part 1)", "Pure As The Driven Snow", "Winter’s Come and Gone", "Keep On The Sunny Side", and "Lucy Gray (part 2)".

Singles from the album include "Can't Catch Me Now", "The Hanging Tree", and "The Old Therebefore". Several songs are featured in the film.

There was collaboration on the songs for the film, discussing historic, philosophical and poetic inspirations for the lyrics and connecting those works to the current world. The songwriter had a background in country music, which provided a foundation for the genre.

Five songs featured in the source novel do not appear on the album. A sixth song is included in the film score. In response to demand, an acapella studio version of a song was released at a later date.
