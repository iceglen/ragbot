The 11th Hunger Games were held one year after a previous event. The victor was a sixteen-year-old female tribute.

Betting, sponsorship, sponsor gifts, and interviews were carried forward. New developments aimed to increase engagement and to encourage stronger tributes to volunteer. These additions included a monetary prize for the victor, food parcels for their entire district, and the creation of a special village for victors. The tributes had minimal training. For the first time, the Games took place in the wild.

The victor valued having allies, and they took turns keeping watch at night. Her main desire was to protect her district partner.

The victor would have been the first to move into the special village and the first to officially receive compensation for winning. Her district would have been the first to receive the promised food parcels. The victor was also the first to embark on a Victory Tour.
