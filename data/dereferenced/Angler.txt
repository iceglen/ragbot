Angler was one of two male tributes from District 4 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Angler was part of the Career pack in these games. After an explosion on the mountain, Angler continued to join forces with two other tributes. They eventually encountered another tribute and tried to attack him. After being stabbed in the legs and falling down in pain, he was killed by a strike to the neck with an ax.

Angler, along with the other three tributes from District 4, wore a shimmering deep sea–blue merman suit during the chariot rides. Their training outfits were deep-sea blue.

The name could either be a reference to the anglerfish, an order of bony deep-sea fish named for their modified luminescent fin ray which acts as a lure for other fish, or to angling, which is fishing with a rod and hook.
