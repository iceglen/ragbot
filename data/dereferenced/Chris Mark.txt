Chris Mark is a Canadian actor, martial artist, stunt performer, and fight choreographer who portrayed the District 5 male tribute in The Hunger Games film.

Mark made an appearance on a popular TV show as a character named Blindspot. Like most tribute actors, he is also a stunt performer. He was the lead in two films, both directed by his brother. Mark has hopes of becoming the next big martial arts actor.

He is the first male actor cast specifically to portray the fight scenes of a Tribute that fell during the initial bloodbath.
