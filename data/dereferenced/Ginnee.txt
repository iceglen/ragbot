Ginnee was a tribute from District 6 for the 10th Hunger Games. She was female and between 12 and 18 years old. She grew up in District 6 and became her district's female tribute. Along with the other tributes, she was taken to the Capitol and placed in the zoo. While in the zoo, she and her district partner began a funny dance, which led to the environment becoming more festive. Before the start of the Games, the tributes and their mentors were taken on a tour of the arena. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, sending shrapnel that killed her, as well as her district partner and their mentors. Her body was later draped on the back of a horse and paraded during a funeral.
