Asterid Everdeen is the mother of Katniss and Primrose Everdeen and the wife of the deceased Burdock Everdeen.

She is a healer and a mother. She was formerly a merchant and pharmacist.

She is described as having blue eyes and pale blonde hair.

She is a healer with extensive knowledge of natural remedies. She helps many people who come to her for medical aid.

After her husband's death, she fell into a deep depression. She later recovers and starts an apothecary business.

She cares for her family. She saves a life after a severe whipping. She works in a hospital following a rebellion.

Following her youngest daughter's death, she relocates to lead a new hospital, unable to return to her previous home due to the pain.

She has two daughters. Her husband died in a mining accident. She also has two grandchildren.

Though never formally trained, she is a skilled healer, using knowledge of natural remedies. She runs an apothecary business and treats injuries and sicknesses.

After being emotionally abandoned by her mother following her father's death, her oldest daughter distrusts her. They later bond while grieving a shared loss.

Her youngest daughter was very close to her and completely forgave her. Together, they ran an apothecary business.

She loved her husband deeply. After he died, she fell into a deep depression.

Her wounds are treated by Asterid after a whipping, though they did not have a close relationship.
