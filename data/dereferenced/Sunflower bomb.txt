The sunflower bomb was an explosive created for a rebel plot to disrupt a major event.

The bombs were designed to be safe, both chemically and structurally, and could not be set off unintentionally. They could only be detonated by being fully assembled and the fuse lit with fire. The explosives were disguised as exact replicas of specific tokens, coated with a shellac that could be dissolved with water and friction, leaving the explosives malleable.

The plan involved replacing components within tribute tokens with explosive material and fuses. The tributes themselves were not aware of the plan. The explosives were to be retrieved from tokens after certain events.

One bomb was successfully planted on a critical target, causing significant damage and malfunction, though it failed to stop the system completely. In retaliation, hostile creatures were unleashed.

A backup fuse was discovered in another token. This final bomb was used on a generator, with the intention of destroying it.
