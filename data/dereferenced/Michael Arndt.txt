Michael Arndt is an Academy Award-winning screenwriter. He is known for writing films such as Little Miss Sunshine, Wanted, and Toy Story 3. He wrote the script for The Hunger Games: Catching Fire. He was one of three writers for Star Wars Episode VII, as well as an associate producer. He wrote an initial draft of script for the film adaptation of the prequel novel The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes. He was nominated for Best Adapted Screenplay for Toy Story 3. He is the first writer in history to be nominated for Best Original and Adapted Screenplays at the Oscars.
