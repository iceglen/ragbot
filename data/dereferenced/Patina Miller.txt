Patina Miller is an American actress and singer who portrayed Commander Paylor in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1 and 2.

In 2005, she was one of three finalists for a role. In 2007, she was cast in a daytime soap opera and appeared in 30 episodes. She performed in a production in the summer of 2008 and was featured in a musical that fall. She is known best for her role in Sister Act. She won a Tony Award for her performance as the Leading Player in Pippin on Broadway in 2013.
