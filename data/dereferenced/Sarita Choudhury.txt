Sarita Choudhury is an English-Indian actress who portrayed Egeria in the Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1.

After her debut film became an art house hit, she was determined not to focus her acting energies on independent film instead. She studied economics before switching to acting. She casually auditioned for the film and wound up cast as the lead in the singular interracial romance. Despite the film's surprise success, she stuck to her non-Hollywood roots, putting her looks and talent to versatile use as a country-western singer, a maid, and a lesbian mother.

She worked with the director again and played the cuckolded queen in a frankly-sensual feminist parable. By the late 1990s, she added a touch of Hollywood to her repertoire with supporting roles in a glossy remake and a retread.
