Sid Abernathy was the younger brother of Haymitch Abernathy. He was 10 years old at the time of the 50th Hunger Games.

He lived with his mother and older brother. His father perished in a coal mine fire.

The morning of the reaping for the 50th Hunger Games, Sid woke up his brother to wish him a happy birthday.

Two weeks after his brother's victory, Sid and his mother were killed as punishment for his brother's game-winning stunt. The Capitol ruled their deaths an accident.

Sid was an innocent and sweet ten-year old boy who looked up to his older brother. He was energetic and a morning person. Sid was loyal to his brother, helping him do chores.
