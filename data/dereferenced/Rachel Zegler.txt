Rachel Zegler is an American actress and singer who portrayed Lucy Gray Baird in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

She was born on May 3, 2001. She began her career starring in several musicals while attending high school. She received award nominations for these performances.

She was selected from a large number of applicants for a role in a film adaptation, marking her film debut. For her performance, she received award nominations and won an award.

She has since been cast in other films. She was cast in the role of Lucy Gray Baird. She reportedly initially turned down the role, fearful of traveling far from home.
