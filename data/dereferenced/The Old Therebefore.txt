"The Old Therebefore" is the third single from an album. The album version included dialogue from a scene in a film. In response to popular fan demand, an acapella studio version of the performance was released as a single on February 9th, 2024.

The lyrics describe a person stating they will join another in the afterlife only after finishing their affairs in the current world. They list various tasks and experiences they must complete, such as finishing a song, paying debts, having no regrets, emptying their cup, crying all their tears, conquering fears, dancing off their shoes, and learning how to love, all "right here in the old therebefore," when nothing is left anymore.
