The bakery was a business in the merchant section of town. It was run by the baker, who lived there with his family.

The kitchen and its ovens were in the back, radiating heat and the scent of baked goods. A trash bin sat behind the bakery, along with a pen that held a pig, and behind that, an apple tree.

The bakery sold a variety of breads and other baked goods. It offered items that most people in the district couldn't afford. They also made tarts and buns with cheese baked into the top.

When a girl was young and her family was starving, the baker's wife shouted at her for searching through their trash bin. Seeing this, the baker's son intentionally dropped two large loaves of bread into the fire, and after feeding the burnt parts to the pig, he tossed the rest out into the rain for the girl to take home.

On the morning of the reaping, a loaf of bakery bread was traded for. After winning the Hunger Games, the baker's son left the bakery and moved into a new house of his own. He was known to go into town to eat dinner with his family.

The bakery was eventually destroyed in the bombing of the district, leaving nothing behind but a melted lump of metal from an oven.

The baker's family usually ate stale bread, as they couldn't afford the things they made themselves.
