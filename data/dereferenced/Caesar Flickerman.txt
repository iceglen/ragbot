Caesar Flickerman is the host for the Hunger Games, responsible for interviewing tributes the night before the competition began. He is the Master of Ceremonies.

He was the host of the Hunger Games for more than forty years. The earliest known edition he hosted was the 50th Hunger Games. That year, he had to interview twice the regular number of tributes. He changes his suit every year.

He is shown to help tributes with their interviews. He helps because some don't do well in front of cameras. He can make a weak response into a memorable one. He is well liked by audiences, yet seems to have some sympathy for the tributes and tries to bring the best out of them and increase their chances of survival.

He appeared virtually the same over the course of his career. Every year, he wore the same pure white makeup, the same hairstyle, and the same ceremonial midnight blue suit. The only thing that changed annually was the color of his hair, and his eyelids and lips were done up to match.

His ultimate fate is not revealed.
