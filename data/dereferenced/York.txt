Soldier York is a trainer for beginners. The group she oversees is mainly made up of fourteen- and fifteen-year-olds, with the exception of two individuals who struggle to keep up with their peers. It is Soldier York's recommendation for an exam that ultimately decides whether or not they are fit for a mission.

One individual initially struggles with the training due to a previous injury. In an effort to help this person succeed, Soldier York sends them for a treatment to speed the healing process, which is administered by twenty-four needle injections. Under York's direction, the two individuals begin to excel in training.

York goes on to recommend them for an exam that consists of four parts: an obstacle course that assesses their physical condition, a written tactics exam, a test of weapons proficiency, and a simulated combat situation in an artificial city block. The last part of the exam targets each individual's weakness. One person's weakness was following orders, but they obeyed an order in the simulation and therefore passed the exam. The other individual did not pass. Their weakness was water, after being traumatized by being soaked with water and then electrocuted. When they flooded the street, it caused a flashback and confusion.

York is a no-nonsense, middle-aged woman.
