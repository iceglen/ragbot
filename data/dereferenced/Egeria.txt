Egeria was the Capitol's Minister of Affairs and one of President Snow's Cabinet members. She had been the Director of Labor Operations in District 2 before becoming the Capitol Minister of Affairs. She led the Capitol Ministry of Information, a branch that extended throughout Panem to improve the nation's functionality through data collection and analysis. Her role was to ensure the divisions worked together efficiently.

Egeria wrote a speech referring to district citizens as rebels. However, Snow disapproved of her terminology because he didn't want to legitimize the movement, so Egeria put forth "radicals" or "criminals" as alternatives. She later tried to dissuade Snow from launching an assault on a field hospital, but he justified the punishment as being derived from the language of her speech. She was also with President Snow in his office when the power cut out after rebels destroyed a dam.

Egeria was present at a dinner with other high-ranking Capitol officials. She watched and did not intervene as Snow poisoned an official. She later woke Snow to inform him that a rebel was still alive and showed him footage of a squad in the city's sewers. When she saw blood on Snow's tissue, she asked if she should call for a doctor, and Snow declined.

After the war, Snow was tried and sentenced to death, and a number of his collaborators were still awaiting trial at the time of his execution. Egeria likely would have been among those arrested.

Egeria had strong management skills. Although Egeria was a high-ranking Capitol official, she showed hesitance and did not appear to entirely agree with the Capitol's harsh regime. It's likely that she tread carefully to save herself from being poisoned like some of Snow's other associates.

Egeria wore a red and gray skirt suit, as well as ivory gloves that reminded her not to fidget. Her hair was elegantly twisted, and she had gold eyeliner and jewel earrings.
