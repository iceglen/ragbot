The Fallen is a special event that takes place every night in the Arena, where its main function is to indicate the number of tributes killed that day so that the rest who are still alive can mourn those who died.

At the end of each day in the arena, the national anthem was played, followed by a display of all tributes that had died that day. The fallen were shown one by one in district order, accompanied by their official Games portrait and district number.
For audiences watching the broadcast, the nightly death roll was accompanied by footage of each tribute's death. This ritual served as a practical mechanism of informing both the audience and the surviving tributes in the arena of the day's casualties and the number of competitors remaining. Inside the arena, however, tributes only saw the portraits of the deceased. This omission preserved a degree of uncertainty, forcing tributes to piece together circumstances of each death from what they had witnessed themselves or could infer from the environment.
Another function it has is that it indicates the end of the day and the beginning of a new one.

The firing of the cannon was another form of the death toll. A cannon fired upon the death of each tribute in the arena. Like the Fallen, this also gave tributes uncertainty as they would not know who had died, unless they had witnessed their death.

In one instance, images of the dead tributes appeared in the sky in color so that those who had died could be identified, since numbers were not used at that time.
In another instance, a glider carries a screen showing images of the deceased tributes.
In a film depiction, the projection had a gridded, scanline pattern. The portrait is centralized. The images were projected onto the dome of the arena. They appeared directly in the sky in an electric blue color. First, a logo appeared, followed by images of the dead. The tributes came out wearing the arena's clothing. Below each tribute, the district number appeared.
A later film depiction maintains a similar style, using a combination of blue colors, but the logo no longer appears.

The images appear in order according to their district. First those from District 1, then those from District 2, and so on until District 12, depending on the number of deaths.
In one account, it seems that the male tribute appears first, followed by the female tribute.
In a film, the order that is usually shown is the order of the list of tributes. First the male and second the female.

The term 'The Fallen' came from the films when the projections started with the text 'The Fallen' before going on to display the portraits.
