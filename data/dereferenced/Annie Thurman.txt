Annie Thurman is an American actress who portrayed the District 9 female in The Hunger Games film. She had originally auditioned for a different role but ultimately lost it. She is one of the last actresses to be cast for a tribute who was only mentioned once and fell during the initial bloodbath. Her action during the bloodbath is completely absent in the films.

After performing in a school play, she decided that she wanted to pursue acting as a career. At age 13, she enrolled in classes and workshops. It was through these workshops that she met her future manager and agent. They encouraged her to go to Los Angeles to learn more about the business. At the age of 14, things started happening when she got a role in a post-apocalyptic film. Shortly thereafter, she was cast as the female tribute from District 9 in The Hunger Games. That became the springboard to launch a role in a supernatural thriller. She also made a television debut as a lead in a holiday presentation. She also acted in national commercials. After a break, she returned for a pilot season and landed a lead role in a pilot, which was soon ordered to series.

In her off time, she enjoys spending time with family and friends. She loves to play musical instruments and enjoys outdoor activities. She rides horses and is also an animal lover and advocate.
