Omid Abtahi is an Iranian-American actor.

He was born on July 12, 1979. He grew up in Orange County, California. He moved to the United States at an early age with his family.

He performed on stage in many theatrical productions. He has also guest starred on television series. He has played roles in television series and films. He learned how to speak Arabic and studied Middle Eastern culture for a role. He has also performed voice work.
