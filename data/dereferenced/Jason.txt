Jason was the male tribute from District 6 who participated in the 74th Hunger Games. He was 16 years old. His occupation was tribute and his home was District 6. His weapon was a sword. His fate was deceased. He was male.

In the Tribute Parade, he was dressed in a flowing costume that resembled a moon. A crescent moon-shaped head piece that curved around his face was worn by both him and his district partner.

He was first seen practicing hand-to-hand combat. Later, he got into a fight with another tribute who thought he took his knife, and was threatened to be targeted in the arena.

For the interviews, he wore a green suit with black shoes with laces. He was seen waiting in line with the other tributes.

He was killed in the initial Cornucopia bloodbath. He stood on the pedestal next to another female tribute. When the gong rang, he ran straight towards the left. After arriving at the Cornucopia, he was targeted, beaten up and left to die, but he was able to get up. He then got into a physical altercation. Afterwards, he fought over a backpack, but was elbowed to the ground. While his district partner was killed near a crate, he looked for supplies. He then attacked another male tribute, pinned him onto a crate and strangled him. That tribute gained an advantage and hurled his head into a crate. He grabbed a sword and attempted to kill another tribute, but was tripped, then a serrated sword was used to slash his stomach twice, and he was finished off with a stab.
