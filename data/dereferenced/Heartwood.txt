Heartwood was one of two male tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the games, Heartwood joined an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Heartwood's district was the first to join this alliance. Heartwood and his district partners were considered decent at knife throwing during training.
Heartwood died in the initial bloodbath along with the other boy from his district.

The District 7 tributes' training outfits were russet brown.
"Heartwood" refers to the central, strongest part of a tree.
