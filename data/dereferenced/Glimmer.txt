Glimmer was the female tribute from District 1 in the 74th Hunger Games. She was a member of the Career pack that banded together to hunt down other competitors. She was killed by tracker jackers.

She was a Career Tribute, having trained for the Games. During the Tribute Parade, she and her district partner wore outfits representing their district's main export of luxury goods. Her tribute token was a ring that concealed a poisoned spike, which was confiscated. During her interview, she wore a provocative dress and stated she was very prepared for the games.

At the Cornucopia bloodbath, she acquired weapons and killed other tributes. The Careers later discovered and allowed another tribute into their pack to help track down a specific competitor. The Career pack found that competitor trapped in a tree. She attempted to shoot the competitor with a bow and arrows but was unsuccessful. The pack decided to wait for the competitor to come down and camped under the tree.

She was killed when a nest of tracker jackers was dropped on the sleeping Career pack. She suffered numerous stings, collapsed, and fell into a coma from the venom and hallucinations. The venom caused severe physical swelling. Her bow and arrows were taken from her body.

Later in the Games, wolf mutts resembling the fallen tributes were unleashed. One mutt resembled her with blond fur and green eyes. After the Games, the victors visited her district. The victor who killed her had recurring nightmares involving her mutated body and her mutt form.

She was described as tall with green eyes and blond hair. Her primary weapons were a bow and arrows and a knife. She also used a sword. She killed several other tributes during the bloodbath and the early stages of the Games. Her name refers to light illumination, in keeping with her district's industry.
