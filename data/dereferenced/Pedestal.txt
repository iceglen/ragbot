Pedestals were fire-resistant metal plates that tributes stood on during the countdown before a Hunger Games. All 24 pedestals were placed at equal distances from the Cornucopia. Tributes had to wait 60 seconds before the Games began, otherwise a mine would explode. These mines automatically disconnected after the gong sounded.

During the 10th Hunger Games, the podiums were red circles with black numbers on them and were used from the first Games until the 10th Hunger Games. They surrounded the center of the amphitheater where the Capitol seal was located, but after the bombing of the Capitol Arena, the center was covered by a mountain of concrete rubble and beams that fell from the ceiling when it collapsed.

For the 50th Hunger Games, which had twice as many tributes, the number of pedestals doubled to 48. These pedestals surrounded the Cornucopia, which was filled with weapons and supplies that could help the tributes during the Games.

In the film version, the pedestals do not form a circle, like in other iterations of the Games, but a semicircle.

The pedestals are on the water surrounding the Cornucopia. Between each pair of pedestals is a path of rocks separating the sectors of the arena.
