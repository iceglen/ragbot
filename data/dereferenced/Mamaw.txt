Mamaw was Haymitch Abernathy's grandmother. She was an elderly woman. Mamaw was the mother of Haymitch's father. She had various sayings about different aspects of life. She stated that smells stick in your memory the strongest, more than sounds or sights. She also said that you never really knew who'd swim in a flood. Her favorite candy was hot pepper cherry taffy. At an unknown time, she passed away. Her family made bean and ham hock soup, which is the traditional meal eaten after a loved one passes away. Mamaw seemed to be kind and knowledgeable.
