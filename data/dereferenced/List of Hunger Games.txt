During the first nine Hunger Games, tributes were held in the Peacekeepers' stables. Soon after, they were locked inside the Capitol Arena with nothing but weapons while the audience watched from the comfort of their own homes. At the conclusion of each competition, the bodies would be removed and the doors locked until the next year with no further fanfare. Certain districts produced more victors in these years, while others were also considered contenders. There was no history of muttations ever being used in the first nine years of the Games.

On the first evening of the 10th Hunger Games, it was said that in terms of fatalities, they were running neck and neck with the previous year. This implies that during the 9th Hunger Games, around ten tributes had died by the afternoon on the first day.

The 10th Hunger Games was the first to introduce mentors. It also introduced betting, sponsorship, sponsor gifts, and interviews. Additionally, snake mutts were released into the arena, making them the first mutts to be used in this way. Prior to the Games, tributes were held in a zoo, and they later competed inside the Capitol Arena, the landscape of which had been altered by a bombing.
Many tributes and their mentors were killed before the Games officially began, and the victor won by cheating with the help of her mentor. Written off as an embarrassing failure, all but one recording of the 10th Hunger Games were destroyed, though aspects like the interviews, betting, sponsorship, and mentorship would be carried forward in future editions.

After the failure of the previous year, the Gamemakers brainstormed ways to engage the districts and the Capitol for the 11th Hunger Games. New additions included incentives to send forward stronger tributes, such as food parcels for the winning district, the creation of the Victors' Village, and monetary prizes for the victors themselves. The victor this year became the first to embark on a Victory Tour.

This was the first Quarter Quell. Every twenty-five years, a new twist would be introduced for a special edition of the Hunger Games. The twist this year was that each district had to elect its own tributes. The victor of these Games died sometime before the third Quarter Quell, which was held fifty years later.

One victor won this year by setting up an electrical trap with parts scavenged from his arena. It was said he had electrocuted six tributes at once.
