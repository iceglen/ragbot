The hanging tree was where executions were held in District 12 around the time of the 10th Hunger Games.

The hanging tree was located at the edge of the woods, in front of a large field of hard-packed earth and weeds. A makeshift gallows with two trapdoors was rigged underneath one of its more horizontal branches. Beneath each trapdoor, there was a fifteen foot drop that would snap the convicts' necks.

The event inspired a song that recounted the execution from the convict's perspective.

The hanging tree was also used as a meeting place for planning escapes.
