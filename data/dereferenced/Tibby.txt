Tibby was an attendant from the Capitol.

Tibby was working as an attendant on the train transporting the four District 12 tributes to the Capitol for the 50th Hunger Games. He offered them a tray of sandwiches with three different meats and asked which one they would like. When one tribute would not answer him, another shouted that they need plates, napkins and cutlery. Tibby told her that they were just sandwiches that could be picked up, but she wanted napkins. Tibby asked if that was okay. When he returned, he encouraged the tributes to try the beef.

Later on, Tibby brought a tribute a birthday cake and led the Peacekeepers with a song. He was disappointed when the tribute did not look happy with it, and was told to take it away.

Tibby appeared again as the Victory Tour started preparation and was told to bring a tribute a sandwich.
