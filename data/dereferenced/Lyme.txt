Lyme was a victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Games from District 2 and a commander in the Second Rebellion. She was middle-aged, over six feet tall, and very muscular. She kept a low profile after her victory. During the rebellion, she commanded forces working to capture District 2. She was upfront and honest, and showed sympathy for fellow district workers. She was not present at a post-war meeting of surviving victors, and it is assumed she had been killed sometime before then.
