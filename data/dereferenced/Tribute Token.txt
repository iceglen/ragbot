A tribute token, also known as a district token, was an item each tribute was permitted to bring with them into the Hunger Games arena, which could represent their district and remind them of home. However, tokens had to be approved by a review board prior to the competition. Items would be confiscated if they broke the rules of the Games, as would anything that could give the tribute an advantage. A token was usually something that could be worn so the tributes could keep it with them easily.
