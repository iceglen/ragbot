Dr. Kay was a female resident of the Capitol and a scientist assigned to the Citadel. She traveled to District 12 to oversee a Peacekeeper project to collect fifty jabberjays and fifty mockingjays to be brought back to the Citadel. She was the head of the original jabberjay project, which failed after the rebels discovered their use. When the birds were freed, she had thought they wouldn't last the winter, as genetically engineered projects often struggled in the wild, but they turned out to be strong. She intercepted him, scolded him for disturbing the mutts, and sent him back down to the elevator. She taught how the jabberjays worked and also explained about the nature of mockingjays. When no mockingjays were caught by the traps, showing that their survival skills had advanced, she seemed pleased by their instincts. She directed the Peacekeepers in setting up mist nets to trap the birds instead, instructing them on making the experience as trauma-free as possible for the birds and removing the first three from the net herself. She cleaned and bandaged his hand herself. Dr. Kay was a rosy woman with oversized, pink glasses, which made her easily recognizable. She also wore a lab coat.
