Io Jasper was a female resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the 10th Hunger Games. She was assigned the male tribute from District 3. She was a natural when it came to biology and partnered with another student in a high biology class at the Academy. During the interviews before the 10th Hunger Games began, her tribute stated that he could start a fire with his glasses. With her scientific knowledge, Io suggested various angles and times of day that would facilitate the task. When interviewed, she discussed her thoughts on what her tribute might do with a drone he had taken and altered, suggesting that he could use its lights to give him an advantage at night. Her tribute died after being bitten by snake mutts sent into the arena.
