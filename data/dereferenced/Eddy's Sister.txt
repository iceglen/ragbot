Eddy's sister and her younger brother, Eddy, were rebels from District 8 that were injured in a bombing. It is unknown if they had any other living family.

Eddy and his sister were both injured when their district was bombed. She ended up in the hospital with an injured leg, and her brother was located a few beds down from her. When another person came to the district, Eddy's sister was the first person to recognize her in the hospital. She was amazed and excitedly yelled to her brother.

Right after the visitor left the hospital, a second air strike targeted the hospital. Eddy and his sister were among the deceased, since there were no survivors.
