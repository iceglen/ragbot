District 13
Cut off from the rest, this district had to become self-sufficient, building its own underground farms to sustain its population. In addition to growing crops, they raised chickens. They also had milk; since they lacked the space for cows, sheep, or horses to graze, and since the taste was not commented on as being different, it was likely goat's milk.
