Tax was the male trainer at the archery station in the Training Center.

When Tax saw that the standing targets were way too easy for a trainee, he threw fake birds into the air which the trainee shot. The trainee took down each bird without fail, so he decided to throw more birds for increased difficulty. When the trainee hit five in one throw, everyone in the Training Center stared and the room fell silent. Their faces showed a mixture of envy, hatred and admiration.
Tax's fate is unknown in the subsequent rebellion, as he was not mentioned again.
