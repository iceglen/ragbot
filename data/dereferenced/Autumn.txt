Autumn was one of two female tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Autumn joined an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Autumn's district was the first to join the alliance. Although she and her district partners were considered unremarkable, they proved to be decent knife throwers during training.
Autumn made it to the top ten along with the other girl from her district. They were killed in combat by two Careers.

The District 7 tributes' training outfits were russet brown.
"Autumn" is a season strongly associated with forestry and tree harvesting.
