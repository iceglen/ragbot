Buttercup is a cat. He is described as a hideous-looking cat. He has a mashed-in nose, half of one ear missing, eyes the color of rotting squash, and a muddy yellow coat. He is a born mouser and can catch rats. He is afraid of thunder and likes to be hidden under a blanket during storms.

He was found as a scrawny kitten with a swollen belly and infested with worms and fleas. He was nursed back to health. He initially hissed at a certain person but stopped when fed entrails. He often guarded a young girl while she slept.

He moved to a new village but disliked the new house, often returning to the old home. After a bombing, he was found fat and healthy, possibly feeding on field mice or other available food. He was taken to a different district and reunited with the young girl. An agreement was made that the cat could be kept, with specific conditions for his care, including a window for coming and going. If he posed security issues, he would be shot.

During a bombing evacuation, he was retrieved and brought to a bunker. He entertained people with a game where a flashlight was wiggled for him to chase. After the young girl's death, the cat traveled a long distance on foot, searching for her. He was injured, with a clawed back paw and prominent bones in his face. He then stayed with the older sister, guarding her while she slept. His injuries were tended to, and he was fed. The two bonded over their shared loss.

The cat is named Buttercup because his muddy yellow coat was said to match the bright yellow buttercup flower.
