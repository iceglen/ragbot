Fabricia Whatnot was a fashion designer in the Capitol. She possessed a talent for derivative fashion, incorporating trends such as feathers, leathers, plastics, or plush into her designs at a reasonable rate. She was the boss of an apprentice designer, who was made to perform menial tasks like giving foot massages and cleaning drains. Despite this treatment, the apprentice admired her and defended her against criticism. She was considered by some to be as ridiculous as her name. She had long, magenta hair and was involved with all the latest fashion trends.
