The President's mansion, also known as the Presidential Palace, is a location which appears in all three books. Found in the City Circle of the Capitol, it was home to the previous resident, and briefly, another president. After being elected the new president, another presumably resides there as well.

During the chariot rides, the tributes rode their chariots in a procession through the Capitol into the City Circle, where they stopped in front of the mansion to listen to a welcoming speech. Following the end of their Victory Tour and arrival in the Capitol, attendees went to a grand party put on in the banquet room. During the Games, the tributes also rode by the mansion.

In the story, a character told a squad about a mission to infiltrate the mansion. However, the squad never did infiltrate the mansion, because the president surrendered after a hovercraft dropped bombs. Later, the mansion became a new home. A character woke up inside the mansion and went to the rose garden, where they met some soldiers guarding it. Inside, they found the previous resident, shackled to a chair and awaiting execution. The previous resident claimed not to have ordered the bombing. Later, the surviving victors met and voted to have a Hunger Games. Immediately following this, the character was to shoot the previous resident, but shot and killed another president instead.

The mansion is very large and presumed to be full of security measures to discourage intruders. It contains a garden, where genetically enhanced roses are grown. It also contains a ballroom, and a meeting room.
