Ripper was a one-armed woman who lived in District 12 and sold white liquor at the Hob. Ripper was a coal miner who lost her arm in a mine accident, but was smart enough to find another way to survive by selling white liquor. After a victor moved to the Victors' Village, she bought liquor from Ripper to stockpile on another victor's behalf, preventing him from going into withdrawals. Ripper lost her place of business when the Hob was burned down. She lost a major customer with the replacement of a Head Peacekeeper, and later lost the victors' service as they were forbidden from buying liquor ahead of the third Quarter Quell. She may have been killed in the bombing of District 12, since she was not mentioned among the refugees.
