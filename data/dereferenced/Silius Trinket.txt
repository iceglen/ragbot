Silius Trinket was the great-uncle of Effie and Proserpina Trinket. He was married to Messalina Trinket.

Silius was active during the war. Somehow, he and his wife brought great disgrace to the Trinket family. During the interviews for the 50th Hunger Games, two tributes wore clothes once belonging to Silius. The aforementioned disgrace involved weapons and blood, as one tribute found a decorative holster and bloodstains on the borrowed clothes.

According to a relative, Silius was competent in his fashion sense. His clothing was roughly the same size as the two tributes who wore it.
