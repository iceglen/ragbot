District 4 is one of Panem's wealthiest districts. Its major industry is fishing, and children are trained for jobs in this industry from a young age. Its citizens are adept with nets and tridents, and are skilled swimmers. The district is known for bringing the bounty of the sea.

District 4 tributes were historically considered strong contenders in the Hunger Games, presumably due to skills from the fishing industry and more stable diets. Eventually, it became a Career district, training tributes for combat in the arena, though this portrayal varies.

The district has a strong rebel underground. During one rebellion, it experienced seafood shortages. In a later Hunger Games, its tributes were involved in a rebel plan to protect a key figure and escape the arena.

The traditional bread from this district is a salty, fish-shaped loaf tinted green by seaweed. Citizens are adept at tying knots, weaving, wielding weapons like spears and tridents, making and using nets, improvising fish hooks, and identifying edible marine life. They presumably also practice aquaculture. The district has a distinct accent.

A local wedding ceremony involves covering the couple with a net woven from long grass. They touch each other's lips with salt water, children sing a wedding song likening marriage to a sea voyage, and the union is sealed with a kiss.

The district's color has been represented as cadet blue, deep sea blue, and plantation teal in different Hunger Games events.
