The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (Original Motion Picture Score) is the official film score for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. The score was composed by James Newton Howard. It also features piano music by Yuja Wang.
Music created for the film that was not part of its score is included on a separate album.
