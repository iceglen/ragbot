Julianne Moore is an American actress who portrayed President Alma Coin in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1 and 2. She began her career with a series of television roles. She made her film debut in 1990, and continued to play supporting roles throughout the early 1990s. She made her breakthrough followed by critically acclaimed performances. Starring roles in subsequent blockbusters established her as a leading actress. She won an Oscar for her performance in Still Alice.
