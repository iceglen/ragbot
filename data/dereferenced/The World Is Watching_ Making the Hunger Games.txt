The World Is Watching: Making the Hunger Games is a feature-length documentary film about the making of The Hunger Games film. It was included as a featurette on DVD releases.

The documentary is split into eight sections, each one detailing a different part of production. It is mostly comprised of retrospective interviews with cast and crew members about working on the film, with behind the scenes footage spread throughout.

The documentary includes behind the scenes looks at two scenes that did not make it into the finished film. In one scene, a character was in the woods with another character, teaching her about snares. In the other, a character wore an arena uniform for a deleted nightmare sequence.
