Lucretius Flickerman, generally known by the nickname "Lucky Flickerman," was a weathercaster and amateur magician who was tapped to host the interviews from the 10th Hunger Games and later the Games themselves. He had a pet parrot named Jubilee.

He first appeared for the 10th Hunger Games for the interviews. He welcomed the audience and explained the new sponsorship system in place for the Games. He then worked through the interviews with those tributes who were available. His performance in the interviews was considered enough of a success that he was tapped to host the Games themselves.

Before the Games began, he hosted a pre-show. He provided his own brand of colorful commentary throughout the Games. He spoke about the relatively slow start to the Games. At one point, he brought his pet parrot Jubilee, trying to get him to speak. He later tried to do a weather report with commentary from the parrot, but the parrot again refused to speak. He then started doing it himself, adopting a high-pitched voice to speak in the parrot's place.

He was described as being "clownish" and dressed in a high-collared blue suit with rhinestone accents, with gelled hair dusted in coppery powder. He spoke in a voice like a bird chirping and liked to entertain the crowd with amateur magic tricks.
