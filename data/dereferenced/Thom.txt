Thom is a male coal miner in his late teens. He is a friend and crewmate of another miner, and they share similar backgrounds and occupations. He is native to a specific area within his home district. His loyalty is demonstrated when he helps carry an injured friend to safety after a punishment, for which he receives a reward. When his home district is destroyed, he is among the survivors evacuated to another district, where he becomes a soldier in a rebellion. He survives the war and is later seen in his rebuilt home district, searching through debris and conversing with an acquaintance, where he relays information about other individuals who did not survive the destruction. He is presumed to now live a normal life.
