Agrippina Sickle was a professor working as the gymnasium mistress at the Academy. She was awarded an ornamental shield for overseeing Academy safety drills during the bombings. Each year on the day of the Reaping, she insisted on holding it for a group photo. She insisted on rigorous exercise, teaching Academy students to march in formation from a young age. She supervised mentor-tribute meetings and chaperoned tours. She had muscular shoulders and was a woman of formidable stature.
