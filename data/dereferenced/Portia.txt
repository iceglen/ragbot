Portia was a stylist for the male tribute from District 12. She was assigned to style the tribute for the 74th and 75th Hunger Games. She worked in partnership with another stylist. Their role was to design the tribute's appearance and costumes for public events leading up to the Games, including the chariot parade and interviews. The stylists aimed to make the tributes memorable to gain audience favor.

For the chariot parade, the stylists designed complementary costumes. They moved away from the district's traditional motif, creating black outfits with capes that simulated fire. They helped position the tributes and managed the special effects.

During training, the stylists dressed the tributes in matching attire. They offered reassurance about training scores, suggesting that low scores could be strategically ignored. They attended meals with the tribute's team.

For the interview, the stylist designed a suit with thematic accents. She affirmed that a genuine reaction was more effective than a planned one.

After the Games, the stylist prepared the victor for a recap interview and a tour. She accompanied the team on the victory tour, attending events and meals.

For the 75th Games, the stylist again created a matching costume for the tribute, this time a black jumpsuit that emitted light. The design was intended to create a specific, detached demeanor. During training, the stylist was present when the tribute revealed a provocative act meant to challenge the authorities. She questioned if the act was coordinated.

The stylist prepared the tribute's interview attire, choosing formal wear to complement another's outfit. She was not present for a final farewell before the Games began.

The stylist was later executed by the ruling authority. Her personality was described as level-headed, encouraging, and comforting. She appeared less out of touch than many of her homeland's citizens, which may have contributed to her effective partnership.
