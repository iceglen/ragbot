Lannie was one of two female tributes from District 10 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Lannie joined the Newcomers alliance between tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. She initially wasn't sure if it was plausible for one of the Newcomers to win, and questioned another tribute about it. She was also doubtful that the Newcomers being inexperienced would be a good selling point for sponsorship.
She died in the initial bloodbath with the other girl from her district.

Lannie, along with the other three tributes from District 10, wore a specific outfit during the chariot rides.

Lannie's name is likely derived from a substance found on sheep wool.

Lannie's token, along with those of the fellow tributes from the home district, was a horseshoe.
