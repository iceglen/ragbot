Iphigenia Moss was a female resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the 10th Hunger Games. She was assigned the female tribute from District 5.

Her father oversaw the Agriculture Department, which controlled the flow of food. She was considered "forgettable," and despite her father's job, always seemed to be on the verge of malnutrition. She gave her school lunches to classmates and even blacked out on occasion. It was said that this was her revenge on her father, but no further details were provided.

When her tribute emerged in the arena, she began to send every bit of food she could, but her tribute was killed. Afterwards, she spoke briefly with a news reporter, referring to her tribute's name uncertainly and saying that she had a funny accent. In response, the reporter congratulated her on getting her tribute to the second half and mistakenly addressed her by the wrong name.
