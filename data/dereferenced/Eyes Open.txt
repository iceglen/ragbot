"Eyes Open" is the third overall single released from the album and the first official single. The song is performed and written by the artist.
This is one of two songs that were written for the film. One song was featured at the end of the film during the credits whereas "Eyes Open" was not.
The artist has since re-recorded many songs. Both songs, now bearing a modified title, were released at midnight after being teased the previous day.
