Cassia was a member of the camera crew stationed in District 12 during the reaping for the 50th Hunger Games.

After a death, another individual tried to console the grieving mother. She tried to convince the Peacekeepers to let her see her son's body for a little while longer, but they wouldn't budge, and she kept insisting. Another individual came over to try and help the situation, so a third individual chooses him as a replacement. A fourth individual told Cassia to record the weeping reaction.
