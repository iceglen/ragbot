Cookie was a Peacekeeper assigned to District 12, where he worked as a cook. He was an old soldier who had lost his left ear in the Dark Days.

At some point he enlisted as a soldier, and he lost his left ear during the Dark Days. He was later assigned to District 12 to cook for the other Peacekeepers in the base.

Given that the Peacekeepers used nicknames for each other, "Cookie" is probably a nickname given in reference to his role as their cook.
