Diamonds are a type of gemstone and one of the most valuable substances. In this setting, diamonds are associated with a district whose primary industry is the manufacture of luxury items such as jewelry. This makes the district very wealthy and creates a favorable relationship with the ruling power. There is said to be a machine that can turn graphite into diamonds. However, this machine may be somewhat useless at the time the series takes place; graphite may no longer be as common as a raw material.

For a Tribute Parade, the tributes from that district were dressed in tunics decorated with jewels. Diamonds may have been among those decorations.
An escort mistakenly says that putting pressure on coal turns it into pearls. It is noted that this is untrue, as putting pressure and heat on coal can result in diamonds, but this process happens only rarely and can take millennia to complete. During a later competition, these mistaken words became something of an inside joke.
A designer used diamonds in some designs: a dark blue velvet dress was studded with diamonds, and diamonds were used to decorate one of the potential wedding dresses.
