Hunter Schafer is an American actress who portrayed Tigris in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

Career
Schafer's debut came when she portrayed a character in a television series. She also co-wrote and co-executive produced a special episode for the series. Later that year, she was named to a list of emerging leaders. She is currently in production on a film.
