Jubilee was a parrot. He was a depressed bird who was battling mange.

In a studio, his owner brought him to a set and tried to get him to say a specific phrase. The bird refused to speak at all at first, but he did eventually say the phrase in a depressed voice.
After that, the bird refused to speak again, so his owner used a high-pitched voice to pretend the bird was speaking instead of himself in a conversation. During an attempted report, with added commentary, the owner used the high-pitched voice to deliver a line about a person. When the camera cut to that person for his reaction, he gave a thumbs-up, but in reality was tired of the antics.
When eight mentors remained, Jubilee was brought out again, this time confined in a cage more suited to a rabbit than a bird. In late afternoon, coverage resumed, and a doctor sat across from the owner, holding Jubilee in his cage. Jubilee rocked back and forth, trying to comfort himself, and was eyed worriedly by his owner.

Jubilee is a word for a time of celebration, ironically contrasted by the bird's depressed demeanor.
Jubilee is the only named pet bird in the series, but not the only pet bird overall; another character had a canary in her youth.
