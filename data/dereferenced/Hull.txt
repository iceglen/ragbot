Hull was one of two male tributes from District 11 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. His occupation was tribute. His weapon was a pitchfork. He is deceased.

After arriving, he was taken to be showered. In the shower room, he kicked another tribute when he was taunting the others. Before the start of the games, Hull joined an alliance between several tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. He and his district partners are considered a game changer.
During training, Hull was observed training with a pitchfork and beheading a dummy.
On the first day of the Games, Hull joined the rest of the alliance on the mountain. He had managed to obtain a pack at the Cornucopia containing a syrup antidote for poison which he shared with his allies when they got sick, as everything in the arena was poisonous.

After a volcanic explosion on the mountain, Hull continued to team up with two allies. They were attacked by a porcupine muttation. Hull had half a dozen quills dangling from his swollen face while he tried to protect his allies, who were writhing on the ground. Though he fought it with a pitchfork, Hull's attacks did not deter the porcupine. Two other tributes arrived to help but Hull received more wounds when the porcupine drove a slew of quills into his thigh.
The two tributes dispatched of the porcupine and tried to save Hull, the only one still alive, by pouring an antidote down his throat. Hull tried to swallow it but the antidote spilled back out and he died.
During the Victory Tour, a surviving tribute faced the distraught, grieving family of Hull.

Hull was strong.

Hull could stand up for himself. He was also very selfless, as he continued to risk his life to try and save his allies when he could have run away, and he gave his life in the process.

"Hull" is another word for "husk", the outer covering of seeds or fruits.
