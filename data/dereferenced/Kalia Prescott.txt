Kalia Prescott is the first of the stunt performers specifically cast to portray the training and fight scenes of a tribute that is mentioned only once, that she fell during the initial bloodbath. She portrayed the District 3 female tribute.

Like most of the tribute actors, she is also a stunt actor. She is the only stunt actor and one of the two tributes which fell during the bloodbath to be officially credited under the actors section.
She is also a Taekwondo black belt and a model.
