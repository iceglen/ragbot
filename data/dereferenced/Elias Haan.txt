Elias Haan is a lumberjack from District 7 who was selected to become a District Hero.

Elias was chosen to become a District Hero presumably because he believes in District 7's mission to respect the greater forces of nature. He has kept the axe handed down to him by his great-grandfather as a reminder of the hardships he and his district have overcome.

Elias has brown eyes, brown hair and a short beard. He is also very muscular, has multiple tattoos along his arms and chest, and he is an amputee, having had one leg amputated below the knee. In his District Hero portrait, he wears leather boots and jeans, and a red flannel shirt lies on the bench beside him. He also has a carved wooden prosthesis that he has removed for the photo.
