This silver compact belonged to a character's mother and was kept after her death. It was a circular silver compact containing a cake of rose-scented powder, decorated with an engraved rose. It was often kept in a drawer and taken out during difficult times.

The compact was later loaned to another character, who used it to store poison in the arena. The compact assisted in poisoning and killing other tributes. After the events, the compact was seized as evidence of cheating. It was eventually reclaimed from a dean's office.

In a film adaptation, the compact was depicted as octagonal in shape with a swirling pattern engraved on its lid.
