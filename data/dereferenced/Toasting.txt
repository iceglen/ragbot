The toasting is a marriage ritual.

Traditionally, the bride would rent a wedding dress that had been worn many times, and the groom wore something clean that wasn't work clothes. They would go to a building to fill out forms and be assigned a house. Friends and family gathered to share a meal, or if they could afford it, some cake. Then the newlywed couple crossed the threshold of their new home as their guests sang a traditional wedding song. Finally, the couple made their first fire together and toasted a piece of bread to share between the two of them. This constituted the wedding ceremony.

At an earlier time, people would sometimes pool their money and have multiple couples get married on the same day to afford live music and entertainment.

It was once falsely claimed that a toasting had taken place in total secrecy before a public event.
