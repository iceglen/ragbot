Plutarch Heavensbee is the Head Gamemaker for the 75th Hunger Games. A secret rebel-insider, he becomes a commander of the rebel forces during the Second Rebellion.

He is a large man. He is exceptionally intelligent and manipulative. As a person who mastered the "long game", he saw the Hunger Games, and later the war, as giant chess games consisting of moves and countermoves. As such he was an ingenious strategist, but this also caused him to be somewhat callous. He is a realist, to the point of expressing a cynical view of humanity.

He works as a Head Gamemaker, but is also a leader for the underground rebellion. He is head of propaganda and makes videos of the Mockingjay. After the war, he is given the job of Secretary of Communications, which means he is in charge of the programming on the airwaves.
