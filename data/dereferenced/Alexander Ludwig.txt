Alexander Ludwig is a Canadian actor who portrayed Cato, the male tribute from District 2, in The Hunger Games film. He is best known for his lead role in Race to Witch Mountain. Ludwig also composed a single entitled "Liv It Up (Teenage Wasteland)." He also starred in the film Grown Ups 2, portraying Braden. Ludwig has also worked as a model. He graduated from university, where he studied theatre. He is best known for his acting in the television series Vikings, representing a character who is the son of two other characters. He has also starred in other films.
