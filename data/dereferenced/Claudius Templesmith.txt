Claudius Templesmith was the announcer of the Hunger Games. He had a deep, booming voice. As the announcer of the Hunger Games, his voice was occasionally projected to the tributes inside the arena. He usually marked the start of the competition, issued invitations to feasts, and generally ended his announcements with the slogan for the Games.

He announced the beginning of the Games. On the tenth day of the Games, after the nightly playing of the anthem, he announced a rule change. Under the new rule, two tributes from the same district could both be declared the victors if they were the last people standing. On the twelfth day, he invited the six remaining tributes to a feast. Rather than food, they were promised something they each needed desperately. These items would be placed into backpacks marked with their district numbers. On the eighteenth day, after a tribute's death, he announced that the earlier revision to the rules had been revoked, so only one winner would be allowed. A second, more frantic announcement quickly followed, declaring two tributes both victors.

He made one announcement to mark the start of the subsequent Hunger Games. His voice featured in one of the rebels' first propaganda films. A line was pulled, unedited, directly from his commentary during a previous Hunger Games.
