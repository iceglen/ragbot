Mayor Undersee's house was a home in District 12 where Mayor Undersee resided with his wife and their daughter.

The mayor's house was a three-story building near the town square. They had a telephone somewhere in the house. There was a piano on the first floor, and the second floor held several guest rooms, the mayor's study, and the daughter's bedroom. The master bedroom was presumably on the third floor. The daughter had a dressing table with a mirror in her own bedroom.

The house was destroyed in the bombing of District 12. The whole family died as well as the two people who worked for them.
