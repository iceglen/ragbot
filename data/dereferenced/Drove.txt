Drove was the male tribute from District 10 who participated in the 74th Hunger Games.

He was 18 years old. His occupation was tribute. His home was District 10. His weapon was a sword. His fate was deceased. He was male.

He appears in the book *The Hunger Games* and the film *The Hunger Games*.

During the Tribute Parade, he and his district partner wore golden cowboy hats, shirts, and pants, representing District 10's livestock industry.

During training, he was shown practicing with a staff against a trainer. He was also seen looking doubtfully at other tributes and briefly wrestling with another tribute.

He was described as being very quiet during his interview.

He survived the initial Cornucopia bloodbath. He ran to the Cornucopia for supplies and grabbed a sleeping bag. He fought off other tributes who tried to take it. He was later seen running in the forest with his bag.

The cannon signaling his death sounded on the eighth day. He placed 10th out of 24 tributes. His death occurred after another tribute's death, at some point during a period when another tribute was sleeping.
