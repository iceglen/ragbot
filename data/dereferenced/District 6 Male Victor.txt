The District 6 male was the victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Games. Years after his initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third Quarter Quell would be reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 6's reaping for the 75th Hunger Games. He stood beside another victor, who was chosen to compete. He did not volunteer to go in their place. He was not present at a later meeting of surviving victors, so he is presumed deceased. He was the oldest known victor from District 6.
