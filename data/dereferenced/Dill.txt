Dill was the female tribute from District 11 in the 10th Hunger Games. She grew up in her district and was selected as a tribute. She was described as having a skeletal frame and a hacking cough. She was very sickly, likely suffering from tuberculosis. During the interviews for the Games, her coughs were barely audible. On the first day of the Hunger Games, she was carried from a tunnel and placed on a piece of charred wood. She was shivering and on the verge of death. She received bottles of water but did not acknowledge them. As the day ended, her body convulsed and she coughed up blood, which soaked her filthy dress. She had a mutual bond with her district partner, who carried her and attempted to comfort her.
