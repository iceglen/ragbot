Coriolanus Snow's granddaughter is the granddaughter of Coriolanus Snow, the President of Panem. She is a student living in the Capitol. She is a minor character. She is shown to admire a certain victor, adopting that victor's signature braid as a fashion trend, which displeases her grandfather. However, he shows affection towards her, particularly when she expresses a desire for profound love. During a televised speech declaring association with a certain symbol illegal, she quickly undid her braid upon realizing what it represented. A proposed plan involving her participation in a final Hunger Games was not implemented. Her ultimate fate is unknown, but she is presumed alive.
