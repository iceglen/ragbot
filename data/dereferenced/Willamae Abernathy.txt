Willamae Abernathy was the mother of two sons. She was widowed young when her husband died in a coal mine fire. She worked as a laundry worker. She was described as incredibly resourceful, ensuring that nothing went to waste. She worked hard and expected her children to do the same, often assigning them difficult tasks. Although she was stern, she tried to instill optimism in her children. Sorrowful experiences had made her wrinkled. Her muscles were visibly straining as she washed clothing. She died when she was burned alive in her house by arson.
