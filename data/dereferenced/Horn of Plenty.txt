"Horn of Plenty" is the national anthem. It is played during the Games, in public announcements, and in other official events or propaganda. During the Games, it is played every night throughout the arena while the faces of dead tributes are displayed.

The lyrics are:

Oh, Horn of Plenty
One Horn of Plenty for us all!
And when you raise the cry
The brave shall heed the call
And we shall never falter
One Horn of Plenty for us all!

Oh, Horn of Plenty.
One Horn of Plenty for us all!
And when we raise the cry
The brave shall heed the call
And we shall never fall

Oh, Horn of Plenty
One Horn of Plenty for us all!
And when we raise the cry
The brave shall heed the call
And we shall never falter
One Horn of Plenty for us all!

Oh, Capitol
Your glorious diamond shine
A tribute to
The darkest days behind
One Horn of Plenty for us all!

The lyrics are supposed to represent unity and prosperity. However, this is false, as the country is totalitarian and ruthlessly ruled, while prosperity is limited to the capital and its most loyal districts, with the remaining districts living in poverty and under harsh conditions.

An instrumental version can be heard before a reaping, meaning the song likely existed earlier but was not adopted as the national anthem until later. A different song was the national anthem during earlier Games.
