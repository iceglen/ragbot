Mitchell was a rebel soldier from District 13 assigned to the sharpshooter propaganda unit, Squad 451. He lived in District 13 and was assigned to Squad 451. He trained alongside his new compatriots for several days before deployment. On their sixth morning, the squad filmed reenactments of their reactions to explosions, and Mitchell badly projected an idea of desperation. The squad's commander accidentally triggered a pod. Triggered by the commotion, another squad member lost control and reacted violently. Mitchell tackled him and attempted to pin him to the ground, but was thrown off into a mislabeled pod, activating a barbed wire net that caught and speared him. Others unsuccessfully attempted to shoot it down. A third pod sent a wave of a tar-like substance to cover everything, including Mitchell's body, forcing the others to flee. On their way out, another member discovered his hand and gun hanging out of the net. Mitchell was described as an "older guy". Since it was emphasized how much strength it must have taken to launch him away, he may have been a larger man. Mitchell did not speak often, but could shoot very accurately, characterizing him as an excellent marksman. Mitchell also became known as the worst actor in the group.
