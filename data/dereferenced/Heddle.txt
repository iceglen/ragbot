Heddle was the female tribute from District 8 who participated in the 74th Hunger Games. She was 13 years old. Her occupation was tribute. Her fate was deceased. She was female.

In the Tribute Parade, she was seen wearing a costume. She was seen in shots during parade scenes. The film omits all of her appearances in the Training Center. She survived the initial Cornucopia bloodbath and managed to escape into the forest. It is unknown if she was able to grab any supplies or weapons.

She watched the Fallen images and tried to sleep, but she gave in to the cold and started a fire near a tree. The light from her fire attracted other tributes to her location. She was stabbed. A cannon sounded. She placed 13th out of 24 in the book and 12th out of 24 in the film.
