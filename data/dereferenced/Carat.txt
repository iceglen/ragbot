Carat was one of two female tributes from District 1 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Carat was part of the Career pack in these Games. In the Training Center, she and her district partners wore specific uniforms.

Carat survived the initial bloodbath but died shortly after when she ate or drank something poisonous. Her death was shown in a recap of the Games after they ended.

The word Carat refers to a unit of mass used for measuring gemstones and pearls.
