Loupe was one of two male tributes from District 1 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. He was part of the Career pack in these games. In the training center, he and his district partners wore a specific color. On the second day, he was killed single-handedly by another tribute with a poisoned dart after breaking away from the Career pack with another tribute. This event was kept accurate in the recap of the games. The victor took a first aid kit from him. The name Loupe refers to a small magnification device frequently used to examine small details more closely.
