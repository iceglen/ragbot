Otto was a tribute from District 6 for the 10th Hunger Games. He was male and between 12 and 18 years old. His weapon was a crossbow. He died before the Games started.

During a tour of the arena before the start of the Games, rebel bombs exploded, killing him. His body was later draped on a horse and paraded down a road during a funeral.

In a film adaptation, he survived until the arena. He shot another tribute with a crossbow. He later hid under rubble and was killed offscreen.
