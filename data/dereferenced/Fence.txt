The fence is an old security setting made to separate the district from the woods. Ostensibly, the fence was supposed to keep the district's streets safe from wild animals. It was supposed to be electrified 24 hours a day, but due to frequent power outages, this was rarely the case, so it was a simple matter to cross it. While trespassing and poaching were technically illegal, the Peacekeepers never enforced those laws. After a certain event, the fence was consistently electrified, cutting the district off from a major source of food. During a bombing, refugees tore down a portion of the fence to escape into the woods. Following a rebel victory, the fence was propped up by branches to keep wild animals out, but citizens were able to freely enter and exit the woods. The fence is described as being a 6 meter high chain link fence topped with barbed wire loops. It is quite old and rusty because it has not been well maintained or used. Despite its oppressive presence, even the fence could offer the district's citizens some resources.
