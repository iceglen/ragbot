Natalie Dormer is an English actress who portrayed Cressida in the movies The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1 & 2.

Dormer was born in Reading, Berkshire. She attended secondary school. She grew up with her stepfather, mother, sister, and brother. She has said that she was the victim of bullying while at school. At school, Dormer was head girl, a straight-A student, and vice-captain of the school netball team. She also got to travel the world with her school's public speaking team.
During her school years, Dormer trained in dance. She describes herself as the "academic hopeful" of the family and hoped to study history at university; but, in her exam, she did not achieve the grade she needed. Dormer decided she would audition for drama schools and decided to train at a dramatic art academy.

Dormer's film debut was in a film released in 2005. She played a role in a television series for which she received highly positive reviews. She also played a role on a television series. She has also acted in some films. It was announced that Dormer was cast as Cressida in both parts of The Hunger Games: Mockingjay. In preparation for the role, she shaved the left side of her head.
