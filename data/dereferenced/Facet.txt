Facet was the male tribute from District 1 in the 10th Hunger Games. He was noted as looking like he could be a strong contender. Along with the other tributes, he was taken to the Capitol and placed in the zoo.

While touring the Capitol Arena, several bombs were detonated. The explosions created a cracked opening. Facet, along with his district partner and other tributes, used the mayhem as an opportunity to attempt an escape. He was shot dead before he could reach the opening. His body was later dragged down a road during a funeral.

He never competed in the 10th Hunger Games. His death was noted as significantly increasing another tribute's odds of winning.
