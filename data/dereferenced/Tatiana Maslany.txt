Tatiana Maslany is a Canadian actress who narrated the 10-year anniversary audiobooks for The Hunger Games trilogy. She is known for playing multiple characters in a science-fiction television series. In addition to acting for film, TV, and the stage, she has voice acted in a number of shows and appeared on various podcasts. The Hunger Games was her first audiobook, released to mark the 10-year anniversary of the first novel's publication. She also recorded versions of the subsequent books in the trilogy, which were both released later. Earlier versions of the audiobooks were narrated by another person.
