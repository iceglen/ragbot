A Holo is a handheld device used to show locations of known pods hidden in the streets. It produces a holographic image, with pods represented by flashing lights both above and belowground. This does not show pods that were unaware of. The most basic commands consist of entering the coordinates of the nearest map grid intersections. It cannot send nor receive signals. A Holo also has fingerprint recognition and a password for additional security. When prime security clearance is transferred, a green shaft of light bursts from the device. A Holo can only be activated by a specific commander's voice. Once on, it responds to other voices from the squadron so someone else can take over if the commander is killed. If anyone in the squadron flicks the switch and says "nightlock" three times in a row, the Holo explodes everything in a five-yard radius, which serves the purpose of killing them if they get caught and preventing the Holo from falling into enemy hands.
