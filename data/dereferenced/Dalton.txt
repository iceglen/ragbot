Dalton was a District 10 refugee that made his way to District 13 by foot many years before another character arrived. During his life in District 10 he had worked on one of the beef ranches, maintaining the genetic diversity of the herd with the implantation of long-frozen cow embryos. He suggests that District 13 needs all the refugees they can get because, ever since a pox epidemic killed many of their own and left even more infertile, they have needed a new genetic gene pool to draw from in order to have children and make their population more sustainable. He was part of a small group who were invited to a meeting to brainstorm ideas for propaganda. At this meeting he suggests that they should "wash her face," and goes on to say, "She's still a girl and you make her look thirty-five. Feels wrong. Like something the Capitol would do." He was a participant in a wedding. He held the marriage ritual because it was similar to District 10's own wedding ceremonies.
