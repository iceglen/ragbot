Tim O'Brien is an American artist who illustrated the covers of The Hunger Games trilogy, The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, and Sunrise on the Reaping, as well as the alternate mockingjay design for the The Hunger Games Collector's Edition.
His illustrations have appeared in several magazines. He has also designed several postage stamps for the US Postal Service. O'Brien is an adjunct professor in Undergraduate Communications Design at the Pratt Institute.

Although characterizing himself as a "traditional painter," O'Brien decided to do the designs digitally, which helped out due to the trilogy's popularity. The creation of the iconic mockingjay pin has been characterized as a collaboration. According to O'Brien, "It is not just a picture of a bird. It is something vulnerable. It is a symbol of the main character, who is tough and beautiful at the same time." Before the final version was adopted, he had submitted a design for an arrow and an alternate version of the logo that had the mockingjay's right wing pierced with a chain. It could have meant that for the first and second books, the main character is under the power of the Capitol; she is being chained to their power.
The process of designing the cover for the second book did not take as long, although O'Brien created several versions of the mockingjay in flight. The circle in the background was first conceived as a clock, then a ring of fire, before it evolved into a target.
