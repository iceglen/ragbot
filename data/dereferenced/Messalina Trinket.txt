Messalina Trinket was the great-aunt of Effie and Proserpina Trinket. She was married to Silius Trinket.

Messalina was active during the war. Because of this, she had to attend many funerals, and thus owned a lot of black clothing. According to Effie, Messalina once brought great disgrace to the Trinket family. The disgrace involved weapons and blood, as a decorative holster and bloodstains were found on borrowed clothes.
The Trinket family still hadn't recovered their reputation by the time of the 50th Hunger Games. Before the interviews, Effie asked Messalina if she could use her clothes, and she acquiesced, presumably feeling that she'd owed Effie for the shame she'd brought to the family.

According to Proserpina, Messalina never threw anything away.
