This unknown District 4 male was the victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Games.

Years after his initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third Quarter Quell would be reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 4's reaping for the 75th Hunger Games, though he was not chosen to compete and did not volunteer.

At the end of the Second Rebellion, he was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so he presumably died in the Victors' Purge.
