A baker is a person whose profession is making and selling bread, as well as other baked goods and pastries.

Bakers sold their wares to other people for regular consumption. However, people in the poorest areas were often unable to afford to buy baked goods, so they made their own from grain. Specialty items like cakes could be purchased for special occasions, if one could afford them. Bakers were also aware of, and may have also made, different types of bread native to other districts.

Despite the expense of bakery goods, people could sometimes acquire them if the baker accepted a trade in place of monetary payment.
