Santino Fontana narrated The Hunger Games prequel book The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes. He has narrated titles such as The Institute, for which he won an award. In addition to his work in audiobooks, he is an actor of film, television, and stage. He is known for film roles such as a voice in an animated film and a role in another film. His television work has included roles in series. As a stage actor, he won an award for his role in a play. Regarding his recording of The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, he stated that he was surrounded by security-level protocols for the title. He stated that he had never done anything this top secret and that they installed a safe in his apartment and a specially manipulated iPad that had nothing else but the book on it. He had to keep the iPad in the safe. He stated further that every communication he received regarding the title contained no details of the story's plot and was simply about character names and pronunciations. He has not read the original The Hunger Games trilogy, but considers himself a big fan of the movie versions. He was chosen for the role of reading the audiobook by the author's editor, who saw him perform in a play and reached out to his agents.
