Cinna was a stylist for the 74th and 75th Hunger Games. He was known for his quiet and supportive manner. His designs were utilitarian with an underlying elegance and often incorporated technological effects for drama. He created the "girl on fire" theme for his tribute, using a costume with artificial flames for the tribute parade. For the interview after the games, he designed a simple dress intended to project an innocent image.

He remained the personal stylist for the Victory Tour, designing numerous outfits. When the tribute was reaped for the subsequent Games, he designed a black jumpsuit that glowed like embers. For the interview, he secretly altered a wedding dress so it would transform into a mockingjay costume, symbolizing rebellion. This act led to his arrest and death.

Before his death, he had already designed a mockingjay uniform for the rebellion. The outfit incorporated body armor, hidden weapons, and protective reinforcements. His final message expressed continued belief in the tribute. His designs were crucial to establishing and maintaining the symbolic image of the rebellion. He was described as appearing normal compared to the flamboyant styles of his home, with minimal makeup and simple clothing.
