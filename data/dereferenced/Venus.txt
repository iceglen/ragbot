Venus was a four-year-old girl who talked to Lucy Gray Baird when she was in the Capitol Zoo. She had an older brother.

Venus was among a group of small children clustered near the bars of the monkey house, where the tributes were located. Lucy Gray Baird walked over and shook hands with her brother, then asked him about Venus, who stood next to him. Venus complimented Lucy Gray's song and nodded when Lucy Gray said she would try to sing another. She also asked Lucy Gray about the snake and told her she had a fish. After this exchange, Venus buried her face in her mother's skirt.
