Lou Lou was a canary owned prior to a major competition. The name of this canary was the inspiration for a nickname for a body double.

Sometime prior to a major competition, the owner came into possession of Lou Lou. Another individual was enraged that the owner had encaged a bird, let alone a songbird, and complained about Lou Lou.

Before a major event, a body double was created. The other participants did not want to refer to this body double by its original name, so they tried to think of a name that was close enough. The owner suggested Lou Lou after her pet canary. The name was adopted.

It is mentioned that after the owner died, the owner's parents gave the canary to another individual. It is not entirely sure if this canary was Lou Lou. The owner words it that she "used to have a pet canary by that name." This could be understood as meaning that either she had Lou Lou before, but that she died sometime prior, or that she had Lou Lou up to the event, but no longer considered Lou Lou hers because she would never see her again. The latter is more likely, because neither source ever indicate that the owner had more than one canary.
