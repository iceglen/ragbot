The Hunger Games: Girl on Fire was a mobile game for iOS inspired by The Hunger Games trilogy, created as a teaser for the series' first film adaptation. Its release date was March 22, 2012, the day before the film premiered.

The game is an action teaser game. The player helps a character make an escape after being ambushed. The player defends the character from attackers with a hunting bow and leaps through treetops with touch controls to help the character return home.
