Leeg 2 was a rebel soldier from District 13 assigned to the sharpshooter propaganda unit, Squad 451. She lived in District 13 with her sister and an unnamed father. The sisters were both assigned to Squad 451 and trained alongside their new compatriots for several days before deployment. During this time, they were given the nicknames "Leeg 1" and "Leeg 2" to distinguish them. Once deployed, they filmed propaganda for several days. On the fourth morning, she hit a mislabeled pod which shot a burst of metal darts through her brain, killing her instantly.

She was a young woman in her twenties. She lacked the yellow flecks seen in her sister's eyes, which was the only way to tell the sisters apart, as they looked very similar in uniform.
