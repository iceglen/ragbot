Bircher was one of two male tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the games, Bircher joined an alliance between several districts' tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Bircher's district was the first to join this alliance. The District 7 tributes suggested a name for the alliance. Bircher and his district partners made a good impression during a knife throwing session in training.
Bircher died in the initial bloodbath with the other boy from his district.

The District 7 tributes' training outfits were russet brown.

The name is related to "birch," a type of tree.
