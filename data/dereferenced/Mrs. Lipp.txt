Mrs. Lipp was an unnamed woman from District 12, implied to be the wife of the mayor and mother of Mayfair Lipp.

She was middle-aged and had dyed blonde hair.

She, her husband, and her daughter rode in their car to the hanging tree for an execution. She stepped out of the car after her husband, and the three of them formed a tight knot at the side of the platform. According to another character, the family was despised by the district's coal miners because of their money, power, and privilege. It was mentioned that the family rode in their car to watch the hangings, suggesting that this may have been a common occurrence.
