Yellow Flicker Beat is the first official single from a soundtrack. The song is performed and written by the artist and was released on September 29th, 2014. The artist is the sole curator for the soundtrack.

The song's lyrics describe a character's transformation and resolve, using imagery of precious stones, fire, and flickering light to convey inner strength and a defiant spirit. The lyrics reference a shift from public acclaim to a more isolated state, and a determination to confront past adversaries.

The song was created after the artist viewed a scene from the related film, aiming to capture a character's instability and determination through the music's atmosphere.

The song achieved chart success in several territories, reaching top 40 positions in multiple countries and a top 5 position in one.

A music video was created for the song, conceived to be filmed in a large, cavernous space.

The song received award nominations for Best Song categories.
