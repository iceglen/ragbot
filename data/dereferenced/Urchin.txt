Urchin was one of two male tributes from District 4 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. He was one of four Career Tributes who died on the first day.

Urchin was part of the Career pack in these Games. He survived the initial bloodbath but still died on the first day after eating or drinking something poisonous.

Urchin appeared to be quite confident. He also had a mean streak.
