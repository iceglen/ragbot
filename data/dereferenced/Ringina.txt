Ringina was one of two female tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Ringina joined an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Ringina's district was the first to join this alliance. During training, the District 7 tributes were present at the knife throwing station. At one point, Ringina hit a bulls-eye. Later, while discussing plans for the alliance to hunt down the Careers first, she asked what they do when they've killed them. She was the first to suggest that the alliance needed a name.

After the cannons sounded for the bloodbath, it was later revealed that Ringina survived the bloodbath.

Ringina made it into the top ten along with the other girl from her district. They were killed by Careers in combat.

Ringina was a slim girl with glossy, black braids. She wore a small carved pin of a tree on her shirt as her tribute token. The District 7 tributes' training outfits were russet brown.

The District 7 tributes are noted as being confident, but not full of themselves. Ringina herself was shown to be a good sport and willing to work and practice training with the other tributes who were not from her district. She also showed concern for another tribute when he was ambushed and attacked. In addition, despite being a skilled fighter, Ringina considered attacking Peacekeepers but knew she couldn't, believing the ruling power to be stronger than the districts.

Ringina is a portmanteau of 'ring', referring to the rings of a tree, and a name meaning 'queen'.
