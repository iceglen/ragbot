Stanley Tucci is an Italian-American actor who portrayed Caesar Flickerman in The Hunger Games film and The Hunger Games: Catching Fire.

He is best known for his roles in Easy A, Burlesque, The Lovely Bones, The Devil Wears Prada, Julie and Julia and Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters.
Tucci was nominated for an Academy Award Best Supporting Actor for the movie Lovely Bones. He won an Emmy and a Golden Globe for his performance in the movie Winchell. He was also nominated for a Grammy for  Best Spoken Word Album for Children for The One and Only Shrek.
