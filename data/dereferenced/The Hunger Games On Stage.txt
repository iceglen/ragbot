The Hunger Games On Stage is a live stage adaptation of The Hunger Games. It is a stage production based on the work.

In a dystopian future, the Hunger Games ignite a battle for survival, where young tributes are pitted against each other in a deadly arena. A fearless and resourceful heroine emerges as a symbol of rebellion as she fights not only for her life but for the hope of a nation oppressed by a ruthless Capitol. It is a gripping tale of courage, defiance, and the unbreakable human spirit.

The stage production has been adapted by a playwright, with collaboration from the author, and it is directed by a director. Other members of the creative team include a set designer, costume designer, choreographer, lighting designer, sound designer, video designer, illusions specialist, fight director, performer flying specialist, casting director, and production manager. Including theater construction expenses, the adaptation cost a significant amount to produce.

The production was originally set to debut in a major theater district. It was later delayed. The show opened at a specific theater. A performer was announced to play the lead role. Other principal castings were subsequently revealed. The full cast was later revealed. The show's running time was cut. Preview performances began, with the first preview unexpectedly delayed. It also took place amidst unfinished construction, provoking complaints. The official opening night was scheduled, and the production was extended due to demand.

The theater was built specifically for the production. It contains a total of seats on all sides of a round stage, with sections named after the districts. One district serves as the stage. Upon a tribute's death, their corresponding section of the audience flashes red. The venue also encourages audience participation by displaying expected reactions on screens during certain scenes. The seats in some sections move throughout the performance as part of the immersive experience. Actors are seen fighting on the walls and in angles in front of the upper balcony. The theatre has multiple floors, including an outside terrace and a restaurant.
