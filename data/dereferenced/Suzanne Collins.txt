Suzanne Collins is an American television writer and novelist, best known for writing The Hunger Games trilogy. She began her career in television, writing episodes and actively participating on the staff of shows. She was also the head-writer of a children's program, and co-writer of a Christmas special. When she started working on a children's show, she met a children's author who suggested that she should try her hand at writing children's books. Her first book was followed by four more books in a series. She wrote the young-adult dystopian novel The Hunger Games. The author claims that her early exposure to Greek mythology, especially a certain story, planted seeds in her mind for the story. It wasn't until much later, when she was flipping between war coverage and reality T.V. programming, that the main character's story finally came to her. The sequel was released the following year. The final book in the trilogy was released later. A prequel to The Hunger Games was released.
