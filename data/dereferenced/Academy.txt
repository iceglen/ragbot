The Academy was an elite, four-year secondary school in the Capitol for the children of the wealthy and powerful.

The school provided students with free meals and uniforms for daily use, though they were required to finance their own clothing for special events. The uniform included underwear and socks. In one depiction, uniforms consisted of a light blue dress shirt, a red blazer, and a golden pin of the Academy's logo. All students wore red skirts over matching red trousers.

The Academy presented monetary prizes to high-performing students at graduation. Those who performed well often went on to attend higher education.

During one year, the Academy's top students were selected to mentor tributes from the districts. This program was discontinued after that year.

Students who committed serious infractions might receive a demerit. Three demerits would result in expulsion. There was a tradition of offering expelled students an alternative to public disgrace. Having an Academy diploma allowed for certain opportunities.

The Academy had a gathering area which hosted events such as viewing the districts' reapings. There was a high biology lab and many other classrooms.
