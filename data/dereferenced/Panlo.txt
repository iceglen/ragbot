Panlo was the male tribute from District 9 in the 10th Hunger Games. He was treated by a veterinarian from an incident with his district partner, but eventually succumbed to his wounds.

Panlo grew up in District 9 and became his district's male tribute. Along with the other tributes, Panlo was taken to the Capitol in a cattle car and placed in the Capitol Zoo.

Before the start of the Games, the tributes were taken on a tour of the Capitol Arena. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, heavily injuring the District 9 duo. He was denied hospital care and eventually succumbed to his wounds. His body was later paraded during a funeral.

In a film adaptation, he survived the bombing, but was the first tribute killed in the arena's bloodbath. When the gong rang, Panlo ran towards a pile of weapons, grabbing a sword. He was then speared in the chest with a trident.
