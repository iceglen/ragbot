Lucy Gray Baird's father was the father of Lucy Gray Baird and two other unnamed children. He was a musician. He was a member of the Covey, a group of nomadic people. He fathered two unnamed children, and three years before the start of the Dark Days, he fathered his youngest, Lucy Gray. He likely had at least two brothers who fathered Lucy Gray's cousins. A few years prior to the events, he, his family, and a number of other Covey were rounded up. He was fatally shot for trying to speak out and fight against being rounded up. According to Lucy Gray, the mother of his children was also killed. Their two oldest children died as well, leaving Lucy Gray the only surviving member of her immediate family.
