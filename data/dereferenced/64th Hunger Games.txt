The 64th Hunger Games was a regular event. The victor was Cashmere from District 1. This Games was preceded by the 63rd Hunger Games and followed by the 65th Hunger Games. The victor was a female tribute from a Career district. She likely formed a typical alliance with other Career Tributes, as she was described as a classic Career. The victor was adept with throwing knives, which may have been her weapon of choice.
