The Citadel was a military institution located within the Capitol. It housed the War Department, guarded at all times by Peacekeepers as a high-security zone. It was the base for Gamemaker subordinates, where they created muttations in underground laboratories.

The Citadel was gated, and visitors had to stop at the Peacekeeper station for a retinal scan, which was checked against the Capitol's files. Any bags they carried could be left at the station. With this done, visitors would go down a long, gray corridor and onto an elevator, either escorted by a Peacekeeper or unaccompanied.

The lab was located at least twenty-five floors belowground. The mutts created were stored there as well.
