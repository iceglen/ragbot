Hazelle Hawthorne is the widowed mother of Gale, Rory, Vick, and Posy. She lived and worked in the Seam of District 12. Her husband died in a coal mining accident. She worked as a washerwoman, collecting people's dirty laundry, washing it, and returning it. In the winter, her hands would crack and bleed. Her business did well until her son was caught poaching and whipped. She comforted him through his pain. Soon afterward, her daughter contracted the measles. People stopped using her services, and her son had to sign up for tesserae. However, she was taken on as a housekeeper, earning extra money. She and her family were among the few who survived the bombing of District 12 and were evacuated to District 13. She was devoted to her family. Her husband died in a mine accident, leaving her alone with her children. She was pregnant at the time. Her oldest son helped take care of the family. Her sons also helped by applying for tesserae. Her youngest children were her reason for working so hard. When her daughter fell ill, she worked extremely hard to nurse her back to health. When her son was whipped, she went to stay with him. She and the rest of her children escaped the District 12 bombing.
