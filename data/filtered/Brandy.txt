Brandy was the female tribute from District 10 who was reaped to participate in the 10th Hunger Games. She had spent her life butchering hogs.

She grew up in District 10, spending most of her time butchering hogs, as the district's main industry is livestock. She became her district's female tribute in the reaping for the 10th Hunger Games. Along with the other tributes, she was taken to the Capitol in a dirty cattle car. From there, they were dumped into the monkey house at the Capitol Zoo.

She died two days after the reaping. Her mentor brought the starved Brandy a sandwich in the zoo, but instead of handing it over, she lectured her and taunted the tribute for the spectating Capitol citizens. However, Brandy grabbed the handle of the mentor's own knife and leaned forward to slit the mentor's throat. After the mentor dropped the sandwich, Brandy retrieved the sandwich and took a furious bite before being gunned down. Brandy was the first of the tributes to die, before the Hunger Games had even begun. During the mentor's funeral, Brandy's bullet-ridden body was hoisted high in the air by a crane affixed to a truck, where the remaining tributes were shackled.
