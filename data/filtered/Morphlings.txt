The morphlings were two tributes, one male and one female. They each won a previous unknown Hunger Games and participated in the 75th Hunger Games. As victors, they were likely given the responsibility of mentoring other tributes.

They had turned to a narcotic pain killer called morphling to cope with the horrors of their previous Games and were thus in very poor physical condition. The morphling had made their skin sag and turn a yellow color.

The two of them were quite fascinated by painting, as they spent most of their time training in the camouflage station painting each other during the training days. They later joined hands with the rest of the tributes as a sign of strength and rebellion at the interviews.

At the start of the Games, the morphlings reached the Cornucopia. During the bloodbath, the male was killed but the female escaped.

Later when a group was attacked by monkey mutts, one tribute was about to be bitten when the female morphling jumped in front, and she took a lethal bite to the chest instead. After the monkeys were finished off, she was comforted by being told to look at the colors of the sunrise. She was shaking and soon died. After she bled to death, her corpse was taken to the water to let it drift off until a hovercraft came to take it away.
