Antonius was Panem's Defense Minister and a member of the Presidential Cabinet, working closely alongside Coriolanus Snow. He was an entrusted and high-ranking member within the Capitol's political system.

Antonius informed the President of a presence in a district and advised that hovercraft could be deployed. He was told to "kill the wounded", meaning everyone within a hospital that had been visited. Later, he stood alongside others when rebels destroyed a hydroelectric dam, cutting the power.

Antonius was responsible for ordering numerous attacks on rebels who attempted to conquer a key arsenal. When the rebels launched a more uniquely devised attack, the arsenal caved in with many forces still trapped inside. Most of these forces had been sent at Antonius's request. As a result of his arrogance, Antonius was poisoned to death during a dinner. His actions had cost a hoped-for victory, as he had inadvertently allowed the rebels the upper hand.

Like his superior, Antonius was ambitious and calculating. However, despite his loyalty, Antonius was also arrogant, stubborn, and power-hungry, which ultimately led to his demise.
