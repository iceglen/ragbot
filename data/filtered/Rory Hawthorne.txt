Rory Hawthorne is the second-oldest child in the Hawthorne family and the brother of Gale, Vick, and Posy.

He lives in the Seam of District 12 with his family of five. He was 12 years old at the time of the Victory Tour.
Before the 75th Hunger Games, Rory was too young to work and had not yet learned the art of hunting. During a food shortage, he took out tesserae to help support the family. There was a desire to try teaching Rory how to hunt.
He and his family were among the few people who survived the bombing of District 12, and they were evacuated to District 13.

Rory has the same olive skin tone, grey eyes, and dark hair typical of residents of the Seam.

Rory has three siblings: Vick, Gale, and his only sister, Posy. His father died in a mine explosion five years prior to the start of the events. During the 74th Hunger Games, the families are referred to as cousins in order to invalidate a relationship.
Rory's mother raised her children by herself since her husband's death when Rory was six or seven.
