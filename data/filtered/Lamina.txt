Lamina was the female tribute from District 7 in the 10th Hunger Games. She was 15 years old. Her weapon was an axe and a knife. She was described as having a skinny, long-limbed frame. At the start of the Games, she was noted for being strangely calm.

During the Games, she initially hid. She later emerged and secured a small axe and a knife. She climbed a tall pole to reach a horizontal beam in the arena. On the beam, she used her axe to end the life of a critically injured tribute. She then claimed the beam as her position, using her balance and skill with the axe as a defense. She received sponsor gifts of water and food while stationed there.

On the third day, she was attacked by a group of tributes. They climbed the poles to reach her on the beam. She fought them while balancing, injuring one. During the fight, she was stabbed and fell from the beam to her death. She finished in 10th place.

In the film adaptation, she is depicted with red hair. Her strategy was similar, taking position on the beam after the initial bloodbath. She was attacked by an alliance of tributes, fought them on the beam, was stabbed, and fell to her death.
