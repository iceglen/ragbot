Kerna was one of two female tributes from District 9 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, it was noticed that everyone from her district had sunflower necklace tokens. They were instructed to join an alliance as the tokens could be used for a plan to sabotage the arena. Their efficiency and capabilities were thought to make them good allies.
While training, another tribute targeted Kerna. She was focusing on building pine needles and when she did not respond to a taunt, her token was grabbed and broken. She was devastated and the token was thought to mean more to her than just a piece of home. The token was fixed and Kerna, along with the rest of her district, decided to join the alliance. Even so, they weren't very sociable and almost appeared exclusive.
Kerna was the last person seen before dashing off into the woods to avoid the bloodbath. After running for a weapon, Kerna died in the initial bloodbath along with everyone else in her district. As the running finished, a young girl's shriek was heard. This may have been Kerna's, as she had just been seen, and she was the smallest girl in her district. Two of District 9's tokens were retrieved.

The District 9 tributes' training outfits were buttery yellow. Like the rest of her district, Kerna was still healing from sunburn, and had calloused hands and lean, muscled arms. She was the smallest tribute from her district.

Kerna, like the rest of her fellow tributes from District 9, weren't very sociable and liked to keep to themselves. However, Kerna was seemingly the most open of them, crying when her token was broken, as well as speaking with others.
