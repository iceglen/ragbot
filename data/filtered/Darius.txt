Darius was one of the youngest known Peacekeepers in District 12. He was later enslaved as an Avox.

He was stationed in District 12. He was easygoing and often joked around. He was willing to intervene against a fellow Peacekeeper when he saw a brutal punishment, suggesting a strong moral compass.

Upon his enslavement as an Avox, his tongue was mutilated so he could no longer speak. While imprisoned, he was tortured. He was beaten and mutilated until he died.
