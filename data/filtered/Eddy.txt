Eddy was a boy of around 12 years old who lived in District 8 during its bombing in the Second Rebellion.

During the bombing, Eddy and his sister were injured and ended up in the makeshift hospital. He was in awe when he got the chance to meet a visiting figure. He and his sister were some of the first patients to notice the visitor.

After the visitor left, an attack destroyed the hospital and killed the people within it. Eddy and his sister were confirmed deceased, as there were no survivors.

Eddy had damp, curly brown hair that covered his forehead. Half of his face was obscured by bandages while he was in the hospital.
