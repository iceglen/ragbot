Sage Blanken was a tribute from District 12 who participated in the 73rd Hunger Games.

Sage was born in the Seam, as was his district partner, and they were both reaped for the 73rd Hunger Games. He was mentored by a mentor. It was mentioned that he and his district partner ate like "savages". This upset another tribute greatly, as they came from the poorest region of District 12, where access to food was limited.

An early version of a script for a film gave his name as Sage Blanken, and his parents were vendors at a location. He was killed with a bar mace on the second day by another tribute.
