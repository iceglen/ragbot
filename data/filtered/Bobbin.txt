Bobbin was the male tribute from District 8 reaped for the 10th Hunger Games. He impressed his mentor during his interview by explaining multiple ways to kill someone with a sewing needle. He died during the night of Day 1 and finished in 13th place of the 24 tributes. He was the first person killed directly by another character. During the Games, he attacked two individuals who had entered the arena. In defense, one of them seized an object and beat Bobbin violently, ending his life. His death contributed to a character's realization about the nature of violence and control.
