Clay Chance was a victim of execution in District 12.

Clay was a member of the Chance family, who were known for their reckless and sometimes rebellious behavior.

The winter after the 46th Hunger Games, Clay Chance was sentenced to be hanged in the gallows for an unknown crime. The night before his execution, someone filed halfway through the rope, so when they tried to hang him in front of a crowd the next day, the rope snapped. Clay fell to the ground, where he was shot dead.

A witness in town reported that they saw a twelve-year-old girl leaving the square, so she was taken for questioning. However, Clay's sister claimed responsibility. She had been living due to a heart condition, so three days after being taken into custody, she died in her cell and the girl was released.
