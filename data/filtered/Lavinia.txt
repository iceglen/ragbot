Lavinia was an Avox girl who worked as a Capitol servant. She is known simply as the "Avox girl". Her name was revealed later.

Before she became an Avox, she and an unknown boy were seen while out hunting in the woods. They appeared to be escaping for unknown reasons. A hovercraft appeared; the boy was killed. She was captured.

Years later, she served as an Avox whose tongue had been cut out for committing crimes. She could not publicly acknowledge anything that was not a direct order. When she later walked in on a mess made in a room she showed some response, indicating by gestures that an apology was not needed, and that there was nothing that could have been done.

During the Quarter Quell, she was once again an Avox. After the games, she was taken hostage, and she was tortured and killed by electrocution.

She has dark red hair, striking features, and porcelain white skin. Being an Avox, her tongue was cut out and she is therefore mute. She is of the lowest social class, essentially a slave. She is described as a matching set with another Avox for their similarly red hair.
