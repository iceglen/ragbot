Boggs was a colonel and the Head of Security of District 13. He served as a second-in-command. He was assigned as a protector for the symbolic figure of the rebellion. He led a squad on a mission to the Capitol. During this mission, he accidentally triggered an explosive device that severed his legs. While fatally wounded, he transferred control of a key device to his charge and warned against trusting certain individuals. He died from blood loss. He was a respectable individual who genuinely believed in the cause of freedom. He had great respect for the symbolic figure he protected and showed absolute trust. In appearance, he was in his mid-forties with close-cropped gray hair and blue eyes, noted for having incredible posture.
