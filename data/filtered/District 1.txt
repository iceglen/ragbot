District 1 is the wealthiest district in Panem, whose primary industry is the manufacture of luxury items. As a result, District 1 has a generally favorable relationship with the Capitol, although they are still forced to participate in the annual Hunger Games. However, their money and privilege allow them to train their tributes for the Games without punishment.

District 1 began to train Career Tributes. Although technically against the rules, their children were trained to prepare them for combat inside the arena. Career training made District 1 tributes popular bets among sponsors. Despite their success in the Games and outward loyalty to the Capitol, some in District 1 secretly resented their arrangement, and they sided with the rebels faster than their counterparts in another district.

Despite District 1’s close relations with the Capitol, they still rebelled in the Second Rebellion, being the second district to do so. As a Career district, it had a large pool of victors; however, none of the victors from District 1 survived the war.

All known characters from District 1 had luxurious or lofty-sounding names, tying in with the luxuries they sell. Tributes from District 1 are known to have started training for the Hunger Games from a very young age. The victors from District 1 are known to have continued training even after winning.

They were in charge of producing luxury items. It was heard that District 1 had a machine that could turn graphite into diamonds. They produced items such as decent liquor, makeup, and tobacco, though they were often hard to get ahold of and very expensive, which made the black market flourish.

Career tributes from District 1, like all other Careers, were strong and well-prepared. However, they were often overconfident or even arrogant.
