Chaff was the male tribute for District 11 who participated in the 75th Hunger Games and was the victor of the 45th Hunger Games. He won the 45th Hunger Games between the ages of 15 and 18, but lost his left hand in the process. Following his victory, he turned to alcohol to cope with his trauma. Being a previous victor, he was reaped for the 75th Hunger Games. He was part of the rebellion and was instructed to try his best to find and protect two other tributes. He was one of the few tributes who knew about the plan to get one of them out of the arena. During training, he became close with another tribute and they trained together. He survived the initial Cornucopia bloodbath and escaped into the jungle. He was not seen much during the Games, most likely seeking the tributes he was meant to protect. He was able to make it to the final eight. Later on in the Games, he finally met up with one of them, only to be killed by another tribute moments later. He placed eighth out of twenty-four. He was at least six feet tall, with dark skin, and had an arm that ended with a stump because he lost his hand. He was in his mid to late forties when he died. He was killed at the very end of the 75th Hunger Games just minutes before the force field surrounding the Arena was destroyed. He seemed to be a troublemaker. He loved to laugh and was close friends with another victor, whom he drank with. He joked by kissing one tribute on the lips and laughing at her reaction. However, he did have a soft side, shown when he was friendly and kind during the training sessions, as well as when he backed his district partner up during her interview, and when he joined hands with the other Victors at the end of the interviews without hesitation.
