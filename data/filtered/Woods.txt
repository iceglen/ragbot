The woods are an area that lies beyond the fence that surrounds District 12.

The woods are located in the region of Appalachia, and they come between District 12 and District 13. They are very wide and vast, though the exact area is unknown.

An electric fence separated the outskirts of District 12 from the woods, but it was rarely on due to power shortages. There were many holes across the length of it that could be used to gain access to the woods. One of the most accessible points was the one by the Meadow. Supposedly, the fence was placed there to keep wild animals out and protect the streets, but it is suspected it was so they could ensure citizens were kept inside. It was later torn down by escaping refugees.

There is a special meeting place: a rock ledge overlooking a valley, well-hidden by a thicket of berry bushes.

A couple of hours into the woods, there is a lake with an old concrete house at its edge. There used to be a lot of other buildings a long time ago, as the foundations were still visible, where people could come to play and fish. After a bombing, survivors were brought to the lake.

The forest is a place some citizens venture to hunt or gather food. In the fall, some would sneak into the woods to harvest apples. People entered the forest regularly to hunt to provide for their families. They hunted rabbits, squirrels, and the occasional deer, and then brought them back to sell or trade. They also collected plants, both edible and medicinal. Mesh nets were set up around strawberry patches so wild animals couldn't eat them. People could also fish in the rivers and lakes, or collect katniss tubers from the shallows.
