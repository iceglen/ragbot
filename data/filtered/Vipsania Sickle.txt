Vipsania Sickle was a female resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the 10th Hunger Games. She was assigned the male tribute from District 7.

During the games, her tribute snatched two water bottles that were sent as sponsor gifts for another tribute, who had failed to claim them. She commented that this was good thinking, as it saved her money, and she didn't have much to work with. She was the only mentor who thought to bring bedding to sleep overnight and thus slept comfortably in it while her fellow mentors sat sleeping in padded chairs. After her tribute was killed, she was escorted away. She thanked the Gamemakers through gritted teeth.
