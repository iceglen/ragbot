Wiress was the victor of the 49th Hunger Games. Known for her odd demeanor and eccentricities, she was nicknamed "Nuts". A woman of impressive intellect, she mentored many tributes over the years following her victory. Despite trauma from her games, she continued to serve the rebellion, joining the victor alliance after being reaped into the 75th Hunger Games.

She was known for her technical prowess and intelligence. During the 75th Hunger Games, she quickly deduced the clock-like design of the arena, which she communicated by repeating "Tick, tock." This discovery was crucial for her allies. While cleaning a spool of wire in the arena, she was killed when her throat was slit. She was described as shy, intellectually brilliant, inventive, and clever, often being the first to figure out the mechanisms of an arena.
