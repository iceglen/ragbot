This four-note song was used to tell fellow harvesters when the working day was over. The user would be the first to see the quitting time flag from a perch high in the trees, then when the tune was sung, the surrounding mockingjays would pick up the song and sing it back to those on the ground.

During a competition, the song was used to signal an ally that the user was safe, but couldn't get back right away. Later, the melody was sung back in answer to let the user know it was safe to come out. In a film adaptation, rather than using the same melody, the user sang four notes, and a different four-note melody was whistled back.

On a subsequent tour, the song was whistled from a crowd as a prearranged signal for the audience to give a three-finger salute.

The four-note melody is quite simple. However, the notes more commonly heard and referred to as the song are actually the reply melody.
