Sylva Mayleaf was the female tribute from District 9 in the 75th Hunger Games, and the victor of an unspecified Hunger Games. She resided in the Victors' Village in District 9.

She participated in and won an unknown edition of the Hunger Games before being reaped for the 75th Hunger Games.

During the Tribute Parade, tributes were costumed as loaves of bread; this was presumably District 9's tributes, since it had food-producing industries. In the film adaptation, she and her district partner were dressed in golden outfits possibly designed to look like wheat or bread instead.

It is unknown if she showed up for training, as she was never mentioned. In the film, she was seen talking to other tributes in the waiting room.

During the interviews, she wore a sleeveless top and a white necklace and a black skirt over a pair of slacks matching her shirt color. After the tributes were interviewed, she, along with the other tributes, joined hands as a sign of rebellion.

She was killed during the initial Cornucopia bloodbath. She was launched into the arena on a pedestal and was killed towards the end of the bloodbath.
