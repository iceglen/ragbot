Octavia is a member of a prep team for a tribute during the Hunger Games. She assists in preparing the tribute for appearances. She has dyed green skin and wears elaborate Capitol fashion, including decorative nails and brightly colored hair. She is described as plump and in her early twenties. After being taken to a district, her natural auburn hair is revealed and her appearance becomes more ordinary. She is fearful following a period of captivity and punishment. She works alongside two other team members. Her role includes preparing the tribute for both the Games and a symbolic role as a mockingjay.
