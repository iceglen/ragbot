Brutus was the male tribute from District 2 for the 75th Hunger Games and the victor of an unknown past Games. He was also a Career Tribute.

During the 75th Hunger Games, he volunteered. In training, he practiced spear-throwing. He received a fairly high training score. He wore a silver chest plate, black shorts, and black boots.

At the start of the Games, he obtained a spear. He and other Career tributes approached the Cornucopia and were later driven off. He and other Careers followed a group of tributes. Over time, the number of tributes decreased. A plan was set to electrocute the water. He pursued two tributes through the jungle. In a subsequent battle, he killed another tribute and was then killed himself. He was the last tribute to die in that Games.
