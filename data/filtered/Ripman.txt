Ripman was one of two male tributes from District 8 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Ripman joined the Newcomers alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. He and his district partners were considered unremarkable.
He died in the initial bloodbath with the rest of his district.

The tributes from District 8 wore peach-colored outfits during the chariot rides and training.

The name suggests ripping or fabric tearing.
