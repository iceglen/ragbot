Maximinius Ravinstill was President of Panem. He was the president during the 10th Hunger Games and had been in office long before a major war. He ruled for the duration of the war. After years of fighting, a district threatened the Capitol with nuclear destruction and demanded to secede. A secret ceasefire deal was negotiated where that district retreated underground and the Capitol bombed the surface to create the illusion of its destruction. Once rebel forces were subdued, he oversaw the implementation of a treaty that imposed many restrictions on the defeated districts, including the creation of the annual Hunger Games. On the day of the reaping for the 10th Hunger Games, he appeared on television and recited a brief passage from the treaty. He later attended a funeral and delivered a eulogy. Due to his age, he is presumed to have died long before a later trilogy. By the time of the 10th Hunger Games, his hair was streaked with silver. He wore a prewar military uniform as a reminder that he had been in control long before the rebellion.
