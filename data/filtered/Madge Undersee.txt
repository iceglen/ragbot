Madge Undersee was the daughter of District 12's mayor. She was a friend of another student from school. She grew up relatively wealthy and did not have to sign up for tesserae. The two were in the same year at school and sat together, though they were not considered close friends.

On the day of the reaping, she was visited by two individuals selling strawberries. She was dressed in nice clothes for the event. She expressed a desire to look nice if she was picked. This led to a tense exchange about the tesserae system.

When one of the individuals was selected as a tribute, she visited them before the Games. She explained that tributes were allowed to wear a token from their district into the arena. She gave them a gold mockingjay pin from her own dress and asked them to wear it. She pinned it on their dress and made them promise to wear it into the arena. This act made the tribute realize she had truly been a friend all along.

After the Games, the two spent more time together. They taught each other skills; one taught archery, and the other tried to teach piano. They also ate meals at each other's houses. During a subsequent Victory Tour, they met again at the mayor's house. The tribute asked about returning the pin, but she insisted it was a gift. She explained the pin had belonged to her aunt.

After another individual was punished, she walked through a snowstorm to offer medicine. She claimed her mother said she could take it and urged its use for the injured friend. This act led to speculation about her feelings.

Her home was destroyed in a bombing. The entire family died. She was later described as quiet, kind, and brave.

She had wavy blonde hair and typically dressed in a school outfit. On the day of the reaping she wore an expensive dress and the mockingjay pin, with her hair done up with a ribbon. Her blonde hair was typical for the wealthier citizens.

She was an only child. Her father was busy as mayor, and her mother often suffered from headaches. It was revealed her mother's sister had been a tribute in a past Games.

She and the tribute spent time together at school but did not speak much. Their friendship deepened after the gift of the pin. The tribute was briefly jealous over implied feelings for the injured friend, but it did not interfere with their friendship. The tribute grieved deeply for her after her death.

She knew the injured individual from selling strawberries. There was a negative interaction on the reaping day due to resentment over wealth. Despite this, she later brought medicine for that individual when they were hurt, which led to implications about her feelings.
