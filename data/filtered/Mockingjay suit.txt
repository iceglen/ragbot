The Mockingjay suit is an outfit based on the mockingjay bird. As the face for the rebel cause, Katniss wore this suit for propos and major public appearances during the Second Rebellion.

The suit was described as a black uniform that was at first glance utterly utilitarian, at second a work of art. Particular features were appreciated, including the swoop of the helmet, the curve to the breastplate, and the slight fullness of the sleeves that allowed the white folds under the arms to show. The design included layers of body armor, hidden weapons in the boots and belt, and special reinforcement over the heart. The suit had short sleeves, leaving the forearms bare. The shoulder of one sleeve had a small pocket that held an emergency suicide pill.

In the film adaptation, the Mockingjay costume was black with very dark blue-black accents for details like the breastplate. It had long sleeves rather than short, there was no helmet, and it lacked the white underarm folds. The armor on the back had ridged wing details, and on the right hand, a specialized archery glove was worn. There was also an arm guard. The design took inspiration from historical armor collections, historical depictions of a figure, and Japanese archery gear. The design was crafted with a concern for the practical application of the outfit in combat, ensuring it was not too flashy. The costume was modular so that more ceremonial details, like the wings or the breastplate, could be removed in moments of combat for mobility purposes. Another aspect was a channel for the quiver to slot in, so it wouldn't bounce around.
