The District 6 male was a tribute from District 6 who participated in the 49th Hunger Games.

He managed to survive the Games until he and another tribute were the only tributes remaining. The other tribute had spent the whole Games in a blind spot where no one could find her, so when she finally emerged, the District 6 male leaped at her. However, the arena was a disorienting "Nest of Mirrors", so he misjudged the reflection, cracking his head on the surface and drowning in the lake.
