Lady was the goat that belonged to Primrose Everdeen. She was given to Prim for her tenth birthday by Katniss Everdeen, Prim's sister, and provided milk and cheese for the family.

When first seen, the goat was lying down in a cart, as her shoulder had been bitten and infected. The goat was originally going to be bought by a butcher for slaughtering, but the butcher recognized the intent to buy the goat and allowed the purchase. After purchasing the goat, a pink ribbon was tied around its neck. Prim was very excited when she received the goat. Even though there was worry about the goat's odds of survival, she was managed to be restored to full health.

After a reaping, it was suggested that cheese could be made from the goat's milk to sell. When another character became badly injured in a competition, the story of how the goat was acquired was told, altering details about how the money was obtained as an entire population was listening.

To cover for an absence, a story was fabricated about having discussed meeting a goat herder about getting the goat pregnant. In the story, directions had been misremembered and the wrong place was gone to, explaining the lengthy absence.

After a home was bombed, only a cat was found, so it was assumed that the goat had died.

Lady was a white goat with black patches, and when first brought home, the goat wore a pink ribbon around her neck.
