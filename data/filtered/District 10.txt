District 10 is one of Panem's thirteen districts. Its main industry is livestock, and it provides meat to the Capitol. The region raises livestock, which becomes the meat for the nation. The district operates slaughterhouses and butchering facilities. Adolescents in the district participate in the livestock industry. The district maintains the genetic diversity of its cattle herds. It is located in the southwest of North America. The flat topography in the region is ideal for raising cattle and other livestock.
