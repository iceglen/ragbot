Coal mining is the main occupation available to the residents of District 12. The job requires its workers to dig for coal in underground mines. The mines are open to either gender, and the only real qualification required is that employees have to be over a certain age.

Coal mining is an extremely dangerous profession, as accidents such as cave explosions are frequent. Some coal miners caught in these accidents are mutilated and often lose their limbs, while many others die in these mining accidents. Some coal miners can also experience trouble breathing at an older age due to working in the coal mines.

In response to a rebellion, the mines are closed for a period. When they reopen, workers' wages are cut, their hours extended and the work sites become more and more dangerous.

Coal mining is one of the few occupations that only opens to residents once their eligibility for a certain event is over, resulting in severely undertrained participants. While the children of some districts have the chance to build their strength and skills in their respective industries for years, the children of District 12 do not have the opportunity to develop special skills or strength that they would gain working in the mines. This helps to explain why District 12 had very few victors over a long period.

However, students in District 12 learn the basics of explosives in preparation for their work in the mines. As a result, a participant was able to use bombs during a specific event.
