Mrs. Plinth's sister was a resident of District 2 and the sister of Mrs. Plinth.

This woman was born and raised in District 2 alongside her sister, who later married and had a son. The family eventually moved away at the end of the Dark Days.
She was the only person from District 2 to keep in touch with Mrs. Plinth after the move; she even sent her a needlepoint of a mountain scene overlaid with the word "home".
