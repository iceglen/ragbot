Naida Dolan is a fisherwoman from District 4 who was chosen to become a District Hero.

Naida is the daughter of a deep-sea fisherman and a sixth-generation pearl diver, chosen to become a District Hero because of her skill in fishing.

Naida is tall with brown eyes and has long dark wavy hair, slightly streaked with brown. In her District Hero portrait, she has pearl earrings and wears a long brown dress made out of nets.
