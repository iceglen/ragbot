Coriolanus Snow's sister was the only daughter and younger sister. She was a newborn at the time of her death. Her home was the Capitol. She is deceased.

About five years after the birth of her older brother, her mother became pregnant with her. A few months into a period of conflict, her mother went into labor during a major air raid. Unable to reach a hospital, she was attended at home. Her mother died of what was likely a hemorrhage. The baby also did not survive. The wording suggests she may have been stillborn or passed very soon after her mother.
