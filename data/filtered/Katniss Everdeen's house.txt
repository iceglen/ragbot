Katniss Everdeen's house is where Katniss Everdeen lived with her mother and her sister Primrose up until her joint victory in the 74th Hunger Games.

The house was located nearly at the edge of District 12 in the Seam, a few gates down from the Meadow, the fence, and the woods.

Katniss described the house as a "squat little place". In the bedroom, Katniss and Prim generally slept in the same bed on a rough canvas-covered mattress, and their mother slept separately. Outside the bedroom, there was a tub and a cracked mirror leaning against the wall, as well as a cloth-covered dining table, a kitchen, a hearth, and a back door. They also seemed to have a problem with rats and mice.

This house was the place where Katniss was raised, so it was also her childhood home when her father was still alive.

After her husband died, Mrs. Everdeen fell into a deep depression, so severe that most days she was unable to move from a chair or her bed, leaving her homebound. Months later, once Mrs. Everdeen recovered, she began to run a small apothecary business for people in the Seam out of her home.

After surviving the Hunger Games, Katniss was given a new house in the Victors' Village as part of her winnings, and her family moved in with her. The old house remained Mrs. Everdeen and Prim's designated residence, and they would have to move back there if Katniss died. However, Katniss was the only person who still made use of the old place, stopping there to change clothes after hunting.

Katniss returned to District 12 a month after its bombing and discovered that her old house had been reduced to ashes, and the chimney was a collapsed heap of bricks. About six days later, she went back again to film propos in the ruins of the kitchen, but found herself "drowning" in "too many memories", so they moved along.
