Pontius was a young boy who talked to Lucy Gray Baird when she was in the Capitol Zoo. He had a younger sister.

Pontius was among a group of small children clustered near the bars of the monkey house, where the tributes were located. When Coriolanus Snow walked over and asked the children if they would like to meet Lucy Gray, Pontius was eager for the opportunity. He shook hands with Lucy Gray and told her about his sister.
