The District 12 music teacher was a woman who taught music at the school in District 12.

On the first day of school at age five, the music teacher asked if anyone knew the valley song. A student's hand shot up, so the teacher stood her up on a stool and had her sing it for the class.

At some point in the next eleven years, the teacher told her students about mountain airs. As a student thought of a song to sing, she thought that her teacher would use that term to describe a particular lullaby. The teacher was briefly mentioned again when a student recounted the music assembly story.
