Posy Hawthorne is the youngest child and the only female child in the Hawthorne family. She grew up in District 12 and has three older brothers. Her mother took care of her and her brothers.

She became ill with measles and was nursed back to health by her mother. Medicine was also provided for her recovery.

In a later event, jabberjays were able to mimic her cries in an arena.

She later found refuge in a district after a bombing. At one meal, she commented on the skin color of a member of a prep team, saying the person would look pretty no matter what shade of color her skin was. This gave the person a fondness for her.

She lived with her mother and her three brothers. She never met her father, as he died just before she was born. Another family is very much like family to them, due to a close relationship. During a public event, the family was publicly referred to as cousins, to keep them safe.

Her voice, along with those of her siblings, was mimicked by jabberjays in an event. She is one of the youngest characters mentioned by age.

All four siblings have four-letter names.
