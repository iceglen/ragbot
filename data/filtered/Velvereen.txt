Velvereen was the female tribute from District 1 for the 10th Hunger Games. She was reaped as a tribute. The tributes were taken to the Capitol and placed in a zoo. While touring the Capitol Arena, several bombs were detonated. These explosions created an opening by the entrance. Velvereen, along with her district partner and other tributes, used the resulting chaos as an opportunity for a possible escape. She was shot dead before she could reach the opening. Her body was later taken away. She never competed in the 10th Hunger Games.
