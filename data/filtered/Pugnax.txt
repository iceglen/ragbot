Pugnax was a District 2 citizen who was formerly employed in the Nut.

Pugnax was among the many workers and miners who survived the Nut's bombing and fled to the city center, where the rebels were waiting for them. He had a ragged hole in his cheek, likely having been injured by falling stone, scorch marks down the back of his shirt, and a gun. He was seen as just another victim from a mine accident. In response, he held his gun to another's head. An impromptu speech was made, which ended when a shot was fired from inside the crowd.

When he fled from the Nut, Pugnax held a bloody cloth to his punctured cheek and had scorch marks down the back of his shirt. He held a gun and had eyes crazed with pain and fear. His speech was garbled, and he smelled of burning things.
