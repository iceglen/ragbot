Haymitch Abernathy was District 12's second and only living Hunger Games victor for 23 years, having won the second Quarter Quell. He worked secretly to incite a rebellion and was the main drive behind the rebel plot during the 75th Hunger Games.

He was a victor and later served as a mentor for tributes. He was known for his tactical mind and observational skills. He often communicated with his tributes through the strategic sending or withholding of sponsor gifts. He was frequently drunk and surly, but could be understanding. He had difficulty sleeping due to nightmares and always slept with a knife in hand.

His personality was often sarcastic and unfriendly. He was tactical and observant. He had a keen intellect and skill at lying. He was handy with a knife and was intelligent, outsmarting other tributes. Although he had low physical endurance, he made up for it with his mental abilities.

After the war, he returned to his district, where he continued to drink out of habit and raised geese. He contributed to a memory book, documenting the tributes he had mentored. He eventually shared his own story of loss. He was content knowing a promise had been fulfilled.
