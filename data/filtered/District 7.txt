District 7 is one of the thirteen districts of Panem. It provided lumber and paper to the Capitol. The district is lush with trees, from which its citizens supply lumber and paper. The people are hardworking and down-to-earth. Its industry involves the handling of potential weapons like axes. Citizens likely begin working in the lumber industry at an early age. The district has a population and claims a number of tesserae. Its territory is located in a region with trees. A dam in the district was destroyed during a rebellion. During a rebellion, loggers scaled trees and used land mines against armed forces. The district's color has been represented as different shades in different years.
