The Infiltration of the Capitol was a rescue mission staged to liberate captured victors from the Capitol's underground prison.

The victors were held in adjacent cells. As part of their torture, they were forced to listen to each other's screams. One was starved, beaten, had her head shaven, and was tortured with electric shocks. Another was subjected to injections of tracker jacker venom and exposed to upsetting stimuli in order to brainwash him.

A rescue mission into the Capitol was launched, aimed at freeing the captives. The rescue was carried out by a small team. As a distraction, rebels detonated a bomb in a government building several miles away, then cut the power to the prison and distributed knockout gas through the vents. One team member received a wound, but three of the four victors were successfully recovered.

The team and rescued victors arrived around midnight. One was lucid and apparently unharmed. Another was still unconscious from the knockout gas, emaciated and bruised with her head shaven. The third was just waking up when he attempted to strangle someone to death. He was knocked out, and it was revealed that he had been hijacked for this exact purpose.

The rescued victors underwent recovery. One remained in the hospital and developed an addiction to a drug, battling withdrawals later. Doctors treated the hijacked victor, using medication to calm him and create positive associations to repair his distorted memories. Despite this, he was not by any means cured when he was later sent with a military squad. Little is known of another captive's recovery, but she was able to have a wedding.
