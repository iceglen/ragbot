Augustus Braun was the victor of the 67th Hunger Games from District 1. He was known as the Cavalier Career. He was a Career Tribute in the 67th Hunger Games. On his Victory Tour, he was described as having a bright and optimistic ensemble that contrasted his unkempt persona cultivated during his Games. He was widely popular. It is unclear if he ever mentored for a Hunger Games. As District 1 victors are known to continue training even after they win, it is likely that he continued his training. Although unknown how he died, he was dead by the end of the Second Rebellion, as he was absent for a vote on a final Hunger Games. He was likely a victim of the Victors' Purge.
