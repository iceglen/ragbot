Circ was the male tribute from District 3 in the 10th Hunger Games. With his district partner, he worked to make use of the drones that delivered sponsor gifts to tributes during the Hunger Games.

He became his district's male tribute in the reaping. Along with the other tributes, he was taken to the Capitol in a cattle car and placed in a zoo enclosure. During interviews, he told the audience that he could start a fire with his glasses.

On the first day of the Games, he and his district partner collected drones that landed near a dead body. While assessing the damage, he removed a battery and gave a thumbs-up. They collected more drones with gifts and ran back to a barricade.

When only seven tributes remained, a swarm of venomous snake mutts was released into the arena. He and his partner stopped to look at the snake tank. His partner managed to narrowly escape the swarm, but he was not so lucky and quickly met his end from the muttations' venom. Bright pus came out of his wounds. Later, his body was collected and placed in a makeshift morgue.

In a film adaptation, he ran toward a pile of weapons when the gong rang. He was pushed and fought with some tributes with a sword. He then went after another tribute before returning to the pile. He was trying to climb the pile when he was struck with a trident.

He wore glasses. His name is a shortening of the word "circuit."
