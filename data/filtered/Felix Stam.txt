Felix Stam is a herdsman from District 10 who was selected to become a District Hero.

Felix was selected to be a District Hero due to his quiet understanding of the ways of animals and the circle of life. He initially rejected the standard contingency fee but quickly reconsidered after being presented the alternative of reduced tesserae.

Felix has fair hair, blue eyes and a beard. In his District Hero portrait, he has a septum nose piercing, smokes a pipe, and wears shaggy furs.

Felix is a male given name that stems from Latin and means "happy" or "lucky".
