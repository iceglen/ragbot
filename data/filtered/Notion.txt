Notion was one of two female tributes from District 8 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Notion joined the Newcomers alliance between several districts in an attempt to counter the Career pack. She and her district partners were considered unremarkable. Her token was a little doll in a sewn outfit, the same as the other girl from her district. She died in the initial bloodbath.

The tributes from District 8 wore peach-colored outfits during the chariot rides and training.

A "notion" is a term for sewing accessories.
