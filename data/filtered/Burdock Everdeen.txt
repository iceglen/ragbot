Burdock Everdeen was the father of Katniss and Primrose Everdeen, as well as being Asterid Everdeen's husband. He lived and worked inside the gates of District 12 as a coal miner and hunter. He taught his daughter Katniss survival skills in the woods, including hunting and making bows. His teachings allowed her to support her family after he died.

He had a beautiful singing voice, which he passed on to his daughter. He taught his daughters a particular song, but was told they should not sing it anymore. He was well-liked across District 12.

He died in a mine explosion. The explosion killed him and other workers. That day, sirens went off and all the children were herded to the entrance of the mine. The elevator brought up many men, but he never emerged and was instantly presumed dead.

He liked to sing, swim, and hunt, and he had a very level head. He taught his daughter all of these things, and many songs as well. In addition to being a loving father, he also seemed to have a rebellious streak, a trait inherited by his eldest daughter. For example, his work as a hunter and gatherer in the woods surrounding District 12 was technically illegal, although the relatively lenient government officials allowed it.
