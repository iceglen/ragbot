The male tribute from District 5 was the male tribute from District 5 in the 75th Hunger Games. He was also the victor of an unspecified Hunger Games.

It is unknown when or how he won his first Games, but he turned to alcohol to cope with his trauma in the aftermath.

During training, he threw up wine on the sword-fighting floor.

He wore a black striped suit during interviews. Afterwards, he joined hands with the other victors in a display of rebellion.

He was the third tribute to make it to the Cornucopia during the bloodbath. He was impaled by a trident. He was the first known casualty that year.
