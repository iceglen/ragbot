The District 6 female was the victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Games. Years after her initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third Quarter Quell would be reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 6's reaping for the 75th Hunger Games. She stood beside the female morphling, who was chosen to compete. She did not volunteer to go in the morphling's place. At the end of the Second Rebellion, she was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so she presumably died in the Victors' Purge.
