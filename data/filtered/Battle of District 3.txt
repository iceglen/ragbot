The Battle of District 3 is an unofficial term referring to the fight to seize control of District 3 from the Capitol in and around the Second Rebellion.

History
Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest
When a victor visited the district on a Victory Tour, the people there expressed genuine elation and under the elation, fury. Their cheers were characterized as cries for vengeance.
Months later, on the day before the reading of the Quarter Quell card, there were shortages of electric gadgets from District 3 that implied rebel action.

Second Rebellion
Several weeks after the end of the 75th Hunger Games, the airing of propos about a hospital bombing boosted morale in the districts. This allowed rebels to take Districts 3 and 11, as well as make inroads in several other districts as well.
