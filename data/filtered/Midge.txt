Midge was one of two female tributes from District 9 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, it was noticed that everyone from Midge's district had sunflower necklace tokens. It was suggested they join an alliance as the tokens could be used for a plan to sabotage the arena. Their efficiency and capabilities were thought to make them good allies.
While training, an offer to join another alliance was made to the largest boy from 9, but he refused. In retaliation, a token from another tribute was broken. It was fixed, and Midge, along with the rest of 9, decided to join the Newcomers alliance. Even so, they weren't very sociable and almost appeared exclusive.
Midge died in the initial bloodbath along with everyone else in her district. Two of their tokens were retrieved.

The District 9 tributes' training outfits were buttery yellow. Like the rest of her district, Midge was still healing from sunburn, had calloused hands and lean, muscled arms. Midge was larger than the other female tribute from her district.
Midge and the others from 9 weren't very sociable and liked to keep to themselves.
