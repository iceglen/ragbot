Purnia was a Peacekeeper in District 12. She was a regular customer at a stall in the Hob. When a character was publicly whipped, one of her fellow Peacekeepers attempted to stop the whipping and was knocked unconscious. After other characters stepped in to protect the victim, Purnia came forward and convinced the commanding officer to decrease the number of lashes. After the officer left, she helped carry her injured fellow Peacekeeper away. She was never seen or mentioned again, so her fate is left unknown. It is mentioned that Peacekeepers were evacuated before the bombing of District 12, so she may have survived, but her ultimate fate is unclear. She is one of the only women ever mentioned among the Peacekeepers' ranks.
