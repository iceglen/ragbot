The District 9 male was the male tribute from District 9 who participated in the 74th Hunger Games. His age was 14. His occupation was tribute. His home was District 9. His weapon was an axe and a sword for training. His fate was deceased. His gender was male. His height was 4'11".

In the Tribute Parade, he is seen on a chariot with his district partner wearing silver suits and headpieces, possibly representing silos. During training, he was seen using a sword and making a fire. He was killed in the initial Cornucopia bloodbath. He grappled over a backpack until a knife was thrown into his back. He coughed blood and then fell to the ground dead. In the film, he picked up an axe and tried to kill another tribute. He placed 24th out of 24 tributes.

He had ashen hair and hazel eyes. In the films, he is shown to be one of the smallest tributes. In the arena, he wore a bottle green jacket. He was the only tribute that fell during the bloodbath to serve a non-trivial role in the games, and the only one with a known physical description. He was one of the mutts that was recognized based on his traits. In the book, he was the first tribute shown to be killed.
