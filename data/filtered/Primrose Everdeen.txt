Primrose "Prim" Everdeen is a fictional character. She is the younger sister of Katniss Everdeen. She is known as Prim and is sometimes called Little Duck. She is a student and a healer. She lived in the Seam, then the Victors' Village, and briefly in District 13. She is deceased.

Prim is a talented young healer with blonde hair and a gentle personality. She is the opposite of her sister in both looks and character. The two have a close relationship. When Prim was selected for the 74th Hunger Games at twelve years old, her sister volunteered in her place. Prim's death near the end of the Second Rebellion left her sister devastated.

Prim loved all living creatures from an early age. She saved a stray kitten and named him Buttercup. She also had a goat named Lady. The milk and cheese from the goat helped her family. She helped her mother, the local healer, with patients. She was already a talented healer at an early age and was not fazed by wounds.

Prim is described as having fair skin, blonde hair, and blue eyes in the books. She had a small stature. She is described as having a face as fresh as a raindrop, as lovely as the primrose for which she was named. When she wore clothing, she continuously had a "duck tail" at the back of her shirt.

Prim loves all living creatures. She has a passion and talent for healing. She is very smart and supportive. She is also very delicate. She is compassionate and has a natural affinity for healing. She is intelligent and offers strategic advice. Her delicate nature contrasts with her sister's resilience.

Prim is the youngest in her family. Her father died in a mine explosion. She lived with her mother and sister.

Prim had a strong relationship with her sister, who was highly protective of her. Her sister worked hard to keep her alive and happy. When Prim was selected for the Games, her sister volunteered in her place. Following Prim's death, her sister was heartbroken.

Prim had a strong, loving relationship with her cat Buttercup. She found and healed him. She was willing to risk her life for him.

After her father was killed, her mother fell into a depression. Prim maintained her love for her mother. After Prim was killed, her mother moved away.

Prim is named after the plant "Evening Primrose," which is a healing herb. She is a healer. She is one of the few known characters to own her own animals. She hoped to become a doctor.
