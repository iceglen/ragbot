Cressida is a film director and rebel. She is in her twenties and female. She originates from the Capitol and later resides in District 13 briefly. She is armed with a gun and survives the events described.

She is a film director from the Capitol who defects to the rebellion along with her camera crew and assistant. They flee to District 13. She is tasked with following a key rebel figure throughout the rebellion to create propaganda films, known as propos, to encourage strength and morale among the rebels across the nation.

She is introduced as an up-and-coming film director who leaves the Capitol by choice to join the rebellion. She and her crew film the rebel figure during a district bombing, capturing footage of the destruction and the rebel's threat to the Capitol. She is unfazed by poor living conditions and the devastation of war, impressing the rebel figure with her fearlessness and pride in her work. Her first propo is aired quickly, with dramatic results, rallying rebels and leading to the control of several districts.

She continues to gather footage, including in a destroyed district where the rebel figure sings. After another bombing, she films the delivery of symbolic white roses. She attempts to guide the emotionally distressed rebel figure by giving specific lines. She later coaxes the rebel figure into another propo, where the rebel declares independence from the Capitol. She also films a propo where a victor reveals secrets about the Capitol's leader, discrediting him severely, which shocks even her. She creates a video memorial for fallen tributes.

She and her crew accompany a military squad into the Capitol, filming their training and a squad member's death for a propo. The propo is stopped as the mission becomes more serious. After a squad leader is injured and dies, she supports a lie that the rebel leader specifically wanted the key rebel figure to kill the Capitol's leader. She continues to support the rebel figure's decisions over more logical options.

The squad moves through the Capitol streets and into a resident's house. They discover on television that the Capitol's leader believes them dead. This gives them time to travel through the Capitol's underground systems. When the Capitol's leader discovers they are alive, he sends mutant creatures after them. Although the camera team lacks military training, they are given guns for defense. One of her assistants activates a trap and dies. The group continues until encountering another trap. Two squad members stay behind to hold off the creatures but die. She, a crew member, and three squad members make it above ground, while three others die holding back the creatures. She stoically continues to show loyalty but is visibly upset over the deaths of her crew members.

She then leads the remaining squad members to a former stylist who is part of the rebellion. The stylist houses them until refugees force them to leave. She and the surviving crew member split from the rebel figure, planning to meet at the Capitol leader's mansion while another creates a distraction. During ensuing chaos in the streets, she and the crew member are thought to be killed but survive. They are later shown filming war damage across the districts. Her final appearance is filming the execution of the Capitol's leader.

In the book, she is described as a young woman with a shaved head tattooed with vivid green vines. In the film adaptation, only the left half of her head is shaved and tattooed, with the vine tattoos extending down her neck and left arm.
