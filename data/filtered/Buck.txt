Buck was one of two male tributes from District 10 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

During the chariot rides, the chariot carrying District 10 narrowly missed colliding with another district. Before the start of the games, Buck joined an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. He gave another tribute a lariat.
During the Games, Buck was with two other tributes when they were attacked by a muttation of a porcupine. He and one other tribute were discovered writhing in pain and pawing at its long metal quills imbedded in their bodies. By the time other tributes were able to chase the porcupine away, Buck was dead.

Buck, along with the other tributes from District 10, wore a specific outfit during the chariot rides. The district was seen wearing crimson during the training sessions.

Male livestock animal (goat, deer), strongly farm-related.

Buck's token, along with those of his fellow tributes from his home district, was a horseshoe.
