The unidentified District 11 citizen was an elderly male from District 11. He was present during a Victory Tour. Following a speech commemorating deceased tributes, he whistled a four-note song. This was a signal for the crowd to give a three-finger salute. Seeing this as a sign of rebellion, Peacekeepers pulled the man from the crowd and executed him. He was described as elderly with dark brown skin and white, graying hair and a beard.
