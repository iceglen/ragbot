The unknown District 2 male was the victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Games. Years after his initial victory, tributes for the third Quarter Quell were reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 2's reaping for the 75th Hunger Games. It is unknown whether or not this man was reaped, but another victor ultimately volunteered to participate. At the end of the Second Rebellion, he was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so he presumably died in the Victors' Purge.
