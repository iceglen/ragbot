Gaius Breen was a male resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the 10th Hunger Games. He was assigned the male tribute from District 9.

He accompanied his tribute on a tour of the Capitol Arena when the arena was rocked by bombs which been planted by rebels. He was hospitalized for critical injuries, having lost both of his legs. He passed away not long afterwards, during the 10th Hunger Games.

His death was announced by the Head Gamemaker of the 10th Hunger Games. As a special punishment, she sent a drone into the Capitol Arena with snake mutts that she had created. After the snake mutts were released, another mentor collected stories about Gaius from the other mentors, and everybody had a joke or funny story to share.

In a film adaptation, he survived the bombing without being hospitalized.

He was considered a "Citadel brat" and was known for having an endless supply of jokes.
