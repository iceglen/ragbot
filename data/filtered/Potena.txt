Potena was one of two female tributes from District 5 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the games, Potena and her district partners were recruited by the Careers to counter the Newcomers. Potena died along with a boy from her district on either the first day during the bloodbath or from the volcanic eruption on the fourth day.

Potena is a name that could be associated with the concept of electrical potential, referring to potential difference in electrical systems.
