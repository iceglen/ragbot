Magno Stift was the stylist for District 12 during the 50th Hunger Games. He had been the District 12 stylist for several years by that time. He promoted live-reptile fashion. He did not take his job seriously and put no effort in helping the tributes gain sponsors through the tribute parade and their television interviews. He did not treat the tributes like human beings, not caring about their lives and making them more presentable.

For the chariot rides before the 50th Hunger Games, he dressed the four District 12 tributes in the same coal miner outfits he had used for years. Before the interviews, he was found sick and inebriated. He arrived late carrying a snake, which one tribute wore during her interview. Before a tribute entered the arena, he could not be found. He was later fired for negligence.

He had sun-leathered skin tattooed with a snakeskin pattern. He was often seen wearing live reptiles. He was older than 60 years.
