The Launch Room was the chamber where a tribute would be prepared by their stylist one last time before being launched into the Hunger Games arena.

Each tribute and their stylist traveled to the location of the arena and descended into the catacombs below. They located the correct Launch Room. There, the tribute could shower and brush their teeth before being seen by their stylist for any finishing cosmetic touches. The stylist received a package containing the tribute's arena uniform on-site and helped them dress. Food and water were available.

After an uncertain amount of time, an announcement stated it was time to prepare to launch. The tribute would then stand on a metal plate, and a glass cylinder lowered to keep them in place just before the platform lifted them into the arena. All tributes were launched at the same time, arranged in a circle. After the launch, each tribute had to remain on their launch plate for a set period; a wrong step would trigger land mines. At the end of this period, the land mines were deactivated, and the Games began.

Launch Rooms and tribute tubes both appeared to contain hidden cameras. Since each arena was only used once, each Launch Room was only ever used by one tribute.

Prior to a later rebellion, arenas and their catacombs became vacation spots for citizens to tour after the Games concluded.

Any known Launch Rooms were not described in great detail. Each one was built brand-new for that year's Games, and known rooms contained a shower, a couch, and the metal plate and tribute tube. One launch room was circular and contained the glass plate and tribute tube, as well as a chair with arena wear.

After the rebellion, the Hunger Games were abolished and all past arenas were completely destroyed. This presumably included the catacombs and Launch Rooms underneath.
