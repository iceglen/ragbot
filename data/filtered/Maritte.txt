Maritte was a Career Tribute from District 4 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. She was a part of the Career alliance, and she placed 5th out of 48 tributes. Her occupation was Tribute. Her weapon was a trident. Her fate was deceased.

In the build-up to the 50th Hunger Games, she received a score of eleven after her private session with the Gamemakers. She was part of the Career pack in these Games. She retrieved a trident from the Cornucopia and wielded it during the initial bloodbath.

She teamed up with another tribute during the final stages of the Games. They were chasing two other tributes when they discovered three Gamemakers repairing a mutt portal inside the arena. She was the first to react and killed a Gamemaker with her trident. She and another tribute then dispatched of the other two. Her ally was horrified that she killed Gamemakers, believing they would never let them win now. The other tribute remarked that District 4 should be on their side. She briefly considered an offer to join the other two tributes, but ultimately decided to leave.

In punishment for killing Gamemakers, she was chased down and killed by a pack of golden squirrel muttations. After the Games, heavily edited recap footage of the 50th Hunger Games was shown. The killing of the Gamemakers was omitted and her death was edited to show her being killed at a different point, even though she was already long dead at that time.

She wore a shimmering deep sea–blue mermaid suit during the chariot rides. Her training outfits were deep-sea blue. Based on her physique, she was considered a contender. She technically finished fifth in the Hunger Games, but as her death was publicly altered, it would have looked like she finished third.
