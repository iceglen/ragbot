Fulvia Cardew is an assistant. Originally a citizen of the Capitol, she works for the rebels. She uses propaganda as her weapon.

It was originally her idea to film propos on a stage with a script; however, it was unsuccessful because the persona didn't translate well unless being authentic, not performing for cameras on command. Her pride is wounded when a better plan is devised to film out where the real action is.

Later, she redeems herself by coming up with the idea of doing a montage of propos dedicated to the fallen tributes of the districts. They would contain commentary with the theme being "We Remember." This would then appeal to the people of the districts on a closer level since many of them would have had personal feelings towards their own tributes.

She is one of the few named rebels that survived the rebellion.

She has silver flowers tattooed onto her plump cheeks.

She is calculating, but struggles with her rich Capitol upbringing when trying to fit in with citizens from the districts. Despite good intentions, she is quite rude.
