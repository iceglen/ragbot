Chicory was one of two female tributes from District 11 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the games, Chicory joined an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. On the way to the arena, one of the other tributes fixated on Chicory's token, a flower made of grass, and sung a harvest song. Chicory was surprised that someone not from her district knew the song. After the Games begin, Chicory survived the initial bloodbath. After a volcanic explosion, Chicory escaped with two allies.

On the fourth day, Chicory and her allies encountered a porcupine muttation, which attacked them by shooting its long, metallic quills. Two other tributes discovered Chicory and one ally writhing in pain from the embedded quills. By the time they chased the porcupine away, Chicory was dead.

Chicory was shown to be an intelligent and kind girl. She showed empathy towards another tribute and offered to find her family if she won the Hunger Games.
