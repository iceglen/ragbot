The 46th Hunger Games took place 4 years prior to a later event and 28 years before the start of a trilogy. The victor was a tribute from District 1.

During the reaping in District 12, the Peacekeepers pulled a kerosene-soaked, burning, smoking flag from under the temporary stage. An individual was arrested for the crime but later let go, even though secretly guilty.

The victor likely formed a typical alliance with other Career Tributes, since they were from one of the Career districts.

This was the first year in which two other individuals were eligible for the reaping. The victor is the second District 1 victor with a known surname. The victor won the year after another victor.
