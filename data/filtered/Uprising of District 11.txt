Uprising of District 11

Conflict: Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest
Date: Mid to late July, 74 ADD
Place: District 11
Outcome: Decisive Capitol victory. Destruction of property and resources. Rebels subdued.
Combatants: The Capitol Peacekeepers versus District 11 citizens.

The uprising of District 11 was the first known uprising to have taken place since the Dark Days. It was a response to a death in the Hunger Games.

The district was heavily impoverished, policed, and repressed. Despite its industry being agriculture, nearly all of its produce was shipped off. The local government was very strict, and its Peacekeepers were particularly brutal. Citizens faced severe punishment for minor infractions.

During the Games, a tribute was fatally wounded. Another tribute performed an act of kindness and a symbolic salute. Moved by this act, citizens watching on a large screen in the district returned the gesture. A man then decided to attack a Peacekeeper, and other citizens quickly followed suit. They fought Peacekeepers and overturned bins of grain and other produce. The riot lasted until after dark, when Peacekeeper reinforcements arrived to quell the uprising. It can be assumed the uprising was subdued within a few days, and the instigators were executed.
