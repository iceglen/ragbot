In the films, the Avenue of the Tributes is a major road in the Capitol. The Training Center sits at one end, while a large governmental building sits at the other.

On the second day leading up to the annual Hunger Games, tributes rode chariots down the Avenue in specially-made costumes inspired by their districts' industries. The procession ended when the chariots pulled up in front of a large government building, where a welcoming speech was given.
An execution was later held on the Avenue of the Tributes. This also made it the site of an assassination.

In the novels, the Training Center and the president's mansion were both located in the City Circle. However, in the films, the Avenue and City Circle appear to be two different locations, placing the Training Center and the president's mansion in different parts of the city.
