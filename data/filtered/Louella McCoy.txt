Louella McCoy was one of two female tributes from District 12 chosen during the reaping to participate in the 50th Hunger Games.

She was a female tribute with gray eyes and black hair. She was known to be a spunky young girl. She would put on a mask of bravery when put in front of cameras, but relied on another to comfort her fears. She had a heart-shaped face and long, dark pigtail braids.

For the second Quarter Quell, the Capitol demanded that twice the number of tributes be reaped for the event. As a result, she was reaped alongside three other tributes from her district. Of the four tributes from District 12 that year, hers was the first name to be called.

During the tribute parade, the horses carrying the District 12 chariot were startled. They bolted and caused the chariot to start buckling. The tributes tried to hold on as the chariot hit others and crashed, but they were thrown backwards in the air. She hit the ground with force, cracked her head open and was killed upon impact.

Her death left District 12 with only three tributes, so the Capitol decided to cover up her death by producing a body double. The other District 12 tributes dubbed her replacement by a nickname. Although the other districts' tributes questioned her sudden reappearance, only two districts' tributes ever learned that she wasn't the real person.

After the Hunger Games ended, her body was returned to District 12 where she was buried.
