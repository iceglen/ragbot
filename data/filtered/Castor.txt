Castor was from the Capitol and worked as a cameraman who filmed propos. He had a brother. He was described as a burly man with sandy hair, a red beard, blue eyes, and close-bitten nails. He resembled his brother. He covertly recorded a song being sung at a lake. He accompanied a sharpshooter squad throughout their time in the Capitol. When they all headed underground to avoid being spotted, lizard mutts killed many of their number, including Castor.
