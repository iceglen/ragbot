The Uprising of District 8 was the first known uprising in Panem since the Dark Days.

Since an unconventional victory in the Hunger Games, the citizens of District 8 had become emboldened. The district citizens were able to discuss planning for the uprising in the textile factories, as the machinery would drown out their conversations.

The uprising took place at night. Ordinarily, it would have been too suspicious for large groups to be gathering together, but an event gave the rebels the element of surprise. The Peacekeepers were quickly caught off guard and overpowered, and the district fell into rebel hands quickly. The rebels were able to take control of key positions, acquiring weapons and ammunition. Hours later, however, reinforcements were sent in; air forces bombed rebel strongholds and deployed mass numbers of ground troops to contain the unrest. Forty-eight hours later, the rebellion was subdued and the district was put in a state of lockdown, with all residents forbidden from leaving their homes. The following week, Peacekeepers arrested mass numbers of suspected rebel instigators, hanging them in the square and televising their executions.

The lockdown was lifted a week later. The Capitol, having learned that the idea of the rebellion had been planned in the factories, bombed one of them, which killed large numbers of District 8 citizens.

A televised report showed a middle-aged woman giving updates on the situation, stating that conditions were deteriorating and that a Level 3 Alert was called. By then, all textile production had ceased and additional Peacekeeping forces were being deployed. Footage showed the main square, where large numbers of citizens were waving banners from the rooftops. The square was packed with angry citizens wearing homemade masks, throwing bricks. Buildings burned, and Peacekeepers shot into the raging crowd at random.
