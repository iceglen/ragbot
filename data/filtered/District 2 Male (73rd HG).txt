This unnamed District 2 male was the victor of the 73rd Hunger Games.

He was a Career Tribute, likely having trained and volunteered to participate. His age was 16 during the Games. His weapon was a brick.

During the Games, he made it to the final two. He overpowered his final opponent, disarmed him, and used a brick from nearby rubble to beat him to death, securing his victory.

He was later spared from competing in a subsequent Hunger Games. His fate is presumed deceased, most likely killed during a purge of victors.

In a film depiction, his arena wear included a red jacket. He was the last Career to become a victor under normal circumstances.
