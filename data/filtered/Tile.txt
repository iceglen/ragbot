Tile was one of two male tributes from District 11 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the games, Tile joined the Newcomers alliance between several districts' tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Tile and his district partners were considered a significant strategic factor.
On the hovercraft to the arena, tributes from another district confessed that one of their tributes had been replaced with a girl originally from District 11. Tile was shocked and asked why they didn't reveal this before.
He died in the initial bloodbath on the first day of the Games.
During the Victory Tour, a victor faced Tile's distraught, grieving family.

Tile was the largest of his district's tributes.

Despite his large size, Tile was shown to have a big heart. He also seemed to hate deception, as he confronted the tributes of another district on why they never said anything about one of their own being replaced by a body double who had origins from his home district.

The name possibly references irrigation tiles used in farming.
