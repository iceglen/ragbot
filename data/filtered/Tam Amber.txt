Tam Amber was a resident of District 12 and a member of the Covey. He was a standout player on his mandolin, performing with lightning-fast fingering while keeping his facial features expressionless and distant.

He was found as a baby and taken in by the traveling Covey. He learned to play the mandolin and performed along with the rest of the group. He was a tall, rawboned young man who wore a feathered hat during performances.

He was skilled with his hands, fashioning items such as a butter churn and fishing equipment. He later became a very skilled and sought-after blacksmith, and many in District 12 owned his creations. One of his creations was a mockingjay pin.
