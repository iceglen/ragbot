Triti Lancaster is a wheat field worker from District 9 who was selected to become a District Hero. She was selected to be a District Hero due to her generosity and kindness to other citizens of her district. She is portrayed as a dark-skinned woman of average build, described as charming and beautiful. In her District Hero portrait, her hair is tied back, she wears a white t-shirt under a yellow apron or overalls, and she carries a sheaf of wheat behind her. She is described as having a gentle, industrious personality. Her name may be derived from the genus which encompasses the many species of wheat, calling back to her district's grain industry.
