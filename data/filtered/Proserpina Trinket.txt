Proserpina Trinket is a female Capitol University student. She is a member of a prep team for a tribute during the 50th Hunger Games. She studies fashion and styling with a focus on tribute presentation. She and a fellow prep team member were set to move up to better district assignments after the Games.

She enters the tribute's cubicle to begin grooming him for the Games. Their goal is to make him attractive to sponsors. They are underclassmen at the University with no say in their assignments. She keeps the tribute's spirits up during the session. She initiates the grooming process and takes required documentation photos. She and her partner shave the tribute's body hair but respect his request to keep his nails long. She offers him a candy as they leave.

Before the interviews, she reports that their assigned stylist is violently ill. She becomes distressed about failing her University assignment. She is advised to do her best and that she could appeal a grade. Her confidence is oblivious to the tributes' situation. She remains focused on grades and appearances while chaos unfolds around her. She is comforted when her older sister arrives to assist. She is excited when vintage clothing is produced for the tributes. She enthusiastically supports her sister's efforts and encourages the makeover energy. She hands her sister a makeup box for touch-ups before the tributes leave. She remains focused on the theatrical aspects, unaware of the gravity of the situation.

She appears during the Victor's Ceremony beneath the stage. She and her partner are excited to be featured, fussing over their makeup. They ascend to applause, reveling in the recognition.

At the Victor's After-Party, she appears tipsy and giddy, offering congratulations with enthusiasm. She does not linger, vanishing back into the crowd.

The morning of the Victory Tour, she and her partner are unable to assist, so her sister prepares the tribute until they can resume their duties. Her involvement remains tied to grooming and public presentation.

She is described as having blonde hair in the film adaptation. She is a girl with puffballs of magenta hair, not looking much older than the tribute she preps. She never wears black, considering it too depressing.

Her personality is marked by a bubbly detachment. Her enthusiasm is not directly cruel but highlights a blindness to the violence. She is a product of a system that causes well-meaning harm.

She has an older sister who is the person she turns to in moments of panic. Their bond is shown when she calls her sister sobbing over a crisis. Her sister reassures her and encourages her. She flings herself into her sister's arms in relief when she arrives. She proudly claims her sister when credit is given for the tributes' looks. Their relationship is marked by familial love and dependence, with the older sister acting as a mentor and rescuer.
