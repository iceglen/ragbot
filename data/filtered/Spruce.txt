Spruce was a man from District 12 who was involved in rebellious activities. He was a generally agreeable individual who wanted to free his sister and escape.

He planned to free his sister from the Peacekeepers' base and escape north. He gathered a cache of weapons for this purpose. When another individual encountered them, Spruce pointed a shotgun at him but relented. When a witness stumbled upon the scene, Spruce told her they had no quarrel with her and suggested she come with them. However, when it became obvious she planned to report them, he raised his gun to shoot her. The barrel was knocked towards the floor, and she was shot by someone else instead.

When a threat was made against another person, Spruce shot the person making the threat through the chest without hesitating. He then told a witness to leave and suggested that another individual should come with them, as otherwise he would likely end up paying for what had happened.

Spruce made his way to the lake, setting down the bag of weapons there. However, he was later captured by Peacekeepers; he was witnessed badly wounded. Sometime not long after, he died of his wounds, and most people believed that he had been involved with the murders.

In a film adaptation, Spruce was executed alongside his sister and another individual.
