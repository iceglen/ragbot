Malcolm Kastel is a man from District 6 who was selected to be a District Hero. He was presumably selected because he encouraged people from District 6 to take pride in their labor and work harder.

Malcolm is a muscular man with a dark crew cut and blue eyes. In his District Hero portrait, he wears a white tank top with a rip at the bottom, trousers made out of tires, and dark boots. His skin and shirt are smeared with oil, and he's shown holding a tire.
