Eddy's sister and her younger brother, Eddy, were rebels from District 8 that were injured in the bombing of District 8. It is unknown if Eddy and his sister had any other living family.

Biography[]
Eddy and his sister were both injured when the Capitol bombed their district. She ended up in the hospital with an injured leg, and her brother was located a few beds down from her. When Katniss Everdeen came to District 8 to filming propos, Eddy's sister was the first person to recognize her in the hospital. She was amazed to see Katniss and excitedly yelled to her brother that Katniss was there.[1]

Death[]
Right after Katniss left the hospital, the Capitol initiated a second air strike on District 8, this time targeting the hospital. Eddy and his sister were among the deceased, since there were no survivors.[1]
