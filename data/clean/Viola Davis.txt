Viola Davis is an actress and producer who portrayed Volumnia Gaul in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Davis first appeared in small productions in Rhode Island. She graduated from the Juilliard School in 1993 and has won many awards for her acting, including an Oscar, a Primetime Emmy Award, two Tony Awards, and a Grammy Award for narrating her memoir.[2]
