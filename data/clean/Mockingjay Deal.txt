
"Buttercup. Hunting. Peeta's immunity. Announced in public. ... I KILL SNOW."
—Katniss's written list of terms for the deal.[src]
The Mockingjay Deal was a deal struck between Katniss Everdeen and District 13's President Alma Coin, along with the other rebel leaders, during the Second Rebellion.[1]

Terms[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Plutarch Heavensbee and Alma Coin listening to Katniss's conditions in Mockingjay - Part 1. 		 	 According to the terms of the deal, Katniss would be the rebels' figurehead, known as the Mockingjay, and go along with their plans for her in exchange for:
The right to keep Buttercup as a family pet.[1]
The right to hunt with Gale Hawthorne in the woods outside District 13.[1]
The right to have Gale by her side on the battlefield and during the making of propos.[1]
An official pardon and criminal immunity for captured victors being held by the Capitol— Peeta Mellark, Johanna Mason, and Enobaria— to be announced in public.[1] Annie Cresta was added to the immunity list just before the announcement later that day.[2]
The right to "flip" Coin for the opportunity to kill President Coriolanus Snow.[1]
When Coin made her public announcement, she introduced a sixth condition of her own: if Katniss deviated from the rebels' mission, the deal would be considered broken, and both she and the captured victors would be at the mercy of District 13 law to decide their fates.[2]

Outcome[]
The Mockingjay Deal protected four victors from retaliation by the rebels,[1][2] which likely contributed to them surviving the Victors' Purge.[3] 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Katniss suited up as the Mockingjay at Snow's execution in Mockingjay - Part 2. 		 	 
The Mockingjay Deal was mostly carried out by Katniss and the rebels, with several significant exceptions. First, Katniss defied orders when she was sent to cover the bombing of District 8;[4] and second, when she was deployed to the Battle of the Capitol with propaganda unit Squad 451,[5] Katniss instead led her own mission to assassinate Snow at his mansion.[6] This mission was ultimately unsuccessful, resulting in the deaths of eight squad members.[7] Finally, Katniss was appointed to carry out the execution of President Snow, but she assassinated Coin in his place.[3]
Following an emergency election, President Paylor was instated, and Katniss was kept in solitary confinement at the Training Center during her trial in absentia. She was found not guilty by reason of insanity, but as a condition of her release, she had to continue treatment with Dr. Aurelius over the phone, and she was confined to District 12 until further notice.[8]

Trivia[]
The term "Mockingjay Deal" was coined by an unknown rebel.[3]
The Mockingjay Deal was made while Katniss was still suffering residual effects from the concussion Johanna Mason gave her at the end of the 75th Hunger Games. She mentioned having a headache, and it took her twenty minutes to write down her simple list of terms before presenting them for negotiation.[1]
