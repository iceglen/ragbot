Palladium Barker was the victor of the 46th Hunger Games from District 1. They were related to Panache Barker, one of the two male District 1 tributes in the 50th Hunger Games.[1] Presumably, Palladium is either Panache's cousin or sibling, due to the close proximity in age between them and Panache.

Biography[]
46th Hunger Games[]
Palladium was presumably a Career Tribute in the 46th Hunger Games and likely  volunteered to participate.

Post Games[]
It is likely that Palladium mentored their relative Panache in the 50th Hunger Games.
As District 1 victors are known to continue training even after they win[2], it is likely that Palladium continued their training.

Death[]
Although the cause and time of death are unknown, Palladium was dead by the end of the Second Rebellion, as they were absent for the vote on a final 76th Hunger Games.[3] It's possible they were a victim of the Victors' Purge.

Etymology[]
Palladium is a silvery-white metal and chemical element with the symbol Pd and atomic number 46. Barker is an English surname meaning "a tanner of leather".

Trivia[]
Gender[]
Palladium's gender is never specified in the English edition.
The Polish translations use female-conjugated verbs in reference to Palladium.
On the other hand, the Russian translations use male-conjugated verbs in reference to Palladium.
