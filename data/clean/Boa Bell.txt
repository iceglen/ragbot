Biography[]
Boa Bell was born approximately four years before the Dark Days.[1]
At some point before or during the rebellion, she met Coriolanus Snow and took a fancy to him. On nights when he went to the nightclub's alley to trade lima beans for more cabbage, Boa's warmth consoled Coriolanus, and he became upset after witnessing Volumnia Gaul melt the flesh off a lab rat and thinking of her ending up in the lab.[2]
Coriolanus later went to visit Pluribus so he could obtain a guitar for Lucy Gray Baird. Boa instantly wove between Coriolanus's legs, purring, and was carefully lifted into his arms.[1]

Physical description[]
Boa was a fluffy white cat with silky fur.[2] At seventeen, she was getting old and had to be handled with care.[1]

Etymology[]
Boa is probably named after the feather boa, a kind of extravagant scarf.

Trivia[]
Boa is one of two known cats in the series. The other is Buttercup, a tomcat belonging to Primrose Everdeen.[3]
