Honor Gillies is a British actress of Iranian/Scottish descent. She portrayed Barb Azure Baird in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Gillies is making her professional acting debut as Barb Azure. She is represented by Marvin Godfrey at Revolution Talent Ltd.[1]

Trivia[]
After being cast as Barb Azure, Honor Gillies posted a video of Nicki Minaj saying "Calling all Barbs" (the name of Minaj's fanbase). Rachel Zegler, who plays Lucy Gray Baird, commented "slapping that super bass"— a play on the song "Super Bass" and Barb Azure's instrument, the stand-up bass. This started a rumor that Nicki Minaj would be on the film's soundtrack.[2]
