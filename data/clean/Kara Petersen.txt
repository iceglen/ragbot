Kara Petersen is an American stunt actress who was specifically cast to portray the training and fight scenes of a tribute that fell during the initial bloodbath and is assigned as the District 6 female tribute in The Hunger Games film.[1] Like most tribute actors who fell during the bloodbath, she is also a stunt actress.  As a stunt actress, she has her own stunt reel. She is also known for stunts in Jurassic World and The Darkest Minds.
