Tatiana Maslany is a Canadian actress who narrated the 10-year anniversary audiobooks for The Hunger Games trilogy.

Biography[]
She is known for playing multiple characters in the science-fiction television series Orphan Black. In addition to acting for film, TV, and the stage, she has voice acted in a number of shows since 2013 and appeared on various podcasts since 2015.

 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Tatiana Maslany with the tenth anniversary edition of The Hunger Games. 		 	 
The Hunger Games was her first audiobook, released on October 30, 2018 to mark the 10-year anniversary of the first novel's publication.[1] She also recorded versions of Catching Fire and Mockingjay, which were both released on June 17, 2019.
