Ayomide Adegun is an actor who portrayed Pliny Harrington in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Adegun is a Nigerian-British actor who trained at the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama. During his final year at drama school, he booked a regular role opposite Mel Gibson in The Continental, produced for Starz by Lionsgate Television, and portrayed the younger version of Lance Reddick's iconic character Charon from the John Wick franchise.
Adegun is represented by Hamilton Hodell and Goodman, Genow, Schenkman, Smelkinson & Christopher.[1]
