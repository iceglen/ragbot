Nina Jacobson is an American film executive who heads American film studio Color Force, which she founded in 2007. She was one of the main producers of the The Hunger Games film and also produced The Hunger Games: Catching Fire.
She returned as a producer for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes[1] and The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.[2]
Jacobson was previously the president of the Buena Vista Motion Pictures Group until July 2006. She has also produced the first three films of the Diary of a Wimpy Kid series.

Trivia[]
Jacobson's daughter Josie made a cameo in The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes film. During the song "Pure As The Driven Snow", she was asked to dance by several Peacekeepers, who then mentioned her name in a later scene at the barracks.[3]
