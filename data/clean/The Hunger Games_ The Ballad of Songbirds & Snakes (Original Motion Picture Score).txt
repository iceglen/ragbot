The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (Original Motion Picture Score) is the official film score for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. As with all previous installations to the franchise, the score was composed by James Newton Howard. It also features piano music by Yuja Wang.
Music created for the film that was not part of its score is included on the album The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (Music From & Inspired By).

Trivia[]
This is the longest of all the albums released in connection to the film franchise, both in terms of tracks and runtime. The second-longest was The Hunger Games: Catching Fire (Original Motion Picture Score), which had 29 tracks and a total runtime of 1:14:58.
