Ashton Moio is an American actor and stuntman who portrayed the male tribute from District 6 in The Hunger Games film.[1]

Career[]
Moio is a stunt actor and has been a stunt double for both TV and film; he is cast specifically to portray the fight scenes of a Tribute that is mentioned only once. Besides The Hunger Games, he has also been a stunt actor in Red Riding Hood and Project X and an actor in Let Me In. Moio was featured on ABC Family's TV show Twisted as the character Rico, but the show was canned after one season.
