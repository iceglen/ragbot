Leevy's brother was a boy from District 12 who lived a few houses down from Katniss Everdeen's house in the Seam.[1]

Biography[]
A year before Catching Fire took place, he caught the measles and Mrs. Everdeen kept him alive.[1] His older sister Leevy survived the bombing of District 12,[2] but it is unknown whether he or any other family members made it out, since they were not mentioned in Mockingjay.
