The Capitol Zoo is located in the Capitol. Before the Dark Days, the Capitol Zoo was full of many types of animals from "the Capitol's genetic ark."[1] After the Dark Days, many of the enclosures were left empty, ravaged by the war. Few animals remained, such as a lone tortoise and a bedraggled toucan.
Following the Dark Days, the zoo was infested by rats. In order to try to control the problem, the Peacekeepers guarding the zoo were supplied with rat poison. The rats, however, were smart and wouldn't go anywhere near the stuff.[2] Coriolanus Snow later gave Lucy Gray Baird his mother's compact, which she used to smuggle the rat poison into the Capitol Arena.[3]

History[]
10th Hunger Games[]
The tributes for the 10th Hunger Games were brought to the Capitol Zoo upon arrival to the Capitol. They were dropped through a chute into the monkey enclosure and held there until the Games began.[4]
Capitol citizens were encouraged to come see the tributes as if they were zoo animals. After Coriolanus Snow made a scene by riding with the tributes to the zoo and being dropped into the cage with them, he encouraged citizens to come visit the tribute he was mentoring, Lucy Gray. Pontius and Venus were among the children that she talked to, and Lepidus Malmsey from Capitol News interviewed her.[5]

 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Sejanus Plinth offering food through the bars 		 	 
After this, Capitol citizens frequented the zoo, bringing food and water for the tributes, feeding them like animals through the enclosure bars. Initially, many of the tributes were hesitant to accept food. However, after the urging of Lucy Gray and Coriolanus, many of them grew comfortable enough to.[1] Many citizens visited the zoo, and several tributes would perform for them, like Otto, Ginnee, and Sheaf, but the tributes remained underfed and mistreated. However, after Coriolanus brought up the issue to Satyria Click, the tributes were fed bread and cheese.[6]
One day, Arachne Crane taunted her tribute, Brandy, with a sandwich. This angered Brandy, and she killed Arachne with Arachne's own knife.[7] This incident caused mass panic, and Peacekeepers were to guard the zoo from then on. Coriolanus had to be given permission from Dr. Gaul just to see his tribute, Lucy Gray Baird.[2]

50th Hunger Games[]
One of the parties that Haymitch Abernathy was forced to attend after winning the 50th Hunger Games was an all-night party at the Capitol Zoo. Afterwards, he was finally sent home to District 12.[8]

Description[]
The monkey house had a stretch of sand, tall artificial rock formations, and rows of metal bars engraved to resemble vines.[4] A shallow moat separated the rocky structures and the bars, but was bone-dry by the time of the 10th Hunger Games. The bars were spaced about four inches apart.[5]

Trivia[]
Capitol Zoo scenes for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes were filmed at Britzer Garten in Berlin, Germany.[9]
