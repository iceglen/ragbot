Luna Steeples is an American actress who portrayed Dill in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Steeples was born in downtown Los Angeles to parents who are also actors and filmmakers. She fell in love with the film industry when she was four years old.[2]
Steeples' credits include the series Overdue. She is represented by Jackson Entertainment Management (JEM) and Skrzyniarz & Mallean.[1]
