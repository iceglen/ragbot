Jason Schwartzman is an actor who portrayed Lucretius Flickerman in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Schwartzman is a Gotham, SAG and WGA Award nominee who was seen as the lead in Wes Anderson's Asteroid City, opposite many well-known actors. In 2023, he performed in a starring role opposite Awkwafina and Sandra Oh in Quiz Lady from director Jessica Yu, and also voiced a role in Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse.
Schwartzman is represented by UTA, Ocean Avenue Entertainment and Sloane, Offer, Weber & Dern.[1]

Trivia[]
During filming for The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, Schwartzman decided that Lucky Flickerman needed a "nemesis", so with permission from Festus Creed's actor Max Raphael, he singled out Festus to pick on. According to Raphael, "We've got a whole backstory here. Festus, maybe he wants a career in broadcasting. Maybe Lucky babysat him when he was a kid, and he's just never gotten over how annoying he was. I don't know. That's for the fans to decide.”[2]
