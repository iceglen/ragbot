Lynn Cohen was an American actress who portrayed Mags, an eighty-year-old woman in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire who volunteered for Finnick Odair's female tribute/victor, Annie Cresta.

Career[]
Cohen started her acting career with the TV series Texas in 1982. She was best known for portraying Magda in Sex and the City and Gola Meir in Steven Speilberg's Munich. She has portrayed Judge Elizabeth Mizener several times on Law and Order. She has also made appearances in Eagle Eye and Invincible. Cohen was also active in theater. She made her debut on Broadway in 1989 in the play Orpheus Descending. In 1997 she also appeared in a play called Ivanov. She died on February 14th, 2020, at age 86.[1] Since her death, she has posthumously been credited for several short films.
