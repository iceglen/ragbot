Sharon Conley is the actress who portrayed Lucia, one of the Gamemakers in The Hunger Games film.

Career[]
Morris appeared in the movie What to Expect When You're Expecting and the TV series Army Wives. She studied speech communications at the University of Georgia. She worked for Coca-Cola after college and helped run a restaurant with her ex-husband. She also appeared in The Blind Side.
