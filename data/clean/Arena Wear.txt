Arena wear was the clothing worn by tributes during their time in the arena. The clothing was new and identical for each tribute. The stylists had no say in the tributes' uniforms, but were often able to use the clothing's composition to provide clues regarding what might be encountered in the arena.[1] Tributes could also wear or carry a personal token in the arena, as long as it didn't give them an unfair advantage in the Games.[2]

10th Hunger Games[]
Since the Hunger Games were not fleshed out in detail in the beginning, the tributes of the 10th Hunger Games did not have standardized or specialized clothes for the arena. The tributes wore whatever they had on at the reaping, which they also wore through the days leading up to the Games. Because of this, most of the tributes' clothes were worn and improper for the event.

Post 10th Hunger Games[]
In a promotional Instagram post for The Hunger Games On Stage, the twelve districts are presented with distinct colors reflecting a set similar to the one mentioned in the Sunrise on the Reaping novel. The exception to this is the black color of District 12, which would not contrast the rest of the post's outline well and was replaced with a dark gray color, though it can be speculated that this may be used to represent District 13 in other contexts. Considering that this novel is the only one in the book series in which district colors apart from that of District 12 is mentioned and that the stage adaptation retains more elements of the source novel than the film adaptation does, it is possible that these colors remain consistent throughout the Hunger Games' history in the book canon.[9]

49th Hunger Games[]
For the 49th Hunger Games, tributes were given shiny tunics to wear for an arena that was reflected entirely with mirrors.[10]

50th Hunger Games[]

Each district had a different color, which was the same for their chariot costumes, training outfits, and interview outfits.[11][12]

74th Hunger Games[]
For the 74th Hunger Games, the outfits were tawny trousers, a light green shirt, sturdy brown belt, and a thin-hooded black jacket that fell down to the thighs. The jacket was designed to reflect heat, as the nights would grow cold in the arena. The outfit also included boots, which were worn over skin-tight socks. The boots were made of soft leather and had a narrow, flexible rubber sole with treads, which were good for running.[1]

The Hunger Games film[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			The 74th Hunger Games tributes wearing their arena outfits. 		 	 
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			District color graphic from Blacksparrow Auctions' catalog. 		 	 
In the movie, the arena uniforms differ slightly from the ones described in the book. All tributes wore olive cargo pants, brown buckle boots, and a hooded nylon jacket with silver stripes that ran down the front, the arms, and back; the jacket also had an inner layer that had an orange reflective tape on the hem. Jackets and shirts (V-necks for the girls, crew necks for the boys) were color-coded according to district.[13]

News reel[]
The first film briefly showed news reel footage of two tributes from a previous year (thought to be the 73rd Hunger Games, corresponding with details from the original script). Both tributes in this scene wore black shoes, black cargo pants, and black, cropped armored vests underneath their colorful jackets. These appear to correspond with the district color coding: red with black stripes for District 2, and gray-brown with yellow stripes for District 10.[14]

75th Hunger Games[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Katniss' arena outfit in the Illustrated Edition of Catching Fire 		 	 For the 75th Hunger Games, the outfits were fitted blue jumpsuits made of sheer material that zippered down the front. Each suit came with a six-inch-wide padded belt covered in purple plastic, which was filled with a purple gel that could be used as a flotation device. The shoes were nylon with rubber soles.[15]
The Hunger Games: Catching Fire[]
 	 	 	 		 		 		 			Katniss wearing her arena uniform in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire 		 	 In the movie, every tribute wore the same outfit: a black wetsuit with short white sleeves, though longer, removable black sleeves covered the rest of the arm. The use of black, white, and grey were supposed to represent a mockingjay. The outfit didn't come with a padded belt, and they wore black boots.[16]
