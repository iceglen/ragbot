The Hangar is a special defense area in District 13 that is located deep underground. This is where all their hovercrafts are kept, and it also contains large amounts of ammunition and weaponry.

History[]
Katniss Everdeen took one of the hovercrafts to District 8 along with Boggs, Haymitch Abernathy, Plutarch Heavensbee, Gale Hawthorne, and a television crew. Almost everyone was dressed in a dark gray military jumpsuit.[1]
Later, Katniss and the crew returned to the Hangar and boarded a hovercraft to District 12, where they film.[2]
