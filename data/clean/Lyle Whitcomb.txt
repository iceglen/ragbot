Lyle Whitcomb was a man from District 12 and the father of Sarshee Whitcomb.[1]

Biography[]
Lyle was the boss of Haymitch Abernathy's father. He died of black lung, leaving his daughter Sarshee Whitcomb an orphan. In the same year, she was reaped to participate in the Hunger Games.[1]
