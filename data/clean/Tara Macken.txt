Tara Macken is a Filipino-Irish actress who portrayed the District 4 female tribute in The Hunger Games film and doubled Enobaria in the sequel. [2]
Unlike in the books, the District 4 female is also shown to fall during the initial bloodbath. She is thus excluded from the 74th Hunger Games Career pack.
Macken has a small part in the web series The Legion of Extraordinary Dancers also known as LXD, along with fellow Hunger Games actor Jeremy Marinas.
