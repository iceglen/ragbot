Tile was one of two male tributes from District 11 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Before the start of the games, Tile joined the Newcomers alliance between District 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack.[2] Tile and his district partners were considered "a game changer" by Haymitch.[3] 
On the hovercraft to the arena, the District 12 tributes confessed that one of their tributes, Louella McCoy, had been replaced with a girl originally from District 11. Tile was shocked and asked why they didn't reveal this before.[4]
He died in the initial bloodbath on the first day of the Games.[5]
During the Victory Tour, Haymitch faced Tile's distraught, grieving family.[6]

Physical description[]
Tile was the largest of his district's tributes.[4]

Personality and traits[]
Despite his large size, Tile was shown to have a big heart. He also seemed to hate deception, as he confronted the tributes of District 12 on why they never said anything about one of their own being replaced by a body double who had origins from his home district.[4]

Etymology[]
Possibly referencing irrigation tiles used in farming.
