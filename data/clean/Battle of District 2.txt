The Battle of District 2 is an unofficial term referring to a pivotal Second Rebellion battle. In this conflict, district rebels, supported by military forces from District 13, clashed with Capitol loyalists for control of District 2. 
This was the final district to fall under rebel control and a most crucial conflict, as this battle all but decimated the Capitol's military, leaving them with little capability to resist the subsequent invasion.

History[]
Background[]
At the end of the Dark Days, the rebel war effort collapsed following the alleged loss of District 13,[1] and the seat of the Capitol's military power moved to District 2.[2] Directly after the Dark Days, the Capitol established a military compound inside a mountain, later nicknamed the Nut during the Second Rebellion.[3] District 2's citizens were generally better-fed than in other districts,[4] but their children were still reaped to participate in the annual Hunger Games with no apparent preferential treatment.
However, over time, District 2 curried enough favor and privilege to be able to train Career Tributes for the event without consequences,[5] and the district became the Capitol's main source of Peacekeepers.[3] District 2's close association with the Capitol offered citizens education and employment opportunities, and Capitol propaganda nurtured an image of the Hunger Games as a unique opportunity to earn untold wealth and glory.[3] As a result of these factors, District 2 had a strong loyalist contingent.

Second Rebellion[]
Main article: Second Rebellion
When war broke out in the other districts, there was mixed support for the uprising in District 2. Nevertheless, the rebels, led in part by former victor Lyme, eventually managed to capture the outer villages. However, the town center was divided, and the Nut remained under loyalist control.[3]

Bombing of the Nut[]
Two weeks after the arrival of Katniss Everdeen and District 13's soldiers, a meeting was held to tackle the issue of the Nut. Lyme guided those assembled through a virtual tour of its interior and fortifications, and walked them through previous failed attempts to claim it. At the end of Lyme's presentation, strategists debated for hours; Beetee Latier suggested that he might be able to override computer systems, and others contemplated using several spies they had on the inside. Throughout the afternoon, people kept coming back to the failed strategy of storming the entrances, frustrating Lyme.[3] 
Eventually, Gale Hawthorne put the idea forth that rather than taking the Nut, they ought to disable it. He and Beetee pointed out avalanche paths that they could strategically bomb, burying the entrances and all the people inside.[3] It was ultimately decided that they would leave a train tunnel into the square open to give evacuees a chance at surrender. The plan proceeded, and the Nut's survivors fled to the city center, where the rebels were waiting for them.[6] 
With prompting from Haymitch Abernathy over an earpiece, Katniss was poised to give a speech to the Nut personnel when she suddenly rushed to the aid of a wounded man, shouting for rebels to hold their fire. He pulled a gun on her and demanded that she give him a reason not to shoot her. She replied that she couldn't, confusing him enough to make him pause. Despairing over all the pain the rebels and Capitol had caused each other, she lowered and even dropped her bow as she told him to shoot her. Continued prompting from Haymitch allowed her to turn her statement back around into a compelling speech about district unity against the Capitol. However, this ended when an unknown man in the crowd shot her.[6] 

Aftermath of the bombing[]
Katniss survived, but she suffered a ruptured spleen and her ribs were badly bruised. She was transported back to District 13 to recover. Meanwhile, District 2 became the last district to ally with the rebels.[7] The destruction of the Nut knocked out much of the Capitol's air force and logistical capabilities, dealing a crushing blow to its war effort.[8] This was the point where the war turned in favor of the rebels and set the stage for the rebels' final assault on the Capitol itself.[9]

Trivia[]
In The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2, Paylor from District 8 also served as a commander in the mission to seize the Nut.[10]
Lyme is thought to have died either in the last stages of this battle, during the Battle of the Capitol, or as a victim of the Victors' Purge.[11]
Berlin Tempelhof Airport served as the filming location for District 2's rebel encampment, Lyme's control center, and the train station.[12][13]
