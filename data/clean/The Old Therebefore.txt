"The Old Therebefore" is the third single from The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (Music From & Inspired By). The album version, "The Old Therebefore / Singing at Snakes", included dialogue from the scene in the film. In response to popular fan demand, an acapella studio version of Rachel Zegler's performance was released as a single on February 9th, 2024.[1]
