Trivia[]
During the Dark Days, the Capitol released a number of different muttations into the wild, such as jabberjays[7] and tracker jackers;[9] however, as these are not naturally-occurring animals, they are not included in the list above.
Mockingjays are a naturally-occurring crossbreed between female mockingbirds and jabberjays, which were exclusively male.[7]
