Smylie Bradwell (born 9 July 2016) is an English child actor. He is set to play Sid Abernathy in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Trivia[]
Smylie had his screen debut at the tender age of 6 years old in the popular crime series Wisting.
He is from Hampshire, England.
