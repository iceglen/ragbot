George Somner is an English actor who portrayed Spruce in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Somner starred in the feature film Kindling and was seen in a guest role in BBC One's adaptation of Adam Kay's This is Going to Hurt. He also reprised his series regular role in Netflix's hit Sex Education. Somner was a leading regular in the Channel 4 comedy Bad Move and was featured in Alexandre Aja's feature film Crawl, starring Kaya Scodelario. He was also part of the UK Tour of the award-winning People, Places, & Things. Further screen credits include a guest role in Grantchester and the short film Eden. 
Somner is represented by A&J Artists and The Artists Partnership in the UK and Luber Roklin Entertainment in the US.[1]
