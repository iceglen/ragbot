Sandra Ellis Lafferty is a Colorado-born actress who portrayed Greasy Sae in The Hunger Games film and The Hunger Games: Catching Fire.

Career[]
Sandra Ellis Lafferty was a respected Colorado theater actress prior to beginning her Hollywood career, which has consisted mainly of small but memorable parts in horror movies. She is also a former member of the company of the Denver Center for the Performing Arts and OpenStage Theatre & Company in Fort Collins.
Her first performance in a film was when she played a crying woman in the 1991 movie Dogfight.
