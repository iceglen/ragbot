Michael Greco is an English actor who portrayed Strabo Plinth in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
In 2023, Greco portrayed Pothinus in African Queens, directed by Jada Pinkett-Smith. He also played Bill Staton in the Emmy and Golden Globe-winning Hatfields & McCoys. Other feature film credits include Misfire, Revenge, and Gone. Greco's most notable British TV credits include Eastenders, Doctors, and Murder in Mind, all for BBC, as well as ITV's Chasing Shadows.
Greco is represented by Mark Jermin Management.[1]
