Flora Li Thiemann is a German actress who portrayed Livia Cardew in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Flora Li Thiemann played a leading role in the coming-of-age drama Tigermilk, as well as a supporting role in the postwar drama The Aftermath. She next will be seen in Amazon's series The Gryphon.
Li Thiemann is represented by Rietz & Casting Agency.[1]
