Tom Blyth is an English actor and producer who portrayed Coriolanus Snow in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

Career[]
Blyth's most notable roles include the films Robin Hood (2010), Benediction (2021), The Gilded Age (2022), and Billy the Kid (2022).[1]
On May 16, 2022, People reported that Blyth had been cast in the role of Coriolanus Snow. Regarding the casting, producer Nina Jacobson stated "Tom's performance will both fulfill and disrupt everything you think you know about Coriolanus Snow. He's a tremendous talent who will show why Snow always lands on top."[2]

Trivia[]
Before Rachel Zegler was officially cast as Lucy Gray Baird, director Francis Lawrence had her do a chemistry read with Blyth over Zoom, and Lawrence asked her to sing a folk song called "Wildwood Flower". Blyth was moved to tears by the performance.[3]
Tom Blyth attended Juilliard with Maya Hawke for a little bit and first met her at auditions for Juilliard. Hawke would later end up being cast as young Wiress in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.[4]
