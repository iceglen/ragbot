Billy Porter is the American actor and singer set to play Magno Stift in the upcoming film The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. Porter is a three-time Tony winner[1] and is known for using flamboyant fashion in a political way, such as to question people's perceptions of masculinity.[2]
Having previously worked with him on the show Pose, Nina Jacobson stated that Porter "is one of those rare performers who can dazzle and devastate in equal measure on stage and screen."[1]
