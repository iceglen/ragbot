Thresh's sister is a citizen of District 11. She was presumably raised by her grandmother along with her brother, Thresh.[1]

Biography[]
She was only seen during the Victory Tour, standing at a special platform constructed at the bottom of the stage.[1] Katniss Everdeen was certain that she was shot, after hearing gunshots that she knew killed an old man, but was proven wrong by Seeder, as when Katniss asked Seeder before the start of the 75th Hunger Games, she answered that they were alive. Her current fate is unknown.[2]

Physical description[]
Katniss described Thresh's sister as tall and muscular, just like her brother.[1]
