The 64th Hunger Games took place 10 years prior to the start of the trilogy. The victor this year was Cashmere, the female tribute from District 1.[1] She and her brother Gloss won in consecutive years.[2]

She likely formed a typical alliance with other Career Tributes, since she was from one of the Career districts and Katniss Everdeen described her as a "classic Career".[3]

Trivia[]
In The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, Cashmere and Gloss were both adept with throwing knives,[4] so that may have been her weapon of choice during her first Games.
Since Finnick Odair won the 65th Hunger Games the following year,[5] Gloss had to have won the 63rd Hunger Games in order for the siblings' wins to be consecutive.
