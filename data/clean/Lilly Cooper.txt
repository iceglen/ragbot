Lilly Cooper is an actress who portrayed Arachne Crane in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Cooper trained at Identity School of Acting in London and played Murta in The Witcher series. She is represented by the Identity Agency Group.[1]
