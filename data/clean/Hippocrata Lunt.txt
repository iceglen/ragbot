Hippocrata Lunt was the school counselor at the Academy.

Biography[]
In the schoolwide assembly after the death of Arachne Crane at the hands of her tribute, Hippocrata invited anyone with grief issues to visit her office, especially if they were having violent impulses towards themselves or others.[1]

Etymology[]
Her first name may be a play on Hippocrates, a Ancient Greek physician sometimes called the father of medicine. Literally translated, Hippocrates means "horsepower" (híppos meaning “horse” and krátos meaning “power”). 
Her surname, Lunt, is a word for a slow-burning match or smoke from a tobacco pipe.
