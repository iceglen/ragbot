The District 10 male was the male tribute from District 10 in the 73rd Hunger Games.

Biography[]
He survived to the final two in the 73rd Hunger Games. In the final battle, the District 2 male overpowered him and beat him to death with a brick.[1]

Physical description[]
In The Hunger Games film, he was portrayed by an Asian-American actor with short black hair. In voiceover, Caesar Flickerman commented that the final fight was between two 16-year-olds.[1]

Trivia[]
His arena wear included a gray-brown jacket, which matched the film's color coding for tributes from District 10.[2]
