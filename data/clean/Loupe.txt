Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Loupe was part of the Career pack in these games. In the training center, he and his district partners wore "snot-green."[2]

Death[]
On Day 2, Loupe was killed single-handedly by Maysilee Donner with a poisoned dart after breaking away from the Career pack with Camilla of District 2.[3] This was one of the few things that the heavily edited recap of the 50th Hunger Games kept accurate.[4] Maysilee took a first aid kit with things such as burn ointment from Loupe.[3]

Etymology[]
Loupe refers to a small magnification device that is frequently used in gemology and the jewelry trade to examine small details more closely.

Trivia[]
In Catching Fire, Loupe was indirectly mentioned by Katniss Everdeen while she watched footage of the 50th Hunger Games.
