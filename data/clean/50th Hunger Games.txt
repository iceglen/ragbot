The 50th Hunger Games was the year of the second Quarter Quell, held 24 years before the start of the trilogy. It is the subject of the prequel novel Sunrise on the Reaping as well as its film adaptation.[1] The victor was Haymitch Abernathy from District 12.[2]

Arena[]
Main article: 50th Hunger Games arena
The arena was described as "the most breathtaking place imaginable." The Cornucopia sat in the middle of a sweet-smelling green meadow, and the sky was azure blue with fluffy, billowing white clouds. The meadow stretched for miles, and far in the distance there were woods on one side, and a snowcapped mountain on the other, which was later revealed to be a deadly volcano.[4]
Everything in the arena was lethally poisonous, including the plants growing in the arena, the water in the stream, the butterfly stings, and even the scent of the flowers when inhaled too directly. The only sources of safe food and drink in the arena were the rainwater and the food from the Cornucopia.[4]

Summary[]
Pre-Games[]
From District 12, the first girl to be reaped was Louella McCoy from the Seam by their escort, Drusilla Sickle. The second tribute was Maysilee Donner, who was clinging to her twin sister and her friend, Asterid March, before "bravely [disengaging] herself". She was followed by Wyatt Callow and Woodbine Chance. However, Woodbine tried to run away after his name was called and was subsequently shot dead. With the need for another male tribute, Haymitch Abernathy was chosen instead.[6][4] 
Upon arriving in the Capitol, the boys and girls from District 12 wore "awful" coal miner costumes for the Tribute Parade.[4] During the parade, the horse pulling their chariot was startled and bolted. In the process, Haymitch and Louella were thrown out and onto the ground. Louella cracked her head on the ground and was killed instantly. In his grief, Haymitch picked up her body and evaded the Peacekeepers long enough to reach President Snow's mansion and lay her body in front of him to make a statement.

Training scores[]
Training was held in the gym of the old Academy. Survival skills booths were placed around the gym with different things that the tributes could learn. The far end had weapon instruction. The tributes wore plain clothing in a colour representing their district. Ampert Latier used the training sessions to form an alliance, named the Newcomers, with several other districts to rival the Career Tributes. His father, as well as, Ampert's assigned mentor, and previous District 3 victor, Beetee Latier, was forced to host a station and watch, as his son had been reaped in punishment for his act of rebellion. His station was designed to show the tributes how to produce electricity out of something as mundane as a potato. Haymitch was attacked by Panache Barker who was quickly tasered and dragged away.

Interviews[]

Caesar: "So, Haymitch, what do you think of the Games having one hundred percent more competitors than usual?"

This year, Caesar Flickerman wore his usual twinkling midnight blue suit, though he had dark green hair and his eyelids and lips were done up to match. In his interview, Haymitch came across as snarky, arrogant, and indifferent.[4] Since there were twice as many tributes, each tribute was only allowed 2 minutes per interview with a minute long "palate cleanser" after every four districts. 

Arena wear[]
The arena wear consists of a white cloak with district coloured outlines, white combat boots, and layered, form-fitting tactical wear.


The Games[]
Day 1[]
Most tributes were distracted by the beauty of the arena when the gong sounded, so Haymitch was able to get a backpack and weapons from the Cornucopia and head into the woods before most of his competitors even left their plates. Maysilee Donner came away from the Cornucopia with a small backpack, which contained a bowl, some dried beef, and a blowgun with twenty-four darts.[4]
In total, 18 of the 48 tributes were killed in the bloodbath, and an alliance of 12 Career Tributes claimed the Cornucopia's remaining supplies. This included the only safe food and drink in the arena.[4] Six of the tributes were killed by Panache Barker who had retrieved a sword.[7] The only Careers to die were a boy and girl from District 5 and the other 16 were all Newcomers.[8]
Carat and Urchin died of the arena's poisons while the Careers formed their pack on the arena's snowcapped mountain.[8] Haymitch nearly fell victim to the poison as well, but realized its existence after spotting a rabbit dying from drinking from a stream. Haymitch was able to treat the poison with charcoal tablets from his backpack, although it left him weak and sick through the night.[9][10]

Day 2[]
The Careers scoured the mountain area for potential victims.[4][7] Maysilee Donner was able to use the arena's poisons to turn her blowgun's darts into deadly weapons.[4] During this day, Maysilee killed Loupe from District 1 on her own after he broke from the Career pack with Camilla from District 2. Although Maysilee may have hit Camilla with a poison dart as well, she survived.[11][8] While several Newcomers were poisoned, Wellie figured it out fast and Hull's backpack contained a big bottle of the syrup antidote, meaning that those who were poisoned survived.[7]
On Day 2, Haymitch shared some of an apple with a rabbit that he encountered. Haymitch was later found by Lou Lou who lacked any pack or supplies of her own. Lou Lou suggested that she was ordered to find Haymitch by the Gamemakers through her earpiece. Haymitch was able to find a quartz rock to use with his flint striker to build a fire while Lou Lou fell asleep, either exhausted or drugged by the Gamemakers through her pump. Loupe died during this time and when Lou Lou woke up, Haymitch searched the berms for a mutt portal. However, Lou Lou was drawn to bee balm plants which, like everything else in the arena, were poisonous. To end her suffering, Haymitch yanked out Lou Lou's pump.[10] Rather than let the hovercraft take her body, Haymitch fled with it, causing the Gamemakers to send butterfly muttations to force Haymitch to let them take her. Hundreds of the butterflies attacked and chased Haymitch until he fell into a berm of gas plants. Haymitch ignited the gas, incinerating most of the butterflies while his clothing protected him from harm. The few survivors then left Haymitch alone. Armed with a makeshift torch, Haymitch followed the butterflies back to their portal under the guise of retaliation and wedged it partially open with a branch.[7]

Day 3[]
On the morning of Day 3, Haymitch received a gift of bread rolls and grape juice. Worn out from the first two days of the Games, Haymitch waited throughout the morning for Ampert to arrive with his fuse and two of the District 9 sunflower bombs before moving to higher ground and using binoculars to survey the arena. After spotting Ampert, Haymitch rendezvoused with him. As a cover, Ampert acted like he had been sent by the Newcomers to bring Haymitch back while Haymitch claimed that his low score made him too dangerous to be around, using Lou Lou as an example. Haymitch exchanged food for an update and Ampert reported that the Newcomers had lost seventeen people now, were separated from Maysilee Donner, and were sticking together on the mountain where the Careers were hunting them.[7]
Under the guise of a tour in the hopes of finding a safer place for the Newcomers, Haymitch led Ampert to the berm with the wedged open mutt portal where they rest until nightfall.[7] The two agreed to let Ampert stay rather than returning to the Newcomers in the dark and he removed his token with the fuse and the sunflower bombs under the guise of using it as a snare to catch something to eat. Ampert gave the spare bomb to Haymitch and when the anthem played, it was revealed that no tributes had died during the day's events.[12]

Day 4[]
Around midnight on the fourth day, Haymitch and Amper put their plan into motion, preparing the explosive and using Haymitch's spear to pry open the mutt portal. Haymitch descended alone into Sub-A and planted the bomb on the water tank with a 60 second fuse, but was attacked by bat muttations on his way out. Haymitch struggled to hold them off with a torch as he climbed out, but ran out of time and used his belt to secure himself to the ladder. Thanks to having secured himself, Haymitch survived the explosion and the subsequent flood of non-poisonous water while the mutts presumably drowned. On the surface, Haymitch discovered that the Gamemakers had retaliated by sending golden squirrel mutts to kill Ampert. Although Haymitch tried to save his friend, the mutts were programmed to target only Ampert and ignored him, quickly devouring the boy and leaving behind only a skeleton that was stripped clean to the bone.[12]
As Haymitch went into a rage, hacking apart the area with his axe, the arena began to malfunction from the damage, with the mutt portals opening and closing, the animals acting strange, an apple tree became a fountain of blue sparks, and clouds of steam rose from a nearby stream. The arena's sky lost power, revealing the real night sky behind it. However, the Gamemakers quickly got most of the arena's malfunctions under control.[12]
After the Gamemakers got the arena mostly back under control, they caused the snowcapped mountain -- in reality an artificial volcano -- to erupt.[12] Haymitch's clothing protected him from the toxic cloud and heated particles in the air and he took cover at the base of a tree, far enough away from the eruption to avoid the worst of the effects. Haymitch wondered if the water tank had been meant to quench the aftermath of the eruption and was concerned that in destroying it, he had destroyed any hope for those who survived the eruption.[13] Twelve tributes -- six Careers and six Newcomers -- who were still on the mountain were killed, falling victim to the flame-shooting eruption, asphyxiation by the ash cloud and burns from the chemical lava. The survivors barely escaped, heading across the meadow into the woods.[11][8] In keeping with the arena's design, the ash was clear and sparkling so that everything appeared to be covered in a layer of rock candy.[13] The lava flows made half the arena inaccessible, driving the remaining tributes into the woods.[4] The lava was actually some kind of gel that gave you chemical burns if it got under your clothes and then turned hard and slippery as ice on the ground.[11] The clothes of the tributes were designed to be fireproof and to provide protection against the toxic wind generated by the eruption, although they were unlikely to be of much help to those caught on the mountain itself.[13]
Haymitch spent the next several hours in a stupor, overwhelmed by recent events. After daylight finally started shining through again, Haymitch realized that a humming noise that he kept hearing was the generator just outside of the arena's top end and deduced that despite whatever disruption the flood had caused to the arena's energy supply, the generator was keeping the arena running. While the brain was damaged, enough of it was still functioning to entertain the audience. Remembering something that Mags Flanagan had told him, Haymitch decided not to give up and instead to try to help his remaining allies in some way. Returning to the butterfly bush berm, Haymitch found signs of damage from the malfunctions, but they had been brought under control by this point. Haymitch was able to recover his supplies, although his spear was gone, so Haymitch replaced it with Ampert's axe.[13]
Haymitch decided that his next step would be to head north and try to break out of the arena to reach and sabotage the generator in an effort to bring an end to the Games. During his trek, it began to rain which allowed Haymitch to collect a bit of water for his water jugs. The rain also cleaned the forest of the ash from the eruption. At the end of the forest, Haymitch found a massive hedge and followed a narrow path that he'd found through it, only to discover that the hedge itself served as a maze. A panicking Haymitch became hopelessly lost in the maze, but eventually encountered a rabbit, possibly the same one that he had shared an apple with, and it led him out.[13]
As soon as Haymitch emerged, he was ambushed by Panache Barker, Barba and Angler. Acting on reflex, Haymitch managed to clumsily deflect Barba's trident and stab her in the gut, mortally wounding her, before hamstringing Angler and cleaving open the Career's neck open with his axe. Haymitch was easily disarmed by the much bigger and better armed Panache and futilely attempted to talk him down. However, Maysilee Donner saved Haymitch, killing Panache with a poison dart to the throat from her blowgun. The two District 12 tributes agreed to an alliance and Maysilee took Barba's knife, but Haymitch declined to take Barba's trident or Panache's sword, unfamiliar with the trident and creeped out by claiming the sword that was stained with the blood of Newcomers. Haymitch wasn't Panache's heir, the new leader of the pack, and he refused to present himself as such.[13][11]
Once the hovercraft took the bodies, Maysilee used a first aid kit that she took from Loupe to treat Haymitch's wounds and they shared their provisions with each other. When the anthem played, the two discovered that 16 tributes had been killed that day, leaving only Silka Sharp and Maritte of the Careers and Haymitch, Maysilee, Hull, Chicory, Ringina, Autumn, Wellie and Buck of the Newcomers. Haymitch and Maysilee then received a sponsor gift of bean and ham hock soup that they shared. The two decided to declare themselves as family, built a fire, and slept, trading with each other on watch.[11]

Day 5[]
On the fifth morning, Haymitch decided to refocus on his plan to get through the hedge and find the generator and he and Maysilee created a funnel to catch any more rainwater. While Maysilee wanted to head for the Cornucopia in search of any remaining food and the other Newcomers, Haymitch argued for heading north, although he was unable to explain his reasoning why without the Gamemakers overhearing. At Maysilee's suggestion, the two searched the area where the fight had taken place and located Panache, Barba and Angler's backpacks and found another hammock, two empty water jugs, three handkerchiefs, a bottle of poison antidote, a second tarp and a blowtorch. The food supplies were limited to a flat tin of sardines, a banana with brown spots, four rolls, a jar of nut butter with about an inch left, Haymitch's two potatoes and Maysilee's three dried beef jerky strips and olives. Maysilee used part of the food to make them sandwiches for breakfast.[11]
The two set out to find the hedge with Maysilee restocking her poison along the way. As the two traveled, encountering no one and nothing else, they sometimes passed casualties of the flooding such as trees that dripped blood instead of sap, a gaping hole where something had exploded, leaving a slimy clear liquid covering everything in its vicinity and a stump that belched sulphureous, glowing gas. Finally reaching the hedge, Haymitch explained that his plan was to cut straight through it, but as they began cutting through, the two tributes were attacked by ladybug mutts that were disguised as the holly berries of the arena's hedge. When Haymitch and Maysilee tried to cut through, the ladybugs revealed themselves, latched on to their flesh, inflated to the size of acorns and began exploding, splattering the tributes' faces with blood.[11] Fleeing from the hedge, Haymitch and Maysilee began plucking the ladybugs out, but the suckers were dug in deep, akin to those on a really determined tick. Gripping near the head and pulling slowly and firmly caused them to pop out in a spray of blood. After clearing himself of the bugs, Haymitch helped Maysilee and the two realized that the Gamemakers were preventing them from going into the hedge.[14]
The two spent time recovering from the mutt attack and realized that Haymitch's plan was unsustainable. A rainstorm lasting about 20 minutes gave Haymitch and Maysilee the chance to refill their water jugs and clean off. During this time,[14] Ringina and Autumn were killed by Silka Sharp and Maritte in combat,[8] leaving Haymitch and Maysilee to wonder what would happen to the Newcomers alliance if the two dead tributes were Silka and Maritte.[14]
With no better ideas, the two headed for the Cornucopia in search of food, picking up what at first sounds like a baby, but on closer examination, was the squeals and mewls of an animal like a goat or a kitten. Hearing a scream from Hull or Buck, Haymitch and Maysilee ran off to help, finding Buck and Chicory with long metallic poisonous quills resembling knitting needles protruding from their flesh shot by a bear sized porcupine mutt. Despite having half a dozen quills in his face, Hull attacked the mutt with a pitchfork in a desperate attempt to save his allies. As Haymitch and Maysilee worked out a plan, the mutt drove a slew more quills into Hull's thigh, causing him to collapse. Haymitch successfully threw his axe into the porcupine's side, causing it to shoot quills at them. The double tarp held up by Maysilee blocked a dozen of them, but one got Haymitch in the nose, another nearly blinded him and a third got Maysilee in the cheek. The mutt went into a meltdown from the attack with the two tributes having to dodge another round of quills and one of the Newcomers died of the poison. Finally, Haymitch hit upon the idea to use Maysilee's olives to try to lure the porcupine away and succeeded in tricking it into leaving. By this point, Buck and Chicory were already dead and Hull died despite Haymitch and Maysilee's attempts to remove the quills and give him the antidote.[14]
The two took the antidote to deal with the poison that they had received from the quills, shut the dead tributes' eyes and tried to arrange their bodies better before taking their own supplies and those of the dead tributes and leaving. Maysilee declared that one of them had to win the Games so that they could tear up the Capitol's scripts, tear down their celebrations, set fire to the Victors' Village and refuse to play their game. Settling in for the night, Haymitch and Maysilee learned that they, Silka, Maritte and Wellie were the only ones left alive now and decided to find Wellie the next day. As he fell asleep, Haymitch figured out how to get through the maze.[14]

Day 6[]
By the time that Haymitch awoke on Day 6, he and Maysilee had received another sponsor gift in the form of breakfast. Under the guise of looking for Wellie, Haymitch led them to the north which Maysilee argued as the little girl was more likely to head for the meadow. Unable to explain his actual reasoning, Haymitch argued that it couldn't go on forever, the arena had to end somewhere, and there had to be something that they could use, potentially even weaponizing the ladybugs for themselves. Maysilee finally, reluctantly, agreed to Haymitch's plan.[15]
On the way, Haymitch and Maysilee were ambushed by Silka Sharp and Maritte and fled towards an electrical hum coming from an as of yet unexplored area to the west. The four tributes ended up falling into an area where three Gamemakers were cleaning up an open berm covered in scarlet poppies, leading to a confrontation. As the Gamemakers made a break for Sub-A, Maritte killed one with her trident, Maysilee killed a second with her blowgun and the third fell headfirst through the berm to her death. Before the tributes could resume their fight, a hovercraft attacked with tear gas bombs, sending them fleeing. Only able to follow one group of tributes, the hovercraft chased after the Careers, allowing the two Newcomers to escape. As they recovered, Maysilee was furious that Haymitch froze when confronted with the Gamemakers, but calmed down.[15]
Returning to the hedge, Haymitch used the blowtorch from one of the dead Careers' backpacks to burn through it while Maysilee used a makeshift torch to burn the ladybugs as they tried to attack, the two tributes singing as they did. As the blowtorch ran out of fuel, Haymitch and Maysilee finally made it through the hedge to find themselves on a sheer cliff overlooking a canyon containing the arena's generator.[15]
During this time, Maritte was attacked and killed by the same golden squirrel mutts that had killed Ampert in retaliation for her murder of a Gamemaker.[8]
With only five tributes remaining, Maysilee broke off the alliance when Haymitch refused to go back,[4] but changed her mind moments later when a cannon fired signaling the death of Maritte. Unsure of what to do, Maysilee decided to retrieve their potatoes and then weigh their options together.[16] Once Maysilee left, Haymitch kicked a pebble off the cliff into the force field over the generator. Surprised to see the pebble bounce back, he threw a larger rock and watched it do the same. However, even if Haymitch dropped a boulder off of the cliff, he wouldn't be able to touch it. Haymitch laughed at his terrible luck, but he was cut off by the sound of Maysilee screaming.[4][16]
By the time he reached her, Maysilee was under attack by candy pink birds who were programmed to target Maysilee alone in a very personal punishment, just like the squirrels had with Ampert. As a result, the birds ignored Haymitch completely as he tried to defend his friend, and one skewered her throat with its beak before being killed by Haymitch. With Maysilee mortally wounded, the mutts cleared out and all that he could do was hold her hand as she died. Haymitch chose not to clean her up so as to make things harder for the people in the Capitol and took Maysilee's blowgun and her copper necklace with the flower as a reminder of her strength.[16]
Stricken, Haymitch sat against a tree in shock as Maysilee's body was retrieved by a hovercraft and placed Maysilee's token alongside District 9's sunflower, Wyatt Callow's scrip coin and his flint striker from Lenore Dove. Having only taken the blowgun itself and not the pouch and poison vial, Haymitch was left with only the poison dart that the blowgun was loaded with. Haymitch was then sent the gift of strawberry ice cream and black coffee, presumably Mags Flanagan's attempt to get Haymitch to stay positive by reminding him of Maysilee's sass.[16]
As night fell and with only two other tributes left, Haymitch decided to track down Wellie who would make a far better, far smarter and far more convincing victor to represent district rights than he would. Haymitch found Wellie and tended to the young girl, spending the night in a tree. Following a rainstorm, the two received a gift of vanilla pudding and chocolate for Wellie. In a strange moment of unity, Silka joined them at the base of the tree, simply huddling there and crying rather than trying to hunt the other two tributes and Haymitch dropped Silka some of the chocolate.[16]

Day 7[]
The next morning, Silka was gone and Haymitch realized that the copper wire of Maysilee's token was actually a backup fuse for the sunflower bombs. Haymitch decided to target the Cornucopia, staging a showdown with Silka and killing them both in the explosion to leave Wellie as the victor. If Haymitch survived, Snow was sure to have the Gamemakers kill him instead which would also leave Wellie as the victor. Using a tarp as cover, Haymitch unwound the fuse from Maysilee's token, but the scared and emaciated little girl begged him not to abandon her. Haymitch left Wellie with Maysilee's blowgun and hid her to gather things for a fire.[17]
As Haymitch searched, he received a pitcher of milk from the Capitol which Haymitch suspected was one of two things: a genuine gift from Plutarch Heavensbee or poison sent by Snow which Haymitch believed to be the more likely option. Due to it being publicly broadcast, Haymitch was forced into a position where he had to either give it to Wellie or drink it himself, but either way, the Newcomers wouldn't be allowed to win. However, before Haymitch could drink, Silka found and decapitated Wellie who managed to shoot her with the blowgun in return, but the dart was blocked by Silka's clothes.[17]
Haymitch and Silka engaged in an axe to axe fight in which they were both fairly evenly matched. Eventually, Silka managed to slice open Haymitch's abdomen and disarm him while Haymitch stabbed out her eye with his knife. Haymitch fled, holding his intestines in while he fled.[17]
Silka chased Haymitch to the cliff and threw her axe, but Haymitch collapsed on the ground, dodging the attack. Due to the severity of his wounds, she thought she could outlast him even without her weapon, but Haymitch knew the axe would be flung back by the force field and lodge itself in her head.[4] However, Silka wasn't instantly killed, giving Haymitch the chance to use his sunflower bomb. Silka finally died as Haymitch prepared to throw the bomb off of the cliff at the arena's generator which was being protected by the force field. Despite the Gamemakers' frantic attempts to stop him, Haymitch successfully threw the bomb, causing a massive explosion that presumably destroyed the generator.[17] It also caused an earthquake that literally and figuratively shook the Capitol.[18]

Aftermath[]
Edited footage[]
Because of the rebellious actions of the Newcomers, the footage of the 50th Hunger Games was heavily edited starting with the reaping of the District 12 tributes and continuing with the Chariot rides. In the interviews, the Careers were made to look more intelligent and the Newcomers less unified with Lou Lou reduced to a girl wearing reptile fashion, Maysilee Donner and Wyatt Callow's memorable turns completely ignored and Haymitch was reduced to one snarky exchange with Caesar Flickerman that made him appear to be a stuck-up, selfish jerk.[8]
The recap of Day 1 was fairly accurate, but things got changed around on Day 2. While it accurately showed Maysilee killing Loupe on her own and the Careers hunting the Newcomers while the tributes were recovering from the arena's poisons, Haymitch's story was twisted around so that he fought the squirrels on Day 2 with no sign of Ampert even though the squirrels weren't there until Day 3 when they killed Ampert, thus making it look like Haymitch was only trying to save himself. Lou Lou's death was shown, but with Haymitch edited out. While Haymitch was shown fleeing from the butterflies, Lou Lou's body was removed so that he was no longer shown carrying it as he ran. As such, the Capitol made it seem like Haymitch had abandoned all of his allies.[8]
For Day 3, the squirrels were made to magically reappear to kill Ampert with no sign of Haymitch. Haymitch's entire alliance with Ampert, their bombing of the tank and the arena going haywire were removed as well. Following the volcanic eruption, the footage made it seem like Haymitch's entire agenda was simply to reach the end of the arena. After Haymitch and Maysilee's alliance, things were on course again until the footage was once again edited to remove Maritte and Maysilee killing three Gamemakers and Haymitch and Maysilee drawing off the porcupine mutt. Instead, Silka and Maritte were shown chasing them through the woods while Buck, Chicory and Hull died from the quills, the porcupine just seeming to wander off on its own.[8]
For Day 4 or 5, the attempts by Haymitch and Maysilee to carve through the hedge were edited into one big sequence involving the ladybugs and the blowtorch and the footage steered clear of showing the generator. The cannon announcing Maritte's death was edited out and it was made to look like Maysilee really ended her alliance with Haymitch, but his discovery of the force field was kept in. For Maysilee's death, they once again made Haymitch look selfish rather than trying to help his friend.[8]
For the final sequence of events, made to seem like Day 5 or 6, Snow's delivery of the poisoned milk was removed, and the sound of Wellie screaming was added which didn't happen in reality, making it appear as if Haymitch had finally remembered he belonged to a wider alliance and went to her rescue. It was then made to appear as if Maritte died next rather than her being already long dead. Haymitch and Silka's final confrontation was kept fairly accurate, but the ending was edited to make it appear as if Silka was instantly killed by her rebounding axe and the 50th Hunger Games ended there.[8]
Twenty-five years later, Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark watched this version of the 50th Hunger Games while they were preparing for the 75th Hunger Games with Haymitch as their mentor.[4]

Retaliation[]
Haymitch's stunt with the force field made the Capitol look foolish. In retaliation, Haymitch's mother, younger brother, and girlfriend were all killed in the two weeks after the Games.[19] Upon Haymitch's return home, he found his house on fire with his mother and brother inside while Lenore Dove soon after fell victim to a poisoned sweet sent by Snow.[20] This caused Haymitch to push everyone away, sure that Snow would kill anyone that he loved.[18] Depressed by the loss of his family and forced to mentor year after year of doomed tributes, Haymitch developed an alcohol dependency and his life fell into disrepair.[21]

Trivia[]
The Games started at 9 o'clock in the morning.
The Games began on July 11th and ended on July 19th, as the reaping takes place on July 4th[22] and is followed by a week of preparations in the capitol.
Haymitch is the second District 12 victor,[21] the district's first male victor, and their first victor in forty years.[23] Haymitch is also the only victor from 12 who had to mentor tributes.
Maysilee Donner was the only known District 12 tribute to make it to the final five who did not survive to become a victor. She was also one of only two known merchant tributes,[4] the other being Peeta Mellark.[24]
An entire chapter was dedicated to recounting the 50th Hunger Games in Catching Fire, when Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark watched an edited recap.[4] This was not included in the film adaptation.[25] As revealed in Sunrise on the Reaping, this was the heavily edited version that the Capitol created rather than the actual sequence of events.
In the second Quarter Quell, 18 out of 48 died in the bloodbath alone. This was the deadliest bloodbath in Hunger Games history.
Despite this, it took four days for 75% to be killed because the number of tributes overall had been doubled. Nearly half the casualties were caused by Gamemaker traps.[4]
In the third Quarter Quell, 18 out of 24 tributes (75%) died within three days, and most casualties were caused by other tributes.
In a reversal of the 50th Hunger Games, the Cornucopia in the 75th Hunger Games only provided weapons, so food and water had to be acquired from the arena itself or through sponsor gifts.[26]
The footage of the 50th Hunger Games was heavily edited after the fact as seen in Sunrise on the Reaping.
This is the second Hunger Games where all the tributes are named.
This is the only known games where a child of a former victor was reaped and participated as a tribute. However, Katniss says in the original trilogy that such a thing happens too often to be purely coincidental.
Both the 50th and the 75th Hunger Games involved a plot by the rebels to destroy the arena and disrupt the Games.
Both Games and plots involved Haymitch Abernathy, Beetee Latier, Wiress, Mags Flanagan and Plutarch Heavensbee.
