Alawna was one of two female tributes from District 8 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Before the start of the Games, Alawna joined the Newcomers alliance between District 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 & 12 tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. She and her district partners were considered "nothing special" by Haymitch Abernathy.[2] Her token was a little doll in a beautiful sewn outfit, the same as the other girl from her district.[3]
At the beginning of the Games, she died in the initial bloodbath, along with the other tributes from her district.[4]

Physical description[]
The tributes from District 8 wore peach-colored outfits during the chariot rides and training.[5]

Etymology[]
Alawna is a portmanteau of Alana, a female given name, and of  "lawn," a lightweight textile.

Trivia[]
Alawna, along with Alpheus, Janus, Nona, Lect, Coil, Bircher, Heartwood, Ryan, Clayton, Midge, and the rest of her district and District 5, is only named on the tribute board.
Alawna's token was a doll dressed in a beautifully-sewn outfit.[6]
