Coriolanus 9 is a solar thermal power plant located in the heart of District 5. It generates over 7% of the Capitol's energy needs by itself, using falling water to produce 20,000 volts of electricity at a time.[1]

Trivia[]
The power plant was featured in a series by Capitol TV to promote the release of The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1 in 2014. The video was uploaded by YouTube channel Veritasium.
Coriolanus 9 is named after the President of Panem, Coriolanus Snow.
