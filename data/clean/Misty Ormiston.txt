Misty Ormiston is an American actress and model who portrayed Leeg 1 in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2.

Career[]
Misty has appeared in some movies such as Meet the Spartans, Blood Out and Broken Blood. She has also appeared in the TV movie, Scream of the Banshee. She and her twin Kim Ormiston are models as well as actresses, and have directed, written, filmed, and edited two films. They have also created their own magazine.
