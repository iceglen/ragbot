Kelvin Harrison Jr. is an American actor set to play young Beetee Latier in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.[1] He started his acting career with small roles in Ender's Game and 12 Years a Slave. Harrison is also well know for acting as Tyler Williams in Waves, Luce Edgar in Luce and Fred Hampton in The Trial of the Chicago 7. 

Trivia[]
Harrison co-starred with Jeffrey Wright, who played Beetee Latier in the original film trilogy in the movie Monster (2018). Harrison played Wright's character's son.
Harrison has the same birthday as Woody Harrelson, who played Haymitch Abernathy in the film trilogy, and the late Philip Seymour Hoffman, who played Plutarch Heavensbee. Harrelson, Hoffman, and Harrison were all born on July 23rd but different years.
