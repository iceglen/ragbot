Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Alpheus was part of the Career pack in the 50th Games which was also the Second Quarter Quell. During the Games, he died on the fourth day, along with the tributes from his home district during a volcanic eruption.[2]

Physical description[]
District 2's tributes wore purple training outfits.[3]

Etymology[]
Alpheus was a Greek river deity and the namesake of the Alfeios River.

Trivia[]
Alpheus is only named on the tribute board.
