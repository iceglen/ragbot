Daniela Grubert is an actress who portrayed Mrs. Plinth in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Grubert is a German actress who studied theater at the Berlin University of the Arts and performed in musicals such as Hair, Little Shop of Horrors and Evita, and played the title role in Aida.[1]
