Predator or Prey: Making The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes is a feature-length documentary film about the making of The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. It is available exclusively on 4K Ultra HD and Blu-ray discs, and is not included in the digital copy.[1]

Summary[]
The documentary is organized into eight sections.[1]

A Different World (Return To Panem)
Songbirds & Snakes (The Acting Ensemble)
Pure as the Driven Snow (The Music Of Lucy Gray Baird)
A Palette of Tactics (On Location In Poland And Berlin)
Humanity Undressed (Costume, Makeup & Hair)
To The Victor Go The Spoils (Stunts & Weapons)
Inner Sanctum (The Post-Production Process)
Snow Lands On Top (Reflections)
