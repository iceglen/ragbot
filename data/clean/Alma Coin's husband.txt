Alma Coin's husband was an unnamed man who married President Alma Coin.[1]

Biography[]
At some point in his life, this man was wed to Alma Coin, and the two of them had a daughter. He and their daughter both died in the District 13 pox epidemic "a few years" prior to the Second Rebellion.[1]

Trivia[]
Coin is never mentioned as having any family in the Mockingjay novel, nor losing anyone to the pox epidemic. This backstory is exclusive to the film adaptation, mentioned briefly in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1.[1]
