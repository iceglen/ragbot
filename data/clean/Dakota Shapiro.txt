Dakota Shapiro is an Australian actor who portrayed Billy Taupe Clade in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Shapiro was one of the top ten finalists for the 2017 Heath Ledger Scholarship. His list of credits include a series regular on Matthew Carnahan's limited series Valley of the Boom (Nat Geo), where he played real life tech pioneer Stephan Paternot, and a series regular for the pilot for The Lost Boys (The CW), where he played the role of David. Shapiro is represented by Clear Talent Group and Henriksen Talent Management.[1]
