Dr. Wane was a doctor who worked at the Capitol Hospital. He took care of Coriolanus Snow and Clemensia Dovecote.

Biography[]
After Clemensia Dovecote was attacked by snake mutts, Dr. Wane was one of the doctors who treated her and administered the antidote. He reassured Coriolanus Snow, who had been there at the time of the attack, and suggested that he return home.[1]
Dr. Wane later treated Coriolanus himself after the bombing of the Capitol Arena, which had occurred during the tour for the tributes and mentors of the 10th Hunger Games. He sent Coriolanus's cousin and grandmother home before giving Coriolanus a quick checkup and explaining his injuries. When asked about Lucy Gray Baird, Dr. Wane responded that he didn't know what had happened to her and told Coriolanus to get rest. 
A few days afterward, he heard that Clemensia had visited Coriolanus in the night, so he talked to his patient and said that he could visit her. After lunch, Dr. Wane released Coriolanus.[2]
