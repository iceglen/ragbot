Leigha Hancock is an American actress and stuntwoman specifically cast to portray the training and fight scenes of a tribute that fell during the initial bloodbath serving the role as the District 7 female tribute in The Hunger Games film,[1] her feature film debut. She has appeared on Make It or Break It as a stunt double.
She is a graduate of North Carolina State University, where she was on the gymnastics team.[2]
