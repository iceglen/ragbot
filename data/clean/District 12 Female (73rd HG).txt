The District 12 female was a tribute from District 12 who participated in the 73rd Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
The District 12 female was born in the Seam along with her district partner, and was reaped for the 73rd Hunger Games. She was mentored by Haymitch Abernathy. Effie Trinket mentioned to Katniss Everdeen that she and her district partner ate like "savages". This upset Katniss greatly, as they came from the poorest region of District 12, where access to food was limited. In a show of defiance, Katniss then ate the rest of her meal with her fingers and wiped her hands on the tablecloth.[1]

Death[]
It's unknown how she died in the Games, but in an early draft of The Hunger Games film script, Claudius Templesmith said that "both tributes of District 12 [were] out" after the boy from her district died on the second day,[2] so it is likely she died on the first.
