Glenn Close is the American actress set to play Drusilla Sickle in the upcoming movie The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. Close has received eight Oscar nominations and is a three-time winner of the Emmys, Tonys, and Golden Globe Awards.[1] 
Producer Nina Jacobson stated that "Glenn Close is a dream Drusilla. She brings so much of her intellect and imagination to each role, creating characters who are unforgettable and iconic."[1]
