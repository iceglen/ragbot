Velo was one of two female tributes from District 6 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.[1] She was revealed to have died on the first day of the games.

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
During the Tribute Parade, the chariot carrying District 6 was crashed into by that of District 12 after the horses were startled and bolted. When they arrived at training, they were bandaged up in a lot of gauze.[2]
Before the start of the games, Velo joined an alliance between District 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 & 12 tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. She and the rest of her district initially hesitated but changed their minds when they were bullied by the Careers during training and Haymitch Abernathy showed them kindness.[3] The District 6 tributes were the sixth district that joined the alliance later named the Newcomers.
Velo died on the first day in the initial bloodbath, alongside Miles, a male tribute from her district.[4]

Physical description[]
Like the others in her district, she was "puny" with rickety limbs and looked like she'd never seen daylight.
Velo, along with the other three tributes from District 6, wore "iridescent gray coveralls with wheel-shaped crowns" during the chariot rides.[5] Haymitch nicknamed the District 6 tributes his "doves"[6]. He is particularly partial to these tributes due to their color reminding him of Lenore Dove.

Etymology[]
"Velo" is short for "velocity", which directly ties to speed and movement. "Vélo" is also French for "bicycle".
