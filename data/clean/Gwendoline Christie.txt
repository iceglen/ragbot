Gwendoline Christie is a British actress who portrayed Lyme in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2.

Career[]
Gwendoline Christie is most famously known for her role on the HBO TV series Game of Thrones, in which she portrays Brienne of Tarth. She also plays the part of Lexi in the TV series Wizards vs Aliens. She was casted to play as Captain Phasma in Star Wars Episode VII, which was released in December 2015.
