Kaitlyn Akinpelumi is a Nigerian-British actress who portrayed Domitia Whimsiwick in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Akinpelumi is known for The School For Good and Evil, which is set to release on Netflix in the fall of 2022. Akinpelumi kickstarted her acting career in film, landing her first-ever role in How To Talk To Girls At Parties from director John Cameron Mitchell, which made its world premiere at the 2017 Cannes Film Festival. Akinpelumi played Scouser Rachel Gibson in season three of Rowan Joffe's revenge crime thriller Tin Star. She also joined the cast of Olivier and the BAFTA-nominated CBBC period drama Hetty Feather, which is based on the novel by Jaqueline Wilson. 
Akinpelumi is represented by LINK Entertainment and Mark Jermin Management.[1]
