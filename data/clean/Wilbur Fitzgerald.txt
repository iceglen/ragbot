Wilbur Fitzgerald is an American actor who portrayed Cray in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire. 

Career[]
He played the role of Bruce Bennett on the television show Prison Break. Wilbur also played District Attorney Darnelle on In the Heat of the Night. While on the series, he joined fellow cast members in recording songs Jingle Bells and Christmas Time's A Comin' for the 1991 In the Heat of the Night Christmas CD. The CD was produced by co-stars Randall Franks and Alan Autry.
