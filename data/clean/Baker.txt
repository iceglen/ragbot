Purpose[]
Bakers sold their wares to other people in the district for regular consumption. However, people in Panem's poorest areas were often unable to afford to buy baked goods, so they made their own from tessera grain.[1] Specialty items like cakes could be purchased for special occasions, if one could afford them. Bakers were also aware of, and may have also made, different types of bread native to other districts.[2]
Despite the expense of bakery goods, people could sometimes acquire them if the baker accepted a trade in place of monetary payment, like Mr. Mellark often did with Katniss Everdeen and Gale Hawthorne in District 12.[1]
