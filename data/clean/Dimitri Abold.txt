Dimitri Abold is a Jamaican-born German actor who portrayed Reaper Ash in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Abold graduated from New York University's Tisch School of the Arts. In Germany, he has worked with renowned film directors, including Marcus H. Rosenmüller and Dominik Graf. He is also playing the lead role in the upcoming German Amazon series Love Addicts.
Internationally, Abold is best known for his supporting role in the Netflix series Warrior Nun and will appear in an episode of the Amazon Original series Tom Clancy's Jack Ryan. He is represented by Baumbauer Actors.[1]

Trivia[]
In The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, during the scene where the tributes met with their mentors, Abold adlibbed Reaper fake-lunging at his mentor Clemensia Dovecote. He ran this by director Francis Lawrence first, but Clemensia's actress Ashley Liao was not told ahead of time.[2]
