Biography[]
Josie Hope is the youngest of three sisters. They are all members of The Bennett Hall Band, which was named after their brother who passed away in 2010.[2]

Trivia[]
In addition to singing, Josie Hope can play the drums, ukelele, and tambourine.[2]
Josie Hope is not named in the film's credits, but she is credited for singing "Keep On The Sunny Side" on The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (Music From & Inspired By).[1]
