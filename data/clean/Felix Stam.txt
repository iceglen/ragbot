Felix Stam is a herdsman from District 10 who was selected to become a District Hero.[1]

Biography[]
Felix was selected to be a District Hero due to his quiet understanding of the ways of animals and the circle of life that unites us all.[2] He initially rejected the "standard contingency fee", but "quickly reconsidered" after he was "presented the alternative of reduced tesserae."

Physical description[]
Felix has fair hair, blue eyes and a beard. In his District Hero portrait, he has a septum noise piercing, smokes a pipe, and wears shaggy furs.[2]

Etymology[]
Felix is a male given name that stems from Latin (fēlix, felicis) and means "happy" or "lucky".

Trivia[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Photo spread of Felix from Tim Palen's Photographs from the Hunger Games. 		 	 
On June 19th, 2014, Capitol Couture released seven portraits of District Heroes as advertisements for The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1. These were original characters, independent from the books or films, serving as pro-Capitol propaganda for each of their respective districts.[1]
Felix is the second named character from District 10, after Dalton.[3]  	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Egeria's letter to Coriolanus Snow about Felix 		 	 
In Egeria's letters, it says that Felix is 36, but according to the District Heroes page, he is 35. Either way, he is the oldest known District Hero.
According to the letters, Felix was threatened with reduced rations to make him agree to be District Hero. Contrary to Capitol propaganda, he was selected by powerful figures in the Capitol rather than his family and friends.
