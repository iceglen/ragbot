Mr. McCoy is the father of Louella McCoy.

Biography[]
Mr. McCoy lived three houses down from Haymitch Abernathy in the Seam[1] with his wife and several children, which included Ima, Louella, and Cayson McCoy.
When Louella was born, Mr. McCoy proudly held her up to the window for the other kids to see.[2]
