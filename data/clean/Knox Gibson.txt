Knox Gibson is an Australian actor and athlete who portrayed Bobbin in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Gibson, who is an amputee, is a national athlete for swimming and is a disability and inclusion advocate. His breakout role was starring as the lead in the Netflix short film Forgive Us Our Trespasses, and he is represented by C Talent.[1]
