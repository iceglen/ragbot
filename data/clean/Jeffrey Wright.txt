Jeffrey Wright is an American actor who portrayed Beetee in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, and The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1 and 2.

Career[]
Before Wright started his career in the film business, he was seen in the theaters. In 1990, he got his first film role in the movie Presumed Innocent. Later, he appeared in movies such as Extremely Loud & Incredibly Close, The Ides of March, Source Code, and W., but is best known for playing CIA agent Felix Leiter in the James Bond films Casino Royale, Quantum of Solace, and No Time to Die. Wright also appeared in the TV series House and Boardwalk Empire.
