Robert Knepper is an American actor who portrayed Antonius in both The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1 and The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2.

Career[]
Knepper never intended to work in film and television projects; however, he began his television and film career in 1986 with The Paper Chase and That's Life!. He has made appearances on well known television series such as Star Trek: The Next Generation, ER and Law & Order. Robert has also starred in the films R.I.P.D and Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters, as well as the well known television series Prison Break, where he portrayed Theodore "T-Bag" Bagswell.

Trivia[]
Knepper came into the role of Antonius because he was the neighbor of Philip Messina, The Hunger Games franchise's production designer. When Knepper asked Messina if there were any roles for him in the upcoming Mockingjay films, Messina replied that he would ask producer Nina Jacobson. According to Knepper, she said, "Yeah, he looks like he could play a bad guy."[1]
