The Snows' apartment building was a residential building located on the Corso, a major street in the Capitol of Panem.[1]

Description[]
The apartment building had a glass door from the lobby opening out onto the street. It also had an elevator with mirrored walls; however, said elevator had broken early into the Dark Days and would not be repaired for over ten years.[2]

Penthouse[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			The Grandma'am, Coriolanus, and Tigris in their apartment. 		 	 
The Snow family lived in the building's penthouse, twelve flights of stairs up. They also had access to the roof, where the Grandma'am tended her rose garden.[1] The apartment itself had twenty-foot-high walls and arched windows, though the rebels' wartime bombings had caused considerable damage throughout. Financial hardship forced the Snows to sell or trade many of their possessions, or even burn them to keep warm in the last winter of the war. As a result, some rooms were vacant, sparsely furnished, or closed off altogether.[1]
The penthouse was once occupied by Crassus Snow, his wife, and their son Coriolanus Snow, alongside the Grandma'am, the Snows' cook, and a maid. After the war, the Grandma'am raised Coriolanus and his cousin Tigris there by herself.[1]
On the morning of the reaping for the 10th Hunger Games, Tigris pried tesserae from inside a cabinet in the maid's bathroom to use as buttons on a dress shirt for Coriolanus.[1]

Sub penthouse[]
Remus Dolittle and his family lived on the floor directly below the Snows.[3] However, the introduction of property taxes in the Capitol[1] meant that the Dolittles could no longer afford to live there.[2]
Strabo Plinth ended up buying the sub penthouse, and he and his wife had moved in by October. He also bought out the Snows' apartment for them, since they could not afford to keep it on their own. There was talk of putting in a spiral staircase and a private elevator between the two families' floors.[4]

Trivia[]
Arachne Crane's apartment faced the Snows'. Coriolanus claimed that even from across the Corso, he could sometimes hear her "bellowing" at night.[5]
Nero Price and his daughter Persephone lived in a town house on the Corso. Coriolanus counted them among his neighbors.[6]
The Snows' apartment was the only set built for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. It was designed to look like a snowflake when viewed from above.[7]
On November 13th, 2023, Instagram posted a tour of the set by actress Hunter Schafer.[8]
In the film, the Snows' address on their eviction notice was Road of Hope 25, Capitol, Panem.[9]
Fans have speculated that this building became the Training Center due to its location on the Corso, the number of floors, and its rooftop garden.
