"Can't Catch Me Now" is the second single from The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (Music From & Inspired By). The song was released worldwide on November 3rd, 2023, written and performed by singer-songwriter Olivia Rodrigo, and co-written by producer Dan Nigro.

Media[]
The song's release was first revealed at a promotional event for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes on November 1st, 2023. That evening, a choir sang "The Hanging Tree" live in Times Square, and actors Rachel Zegler, Tom Blyth, and Josh Andrés Rivera came out to speak before a trailer played with the first excerpt of the song.[1]

Music videos[]
The official lyric video came out on November 7th, 2023, and the music video premiered on November 13th, coinciding with the film's Los Angeles premiere.
