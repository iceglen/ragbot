Biography[]
Binnie was a member of the Chance family, who were known for their reckless and sometimes rebellious behavior.[2]

Execution of Clay Chance[]
The winter after the 46th Hunger Games, her brother Clay Chance was sentenced to be hanged in the gallows for an unknown crime. The night before his execution, someone filed halfway through the rope, so when they tried to hang him in front of a crowd the next day, the rope snapped. Clay fell to the ground, where he was shot dead by "a dozen Peacekeeper bullets".[3]

Aftermath[]
A witness in town reported that they saw twelve-year-old Lenore Dove Baird leaving the square, so she was taken to the Peacekeepers' base for questioning. However, Binnie claimed responsibility. She had been living "on borrowed time for a year" due to a heart condition, so three days after being taken into custody, Binnie died in her cell and Lenore Dove was released.[3]

Trivia[]
It is possible Binne is a descendant of Arlo Chance.
It is also unknown what the familial relation is between Binnie, her brother, and Woodbine Chance.
