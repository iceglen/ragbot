Maya Hawke is the American actress, model, and singer set to play young Wiress in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.[1]

Career[]
Hawke's debut role was Jo March in the 2017 BBC adaptation of Little Women. She stars as Robin Buckley in the Netflix TV series, Stranger Things in season three. She is also known for Do Revenge, Inside Out 2, Fear Street: Part One - 1994, and Asteroid City.[2]

Trivia[]
Maya Hawke attended Juilliard School for a little bit and met Tom Blyth, who would later be cast as young Coriolanus Snow in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes at auditions for Juilliard.[3]
