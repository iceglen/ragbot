Career[]
Schafer's debut came when she portrayed Jules in the HBO series Euphoria. She also co-wrote and co-executive produced a special episode for the series, which aired in January 2021. Later that year, Time named her to its Next list of 100 emerging leaders who are shaping the future. Schafer is currently in production on the Neon film Cuckoo from director Tilman Singer. She is represented by CAA, Untitled Entertainment, and Sloane, Offer, Weber & Dern.[1]
