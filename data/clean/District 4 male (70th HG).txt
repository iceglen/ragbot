The District 4 male was a tribute from District 4 who participated in the 70th Hunger Games. He was beheaded, which led to Annie Cresta going mad.

Biography[]
He lived in District 4 until he either volunteered or was reaped for the 70th Hunger Games. His district partner was Annie Cresta.[1]

Death[]
At some point during the Games, he was beheaded in front of Annie, who then "ran off by herself and hid". She survived to become the victor, though not without significant, long-lasting trauma.[1] It is unclear whether he was decapitated by another tribute, a muttation, or a Gamemaker trap.

Trivia[]
He is one of two characters known to be beheaded in the Games - the other being Wellie in the 50th Hunger Games.
However, many characters were decapitated by lizard mutts during the Battle of the Capitol, including Peacekeepers and various members of Squad 451. Finnick Odair, Annie's husband, was one of the mutts' victims.[2]
