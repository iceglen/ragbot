Mackenzie Lansing is an actress who portrayed Coral in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Lansing is a bilingual French-American actress best known for her work on HBO's The Deuce and Mare of Easttown. She was seen in Allswell, debuting at Tribeca, and filmed a role for Gareth Edwards' True Love with John David Washington. Lansing studied at the American Academy of Dramatic Arts, and rounded out her conservatory education by attending The Labyrinth Summer Intensive and Maggie Flanigan Studio. Her transition into film and television began with guest spots on Red Oaks and Tell Me a Story. She is represented by Clear Talent Group and MJ Management,[1] and is based in New York City and Los Angeles.[2]
