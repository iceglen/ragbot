Naida Dolan is a fisherwoman from District 4 who was chosen to become a District Hero.[1]

Biography[]
Naida is the daughter of a deep-sea fisherman and a sixth-generation pearl diver, chosen to become a District Hero because of her skill in fishing.[1]

Physical description[]
Naida is tall with brown eyes and has long dark wavy hair, slightly streaked with brown. In her District Hero portrait, she has pearl earrings and wears a long brown dress made out of nets.[1]

Etymology[]
Naida's given name likely comes from the naiads, a type of water nymph from Greek mythology that presides over fresh water. Dolan is an Irish surname, anglicized from Ó Dubhláin, which combines the Irish elements "dubh" (black) and "slán" (challenge).

Trivia[]
On June 19th, 2014, Capitol Couture released seven portraits of District Heroes as advertisements for The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1. These were original characters, independent from the books or films, serving as pro-Capitol propaganda for each of their respective districts.[2]
