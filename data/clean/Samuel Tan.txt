Samuel Tan is an American stunt actor specifically cast to portray a Tribute that is mentioned only once and fell during the initial bloodbath. He portrayed the District 8 male in The Hunger Games film. [1] He also appeared in the "Rocketeer" music video by the band Far East Movement. Tan is known for his work on Mano Po (2002) and Mano Po 6: A Mother's Love (2009). Of late, Sam hasn't been very active, but he is still good friends with the actor who played the District 6 male, Ashton Moio.
