The 45th Hunger Games took place thirty years before the events of Catching Fire. These Games were won by Chaff, the male tribute from District 11.[1]

Known tributes[]
Chaff - District 11 (victor)
The Games[]
Sometime during his Games, Chaff lost his hand. Katniss Everdeen thought that the Capitol probably offered him a prosthesis like Peeta Mellark's leg, but he must have refused it.[1]

Aftermath[]
Chaff seemed to depend on alcohol to cope in the aftermath of his Games. He could often be seen passing a bottle back and forth with Haymitch Abernathy over the years.[1]
