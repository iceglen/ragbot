Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Janus was part of the Career pack in these games. He died on the fourth day during a volcanic eruption.[2]

Physical description[]
District 2's tributes wore purple training outfits.[3]

Etymology[]
Janus was a Roman god associated with gates, duality, doors, transitions, and passageways.

Trivia[]
Janus is only named on the tribute board.
