Burn Gorman is an American-born English actor and musician who portrayed Commander Hoff in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Burn Gorman was born on September 1, 1974 in Hollywood, Los Angeles, California, USA. He is an actor, known for Pinocchio (2022), The Dark Knight Rises (2012),  Pacific Rim: Uprising (2018) and Torchwood (2006-8). He married to Sarah Beard in July 2004, but they divorced in 2017.
