Aamer Husain is an actor who portrayed Felix Ravinstill in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Husain attended the University of Texas and speaks fluent English, Urdu, and Arabic. He is also skilled in gymnastics.[2]
Husain appeared on Hudson & Rex and is represented by The Characters Agency & David Dean Management.[1]
