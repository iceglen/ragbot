Luna Kuse is an actress who portrayed Brandy in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[2]

Career[]
Kuse played a recurring role in Schloss Einstein, a German television show. After fives seasons, she left the show and appeared in movies such as Vampirates and Himbeeren mit Senf. Kuse will also join the cast of a new teen comedy show. She is represented by Rietz Casting & Agentur.[2]
