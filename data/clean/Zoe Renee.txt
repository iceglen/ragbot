Zoe Renee is an actress who portrayed Lysistrata Vickers in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Renee starred opposite Regina Hall in the Amazon Studios thriller Master, which premiered at the 2022 Sundance Film Festival. She was also seen in Disney+'s high school basketball story Chang Can Dunk in 2023. Renee is best known for her breakout role in the critically acclaimed festival feature Jinn, where she played Summer. She followed Jinn with the motion picture indie Gully opposite Kelvin Harrison Jr., Amber Heard, and Terrance Howard. 
She is represented by PANTHEON, Elevate Entertainment, and Meyer & Downs.[1]
