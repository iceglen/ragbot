Isobel Jesper Jones is an actress who portrayed Mayfair Lipp in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Jones starred as Jessica, one of the leading roles in the Netflix series The Bastard Son & the Devil Himself from The Imaginarium, with director Colm McCarthy. She is represented by Gabriella Capisani at United Agents.[1]
