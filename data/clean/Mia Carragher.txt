Mia Carragher is an English actress who plays Katniss Everdeen in The Hunger Games On Stage.[1] She is the daughter of former Liverpool player Jamie Carragher.

Career[]
Carragher graduated from the Tring Park School for the Performing Arts and pursued acting at the Lee Strasberg Theatre and Film Institute in New York. Her first film was One Night in Istanbul (2014). She also appeared in the Channel 4 series The Gathering (2024) and performed alongside Josh Hutcherson in the film Let's Love.[1]
