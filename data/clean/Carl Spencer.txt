Carl Spencer is an actor known for Rocketman (2019), and Matilda (2022), and The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, in which he portrayed Smiley.[1]

Career[]
He was cast as Richard in Rocketman (2019) and Magnus the Escapologist in Matilda (2022). On September 16, 2022, he was announced to be cast as Smiley in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes by a Deadline article and his Instagram account.[2] [3]
He stars in the music video for Shan Ako's God's Best and plays Phoenix in Doctors (2019).[1]
