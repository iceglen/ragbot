Karan Kendrick is an American actress who portrayed Atala in The Hunger Games film. 

Career[]
Karan has acted in many movies and television shows. Some of her most famous film work includes Parental Guidance, The Odd Life of Timothy Green and Little Red Wagon (2012). She has also had many television roles, including The Wronged Man, Fab Five: The Texas Cheerleading Scandal, Drop Dead Diva, Army Wives and Tyler Perry's House of Payne.
