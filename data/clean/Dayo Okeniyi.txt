Dayo Okeniyi is a Nigerian actor who portrayed Thresh in The Hunger Games film, his major motion picture debut.[1] In 2013, he appeared in The Spectacular Now.

Trivia[]
His father is Nigerian and his mother is Kenyan.
Dayo earned his bachelor's degree at Anderson University in Anderson, IN.
