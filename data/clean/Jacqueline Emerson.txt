Jacqueline Emerson is an American actress and singer who portrayed Foxface, the female tribute from District 5, in The Hunger Games film. [1] She also had a role in the thriller movie The Curse of Downers Grove, which was released in 2015.
Emerson made her singing debut with two songs, "Peter Pan" and "Catch Me if You Can".

Trivia[]
At the Los Angeles premiere of The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes on November 13, 2023, Jacqueline was asked to share her opinion on a popular fan theory: that Foxface had recognized the nightlock berries as poisonous and intentionally ate them, dying by suicide. According to Jacqueline, director Gary Ross said her character's death was accidental; however, she personally believed it was suicide because the film showed the character quizzing herself on plants.[2]
