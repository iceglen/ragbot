Horace was a ten-year-old boy who gave Lucy Gray Baird a potato while she was in the Capitol Zoo.

Biography[]
At some point, Horace heard of the tributes from the 10th Hunger Games and realized that he wanted to help feed them. He saved a boiled potato from dinner, then went to the zoo with his father, hovering in the crowd until Coriolanus Snow approached him. When Lucy Gray arrived, Horace held the potato out to her and introduced himself. Lucy Gray complimented it and thanked him.[1]

Etymology[]
Horace was the leading Roman poet during the reign of Augustus.
