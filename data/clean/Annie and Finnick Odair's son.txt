Annie and Finnick Odair's son is the child of Annie Cresta and Finnick Odair.[1]

Biography[]
Sometime between Annie's rescue from the Capitol[2] and Finnick's deployment,[3] the couple conceived a child together in District 13.[1] Finnick died in the Battle of the Capitol,[4] but Annie survived and gave birth to their son after the war ended. She sent a photo of him to Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark, who preserved it in their memory book.[1]

Trivia[]
Following the time skip in the epilogue, he would be around 15 years old.[5]
