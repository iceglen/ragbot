Rue's aunt[1] was a citizen of District 11. 

Biography[]
Rue's aunt attended the Victory Tour for the 74th Hunger Games, along with her nephew and four nieces. They stood at stage with a monitor displaying Rue's image. During Katniss' speech she became emotional and started to cry, holding her arms around her nephew and one of her nieces.

Trivia[]
Rue's aunt does not appear in the novel. Instead, Rue's mother and father attend the Victory Tour with their children.[2]
In the script Rue's mother is at the Victory Tour, rather than her aunt.[3]
