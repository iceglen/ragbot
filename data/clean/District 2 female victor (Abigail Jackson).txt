Biography[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Behind the scenes photo of the District 2 reaping. 		 	 
Years after her initial victory, President Coriolanus Snow announced that tributes for the third Quarter Quell would be reaped from the existing pool of victors, forcing them to compete in the Games regardless of age. As a result, this victor was present at District 2's reaping for the 75th Hunger Games. It is unknown whether or not she was reaped for the event, but she did not volunteer, and Enobaria became the district's female tribute.[2]

Death[]
At the end of the Second Rebellion, she was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so she presumably died in the Victors' Purge.[3]

Trivia[]
She did not appear directly in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, but she could be seen behind Enobaria in a behind the scenes photo on Meta Golding's Instagram.[4]
