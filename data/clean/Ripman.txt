Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Before the start of the Games, Ripman joined the Newcomers alliance between District 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 & 12 tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Ripman and his district partners were considered "nothing special" by Haymitch Abernathy.[2]
He died in the initial bloodbath with the rest of his district.[3]

Physical description[]
The tributes from District 8 wore peach-colored outfits during the chariot rides and training.[4]

Etymology[]
Suggesting ripping or fabric tearing.

Trivia[]
Ripman, along with Alpheus, Janus, Nona, Lect, Coil, Bircher, Heartwood, Ryan, Clayton, Midge, and the rest of his district and District 5, is only named on the tribute board.
