Cassia was a member of the camera crew stationed in District 12 during the reaping for the 50th Hunger Games.

Biography[]
After the death of Woodbine Chance, Lenore Dove Baird tried to console his grieving mother. She tried to convince the Peacekeepers to let her see her son's body for a little while longer, but they wouldn't budge, and she kept insisting. Haymitch Abernathy came over to try and help the situation, so Drusilla Sickle chooses him to replace Woodbine. Plutarch told Cassia to record Lenore Dove's weeping reaction.[1]
