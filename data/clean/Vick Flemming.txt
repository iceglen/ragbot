Vick Flemming was the first governor of District 2. He appeared on the 20 Panar bill— prop currency used in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Physical description[]
Flemming was an older white man.[1]

Trivia[]
It is unknown what precise functions a district's governor had in Panem. It is also unclear how governors were distinct from mayors, and if they came from the district they served, or if they were Capitol citizens appointed as overseers.
It is possible that certain districts require governors due to their greater population size.
It is also possible that governors held the same responsibilities as mayors and that the former is an archaic term.
He shares his first name with Vick Hawthorne.
