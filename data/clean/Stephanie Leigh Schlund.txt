Stephanie Leigh Schlund is an American actress who portrayed Cashmere in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire[1].

Career[]
Schlund is best known for her role in The Last Song, alongside Miley Cyrus and fellow cast member Liam Hemsworth, and her roles in the television shows Drop Dead Diva, and The Following. She has also appeared in Stomp The Yard and was a model on The Price is Right before becoming an actress.
In a behind the scenes featurette, Gloss's actor Alan Ritchson mentioned that he and Schlund had quickly developed a "brotherly-sisterly kind of love" while filming. They messed around with each other on set, which created the sense of a real sibling relationship.[2]
