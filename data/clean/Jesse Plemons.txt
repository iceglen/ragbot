Jesse Plemons is the American actor set to play a young Plutarch Heavensbee in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.[1]

Trivia[]
Jesse Plemons and the late Philip Seymour Hoffman (who played Plutarch Heavensbee in the original film trilogy) co-starred in the movie "The Master" (2012). Jesse played Philip Seymour Hoffman's son in the movie.
