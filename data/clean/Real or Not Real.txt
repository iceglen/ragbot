"Real or Not Real" was a game created and played by members of Squad 451, most notably Peeta Mellark and Katniss Everdeen.[1]

History[]
Background[]
Peeta Mellark was captured by Capitol forces in the immediate aftermath of the 75th Hunger Games.[2] While imprisoned, he was subjected to a highly secretive form of torture known as hijacking, which used tracker jacker venom to modify his memories of Katniss. These changes were designed to make her appear more threatening, prompting him to attack her on sight.[3] Peeta made some progress in his recovery, but despite his continued instability, he was sent to join Squad 451 during the Battle of the Capitol.[4] 

Creation[]
The morning after Peeta's arrival, Jackson devised the game "Real or Not Real" to help him sort out his real memories from the false ones. Peeta would mention something he thought had happened, and his fellow soldiers would tell him if it was real or not real, with an explanation as necessary. The game was also based off the word of Finnick Odair; he was the first to suggest that Peeta ask when he was uncertain of reality, since that was what Annie Cresta did.[1]

Trivia[]
Although the game started out as a coping skill for Peeta, Katniss also asked Peeta her own Real or Not Real question once.[8] She later quoted the game in her internal monologue directly after the City Circle bombing: "Real or not real? I am on fire."[14]
Out of all the known rounds of the game, only one statement was ever answered with "not real".[1]
