Ashley Liao is an actress who portrayed Clemensia Dovecote in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Liao played the lead role in the ACE Entertainment feature Loveboat Taipei. She played the teenage version of Ali Wong in the Netflix romantic comedy Always Be My Maybe, and starred as Princess Eleanor in the Disney+ original movie Secret Society of Second Born Royals. She also had a leading role in the Debby Ryan-produced film Jessica Darling’s It List. She had a series regular role in the Apple TV+ series Physical opposite Rose Byrne. Liao is best known for her role as Lola in the Netflix series Fuller House, and has also guest starred on many series. She is represented by Innovative Artists, Semler Entertainment, and Skrzyniarz & Mallean.[1]

Trivia[]
In The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, actor Dimitri Abold adlibbed Reaper Ash fake-lunging at Clemensia during the tribute-mentor meeting. He ran this by director Francis Lawrence first, but Liao was not told ahead of time.[2]
