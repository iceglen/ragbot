The 38th Hunger Games took place thirty-six years before the start of the trilogy. They were won by Porter Millicent Tripp, the female tribute from District 5.[1]

The Games[]
Porter sustained a spinal injury in the final showdown on the last day of her Games.[1]

Aftermath[]
Due to her injury, Porter had to wear a halo brace throughout her Victory Tour.[1]

Trivia[]
Porter and her Games were not mentioned in the original trilogy, but revealed by Capitol Couture, a Hunger Games website promoting the movies.[1]
