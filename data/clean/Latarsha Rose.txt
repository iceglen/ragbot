Latarsha Rose is an American actress who portrayed Portia, Peeta Mellark's stylist, in the The Hunger Games film, which was her feature film debut.[1] Rose was going to quit acting until she was called for an opportunity to audition for the role.[2] Rose announced via Twitter that she would not return for The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, and that audiences would have to wait until Mockingjay to find out what happened to Portia.
