Imanol Yepez-Frias is primarily a theatre actor who portrayed the District 9 male tribute in The Hunger Games film, his major motion picture debut.[1] Imanol is also an expert pianist and has been playing since he was 6.
However, the actual fight scene his character is known for is doubled by Sam Ly. The roles of the tributes that fell during the initial bloodbath are mostly filled by stunt actors.
