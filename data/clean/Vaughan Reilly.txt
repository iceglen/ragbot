Vaughan Reilly is a New York native actress and singer who portrayed Maude Ivory Baird in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Reilly hails from four generations of circus performers. She made her small screen debut co-starring opposite Natasha Lyonne in Season 2 of Russian Doll when she was nine years old. Reilly works in animation and voiced characters for two episodes of Teenage Euthanasia in 2023, directed by Scott Adsit. She is represented by Stewart Talent.[1]
