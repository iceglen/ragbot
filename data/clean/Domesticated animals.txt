Other districts[]
District 13[]
Cut off from the rest of the districts, District 13 had to become self-sufficient, building its own underground farms to sustain its population. In addition to growing crops, they raised chickens.[12] They also had milk;[13] since they lacked the space for cows, sheep, or horses to graze, and since Katniss Everdeen did not comment on the taste being any different than she was used to, it was likely goat's milk.

Trivia[]
Throughout the series, there have been two cats (Buttercup and Boa), two named goats (Lady and Shamus), and two pet birds (Maysilee's canary and Jubilee).
