Wes Bentley is an American actor. He portrayed Head Gamemaker Seneca Crane in the The Hunger Games film.[1]
Bentley was born in Jonesboro, Arkansas to Cherie Baker and David Bentley. He attended the Julliard School's Drama Division, but quit after his first year. He started acting in 1995.

Career[]
Bentley has starred in several movies, including the Oscar-winning movie American Beauty, The Four Feathers, P2 and Ghost Rider. He also appeared in the fourth and fifth seasons of American Horror Story, playing the role of Edward Mordrake in the two-part Halloween special of Freak Show and the lead role of Detective John Lowe in Hotel. Additionally, Bentley starred in the 2014 film Interstellar.
