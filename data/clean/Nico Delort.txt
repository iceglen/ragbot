Nico Delort is a Canadian and French illustrator who drew the art for illustrated editions of The Hunger Games and Catching Fire. They work out of Paris, France.[1]

Career[]
Delort's body of work includes black-and-white scratchboard illustrations for books, magazines, movie posters, and galleries.[1]
