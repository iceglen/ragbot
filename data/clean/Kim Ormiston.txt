Kim Ormiston is an American actress who portrayed Leeg 2 in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2. 

Career[]
Kim is a model who mainly does shoots along with her twin sister, Misty Ormiston. One of her most famous shoots was in 2003, where they were featured in an issue of Maxim.
Kim has played a few small roles on such TV shows as Treme and New Orleans Pie. She has also appeared in the TV movie, Scream of the Banshee, and also the short movie, Theraphobia.
