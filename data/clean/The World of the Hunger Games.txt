The World of the Hunger Games is the fourth and final of the official tie-in books to The Hunger Games.[1] It is the second of the tie-ins by Kate Egan and is hardback, unlike the other tie-in books. The World of the Hunger Games was released on March 23, 2012, the same day as the film. It is primarily a visual chronicle of the film, covering basic information on the Hunger Games universe with quotes by Suzanne Collins that provide additional insight.[2]

Description[]
Welcome to Panem, the world of The Hunger Games. This is the definitive, richly illustrated, full-color guide to all the districts of Panem, all the participants in The Hunger Games, and the life and home of Katniss Everdeen.[3]
