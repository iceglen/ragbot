Rue's sister is a citizen of District 11 and is one of Rue's five younger siblings. She appeared at the Victory Tour with the rest of her family. Katniss Everdeen thought she looked about nine years old, and she resembled Rue very closely.[1]

Biography[]
Rue's sister was present during Katniss and Peeta's Victory Tour after the 74th Hunger Games. Despite their allotted time for speaking having run out, her look told Katniss that she needed to say something to the people of District 11 about her sister[1], Rue, who died when Marvel threw a spear into her stomach[2][3]. Rue had been of great assistance to Katniss[4][5][6][7] and a great friend to her[1], as she reminded Katniss of her sister, Prim[8][1]. When Rue died, Katniss covered her fatal wound with flowers she picked, and gave the people of District 11 the farewell gesture that they used back in District 12[3].
Rue's sister's expression made it clear to Katniss that if her sister and her district partner, Thresh, weren't thanked, they would not be forgiven. Because of this, Katniss gave a long and heartbreaking speech to the people of District 11, saying that although she didn't know Thresh well, he had saved her from Clove and spared her life. Next, she described how much Rue meant to her, stating that she wouldn't be forgotten. That speech is what inspired District 11 to start an uprising.[1]
Before the 75th Hunger Games, Seeder, a previous victor from District 11, told Katniss that the families of Rue and Thresh were alive.[9] However, Rue's sister was never mentioned after this and her fate is thus unknown.
