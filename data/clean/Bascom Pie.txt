Bascom Pie was a man who lived in District 12.[1]

Biography[]
Bascom Pie was a bootlegger in District 12 who sold rotgut to anyone who wanted it, even if its sale was not conscientious. Among his customers were the reckless Woodbine Chance and his family.[1]
After running out of sleep syrup to help him cope with the death of his mother, brother, and girlfriend, Haymitch Abernathy began to buy sacks of rotgut from Bascom Pie in desperation.[2]
