Scott Folan is an actor who portrayed Junius, better known as Beanpole, in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Folan's credits include the Comedy Central sitcom Brotherhood and the feature film Nativity 2. He is also a singer-songwriter whose music is available to stream on all major music platforms. He is represented by Denton Brierley and Unbreakable Entertainment.[1]
