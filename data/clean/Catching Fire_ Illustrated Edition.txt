Catching Fire: Illustrated Edition is an edition of Catching Fire illustrated by artist Nico Delort. It released on October 7, 2025.
Like The Hunger Games: Illustrated Edition, it contains over 30 black-and-white scratchboard drawings, with each part opener integrating a different plant.
