The first Mellark son was the first child of Mr. and Mrs. Mellark. He was also the older brother of an unnamed boy and Peeta Mellark.

Biography[]
The Mellarks' first son was born in District 12. He was never reaped for the Hunger Games. According to Katniss Everdeen, he was often at the Mellark bakery and was older than 18 when Peeta was reaped for the 74th Hunger Games; he would have been able to volunteer if he were younger.[1]
When District 12 was bombed by the Capitol, he was killed along with the rest of his family, excluding Peeta.[2]
