Corrina Blanken was a resident of District 12 whose son, Sage, participated in the 73rd Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
Corrina was only mentioned in an early version of a script for The Hunger Games film. She and her husband Tygart Blanken lived in the Seam, and their son was reaped for the 73rd Hunger Games. The District 3 male killed him with a bar mace on the second day as Corrina watched on a big screen in the town square, and she wailed in grief before she was led away by her neighbors.[1]
Later that day, Katniss Everdeen came across the Blankens at the Hob: Tygart was "in a fog" as he tried to sell his wares, and Corrina was "catatonic". Katniss gave Tygart two purple berries she had picked earlier, explaining that they would help Corrina sleep, and that her mother took them.[1]
After Katniss and Peeta Mellark won the 74th Hunger Games, Corrina was mentioned among the 8,000 citizens celebrating in the town square. She was also at the train station when they returned to District 12.[1]
