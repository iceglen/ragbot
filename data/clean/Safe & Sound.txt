"Safe & Sound" is the first single released from The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond. The song is performed and written by Taylor Swift and features The Civil Wars. This is one of two songs by Taylor Swift that were written for the film; the other song being "Eyes Open." Although the song was written for the film, it isn't featured in the film, but in the credits.
Taylor has since re-recorded many of her songs that were originally written and published prior to 2018, "Safe & Sound" and "Eyes Open" included. Both songs, now bearing the title of "(Taylor's Version)," were released at midnight on March 17th 2023 after being teased by Swift the previous day.

By January 2014, "Safe & Sound" has sold 1,628,000 copies in the American territory according to RIAA, being certified Platinum.

Music video[]
The music video for Safe & Sound, which features the Civil Wars, premiered on Monday, February 13, 2012, during "MTV First:Taylor Swift". The video features Swift walking barefoot through the woods, wearing a long white gown. In between scenes The Civil Wars are singing in front of a fireplace, as they accompany Swift. At one point in the video, Swift is shown holding a mockingjay pin.
