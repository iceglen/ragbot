Jeremy Marinas portrayed the District 10 male tribute in The Hunger Games film. He also portrayed an extra bloodbath death in the 74th hunger games. [1] 

Biography[]
Marinas is primarily a stunt actor and performer. Along with fellow tribute actor Tara Macken, he was on the web series The Legion of Extraordinary Dancers. Marinas is a very successful stunt double, having appeared in Captain America: Civil War, The Green Hornet, After Earth, The Tomb, and several other films. He is of Filipino descent and lives in California.
