Scrips were a form of coin used in the Capitol store in District 12. Although they were only officially valid in the Capitol store, they were accepted by many of the town merchants, as well as Mrs. Abernathy for her laundry business. However, the Donner family would not accept scrips. It was distinct from cash. 

History[]
When Haymitch Abernathy traded his bottle of white liquor for cash with Asterid March, he was grateful that she gave him cash and not scrips, because he was going to the Donner store next, and the Donners were one of the merchant families that did not accept scrips.[1]
When Louella McCoy saw Wyatt Callow fiddling with a scrip coin, she realized that he was one of the Booker Boys. That night, she told Haymitch that she didn't want Wyatt to be one of their allies.[2] During training, Maysilee Donner wove a zinc scrip coin into Wyatt's token, a cord. She designed it so it would be easy to slip the coin out, since flipping it through his fingers helped Wyatt process things.[3] Haymitch carried Wyatt's scrip coin with him in the 50th Hunger Games after his death in the Cornucopia bloodbath,[4] having received it from Ampert, who recovered the coin after Wyatt's death.[5]
