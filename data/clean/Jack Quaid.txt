Jack Quaid is an American actor who portrayed Marvel in The Hunger Games film, his feature film debut.[1] He is the son of actors Dennis Quaid and Meg Ryan and the nephew of actor Randy Quaid.
Quaid attends New York University, Tisch School of the Arts, Experimental Theatre Wing, and is a member of the sketch comedy group Hammerkatz.[2] In past years, he has also starred in many comedy videos that he and his peers from college have posted on YouTube.
Outside of The Hunger Games, he is most well known for his role as Hughie Campbell in Amazon Prime's TV series The Boys.
In 2020, he took on the voice role of Brad Boimler in Star Trek: Lower Decks.
