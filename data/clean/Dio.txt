Dio was one of two female tributes from District 3 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Before the start of the Games, Dio joined Ampert's Newcomers alliance between District 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 & 12 tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack.  She was interested in Wyatt's system of odds.[2] She was the first in her district to be interviewed by Caesar Flickerman where she revealed the news of the Newcomers alliance to the public for the first time, and outlined everyone who joined and their skills.[3]

Death[]
Dio died on the fourth day with the rest of District 3's tributes after a volcanic eruption.[4]

Etymology[]
"Dio" is a shortening of diode, an electrical component that allows the flow of current in only one direction.
