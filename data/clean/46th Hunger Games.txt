The 46th Hunger Games took place 4 years prior to Sunrise on the Reaping and 28 years before the start of the trilogy. The victor this year was Palladium Barker, a tribute from District 1.[1]

Summary[]
Reaping[]
During the reaping for the 46th Hunger Games in District 12, the Peacekeepers pulled a kerosene-soaked, burning, smoking flag of Panem from under the temporary stage. Lenore Dove Baird was arrested for the crime, but later let go, even though she was secretly guilty.[2]

Known tributes[]
Palladium Barker - District 1 (victor)
The Games[]
Palladium likely formed a typical alliance with other Career Tributes, since they were from one of the Career districts.

Trivia[]
Palladium was related to Panache Barker, one of the two male District 1 tributes in the 50th Hunger Games.[1]
This was the first year in which Haymitch Abernathy and Lenore Dove were eligible for the reaping.[2]
Palladium is the second District 1 victor with a known surname, the other being Augustus Braun.
Palladium won the year after Chaff.
