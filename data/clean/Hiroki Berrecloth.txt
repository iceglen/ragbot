Hiroki Berrecloth is a British-Japanese actor who portrayed Treech in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Berrecloth appeared in the BBC's Red Rose and the short films Black Lichen, The Intruder, and Why Do You Look at Me with Those Eyes?. He is represented by Simon & How.[1]
Berrecloth originally auditioned to play the role of Sejanus Plinth, whose character he "immediately... connected with," before being called back to audition as Treech. In an interview, he stated that he read the books and watched the films as a kid, and Primrose Everdeen was a "standout character" from the original trilogy. The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes was his first feature film.[2]
