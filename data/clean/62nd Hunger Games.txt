The 62nd Hunger Games took place twelve years prior to the start of the trilogy. The victor was Enobaria, the female tribute from District 2.[1]

The Games[]
According to Capitol Couture, Enobaria volunteered for the 62nd Hunger Games.[1] She likely formed a typical alliance with other Career Tributes, since she was from one of the Career districts and Katniss Everdeen described her as a "classic Career".[3] At some point during the Games, Enobaria was doing hand-to-hand combat with an unknown male tribute and she ripped open his throat with her teeth.[2]

 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Enobaria's fangs in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire. 		 	 
Aftermath[]
Enobaria was so famous for ripping out that tribute's throat that, after her victory, she had her teeth surgically altered so each one ended in a point like a fang, inlaid with gold. According to Katniss, she had "no shortage of admirers" in the Capitol.[2]
