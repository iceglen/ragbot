The Games[]
The arena was a "burning desert".[1]

Trivia[]
Katniss recalled an edition of the Hunger Games where the arena was made up of sand, boulders, and scruffy bushes, and many tributes went insane from thirst.[2] This was likely the 69th Hunger Games as it matches Atala's description of a desert arena.
