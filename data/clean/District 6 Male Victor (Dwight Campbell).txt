Biography[]
Years after his initial victory, President Coriolanus Snow announced that tributes for the third Quarter Quell would be reaped from the existing pool of victors, forcing them to compete in the Games regardless of age. As a result, this victor was present at District 6's reaping for the 75th Hunger Games. He did not volunteer to go in the male morphling's place.[2]

Death[]
At the end of the Second Rebellion, he was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so he presumably died in the Victors' Purge.[3][4]

Physical description[]
He was an African American man, and at the reaping, he wore a bowler hat.

Trivia[]
He was not seen in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, but appeared in behind the scenes photos of the reaping in District 6.
In addition to portraying this victor, Dwight Campbell was one of the film's camera operators.[1]
