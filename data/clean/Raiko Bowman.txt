Raiko Bowman is an American actress who portrayed Mrs. Mellark in The Hunger Games film in her major motion picture debut. Bowman lives in New York. 

Career[]
Bowman started working as an actress in 2006. While she was still attending classes, she acted in several short films, industrials and television commercials. She played the lead role in the short film VEER, for which she was nominated for an award in the Gen Art Film Festial in New York.
