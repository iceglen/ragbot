Jerome Lance is an English actor who portrayed Marcus in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Lance was born in 1990 in London, and graduated from the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama. He is represented by The Artists Partnership.[1][2]
