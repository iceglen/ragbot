Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
During the chariot rides, the chariot carrying District 10 narrowly missed colliding with District 11.[2] Before the start of the games, Peeler and her district joined the Newcomers alliance between District 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11 and 12 tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Her token was a horseshoe.[3] 
She died in the initial bloodbath with the other girl from her district.[4]

Physical description[]
Peeler, along with the other three tributes from District 10, wore a "sexy red cowboy suit" during the chariot rides.[2]

Etymology[]
Could reference tools used for animal hides.

Trivia[]
Peeler is only named on the tribute board.
Peeler's token, along with those of her fellow tributes from her home district, was a horseshoe.[5]
