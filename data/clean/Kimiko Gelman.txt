Kimiko Gelman is an American actress who portrayed Venia in The Hunger Games film[1]. For unknown reasons, she did not return in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire. She has appeared in television shows such as Rags To Riches, Knots Landing, and Freddy's Nightmares.
