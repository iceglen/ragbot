Stamp was one of two male tributes from District 10 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
During the chariot rides, the chariot carrying District 10 narrowly missed colliding with District 11.[2] Before the start of the games, Stamp joined the Newcomers, an alliance between the tributes of Districts 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11 and 12 in an attempt to counter the Career pack.[3] During the games, Stamp died on the fourth day, likely due to the volcanic eruption on the mountain he and the other Newcomers had been staying on.[4]

Etymology[]
Suggestive of branding livestock.

Physical description[]
Stamp, along with the other three tributes from District 10, wore a "sexy red cowboy suit" during the chariot rides.[2]

Trivia[]
Stamp is only named on the tribute board.
Stamp's token, along with those of his fellow tributes from his home district, was a horseshoe.[5]
