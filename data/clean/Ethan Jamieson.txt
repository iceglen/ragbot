Ethan Jamieson portrayed the District 4 male tribute in The Hunger Games film, his major motion picture debut.[1] Jamieson has also appeared in One Tree Hill. He hasn't been very active of late, but still has his curly hair.
He is the only non-stunt actor (and one of the two listed as actors in the credits) to portray a Tribute that fell during the initial bloodbath. He is tied with Rue as the smallest and youngest Tribute. In fight sequences, he is shown hiding under the supply crates in the Cornucopia away from action; this is probably an intentional casting choice.
