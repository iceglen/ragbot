Administration[]
Dean Casca Highbottom oversaw the school. The Academy provided students with free meals and uniforms for daily use, though they were required to finance their own clothing for special events.[1]

Uniform[]
The Academy-issued uniform was not described in The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes novel, but it was noted as including underwear and socks.[1] In the film adaptation, uniforms consisted of a light blue dress shirt with a mandarin collar and covered placket, a red lapelless blazer in a shade that Lucky Flickerman identified as "Academy rouge", and a golden pin of the Academy's logo. Regardless of gender, all students wore red skirts over their matching red trousers with a slit on each side, presumably for ease of pocket access.[2]

Academic standing[]
At graduation in midsummer, the Academy presented monetary prizes to high-performing students. Those students who performed well at the Academy often went on to attend higher education at the University.[1]

Mentor program[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			The 10th Hunger Games mentors. 		 	 
During the 10th Hunger Games, the Academy's top 24 students were selected to mentor tributes from the twelve districts. This was used as a means to make citizens of the Capitol more interested in the Games.[1] However, the program resulted in the deaths of four students, so it was discontinued once the Games concluded that year. The Head Gamemaker, Dr. Volumnia Gaul, considered it a catastrophic and embarrassing failure.[9]

Disciplinary action[]
Students at the Academy who committed serious infractions might receive a demerit. Three such demerits would result in a student's expulsion.[3] The Academy had a tradition of offering expelled students a lifeline. For Coriolanus Snow, this came in the form of an offer to join the Peacekeepers for a twenty-year-term, as an alternative to public disgrace.[10] However, after being expelled, Snow received a diploma indicating he had graduated with high honors, which Sejanus Plinth made a condition of accepting his own offer to join the Peacekeepers after he was expelled as well. Having an Academy diploma allowed Snow to take the Peacekeeper officer test[11] and later to enroll at the University after Dr. Gaul arranged for his honorable discharge from the Peacekeepers.[9]

Campus[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Heavensbee Hall. 		 	 
The Academy had a gathering area called Heavensbee Hall, which hosted events such as viewing the districts' reapings.[1] The high biology lab hosted the mentors' meetings with Dr. Gaul,[6] and there were at least fourteen other classrooms, since they were able to accommodate 14 different mentor-tribute pairs as they prepared for their interviews.[12]

Trivia[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			The Academy auditorium. 		 	 
Classroom scenes were filmed in the Tieranatomisches Theater at Humboldt University of Berlin in Germany.
The auditorium scenes were filmed in the Kuppelsaal (Dome Hall), where fencing events were held at Olympiapark Berlin.[13]
The Academy was also the setting of Academy Adventures, an officially licensed Roblox game created in order to promote the film.
