Hazel Frand was Caesar Flickerman's personal assistant and part-time stylist. She gave Peeta Mellark touch-ups before his interview with Flickerman on Capitol TV.[citation needed]

Behind the Scenes[]
Hazel Frand was created for promotion of The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2 and was mentioned in an article on Capitol TV's website.[citation needed]
