Biography[]
Professor Sickle was awarded an ornamental shield at the end of the Dark Days for overseeing Academy safety drills during the bombings. Each year after that on the day of the Reaping, she insisted on holding it for a group photo.[1] Sickle also insisted on rigorous exercise, teaching Academy students to march in formation since they were twelve years old, something that later served Coriolanus Snow well in his Peacekeeper training.[2]
The year of the 10th Hunger Games, she bade good morning to Satyria Click, with whom she shook hands. She then stated that she thought Sejanus Plinth was looking "too smart" in his outfit, but believed Coriolanus Snow's shirt to be fitting.[1]
She was the aunt of Vipsania Sickle, one of the 24 top-performing students at the Academy selected to be a mentor to a tribute in the 10th Hunger Games. Both Sickles had a love of competition.[3]
At the first mentor-tribute meeting, Sickle banged the door shut and ordered the mentors to meet their tributes and begin talking, later reminding them when their time was nearly up and instructing them to meet Volumnia Gaul at the high biology lab.[4] After Coriolanus made an effort to help Arachne Crane shortly before her death, she commended him, as well as Festus Creed, though to a lesser extent.[5] Later, when the tributes and mentors were led on a tour of the Capitol Arena, Professor Sickle was their chaperone.[6]
When Sejanus was on the verge of expulsion after having rebelliously entered the arena during the Games, Strabo Plinth persuaded the board to let both him and Coriolanus graduate the Academy and enter the Peacekeepers. However, Professor Sickle didn't agree until Strabo bought her a new gymnasium.[7]

Physical description[]
Agrippina Sickle had muscular shoulders and was a woman of formidable stature.[1]

Etymology[]
Agrippina is the feminine form of the name Agrippa, and her surname, Sickle, is a tool used in the harvest of crops. In Ancient Rome, two notable Agrippinas mothered two different emperors: Agrippina the Younger was the mother of Nero, and Agrippina the Elder was the mother of Caligula.
