Chris Mark is a Canadian actor, martial artist, stunt performer, and fight choreographer who portrayed the District 5 male tribute in The Hunger Games film.[1]

Biography[]
Mark made an appearance on the popular TV show "The Boys" as a character named Blindspot and reunited with Jack Quaid, the actor that played Marvel. Like most tribute actors, he is also a stunt performer. He was the lead in Kill Order and Enhanced, both directed by his brother, James Mark. Mark has hopes of becoming the next big martial arts actor. He lives in Toronto, Ontario.
He is the first male actor cast specifically to portray the fight scenes of a Tribute that fell during the initial bloodbath.
