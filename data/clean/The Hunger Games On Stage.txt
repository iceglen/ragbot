Synopsis[]

"In a dystopian future, [t]he Hunger Games ignite a thrilling battle for survival, where 24 young tributes are pitted against each other in a deadly arena. Katniss Everdeen, a fearless and resourceful heroine, emerges as a symbol of rebellion as she fights not only for her life but for the hope of a nation oppressed by a ruthless Capitol. In a brand new epic stage production, The Hunger Games is a gripping tale of courage, defiance, and the unbreakable human spirit."
—Official synopsis from the stage play's website

Production[]
 	  	 	 			   	 	 		 			 		 		 			The Hunger Games On Stage - London 2024 		 		 			The Hunger Games On Stage - London 2024 (Official Trailer) 		 	 
The stage production has been adapted by playwright Conor McPherson, with collaboration from author Suzanne Collins, and it is directed by Matthew Dunster.[1]
Other members of the creative team include Miriam Buether (set designer), Moi Tran (costume designer), Charlotte Broom (choreographer), Lucy Carter (lighting designer), Ian Dickinson (sound designer), Tal Rosner (video designer), Chris Fisher (illusions), Kev McCurdy (fight director), John Maddox (performer flying), Amy Ball (casting director), and Lloyd Thomas (production manager).[1]
Including theater construction expenses, the adaptation cost 26 million pounds to produce.[4]

Production history[]
The production was originally set to debut on West End in London, England, in autumn of 2024.[1] It was later delayed to January 2025,[5] then again to autumn 2025.[2] On February 27, they announced that the show would open October 20 at the Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre.[3]
Mia Carragher, daughter of former football player Jamie Carragher, was announced to play Katniss Everdeen on July 29, 2025.[6] On July 31, it was revealed that Euan Garrett and Joshua Lacey were cast as Peeta Mellark and Haymitch Abernathy, respectively. On August 5, Tristan Waterson was cast as Gale Hawthorne, Ruth Everett was cast as Mrs. Everdeen, and Sophia Ally as Primrose Everdeen. On August 6, Tamsin Carroll was announced as Effie Trinket, Stavros Demetraki as Caesar Flickerman, and Nathan Ives-Moiba as Cinna.[7] The full cast was revealed on August 8.[8]
The show was cut from 3 hours to 2 hours and 15 minutes. Preview performances began on October 20, 2025, with the first preview unexpectedly delayed due to a cast injury. It also took place amidst unfinished construction, provoking complaints about accessibility. The official opening night was scheduled for November 12, and the production has been extended until October 18, 2026 due to demand.[9]

Venue[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre 		 	 
The Troubadour Canary Wharf Theatre, located in Canary Wharf, London, was built specifically for The Hunger Games. It contains a total of 1200 seats on all sides of a round stage, with sections named after the twelve districts.[3] District 12 serves as the stage. Upon a tribute's death, their corresponding section of the audience flashes red. The venue also encourages audience participation by displaying expected reactions on screens during scenes with Caesar Flickerman.[9]
The seats in District 1 and District 2 move throughout the performance as part of the immersive experience.[10] Actors are seen fighting on the walls and in 90-degree angles in front of the upper balcony.[4]
The theatre has three floors, including an outside terrace[3] and a restaurant named Songbird.[11]
