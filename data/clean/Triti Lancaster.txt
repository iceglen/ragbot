Biography[]
Triti was selected to be a District Hero due to her generosity and kindness to other citizens of District 9. Triti's family quickly agreed to the remuneration they would receive for Triti's service.

Physical description[]
Triti is portrayed as a dark-skinned woman of average build, described by Egeria as charming and beautiful. In her District Hero portrait, her hair is tied back, she wears a white t-shirt under a yellow apron or overalls, and she carries a sheaf of wheat behind her.[2]

Personality[]
In Egeria's letter, she states that although Triti doesn't evoke the same "emotional connection" as her fellow District Heroes, she believes her "gentle, industrious personality [should] excel in illustrating the variety of the districts."

Trivia[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Egeria's letter to Coriolanus Snow on Triti 		 	 
On June 19th, 2014, Capitol Couture released seven portraits of District Heroes as advertisements for The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1. These were original characters, independent from the books or films, serving as pro-Capitol propaganda for each of their respective districts.[4]
According to the District Heroes banners, Triti is 17, however, in Egeria's letters, she is said to be 19. This inconsistency also occurs with Felix Stam.
Triti was the first named character from District 9.
She remained the only named character from her district until the release of The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes.
