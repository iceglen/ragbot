Delly Cartwright's brother is the younger brother of Delly Cartwright.

Biography[]
He was born in District 12 and raised alongside his older sister, Delly, in the merchant sector of town. Their parents ran the shoe shop,[1]  and it was typical for merchants to live above their stores,[2] so the Cartwrights likely lived right above theirs.
He and Delly both survived the bombing of District 12, though their parents did not. The siblings were evacuated to District 13 with the other refugees.[1] They accounted for two of "fewer than a dozen" total merchant survivors.[3]

Etymology[]
His surname, Cartwright, is an English occupational surname for a maker of carts.
