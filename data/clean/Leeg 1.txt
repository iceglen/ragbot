Biography[]
Leeg 1 lived in District 13 with her sister and an unnamed father.[2] The sisters were both assigned to Squad 451, and they trained alongside their new compatriots for several days before deployment for the Battle of the Capitol. During this time, they were given the nicknames "Leeg 1" and "Leeg 2" to distinguish them. Once in the Capitol, they filmed propos for several days. However, Leeg 1's sister hit a mislabeled pod on the fourth morning, which shot a "sunburst of metal darts" through her brain, killing her instantly. The following evening, Peeta Mellark was sent to take her place.[1] That night, Leeg 1 broke down and sobbed over her sister's death inside her tent in the rebel encampment.[3]

The next morning, she participated in the filming of another propo. After it went awry, killing Mitchell and fatally wounding their commander Boggs, Leeg 1 and Gale Hawthorne shot through the front door lock of a building so they could take refuge inside, and they unsuccessfully attempted to shoot Mitchell down from his barbed wire net. After Boggs succumbed to his injuries, she asked about taking his body with them as they left to fulfill Katniss Everdeen's falsified assassination mission against President Snow.[2] 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Leeg 1 running past Katniss. 		 	 
Peacekeepers launched an assault on the building they'd just been in, and upon seeing herself declared dead on television, Leeg 1 felt badly for her father, who would now be grieving both of his daughters.[2]

Death[]
The squad traveled underground through the sewers into the Transfer, at which point they were attacked by lizard mutts, activated pods, and Peacekeepers who were eventually decapitated by the mutts. Jackson and Leeg 1 stayed behind to hold the creatures off at a pod known as the Meat Grinder, attempting to buy time for the rest of the squad to make it to safety. It's assumed that Leeg 1 was either killed by the Meat Grinder or the mutts.[4]

Film adaptation[]
In The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2, Leeg 2 survived to see Peeta join the squad, and she was injured in the incident that killed Mitchell and Boggs. Leeg 1 stayed behind to take care of her, and when the Peacekeeper assault on the building began, they returned fire. However, it's assumed that they were ultimately killed.[5]

Physical description[]
She was a young woman in her twenties with "weird yellow flecks in her eyes". This was the only way Katniss could distinguish her from her sister, since they looked so similar in uniform.[1] In the film, they were identical twins with blonde hair.[5]

Trivia[]
The Leeg sisters, despite being played by identical twin actresses in Mockingjay - Part 2, are not explicitly referred to as twins in the book.
She and her sister were one of two sets of siblings in their squad. The other set was Castor and Pollux from the camera crew.[6]
