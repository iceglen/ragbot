Amanda Plummer is an American actress who portrayed Wiress in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire. [1] 

Biography[]
Plummer was born in New York to film legend Christopher Plummer and actress Tammy Grimes, later attending college in Vermont.  She started acting in 1979 and is best known for her roles in films such as Pulp Fiction, So I Married an Axe Murderer and The Fisher King. Plummer was nominated for several awards. She has won two Emmys and one Tony.
