						In the 50th Hunger Games, or the second Quarter Quell, Maysilee Donner, Mrs. Everdeen's best friend, was selected as a female tribute. Unfortunately, she was killed in the arena. Mrs. Everdeen and Mrs. Undersee were televised during the reaping.

					How did Mrs. Everdeen cope with the loss of her best friend, Maysilee Donner?
						
				
				
					
						Maysilee Donner's parents entrusted her pet canary to Mrs. Everdeen following Maysilee's death. This gesture served as a means for Mrs. Everdeen, also known as Katniss Everdeen's mom, to cope with her best friend's loss. She shared her sorrow with Mrs. Undersee, Maysilee's sister. However, she refrained from discussing Maysilee with her daughters, probably to evade the topic of her death.

					What role does Mrs. Everdeen play in the Hunger Games series?
						
				
				
					
						In the Hunger Games series, Mrs. Everdeen, Katniss Everdeen's mother, is a healer and former merchant/pharmacist. After her husband's death in a mining accident, she fell into depression, neglecting her children and forcing Katniss to become the family's provider. She lived in several locations, including The Seam, Victors' Village in District 12, District 13, and District 4. Notably, she saved Gale's life.

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
On the day of the reaping for the 50th Hunger Games, Asterid was working in the apothecary. Burdock Everdeen visited and traded some game and wildcrafting. He gave her a sprig of chamomile and told her it would bring her good luck. Haymitch Abernathy later visited to trade white liquor and she also gave him a sprig of chamomile, and blushed when he asked if Burdock told her it was good luck.[6]
Her best friend Maysilee Donner was chosen as one of two female tributes for the 50th Hunger Games, also known as the second Quarter Quell, along with Haymitch Abernathy. Asterid, along with Merrilee, was seen on the television during the reaping.[7] Maysilee died, leaving her sister, Merrilee Donner and Asterid in their grief. Maysilee's parents gave Maysilee's songbird to Asterid after Maysilee's death in the arena.[8]
After Haymitch realized President Snow would not let anybody he cares for stay alive, he started drawing off from all his loved ones. Burdock kept trying to hold on, and he brought Asterid, who took Haymitch sleeping medicine. To get them to leave, Haymitch threw rocks at Asterid and Burdock, hitting Asterid on the forehead, drawing blood. Hurting Asterid felt worse for Haymitch than everything he suffered in the arena.[9]

The Hunger Games[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Prim and Mrs. Everdeen are distraught after Katniss volunteers to take Prim's place in the 74th Hunger Games. 		 	 
On the day of the reaping, Asterid gave Katniss a blue dress for the reaping and braided her hair. During the reaping, Asterid was shocked when her youngest daughter Prim was chosen[1] and even more when her oldest daughter, Katniss volunteered in Prim's place.[10] Asterid came to see Katniss to say her final farewells. While in there, Asterid wept due to the high odds of her not coming back. Katniss made her promise not to leave Prim alone and take care of her no matter what happens to her. Katniss yells at her, but then hugs her goodbye. Asterid promises and stays with her until the Peacekeepers come in and take her and Prim away. Then surprisingly, Mr. Mellark, the baker, visits Katniss and gives her a bag of cookies. The baker was teary after probably then visiting his son, Peeta. Madge Undersee also visits Katniss and gives the legendary Mockingjay pin,[11] originally owned by her aunt,[12] Maysilee Donner, who was Asterid's friend and a tribute in the 50th hunger games,[8] who was murdered in the arena.[7] Then Gale visits and he and Katniss discuss a lot of things like, the survival of Prim and Katniss' Mom, and the need of a bow and arrow to compete and saying goodbye possibly once and for all[11].

Catching Fire[]
Asterid was nervous when President Snow visits them.[4] She tells Katniss that he looks pale and strange. During President Snow's and Katniss' conversation, Asterid comes in and offers him cookies and tea. She took this as a way to check up on them, and on her way out she gave Katniss a look to know if everything is well.[13] After he leaves, Katniss lies and tells her that everything is good; this relieves her.[14] 

 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Mrs. Everdeen and Prim in their Victor's Village house. 		 	 
Asterid takes care of her family[14][2][15] and anyone who goes to her for medical help.[2][16] When Gale is whipped,[12] Katniss and Peeta take him to her. Asterid and Prim care for Gale's and Katniss' injuries.[2] Asterid uses a special medical treatment with snow.[16] Katniss yells at her, telling her to do everything she can and questioning her about everything she does to save Gale.[2] Asterid joins Haymitch and Katniss and fills them in on Gale's condition, Katniss apologizes for her behavior to her mother and she forgives her. Asterid saves Gale's life.[16] Asterid helps Katniss during her wedding dress shoot.[8] When the third Quarter Quell is announced, Asterid is surprised when President Snow tells all of Panem it will reap past victors; Katniss will be reaped since she is the only living female victor from District 12.[8] Asterid, along with Prim, does everything to comfort Katniss after it is announced. After Katniss is reaped, Asterid and Prim go to the justice building but cannot say their goodbyes due to a new procedure.[17] After Katniss leaves, Asterid and Prim cry together knowing the odds are strongly against her.[18] When District 12 is bombed, Gale saves them.[19] Asterid and Prim set up a medical station in the woods.[20] 

Mockingjay[]
Asterid works at the hospital in District 13, but is treated as a nurse, not a full healer.[3] She takes care of the ill along with Prim.[21] The Everdeen family is still close and remain that way.[21] They move to a larger room[3] after Katniss demands to keep Buttercup for Prim. When Katniss finds her prep team abused,[22] Katniss takes them to be treated by her mother. Mrs. Everdeen treats them back to health and tells her to take it easy on them since they are still hurt mentally due to the abuse.[3] After Katniss returns from shooting a propo in District 8 and recovering from an attack by the Capitol,[23] Asterid cares for Katniss in the hospital. When Katniss awakens, Asterid is upset that she did not notify her that she was leaving. Katniss apologizes and promises her that next time she will. Asterid continues to care for Katniss in the hospital until she is fully healed.[24]
During the bombing of District 13 by the Capitol, Asterid makes it to the bunker she, Katniss and Prim were assigned to. When Katniss questions Prim's location, Asterid becomes worried because she left ten minutes before her. She is relieved when Prim and Gale show up right before the door was shut and locked. Asterid stays with her family in one area during the first bombing, and afterwards goes to the first aid station to help heal the wounded and ill.[21] Before Katniss goes to the Capitol with her squad, she says her final goodbye to her family.[25] When Prim is killed in the bombing at President Snow's mansion,[26][27] Asterid goes to District 4 to head up a new hospital rather than return to District 12 which was now too painful for her. Haymitch gives Katniss a note that has a phone number Katniss can call her mother with. The two mourn over Prim's death together over the phone.[5]

Physical description[]
Asterid was described to have a pretty merchant child's appearance in her youth[7] and looking not so beaten down in her sleep. It is said that her youngest child, Prim, took after her in appearance. So it is fair to think that she had fair skin, blonde hair, and blue eyes, unlike the majority of the residents of the Seam in District 12.[1] She has long, tapered fingers.[2]

Family[]
Asterid was the daughter of an apothecary and grew up in the merchant sector of district twelve. During those years, she learned to care for illnesses and injuries. When she married her husband, she moved into the Seam to live with him. Since the Seam is much poorer than the merchant sector, Katniss took that as a sign that she loved her husband very much.[1] In Catching Fire, she still had a tea service set that she had taken with her.[13]
She has two daughters, Katniss and Primrose. Her husband died in a coal mining accident five years before the events of The Hunger Games book. She also has two grandchildren, an elder girl and younger boy, from Katniss and Peeta.[28]
Primrose died in Mockingjay,[26][27] leaving Asterid unable to go back to District 12 due to the pain of her death.[5]

Work[]
Though never formally trained, Asterid is a healer, with extensive knowledge of natural remedies from having grown up in the apothecary in the merchant's district. Following her recovery from her grief over her husband's death, Asterid started a small apothecary business for people in the Seam.[11] She has helped many people in the Seam who go to her for various things including both injury and sickness. In Catching Fire, Haymitch tells Katniss that there used to be a lot of whipping before the Head Peacekeeper Cray took over and that it was Katniss' mother that people would go to for aid.[2] Head Peacekeeper Thread whips Gale[12] (and accidentally Katniss[2]) and Asterid treats Gale and Katniss with the help of her youngest daughter, Prim.[2][16]

Relationships[]
Katniss Everdeen[]
After being emotionally abandoned by her mother for months after her father's death, Katniss came to distrust her and never truly forgave her.[1] Katniss also took over the role of caring for Prim. After volunteering,[29] Katniss told Asterid that she had to take care of Prim, and her mother promised to.[11] After the Quarter Quell is announced, Asterid screamed.[8] In Mockingjay, after Prim's death,[26][27] she buried her grief in her work.[27] She moved to District 4, abandoning Katniss again. However, they bonded over the phone, grieving Prim's death. Asterid helped Katniss with her book for remembering the good in the world.[5]

Primrose Everdeen[]
Prim was very close to her mother and completely forgave her for abandoning her daughters after Burdock's death. Together, they held an apothecary business. After the District 12 bombing,[19] they took care of the wounded until they were rescued by District 13.[20] After Prim's death,[26][27] Asterid was devastated and could not go back to District 12.[5]

Burdock Everdeen[]
Asterid loved Burdock Everdeen to the point where she left her merchant life for the Seam so she could be with him. After he died, she fell into a deep depression and left her two young daughters to fend for themselves.[1]

Gale Hawthorne[]
After he is whipped by Thread, Gale's wounds are treated by Asterid and Prim, though Asterid did not have a close relationship with the boy.[2][16] 
Otho Mellark had always loved Asterid, but she "ran off with a coal miner".[30]

Etymology[]
Her surname, Everdeen, comes from Bathsheba Everdene, the main character in Far from the Madding Crowd.[31]
Asterid could come from aster, a flowering plant symbolizing patience, love, and healing—reflecting her role as a healer and caregiver throughout the series. It also relates to the Scandinavian name Astrid, meaning "beloved by the gods". The surname March references springtime and rebirth and the time when the flowers begin to bloom. The surname also subtly aligns with qualities mirrored in the March family from Little Women, a family known for healing, and overcoming difficult times.

Trivia[]
Although her name is not revealed in the original books trilogy or films, Paula Malcolmson, who played Mrs. Everdeen, revealed in an interview that she named her character 'Clara'. Malcolmson said the name "just came to her".[32]
Interestingly, burdock (her future husband) and asters (represented by "Asterid") are both plants that are part of the Asteraceae family, commonly known as the daisy or sunflower family.
