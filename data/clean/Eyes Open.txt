"Eyes Open" is the third overall single released from the album, The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond and the first official single (the first two were promotional singles). The song is performed and written by Taylor Swift.            
This is one of two songs that Taylor Swift wrote for The Hunger Games film. "Safe and Sound" was featured at the end of the film during the credits whereas "Eyes Open" was not.
Swift has since re-recorded many of her songs that were originally written and published prior to 2018, "Safe & Sound" and "Eyes Open" included. Both songs, now bearing the title of "(Taylor's Version)," were released at midnight on March 17th 2023 after being teased by Swift the previous day.

No official music video was released for the song, but a lyric video was released on May 17, 2012. The video features animations of the lyrics to the song.

Trivia[]
The song was the second top 40 at the Billboard Hot 100 which was featured in the soundtrack of the Hunger Games; the first was Safe & Sound which reached #30.
