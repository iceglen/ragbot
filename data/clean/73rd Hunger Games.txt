"Remember this year? Oh, one of my favorite years and one of my favorite arenas. The use of the rubble, the ruined city, very exciting. And this moment here, this moment is a moment you'll never forget. The moment when a tribute becomes a victor."
—Caesar Flickerman on the 73rd Hunger Games[src]
The 73rd Hunger Games took place one year prior to the start of the trilogy. These Hunger Games were won by an unnamed boy, the male tribute from District 2.

Arena[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Costumes used in the news reel footage 		 	 
The 73rd Hunger Games were set in the rubble of a ruined city. Caesar Flickerman said this arena was one of his favorites.[1]

Arena uniform[]
In the film, both tributes shown wore black shoes, black cargo pants, and black, cropped armored vests underneath their colorful jackets. These appeared to correspond with the district color coding also used for the 74th Hunger Games: red with black stripes for District 2, and gray-brown with yellow stripes for District 10.[1][2]

Summary[]
Pre-Games[]
Effie Trinket, the District 12 escort, mentioned that the tributes from 12 had poor table manners that year, complaining that they ate like "a couple of savages". According to Katniss Everdeen, both had been from the Seam, so they'd had never had a decent meal in their lives.[3]
In The Hunger Games film, this was Seneca Crane's second year as Head Gamemaker, as the following year was his third and final.[1]

Interviews[]
Caesar Flickerman's hair and make-up color this year was crimson, making it appear as though he was bleeding.[4]

The Games[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			The boy from 2 killing the boy from 10, making him the victor. 		 	 
In the film, Katniss briefly watched news reel footage on the train to the Capitol. This footage portrayed the ending of the 73rd Hunger Games', commentated on by Caesar Flickerman and Claudius Templesmith looking back on the event as "the moment when a tribute becomes a victor." The victor was a Career Tribute, an unnamed boy from District 2, who bludgeoned the boy from District 10 to death with a brick.[1]

Aftermath[]
The victor of this Games would have been one of 32 unknown victors who were killed by either Capitol or rebel forces during the Victors' Purge, after the third Quarter Quell.[5]

Early script[]
In an early version of the script, the ruined city was said to be Aspen, Colorado, 300 years in the future. The boy from 12 was named Sage Blanken, and his parents, Corrina and Tygart Blanken, were vendors at the Hob.[6] 
The District 12 female was killed sometime on the first day. Unlike a typical Games where the Cornucopia bloodbath would occur on the first day, a bloodbath had taken place on the second day, with at least 8 tributes in attendance: Sage, Prospero from District 3, an unknown girl, an semi-impaled tribute, and four more unknown tributes using a knife, spear, whip, and bow. Sage narrowly escaped the tributes pursuing him only to be killed with a mace by Prospero.[6] 
While they were hunting in the woods outside District 12, Gale Hawthorne told Katniss who he thought would win, guessing correctly that it would be the boy from 2.[6]
In interviews for the 74th Hunger Games, Caesar mentioned to Cato that the 72nd and 73rd Hunger Games were both won by District 2 tributes.[6]

Trivia[]
This is the final Hunger Games which ended normally with a single victor. After an attempted double suicide, the 74th Hunger Games had two victors,[7] and the 75th Hunger Games were interrupted by the destruction of the arena, so a victor was never crowned.[8]
This was the last victory of a Career Tribute, as the 74th Hunger Games were won by District 12, and the 75th had no victor.
However, another District 2 victor, Enobaria, would go on to survive both the 75th Hunger Games and the Second Rebellion.[5]
