Carat was one of two female tributes from District 1 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Carat was part of the Career pack in these Games. In the Training Center, she and her district partners wore "snot-green."[2]

Death[]
Carat survived the initial bloodbath but died shortly after when she ate or drank something poisonous. Her death was shown in the Capitol's recap of the Games after they ended.[3]

Etymology[]
The word Carat refers to a unit of mass used for measuring gemstones and pearls.
