Antonius was Panem's Defense Minister and a member of the Presidential Cabinet, working closely alongside Coriolanus Snow. He was an entrusted and high-ranking member within the Capitol's political system.

Biography[]
Antonius informed President Snow of Katniss Everdeen's presence in District 8 and advised him that hovercraft could be deployed from the border of District 11. Snow told Antonius to "kill the wounded", meaning everyone within the hospital Katniss had visited. Later, Antonius stood alongside Snow and Egeria when rebels destroyed the hydroelectric dam in District 5, cutting the power in the Capitol.[1]
Antonius was responsible for ordering numerous attacks on rebels who attempted to conquer the Nut, the Capitol's arsenal within the mountains of District 2. When the rebels launched a more uniquely devised attack, the Nut caved in with many Peacekeepers still trapped inside. Most of these Peacekeepers had been sent at Antonius's request, many of whom were, as Snow called them, the "best" of the Capitol. As a result of his arrogance, Antonius was poisoned to death during a dinner with the other Cabinet members. Antonius's stunt had cost the Capitol the victory Snow was hoping for, as Antonius had inadvertently allowed the rebels the upper hand.[2]

Personality and traits[]
Like Snow, Antonius was ambitious and calculating. However, despite his loyalty to the president, Antonius was also arrogant, stubborn, and power-hungry, which ultimately led to his demise.

Etymology[]
"Antonius" is a reference to Mark Antony, a Roman general under Julius Caesar.

Trivia[]
Contrasting Finnick Odair's recollection of Snow's poisonings,[3] Snow does not drink from the same cup as Antonius. This is speculated to be because of Snow's deteriorating health and that the poisoning was public as it happened during a dinner.
