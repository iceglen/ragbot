Heartwood was one of two male tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Before the start of the games, Heartwood joined an alliance between District 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 & 12 tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Heartwood's district was the first to join the Newcomers alliance. Heartwood and his district partners were considered "nothing special" by Haymitch Abernathy, but were decent at knife throwing during training.[2]
Heartwood died in the initial bloodbath along with the other boy from his district, Bircher.[3]

Physical description[]
The District 7 tributes' training outfits were russet brown.[4]

Etymology[]
"Heartwood" refers to the central, strongest part of a tree.

Trivia[]
Heartwood, along with Alpheus, Janus, Nona, Lect, Coil, Bircher, Ryan, Clayton, Midge, and all of Districts 5 and 8, is only named on the tribute board.
