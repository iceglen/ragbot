Silius Trinket was the great-uncle of Effie and Proserpina Trinket. He was married to Messalina Trinket.

Biography[]
Silius was active during the war. Somehow, he and his wife brought great disgrace to the Trinket family. During the interviews for the 50th Hunger Games, Haymitch Abernathy and Wyatt Callow wore clothes once belonging to Silius. The aforementioned disgrace involved weapons and blood, as Haymitch found a "decorative" holster and bloodstains on the clothes he borrowed from Silius.[1]

Personality and traits[]
According to Effie, Silius was competent in his fashion sense. His clothing was roughly the same size as Haymitch's and Wyatt's.[1]

Etymology[]
Silius was most likely named after the Roman Senator Gaius Silius. He was the lover of Valeria Messalina, third wife of the emperor Claudius. She had him divorce his wife and the two married while the emperor was away. They were both executed,[2] and the Roman Senate ordered that Messalina be erased from memory.[3]
The name "Trinket" refers to a small ornament or item of jewelry that is of little value. This is very fitting for Effie, and the rest of the Capitol citizens, as they prize meaningless possessions that though may have cost a lot, have no real purpose and are just for show. This helps to emphasize the poverty and starvation in the Districts, where people can not even afford to eat, let alone waste money on flashy objects and clothing.
