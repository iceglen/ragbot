Mayor Undersee's house was a home in District 12 where Mayor Undersee resided with his wife and their daughter, Madge Undersee.[1]

Description[]
The mayor's house was a three-story building[2] near the town square.[3] They had a telephone somewhere in the house.[4] There was a piano on the first floor, and the second floor held several guest rooms, the mayor's study, and Madge's bedroom. The master bedroom was presumably on the third floor, since that was where Mrs. Undersee rested whenever she had one of her severe headaches.[2] Madge had a dressing table with a mirror in her own bedroom.[5]

History[]
74th Hunger Games[]

"But I like Mayor Undersee's house, especially now that his daughter, Madge, and I are friends."
—Katniss about the mayor's house[src]
Katniss Everdeen and Gale Hawthorne often went to the mayor's back door to sell him wild strawberries, and they did so on the morning of the reaping for the 74th Hunger Games.[1] After Katniss and Peeta Mellark won the Games, Katniss grew closer to Madge, and the girls spent time at each other's houses. At the mayor's house, Madge and Katniss sometimes ate together, and Madge tried to teach Katniss to play the piano.[2]

Victory Tour[]
Katniss and Peeta's last stop on their Victory Tour was their home district. After arriving at the train station, they spent that night at the mayor's house for a dinner with their families. Katniss briefly hugged Madge before her prep team took her to the third floor to get ready, and once she was done, Katniss went down to the second floor to see her friend.[2]
Katniss stuck her head into the mayor's study to say hello along the way only to find it empty. She stood and watched television coverage of the Victory Tour for a moment. However, when she turned to leave, a beeping noise directed her attention back to the screen for a confidential news update about the uprising of District 8.[2] Katniss hurried away, and she had an innocuous exchange with the mayor in the hallway before she went into Madge's room. The girls talked about the mockingjay pin Madge had given Katniss as her tribute token.[5]
In addition to Katniss and Peeta's families, Hazelle Hawthorne and her three youngest children— Rory, Vick, and Posy— attended the Victory Tour dinner. Gale was "conspicuously absent", but when Katniss asked Vick about it, he said Gale had been hunting. Katniss concluded that this was probably true.[5]

Bombing of District 12[]

"I pass the wreckage of the mayor's house, where my friend Madge lived. No word of her or her family. Were they evacuated to the Capitol because of her father's position, or left to the flames?"
—Katniss wondering what became of Madge and her family[src]
The mayor's house was destroyed in the bombing of District 12. Katniss initially speculated that they could have been evacuated to the Capitol,[3] but when she asked Thom, who had been recovering human remains from the wreckage, he told her that the whole family had died as well as the two people who worked for them.[6]

Trivia[]
In The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, Katniss learned about the uprising on the train from the Capitol back to District 12 at the end of the tour. She was walking down the hallway late at night when she came to a stop outside a control room door to watch the footage.[7]
