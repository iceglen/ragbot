Mckenna Grace is an American actress set to play Maysilee Donner in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Trivia[]
Grace was co-stars with Ashley Liao in a couple episodes of the TV Series Fuller House. Liao played Clemensia Dovecote in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.
In a conversation with a fan, she states that she listens to “Von dutch” by Charli xcx to get into character before filming as Maysilee. [2]
She co-star with Josh Hutcherson in Five Nights at Freddy's 2, who played Peeta Mellark in The Hunger Games film trilogy.
