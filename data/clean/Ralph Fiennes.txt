Ralph Fiennes is an English actor of film, television and theater who is set to play the role of Coriolanus Snow in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. He succeeds Tom Blyth in The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes and the late Donald Sutherland in the original films.[1]

Career[]
Fiennes is perhaps most known for also having played the main villain of the Harry Potter film franchise, the dark wizard Lord Voldemort. His other major projects have included the Holocaust epic Schindler's List, the comic film The Grand Budapest Hotel, and the political thriller Conclave.[2]

Trivia[]
Ralph Fiennes shared the screen with several prominent actors from The Hunger Games franchise:
He co-starred in Land of the Blind (2006) with Donald Sutherland, his late predecessor for the role of Snow.
He starred with Philip Seymour Hoffman (Plutarch Heavensbee) in Red Dragon (2002).
He also starred in Conclave (2024) with Stanley Tucci (Caesar Flickerman).
