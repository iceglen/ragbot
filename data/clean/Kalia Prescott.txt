Kalia Prescott is the first of the stunt performers specifically cast to portray the training and fight scenes of a tribute that is mentioned only once, that she fell during the initial bloodbath. She portrayed the District 3 female tribute in The Hunger Games film.[1] 

Career[]
Like most of the tribute actors, she is also a stunt actor, having done various stunts for films such as Spider-Man 2 and Red Dawn. She is the only stunt actor and one of the two tributes which fell during the bloodbath (the other is the District 4 male by Ethan Jamieson) to be officially credited under the actors section.
She is also a Taekwondo black belt and a model and is most famous for her contract with Gucci, which ended in 2011. Since then, she has been modelling for various other businesses as an independent model. In 2013, she participated in the individual movie Sugar, playing the major role of the movie (Sugar, a 16-year-old girl).
