Wes Chatham is an American actor who portrayed Castor in the movie, The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1.

Career[]
Wes Chatham has appeared in a few movies, such as In the Valley of Elah (2007), The Philly Kid (2012), The Help (2011) and The Town That Dreaded Sundown (2014). He has also appeared in multiple TV series, including The Unit, portraying Sam McBride, Barbershop as Issac Brice, and The Expanse as Amos Burton.
