Anni Baumann is a German actress who portrayed Iphigenia Moss in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

Biography[]
She is represented by Rietz Management.[1] Baumann has also participated in Der Lehrer, 2009, and Slave Trade, 2018.
