Clemens Schick is a German actor and model who portrayed Nero Price in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes film.

Trivia[]
In the film, his character was only listed in the credits as "Respectable Man".[1]
The actor originally chosen for the role of Nero had gotten a "monk haircut" for a different role and no longer looked the part, so it was recast.[2]
