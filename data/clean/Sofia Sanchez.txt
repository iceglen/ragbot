Sofia Sanchez is an actress who portrayed Wovey in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Sanchez is a Ukrainian-American actress, model, and advocate, known for her viral video Down Syndrome Is Not Scary. She made her acting debut in the television series Switched at Birth in 2015, and her first movie role in For Paloma in 2023. Additionally, her adoption story inspired two of her published Scholastic books, You are Enough and You are Loved. Sanchez is represented by KMR & Associates.[1]
