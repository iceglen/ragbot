The SJ-7 Peacekeeper Assault Transport is the only ground armored military vehicle used in service of Panem's Peacekeepers.[1]

Description[]
The vehicle's windows are equipped with 70mm bulletproof glass, and its armor can hold against high caliber small arms fire and explosives up to K5 rating. The V-Hull design can protect passengers from detonations even if a detonation occurs beneath the vehicle. The vehicle's "Granthom 12" liquid cooled engine's trainpower can generate up to 827 horse-brake power, and its suspensions allow the vehicle to transport and deploy assets in any kind of environment.[1]
