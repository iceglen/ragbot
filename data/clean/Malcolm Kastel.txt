Malcolm Kastel is a man from District 6 who was selected to be a District Hero.[2]

Biography[]
Malcolm was presumably selected to be a District Hero because he encouraged people from District 6 to take pride in their labor and work harder.

Physical description[]
Malcolm is a muscular man with a dark crew cut and blue eyes. In his District Hero portrait, he wears a white tank top with a rip at the bottom, trousers made out of tires, and dark boots. His skin and shirt are smeared with oil, and he's shown holding a tire.[2]

Etymology[]
Malcolm is a Scottish Gaelic name meaning "devotee of Saint Columba". "Maol" also means bald in both Irish and Scots Gaelic, and Malcolm is portrayed with a closely shaven head. It could also refer to automotive businessman Malcolm Bricklin, as District 6 is the transportation district.
His surname, Kastel, is a topographic name for someone living near a castle or fort, from the Latin "castellum".

Trivia[]
On June 19th, 2014, Capitol Couture released seven portraits of District Heroes as advertisements for The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1. These were original characters, independent from the books or films, serving as pro-Capitol propaganda for each of their respective districts.[3]
