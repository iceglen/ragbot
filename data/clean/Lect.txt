Lect was one of two male tributes from District 3 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.[1]

Biography[]
50th Hunger Games[]
Before the start of the Games, Lect joined the Newcomers alliance formed by his fellow District 3 tribute Ampert in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Lect was interested in Wyatt's system of odds.[2]
Lect died on the fourth day after a volcanic eruption.[3]

Etymology[]
"Lect" likely refers to e-lect-ricity, aligning with District 3's technology-themed naming habits. It could also derive from e-lect-tron, further reinforcing this pattern.

Trivia[]
Lect is only named on the tribute board.
