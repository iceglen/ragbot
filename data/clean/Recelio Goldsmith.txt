Recelio Goldsmith was a citizen of the Capitol and a clerk for the Capitol Residential Real Estate Company. His name appeared on an eviction notice in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			The eviction notice 		 	 
At the time of the 10th Hunger Games, Goldsmith was the clerk who sent an eviction notice to the Snow family, stating that they had to move out of their apartment in the Corso within 15 days.[1]

Etymology[]
Recelio is a name of unknown origin, but it may be used here as a pun on reselling, since the real estate company meant to evict the Snows and resell their apartment to new buyers. Goldsmith is an occupational surname for one who made or sold gold, but eventually became associated with banking.
