Cooper Dillon is a German/American actor who portrayed Mizzen in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Dillon started his career at the age of 8 with a lead role in the German version of the Broadway musical Mary Poppins. Since then, he has had recurring and guest supporting roles in some critically acclaimed German TV shows, as well as playing the lead in two independent short films and a just finished production about the ballet dancer Rudolf Nureyev as a boy.[2] Dillon is represented by Rietz Casting & Agentur.[1]

Trivia[]
Dillon's audition scene for The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes was Mizzen's death scene.[3]
