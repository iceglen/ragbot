He’s seen by his community as one thing, but he’s another thing underneath, and he’s a contradiction. He’s a logician, but he’s got a really big heart, right? Ben Wang is a Chinese-American actor set to play Wyatt Callow in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.[1]

Career[]
Ben Wang is well known for playing Jin Wang in American Born Chinese and a main role as Li Fong in Karate Kid: Legends, and is a minor character as Bo in Chang Can Dunk.
In 2025, Wang starred as Hank Olson in a similar dystopian film, The Long Walk, also directed by Francis Lawrence.
