Carolyn McCormick is an American actress who narrated the audiobook versions of the The Hunger Games trilogy. Recognized by AudioFile as the 2009 Best Voice in Young Adult for her readings of The Hunger Games and Catching Fire,[1] the trilogy was her first foray into audiobook narration for young-adult fiction, as she had previously narrated crime fiction.
McCormick began acting in 1985 and has worked in TV, movies, theatre and voice acting. She is most known for the role of Dr. Elizabeth Olivet in the Law & Order franchise. Olivet is one of only five characters who have appeared in all four of the Law & Order series set in New York City.
