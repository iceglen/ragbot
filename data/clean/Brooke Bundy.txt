Brooke Bundy, also known professionally as Bruce Bundy, portrayed Octavia in The Hunger Games film and also appeared in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire,[1] The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1, and The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2.[2] She was also in the film Into the Woods.
