Michael Lesslie is a British writer and producer who wrote the screenplay for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes with Michael Arndt. He is also known for writing a TV adaptation of The Little Drummer Girl, as well as the 2016 film Assassin's Creed.
