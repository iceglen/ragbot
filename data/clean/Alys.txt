Alys was a student at the Academy who appeared in the officially licensed Academy Adventures Roblox game, as part of promotion for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

Biography[]
Alys ripped her graduation robe and asked Tigris to fix it at the last minute. The player's goal in the game's fourth quarter was to gather Tigris's sewing supplies from around the campus, then escort her to Alys and help her mend the gown just before the ceremony.

Physical description[]
Alys had brown skin and wore black glasses.

Etymology[]
Alys is an alternative spelling of the name Alice, meaning "noble" or "of nobility".

Trivia[]
Alys is the only named original character featured in the game.
