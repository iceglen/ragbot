Irene Boehm is an actress who portrayed Lamina in the The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[2]

Biography[]
Boehm was born in Berlin, Germany. She is best known for playing Toni Ritter in the television show Babylon Berlin, which was directed by Tom Tykwer, Achim von Borries and Hendrik Handloegten. She is represented by Rietz Casting & Agentur.[2]
