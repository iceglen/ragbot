Biography[]
Magno had been the District 12 stylist for several years by the time of the Second Quarter Quell, at least 10 years, since Magno was 12's stylist for as long as Haymitch Abernathy could remember. He promoted live-reptile fashion, and for the 49th Hunger Games, a turtle he was using as a belt buckle fell off and bit Drusilla Sickle, the escort, who then bit Magno back.[2] He sent the tributes to their interviews in their training outfits and was placed on probation because of it.[3]

50th Hunger Games[]
Magno arrived late to prepare his tributes for the chariot rides. Although he had promised new outfits for the Quarter Quell, he dressed the four District 12 tributes—Haymitch Abernathy, Wyatt Callow, Maysilee Donner, and Louella McCoy—in the same coal miner outfits he had used for years.[2]
When Proserpina and Vitus arrived at Magno's apartment to plan the makeup and hair for the District 12 tributes' interviews, they found him sick and inebriated from licking toads for their hallucinogenic venom.[1] Before the interviews, Magno arrived late carrying a snake, saying he needed to dress his tributes (who had already been dressed with clothes from Messalina and Silius Trinket). When Lou Lou saw the snake, she said "mine," and Magno instructed her on how to hold it. She wore the snake during her interview.[4]
Before Haymitch entered the arena, Effie had to meet him in the launch room because nobody was able to find Magno.[5]
As Haymitch set off on his Victory Tour, Plutarch Heavensbee informed him that Magno had been fired for negligence.[6]

Physical description[]
Magno had sun-leathered skin tattooed with a snakeskin pattern. He was often seen wearing live reptiles, including as a turtle belt buckle and garter snake earrings. For the chariot rides before the 50th Hunger Games, he wore a long shirt made of metal diamonds, sandals that laced all the way up his legs, and no pants.[2] He was older than 60 years, since he had been licking toads since the war, which ended 50 years prior.[1]

Personality and traits[]
Despite being the stylist for the tributes of District 12, Magno does not take his job seriously and puts no effort in helping the tributes gain sponsors through the tribute parade and their television interviews. Like his wife Drusilla, Magno does not treat the tributes like human beings, not caring about their lives and making them more presentable to the Capitol.
