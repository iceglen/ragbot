Eike Onyambu is a Kenyan actor who portrayed Tam Amber in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Biography[]
Onyambu graduated from the Music and Arts University of Vienna in 2022. As a child actor, he was involved in radio programs and did voiceovers. He also plays several instruments, including the violin, viola, and saxophone.[2]
Onyambu's credits include playing Romeo in the world premiere of the 2022 musical Romeo and Juliet, and the role of Henri in the operetta Der Opernball, both of which received critical acclaim.[1]
