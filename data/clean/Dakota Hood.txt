Dakota Hood is an American actress and singer who portrayed the District 10 female tribute in The Hunger Games film. She is one of the last actresses to be cast for a tribute that is mentioned only once and fell during the initial bloodbath, and is not involved in the film's bloodbath scenes.

Career[]
Apart from The Hunger Games, Hood has also been in an episode of Redrum as Stacy and was in the film Changeover. [1]
In 2014, she collaborated on the series Constantine, which was released on NBC. Hood has also released her debut single called "Front Page News" in 2018.
