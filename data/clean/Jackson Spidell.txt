Jackson Spidell portrayed the District 10 male tribute in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire. He is also Sam Claflin's stunt double.

Career[]
Previously, Spidell was a stuntman in The Hunger Games film. He has also appeared in The Expendables 2 and TRON: Legacy.
