Alma Coin's daughter was an unnamed girl who was born to President Alma Coin and an unnamed man.[1]

Biography[]
This girl was born and raised in District 13. She and her father both died in the pox epidemic "a few years" prior to the Second Rebellion.[1] It is unknown when she was born or how old she was when she passed.

Trivia[]
Coin is never mentioned as having any family in the Mockingjay novel, nor losing anyone to the pox epidemic. This backstory is exclusive to the film adaptation, mentioned briefly in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 1.[1]
