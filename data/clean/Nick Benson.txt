Nick Benson is an American actor who portrayed Jessup Diggs in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.[1]

Career[]
Benson graduated from the University of Southern California with a BFA in Acting for Screen, Stage, and New Media. He has played recurring roles in Netflix's Boo, Bitch and Disney+'s Big Shot. Benson is represented by SMS Talent and Untitled Entertainment.[1]
