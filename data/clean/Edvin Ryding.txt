Edvin Ryding is a Swedish actor set to play Vitus in the upcoming film, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.[1]

Career[]
Ryding became internationally known for his starring role as Prince Wilhelm in the Netflix show Young Royals.  Before his global breakthrough, he had already established a strong career in Sweden, appearing in roughly 20 Swedish television shows and films since the age of five. In 2022, he was recognized on the Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe list. He later made his Hollywood debut as Erik Sundqvist in 28 Years Later, this being his first Hollywood role.
