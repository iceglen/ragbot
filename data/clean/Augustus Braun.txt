Augustus Braun was the victor of the 67th Hunger Games from District 1. He was hailed as Panem's Favorite Son and was known as the "Cavalier Career".[1]

Biography[]
67th Hunger Games[]
Augustus was a Career Tribute in the 67th Hunger Games and presumably volunteered to participate.[1]

 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Augustus Braun's Victory Tour poster 		 	 
Victory Tour[]
On his Victory Tour, Capitol Couture describes him as having a bright and optimistic ensemble that contrasted his unkempt persona he cultivated during his Games. Furthermore, he was widely popular in the Capitol; they reportedly sang his praises and labelled him as a figure of reconciliation of the people due to his patriotic manner.[1]

 	 	 	 		 			 		 		 		 			Second poster of Augustus Braun 		 	 
Post Games[]
It is unclear if he ever mentored for a Hunger Games, as Gloss and Cashmere are known to have mentored for the 74th Hunger Games.
As District 1 victors are known to continue training even after they win[2], it is likely that Augustus continued his training.

Death[]
Although unknown how he died, Augustus was dead by the end of the Second Rebellion, as he was absent for the vote on a final 76th Hunger Games. He was likely a victim of the Victors' Purge.[3]

Etymology[]
Augustus was the first emperor of the Roman Empire. "Braun" is a German surname that also functions as a homonym for "brawn", meaning physical strength.

Trivia[]
Augustus was not mentioned in the original trilogy, but revealed by Capitol Couture, a Hunger Games website promoting the movies.[1]
