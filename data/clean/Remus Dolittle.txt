Remus Dolittle was a Gamemaker-in-training and resident of the Capitol. He was a downstairs neighbor of the Snow family, which comprised of Coriolanus Snow, Tigris, and their grandmother. 

Biography[]
Shortly after Remus had graduated the University, his family pulled in every favor they had to get him the position of Gamemaker-in-Training, hoping that it would provide a stepping stone to the future.[1]
Before the reaping of the 10th Hunger Games, Tigris ran down to the Dolittle's so she could have a proper iron to use with the shirt Coriolanus would wear later that day.[2] Tigris later stated that she knew the Dolittles would consider selling their home for the right price; they had claimed their apartment was too big for them, although they mainly planned on selling it due to the new taxes.[3]
On the morning of the 10th Hunger Games, Remus appeared at the Academy to inform the staff there that the male tribute from District 5, Hy, had passed away due to complications from asthma and couldn't be saved. Because of this, Casca Highbottom and Volumnia Gaul's conversation with Coriolanus Snow had to be halted for them to address the new loss.[4]
After the taxes were implemented and Coriolanus was sent to District 12 to become a Peacekeeper, the Dolittles helped Tigris and her grandmother put their apartment on the market.[5] His family later sold their apartment to Strabo Plinth, who began living there with his wife, Mrs. Plinth, so they could be closer to the Snows.[6]

Etymology[]
In Roman mythology, Remus was the twin brother of Romulus, who went on to found and rule Rome. Remus was killed either by his brother or one of his brother's supporters for criticizing the city. The surname Dolittle is rather literal and befitting of the Capitol's upper class: one who does little, an idler.
