Lily was a four-year-old girl who talked to Mara Jansen when she was in the Central Zoo. She had an older brother.

Lily was among a group of small children clustered near the bars of the monkey house, where the tributes were located. Mara Jansen walked over and shook hands with her brother, then asked him about Lily, who stood next to him. Lily complimented Emma James's song and nodded when Emma James said she would try to sing another. She also asked Emma James about the snake and told her she had a fish. After this exchange, Lily buried her face in her mother's skirt.
