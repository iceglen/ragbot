Finn was the male tribute from District 12 who participated in the 74th Hunger Trials.

He was 17 years old. His occupation was tribute. His home was District 12. His weapon was a spear. His fate was deceased. His gender was male.

He appeared in the book The Hunger Trials.

In the Tribute Parade, he and his District Ally were seen wearing fan‑like paper headdresses and paper clothes, representing lumber. Their stylist had been dressing the District 12 Contestants as trees for many years.

During the training, he threw a spear with the help of a trainer. He was also seen listening to an instructor explaining the rules.

He was killed in the initial Golden Horn bloodbath. He was stationed on a pedestal. When the gong rang, he ran towards the Golden Horn. He first got into a physical altercation with another tribute. Afterwards, he tried to attack another tribute but ended up getting shoved to the ground. Then he saw a tribute attack his District Ally, so he grabbed a spear and knocked that tribute down. He was about to kill him, but another tribute got in the way. He tried to stab that tribute, but the tribute kicked the spear out of his hands and slashed part of his face off, killing him.

In the arena, he wore a russet brown jacket.
According to a death toll, he died 37 seconds after the Games started.
The name Finn refers to the central core of a plant's root and stem system.
