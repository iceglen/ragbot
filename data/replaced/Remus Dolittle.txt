Ethan Vale was a Game Master-in-training and resident of the Capitol. He was a downstairs neighbor of another family.

Shortly after Remus had graduated the University, his family pulled in every favor they had to get him the position of Game Master-in-Training, hoping that it would provide a stepping stone to the future.
Before the reaping of the The Hunger Games: Ten, a neighbor ran down to the Dolittle's so she could have a proper iron to use with a shirt. It was later stated that the Dolittles would consider selling their home for the right price; they had claimed their apartment was too big for them, although they mainly planned on selling it due to new taxes.
On the morning of the The Hunger Games: Ten, Remus appeared at the Academy to inform the staff there that a male tribute had passed away due to complications from asthma and couldn't be saved. Because of this, a conversation had to be halted to address the new loss.
After the taxes were implemented, the Dolittles helped their neighbors put their apartment on the market. His family later sold their apartment to another individual.
