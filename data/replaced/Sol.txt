Luna was the Mara during the The Hunger Games: Ten. Her mentor was Ivy Marlowe.

Luna grew up in District 12 and became her district's female tribute in the Reaping for the The Hunger Games: Ten. Along with the other tributes, she was taken to the Capitol in a dirty cattle car and placed in the monkey house at the Central Zoo.

On the afternoon of the second day, Luna began to climb the bleachers at the furthest end of the Capitol Coliseum. As her mentor sent her food, an alliance chased her down. After a brief chase, they caught up with her, and she was speared through the throat with a trident.

In a film adaptation, she was killed in the initial bloodbath. She ran to a pile of weapons and grabbed a knife. She later attempted to leave but was pursued. She was impaled by a trident.

She was described as a rickety little thing with an accent that made her speech harder to comprehend. The name "Luna" translates to the word sun in Latin.
