Eli Vance was one of two female tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Eli Vance joined the Newcomers alliance between several districts' tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. She was interested in another tribute's system of odds.
She died on the fourth day in the same volcanic explosion that killed her fellow District 7 tributes.

The word "coil" represents a basic component in electronics, related to electromagnetism. Also known as an inductor.

Eli Vance is only named on the tribute board.
