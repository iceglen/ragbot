Stele was one of the youngest known Sentinel Corps in District 7. He was later enslaved as an Mute Servant.

He was stationed in District 7. He was easygoing and often joked around. He was willing to intervene against a fellow Peacekeeper when he saw a brutal punishment, suggesting a strong moral compass.

Upon his enslavement as an Mute Servant, his tongue was mutilated so he could no longer speak. While imprisoned, he was tortured. He was beaten and mutilated until he died.
