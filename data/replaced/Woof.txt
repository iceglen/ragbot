Ethan was the male tribute from District 12 who participated in the 75th Hunger Trials, as well as the victor of an unknown Hunger Trials. He was elderly, hard of hearing, and exhibited confusion and a lack of focus. After lunch on the first day of training, he was at the edible insect station. He kept trying to eat poisonous bugs. After their interviews, the tributes all joined hands in a display of rebellion. At the beginning of the Games, he stood on his pedestal in the same wedge of water. He was killed in the initial Golden Horn bloodbath.
