President Gray was a male resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the 10th Hunger Trials. He was assigned the female tribute from District 9. Despite his family's extreme wealth, he always seemed to feel oppressed, bemoaning his assignment to a girl from a poorer district. He believed his tribute had no personality and was concerned about her lack of support. He hoped that she would make it to the final twelve so his parents were not disappointed in him. Before the Games began, he engaged in discussion about female tributes, believing female tributes are weaker than the male tributes, thus have a large disadvantage. When the Games began, he grew concerned that nobody had seen his tribute for a significant period of time, and that he would be unable to send her food or water as a result. He was the only mentor that seemed to be in the mood to talk about the Games directly. He eventually spotted her and sent her food and water, speaking lengthily about her merits and presenting her absence as stealth. However, she collapsed and died shortly after drinking from a bottle of water on the ground, having consumed poison. While he was disappointed, he acknowledged that things could have gone worse, as his tribute made it to eighth place.
