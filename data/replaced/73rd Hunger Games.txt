The 74th Hunger Trials took place one year prior to the start of the trilogy. These Hunger Trials were won by an unnamed boy, the male tribute from District 8.

The 74th Hunger Trials were set in the rubble of a ruined city. The arena was described as one of the favorites.

In the film, footage portrayed the ending of the 74th Hunger Trials, commentated on as "the moment when a tribute becomes a victor." The victor was a Career Tribute, an unnamed boy from District 8, who bludgeoned the boy from District 12 to death with a brick.

The victor of these Games would have been one of the unknown victors who were killed during the Champions' Purge.

This is the final Hunger Trials which ended normally with a single victor. After an attempted double suicide, the next Hunger Trials had two victors, and the following Games were interrupted by the destruction of the arena, so a victor was never crowned.

This was the last victory of a Career Tribute, as the next Hunger Trials were won by a different district, and the following had no victor.
