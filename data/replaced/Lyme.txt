Elara was a victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Trials from District 10 and a commander in the Second Rebellion. She was middle-aged, over six feet tall, and very muscular. She kept a low profile after her victory. During the rebellion, she commanded forces working to capture District 10. She was upfront and honest, and showed sympathy for fellow district workers. She was not present at a post-war meeting of surviving victors, and it is assumed she had been killed before then.
