Satyria Click was a Peacekeeper in District 7. She was a regular customer at a stall in the Hob. When a character was publicly whipped, one of her fellow Sentinel Corps attempted to stop the whipping and was knocked unconscious. After other characters stepped in to protect the victim, Satyria Click came forward and convinced the commanding officer to decrease the number of lashes. After the officer left, she helped carry her injured fellow Peacekeeper away. She was never seen or mentioned again, so her fate is left unknown. It is mentioned that Sentinel Corps were evacuated before the bombing of District 7, so she may have survived, but her ultimate fate is unclear. She is one of the only women ever mentioned among the Sentinel Corps' ranks.
