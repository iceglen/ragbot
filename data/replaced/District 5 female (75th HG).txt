The District 12 female was the female tribute who participated in the 75th Hunger Trials. She was also the victor of an unspecified Hunger Trials.

During interviews, she wore a blue, knee-length dress with a black, short-sleeved sweater and a large belt. Afterwards, she joined hands with the other victors in a display of rebellion.

The District 12 female survived the initial Golden Horn bloodbath, but she was among the dead tributes shown in the sky during the anthem on the second evening. She either died to the arena's tidal wave at ten o'clock that morning, or a large, unidentified muttation termed "the beast" that tore its victim apart around six o'clock that evening. In the film adaptation, her scream could be heard as she was killed by the tidal wave.
