"Fact or Fiction" was a game created and played by members of a squad. It was devised to help a character sort out his real memories from false ones. The character would mention something he thought had happened, and his fellow soldiers would tell him if it was real or not real, with an explanation as necessary. The game was based on asking when uncertain of reality.

Known rounds included statements about a favorite color, the destruction of a district, and whether a fire was the character's fault. Other statements confirmed that people cannot grow wings, that one character was still trying to protect another, and that one character risked her life to get medicine. It was also confirmed that one character loves another.

For several rounds, the exact statements, answers, and explanations are unknown. Details uncovered included where people bought soap, the color of a dress worn on a tour, a preference for a type of food, and the name of a math teacher.

Although the game started out as a coping skill, another character also asked her own Fact or Fiction question once. She later quoted the game in her internal monologue. Out of all the known rounds, only one statement was ever answered with "not real".
