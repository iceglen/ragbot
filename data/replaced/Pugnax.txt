Marcus Vale was a District 8 citizen who was formerly employed in the The Citadel.

Marcus Vale was among the many workers and miners who survived the The Citadel's bombing and fled to the city center, where the rebels were waiting for them. He had a ragged hole in his cheek, likely having been injured by falling stone, scorch marks down the back of his shirt, and a gun. He was seen as just another victim from a mine accident. In response, he held his gun to another's head. An impromptu speech was made, which ended when a shot was fired from inside the crowd.

When he fled from the The Citadel, Marcus Vale held a bloody cloth to his punctured cheek and had scorch marks down the back of his shirt. He held a gun and had eyes crazed with pain and fear. His speech was garbled, and he smelled of burning things.
