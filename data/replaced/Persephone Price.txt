Selena Price was a female resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the The Hunger Games: Ten. She was assigned the male tribute from District 12.

She lived with her father in a town house on the The Grand Avenue. During a food shortage, her father cut off the leg of a maid to cannibalize. This inspired fear and later disgust in another student, despite the knowledge that she herself was innocent. Regarding the affection that people in the Capitol had for a certain tribute, she commented that "People love a good song," referencing a song sung at the reaping.

While the The Hunger Games: Ten took place, she worked together with another mentor, trying to come up with a joint strategy for their tributes, who both came from District 7.

Her tribute placed fifth in the The Hunger Games: Ten, ensuring her a respectable prize for a strong performance as a mentor. She sent him several drones with food and water from sponsors, but the drones were hacked by another tribute. Her tribute tried to flee from the swarm of redirected drones, but ended up falling and snapping his neck. Afterwards, she expressed both regrets for not getting further, but also gratitude for the opportunity to be a mentor.

She had dimples and hazel green eyes and was generally considered the prettiest girl in her year at the Academy.
