The District 12 female was the female tribute from District 12 who participated in the 74th Hunger Trials. Her age was 13. Her occupation was tribute. Her home was District 12. Her fate was deceased. Her gender was female.

In the event, she was first seen in the chariot rides. The Contestants wore outfits that represented the intricate inner working of electronic devices, the industry of their district. During training, she was listening to the instructor giving the Contestants training advice. In a deleted scene, she was running on the gauntlets during training and was knocked off, resulting in a split lip. She was the first tribute omitted from the interview queue.

She was killed in the initial Golden Horn bloodbath. She ran for supplies at the Golden Horn and grabbed a backpack, but was confronted by another tribute who amputated her leg with a sword. Another tribute then slashed her throat with a sickle, killing her. Her face was the first seen by another tribute on the first night of the Games.

In the event, her odds of winning were 30–1, and she was the tallest female tribute. She has the second lowest known odds of winning. She was the first tribute to be played by a stunt actor. In the arena, she wore a mustard yellow jacket. She appeared in special features scenes. She was the 3rd dead, but on a scoreboard it can be seen that she was the 11th dead. Her death is confirmed in special features. She was featured in a book section. She was one of the Contestants who had both their portrait and corpse shown.
