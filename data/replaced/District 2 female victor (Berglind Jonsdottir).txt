This unknown District 8 female was the victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Trials.

Years after her initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third 25-Year Hunger Games would be reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 8's reaping for the 75th Hunger Games. It is unknown whether or not she was reaped for the event, but she did not volunteer.

At the end of the Second Rebellion, she was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so she presumably died.
