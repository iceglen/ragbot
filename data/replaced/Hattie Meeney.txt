Mara was a woman from District 7 who brewed white liquor. She lived in District 7 where she brewed white liquor. The local Sentinel Corps turned a blind eye to her still and stall. She brewed the liquor in the woods outside the District and took on help by the time he was sixteen years old. On the day of the reaping for the 50th Hunger Games, he met her in the woods. She assigned him more responsibility than usual and then allowed him to have a drink of the white liquor as it was his birthday. At the end of their work, she told him to go and meet his girlfriend. Following his return and her death, he drove away the people he cared about, including Mara, for fear that they would be targeted next.

Mara was very hardworking, and she expected a lot from him. Despite her advanced age, she could do more than those half her age. Mara smile is described as "broken-toothed." He did a lot of the heavy lifting for Mara stall. Mara endeared herself to him, who she was likely training to be a partner in her liquor business, by giving him tips sometimes for his work.
