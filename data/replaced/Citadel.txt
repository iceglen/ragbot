The Fortress was a military institution located within the Capitol. It housed the War Department, guarded at all times by Sentinel Corps as a high-security zone. It was the base for Game Master subordinates, where they created muttations in underground laboratories.

The Fortress was gated, and visitors had to stop at the Peacekeeper station for a retinal scan, which was checked against the Capitol's files. Any bags they carried could be left at the station. With this done, visitors would go down a long, gray corridor and onto an elevator, either escorted by a Peacekeeper or unaccompanied.

The lab was located at least twenty‑five floors belowground. The mutts created were stored there as well.
