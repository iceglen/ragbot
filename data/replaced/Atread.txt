Atread was one of two male tributes from District 12 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

During the Tribute Parade for the 50th Hunger Games, the chariot carrying District 12 was crashed into by that of District 7 after the horses were startled by a firework and bolted. When they arrived at training, they were bandaged up in a lot of gauze.
Before the start of the Games, Atread joined the Newcomers, an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. He and the rest of his district hesitated at first but changed their minds when they were bullied by the Careers.
He died on the fourth day, likely due to the volcanic eruption on the mountain he and the other Newcomers had been staying on.

Like the others in his district, he had rickety limbs and looked like he'd never seen daylight.
Atread, along with the other three tributes from District 12, wore iridescent gray coveralls with wheel-shaped crowns during the chariot rides.

His name is suggestive of tire treads, associated with vehicles.
