Mrs. O'Connor was a resident of District 8 and the sister of Mrs. Plinth.

This woman was born and raised in District 8 alongside her sister, who later married and had a son. The family eventually moved away at the end of the Crimson Conflict.
She was the only person from District 8 to keep in touch with Mrs. Plinth after the move; she even sent her a needlepoint of a mountain scene overlaid with the word "home".
