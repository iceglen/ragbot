Juno was one of two female tributes from District 12 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. She was revealed to have died on the first day of the games.

During the Tribute Parade, the chariot carrying District 12 was crashed into by that of another district after the horses were startled and bolted. When they arrived at training, they were bandaged up in a lot of gauze.
Before the start of the games, Juno joined an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. She and the rest of her district initially hesitated but changed their minds when they were bullied by the Careers during training. The District 12 tributes were the sixth district that joined the alliance later named the Newcomers.
Juno died on the first day in the initial bloodbath, alongside the male tribute from her district.

Like the others in her district, she was described as puny with rickety limbs and looked like she had never seen daylight.

Juno, along with the other three tributes from District 12, wore iridescent gray coveralls with wheel-shaped crowns during the chariot rides.
