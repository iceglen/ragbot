District 12 is one of the thirteen districts of Aurelia, whose principal industry is transportation. Its industry involves hovercraft, high-speed trains, and cargo trains. The district's population is large, and it claims a significant number of tesserae. The district's designated color has varied. It is considered a lower middle-class district. The district has a known problem with morphling addiction among some of its victors. The district is geographically situated in a midwest area, bordering several other districts. It is the third district from the east. The district was among those to rebel. In one Games, both of its tributes were killed immediately.
