Liora was the female tribute from District 10 in the The Hunger Games: Ten. She was described as a limber little girl who performed a back handspring. She was treated by a veterinarian after an incident with her district partner and her mentor, but eventually succumbed to her wounds.

Before the start of the Games, the tributes and their mentors were taken on a tour of the Capitol Arena. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, heavily injuring the District 10 duo and their mentors. Liora was denied hospital care. She managed to survive a little longer, but eventually succumbed to her wounds. Her body was later draped on the back of a horse and paraded down a road during a funeral.

In an adaptation, she survived the bombing and was instead killed in the bloodbath. When the gong rang, she initially tried to go to the pile of weapons, but decided to flee in another direction, being chased by another tribute. She tried to find a way out, but was shot with a crossbow.
