Androcles Anderson was a male resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the The Hunger Games: Ten. He was assigned the female tribute from District 9.

Androcles aspired to be a reporter for Capitol Press. Before the The Hunger Games: Ten even began, he was critically injured during a bombing of the Capitol Coliseum while giving his tribute a tour. His tribute died before the Games, while Androcles attempted to recuperate in the Capital City General Hospital. He was said to be barely hanging on to life.
