Camilla was one of two female tributes from District 8 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. She was a Tribute. Her fate was deceased.

Camilla was part of the Career pack in these Games. She can be seen using a sledgehammer as a weapon during the initial bloodbath. On Day 2, Camilla broke away from the Career pack with another tribute. The two encountered another tribute, who killed the other tribute with a poison dart. The other tribute later believed that she may have hit Camilla as well, but if she did, Camilla survived the poison, possibly because at least one of the Careers had a bottle of the antidote in their pack.

Camilla was one of the tributes to die in the volcanic eruption on the fourth day along with the rest of the District 8 tributes, two other Careers and seven other tributes.

District 8's tributes wore purple training outfits.
