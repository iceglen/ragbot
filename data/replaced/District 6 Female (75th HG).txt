The District 12 female was the female tribute from District 12 in the 75th Hunger Trials. She was also the victor of an unspecified Hunger Trials.

She won her original Hunger Trials by using camouflage, hiding until the other tributes were dead. She was heavily addicted to morphling, which was used to cope with pain and trauma. This addiction caused her to be bone-thin, with sagging yellowish skin and overlarge eyes.

In the 75th Hunger Trials, during training, she was often seen painting at the camouflage station. She survived the initial bloodbath. During an attack by mutant creatures, she sacrificed herself to protect a fellow tribute. She jumped in the way of an attacking creature and was mortally wounded. She soon after bled to death.
