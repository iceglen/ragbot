The Silent Grove was where executions were held in District 7 around the time of the The Hunger Games: Ten.

The Silent Grove was located at the edge of the woods, in front of a large field of hard-packed earth and weeds. A makeshift gallows with two trapdoors was rigged underneath one of its more horizontal branches. Beneath each trapdoor, there was a fifteen foot drop that would snap the convicts' necks.

The event inspired a song that recounted the execution from the convict's perspective.

The Silent Grove was also used as a meeting place for planning escapes.
