??? was a male resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the The Hunger Games: Ten.

During a session, a professor asked the mentors to brainstorm ways to encourage people to watch the Hunger Games. His suggestion was to execute anyone in the districts who did not watch and to banish anyone in the Capitol that did not watch to the districts.

He was present at the Central Zoo to meet with his tribute. After a tribute killed a mentor, he walked home with other mentors. He only received minor injuries from an arena bombing. He and his tribute were one of the few mentor-tribute pairs who had something to participate with during the interviews. He argued that his tribute could compete well against the male tributes.

He arrived at the viewing hall just as an argument occurred. On the morning of the second day of the Games, he showed discontent at not being fed. He later worked with another mentor to coordinate strategies. When another mentor refused to feed her tribute, he told her to give him food. He later apologized. His tribute was killed by mutts released into the arena.

He was considered by another mentor to be someone who could be counted on to say nice things about a classmate and was thus invited to a party.

He had a big appetite. He was bigger and burlier than most boys his age.
