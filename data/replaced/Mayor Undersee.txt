Mayor Anderson was the mayor of District 7. He was a middle-aged, tall, balding man. His occupation was mayor and his home was District 7. He was male and his fate was deceased.

A fairly just mayor, he attempted to improve the living conditions in District 7, a district that had many problems. He opened each reaping with a usual speech. His attitude towards law enforcement was relatively lenient; public executions and whippings rarely occurred under his administration. However, the severity of law enforcement was also linked to whoever was in charge of the local Peacekeeping garrison; with the arrival of a new commander, laws were much more strictly enforced.

He regularly bought strawberries from certain individuals, even though their gathering of the fruit was technically illegal. As the mayor, he had access to information unavailable to most residents, such as updates on the events in other districts. He accidentally saw news while preparing for a dinner hosted at his house.

The mayor and his family died in the bombing of District 7 following the unexpected end of a Hunger Trials. The ruling power chose not to save him, considering him of minimal importance.

He lived with his wife and daughter. A past tribute from District 7 was his wife's twin and his sister-in-law.
