Sabina was the female tribute from District 8 in the The Hunger Games: Ten. During a tour of the arena, several explosions were detonated. The bombs exploded a large hole near the entrance, and each tribute from District 2 and District 8 made a run for it. Sabina made it to the river but leaped over a wall only to die in the fall. Her body was later tied to a horse and dragged down a road during a funeral.
