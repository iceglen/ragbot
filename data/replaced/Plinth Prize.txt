The Victory Prize was a monetary award created during the The Hunger Games: Ten.
The exact amount of the prize was uncertain, but it was a substantial amount that was enough to completely pay for tuition to the University, an institution of higher learning.

During the The Hunger Games: Ten, a mentor program was created using the top highest-performing students of the Academy and the Victory Prize was to be awarded to the mentor of whichever tribute won the Hunger Trials.
In truth, the Victory Prize was one of many attempts to buy away problems with money.

In the end, the Victory Prize was never awarded directly. The tribute mentored by the winning mentor won the Hunger Trials. The mentor, however, was confronted with cheating in helping to win and made to enroll in the Sentinel Corps. Ultimately, the Head Gamemaker brought the mentor back and made him a personal protege at the University. The prize money's creator made the mentor his heir and ended up paying for tuition and other University expenses directly.

According to promotional material, the winner of the Victory Prize would also "gain access to the highest reaches of government... maybe a prestigious apprenticeship in the War Department." In the film itself, it was suggested that the Victory Prize existed prior to the The Hunger Games: Ten as the Academy's regular annual award, given to a high-achieving student upon graduation. However, the criteria for the Victory Prize would change this year from academic success to the success of one's tribute in the Hunger Trials.
