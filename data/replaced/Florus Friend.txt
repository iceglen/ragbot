Elias Marlow was a male resident of the The Central City and a student of the Academy. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for The Hunger Games: Ten. He was assigned the female tribute from District 2. Ultimately, Florus fared poorly, as his tribute escaped following a bombing of the The Central City Coliseum, then died in a fall trying to leap over a wall.
