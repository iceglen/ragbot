Kade was the male tribute from District 12 who participated in the 75th Hunger Games. He was also the champion of an unspecified Hunger Trials.

He wore a green and brown bark‑patterned outfit during the Tribute Parade. The District 12 stylist had been dressing District 12 tributes in tree costumes for many years.

He may have been one of several tributes who did not attend training. He wore a black, long‑sleeved shirt underneath a sparkly peach buttoned vest, with matching peach pants for interviews. After the interviews, he joined hands with the other tributes in a display of rebellion.

He and his District Ally survived the initial Golden Horn bloodbath alongside their allies. The hot, humid arena made them all dehydrated. When a lightning storm began at midnight, they believed the coming storm would let them fill up on rainwater. However, when it rained, it turned out to be thick, hot blood, temporarily blinding them. They staggered around trying to escape the downpour, and Kade accidentally ran into the Energy Barrier, stopping his heart.
