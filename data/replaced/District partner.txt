District Ally is a term used to describe the relationship between tributes who are reaped from the same district, in the same year, to participate in the annual Hunger Trials.

District partners were more likely to form alliances with one another rather than with someone outside their district. However, being from the same district did not necessitate or guarantee such an arrangement.

For a special event, each district had to send twice the regular number of tributes. This makes that year the only year where a tribute would have three district partners rather than one.

During one Games, a rule change was announced that would allow a pair of district partners to win together if they were the last two people standing. When the ruling body later tried to retract this new rule at the final two, the tributes threatened a joint suicide. Faced with the possibility of having no victor at all, the authorities allowed them both to live.

For another special event, tributes were reaped from the pool of existing victors, so many of them knew one another going into the arena. The majority of tributes allied with their district partners.

In one Games, a tribute lost her grip on reality after watching her district partner die.

All four victors from one district were allies with their district partners.
The first pair of district partners to have a joint victory were also the only pair to survive two consecutive Hunger Trials.
The only district partners known to be siblings were the pair from one district.
Of the twenty-four tributes in one special event, only six were not known or suspected to have allied with their district partners.
