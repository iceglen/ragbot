Ryan was one of two male tributes from District 10 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, it was noticed that everyone from his district had sunflower necklace tokens. There was an instruction to get them to join a particular alliance as their tokens could be used for a plan to sabotage the arena. It was thought their efficiency and capabilities would make them good allies.

While training, an offer was made to join a different pack but it was refused. In retaliation, one of their tokens was broken. It was later fixed and he, along with the rest of his district, decided to join the specified alliance. They weren't very sociable and almost appeared exclusive.

He died in the initial bloodbath along with everyone else in his district. Two of their tokens were retrieved by another tribute.
