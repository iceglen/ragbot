"Sly Vixen" was the Lena from District 12 in the 74th Hunger Games. She was nicknamed "Sly Vixen" because of her sly, fox‑like appearance.

She was reaped as the Lena from District 12. She avoided confrontations and survived by scavenging, foraging, and stealing from other contestants. She observed other tributes from afar to decipher their plans. She figured out a path through a minefield to steal a small amount of supplies from a stockpile. She hid inside a structure during a feast, quickly snatched a backpack, and evacuated the area without confrontation. Her strategy ended when she unwittingly consumed poisonous nightlock berries she had stolen, resulting in her death. She placed 4th in the Games.

She avoided confronting other tributes and kept a distance. She observed other tributes from afar to understand their plans. She took unnoticeable amounts of food and supplies. She deciphered a safe path through explosives to reach supplies. She worked alone and was sly and elusive. She managed to reach the final four tributes without making a single kill.

She had red hair and a face compared to that of a fox. She was described as small with a red coat and amber eyes.

She proved to be one of the smartest, trickiest, and stealthiest tributes. She stole supplies, made herself forgettable to avoid attention, and could find things others overlooked.
