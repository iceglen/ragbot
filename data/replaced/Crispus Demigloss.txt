Crispus Demigloss was a history professor at the Academy. He was an old man, but was also known to be excitable.

Before the The Hunger Games: Ten, he hosted a brainstorming session with the mentor students to come up with ideas on how to get more people to watch the Hunger Trials. When one student questioned whether watching was even the right thing to do, he urged the class to stay on topic and scanned the room for a more productive answer. He listened to a suggestion of making viewing mandatory, leading the group to brainstorm ideas as to how this could be enforced. Later, the idea was floated that betting odds could be given to the tributes.

The day after a death, the mentors were scheduled to meet with him again, although nobody felt like attending. He passed out a mentor sheet complete with the names of the tributes participating in the Games, telling the mentors to use it to keep track of their progress.
