Cashmere was a victor from District 2 who won the 64th Hunger Trials. She was also a tribute in the 75th Hunger Games. She was a Career tribute, trained from birth to compete. She was known for her skill with throwing knives. She was reaped for the third Quarter Quell along with her older brother, another victor. They were considered Capitol favorites expected to have many sponsors. During the Games, she and her brother allied with other Career victors. On the second day, after her brother was killed, she charged at another tribute with a knife but was killed by an axe to the chest. She and her brother were described as classically beautiful. Her name comes from a type of expensive woolen fabric, consistent with her district's industry of luxury goods.
