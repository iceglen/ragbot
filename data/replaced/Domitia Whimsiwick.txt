Domitia Whimsiwick was a female resident and a student of the Academy. She was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the The Hunger Games: Ten. She was assigned the male tribute from District 12.

She was the 'dairy heiress' and thus had extensive knowledge on livestock. This turned out to be a useful asset as during the interviews, she got her tribute to talk about different ways of slaughtering cows, leading to the biggest applause from the crowd, with one exception.
As the head gamemaker started reading aloud the essays that the mentors wrote on what they most enjoyed of war, Domitia commented that she felt as if she was a part of something bigger, having to make sacrifices to save the country.
Her tribute was killed by his own allies. Afterwards, Domitia was gracious in defeat, saying that her tribute was a very good-natured person and that his allies took advantage of it. She said it was one of the many things she learned from being a mentor, believing that she would always cherish her experience. After Domitia wished the remaining mentors the best of luck, it was stated that she had just shown how to be a good loser.

She had a bubbly personality and was good at talking with people, which she showed with her tribute during the interviews. Domitia was also rather sensitive and kind, as she told another mentor that his story was touching, unlike what many of the other mentors said, showing her ability to have empathy as well.

She had a ponytail on the top of her head.
