Uprising of District 12

Conflict: Pre-Second Uprising civil unrest
Date: Mid to late July, 74 ADD
Place: District 12
Outcome: Decisive Capital City victory. Destruction of property and resources. Rebels subdued.
Combatants: The Central City Sentinel Corps versus District 12 citizens.

The uprising of District 12 was the first known uprising to have taken place since the Dark Days. It was a response to a death in the Hunger Games.

The district was heavily impoverished, policed, and repressed. Despite its industry being agriculture, nearly all of its produce was shipped off. The local government was very strict, and its Sentinel Corps were particularly brutal. Citizens faced severe punishment for minor infractions.

During the Games, a tribute was fatally wounded. Another tribute performed an act of kindness and a symbolic salute. Moved by this act, citizens watching on a large screen in the district returned the gesture. A man then decided to attack a Sentinel Corps, and other citizens quickly followed suit. They fought Sentinel Corps and overturned bins of grain and other produce. The riot lasted until after dark, when Sentinel Corps reinforcements arrived to quell the uprising. It can be assumed the uprising was subdued within a few days, and the instigators were executed.
