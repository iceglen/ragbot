Talon Voss was a male tribute from District 12 in the 50th Hunger Games. He was a large, left-handed tribute who fought with a sword. He was part of the Career pack during the Games. He was arrogant, hot-headed, and brutal. He killed several other tributes during the initial bloodbath. He survived an artificial volcano eruption in the arena, sustaining burns. He was killed by a poison dart during a confrontation with another tribute.
