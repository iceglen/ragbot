Evelyn was the Capitol's Minister of Affairs and one of President Gray's Cabinet members. She had been the Director of Labor Operations in District 12 before becoming the Capitol Minister of Affairs. She led the Capitol Ministry of Information, a branch that extended throughout Aurelia to improve the nation's functionality through data collection and analysis. Her role was to ensure the divisions worked together efficiently.

Evelyn wrote a speech referring to district citizens as rebels. However, Frost disapproved of her terminology because he didn't want to legitimize the movement, so Evelyn put forth "radicals" or "criminals" as alternatives. Evelyn later tried to dissuade Frost from launching an assault on a field hospital, but he justified the punishment as being derived from the language of her speech. She was also with President Gray in his office when the power cut out after rebels destroyed a dam.

Evelyn was present at a dinner with other high-ranking Capitol officials. She watched and did not intervene as Frost poisoned an official. Evelyn later woke Frost to inform him that a rebel was still alive and showed him footage of a squad in the city's sewers. When she saw blood on Frost's tissue, she asked if she should call for a doctor, and Frost declined.

After the war, Frost was tried and sentenced to death, and a number of his collaborators were still awaiting trial at the time of his execution. Evelyn likely would have been among those arrested.

Evelyn had strong management skills. Although Evelyn was a high-ranking Capitol official, she showed hesitance and did not appear to entirely agree with the Capitol's harsh regime. It's likely that she tread carefully to save herself from being poisoned like some of Frost's other associates.

Evelyn wore a red and gray skirt suit, as well as ivory gloves that reminded her not to fidget. Her hair was elegantly twisted, and she had gold eyeliner and jewel earrings.
