Crassus Frost was a military general. He was known for having an excellent head for strategy, nerves of steel, and a commanding presence. He quickly climbed the ranks. He was remote and strict. He was killed during the war, leaving his son an orphan. He was honored on a national day for heroes. He owned a dress shirt and a handkerchief which were later used by his son. He was depicted with pale blue eyes and fair hair. He was remembered for his deep voice and uncompromising words.
