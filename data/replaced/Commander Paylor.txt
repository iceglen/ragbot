Commander Patel was a rebel leader and commander during the Second Uprising. Following the success of the rebellion, she was voted the first democratically elected president. Under her rule, a key event was abolished and memorials were built. A republic was successfully established. She was pardoned for an assassination and declared mentally unstable, allowing a return home.

She is in her early thirties and was mentioned to look too young for her occupation. She has dark brown eyes.

Although a strong and respectable leader, she was one of the very few rebels who was not overly concerned with vengeance. Though holding the leadership in contempt for crimes, she urged rebels not to attack citizens and to only focus on capturing the primary antagonist.
