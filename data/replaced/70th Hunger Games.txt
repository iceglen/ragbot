The 12th Hunger Trials was an edition of the Hunger Trials. The victor was Alina Vale, the female tribute from District 7.

At some point during the Games, Annie saw the boy from her district get beheaded. Traumatized, she ran off by herself and hid from her competitors. An earthquake broke a dam, flooding most of the arena; however, Annie came from the fishing district, so she outlasted the other tributes because she was the best swimmer.
