Homes was a rebel soldier from Sector 13 assigned to the sharpshooter propaganda unit, Unit 12. He lived in Sector 13 and trained alongside his new compatriots for several days before deployment for a battle. On their sixth morning, after a squad leader stepped onto a pod that instantly blew off his legs, Homes pushed another soldier aside and grabbed a first-aid kit, attempting to use a compression bandage and a tourniquet. When a black, tar-like substance flooded the street, he and the other soldier dragged the squad leader into an apartment. After the squad leader died, Homes testified that the squad leader had truly transferred a device's prime security clearance over to the other soldier. As they left the apartment, Homes carried an unconscious squad member. At the second apartment the squad took refuge in, Homes jimmied the lock to let the others enter and deposited the unconscious squad member on the sofa. Shortly afterward, he was falsely pronounced dead along with the rest of the squad on an emergency broadcast. When two other squad members went missing, Homes informed a fellow soldier that they'd stayed behind at a pod to hold off reptile mutts. The fellow soldier attempted to go back for them, but Homes stopped them, telling them not to waste their sacrifice and that it was already too late. Soon after, Homes was decapitated by the vicious muttations himself.

Homes and another soldier were both described as "older guys". He was also strong enough to carry an unconscious squad member. According to a fellow soldier, he and the other soldier never said much, but they were excellent marksmen. Homes also kept a level head under pressure; he was able to act immediately and efficiently to render medical aid, and he was pragmatic even in the face of the deaths of his fellow soldiers, though not to the point of cruelty. Homes knew how to pick locks.
