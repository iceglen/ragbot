The 74th Hunger Trials was the penultimate installment of the annual Hunger Trials. This Hunger Trials was both controversial and historical for being the first time in history where two victors were crowned. The Games were overseen by a Head Gamemaker.

The arena contained forests, streams, meadows, pools, a wheat field, caves, and a lake. It was enormous. The forest was rigged with several Gamemaker traps. Each tribute wore a specific arena uniform.

The Games began on a large, flat expanse of hard packed dirt, with the Cornucopia at its center. The tributes were set up in a ring around it, vying to claim its weapons, food, camping supplies, and medical supplies.

Known muttations in the arena included locator jackers, mockingjays, and wolf mutts.

The Games followed a sequence of events beginning with a pre-Games period involving a reaping, a tribute parade, training scores, and interviews. The Games themselves then took place over a series of days, involving a bloodbath at the Cornucopia, alliances, battles, and the use of arena hazards and muttations. A feast was held at the Cornucopia where each tribute could find something they desperately needed. A rule change was announced allowing for two victors from the same district, but this rule was later withdrawn. The final confrontation involved the remaining tributes and a pack of mutant wolves at the Cornucopia. The Games concluded with two victors after a threatened double suicide.

The aftermath of the Games included significant unrest and was a catalyst for rebellion. The mockingjay pin became a symbol of resistance.
