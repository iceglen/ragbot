Mara Jansen was a female tribute from District 7 and the victor of the The Hunger Games: Ten. She was a musician and a member of a nomadic group. She was reaped as a tribute for the games. During the reaping ceremony, she sang defiantly. Her mentor was a top-performing student at the Academy.

Before the games, her family members were killed. She and other surviving members of her group were trapped in District 7, where  they made  living  through musical performances.

At the start of the games, she fled from the initial weapon scramble. During the games, she was chased by her fellow tribute, who was suffering from a disease. She comforted him as he died. She used poison to kill another tribute. Specially bred snake muttations were released into the arena. She sang to the snakes, which gathered around her without attacking. She later used a snake against another tribute in self-defense. When only two tributes remained, she poisoned a water source and exhausted her opponent, leading to his death. She was declared the victor.

After the games, she returned to District 7 and resumed performing. She planned to run away. She and her mentor met in the woods. He became suspicious of her and she disappeared into the woods while singing. Her ultimate fate is unknown. Recordings of her games were erased and her name faded from common memory. She is considered a mysterious victor who vanished.

She was known for her confident stage presence and ability to charm audiences. She showed signs of trauma from her experiences. She was described as wearing a colorful, ruffled dress. Her name originates from a poem. She wrote a song about a hanging tree. She knew about edible wild plants.
