Jax Guerrero was a resident of District 7 whose son participated in the The Fire Games.

Tygart was only mentioned in an early version of a script for a film. He and his wife lived in the Seam, and their son was reaped for the The Fire Games.
The son was killed during the Games. Later that same day, another character saw his wife and Tygart at the Hob. He was selling chestnuts when she gave him two purple berries she had picked earlier, explaining that they would help his wife sleep.
