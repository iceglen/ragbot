In the films, the Capital Capital Avenue is a major road in the Capitol. The The Training Hub sits at one end, while a large governmental building sits at the other.

On the second day leading up to the annual Hunger Games, tributes rode chariots down the Capital Avenue in specially‑made costumes inspired by their districts' industries. The procession ended when the chariots pulled up in front of a large government building, where a welcoming speech was given.
An execution was later held on the Capital Capital Avenue. This also made it the site of an assassination.

In the novels, the The Training Hub and the Presidential Palace were both located in the Capital Loop. However, in the films, the Capital Avenue and Capital Loop appear to be two different locations, placing the The Training Hub and the Presidential Palace in different parts of the city.
