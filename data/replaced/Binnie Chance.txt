Bethany Collins was a resident of District 7. She was a member of a family known for their reckless and sometimes rebellious behavior.

Her brother was sentenced to be hanged. The night before his execution, someone filed halfway through the rope, so when they tried to hang him the next day, the rope snapped. He fell to the ground and was shot dead.

A witness reported seeing another individual leaving the square, so that person was taken for questioning. However, Bethany Collins claimed responsibility. She had been living with a heart condition, so three days after being taken into custody, Bethany Collins died in her cell and the other individual was released.
