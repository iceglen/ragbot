Pliny "Pup" Harrington was a male resident of the The Central City and a student of the Academy. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the The Hunger Games: Ten. He was assigned the female tribute from District 12.

His nickname "Pup" was given to distinguish him from his father. He had a crew cut and shined shoes but was a natural slob who often had bits of food stuck in his braces. He did not think much of his tribute, and was unable to get anything out of her during a mentor-tribute session. He also failed to prepare her for the interviews.

However, his tribute took the first kill of the day, and he gave a fist pump before giving her a bottle of water as a sponsor gift. This was the first attempt to deliver a gift to a tribute in the arena. He was later invited to join an interview, where he rattled off every detail he could remember about his tribute, including some possibly fabricated ones. He later sent his tribute a slice of bread after another event in the arena.

When a prize for the mentor whose tribute won the games was announced, he let out a whoop. On the third day of the Games, his tribute was approached by other tributes. He kept shouting out his thoughts and advice, getting on the other mentors' nerves. When his tribute was killed, he cried out in shock. He left the mentor section, slammed his chair down and strode out of the hall, attempting not to cry.
