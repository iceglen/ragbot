Luna Wheat was the female tribute from District 10 in the 75th Hunger Games, and the victor of an unspecified Hunger Trials. She resided in the Champion's Village in District 10.

She participated in and won an unknown edition of the Hunger Trials before being reaped for the 75th Hunger Games.

During the Tribute Parade, tributes were costumed as loaves of bread; this was presumably District 10's tributes, since it had food-producing industries. In the film adaptation, she and her district partner were dressed in golden outfits possible designed to look like wheat or bread instead.

It is unknown if she showed up for training, as she was never mentioned. In the film, she was seen talking to other tributes in the waiting room.

During the interviews, she wore a sleeveless top and a white necklace and a black skirt over a pair of slacks matching her shirt color. After the tributes were interviewed, she, along with the other tributes, joined hands as a sign of rebellion.

She was killed during the initial Cornucopia bloodbath. She was launched into the arena on a pedestal and was killed towards the end of the bloodbath.
