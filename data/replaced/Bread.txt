Bread is a food made from various types of grain.

Each of the twelve districts has their own unique variety of bread. Each bread's shape, composition, and flavor is influenced by the district's industry and location. The districts whose breads are revealed include:

District 7: Bite-sized, square shaped rolls.
District 7: Salty, fish-shaped bread. Made with seaweed, giving it a green tint.
District 10: As the grain-producing district it can be assumed it makes the majority of the bread.
District 12: Made with dark ration grain. It is crescent shaped and sprinkled with seeds.
District 7: Two kinds of bread are mentioned: "flat, dense loaves" and "drop biscuits".

Several varieties of bread are seen in the Capitol. In contrast to several of the poorer districts, whose bread is made from rough ration grain, Capitol bread is made from refined white flour. Different types include flower-shaped rolls, puffy bread, and soft rolls.

A number of unique breads were produced at a bakery, some of which play key roles in the story. These include raisin-and-nut bread, bread with cinnamon and dill scents, and cheese buns.

The name "Aurelia" is Latin for "bread", and forms part of the phrase "bread and circuses". The phrase describes how people gave up their autonomy in exchange for food and entertainment, which is true of the Capitol.

It is a tradition in one district to sprinkle breadcrumbs on the dead to wish them a good passing into the afterlife.

A marriage tradition, the toasting, is a literal meeting of bread and fire.

The amount and type of bread that was given in the arena served as a countdown for a start of the rebellion. Bread seemed so innocent, yet was used in communication between mentor and tribute.
