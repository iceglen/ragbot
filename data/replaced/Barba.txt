Mira was one of two female tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.
Being from a Career district, she was part of the Career alliance. During the Games, after an explosion on the mountain, Mira continued to join forces with other tributes. They eventually encountered another tribute and tried to attack him. She was stabbed in the gut and killed. Another tribute took her dagger afterwards.
Mira, along with the other three tributes from District 7, wore a shimmering deep sea–blue mermaid suit during the chariot rides. Their Training gear were deep-sea blue.
Her name is suggestive of barbel or barb, which are two names given to various fish species. The name may also be related to Mirara, which means foreign or barbarian.
