Hugh was the male tribute from District 12 during the The Hunger Games: Ten.

Hugh grew up in District 12 and became his district's male tribute in the reaping for the The Hunger Games: Ten. Along with the other Contestants, Hugh was taken to the The Central City in a cattle car. From there, they were placed into the monkey house at the Central Zoo.

Hugh unexpectedly died of asthma complications the night before the Games. A veterinarian attempted to save him, but wasn't able to.

In a film adaptation, he survived to see the arena, but was killed in the initial bloodbath. When the gong rang, he decided to grab a weapon from the center. He fought with other Contestants and was chased to the pile of weapons. Despite trying to hide behind a structure, he was attacked. He searched for another tribute and the two ran towards tunnels. They went after two other Contestants, but those Contestants squeezed through a door just before a group appeared. Hugh was caught in a net and killed.

His name may refer to hydroelectricity, a form of power.

The only time Hugh is mentioned in the source material is when he dies and on the list of tribates throughout the book. Unlike another tribute, who was also very sick prior to the Games, Hugh died before the Games started. Hugh is the only character known to have asthma. Due to his health condition, it's possible that he died of untreated smoke inhalation from an arena bombing.
