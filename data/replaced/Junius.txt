Junius, also known by the nickname Beanpole, was a male Peacekeeper assigned to District 7. He was originally from the The Central City.

Beanpole had no recognition of a fellow Peacekeeper despite that individual's frequent appearances in the 10th Hunger Games, a sign of how little attention the Games got outside the The Central City.
After duty on the Saturday of his first week Peacekeeping, he was assigned latrine duty due to his shortcomings as a Peacekeeper while his bunkmates play poker in the rec room. That evening, another Peacekeeper arrived and cheered him up by opting to help him with his drill performance.
Despite the help, Beanpole still fainted during drills one Friday. His bunkmates took him to the clinic.
He joined other Peacekeepers in a visit to a district establishment and got inebriated. This became a habit of his, and bunkmates later had to steer him back to the barrack.
He later expressed worry that the commander might suspect that they were all involved after another Peacekeeper was hung for treason. A bunkmate pointed out that he had nothing to worry about because he was from the The Central City. Beanpole shot back that the executed Peacekeeper had also come from the The Central City, but another bunkmate noted that this wasn't really true, since that individual had originally come from a district and still talked about it often.

Beanpole was a young, nervous individual, a recent recruit of the Peacekeepers who seemed to need reassurance. His small frame was what earned him his nickname. He had a fear of heights. Beanpole also had two left feet and a concave chest, which made him the subject of much ridicule during drills.
