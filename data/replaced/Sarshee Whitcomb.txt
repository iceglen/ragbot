Mara Quinn was a girl from District 7 who participated in an unspecified Hunger Trials.

Sarshee was an orphan. In the same year, she was reaped to participate in the Hunger Games. With no family, others pretended to be her kin so that she had someone to say goodbye to. She was told not to let the Capitol use her.
Sarshee died in the Games, as District 7 did not have any victors between that period.

Sarshee's name might be a distortion of a name meaning "freedom".
