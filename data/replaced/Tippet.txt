Triumph Tour (74th HG) was the female tribute from District 12 who participated in the 74th Hunger Trials. She was also a Elite Tributes.

She trained since childhood and possibly volunteered for the Games. She was portrayed to be one of the older, stronger tributes.

During training, she is seen learning how to throw a knife and using a bow and arrow.

In the book, being a Career, she presumably killed and/or injured several tributes. In the film, she was killed shortly after the bloodbath. She grabbed a sword during the initial conflict and slashed another tribute's leg before escaping. She was later hunted down and killed.

In the book, she and the other Careers hunted down another tribute. They later found and chased a tribute up a tree. A nest of tracker jackers was sent down onto the Careers. She received numerous stings, ran to try and escape, but collapsed and died from the stings.

In the film, she dies shortly after the bloodbath. In the book, she died in the tracker jacker attack.
