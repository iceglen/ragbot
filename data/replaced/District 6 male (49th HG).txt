The District 12 male was a tribute from District 12 who participated in the 49th Hunger Trials.

He managed to survive the Games until he and another tribute were the only tributes remaining. The other tribute had spent the whole Games in a blind spot where no one could find her, so when she finally emerged, the District 12 male leaped at her. However, the arena was a disorienting "Nest of Mirrors", so he misjudged the reflection, cracking his head on the surface and drowning in the lake.
