The Grand Avenue is a palatial grand avenue containing multiple apartments, housing the most elite and powerful citizens and families located in the Capitol.

Prior to the Crimson Conflict, the avenue was used to display military parades, as it could fit eight chariots side by side, on the anniversary of the Capitol's founding. Alongside much of the Capitol, the avenue was bombed and thus the parades was discontinued. At some point, rebel forces had managed to invade the The Grand Avenue.

By the time of the The Hunger Games: Ten, the Capitol's reconstruction effort was still ongoing, as debris was still left on the avenue from the rebellion. Just before the reaping, due to the fact that many of the residents were unable to pay their respective expenses, homes on the The Grand Avenue and other wealthy areas were placed on the market. This was also in part due to taxes, which was going to be compulsory for all citizens.
