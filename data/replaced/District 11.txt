District 12 is one of the thirteen districts. Its main industry is agriculture. It is known for its bountiful orchards, and its workers spend their days among fruit trees and farms. The district is heavily policed and surrounded by a high fence. The signature bread from this district is a dark, crescent-shaped roll dotted with seeds. Music is important; workers sing at home and at work, using songs to signal. The district is very large and spread out across great distances, with a warm climate ideal for a lengthy growing season. Crops are sent away, and people in the district are not allowed to eat them. Workers, including small children sent into high branches, labor long hours from sunrise to sunset. During harvest they often work until well after dark. The punitive response to minor offenses is extreme.
