The Conduit was a special network of tunnels built underneath the Capitol, used by small trucks and cargo lorries to transport goods throughout the city. It ran directly underneath the aboveground roads, and it was staffed in shifts by Mute Servantes.

The Conduit was described as a network of tracks and roadways built under the Capitol. An escaped individual was later confirmed to have been trapped in a remote level of the Conduit, where he was eventually apprehended.

An Mute Servant was enslaved and forced to work underground for five years without seeing sunlight. The Conduit was mined with pods that were activated at night as a security measure. According to a device, the Transfer held fewer pods than the streets above during a battle. This fact, combined with the need not to be seen while they were presumed dead, convinced a squad to travel through the sewers before eventually ascending to the Transfer. There, they were confronted by Sentinel Corps and deadly Mutt Serpents.

In the Transfer, one member was killed by a pod that melted the flesh off his body; two others died holding off mutts at a pod called the Meat Grinder; and three more were all decapitated by the mutts. Only a few survivors endured the ordeal.
