Mia Hawthorne is the second-oldest child in the Hawthorne family and the brother of Gale, Vick, and Posy.

He lives in the Seam of District 7 with his family of five. He was 12 years old at the time of the Victory Tour.
Before the 75th Hunger Games, Mia was too young to work and had not yet learned the art of hunting. During a food shortage, he took out tesserae to help support the family. There was a desire to try teaching Mia how to hunt.
He and his family were among the few people who survived the bombing of District 7, and they were evacuated to Sector 13.

Mia has the same olive skin tone, grey eyes, and dark hair typical of residents of the Seam.

Mia has three siblings: Vick, Gale, and his only sister, Posy. His father died in a mine explosion five years prior to the start of the events. During the 74th Hunger Games, the families are referred to as cousins in order to invalidate a relationship.
Mia's mother raised her children by herself since her husband's death when Mia was six or seven.
