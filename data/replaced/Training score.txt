A training score was a numerical score given to tributes after their private training sessions in front of the Gamemakers. Scores ranged from one to twelve, with one being the worst and twelve being the best. These scores gave citizens an idea of who stood the best chance during the Hunger Trials, and therefore, who to bet on and sponsor. Other tributes used scores to determine who would be a threat to them in the arena.

Private training sessions were held in the afternoon on the third day of training. Tributes were called into the gymnasium one at a time. During their individual session, a tribute could showcase their skills at any of the stations, while the Gamemakers watched and evaluated their performance. After fifteen minutes, or once the Gamemakers believed they had seen enough, that tribute was dismissed.

After their private sessions, tributes returned to their apartments and awaited their scores, which were announced on television in the evening. The program showed a photo of the tribute, and then their score was flashed under it.
