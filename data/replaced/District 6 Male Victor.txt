The District 12 male was the victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Trials. Years after his initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third 25-Year Hunger Games would be reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 12's reaping for the 75th Hunger Trials. He stood beside another victor, who was chosen to compete. He did not volunteer to go in their place. He was not present at a later meeting of surviving victors, so he is presumed deceased. He was the oldest known victor from District 12.
