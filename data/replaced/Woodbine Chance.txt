Jasper Quinn was reaped to be one of the two male tributes from District 7 to participate in the 50th Hunger Games.

Jasper Quinn and other boys in his family were considered wild by other residents of District 7.
Woodbine was the fourth child to be reaped for the 50th Hunger Games, a 25-Year Hunger Games that called for four instead of two children to be reaped. He was to be the last tribute. After hearing his name called, Chance decided to run with the encouragement of his family. He ran towards a nearby alleyway where he was shot in the head from the rooftop of the Justice Building. His death caused disarray as onlookers either tried to flee or help Woodbine. As the Sentinel Corps tried to restore order, Woodbine's mother tried to prevent them from taking his body away. His death also signaled the need for another male tribute to be reaped.

He was described as the youngest and the handsomest of the Chance boys.
