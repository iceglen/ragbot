Miz was the male tribute from District 12 during the The Hunger Trials: Ten. He finished in 5th place. He was in an alliance with his district partner and another male tribute. Early in the Games, the alliance attacked mentors exiting the arena. On the second day, the alliance killed a female tribute. On the third day, Miz mounted a tall pole to attack a tribute positioned on a horizontal beam. He was fought off and injured his knee. His district partner then killed the tribute on the beam. Afterward, the alliance betrayed and killed their other ally. Miz and his district partner retreated to the tunnels. On the fourth day, Mutt Serpents were released into the arena. His district partner was killed by the snakes. Miz climbed a pole to safety and refused to help another tribute also seeking refuge. He decided to stay on the beam. On the fifth day, the other tribute returned and used modified drones to attack him. The drones circled and hit him, damaging his eye. As he tried to escape, he put too much weight on his injured knee, fell from the beam, and died from the impact.
