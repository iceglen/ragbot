Mayor Anderson was the mayor of District 7 during the 50th Hunger Trials.

A deal was worked out that allowed a person to practice music on the piano in the mayor's house, provided they occasionally performed at dinners and gatherings.

At the start of the reaping for the 50th Hunger Trials, the mayor tried to neutrally read the Treaty of Dominion, but a hint of disapproval was evident. As such, she was likely to be replaced soon after. She was considered a decent mayor by the people of the district.
