Cassandra Snow is the granddaughter of Corin Snow, the President of Aurelia. She is a student living in the The Central City. She is a minor character. She is shown to admire a certain victor, adopting that victor's signature braid as a fashion trend, which displeases her grandfather. However, he shows affection towards her, particularly when she expresses a desire for profound love. During a televised speech declaring association with a certain symbol illegal, she quickly undid her braid upon realizing what it represented. A proposed plan involving her participation in a final The Fire Games was not implemented. Her ultimate fate is unknown, but she is presumed alive.
