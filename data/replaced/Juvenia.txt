Eli Grayson was the escort for the District 2 tributes during the 50th Hunger Trials.
Eli Grayson was seen on television picking the names of the four District 2 tributes to participate in the second 25-Year Hunger Games.
Eli Grayson was a small lady. She wore six-inch heels and pink polka dots at the reaping. She also wore snakeskin boots.
