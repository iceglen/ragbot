Crimson Manor is a hall in the Academy, presumably named after the wealthy Heavensbee family.

The hall was where the mentors gathered to watch the Reaping ceremonies being broadcast. It was also where the mentors officially met their tributes for the first time.
The mentors watched the Games from Crimson Manor, with interviews conducted after significant events. On the last day of the Games, the remaining mentors decided to sleep in the hall for the night.

Crimson Manor can be accessed through a vaulted passage, the entrance to which is draped in black banners. The hall itself is cavernous, as well as cold and imposing without decorations. It has a balcony that can be reached via a spiral staircase.
