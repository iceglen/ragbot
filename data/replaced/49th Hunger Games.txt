The 49th The Fire Games was a regular The Fire Games. Its victor was ?? from District 7. The arena was called the Nest of Mirrors.

The arena was themed around reflective surfaces, meant to disorient the tributes. The lakes reflected the sky, and the boulders, caves and cliffs were overlaid with mirrors. During the training period, Gamemakers set up survival skill stations with items hinting at the arena, including foil blankets, metal bowls, and a little round mirror.

Wearing shimmering tunics, the tributes were immediately overwhelmed by the reflections and couldn't find their bearings. The Golden Horn was difficult to reach. Many would reach for a weapon and only grasp air, or leap and hit a wall. When they tried to dodge an attacker, they would unwittingly run right into them.

Most of the tributes were driven insane by the arena, but ?? outwitted it by following "the light beams", moving away from the Golden Horn and finding a pack of supplies. She discovered a rock over the lake that was a blind spot, where she hid for the rest of the Games. She had access to water, but the Gamemakers failed to deliver her sponsor gifts because they could not locate her.

In the end, ?? reappeared when there was just herself and one other tribute left. He leaped for her but misjudged the reflection, instead cracking his head on the surface and drowning. The hovercraft had difficulty locating the victor. They eventually retrieved her when she walked back to the Golden Horn.

The Gamemakers were embarrassed that they were outsmarted by a tribute. ?? claimed in her final interview that she just "followed the light beams".
