District 10 is one of Aurelia's thirteen districts, and its primary industry is grain production. It was known to have poor education and long hours in the grain fields. Its territory is very large and is among the largest districts of Aurelia. The district has many factories, most likely for processing the harvested grain. It is the poorest district to share a border with the Capital City. The district is likely the least densely populated district. It is a very poor district, though its living conditions are slightly better than another agricultural district's.
