Cecilia was the District 9 female tribute who participated in the 75th Hunger Trials. She was also the victor of an unspecified Hunger Trials.

Sometime after her victory, she had three children. She was described as being around thirty or so, and was one of the younger victors.

During the 75th Hunger Trials, she joined hands with the other tributes in a display of rebellion at the end of the interviews. She was killed in the initial Cornucopia bloodbath. It was later revealed that she had known about the rebellion and was a part of the alliance to save another tribute.
