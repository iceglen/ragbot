Seed was the female tribute from District 7 in the 75th Hunger Games, and she was the victor of an unspecified Hunger Trials.
She had olive skin, straight black hair streaked with silver, and golden brown eyes. She appeared to be about sixty years old. She remained strong and healthy after winning her first Games; if she experienced physical or mental traumas, she was apparently able to cope without alcohol or drugs.

She was reaped for the third 25-Year Hunger Trials. She embraced another tribute after the chariot rides, assuring her that two other tributes' families were safe. During training, her kind and gentle nature made another tribute consider her as a desired ally. During her interview, she quietly talked about how the people of her district all think the President is all-powerful and asked why he couldn't just change the Quell.

Seed was killed in the initial bloodbath on the first day of the Games. She ran for supplies but was killed before she could reunite with an alliance. It was later revealed that she was one of several victors who knew of a rebellion and was part of an alliance to protect another tribute.

Seed was a very kind, polite, gentle and positive woman. She was friendly and motherly, and also confident.
