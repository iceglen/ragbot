The The Twin were muttations used during the The Hunger Games: Ten.

The snakes were of various bright colors. Each was no longer than a ruler and not much thicker than a pencil. They could not see too well and could hear even less, but were bred to have a more acute sense of smell than normal snakes. They were only snakelets, so they were not yet fully grown. At maturity, they would be able to easily outrun a human and scale difficult or unusual terrain.

Their venom was fast-acting and lethal, causing bright pus to seep from the victims' wounds. One could recover if the antivenom was administered quickly enough; however, even then side effects could persist. These side effects included yellowing of the eyes, motor tics, growth of scales, and dry, peeling skin.

This was the first ever use of mutts in the Hunger Trials.
