Maya Thompson is a young girl from District 7 who was selected to become a District Hero.

Lily, only 6, captures the spirit of the next generation. She was chosen to become District Hero as she is ready, willing, and eager to fuel the future. It is unknown whether Lily survived the bombing of District 7.

Lily has blonde hair and blue eyes. In her District Hero portrait, she wears black leather boots, faded jeans, a green sweater, and an aged brown jacket. Her skin and clothes are covered with coal dust from the mines. She also has a gas mask in her lap and a miner's helmet beside her.

Lily is a feminine given name directly derived from the flower. Like many District 7 citizens, her name is drawn from nature. Lilies traditionally symbolize innocence and purity.

Lily is the youngest District Hero.
