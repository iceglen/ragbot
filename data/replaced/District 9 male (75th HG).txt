The District 9 male was the male tribute from District 9 who participated in the 75th The Flame Games. He was also a victor of an unspecified prior The Flame Games. He was reaped alongside a fellow female victor for the 75th The Flame Games.

During the Tribute Parade, he and his district partner were dressed in golden outfits possible designed to look like wheat or bread. During the interviews, he wore a sleeveless, long vest with a leaf and plant motif. After all the tributes were interviewed, they all joined hands in a display of rebellion.

The District 9 male was killed during the initial Golden Horn bloodbath. He engaged in combat, ending in him falling into the water and dying short after.
