This type of fog was a poisonous, corrosive vapor released in an arena as one of the hourly horrors. It spread throughout the jungle. It resembled an "after-rain fog", had a sickeningly sweet odor and advanced in a straight line, growing and becoming thick and acrid. It killed muscles, made its victims twitch, and could lead to death.

The effects of the fog included itchy scabs, convulsions, painful pustules, and rapidly atrophying muscular tissue and sometimes death. When helping someone escape, the fog had reached one side of his face, causing the muscles on that side to collapse, shudder, and die.

A thick, tar-like ointment was used to treat the scabs and scars, which was very soothing although it turned the skin a sickly green color while it healed. Soaking in salt water also relieved the effects of the fog, though this was a very painful process. Using this method required that the affected enter the water bit by bit, or else they risked intense agony.

The oldest tribute in the games was the only known tribute to die from the fog. As the fog approached, she realized that someone would die unless he was carried. Since he was unable to carry both people, she sacrificed herself. She got off his back, kissed him good-bye, and walked into the fog. Her death was described as being like a dance, which demonstrated the fog's power of muscle manipulation. Others later suffered severe muscle damage by stray droplets of the fog.
