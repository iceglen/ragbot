Jasper was one of two male tributes from District 12 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

During the chariot rides, the chariot carrying District 12 narrowly missed colliding with another district. Before the start of the games, Jasper joined an alliance between the tributes of several districts in an attempt to counter the Career pack. During the games, Jasper died on the fourth day, likely due to a volcanic eruption on the mountain he and the other alliance members had been staying on.

Jasper, along with the other three tributes from District 12, wore a distinctive outfit during the chariot rides.
