The District 12 male was the male tribute from District 12 who participated in the 75th Hunger Trials. He was also the victor of an unspecified Hunger Trials. He appears to have been quite young, so it can be presumed he won somewhere in the 60s.

During the Tribute Parade, he and the District 12 female were dressed up as cows with flaming belts. While the tributes were waiting to show their skills to the Gamemakers, he could be seen talking to the District 10 female and his district partner. During interviews, he wore a dark brown suit with long horns on a harness on his chest. Afterwards, he joined hands with the other victors in a display of rebellion.

He survived the Golden Horn bloodbath. He was among the dead tributes shown in the sky during the anthem on the second evening of the Games. He either died to the tidal wave at ten o'clock that morning, or a large, unidentified muttation named "the beast" that tore its victim apart around six o'clock that evening.
