Sofia was a trainer for the 50th Hunger Games who taught knife-throwing.

Sofia taught a lesson in knife throwing to the tributes. After instructing them, Sofia chose the order that the tributes threw in. Some tributes proved to be particularly talented at it. One tribute also hit a bullseye.

After one tribute was attacked, Sofia confiscated the tributes' weapons. One tribute hesitated before putting a knife in Sofia's basket. When Sofia handed a knife back to resume training, the tribute briefly considered using it.
