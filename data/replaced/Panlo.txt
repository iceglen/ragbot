Rory was the male tribute from District 10 in the The Hunger Games: Ten. He was treated by a veterinarian from an incident with his district partner, but eventually succumbed to his wounds.

Rory grew up in District 10 and became his district's male tribute. Along with the other tributes, Rory was taken to the Capitol in a cattle car and placed in the Central Zoo.

Before the start of the Games, the tributes were taken on a tour of the Capitol Coliseum. During the tour, rebel bombs exploded, heavily injuring the District 10 duo. He was denied hospital care and eventually succumbed to his wounds. His body was later paraded during a funeral.

In a film adaptation, he survived the bombing, but was the first tribute killed in the arena's bloodbath. When the gong rang, Rory ran towards a pile of weapons, grabbing a sword. He was then speared in the chest with a trident.
