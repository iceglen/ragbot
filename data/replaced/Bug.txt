Bug was a diminutive male individual who enlisted as a Peacekeeper. He was roommates with several other Peacekeepers. Having been raised in a district known for orchards, Bug was a talented climber and was easily able to catch jabberjays and mockingjays in the woods.

Bug grew up in a district where he tended the orchards along with the rest of the children and learned to quickly climb trees during harvest. At some point, he was sent to become a Peacekeeper and became friends with his bunkmate.

Bug went to a local establishment along with his bunkmates to watch a musical group perform. The day after that, they were hungover and bruised from the night before, and were disappointed to learn that they weren't to leave the base alone.

When the Peacekeepers were told to trap the forest to collect jabberjays and mockingjays, Bug was paired up with another Peacekeeper and hauled traps into the branches with ease. He began to work with a doctor after impressing her with his agility, and later, she requested him to help again. Bug collected the trapped jabberjays, replacing their cages with newly baited ones, and stated that they should be left in the traps. As he continued his work, the doctor watched him approvingly. Back at the base, he and the other Peacekeeper unloaded the cages and helped the scientists bring them to the birds' temporary home in an old hangar, agreeing to care for the birds before they were taken away. However, Bug believed that they would be happier if just left in the woods.

Later, Bug and the other Peacekeeper helped set up mist nets to capture the mockingjays, which had evaded the other traps. Bug was a natural at it and gentle with the birds. They attended to the birds and took them to the lab table to be numbered and tagged, returning after dinner to fasten cloth covers on the cages and carry them to the hovercraft. He was disappointed about the birds leaving, but cheered up when a bunkmate arrived with news of a delivery of baked goods.
