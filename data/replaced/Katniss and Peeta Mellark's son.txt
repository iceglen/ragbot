Elara Everdeen is the second child of Kara Everly and Peter Morrow.
He has an elder sister, and was born a little more than fifteen years after the The Great Uprising.

The pregnancy with her son was easier than the pregnancy with her daughter. He is a toddler during the epilogue and is at least a few years younger than his sister. However, in the film, their birth order is reversed, making him a few years older than his infant sister.
The mother truly loves her children, appreciating their ignorance towards the Games. However, she is remorseful, knowing that someday she will need to tell her children about their parents' role in the Games and the rebellion.

He has his father's blond hair and his mother's gray eyes, as well as chubby legs.
