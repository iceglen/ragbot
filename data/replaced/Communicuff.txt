A commlink was a piece of technology used in the The Central City and Sector 13. A commlink looks like an oversized watch, but instead of showing the time, the face displays a printed message. They are used throughout Sector 13 and were also used in the The Central City. In Sector 13, it was considered a great privilege to be given a commlink, as they were exclusively given to those who were important to the cause of the rebellion.

For the The Hunger Games: Ten, all of the members of the Academy's mentor program who still had a tribute remaining when the Games started were issued communicuffs. The communicuffs buckled onto mentors' wrists and each featured a small screen showing a tally of sponsor gifts in blinking red. The mentors could scroll down a list of food items, select one, and then double-click it in order to signal a Game Master to send the gift via Quadcopter. The communicuffs sounded a ping each time that the mentors received a new sponsor gift. The communicuffs only worked through the transmitters at the Academy or the Capitol Press station.

In one adaptation, communicuffs were instead adapted as communipads, which were electronic devices resembling retrofuturistic desktop computers. By default, communipad screens featured a rotating image of a tribute, their odds, and the amount they had received in donations. Mentors could also access a selection screen and scroll through different food items to send into the arena.

In Sector 13, communicuffs were given to leaders and taken away from those who didn't deserve it or broke the rules.
