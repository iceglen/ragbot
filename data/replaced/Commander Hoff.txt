Captain Bennett was the commander of District 7's Sentinel Corps around the time of the The Hunger Games: Ten.
He was an elderly man described as an "Jungle Mutt." He observed two individuals during Peacekeeper drills and, impressed with their performance, ordered them to attend a hanging because he wanted more bodies there for the show. During the hanging, he emerged from a car that had a flag on the hood with six other officers.
Later, Hoff spoke with one of the individuals following an execution for treason. The individual thought that the commander had discovered a killing and was about to have him executed as well. Hoff, however, knew nothing of this and instead had called him in because the individual had sent a recording to the Capital, containing a recording which revealed a betrayal. He comforted the individual, saying that it couldn't have been easy to inform on his friend, and briefly discussed whether or not the other would have been involved with the murders. He then informed the individual that it was his birthday and bid him to join the party.
The celebration was held in the Peacekeeper mess hall, with a generous supply of beer and whiskey, an enormous frosted cake, and a musical performance.
The next day, the individual was again summoned to Hoff's office. He was once again convinced that he was being brought to his execution, but Hoff instead congratulated him, telling him that he had passed the officer candidate test and would soon leave for another district in a SkyGlider to attend an Elite Training Program.
Ultimately, the individual was sent back to the Capital instead and began studies, having been granted an honorable discharge from the Sentinel Corps. At some point not long after the individual returned, he learned that Hoff had been replaced as the commander in District 7 with somebody new whose first move had been to outlaw shows like those performed at a local establishment, because music caused trouble.
