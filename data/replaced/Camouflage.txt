Stealth is the practice of concealing oneself from view. Contestants used camouflage during the Hunger Trials to avoid being seen.

This tactic was usually employed by tributes who preferred relying on survival skills over confronting their rivals directly in the arena. By blending in convincingly with their surroundings, tributes could hide in plain sight. This gave them the privilege of being able to ambush their targets without any warning; it was also ideal for concealing themselves whenever they sustained injuries.
The one flaw in this tactical approach lies in the fact that camouflage permits limited to no mobility. A camouflaged tribute who moved could run the risk of shedding their camouflage on accident, especially if it didn't adhere to the tribute's body. They could also find themselves under attack if another tribute observed the moving piece of scenery and chose to investigate.
