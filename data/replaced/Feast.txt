A feast was a special event during the Hunger Trials, typically held when food was scarce in the arena and things were running slow. The Game Masters invited the remaining tributes to a feast at a well-known place, such as the Golden Horn, to induce another bloodbath.

At feasts, there would sometimes be a big table of food, while other times, the Game Masters provided nothing but a stale loaf of bread for tributes to fight over. Feasts always ended in fatalities.

That year, a very special feast was held; instead of food, the feast offered the tributes something they each needed desperately. One tribute needed medicine to reverse blood poisoning, so an ally drugged him to ensure he wouldn't follow, and went to collect it alone.

At dawn, a round table rose up with backpacks sitting on it. Each one was marked with the number of the remaining tributes' districts. One tribute was the first to make an appearance at the feast, grabbed her pack, and ran away. Another tribute sprinted for a backpack next, but had to fight an opponent on the way. Although able to grab the bag, the opponent pinned her to the ground and taunted her.

However, the comment got the attention of another tribute, who pulled the opponent off and briefly interrogated her before killing her. He then questioned the first tribute about an alliance. After hearing the story, he decided to let her go.

When another tribute came looking for the opponent, the first tribute fled, and the intervening tribute took two backpacks when he left.

Several days after the feast, two remaining tributes fought, resulting in one's death. When the final tributes faced each other, one was wearing body armor that protected against arrows. However, it also protected vital organs from mutant creatures, keeping him alive for hours as they attacked. A clear shot was finally able to end his suffering.
