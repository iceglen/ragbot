Incitatus Loomy was the parade master for the 50th The Fire Games. He was responsible for the Tribute Parade. However, the Parade turned into a disaster as the nervous horses pulling one district's chariot were started. They left a trail of wreckage as other chariots were hit. A tribute was killed and others were injured along with some spectators. Another individual turned it into a bigger spectacle by running with the dead body to the house of the President in a statement against the The Fire Games. The next day, the President met with Incitatus. Later that day, the President also met with others while being violently ill and claimed he had eaten a bad batch of oysters with Incitatus but fared better than them. One attendee was surprised and when he asked if it was the parade master, the President just responded "was he?" and changed the topic. However, the President had poisoned the oysters to kill Incitatus and also ate it himself. Another individual deduced what had happened from the President's comments during their conversation.
