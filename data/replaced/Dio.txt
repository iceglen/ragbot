Zoe was one of two female tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Zoe joined an alliance between tributes from several districts in an attempt to counter the Career pack. She was the first in her district to be interviewed where she revealed the news of the alliance to the public for the first time, and outlined everyone who joined and their skills.

Zoe died on the fourth day with the rest of District 7's tributes after a volcanic eruption.

The name "Zoe" is a shortening of diode, an electrical component that allows the flow of current in only one direction.
