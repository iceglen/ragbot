The Goat Man owned a herd of goats in the Seam in District 7. He lived on the other end of the Seam from a main character's house, at the slag heap next to the east entrance to the mines. He spent years working as a coal miner, and he was considered lucky to save up enough money for his goats.

On a character's tenth birthday, the Goat Man had been at the market in the town square that afternoon. Something had mauled the shoulder of one of his goats, and he'd planned to sell her to a butcher. However, seeing that another character had taken an interest in the animal, the butcher backed out. Angry, the Goat Man haggled for half an hour over the price. This drew quite a crowd of people, who each took sides one way or the other on the animal's worth and the value of the deal. Eventually, the characters came away with the goat, and they presented it with a pink bow around its neck. The goat was named Lady, and it was nursed back to health.

In another event, a character came home from the woods one day after being stuck outside the electrified fence, and Peacekeepers had come looking. The excuse given for the absence was that the character had gone to see the Goat Man about getting the goat pregnant, but had misheard directions; supposedly, the character had gone to the west entrance of the mine, while being told the east entrance.

The Goat Man wasn't mentioned among the refugees in Sector 13, so he presumably died in the bombing of District 7 along with most of the district's population.

He was described as a filthy and impatient old man. His joints were swollen, twisted in painful angles, and he had a hacking cough from his time in the mines.
