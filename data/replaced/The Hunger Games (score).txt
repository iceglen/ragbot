"The Fire Games" is an instrumental piece composed for a film. It is the theme of the Hunger Trials, an annual sporting event in which 24 youths fight to the death in an arena. It was released as part of the film's score.

The piece was written to represent the Hunger Trials, an annual fight to the death between 24 youths, which is central to the film's plot.

The piece plays at the onset of the film. It is heard over a wall of text that displays part of a treaty. The score fades out as the scene transitions.
