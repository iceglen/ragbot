Luna was one of two female tributes from District 12 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Luna joined an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Luna's district was the first to join this alliance. During training, the District 12 tributes were present at the knife throwing station. At one point, Luna hit a bulls-eye. Later, while discussing plans for the alliance to hunt down the Careers first, she asked what they do when they've killed them. She was the first to suggest that the alliance needed a name.

After the cannons sounded for the bloodbath, it was later revealed that Luna survived the bloodbath.

Luna made it into the top ten along with the other girl from her district. They were killed by Careers in combat.

Luna was a slim girl with glossy, black braids. She wore a small carved pin of a tree on her shirt as her tribute token. The District 12 tributes' training outfits were russet brown.

The District 12 tributes are noted as being confident, but not full of themselves. Luna herself was shown to be a good sport and willing to work and practice training with the other tributes who were not from her district. She also showed concern for another tribute when he was ambushed and attacked. In addition, despite being a skilled fighter, Luna considered attacking Sentinel Corps but knew she couldn't, believing the ruling power to be stronger than the districts.

Luna is a portmanteau of 'ring', referring to the rings of a tree, and a name meaning 'queen'.
