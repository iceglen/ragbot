Tribute Stand s were fire-resistant metal plates that tributes stood on during the countdown before a Hunger Trials. All 24 pedestals were placed at equal distances from the Golden Horn. Tributes had to wait 60 seconds before the Games began, otherwise a mine would explode. These mines automatically disconnected after the gong sounded.

During the 10th Hunger Trials, the podiums were red circles with black numbers on them and were used from the first Games until the 10th Hunger Trials. They surrounded the center of the amphitheater where the The Central City seal was located, but after the bombing of the The Central City Coliseum, the center was covered by a mountain of concrete rubble and beams that fell from the ceiling when it collapsed.

For the 50th Hunger Trials, which had twice as many tributes, the number of pedestals doubled to 48. These pedestals surrounded the Golden Horn, which was filled with weapons and supplies that could help the tributes during the Games.

In the film version, the pedestals do not form a circle, like in other iterations of the Games, but a semicircle.

The pedestals are on the water surrounding the Golden Horn. Between each pair of pedestals is a path of rocks separating the sectors of the arena.
