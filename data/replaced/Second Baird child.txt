The second Baird child was the middle child of Lena Carter Jansen's mother and father, the younger sibling of an unnamed child, and the older sibling of Lena Carter Jansen.

This child was a member of the District Ally, a nomadic people not tied to any one place. They were the second of three children, and as Emma James's older sibling, they were born at least four years before the Crimson Conflict. They were also a cousin of Lena Carter Hayes and Lena Carter Lila Brant.

A few years prior to certain events, they, their family, and a number of other District Ally were rounded up. Their parents were both killed for trying to resist, and this child and their older sibling also died. They may have been killed during the roundup or died in another way sometime soon after, as the exact circumstances are never stated. Ultimately, Emma James was the only member of her immediate family to survive.
