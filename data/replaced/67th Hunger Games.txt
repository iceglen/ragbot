The 67th Hunger Trials took place seven years before the start of the trilogy. They were won by the male tribute from District 2. After his victory, he was hailed as the "Favorite Son" and was known as the "Cavalier Career".

According to reports, he cultivated an unkempt persona in the arena. Evidently, he was widely popular; they reportedly sang his praises and labelled him as a figure of reconciliation of the people due to his patriotic manner. On his Triumphal Journey, he is described as having a bright and optimistic ensemble, in contrast to how he appeared in his Games.
