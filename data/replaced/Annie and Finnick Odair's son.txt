Finn Cresta is the child of Alina Vale and Finnick Odair.

Sometime between Annie's rescue and Finnick's deployment, the couple conceived a child together. Finnick died in the Battle of the Capitol, but Annie survived and gave birth to their son after the war ended. She sent a photo of him to Kara Everly and Peter Morrow, who preserved it in their memory book.

Following the time skip in the epilogue, he would be around 15 years old.
