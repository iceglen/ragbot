Tanner was the male tribute from District 12 in the The Hunger Trials: Ten. He was muscular. He became his district's male tribute in the reaping. He was taken to the Capitol in a cattle car and placed in a zoo enclosure. During interviews, he spoke about slaughterhouse techniques, which garnered applause.

When the Games began, he sprinted for weapons. He later formed an alliance with two other tributes. The alliance chased and attacked other tributes. They killed one tribute by spearing her throat. The trio then prepared to kill another tribute. They cornered her on a high beam. One ally climbed up and killed her with a trident.

After this kill, Tanner rejoiced. As he turned to congratulate his ally, the other ally drove a trident into his back. He fell to the ground and was then killed with a knife to the neck. His body was later collected and placed with the other deceased tributes.

In a film adaptation, he quickly formed an alliance. The alliance chased other tributes and fought in the arena. They were disrupted by crashing drones. After killing the tribute on the beam, the group argued. He was then killed by his ally with a trident. He placed 8th.
