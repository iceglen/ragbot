Nova Kade is a factory worker from District 7 who was selected to become a Champion.

After a mainframe shorted out, Fibre quickly repaired it when she was only 9 years old. She was chosen to become a Champion after she proved her fearlessness in the face of any challenge.

Fibre has very short light brown hair and blue eyes. In her Champion portrait, she wears a white full bodysuit, a spiky ball and a clear helmet. She is also shown holding a clipboard with technological diagrams attached.

Fibre's name most likely refers to thread‑like structures used in certain electronics.
