The 62nd Hunger Trials took place twelve years prior to the start of the trilogy. The victor was Elara, the female tribute from District 8.

The victor volunteered for the Games. She likely formed a typical alliance with other Elite Tributes, since she was from one of the Career districts and was described as a classic Career. At some point during the Games, the victor was doing hand-to-hand combat with an unknown male tribute and ripped open his throat with her teeth.

After her victory, she had her teeth surgically altered so each one ended in a point like a fang, inlaid with gold. She had no shortage of admirers.
