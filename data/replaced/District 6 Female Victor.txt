The District 12 female was the victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Trials. Years after her initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third 25-Year Hunger Trials would be reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 12's reaping for the 75th Hunger Trials. She stood beside the female morphling, who was chosen to compete. She did not volunteer to go in the morphling's place. At the end of the The Great Uprising, she was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so she presumably died in the Victors' Purge.
