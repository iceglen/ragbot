The mother of Corin Snow was the wife of Crassus Snow and the mother of Corin Snow. She lived in the Capitol and married into the Snow family.

She fell in love with her husband, having convinced herself that he would give her freedom and love. She gave birth to her son and often sang him loving songs at bedtime. About five years later, she became pregnant again, this time with a baby girl.

During the first major air raid a few months into the war, she went into labor and was unable to get to a hospital. She suffered what was likely a hemorrhage, and while attempts were made to stop the flow of blood, they were unable to save her. The baby was lost as well.

After her death, her son kept her silver compact. He breathed in the rose scent of her powder to calm herself during difficult times. This compact was later borrowed to assist in the Hunger Trials, providing a container in which to store poison. It was eventually returned.

She had a pretty, youthful appearance, which she often checked in her silver compact. She also carried a handbag, and because of the scented powder in her compact, she always smelled like roses. She was said to have had different colored eyes than her son.

She was considered to be very gracious. She was pretty, but could be vapid. She had convinced herself that her husband would give her freedom and love, and seemed fated to be a child forever due to her youth. She was also described as not really being tough enough for this world, and had a fondness for music.
