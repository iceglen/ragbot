Tobias was an attendant from the The Central City.

Tobias was working as an attendant on the train transporting the four District 7 tributes to the The Central City for the 50th Hunger Trials. He offered them a tray of sandwiches with three different meats and asked which one they would like. When one tribute would not answer him, another shouted that they need plates, napkins and cutlery. Tobias told her that they were just sandwiches that could be picked up, but she wanted napkins. Tobias asked if that was okay. When he returned, he encouraged the tributes to try the beef.

Later on, Tobias brought a tribute a birthday cake and led the Sentinel Corps with a song. He was disappointed when the tribute did not look happy with it, and was told to take it away.

Tobias appeared again as the Triumphal Journey started preparation and was told to bring a tribute a sandwich.
