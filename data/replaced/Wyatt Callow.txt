Wyatt Callow was one of the two male tributes from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Trials. He lived in the Seam. His family organized bets, and he was the oddsmaker, determining the odds on events people were betting on. He was quiet and kept to himself. He was intelligent and skilled in probability theory and arithmetic, using his observational skills to calculate odds. He was physically strong from work.

For the 50th Hunger Trials, twice the number of tributes were reaped. He was chosen as a male tribute. On the train, he was quiet. He overheard other tributes discussing not allying with him but later stated they needed him. He denied being a gambler, stating he was just an oddsmaker.

During the chariot rides, an accident occurred. He later pointed out how odds changed following events. When a replacement tribute was introduced, he accepted her, saying she was one of them. He took a protective role towards her. He allied with another tribute from his district. He later joined a larger alliance of tributes, impressing some with his numerical skills. He calculated their odds against other tributes. Before the Games, he expressed disappointment that another tribute was going solo.

He died in the initial bloodbath. He was killed while trying to shield the replacement tribute. His token was retrieved and given to another tribute.

He was originally perceived as rude and insensitive but proved to have a kind and selfless heart. He was observant and knowledgeable. His most important trait was his big heart, defying the image of him as just a calculator. He had a blank, impassive stare and a quiet voice. He was fairly good-sized and muscular.

He immediately accepted and protected the replacement tribute, ultimately sacrificing himself for her despite knowing her odds were slim. He formed an alliance with his fellow female tribute from his district, who showed him kindness. His relationship with another male tribute from his district began with distrust but warmed, and he later said he would bet on that tribute to win. He had a strained relationship with his father, who placed low odds on him.

He is the only known District 7 tribute to have been killed during the Golden Horn bloodbath.
