Professor Asha Lancaster was a communications professor at the Academy. She was known to be unpredictable, and could be even more so if she had too much to drink. She was amusing and not overly uptight, as she was one of the few professors at the Academy that allowed students to call her by her first name. However, she had a habit of drinking excessively. She was often unpredictable, a trait that could be heightened with more drink.
