Twill was a woman who escaped from District 12 during an uprising.
She and her student met another character in the woods outside District 7 while on their way to Sector 13.
She had light brown eyes and appeared to be about thirty-five years old. Her fate is unknown.

Twill was a teacher, and after school let out she would work at the inspection dock of a factory where they made uniforms.
They intended to escape and contact other districts about the rebellion.
Once District 12 started the uprising, the district appeared to be under rebel control, until the Capitol deployed Sentinel Corps into District 12 to take back the district.
They were beaten down and defeated.
As punishment as well as to regain control in District 12, the Capitol placed the district into a full lockdown state for approximately a week, with no food or coal.
When they were finally allowed to go back to work, Twill and one of her students were late on their way to the factory after being unable to cross a bombed street.
The factory was bombed before they arrived.
Although the two were far enough away to survive, the explosion killed Twill's husband and the student's entire family.

The two disguised themselves in stolen uniforms.
Twill robbed a gun, and they boarded a boxcar headed to District 12.
After fleeing the train, they traveled on foot to the woods surrounding District 7.
However, the student twisted an ankle there, forcing them to stop.
In the woods, they met another character, who was hunting.
She gave them food, then taught them how to make tea and use the gun to get meat.
They in turn told her their story and their goal of finding Sector 13.
Rebels in District 12 had noticed that the Capitol always reused old footage of Sector 13, creating hope that it still existed, independent of the Capitol.

In a later book, it was learned that the two never made it to Sector 13 and it was assumed that they died in the woods.

Twill refers to a type of fabric with a diagonal weave.
