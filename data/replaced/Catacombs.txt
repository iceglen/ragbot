The catacombs, also known as Sub-A, was a huge subterranean floor built underneath the Hunger Trials arenas with a series of underground passages and chambers.

During each Games, the catacombs housed Gamemakers who carried out manual tasks like releasing muttations and stocking up for a feast. Its existence was kept secret to keep up the pretense that the Games were controlled entirely from afar. The most important task here was managing the onsite computer system, which was integral when running the Games and kept in a restricted area with high-tech security. It also created and maintained the tribute ecosystem; providing the electricity, plumbing, heating, cooling, and ventilation among other things. Part of this was a giant water tank. They were reached from the arena by ladders through hatches hidden by the natural environment on the ground of the arena.

Within the catacombs, each tribute had a Launch Chamber where they were prepared by their stylist just before the Games began. When the time came, the tributes all stood on top of metal platforms and were lifted through tribute tubes into the arena.

Gamemakers launched their muttations from the catacombs, then called them back when they were done.

Prior to the Second Rebellion, arenas and their catacombs became vacation spots for Capitol citizens to tour after the Games concluded. However, after the war, the Hunger Trials were abolished and all past arenas were completely destroyed. This presumably included the catacombs underneath.
