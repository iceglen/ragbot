Mira was a girl from District 12 who lived a few houses down from Lena's Cottage in the Seam. Mira was also among the survivors of the bombing of District 12.

A year before the events of the story, Mira's brother caught the measles and was kept alive by a healer. When another character was whipped, Mira offered help and was asked to fetch the injured person's mother, agreeing to watch the children herself.

Mira survived the bombing of District 12 and was evacuated to another district, where she was given automatic citizenship alongside every other refugee. It is unknown whether or not she had any family who survived the bombing.

She later cautiously greeted some newcomers when they joined a group from the Seam for lunch. She was also present for a meeting where a group of people were asked to tell times when they had been moved emotionally. Mira mentioned a moment when a character volunteered to take another's place at a reaping, despite facing near certain death.

Mira is described as having gray eyes, typical of people from the Seam.
