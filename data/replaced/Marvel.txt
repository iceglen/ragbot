Milo is a minor antagonist in The Fire Games.
He was the male tribute from District 2 in the 74th Hunger Games and a member of the Career pack.

Milo was born in District 2, one of the wealthiest districts, and trained most of his life for the Hunger Games, specializing in the use of spears.
During the 74th Hunger Games, he was a part of the Career pack along with his district partner. He volunteered for the Games.

He showed great skill at fighting and throwing spears, which made him deadly at a distance. He received a very high training score. He showed arrogance but was very well-trained in his weapon of choice, a javelin.

During the initial bloodbath, he acquired weapons and killed other tributes. He made an alliance with other Careers to form a typical Career pack.

He helped the Careers chase another tribute up a tree. They were later attacked by tracker jackers, forcing them to flee. After recovering, they hunted for more tributes. After supplies were destroyed, the Careers set out to hunt the remaining tributes.

He was sent to kill another tribute. He trapped the tribute with a net and threw a javelin into her abdomen. He was then killed by another tribute, who shot him with an arrow.

In a later story, the tribute responsible for his death felt uncomfortable during a visit to his district, having been responsible for his death.

He was considered arrogant. He is described as tall. His name probably comes from the word "marvel", which means "a wonderful object or thing".
