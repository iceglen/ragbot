Mira was one of two female tributes from District 10 who participated in the 50th Hunger Trials.

Before the start of the Games, it was noticed that everyone from Mira's district had sunflower neonlace tokens. It was suggested they join an alliance as the tokens could be used for a plan to sabotage the arena. Their efficiency and capabilities were thought to make them good allies.
While training, an offer to join another alliance was made to the largest boy from 9, but he refused. In retaliation, a token from another tribute was broken. It was fixed, and Mira, along with the rest of 9, decided to join the Newcomers alliance. Even so, they weren't very sociable and almost appeared exclusive.
Mira died in the initial bloodbath along with everyone else in her district. Two of their tokens were retrieved.

The District 10 tributes’ training outfits were buttery yellow. Like the rest of her district, Mira was still healing from sunburn, had calloused hands and lean, muscled arms. Mira was larger than the other female tribute from her district.
Mira and the others from 9 weren’t very sociable and liked to keep to themselves.
