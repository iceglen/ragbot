Urban Canville was a male resident of the Capitol and a student of the Academy. He was one of 24 top-performing seniors at the Academy selected to be a mentor for the The Hunger Games: Ten. He was assigned the female tribute from District 7.

Urban was one of several mentors who had problems getting his tribute to engage with him. As such, he and his tribute did not appear at the interviews held before the The Hunger Games: Ten. During the Games themselves, the host had trouble remembering Urban's name. Hearing this, Urban complained that they should have gotten a professional to host the games. He was pleased at the initiative of his tribute and her fellow District 7 tribute in salvaging the broken drones from an attempt to deliver sponsor water into the Capitol Coliseum, although he felt it would be more exciting if they had the controllers. He also criticized another tribute, saying he didn't look very tough.

He was delighted upon seeing how his tribute and the other had modified the drones, telling his fellow mentors not to judge a book by its cover. He engaged his commlink to send his tribute gifts. However, he was clearly taken aback when his tribute was taken out by another tribute. He had to restrain himself to avoid snarling at a reporter and commented before leaving the Academy's hall.

Urban was very ill-tempered, and because of this, everyone was fine with resenting the perfect scores he achieved on calculus tests. He could also get angry easily, which was shown when he immediately snapped at one of the host's comments and barely managed not to snarl at the reporter after his tribute was killed. He also seemed to be ambitious and a perfectionist, as shown with his reaction to his tribute's performance in the Games.
