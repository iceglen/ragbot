The District 12 male was the male tribute from District 12 who participated in the 74th Hunger Games.

During the Tribute Parade, he and his fellow tribute wore shiny and flashy silver costumes that represent the district's main export, power and electricity.

The District 12 male was seen in several scenes in the training. During training after another tribute displays strength, he and another male tribute are seen in the background looking doubtfully at the Career tributes. It is unknown if he made any alliances at this time.

For the interviews, he wore a pink suit. When his fellow tribute has her interview, his face is shown on the screen.

The District 12 male was killed in the initial bloodbath. He stood on the pedestal next to another male tribute. Like the majority of the tributes, he ran for supplies. After arriving at the Cornucopia, he spots a female tribute on the ground with a backpack. He grabs a sickle, slashes her throat and steals her bag. At some point in the bloodbath, he and another male tribute try to kill a Career tribute while another male tribute watches. He is about to kill a female tribute but then, a male tribute confronts him and knocks him down with a spear. Another tribute passes by, distracting the attacker. He crawls away, but is attacked by another male tribute, who strangles him onto a crate. He gains advantage and hurls the attacker's head into the crate multiple times. He later gets into a fight with another tribute and she kills him by stabbing him in the abdomen.
