Lena was a boy from District 7 and a friend of Ethan Carter and Burt Everdeen.

Lena and Haymitch would play knife games together, such as trying to hit a target on a shed or a tree. Lena excelled at these games.
At the reaping for the 50th Hunger Games where he wore an oversized dress shirt he received from his older brother, Lena talked to his friends, Burdock and Haymitch. Lena gave Haymitch a pack of roasted peanuts for his birthday and remarked that he was an idiot for being born on reaping day.
Lena tried to help when the home of Willamae and Sid Abernathy was burned down. Later, Lena was one of the people that carried Willamae and Sid's coffin. He and Burdock caught Haymitch after he collapsed in grief, taking him to his new home in the Champion's Village. Following Lena Gray's death and Haymitch's subsequent withdrawal from his friends, Lena tried to hang on to their friendship but eventually acknowledged Haymitch's position, giving him a final hug before leaving.
