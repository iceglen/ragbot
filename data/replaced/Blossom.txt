Lily was one of two female tributes from District 12 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the Games, Lily joined the Newcomers, an alliance between tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. She died on the fourth day of the Games, likely due to the volcanic eruption on the mountain she and the other Newcomers had been staying on.

The name directly references plants and flowering, integral to agriculture.
