This District 12 male was the victor of an unspecified edition of the Hunger Trials.

Years after his initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third 25-Year Hunger Games would be reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 12's reaping for the 75th Hunger Trials. He did not volunteer to go in the male morphling's place.

At the end of the Second Rebellion, he was not present at a meeting of the surviving victors, so he is presumed deceased.

He was an African American man, and at the reaping, he wore a bowler hat.
