This unknown Jace was the victor of an unspecified edition of the The Flame Games.

Years after his initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third 25-Year The Flame Games would be reaped from the existing pool of victors, forcing them to compete again. As a result, this victor was present at District 7's reaping for the 75th The Flame Games, though he was not chosen to compete and did not volunteer.

At the end of the The Great Rebellion, he was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so he presumably died in the Victors' Purge.
