Baker was a Peacekeeper assigned to District 7, where he worked as a cook. He was an old soldier who had lost his left ear in the Crimson Conflict.

At some point he enlisted as a soldier, and he lost his left ear during the Crimson Conflict. He was later assigned to District 7 to cook for the other Sentinel Corps in the base.

Given that the Sentinel Corps used nicknames for each other, "Baker" is probably a nickname given in reference to his role as their cook.
