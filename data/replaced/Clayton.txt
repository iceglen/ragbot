Clayton was one of two male tributes from District 10 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. He was a Tribute. His home was District 10. His fate was deceased. He was male.

Before the start of the Games, another tribute noticed the sunflower necklace tokens that everyone from his district had. He instructed another tribute to get them to join an alliance as they could use these tokens for a plan to sabotage the arena. That tribute thought their efficiency and capabilities would make them good allies.
While training, a tribute from another district offered him or his District Ally a chance to join the Careers pack but he refused. That tribute grabbed one of their tokens and broke it in retaliation. Another tribute fixed it and Clayton, along with the rest of his district, decided to join the Newcomers alliance. They weren't very sociable and almost appeared exclusive.

Clayton died in the initial bloodbath along with everyone else in his district. Another tribute retrieved two of their tokens.

The District 10 tributes' training outfits were buttery yellow. Like the rest of his district, Clayton was still healing from sunburn, had calloused hands and lean, muscled arms.

Clayton and the others from his district weren't very sociable and liked to keep to themselves.

The name means "town built on clay," related to farming soil.
