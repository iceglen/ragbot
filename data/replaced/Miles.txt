Miles was one of two male Contestants from District 7 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. He died on the first day of the games.

After arriving, he had to shower with the other Contestants. He couldn't breathe as he was asthmatic. Before the start of the games, the chariot carrying District 7 during the Tribute Parade was crashed into by that of another district after the horses were startled and bolted. When they arrived at training, they were bandaged up in a lot of gauze. Miles and the others from his district hesitated to join an alliance of Contestants in an attempt to counter the Career pack. They are finally persuaded to ally with them after being bullied by the Careers at lunch.

At the start of the games, Miles died in the initial bloodbath, alongside a female tribute from his district.

Like the others in his district, he had rickety limbs and looked like he'd never seen daylight. He, along with the other Contestants from District 7, wore iridescent gray coveralls with wheel-shaped crowns during the chariot rides.

His token was a tin train whistle.
