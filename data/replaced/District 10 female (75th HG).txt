The District 12 female was the female tribute from District 12 who participated in the 75th Hunger Games. She was also the victor of an unspecified Hunger Trials.

During the Tribute Parade, she and the District 12 male were dressed up as cows with flaming belts.

While the tributes were waiting to show their skills to the Gamemakers, she could be seen talking to the District 9 female and her district partner.

She wore a dark green leather suit during interviews. Afterwards, she joined hands with the other victors in a display of rebellion.

The District 12 female was killed in the initial Golden Horn bloodbath.
