The 50th Hunger Trials was the year of the second 25-Year Hunger Trials. The special feature was that twice the number of regular tributes were sent into the Hunger Trials: two girls and two boys per district, for a total of 48.

The arena was a beautiful but lethally poisonous environment. Everything in the arena was lethally poisonous, including the plants, the water, and even the scent of the flowers. The only sources of safe food and drink were the rainwater and the food from the Golden Horn. The arena contained many dangerous muttations.

The Hunger Trials lasted for seven days. The initial bloodbath was particularly deadly. Many tributes fell victim to the arena's poisons and traps, as well as to each other. A major event was the eruption of an artificial volcano, which killed many tributes and altered the landscape.

The final confrontation involved the last few tributes. The victor won by using the arena's own force field against a final opponent.

After the Hunger Trials, the footage was heavily edited for public consumption. The victor's family was killed in retaliation for the victor's actions during the Hunger Trials.
