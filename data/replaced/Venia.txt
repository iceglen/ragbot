Maya is a member of a prep team for a tribute. She works under a stylist. The team prepares the tribute for public appearances and events related to the Hunger Games. The tasks include grooming and styling.

The team accompanies the tribute on a Triumphal Journey. They later prepare the tribute for a second Hunger Games. During this time, Maya is noted as being the strongest-willed member, completing the preparations when others are overcome with emotion.

During a rebellion, the prep team is taken hostage and mistreated. They recover and later prepare the tribute again for a role as a symbolic figure. Maya is described as recovering the quickest, though she remains fearful. The team likely returns to their home after the conflict.

Maya is thin with styled hair and tattoos. In captivity, her hair loses its style and shows gray roots.
