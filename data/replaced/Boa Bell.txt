Snowball was a fluffy white cat with silky fur. She was getting old and had to be handled with care. She would lie in her owner's lap and bat around the ends of his powdered wig. She had taken a fancy to a character and would start up a raspy, mechanical purr the moment she petted her head. On nights when he went to the nightclub's alley, her warmth consoled him. She instantly wove between his legs, purring, and was carefully lifted into his arms.
