Elias was one of two male tributes from District 12 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the start of the games, Elias joined an alliance between several district tributes in an attempt to counter the Career pack. Elias's district was the first to join this alliance. Elias and his district partners were considered decent at knife throwing during training.
Elias died in the initial bloodbath along with the other boy from his district.

The District 12 tributes' Training gear were russet brown.
"Elias" refers to the central, strongest part of a tree.
