Leeg 3 was a rebel soldier from Sector 13 assigned to the sharpshooter propaganda unit, Unit 12. She was in her twenties and female. She lived in Sector 13 with her sister and an unnamed father. She and her sister were given the nicknames "Leeg 3" and "Leeg 3" to distinguish them due to their striking similarity. The only noticeable difference was that Leeg 3 had yellow flecks in her eyes.

She trained alongside her squad for several days before deployment. Once deployed, the squad filmed propaganda films for several days. Her sister was killed after triggering a deadly trap. The following evening, a replacement soldier was sent to take her sister's place. That night, Leeg 3 broke down and sobbed over her sister's death.

The next morning, she participated in filming another propaganda film. After the filming went awry, killing one soldier and fatally wounding their commander, she and another soldier shot through a lock to take refuge inside a building. After the commander succumbed to his injuries, she asked about taking his body with them as they left to fulfill a falsified assassination mission. Sentinel Corps then launched an assault on the building they had just left.

The squad later traveled underground through the sewers, at which point they were attacked by mutant creatures and automated traps. She stayed behind with another soldier to hold the creatures off at a trap known as the Mortal Grinder, attempting to buy time for the rest of the squad to escape. It is assumed she was killed.
