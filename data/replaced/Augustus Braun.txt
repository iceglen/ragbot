Adrian Keller was the victor of the 67th Hunger Trials from District 2. He was known as the Elite Tributes. He was a Career Tribute in the 67th Hunger Trials. On his Triumph Tour, he was described as having a bright and optimistic ensemble that contrasted his unkempt persona cultivated during his Hunger Trials. He was widely popular. It is unclear if he ever mentored for a Hunger Trials. As District 2 victors are known to continue training even after they win, it is likely that he continued his training. Although unknown how he died, he was dead by the end of the The Great Uprising, as he was absent for a vote on a final Hunger Trials. He was likely a victim of the Victors' Purge.
