Mayor Keller was the mayor of District 7 at the time of the The Hunger Games: Ten. He conducted the district's reaping. After reaping a female tribute, he watched in shock as the girl slipped a snake down his daughter's dress. He ran down the steps to his daughter, helping her to her feet and handing her to a Peacekeeper to lead away. Back at the stage, he struck the female tribute in the face and was taken away by the Peacekeepers himself to calm down before being brought back to pull out the name of the district's male tribute. He was present during a hanging, along with his wife and daughter. They arrived at the hanging tree in an old car and formed a tight knot at the side of the platform. After his daughter was killed, he went to the Peacekeeper base about his daughter's murder, but he was not taken seriously. The mayor was also sure that the female tribute had killed both his daughter and another individual. He Trent his car down to a house, waiting outside for hours, and was determined to get the female tribute arrested. When she disappeared into the woods, many people suspected that the mayor himself had killed her, though they couldn't prove it. Mayor Keller was a squat, freckled man. During the reaping he wore a hopelessly outdated suit. He had a permissive attitude towards his daughter. After her murder, the opinion among Peacekeepers seemed to be that he had spoiled his daughter. Nothing is known of his relationship with his wife, save that he had one.
