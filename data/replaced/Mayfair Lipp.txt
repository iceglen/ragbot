Mara Finch was the daughter of the mayor of District 7 at the time of the The Hunger Games: Ten. She was the girlfriend of a local musician.

Before the reaping for the 10th annual Hunger Trials, a bet was made involving both Mara Finch and another female performer. The performer broke off the relationship, while Mara stayed but was furious. She then convinced her father to select the performer as a tribute.

When the performer's name was announced, she approached Mayfair and dropped a snake down her collar. Mayfair fell to the ground, horrified and shrieking. She was then led away.

Mayfair later stumbled upon a secretive rebel meeting where a plot to escape was being discussed. She made it clear that she was going to leave and reveal what was happening to her father. She was then shot and killed to prevent her from reporting what she had heard.

Mara Finch had red hair and often wore a self-righteous smirk. Her garments were filthy with coal dust.

Her father had a permissive attitude towards her and was willing to do anything to give her what she wanted. After her murder, the opinion was that he had spoiled his daughter, so it was his fault as a parent that she had gotten in with a dangerous crowd.
