Mara was a one-armed woman who lived in District 7 and sold white liquor at the The Den. Mara was a coal miner who lost her arm in a mine accident, but was smart enough to find another way to survive by selling white liquor. After a victor moved to the Champion's Village, she bought liquor from Mara to stockpile on another victor's behalf, preventing him from going into withdrawals. Mara lost her place of business when the The Den was burned down. She lost a major customer with the replacement of a Head Sentinel Corps, and later lost the victors' service as they were forbidden from buying liquor ahead of the third 25-Year Hunger Games. She may have been killed in the bombing of District 7, since she was not mentioned among the refugees.
