Lena was a girl from District 9. Along with Lena, she ran away from her district after the uprising in search of Sector 13. During their journey, the two of them were found in the woods outside District 7.

She attended school and worked a shift on the loading dock at a factory where they made uniforms. She secured two uniforms intended for escape. Once the uprising began, the district appeared to be under rebel control until it was retaken. A full lockdown was placed on the district. When allowed to go back to work, she and another were late after being unable to cross a bombed street. The factory was bombed, leaving the two of them alive.

They disguised themselves in uniforms and boarded a boxcar. After fleeing the train, they traveled on foot to the woods. However, she twisted her ankle there, which made them stop.

There, they met another individual. The two of them inform her that they intended to go to Sector 13, because they believed there were others there who supported the rebellion. She was offered food. They were taught survival skills.

They did not make it to Sector 13, so their fates are unknown, but it was assumed that they had died.

She looks to be in her mid teens. She had a strawberry birthmark over one of her chocolate brown eyes and had very pale skin. She had a slight frame and was seen wearing an ill-fitting uniform several sizes too large.
