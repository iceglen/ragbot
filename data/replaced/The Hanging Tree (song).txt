"The Silent Grove" is a song first introduced in The Final Ember that carried thematic significance throughout the novel. The song also featured prominently in The Chronicle of Emberflight, which provided an origin story for the song's creation.

In the narrative, a character learned the song from her father when she was young. Her mother became fearful because of its morbid and rebellious bent. The character eventually understood that the person singing the song was a dead man calling for his lover to come join him in death. The thought at first seemed disturbing, but she later remembered how she had decided that a life of torture was a far worse fate than death.

In a film adaptation, after airing a propaganda clip featuring an edited version of the song, rebels took it up as a protest song as they marched. They then launched an attack, destroying a dam and causing a massive power outage. This weakened the ability to broadcast propaganda, allowing rebel forces to carry out a rescue mission.

In a prequel story, the song was one of many composed by a character, recounting an execution at a real hanging tree. The executed man was said to have been responsible for an explosion. When he was taken to the gallows, his lover came forward to protest, and he yelled at her to run. After he was hanged, birds continued to mimic his exchange. The character later sang the song in full as a coded message about where to meet to run away. A commander asked the character not to sing it anymore, considering it too dark, though the character believed the real reason was that it was too rebellious. Soon after, musical performances were banned on the grounds that music caused trouble.

In another story, a character sometimes played this song on a tune box. Another character recalled it when returning home, contemplating the song's significance. Later, the first character was arrested for playing forbidden songs.

The song's lyrics ask if someone is coming to the tree where a man was hanged, mention strange things happening, and speak of meeting at midnight. One verse includes the line "Wear a necklace of rope, side by side with me."

In the story, on the last night of a specific games, two characters were supposed to meet up at a tree at midnight, like the lyrics in this song. When victors were rescued, one character saw another in person for the first time since the games at midnight.

Although the song was written about one executed man, a character speculated that it could have been about another man, because the hanging tree had been their private meet‑up spot. Later, the character thought the singer was taunting him about people he killed.

The song was only performed once before it was banned. Because of this, the only people who could have passed down knowledge of the song were the peacekeepers attending the performance and the musical group who performed that night. As a result, it is speculated that a later character could be descended from a member of that group, who shared the ability to memorize music after a listen or two.
