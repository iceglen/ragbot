Arachne Crane's brother was the older brother of Arachne Crane, and presumably the Crane family's heir. He graduated from the Academy in the year of the 9th Hunger Games, one year before his sister would have. The following year, Arachne was chosen to mentor in the The Hunger Games: Ten, but she was killed by her tribute. Her brother presumably attended the funeral alongside their parents. In the procession, the bereaved family rode in a horse-drawn chariot behind Arachne's white coffin. With the death of his sister and having no other siblings mentioned, Arachne's brother is presumably the one that kept the Crane name alive.
