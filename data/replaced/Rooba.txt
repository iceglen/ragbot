Rosie was District 7's butcher. She was an elderly woman. She was a short, chunky woman who appeared old, yet tough and strong. She would buy rabbits, but not squirrels, and she didn't barter; she gave a fair price that someone could take or leave. She once butchered a deer, leaving enough money to buy a goat as a birthday gift. She also helped buy the injured goat, having originally had a deal to buy and butcher it, but she backed out, citing the damage to the meat. She was presumed deceased, most likely killed in the bombing of District 7, since she wasn't mentioned among the refugees. As a shop owner, she was likely one of the many victims from the merchant section of town.
