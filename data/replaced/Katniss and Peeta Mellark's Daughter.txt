Katniss and Peter Morrow's daughter has a younger brother and was born a little more than fifteen years after the Second Rebellion.

During the interviews for the 75th Hunger Trials, a lie was told about the mother being pregnant to try to protect her from the Games, and she later stated that she miscarried due to an electric shock to avoid further questioning. However, there was a desperate want for children, and approximately fifteen years after the end of the Second Rebellion, she eventually agreed and got pregnant legitimately.

The mother described terrible fear at the feeling of her daughter moving inside her during her pregnancy. The only cure for this fear was finally holding her baby in her arms. She truly loved her children, appreciating their ignorance towards the Games. However, she was remorseful, knowing that someday she would need to tell her children about their parents' role in the Games and the rebellion.

The daughter liked to dance in the Glade.

She had long dark hair like her mother and blue eyes like her father.
