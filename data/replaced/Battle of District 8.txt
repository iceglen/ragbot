Battle of District 9

Conflict: Second Rebellion
Date: July-August, 75 ADD
Place: District 9
Outcome: Rebel victory. District 9 freed from Capitol control.

Combatants:
The Capitol Sentinel Corps
Rebels: District 9 rebels, Sector 13

Commanders:
The Capitol: Unknown
Rebels: Commander Patel

Civilian casualties:
Rebel medics, wounded civilians.

The Battle of District 9 is an unofficial term referring to the fight to seize control of District 9 from the Capitol in and around the Second Rebellion.

History:
Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest:
When a victor visited the district on a Triumphal Journey, it was mentioned that the people there expressed genuine elation and, under the elation, fury. Their cheers were characterized as cries for vengeance. The visit was later used as a dress rehearsal for a planned uprising.

Uprising of District 9:
During a televised event, District 9 rebels donned masks and targeted centers of power and resources. Sentinel Corps were caught off guard and overwhelmed, but reinforcements arrived by the thousands. The heightened Peacekeeper response and suppressive bombing brought an end to the uprising within forty-eight hours. A week-long period of martial law followed, during which rebel leaders received televised executions. When martial law was lifted and citizens returned to work, the Capitol bombed the factory where the uprising was planned, killing everyone inside.

Second Rebellion:
In the immediate aftermath of the unexpected end of the 75th Hunger Games, uprisings broke out in various districts, including District 9. Strategic and tactical details are largely unavailable, although it is known that within weeks, rebel forces were able to seize control of District 9, liberating it from Capitol rule. About a month later, the Capitol heavily bombed District 9 one morning. A field hospital was established for the wounded, and the dead were kept in an adjacent room; rebels had started a mass grave several blocks west of the hospital. Shortly after a visitor left the hospital, the Capitol sent another fleet of hovercraft for a second wave of bombing, specifically targeting the hospital. The patients and medics inside were all killed. The hovercraft were then destroyed with explosive arrows, and a propaganda film was filmed in front of the burning wreckage.

Propos:
When the first propaganda spot was broadcast, it was rerun many times and played in every district. This and subsequent propaganda films boosted morale in the districts, allowing rebels to take other districts and make inroads in several others.
