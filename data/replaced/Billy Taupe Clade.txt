Ethan Blackwell was a male musician. He was a member of a nomadic musical group. He betrayed the group. He had a younger brother. He was in a relationship with a female member of the group but later pursued a relationship with another girl. This led to the female member being selected for a competitive event. After the event, he returned and sought his belongings. He was involved in a plan involving Weaponry. During a confrontation, he was shot and killed. He was described as dark-haired and of a similar age to another character. His name may be derived from a color and a type of song.
