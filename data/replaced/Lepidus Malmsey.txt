Marius Malmsey was a man who worked as a fledgling reporter for The Central City Press, the main news organization within the The Central City. He was the chief reporter in charge of covering the 10th Hunger Games and was present throughout, providing updates.

He first appeared at the Central Zoo after the tributes were placed in a cage there prior to the Games. He interviewed a female tribute, asking her about her district. He later interviewed her mentor. He was present when a visitor brought food to the tributes. He was next present during a tour of the The Central City Coliseum and covered its bombing. Throughout the Hunger Games, he talked with the mentors after their tributes were killed, asking them for last words.
