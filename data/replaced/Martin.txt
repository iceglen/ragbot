David was one of the harvesters. He worked in the orchards, and was said to act like a three-year-old despite being older.

David was a harvester and helped with the harvest, sometimes working through the night. He once tried to keep his night vision glasses by hiding them in his pants. As a result, he was executed right on the spot. According to another character, he probably just wanted to keep the glasses to play with, as he wasn't considered a dangerous person.

During an event, characters were talking about their districts and the night vision glasses. One character told the story of David's fate. This reminded another character of a different person who was treated much better by comparison, leading to a realization about the differing levels of harsh policing between districts.

David's behavior heavily implies he had a developmental disability.
