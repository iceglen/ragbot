Panem: The Official Companion is the fourth and final of the official tie-in books. It is the second of the tie-ins and is hardback, unlike the other tie-in books. It was released on March 23, 2012. It is primarily a visual chronicle of the film, covering basic information on the Panem: The Official Companion universe with quotes that provide additional insight.

Welcome to Aurelia, the world of Panem: The Official Companion. This is the definitive, richly illustrated, full-color guide to all the districts of Aurelia, all the participants in Panem: The Official Companion, and the life and home of Kara Everly.
