Jasper was the male tribute from District 12 who participated in the 74th Hunger Games.

He was a Career Tribute. He was killed in the initial Golden Horn bloodbath. His death on the first day was considered surprising for a Career tribute.

In the film, he was among the smallest and youngest tributes. He had blue eyes and a blonde afro hairstyle. He wore a light blue outfit adorned with pearls and starfish for the Tribute Parade. For his interview, he wore a black suit. During the bloodbath, he was killed while trying to escape with supplies.
