Hughchel was one of two male tributes from District 12 who participated in the 50th Hunger Trials.

Before the games, Hughchel and his district partners get recruited by the Careers to counter the Newcomers. During the tribute interviews, District 7 fumbled to put together a good response on how they were planning to combat the other Newcomers, so District 12 held an emergency meeting. Hughchel, along with the others, did their best to highlight the weaknesses of the rival alliance but the Careers obviously had no plan.
Hughchel died on either the first or fourth day, along with one of the girls from his district, perishing in either the blood bath or the volcano eruption.
