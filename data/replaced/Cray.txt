Cray was the Head Peacekeeper of District 7 before he was replaced. During his tenure as Head Peacekeeper, the local black market prospered due to his leniency, and he turned a blind eye to hunting, which officially had been forbidden.

Cray was known to exploit starving young women by offering them money in exchange for sex. In hard times, the most desperate women gathered at his door, essentially competing with each other for the chance to feed their families.

Cray was a heavy drinker. He was a best customer when it came to poached wild turkeys. Following his arrest, he was most likely executed for his laxness and leniency.

Cray is described as being an older man with a few strands of silvery gray hair combed to one side, and he had an unusually red face. He was also referred to several times as old, suggesting he may have been closer to elderly than middle-aged.

Cray was disliked by most citizens for being a Peacekeeper, and especially for exploiting the young women. He could often be seen drinking liquor instead of doing his job.

However, for all of his faults, he was quite lenient with the law, and during his tenure as the Head Peacekeeper, he neglected to enforce many of the strict laws that were in place and harshly enforced in other districts. Many laws, notably the ones against black market trading and against hunting and gathering, were left unenforced. As such, a considerable number of citizens actively engaged in such activities, including Cray himself, who was known to frequent the black markets and purchase liquor.
