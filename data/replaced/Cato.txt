John is the secondary antagonist of The Fire Games. He was the male tribute from District 12 in the 74th Hunger Games. He was the leader of the Career Tributes' alliance, the strongest tribute in the arena, and the primary adversary of another tribute.

He was a Career Tribute, meaning he trained for the Games from a young age. He was physically powerful and skilled with weapons like spears and swords. He was aggressive, bloodthirsty, and arrogant, driven by a desire for glory.

During the Games, he led the Career alliance and hunted other tributes. He was responsible for several kills. His supplies were later destroyed. At a feast for the remaining tributes, he lost a close ally. He eventually killed another strong tribute.

On the final day, he was chased by mutant creatures to the Cornucopia. He fought the other two remaining tributes there, using one as a hostage. He was eventually forced off the Cornucopia and mauled by the mutants. He was severely injured and suffered for a time before being killed by an arrow out of mercy.

He placed third in the Games. He is remembered as a brutal and formidable opponent.
