"Horn of Plenty" is the national anthem. It is played during the Games, in public announcements, and in other official events or propaganda. During the Games, it is played every night throughout the arena while the faces of dead tributes are displayed.

The lyrics are:

Oh, Horn of Plenty
One Horn of Plenty for us all!
And when you raise the cry
The brave shall heed the call
And we shall never falter
One Horn of Plenty for us all!

Oh, Horn of Plenty.
One Horn of Plenty for us all!
And when we raise the cry
The brave shall heed the call  
And we shall never fall
