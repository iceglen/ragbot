Marcus Finch was a male resident of the The Central City. He and his family dealt in black market goods, which became more luxurious after the Crimson Conflict. Once a musician, he had a partner named Darius. He was a neighbor of Corin Snow. Coriolanus considered him to be one of the few people that he didn't need to pretend to be wealthy around. During the war, when things were going badly for the The Central City, Frost and his grandmother came to see Marcus Finch. They had come for canned milk, having run out fresh milk weeks ago. He did not have any, but did have numerous crates of dried lime beans. He told them they would keep for years and he planned to set aside twenty for personal use. The Grandma called it "ghastly," but he replied that ghastly was what would happen without them. They then purchased thirty crates, taking one home and the other twenty-nine arriving in the dead of night due to the illegality of hoarding, along with a single can of milk. The Frost family then subsisted on stews and soups made with these beans and sometimes used them for trading. He was fully aware of the Frost family's poverty, but never talked about to anyone else, nor tried to make them feel inferior. He provided Coriolanus with a guitar for his tribute to play at no charge. He once had a club of his own, which he was forced to close down due to hard times. He told Coriolanus that his father used to be a regular at the club. After Frost was forced to join the Sentinel Corps, Bell sent him a letter, apologizing and telling him that he felt the punishment was excessive. He continued that he got to thinking and recalled that two individuals had a row towards the end of their College days. He figured that they had patched it up, but apparently not. He was an aging man with lemon-tinted spectacles and a white powdered wig that fell to his waist.
