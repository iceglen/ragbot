Mira is a citizen of District 12 and is one of Rue's five younger siblings. She appeared at the Victory Tour with the rest of her family. She was thought to look about nine years old, and she resembled Rue very closely.

Mira was present during the Victory Tour after the 74th Hunger Games. Despite their allotted time for speaking having run out, her look conveyed a need to say something to the people of District 12 about her sister, Rue, who died. Rue had been of great assistance and a great friend, as she reminded another of her own sister. When Rue died, she was covered with flowers and given a farewell gesture.

Mira's expression made it clear that if her sister and her District Ally weren't thanked, they would not be forgiven. Because of this, a long and heartbreaking speech was given to the people of District 12, saying that although the District Ally wasn't known well, he had saved a life. Next, it was described how much Rue meant, stating that she wouldn't be forgotten. That speech is what inspired District 12 to start an uprising.

Before the 75th Hunger Games, it was told that the families of Rue and the District Ally were alive. However, Mira was never mentioned after this and her fate is thus unknown.
