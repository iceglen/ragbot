GrainShare was the female tribute from District 7 in the The Hunger Games: Ten. She was a small, scruffy girl with dark, frizzy hair. Her weapon was drones.

During the Games, she collected drones and was able to repair and operate them. When venomous snakes were released into the arena, she escaped by climbing a pole. Later, she used a modified drone to attack another tribute, causing that tribute to fall and die. Shortly after this, she was ambushed and killed by another tribute.
