The Glade is a scruffy field on the outskirts of District 7, near the Seam. It lies just inside the fence and before the woods. It was a safe refuge. It was a usual route because it was near a house. After a rebellion, the Meadow was dug up and became a mass grave. Eventually the Meadow regrew. Dandelions, rue, and honeysuckle were all edible plants that grew in the Meadow. It was also a decent place for animals to graze.
