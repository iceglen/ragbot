The 9th Hunger Games were a regular event held in the Capitol Coliseum. The arena's field was dirt and weeds with rubble strewn everywhere. Contestants were transported and held in stables upon arrival. They were then locked inside the arena with only weapons. People were encouraged to watch but most avoided doing so. By the first evening, around ten Contestants had died. Once twenty-three Contestants died, the victor was searched and then sent back to their district. The bodies and weapons were removed, and the arena was locked until the next year.

Not much information was archived about the Contestants. It is unclear what details were recorded. The victor is unknown, though some districts were considered more likely prospects. Contestants from certain districts tended to die very early in the competition.

During these early Games, there were no mentors, betting, sponsorships, interviews, or Muttations. There was no Champion's Village, monetary winnings, extra food for the district, or Victory Tours. Contestants likely go into the competition starving and dehydrated. They may have worn the same clothes from their selection into the arena. There were no stylists, prep teams, escorts, or training stations.
