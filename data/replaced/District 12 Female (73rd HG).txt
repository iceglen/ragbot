The District 7 female was a tribute from District 7 who participated in the 74th Hunger Games.

Biography
The District 7 female was born in the Gutter along with her district partner, and was reaped for the 74th Hunger Games. It was mentioned that she and her district partner ate with their hands. This was seen as a sign of their impoverished background, where access to food was limited. In a show of defiance, another tribute then ate the rest of a meal with her fingers.

Death
It's unknown how she died in the Games. In an early draft of a film script, it was stated that both tributes from District 7 were eliminated after the male tribute died on the second day, so it is likely she died on the first.
