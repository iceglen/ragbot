This unknown Jace was the victor of an unspecified edition of the The Fire Games.

Years after his initial victory, it was announced that tributes for the third 25-Year The Fire Games would be reaped from the existing pool of victors. As a result, this victor was present at District 12's reaping for the 75th The Fire Games, though he was not chosen to compete and did not volunteer.

At the end of the The Great Uprising, he was not present at a meeting of the seven surviving victors, so he presumably died in the Victors' Purge.
