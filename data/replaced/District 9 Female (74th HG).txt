The Mira (District 9 tribute) was the female tribute from District 9 who participated in the 70th Hunger Games.

In the film, she is first seen in the Tribute Parade with her District Ally, wearing a silver studded outfit and a silver half-circle behind her, and waving to the crowd.

The Mira (District 9 tribute) was killed in the initial Golden Horn bloodbath. Her pedestal was the farthest to the left.

In the film, she was 14 years old. In the arena, she wore a bottle green jacket. She is seen in the sky when the fallen images are shown. Her face was also the last one seen on day one.
