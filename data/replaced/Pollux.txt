Rex is an Mute Servant who escaped his slavery and joined the rebels as a cameraman. He was forced to work in the city's underground utility corridors and the Transfer. He began to work as a cameraman. His experience working underground made him very knowledgeable about the underground, so he was able to guide a squad through the passages. He survived the war and filmed war damage in the postwar period.

Rex is described as a burly man with sandy hair, a red beard, and deep blue eyes. He has no tongue, as it was cut off when he became an Mute Servant.
