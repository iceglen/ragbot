This pearl was found inside an oyster while diving for seafood in the 75th Hunger Games arena. It was given as a gift and a promise of protection. Although a small token, the pearl was a clear message of love, and it was kept close and constantly protected. While residing in a district following a rescue, it was kept in a drawer that held clothing and few possessions.

After a plan was made to shock the water in the arena, the tributes decided to make a feast of seafood. This included diving for oysters, and when one was pried open, the pearl was found. It was given with the words "for you", and the gift was closely guarded, thought to be the only gift that could be truly accepted. There was a belief that if the giver died, the pearl would be returned to them.

The pearl was later brought to another district, where it was looked at in the middle of the night. It was kept in a drawer inside a Gold parachute that also held another item received in the arena. Just before a bombing, it was retrieved along with other personal belongings.
The pearl was also brought on a mission to assassinate a leader; it was kept in a pocket in a uniform throughout a battle. When keys were dropped into the pocket, they were heard clinking together. After that, the pearl was returned to a house, along with other belongings.
