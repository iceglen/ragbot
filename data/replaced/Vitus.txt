Silas was a member of the prep team for a tribute during the 50th Hunger Games. He was a university student. He grew up in the Capitol and was a student at the time of the Games. He and another prep team member entered the tribute's cubicle to begin grooming him. He reassured the tribute about the stylist. He and the other member photographed the tribute, then shaved his body, trimmed his nails, and treated his hair. In preparation for the interviews, he and the other member went to the stylist's apartment but found him ill. During the Triumph Ceremony, he and the other member fussed over each other's makeup before being introduced. At the after-party, they became tipsy and stopped to congratulate the victor. He had metal apples studding his cheeks and never wore black.
