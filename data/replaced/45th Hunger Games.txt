The 45th Hunger Games took place thirty years before the events of Fires of Hunger. These Games were won by the male tribute from District 12.

Sometime during his Games, the victor lost his hand. It was thought that the Capitol probably offered him a prosthesis, but he must have refused it.

The victor seemed to depend on alcohol to cope in the aftermath of his Games. He could often be seen passing a bottle back and forth with another victor.
