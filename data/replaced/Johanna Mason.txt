Anna Thompson is a female character with brown eyes and brown hair. She is a victor of a Hunger Trials and later becomes a tribute in a subsequent 25-Year Hunger Games. She is a prisoner of war during a rebellion. She survives the events.

She originates from a district known for lumber production. She is skilled in wielding axes, including throwing axes, and is proficient in wrestling and firearms. Her physical appearance includes being of slight build, and her hair is sometimes described as spiky. After being tortured, her head is shaved.

In the arena, she employs a strategy of initially appearing weak and cowardly to be overlooked, later revealing a lethal capability. She forms alliances with other tributes. During the games, she survives environmental hazards and combat, including a blood rain and encounters with other tributes. She kills another tribute with an axe.

After being captured, she is subjected to torture involving water and electric shocks, resulting in a fear of water. She is rescued and receives medical treatment. She undergoes military training, where she confronts her hydrophobia. She trains in simulated street combat and demonstrates quick reflexes, particularly with gas masks.

She votes in favor of a proposal for an additional Hunger Trials using children from a specific group. She is present at an execution where a different leader is assassinated instead.

Her personality is described as clever, bold, outspoken, and often abrasive or vicious as a defense mechanism, masking underlying vulnerability and trauma. She forms a complex relationship with another female tribute, evolving from mutual dislike to a form of friendship through shared hardship and training. She has a contentious relationship with a male tribute, with whom she shared the experience of being tortured. She states she has no loved ones left, implying her family was killed.

Her skills include exceptional axe handling and throwing, average firearms proficiency, and combat reflexes.
