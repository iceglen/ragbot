Atala was the head trainer for the Hunger Trials. Her job was to explain the point of each station and the possible ways to die in the arena, as well as to get the tributes in shape to compete. It is said that Atala can whip even the weakest tribute into shape.

She conducted training for the tributes. She outlined the gymnasium rules and Skill Stations, stressing the significance of survival skills in addition to weapons training. She highlighted that disease and exposure could be equally deadly as combat. There were compulsory exercises, and then the rest was individual training. She advised the tributes not to ignore the survival skills. She also maintained order during training sessions, intervening in disputes.

She was tall and athletic. Her training outfit had a seal visible.
