The Sector 13 head doctor is a doctor who specialized in mental health care in Sector 13.

This doctor spent his whole life in Sector 13. Following the rebels' extraction of captured victors from the Capitol, he took on a client while she was recovering from her torture. He came by for daily sessions to help her recover. Because of his hijacking, another patient was only treated by Sector 13 natives, so this doctor may have been a part of his recovery team as well.

When the client sought discharge from the hospital, she was denied the opportunity to live alone even if she kept up daily sessions with her head doctor. With convincing from others, the client was approved to live in a compartment soon after.

According to the client, her head doctor was right about one thing: "There's no going back" to the people they were before the Hunger Games. She also quoted him as warning her against censoring her thoughts, as a part of her therapy.
