Trevor was the male tribute from District 12 in the 10th Hunger Games. His age was between 12 and 18. He was also known as Treacherous Trevor and Lumberjack. His weapon was an ax. His fate was deceased.

He became his district's male tribute in the Reaping for the 10th Hunger Games. On the first day of the Games, he stole water from another tribute. He emerged after his district partner died, by taking her ax, now armed with a weapon. He later attempted to kill other tributes but they ran. He looked at a snake tank dropped into the arena. When the tank burst, he ran right away, which saved his life. He managed to climb out onto a narrow shelf.

His first kill was another tribute while she was distracted. He dug his ax into her skull. He then collected parcels of food and withdrew behind a barricade. He later tried to kill another tribute on the final day of the Games, but failed.

When he attempted to attack, he swung out from behind the barricade with his ax and caught her wrist. However, she dodged the blow, diving towards him and pulling him into what appeared like an embrace. In the process, she dropped a Mutt Serpents down his shirt, and it killed him as he smashed it into the ground. He reached third place.

He had black hair. His name comes from the word "tree." He was mainly reliant on other tributes throughout the Games. He has the most failed attempts at killing other tributes. However, he proves to be quite sneaky and deadly despite not grabbing a weapon at the Games's start. His betrayal of his district partner even seemed to be looked down upon.
