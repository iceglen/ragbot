Tena was one of two female tributes from District 12 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games.

Before the games, Tena and her district partners were recruited by the Careers to counter the Newcomers. Tena died along with a boy from her district on either the first day during the blood‑bath or from the volcanic eruption on the fourth day.

Tena is a name that could be associated with the concept of electrical potential, referring to potential difference in electrical systems.
