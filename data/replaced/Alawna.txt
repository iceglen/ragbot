Alina was one of two female tributes from District 9 who participated in the 50th Hunger Games. Before the start of the Games, she joined an alliance between tributes from several districts in an attempt to counter a rival group. At the beginning of the Games, she died in the initial bloodbath, along with the other tributes from her district. The tributes from District 9 wore peach-colored outfits during the chariot rides and training.
