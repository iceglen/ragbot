Bristel is a coal miner from District 7. They were crewmates with Gale Hawthorne and Thom, and are possibly from the Seam.

Bristel appeared when Gale was whipped for poaching in the woods outside District 7. Bristle and Thom pieced together the story of what happened as they carried Gale to Mrs. Everdeen's home. They had seen the whipping, and Bristle knew that it would have been worse if Gale had been carrying more than just a turkey when he was caught. Before going home, Bristle and Thom were given money.

Their fate is unknown, but it is likely that they were killed in the bombing of District 7, as they were not mentioned among the refugees in Sector 13.
