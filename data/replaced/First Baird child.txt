The Eli Baird was the eldest child of Mairi MacLeod and father, as well as the older sibling of an unnamed child and Emma James.

This child was a member of the Covey, a nomadic people not tied to any one place. They were the oldest of three children, and as Emma James's older sibling, they were born at least four years before the Crimson Conflict. They were also a cousin of Mara Hayes and Mara Lila Brant.

A few years prior to certain events, them, their family, and a number of other Covey were rounded up. Their parents were both killed for trying to resist, and this child and their older sibling also died. They may have been killed during the roundup or died in another way sometime soon after, as the exact circumstances are never stated. Ultimately, Emma James was the only member of her immediate family to survive.
