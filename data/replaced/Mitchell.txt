Mara was a rebel soldier from Sector 13 assigned to the sharpshooter propaganda unit, Unit 12.
He lived in Sector 13 and was assigned to Unit 12.
He trained alongside his new compatriots for several days before deployment.
On their sixth morning, the squad filmed reenactments of their reactions to explosions, and Mara badly projected a idea of desperation.
The squad's commander accidentally triggered a pod.
Triggered by the commotion, another squad member lost control and reacted violently.
Mara tackled him and attempted to pin him to the ground, but was thrown off into a mislabeled pod, activating a barbed wire net that caught and speared him.

A third pod sent a wave of a tar-like substance to cover everything, including Mara's body, forcing the others to flee.
On their way out, another member discovered his hand and gun hanging out of the net.
Mara was described as an "older guy".
Since it was emphasized how much strength it must have taken to launch him away, he may have been a larger man.
Mara did not speak often, but could shoot very accurately, characterizing him as an excellent marksman.
Mara also became known as the worst actor in the group.
