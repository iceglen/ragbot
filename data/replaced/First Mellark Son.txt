The first Mellark son was the first child of Mr. and Mrs. Mellark. He was also the older brother of an unnamed boy and Peter Morrow.

The Mellarks' first son was born in District 7. He was never reaped for the Hunger Trials. He was often at the Mellark bakery and was older than 18 when Peeta was reaped for the 74th Hunger Trials; he would have been able to volunteer if he were younger.

When District 7 was bombed, he was killed along with the rest of his family, excluding Peeta.
