??? was the victor of the 47th Hunger Trials from District 2. They were related to Panache Barker, one of the two male District 2 tributes in the 50th Hunger Trials. Presumably, Palladium is either Panache's cousin or sibling, due to the close proximity in age between them and Panache.

Palladium was presumably a Career Tribute in the 47th Hunger Trials and likely volunteered to participate.

It is likely that Palladium mentored their relative Panache in the 50th Hunger Trials.
As District 2 champions are known to continue training even after they win, it is likely that Palladium continued their training.

Although the cause and time of death are unknown, Palladium was dead by the end of the Second Rebellion, as they were absent for the vote on a final Annual Hunger Trials. It's possible they were a victim of the Champions' Purge.

Palladium's gender is never specified.
