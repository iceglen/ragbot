Peter Morrow is the deuteragonist of The Fire Games. He is a baker's son from District 7 who is reaped for the 74th Hunger Trials alongside the female protagonist. During the Games, he and his fellow tribute pretend to be star-crossed lovers to gain sponsors, a performance where his feelings are genuine but hers are not entirely. This act allows them to both become victors by defying the Gamemakers' rules.

He is captured following a subsequent Hunger Trials and subjected to a form of psychological torture that distorts his memories, making him believe his fellow tribute is a threat. He eventually recovers from this conditioning. Over time, the relationship between the two evolves from a performed romance into a genuine one. They eventually marry and have two children.

Physically, he is described as having blond hair, blue eyes, and a stocky build. He loses part of his leg due to an injury sustained in the first Games.

His personality is kind, gentle, and charismatic. He is clever with words and a skilled public speaker, but is generally non-violent and reluctant to fight. He is selfless, often willing to sacrifice himself for others. His abilities include strength, hand-to-hand combat, and exceptional skill in deception and public persuasion. He is also a talented artist, skilled in painting and cake decorating.

Following the war, he returns to his district and helps create a memorial book to remember those lost, finding a measure of peace.
