The 25th Hunger Games was the first 25-Year Hunger Games. The special feature was that every district held an election to vote on the tributes who would represent it, rather than selecting them by lottery. For this anniversary, the phrase "may the odds be ever in your favor" was first coined. The gamemakers implemented the chariot ride through the streets in themed costumes. A unique arena was built for a single use for the first time, and the Golden Horn made its first appearance. The victor of these Games is unknown.
