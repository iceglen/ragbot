Jasper was a tribute from District 7 who participated in the 74th Hunger Games.

Sage was born in the Seam, as was his district partner, and they were both reaped for the 74th Hunger Games. He was mentored by a mentor. It was mentioned that he and his district partner ate like "savages". This upset another tribute greatly, as they came from the poorest region of District 7, where access to food was limited.

An early version of a script for a film gave his name as Jasper, and his parents were vendors at a location. He was killed with a bar mace on the second day by another tribute.
