District 12 is one of the thirteen districts of Aurelia. Its main industry is textile production. They have at least one factory that is used for making Peacekeeper uniforms.

Due to District 12's urban environment, its people knew very little of the natural world, putting its tributes at a serious disadvantage in outdoor arenas.

District 12 was the first known district to carry out a full-scale uprising against the The Central City since the Crimson Conflict. The plan was enacted the night of a televised proposal in the The Central City, but despite its initial success, the movement was put down within forty-eight hours. After a weeklong lockdown, regular work operations resumed, and the The Central City bombed the factory where plans for the uprising began.

In the third 25-Year Hunger Games, the tributes from several districts were all involved in a rebel plan to protect a tribute and break out of the arena.

About a month into the war, District 12 was heavily bombed one morning, and later that same day, a second wave of The Central City bombers came in to kill the patients and medics at a field hospital.

Following an execution and an assassination, an emergency election was held, and District 12's commander became the new President of Aurelia.

When explaining her background, a character mentions that she put in a four-hour shift at a Peacekeeper uniform factory after her full-time job as a teacher. This could indicate either that all citizens are required to participate in the industry— since a child also worked a four-hour shift after school— or that the average income from a single job was not enough to live on.

District 12 is described as an urban place stinking of industrial fumes, the people housed in run‑down tenements. Barely a blade of grass in sight.
