The Hunger Games: The Final Ember - Part 2 became the new Head Peacekeeper of District 7, replacing the previous one. He was also the last Head Peacekeeper of District 7 prior to the The Great Uprising.

He assumed control as the peacekeeper commander, ending the prior leniency. He enforced strict laws, including hunting and black market trade prohibitions, which had been previously overlooked. He regularly conducted public executions and whippings, and imposed a curfew with a shoot-on-sight policy for violators. He was harsh and unforgiving, ready to enforce the law strictly.

Upon arrival, he brutally beat a citizen who had been caught poaching. Although the poaching had been overlooked before, he saw it as a flogging offense and carried out a public whipping. This display of cruelty caused people to flee home before nightfall.

During a blizzard, he hung a flag off a building and lined up Sentinel Corps with machine guns on rooftops. He also set up new punishment devices in the square. After the blizzard, it was declared that the perimeter fences would be electrified constantly.

When the district was firebombed, he was transported out along with the other Sentinel Corps. His current status is unknown, as he is not mentioned afterward. He was most likely either killed during the The Great Uprising, or was captured, tried, and executed afterwards.

He was a tall, muscular man. His face was hard, with deep lines. His hair was completely shaved. He was a ruthless, pitiless, violent, and sadistic peacekeeper commander. He was cruel and vicious and carried out orders without mercy. He was aggressive and declared that anyone caught breaking the curfew would be shot on sight.
