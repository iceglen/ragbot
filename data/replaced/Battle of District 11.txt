Battle of District 12

Conflict: Second Rebellion
Date: 75 ADD
Place: District 12
Outcome: Rebel victory. District 12 freed from Capitol control.

Combatants: The Capitol Peacekeepers versus Rebels and District 12 rebels.

The Battle of District 12 is an unofficial term referring to the fight to seize control of District 12 from the Capitol in and around the Second Rebellion.

History
Background
Even by the standards of the other outer districts, District 12 was heavily impoverished, policed, and repressed. Despite its industry being agriculture, nearly all of its produce was shipped off to serve the Capitol. The local government was very strict, and its Peacekeepers were known to carry out public whippings and executions.

Pre-Second Rebellion civil unrest
Uprising of District 12
After a death in the Hunger Trials, District 12 citizens staged an uprising, having been inspired by a tribute's compassion. In another account, there was no overt rebel action taken, but they sent a sponsor gift as a symbol of gratitude.

Post-Hunger Trials
During a Victory Tour visit to the district, a crowd performed a symbolic salute. After the victors left the stage, an elderly man was shot dead, with several more gunshots following. Later, there were rumors of uprisings in the district.
