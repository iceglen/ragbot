A rose garden lies within the confined walls of a mansion. It was where discussions took place regarding events and tactics for a competition.

The garden is located at the end of a short hallway, behind glass doors. It contains many rows of roses in various colors. The scent of the roses is sweet and incredibly overpowering, and it has a lingering effect. The smell is so strong it begins to flatten out, as if no more can be absorbed. The damp, mild air feels good on hot skin. The roses are glorious, with row after row of sumptuous blooms in lush pink, sunset orange, and pale blue. One must be careful not to touch, as these beauties can be deadly.

The garden was once maintained on the roof of a home, with roses kept both outdoors and in a small solar greenhouse. During a rebellion, a figure was chained to a chair in the center of the garden for a discussion. A white rose was taken from the garden and later worn over a heart. The scent of the roses is often associated with a powerful, haunting presence. A rose from the garden was also left in a home after a bombing.
