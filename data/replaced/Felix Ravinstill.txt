The Hunger Games was a great-nephew of the President and a student at the The Central City's prestigious Academy.
He was one of the school's 24 top-performing seniors selected to be mentors for the The Hunger Games: Ten.
He was assigned the female tribute from District 12.

He tried to promote his tribute during the interviews, but his tribute's sickly disposition gave him little opportunity to make an impact.
He seemed to know the dean of the Academy well.
His tribute placed fourteenth, passing away from complications of tuberculosis shortly after entering the arena.
He commented that he was surprised that she had lasted the day when he saw her emerge, sending her two bottles of water, although they were stolen by another tribute.
He was graceful in defeat and was complimented excessively by an interviewer.

In a film adaptation, he was the son of the president.
He was hospitalized and in critical condition after the bombing of the The Central City Coliseum, succumbing to his injuries during the Games only a few days later.
An image of his body was televised during the broadcast, and his death was the justification for sending Mutt Serpents into the arena.
